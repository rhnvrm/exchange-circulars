## Summary

In view of current and evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has announced a Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) for an amount of ₹50,000 crore with a tenor of 4 days, scheduled for November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Auction Type:** Variable Rate Repo (VRR)
- **Notified Amount:** ₹50,000 crore
- **Tenor:** 4 days
- **Window Timing:** 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM on November 09, 2026
- **Date of Reversal:** November 13, 2026 (Friday)
- **Operational Guidelines:** As per RBI Press Release 2021-2022/1572 dated January 20, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine liquidity management operation under the existing LAF framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible market participants must adhere to the operational guidelines specified in RBI Press Release 2021-2022/1572 for bidding during the specified window.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** November 09, 2026 (Window: 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM)
- **Reversal Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Helps absorb or manage short-term liquidity fluctuations in the banking system through market-based variable rate repo operations.
