## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued a notification regarding updates made by the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions Committee. Regulated financial entities are required to comply with Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, by ensuring they maintain no accounts or business relations with the listed individuals and entities.

## Key Points

- Updates enacted by the UNSC Committee pursuant to resolution 1988 (2011) regarding amendments to 05 entries on the Taliban Sanctions List (press release SC/16494 dated October 29, 2026).
- Affected individuals include Abdul Latif Mansoor, Abdul Ghani Baradar, Amir Khan Muttaqi, Abdul-Haq Wasseq, and Khairullah Khairkhwah.
- Regulated entities must verify that no accounts exist in the names of the listed individuals/entities.
- De-listing requests are to be forwarded electronically to the Joint Secretary (CTCR), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA).

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendments to 05 entries on the UN 1988 Taliban Sanctions List, including passport number updates and other identifying details.

## Compliance Requirements

- Screen existing databases and customer lists against the updated UNSC Taliban Sanctions List.
- Strictly adhere to Chapter IX of the RBI Know Your Customer (KYC) Directions, 2025 and the UAPA Order dated February 02, 2021 (amended on April 22, 2024).
- Forward any de-listing requests received from customers electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA.

## Important Dates

- UNSC Press Release Date: October 29, 2026
- Circular Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for banks and financial institutions to update sanction screening systems and ensure immediate compliance with asset freeze and restriction mandates.
