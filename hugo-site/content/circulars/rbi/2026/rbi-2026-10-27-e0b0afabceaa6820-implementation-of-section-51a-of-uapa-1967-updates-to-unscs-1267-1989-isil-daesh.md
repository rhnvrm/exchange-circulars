## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council’s (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967. Three entries have been removed from the sanctions list following conclusion of delisting requests by the Committee.

## Key Points

- Removal of 03 individual entries from the UNSC ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List: Shafi Sultan Mohammed Sultan Al-Ajmi (QDi.338), Bassam Ahmad Al-Hasri (QDi.399), and Iyad Nazmi Salih Khalil (QDi.400).
- Asset freeze, travel ban, and arms embargo no longer apply to these deleted entries.
- Regulated entities must update their screening systems and databases in accordance with Chapter IX of the RBI Know Your Customer (KYC) Directions, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List reflecting the removal of three individuals as communicated via MEA and UNSC press release SC/16488 dated October 26, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities (Commercial Banks, NBFCs, FIs, etc.) must ensure that their customer databases and screening mechanisms reflect the delisting.
- Verify whether any accounts or assets were previously frozen for these individuals and take appropriate action in accordance with Chapter IX of the RBI KYC Directions, 2025 and the UAPA Order dated February 2, 2021.
- Forward any requests for de-listing received from customers electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- UNSC Press Release Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to updating compliance screening systems and releasing any restrictions on the specified delisted individuals if applicable, ensuring adherence to anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CFT) frameworks.
