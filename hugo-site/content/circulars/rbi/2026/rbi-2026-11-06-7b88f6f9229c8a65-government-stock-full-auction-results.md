## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has released the full auction results for the Government Stock sale featuring 7.06% GS 2041 and 7.43% GS 2076 with notified amounts of ₹23,000 crore and ₹13,000 crore respectively.

## Key Points

- **7.06% GS 2041**: Notified amount ₹23,000 crore; competitive bids received amounted to ₹52,879.800 crore across 387 bids; cut-off price established at ₹97.07 (YTM: 7.3881%); weighted average price at ₹97.11 (WAY: 7.3835%).
- **7.43% GS 2076**: Notified amount ₹13,000 crore; competitive bids received amounted to ₹20,751.000 crore across 144 bids; cut-off price established at ₹95.70 (YTM: 7.7708%); weighted average price at ₹96.07 (WAY: 7.7403%).
- **Non-Competitive Bids**: ₹80.036 crore accepted for 2041 stock and ₹8.107 crore for 2076 stock.
- **Devolvement**: Nil devolvement on primary dealers for both securities.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine market operations announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants outside standard settlement obligations for successful bidders.
