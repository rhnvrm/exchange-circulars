## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List, pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967. The updates involve amendments to 03 entries (individuals: Emilie Edwige Konig, Oumar Diaby, and Peter Cherif).

## Key Points

- Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) communicated UNSC press release SC/16490 dated October 26, 2026.
- Amendments made to 03 entries on the ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Regulated entities must ensure compliance with Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 and RBI Master Directions on KYC.
- Entities must verify that no accounts are held by individuals or entities appearing on the updated sanctions lists.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates and amendments to details (such as aliases, passport numbers, national identification numbers, addresses, and status of detention) for three listed individuals: Emilie Edwige Konig (QDi.340), Oumar Diaby (QDi.342), and Peter Cherif (QDi.376).

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities (Commercial Banks, SFBs, Payments Banks, UCBs, StCBs, RRBs, LABs, NBFCs, ARCs, and AIFIs) must screen customer databases against the updated UNSC sanctions list.
- Immediately freeze funds, financial assets, or economic resources of designated individuals/entities if identified.
- Follow the procedure laid down in the UAPA Order dated February 2, 2021 (amended April 22, 2024).
- De-listing requests received by banks must be forwarded electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- UNSC Press Release Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High compliance impact requiring immediate database screening and freezing mechanisms update across all regulated financial entities to prevent violation of UAPA provisions and international sanctions.
