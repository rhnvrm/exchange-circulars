## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026, effective from October 01, 2026. The amendments streamline the Export Declaration Form (EDF) submission process for service exports and revise IDPMS entry requirements for service imports.

## Key Points

- Introduces a formal definition for "resident individual" as a natural person resident in India.
- Streamlines EDF submission for resident individuals exporting services upon providing payment receipt information to Authorized Dealers.
- Sets a threshold of ₹10 Lakh for inward remittances where non-resident individual/other exporters are deemed to have submitted the EDF.
- Modifies IDPMS entry requirements for import of services by entities other than resident individuals for invoice or contract values exceeding ₹10 Lakh.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Definition Added:** Regulation 2(1)(da) defines "resident individual" as a natural person resident in India.
- **EDF Submission (Exports):** Resident individuals exporting services are deemed to have submitted the EDF when providing payment receipt information to the Authorized Dealer. For entities other than resident individuals, EDF submission is deemed complete for inward remittances up to ₹10 Lakh.
- **IDPMS Entries (Imports):** Authorized Dealers must enter import of service details in IDPMS for importers other than resident individuals where the invoice or contract value exceeds ₹10 Lakh within five working days.

## Compliance Requirements

Exporters and importers must adhere to the revised EDF submission procedures and IDPMS reporting thresholds as applicable to resident individuals and corporate entities.

## Important Dates

- **Notification Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reduces compliance and reporting friction for resident individuals and smaller service exports/imports while maintaining robust tracking for higher-value transactions.
