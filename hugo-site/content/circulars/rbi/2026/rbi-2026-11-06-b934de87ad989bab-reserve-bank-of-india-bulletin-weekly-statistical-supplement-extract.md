## Summary

This Weekly Statistical Supplement from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for the week ended October 30, 2026, provides key financial data including RBI's liabilities and assets, foreign exchange reserves, scheduled commercial banks' business in India, money stock components and sources, and liquidity operations.

## Key Points

- **Total Foreign Exchange Reserves:** Stood at US$ 717,056 million (₹ 6,808,728 crore) as on October 30, 2026, registering a decrease of US$ 2,691 million over the week.
- **Foreign Currency Assets:** Decreased by US$ 2,088 million to US$ 591,118 million.
- **Gold Reserves:** Decreased by US$ 559 million to US$ 102,591 million.
- **Scheduled Commercial Banks (as on Oct 15, 2026):** Aggregate deposits stood at ₹ 2,78,75,910 crore (down 1.8% over the fortnight), while bank credit stood at ₹ 2,27,42,777 crore (down 1.1% over the fortnight).
- **Money Stock (M3):** Stood at ₹ 3,33,00,300 crore as on October 15, 2026, recording a year-on-year growth of 16.0%.
- **Liquidity Operations:** RBI conducted variable rate repo and reverse repo operations, managing net liquidity injections and absorptions through the Liquidity Adjustment Facility and Standing Facilities.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; data update reflects ongoing statistical reporting and the implementation of the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 (revision of fortnight definition to 15th and last calendar day of a month w.e.f. December 15, 2025).

## Compliance Requirements

- Regular periodic reporting and compliance under existing RBI statutory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Data reference dates: October 30, 2026 (for Reserves and RBI assets/liabilities) and October 15, 2026 (for SCBs business and Money Stock).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency into banking sector liquidity, money supply expansion, and foreign exchange reserve dynamics, supporting macroeconomic monitoring.
