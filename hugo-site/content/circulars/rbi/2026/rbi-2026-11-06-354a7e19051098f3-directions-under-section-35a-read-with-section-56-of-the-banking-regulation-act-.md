## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the validity of the directions issued under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, to Sri Guru Raghavendra Sahakara Bank Niyamitha, Bengaluru. The directive is extended for another six months from the close of business on November 10, 2026, to the close of business on May 10, 2027, subject to review.

## Key Points

- The original directive was issued on January 02, 2020, vide Directive No. DoS.CO.UCB.BSD-III.D-2/12.23.283/2019-20.
- The current extension period runs from November 10, 2026, to May 10, 2027.
- The extension does not imply that RBI is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.
- All other terms and conditions of the original directive remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of regulatory directions and operational restrictions imposed under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

## Compliance Requirements

- Sri Guru Raghavendra Sahakara Bank Niyamitha must maintain compliance with all stipulated restrictions and regulatory oversight measures outlined in the directive.

## Important Dates

- Extension Start Date: Close of business on November 10, 2026
- Extension End Date: Close of business on May 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Ongoing supervisory controls and restrictions continue to apply to the bank's operations, protecting depositor interests while regulatory evaluation continues.
