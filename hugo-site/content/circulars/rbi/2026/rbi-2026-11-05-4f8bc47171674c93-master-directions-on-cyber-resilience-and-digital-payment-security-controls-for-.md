## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank Payment System Operators (PSOs), setting rigorous standards for cybersecurity, governance, and operational resilience in digital payments.

## Key Points

- Mandates comprehensive cyber resilience frameworks for non-bank Payment System Operators.
- Establishes baseline security controls for digital payment transactions and infrastructure.
- Requires robust incident management, reporting, and risk mitigation mechanisms.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduces binding regulatory standards and security controls specifically designed to safeguard non-bank payment systems against emerging cyber threats.

## Compliance Requirements

Non-bank PSOs are required to audit, implement, and maintain the prescribed digital payment security controls and cyber resilience measures.

## Important Dates

Published in accordance with RBI notification schedules.

## Impact Assessment

Significantly strengthens the security posture and consumer trust across non-bank payment ecosystems.
