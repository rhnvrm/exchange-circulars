## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) for a total notified amount of ₹23,000 crore across 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day tenors.

## Key Points

- **91-Day Treasury Bill:** Notified amount of ₹8,000 crore.
- **182-Day Treasury Bill:** Notified amount of ₹8,000 crore.
- **364-Day Treasury Bill:** Notified amount of ₹7,000 crore.
- **Auction Method:** Price-based auction using the multiple price method.
- **Platform:** Bidding via RBI's Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber system).
- **Retail Participation:** Eligible retail investors can participate on a non-competitive basis up to 5% of the notified amount through the Retail Direct portal.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted under the General Notification F.No.4(2)-B(W&M)/2018 dated March 26, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible participants including State Governments, Union Territories, provident funds, foreign central banks, and authorized institutions/individuals must submit bids in electronic format on the E-Kuber system within specified timings.

## Important Dates

- **Competitive Bidding Timing:** November 11, 2026, 10:30 am - 11:30 am
- **Non-Competitive Bidding Timing:** November 11, 2026, 10:30 am - 11:00 am
- **Auction Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine government borrowing and monetary operations absorbing or providing short-term liquidity in the banking system with standard market impact.
