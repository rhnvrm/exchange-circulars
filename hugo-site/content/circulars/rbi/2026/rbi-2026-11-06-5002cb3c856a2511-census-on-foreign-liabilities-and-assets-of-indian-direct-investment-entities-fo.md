## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) released the provisional results of the 2025-26 round of regional and annual Census on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA). The census captures detailed cross-border liabilities and assets of Indian entities—including companies, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), and partnership firms—with inward or outward direct investment.

## Key Points

- **Inward FDI:** Outstanding inward Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) stood at ₹70.72 lakh crore at end-March 2026, registering an 8.5% growth.
- **Outward Direct Investment (ODI):** ODI rose significantly by 40.8% to ₹16.71 lakh crore, bringing the FDI-to-ODI ratio to 4.2 times.
- **Entity Distribution:** Approximately 97% of reporting entities were unlisted as of end-March 2026.
- **Sectoral Share:** Non-financial companies accounted for over 90% of FDI equity at face value. By market value, manufacturing accounted for 50.2% and services for 44.8% of total FDI.
- **Top Partners:** The United States, Mauritius, Singapore, and the United Kingdom were the leading sources of inward FDI.

## Regulatory Changes

- The census data collection is mandated under FEMA, 1999, providing crucial updates for compiling India's Balance of Payments (BoP) and International Investment Position (IIP) statistics.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible Indian entities with inward or outward direct investment must accurately report their annual FLA data through the FLAIR portal.

## Important Dates

- Provisional results released on November 6, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances macroeconomic transparency, aids policy formulation regarding cross-border capital flows, and ensures regulatory compliance for entities engaged in international direct investments.
