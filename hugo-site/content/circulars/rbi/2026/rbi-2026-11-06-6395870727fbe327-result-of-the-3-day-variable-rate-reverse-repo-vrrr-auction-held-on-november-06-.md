## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the results of the 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction held on November 06, 2026. Out of the notified amount of ₹25,000 crore, total offers of ₹18,133 crore were received and accepted in full.

## Key Points

- Tenor: 3-day
- Notified Amount: ₹25,000 crore
- Total Amount of Offers Received: ₹18,133 crore
- Amount Accepted: ₹18,133 crore
- Cut-off Rate: 5.49%
- Weighted Average Rate: 5.49%
- Partial Acceptance Percentage at cut-off rate: NA
- Press Release: 2026-2027/1457

None.

- Auction Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The auction successfully absorbed ₹18,133 crore from the banking system for a 3-day tenor at 5.49%, reflecting standard liquidity management operations by the central bank.
