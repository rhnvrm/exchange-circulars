## Summary

Data on sectoral deployment of credit by non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) and housing finance companies (HFCs) for September 2026 indicates a year-on-year (y-o-y) credit growth of 15.4 per cent, compared to 13.3 per cent a year ago. The data covers Upper and Middle Layer NBFCs and HFCs, accounting for approximately 87 per cent of total credit.

## Key Points

- NBFC credit registered y-o-y growth of 15.4% in September 2026 (vs 13.3% a year ago).
- Agriculture and allied activities credit grew robustly by 17.8% y-o-y (vs 5.2% a year ago).
- Industry sector credit growth declined to 7.4% y-o-y (vs 10.2% in September 2025), with infrastructure remaining subdued.
- Services sector credit growth moderated to 17.3% y-o-y (vs 23.0% a year ago), though commercial real estate saw buoyant expansion alongside trade and transport operators.
- Retail loans growth accelerated to 21.9% y-o-y (vs 16.8% a year ago), driven by housing loans, loans against gold jewellery, and vehicle loans.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic release and sectoral monitoring of credit deployment statistics by RBI based on data reported by major NBFCs and HFCs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Reporting of monthly sectoral credit deployment data by Upper and Middle Layer NBFCs and HFCs to the Reserve Bank of India.

## Important Dates

- **Data as of:** September 30, 2026
- **Release Date:** November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The data reflects healthy credit expansion across retail and agricultural segments by NBFCs, while industrial credit growth moderates. This provides vital macroeconomic indicators for financial sector stability, liquidity tracking, and monetary policy evaluation.
