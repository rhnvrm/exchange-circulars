## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued a circular regarding the re-organisation of districts in the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi, based on the Government Gazette Notification dated December 25, 2025. Three new districts have been formed, Shahdara district has ceased to exist, and lead bank responsibilities for specific existing districts have been modified.

## Key Points

- **Newly Created Districts & Lead Bank Assignments:**
  - Old Delhi: Assigned to Canara Bank (District Working Code: 03E)
  - Central North: Assigned to State Bank of India (District Working Code: 03F)
  - Outer North: Assigned to Punjab National Bank (District Working Code: 03G)

- **Modified Lead Bank Responsibilities:**
  - Central district: Changed from Canara Bank (Code 358) to State Bank of India.
  - Northwest district: Changed from Punjab National Bank (Code 362) to Union Bank of India.

- **Revocation of Defunct District:**
  - Shahdara district has ceased to exist; lead bank responsibility previously assigned to Bank of Baroda stands revoked.

- **Unaffected Districts:**
  - Lead bank responsibilities in all other districts remain unchanged until further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Realignment of Lead Bank responsibilities and district working codes to reflect administrative boundary changes notified by the Government of NCT of Delhi.

## Compliance Requirements

- Concerned Lead Banks must ensure smooth transition and coordination in newly assigned and modified districts.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Government Notification Date: December 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and operational realignment for concerned banks operating in the NCT of Delhi with no direct financial market impact.
