## Summary

In the underwriting auction conducted on November 06, 2026, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers.

## Key Points

- 7.06% GS 2041: Notified amount ₹23,000 crore, MUC amount ₹11,508 crore, ACU accepted ₹11,492 crore, Total underwritten ₹23,000 crore, ACU commission cut-off rate 0.74 paise per ₹100.
- 7.43% GS 2076: Notified amount ₹13,000 crore, MUC amount ₹6,510 crore, ACU accepted ₹6,490 crore, Total underwritten ₹13,000 crore, ACU commission cut-off rate 1.14 paise per ₹100.
- Auction for the sale of securities scheduled for November 06, 2026.
- Press Release: 2026-2027/1456.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Primary Dealers must adhere to the accepted underwriting commitments and commission cut-off rates determined by the RBI.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparent pricing and underwriting support for government borrowing programs through Primary Dealers.
