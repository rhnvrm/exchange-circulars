## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction results for Government Stocks, detailing the notified amounts, cut-off prices, implicit yields, and amounts accepted for 7.06% GS 2041 and 7.43% GS 2076.

## Key Points

- **7.06% GS 2041**: Notified and accepted amount of ₹23,000 crore; Cut-off price ₹97.07 with an implicit yield of 7.3881%; Devolvement on Primary Dealers: NIL.
- **7.43% GS 2076**: Notified and accepted amount of ₹13,000 crore; Cut-off price ₹95.70 with an implicit yield of 7.7708%; Devolvement on Primary Dealers: NIL.
