## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a revision to the arbitration fee structure applicable to members and constituents, effective from February 6, 2008, under Business Rule 37.

## Key Points

- Revised arbitration fee structure inserted in Clause 5 (a) of Business Rule 37.
- Tiered fee structure established for both Exchange members and constituents acting as Applicants or Respondents.
- For constituents, arbitration fees for claims up to Rs. 5,00,000/- are borne by the Exchange.
- Counter claims and additional claims are subject to the same fee structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Member Arbitration Fees:**
  - Upto Rs. 5,00,000/-: Rs. 8,000/-
  - Rs. 5,00,001/- to 25,00,000/-: Rs. 10,000/-
  - Rs. 25,00,000/- and above: Rs. 14,000/-

- **Constituent Arbitration Fees:**
  - Upto Rs. 5,00,000/-: Nil (borne by the Exchange on behalf of the constituent)
  - Rs. 5,00,001/- to 25,00,000/-: Rs. 8,000/-
  - Rs. 25,00,000/- and above: Rs. 14,000/-

- **Counter/Additional Claims:** Parties making counter or additional claims must deposit additional amounts as per the applicable fee structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and constituents involved in arbitration proceedings must deposit the applicable arbitration fees with the Exchange according to the revised fee schedule.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: February 5, 2008
- Effective Date: February 6, 2008

## Impact Assessment

- Alters the financial obligations for dispute resolution and arbitration filings for market participants and clients on the MCX platform.
