## Summary

MCX has established Regional Arbitration Centres (RACs) to facilitate the adjudication of disputes through the Exchange's arbitration mechanism for both members and constituents. Business Rule 37 has been amended to introduce sub-clauses 5A, 5B, and 5C detailing the seats of arbitration, criteria for selection, and application procedures.

## Key Points

- Four Regional Arbitration Centres (RACs) established: Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, and Mumbai, covering specific states across India.
- Seat of arbitration criteria defined for Member vs Member, Member vs Client, and Client vs Member disputes based on the applicant's choice and respondent's/client's region.
- Internet trading disputes: Seat of arbitration shall be at the RAC where the client ordinarily resided at the time of the relevant trade, unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- Applications and related correspondence must be submitted to the Exchange's Head Office in Mumbai.

## Regulatory Changes

- Insertion of sub-clauses 5A, 5B, and 5C after sub-clause 5 of Business Rule 37 of the Exchange.
- Defines regional jurisdiction for Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, and Mumbai RACs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and constituents must file arbitration applications through the prescribed format to the Exchange's Head Office in Mumbai.
- Parties must adhere to the specified seats of arbitration and hearing locations based on regional criteria.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2008

## Impact Assessment

- Decentralizes the arbitration process, improving accessibility and convenience for members and constituents located across different regions of India.
