## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) issued a circular notifying members about the intent to procure PC Span software, marketed by Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME). The software enables members to calculate client margin requirements accurately using risk parameter files provided by the exchange, thereby enhancing risk management.

## Key Points

- MCX is implementing SPAN Margining and advises members to have PC Span software.
- PC Span calculates margin requirements based on exchange-provided risk parameter files.
- The cost per license is Rs. 10,000/-, which will be debited from the member's settlement account.
- Members must communicate their intention to procure the license through the specified Annexure 1 format.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of SPAN Margining framework by the Exchange requiring member-level software support.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members interested in purchasing the PC Span license must submit their intention via fax (022 – 6649 4151) using Annexure 1.
- Settlement accounts of participating members will be debited for the license fee of Rs. 10,000/-.

## Important Dates

- **February 13, 2008**: Date of circular issuance.
- **February 20, 2008**: Deadline for members to convey their intention to procure the license.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves operational efficiency and risk management standards for trading members.
- Involves a fixed financial cost of Rs. 10,000/- per license for participating members.
