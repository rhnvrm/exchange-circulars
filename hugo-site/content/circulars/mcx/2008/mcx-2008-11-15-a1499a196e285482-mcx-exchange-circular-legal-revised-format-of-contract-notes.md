## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular notifying the revised format of Contract Notes. Following the amendment of Business Rule 37 by inserting Clauses 5A to 5C regarding the setup of Regional Arbitration Centres, members are required to incorporate these amended provisions on the reverse of the Contract Note.

## Key Points

- Business Rule 37 amended to include provisions for Regional Arbitration Centres.
- Contract Note format at Annexure–XIV to the Business Rules stands revised.
- Members must update their stationary and software accordingly.
- Effective date for the revised format is January 1, 2009.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporation of Regional Arbitration Centre adjudication provisions on the reverse of Contract Notes as per clause 27(p) of the Business Rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange members must update software modifications and print new stationery to include the revised Contract Note format containing arbitration details.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 15, 2008
- Effective Date: January 1, 2009

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on exchange members requiring software changes and printing of new contract note stationery before the January 1, 2009 deadline.
