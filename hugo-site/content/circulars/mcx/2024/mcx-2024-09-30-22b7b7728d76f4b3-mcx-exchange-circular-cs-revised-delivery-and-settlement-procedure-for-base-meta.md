## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued revised delivery and settlement procedures for base metal contracts pursuant to modifications in the staggered delivery period introduced for metal contracts. These changes apply to contracts expiring in January 2025 and onwards.

## Key Points

- **Staggered Delivery Tender Period:** Last 3 trading days (including expiry day) of the contracts, excluding special sessions like Muhurat Trading.
- **Staggered Tender Period Margin:** 8.34% incremental margin for the last 3 trading days on all outstanding positions, in addition to initial, special, and other margins.
- **Intent Submission:** Buyers and sellers must submit delivery intentions through MCXCCL eClear till 5.00 p.m. on respective tender days.
- **Delivery Period Margins:** Higher of 3% + 5-day 99% VaR of spot price volatility or 25%.
- **Exemption:** Sellers are exempted from all types of margins if goods are tendered as early pay-in with all documentary evidences (subject to ongoing MTM collection).
- **Delivery Pay-in / Pay-out:** Delivery pay-in through ComRIS account by earmarking valid commodity balance. On tender days by 5.00 p.m.; on expiry, delivery pay-in is on E+1 working day by 2.00 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of staggered delivery period and operational workflow for base metal commodity futures.
- Implementation of incremental and delivery period margins specifically for base metal tender periods.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and constituents must take note of the revised delivery and settlement timelines, ComRIS account earmarking requirements, and margin obligations for base metal contracts starting January 2025 expiry.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** January 2025 and onwards expiry contracts.
- **Circular Date:** September 30, 2024.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading base metal futures must adapt their delivery intention submissions, early pay-in processes, and margin allocations in line with the updated MCXCCL circular.
