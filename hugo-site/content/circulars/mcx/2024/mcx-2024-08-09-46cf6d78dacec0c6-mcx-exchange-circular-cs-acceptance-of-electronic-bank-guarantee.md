## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has notified clearing members regarding the addition of HDFC Bank Limited to the list of approved banks for providing Bank Guarantees in electronic form (E-BG).

## Key Points

- HDFC Bank Limited has been added for E-BG issuance under MCXCCL.
- Effective date of the circular is Monday, August 12, 2024.
- Revised list of empaneled E-BG banks includes Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, IDFC First Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of HDFC Bank Limited in the approved list of E-BG issuers for clearing members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members can utilize HDFC Bank Limited for issuing Electronic Bank Guarantees in compliance with MCXCCL rules.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 09, 2024
- Effective Date: August 12, 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clearing members with an additional banking option for submitting Electronic Bank Guarantees as collateral.
