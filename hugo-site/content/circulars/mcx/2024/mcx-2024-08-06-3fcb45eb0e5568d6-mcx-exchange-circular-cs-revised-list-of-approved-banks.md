## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a revised list of approved banks for accepting Bank Guarantees (BG) and Fixed Deposits (FD) as collateral, following the inclusion of The Federal Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- The Federal Bank Limited has been added to the list of approved banks, bringing the total count to 17.
- The revised list governs the acceptance of Bank Guarantees and Fixed Deposits as collateral from Clearing Members.
- Members are required to verify bank-wise available limits and maximum allowable limits per member via the MCXCCL website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of The Federal Bank Limited in the approved list of banks for collateral acceptance under MCXCCL norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must check available bank-wise limits and maximum allowable limits per member on the MCXCCL website before placing collateral deposits.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 6, 2024.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the banking options available to clearing members for submitting collaterals and managing margins.
