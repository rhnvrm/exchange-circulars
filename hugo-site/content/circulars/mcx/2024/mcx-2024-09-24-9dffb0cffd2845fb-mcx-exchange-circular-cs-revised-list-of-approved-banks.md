## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a revised list of approved banks for accepting Bank Guarantees (BG) and Fixed Deposits (FD) as collateral, following the inclusion of IDBI Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- IDBI Bank Limited has been added to the approved list of banks.
- The revision supersedes previous circular MCXCCL/C&S/189/2024 dated July 29, 2024.
- Members must check bank-wise available limits and maximum allowable limits per member prior to placing collateral.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated list of 19 approved banks for collateral acceptance under MCXCCL norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members must verify bank-wise available limits and maximum allowable limits per member on the MCXCCL website before placing collateral deposits.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2024
- Effective: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clearing members with an additional approved banking partner (IDBI Bank Limited) for submitting Bank Guarantees and Fixed Deposits as collateral.
