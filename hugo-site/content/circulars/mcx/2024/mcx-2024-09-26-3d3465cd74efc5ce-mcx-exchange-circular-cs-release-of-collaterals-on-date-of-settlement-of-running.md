## Summary

MCXCCL has issued guidelines regarding the release of collaterals (Cash / FDR / BG) on the date of settlement of running account of client's funds, in reference to SEBI circular dated July 27, 2022.

## Key Points

- Clearing members can submit requests for Cash / FDR / BG to be released on an immediate basis till 7:00 PM on the settlement date.
- MCXCCL endeavours to release Cash / FDR / BG within one hour of receiving the request on the date of one-day settlement, subject to unallocated balances in the member pool account.
- Queries can be directed to the Collateral Management department via settlement@mcxccl.com or collaterals@mcxccl.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of procedures aligning with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOP/P/CIR/2022/10 and previous MCXCCL circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must submit immediate release requests by 7:00 PM on settlement dates and ensure sufficient unallocated balances in the member pool account.

## Important Dates

- Effective from September 26, 2024.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational flexibility and clarity for clearing members managing collaterals during running account settlements.
