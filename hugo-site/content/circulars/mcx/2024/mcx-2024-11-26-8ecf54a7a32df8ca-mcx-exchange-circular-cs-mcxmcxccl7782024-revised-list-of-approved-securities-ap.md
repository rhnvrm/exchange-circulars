## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has released a revised list of approved securities, approved commodities, and approved banks for collateral purposes, superseding circular no. MCXCCL/C&S/275/2024 dated October 28, 2024. The circular is effective from Monday, December 02, 2024.

## Key Points

- Covers Annexure 1 (Approved Securities including equity shares, ETFs, mutual fund units), Annexure 2 (Government Securities, T-Bills and Sovereign Gold Bonds), Annexure 3 (Approved Commodities), and Annexure 4 (Approved Banks).
- Clearing members must ensure they do not submit collaterals (such as FDs, BGs, equity, or debt securities) issued by themselves or their group/associate entities.
- G-Sec and T-Bills provided as collateral by banking clearing members should not be reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.
- Margin benefits on approved Government Securities, T-Bills, and Sovereign Gold Bonds will be withdrawn two days prior to maturity.
- All SGB/T-Bills issued by RBI during the month are accepted by MCXCCL.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of approved collateral securities, commodities, and banks in accordance with MCXCCL rules and regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must verify and adhere to the restrictions regarding self/associate-issued collaterals.
- Banking clearing members must ensure G-Sec/T-Bills used as collateral are excluded from SLR calculations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** December 02, 2024
- **Previous Circular Date:** October 28, 2024
- **Circular Date:** November 26, 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for clearing members regarding eligible collateral components, valuation haircuts (VaR rates), and deposit limits (maximum 25% per security for eligible equity shares).
