## Summary

MCX has announced an amendment to Business Rule 37 pertaining to arbitration, specifically updating the definition of the "Relevant Authority".

## Key Points

- Business Rule 37, clause 15(1) has been amended.
- The term "Relevant Authority" now means and refers to the Managing Director, Deputy Managing Director, or any officer(s) authorized by them.
- Effective date: February 14, 2007.

## Regulatory Changes

- Substitution of clause 15(1) of Business Rule 37 to clarify delegation of authority for arbitration matters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and constituents are requested to take note of the revised definition.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: February 14, 2007

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational impact; streamlines internal administrative processes for arbitration cases.
