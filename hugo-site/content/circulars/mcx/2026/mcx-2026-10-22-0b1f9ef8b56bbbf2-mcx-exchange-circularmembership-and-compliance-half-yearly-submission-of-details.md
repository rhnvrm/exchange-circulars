## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding the half-yearly submission of details of Associate and Subsidiary Entities under the Risk Based Supervision (RBS) framework. Based on representations from the Brokers Industry Standard Forum (ISF) and in consultation with SEBI, certain RBS information will be sourced internally or through other member submissions, while associate and subsidiary details require a separate submission.

## Key Points

- All Trading Members must submit details of their Associate/Subsidiary Entities on a half-yearly basis.
- Submission for the half-year ended March 31, 2026, is due on or before November 15, 2026.
- Members must notify the Exchange of any changes (additions, deletions, modifications) within 30 calendar days of occurrence.
- Members registered with both NSE and MCX are required to submit details to NSE under technology-based sharing mechanisms, which NSE will share with MCX.
- Submissions can be done via the Enhanced Supervision Portal under the Inspection tab.

## Regulatory Changes

- Streamlining of Risk Based Supervision (RBS) information gathering by sourcing certain details internally or via existing submissions while requiring dedicated reporting for associate/subsidiary entities.
- Inter-exchange data sharing mechanism enabled for members registered across multiple exchanges (e.g., NSE and MCX).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must log in to the Enhanced Supervision Portal (https://member.mcxindia.com), select 'Submit Details' under the 'Associate Details' tab, download and fill out the specified Excel template without changing sheet names, and upload it.
- Ensure no duplicate serial numbers and follow mutual exclusivity rules between associate and subsidiary columns per row.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Submission Deadline (Half-year ended March 31, 2026): November 15, 2026
- Real-time change notification deadline: Within 30 calendar days of any change

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must establish internal tracking for associate and subsidiary entities and ensure timely submissions through the member portal to avoid non-compliance penalties under the RBS framework.
