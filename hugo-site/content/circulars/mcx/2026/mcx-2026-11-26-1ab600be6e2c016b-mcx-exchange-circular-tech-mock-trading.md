## Summary

MCX has announced a mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, allowing members to test trading applications, disaster recovery sites, and the new UCC interface.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- Special Session timing: 04:55 pm – 04:59 pm.
- Mock trading timing: 05:00 pm – 07:00 pm.
- Trading members can test third-party platforms, in-house systems via ETI API (version 1.4.2), and Disaster Recovery site connectivity.
- Internet connectivity via live SSL VPN link (https://trade.mcxindia.com) or existing leased line.
- UCC interface testing via https://uccapp.mcxindia.com.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any obligation for pay-in and pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session from all trading software and ensure systems are restored to production state after the session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational test session with no financial or settlement impact.
