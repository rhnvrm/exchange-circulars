## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has announced the empanelment of Bull8.Ai Solutions Pvt. Ltd. as an approved Algo Provider to offer algorithmic trading facilities to trading members.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Name:** Bull8.Ai Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
- **Registered Address:** Tower D-1308, Spectrum Metro Mall, Sector – 75, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Noida, Uttar Pradesh - 201301
- **Contact Details:** Phone: +91 97119 30937, Email: support@bull8.ai
- **Applicability:** Effective with immediate effect (May 15, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular communicates standard administrative empanelment of an algorithmic trading vendor.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing or intending to utilize algorithmic trading services should note the vendor's empanelment status and ensure compliance with exchange CTCL guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** May 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with an additional empaneled technology partner option for deploying algorithmic trading strategies on the exchange platform.
