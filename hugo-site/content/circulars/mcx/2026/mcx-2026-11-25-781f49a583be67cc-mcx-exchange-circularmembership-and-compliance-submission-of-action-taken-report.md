## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding the submission of Action Taken Reports (ATRs) and status of closure for observations reported during the inspection of Authorised Persons (APs) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Members must submit the ATR by December 31, 2026, via the Member Portal.

## Key Points

- Refers to Exchange Circular No. MCX/COMP/480/2026 dated August 21, 2026, regarding submission of ATRs on observations from AP inspections.
- Outlines quarterly reporting schedules covering inspection periods, report submission due dates, disciplinary action due dates, and ATR due dates.
- ATR submission functionality is available on the Member Portal (Enhanced Supervision Portal) with detailed steps provided in Annexure A.
- Non-submission of an AP inspection report for a particular period will be treated as no AP inspections having been conducted during that period.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiterates adherence to established quarterly timelines and compliance verification procedures for Authorised Persons inspections.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members with 'Not Complied' observations in quarterly AP inspections must rectify/comply prior to ATR submission.
- Members must log in to the Enhanced Supervision Portal, navigate to Inspection > AP ATR Submission, download the template for eligible AP codes, and submit the ATR.
- ATR for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, must be submitted by December 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2026:** End of Q2 AP inspection period.
- **December 31, 2026:** ATR submission due date for Q2 inspections.

## Impact Assessment

Members must ensure adherence to reporting timelines and closure of inspection observations to avoid regulatory compliance breaches.
