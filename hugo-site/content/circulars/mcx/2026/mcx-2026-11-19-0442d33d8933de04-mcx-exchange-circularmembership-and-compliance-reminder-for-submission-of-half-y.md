## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a reminder for members to submit their Half Yearly Networth Certificate along with the computation of networth as on September 30, 2026, by November 30, 2026. Members registered with NSE must submit to NSE only as per common submission mechanisms. Non-registered members must submit online via member.mcxindia.com.

## Key Points

- Deadline for submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, is November 30, 2026.
- Members registered with NSE are required to submit their networth certificate to NSE only.
- Members not registered with NSE must submit online on the MCX member portal.
- Penalties and disciplinary actions apply for non-submission, non-submission of revised certificates upon shortfall, or unrectified deficiencies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiterates technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among exchanges where members registered with NSE submit exclusively to NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange members must ensure submission of the Half Yearly Networth Certificate and computation as on September 30, 2026, on or before November 30, 2026.
- Address any deficiencies or networth shortfalls within stipulated timelines to avoid penalties under circular MCX/COMP/213/2026 and MCX/MEM/402/2026.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2026: Networth computation date.
- November 30, 2026: Due date for submission.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational compliance requirement for all MCX members; failure to comply results in financial penalties and disciplinary actions.
