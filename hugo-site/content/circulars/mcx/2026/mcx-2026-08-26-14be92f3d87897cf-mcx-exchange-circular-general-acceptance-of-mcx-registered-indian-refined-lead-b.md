## Summary

MCX has empanelled Leading Metalloys LLP's 'LEADING METALLOYS' brand of refined lead for delivery settlement under its Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts, effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refiner: Leading Metalloys LLP
- Brand Name: LEADING METALLOYS
- Plant Address: B-12, RIICO Industrial Area, Karoli, Bhiwadi Dist. Alwar, Rajasthan – 301707
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of new approved brand for delivery settlement in Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members are requested to take note of the empanelled brand for delivery settlement.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the approved delivery options and supply base for market participants trading in Lead and Lead Mini futures.
