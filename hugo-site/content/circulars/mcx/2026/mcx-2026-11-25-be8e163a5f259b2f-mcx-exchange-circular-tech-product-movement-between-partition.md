## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the movement of selected product symbols from one system partition to another to optimize performance and manage data streams, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected products: OFNATGASMINI, FUSILVERMIC, and FUGOLD.
- Changes will be reflected in the product master file (Mcxscrip.bcp) and Stream Master on SFTP (/Common/Contract Master).
- No changes in MDI and ETI APIs.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational infrastructure adjustment by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must update their systems and reference the updated master files accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on members, requiring only minor technical updates to align with the new partition mapping.
