## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued an advisory regarding order-based surveillance measures. The Exchange observed persistent order modifications and cancellations in certain commodities and contracts that lead to deferred or lowered execution priority with apparent intent to avoid execution, creating unwarranted market noise and system load.

## Key Points

- Excessive order modifications and cancellations ("noise") impair efficient price discovery and order book quality.
- Trading members are advised to maintain healthy message-to-trade ratios.
- Members must ensure trading users do not engage in repeated order modifications/cancellations without resulting in trades.
- Internal surveillance and risk controls must monitor order-level behavior at the Client / Proprietary account level.
- Trading strategies and algorithms must be reviewed and optimized.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory rule changes, but heightened surveillance and monitoring of high order modification/cancellation patterns have been instituted.

## Compliance Requirements

- Review and optimize trading strategies and algorithms with respect to order placement, modification, and cancellation practices.
- Implement internal surveillance and risk controls to monitor order-level frequency of modifications and cancellations.
- Respond promptly to any information or clarification sought by the Surveillance department if unusual patterns are observed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 24, 2026 (Circular issuance date).

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and algorithmic trading desks must audit their order management systems and algorithmic strategies to avoid penalties or supervisory scrutiny regarding excessive message-to-trade ratios and order churn.
