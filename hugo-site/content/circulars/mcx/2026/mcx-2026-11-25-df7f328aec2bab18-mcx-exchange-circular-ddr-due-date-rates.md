## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has notified the Due Date Rates (DDR) for futures contracts that expired on November 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Natural Gas (NATURALGAS) and Natural Gas Mini (NATGASMINI) expired on November 24, 2026.
- Due Date Rate is fixed at 308.40 per MMBTU for both contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants are requested to take note of the Due Date Rates for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Expiry Date: November 24, 2026
- Circular Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement price notification for expired natural gas futures contracts with no broader market impact.
