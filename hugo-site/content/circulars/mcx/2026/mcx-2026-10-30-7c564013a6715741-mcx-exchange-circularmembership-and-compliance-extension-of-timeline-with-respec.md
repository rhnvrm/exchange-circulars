## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding the extension of the timeline for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars. Based on SEBI circular HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026, the deadline has been extended to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Extension granted for the appointment of IAAP Auditor, conduct of Accessibility Audit for digital platforms, and remediation of findings.
- New compliance deadline is January 31, 2027.
- All other provisions of the earlier circulars remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI and MCX have extended timelines for compliance with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and rules made thereunder for all regulated entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete the appointment of an IAAP auditor, conduct accessibility audits of their digital platforms, and remediate findings by January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Extended Compliance Deadline:** January 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulated entities additional time to ensure full compliance with digital accessibility standards for persons with disabilities without immediate penal actions.
