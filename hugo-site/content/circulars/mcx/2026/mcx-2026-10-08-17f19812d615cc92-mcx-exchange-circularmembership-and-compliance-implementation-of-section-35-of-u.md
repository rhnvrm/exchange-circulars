## Summary

MCX has issued circular no. MCX/COMP/571/2026 dated October 08, 2026, regarding the implementation of Section 35 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA) 1967. This follows SEBI's press release and Ministry of Home Affairs Gazette Notification (CG-DL-E-16092026-276236) designating the "Shahzad Bhatti Network (SBN)" as a Terrorist Organisation and listing it in the Fourth Schedule of the UAPA 1967.

## Key Points

- SEBI and Ministry of Home Affairs have designated 01 entity ("Shahzad Bhatti Network (SBN)") as a Terrorist Organisation under Section 35(1)(a) of the UAPA 1967.
- Members are required to implement rigorous, periodic screening of all client databases against applicable Sanctions Lists.
- Any identified matches must be immediately frozen and reported to relevant authorities.
- Reference is made to Clauses 62 and 63 of the SEBI Master Circular on AML/CFT standards dated June 06, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of "Shahzad Bhatti Network (SBN)" to the Fourth Schedule of the UAPA 1967 under Section 35(1)(a).
- Adherence to procedures outlined in the Government of India's Order dated February 02, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must conduct rigorous periodic screening of client databases.
- Immediate freezing of accounts linked to the designated entity.
- Reporting of any matches to appropriate regulatory and law enforcement authorities.

## Important Dates

- Gazette Notification Date: September 16, 2026 (CG-DL-E-16092026-276236)
- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate compliance required upon issuance

## Impact Assessment

Ensures stringent adherence to counter-terrorist financing (CFT) and anti-money laundering (AML) mandates across commodities market intermediaries, mitigating regulatory and legal risks.
