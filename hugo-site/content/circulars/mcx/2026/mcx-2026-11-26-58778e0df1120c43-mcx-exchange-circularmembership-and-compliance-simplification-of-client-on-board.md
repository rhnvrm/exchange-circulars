## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular informing members that SEBI has released guidelines regarding the simplification of client on-boarding and the rationalization of the risk management framework at KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs).

## Key Points

- SEBI issued Circular Ref No. HO/38/14/(25)2026-MIRSD-POD3 dated November 25, 2026.
- Recommendations formulated via a working group comprising market participants, MIIs, KRAs, and industry associations.
- Centralization of common supplementary client information (collected in Part II of the account opening form) at the KRA level to ease onboarding across multiple intermediaries.
- Mandatory uploading of CKYC ID, DIGIPIN (if available), and Officially Valid Document (OVD) expiry/validity dates into the KRA system by intermediaries.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendments to SEBI Master Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/SECFATF/P/CIR/2023/169 dated October 12, 2023.
- Centralized handling of supplementary client information across KRAs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and intermediaries must take note of the revised KYC norms and ensure adherence to the updated onboarding procedures.
- Intermediaries are required to upload CKYC IDs, DIGIPINs, and OVD expiry/validity details directly into the KRA system.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- MCX Circular Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines and expedites client onboarding when dealing with multiple intermediaries by centralizing supplementary data at KRAs.
- Enhances regulatory compliance and risk management frameworks within KYC registration agencies.
