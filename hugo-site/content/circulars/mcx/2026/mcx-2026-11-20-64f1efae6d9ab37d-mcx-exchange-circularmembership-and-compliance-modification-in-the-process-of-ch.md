## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a modification in the process for reporting "Change in Shareholding" under Exchange Master Circular No. MCX/MEM/259/2026, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.
- Master Circular Reference: MCX/MEM/259/2026 dated April 30, 2026.
- Paragraph Amended: 14.2.7. vi). b).

## Regulatory Changes

- **Existing Process:** In case of an unlisted public limited entity, if there is a change in less than 2% shareholding in the non-promoter group, only intimation was required to be done to the Exchange.
- **Revised Process:** In case of an unlisted entity, if there is a change in less than 2% shareholding in the promoter / non-promoter group, then only intimation is required to be done to the Exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

Exchange members must note the expanded scope covering both promoter and non-promoter groups for unlisted entities regarding shareholding changes of less than 2% requiring intimation.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Circular Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides clarity and broadens the intimation requirement to include promoter groups for unlisted entities undergoing minor (< 2%) shareholding modifications.
