## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has announced the empanelment of Clearmind Consultancy Private Limited as an Algo Provider to provide algorithmic trading facilities to trading members of the exchange.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: Clearmind Consultancy Private Limited
- Registered Address: PL 396 to 398, FL 15, Goldfield Enclave, Koregaon Park, Pune - 411001
- Contact Details: Phone: +91-9372810916, Email ID: pkucheria@gmail.com
- Applicability: With immediate effect
- The updated list of empaneled Algo Providers is available on the MCX India website.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative empanelment notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to utilize algorithmic trading facilities via the empaneled vendor should take note of the contact and registration details.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the choices available for trading members seeking empaneled algorithmic trading service providers on the exchange platform.
