## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a revision in trading hours for the period from November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027 (both days inclusive), on account of the change in US daylight saving timings in the fall season.

## Key Points

- Revised trading hours are applicable from November 02, 2026 to March 12, 2027.
- Non-Agri Commodities trade timings: 9:00 AM to 11:55 PM (Client Code Modification: 9:00 AM to 11:59 PM).
- Select Agri Commodities (Cotton, Cotton Seed Wash Oil & Kapas) trade timings: 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM (Client Code Modification: 9:00 AM to 9:15 PM).
- All other Agri Commodities trade timings: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Client Code Modification: 9:00 AM to 5:15 PM).

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes implemented in accordance with SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/136 dated August 04, 2023, and exchange rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are required to note the revised timings and align their trading systems and operations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective End Date: March 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in commodity futures and options will operate under adjusted closing schedules during the US daylight saving fall season.
