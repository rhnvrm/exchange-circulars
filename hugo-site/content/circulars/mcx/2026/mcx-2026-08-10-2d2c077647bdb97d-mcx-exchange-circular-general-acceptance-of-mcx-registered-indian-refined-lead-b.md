## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the empanelment of Aadishakti Green Recycling Pvt. Ltd. as a registered brand for Refined Lead towards delivery settlement of Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts.

## Key Points

- **Empanelled Refiner:** Aadishakti Green Recycling Pvt. Ltd.
- **Plant Address:** Survey No 225-226, Revenue Circle, Near Mokha Toll, Mundra, Kundrodi, Kachchh, Gujarat, 370410
- **Brand Name:** AADISHAKTI
- **Applicable Contracts:** Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine empanelment circular under the Rules, Bye-Laws, and Business Rules of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants dealing in Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts are requested to take note of the empanelled brand for delivery settlements.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the approved delivery mechanism for Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts by adding a certified domestic refined lead brand, ensuring smooth physical settlement operations.
