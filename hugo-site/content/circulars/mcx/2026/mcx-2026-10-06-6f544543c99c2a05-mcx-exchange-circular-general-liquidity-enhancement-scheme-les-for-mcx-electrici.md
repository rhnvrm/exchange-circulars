## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the extension of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for its Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contracts until October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Electricity Futures Contracts, originally notified via circular no. MCX/PMT/204/2026 dated April 16, 2026, has been extended.
- The scheme will now remain in effect up to October 30, 2026.
- All other provisions, terms, and conditions stated in the aforementioned circular continue to apply unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this circular merely extends the timeline for an existing liquidity enhancement scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in Electricity Futures contracts should take note of the extended validity period through October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- October 07, 2026: Circular issuance date.
- October 30, 2026: Expiry/extension deadline of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Electricity Futures.

## Impact Assessment

Provides continued liquidity support and market maker engagement for MCX Electricity Futures contracts through October 30, 2026.
