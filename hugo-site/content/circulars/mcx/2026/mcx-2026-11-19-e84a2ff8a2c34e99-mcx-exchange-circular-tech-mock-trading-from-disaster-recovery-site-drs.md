## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd. (MCX) has announced a mock trading session from its Disaster Recovery Site (DRS) scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members using CTCL facilities, empaneled vendor platforms, in-house systems, or approved algorithms are required or advised to participate and test their respective applications.

## Key Points

- **Date of Mock Trading:** Saturday, November 21, 2026.
- **Special Session Timing:** 11:55 am – 11:59 am.
- **Mock Trading Timing:** 12:00 pm – 04:00 pm.
- **Mandatory Participation:** Mandatory for members having approved algorithm strategies, using all algorithm strategy/strategies.
- **Connectivity:** Available through existing leased line connectivity and live (existing) SSL VPN ids via https://trade.mcxindia.com.
- **Settlement:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any obligation for pay-in and pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine mock trading session as per exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members having approved algorithm strategies must mandatorily participate in the mock trading session using all algorithm strategies.
- Members must put in place an adequate mechanism to restore their trading systems to the production state at the end of the mock session to ensure system integrity.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational test for trading members to verify disaster recovery site connectivity, algorithmic execution, and system readiness without live market risk or financial obligations.
