## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has announced the empanelment of Stratzy Fintech Pvt. Ltd. as an Algo Provider to offer algorithmic trading facilities to its trading members.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: Stratzy Fintech Pvt. Ltd.
- Registered Address: Flat No. 1501, Center Point, Mulund East, Mumbai - 400081
- Contact Details: Phone: +91 85880-29967, Email ID: support@stratzy.in
- Applicability: With immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative empanelment circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to utilize algorithmic trading facilities through Stratzy Fintech Pvt. Ltd. may take note of the empanelment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: May 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with an additional approved vendor option for algorithmic trading services on the exchange.
