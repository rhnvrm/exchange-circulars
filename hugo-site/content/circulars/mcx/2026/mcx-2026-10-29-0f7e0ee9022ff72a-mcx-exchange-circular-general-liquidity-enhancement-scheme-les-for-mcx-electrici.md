## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the extension of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for its Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contract until November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for MCX Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contract is extended.
- Extension validity is up to November 13, 2026.
- All other provisions from the previous circular (MCX/PMT/204/2026 dated April 16, 2026) continue to apply.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; continuation of existing LES terms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in the Electricity Futures contracts should note the extended timeline and continue adhering to the existing provisions.

## Important Dates

- **Extension Expiry Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Previous Reference Date:** April 16, 2026 (Circular no. MCX/PMT/204/2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures continued market liquidity and support for the MCX Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contract through mid-November 2026.
