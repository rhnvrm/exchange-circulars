## Summary

MCX has announced an update regarding its data center migration. Following stakeholder interactions and inputs received, the Exchange is reviewing its plan and timelines to determine an optimal approach for moving to the new data center.

## Key Points

- References previous circular MCX/TECH/424/2026 dated July 21, 2026, regarding operational expansion and data center migration.
- The Exchange is currently reviewing its migration plan, timelines, and options based on stakeholder feedback.
- Members will be informed of any required actions and revised timelines in due course.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; operational update concerning exchange infrastructure.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are not required to take any immediate action at this stage and should await further communications from the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- October 19, 2026: Circular issued.
- July 21, 2026: Initial data center migration circular (MCX/TECH/424/2026).
- Future dates: Revised timelines to be announced.

## Impact Assessment

Provides temporary flexibility as the Exchange re-evaluates timelines for data center migration, allowing members additional time to align their technological infrastructure.
