## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the schedule for reporting of holding statements on account of the Diwali Muhurat Trading Session held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Specific reporting due dates are adjusted for trade dates spanning November 06, 2026 to November 10, 2026.
- Ensures members comply with regulatory reporting requirements during the holiday and special trading period.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must submit holding statements according to the revised schedule: trade dates 06-Nov-2026, 07-Nov-2026, and 08-Nov-2026 are due on 09-Nov-2026; trade dates 09-Nov-2026 and 10-Nov-2026 are due on 11-Nov-2026.

## Important Dates

- Trade Dates 06-Nov-2026, 07-Nov-2026, 08-Nov-2026: Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- Trade Dates 09-Nov-2026, 10-Nov-2026: Due Date 11-Nov-2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustments for members to ensure timely compliance reporting around the Diwali Muhurat trading session.
