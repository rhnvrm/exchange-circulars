## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of Cyber Security Incident reports (occurred or non-occurred) for the quarter ending September 30, 2026. Members registered with NSE are required to submit details via NSE, while other members must submit directly through the MCX portal or SFTP path by October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Quarterly reporting requirement for cyber security incidents covering the period up to September 30, 2026.
- NSE-registered trading members must submit details to NSE, which shares submissions with other exchanges including MCX.
- Members not registered with NSE must submit directly via the MCX member portal or SFTP.
- Deadline for submission is October 15, 2026.
- Non-compliance will render members liable for disciplinary action by the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration of existing reporting mechanisms and technology-based common submission sharing across exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must report cyber incident status (occurred/non-occurred) for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, on the Exchange portal (https://member.mcxindia.com) or SFTP common path on or before October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict adherence to cybersecurity standards and regulatory reporting obligations across exchange members.
