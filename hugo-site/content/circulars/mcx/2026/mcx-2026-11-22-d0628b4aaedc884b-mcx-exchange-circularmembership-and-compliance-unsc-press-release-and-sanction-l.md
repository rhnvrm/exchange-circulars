## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) Sanctions List, referencing SEBI's press release on the implementation of Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Updates to UNSC’s 1988 (2011) Sanctions List (Amendment of 5 entries).
- Members must follow procedures under the Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021, for implementing Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.
- Rigorous screening of all existing and future client accounts against the sanction lists is mandatory.
- De-listing requests must be forwarded to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA, and MEA Joint Secretary (UNP).

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporation of amended entries in the UNSC 1988(2011) Sanctions List concerning individuals and entities linked to the Taliban.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no association with listed entities or individuals.
- Adhere to clause 54 of SEBI Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Guidelines dated June 06, 2024.
- Regularly monitor the SEBI website for updates regarding the UNSC Sanctions Committee List.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures stringent compliance and screening mechanisms across member broker databases to prevent financing of terrorism and adhere to national and international sanction mandates.
