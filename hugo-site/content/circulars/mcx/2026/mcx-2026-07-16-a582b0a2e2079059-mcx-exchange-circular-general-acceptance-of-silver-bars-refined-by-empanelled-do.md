## Summary

In accordance with SEBI guidelines and MCX Good Delivery Norms, the Exchange has added Parker Precious Metals LLP as an empanelled domestic refiner. Silver bars produced by this refiner are eligible for delivery to settle delivery obligations in Silver (30kg), Silver Mini (5kg), Silver Micro (1kg), and Silver100 (100gram) futures contracts.

## Key Points

- Parker Precious Metals LLP added as an empanelled domestic refiner for silver bars.
- Applicable to Silver (30kg), Silver Mini (5kg), Silver Micro (1kg), and Silver100 (100gram) futures contracts.
- Effective from July 27, 2026, across specified contract expiries.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DOP/CIR/P/2019/135 regarding commodity derivatives contract specifications and MCX Good Delivery Norms for BIS standard silver.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the addition of the new empanelled refiner and ensure compliance with contract specifications for physical delivery obligations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: July 17, 2026
- Effective Date: July 27, 2026
- Applicability by Expiry:
  - Silver (30kg): September 2026 expiry and onwards
  - Silver Mini (5kg): August 2026 expiry and onwards
  - Silver Micro (1kg): August 2026 expiry and onwards
  - Silver100 (100gram): July 2026 expiry and onwards

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding or settling delivery obligations in silver futures have an additional approved domestic refiner source, enhancing operational flexibility and delivery liquidity.
