## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced order-based surveillance measures to monitor total order messages generated by trading members during trading days. In cases of significant order placement, modification, or cancellation accompanied by a high message-to-trade ratio, the exchange reserves the right to take appropriate actions, including the intraday reduction of Order per Second (OPS) limits.

## Key Points

- Monitoring of total order messages, modifications, and cancellations across commodities and contracts.
- Tracking of message-to-trade ratios to identify excessive messaging.
- Potential intraday reduction of Order per Second (OPS) limits for all user IDs upon detecting anomalies.
- Continuation of surveillance measures following circular no. MCX/S&I/661/2026 dated November 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of proactive order surveillance mechanisms to curb excessive order placement/cancellation messaging.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review their algorithmic and manual trading strategies, order placement, modification, and cancellation practices to maintain optimal message-to-trade ratios and avoid OPS limit reductions.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as of circular date November 27, 2026 (continuing from November 26, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members utilizing high-frequency or high-volume order modification/cancellation strategies may experience operational disruptions if OPS limits are reduced intraday. Proper strategy optimization is required.
