## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding updates to the UNSC Sanctions List pursuant to communication received from SEBI. Members are advised to review the removal of 03 entries and amendment of 03 entries under the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List and comply with Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- SEBI notification regarding updates to the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List (removal of 3 entries and amendment of 3 entries).
- Access links provided for the consolidated UNSC sanctions list, 1267 aq sanctions list, and 1988 Taliban sanctions list.
- Members are required to follow procedures under the Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021, for implementing Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.
- Mandate to scan all existing and future accounts against the sanction list in accordance with clause 54 of SEBI AML/CFT guidelines (June 06, 2024).
- Instructions for forwarding de-listing requests electronically to MHA and MEA.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates (removals and amendments) to the UNSC Sanctions Lists (1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida, and 1988 Taliban lists).

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan existing and future accounts to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to entities or individuals on the sanctions list.
- Implement Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021 under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.
- Forward de-listing requests to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA (jsctcr-mha@gov.in) with a copy to MEA Joint Secretary (UNP) (jsunp@mea.gov.in).
- Direct individuals/entities seeking de-listing to apply via the UN Ombudsperson.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Central Government Order Reference: February 02, 2021
- SEBI AML Guidelines Reference: June 06, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must maintain robust screening mechanisms to detect and freeze assets or accounts linked to listed individuals/entities, ensuring ongoing regulatory compliance with anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing frameworks.
