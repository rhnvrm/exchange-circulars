## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the segregation of the option contract symbol OFGOLD into separate Product IDs and Product Names for Call (OFGOLDC) and Put (OFGOLDP) options, effective October 26, 2026. This change enhances operational efficiency and applies to specified commodity option contracts.

## Key Points

- Segregation of symbol OFGOLD into OFGOLDC (Call Options) and OFGOLDP (Put Options).
- Effective date for the segregation is October 26, 2026.
- Mass order cancellation requests must be submitted using the respective separate Product IDs.
- All active GTC/GTD orders associated with the affected contracts will be cancelled upon introduction of the split Product IDs.
- No changes to symbol names, API structures, Bhav Copy, Trade Files, or RT feeds.
- Market Data feeds (MDI, EMDI, EOBI) will contain the linked product ID of split products.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition from a common Product ID for option contracts to distinct Product IDs for Call and Put options, starting with GOLD option contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and vendors must ensure readiness of their front-end systems and make necessary configurations to support split Product IDs for order management and related processes.
- Members must submit separate cancellation requests for Call and Put option orders during mass order cancellations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their order management and front-end systems to handle separate Product IDs for Call and Put options. Active GTC/GTD orders on affected contracts will be cancelled, requiring members to re-enter orders if necessary.
