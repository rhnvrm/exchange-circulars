## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a revision in the launch date for Natural Gas and Natural Gas Mini April 2027 Futures and January 2027 Options expiry contracts. In partial modification of earlier circulars MCX/TRD/371/2026 and MCX/TRD/552/2026, these contracts will now be available for trading effective October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised contracts: Natural Gas and Natural Gas Mini Futures (April 27, 2027 expiry) and Options (January 21, 2027 expiry).
- New availability date for trading: October 28, 2026.
- Supersedes relevant provisions of circulars MCX/TRD/371/2026 and MCX/TRD/552/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification of exchange trading schedules and contract launch dates for specific natural gas derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the revised schedule and inform their respective clients and constituents accordingly.

## Important Dates

- October 23, 2026: Issuance date of the circular.
- October 28, 2026: Effective date for trading availability of the specified futures and options contracts.

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact operational adjustment ensuring market participants are aligned on the availability of new expiry contracts for natural gas.
