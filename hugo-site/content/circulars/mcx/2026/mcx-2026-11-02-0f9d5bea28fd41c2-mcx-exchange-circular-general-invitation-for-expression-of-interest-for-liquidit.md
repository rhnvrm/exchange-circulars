## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular inviting Expressions of Interest (EOI) from eligible participants for appointment as Market Makers under the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for MCX Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contracts in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for MCX Electricity Futures was launched on July 10, 2025, and the Exchange proposes to continue it.
- Interested Market Makers must fulfill specific eligibility criteria including a minimum of 3 years of experience in commodities/securities/electricity markets, minimum net worth of Rs. 5 Crores, manpower capability of at least 10 employees, and adequate technological capabilities.
- Applicants who pass eligibility criteria and technical readiness will be selected based on lowest bids for near, second, and third-month contracts.
- Separate annexures (Annexure B, C, D) are specified for submitting bids for near, second, and third-month market making.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for electricity futures as permitted by SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DMP/CIR/P/2018/55 dated March 26, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible members must submit bids and meet order-level obligations, margin requirements, and technical testing standards in the Exchange's test environment.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** November 06, 2026 (till 5 PM)
- **Tie Resolution (if any):** T+1 days for revised bids

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and market depth in MCX Electricity Futures contracts by appointing dedicated market makers across near, second, and third-month tenors.
