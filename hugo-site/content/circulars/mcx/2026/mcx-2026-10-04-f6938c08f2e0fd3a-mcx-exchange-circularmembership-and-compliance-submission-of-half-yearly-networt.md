## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular reminding all members to submit their Half Yearly Networth Certificate and Computation of Networth as on September 30, 2026. The prescribed deadline for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- All members must maintain minimum Networth at all points of time and submit half-yearly certificates.
- Members registered with NSE are required to submit their Networth Certificate to NSE only, under the common submission sharing mechanism between exchanges. NSE will share the data with MCX.
- Members NOT registered with NSE must submit online in electronic form via the MCX member portal (https://member.mcxindia.com).
- The variable networth requirement (10% of average daily cash balance of clients retained across segments/exchanges in the previous 6 months) continues to apply as per circular MCX/MEM/065/2026.
- In case of any shortfall, members must submit a revised Networth Certificate meeting the requirement along with the original certificate.
- Disciplinary actions and penalties will be levied for non-submission, shortfalls, or failure to rectify deficiencies within 15 calendar days.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of continuing membership norms, common exchange submission mechanisms, and penalty structures for networth shortfalls or late filings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submit Half Yearly Networth Certificate and Computation as on September 30, 2026, by November 30, 2026.
- NSE-registered members submit via NSE; non-NSE members submit via the MCX member portal.
- Rectify any reported deficiencies or shortfalls within specified timelines to avoid disciplinary penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Networth as on Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 05, 2026
- **Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures financial stability and regulatory compliance of members. Failure to comply leads to disciplinary actions, fines, and penalties as outlined in prior circulars.
