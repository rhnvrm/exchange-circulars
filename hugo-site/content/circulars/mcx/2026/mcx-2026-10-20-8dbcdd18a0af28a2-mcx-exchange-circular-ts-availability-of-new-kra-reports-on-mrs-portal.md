## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued Circular No. MCX/S&I/594/2026 dated October 21, 2026, announcing the availability of new KRA reports on the Member Reporting System (MRS) Portal in continuation of earlier circular MCX/S&I/273/2026.

## Key Points

- Two new KRA reports are now accessible on the MRS Portal (mrs.mcxindia.com):
  1. Indicative UCCs – PAN Not Available in KRA
  2. UCCs - KRA Status Details
- Members can access these reports via the MRS Portal navigation path: Login → Download Reports.
- Members are required to ensure that clients whose KYCs are not validated by KRAs are restricted from trading.
- Specific monitoring of open positions for non-compliant clients is advised.

## Regulatory Changes

Enhancement of member reporting and surveillance obligations regarding KYC and KRA status validation for Unique Client Codes (UCCs).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must download and review the new KRA reports from the MRS portal, take necessary actions for clients with 'Not Available' or unvalidated status, ensure such clients are not permitted to trade, and closely monitor any open positions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must strengthen their compliance and monitoring mechanisms regarding client KYC validation via KRA to avoid regulatory penalties and restrict trading access for non-compliant clients.
