## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued the Master Circular on Computer to Computer Link (CTCL), consolidating all relevant circulars and directions applicable as on March 31, 2026. This master circular replaces existing Business Rules of the Exchange related to CTCL and rescinds earlier circulars listed in Schedule I.

## Key Points

- Consolidates regulatory guidelines and operational instructions on Computer to Computer Link (CTCL).
- Covers empanelment of Vendors / ISVs (Independent Software Vendors), criteria, documentation, and procedures.
- Rescinds previous circulars and communications as specified in Schedule I, while preserving past actions, investigations, and liabilities.
- Replaces existing Business Rules of the Exchange pertaining to CTCL.

## Regulatory Changes

- Supersedes and consolidates earlier circulars including MCX/CTCL/223/2025, MCX/CTCL/364/2025, MCX/CTCL/471/2025, and corrigendum MCX/CTCL/099/2026.
- Replaces the existing Business Rules of the Exchange with the provisions set forth in this Master Circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must review their CTCL setups and ensure ongoing compliance with the consolidated guidelines set out in the Master Circular.
- ISVs and trading members must adhere to the empanelment criteria, documentation requirements, and operational terms defined herein.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: April 30, 2026
- Effective Date / Applicability: Applicable based on consolidated guidelines up to March 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational continuity is maintained as previous actions and liabilities remain unaffected under the rescission clauses. Provides a single point of reference for all CTCL-related compliance and technical standards for members and ISVs.
