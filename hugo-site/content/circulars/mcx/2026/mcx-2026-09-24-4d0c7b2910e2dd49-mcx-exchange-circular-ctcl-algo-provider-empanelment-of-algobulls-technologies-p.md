## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the empanelment of M/s AlgoBulls Technologies Private Limited as an Algo Provider to offer Algorithmic Trading Facility to the trading members of the Exchange, with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Name:** M/s AlgoBulls Technologies Private Limited
- **Registered Address:** Office #1203, Cello Triumph, B-wing, IB Patel Road, off the W-E highway, Goregaon East, Mumbai 400063
- **Contact Details:** Phone: 7703935116, Email ID: rishabh.kashyap@algobulls.com
- **Applicability:** Effective with immediate effect (September 25, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular is an operational vendor empanelment notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members interested in utilizing algorithmic trading services through the empaneled vendor can coordinate using the provided contact details and refer to the MCX website for the updated vendor list.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the availability of empaneled third-party algorithmic trading vendors for members operating on the MCX platform.
