## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has released the futures and options contracts launch calendar for the month of October 2026 under Circular No. MCX/TRD/552/2026 dated September 29, 2026. Trading members are notified of the launch dates, expiry dates, and reference circulars for various commodity futures and options contracts.

## Key Points

- Lists launch and expiry schedules for October 2026 across various commodity contracts including Alumini, Aluminium, Cardamom, Copper, Cottonoil, Elecdmbl, Gold, Goldm, Goldguinea, Goldpetal, Goldten, Lead, Leadmini, Menthaoil, Nickel, Silver 100, Silverm, Silvermic, Steelrebar, Zinc, Zincmini, Mcxmetldex, and Mcxbulldex.
- Most base contracts are scheduled for launch on October 1, 2026, with specific schedules for Goldm (October 6, 2026), Mcxmetldex (October 15, 2026), Gold and Silverm (October 16, 2026), and Mcxbulldex (October 20, 2026).
- Members are advised to refer to individual contract specifications and delivery/settlement procedures available on the MCX website.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine monthly futures and options contracts launch calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the launch dates, contract expiry dates, and respective circular references for trading participation in October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Contract Launch Dates: October 01, 2026 onwards (specific dates vary by contract up to October 20, 2026)
- Expiry Dates: Ranging from January 2027 through November 2027 depending on the contract.

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and advance notice to market participants and members regarding contract availability, launch schedules, and expiry timelines for October 2026 on MCX.
