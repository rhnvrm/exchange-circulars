## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has announced a mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026. Participation is mandatory for members using approved algorithm strategies, and members are requested to participate for a minimum of one hour at both the primary and disaster recovery (DR) sites.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- Special Session: 01:55 pm – 01:59 pm; Mock trading timing (Primary): 02:00 pm – 04:00 pm.
- Abrupt primary site disconnection between 03:55 pm and 04:00 pm to test migration to DR site.
- DR Mock trading timing: 04:30 pm – 06:30 pm.
- Mandatory participation for all members having approved algorithm strategies, using all algorithm strategies.
- Connectivity available through existing leased line or internet via SSL VPN link (https://trade.mcxindia.com).
- UCC interface mock session available at https://uccapp.mcxindia.com.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any obligation for pay-in and pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational and testing circular issued under exchange rules, bye-laws, and regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members with approved algorithm strategies must mandatorily participate using all algorithm strategies.
- Minimum participation period of one hour required at both primary and disaster recovery sites.
- Members must put in place adequate mechanisms to restore trading systems to production state at the end of the mock session.
- Members should test UCC interface login and connectivity details provided in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness and software stability check for trading members, CTCL users, and algorithmic traders ahead of live market operations, ensuring smooth failover capabilities to disaster recovery sites.
