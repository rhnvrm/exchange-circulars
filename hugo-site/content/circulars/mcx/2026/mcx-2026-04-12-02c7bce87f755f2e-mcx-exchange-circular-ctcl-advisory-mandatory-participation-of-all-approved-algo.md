## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has issued an advisory reminding all Trading Members that participation of all approved algorithms (including Algo Strategy IDs whether enabled or not enabled on user IDs) in monthly mock or simulation trading sessions is strictly mandatory, regardless of whether the underlying algorithm has undergone changes or remained static.

## Key Points

- Mandatory requirement applies to all approved algorithms irrespective of recent code changes or static status.
- The Exchange will strictly monitor participation; non-participation will trigger a call for formal justification.
- Failure to respond or providing unsatisfactory justification will result in disciplinary action, including suspension of proprietary trading rights for a minimum of one trading day.
- Members must ensure necessary infrastructure and personnel are in place to participate for a minimum of one hour in upcoming mock trading sessions or via the Exchange-provided simulation environment once every month.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiterates and reinforces previous exchange circulars regarding mandatory algorithm participation in mock/simulation trading sessions (MCX/CTCL/891/2020, MCX/CTCL/223/2025, and MCX/CTCL/364/2025).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review their approved algorithms, maintain required testing infrastructure, and participate for at least one hour monthly in mock sessions or the simulation environment, notifying the Exchange accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: April 13, 2026
- Frequency: Monthly ongoing requirement for mock/simulation trading sessions.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances system robustness and operational readiness of algorithmic trading infrastructures. Non-compliance carries direct operational risks, including potential suspension of proprietary trading rights.
