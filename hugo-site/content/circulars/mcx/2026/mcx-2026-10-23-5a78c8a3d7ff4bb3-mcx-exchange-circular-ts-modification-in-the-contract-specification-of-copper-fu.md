## Summary

MCX has announced a modification in the quality specifications and shape for Copper Futures contracts, effective November 3, 2026, applicable to July 2027 expiry and onwards.

## Key Points

- Modification in Quality Specifications & Shape for Copper Futures contracts.
- Effective Date: November 3, 2026.
- Applicability: July 2027 expiry contracts and onwards.
- Streamlines quality standards referencing approved brands by MCX or LME with reliance on the Certificate of Analysis (CoA) issued by the producer.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated quality specifications for Copper Cathodes, referencing approved brands by either MCX or LME and relying on the Certificate of Analysis (CoA) issued by the producer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take note of the modifications and notify their respective clients and constituents.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Applicability: July 2027 expiry and onwards

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances contract specification clarity and alignment with approved exchange brands for copper futures trading.
