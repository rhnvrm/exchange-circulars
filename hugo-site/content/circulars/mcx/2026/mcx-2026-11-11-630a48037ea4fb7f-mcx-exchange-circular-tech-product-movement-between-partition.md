## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the movement of the OFCOPPERP product from one partition to another to optimize system performance and manage data streams, effective November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Product symbol being moved: OFCOPPERP.
- Changes will be reflected in the product master file (Mcxscrip.bcp) and Stream Master on SFTP (/Common/Contract Master).
- No changes in MDI and ETI API.
- Effective live date: November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to make necessary changes at their end to accommodate the product movement.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact for members trading OFCOPPERP to update their systems, master files, and stream configurations accordingly.
