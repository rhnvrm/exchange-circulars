## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding revisions in formats and introduction of new requirements for the registration of Computer-to-Computer Link (CTCL) software. This includes the mandatory submission of a Vendor Confirmation Letter under the Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF), updated audit reports, certificates, and undertakings for trading members and algo providers.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Confirmation Letter (CSCRF):** Trading members must submit a Vendor Confirmation Letter confirming compliance with minimum software/hardware requirements and security patch levels per SEBI CSCRF guidelines.
- **Revised Audit Reports and Certificates:** Updated pre-approval audit reports, audit certificates, and in-house software development undertakings are introduced.
- **Algo Provider Updates:** Revisions to Chapter 14 Annexure C ("UNDERTAKING To be provided by Algo Provider") incorporating additional clauses on affiliation with other entities, along with specified VAPT report formats.
- **Transition Period:** Applications submitted in old formats (per circular MCX/CTCL/256/2026) will be accepted until November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of Vendor Confirmation Letter (Annexure A) for CTCL registration pursuant to SEBI CSCRF framework and MCX circular MCX/TECH/501/2026.
- Revision of Auditor Report (Annexure) and Auditor Certificate (Annexure 28).
- Revision of Undertaking for In-house software development by Member for CTCL/IBT/WT/ATF/DMA (Annexure 20).
- Revision of Algo Provider Empanelment Undertaking (Annexure C) and VAPT Report format.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and empanelled vendors/ASPs/algo providers must adopt the revised document formats and submit necessary CSCRF confirmation letters.
- Ensure all software registration requests submitted from December 1, 2026 onwards strictly use the revised formats.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** Immediate (November 19, 2026).
- **Old Format Acceptance Deadline:** November 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines software registration compliance and strengthens cybersecurity governance across trading members and algo providers on the MCX platform.
