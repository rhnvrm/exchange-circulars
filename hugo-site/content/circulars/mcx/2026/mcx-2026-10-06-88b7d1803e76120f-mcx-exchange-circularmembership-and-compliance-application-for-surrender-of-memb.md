## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has received an application for surrender of membership from Emkay Commotrade Limited (Member ID: 12830, SEBI Registration No: INZ000044936). The Exchange has invited any objections from interested parties within 7 days of the issuance of the circular.

## Key Points

- Member Name: Emkay Commotrade Limited
- Member ID: 12830
- SEBI Registration Number: INZ000044936
- Circular Reference: MCX/MEM/565/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine membership cessation notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Any objections in connection with the surrender of membership must be sent by email to the Membership Department at sebiregistration@mcxindia.com within the stipulated deadline.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 07, 2026
- Objection Deadline: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with no direct impact on general market participants, barring any pending claims or objections against the member.
