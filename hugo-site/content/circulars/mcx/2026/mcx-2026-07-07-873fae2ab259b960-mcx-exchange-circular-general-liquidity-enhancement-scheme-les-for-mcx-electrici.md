## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the extension of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for its Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) contracts until October 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Electricity Futures (Monthly Base Load) is extended until October 9, 2026.
- This circular references the earlier Exchange circular MCX/PMT/204/2026 dated April 16, 2026.
- All other terms, conditions, and provisions stated in the aforementioned circular remain unchanged and continue to apply.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an extension of an existing liquidity enhancement program.

## Compliance Requirements

Members participating in the Electricity Futures contracts should note the extended timeline.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: July 08, 2026
- LES Extension Deadline: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; provides continued support for market liquidity in electricity futures contracts.
