## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced amendments to its Bye-laws and Rules, pursuant to Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) approval letter dated October 07, 2025. The amendments were published in the Gazette of India on April 25, 2026, and have come into force immediately from that date.

## Key Points

- SEBI approved the amendments to MCX Bye-laws and Rules vide letter no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-RAC-1/P/OW/2025/000026134/1 dated October 07, 2025.
- The amended Bye-laws and Rules were officially published in the Gazette of India (Weekly Gazette No. 17) on April 25, 2026.
- The changes are effective from April 25, 2026.
- Market participants and members are advised to take note of the updated legal framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal amendments to MCX Bye-laws and Rules as approved by SEBI under regulatory oversight frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note of the updated Bye-laws and Rules effective from April 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Approval Date: October 07, 2025
- Gazette Publication & Effective Date: April 25, 2026
- Circular Issuance Date: April 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational and legal clarity update for exchange members aligning governance documents with SEBI's approved regulatory framework. No direct trading disruption or adverse compliance burden expected.
