## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has issued a reminder to all eligible trading members (Type – III) regarding the submission of the System Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026. The deadline for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all eligible trading members (Type – III).
- Mandatory for members who traded using algorithm software or CTCL trading software across segments between April 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026.
- Submission must be completed electronically via the Enhance Member Portal.
- Failure to submit by the due date will attract penalties or disciplinary action as per Exchange Circular Ref No. MCX/COMP/213/2026 dated April 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; reminder of existing reporting mandates for algorithmic and CTCL trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members must submit the System Audit Report for the period ended September 30, 2026, through the Enhance Member Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 17, 2026
- Due Date for Submission: November 30, 2026
- Audit Period: April 01, 2026 to September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures continued technological and algorithmic compliance among trading members. Non-compliance risks financial penalties and disciplinary actions by the exchange.
