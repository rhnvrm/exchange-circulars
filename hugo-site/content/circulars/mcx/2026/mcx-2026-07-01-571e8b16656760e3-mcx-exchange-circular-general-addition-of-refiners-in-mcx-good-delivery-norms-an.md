## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the addition of three new domestic refiners and expanded the Good Delivery list for gold futures contracts in adherence to SEBI guidelines and MCX Good Delivery Norms.

## Key Points

- **New Refiners Added:** Three domestic refiners (M. D. Overseas Pvt Ltd, Kundan Refinery Pvt Ltd, and Zaveri and Company Pvt Ltd) have been empanelled under MCX Good Delivery Norms for specific gold contracts.
- **Expansion for Existing Refiners:** Existing empanelled refiners including Titan Company Ltd, Augmont Enterprises Ltd, Parker Precious Metals LLP, and Sovereign Metals Limited have been added to the Good Delivery list of remaining gold futures contracts.
- **Eligible Contracts:** Applicable to running futures contracts of Gold (1kg), Gold Mini (100gm), Gold Ten (10gm), Gold Guinea (8gm), and Gold Petal (1gm).

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DOP/CIR/P/2019/135 dated November 14, 2019, concerning modifications in commodity derivatives contract specifications.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note of the expanded list of empanelled refiners and eligible gold bars/coins for meeting delivery obligations in gold futures contracts.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 01, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and delivery efficiency in gold futures contracts across various denominations by expanding the pool of acceptable Good Delivery refiners.
