## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding reporting requirements under the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) and Common Reporting Standard (CRS), revised through the Income-Tax Act, 2025 read with Income-Tax Rules, 2026. This follows SEBI circular no. HO/38/14/(18)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25560/2026 dated November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI circular updates reporting requirements and due diligence under FATCA and CRS following the replacement of the Income-Tax Act, 1961 with the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026 effective April 1, 2026.
- Reporting obligations apply to entities qualifying as Reporting Financial Institutions (RFIs) under Rule 238(8) of the IT Rules, 2026.
- Registered intermediaries who are RFIs must take necessary steps to ensure compliance with Rules 238 to 240 of the IT Rules, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- The Income-Tax Act, 1961 and Income-Tax Rules, 1962 have been replaced by the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Income-Tax Rules, 2026 effective April 1, 2026.
- AEOI framework reportable accounts under FATCA/CRS reporting are now governed in terms of Section 508 of the Income-Tax Act, 2025, read with Rules 238, 239, and 240, and Form 166 of the IT Rules, 2026.
- Rule 239(12)(a) of the IT Rules, 2026 mandates regulators to issue instructions/guidelines to regulated financial institutions.

## Compliance Requirements

- All registered intermediaries qualifying as Reporting Financial Institutions (RFIs) must ensure full compliance with Rules 238 to 240 of the IT Rules, 2026.
- Maintain account information and ensure availability to satisfy reporting obligations as prescribed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of IT Act, 2025 / IT Rules, 2026: April 1, 2026.
- SEBI Circular Date: November 11, 2026.
- MCX Circular Date: November 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Financial institutions and intermediaries must align their reporting mechanisms and due diligence procedures with the new Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026 framework for FATCA and CRS compliance.
