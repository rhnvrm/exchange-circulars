## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the launch of futures trading in the Kapas November 2027 contract. The contract will be available for trading starting from November 02, 2026, and is set to expire on November 30, 2027.

## Key Points

- **Contract Name**: Kapas November 2027 Futures
- **Contract Launch Date**: November 02, 2026
- **Contract Expiry Date**: November 30, 2027
- **Trading Unit**: 1 lot (200 maunds of 20 kg each i.e. 4 MT)
- **Quotation/Base Value**: Rs per 20 kg (Ex-Warehouse Rajkot, exclusive of GST/Sales Tax)
- **Tick Size**: 50 Paise
- **Maximum Order Size**: 50 Lots i.e. 200 MT

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of new futures contract specifications and trading parameters in accordance with the Rules, Bye-Laws, and Business Rules of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must adhere to the specified contract specifications, trading parameters, daily price limits (DPL), and margin requirements issued by MCX and MCXCCL.

## Important Dates

- **Launch Date**: November 02, 2026
- **Expiry Date**: November 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities for market participants and hedgers in the agricultural commodity (Kapas) segment.
