## Summary

MCX has announced the introduction of separate Product IDs and Product Names for Call and Put option contracts, starting with GOLD options (segregating symbol OFGOLD into OFGOLDC for Call and OFGOLDP for Put) effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Segregation of symbol OFGOLD into separate products: OFGOLDC (Call Options) and OFGOLDP (Put Options) effective November 02, 2026.
- Mass Order Cancellation requests must be submitted using the respective split Product ID for Call and Put option orders.
- All active GTC/GTD orders associated with affected contracts will be cancelled upon introduction of split Product IDs.
- No changes to Symbol name, Bhav Copy, Trade Files, Real-Time feeds, or other API structures.
- Market Data MDI, EMDI, EOBI will contain the linked product ID of split products.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of bifurcated Product IDs for commodity option contracts to enhance operational efficiency.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and vendors must ensure front-end system readiness and configurations to support split Product IDs for order management and related processes.
- Separate cancellation requests required for Call and Put option orders.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on member front-end systems, order management, and mass order cancellations requiring separate handling for Call and Put options. Active GTC/GTD orders will be cancelled.
