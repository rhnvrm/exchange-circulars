## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, emphasizing the simplification of digital interfaces, use of vernacular languages, and AI-powered translation platforms such as Bhashini. Trading members are advised by SEBI and MCX to facilitate the development of AI and ML-based public query systems for common securities market aspects.

## Key Points

- RBI released NSFI 2025–30 highlighting simpler digital interfaces and use of AI.
- Emphasis on addressing language barriers through vernacular languages and AI-powered translation platforms like Bhashini.
- Recommendation for developing AI & ML-based public query systems on common banking, finance, and securities market aspects.
- Trading members advised to implement query systems via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of RBI's National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30 guidelines across trading member platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take appropriate steps to simplify digital interfaces, address language barriers using tools like Bhashini, and develop AI/ML-based public query systems for securities market-related queries on their websites and social media extensions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- NSFI Reference Date: December 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Enhances accessibility, customer support, and financial inclusion through advanced digital interfaces and AI/ML-powered public query systems on trading member platforms.
