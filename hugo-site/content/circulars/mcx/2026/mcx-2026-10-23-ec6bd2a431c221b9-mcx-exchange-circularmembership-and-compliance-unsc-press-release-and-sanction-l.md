## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding updates to the UNSC Sanctions List and implementation procedures under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, and SEBI AML/CFT guidelines.

## Key Points

- Notification of amendments made to the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List (3 entries updated).
- Reference to SEBI and UN consolidated lists of individuals and entities subject to asset freezes and financial service restrictions.
- Reminder to follow Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021, on UAPA Section 51A implementation.
- Mandate to scan all existing and future client accounts against the updated sanction lists.
- Procedures outlined for forwarding de-listing requests to MHA and MEA.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of amended UNSC Sanction Lists pursuant to resolution 1267 (1999) and 1988 (2011).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no association with listed entities or individuals as per clause 54 of SEBI AML/CFT guidelines.
- De-listing requests must be forwarded electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA, and Joint Secretary (UNP), MEA.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for brokers and members to update screening systems and perform database scans against the latest UNSC sanctions list updates.
