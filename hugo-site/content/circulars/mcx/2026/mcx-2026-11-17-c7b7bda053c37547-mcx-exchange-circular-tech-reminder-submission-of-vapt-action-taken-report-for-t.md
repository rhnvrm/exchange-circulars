## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has issued a reminder regarding the submission of the Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) Action Taken Report (ATR) for the financial year 2025-26. Trading members with open vulnerabilities identified by CERT-In empaneled auditors must submit the ATR through the Member Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Deadline for submission of VAPT Action Taken Report (ATR) / Compliance Report is November 30, 2026.
- Submission is complete only upon uploading the digitally signed report to the Exchange member portal.
- NSE-registered trading members who are also registered with MCX should submit cyber security incident details to NSE as per shared mechanisms, while members not registered with NSE continue submissions directly to MCX.
- Non-compliance or failure to submit attracts penalties as prescribed in Exchange circular MCX/COMP/213/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration of existing technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among exchanges and standardization of penalties for non-compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit their VAPT Action Taken Report (ATR) or Compliance Report digitally signed via the online Member Portal by November 30, 2026, addressing any open vulnerabilities identified by CERT-In empaneled auditors.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- VAPT ATR Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures robust cybersecurity standards across trading members and mitigates operational and technological risks. Failure to comply leads to financial penalties and disciplinary actions.
