## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has empaneled Modern Algos Private Limited as an Algo Provider to offer algorithmic trading facilities to trading members, effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: Modern Algos Private Limited
- Registered Address: H. No. 6-3-1192/1/1, Office No. 212, Whitehouse, Block II, Begumpet, Hyderabad, Telangana - 500016
- Contact Details: +91-40-4507 8889, +91-98850-78922 | sbaid@modernalgos.com
- Circular No: MCX/CTCL/471/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; administrative empanelment notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can engage with the empaneled vendor for algorithmic trading solutions in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the list of available empaneled algo providers for trading members on the exchange platform.
