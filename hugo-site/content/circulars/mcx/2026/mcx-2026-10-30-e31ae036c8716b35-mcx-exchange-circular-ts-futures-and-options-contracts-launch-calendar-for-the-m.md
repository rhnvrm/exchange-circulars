## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued circular no. MCX/TRD/622/2026 dated October 30, 2026, outlining the launch calendar for futures and options contracts scheduled for trading during the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Details various futures contracts including Alumini, Aluminium, Cardamom, Copper, Cottonoil, ElecDmbl, Goldguinea, Goldpetal, Goldten, Kapas, Lead, Leadmini, Menthaoil, Nickel, Silver 100, Steelrebar, Sunoil, Zinc, Zincmini, Goldm, Mcxmetldex, Crudeoil, Crudeoilm, Mcxbulldex, Naturalgas, and Natgasmini launching in November 2026.
- Outlines options contracts launch schedules for Copper Option and Zinc Option.
- Members are advised to refer to relevant contract specifications, delivery, and settlement procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine contract launch schedule notification in accordance with Exchange rules and bye-laws.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the launch dates and contract specifications for November 2026 and ensure adherence to applicable delivery and settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Contract Launch Dates: Throughout November 2026 (starting November 2, 2026 onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Standard operational notification providing market participants with clear visibility on upcoming commodity derivatives contract launches for November 2026.
