## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the postponement of the activity related to the introduction of separate Product IDs and Product Names for Call and Put Options, originally notified via circular MCXTECH/599/2026 dated October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- The scheduled rollout for separate Product IDs and Product Names for Call and Put Options stands postponed.
- A separate circular with revised implementation dates will be issued in due course.
- Members are requested to note the deferment and adjust arrangements accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a postponement of a technical and operational implementation schedule.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should suspend any pending technical integrations or system changes related to the new Product IDs and Product Names until revised dates are published.

## Important Dates

- **October 24, 2026**: Postponement circular issued.
- **Revised Date**: To be announced separately.

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Members have additional time to prepare for the technical changes once the revised schedule is announced.
