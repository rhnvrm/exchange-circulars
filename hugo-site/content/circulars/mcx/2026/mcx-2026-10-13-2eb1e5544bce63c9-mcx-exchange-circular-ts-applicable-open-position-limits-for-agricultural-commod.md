## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued circular no. MCX/S&I/579/2026 dated October 14, 2026, specifying the applicable open position limits for agricultural commodities at client and member levels pursuant to relevant SEBI circulars.

## Key Points

- Numerical position limits are prescribed for agricultural commodities including Cotton, Kapas, Cottonseed Wash Oil, Crude Sunflower Oil, Mentha Oil, and Cardamom.
- Client overall open interest limit is the absolute number specified, with the near-month limit set at one-fourth of the overall position limit.
- Overall member-level limits are set as numerical limits or 15% of market-wide open position (whichever is higher), with near-month limits equivalent to one-fourth of the overall member-level limit.
- Exchange-wide position limits are established at 50% of deliverable supply for the respective commodities.
- Crude Sunflower Oil futures contracts were newly launched on October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in continuation of previous Exchange circulars and SEBI circulars governing commodity derivatives position limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and their respective clients must ensure compliance with the specified client-level, member-level, and exchange-wide position limits.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory clarity and risk control for market participants trading agricultural commodity derivatives on the exchange.
