## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the launch of Crude Sunflower Oil Futures contracts (Symbol: SUNOIL) with effect from October 12, 2026, under Circular No. MCX/TRD/568/2026 dated October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Contract Launch Date:** October 12, 2026.
- **Contracts Available:** November 2026, December 2026, and January 2027 expiry contracts.
- **Trading Unit:** 5 MT.
- **Quotation/Base Value:** Rs./10 Kg, Ex-Tank JNPT, exclusive of Sales tax/GST.
- **Tick Size:** 10 paise.
- **Daily Price Limits (DPL):** Initial slab limit of 4%, increasing further by enhanced slab of 2% after 15 minutes (total DPL of 6%).
- **Margins:** Minimum initial margin of 12% or based on SPAN (whichever is higher), with a minimum 1% extreme loss margin.
- **Position Limits:** 61,600 MT for individual clients; 6,16,000 MT or 15% of market wide open position (whichever is higher) collectively for a member.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of new trading parameters and contract specifications for Crude Sunflower Oil futures contracts in accordance with the Rules, Bye-Laws, and Business Rules of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to the contract specifications, trading parameters, daily price limits, and margin requirements specified by MCX and MCXCCL.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Contract Launch Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Contract Expiry Dates:** November 30, 2026, December 31, 2026, and January 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides hedging and trading opportunities for market participants dealing in edible oils and agri-commodities, specifically Crude Sunflower Oil.
