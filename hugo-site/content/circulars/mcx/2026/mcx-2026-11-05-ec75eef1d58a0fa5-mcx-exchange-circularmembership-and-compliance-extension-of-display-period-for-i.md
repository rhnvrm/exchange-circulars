## Summary

MCX has announced the extension of the display period for investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook until December 31, 2026, pursuant to SEBI communications. Additionally, effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, are extended to November 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Display period for Project Jagrook investor awareness messages extended to December 31, 2026.
- Effective dates under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, extended to November 15, 2026.
- Messages must be displayed in a bilingual format (Hindi and English) on trading applications and websites.
- Continues previous Exchange Circular No. MCX/MEM/560/2026 dated October 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of deadlines for displaying investor awareness messages as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must continue displaying Project Jagrook investor awareness messages on trading applications and websites in bilingual format (Hindi and English) through December 31, 2026.
- Ensure compliance with extended effective dates of November 15, 2026, for specific provisions.

## Important Dates

- October 05, 2026: Reference date of previous circular (MCX/MEM/560/2026).
- November 06, 2026: Issuance date of current circular.
- November 15, 2026: Extended effective date for paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026.
- December 31, 2026: Extended display period deadline for Project Jagrook messages.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; routine compliance extension for members regarding investor awareness displays.
