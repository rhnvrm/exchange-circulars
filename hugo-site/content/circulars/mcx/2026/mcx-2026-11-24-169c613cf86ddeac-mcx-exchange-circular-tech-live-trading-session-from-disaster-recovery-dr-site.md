## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd. (MCX) has announced that it will conduct live trading sessions from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site starting from November 24, 2026 (2nd Session) through November 27, 2026. During this period, all operational activities of the Exchange will be handled from the DR site.

## Key Points

- **Event:** Live trading session from Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- **Start Date:** November 24, 2026 (2nd Session).
- **End Date:** November 27, 2026.
- **Scope:** All operational activities will be conducted from the DR site.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is a routine business continuity and disaster recovery operations procedure for market infrastructure institutions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the operational shift to the DR site and ensure their systems and connectivity are aligned accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Commencement:** November 24, 2026 (2nd Session)
- **Conclusion:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants must note the transition to the DR site. Operational coordination and support continue via the Customer Service Team (022-66494040 / customersupport@mcxindia.com).
