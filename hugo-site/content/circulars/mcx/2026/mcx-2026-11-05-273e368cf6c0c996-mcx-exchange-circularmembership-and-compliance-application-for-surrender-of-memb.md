## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has received applications for surrender of membership from three members: A.P.T. Commodities Private Limited, Purvag Commodities & Derivatives Private Limited, and Pentad Securities Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Surrendering Members:**
  - A.P.T. Commodities Private Limited (Member ID: 40800, SEBI Reg. No.: INZ000096932)
  - Purvag Commodities & Derivatives Private Limited (Member ID: 10000, SEBI Reg. No.: INZ000031236)
  - Pentad Securities Private Limited (Member ID: 56830, SEBI Reg. No.: INZ000168432)
- **Objection Submission:** Any objections regarding the surrender must be sent to sebiregistration@mcxindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational membership surrender notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders or members having objections to the surrender of membership by the aforementioned entities must submit their objections within the stipulated timeline.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Objection Deadline:** November 13, 2026 (within 7 days of the circular)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process with no broader market impact.
