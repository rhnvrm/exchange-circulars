## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the introduction of separate Product IDs and Product Names for Call and Put option contracts, starting with symbol SILVERM effective November 25, 2026, to enhance operational efficiency.

## Key Points

- Bifurcation of SILVERM options into OFSILVERMC (Call Options) and OFSILVERMP (Put Options).
- Mass Order Cancellation requests must be submitted using the respective separate Product IDs.
- All active GTC/GTD orders associated with the affected contract will be cancelled upon introduction of split Product IDs.
- No changes to Symbol name, existing security details in MCXScrips.bcp, API structures, or Bhav Copy/Trade Files.
- Market data feeds (MDI, EMDI, EOBI) will contain the linked product ID of split products.

## Regulatory Changes

- Structural change in product identification for commodity options, segregating common Product IDs into distinct Call and Put product identifiers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure readiness of front-end systems and configure order management to support split Product IDs for Call and Put options.
- Separate cancellation requests must be submitted for Call and Put option orders during mass order cancellations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members need to update order routing and management systems to handle separate product IDs for SILVERM options, ensuring proper handling of GTC/GTD order cancellations and mass cancellations.
