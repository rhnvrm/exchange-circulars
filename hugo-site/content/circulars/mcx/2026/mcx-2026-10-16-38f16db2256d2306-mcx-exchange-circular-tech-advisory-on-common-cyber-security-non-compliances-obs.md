## Summary

SEBI, in coordination with Stock Exchanges, conducted a special purpose cyber revalidation audit to evaluate the effectiveness of cyber security and cyber resilience audits and CERT-In Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK) platform monitoring by stock brokers. Based on these audits, MCX has issued an advisory highlighting common non-compliances to enhance awareness and compliance with the Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).

## Key Points

- Special purpose cyber revalidation audit conducted by SEBI and Stock Exchanges on stock brokers' systems.
- Observations highlight gaps in understanding and implementation of CSCRF requirements.
- Commonly observed non-compliances include issues with asset categorization, inventory details, internal policy exceptions/deviations, and CISO positioning.
- Trading members are advised to strengthen their cyber security posture and internal controls.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces compliance with existing Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) mandates issued by SEBI and exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must put in place necessary systems, controls, and procedures to address identified non-compliances.
- Ensure accurate asset classification and comprehensive critical asset inventories (including end-of-life, end-of-support, and installation dates).
- Update internal cyber security policies to clearly define coverage, exceptions, and deviations.
- Position the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) at a level equivalent to CTO/CIO to maintain independence.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market integrity and operational resilience by tightening cyber security oversight across trading members and stock brokers.
