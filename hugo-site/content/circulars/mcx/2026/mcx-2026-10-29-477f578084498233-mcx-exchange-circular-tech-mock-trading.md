## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd. (MCX) has announced a mock trading session for members on Saturday, October 31, 2026. The session includes testing across the primary data center and subsequent migration to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Special Session Time:** 01:55 pm – 01:59 pm.
- **Primary Site Mock Timing:** 02:00 pm – 04:00 pm.
- **DR Site Migration & Timing:** Trading will be abruptly disconnected from the primary site between 03:55 pm and 04:00 pm. Mock trading resumes from the DR site between 05:00 pm – 08:00 pm.
- **Connectivity:** Available through existing leased lines and SSL VPN link (https://trade.mcxindia.com) for internet users.
- **API Version:** MCX Enhanced Trading Interface (ETI) API version 1.4.2 dated August 14, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine exchange operational drill.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using CTCL facility are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session using CTCL software.
- Members should ensure their trading systems are restored to the production state at the end of the mock session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any obligation for pay-in and pay-out.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No financial impact; operational test only to verify system readiness and DR site failover mechanisms.
