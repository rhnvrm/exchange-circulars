## Summary

MCX has announced a mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026. Members are requested to participate using the MCX Enhanced Trading Interface (ETI) API version 1.4.2 to test their trading applications and Disaster Recovery site.

## Key Points

- Mock Trading Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- Special Session Timing: 02:25 PM to 02:29 PM.
- Mock Trading Timing: 02:30 PM to 06:30 PM.
- Connectivity available via existing leased lines or internet via SSL VPN link (https://trade.mcxindia.com).
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no obligations for pay-in and pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members using CTCL facility should actively participate in the mock trading session. Members must also ensure their trading systems are properly restored to the production state at the end of the session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness test for trading members with zero financial impact.
