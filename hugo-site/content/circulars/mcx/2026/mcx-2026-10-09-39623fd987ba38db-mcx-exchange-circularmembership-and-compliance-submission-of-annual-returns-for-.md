## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) has issued a circular regarding the mandatory submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year ended March 31, 2026. Trading members must submit annual audited networth certificates, computation of networth, auditor’s reports, audited annual accounts, director’s reports, shareholding details, and financial strength details on or before October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Deadline:** Submission of annual returns and confirmations is due on or before October 31, 2026.
- **Enhanced Supervision Guidelines:** In accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD2/CIR/P/2016/95 dated September 26, 2016, all trading members (including non-corporate members) must submit financial statements in the format prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013.
- **Common Submission Mechanism with NSE:** Members registered with NSE are required to submit their Annual Returns to NSE only; NSE will share the compliance reports with MCX. Submitting to NSE is treated as full compliance for MCX requirements.
- **Non-NSE Members:** Members not registered with NSE must submit their documents online via the MCX member portal (https://member.mcxindia.com).
- **Mandatory Annexures:** Required documents include Annual Accounts & Auditor's Report (Annexure 1), Networth Certificate & Computation (Annexure 2), Details of Membership on other exchanges/listing (Annexure 3), Details of Directors/Partners (Annexure 4), Shareholding/Sharing Pattern (Annexure 5), or Undertaking of no changes in documents C-2 to C-7 (Annexure 6), along with other relevant annexures for Networth requirement, PEP undertaking, disciplinary actions, and online submission procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces adherence to SEBI's Enhanced Supervision guidelines requiring financial statement formats under the Companies Act, 2013 for all trading members irrespective of corporate status.
- Utilizes the technology-based common submission mechanism between exchanges for dual-registered members (NSE and MCX).

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members must submit the prescribed Annual Returns, Annual Accounts, and Annual Confirmations.
- Dual-registered members (NSE & MCX) must file through NSE.
- Standalone MCX members must complete online submissions via the MCX member portal.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance, robust financial monitoring, and transparency of trading members through standardized annual reporting and streamlined cross-exchange submission mechanisms.
