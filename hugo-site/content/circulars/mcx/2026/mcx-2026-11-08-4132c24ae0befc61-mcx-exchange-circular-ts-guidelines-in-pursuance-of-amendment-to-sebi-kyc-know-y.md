## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI KRA regulations and circulars regarding KYC validation requirements and centralized mechanisms for reporting investor demise. Trading members must ensure client KYCs are duly uploaded and validated ("KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated"). Clients with "On Hold" or "Not Available" KYC status face trading and position restrictions effective November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Clients whose KYC records uploaded between October 01, 2026, and October 31, 2026, remain "On Hold" with KRAs will not be permitted to trade or square up open positions effective November 23, 2026.
- Open positions for non-compliant clients will naturally expire on the respective contract expiry date.
- Trading Members must ensure that only clients with "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status are permitted to trade.
- Regulated entities must block debit transactions, suspend trading accounts, and inactivate/close UCCs for deceased investors based on daily KRA demise data.
- Lists of clients with "On Hold" and "Not Available" status are available via the MRS Portal and designated email IDs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of strict KRA validation checks for records uploaded from October 01, 2026, to October 31, 2026.
- Enforcement of centralized mechanism for reporting the demise of investors through KRAs across all trading accounts and UCCs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must review lists of clients with "On Hold" and "Not Available" KYC status provided via the MRS Portal or email.
- Ensure non-compliant clients are restricted from trading and squaring up open positions effective November 23, 2026.
- PANs becoming KRA compliant subsequently will be permitted to trade on T+1 after receipt of KRA confirmation by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **November 09, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **November 23, 2026**: Effective date for trading and position restrictions on clients with unvalidated/"On Hold" KYC records.
- **T+1**: Resumption of trading permission for PANs achieving KRA compliance.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and clients must ensure timely KRA validation of KYC records to avoid abrupt trading restrictions and position freeze. Open positions for affected clients cannot be squared up manually and will be subject to natural expiry.
