## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a reminder to all members to submit data pertaining to Risk Based Supervision and Internal Audit Reports for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reminder to submit Data/Details/Information for the period April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, pertaining to Risk Based Supervision.
- Reminder to submit the Internal Audit Report for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be completed on or before November 30, 2026.
- Filings must be made via the Exchange Portal at https://member.mcxindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; this circular reinforces previous requirements outlined in MCX/COMP/558/2026 and MCX/COMP/559/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Members who have not yet submitted the required data and Internal Audit Reports must do so in the prescribed format on the Exchange Portal. Non-submission, delayed submission, or deficiencies will attract penalties and disciplinary actions as per Exchange Circular no. MCX/COMP/213/2026.

## Important Dates

- **Submission Deadline:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Failure to meet the deadline will lead to penalties and disciplinary measures. Members should ensure timely compliance to avoid regulatory sanctions.
