## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI KYC KRA Regulations, 2011. Effective November 02, 2026, clients whose KYC records remain 'On Hold' or 'Not Available' with KRAs are barred from trading or squaring up open positions until compliance is achieved.

## Key Points

- Applies to clients whose KYC records uploaded between September 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026, remain 'On Hold' (both Aadhaar and Non-Aadhaar based OVD).
- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded and validated ('KYC Registered' or 'KYC Validated').
- Exchange provides a file for PANs not uploaded by members (status 'Not Available').
- Demise data reported by KRAs requires blocking debit and trading accounts and inactivating UCCs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI circulars regarding KYC validation requirements and centralized reporting mechanisms for investor demise.
- Strict enforcement of trading prohibitions for non-compliant client accounts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must review lists of clients with 'On Hold' and 'Not Available' KRA status provided on designated email IDs.
- Ensure clients complete KYC validation requirements to avoid trading suspension.
- PANs becoming KRA compliant subsequent to restrictions will be permitted to trade on T+1 basis.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Effective Date of Restrictions:** November 02, 2026
- **KYC Upload Period Covered:** September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clients with non-compliant or unvalidated KYC statuses will be unable to execute fresh trades or square up open positions (which will naturally expire on contract expiry dates).
- Operational impact on trading members to coordinate with KRAs and rectify client KYC deficiencies promptly.
