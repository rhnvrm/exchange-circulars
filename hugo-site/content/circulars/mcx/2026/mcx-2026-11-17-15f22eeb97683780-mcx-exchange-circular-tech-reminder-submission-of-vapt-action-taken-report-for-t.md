## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has issued a reminder regarding the submission of the Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) Action Taken Report (ATR) for the financial year 2025-26. Trading members with open vulnerabilities identified by a CERT-In empaneled auditor must submit their ATR through the online member portal by November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to trading members who have open vulnerabilities identified in their VAPT Report for FY 2025-26.
- Submission deadline is November 30, 2026, via the online Member Portal.
- Submission is complete only upon uploading the digitally signed report.
- NSE trading members registered across other exchanges can submit via NSE as per common sharing mechanisms; members not registered with NSE must submit directly to MCX.
- Non-compliance may attract penalties as prescribed in Exchange circular no. MCX/COMP/213/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an operational reminder enforcing existing VAPT reporting and compliance mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review their CERT-In empaneled auditor VAPT reports for FY 2025-26, address open vulnerabilities, prepare the Action Taken Report (ATR), digitally sign it, and submit it through the online Member Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- November 17, 2026: Issuance of reminder circular (MCX/TECH/646/2026).
- November 30, 2026: Due date for submission of VAPT Action Taken Report / Compliance Report.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely submission to avoid financial penalties or disciplinary actions outlined under the rationalized penalty structure.
