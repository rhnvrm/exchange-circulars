## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the commencement of futures trading in the Gold Ten (10 gram) April 2027 contract, effective from November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Contract Symbol: GOLDTEN
- Contract Launch Date: November 02, 2026
- Contract Expiry Date: April 30, 2027
- Trading Unit & Quotation: 10 grams
- Tick Size: Re. 1 per 10 grams
- Initial Margin: Minimum 6% or based on SPAN, whichever is higher
- Extreme Loss Margin: Minimum 1%

## Regulatory Changes

None. The contract is launched in terms of provisions of the Rules, Bye-Laws and Business Rules of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must adhere to the contract specifications, trading parameters, applicable margins, and delivery/settlement details issued by MCX and MCXCCL from time to time.

## Important Dates

- Contract Launch Date: November 02, 2026
- Last Trading Day / Expiry Date: April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and hedgers with an additional liquidity window and contract maturity for gold derivatives.
