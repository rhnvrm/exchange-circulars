## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the revision of its Good Delivery Norms for BIS – Standard Gold/Silver. Building upon the initial launch via circular No. MCX/PMT/216/2022, the updated norms revise the eligibility criteria and the procedure for empanelment of domestic silver refiners to facilitate their participation and silver delivery on the MCX platform.

## Key Points

- Revision of MCX Good Delivery Norms specifically aimed at domestic silver refiners.
- Updated eligibility criteria and empanelment procedure for domestic refiners.
- Eligible domestic silver refiners are invited to apply for empanelment under the revised norms.
- Contact details provided for further clarifications through MCX Bullion Refiners' Empanelment Committee (MCX-BREC) procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amended eligibility conditions, financial audits, net worth computation rules, and technical qualification standards for domestic silver refiners seeking empanelment.
- Updated guidelines covering screening audits, clearance by MCX-BREC, proficiency testing, and responsible sourcing compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Domestic silver refiners wishing to deliver silver on the MCX platform must submit required documents, undergo technical and financial screening audits, and comply with anti-money laundering and anti-corruption laws.
- Adherence to revised specifications for gold and silver bars and continuous compliance with periodic assaying capability audits.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: May 17, 2026
- Effective immediately as per Exchange notification.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables domestic silver refiners to participate directly on the MCX platform, broadening the pool of deliverable silver stocks and enhancing market liquidity and transparency.
