## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued an advisory reiterating guidelines for trading members regarding the implementation of the Retail Algo Facility, in continuation of previous circulars and SEBI guidelines on safer retail participation in algorithmic trading.

## Key Points

- Members must deal only with empanelled algo providers, treating them as broker agents responsible for handling related complaints.
- Algo trading facility must be provided only after obtaining explicit exchange permission for each algo.
- All algo orders must be tagged with a unique exchange-provided identifier for audit trail purposes.
- Open APIs are prohibited; access is restricted to unique vendor client-specific API keys and static IP whitelisting with two-factor authentication (2FA).
- Broker servers routing API/algo orders must be located in India, maintaining at least 5 years of audit trail data.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration and enforcement of SEBI circular dated February 4, 2025, on safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading and previous exchange implementation standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Perform due diligence before onboarding empanelled algo providers.
- Ensure all API orders are routed through the member's trading system located in India.
- Implement robust systems to detect, identify, and categorize all orders as algo orders and appropriately tag them.
- Maintain audit trails and actual user-id identifications for at least 5 years.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective: Immediate enforcement of advisory guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members offering algorithmic trading facilities must audit and verify their API connections, vendor whitelisting, two-factor authentication, and unique algo identification mechanisms to ensure full compliance with exchange and SEBI standards.
