## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd. (MCX) has announced the empanelment of Multiicon Ideotechnology Pvt. Ltd. as an Algo Provider to offer algorithmic trading facilities to trading members.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Name:** Multiicon Ideotechnology Pvt. Ltd.
- **Registered Address:** 3rd Floor, Rumi Plaza, Airport Road, Rajkot-360 001, Gujarat.
- **Applicability:** With immediate effect.
- **Reference:** Circular no. MCX/CTCL/584/2026 dated October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard vendor empanelment notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members intending to use services from the empaneled algo provider should take note and ensure appropriate arrangements.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 15, 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

Expands the availability of approved algorithmic trading technology providers for MCX members.
