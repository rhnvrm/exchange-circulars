## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has notified members regarding the enablement of additional market data streams at the Primary Data Center (PDC) and Disaster Recovery Site (DRS), in continuation of circular MCX/TECH/587/2026 dated October 16, 2026. Members are required to enable the respective IP addresses and ports to ensure uninterrupted connectivity during live trading or unannounced failovers.

## Key Points

- Additional market data streams enabled at PDC and DRS.
- Members must configure IP addresses for both PDC and DRS in their end systems.
- Ensures seamless connection during unannounced live trading from DRS without manual intervention.
- Detailed IP addresses and TCP ports provided for host lookup, gateways, MTS/MCS, CTCL, Algo, and Drop Copy services across Annexures 1, 2, and 3.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational and technical update regarding trading infrastructure connectivity and disaster recovery readiness.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must update their end systems with the notified IP addresses and routing details for PDC and DRS connectivity.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026 (Circular date).

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is medium, ensuring members maintain robust connectivity and disaster recovery preparedness for trading and market data feeds.
