## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has extended the due date for the submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26 to December 31, 2026. This extension is in accordance with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular no. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- The due date for filing financial statements and Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 is extended from October 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
- Non-submission within the extended due date will attract disciplinary actions as per Exchange circular no. MCX/COMP/213/2026 dated April 17, 2026.
- Members must maintain net worth at all points of time and submit revised net worth certificates within stipulated timelines to avoid penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment with MCA General Circular ref. no. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026, extending the ROC filing due date to December 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to submit the Annual Returns for the Financial Year ended March 31, 2026, on or before December 31, 2026.
- Ensure ongoing maintenance of net worth and adherence to periodic reporting guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Extended Due Date for Annual Returns / Financial Statements: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Members receive necessary operational relief and time extensions for regulatory filings, avoiding immediate penalties while emphasizing strict adherence to net worth maintenance.
