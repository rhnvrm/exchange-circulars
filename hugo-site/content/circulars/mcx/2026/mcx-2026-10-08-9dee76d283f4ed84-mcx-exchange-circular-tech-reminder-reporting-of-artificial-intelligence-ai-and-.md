## Summary

MCX has issued a reminder for all active trading members using algorithmic software to report their Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) applications and systems on or before October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission applies to all active trading members using algorithmic software during the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Trading members not using AI/ML systems must submit a NIL report.
- Trading members of NSE who are registered with MCX and use algorithmic software across exchanges should submit AI/ML details to NSE, which shares them with respective exchanges.
- Members not registered with NSE must submit directly via the MCX Enhanced Portal (https://member.mcxindia.com).

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of previous circulars regarding mandatory AI/ML reporting for algorithm users, reiterating cross-exchange common submission sharing mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Active algo trading members must submit AI/ML applications and systems details or NIL reports on the MCX Enhanced Portal by the specified deadline. Non-compliant members are liable for disciplinary action.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Deadline: October 15, 2026
- Period under review: April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory oversight and transparency over the usage of AI and ML technologies in algorithmic trading across exchange platforms.
