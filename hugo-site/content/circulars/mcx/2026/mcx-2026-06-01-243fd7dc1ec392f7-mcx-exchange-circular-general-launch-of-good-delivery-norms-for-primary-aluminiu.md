## Summary

MCX has announced the launch of Good Delivery Norms for Primary Aluminium, Refined Copper, and Refined Zinc. This initiative extends the existing good delivery framework (previously available for Refined Lead) to these base metals, ensuring that deliverable brands meet rigorous international quality and process standards in alignment with the Atmanirbhar Bharat mission.

## Key Points

- Applies to MCX Aluminium, Aluminium Mini, Copper, Zinc, and Zinc Mini contracts.
- Permits delivery of metals from LME-approved or MCX-approved empanelled brands.
- Outlines applicant producer eligibility, technical audits, testing requirements, and application procedures across dedicated annexures for Aluminium, Copper, and Zinc.
- Aimed at promoting domestic manufacturing standards and quality assurance under Atmanirbhar Bharat.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of formalized Good Delivery Norms and empanelment processes for producers of Primary Aluminium, Refined Copper, and Refined Zinc participating in MCX derivatives contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Producers seeking brand empanelment must fulfill eligibility criteria, submit required documentation, undergo technical audits by Authorized Technical Auditors, and complete testing through Authorized Testers as specified in the respective annexures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: June 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances transparency, quality control, and standardization in base metal derivatives settlement on MCX, providing participants with reliable domestic and international brand options.
