## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) Sanctions List, forwarded via SEBI. Members are reminded to ensure compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) and combating the financing of terrorism (CFT) standards.

## Key Points

- SEBI communicated updates regarding the UNSC 1718 Sanctions Committee list concerning the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).
- Members are requested to regularly check the SEBI website under 'Media and Notifications' -> 'UNSC Sanctions Committee List' for updates.
- Mandatory periodic screening of all client databases must be conducted against applicable Sanctions Lists.
- Any identified matches must be immediately frozen and reported to relevant authorities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updates to the UNSC Sanctions List as communicated by SEBI and the United Nations Security Council.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must conduct rigorous and periodic screening of all client databases to ensure no accounts are linked to individuals or entities on applicable Sanctions Lists.
- Any matched accounts must be frozen immediately and reported as prescribed under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967 and SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 23, 2026
- Annexure A / UNSC Update Notice Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal for compliant members already performing regular AML/CFT database screening. Ensures adherence to national security mandates and regulatory standards.
