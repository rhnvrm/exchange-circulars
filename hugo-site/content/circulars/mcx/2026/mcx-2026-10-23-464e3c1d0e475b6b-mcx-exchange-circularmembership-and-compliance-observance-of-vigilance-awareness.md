## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has issued a circular informing members about the observance of Vigilance Awareness Week (VAW) 2026, scheduled from October 26, 2026, to November 01, 2026, pursuant to Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) Circular No. 04/08/2026. The theme for VAW 2026 is "सत्यनिष्ठा से समृद्धि" / "Probity for Prosperity".

## Key Points

- **Event Period:** October 26, 2026, to November 01, 2026.
- **Theme:** "सत्यनिष्ठा से समृद्धि" ("Probity for Prosperity").
- **Action Items:** Members are advised to promote VAW 2026 across websites and social media, take the CVC e-pledge, and notify stakeholders via bulk messaging.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory rule changes; adherence to CVC guidelines for awareness campaign.

## Compliance Requirements

- Publish information about VAW 2026 on member websites and social media accounts.
- Provide links to the CVC e-pledge website (https://pledge.cvc.nic.in/) and encourage participation.
- Send bulk SMS/WhatsApp messages to listed companies, intermediaries, investors, and demat account holders regarding the observance.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Vigilance Awareness Week:** October 26, 2026 – November 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible direct operational impact; serves as a corporate governance and ethical awareness initiative for exchange members and market participants.
