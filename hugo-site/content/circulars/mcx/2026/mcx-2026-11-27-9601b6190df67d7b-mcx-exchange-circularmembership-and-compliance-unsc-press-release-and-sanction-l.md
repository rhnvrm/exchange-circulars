## Summary

MCX has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) 1267(1999) ISIL (Da’esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions Committee's Sanctions List pursuant to SEBI communication and Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Notification of UNSC Notes SC/16515 and SC/16516 dated November 20, 2026, amending entries in the ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Members must perform rigorous, periodic screening of all client databases against applicable Sanctions Lists.
- Any identified matches must be immediately frozen and reported to relevant authorities as prescribed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updates to the UNSC 1267(1999) ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List in accordance with Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 and SEBI Master Circular AML standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to regularly monitor the SEBI website under the "UNSC Sanctions Committee List" tab for updates.
- Conduct ongoing and periodic screening of client databases to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to sanctioned entities/individuals.
- Immediately freeze identified matches and report them to relevant authorities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Annexure A Date: November 26, 2026
- UNSC Notes Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High operational compliance impact requiring members to maintain robust AML/CFT screening systems to avoid regulatory penalties under UAPA.
