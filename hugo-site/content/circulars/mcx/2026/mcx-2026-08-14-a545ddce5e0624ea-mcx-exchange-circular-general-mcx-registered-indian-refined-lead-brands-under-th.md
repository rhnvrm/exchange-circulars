## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced that fresh material from POCL Enterprises Limited under the brand name 'POEL' will not be accepted towards the settlement of Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts with immediate effect until further notice.

## Key Points

- Fresh material from POCL Enterprises Limited (Plant: Maraimalai Nagar, Kanchipuram District, Tamil Nadu) is barred from settlement effective August 14, 2026.
- Existing inventory of the POEL brand in MCX accredited warehouses remains valid until withdrawal, after which it ceases to qualify as 'Good Delivery'.
- Applies to Lead and Lead Mini futures contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of acceptance of fresh material for 'Good Delivery' status for POEL brand refined lead.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note and inform constituents regarding the non-acceptance of fresh POEL brand lead for settlements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on market participants utilizing or holding POEL brand refined lead for MCX futures delivery and settlement.
