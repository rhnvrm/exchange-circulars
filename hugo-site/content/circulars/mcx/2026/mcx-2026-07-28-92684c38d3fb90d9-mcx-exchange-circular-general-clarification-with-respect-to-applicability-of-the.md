## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has released a circular communicating SEBI's clarification on the applicability of the benefit of early pay-in in the Commodity Derivatives Segment. Clearing Corporations shall provide early pay-in facility permitting market participants to deposit certified goods against relevant derivatives contracts into accredited warehouses.

## Key Points

- Based on stakeholder representations and recommendations from the Working Group and CDAC, paragraph 11.3.1 of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives Segment has been revised.
- Clearing Corporations may exempt all types of margins (except mark-to-market margins) for positions where early pay-in of certified goods has been made, based on risk perception.
- The provisions come into effect from September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Paragraph 11.3.1 of Chapter 11 of the SEBI Master Circular dated August 4, 2023, stands revised to state that Clearing Corporations shall provide early pay-in facility by accepting certified goods in accredited warehouses, exempting non-MTM margins while continuing to collect mark-to-market margins.

## Compliance Requirements

Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations are advised to make necessary changes to their systems for implementation and disseminate the provisions to members and on their websites.

## Important Dates

- **MCX Circular Date:** July 28, 2026
- **SEBI Circular Date:** June 19, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants depositing certified goods as early pay-in will benefit from margin exemptions (excluding MTM margins), improving capital efficiency and settlement operations in the Commodity Derivatives Segment.
