## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a special Muhurat Trading session on the occasion of Diwali (Laxmi Pujan Day) on Sunday, November 08, 2026, for all commodity and index contracts.

## Key Points

- **Event:** Muhurat Trading Session on account of Diwali (Laxmi Pujan Day).
- **Date:** Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- **Applicable Contracts:** All commodities and indices.
- **Special Session:** 2:05 pm – 2:19 pm.
- **Trading Session:** 2:20 pm – 3:30 pm.
- **Client Code Modification Session:** 2:20 pm – 3:45 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in terms of Rules, Bye-Laws and Business Rules of the Exchange and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the timings and ensure necessary operational arrangements are in place.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Muhurat Trading Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized operational impact, facilitating customary festive trading.
