## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Ltd (MCX) has issued circular no. MCX/TECH/587/2026 dated October 16, 2026, announcing the enablement of additional market data streams at the Primary Data Center (PDC) and Disaster Recovery Site (DRS). Members are instructed to update their systems with the provided IP addresses and port details to ensure seamless connectivity during unannounced live trading from DRS.

## Key Points

- Additional market data stream enabled at PDC and DRS.
- Members must enable PDC and DRS IP addresses for seamless unannounced live trading failover from DRS without manual intervention.
- Detailed encryption gateway connectivity parameters (Host lookup, Gateways 1 through 13, TCP ports for MTS/MCS, CTCL, Algo, and Drop Copy) provided in Annexures.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not applicable - Operational and technical circular]

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to make necessary configuration changes at their end for connecting to the exchange trading interfaces and enabling the specified IP addresses and routes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Reference Circular: MCX/TECH/509/2026 dated September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures robust connectivity and automated disaster recovery routing for trading members, minimizing downtime and manual intervention during live trading sessions.
