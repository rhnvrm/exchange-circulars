## Summary

MCX and MCXCCL have issued a circular notifying the Due Date Rates (DDR) for Crude Oil and Crude Oil Mini future contracts that expired on October 20, 2025.

## Key Points

- Commodity: CRUDEOIL and CRUDEOILM
- Expiry Date: October 20, 2025
- Due Date Rate (DDR Price): 5049.00
- Base Quantity / Unit: 1 BARREL

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants are requested to take note of the fixed Due Date Rates for final settlement of contracts.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2025
- Expiry Date: October 20, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification ensuring accurate settlement prices for expired crude oil derivative contracts.
