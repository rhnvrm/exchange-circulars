## Summary

MCXCCL has announced the addition of Kotak Mahindra Bank to the empaneled list of banks authorized to issue Electronic Fixed Deposit Receipts (e-FDRs) for clearing members.

## Key Points

- Kotak Mahindra Bank is newly added for e-FDR issuance.
- The updated list of empaneled banks comprises Axis Bank Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Indian Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.
- Clearing members are requested to take note of the updated list for collateral submissions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the approved banking panel for e-FDRs accepted by MCXCCL.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure e-FDRs are obtained from empaneled banks as per the revised list.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clearing members with an additional banking option for placing collateral in the form of e-FDRs.
