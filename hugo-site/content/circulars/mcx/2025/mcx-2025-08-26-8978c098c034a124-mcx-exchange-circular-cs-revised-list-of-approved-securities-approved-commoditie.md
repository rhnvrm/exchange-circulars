## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a revised list of approved securities, approved commodities, and approved banks vide circular MCX/MCXCCL/427/2025 dated August 26, 2025. This circular supersedes MCXCCL Circular No. MCXCCL/C&S/150/2025 dated July 25, 2025, and becomes effective from Monday, September 01, 2025.

## Key Points

- Clearing members cannot submit collaterals (including FDs, BGs, equity, or debt securities) issued by themselves or their group/associate entities.
- Clearing Members who are also Banks must ensure G-Secs and T-Bills provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.
- Margin benefit on approved Government Securities, T-Bills, and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) will be withdrawn two days prior to maturity.
- Single bank exposure limits reduced due to lower average three-month exposure of MCXCCL can be applied on a last-in-first-out (LIFO) basis.
- All SGBs and T-Bills issued by RBI during the month will be accepted by MCXCCL.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated collateral eligibility criteria and haircut guidelines (VAR rate or 25% whichever is higher for equity shares).
- Revised annexures detailing approved securities, government securities, T-Bills, SGBs, approved commodities, and approved banks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members must verify that deposited securities adhere to maximum limits (e.g., 25% of total collateral per security for equity shares) and exclude self or group-issued instruments.
- Members must account for maturity timelines and margin withdrawal rules for G-Secs, T-Bills, and SGBs.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 26, 2025
- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Affects clearing members' collateral planning, margin calculations, and banking exposures when trading and clearing on MCXCCL.
