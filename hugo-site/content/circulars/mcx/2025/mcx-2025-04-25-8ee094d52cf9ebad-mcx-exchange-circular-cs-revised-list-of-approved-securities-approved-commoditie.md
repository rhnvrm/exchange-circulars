## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the commencement of trading in the Gold Mini Option 26 June 2024 contract with Gold Mini (100 grams) Futures as the underlying asset, effective from April 08, 2024.

## Key Points

- Contract: Gold Mini Option 26 June 2024
- Underlying Futures Contract: Gold Mini 05 July 2024
- Contract Start Date: April 08, 2024
- Option Contract Expiry Date: June 26, 2024
- All applicable margins and trading parameters are governed by circular no. MCX/TRD/874/2023 dated December 14, 2023, and latest circulars from MCXCCL.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational introduction of a derivative contract under existing Exchange rules and bye-laws.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to contract specifications, trading parameters, and latest margin requirements issued by Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL).

## Important Dates

- Contract Start Date: April 08, 2024
- Sensitivity Report provided on: June 20, 21, 24 & 25, 2024
- Option Devolvement Intimation: June 24, 2024 to June 26, 2024
- Option Devolvement Margin First Day: June 25, 2024
- Option Devolvement Margin Second Day: June 26, 2024
- Option Contract Expiry: June 26, 2024
- First day of Trading after Devolving into Futures: June 27, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading choices for market participants in bullion derivatives, offering refined hedging capabilities against gold price volatility.
