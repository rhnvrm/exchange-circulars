## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) and Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) have notified the Due Date Rates (DDR) / Final Settlement Price for expiring index futures contracts as of September 08, 2023.

## Key Points

- **Contract:** MCX iCOMDEX Energy Index Futures (September 2023 Expiry).
- **Date:** September 08, 2023.
- **DDR Price:** 6475.00.
- **Base Quantity / Unit:** 1 Index Point.
- **Settlement:** Cash settled at the computed Due Date Rate / Final Settlement Price.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular regarding settlement pricing upon contract expiry.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and market participants with open positions in the expiring index futures contracts are required to account for the notified Due Date Rate in their settlement calculations.

## Important Dates

- **Expiry / DDR Date:** September 08, 2023

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts participants holding open positions in the expiring September 2023 MCX iCOMDEX Energy Index Futures contracts by establishing the final cash settlement price.
