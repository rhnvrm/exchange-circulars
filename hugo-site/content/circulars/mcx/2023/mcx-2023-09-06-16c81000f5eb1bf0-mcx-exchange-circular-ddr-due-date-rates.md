## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a circular notifying the due date rates for futures contracts that expired on September 05, 2023.

## Key Points

- **GOLDM**: Due date rate fixed at 59193 for 10 grams (Date: 05-09-2023).
- **SILVER**: Due date rate fixed at 73384 for 1 kg (Date: 05-09-2023).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the fixed due date rates for settlement and operational purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Expiry Date**: September 05, 2023
- **Circular Date**: September 06, 2023

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for clearing and settlement of expired gold and silver futures contracts with no broad market impact.
