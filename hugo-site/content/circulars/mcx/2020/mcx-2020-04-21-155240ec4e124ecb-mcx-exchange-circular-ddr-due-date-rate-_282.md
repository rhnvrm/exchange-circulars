## Summary

MCX has announced the Due Date Rate for Crude Oil futures contracts that expired on April 20, 2020, fixed at ₹ (-) 2884 per barrel following the NYMEX WTI crude oil front month contract settlement price (-$37.63) converted into Indian Rupees.

## Key Points

- Commodity: Crude Oil futures contract expired on April 20, 2020.
- Due Date Rate: ₹ (-) 2884 per barrel (negative pricing).
- Conversion basis: NYMEX WTI crude oil front month contract settlement price (-$37.63) converted at RBI USDINR reference rate (76.6335) and rounded off to the nearest tick.
- Final settlement difference accounted for in the obligation for trade date April 21, 2020 and settled on April 22, 2020.

## Regulatory Changes

- Settlement of Crude Oil futures contracts based on NYMEX WTI settlement price converted to INR as per standard contract specifications during extreme market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to take note of the negative due date rate and ensure final settlement adjustments are accounted for in the obligation for trade date April 21, 2020, and settled on April 22, 2020.

## Important Dates

- Expiry Date: April 20, 2020
- Trade Date for Obligation Adjustment: April 21, 2020
- Settlement Date: April 22, 2020

## Impact Assessment

- Unprecedented negative pricing in crude oil contracts significantly impacts market participants, clearing members, and traders with open positions or final settlement obligations.
