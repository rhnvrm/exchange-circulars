## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued due date rates for eleven futures contracts that expired on January 31, 2020.

## Key Points

- Due date rates fixed for 11 expired futures contracts on January 31, 2020.
- Covered commodities include Alumini, Copper, Cotton, CPO, Goldguinea, Goldpetal, Leadmini, Menthaoil, Nickel, Rbdpmolein, and Zincmini.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine settlement rate notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the due date rates for clearing and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Expiry and DDR Fixation Date: January 31, 2020

## Impact Assessment

Routine market settlement impact for members holding positions in the expired futures contracts.
