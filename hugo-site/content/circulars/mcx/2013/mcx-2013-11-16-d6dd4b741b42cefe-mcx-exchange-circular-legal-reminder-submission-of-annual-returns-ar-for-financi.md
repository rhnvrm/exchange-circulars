## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) issued a reminder to its members regarding the submission of Annual Returns (AR) for the Financial Year 2012-13.

## Key Points

- Members must submit Annual Returns (AR) in soft form through "MCX eXchange" and in hard form to the Exchange.
- Inactive members are required to submit documents as specified under clause II of circular no. MCX/MEM/363/2013.
- Hard form submissions can be done following the guidelines on the MCX website under Membership / Compliance / Annual Submissions / Annual Returns.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a procedural reminder of existing compliance obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of Annual Returns (AR) for FY 2012-13 latest by November 30, 2013.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 16, 2013
- **Submission Deadline:** November 30, 2013

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures adherence to regulatory timelines and maintenance of accurate membership compliance records.
