## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a circular regarding the fixation of the due date rate for the Natural Gas futures contract that expired on November 25, 2019. The due date rate has been officially fixed at ₹ 181.30 per MMBTU.

## Key Points

- **Commodity:** Natural Gas futures contract
- **Contract Expiry Date:** November 25, 2019
- **Due Date Rate:** ₹ 181.30 per MMBTU
- **Circular Reference:** MCX/MCXCCL/668/2019 and MCXCCL/DDR/276/2019

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard periodic pricing and settlement notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and market participants are required to take note of the fixed due date rate for final settlement and reconciliation of open positions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2019
- **Contract Expiry Date:** November 25, 2019

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate financial settlement and clearing operations for market participants holding positions in the expired Natural Gas futures contract.
