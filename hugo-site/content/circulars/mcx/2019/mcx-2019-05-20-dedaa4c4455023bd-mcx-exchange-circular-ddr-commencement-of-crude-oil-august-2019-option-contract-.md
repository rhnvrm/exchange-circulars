## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the commencement of trading in the Crude Oil Option 14 August 2019 contract, with Crude Oil 19 August 2019 futures as the underlying asset, effective from May 21, 2019.

## Key Points

- Contract Name: Crude Oil Option 14 August 2019
- Underlying Futures Contract: Crude Oil 19 August 2019
- Contract Start Date: May 21, 2019
- Option Contract Expiry Date: August 14, 2019
- Sensitivity Report: Provided on August 8, 9, 12 & 13, 2019 at End of Day
- Option Devolvement Intimation: August 12, 2019 to August 14, 2019
- Option Devolvement Margin First Day: August 13, 2019 (from Begin of Day)
- Option Devolvement Margin Second Day: August 14, 2019 (from Begin of Day)
- First day of Trading after Option Positions Devolving into Futures Positions: August 16, 2019

## Regulatory Changes

Trading parameters, devolvement, and settlement procedures are governed in accordance with circular no. MCX/TRD/210/2019 dated April 18, 2019.

## Compliance Requirements

All members and constituents trading through them must adhere to the contract specifications, trading parameters, and settlement procedures specified by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- May 21, 2019: Contract Start Date
- August 8, 9, 12 & 13, 2019: Sensitivity Report at End of Day
- August 12 - 14, 2019: Option Devolvement Intimation
- August 13, 2019: Option Devolvement Margin First Day
- August 14, 2019: Option Devolvement Margin Second Day & Expiry Date
- August 16, 2019: First day of Trading after Option Positions Devolving into Futures Positions

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants with additional hedging and trading instruments in the energy commodity segment.
