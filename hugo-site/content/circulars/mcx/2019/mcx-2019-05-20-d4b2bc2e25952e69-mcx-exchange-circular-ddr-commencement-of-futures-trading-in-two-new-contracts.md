## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced the commencement of futures trading in two new Crude Oil contracts (November 2019 and Crude Oil Mini November 2019) starting May 21, 2019. Additionally, LTP-based Spread Trading Facility combinations have been enabled for these contracts.

## Key Points

- Commencement of futures trading in Crude Oil (November 2019) and Crude Oil Mini (November 2019) contracts.
- Contract specifications and trading parameters follow previously issued circulars (MCX/TRD/086/2019 and MCX/TRD/087/2019 dated February 19, 2019).
- LTP-based Spread Trading Facility available in specified near-month and far-month combinations effective May 21, 2019.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine contract launch in accordance with Exchange Rules, Bye-Laws, and Business Rules.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take note of the new contract availability and spread trading combinations, ensuring constituents trading through them are duly informed.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** May 20, 2019
- **Effective Date:** May 21, 2019
- **Spread Contract End Dates:** June 19, 2019 and July 19, 2019

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading opportunities and spread trading facilities for market participants dealing in crude oil commodity derivatives.
