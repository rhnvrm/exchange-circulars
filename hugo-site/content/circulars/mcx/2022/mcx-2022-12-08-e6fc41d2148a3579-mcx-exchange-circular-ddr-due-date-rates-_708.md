## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has issued a circular regarding the Due Date Rates for expiring index futures contracts in accordance with the relevant contract specifications and rules.

## Key Points

- Announces the Due Date Rate / Final Settlement Price for expiring index futures contracts.
- Commodity/Index: MCXENRGDEX Nov 2022 (MCX iCOMDEX Energy Index Futures contracts).
- Due Date Rate (DDR Price): 7,385.00.
- Expiry Date: December 08, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard contract expiration and cash settlement procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and market participants are required to take note of the notified Due Date Rate for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: December 08, 2022
- Expiry / DDR Date: December 08, 2022

## Impact Assessment

Operational settlement update for participants holding positions in the expiring MCX iCOMDEX Energy Index Futures contract.
