## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) has announced the due date rates for futures contracts that expired on March 04, 2022.

## Key Points

- Due date rate for Gold Mini (GOLDM) fixed at 51406 per 10 grams (Expiry date: 04-03-2022).
- Due date rate for Silver fixed at 67394 per 1 kg (Expiry date: 04-03-2022).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine due rate fixation in terms of Rules, Bye-Laws, and Regulations of MCXCCL.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the due date rates for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Expiry Date: March 04, 2022
- Circular Date: March 07, 2022

## Impact Assessment

Operational finalization of expired futures contracts settlement without broader market impact.
