## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) and Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited (MCXCCL) have announced the Due Date Rates (DDR) / Final Settlement Price for expiring index futures contracts (MCXENRGDEX) for July 08, 2022.

## Key Points

- Expiry Date: July 08, 2022
- Commodity/Index: MCXENRGDEX July 2022
- DDR / Final Settlement Price: 9,094.00
- Base Quantity: 1
- Base Unit: Index Point

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and market participants are required to take note of the Due Date Rates for the cash settlement of expiring index futures contracts.

## Important Dates

- Expiry / DDR Date: July 08, 2022

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparent and accurate cash settlement of expiring MCXENRGDEX index futures contracts.
