## Summary

Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited (MCX) has announced a revised format for contract notes, specifically updating the provisions to be printed on the reverse side concerning arbitration. This revision follows previous circulars and amended Bye-law No. 15.11 which came into effect on April 2, 2011.

## Key Points

- Revised provisions to be printed on the reverse of the Contract Note as per Annexure XIV to the Business Rules.
- Incorporates extracts from the Byelaws & Business Rules pertaining to arbitration (e.g., Reference to Arbitration, Trades, Contracts, Deliveries and Transactions Subject to Arbitration).
- Effective date for implementation is January 1, 2012, giving members time to print new stationery and update software if necessary.
- Limitation period is applicable from the date of publication in the Gazette of India on April 2, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates standard legal disclosures and arbitration terms required on the reverse of client contract notes pursuant to amended MCX Bye-law No. 15.11.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that all contract notes issued on or after January 1, 2012, contain the revised text on the reverse.
- Members are permitted to print new stationery and modify software accordingly before the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 3, 2011
- Effective Date: January 1, 2012
- Underlying Gazette Publication Date: April 2, 2011

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is moderate, requiring members to update their printing templates and stationery for contract notes to ensure compliance with updated exchange bye-laws.
