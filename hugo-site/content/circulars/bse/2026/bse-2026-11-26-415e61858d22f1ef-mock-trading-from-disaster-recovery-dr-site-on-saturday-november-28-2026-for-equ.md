## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test their trading applications, algorithms, and software versions under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading is conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- Covers login, pre-open, continuous trading, closing auction session (CAS), trade modification, and trade annulment timings.
- New version of BOLT Pro TWS 13.90 is released for testing changes related to the closing auction session and market timings.
- Closing Reference Price for stock and index futures will be calculated based on VWAP of trades executed between 11:45 am and 12:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation and testing of changes regarding the Review of certain aspects of the Closing Auction Session, market timings, and settlement methodology for derivatives contracts.
- Release of new version of Market data API and updated Operating Guidelines of Closing Auction Session in Equity Derivatives Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members may participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020, and Notice No. 20201201-22.
- Members must ensure that the existing version of BOLT Pro TWS 13.80 is used for live trading starting Monday, November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026
- **BOLT Pro TWS 13.90 Download:** November 28, 2026 (via DLOAD / lease line connectivity)
- **Live Trading with BOLT Pro TWS 13.80:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness drill for trading members and vendors to test disaster recovery failover, API connectivity, and updated closing auction session mechanics without impacting live market operations.
