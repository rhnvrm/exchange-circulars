## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about revisions to the minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) investment amount and minimum number of installments for specific schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund, effective November 02, 2026, as requested by HDFC AMC.

## Key Points

- **Affected Schemes:** HDFC Value Fund and HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund.
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Frequency:** Daily frequency under the SIP facility.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Existing Criteria (Daily Frequency):** Minimum installment number: 6; Minimum amount: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Rs. 1/-.
- **Revised Criteria (Daily Frequency):** Minimum installment number: 30; Minimum amount: Rs. 20 and in multiples of Rs. 1/-.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the revised SIP criteria when executing transactions in the aforesaid HDFC Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for mutual fund distributors and investors transacting in daily SIPs for HDFC Value Fund and HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund, requiring adjustments to minimum investment thresholds and installment counts.
