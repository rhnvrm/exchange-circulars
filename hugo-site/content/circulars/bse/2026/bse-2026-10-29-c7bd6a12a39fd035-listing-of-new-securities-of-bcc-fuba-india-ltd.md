## Summary

BSE has notified the listing and trading permission for 1,52,357 equity shares of BCC Fuba India Ltd (Scrip Code: 517246) with effect from October 30, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company: BCC Fuba India Ltd
- Scrip Code: 517246
- ISIN: INE788D01016
- Quantity: 1,52,357 equity shares (comprising 1,39,896 shares with distinct nos. 19742271 to 19882166 and 12,461 shares with distinct nos. 19882278 to 19894738)
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the listing of new securities and ensure proper settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action reflecting the conversion of partly paid-up shares into fully paid-up equity shares, expanding the fully paid equity capital base.
