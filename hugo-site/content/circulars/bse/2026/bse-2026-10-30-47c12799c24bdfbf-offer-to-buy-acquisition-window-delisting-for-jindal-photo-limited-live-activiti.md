## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window for Delisting) of shares of Jindal Photo Limited (Security Symbol: JINDALPHOT, Scrip Code: 532624, ISIN: INE796G01012). The bidding period is scheduled from Monday, 2nd November 2026 to Friday, 6th November 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: JINDAL PHOTO LIMITED
- Security Symbol: JINDALPHOT
- ISIN: INE796G01012
- Scrip Code: 532624
- Offer Size: Upto 26,46,183 Equity Shares
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00
- Offer Price: Rs. 1119.50
- Opening Date: Monday, 2nd November 2026
- Closing Date: Friday, 6th November 2026
- Bid Modification/Cancellation available until Thursday, 5th November 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None specific to regulations; operational schedule and accessibility details for the OTB platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members to ensure UCC of the Investor is registered and/or updated with correct PAN Number and Category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for Investors having shares in demat form.
- Members and Custodians to use iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms as per specified URLs.

## Important Dates

- Issue Period / Bid Offering Period: 2nd November 2026 to 6th November 2026
- Upper Modification and Cancellation: 2nd November 2026 to 5th November 2026
- Give-up / Take-up (last day of offer): 6th November 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders and trading members to participate in the delisting offer to buy for Jindal Photo Limited with clearly defined timelines and operational parameters.
