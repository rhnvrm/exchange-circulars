## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 19,99,996 new equity shares of EFC (I) Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 512008
- ISIN: INE886D01026
- Number of Shares: 19,99,996 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 270/- per share (including premium of Rs. 268/-)
- Date of Allotment: 01/10/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 147946163 to 149946158
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 29/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing of securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants note the trading commencement date and lock-in period for the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 1, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 28, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized impact limited to the equity capital expansion and liquidity of EFC (I) Limited.
