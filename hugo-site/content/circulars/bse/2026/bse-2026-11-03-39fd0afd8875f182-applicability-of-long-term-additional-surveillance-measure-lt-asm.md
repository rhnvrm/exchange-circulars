## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the notice, specific surveillance measures such as 100% margins and lower price bands apply starting November 04 and November 06, 2026, based on periodic reviews and XBRL submissions by companies.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective November 06, 2026, on all open positions as of November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Lower price band applicable effective November 04, 2026, for securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective November 06, 2026.
- Review includes securities moving out of the framework and consolidated lists provided in respective annexures.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework on eligible securities, involving margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for applicable securities and monitor lower price bands and group shifts effective November 04 and November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2026**: Lower price bands effective; movement of securities to higher stages or out of LT-ASM framework.
- **November 05, 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions regarding margin applicability.
- **November 06, 2026**: 100% margins applicable on open and new positions; transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will experience stricter risk management measures, including full margin requirements and price band restrictions, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
