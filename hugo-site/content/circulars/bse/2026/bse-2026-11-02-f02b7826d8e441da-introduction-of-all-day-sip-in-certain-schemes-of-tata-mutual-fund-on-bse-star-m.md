## Summary

BSE has announced the rollout of the All Day SIP facility for select schemes of Tata Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective November 03, 2026. Members and investors can utilize this facility based on the scheme-wise details provided in the attached addendum and annexure.

## Key Points

- Introduction of All Day SIP facility for select schemes of Tata Mutual Fund.
- Enabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Effective date of implementation is November 03, 2026.
- Notice-cum-Addendum and Annexure issued by Tata Mutual Fund are enclosed for member reference.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding platform features for mutual fund investments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the facility and ensure that transactions are executed in accordance with the scheme-wise details specified in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Nov 2026
- Effective Date: 03 Nov 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with enhanced flexibility for systematic investment planning across eligible Tata Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.
