## Summary

BSE has sought clarifications from companies regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to verify accuracy for investors. A reply has been received from Tanla Platforms Ltd, while a reply is awaited from Spicejet Ltd.

## Key Points

- Clarification sought from Tanla Platforms Ltd (Scrip Code: 532790) regarding a news article on LiveMint; reply received and attached.
- Clarification sought from Spicejet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) regarding a news article on Moneycontrol; reply is awaited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to provide timely clarifications on news articles as requested by the exchange surveillance department.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine surveillance update with minimal direct market impact pending specific disclosures from the companies.
