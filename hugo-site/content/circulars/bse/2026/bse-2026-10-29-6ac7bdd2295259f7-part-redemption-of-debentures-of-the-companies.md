## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption on their non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective dates.

## Key Points

- **Power Grid Corporation of India Limited**: Scrip Codes 974593, 974698, and 974737 have record dates and ex-dates on 02/11/2026, with reduced face values of Rs. 62,500, Rs. 65,000, and Rs. 65,000 per debenture respectively.
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited**: Scrip Code 977383 has a record date and ex-date on 02/11/2026, with a reduced face value of Rs. 1,538.47 per debenture.
- **Regency Fincorp Limited**: Scrip Code 978068 has a record date and ex-date on 04/11/2026, with a reduced face value of Rs. 8,000 per debenture.
- **Vivriti Capital Limited**: Scrip Code 978069 has a record date and ex-date on 04/11/2026, with a reduced face value of Rs. 95,716 per debenture.
- **Annapurna Finance Private Limited**: Scrip Code 976927 has an ex-date on 06/11/2026 and record date on 07/11/2026, with a reduced face value of Rs. 33,333.28 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in debentures of the specified companies will proceed with reduced face values as per the corporate action schedules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised face values and record/ex-dates for debt segment trading.

## Important Dates

- **02/11/2026**: Record date and ex-date for Power Grid Corporation of India and Akara Capital Advisors.
- **04/11/2026**: Record date and ex-date for Regency Fincorp and Vivriti Capital.
- **06/11/2026**: Ex-date for Annapurna Finance.
- **07/11/2026**: Record date for Annapurna Finance.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding these NCDs will receive part redemption payouts and interest as applicable, with trading subsequently conducted on the reduced face value.
