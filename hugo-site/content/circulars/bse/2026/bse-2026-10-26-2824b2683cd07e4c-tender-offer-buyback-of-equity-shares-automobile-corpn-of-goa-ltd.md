## Summary

Automobile Corporation of Goa Ltd. has announced October 28, 2026, as the record date and ex-entitlement date for determining shareholders eligible to participate in the equity share buyback tender offer (DR- 744/2026-2027).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Automobile Corporation of Goa Ltd.
- **Scrip Code / Reference:** 505036 (DR- 744/2026-2027)
- **Record Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Purpose:** Determination of entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback offer.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate action notification under BSE listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for rolling settlement segment transactions in dematerialised securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Record Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares as of the record date are eligible to participate in the buyback tender offer.
- Trading members should ensure proper settlement and ex-entitlement adjustments as per exchange guidelines.
