## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and commencement of trading for the equity shares of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited effective from Friday, November 27, 2026. A total of 6,61,83,63,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each, issued at Rs. 136/- per share, will be admitted to dealing under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Mahanadi Coalfields Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545032
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** MAHACOAL
- **ISIN:** INE03K401026
- **Total Securities:** 6,61,83,63,000 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 2/- each)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 136/- per share (Face value Rs. 2/- + premium of Rs. 134/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** November 25, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 27, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1 share
- **Group:** 'B'

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; standard listing notification complying with SEBI guidelines for dematerialised trading and IPO Special Pre-open Session (SPOS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed by the company to reflect lock-in periods in depository records for applicable shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 25, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** November 27, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Commencement Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited provides liquidity to investors of the public issue and expands trading options on the BSE equity segment. The scrip will participate in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on its listing date.
