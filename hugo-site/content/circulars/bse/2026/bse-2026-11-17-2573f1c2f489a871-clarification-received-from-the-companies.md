## Summary

The BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have timely and relevant information. Replies have been received from eight companies, while responses from nineteen companies are still awaited.

## Key Points

- Replies received from 8 companies: Billwin Industries Ltd, Borosil Ltd, Flexituff Ventures International Ltd, Indraprastha Medical Corporation Ltd, Jindal Worldwide Ltd, North Eastern Carrying Corporation Ltd, RPSG Ventures Ltd, and STEL Holdings Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 19 companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Covance Softsol Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Geetanjali Credit and Capital Ltd, Gujarat Raffia Industries Ltd, HBL Engineering Ltd, Krupalu Metals Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Sangam Finserv Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Symbiox Investment & Trading Company Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Vedant Asset Ltd.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE on November 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; part of standard ongoing surveillance operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities identified with pending replies must submit their clarifications to the exchange in a timely manner. Investors should exercise caution and refer to company disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases market transparency and investor awareness regarding unusual trading activity in specific scrips. May cause short-term volatility in stocks with pending clarifications.
