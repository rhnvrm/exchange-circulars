## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of three companies—Eris Lifesciences Limited, Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited, and Moneyboxx Finance Ltd—following record dates fixed for interest payment and redemption.

## Key Points

- Eris Lifesciences Limited (Scrip Code: 975731, ISIN: INE406M08011): Record date 22/11/2026, Ex-date 20/11/2026.
- Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited (Scrip Code: 976510, ISIN: INE774L07099): Record date 22/11/2026, Ex-date 20/11/2026.
- Moneyboxx Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 976440, ISIN: INE296Q07084): Record date 22/11/2026, Ex-date 20/11/2026.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures during the suspension period.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural notice regarding trading suspension for corporate debt actions (interest payment/redemption).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the specified debentures of the listed companies around the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19/11/2026
- Ex-Date: 20/11/2026
- Record Date: 22/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specific NCDs of the mentioned companies to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
