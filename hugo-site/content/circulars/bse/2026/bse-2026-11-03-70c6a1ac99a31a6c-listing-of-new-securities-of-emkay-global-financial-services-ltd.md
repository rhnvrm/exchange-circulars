## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 532737) with effect from November 4, 2026. A total of 1,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 229.50/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company: Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 532737, ISIN: INE296H01011).
- Securities Issued: 1,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
- Issue Price: Rs. 239.50/- per share (including premium of Rs. 229.50/-).
- Allottee: Promoter on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Effective Date of Trading: November 4, 2026.
- Allotment Date: July 29, 2026.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: 1,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 27525581 to 27625580) locked in up to May 4, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of securities issued via preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must adhere to lock-in requirements and ensure proper credit of shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: July 29, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 4, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 4, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in a minor expansion of equity capital through preferential allotment and warrant conversion.
