## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in the non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Lodha Developers Limited with effect from November 19, 2026, due to the record date fixed for the payment of interest and early redemption on the exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Lodha Developers Limited
- **Security:** Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) - LDL-8.14%-5-6-28-PVT DR- 760/2026-2027
- **Scrip Code:** 976764
- **ISIN:** INE670K07281
- **Record Date:** 19/11/2026
- **Purpose:** Payment of Interest and Early Redemption on Exercise of Call Option
- **No Dealings From:** 19/11/2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members of the Exchange are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company as mentioned from November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / No Dealings From:** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debentures is suspended to facilitate the payment of interest and early redemption pursuant to the exercise of the call option.
