## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the System Audit Report for the period ended September 30, 2026. Members must submit the report electronically through the BEFS Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reference to Exchange Notice No. 20261008-50 dated October 8, 2026.
- Due date for submission is November 30, 2026.
- Submission must be made electronically via the BEFS Portal.
- Non-compliance will attract penalties and disciplinary action as prescribed in Exchange circular no. 20260417-45.
- User manuals for members and auditors are enclosed as Annexure A and Annexure B.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a compliance reminder reinforcing existing audit submission mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit their System Audit Report for the period ended September 30, 2026, via the BEFS Portal on or before November 30, 2026, to avoid penalties and disciplinary actions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Audit Period Ended: September 30, 2026
- Submission Due Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Timely submission ensures regulatory compliance and adherence to exchange standards. Failure to meet the deadline will lead to penalties and potential disciplinary action.
