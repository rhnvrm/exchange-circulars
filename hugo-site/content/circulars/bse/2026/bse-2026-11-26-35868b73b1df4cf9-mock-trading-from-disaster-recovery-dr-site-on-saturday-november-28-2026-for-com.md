## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Trading members can use this opportunity to test their trading applications, ETI APIs, and third-party vendor platforms under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Log-in timing: 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM; Continuous Trading: 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
- Members can participate in the mock session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements pursuant to SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to submit feedback by 1:00 PM on the day of the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; conducted in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020, and Exchange Notice No. 20201201-22.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems through ETI APIs may participate to fulfill software and risk management testing requirements. Feedback must be submitted via email or contact numbers provided by 1:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026 (Saturday)
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Feedback Deadline:** November 28, 2026 by 01:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill with no financial or margin implications. Helps members verify system robustness and Disaster Recovery switchover readiness.
