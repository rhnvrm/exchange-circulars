## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members regarding the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of MIHIKA INDUSTRIES LIMITED, effective from Thursday, November 19, 2026. The RE trading period runs from November 19, 2026, to December 01, 2026, while the underlying Rights Issue opens on November 19, 2026, and closes on December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** MIHIKA INDUSTRIES LIMITED
- **Security Type:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Scrip Code:** 751160
- **Scrip ID:** MIHIKA-RE
- **ISIN No.:** INE779Q20017
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **RE Trading Period:** November 19, 2026 to December 01, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 19, 2026 to December 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action trading notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that REs must not be mistaken for ordinary shares.
- A prominent disclaimer must be included in contract notes and bills for RE purchases, explaining that purchase grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue by applying with requisite application money or renouncing before closure.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.
- Trades shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 19, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** December 01, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly renunciation and trading of Rights Entitlements for shareholders of Mihika Industries Limited under T+1 settlement in demat form.
