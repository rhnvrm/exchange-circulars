## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the acquisition window for the share buyback of Genus Power Infrastructures Limited. The buyback involves up to 47,50,000 fully paid-up equity shares at a price of ₹312 per share, aggregating to up to ₹148.20 Crores, through a tender offer on a proportionate basis.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Genus Power Infrastructures Limited
- **Buyback Size:** Up to 47,50,000 equity shares (representing ~1.56% of total issued, subscribed, and paid-up equity shares) or up to ₹148.20 Crores.
- **Buyback Price:** ₹312/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Record Date:** Friday, October 30, 2026.
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, November 4, 2026, to Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (excluding SEBI holiday on Tuesday, November 10, 2026).
- **Method:** Acquisition window through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under SEBI takeover and buyback regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016, along with relevant Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate and facilitate bids in accordance with the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange pursuant to tender offers.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** November 4, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (No Trading):** November 10, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Eligible shareholders holding shares as of the record date can tender their shares in the buyback window. The ₹148.20 crore buyback provides capital return and liquidity to eligible shareholders.
