## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the monthly reconstitution results for various BSE Indices. The changes will be effective at the open of trading on Monday, November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monthly reconstitution results announced for BSE Indices.
- Effective date: Monday, November 23, 2026.
- Includes constituent updates and actions for companies such as Lenskart Solutions, Emmvee Photovoltaic Power, Physicswallah, Pine Labs, and others.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Regular monthly index maintenance and reconstitution performed by BSE Index Services.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and algorithmic systems should update index constituents and tracking parameters in accordance with the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects portfolio tracking, index funds, and benchmarks referencing BSE indices containing the affected stocks.
