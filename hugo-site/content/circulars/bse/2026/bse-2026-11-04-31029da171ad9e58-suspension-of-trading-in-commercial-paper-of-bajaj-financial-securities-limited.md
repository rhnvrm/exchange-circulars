## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in the commercial paper of Bajaj Financial Securities Limited following the fixation of the record date for redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bajaj Financial Securities Limited
- Scrip Code: 732868
- Scrip ID: BFSL-6-11-26- 750/2026-2027
- ISIN: INE01C314IP6
- Record Date: 05/11/2026
- Ex-Date: 05/11/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action for commercial paper redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial paper of the company as specified.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 05/11/2026
- Ex-Date: 05/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

No broader market impact; standard operational procedure for commercial paper redemption.
