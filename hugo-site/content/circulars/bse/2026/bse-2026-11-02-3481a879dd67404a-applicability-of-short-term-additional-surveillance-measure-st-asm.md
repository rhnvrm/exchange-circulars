## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective November 4, 2026, on open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, effective November 4, 2026, on open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Inclusion under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to the original applicable bands, subject to no other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for specified securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements for open positions as of November 3, 2026, and new positions created from November 4, 2026, onwards for the securities listed in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience higher margin blocking, which may impact liquidity and trading volume in those specific scrips.
