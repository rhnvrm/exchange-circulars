## Summary

INVESCO Mutual Fund is offering the NFO for INVESCO INDIA BSE TOP 10 BANKS ETF and its Direct Plan on the BSE StAR MF Platform from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: INVESCO INDIA BSE TOP 10 BANKS ETF and INVESCO INDIA BSE TOP 10 BANKS ETF - DIRECT PLAN.
- NFO Period: November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 30, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 04, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 04, 2026.
- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure clear funds are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 04, 2026.
- Client funds for transactions must be initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day.
- Switch-out funds must be received prior to the allotment date to be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: November 16, 2026
- NFO End Date: November 30, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investment participation in the Invesco India BSE Top 10 Banks ETF via the BSE StAR MF platform.
