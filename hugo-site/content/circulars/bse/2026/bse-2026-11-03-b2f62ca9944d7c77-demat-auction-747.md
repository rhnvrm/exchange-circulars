## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-747/2026-27. The auction offer entry, matching, delivery statement, and money statement schedules have been specified for November 3, 2026, followed by pay-in on November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:30 p.m. to 03:15 p.m. on November 3, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 03:15 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:30 p.m. to 04:00 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 04:05 p.m. to 06:00 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in settlements where they short delivered is now removed post implementation of Uniform Membership structure in Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notice under existing settlement frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Members are requested to refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and monitor corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 03, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: November 04, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Operational process governing short-delivery auctions for trading members with no broader market-wide structural impact.
