## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. (BSECL) has announced timelines and operational guidelines for the Commodity Derivatives segment in connection with cash and derivative operations for the Muhurat Trading session for Diwali Laxmi Pujan, pursuant to BSE Circular No. 20261019-12.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Obligations:** Due to trading activities conducted on November 08, 2026, the pay-in/pay-out obligations pertaining to trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled on November 09, 2026, at 08:30 hrs.
- **Client Margin Reporting:** The schedule for client margin reporting is governed by BSECL Circular No. 20261030-14.
- **CP Code Modification:** Custodian Participant (CP) code modification and give-up confirmations for trades executed on November 08, 2026, are scheduled between 14:20 hrs. and 15:40 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary adjustment of settlement cycles and operational timelines specifically tailored for the Muhurat Trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure adherence to the revised pay-in/pay-out schedules, margin reporting requirements, and complete CP code modifications or give-up confirmations within the designated time window.

## Important Dates

- **November 06, 2026 & November 08, 2026:** Trade dates subject to revised settlement.
- **November 08, 2026:** Date of Muhurat Trading session and CP code modification window (14:20 hrs. - 15:40 hrs.).
- **November 09, 2026 (08:30 hrs.):** Settlement of pay-in/pay-out obligations for trade dates November 06 and November 08, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clear operational continuity and settlement clarity for market participants engaging in commodity derivatives during the Diwali festive trading window.
