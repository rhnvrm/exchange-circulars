## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the empanelment of Equitypulse Tech Private Limited as an approved Algo Provider for supplying Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **Empanelled Vendor:** Equitypulse Tech Private Limited
- **Registered Address:** Plot No V-4, RK Complex, 1st Floor, KSSIDC Industrial Estate, Electronic City Phase1 Hosur Road, Doddathogur, Begur Hobli, Electronic City, Karnataka, Bangalore 560100
- **Contact Number:** +91 8904007737
- **Applicability:** Effective immediately with notice date 17 November 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational empanelment notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members intending to utilize algorithmic trading solutions from the empanelled vendor must ensure adherence to exchange guidelines and appropriate due diligence.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 17 November 2026
- **Effective Date:** Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with an additional empanelled vendor option for procuring approved algorithmic trading solutions.
