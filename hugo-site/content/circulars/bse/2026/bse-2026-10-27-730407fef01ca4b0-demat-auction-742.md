## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 742 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-742/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offers will be accepted on trading terminals on October 27, 2026.
- Session Timings:
  - Auction offer entry: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
  - Auction Matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
  - Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
  - Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for Wednesday, October 28, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while entering securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 27, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 28, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for settling short deliveries in rolling settlement.
