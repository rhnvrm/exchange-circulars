## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for **Mirae Asset Contra Fund** along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Mirae Asset Contra Fund (Available in Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Period:** November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 18, 2026.
- **Scheme ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: `INF769K01SB0`
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF769K01SD6`
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF769K01SC8`
  - Direct Plan - Growth: `INF769K01SE4`
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF769K01SG9`
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF769K01SF1`

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 17, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (December 11, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** November 27, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** December 11, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** December 17, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to participate in the Mirae Asset Contra Fund NFO seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF platform with structured operational timelines.
