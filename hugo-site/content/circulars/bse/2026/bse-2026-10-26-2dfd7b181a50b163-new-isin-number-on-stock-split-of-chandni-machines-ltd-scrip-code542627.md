## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Chandni Machines Ltd following a stock split from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Chandni Machines Ltd
- Scrip Code: 542627
- New ISIN No.: INE01GZ01029
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- Ex-Date: 29-10-2026 (DR-745/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISIN number effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 Oct 2026
- Ex-Date: 29-10-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members to reflect the new ISIN for trades in Chandni Machines Ltd from the ex-date.
