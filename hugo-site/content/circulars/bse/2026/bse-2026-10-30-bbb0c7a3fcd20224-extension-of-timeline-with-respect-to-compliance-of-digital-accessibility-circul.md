## Summary

SEBI has granted an extension of the timeline until January 31, 2027, regarding compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars, covering the appointment of IAAP Auditors, conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms, and remediation of audit findings.

## Key Points

- Extension granted till January 31, 2027.
- Applies to appointment of IAAP Auditor, conduct of Accessibility Audit for digital platforms, and Remediation of audit findings.
- References SEBI Circular No. HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026.
- Builds upon previous SEBI circulars and exchange notices pertaining to the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of deadlines for digital accessibility compliance for all regulated entities under the SEBI framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete the appointment of IAAP Auditors, conduct accessibility audits, and remediate findings by January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026.
- Compliance Deadline: January 31, 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities additional time to ensure full compliance with digital accessibility standards and remediation of audit findings.
