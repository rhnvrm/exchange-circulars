## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework with effect from November 26, 2026, pursuant to the provisions of the GSM Framework.

## Key Points

- Securities moved to higher GSM stages attract stringent surveillance actions and trading restrictions.
- Effective date for the transition is November 26, 2026.
- Settlement for the affected securities shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.
- Market participants and trading members are advised to exercise adequate caution.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I**: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- **Stage II**: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III**: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value deposited by buyers.
- **Stage IV**: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value deposited by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must enforce mandatory margin requirements and collect Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as prescribed.
- Ensure strict trade-to-trade settlement processes with zero netting off.
- Refer to the official BSE FAQ or contact bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: November 25, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Substantial liquidity impact and increased capital requirements for trading in affected securities due to trade-to-trade settlement restrictions and heavy surveillance deposits.
