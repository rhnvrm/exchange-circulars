## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective October 27, 2026. These measures include trading restrictions, increased margin requirements, and trade-to-trade settlement without netting off.

## Key Points

- Effective date: October 27, 2026.
- Measures applied based on GSM stages (Stage I to IV), ranging from 100% margin rates, 5% price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, to Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) up to 100%.
- Settlement on trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of GSM framework provisions published on February 27, 2017, and subsequent notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, as settlement occurs on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off.
- Buyers must deposit Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) as applicable for stages II, III, and IV.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on liquidity and trading activity for specific securities moved into higher GSM stages due to strict surveillance actions and trade-for-trade settlement.
