## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Lloyds Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 512463) has forfeited 3,35,552 partly paid-up equity shares due to non-payment of allotment or call monies.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lloyds Enterprises Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 512463
- Number of Shares Forfeited: 3,35,552 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- Distinctive Numbers: 1526216394 to 1526551945.
- Reason: Non-payment of allotment/call monies on partly paid-up shares issued pursuant to a rights issue.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the share forfeiture and update records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the specific shareholders whose partly paid-up shares have been forfeited due to unpaid calls.
