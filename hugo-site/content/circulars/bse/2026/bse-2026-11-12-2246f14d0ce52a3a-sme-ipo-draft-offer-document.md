## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Uranus Stone Products Limited for its SME IPO. The document has been filed pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is open for public comments for a period of 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Uranus Stone Products Limited
- Filing Date: November 06, 2026
- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Public Comments Window: 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific; standard SME IPO draft offer document disclosure procedure under Schedule VI of ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public comments on the draft offer document must be submitted within 21 days of the notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Comments Deadline: December 3, 2026 (21 days from notice date)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and invites public stakeholder feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Uranus Stone Products Limited prior to final issuance.
