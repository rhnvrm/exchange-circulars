## Summary

Effective from Friday, November 27, 2026, 83,64,036 partly paid-up equity shares of Indrayani Biotech Ltd issued on a rights basis shall be listed and permitted for trading on the Exchange under the 'X' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 83,64,036 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each, partly paid-up of Rs. 2.50/- each.
- **Scrip Code:** 890243
- **Scrip ID / BOLT Name:** INDRAYANIPP
- **ISIN No:** IN9007C01029
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/-
- **Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2.50/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- (Rs. 2.50/- paid on application and Rs. 7.50/- payable on subsequent calls).
- **Date of Allotment:** 03/11/2026
- **Dividend Entitlement:** Proportionate to the amount paid-up.

## Regulatory Changes

Temporary ISIN No. IN8007C01020 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular Nos. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 regarding activation of ISIN in case of additional issue of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the suspension of temporary ISIN IN8007C01020 and the activation of the new ISIN IN9007C01029 effective November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 3, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading of partly paid-up rights equity shares for shareholders of Indrayani Biotech Ltd.
