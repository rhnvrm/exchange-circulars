## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further equity shares issued by 15 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the exchange effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 15 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading admission: November 30, 2026.
- Companies include major financial institutions, banks, and corporate entities such as Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Bandhan Bank, SBI Life Insurance, ICICI Lombard General Insurance, Varun Beverages, and others.
- All listed securities are ranked pari passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in status for all mentioned tranches is marked as NA (Not Applicable).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine listing of securities pursuant to corporate ESOP/ESOS allotments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participating companies must ensure proper accounting and demat credit of the listed equity shares. No additional compliance actions are required from investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase resulting from ESOP exercises, causing minor equity dilution and capitalization adjustments for the respective companies without broader market impact.
