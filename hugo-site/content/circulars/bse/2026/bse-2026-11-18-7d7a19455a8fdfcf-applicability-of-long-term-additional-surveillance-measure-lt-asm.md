## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261118-25 regarding the application, review, and movement of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group transfers effective November 19 and November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 23, 2026 on all open positions as on November 20, 2026 and new positions created from November 23, 2026 onwards.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands become applicable w.e.f. November 19, 2026.
- Certain securities continued in LT-ASM framework but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and imposition of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including margin requirements, price band restrictions, and segment/group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and lower price band restrictions on applicable securities. Members can contact bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Higher stages / Price band changes / Moving out: November 19, 2026
- Open positions cutoff for margin: November 20, 2026
- 100% margins & Group transfers (T/XT/MT/TS): November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on trading and liquidity for securities placed under or moving across stages of the LT-ASM framework due to stringent margin requirements and price band restrictions.
