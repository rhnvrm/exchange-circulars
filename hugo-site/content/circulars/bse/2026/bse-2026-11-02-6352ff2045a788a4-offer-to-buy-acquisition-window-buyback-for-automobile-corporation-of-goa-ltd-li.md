## Summary

BSE has issued the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window) for the buyback of equity shares of Automobile Corporation of Goa Ltd (Scrip Code: 505036, ISIN: INE451C01013). The buyback offer is scheduled from Tuesday, November 3, 2026, to Monday, November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Automobile Corporation of Goa Ltd (Symbol: ACGL, Scrip Code: 505036, ISIN: INE451C01013).
- Offer Size: Up to 1,44,639 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 2005.00 per share.
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00; Market Lot: 1.
- Opening Date: Tuesday, 3rd November 2026; Closing Date: Monday, 9th November 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid Offering Period and Bid Modifications/Cancellation run from 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. on all issue days.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural notification for a corporate buyback acquisition window under existing exchange frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure investor UCC is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bidding is permitted for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note operational guidelines via designated iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 3rd November 2026
- Closing Date: 9th November 2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 3rd November 2026 to 6th November 2026 (Give-up last day: 9th November 2026; Take-up last day: 9th November 2026 up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and participation in the buyback process for eligible shareholders and trading members through designated exchange platforms.
