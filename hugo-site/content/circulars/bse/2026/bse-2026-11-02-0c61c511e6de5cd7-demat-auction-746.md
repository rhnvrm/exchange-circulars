## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 746 for Rolling Settlement DR-746/2026-27. Trading members are invited to submit offers for the purchase of securities in auction on November 2, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 2, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: November 3, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular reiterates standard auction procedures and notes that following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, the restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in settlements where they had short delivered such securities is removed.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times. Members must exercise utmost care while entering security codes and rates, as punching errors cannot be corrected.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 2, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 3, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures efficient clearing and settlement of short deliveries in the equity cash segment by providing structured auction windows and pay-in timelines.
