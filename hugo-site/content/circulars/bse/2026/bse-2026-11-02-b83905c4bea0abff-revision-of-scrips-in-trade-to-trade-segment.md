## Summary

In continuation of Exchange notice no. 20261029-33 dated October 29, 2026, BSE has announced that nine securities are eligible to move out of the trade-for-trade category into normal trading segments effective November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Nine securities are transitioned from trade-to-trade (T/XT) groups to normal rolling settlement (B/X) groups.
- Asahi Songwon Colors Ltd and Scan Steels Ltd move from Group T to Group B.
- Ceenik Exports India Ltd, NCL Research & Financial Services Ltd, Quantum Digital Vision India Ltd, SC Agrotech Ltd, Spenta International Ltd, Stellant Securities (India) Ltd, and Veerhealth Care Ltd move from Group XT to Group X.

## Regulatory Changes

- Affected securities are relieved from trade-to-trade settlement restrictions where mandatory delivery was required without intraday netting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading and risk management systems to reflect the revised segments for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026.
- Reference Notice Date: October 29, 2026 (Notice No. 20261029-33).

## Impact Assessment

- Restores normal liquidity and trading operations for the specified scrips, benefiting investors trading in these securities.
