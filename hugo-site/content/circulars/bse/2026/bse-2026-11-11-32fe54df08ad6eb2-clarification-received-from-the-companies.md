## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. Clarifications have been received and attached from IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd and Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Ltd, while replies are awaited from 24 other listed entities.

## Key Points

- Price and volume movement clarification requested by BSE surveillance department.
- Replies received and attached from:
  - IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd (Scrip Code: 524164)
  - Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 532872)
- Replies are awaited from 24 listed companies including Bharat Immunologicals, Billwin Industries, CMX Holdings, Deep Polymers, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must provide prompt clarifications on significant price and volume movements to maintain market transparency and ensure investors have access to latest relevant information.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor awareness regarding abnormal stock price and volume movements.
