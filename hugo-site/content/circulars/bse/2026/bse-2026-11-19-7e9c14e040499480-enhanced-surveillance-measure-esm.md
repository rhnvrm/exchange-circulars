## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities meeting inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or shifting to Stage II with trade-for-trade settlement, price bands, and periodic call auctions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 23, 2026 on all open positions as on November 20, 2026 and new positions created from November 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are subject to Trade-for-Trade settlement with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework are listed in Annexure II, and the consolidated list is in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measures, including 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, 2% price bands, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection and compliance with trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Stage II / lower stage movements / moving out effective date: November 20, 2026
- 100% margin and trade-for-trade shift effective date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading mechanisms for shortlisted securities are constrained due to strict margin norms, trade-for-trade delivery requirements, and periodic call auction sessions.
