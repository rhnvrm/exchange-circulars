## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 2,34,82,500 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 63.50/- to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants by Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited
- Scrip Code: 539056
- ISIN: INE172N01012
- Number of Shares: 2,34,82,500 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 73.50/- (Face value Rs. 10/- + Premium Rs. 63.50/-)
- Effective Date: November 8, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 24, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 
  - 1,70,06,800 shares (Dist. Nos. 565861448 - 582868247) locked-in up to 10/05/2028
  - 64,75,700 shares (Dist. Nos. 582868248 - 589343947) locked-in up to 10/05/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and distribution numbers specified for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: November 8, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry: May 10, 2027 and May 10, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of Imagicaaworld Entertainment Limited following warrant conversion, with standard lock-in restrictions applicable to promoter and non-promoter allottees.
