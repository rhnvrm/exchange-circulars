## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of three new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) on the exchange's debt segment under the 'G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS' category, effective from Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Trading commences on November 20, 2026.
- **Instruments Listed:** Three new Treasury Bills spanning 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day tenors.
- **Market Lot:** 1 for all listed securities.
- **Trading Restriction:** Securities will not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective maturity/redemption dates.

### Details of Listed T-Bills

| Scrip Code | ISIN | Market Lot | Symbol | Maturity Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 805292 | IN002026X339 | 1 | 91TB180227 | 18/02/2027 |
| 805293 | IN002026Y337 | 1 | 182TB20527 | 20/05/2027 |
| 805294 | IN002026Z334 | 1 | 364T181127 | 18/11/2027 |

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for government securities issued by the Reserve Bank of India.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading restrictions, noting that trading in these T-Bills ceases two working days prior to their maturity/redemption dates (excluding bank holidays).

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** February 18, 2027 (91-day), May 20, 2027 (182-day), and November 18, 2027 (364-day)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for investors in government Treasury Bills.
