## Summary

BSE has notified trading members about the change in name of GSB Finance Ltd to Coffers Finvest Limited (Scrip Code: 511543). Consequent to the name change, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: GSB Finance Ltd
- New Company Name: Coffers Finvest Limited
- Scrip Code: 511543
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): COFI
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): COFI

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action communication regarding name and identifier updates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to update their internal systems, client databases, and trading terminals with the new company name and BOLT Plus identifiers for Scrip Code 511543.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact restricted to updating database records and trading system nomenclature for the company.
