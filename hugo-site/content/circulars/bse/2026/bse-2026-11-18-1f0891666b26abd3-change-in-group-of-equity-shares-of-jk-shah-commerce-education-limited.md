## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in trading group for the equity shares of J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited. The equity shares of the company will be permitted to trade under the 'B' group effective from Thursday, December 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited
- BSE Scrip Code: 544956
- New Trading Group: 'B'
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to note the change in trading group for operational and compliance tracking.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard administrative category/group migration on the exchange platform with minimal direct market impact.
