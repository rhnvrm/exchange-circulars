## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about a revision in the minimum application and additional purchase amounts for the scheme Axis Silver Fund of Fund, effective November 16, 2026, as communicated by Axis Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Axis Silver Fund of Fund
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026
- Revised Minimum Application Amount: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Rs. 1/- thereafter (previously Rs. 5000 and in multiples of Rs. 1/-).
- Revised Minimum Additional Purchase Amount: Rs. 100 and in multiples of Rs. 1/- thereafter (previously Rs. 1000 and in multiples of Rs. 1/-).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - Operational update regarding fund scheme parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must update their systems and order placement workflows on the BSE StAR MF platform to reflect the revised minimum purchase amounts.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Lowering the minimum investment threshold makes the fund more accessible to retail investors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
