## Summary

Keystone Realtors Limited has informed the Exchange regarding the actual reduced face value following the part redemption of its debentures (Scrip Code: 977174, ISIN: INE0MYJ07120). Trading members and market participants are notified that trading in the debentures will take place with the reduced face value.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Keystone Realtors Limited
- Scrip Code: 977174
- ISIN: INE0MYJ07120
- New Reduced Face Value: Rs. 52,059.70/- per debenture
- Notice Reference: 20261111-23 (DR- 755/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notification by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the reduced face value for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational and market impact, limited to holders and traders of the specified debentures reflecting the revised face value.
