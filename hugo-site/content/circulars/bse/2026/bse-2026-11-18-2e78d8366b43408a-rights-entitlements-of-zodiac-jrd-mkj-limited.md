## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of ZODIAC-JRD-MKJ LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: ZODIAC-JRD-MKJ LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751159
- Scrip ID: ZODJRD-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE077B20026
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 19, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: November 19, 2026 to December 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Rights Entitlements (REs) shall be tradable in dematerialized form only.
- Settlement shall be on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, mentioning the ISIN, and clarifying that REs are not ordinary shares of the company.
- Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes or bills for the purchase of REs, explaining that purchase grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce REs before closing.
- REs neither subscribed by application with requisite money nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Rights Entitlements Trading Start Date: November 19, 2026
- Rights Entitlements Trading End Date: November 30, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: November 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders and trading participants are provided with clear timelines and operational guidelines for trading REs and participating in the rights issue of ZODIAC-JRD-MKJ Limited.
