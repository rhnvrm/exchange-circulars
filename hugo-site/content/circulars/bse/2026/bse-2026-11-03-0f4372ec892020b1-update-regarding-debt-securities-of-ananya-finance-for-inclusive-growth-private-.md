## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the update to debt securities of Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited. Effective November 4, 2026, the scrip code 975412 will trade with a revised ISIN.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 975412
- Existing ISIN: INE774L07081
- Revised ISIN: INE774L07107
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of ISIN for existing debt securities as per corporate update.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the revised ISIN for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update affecting debt trading participants holding or trading the specified scrip code.
