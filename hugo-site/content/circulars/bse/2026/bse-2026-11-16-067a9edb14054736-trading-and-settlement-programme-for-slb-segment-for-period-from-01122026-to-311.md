## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment covering the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026, detailing settlement numbers, trading dates, and pay-in/pay-out schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement numbers from SL-167/26-27 to SL-188/26-27.
- Specifies daily trading dates, SLB entry dates, confirmation dates, and pay-in/pay-out dates throughout December 2026.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 08:00 a.m. with members required to submit pay-in instructions to depositories/banks latest by 07:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clearing members must adhere to the specified pay-in/pay-out timings and settlement dates for the SLB segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026
- **First Settlement (SL-167/26-27):** December 1, 2026 (Trading) / December 2, 2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out)
- **Final Settlement (SL-188/26-27):** December 31, 2026 (Trading) / January 1, 2027 (Pay-in/Pay-out)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement execution for participants active in the Securities Lending and Borrowing segment during December 2026.
