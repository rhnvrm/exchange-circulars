## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 3,30,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each of Caprihans India Ltd. issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Caprihans India Ltd. (Scrip Code: 509486, ISIN: INE479A01018).
- **Securities Issued:** 3,30,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue price: Rs. 200/- per share).
- **Allottees:** Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants).
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted from Wednesday, November 18, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distribution Numbers:** 15253972 to 15583971.
- **Date of Allotment:** 24/03/2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 3,30,000 shares under lock-in up to 29/05/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice for securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities listed and the lock-in restrictions applicable until May 29, 2028.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** March 24, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Involves the addition of 3,30,000 equity shares to the paid-up capital of Caprihans India Ltd. following warrant conversion by promoters. Minimal direct impact on general market liquidity, with shares subject to a lock-in period until May 2028.
