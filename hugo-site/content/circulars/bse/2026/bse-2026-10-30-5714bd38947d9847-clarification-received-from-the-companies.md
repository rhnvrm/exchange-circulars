## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice detailing clarifications received from select companies regarding significant price and volume movements observed in their scrips in the recent past. Additionally, the Exchange has published a list of companies from which replies are still awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 5 companies: Century Business Media Ltd, Filtron Engineers Ltd, NMS Global Ltd, Sjp Ultrasonics Ltd, and Suyog Telematics Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 21 other listed entities including Amtech Esters Ltd, Anna Infrastructures Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, and others.
- Issued by BSE Surveillance Department to ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies notified by the Exchange are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding scrips experiencing unusual market activity and highlights companies with pending disclosure requirements.
