## Summary

The BSE has sought clarification from various listed companies regarding news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure accuracy for investors. Replies have been received from EKI Energy Services Ltd, Gulf Oil Lubricants India Ltd, and Solex Energy Ltd, while the reply from Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd is awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications sought by BSE Surveillance department (DOSS) regarding media reports.
- EKI Energy Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 543284) provided clarification linking to Moneycontrol.
- Gulf Oil Lubricants India Ltd (Scrip Code: 538567) provided clarification linking to Economic Times.
- Solex Energy Ltd (Scrip Code: 544862) provided clarification linking to Hindu BusinessLine.
- Reply awaited from Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 524715).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; routine compliance verification under exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must promptly respond to exchange inquiries regarding media reports and rumors affecting market integrity.

- Notice Date: 02 Nov 2026

Minimal direct operational or market impact as this is a standard surveillance disclosure notice.
