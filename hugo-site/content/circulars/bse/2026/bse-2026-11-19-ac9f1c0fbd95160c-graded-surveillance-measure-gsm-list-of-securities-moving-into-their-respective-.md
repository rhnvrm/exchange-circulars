## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of select securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 20, 2026. Trading members are required to comply with specific surveillance actions corresponding to each GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Securities are moving into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- Effective date for the revised GSM stages is November 20, 2026.
- Actions include 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and trading frequency restrictions.
- Settlement shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework based on the security's assigned stage (Stages I, II, III, or IV), involving restrictive price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, and mandatory Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD ranging from 50% to 100% of trade value).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities. Settlements must strictly follow trade-to-trade mechanisms without netting off, and applicable margins and ASD must be deposited by buyers as stipulated.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 November 2026
- Effective Date: 20 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and market participation for the affected securities will be restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement, mandatory margins, and Additional Surveillance Deposits, serving to curb excessive volatility and speculative activities.
