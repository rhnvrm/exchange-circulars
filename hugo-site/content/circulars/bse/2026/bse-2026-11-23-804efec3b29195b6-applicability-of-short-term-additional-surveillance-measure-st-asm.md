## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The framework entails enhanced margin requirements effective from November 26, 2026, on open positions and new positions.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting specified criteria are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance actions is November 26, 2026, applicable on open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance measures, enforcing stricter margin requirements on short-listed scrips in conjunction with existing surveillance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on open and new positions for the affected securities starting November 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin obligations (up to 100%), which may impact liquidity and volatility in the affected scrips.
