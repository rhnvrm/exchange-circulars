## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. has issued operational timelines and settlement guidelines for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment on account of the Diwali Laxmi Pujan Muhurat Trading session, pursuant to BSE Circular No. 20261019-13.

## Key Points

- Client margin reporting schedule is governed by BSECL Circular No. 20261030-13.
- Early Pay-In (EPI) cut-off for funds and securities for trades executed on November 08, 2026, is set at 17:45 hrs.
- Pay-in and pay-out obligations for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled according to BSECL circular no. 20261022-4.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational schedule adjustments for the special Muhurat Trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with the specified EPI cut-off time of 17:45 hrs on November 08, 2026, and follow respective settlement circulars.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 October 2026
- Trade Date: November 08, 2026
- Affected Trade Dates for Settlement: November 06, 2026 and November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly clearing and settlement operations for market participants in the EGR segment during the festive trading session.
