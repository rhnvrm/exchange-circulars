## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of three newly listed companies—BLS Polymers Limited, SFC Environmental Technologies Limited, and Wiscon Industries Limited—to its respective indices following their listing effective Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- BLS Polymers Limited (Ticker: 545011) and SFC Environmental Technologies Limited (Ticker: 545009) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Thursday, November 5, 2026.
- Wiscon Industries Limited (Ticker: 545010) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Wednesday, November 4, 2026.
- All actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; this is an informational notice regarding index updates.

## Important Dates

- November 3, 2026: Effective listing date for BLS Polymers, SFC Environmental Technologies, and Wiscon Industries.
- November 4, 2026: Wiscon Industries added to the BSE SME IPO index.
- November 5, 2026: BLS Polymers and SFC Environmental Technologies added to the BSE IPO index.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion for newly listed entities with minimal operational impact on existing market participants.
