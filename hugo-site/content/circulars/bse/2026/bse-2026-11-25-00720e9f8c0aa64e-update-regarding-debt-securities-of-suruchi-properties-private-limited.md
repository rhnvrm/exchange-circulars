## Summary

BSE has announced that the debt securities of Suruchi Properties Private Limited will be available for trading with revised terms effective from November 26, 2026, following debt restructuring.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suruchi Properties Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 976775
- ISIN: INE217T07027
- Event: Debt restructuring and revised trading terms
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Debt securities made available for trading under revised terms consequent to debt restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the revised terms and ensure compliance during order placement and execution.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly trading of restructured debt securities for market participants and investors holding the instrument.
