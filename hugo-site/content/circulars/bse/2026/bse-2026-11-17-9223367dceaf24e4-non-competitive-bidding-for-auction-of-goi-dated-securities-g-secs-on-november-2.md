## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for November 20, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated November 16, 2026. Trading members can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered:**
  - 6.53% GS 2031 (Symbol: `653GS31N26`, Re-issue, Issue Size: ₹19,000 Cr, Min Sub: 100 units & multiples, Max Amt: ₹2 crs)
  - 7.71% GS 2066 (Symbol: `771GS66N26`, Re-issue, Issue Size: ₹14,000 Cr, Min Sub: 100 units & multiples, Max Amt: ₹2 crs)
- **Bidding Platform:** iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module)
- **Bid Collection Start Date:** November 17, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** November 19, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** November 20, 2026, till 8:00 am
- **Auction Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Routine operational circular following RBI's auction announcement)

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS platform. Pay-in and obligation requirements must be fulfilled through ICCL operations.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start:** November 17, 2026, 10:00 AM
- **Direct Investor Bid End:** November 19, 2026, 5:00 PM
- **Member Bid End:** November 20, 2026, 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides institutional and retail investors via trading members an organized mechanism to participate in Government securities auctions through non-competitive bidding.
