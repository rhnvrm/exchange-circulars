## Summary

SML Mahindra Limited has announced a rights issue of equity shares, fixing November 20, 2026, as the record date. Eligible shareholders will receive 2 equity shares for every 7 equity shares held at an issue price of Rs. 3506/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: SML Mahindra Limited (Scrip Code: 505192, Notice Ref: DR- 761/2026-2027)
- Record Date: November 20, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: November 20, 2026
- Rights Ratio: 02 (Two) Equity Shares for every 07 (Seven) Equity Shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 3506/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/- plus premium of Rs. 3496/-)
- Payment Terms: Entire rights issue price of Rs. 3506/- per share is payable at the time of making the application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the ex-rights date and record date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Record Date: November 20, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible to participate in marketplace rights entitlement trading and application.
