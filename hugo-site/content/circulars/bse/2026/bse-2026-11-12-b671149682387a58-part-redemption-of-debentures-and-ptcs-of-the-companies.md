## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for part redemption and payment of interest on Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in these securities will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- InCred Financial Services Limited (Scrip: 976209, ISIN: INE321N07475): Record Date 13/11/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 33,333.36 per debenture.
- Aurum PTC Series G-1 (Scrip: 977937, ISIN: INE2XFR15029): Record Date 13/11/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 0.87 per debenture.
- NAOMI 05 2024 PTC (Scrip: 975673, ISIN: INE0WLO15020): Record Date 13/11/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 28,191.04 per debenture.
- Magnite Developers Pvt Ltd (Scrip: 974290, ISIN: INE0M1Q07015): Record Date 13/11/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 2,40,112/- per debenture.
- Total Environment Resorts Private Limited (Scrip: 977072, ISIN: INE06BX07013): Record Date 15/11/2026 (Ex-Date 13/11/2026); Reduced face value of Rs. 86,000/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural notice regarding debt security corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure settlements reflect the correct valuation and record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 12 November 2026
- Record Dates: 13 November 2026 and 15 November 2026
- Ex-Date: 13 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is restricted to investors holding the specific NCDs and PTCs mentioned, reflecting capital repayment and adjusted trading valuations.
