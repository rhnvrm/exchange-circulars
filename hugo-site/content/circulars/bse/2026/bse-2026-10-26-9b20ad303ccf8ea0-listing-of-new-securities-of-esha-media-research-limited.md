## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 10,00,000 new equity shares of Esha Media Research Limited (Scrip Code: 531259, ISIN: INE328F01016) issued on a preferential basis to promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Esha Media Research Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 531259
- **ISIN:** INE328F01016
- **Security Details:** 10,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 5/- (Issue Price: Rs. 15/-) on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 9763231 to 10763230
- **Date of Allotment:** 06/08/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 30/05/2028 (10,00,000 shares, Dist. Nos. 9763231 to 10763230).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate listing procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the listing and effective trading date of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** August 6, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of Esha Media Research Limited through promoter preferential allotment. Minimal direct impact on general market liquidity given promoter lock-in provisions.
