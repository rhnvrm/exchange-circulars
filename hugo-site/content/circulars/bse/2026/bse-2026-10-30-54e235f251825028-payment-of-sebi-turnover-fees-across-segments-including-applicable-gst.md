## Summary

BSE has announced the debit schedule and procedures for SEBI turnover fees along with applicable 18% GST across cash, derivatives, currency derivatives, commodity derivatives, interest rate derivatives, new debt, repo, and EGR segments for October 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI turnover fees across all exchange segments are subject to 18% GST.
- Debit date: November 04, 2026 (3rd working day of the month) from member settlement (valan) / Exchange dues accounts.
- Extranet availability: Turnover details and invoice files will be made available on or before November 03, 2026.
- Relevant file naming conventions include TF2Ammyy through TF2Jmmyy and SEBIOCT2026.Member_Code.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of statutory 18% GST levy on SEBI turnover fees across exchange segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure sufficient funds are available in their settlement (valan) account or Exchange dues account for the debit of SEBI turnover fees.
- Verify turnover details via Extranet (current date > EQ > Transaction) and column no. 26 of the TF2A file.
- Download monthly invoices from the Extranet path (Periodic files > 2026 > ZIP with file name SEBIOCT2026.Member_Code).

## Important Dates

- November 03, 2026: Availability of turnover details and invoices on Extranet.
- November 04, 2026: Debit of SEBI turnover fees including GST from settlement / Exchange dues accounts.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct operational requirement for trading members to maintain adequate liquidity in settlement accounts to facilitate seamless collection of statutory turnover fees and GST.
