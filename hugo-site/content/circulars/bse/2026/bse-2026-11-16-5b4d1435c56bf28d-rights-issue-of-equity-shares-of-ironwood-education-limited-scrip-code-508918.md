## Summary

IRONWOOD EDUCATION LIMITED has announced a Rights Issue of Equity Shares. The company has fixed the Record Date as November 18, 2026, and transactions will be on an Ex-Rights Basis starting from the same date.

## Key Points

- **Issue Details**: 34 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 31/- per share (Total issue price: Rs. 41/- per share) on a Rights Basis for every 100 Equity Shares held.
- **Record Date**: November 18, 2026.
- **Ex-Rights Date**: November 18, 2026.
- **Payment**: The entire Rights Issue Price of Rs. 41/- per share is payable on Application.
- **Scrip Code**: 508918.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the record date and ex-rights trading commencement for Ironwood Education Limited.

- **Record Date**: November 18, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date**: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible to participate in the rights issue. Share prices will adjust on an ex-rights basis from the effective date.
