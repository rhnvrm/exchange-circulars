## Summary

BSE has notified market participants that trading in the equity shares of IGL Spirits Limited will be shifted to the 'B' group effective Thursday, November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: IGL Spirits Limited
- BSE Scrip Code: 545013
- New Trading Group: 'B' group
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of trading group category on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the group change for order placement and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational notice with minimal direct impact on broader market liquidity or valuation; affects trading group classification for IGL Spirits Limited.
