## Summary

Nippon India Mutual Fund is offering NIPPON INDIA QUARTERLY INTERVAL FUND - SERIES II during the Specified Transaction Period (STP) from November 09, 2026, to November 11, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Specified Transaction Period (STP): November 09, 2026 to November 11, 2026.
- Eligible plans and ISINs include IDCW and Growth plans (Regular and Direct): INF204K01DO3, INF204K01DP0, INF204K01DN5, INF204K01F46, INF204K01F53, and INF204K01F61.
- MICR Cheque, Nodal, and OTM payment modes are not accepted during the STP.
- Clear funds must be received by the Exchange by 2:30 PM on the last date of the STP to avoid rejection.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard operational notification for mutual fund scheme transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure orders and clear funds are submitted within prescribed cut-off times and acceptable payment modes.

## Important Dates

- STP Start Date: November 09, 2026
- STP End Date: November 11, 2026 (2:30 PM deadline for clear funds)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates subscription and redemption for investors in the specified interval fund scheme during the defined window.
