## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 16,13,000 new equity shares of Commercial Syn Bags Limited (Scrip Code - 539986) with effect from November 13, 2026, following preferential allotment upon warrant conversion to promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Commercial Syn Bags Limited
- Scrip Code: 539986
- Security Details: 16,13,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 62/- (Issue Price: Rs. 72/-)
- Date of Allotment: 17/09/2026
- ISIN: INE073V01015
- Distinctive Numbers: 40339201 to 41952200
- Ranking: Pari-passu with old equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in of 16,13,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 40339201 to 41952200) applicable up to 31/05/2028.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 17, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Involves expansion of equity capital base through promoter warrant conversion. Limited overall market impact.
