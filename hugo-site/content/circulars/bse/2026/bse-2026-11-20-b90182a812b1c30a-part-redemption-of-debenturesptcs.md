## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that certain companies have fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) / PTCs / Bonds. Trading in these securities will take place with reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- **LoanX Verbena (Scrip Code: 977074, ISIN: INE2GIZ15013):** Record Date and Ex-Date are 23/11/2026 with a reduced face value of Rs. 2030.55 per debenture.
- **Best Capital Services Limited (Scrip Code: 978400, ISIN: INE04UP07154):** Record Date and Ex-Date are 23/11/2026 with a reduced face value of Rs. 54166.67 per debenture.
- Trading will resume with the reduced face value effective from the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the reduced face values and record dates for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 23/11/2026
- **Ex-Date:** 23/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for debt segment trading members reflecting the updated face value of debentures following part redemption.
