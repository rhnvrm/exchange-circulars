## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261127-28 dated November 27, 2026, announcing the movement of certain securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, or IV).
- Effective date for the revised GSM stages is November 30, 2026.
- Settlement for these securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance actions aligned with the GSM framework established on February 27, 2017 (and subsequent amendments).
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with 5% or lower price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value.
- Stage III: Trade-for-trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- Stage IV: Trade-for-trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Settlement must adhere strictly to trade-to-trade mechanics without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affected securities face severe liquidity constraints, higher margin requirements or ASD deposits, and restricted trading frequencies depending on their assigned GSM stage.
