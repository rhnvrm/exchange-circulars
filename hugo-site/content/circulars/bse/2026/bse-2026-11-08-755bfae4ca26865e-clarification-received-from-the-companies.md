## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. The Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarification reply has been received from TVS Electronics Ltd (Scrip Code: 532513).
- Replies are awaited from 23 other listed companies, including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd, and others.
- The notice aims to maintain market transparency and protect investor interests regarding abnormal stock price or volume behavior.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance disclosure action.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies queried by the exchange are required to submit clarifications regarding any material information, event, or announcement that may have a bearing on price/volume movement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact. Investors should monitor pending company clarifications before making trading decisions on the listed scrips.
