## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule and bidding timeline for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Mayur Leather Products Limited (Scrip Code: 531680). The bidding window will operate from December 2, 2026, to December 15, 2026, under Settlement No. 2026145.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026145
- **Bid Start Date:** 02/12/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 15/12/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 29/12/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 30/12/2026
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled operational notice concerning the takeover acquisition window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026145 in the depository system.
- Early pay-in of securities must be completed sufficiently in advance so that earmarking/blocking in client accounts reflects in BSE/BSECL systems before bidding/confirmation window closure.
- No EPN file upload is required by members and custodians.
- Stamp duty charges should be verified from the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** December 2, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** December 15, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** December 29, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** December 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of shares tendered under the takeover offer for Mayur Leather Products Limited, ensuring compliance with depository early pay-in guidelines.
