## Summary

BSE has issued the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) for Jyothy Labs Ltd. The bidding window is scheduled to be conducted from November 17, 2026, to November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Jyothy Labs Ltd
- **Bidding Period:** November 17, 2026 to November 23, 2026
- **Settlement Type & No.:** Buyback, Settlement No. 2026138
- **Obligation Download:** November 30, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** December 01, 2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Buyback)" and settlement No. 2026138.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for bidding and confirmation.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- No EPN file upload is required by members and custodians.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** November 30, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth execution and settlement of the Jyothy Labs Ltd share buyback through the stock exchange acquisition window, ensuring timely delivery and funds settlement for participating shareholders.
