BSE has sought clarification from Spicejet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to verify accuracy and inform investors. As of the notice date, the reply from the company is awaited.

- **Notice Number:** 20261123-14
- **Notice Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Department:** Surveillance (DOSS)
- **Company Involved:** Spicejet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285)

None.

The listed entity is required to provide clarification to the exchange regarding the news articles in accordance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations.

Minimal immediate impact on market operations; pending clarification response from the company.
