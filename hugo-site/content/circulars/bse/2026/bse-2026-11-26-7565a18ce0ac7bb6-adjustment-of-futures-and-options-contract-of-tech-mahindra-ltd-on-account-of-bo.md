## Summary

BSE has announced adjustments to all available Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Tech Mahindra Ltd (Derivatives Asset Code: TECM) on account of a bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1. The adjustments will take effect at the end of the day on November 27, 2026, with the ex-date being November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bonus Ratio:** 1 (One) Bonus Equity Share of Rs. 5/- each for every 1 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs. 5/- each.
- **Record Date:** November 30, 2026.
- **Adjustment Factor:** Final adjustment factor is 2.
- **Strike Price:** Old strike price divided by the adjustment factor (2).
- **Market Lot:** Old market lot multiplied by the adjustment factor (2), revising the market lot from 600 to 1200.
- **Futures Price:** Old futures price divided by the adjustment factor (2), rounded off to the nearest tick size.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of SEBI guidelines for adjustment of Futures & Options Contracts on announcement of corporate actions, standard adjustment formulas are applied.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Equity Derivatives Segment must note the revised strike prices, market lots, and futures prices for Tech Mahindra F&O contracts effective from November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Last Cum-Date / EOD Adjustment Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Record Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives traders holding positions in Tech Mahindra F&O contracts will see proportional adjustments in strike prices, lot sizes, and futures prices without any economic value alteration.
