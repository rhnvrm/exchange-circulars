## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reinforcing compliance guidelines on 'Safer participation of Retail Investors in Algorithmic trading' following complaints regarding the misuse of Static IP facilities by exchange-empaneled Algo Providers and trading members.

## Key Points

- Static IP access is strictly restricted to 'Tech-savvy Retail Investors' who develop their own algorithms using programming knowledge as per SEBI guidelines.
- Trading Members and Algo Providers are prohibited from providing Static IP access to retail clients who do not meet the tech-savvy criteria.
- Trading Members registering Tech Savvy Clients must obtain an undertaking confirming that clients meet SEBI definitions and waive recourse to exchange investor grievance mechanisms for losses incurred from algorithm logic.
- Financial penalties are introduced for non-adherence, starting at Rs. 5,000 per client (subject to a maximum of Rs. 1 lakh) for the first instance of failing to obtain undertakings, with 50% to 100% escalation for repeat instances.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict enforcement and monitoring of Static IP usage under the Retail Algo framework effective since April 01, 2026.
- Mandatory collection of signed undertakings waiving investor grievance recourse for self-developed algorithms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must verify client eligibility and secure mandatory undertakings before registering Tech Savvy Clients.
- Algo Providers and Trading Members must immediately halt unauthorized Static IP allocations to non-compliant retail clients.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Retail Algo Framework Effective Date: April 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High operational and regulatory impact on trading members and algo providers offering algorithmic trading solutions, requiring rigorous client onboarding verifications and risk controls.
