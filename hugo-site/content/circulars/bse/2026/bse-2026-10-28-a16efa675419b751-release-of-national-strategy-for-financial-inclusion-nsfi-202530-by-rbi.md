## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, emphasizing simplified digital interfaces, vernacular language support, and AI/ML integration. In line with SEBI's directions, Investment Advisers are required to adopt these measures to enhance financial inclusion and customer accessibility.

## Key Points

- Implementation of RBI's National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30 by Investment Advisers.
- Simplification of digital interfaces to ensure ease of use and prevent customer confusion.
- Addressing language barriers by incorporating vernacular languages and utilizing AI-powered translation tools like Bhashini.
- Development of an AI & ML-based public query system on common securities market-related aspects via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

Investment Advisers must align their digital platforms with RBI's NSFI 2025–30 guidelines, specifically addressing Chapter IV paragraphs I.6(f) and IV.6(a).

## Compliance Requirements

- Simplify digital interfaces for ease of use.
- Integrate vernacular language support using AI translation platforms such as Bhashini.
- Implement an AI/ML-based public query system for common securities market-related aspects on websites and social media channels.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 October 2026
- NSFI 2025–30 Date: December 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Investment Advisers will need to update their web and digital portals, incorporate vernacular options, and deploy AI-driven query bots to assist clients with common securities market queries, enhancing customer accessibility and compliance.
