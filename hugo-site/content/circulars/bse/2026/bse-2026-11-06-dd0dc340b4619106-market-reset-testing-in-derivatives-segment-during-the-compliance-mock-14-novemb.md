## Summary

BSE has scheduled a compliance mock trading session on 14 November 2026 to enable trading members and vendors to validate their applications for Market Reset scenarios in the derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- ETI Manual Version 1.6.15 updates the scope of order cancellation during a market reset.
- Existing mechanisms support the revised cancellation scope; no structural changes or application development are required.
- Market Reset messages will contain Template ID 10307 and Enum value 102.
- Continuous trading resumes immediately after each Market Reset.
- Schedule for partitions on 14 November 2026:
  - Partition 7: 12:30 PM
  - Partition 8: 1:00 PM
  - Partition 9: 1:30 PM
  - Partition 10: 2:00 PM

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; clarification provided via ETI Manual Version 1.6.15 regarding order cancellation scope during market resets.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and vendors are encouraged to participate in the compliance mock to validate applications for HF and LF sessions, verify market data (EMDI, EOBI, and NFCAST), and share feedback with BSE Tech Support.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 November 2026
- Compliance Mock Date: 14 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Testing allows members to ensure correct handling of market reset events without mandatory code updates.
