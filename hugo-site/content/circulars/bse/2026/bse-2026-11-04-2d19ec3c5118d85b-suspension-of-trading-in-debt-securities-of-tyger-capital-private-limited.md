## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Tyger Capital Private Limited due to their maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tyger Capital Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 975186
- ISIN: INE01EQ07087
- Security Description: TCPL-9.65%-6-11-26-PVT
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members of the Exchange are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures of the company with effect from November 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 5, 2026
- Maturity Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard cessation of trading for maturing debt securities with minimal market impact.
