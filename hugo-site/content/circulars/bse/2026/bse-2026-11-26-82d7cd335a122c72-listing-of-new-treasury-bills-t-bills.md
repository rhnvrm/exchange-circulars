## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of three new Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) with 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day tenors, effective from November 27, 2026, under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS category.

## Key Points

- **91-Day T-Bill**: Scrip code 805295, ISIN IN002026X347, Symbol 91TB250227, maturing on 25/02/2027.
- **182-Day T-Bill**: Scrip code 805296, ISIN IN002026Y345, Symbol 182TB27527, maturing on 27/05/2027.
- **364-Day T-Bill**: Scrip code 805297, ISIN IN002026Z342, Symbol 364T251127, maturing on 25/11/2027.
- Market lot size for all listed T-Bills is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard issuance and listing procedure for sovereign treasury bills.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the listed securities will not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective Maturity/Redemption Date.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date**: November 27, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: February 25, 2027 (91-day), May 27, 2027 (182-day), November 25, 2027 (364-day)

## Impact Assessment

Routine addition of short-term government securities offering participants enhanced liquidity and investment options in the debt segment.
