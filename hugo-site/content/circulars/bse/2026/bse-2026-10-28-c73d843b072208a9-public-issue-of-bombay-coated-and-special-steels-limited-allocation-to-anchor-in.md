## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited has successfully allocated 65,97,500 equity shares to anchor investors at Rs. 82 per equity share as part of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited
- Anchor Portion: 65,97,500 Equity Shares subscribed
- Issue Price: Rs. 82/- per equity share
- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Department: Listing Business Relationship

None. Routine procedural notice regarding public issue anchor investor allocation.
