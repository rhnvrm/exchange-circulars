## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on its debt market segment, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- New commercial paper securities issued on a private placement basis are admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective October 30, 2026.
- Issuing companies include ONGC Petro Additions Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, Ugro Capital Limited, and Nuvama Wealth Management Limited.
- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof.
- Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to dematerialised form requirements, standard denominations, and ISIN specifications.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 29, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: Between December 2026 and March 2027 (e.g., 11/12/2026, 27/01/2027, 28/01/2027, 04/03/2027, 10/03/2027).

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading venue for privately placed commercial debt instruments.
