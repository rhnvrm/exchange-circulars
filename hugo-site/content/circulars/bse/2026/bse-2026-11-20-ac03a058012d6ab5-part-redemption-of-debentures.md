## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the record dates fixed for part redemption and payment of interest on non-convertible debentures, PTCs, and bonds by specified companies. Trading in these securities will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Krazybee Services Ltd:** Scrip Code 977094 (ISIN: INE07HK07833), Record Date & Ex-Date: 25/11/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 25,000/- per debenture.
- **Share India Securities Limited:** Scrip Code 977430 (ISIN: INE932X07031), Record Date & Ex-Date: 25/11/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 50,000/- per debenture.
- **LoanX Bramble Jul 2025:** Scrip Code 976995 (ISIN: INE2DDX15016), Record Date & Ex-Date: 26/11/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 4,526.99 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational notice regarding corporate actions in the debt segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and ensure settlement and trading are conducted accordingly with effect from the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 20/11/2026
- **Krazybee Services Ltd Record/Ex-Date:** 25/11/2026
- **Share India Securities Limited Record/Ex-Date:** 25/11/2026
- **LoanX Bramble Jul 2025 Record/Ex-Date:** 26/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; affects only holders and trading participants dealing in the specific debt securities undergoing part redemption.
