## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Paisalo Digital Ltd (Scrip Code: 731624) due to its maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in this commercial paper effective from November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paisalo Digital Limited
- Scrip Code: 731624
- ISIN: INE420C14284
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 03, 2026
- Notice Reference: Notice No. 20261102-47 (DR-748/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine market operation enforcing trading suspension upon debt instrument maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid Commercial Paper of the company with effect from November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational restriction limited to the specific commercial paper maturity of Paisalo Digital Ltd; no broader market impact.
