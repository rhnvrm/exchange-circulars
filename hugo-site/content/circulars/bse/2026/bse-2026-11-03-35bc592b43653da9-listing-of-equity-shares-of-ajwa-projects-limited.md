## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, November 4, 2026, the equity shares of Ajwa Projects Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME platform under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ajwa Projects Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545016
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** AJWA
- **ISIN:** INE1NMO01017
- **Market Lot:** 600 Equity Shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 203/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 193/-)
- **Total Securities:** 5,500,600 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** November 2, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- **Registrar:** KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; standard listing guidelines and trade-for-trade surveillance measures for SME IPOs apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form in minimum market lots of 600 shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.
- Lock-in period has been marked in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 2, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Ajwa Projects Limited shares on the BSE SME exchange, providing liquidity to investors while implementing initial trade-for-trade surveillance controls.
