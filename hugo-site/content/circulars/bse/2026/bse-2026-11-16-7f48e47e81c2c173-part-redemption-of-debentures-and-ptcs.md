## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that several companies and issuers have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs), PTCs, and bonds. Trading in these securities will take place with reduced face values starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261116-53 (Date: 16 Nov 2026).
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Covers multiple issuances including Sansar Aug 2024 Trust, Moneyboxx Finance Limited, Mufin Green Finance Limited, Indigo series PTCs, ECL Finance Limited, and Vistaar Financial Services Pvt Ltd.
- Record and Ex-Dates fall on November 18, 2026, and November 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory policy changes; standard operational notice for debt segment corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure settlements reflect the correct parameters for the respective record dates.

## Important Dates

- **18/11/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Sansar Aug 2024 Trust, Moneyboxx Finance, Mufin Green Finance, and Indigo 048, 050, 051, 052.
- **19/11/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for ECL Finance, Vistaar Financial Services, and Indigo 044.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine impact limited to debt segment participants holding or trading the specified NCDs and PTCs with adjusted face values.
