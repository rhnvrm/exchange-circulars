## Summary

BSE has announced the non-competitive bidding schedule and details for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for November 03, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection for direct investors starts on November 02, 2026 at 10:00 am and ends on November 02, 2026 at 5:00 pm.
- Bid collection for members starts on November 02, 2026 at 10:00 am (24 hours availability) and ends on November 03, 2026 at 8:00 am.
- Auction date is November 03, 2026, and settlement date is November 04, 2026.
- Covers multiple State Government Securities (re-issues and new issuances) across states such as Andhra Pradesh, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Telangana, and West Bengal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates non-competitive participation in State Government Securities auctions conducted by RBI pursuant to RBI press release and Exchange circular no. 20191122-28.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and eligible direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines via the NCB-GSec module of iBBS (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: November 02, 2026, 10:00 am
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): November 02, 2026, 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): November 03, 2026, 8:00 am
- Auction Date: November 03, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides retail and eligible non-competitive investors structured access to participate in State Government Securities auctions with specified minimum amounts and unit multiples.
