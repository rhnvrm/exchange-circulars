## Summary

Mr. Sanjay Natvarlal Mandavia and Ms. Rupal Sanjay Mandavia (Acquirers) have announced an open offer to acquire up to 3,70,47,634 fully paid-up equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each, representing 26.00% of the emerging voting share capital of ACI Infocom Limited (Target Company). The offer price is Rs. 1.53/- per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** ACI Infocom Limited (ACI INFO)
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Sanjay Natvarlal Mandavia and Ms. Rupal Sanjay Mandavia
- **Offer Size:** Up to 3,70,47,634 equity shares (26.00% of emerging voting share capital)
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 1.53/- per share in cash
- **Offer Period:** Monday, November 30, 2026, to Friday, December 11, 2026
- **Regulatory Basis:** Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

This open offer is conducted in accordance with SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeovers, Buy Back, and Delisting (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 09, 2016), along with relevant exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window mechanism for tender offers and ensure compliance with SEBI (SAST) Regulations and exchange guidelines during the offer period from November 30, 2026, to December 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders of ACI Infocom Limited have the opportunity to tender their equity shares during the acquisition window through stock exchange trading members in accordance with the specified tender offer guidelines.
