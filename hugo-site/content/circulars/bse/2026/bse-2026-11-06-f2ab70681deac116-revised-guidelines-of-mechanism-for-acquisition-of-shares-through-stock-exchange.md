## Summary

BSE has issued revised operational guidelines and instructions for the acquisition of shares through the Stock Exchange pursuant to tender-offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delistings, incorporating the Offer to Buy (OTB) mechanism under the Interoperability framework amongst Clearing Corporations.

## Key Points

- The Offer To Buy (OTB) mechanism is now under the Interoperability framework amongst Clearing Corporations.
- Necessary changes and developments have been made in the Exchange's OTB module.
- There are no changes to the existing Early Pay-in process by investors and custodians.
- Detailed operational instructions and guidelines are provided in Annexure A of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of the Offer to Buy (OTB) mechanism within the Interoperability framework amongst Clearing Corporations in accordance with previous SEBI circulars and exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members, Custodians, Acquirers, Promoters, and Companies must take note of the updated OTB module guidelines and coordinate with designated exchange and clearing corporation departments for operational queries.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective: Immediate / ongoing as per circular instructions.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency for tender-offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delistings through seamless interoperability across clearing corporations, with no disruption to the existing early pay-in process.
