## Summary

BSE has announced that specified securities will move into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework with effect from November 11, 2026, triggering strict surveillance and trading restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities specified in the Annexure move to higher GSM stages starting November 11, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value.
- Stages III & IV: Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/first trading day), and 100% ASD deposited by buyers (with no upward price movement for Stage IV).
- All settlements are conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance actions under the BSE GSM framework to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility in vulnerable securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precautions while trading in these securities and account for trade-to-trade settlement constraints and high margin/ASD requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on liquidity and trading activity for affected securities due to mandatory trade-for-trade settlement, restricted trading frequency in higher stages, and substantial cash lock-in via 100% margins and ASD.
