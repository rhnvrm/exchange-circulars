## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 4 companies: Fabtech Technologies Ltd, Pharmaids Pharmaceuticals Ltd, Sigachi Industries Ltd, and Simmonds Marshall Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 14 companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Covance Softsol Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Geetanjali Credit and Capital Ltd, HBL Engineering Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Sangam Finserv Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Vedant Asset Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements as requested by the exchange under surveillance guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to market participants regarding ongoing surveillance inquiries and company responses concerning abnormal market activity.
