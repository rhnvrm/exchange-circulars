## Summary

BSE has announced the release of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of Jamnagar Agro Industries Limited, filed on November 05, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public review and comments in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Jamnagar Agro Industries Limited
- Filing Date: November 05, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. The notice is a standard procedural hosting of a draft offer document under SEBI (ICDR) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public and stakeholders are invited to submit comments, if any, on the draft offer document within 21 days from the date of notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates transparency and stakeholder feedback for the proposed SME IPO of Jamnagar Agro Industries Limited.
