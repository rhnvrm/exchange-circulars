## Summary

BSE has announced that the upgraded trading platform will be made available in the Simulation Environment for the Currency Derivatives Segment effective October 30, 2026, following previous circulars regarding exchange trading platform upgrades.

## Key Points

- Upgraded trading platform available in Simulation Environment for Currency Derivatives Segment starting October 30, 2026.
- Connection parameters for connectivity to the upgraded simulation environment are provided in the circular's Annexure.
- Trading members can use existing simulation user credentials to connect and execute testing activities.
- Members are advised to undertake necessary testing to ensure a seamless transition.

## Regulatory Changes

- Infrastructure upgrade of the Exchange trading platform aimed at strengthening market infrastructure and enhancing operational efficiency for market participants.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating in the Currency Derivatives segment must conduct testing in the simulation environment using the provided connection parameters.

## Important Dates

- October 29, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- October 30, 2026: Upgraded platform made available in Simulation Environment for Currency Derivatives Segment.
- September 25, 2026: Reference circular date (20260925-31).

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate operational impact on brokers and trading members dealing in currency derivatives, requiring API integration validation and testing in the simulation environment prior to live deployment.
