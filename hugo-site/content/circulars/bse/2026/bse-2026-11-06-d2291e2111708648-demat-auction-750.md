## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 750 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-750/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry is scheduled on Friday, November 06, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching will take place from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for Monday, November 09, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard auction procedure notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- Auction Offer Entry: November 06, 2026 (02:00 PM - 02:45 PM)
- Auction Matching: November 06, 2026 (02:45 PM - 03:00 PM)
- Delivery Statement and Report: November 06, 2026 (03:00 PM - 03:30 PM)
- Money Statement & Final Reports: November 06, 2026 (03:35 PM - 05:30 PM)
- Auction Pay-in: November 09, 2026 (08:00 AM)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact for trading members participating in settlement auctions, ensuring timely fulfillment of pay-in obligations.
