## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Praxis Home Retail Limited has fixed November 18, 2026, as the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Praxis Home Retail Limited (Scrip Code: 540901)
- Record Date: November 18, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 18, 2026 (DR-759/2026-202)
- Rights Ratio: 5 equity shares for every 9 equity shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 5/- each for cash at par
- Payment Terms: Entire rights issue price of Rs. 5/- per share is payable on application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ex-rights date for executing transactions in the company's equity shares on an ex-rights basis.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Record Date: November 18, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of Praxis Home Retail Limited as of the record date are eligible for the rights entitlement in the ratio of 5:9 at Rs. 5 per share, impacting equity capital structure and liquidity.
