## Summary

BSE has issued a notice following SEBI's email dated November 17, 2026, directing compliance with the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated November 2, 2026. The order mandates appellants in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips to deposit full penalty and disgorgement amounts as a condition for interim relief.

## Key Points

- SAT Order Date: November 2, 2026.
- SEBI Directive Date: November 17, 2026.
- Appellants involved: Arvind Shantilal Shah, Rupal Bhavin Shah, Bharati Arvind Shah, Bhavin Arvind Shah, Yash Manish Mehta, and Ramnaresh Dashadeen Nirmal.
- Condition: Deposit of full penalty and full disgorgement amounts in fixed deposits with a lien marked in SEBI's favour.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of SAT interim order conditions directing monetary deposits by specified appellants prior to relief.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and SEBI directive regarding compliance for the named appellants and scrips.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: November 02, 2026
- Notice / Directive Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures adherence to tribunal and regulatory orders concerning financial penalties and disgorgement in specific securities cases.
