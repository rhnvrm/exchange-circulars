## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of RDB Infrastructure and Power Limited (Scrip Code - 533285) with effect from November 17, 2026. The shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** RDB Infrastructure and Power Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 533285
- **ISIN:** INE245L01028
- **Security Details:** 1,36,50,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 39.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 40.50/- per share).
- **Allottees:** Promoters and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 210009001 to 223659000
- **Date of Allotment:** 27/05/2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 94,50,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 210009001 - 219459000) locked-in up to 31/05/2028.
  - 42,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 219459001 - 223659000) locked-in up to 31/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential share allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities and their respective lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 27, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** May 31, 2027 and May 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of RDB Infrastructure and Power Limited through warrant conversion. The shares are subject to statutory lock-in periods, ensuring promoter/investor commitment.
