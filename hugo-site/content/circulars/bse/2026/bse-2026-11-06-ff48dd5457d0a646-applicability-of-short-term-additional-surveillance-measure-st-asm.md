## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities meeting specific surveillance criteria, effective November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%).
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II will attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 8, 2026, and new positions created from November 9, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework parameters for identified securities, enforcing enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open positions and new positions in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 9, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 8, 2026, and new positions thereafter).

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for traders holding positions in scrips identified under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those specific securities.
