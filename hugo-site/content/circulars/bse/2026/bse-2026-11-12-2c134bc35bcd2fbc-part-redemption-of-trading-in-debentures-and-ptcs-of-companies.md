## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in these securities will proceed with reduced face values effective from November 16, 2026, and November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Involves multiple issuers including Loanx Velvet Ash, Andhra Pradesh State Beverages Corporation Limited, Dhruva XXI 2023, Dhruva XXII, Regency Fincorp Limited, Si Creva Capital Services Private Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, and Laxmi India Finance Limited.
- Record and ex-dates are scheduled for November 16, 2026, and November 17, 2026.
- Trading continues with updated reduced face values per debenture/PTC as applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of corporate actions reflecting debt redemption and face value reduction in the debt trading segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must account for the revised face values when executing trades in the specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: November 16, 2026, and November 17, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Bondholders and investors receive scheduled part redemption proceeds, with secondary market trading adjusting to the reduced face value.
