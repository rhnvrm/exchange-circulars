## Summary

Indus Finance Limited has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 5:9 (5 bonus equity shares of Rs. 10/- each for every 9 existing equity shares held). The record date has been fixed as December 1, 2026, with trading on an ex-bonus basis commencing from the same date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Indus Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 531841
- **Bonus Ratio:** 5:9 (5 bonus shares for every 9 shares held)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Record Date:** December 1, 2026
- **Ex-Bonus Date:** December 1, 2026
- **Expected Allotment:** 5,143,500 Equity Shares to be allotted on or around December 2, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action notification in compliance with SEBI listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ex-bonus trading date to ensure proper settlement of trades in Indus Finance Limited.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Record Date:** December 1, 2026
- **Ex-Bonus Date:** December 1, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** December 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive additional equity shares in proportion to their holding, resulting in a price adjustment on the ex-bonus date and increased liquidity for the scrip.
