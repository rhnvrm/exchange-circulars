## Summary

BSE has announced that pursuant to Notice No. 20261016-36 dated October 16, 2026, the Samco Equity Savings Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopening for ongoing transactions effective November 11, 2026.
- Covers both Regular and Direct plans (Growth option).
- Available on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Scheme Details

- **Scheme Name 1:** SAMCO EQUITY SAVINGS FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH | ISIN: INF0K1H01347
- **Scheme Name 2:** SAMCO EQUITY SAVINGS FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH | ISIN: INF0K1H01339

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions, systematic investment plans (SIP), systematic transfer plans (STP), and systematic withdrawal plans (SWP) for Samco Equity Savings Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
