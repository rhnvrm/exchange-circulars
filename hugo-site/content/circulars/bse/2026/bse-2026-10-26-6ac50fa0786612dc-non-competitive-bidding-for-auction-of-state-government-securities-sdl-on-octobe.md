## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding process for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for October 27, 2026, pursuant to the RBI press release. Trading members can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Auction Date: October 27, 2026.
- Settlement Date: October 29, 2026.
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): October 26, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 27, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Covers multiple SDL securities across various states (e.g., Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Kerala, Manipur, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand).

## Regulatory Changes

- Enables eligible investors and trading members to participate in RBI-conducted State Government Securities auctions through the Exchange's NCB-GSec module.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module at https://ibbs.bseindia.com adhering to prescribed timelines, minimum subscription sizes, unit multiples, and maximum amounts.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: October 24, 2026 (10:00 AM onwards)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): October 26, 2026 (till 5:00 PM)
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 27, 2026 (till 8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: October 27, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates organized primary market participation in state-level debt instruments with clear operational timelines and contact support desks for trading operations and settlements.
