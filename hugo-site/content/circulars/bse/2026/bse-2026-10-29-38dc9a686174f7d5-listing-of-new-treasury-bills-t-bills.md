## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS, effective from Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers three tranches of Treasury Bills: 91-day (Scrip code 805283), 182-day (Scrip code 805284), and 364-day (Scrip code 805285).
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is 1.
- Securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective maturity/redemption dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine debt instrument listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of trading restrictions prior to maturity dates.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Listing Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** January 28, 2027 (91-day), April 29, 2027 (182-day), and October 28, 2027 (364-day).

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine market impact, expanding investment and liquidity options in government debt instruments.
