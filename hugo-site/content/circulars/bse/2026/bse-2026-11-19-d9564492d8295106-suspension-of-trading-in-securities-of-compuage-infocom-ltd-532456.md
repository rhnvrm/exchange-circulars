## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the equity shares of Compuage Infocom Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532456) effective November 20, 2026. This action follows the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench order dated November 18, 2026, which admitted the company into liquidation and appointed Mr. Ashok Mittal as Liquidator.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Compuage Infocom Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 532456
- **Regulatory Authority:** National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench
- **Liquidator Appointed:** Mr. Ashok Mittal
- **Order Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Initiation of liquidation proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the suspension of trading in equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Order Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of Compuage Infocom Ltd. is halted indefinitely due to liquidation proceedings, directly impacting investors and market participants holding the securities.
