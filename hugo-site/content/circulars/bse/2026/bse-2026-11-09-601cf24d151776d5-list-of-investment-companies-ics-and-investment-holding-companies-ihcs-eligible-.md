## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of several Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) from the trade-for-trade segment to their respective groups, effective on various dates in November 2026, in continuation of Notice No. 20261015-34.

## Key Points

- Companies transferred from trade-for-trade segment with effective dates:
  - Kama Holdings Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532468) -> Group B, effective 17.11.2026
  - TVS Holdings Ltd. (Scrip Code: 520056 / 120056) -> Group A, effective 17.11.2026
  - Maharashtra Scooters Ltd. (Scrip Code: 500266 / 100266) -> Group A, effective 17.11.2026
  - Nalwa Sons Investment Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532256) -> Group B, effective 18.11.2026
  - Pilani Investment and Industries Corporation Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539883) -> Group B, effective 18.11.2026
  - GFL Ltd. (Scrip Code: 500173) -> Group B, effective 18.11.2026
  - SIL Investments Ltd. (Scrip Code: 521194) -> Group B, effective 20.11.2026
  - Kalyani Investment Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 533302) -> Group B, effective 25.11.2026
- Companies continuing in the Trade for Trade segment and under call auction till further notice:
  - Elcid Investments Ltd. (Scrip Code: 503681) -> Group T
  - Haryana Capfin Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532855) -> Group XT
  - Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (Scrip Code: 519224) -> Group T

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of eligible Investment Companies and Investment Holding Companies from the trade-for-trade surveillance segment to regular trading groups (A and B), while specific scrips continue in trade-for-trade and call auction mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the group changes and effective dates for executing trades accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Dates for Group Changes: November 17, 2026 to November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves liquidity and normalizes trading for eligible investment and holding companies moving out of trade-for-trade, while maintaining strict monitoring for those remaining in trade-for-trade/call auction.
