## Summary

Effective from Friday, November 6, 2026, the equity shares of Bright Outdoor Media Limited, previously listed on the BSE SME Platform, are migrated and admitted to dealings on the BSE Mainboard Platform under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bright Outdoor Media Limited
- Scrip Code: 543831
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: BRIGHTOUTD
- ISIN: INE0OMI01019
- Total Securities: 21,823,166 Equity shares of Rs. 10/-
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Registered Office: 801, 8th Floor, Crescent Tower, Near Morya House, Fame Infinity Mall, Off New Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai - 400053

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company are transitioned from the SME segment to the Mainboard platform in accordance with applicable exchange and SEBI framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form with a unit market lot of one share.
- Audited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and shareholding pattern as on September 30, 2026, are provided via Annexure I and Annexure II.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Migration Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Migration to the Mainboard platform enhances visibility, liquidity, and accessibility for a broader investor base while subjecting the company to mainstream listing requirements.
