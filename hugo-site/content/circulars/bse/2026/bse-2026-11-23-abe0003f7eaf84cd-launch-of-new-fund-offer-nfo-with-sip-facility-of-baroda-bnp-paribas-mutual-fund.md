## Summary

Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the **Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund** along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window opens on November 25, 2026, and closes on December 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund (Regular and Direct plans available in Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Window:** November 25, 2026, to December 08, 2026 (Extended till midnight on December 08, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 14, 2026.
- **SIP Registrations:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Operational notice regarding NFO launch and guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 14, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** November 25, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** December 08, 2026 (Midnight for Lumpsum; SIP/XSIP authentication by 3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 14, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in/Clear Funds Deadline:** December 14, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new hybrid fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
