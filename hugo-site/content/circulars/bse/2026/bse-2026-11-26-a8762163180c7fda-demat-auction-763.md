## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-763/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry session timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 26, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction pay-in will be held on Friday, November 27, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine market operations notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 26, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operations affecting trading members involved in short deliveries for Rolling Settlement DR-763/2026-27.
