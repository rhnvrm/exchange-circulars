## Summary

BSE has announced a change in the trading symbol for Bright Outdoor Media Limited (Scrip Code: 543831, ISIN: INE0OMI01019) from BRIGHT to BRIGHTOUTD, effective from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bright Outdoor Media Limited
- Scrip Code: 543831
- ISIN: INE0OMI01019
- Existing Symbol: BRIGHT
- New Symbol: BRIGHTOUTD
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative symbol change.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants must update their trading terminals and systems with the new symbol for order placement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on trading operations, limited to updating trading symbol references in systems.
