## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Novartis India Ltd has changed its name to Evonile Pharma Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Novartis India Ltd
- New Company Name: Evonile Pharma Limited
- Scrip Code: 500672
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus System): EVONILE
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus System): EVONILE
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the name change and ensure that their systems and records are updated with the new scrip ID and abbreviated name.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard administrative corporate action with minimal direct market impact, affecting only trading identifiers and records for the company.
