## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that BN Agrochem Ltd (Scrip Code: 526125) has fixed November 16, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of its existing equity shares. Each existing equity share with a face value of Rs. 10/- will be sub-divided into ten equity shares with a face value of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** BN Agrochem Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 526125
- **Sub-Division Ratio:** 1 equity share of Rs. 10/- into 10 equity shares of Re. 1/-
- **Record Date:** November 16, 2026
- **ISIN Status:** Existing ISIN INE00HZ01011 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on or after November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Change in the face value and equity share structure of BN Agrochem Ltd.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure transactions on and after November 16, 2026, comply with the new share structure and upcoming ISIN notification.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders will receive ten shares for every one share held as of the record date.
- Market participants must account for the temporary suspension/change of ISIN pending the notification of the new ISIN by the Exchange.
