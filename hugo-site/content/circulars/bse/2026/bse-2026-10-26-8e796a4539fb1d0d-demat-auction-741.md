## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 741 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-741/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 26, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 26, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 26, 2026.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 26, 2026.
- Auction pay-in scheduled for Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they had short delivered is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; operates under established uniform membership and auction frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 26, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process ensuring settlement of short deliveries in the rolling settlement cycle.
