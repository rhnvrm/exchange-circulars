## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure will transition to higher GSM stages starting November 25, 2026.
- Surveillance measures applied depend on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I through IV).
- Settlement for affected securities will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of heightened surveillance measures under the GSM framework established on February 27, 2017.
- Stage-specific actions include 100% margin rates, 5% or lower price bands, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% or 100% of trade value, and trading frequency restricted to once a week for Stages III and IV.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise caution when trading in these securities due to mandatory trade-to-trade settlement and prohibition of netting off.
- Buyers must deposit the applicable Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) as required by the security's GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Stricter margin requirements and trading restrictions increase liquidity constraints and friction for securities entering higher GSM stages.
