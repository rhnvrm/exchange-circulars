## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the admission of new trading members and additional segment approvals effective 23rd October 2026.

## Key Points

- Tradomate Broking Private Limited admitted as Trading Member for Cash and Equity Derivatives segments (Member No. 3315).
- Stockology Securities Private Limited admitted for Cash, Equity Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments (Member No. 3316).
- Capri Global Securities Private Limited admitted for Cash and Debt segments (Member No. 3317).
- Sunrise Gilts & Securities Pvt. Ltd. admitted for an additional segment in Commodity Derivatives and EGR (Member No. 4071).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must operate in compliance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Admission Date: 23 October 2026
- Notice Date: 26 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine membership update with no direct market impact.
