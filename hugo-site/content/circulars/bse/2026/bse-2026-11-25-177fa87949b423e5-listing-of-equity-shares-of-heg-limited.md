## Summary

Effective from November 27, 2026, the equity shares of HEG Limited (formerly known as HEG Graphite Limited) are listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the 'T' Group of Securities. The listing follows a Composite Scheme of Arrangement approved by the NCLT Indore Bench on August 13, 2026, involving the demerger of the Graphite Business of HEG Advanced Materials Limited into HEG Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 544896
- **Scrip ID:** HEGRAPHITE
- **ISIN:** INE0Y9J01020
- **Face Value & Paid-up:** Rs. 2/- each fully paid-up
- **Total Securities Listed:** 19,29,77,530 Equity Shares
- **Trading Segment:** T Group (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- **Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS):** Applicable for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shares are admitted under the 'T' Group and placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for the initial 10 trading days.
- Participants should adhere to SPOS guidelines and reference Exchange Notice no. 20120216-29 for operational details.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Approval Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Appointed Date:** April 1, 2024
- **Effective Date (Scheme):** September 1, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 11, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of HEG Advanced Materials Limited receive equity shares of HEG Limited pursuant to the demerger of the graphite business.
- Trading will be subject to Trade-for-Trade settlement for the first 10 trading days, ensuring orderly price discovery during the initial listing phase.
