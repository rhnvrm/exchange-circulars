## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about updates to the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, involving the removal of 03 entries and amendment of 03 entries.

## Key Points

- SEBI communicated notifications regarding updates to the UNSC Sanctions Committee List.
- The update covers the removal of 03 entries and the amendment of 03 entries on the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Members are required to scan existing and future accounts to ensure compliance with asset freezing and sanctions obligations.
- De-listing requests or clarifications must be forwarded to the designated authorities.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the UNSC Sanctions List (1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida) as communicated by SEBI pursuant to Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no account is held by or linked to entities/individuals in the updated list.
- Follow procedures outlined in the Central Government Order dated February 2, 2021.
- Forward any de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA (jsctcr-mha@gov.in) with a copy to MEA Joint Secretary (UNP) (jsunp@mea.gov.in).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must maintain robust AML/CFT screening mechanisms to instantly incorporate UNSC sanctions updates, ensuring strict adherence to UAPA provisions and avoiding regulatory penalties.
