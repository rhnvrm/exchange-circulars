## Summary

Trading members are informed that Kabra Drugs Ltd has changed its name to Aanjaay Industries Limited. The changes in Scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Kabra Drugs Ltd
- New Company Name: Aanjaay Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 524322
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: AANJAAY
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: AANJAAY
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading symbols and system identifiers updated on the exchange platform to reflect the new corporate name.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviations for orders placed on or after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal market impact, limited to system display names and identifiers for trading members.
