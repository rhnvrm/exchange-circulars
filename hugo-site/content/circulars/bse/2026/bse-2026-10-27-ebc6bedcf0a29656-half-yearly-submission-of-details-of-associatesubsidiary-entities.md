## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the half-yearly submission of details pertaining to Associate/Subsidiary Entities under the Risk Based Supervision (RBS) framework. Based on consultations with SEBI and representations from the Brokers Industry Standard Forum (ISF), certain RBS details will now be captured through a separate standalone submission. Trading members are required to submit these details for the half-year ended March 31, 2026, on or before November 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory half-yearly submission of Associate/Subsidiary entity details for trading members.
- Reporting period: Half-year ended March 31, 2026.
- Submission Deadline: November 15, 2026.
- Online submission via BEFS Portal (Path: BEFS Portal → Capturing Member Details → Member Details → Associate/Group Company details → Associate/Subsidiary/Group Company).
- Subsequent changes (additions, deletions, or modifications) must be notified to the Exchange within 30 calendar days of occurrence.
- Cross-exchange streamlining: BSE trading members who are also registered with NSE are required to submit associate/subsidiary details to NSE only. Exclusive BSE members will use the BSE BEFS portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of specific Risk Based Supervision (RBS) disclosures concerning associate and subsidiary entities into a dedicated half-yearly submission process.
- Implementation of technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among exchanges to avoid duplicate filings.

## Compliance Requirements

- All eligible trading members must submit associate and subsidiary entity details through the designated BEFS portal path on or before November 15, 2026.
- Dual-registered members (BSE and NSE) must submit exclusively to NSE.
- Trading members must promptly notify the Exchange of any changes in associate/subsidiary details within 30 calendar days.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Submission Deadline (Half-year ended March 31, 2026): November 15, 2026
- Change Reporting Timeline: Within 30 calendar days of any modification

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures structured operational compliance and accurate mapping of member networks under the SEBI Risk Based Supervision framework.
