## Summary

BSE has announced the shortlisting of securities under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II pursuant to the review of market surveillance criteria. Applicable surveillance actions including enhanced margin rates will come into effect from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin provisioning (50% or 100%) for open positions as of November 27, 2026, and all new positions created from November 30, 2026, in securities listed in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Reference Date for Open Positions: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for impacted scrips may lead to higher capital commitments for traders and potential volatility adjustments in the shortlisted securities.
