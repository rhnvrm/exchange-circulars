## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 08, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and settlement restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher stages of GSM.
- Effective date of implementation is November 08, 2026.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed for affected securities.
- Specific surveillance actions apply based on the GSM stage (Stage I to IV).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework as per previous notices (February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates), moving specified securities into higher surveillance tiers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the specified securities.
- Ensure compliance with applicable margin rates, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) requirements, and trading frequency restrictions (e.g., trading permitted once a week for Stages III and IV).
- No netting off allowed as settlement is on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to higher margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement rules, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
