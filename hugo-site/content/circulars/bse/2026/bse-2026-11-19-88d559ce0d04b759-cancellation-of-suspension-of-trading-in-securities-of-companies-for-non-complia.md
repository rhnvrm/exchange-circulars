## Summary

BSE has announced the cancellation of the suspension of trading in securities for CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217), superseding the earlier notice regarding non-compliance with Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 for the consecutive quarters of March 2026 and June 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading suspension for CMX Holdings Ltd w.e.f. November 23, 2026 has been cancelled.
- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261021-17 dated October 21, 2026 is modified.
- All other provisions of the aforesaid notice remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an administrative reversal of a planned trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must ensure continuous compliance with Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 to avoid penal actions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026 (Suspension not proceeding).
- **Previous Notice Date:** October 21, 2026 (Notice No. 20261021-17).
- **Notice Date:** November 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct positive impact for CMX Holdings Ltd and its investors as trading in its securities will continue uninterrupted without suspension.
- Minimal broader market impact.
