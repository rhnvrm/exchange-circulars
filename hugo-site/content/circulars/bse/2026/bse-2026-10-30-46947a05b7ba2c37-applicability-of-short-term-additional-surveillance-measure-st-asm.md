## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities satisfying the criteria. The measures include enhanced margin requirements effective from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher).
- Applicable w.e.f. November 03, 2026 on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created from November 03, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions with enhanced margin caps for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on open positions as of November 02, 2026, and all new positions created from November 03, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Applicable on open positions as of:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I or II will face higher capital/margin blockages, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
