## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for November 18, 2026, following the RBI press release dated November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills available for auction: 91 Day T-Bill (Rs. 8,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (Rs. 8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (Rs. 7,000 Cr).
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based platform.
- Bid Collection Start Date: November 16, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date for Direct Investors: November 17, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid Collection End Date for Members: November 18, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Auction Date: November 18, 2026.
- Settlement Date: November 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors participating in the non-competitive auction must ensure bid submissions are completed within the designated timelines via the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS web-based system.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start:** November 16, 2026, 10:00 AM
- **Direct Investors Bid End:** November 17, 2026, 5:00 PM
- **Members Bid End:** November 18, 2026, 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for members and direct retail/institutional investors to participate in Central Government borrowing programs through non-competitive bidding on the exchange infrastructure.
