## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing of new securities of Lloyds Engineering Works Limited (Scrip Code: 539992) effective from Thursday, November 26, 2026. A total of 12,36,546 equity shares of Re. 1/- each have been listed pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lloyds Engineering Works Limited
- Scrip Code: 539992
- ISIN: INE093R01011
- Total New Securities: 12,36,546 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- Purpose: Conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 6,05,674 shares (1471003834 to 1471609507) and 6,30,872 shares (1471609508 to 1472240379)
- Ranking: Pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding security listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update allowing trading in the converted fully paid-up equity shares without significant market disruption.
