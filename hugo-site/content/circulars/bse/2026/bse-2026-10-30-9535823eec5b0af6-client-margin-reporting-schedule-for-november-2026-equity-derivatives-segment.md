## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the schedule for client margin reporting and associated penalty dates for the Equity Derivatives Segment for November 2026. Trading members are required to comply with the stipulated reporting deadlines to avoid penalties.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for November 2026.
- Covers trade dates starting from November 2, 2026, through November 30, 2026.
- Applies to all trading members operating in the Equity Derivatives Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes timeline and deadlines for margin reporting and penalty enforcement for the month of November 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure accurate and timely client margin reporting by the specified reporting deadlines to prevent regulatory penalties.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30-10-2026
- Schedule Period: November 02, 2026 to November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity and predictable timelines for risk management and margin reporting in the derivatives segment.
