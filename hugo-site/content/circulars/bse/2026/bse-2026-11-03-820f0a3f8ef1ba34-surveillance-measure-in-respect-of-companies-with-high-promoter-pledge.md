## Summary

BSE has announced surveillance measures regarding companies with high promoter pledge, detailing securities entering the framework (attracting a minimum 35% margin in equity and equity derivatives segments w.e.f. November 6, 2026), securities moving out (effective November 4, 2026), and a consolidated list of securities.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective November 6, 2026, on all open positions as on November 5, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective November 4, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Representations regarding inclusion must be submitted by 5:00 PM on November 4, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of a minimum 35% margin on securities with high promoter pledge under market surveillance measures.
- Measures operate in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the minimum 35% margin is maintained for applicable securities in equity and equity derivatives.
- Companies/entities seeking representations must contact the Exchange by November 4, 2026, 5:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- November 4, 2026: Effective date for securities moving out of the framework and deadline for company representations (5:00 PM).
- November 5, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to the margin.
- November 6, 2026: Effective date for minimum 35% margin on applicable securities and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities with high promoter pledge will experience increased margin requirements (minimum 35%) starting November 6, 2026, affecting trading positions and capital allocation.
