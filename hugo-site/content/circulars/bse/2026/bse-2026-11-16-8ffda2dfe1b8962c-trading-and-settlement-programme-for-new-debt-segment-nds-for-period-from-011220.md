## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the New Debt Segment (NDS) covering the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The schedule outlines settlement numbers, trading dates, entry dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers 22 settlement cycles (NDS-163/26-27 to NDS-184/26-27) for December 2026.
- Specifies timelines for regular trading, entry, pay-in/pay-out, and auctions.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 11:00 a.m. (members to submit instructions by 10:50 a.m.).
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:30 a.m. (members to submit instructions by 9:20 a.m.).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must adhere to the specified timelines for fund and securities pay-in and auction participation during December 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start:** December 1, 2026
- **Period End:** December 31, 2026
- **First Settlement (NDS-163):** December 1, 2026 (Trading) / December 2, 2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out)
- **Final Settlement (NDS-184):** December 31, 2026 (Trading) / January 1, 2027 (Pay-in/Pay-out)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement processing for trading members operating in the New Debt Segment (NDS) on BSE/ICCL.
