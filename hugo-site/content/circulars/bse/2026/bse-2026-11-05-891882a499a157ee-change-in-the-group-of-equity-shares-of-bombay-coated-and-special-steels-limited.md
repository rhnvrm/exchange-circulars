## Summary

In continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20261104-40 dated November 04, 2026, trading members are informed that the equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group) effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited
- Scrip Code: 545018
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment / B Group
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moving out of the Trade for Trade segment into regular rolling settlement under Group B.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems and take note of the revised settlement category effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the equity shares will no longer be restricted to gross delivery without intraday netting (Trade for Trade), facilitating normal market operations and potentially improving trading liquidity.
