## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility for NIPPON INDIA CRISIL - IBX SDL - JUNE 2037 INDEX FUND on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period is scheduled from November 18, 2026, to November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** NIPPON INDIA CRISIL - IBX SDL - JUNE 2037 INDEX FUND
- **Plans & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF204KC1HZ6)
  - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF204KC1IA7)
- **Scheme Category:** Debt
- **NFO Window:** November 18, 2026 to November 26, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 26, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational circular regarding a new mutual fund scheme launch.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 27, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (November 26, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** November 18, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** 6:00 PM on November 27, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors access to subscribe to the new Nippon India debt index fund offering on the BSE StAR MF platform with clearly defined operational timelines and fund clearance procedures.
