## Summary

BSE has announced the release of BOLT Pro TWS Version 13.80, which becomes compulsory for trading members across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments starting November 16, 2026. This version integrates cautionary pop-up alert messages for ETF order entry.

## Key Points

- **Compulsory Release**: Version 13.80 is mandatory across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- **Effective Date**: Goes live on Monday, November 16, 2026.
- **ETF Cautionary Messages**: Incorporates cautionary pop-up alert messages during order entry in the Equity segment for ETFs, as referenced in notice no. 20261016-49.
- **Download Availability**: Available for download from Friday, November 13, 2026 (7:00 PM onwards) via Auto-Download Interface or BSE help website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of mandatory cautionary pop-up alerts on trading terminals for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) during order placement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using Leased Line Connections or other setups must upgrade their trading workstations to BOLT Pro TWS Version 13.80 prior to market opening on November 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Download Start Date**: November 13, 2026 (7:00 PM onwards)
- **Effective / Go-Live Date**: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance with risk management alerts on ETF orders and maintains standardized trading terminal operations across all segments.
