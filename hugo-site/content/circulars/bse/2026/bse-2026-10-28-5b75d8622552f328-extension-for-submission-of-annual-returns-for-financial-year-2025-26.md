## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has extended the due date for the submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26 to December 31, 2026. This extension is aligned with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) Circular Ref. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026, which extended the due date for filing Financial Statements with the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

## Key Points

- Due date for submitting Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 extended to December 31, 2026.
- Clearing/Self Clearing Members registered with NSE must submit their Annual Return to NSE only under the Samuhik Prativedan Manch framework.
- Professional Clearing Members registered with BSECL shall submit Annual Returns through the BSECL portal (ICCL - IEFS).
- Non-submission within the extended due date or networth shortfalls will attract actions stipulated in BSECL Notice No. 20261008-15.

## Regulatory Changes

- MCA Circular Ref. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026, relaxed additional fees and extended filing timelines for Financial Statements and Annual Returns under the Companies Act, 2013 to December 31, 2026.
- BSECL mirrored this timeline extension for its members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit their Annual Returns for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, by December 31, 2026.
- Technical issues during BSECL portal uploads must be reported to bsecl.membership@bseclearing.com with the subject line 'Annual Return for the Financial Year 2025-26 – Issues in Submission - <Name of the Clearing member and Member code>'.

## Important Dates

- October 28, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- December 31, 2026: Extended due date for submission of Annual Returns for FY 2025-26.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory relief to clearing members by aligning exchange compliance deadlines with ROC/MCA extensions, avoiding immediate penalty actions.
