## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 751 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-751/2026-27. Trading members can submit auction offers on November 9, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offers accepted on trading terminals on Monday, November 9, 2026.
- Timings: Auction offer entry (02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.), Auction Matching (02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.), Delivery Statement (03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.), and Money Statement (03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.).
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Wednesday, November 11, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of Uniform Membership structure in Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding rolling settlement auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen".

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** November 9, 2026
- **Offer Entry Time:** 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- **Auction Matching Time:** 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- **Pay-in Date:** November 11, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process ensuring smooth clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities in the equity cash segment without broader market impact.
