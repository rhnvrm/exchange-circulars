## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 10,78,974 new equity shares of ASM Technologies Ltd. issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ASM Technologies Ltd. (Scrip Code: 526433)
- Number of Shares: 10,78,974 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
- Issue Price / Premium: Issued at a price of Rs. 4875/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 4865/-).
- Distribution & ISIN: Dist. Nos. 14614391 to 15693364; ISIN: INE867C01010.
- Lock-in Details: Shares are under lock-in up to May 20, 2027.
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A - Standard listing of securities issued on a preferential basis pursuant to exchange regulations.]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the listing, distribution numbers, and lock-in period applicable to the newly allotted preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 18, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 20, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The preferential allotment increases the total paid-up equity capital of ASM Technologies Ltd., expanding equity base and resulting in proportional equity dilution for existing shareholders, while strengthening the company's capital reserves through the share premium.
