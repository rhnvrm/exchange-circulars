## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund has announced the launch of Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth and IDCW Payout) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period is open from November 02, 2026, to November 04, 2026, with the tentative allotment date scheduled for November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Offerings**: Includes Regular and Direct plans for both Growth and IDCW Payout options under Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336.
- **NFO Window**: Open from November 02, 2026, to November 04, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on November 04, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: November 06, 2026.
- **Fund Type**: Debt Fund (ISINs: INF174KA1I05, INF174KA1I13, INF174KA1H89, INF174KA1H97).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 05, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date**: November 02, 2026
- **NFO End Date**: November 04, 2026 (6:00 PM for Lumpsum)
- **Clear Funds Deadline**: November 05, 2026 by 6:00 PM
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless subscription processing for investors participating in the Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
