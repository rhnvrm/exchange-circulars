## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification notice regarding news articles appearing in various media outlets concerning select Tata Group companies. Clarifications were sought to verify accuracy and inform investors, with replies received from Tata Chemicals Ltd and Tata Investment Corporation Ltd, while the reply from Tata Motors Ltd remains awaited.

## Key Points

- BSE surveillance sought clarification from companies regarding news articles in media, websites, or television channels.
- Reply received from Tata Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 500770).
- Reply received from Tata Investment Corporation Ltd (Scrip Code: 501301).
- Reply awaited from Tata Motors Ltd (Scrip Code: 544569).

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance verification procedure under stock exchange listing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to respond to exchange queries regarding media reports to ensure transparent dissemination of accurate information to investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market surveillance activity ensuring market transparency and verification of media reports without immediate broad market impact.
