## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of M. K. SONS FINE JEWELS LIMITED effective from Wednesday, November 4, 2026. A total of 5,62,90,760 equity shares will be traded under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: M. K. SONS FINE JEWELS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 545017
- Scrip ID / BOLT Symbol: MKJEWELS
- ISIN: INE2IXO01012
- Total Securities: 5,62,90,760 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 10/- each, fully paid up)
- Issue Price: Rs. 162/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 152/-)
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: 'B'
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; standard listing procedures and dematerialized trading guidelines apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Corporate action for marking the lock-in period in depository records has been successfully executed by the company.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 2, 2026
- Notice Date: November 3, 2026
- Effective Listing / Trading Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access for shareholders of M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited while expanding investment options on the BSE platform.
