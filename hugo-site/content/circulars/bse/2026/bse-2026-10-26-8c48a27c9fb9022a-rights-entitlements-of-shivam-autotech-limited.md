## Summary

BSE has announced the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for the renunciation of SHIVAM AUTOTECH LIMITED starting October 28, 2026. The rights issue opens on October 28, 2026, and closes on November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SHIVAM AUTOTECH LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751148
- Scrip ID: SHIVAM-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE637H20024
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: October 28, 2026 to November 13, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: October 28, 2026 to November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Rights Entitlements (REs) are permitted for trading on the Exchange under T+1 Rolling Settlement on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer stating that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: October 28, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: November 13, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: October 28, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or trading Rights Entitlements must execute transactions within the strict window under T+1 settlement and trade-for-trade rules, ensuring unexercised REs are renounced or subscribed before expiry.
