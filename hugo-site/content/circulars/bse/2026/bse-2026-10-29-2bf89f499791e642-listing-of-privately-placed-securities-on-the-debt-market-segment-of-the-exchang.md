## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers (CPs) issued on a private placement basis by multiple entities effective October 29, 2026. The issuances include companies such as Bajaj Finance Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, and DSP Finance Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities listed: Commercial Papers issued on a private placement basis.
- Trading format: Dematerialised form under designated ISINs with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples.
- Tick size: 1 paise.
- Issuing companies include Bajaj Finance Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, and DSP Finance Private Limited.
- Coupon/Interest rates range from 7.75% to 8.75% p.a. with varying maturity dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notice for privately placed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading requirements and standard denomination rules for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: October 28, 2026, and October 29, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 29, 2026
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: January 27, 2027; March 10, 2027; October 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing providing secondary market liquidity and trading access for the listed commercial papers.
