## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of PC Jeweller Limited with effect from November 25, 2026. A total of 1,18,52,500 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 17/- to promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PC Jeweller Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 534809
- **ISIN:** INE785M01021
- **Security Details:** 1,18,52,500 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 17/-
- **Issue Type:** Preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants to promoters
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9759542356 to 9771394855
- **Date of Allotment:** 23/09/2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; standard listing of securities issued via preferential allotment following warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the addition of the new securities to the trading segment and adhere to lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 23, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** May 30, 2028 (for 1,18,52,500 shares, dist. nos. 9759542356 - 9771394855)

## Impact Assessment

The expansion of equity capital through warrant conversion increases the total paid-up capital of PC Jeweller Limited and alters promoter equity stakes. Shares are subject to a lock-in period until May 30, 2028.
