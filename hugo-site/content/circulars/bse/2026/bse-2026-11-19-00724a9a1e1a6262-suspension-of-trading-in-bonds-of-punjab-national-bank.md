## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific bonds of Punjab National Bank (Scrip Code: 973638, ISIN: INE160A08209) effective from November 23, 2026, on account of the record date for payment of interest and redemption on exercise of call option.

## Key Points

- Company: Punjab National Bank
- Security Details: PNB-8.40%-PERPETUAL-PVT DR- 762/2026-2027 (ISIN: INE160A08209, Scrip Code: 973638)
- No Dealings Period: From November 23, 2026
- Record Date: November 24, 2026
- Purpose: Payment of Interest and Redemption on Exercise of Call Option

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified bonds of Punjab National Bank from the effective no dealings date.

## Important Dates

- No Dealings From: November 23, 2026
- Record Date: November 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in the specified bond series for corporate action processing (interest payment and call option redemption).
