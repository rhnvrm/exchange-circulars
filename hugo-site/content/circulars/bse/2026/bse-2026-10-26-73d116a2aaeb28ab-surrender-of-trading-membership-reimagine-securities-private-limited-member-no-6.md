## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has approved the surrender of trading membership for Reimagine Securities Private Limited (Member no. 6941), as announced in the notice dated October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Member Name: Reimagine Securities Private Limited
- Member Number: 6941
- Department: Membership Department
- Action: Surrender of Trading Membership approved by the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and concerned stakeholders are requested to take note of the surrender of membership.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact, as this is a routine administrative surrender of trading membership by a broker.
