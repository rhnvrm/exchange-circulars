## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for November 13, 2026. Trading members and eligible direct investors can participate through the NCB-GSec module on the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Security:** 6.94% GS 2036 (Symbol: 694GS36N26, Re-issue, Issue Size: Rs. 34,000 Cr).
- **Bidding Platform:** iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- **Bid Collection Start Date:** November 11, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** November 13, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** November 12, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- **Auction Date:** November 13, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 16, 2026.
- **Minimum Subscription:** Rs. 10,000 and in multiples thereof. Maximum amount: Rs. 2 Crores per security per RBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Participates in the RBI-announced Government of India dated securities auction under the non-competitive bidding facility (Reference: Exchange circular no. 20180423-42, dated April 23, 2018).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines via the NCB-GSec module on iBBS.
- Obligation and pay-in related queries to be directed to ICCL Operations (bse.csd@icclindia.com).

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** November 11, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- **Direct Investor Bid End Date:** November 12, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- **Member Bid End Date:** November 13, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates non-competitive participation in GoI dated securities auctions for retail and eligible institutional investors through BSE trading members.
