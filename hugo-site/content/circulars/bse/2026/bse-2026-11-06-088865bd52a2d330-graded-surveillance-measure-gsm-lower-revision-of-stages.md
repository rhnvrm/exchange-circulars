## Summary

BSE has announced a review of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Eligible securities will move to lower stages of the GSM framework effective November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Review conducted in coordination with other exchanges under the GSM framework established via notice no. 20170223-44 and updated via 20191129-23.
- Securities listed in the Annexure are eligible to move to lower surveillance stages.
- Effective date for the revision of stages is November 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of eligible securities to lower tiers/stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), easing specific trading restrictions associated with higher surveillance stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the revised list of securities under GSM and adjust trading parameters accordingly based on the Annexure.
- For clarifications, members can refer to the designated FAQ link or contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Securities moving to lower GSM stages experience relaxed trading restrictions, improving liquidity and trading access for investors holding or trading those scrips.
