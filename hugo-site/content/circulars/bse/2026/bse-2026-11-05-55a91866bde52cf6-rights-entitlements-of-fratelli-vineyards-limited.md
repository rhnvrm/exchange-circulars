## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Fratelli Vineyards Limited will be permitted on the Exchange with effect from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fratelli Vineyards Limited
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751151
- Scrip ID: FRATEL-RE
- ISIN No.: INE401Z20019
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Rights Issue Period: November 06, 2026 to November 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Authorization of Rights Entitlements for trading and renunciation on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- REs shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.
- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and clarify that they are not ordinary shares.
- Contract notes and bills for purchases of REs must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that REs only grant the right to participate in the rights issue or renounce before closure.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- RE Trading Start Date: November 06, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: November 12, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: November 06, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides shareholders and market participants the ability to trade or renounce rights entitlements within the specified window to participate in the company's capital raise.
