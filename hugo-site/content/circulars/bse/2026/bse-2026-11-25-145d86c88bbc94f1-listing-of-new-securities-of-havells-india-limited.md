## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 61,135 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued by Havells India Limited under its Employees Stock Purchase Scheme 2022 (ESPS 2022) are listed and permitted to trade effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 517354
- ISIN: INE176B01034
- Number of Shares: 61,135 equity shares
- Issue Type: Employees Stock Purchase Scheme 2022 (ESPS 2022)
- Distinctive Numbers: 627561653 to 627622787
- Date of Allotment: 26/10/2026
- Trading Effective Date: Thursday, November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of securities under employee benefit scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to a lock-in period of a minimum of 12 months from the date of credit into the respective employees' Demat Accounts.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 26, 2026
- Trading Commencement Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base under ESPS with standard lock-in restrictions, having negligible immediate impact on market liquidity.
