## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 06, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and take adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher GSM stages starting November 06, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD (with no upward movement for Stage IV).
- Settlement on trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as per established guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, noting that settlement is on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements, trading frequency limitations (for higher stages), and trade-to-trade settlement restrictions, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
