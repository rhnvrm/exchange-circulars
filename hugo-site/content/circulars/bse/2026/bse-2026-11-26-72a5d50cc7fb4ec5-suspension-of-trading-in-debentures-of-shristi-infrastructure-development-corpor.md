## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in debentures of Shristi Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd. effective from November 27, 2026, due to their upcoming maturity on November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 955319
- ISIN: INE472C07024
- Particulars: SIDC-10%-30-11-26-PVT
- Maturity Date: November 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures of the company with effect from November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: November 27, 2026
- Maturity Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational suspension for maturing debentures with no broader market impact.
