## Summary

SEBI, vide its order no. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-2/32866/2026-27 dated November 26, 2026, issued an ex-parte interim order in the matter of Le Lavoir Limited (LLL) and others, imposing market access and trading restrictions on several noticees.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 2 (Ashok Dilipkumar Jain) is restrained from accessing the securities market and prohibited from buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly.
- Noticee No. 2 is permitted to square off open derivative positions within 3 months and settle pre-existing pay-in/pay-out obligations.
- Noticees No. 3 to 15 are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in the scrip of Le Lavoir Limited (LLL) either directly or indirectly until further orders.
- The order covers Le Lavoir Limited (Noticee 1) alongside 14 other individual and corporate noticees.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action via SEBI interim directions restricting market access and trading in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the order and ensure strict compliance by preventing restricted entities and clients from executing prohibited transactions in the securities market and the scrip of Le Lavoir Limited.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026
- Derivative Square-off Deadline: Within 3 months from the date of the order

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors associated with the named noticees must immediately halt prohibited trading activities in Le Lavoir Limited and ensure compliance with market exclusion mandates.
