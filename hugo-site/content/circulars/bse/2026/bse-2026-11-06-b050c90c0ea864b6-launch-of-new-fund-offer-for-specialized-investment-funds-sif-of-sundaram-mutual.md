## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the extension of the allotment date for the New Fund Offer (NFO) of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) - SELECT EQUITY EX-TOP 100 LONG-SHORT FUND of Sundaram Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Key Points

- Allotment date extended from November 06, 2026, to November 09, 2026.
- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Payment-initiated transactions remain pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on November 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds are available in the ICCL account by the stipulated deadlines and note transaction cancellation rules for uninitiated funds.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 09, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: 6:00 PM on November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for members participating in the Sundaram Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
