## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 4,32,777 new equity shares of Sigma Advanced Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 532408, ISIN: INE933B01012) issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sigma Advanced Systems Limited
- Scrip Code: 532408
- ISIN: INE933B01012
- Number of Shares: 4,32,777 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 346.74/- per share (including premium of Rs. 336.74/-)
- Allotment Date: August 4, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 27, 2027 (Distinctive Nos. 189496176 to 189928952)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and trading permissions of the new securities effective from November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 30, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 27, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity base through preferential allotment of shares to non-promoters with standard lock-in compliance.
