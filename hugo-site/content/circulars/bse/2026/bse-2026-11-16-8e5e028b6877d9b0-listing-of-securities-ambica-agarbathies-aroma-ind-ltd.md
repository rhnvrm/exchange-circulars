## Summary

Ambica Agarbathies & Aroma Ind. Ltd. has announced the listing and permission to trade of 8,48,600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 15/- to promoters on a preferential basis, effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ambica Agarbathies & Aroma Ind. Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532335
- ISIN: INE792B01012
- Security Details: 8,48,600 equity shares issued at Rs. 25/- each (face value Rs. 10/- + premium Rs. 15/-) on a preferential basis to promoters.
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026.
- Date of Allotment: 25/08/2026.
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details:
  - 2,12,150 shares (Dist. Nos. 17177401 to 17389550): Lock-in up to 30/06/2028
  - 2,12,150 shares (Dist. Nos. 17389551 to 17601700): Lock-in up to 29/06/2028
  - 2,12,150 shares (Dist. Nos. 17601701 to 17813850): Lock-in up to 29/06/2028
  - 2,12,150 shares (Dist. Nos. 17813850 to 18026000): Lock-in up to 30/06/2028

## Regulatory Changes

Listing and trading permission granted under BSE operational guidelines for preferential allotment of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods for allotted shares as specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 17, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: June 29, 2028 and June 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increase in paid-up equity share capital of the company through preferential allotment to promoters, with specified lock-in restrictions ensuring compliance and stability.
