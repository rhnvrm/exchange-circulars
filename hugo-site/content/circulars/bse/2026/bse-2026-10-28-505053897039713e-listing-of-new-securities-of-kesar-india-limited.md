## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 12,85,713 new equity shares of KESAR INDIA LIMITED (Scrip Code: 543542) effective from October 29, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Kesar India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543542
- **ISIN:** INE0L1C01019
- **Securities Issued:** 12,85,713 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 340/- (Issue Price: Rs. 350/- per share).
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 31248506 to 32534218.
- **Lock-in Details:** 12,85,713 shares are under lock-in up to May 20, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential issue securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the lock-in period and distribution numbers for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 1, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 20, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Kesar India Limited through warrant conversion with minimal market impact.
