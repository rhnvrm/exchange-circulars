## Summary

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the revision of price bands for 26 scrips with effect from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 26 scrips.
- Effective date: November 03, 2026.
- Includes scrips with revised price bands of 2%, 5%, and 10%.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specified scrips have been modified from their existing levels as part of BSE surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and take note of the revised price limits for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts intraday price volatility and trading parameters for the affected securities.
