## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the part redemption of debentures of Keystone Realtors Limited, resulting in a reduced face value of Rs. 49,074.63 per debenture effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Keystone Realtors Limited
- Scrip Code: 977174
- ISIN: INE263M07109
- Scrip ID: KRL-9.5%-29-9-28-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 49,074.63 per debenture effective November 19, 2026.
- Record Date for Part Redemption: 22/11/2026 (Ex-Date: 20/11/2026) with a reduced face value of Rs. 47,582.09 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the part redemption and trade the debentures with the revised face value.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 20, 2026
- Record Date: November 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the debentures will undergo part redemption and face value adjustment in trading operations.
