## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited (Scrip Code: 542759) with effect from Thursday, November 19, 2026. A total of 1,43,442 new equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 220/- are being listed.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- Scrip Code: 542759
- ISIN: INE572J01011
- Total New Securities Listed: 1,43,442 equity shares (issued at Rs. 10/- face value with a premium of Rs. 220/-)
- Breakdown of securities and distinctive numbers:
  - 1,43,224 shares: Distinctive numbers 86392887 to 86536110
  - 218 shares: Distinctive numbers 88210118 to 8210335
- Date of Allotment: 29/09/2026
- Ranking: These new shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; this is a standard corporate securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the listing and trading permissions for the newly allotted securities of the company.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited with minimal impact on market operations as the shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
