## Summary

BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in several scrips in the recent past and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 5 companies: Anna Infrastructures Ltd, Black Opal Consultants Ltd, Dhvija Finance Ltd, Olatech Solutions Ltd, and Tera Software Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 21 companies including Amtech Esters Ltd, Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, and others.
- The notice maintains market transparency and protects investor interests regarding abnormal price/volume behavior.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine surveillance disclosure action by BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify and disclose any price-sensitive information or reasons for significant price/volume movements to the exchange upon request.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Nov 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to market participants regarding companies undergoing unusual price and volume movements and the status of their disclosures.
