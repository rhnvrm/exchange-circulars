## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the corporate debt segment (ICDM T+0 and T+1) covering the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Details transaction dates, securities/funds pay-in dates, and securities/funds pay-out dates for ICDM T+0 and T+1.
- Spans settlement numbers from 2627164 to 2627185 throughout December 2026.
- Applies to all market participants operating in the corporate debt segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational scheduling notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure timely execution of pay-in and pay-out obligations in accordance with the published ICDM T+0 and T+1 schedules.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Schedule Effective Period:** December 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly settlement operations and clear timelines for corporate debt trading participants on the BSE platform.
