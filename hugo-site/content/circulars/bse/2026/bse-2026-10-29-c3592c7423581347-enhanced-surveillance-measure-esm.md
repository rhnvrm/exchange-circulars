## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, moving to lower stages, or moving out of the framework effective October 30, 2026, and November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin effective November 02, 2026, on open positions as of October 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective October 30, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective November 02, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework, including margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band modifications in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the margin requirements, settlement segment changes, and price band restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026:** Effective date for movement to lower stages, Stage II restrictions (Trade for Trade, 2% price band, Periodic Call Auction), and removal of securities.
- **November 02, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and trading mechanics for shortlisted securities through enhanced margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.
