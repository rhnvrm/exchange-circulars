## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window / Takeover) for Kuber Udyog Limited. The bidding period opens on Wednesday, 28th October 2026, and closes on Wednesday, 11th November 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: KUBER UDYOG LIMITED (Symbol: KUBERJI, ISIN: INE594R01018, Scrip Code: 539408).
- Offer Size: Up to 3,19,71,680 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 23.35 per share (Face Value: Rs. 10.00, Market Lot: 1).
- Opening Date: Wednesday, 28th October 2026.
- Closing Date: Wednesday, 11th November 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid offering, modifications, and cancellations are scheduled daily from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. during the offer period.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; operational schedule issued pursuant to takeover regulations and established exchange procedures for Offer to Buy (OTB).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure investor UCCs are registered and updated with correct PAN numbers and categories in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Access URLs provided for iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms for participating members and custodians.

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: 28th October 2026
- Offer Closing Date: 11th November 2026
- Last day for Give-up / Take-up: 9th November 2026 / 11th November 2026 (Take-up up to 4:00 p.m. on the last day)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover offer-to-buy for shareholders of Kuber Udyog Limited through the exchange acquisition window.
