## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced that the surrender of trading membership by Pentad Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 6560) in the currency derivative and interest rate derivative segments has been approved by the Exchange with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: Pentad Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member No.: 6560
- Segments: Currency derivative and interest rate derivative segments
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026
- Department: Membership - Registrations Operation & Compliance

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is an administrative approval of trading member surrender under standard exchange procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members seeking clarifications regarding this notice can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 17, 2026
- Notice Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted to the specific member and derivative segments, with negligible impact on broader market operations.
