## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 16,89,859 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 493/- (issue price Rs. 503/-) on a preferential basis to non-promoters by Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited (Scrip Code: 543597), effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited
- Scrip Code: 543597
- ISIN: INE0I0T01010
- Number of Shares: 16,89,859 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 503/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 493/-)
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis
- Distinctive Numbers: 34251072 to 35940930
- Date of Allotment: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 23, 2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 29, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes introduced; routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants must ensure adherence to the lock-in period applicable to the shares up to May 29, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 15, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the equity capital base of Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited. The shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares, subject to specified lock-in conditions.
