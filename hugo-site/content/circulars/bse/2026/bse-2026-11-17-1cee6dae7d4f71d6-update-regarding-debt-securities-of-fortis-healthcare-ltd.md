## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued an update regarding the debt securities of Fortis Healthcare Ltd. In continuation of earlier notice dated December 19, 2025, specific scrip codes and ISINs of Fortis Healthcare Limited will be suspended from trading due to debt restructuring.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects specific debt securities of Fortis Healthcare Ltd.
- Reason for suspension is debt restructuring.
- Effective date of suspension is Friday, November 20, 2026.
- Affected Scrip Codes and ISINs:
  - Scrip Code: 976242 | ISIN: INE061F08018
  - Scrip Code: 976243 | ISIN: INE061F08026
  - Scrip Code: 976244 | ISIN: INE061F08034

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in specified debt securities has been restricted in connection with ongoing corporate debt restructuring processes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no trades are executed in the affected scrip codes and ISINs from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Holders and trading members dealing in the debt securities (NCDs) of Fortis Healthcare Ltd under the specified ISINs will face trading restrictions during the restructuring phase.
