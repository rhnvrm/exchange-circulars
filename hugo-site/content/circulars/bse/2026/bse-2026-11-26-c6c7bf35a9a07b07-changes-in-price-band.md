## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of existing price bands for 14 securities in the equity segment, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 14 scrips including ACI Infocom Ltd, Indian Energy Exchange Ltd, and Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Ltd.
- Effective date of revised price bands is November 27, 2026.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified securities under surveillance review.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the revised price bands and configure their trading terminals accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the affected securities must adhere to the new price limits to prevent order rejections or compliance breaches.
