## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 12 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan / Scheme (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 12
- Effective date for trading: November 17, 2026
- Notable issuers include Axis Bank Limited, Dixon Technologies (India) Limited, Federal Bank Limited, FSN E-Commerce Ventures Limited, and Yes Bank Limited.
- All securities listed are equity shares issued under ESOP/ESOS with no lock-in (NA) specified across all listed entries.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with standard depository and exchange guidelines for ESOP share listings.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for the respective companies due to exercise of stock options by employees. Minimal market impact.
