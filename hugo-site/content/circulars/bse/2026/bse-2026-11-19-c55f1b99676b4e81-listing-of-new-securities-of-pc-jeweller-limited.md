## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 74,07,500 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each of PC Jeweller Limited (Scrip Code: 534809) with effect from November 20, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants at an issue price of Rs. 18/- per share (face value Re. 1/- plus premium of Rs. 17/-).

## Key Points

- Company Name: PC Jeweller Limited (Scrip Code: 534809, ISIN: INE785M01021)
- Securities: 74,07,500 equity shares issued pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Issue Price: Rs. 18/- per share (Re. 1/- face value + Rs. 17/- premium).
- Allotment Date: September 22, 2026.
- Listing Date: November 20, 2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 9752134856 to 9759542355.
- Lock-in Details: All 74,07,500 shares are under lock-in up to May 20, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice regarding preferential allotment and warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants must note the lock-in restrictions applicable up to May 20, 2028, for the specified distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 20, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity share capital of PC Jeweller Limited by 74,07,500 shares. Reflects successful conversion of warrants by promoters into equity, impacting promoter shareholding structure while being subject to mandatory lock-in stipulations.
