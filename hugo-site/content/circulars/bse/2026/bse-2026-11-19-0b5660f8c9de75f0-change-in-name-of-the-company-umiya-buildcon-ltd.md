## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the change in name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name for MRO-TEK NETWORKS LIMITED (Scrip Code: 532376) on the BOLT Plus trading system, effective November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 26, 2026
- Scrip Code: 532376
- New Scrip ID on BOLT Plus System: MROTEK
- New Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: MROTEK

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of company name and identifiers across exchange trading systems and records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to update their internal order management and trading systems with the new scrip ID and company name.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and operational update for trading members with negligible impact on market operations.
