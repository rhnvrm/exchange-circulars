- Company: Fresita Proteins Limited
- Action: Extension of Rights Issue
- Previous Closing Date: November 26, 2026
- New Closing Date: December 11, 2026
- Reference Notice: 20261125-5

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the extended timeline for submitting rights issue applications.
