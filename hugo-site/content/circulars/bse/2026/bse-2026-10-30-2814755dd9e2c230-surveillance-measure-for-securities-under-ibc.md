## Summary

BSE has announced the application and review of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for securities of companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure for IRP/IBC are detailed in Annexure I effective November 02, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the IBC Framework is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are provided in Annexure IV.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance measures and price band restrictions for companies undergoing insolvency resolution under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the shortlisted securities, list modifications, and price band reinstatements effective from November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Notice Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading controls, surveillance monitoring, and price band limits for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings or exiting the framework.
