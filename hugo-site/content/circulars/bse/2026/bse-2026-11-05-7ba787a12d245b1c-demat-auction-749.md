## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the schedule for Demat Auction 749 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-749/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 5, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on November 5, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction scheduled for Friday, November 6, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must exercise caution while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 5, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 6, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlement DR-749/2026-27 with no broad market impact.
