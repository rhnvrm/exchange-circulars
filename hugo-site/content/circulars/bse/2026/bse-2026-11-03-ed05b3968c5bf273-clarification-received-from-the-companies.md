## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movement in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received and attached from Amtech Esters Ltd and ITI Ltd.
- Clarifications remain awaited from 23 other listed companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and others.
- Routine surveillance measure to promote market transparency and protect investor interests.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This notice reflects ongoing market surveillance activities by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price/volume movements when requested by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors should review clarifications provided by individual companies before making investment decisions in the specified scrips.
