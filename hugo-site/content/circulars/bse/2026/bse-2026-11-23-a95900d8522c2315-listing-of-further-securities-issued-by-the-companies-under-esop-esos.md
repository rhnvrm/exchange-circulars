## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 10 companies under ESOP / ESOS are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 10 companies listed for further issuance of securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: November 25, 2026.
- Companies include Archean Chemical Industries, Asian Energy Services, Eureka Forbes, ICICI Bank, ITC Hotels, PNB Housing Finance, Tech Mahindra, The Phoenix Mills, TVS Supply Chain Solutions, and Wonderla Holidays.
- Details include ISINs, scrip codes, number of shares, face value, and distinctive number ranges.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Standard corporate action - ESOP/ESOS listing).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure adherence to BSE listing operations and guidelines for ESOP/ESOS allotments.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of equity base for the mentioned companies through employee stock option plans with minimal market impact.
