## Summary

Franklin Templeton Mutual Fund has announced the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, and systematic registrations (SIP/STP) in specific schemes, effective December 01, 2026. The changes have been implemented on the BSE StAR MF Platform pursuant to the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by Franklin Templeton AMC.

## Key Points

- Affected schemes include FRANKLIN U.S. OPPORTUNITIES EQUITY ACTIVE FUND OF FUNDS and FRANKLIN ASIAN EQUITY FUND.
- Fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) are disabled from the close of business hours on November 30, 2026.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STPAMC) will be paused and will not be triggered or processed until further notice.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions remain fully permitted without any amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of investment restrictions on select international and equity schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform as per AMC addendum.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no fresh subscriptions or new SIP/STP registrations are accepted for the affected schemes after the cutoff.
- Members should notify clients regarding the pausing of existing systematic registrations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Cutoff for Fresh Registrations: Close of business hours on November 30, 2026
- Effective Date for Discontinuation: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or planning investments in Franklin U.S. Opportunities Equity Active Fund of Funds and Franklin Asian Equity Fund will experience restrictions on fresh inflows and systematic investments, while exit routes via redemption and switch-out remain open.
