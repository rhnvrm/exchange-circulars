## Summary

INDERGIRI FINANCE LTD. (Scrip Code: 531505) has announced a Rights Issue of equity shares. The company has established November 25, 2026, as the Record Date for determining shareholders eligible for the rights entitlement.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** INDERGIRI FINANCE LTD.
- **Scrip Code:** 531505
- **Notice Number:** 20261120-46
- **Record Date:** 25/11/2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date:** 25/11/2026
- **Rights Ratio:** 07 (SEVEN) Equity Shares for every 04 (FOUR) Equity Shares held.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Re. 1/- per share.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate action notification regarding a rights issue.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the ex-rights basis date and settlement details (DR- 763/2026-2027) for rolling settlement segment transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Record Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of Indergiri Finance Ltd. are impacted by the rights issue entitlement and pricing. Transactions in the company's equity shares will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from November 25, 2026.
