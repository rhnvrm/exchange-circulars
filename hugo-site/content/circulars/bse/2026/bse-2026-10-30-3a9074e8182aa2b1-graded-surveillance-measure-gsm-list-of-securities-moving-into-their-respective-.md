## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, and IV) based on objective surveillance criteria.
- Effective date of implementation: November 02, 2026.
- Settlement for affected securities will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.
- Surveillance actions include 100% margins, 5% price bands, trading frequency restrictions, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% or lower price band, and an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday / first trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week with no upward price movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution when dealing in the listed securities.
- Ensure settlement is handled on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.
- Direct inquiries or clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities will experience constraints due to strict margin rules, trade-to-trade settlement mandates, and surveillance deposit requirements.
