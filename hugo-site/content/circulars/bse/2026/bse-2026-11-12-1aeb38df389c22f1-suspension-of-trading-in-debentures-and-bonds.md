## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of various companies due to the fixation of record dates for payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261112-7 dated November 12, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment.
- Affected issuers include Edelweiss Rural & Corporate Services, APSBCL, ICL Fincorp, Akara Capital Advisors, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company, JM Financial Products, Axis Finance, Mufin Green Finance, and Muthoot Finance.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures as per the specified record and ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no dealings take place in the specified debentures during the suspension period.

## Important Dates

- Record/Ex-Dates: November 13, 2026 to November 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational suspension for debt corporate actions with minimal market impact.
