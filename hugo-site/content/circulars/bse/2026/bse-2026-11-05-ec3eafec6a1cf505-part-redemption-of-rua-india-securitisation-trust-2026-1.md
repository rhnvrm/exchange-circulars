## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that RUA India Securitisation Trust 2026-1 has fixed a record date for the part redemption of its Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in the PTCs will take place with a reduced face value effective from the record date.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 977663
- **Scrip ID:** RIST261-12.50%-23-01-33-PTC
- **ISIN:** INE2SW315013
- **Record Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Ex-Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Reduced Face Value:** Rs. 78,775.62 per PTC

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the reduced face value and record date for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Ex-Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding PTCs of RUA India Securitisation Trust 2026-1 will receive part redemption payouts as per the reduced face value of Rs. 78,775.62 per PTC.
