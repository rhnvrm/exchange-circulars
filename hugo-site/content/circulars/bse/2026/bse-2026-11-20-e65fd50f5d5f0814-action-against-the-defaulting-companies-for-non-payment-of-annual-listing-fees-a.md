## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension of trading in the equity shares of Sarup Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 514412) following compliance with Annual Listing Fees (ALF) requirements pursuant to Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Trading suspension in the equity shares of Sarup Industries Limited is revoked effective Thursday, November 26, 2026.
- Trading will resume in the Normal Segment, shifting from Group XT to Group X.
- Action taken in continuation of earlier Exchange notices regarding outstanding Annual Listing Fees.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; administrative revocation of trading restrictions due to fee compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must maintain continuous compliance with Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding timely payment of Annual Listing Fees.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date of Revocation: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume normal trading operations in the equity shares of Sarup Industries Limited under Group X starting November 26, 2026.
