## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their Commercial Papers (CPs). Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261119-46
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Companies involved include Adani Enterprises Ltd., Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, GIC Housing Finance Ltd., Sharekhan Limited, Samvardhana Motherson International Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Infina Finance Private Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, and Avanse Financial Services Limited.
- Record Dates and Ex-Dates for redemption are set for 26/11/2026 and 27/11/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard market operations procedure for commercial paper redemption and suspension of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies effective from the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 November 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 26/11/2026 and 27/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to trading members and investors holding the respective commercial papers undergoing redemption.
