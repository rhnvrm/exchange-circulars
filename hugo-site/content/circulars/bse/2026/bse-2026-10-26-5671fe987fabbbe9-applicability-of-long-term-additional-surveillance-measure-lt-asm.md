## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the applicability, stage revisions, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfers to T / XT / MT / TS groups for specified securities.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 29, 2026 on all open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM framework or subjected to Lower Price Band w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- Review of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework effective October 27, 2026.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework based on XBRL submissions by listed companies, in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for applicable securities, adhere to revised price bands, and account for group transfers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band & Movement / Removal: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: October 29, 2026
- Reference Date for Open Positions: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for impacted securities will be significantly tightened due to 100% margin imposition and price band restrictions, requiring careful risk management by market participants.
