## Summary

BSE has announced revised operating guidelines and trading modalities for the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Derivatives segment, in accordance with SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026. These changes become effective from December 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised trading timings introduced for the Equity Derivatives segment, including pre-open, continuous trading, and trade modification windows.
- Index derivatives contracts trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days, except expiry days where expiring contracts cease at 3:30 p.m.
- Stock derivatives contracts trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days, including expiry days.
- Reference price calculation for stock and index futures price bands is aligned with the CAS in the Equity Cash segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised operational guidelines under Clause 5 of SEBI circular ref HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/23843/2026 dated October 15, 2026, and earlier Exchange notices.
- Daily closing price continues to be determined under the existing mechanism, with VWAP calculation period set from 3:15 p.m. to 3:45 p.m.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the updated trading sessions, order entry periods, and price band alignment mechanisms effective December 7, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: December 7, 2026
- Notice Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances alignment between equity derivatives and equity cash segment Closing Auction Sessions, improving market efficiency and price discovery during closing hours.
