## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 3,00,00,000 partly paid-up equity shares of Century Extrusions Ltd issued on a rights basis, effective from Thursday, October 29, 2026, under the 'B' group of securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Extrusions Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 890241
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: CENTEXTPP
- ISIN No.: IN9281A01016
- Face Value: Re. 1/- each, Paid-up Value: Re. 0.50/- each.
- Issue Price: Rs. 15/- (Rs. 7.50/- paid on application and Rs. 7.50/- to be paid on subsequent calls).
- Total Securities: 3,00,00,000 Equity Shares.
- Date of Allotment: 22/10/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary ISIN No. IN8281A01025 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular Nos. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 regarding activation of ISIN in case of additional issue of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the suspension of temporary ISIN and the activation of new ISIN IN9281A01016 for trading effective October 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 22, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of newly allotted partly paid-up rights equity shares for Century Extrusions Ltd, providing liquidity to investors who participated in the rights issue.
