## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund announces the availability of Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing transactions following Notice No. 20261001-59 dated October 01, 2026.
- Facilities available include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers multiple plans: Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors must ensure proper scheme codes and ISINs are used for transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and distributors to execute transactions and set up systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
