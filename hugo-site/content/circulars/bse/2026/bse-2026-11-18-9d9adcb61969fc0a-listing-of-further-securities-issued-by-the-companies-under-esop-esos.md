## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 22 companies pursuant to their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS). These securities are effective for trading starting November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total Companies Involved: 22 listed entities issuing further shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective Date: Trading of the newly listed securities commences on November 19, 2026.
- Participating Companies Include: 360 One Wam Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, PB Fintech Limited, RBL Bank Limited, and others.
- Ranking: The newly issued shares rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate capital update.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities and trading members must adhere to standard depository and exchange guidelines regarding share credit and trading admission.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; represents routine equity dilution and capital expansion from employee stock option exercises.
