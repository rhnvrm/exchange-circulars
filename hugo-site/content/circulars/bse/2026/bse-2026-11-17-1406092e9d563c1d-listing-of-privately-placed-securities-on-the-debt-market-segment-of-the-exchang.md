## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by several companies on its debt market segment, effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers listed for issuers including InCred Financial Services Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.
- Securities are issued in dematerialised form with standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.
- Ratings range from CRISIL A1+ and ICRA A1+.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers and adhere to standard debt segment trading norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: November 16-17, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from December 24, 2026, to August 13, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing providing liquidity and secondary market trading access for privately placed debt instruments.
