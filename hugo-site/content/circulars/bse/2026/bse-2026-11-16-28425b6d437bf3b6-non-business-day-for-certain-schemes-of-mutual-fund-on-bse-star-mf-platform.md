## Summary

BSE has announced non-business days for specific mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform following communication received from respective mutual funds regarding market closures.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund**: November 23, 2026 declared as a Non-Business Day.
- **Bandhan US Equity Fund of Fund**: November 26, 2026 declared as a Non-Business Day.
- Transactions including subscription, redemption, switch, SIP/XSIP, STP, and SWP will not be permitted for these schemes on their respective non-business days.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the non-business days and ensure transaction execution on the BSE StAR MF Platform is aligned accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **November 23, 2026**: Non-Business Day for ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund.
- **November 26, 2026**: Non-Business Day for Bandhan US Equity Fund of Fund.

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members will temporarily be unable to process transactions (subscriptions, redemptions, switches, SIPs, etc.) for the specified mutual fund schemes on the respective dates.
