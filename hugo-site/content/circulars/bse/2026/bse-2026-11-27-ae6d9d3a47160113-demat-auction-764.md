## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-764/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 27, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on November 27, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction: Monday, November 30, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must ensure correct entry of security codes and rates.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 27, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for settlement participants regarding auction 764.
