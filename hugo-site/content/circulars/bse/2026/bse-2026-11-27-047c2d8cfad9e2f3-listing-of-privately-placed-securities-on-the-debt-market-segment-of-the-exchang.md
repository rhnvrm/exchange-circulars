## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by various companies on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers for DSP Finance Private Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Ugro Capital Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited have been listed.
- Securities will be traded only in dematerialized form under standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Allotment dates range from November 26, 2026, to November 27, 2026, with maturity/redemption dates between February 2027 and March 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialized trading and standard denomination rules for debt market transactions.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: November 26, 2026 - November 27, 2026
- Redemption / Interest Payment Dates: February 25, 2027 - March 18, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for privately placed commercial debt instruments.
