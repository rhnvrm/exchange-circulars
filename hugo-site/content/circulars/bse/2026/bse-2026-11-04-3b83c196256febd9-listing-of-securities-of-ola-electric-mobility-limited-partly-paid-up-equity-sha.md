## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for 37,02,72,665 partly paid-up equity shares of Ola Electric Mobility Limited under the 'A' Group of Securities, effective November 06, 2026. The shares were issued on a rights basis with a face value of Rs. 10/- and paid-up value of Rs. 6/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ola Electric Mobility Limited
- **Security Details:** 37,02,72,665 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each, partly paid-up of Rs. 6/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 10/- with Rs. 6/- paid on application and Rs. 4/- payable on subsequent calls).
- **Scrip Code:** 890242
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** BOLT OLAPP
- **ISIN:** IN90LXG01014
- **Group & Market Lot:** 'A' Group, Market Lot: 1
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1 to 370272665
- **Date of Allotment:** 02/11/2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** November 06, 2026
- **Temporary ISIN Suspension:** Temporary ISIN IN80LXG01023 is suspended in terms of SEBI circulars regarding activation of ISIN for additional issue of securities.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this notice pertains to standard corporate action listing and ISIN activation procedures pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the activation of the new ISIN (IN90LXG01014) and the simultaneous suspension of the temporary ISIN (IN80LXG01023) in compliance with SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 02, 2026
- **Effective Listing & Trading Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders and trading members to trade the newly listed partly paid-up rights equity shares of Ola Electric Mobility Limited under the permanent ISIN, ensuring smooth settlement and operational continuity.
