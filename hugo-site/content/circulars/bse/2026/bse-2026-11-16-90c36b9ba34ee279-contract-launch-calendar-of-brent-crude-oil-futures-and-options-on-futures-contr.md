## Summary

BSE has released the contract launch and expiry calendar for Brent Crude Oil Futures and Options on Futures contracts within the commodity derivatives segment, covering the period from November 2026 to December 2027.

## Key Points

- Outlines the contract launch dates and contract expiry dates for Brent Crude Oil Futures contracts.
- Details contract launch dates, contract expiry dates, and underlying expiry dates for Brent Crude Oil Options on Futures contracts.
- Builds upon previous notice specifications for commodity derivatives.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes the schedule and calendar parameters for trading Brent Crude Oil derivative instruments on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must execute trades and manage positions in accordance with the specified launch and expiry dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Contract Schedule:** November 2026 to December 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures transparency and operational readiness for trading members dealing in commodity derivatives and energy contracts.
