## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the revised settlement schedule, obligation download, and settlement dates pertaining to the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for India Cements Capital Limited (Settlement No. 2026130, originally conducted from October 28, 2026, to November 11, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Company:** India Cements Capital Limited
- **Settlement Type & No.:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026130
- **Original Bidding Period:** 28/10/2026 to 11/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 03/12/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 04/12/2026

## Regulatory Changes

Revised timeline updates for settlement of shares acquired through the stock exchange acquisition window mechanism for takeover offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026130 in the depository system.
- Reference should be made to Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020, regarding settlement under revised guidelines.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Original Bid Period:** 28/10/2026 – 11/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 03/12/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 04/12/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational clearing and settlement of shares for market participants and custodians participating in the India Cements Capital Limited takeover offer.
