## Summary

BSE has announced the temporary suspension of subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh systematic registrations (SIP/STP) in select schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected schemes: INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Equity Income Fund of Fund, INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Pan European Equity Fund of Fund, and INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund.
- Fresh lump-sum purchases and switch-in transactions are suspended.
- Fresh registrations for SIP and STP have been disabled from the close of business hours on November 3, 2026.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STPAMC) are paused and will not be processed until further notice.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, and STP-out transactions continue to be permitted without amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of temporary subscription restrictions on specific mutual fund schemes as per communication received from Invesco Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions, SIP/STP registrations, and ensure existing systematic registrations are paused for the specified schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or planning investments in the affected Invesco Fund of Fund schemes will be unable to make fresh lump-sum investments or start new SIPs/STPs until restrictions are lifted, while exit options (redemptions and switch-outs) remain fully operational.
