## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance. Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. November 30, 2026, on open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework effective November 26, 2026.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- This measure is in continuation of exchange notice no. 20220131-43 dated January 31, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of a minimum 75% margin requirement on securities identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under the surveillance framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities and ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirements on open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- **November 26, 2026:** Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework.
- **November 27, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions affected by the 75% margin.
- **November 30, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 75% margin implementation on Annexure I securities.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in securities listed under Annexure I will experience higher margin requirements (75%), impacting liquidity and leverage in the equity and derivatives segments.
