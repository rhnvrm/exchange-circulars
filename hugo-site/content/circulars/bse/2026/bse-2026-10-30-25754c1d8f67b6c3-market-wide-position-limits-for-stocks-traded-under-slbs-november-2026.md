## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. (BSECL) has announced the stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable to Clearing Members, Institutional Investors, and Clients under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Specifies stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for November 2026 under SLBS.
- Outlines position limits for Clearing Members (CM), Institutional Investors, and Clients.
- Issued pursuant to SEBI Circular No. MRD/DoP/SE/Dep/Cir-14/2007 dated December 20, 2007.
- Complete details are available in Annexure I of the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine periodic notice implementing existing frameworks under the Securities Lending Scheme, 1997 and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clearing members must ensure compliance with the specified position limits for stocks traded under the SLBS segment during November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 October 2026
- Effective Period: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notice ensuring market participants adhere to prescribed position limits under the SLBS segment for November 2026.
