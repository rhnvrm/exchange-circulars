## Summary

BSE Limited has notified trading members that the equity shares of Antala Industries Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Antala Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 545024
- Symbol: ANTALA
- ISIN: INE15Z101013
- Segment: SME
- The specific date of listing and detailed security parameters will be announced through a separate notice.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the upcoming admission of securities and prepare their systems for trading in Antala Industries Limited once the operational date is published.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced separately

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the onboarding and trading readiness of market participants for the new SME listing.
