## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the inclusion of two newly listed companies, Vectras Enprocon Limited and Johnsoncab Electricals Limited, into the BSE SME IPO Index effective at the open of Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Vectras Enprocon Limited (Exchange ticker: 545020) and Johnsoncab Electricals Limited (Exchange ticker: 545021) are added to the BSE SME IPO Index.
- Effective date for the index inclusion is November 08, 2026.
- The action is included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice regarding index constituents.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal direct market impact, reflecting recent SME listings on the exchange.
