## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, and certain securities are moving to lower stages or shifting from the rolling settlement segment to the trade-for-trade segment effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 06, 2026 on open positions as on November 05, 2026 and new positions.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under ESM Framework w.e.f. November 05, 2026 are listed in Annexure I.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are subject to Trade for Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 05, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of ESM Framework w.e.f. November 05, 2026 is given in Annexure II, and consolidated list is in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement shifts, and stage movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements, settlement segment changes, and price band restrictions for affected securities in their client accounts.

## Important Dates

- **November 05, 2026**: Movement of securities to lower stages/Stage II, shifting out of ESM for specific securities.
- **November 06, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanics for shortlisted securities due to mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.
