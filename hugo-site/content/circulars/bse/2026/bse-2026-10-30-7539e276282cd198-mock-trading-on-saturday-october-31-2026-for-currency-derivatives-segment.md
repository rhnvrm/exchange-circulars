## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives, Cross Currency Derivatives, and Interest Rate Derivatives segments on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms, empaneled vendors, or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- Segments: Currency Derivatives, Cross Currency Derivatives, and Interest Rate Derivatives.
- No new BOLT TWS Release will be deployed.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Member feedback is requested by 04:00 PM on the day of the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are encouraged to participate actively to test their trading applications, ETI APIs, and vendor platforms. Feedback must be submitted via email or contact numbers provided by 04:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 31, 2026.
- Log-in Time: 01:15 PM to 02:20 PM.
- Continuous Trading: 02:20 PM to 03:30 PM.
- Trade Modification: 02:20 PM to 04:00 PM.
- Trade Annulment Request: 04:05 PM.
- Feedback Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026, by 04:00 PM.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal as this is a non-live testing session. Helps members ensure system robustness and readiness for live market conditions.
