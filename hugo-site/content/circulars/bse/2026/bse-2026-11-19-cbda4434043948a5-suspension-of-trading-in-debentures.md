## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debentures and bonds of specified companies as they have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the mentioned companies starting from their respective ex-dates/record dates.
- Affected Issuers & Securities:
  - **Mindspace Business Parks REIT**: Scrip Code 975068, ISIN INE0CCU07090, Record/Ex-Date: 25/11/2026.
  - **IFCI Ltd.**: Scrip Code 947841, ISIN INE039A09OA4, Record/Ex-Date: 27/11/2026.
  - **IFCI Ltd.**: Scrip Code 972706, ISIN INE039A09NZ3, Record/Ex-Date: 27/11/2026.
  - **Nirmal Bang Securities Private Limited**: Scrip Code 976778, ISIN INE681R08117, Record/Ex-Date: 27/11/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice for debt segment corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified debentures of the aforementioned companies on and from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **25/11/2026**: Record Date / Ex-Date for Mindspace Business Parks REIT
- **27/11/2026**: Record Date / Ex-Date for IFCI Ltd. and Nirmal Bang Securities Private Limited

## Impact Assessment

Routine temporary trading suspension in the debt segment for the specified ISINs to facilitate interest payments and redemption processing.
