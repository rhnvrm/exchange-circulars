## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Avenique Limited (formerly known as KDJ Holidayscapes & Resorts Limited) effective November 05, 2026, under the XT Group in the Trade-to-Trade segment, following an NCLT-approved resolution plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 05, 2026.
- Scrip Code: 530701; ISIN: INE089E01033; Group: XT.
- Paid-up equity shares reduced from 5,46,56,000 to 5,00,000 shares of Rs. 2/- each pursuant to the NCLT-approved resolution plan dated March 04, 2025.
- Allotments include 25,000 equity shares to public shareholders and 4,75,000 equity shares to the Successful Resolution Applicant (Promoter group).
- Entire shareholding of erstwhile promoters stands extinguished.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of capital reduction and corporate restructuring approved by NCLT under IBC, 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading to take place in the Trade-to-Trade segment under XT Group.
- Securities subject to lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026.
- NCLT Order Date: March 04, 2025.
- Record Date for Resolution Plan: April 30, 2026.
- Effective Listing Date: November 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Resumption of trading for the restructured entity under a new name and capital structure, providing liquidity to eligible public and promoter shareholders under monitoring segments.
