## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Takeover) of shares of India Cement Capital Ltd (Security Symbol: INDCEMCAP, Scrip Code: 511355, ISIN: INE429D01017). The acquisition window is scheduled from Wednesday, 28th October 2026 to Wednesday, 11th November 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** India Cement Capital Ltd
- **Security Symbol / Scrip Code:** INDCEMCAP / 511355
- **ISIN:** INE429D01017
- **Offer Size:** Upto 56,43,612 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 12.00 per share (Face Value: Rs. 10.00)
- **Opening Date:** 28th October 2026
- **Closing Date:** 11th November 2026
- **Session Timings:** Bid Offering & Modifications from 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Take-up on last day up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; standard operational schedule notice for takeover offer-to-buy window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of investors is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and investor category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should refer to Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29 for detailed operational instructions.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** 28th October 2026
- **Offer Closing Date / Give-up Last Day:** 11th November 2026 (Take-up up to 4.00 p.m.)
