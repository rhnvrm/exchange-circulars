## Summary

BSE has issued an updated list of securities under the Add-on Price Band Framework following its periodic review. The changes regarding inclusions, continuations, and exclusions are effective from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities identified in Annexure I are included under the Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Securities listed in Annexure II continue to remain under the framework.
- Securities listed in Annexure III are moved out of the framework.
- Effective date for the changes is November 03, 2026.
- References Exchange circular no. 20210811-6 dated August 11, 2021.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review of securities under the Add-on Price Band Framework pursuant to existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the updated price band applicability for the specified securities effective November 03, 2026. For clarifications, members may contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading restrictions and price band frameworks for the affected securities are updated, impacting trading operations for market participants dealing in these scrips.
