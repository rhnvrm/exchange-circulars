## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Antala Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 545024) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Thursday, November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Antala Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 545024
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026
- Reference: In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261109-31 dated November 9, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in equity shares shifts from the Trade for Trade settlement category to normal rolling settlement, allowing regular trading and liquidity mechanics.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure appropriate system updates for trading in Antala Industries Limited shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the scrip, potentially improving liquidity and trading convenience for investors.
