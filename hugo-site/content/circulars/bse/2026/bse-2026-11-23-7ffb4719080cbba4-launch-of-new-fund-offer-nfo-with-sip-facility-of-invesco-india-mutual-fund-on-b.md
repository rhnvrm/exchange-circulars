## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility for the INVESCO INDIA FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 25, 2026, to December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: INVESCO INDIA FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND
- Scheme Category: DEBT
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 11, 2026
- NFO Window: November 25, 2026 to December 07, 2026 (extended till midnight on December 07, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 11, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 25, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: December 07, 2026
- SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline: December 07, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 11, 2026
- Clear Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members operational clarity and timelines for subscribing to the INVESCO INDIA FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
