## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to the revised LT-ASM framework. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, transfers to T/XT/MT/TS groups, and exclusions for certain securities effective October 30 and November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **100% Margins:** Applicable w.e.f. November 03, 2026, on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands:** List of securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM with Lower Price Band applicable w.e.f. October 30, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- **Group Transfers:** Securities continuing in LT-ASM framework transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 03, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- **Exits from Framework:** Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework w.e.f. October 30, 2026 (Annexure II).
- **Consolidated List:** Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework measures including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and lower price band restrictions on applicable scrips.
- Members can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Higher Stages / Price Bands / Exits):** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date (100% Margins / Group Transfers):** November 03, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in short-listed securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, margin requirements, and potential trading group reclassifications, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
