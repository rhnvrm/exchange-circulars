## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Core4 Engineers Limited shall be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Monday, November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Core4 Engineers Limited (SME IPO)
- Scrip Code: 545007
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, November 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moved out of the compulsory Trade for Trade settlement mechanism into the regular rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems and take note of the segment change for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading and enhanced liquidity for investors as the scrip transitions to the rolling segment.
