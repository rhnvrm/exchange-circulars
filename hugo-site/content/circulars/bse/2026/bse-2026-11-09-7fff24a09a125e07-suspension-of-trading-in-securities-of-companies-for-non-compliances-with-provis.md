## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in the securities of 8 companies effective December 10, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (non-submission of financial results for two consecutive quarters: March 2026 and June 2026). Additionally, the entire shareholding of promoters and other securities in their demat accounts will remain frozen during the suspension period.

## Key Points

- Suspension Effective Date: December 10, 2026 (if non-compliance continues).
- Compliance Deadline: December 07, 2026, to avoid suspension by complying with all provisions.
- Affected Companies: A.F. Enterprises Ltd (538351), Gian Life Care Ltd (542918), Hampton Sky Realty Ltd (526407), Karnawati Innovation Ltd (531671), KCD Industries India Ltd (540696), Laddu Gopal Online Services Ltd (537707), Spicejet Ltd (500285), Star Housing Finance Ltd (539017).
- Post-Suspension Trading: 15 days after suspension, trading allowed on Trade for Trade basis in Z group only on the first trading day of every week for six months.
- Promoter Shareholding: Entire promoter shareholding and demat holdings frozen during suspension.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforced pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated July 11, 2023, and updated January 30, 2026, prescribing penal actions and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for suspension and revocation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must comply with SEBI LODR Regulations (specifically Regulation 33 for financial results) to the satisfaction of the Exchange on or before December 07, 2026.
- Revocation of suspension is subject to complying with extant norms and paying applicable fines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 09, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: December 07, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: December 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational and market impact for the affected companies and their investors, resulting in trading suspension and freezing of promoter shareholdings.
