## Summary

360 ONE Mutual Fund (SIF) has announced a revision in the minimum application amounts for Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), alongside the introduction of STP and SWP facilities for the DynaSIF Equity Long-Short Fund, effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 17, 2026.
- **Schemes Affected:** 
  - DynaSIF Equity Long-Short Fund
  - DynaSIF Equity Ex-Top 100 Long - Short Fund
  - DynaSIF Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund
- **SIP / STP Revisions:**
  - Monthly SIP: Revised from Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 1,000 per month (minimum period of 6 months).
  - Quarterly SIP: Revised from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 3,000 per quarter (minimum period of 6 months).
- **Facility Additions:** Introduction of STP and SWP facilities for DynaSIF Equity Long-Short Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational scheme addendum issued by the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the revised minimum application amounts and facility updates while submitting SIP, XSIP, AMCSTP, or SWP registrations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** 17 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Lower minimum investment thresholds for SIPs make the affected SIF schemes more accessible to investors, while the introduction of new transfer and withdrawal facilities improves operational flexibility.
