## Summary

Effective from Friday, November 6, 2026, the equity shares of Johnsoncab Electricals Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545021
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: JOHNSONCAB
- ISIN: INE1ZSR01011
- Total Securities: 10,570,800 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 10570800
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 200/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 190/-)
- Date of Allotment: November 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day (November 6, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate actions executed to mark the lock-in period in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Allotment Date: November 4, 2026
- Listing & Trading Effective Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Johnsoncab Electricals Limited on the BSE SME platform.
