## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of SI CREVA Capital Services Private Limited (Scrip Code: 978300, ISIN: INE996U07438) in connection with the record date fixed for the payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SI CREVA CAPITAL SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 978300
- Scrip ID: SCCSPL-12.28%-13-12-26-UL-PVT
- ISIN: INE996U07438
- Ex-Date: 27/11/2026
- Record Date: 28/11/2026
- Settlement DR: 765/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 27/11/2026
- Record Date: 28/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt instruments undergoing interest payment or redemption record date, restricting secondary market trading temporarily.
