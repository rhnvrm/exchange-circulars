## Summary

Edelweiss Mutual Fund is launching the NFO for the Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from October 30, 2026, to November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF (Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth).
- NFO Period: October 30, 2026, to November 13, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 13, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 20, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 18, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 30, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 13, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 18, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investment access to the Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard NFO operational and settlement guidelines.
