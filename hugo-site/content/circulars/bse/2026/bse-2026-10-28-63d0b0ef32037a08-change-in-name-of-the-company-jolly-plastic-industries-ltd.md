## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Jolly Plastic Industries Ltd has changed its name to Sahaj digital limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Jolly Plastic Industries Ltd
- New Company Name: Sahaj digital limited
- Scrip Code: 507968
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus System): SAHAJDIGI
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus System): SAHAJDIGI

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate identity and system listing update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems to reflect the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name for scrip code 507968.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminals and records with the new name and scrip ID.
