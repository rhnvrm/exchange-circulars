## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by eight companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS), effective from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Total Companies:** Eight companies have issued further equity shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- **Effective Date:** Securities are admitted for trading with effect from Friday, November 13, 2026.
- **Companies Involved:** Atishay Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, IDFC First Bank Limited, KFin Technologies Limited, Polycab India Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, V-Guard Industries Limited, and WeWork India Management Limited.
- **Lock-in:** All listed securities have 'NA' (Not Applicable) under lock-in.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with BSE listing requirements and operational guidelines for issuance and admission of ESOP/ESOS shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital of the respective companies due to ESOP exercise. Minimal direct market impact.
