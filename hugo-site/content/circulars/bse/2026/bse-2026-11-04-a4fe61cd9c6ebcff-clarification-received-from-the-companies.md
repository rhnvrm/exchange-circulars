## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies that have submitted clarifications regarding significant price and volume movements in their scrips, alongside a list of companies whose replies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 8 companies including Dhillon Freight Carrier Ltd, Quality RO Industries Ltd, Aegis Logistics Ltd, CIAN Agro Industries & Infrastructure Ltd, Abram Food Ltd, Abril Paper Tech Ltd, Innovision Ltd, and Marsons Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 22 companies including Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Ltd (MTNL), Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals, and others.
- Surveillance action taken by the Exchange to ensure trading members and investors have access to latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandatory clarification disclosures required under BSE surveillance framework for scrips experiencing unusual price and volume movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities notified by the Exchange must provide timely clarifications regarding material price/volume movements to ensure transparent market operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes investor protection and market integrity by maintaining transparent disclosures for scrips with volatile price or volume movements.
