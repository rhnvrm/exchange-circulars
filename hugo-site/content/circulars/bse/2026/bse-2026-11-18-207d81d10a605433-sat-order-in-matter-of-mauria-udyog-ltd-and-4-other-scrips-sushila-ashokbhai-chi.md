## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 5, 2026, in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips. SAT granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks in an interest-bearing account by SEBI. SEBI has directed compliance with this SAT order via email dated November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **SAT Order Date:** October 05, 2026.
- **Notice Date:** November 18, 2026.
- **Appellants & Entities Involved:**
  - Sushila Ashokbhai Chiripal (PAN: AHWPC2333D)
  - Chirpal Suryansh Hari (PAN: BHWPC6616A)
  - Anil Dhanuka (PAN: AAPPD9931Q)
  - Purple Entertainment Ltd. (PAN: AAACH5273P)
  - Rajvi Naresh Shah (PAN: BJAPS6060A)
  - Jignesh Sudhirbhai Shah (PAN: BBNPS1083E)
- **Condition of Stay:** Deposit of full penalty within four weeks from the order date to be kept in an interest-bearing account by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SAT interim stay order directing deposit of penalty by appellants.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and concerned appellants are advised to take note of the SAT order and ensure compliance with the penalty deposit requirements as directed by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **SAT Order Date:** October 05, 2026
- **SEBI Direction Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Notice Issue Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides interim relief via stay of penalty subject to deposit compliance for the named entities and scrips.
