## Summary

BSE has announced that the UTI Financial Services Debt Fund will reopen for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from November 5, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261026-27 dated October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include Regular and Direct plans for both Growth and IDCW Payout options.
- Facilities enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026.
- Eligible ISINs:
  - INF789F1AI17 (UTI FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH)
  - INF789F1AI33 (UTI FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT)
  - INF789F1AI25 (UTI FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH)
  - INF789F1AI41 (UTI FINANCIAL SERVICES DEBT FUND - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational availability notice for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can execute transactions in the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with standard operating procedures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume seamless transactions, SIPs, and redemptions across regular and direct plans of UTI Financial Services Debt Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
