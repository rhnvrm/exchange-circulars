## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for INVESCO India Silver ETF and its Direct Plan on the BSE StAR MF Platform, scheduled from October 27, 2026, to November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details:** INVESCO India Silver ETF and INVESCO India Silver ETF - Direct Plan under Equity category.
- **NFO Period:** October 27, 2026, to November 09, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 09, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 16, 2026.
- **Payment & Funds Rules:** Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 16, 2026. Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 27, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** November 16, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation in the INVESCO India Silver ETF NFO for trading members and investors via the BSE StAR MF platform.
