## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,44,04,204 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1304.89/- to promoters on a preferential basis by Gabriel India Ltd. (Scrip Code: 505714), effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Gabriel India Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 505714
- **ISIN:** INE524A01029
- **Securities Details:** 1,44,04,204 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1304.89/- to Promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1305.89/-
- **Date of Allotment:** 11/09/2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 184412221 to 198816424
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,44,04,204 shares locked-in up to 19/05/2028.
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted with effect from Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for newly allotted preferential shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard depository and exchange lock-in norms for preferential allotment.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 11, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 19, 2028

## Impact Assessment

This listing increases the paid-up equity share capital of Gabriel India Ltd. due to preferential allotment to promoters, with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
