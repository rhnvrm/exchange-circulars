## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance Company Limited due to the record date for payment of interest and redemption on the exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance Company Limited
- Security Code: 973603
- ISIN: INE951F08036
- Debenture Series: ABSLICL-7.63%-30-11-31-PVT (DR-756/2026-2027)
- Record Date: 15/11/2026
- No Dealings From: 13/11/2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Exchange are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company from the specified date onwards.

## Important Dates

- No Dealings From: 13/11/2026
- Record Date: 15/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational suspension of trading in specific debt instruments for interest payment and call option redemption processing.
