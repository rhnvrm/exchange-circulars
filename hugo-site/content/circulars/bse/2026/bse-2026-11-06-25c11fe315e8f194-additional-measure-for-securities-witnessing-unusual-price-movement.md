## Summary

BSE has conducted a review for the identification of securities to be placed under or removed from the additional measure for securities witnessing unusual price movement, following previous circulars on the subject.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the inclusion criteria are listed in Annexure I.
- Securities moving out of the framework are listed in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Changes become effective from November 08, 2026.
- Members can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance and trading measures on identified securities exhibiting unusual price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must monitor the updated lists of securities subject to additional surveillance measures and ensure compliance with applicable trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced surveillance and risk management for volatile securities, impacting trading dynamics for securities listed in the annexures.
