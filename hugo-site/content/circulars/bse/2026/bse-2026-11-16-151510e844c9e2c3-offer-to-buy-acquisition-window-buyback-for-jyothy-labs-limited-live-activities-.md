## Summary

BSE has issued the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) of shares of JYOTHY LABS LIMITED. The bidding and acquisition window is scheduled from November 17, 2026, to November 23, 2026, with an offer price of Rs. 240.00 per share for up to 61,25,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Security Name: JYOTHY LABS LIMITED (Symbol: JYOTHYLAB, Scrip Code: 532926, ISIN: INE668F01031)
- Offer Size: Upto 61,25,000 Equity Shares at Rs. 240.00 per share.
- Opening Date: Tuesday, 17th November 2026; Closing Date: Monday, 23rd November 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid offering and modifications/cancellations from 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. during the issue period.
- Platforms: Accessible via iBBS and RTRMS platforms (Internet and Leased Line).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure UCC of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for investors having shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must adhere to operational instructions outlined in Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 17th November 2026
- Closing Date: 23rd November 2026
- Give-up / Take-up (Last day of offer): 23rd November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly participation of eligible shareholders and trading members in the buyback offer of Jyothy Labs Limited through the acquisition window mechanism.
