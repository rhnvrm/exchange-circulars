## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited has fixed November 4, 2026, as the Record Date for its upcoming Rights Issue of Equity Shares. Eligible shareholders will be issued 3 equity shares of Re. 1 each for cash at a premium of Re. 0.50 per share (total issue price of Re. 1.50 per share) for every 8 equity shares held.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited (Scrip Code: 543281)
- Record Date: November 4, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: November 4, 2026
- Rights Issue Ratio: 3 (three) equity shares for every 8 (eight) equity shares held
- Issue Price: Re. 1.50 per share (Face Value Re. 1.00 + Premium Re. 0.50), payable entirely at the time of application.
- Department: Listing Operations

## Regulatory Changes

None. This notice pertains to corporate action operations for a rights issue.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure transactions are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from November 4, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026
- Record Date: November 4, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders on the record date receive rights entitlements, while trades executed on or after the ex-rights date do not carry rights benefits.
