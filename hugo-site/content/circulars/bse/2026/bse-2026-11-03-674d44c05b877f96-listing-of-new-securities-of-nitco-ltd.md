## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 65,04,065 new equity shares of Nitco Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532722) with effect from November 4, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Nitco Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532722)
- **Number of Shares:** 65,04,065 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 92.25/- per share (including premium of Rs. 82.25/-)
- **ISIN:** INE858F01012
- **Distribution Nos.:** 240566106 to 247070170
- **Allotment Date:** 23/07/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to May 7, 2028
- **Trading Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. This is a routine corporate securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution numbers as specified by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 23, 2026
- **Trading Commencement Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 7, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increase in paid-up equity capital of the company due to warrant conversion by promoters. Minimal direct impact on broader market operations.
