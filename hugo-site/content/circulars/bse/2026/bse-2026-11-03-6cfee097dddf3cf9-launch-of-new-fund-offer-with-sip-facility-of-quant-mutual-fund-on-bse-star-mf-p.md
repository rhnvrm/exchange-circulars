## Summary

Quant Mutual Fund is launching the Quant Silver ETF FOF NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from November 04, 2026, to November 06, 2026. The notice outlines scheme details, ISINs, and operational guidelines for members.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Quant Silver ETF FOF (Regular and Direct plans across IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth options).
- **NFO Period:** November 04, 2026, to November 06, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 06, 2026, for lumpsm purchases).
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026.
- **ISINs:** INF966L01FL6, INF966L01FM4, INF966L01FK8, INF966L01FI2, INF966L01FJ0, INF966L01FH4.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 11, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 06, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** November 04, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** November 06, 2026 (Midnight for Lumpsum)
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** November 06, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Clear Funds Cut-off:** November 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with structured access to Quant Silver ETF FOF units through the BSE StAR MF platform with clear settlement and funding guidelines.
