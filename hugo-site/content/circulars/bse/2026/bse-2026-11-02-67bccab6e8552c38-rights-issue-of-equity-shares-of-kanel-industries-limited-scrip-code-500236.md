## Summary

KANEL INDUSTRIES LIMITED has fixed November 13, 2026, as the Record Date for its upcoming Rights Issue of equity shares. Trading members and investors are informed that transactions in the company's equity shares will be on an ex-rights basis starting from the same date.

## Key Points

- **Issue Ratio**: 54 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each for every 10 equity shares held.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 39.95 per share (including a premium of Rs. 29.95 per share), payable entirely on application.
- **Record Date**: November 13, 2026.
- **Ex-Rights Date**: November 13, 2026.
- **Scrip Code**: 500236.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure transactions are executed on an ex-rights basis from November 13, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: November 13, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date**: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Share price is subject to price adjustment on the ex-rights date.
