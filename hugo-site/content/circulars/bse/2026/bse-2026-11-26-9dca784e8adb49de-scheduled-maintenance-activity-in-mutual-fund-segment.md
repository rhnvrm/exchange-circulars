## Summary

BSE has announced a scheduled maintenance activity for the BSE StAR MF Version 1.0 platform on Saturday, November 28, 2026, from 06:00 AM to 11:00 AM. During this timeframe, access to the platform via both the web portal and API interfaces will be unavailable.

## Key Points

- **Activity:** Scheduled maintenance for BSE StAR MF Version 1.0.
- **Date & Time:** Saturday, November 28, 2026, from 06:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
- **Affected Interfaces:** Web portal (https://bsestarmf.in/Index.aspx) and API connections.
- **Action Required:** Members are advised to notify internal technical teams and vendors to plan their activities accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants utilizing BSE StAR MF must ensure that automated scripts, API calls, and manual operations are paused or rescheduled around the maintenance window to prevent connection errors.

## Important Dates

- **Maintenance Date:** November 28, 2026 (06:00 AM to 11:00 AM)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary downtime during a Saturday morning window, resulting in minimal to no impact on live market trading operations.
