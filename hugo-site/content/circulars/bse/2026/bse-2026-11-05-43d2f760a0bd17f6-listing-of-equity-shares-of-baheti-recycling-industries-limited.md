## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Baheti Recycling Industries Limited effective from Friday, November 6, 2026. The shares are admitted in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Baheti Recycling Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 545019
- ISIN: INE029Q01017
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: BAHETI
- Total Equity Shares: 1,04,46,905 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Nos. 1 to 10446905).
- Market Lot: 1 (One) share in dematerialised form.
- Registered Office: A-2/3 L R Apartment, Opp: Police Commissioner Office, Shahibaug, Ahmedabad– 380004, Gujarat.
- Registrar: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Physical shares enfaced with non-transferability and demat shares locked-in in depository records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must execute trades in unit market lot (1 share) in dematerialised form.
- Adherence to all applicable BSE and SEBI listing and trading regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and trading liquidity for the equity shares of Baheti Recycling Industries Limited on the BSE exchange.
