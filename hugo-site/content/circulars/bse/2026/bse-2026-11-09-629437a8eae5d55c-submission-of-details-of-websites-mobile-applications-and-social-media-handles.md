## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all registered Research Analysts (RAs) to submit or update the details of their websites, mobile applications, and social media handles via the Exchange portal in compliance with SEBI circulars and previous Exchange notices. The deadline for completion is November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Facility available on the Exchange portal under the “Digital Platform” option in existing login.
- Pre-submitted details will be auto-populated for verification and modification; blank fields appear if unpopulated previously.
- Reported digital platforms will be considered for whitelisting with Meta and Google.
- Links must point to main landing pages or profiles, not individual posts, reels, or stories.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of disclosure and registration requirements for digital platforms used by Research Analysts for research services activities, pursuant to SEBI Circular No. HO/(79)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC dated February 26, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All registered Research Analysts must log in to the Exchange portal and furnish/verify details of their websites, mobile applications, and social media handles.
- Ensure information is accurate, complete, and up to date.
- Non-reporting within the stipulated timeline may lead to exclusion from whitelisting and potential deletion, removal, or regulatory action.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 9, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory oversight and standardizes digital channel disclosures for Research Analysts, facilitating official platform whitelisting with major tech providers (Meta and Google).
