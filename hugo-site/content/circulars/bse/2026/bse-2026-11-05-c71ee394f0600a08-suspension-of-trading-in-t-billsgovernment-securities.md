## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific State Development Loans (SDLs) / Government Securities due to their maturity, effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: 757GUJSDL26 (ISIN: IN1520220154).
- Effective date of suspension: November 06, 2026.
- Reason: Maturity and redemption of the security (DR- 751/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine maturity of a state government security with minimal direct market impact.
