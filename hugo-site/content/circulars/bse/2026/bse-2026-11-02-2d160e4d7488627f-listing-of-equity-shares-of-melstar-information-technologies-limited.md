## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Melstar Information Technologies Limited effective from November 04, 2026, under the T Group (Trade-to-Trade segment). The total paid-up equity shares stand at 27,93,661 following capital reduction and preferential allotment under an NCLT-approved Resolution Plan pursuant to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 532307
- **ISIN:** INE817A01050
- **Group/Segment:** T Group (Trade-to-Trade)
- **Total Securities:** 27,93,661 fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Breakup of Shares:** 
  - 1,51,661 fully paid-up equity shares allotted post capital reduction (public shareholders).
  - 26,42,000 fully paid-up equity shares allotted on preferential basis to the Successful Resolution Professional / Resolution Applicant.
- **Promoter Shareholding:** Entire shareholding of erstwhile promoters and promoter group extinguished.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the NCLT-approved Resolution Plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, approved via orders dated November 01, 2023, June 25, 2024, and August 09, 2024.
- Capital restructuring reducing issued, subscribed, and paid-up share capital from Rs. 14,28,31,390/- (1,42,83,139 shares) to Rs. 2,79,36,610/- (27,93,661 shares).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note trading availability in the Trade-to-Trade segment (T Group).
- Inclusion in the special pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per SEBI Circulars dated January 20, 2012.
- Shares allotted in dematerialized mode credited under temporary ISIN as per SEBI circular dated August 02, 2012.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Order Date:** November 01, 2023 (read with June 25, 2024 and August 09, 2024)
- **Record Date:** August 30, 2024
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restoration of trading in Melstar Information Technologies Limited under Trade-to-Trade segment following corporate insolvency resolution process, enabling liquidity for restructured equity holding.
