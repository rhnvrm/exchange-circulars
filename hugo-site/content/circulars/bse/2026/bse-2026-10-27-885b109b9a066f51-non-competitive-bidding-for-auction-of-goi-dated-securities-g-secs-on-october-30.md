## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for October 30, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four dated securities are offered for auction: 6.20% GS 2029, 6.57% GS 2033, 7.63% GS 2056, and 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056.
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Minimum subscription size is Rs. 10,000 and in multiples thereof, with a maximum amount of Rs. 2 crores per security/bidder.

## Regulatory Changes

- Participation is facilitated under the non-competitive bidding facility for Government Securities conducted by RBI as per established guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the iBBS web-based platform.
- Pay-in and obligation related queries must be directed to ICCL Operations.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date (Members & Direct Investors): October 27, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): October 29, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 30, 2026, till 8:00 am
- Auction Date: October 30, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables eligible members and retail/direct investors to participate smoothly in the GoI dated securities auction with defined subscription limits and timelines.
