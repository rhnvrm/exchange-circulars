## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 3,06,000 new equity shares of Retaggio Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 544391) with effect from November 20, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Retaggio Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544391
- ISIN: INE0KWJ01014
- Number of Shares: 3,06,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 16/- (Issue Price: Rs. 26/-).
- Allotment Date: 12/05/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 19230161 to 19536160
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 15/06/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with lock-in requirements up to June 15, 2028.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Trading: November 20, 2026
- Allotment Date: May 12, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: June 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital of the company following warrant conversion by promoters. Minimal direct impact on general market liquidity.
