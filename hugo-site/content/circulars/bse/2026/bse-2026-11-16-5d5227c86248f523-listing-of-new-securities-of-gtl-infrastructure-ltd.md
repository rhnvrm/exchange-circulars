## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 2,99,637 equity shares of GTL Infrastructure Ltd. with effect from November 17, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** GTL Infrastructure Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 532775
- **ISIN Number:** INE221H01019
- **Number of Shares:** 2,99,637 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par.
- **Reason for Issuance:** Conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).
- **Effective Date:** November 17, 2026.
- **Date of Allotment:** July 29, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 12809111896 to 12809411532.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that the newly allotted shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving equity capital expansion due to FCCB conversion with minimal immediate market impact.
