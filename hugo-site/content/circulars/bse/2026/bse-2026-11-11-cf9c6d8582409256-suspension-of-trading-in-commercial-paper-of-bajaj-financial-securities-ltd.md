## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Bajaj Financial Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 732107, ISIN: INE01C314GL9) due to the redemption of the commercial paper. Trading members are advised not to deal in this commercial paper as the record date and ex-date are set for November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bajaj Financial Securities Limited
- Scrip ID: BFSL-12-11-26-CP
- Scrip Code: 732107
- ISIN: INE01C314GL9
- Record Date: 12/11/2026
- Ex-Date: 12/11/2026
- Purpose: Redemption of Commercial Paper

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Paper of Bajaj Financial Securities Limited.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 12/11/2026
- Ex-Date: 12/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving the redemption and suspension of trading of commercial paper with no broader market impact.
