## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers (CPs) issued on a private placement basis by multiple companies on the debt market segment effective November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- New commercial papers admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective November 5, 2026.
- Issuing companies include Mirae Asset Financial Services (India) Private Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Ugro Capital Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, and Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited.
- Traded in dematerialised form with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing and trading admission of privately placed commercial papers in accordance with BSE debt segment guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form under the specified ISIN numbers and standard denominations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Allotment Dates: November 04, 2026 / November 05, 2026
- Redemption/Interest Payment Dates: February 2027 to March 2027 (varying by issuance)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and trading venue for privately placed commercial debt instruments issued by corporate entities.
