## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 12 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Twelve companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Trading commences on November 5, 2026.
- Affected companies include Alivus Life Sciences, Clean Max Enviro, Fujiyama Power, ICICI Bank, Indoco Remedies, Intellect Design Arena, Medplus Health, Raymond, SBI Cards, Sterlite Technologies, Tata Consumer Products, and Tata Motors.
- Lock-in status for all listed tranches is Not Applicable (NA).

## Regulatory Changes

Routine issuance and listing of equity shares pursuant to employee stock option schemes under applicable SEBI and exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with exchange guidelines regarding trading of newly listed ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marginal increase in paid-up equity capital of the participating companies with no immediate adverse market impact.
