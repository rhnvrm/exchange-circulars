## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in debentures of 360 ONE Prime Limited (Scrip Code: 974688) with effect from October 30, 2026, following the non-exercise of the put option by the company.

## Key Points

- **Company:** 360 ONE Prime Limited
- **Security:** Debentures (Scrip Code: 974688)
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026 (DR-746/2026-2027)
- **Reason:** Put option was not exercised by the company
- **Reference:** Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260615-47 dated June 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice regarding trading resumption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note that the debentures are available for trading effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **June 15, 2026:** Previous notice regarding record date (Notice No. 20260615-47)
- **June 17, 2026:** Record date for redemption
- **October 30, 2026:** Resumption of trading effective date

## Impact Assessment

Restoration of trading availability and liquidity for investors holding the debentures of 360 ONE Prime Limited under Scrip Code 974688.
