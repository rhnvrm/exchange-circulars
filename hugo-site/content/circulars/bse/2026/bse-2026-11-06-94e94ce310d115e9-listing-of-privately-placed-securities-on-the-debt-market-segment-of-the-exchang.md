## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by various companies on the debt market segment effective November 06, 2026. The securities are traded in dematerialised form with standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs and 1 paise tick size.

## Key Points

- **TATA STEEL LIMITED**: Issued 20,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732894, Scrip ID: TSL051126, ISIN: INE081A14HU8) at an issue price of Rs. 495,650.00 with a 7.45% p.a. interest rate, maturing on 18/12/2026.
- **360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited**: Issued multiple tranches of Commercial Papers (Scrip Codes: 732895, 732896, 732897; ISINs: INE0TQI14715, INE0TQI14723, INE0TQI14731) with interest rates ranging from 8.00% to 9.00% p.a. and maturities through November 2027.
- **Infina Finance Private Limited**: Issued Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732898, Scrip ID: IFPL051126, ISIN: INE879F14OB0) at 8.60% p.a. interest rate.
- **Trading Parameters**: Traded in dematerialised form in standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- **Issuing and Paying Agent**: HDFC Bank Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; this notice pertains to standard debt segment listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to dematerialised trading rules and standard market denominations for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: 05/11/2026
- **Listing/Effective Date**: 06/11/2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from 18/12/2026 to 04/11/2027 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for privately placed commercial debt instruments issued by corporate entities.
