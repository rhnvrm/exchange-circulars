## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing of new equity shares of Kati Patang Lifestyle Ltd effective from November 4, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kati Patang Lifestyle Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 531126
- ISIN: INE237C01016
- Securities: 12,07,842 Equity shares of Rs.10/- each issued on a rights basis (conversion of partly paid to fully paid-up).
- Distinctive Numbers: 43014853 to 44222694
- Effective Date: November 4, 2026
- Status: Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 3, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action enabling trading of converted fully paid-up equity shares with no direct impact on overall market operations.
