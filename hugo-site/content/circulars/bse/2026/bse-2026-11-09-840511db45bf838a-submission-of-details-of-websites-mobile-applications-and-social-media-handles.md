## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all registered Investment Advisers (IAs) to report and update details of their websites, mobile applications, and social media handles via the Exchange portal on or before November 25, 2026. This is pursuant to SEBI Circular No. HO/(79)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC dated February 26, 2026, and preceding Exchange notices. Reported digital platforms will be considered for official whitelisting with Meta and Google.

## Key Points

- Mandatory requirement for all registered Investment Advisers.
- Covers all websites, mobile applications, and social media handles used for investment advisory activities.
- Reported platforms will be evaluated for whitelisting with Meta and Google.
- Deadline for completion is November 25, 2026.
- Failure to report within the timeline may result in lack of whitelisting and potential regulatory or platform removal actions.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforces the disclosure and verification framework for digital platforms used by Investment Advisers as mandated by SEBI and BSE circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Log in to the Exchange portal and access the 'Digital Platform' option.
- Verify auto-populated existing details or furnish new information for websites, mobile apps, and social media handles.
- Provide links strictly for main landing pages/profiles rather than individual posts, reels, or stories.
- Complete the submission on or before November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investment Advisers must comply within the stipulated timeline to ensure regulatory standing and secure necessary digital platform whitelisting with major tech providers like Meta and Google.
