## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Light Microfinance Private Limited on the debt market segment effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Light Microfinance Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 978359, Scrip ID: 1145LMPL28
- ISIN Number: INE366T07212
- Quantity: 30,000 securities
- Face Value / Issue Price: Rs. 10,000.00
- Interest Rate: 11.45% p.a., payable quarterly
- Date of Allotment: 30/10/2026
- Date of Redemption: 12/11/2028
- Credit Rating: CRISIL BBB+/Stable

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification for privately placed debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed that the securities will trade only in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 02, 2026
- Interest Payment Dates: Quarterly from February 12, 2027 to November 12, 2028
- Date of Redemption: November 12, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and liquidity for the privately placed debt securities of Light Microfinance Private Limited on the BSE debt market segment.
