## Summary

BSE has announced surveillance action regarding the circulation of unsolicited videos and messages on social media platforms, placing specific shortlisted securities under stringent framework measures similar to Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) Stage IV. Additionally, certain securities specified in Annexure II will exit the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities are shortlisted based on the circulation of unsolicited videos/messages and unusual price or volume movements.
- Imposition of measures comparable to GSM Stage IV on shortlisted scrips.
- Certain securities specified in Annexure II will move out of the framework effective November 30, 2026.
- Actions are taken strictly for market surveillance purposes and do not constitute adverse actions against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance measures for scrips impacted by unauthorized social media promotions, aligning with existing exchange surveillance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trade for Trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower effective November 30, 2026.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of trade value to be deposited by buyers effective December 01, 2026.
- Trading permitted only once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week) effective December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- November 30, 2026: Effective date for Trade for Trade with 5% price band and removal of Annexure II securities from the framework.
- December 01, 2026: Effective date for 100% ASD deposit requirement.
- December 07, 2026: Effective date for weekly trading restriction.

## Impact Assessment

High liquidity and operational impact on market participants trading the shortlisted scrips, severely restricting trading frequency and substantially raising capital commitment through 100% ASD.
