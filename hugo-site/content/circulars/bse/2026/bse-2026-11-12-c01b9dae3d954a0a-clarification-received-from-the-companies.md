## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice (Notice No. 20261112-22) dated November 12, 2026, addressing significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips. Clarifications have been received from 6 companies, while replies are awaited from 23 other companies to ensure investors have timely and relevant market information.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261112-22, issued on November 12, 2026 by the Surveillance Department (DOSS).
- Clarifications received from 6 companies including Shine Fashions (India) Ltd, Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd, Avantel Ltd, Bombay Coated and Special Steels Ltd, Shakti Pumps India Ltd, and Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 23 companies including Astec Lifesciences Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory rules enacted; this represents routine surveillance enforcement to ensure prompt disclosures by listed entities experiencing unusual market activity.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies experiencing significant stock price or volume fluctuations must promptly respond to Exchange queries and disclose all relevant price-sensitive information to maintain market integrity.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes market transparency and investor protection by highlighting companies undergoing unusual trading activity and tracking compliance with exchange clarification requests.
