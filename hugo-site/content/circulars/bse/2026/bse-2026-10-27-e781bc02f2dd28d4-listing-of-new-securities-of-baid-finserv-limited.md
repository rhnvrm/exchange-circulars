## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 72,04,099 new equity shares of Baid Finserv Limited (Scrip Code: 511724) effective from October 28, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Baid Finserv Limited
- Scrip Code: 511724
- ISIN: INE020D01022
- Number of Shares: 72,04,099 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 13.10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 15.10/-
- Allotment Date: August 26, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 28, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 72,04,099 shares (Distinctive Nos. 154888108 to 162092206) locked-in up to May 31, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to take note of the new securities and their lock-in status.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 26, 2026
- Listing / Trading Date: October 28, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The expansion of the equity base through preferential warrant conversion increases paid-up capital, with new shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
