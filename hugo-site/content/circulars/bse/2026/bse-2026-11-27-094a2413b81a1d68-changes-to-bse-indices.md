## Summary

BSE has announced changes to several of its indices resulting from a fast-tracked IPO review, effective at the open of trading on Friday, December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Jio Platforms Ltd (Ticker: 545004) has been added to major indices including BSE AllCap, BSE 1000, BSE 500, BSE LargeCap, BSE LargeMidCap, BSE Total Market, and BSE 200.
- Ugro Capital Ltd (Ticker: 511742), Latent View Analytics Ltd (Ticker: 543398), Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd (Ticker: 543237), SJVN Ltd (Ticker: 533206), and P.I. Industries Ltd (Ticker: 523642) are among the stocks undergoing additions or exclusions across specific indices.
- Changes are effective from the open of Friday, December 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine index composition updates pursuant to Notice No. 20261022-22 issued by BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. under fast-tracked IPO review guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, index funds, and algorithmic trading systems must update their benchmark indices and tracking portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: December 04, 2026 (Market Open)

## Impact Assessment

Constituent changes will affect index funds, ETFs, and institutional portfolios tracking BSE indices, leading to portfolio rebalancing adjustments around the effective date.
