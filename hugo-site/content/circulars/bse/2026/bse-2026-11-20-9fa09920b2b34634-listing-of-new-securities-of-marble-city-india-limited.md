## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 3,85,000 equity shares of Marble City India Limited with effect from November 23, 2026. These shares were issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Marble City India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 531281
- **ISIN:** INE807H01023
- **Security Details:** 3,85,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 95.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 100.50/-)
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters on a preferential basis
- **Effective Date of Trading:** November 23, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 25645109 to 26030108

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with applicable lock-in periods and distribution numbers for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Lock-in Details & Expiry Dates:**
  - 15,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 25645109 to 25660108): Lock-in upto May 30, 2027
  - 2,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 25660109 to 25860108): Lock-in upto May 29, 2027
  - 1,70,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 25860109 to 26030108): Lock-in upto May 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion via warrant conversion with standard lock-in restrictions, resulting in negligible immediate market impact.
