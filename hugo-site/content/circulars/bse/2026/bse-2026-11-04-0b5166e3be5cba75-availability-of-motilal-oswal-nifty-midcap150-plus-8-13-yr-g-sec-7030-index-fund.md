## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund has announced the reopening of the Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026.
- **Schemes Involved:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HR4)
  - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HQ6)
- **Facilities Available:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate ongoing transactions for investors starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume regular transactions and systematic facilities in the specified Motilal Oswal index fund scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform.
