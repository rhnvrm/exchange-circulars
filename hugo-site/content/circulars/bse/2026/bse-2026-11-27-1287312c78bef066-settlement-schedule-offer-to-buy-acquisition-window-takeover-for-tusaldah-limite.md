## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule and procedural guidelines for the Offer to Buy - Acquisition Window (Takeover) of Tusaldah Limited, scheduled to take place from December 02, 2026, to December 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026144
- Bid Start Date: 02/12/2026
- Bid End Date: 15/12/2026
- Obligation Download: 18/12/2026
- Settlement Date: 21/12/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL and NSDL).
- Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026144 must be specified by members and custodians in the depository system.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Governed under existing revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure that the early pay-in effect is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for bidding and custodian confirmation.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 02/12/2026
- Bid End Date: 15/12/2026
- Obligation Download: 18/12/2026
- Settlement Date: 21/12/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly execution, timely bidding, and smooth clearing and settlement of the takeover offer for Tusaldah Limited.
