## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Shraddha Prime Projects Limited starting Thursday, November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shraddha Prime Projects Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 751153
- **Scrip ID:** SPPL-RE1
- **ISIN:** INE311M20026
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** November 12, 2026 to November 17, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 12, 2026 to November 20, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in Rights Entitlements is permitted on the Exchange under Group R on a trade-for-trade basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about RE trading, ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares, and include a mandatory disclaimer in contract notes regarding application or renunciation before the issue closes. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse after the issue closing date.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 12, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders receive REs that can be traded or used to subscribe to the rights issue. Transactions settle on a T+1 rolling settlement basis in dematerialized form.
