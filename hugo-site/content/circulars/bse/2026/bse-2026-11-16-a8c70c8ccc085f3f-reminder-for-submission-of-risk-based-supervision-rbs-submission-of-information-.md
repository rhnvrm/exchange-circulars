## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all active trading members regarding the submission of information for Risk Based Supervision (RBS) for the period April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The submission deadline is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Submission of RBS data is mandatory for all active members (those who executed at least one trade during April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026).
- Submissions must be made through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).
- Failure to submit by the due date will attract penal charges and/or disciplinary action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reminder of existing compliance mandates under Exchange Notice No. 20261001-34 and 20260417-45.

## Compliance Requirements

- Active trading members must compile and upload the required RBS information via BEFS.
- Ensure submission is completed on or before November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Period: April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026
- Due Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Failure to comply results in financial penalties and potential disciplinary actions by the Exchange.
