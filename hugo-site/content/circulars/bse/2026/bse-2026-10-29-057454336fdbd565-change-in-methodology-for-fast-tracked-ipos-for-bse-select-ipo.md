## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt Ltd (formerly Asia Index Pvt. Ltd.) has announced a change in methodology regarding Fast-Tracked IPOs for the BSE Select IPO index, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026.
- **Eligibility Criteria:** To qualify for fast-tracked inclusion, an IPO must rank among the top 30 companies based on average daily total market capitalization.
- **Listing History:** A minimum listing history of one month is required.
- **Notice Period:** Qualifying companies are added to the index with five days' notice to clients where possible.
- **Data Reference Period:** One month from the IPO listing date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of formal methodology guidelines for fast-tracked IPO inclusions in the BSE Select IPO index, specifying clear market capitalization and listing history requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clients, index watchers, and market participants should take note of the revised inclusion criteria for index tracking and modeling.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Primarily affects index compilation and maintenance procedures by BSE Index Services and benefits stakeholders tracking newly listed significant companies.
