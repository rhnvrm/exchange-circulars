## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying inclusion criteria, movement across stages, and removal from the framework effective November 11 and November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 12, 2026, on all open positions as on November 11, 2026 and new positions created from November 12, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 11, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- The framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of strict surveillance controls including 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auctions for designated securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must account for enhanced margin requirements and trading restrictions on affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II restrictions and movement out of framework: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced trading controls and margin requirements reduce speculative activity and increase settlement safety for shortlisted securities.
