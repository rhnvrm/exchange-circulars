## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I require an applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II require an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Surveillance measures apply to all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created from November 19, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely a market surveillance action and is not an adverse regulatory action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II frameworks in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.
- Mandatory enhancement of margin rates for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the collection of enhanced margins (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on applicable open and new positions.
- Members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in the shortlisted securities will face higher margin obligations (up to 100%), impacting leverage and trading liquidity for those specific scrips.
