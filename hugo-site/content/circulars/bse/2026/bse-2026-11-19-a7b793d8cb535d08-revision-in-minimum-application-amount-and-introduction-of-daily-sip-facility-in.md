## Summary

UNIFI Mutual Fund has issued a notice regarding the revision of minimum application amounts and the introduction of the Daily Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility for select schemes, effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 19, 2026.
- Applicable Schemes:
  - Unifi Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund
  - Unifi Flexi Cap Fund
  - Unifi Liquid Fund
- Changes include revisions to minimum application amounts for SIP, Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Introduction of the Daily SIP facility.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational update by UNIFI AMC regarding transaction parameters and introduction of new frequency options for systematic investments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants are required to take note of the revised minimum application amounts and new SIP options while submitting SIP, XSIP, AMC STP, or SWP registrations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to investors and distributors transacting in the specified UNIFI Mutual Fund schemes, providing greater flexibility with Daily SIP options.
