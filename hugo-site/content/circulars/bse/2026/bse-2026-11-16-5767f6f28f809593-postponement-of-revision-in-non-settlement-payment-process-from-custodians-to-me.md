## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has announced the postponement of the revision in the Non-Settlement Payment Process from Custodians to Members under the T+1 Settlement Cycle, which was originally scheduled to be implemented on November 16, 2026. The existing process will continue until further notice.

## Key Points

- Implementation of the revised Non-Settlement Payment Process between custodians and members is postponed until further notice.
- Originally scheduled for implementation from November 16, 2026 (referencing Notice No. 20261105-1).
- The existing payment process remains in full force and effect.
- Members and custodians can reach out to the BSECL Help Desk for clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; continuation of existing operational procedures for non-settlement payments under T+1 settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians should continue following the existing non-settlement payment process without adopting the previously announced revisions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 November 2026
- Original Implementation Date: 16 November 2026 (Postponed)
- Revised Implementation Date: To be announced

## Impact Assessment

- Operational continuity is maintained as current workflows remain unchanged, avoiding immediate process disruptions for custodians and members.
