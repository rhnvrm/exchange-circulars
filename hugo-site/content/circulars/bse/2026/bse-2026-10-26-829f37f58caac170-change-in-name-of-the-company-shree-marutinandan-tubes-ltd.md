## Summary

Shree Marutinandan Tubes Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding a change in the company name. The company will now be known as Shree Marutinandan Industries Limited, and the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated accordingly effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Shree Marutinandan Tubes Ltd
- New Name: Shree Marutinandan Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544083
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate name change notification processed by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems, databases, and client records with the new company name and BOLT Plus identifiers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market operations, affecting only display names and scrip identifiers on trading terminals.
