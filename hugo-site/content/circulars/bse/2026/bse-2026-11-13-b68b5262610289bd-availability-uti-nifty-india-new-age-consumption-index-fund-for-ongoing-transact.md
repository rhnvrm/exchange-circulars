## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund has announced the availability of ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities for the UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026.
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Eligible Schemes:**
  - UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH91)
  - UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AI09)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification regarding mutual fund transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants using the BSE StAR MF platform can facilitate transactions in the mentioned schemes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables seamless trading and systematic investment operations for investors and distributors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
