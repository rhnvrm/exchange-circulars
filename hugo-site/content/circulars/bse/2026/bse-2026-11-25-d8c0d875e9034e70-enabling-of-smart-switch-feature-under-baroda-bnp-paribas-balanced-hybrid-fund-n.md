## Summary

BSE has announced the enabling of the SMART Switch feature under the Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from November 25, 2026, until December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- The SMART Switch feature is available through user interfaces, bulk uploads, and APIs for eligible Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund schemes.
- Applicable exclusively for lump sum subscriptions in non-Demat mode.
- Transactions under the SMART Switch feature will be accepted up to 1:00 PM on December 04, 2026.
- Switch out transactions at the AMC/RTA level are processed on a FIFO basis for newly purchased units.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized operational guidelines and switch processing workflows have been introduced for NFO SMART Switch transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members utilizing Direct APIs, API-based platforms, or bulk upload options must use appropriate scheme credentials strictly in accordance with investor requirements.
- Purchase transactions must be reported to the RTA on the transaction day (with switches reported on December 08, 2026).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026
- Closing Date & Time: December 04, 2026 up to 1:00 PM
- RTA Switch Reporting Date: December 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines participation in the Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO for investors and members on the BSE StAR MF platform by defining clear automation logic and operational timelines.
