## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the Mutual Fund segment covering the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The circular outlines settlement numbers, transaction dates, fund pay-in, and payout dates for both liquid and non-liquid mutual fund subscriptions as well as redemptions.

## Key Points

- Covers mutual fund subscription and redemption settlement schedules for December 2026 (Settlement Nos. 2627165 to 2627186).
- Funds pay-out to AMCs for Non-Liquid transactions processed will happen before 3:00 PM on T day.
- Funds pay-out to AMCs for NFO (MF) schemes will happen on T day based on fund confirmation received by BSECL until 6:00 PM on T day.
- Liquid fund subscriptions have same-day payout (T day payout).

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Routine operational schedule).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and AMCs must adhere to the specified pay-in and payout timelines for mutual fund transactions during December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Period Start Date: December 1, 2026
- Period End Date: December 31, 2026
- First Settlement Date (2627165): December 1, 2026
- Last Settlement Date (2627186): December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational clearing and settlement for mutual fund transactions on the BSE platform for the specified month without any unexpected market disruption.
