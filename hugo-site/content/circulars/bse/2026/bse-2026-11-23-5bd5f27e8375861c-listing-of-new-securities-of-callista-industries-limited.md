## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for 10,00,000 new equity shares of Callista Industries Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Callista Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 539335
- ISIN: INE714Q01014
- Security Details: 10,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- Distribution Nos.: 3046589 to 4046588
- Allotment Date: June 22, 2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of securities pursuant to preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in up to May 31, 2027, for all 10,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 3046589 - 4046588).

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: November 25, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of preferential shares expanding the paid-up equity capital base of Callista Industries Limited with established lock-in compliance.
