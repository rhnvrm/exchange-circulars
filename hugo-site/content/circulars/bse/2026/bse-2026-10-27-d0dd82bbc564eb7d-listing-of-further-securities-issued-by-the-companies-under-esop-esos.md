## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 7 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Wednesday, October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 7
- Effective date for trading: October 28, 2026
- Companies involved: Deccan Gold Mines Limited, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, M & B Engineering Limited, PDS Limited, Rubicon Research Limited, and Suzlon Energy Limited.
- All securities are issued under lock-in status 'NA' (Not Applicable).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the listing and admission of these further securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of the respective companies with negligible market impact.
