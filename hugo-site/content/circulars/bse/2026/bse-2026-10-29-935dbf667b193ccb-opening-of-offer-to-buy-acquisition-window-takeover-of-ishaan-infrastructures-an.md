## Summary

M/s Misun Pure Lights Private Limited and other co-acquirers, along with Persons Acting in Concert (PACs), have announced an open offer to acquire up to 63,48,500 fully paid-up equity shares (10.30% of the total expanded voting equity share capital) of Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Limited at an offer price of INR 14/- per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Limited.
- **Acquirers & PACs:** Acquirers include Misun Pure Lights Pvt Ltd, Ravi Prakash Bothra, Vaaibhav Bothrra, Rajesh Arora, and Ashish Arora, along with 8 PACs including Bothra Corp LLP.
- **Offer Price:** INR 14/- per fully paid-up equity share in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 63,48,500 equity shares representing 10.30% of the total expanded voting equity share capital.
- **Tendering Period:** From November 02, 2026 to November 16, 2026 (excluding SEBI holiday on November 10, 2026).
- **Regulatory Basis:** Regulations 3(1) & 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeover regulations as per SEBI circulars and exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are required to facilitate the acquisition of shares through the stock exchange acquisition window for tender offers under takeover guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Open Offer Start Date:** November 02, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (Exclusion):** November 10, 2026
- **Open Offer Closure Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Limited an opportunity to tender their shares and exit or participate in the open offer takeover acquisition window.
