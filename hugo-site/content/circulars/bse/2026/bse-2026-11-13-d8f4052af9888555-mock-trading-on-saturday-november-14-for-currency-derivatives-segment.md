## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments on Saturday, November 14, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test their trading applications, risk management software, and new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026, from Primary Site (PR).
- Applies to Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments.
- Helps members fulfill regulatory testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80 will be released.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading notice facilitating compliance with existing SEBI software testing mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems can participate actively in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill software testing requirements. Feedback on the session must be provided by 4:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026
- Log-in Time: 10:15 am to 11:00 am
- Continuous Trading: 11:00 am to 03:30 pm
- Trade Modification: Up to 04:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The session is strictly for testing and familiarization purposes without any financial or settlement implications.
