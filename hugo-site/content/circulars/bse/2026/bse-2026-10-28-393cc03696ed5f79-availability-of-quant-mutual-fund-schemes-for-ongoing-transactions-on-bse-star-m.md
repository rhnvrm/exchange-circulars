## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of Quant Mutual Fund schemes for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes made available: Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund, Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund, and Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund.
- Facilities enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the aforementioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment options for investors on the BSE StAR MF platform by adding Quant Mutual Fund index schemes.
