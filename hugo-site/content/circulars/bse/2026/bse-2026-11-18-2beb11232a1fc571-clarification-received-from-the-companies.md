## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past and sought clarification from the respective companies to ensure investors have the latest relevant information. Replies have been received from several companies, while responses from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Replies received from 7 companies including Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Gujarat Raffia Industries Ltd, Krupalu Metals Ltd, IKIO Technologies Ltd, Triveni Glass Ltd, Sanghvi Brands Ltd, and Dhanlaxmi Bank Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 16 companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Covance Softsol Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, and others.
- Surveillance department action aimed at maintaining market integrity and investor protection.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine surveillance and clarification notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies observed with significant price/volume movements are required to submit clarifications to the Exchange as mandated under SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances transparency for investors regarding scrips experiencing unusual market activity and monitors company compliance on disclosures.
