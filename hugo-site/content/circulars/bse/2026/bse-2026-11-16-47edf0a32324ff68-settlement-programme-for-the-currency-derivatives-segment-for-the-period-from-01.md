## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. (BSECL) has issued the settlement programme for the currency derivatives segment covering trade dates from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Details the complete schedule mapping daily trade dates to their respective settlement dates for December 2026.
- Specifies the expiry date (last trading day) for December 2026 currency future contracts as December 29, 2026.
- Sets the final settlement date for December 2026 currency future contracts to December 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard administrative schedule update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clearing members must align their operational workflows, pay-in, and pay-out schedules in accordance with the announced settlement calendar.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Period: December 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026
- Expiry Date (Dec 2026 Currency Futures): December 29, 2026
- Final Settlement Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; operational guidance ensuring predictable settlement cycles for currency derivatives.
