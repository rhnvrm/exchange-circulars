## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision of stages, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective dates for various surveillance actions are set for November 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 09, 2026 on all open positions as on November 08, 2026 and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Movement of securities to higher stages of LT-ASM framework with Lower Price Band applicable w.e.f. November 06, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities in LT-ASM framework to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 09, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 06, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures as per prior framework guidelines, imposing strict margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Notice / Review: 05 Nov 2026
- Higher Stages / Moving Out / Price Band Effective Date: 06 Nov 2026
- 100% Margins / Group Transfers Effective Date: 09 Nov 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities will experience stricter margin norms and price band constraints, mitigating excessive volatility and protecting market integrity.
