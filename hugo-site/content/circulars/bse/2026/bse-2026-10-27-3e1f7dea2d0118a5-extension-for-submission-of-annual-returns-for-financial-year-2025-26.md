## Summary

BSE has extended the due date for the submission of Annual Returns, including the Networth Certificate, Auditor's Report, and Audited Annual Accounts for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, from October 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026. This extension follows the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Due date extended from October 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
- Applies to submission of Networth Certificate, Auditor's Report & Audited Annual Accounts for FY 2025-26.
- Aligned with MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.
- Dual-registered members (BSE and NSE) must submit their Annual Audited Networth Certificate to NSE only, while exclusive BSE members submit to BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) vide General Circular No. 05/2026 extended the due date for filing financial statements and annual returns with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) for FY 2025-26 to December 31, 2026. Concurrently, BSE extended its internal submission deadlines for trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 through the BEFS portal on or before December 31, 2026.
- Failure to submit within the due date or shortfalls/non-submission of revised Networth certificates will invite disciplinary actions and penalties as stipulated in earlier exchange notices (20260417-45 and 20260713-28).
- Exclusive BSE members make submissions to BSE; members registered with NSE must submit their Annual Audited Networth Certificate to NSE only.

## Important Dates

- **October 24, 2026:** MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 issued.
- **October 27, 2026:** BSE Notice issued.
- **December 31, 2026:** Extended due date for submission of Annual Returns for FY 2025-26.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members get additional time (two months) to finalize and submit their audited annual accounts and networth certificates without incurring immediate disciplinary action or late penalties, easing compliance burdens during the filing season.
