## Summary

Invesco Mutual Fund has issued an update regarding the New Fund Offer (NFO) and SIP facility of the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The revised reopening date will be communicated separately, and the scheme remains closed for subscriptions until further notice.

## Key Points

- References previous Notice No. 20261026-26 dated October 26, 2026.
- The revised reopening date for Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund will be announced at a later date.
- The scheme remains closed for subscriptions until further notice.
- Any registrations due during the period of change are liable for rejection by the RTA/AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, distributors, and investors must take note that any subscription or SIP registrations scheduled during the interim closure period are liable for rejection by the Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) or Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Reopening Date: To be communicated separately

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members planning transactions in the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund need to pause submissions until official communication regarding the reopening date is released.
