## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 744 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-744/2026-27, detailing offer entry and matching schedules for October 29, 2026, and pay-in obligations for October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 29, 2026.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 29, 2026.
- Delivery Statement & Report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 29, 2026.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 29, 2026.
- Pay-in of Auction: Friday, October 30, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 29, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 30, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations notice affecting clearing and settlement participants involved in rolling settlement auctions.
