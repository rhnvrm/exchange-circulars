## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of three newly listed companies—M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited, Carver Roboweld Limited, and Ajwa Projects Limited—into their respective BSE indices effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited (Ticker: 545017) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Friday, November 06, 2026.
- Carver Roboweld Limited (Ticker: 545015) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Thursday, November 05, 2026.
- Ajwa Projects Limited (Ticker: 545016) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Thursday, November 05, 2026.
- All three companies commenced listing on BSE effective Wednesday, November 04, 2026.
- Actions have been processed in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance requirements for market participants. For queries, contact bseindex@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2026:** Listing date for all three companies.
- **November 05, 2026:** Effective addition date for Carver Roboweld Limited and Ajwa Projects Limited to BSE SME IPO index.
- **November 06, 2026:** Effective addition date for M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited to BSE IPO index.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal operational impact, affecting only index tracking mechanisms and end-of-day corporate action data files.
