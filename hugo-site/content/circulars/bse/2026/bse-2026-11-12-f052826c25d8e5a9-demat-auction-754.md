## Summary

BSE Limited has issued notice regarding Demat Auction 754 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-754/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: November 12, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Friday, November 13, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is removed following the implementation of Uniform Membership structure in Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural auction notice under established settlement frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted.
- Members should refer to the cut-off rate on the Auction offer Entry Screen and corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date / Offer Entry: November 12, 2026
- Auction Pay-in: November 13, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational clearing and settlement process for short deliveries in rolling settlement DR-754/2026-27.
