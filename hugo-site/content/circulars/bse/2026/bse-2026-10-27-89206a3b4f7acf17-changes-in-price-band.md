## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 9 scrips with effect from October 28, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 9 scrips including Dhvija Finance Ltd, Fraser and Company Ltd, Highway Infrastructure Ltd, and others.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 28, 2026.
- Revisions range from 5% to 10% across the affected scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price band limits updated for specified scrips under Surveillance department (DOSS) guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and ensure compliance with the revised price band limits for the listed scrips effective from October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate operational impact for traders and investors holding or trading in the affected scrips to account for the new price movement limits.
