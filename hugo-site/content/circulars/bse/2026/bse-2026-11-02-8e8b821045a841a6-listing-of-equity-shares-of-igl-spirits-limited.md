## Summary

Effective from November 4, 2026, the equity shares of IGL Spirits Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days. The listing is pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement involving the demerger of the Spirit and Biofuel Undertaking of India Glycols Limited.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** IGL Spirits Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545013
- **ISIN:** INE2W6901017
- **Face Value:** Rs. 5/- each fully paid
- **Securities Admitted:** 6,70,26,765 equity shares issued pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Effective Date:** November 4, 2026.
- **Trading Segment:** 'T' Group (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days).
- **Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS):** Applicable as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shares admitted to dealings pursuant to the NCLT Prayagraj Bench order dated July 17, 2026, approving the Scheme of Arrangement between India Glycols Limited and its resulting companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the Trade-for-Trade segment restriction for the first 10 trading days and adhere to SPOS guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 4, 2026
- **NCLT Approval Date:** July 17, 2026
- **Appointed Date:** April 1, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of India Glycols Limited receiving shares via demerger can trade their holdings starting November 4, 2026, subject to initial Trade-for-Trade surveillance measures.
