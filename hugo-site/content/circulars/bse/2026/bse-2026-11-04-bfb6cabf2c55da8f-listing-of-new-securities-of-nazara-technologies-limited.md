## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for 1,42,31,000 new equity shares of Nazara Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 543280, ISIN: INE418L01047) with effect from Thursday, November 5, 2026. These shares are issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Nazara Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 543280
- ISIN: INE418L01047
- Total Shares Listed: 1,42,31,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2.00/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 258/- (Issue Price: Rs. 260/-).
- Basis of Allotment: Preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants to Promoters and Non-Promoters.
- Effective Date of Trading: November 5, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: July 30, 2026 and August 3, 2026.
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 5, 2026.
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: May 6, 2027, May 7, 2027, and May 7, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital of Nazara Technologies Limited following the preferential conversion of warrants, expanding equity participation by promoters and non-promoters.
