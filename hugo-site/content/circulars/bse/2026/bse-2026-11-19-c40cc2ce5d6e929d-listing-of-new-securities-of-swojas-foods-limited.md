## Summary

BSE has notified trading members regarding the listing and permission to trade 16,00,000 new equity shares of Swojas Foods Limited issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swojas Foods Limited (Scrip Code - 530217)
- Security Details: 16,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 6.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 16.50/-)
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Distribution Numbers: 48288313 to 49888312
- ISIN: INE295B01016

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in restrictions applicable to the newly issued shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 24, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity share capital with minimal market impact.
