## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Enhanced margin requirements become effective from November 06, 2026, on open positions as of November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions from November 06, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions with enhanced margin requirements for shortlisted securities in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for all applicable open and new positions effective November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026 (on open positions as of November 05, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Traders and investors holding securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I or II will experience higher margin blocking, affecting leverage and liquidity for the affected scrips.
