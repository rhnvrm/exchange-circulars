## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited (Scrip Code: 543193) with effect from October 28, 2026. A total of 9,97,894 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 104/- upon conversion of warrants issued to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 543193
- ISIN: INE0B1K01014
- Number of Shares: 9,97,894 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 114/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- + Premium Rs. 104/-)
- Allotment Date: 02/07/2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in periods applicable to specific blocks of shares as detailed in the allotment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: 28/10/2026
- Allotment Date: 02/07/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 30/04/2027 and 30/04/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via warrant conversion with specific lock-in periods for promoters and non-promoters.
