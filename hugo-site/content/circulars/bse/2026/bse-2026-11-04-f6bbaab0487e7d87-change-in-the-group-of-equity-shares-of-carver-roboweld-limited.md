## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Carver Roboweld Limited (Scrip Code: 545015) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Thursday, November 19, 2026, in continuation of Notice No. 20261103-32.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Carver Roboweld Limited
- Scrip Code: 545015
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of securities trading segment from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the change in trading segment for order placement and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates regular rolling settlement for the company's equity shares, improving operational liquidity and market access.
