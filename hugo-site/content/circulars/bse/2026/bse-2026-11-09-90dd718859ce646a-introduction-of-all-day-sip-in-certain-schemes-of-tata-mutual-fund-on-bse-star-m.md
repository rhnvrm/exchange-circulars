## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued an update regarding the introduction of the All Day SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) facility for certain schemes of Tata Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately. This follows earlier notice No. 20261102-40 dated November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- All Day SIP facility is now available for selected Tata Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Affected schemes include TATA GOLD ETF FUND OF FUND, TATA NIFTY 50 INDEX FUND, TATA NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 INDEX FUND, TATA NIFTY NEXT 50 INDEX FUND, and TATA SILVER ETF FUND OF FUND.
- Effective date: Immediate.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding platform feature availability for specific fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors are advised to note the inclusion of these schemes under the All Day SIP facility on BSE StAR MF.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances investment flexibility and operational convenience for investors setting up Systematic Investment Plans across these specific Tata Mutual Fund schemes.
