## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is launching the Kotak Nifty Pharma Index Fund NFO along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Kotak Nifty Pharma Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans available for Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout).
- **NFO Period:** November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 03, 2026.
- **ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: `INF174KA1I54`
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF174KA1I70`
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF174KA1I62`
  - Direct Plan - Growth: `INF174KA1I21`
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF174KA1I47`
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF174KA1I39`

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 03, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** November 16, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** November 30, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases)
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** November 30, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 03, 2026
