## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies and issuers have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs), PTCs, and bonds. Trading in these securities will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261123-33, dated November 23, 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Affected issuers include Unigold Finance Limited, Finnable Credit Private Limited, Aavas Financiers Limited, Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd, Moneyboxx Finance Limited, NeoGrowth Credit Private Limited, Progfin Private Limited, Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, and Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates are set across late November 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational corporate action notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face value and record dates for trading in the respective debentures, bonds, and PTCs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Record Dates: November 26, 2026 and November 28, 2026
- Ex-Dates: November 26, 2026 and November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for debt segment trading members ensuring accurate valuation and settlement following part redemption of debentures and bonds.
