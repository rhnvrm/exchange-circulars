## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple corporate entities on the debt market segment effective November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Navi Finserv Limited issued commercial papers totaling 3,500 units across two tranches (Scrip Codes: 732877, 732878; ISINs: INE342T14IH9, INE342T14IL1) with interest rates of 9.86% and 9.40% p.a.
- DSP Finance Private Limited issued 1,000 commercial paper units (Scrip Code: 732879; ISIN: INE422H14784) at an 8.55% interest rate p.a. maturing on 22/03/2027.
- Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited issued 100 commercial paper units (Scrip Code: 732880; ISIN: INE138I14107) at a 9.25% interest rate p.a. maturing on 26/03/2027.
- Network 18 Media & Investments Limited issued 4,000 commercial paper units (Scrip Code: 732881; ISIN: INE870H14YB4) at a 7.78% interest rate p.a.
- All securities are traded in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure all transactions comply with standard debt market trading and dematerialisation norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: November 3 and November 4, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Between February 2027 and May 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine debt market listing providing liquidity and secondary market access for commercial papers.
