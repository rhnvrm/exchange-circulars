## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion, stage movement, and exclusion of various securities effective from November 13, 2026, and November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 16, 2026, on all open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to lower Stage I w.e.f. November 13, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, and securities moving out of the ESM framework are listed in Annexure II.
- The price band of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework rules including 100% margin requirements and movement across ESM stages.
- Shifting of qualifying securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Application of 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction for securities moving to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the margin requirements and settlement changes for securities listed in the annexures.
- Ensure compliance with trade-for-trade settlement and price band restrictions for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- **November 13, 2026:** Effective date for movement of securities to lower Stage I, movement to Stage II (with Trade-for-Trade, 2% price band, and Periodic Call Auction), and removal of securities from ESM.
- **November 16, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin requirement on open positions as of November 13, 2026, and new positions, as well as shifting of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for impacted securities will face stricter margin norms, trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and price band caps, which may impact liquidity and short-term volatility.
