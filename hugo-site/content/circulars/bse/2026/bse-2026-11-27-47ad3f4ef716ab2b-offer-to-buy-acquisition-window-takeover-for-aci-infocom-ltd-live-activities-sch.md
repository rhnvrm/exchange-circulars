## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window / Takeover) for ACI Infocom Ltd. The bidding period opens on November 30, 2026, and closes on December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: ACI Infocom Ltd (Scrip Code: 517356, ISIN: INE167B01025, Symbol: ACIIN).
- Offer Size: Up to 3,70,47,634 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 1.53 per share (Face Value: Rs. 1.00).
- Opening Date: Monday, November 30, 2026; Closing Date: Friday, December 11, 2026.
- Platforms for access include iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet for files.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational guidelines for Offer to Buy (OTB) adhere to previous Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure UCC of the investor is registered/updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bidding is allowed for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and Custodians should note that no End of Issue files will be generated on Extranet if no securities are accepted.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date (Bid Offering Period): November 30, 2026 (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM)
- Closing Date (Bid Offering Period): December 11, 2026 (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM)
- Give-up / Take-up Period: November 30, 2026 to December 12, 2026
- Take-up (Last Day of Offer): December 11, 2026 (9:15 AM to 4:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and participation in the takeover acquisition window for shareholders and trading members of ACI Infocom Ltd.
