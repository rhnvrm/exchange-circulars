## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance in accordance with Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Effective date for margin implementation is October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026.
- Securities listed in Annexure II move out of the framework effective October 27, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of a minimum 75% margin on securities identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities and ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirements on applicable open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- Effective date out of framework (Annexure II): October 27, 2026
- Position date for open positions: October 28, 2026
- Effective date for margin requirement (Annexure I): October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities with high encumbrance levels will face heightened margin requirements (minimum 75%) in equity and derivative segments, impacting liquidity and capital allocation for open positions.
