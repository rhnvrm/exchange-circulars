## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Eiko Lifesciences Limited (Scrip Code: 540204) with effect from October 29, 2026. A total of 3,05,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 45/- (issue price Rs. 55/- per share) to promoter and non-promoter categories on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Eiko Lifesciences Limited
- Scrip Code: 540204
- ISIN: INE666Q01016
- Number of Shares: 3,05,000 equity shares
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each at an issue price of Rs. 55/- (including premium of Rs. 45/-)
- Allotment Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 29, 2026
- Lock-in Details:
  - 1,30,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 14345855 to 14475854) locked-in up to April 29, 2028.
  - 1,75,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 14475855 to 14650854) locked-in up to April 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Complies with regulatory guidelines for listing of shares issued on a preferential basis upon warrant conversion under SEBI (ICDR) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading permissions effective from October 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 18, 2026
- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 29, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: April 30, 2027 and April 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital of Eiko Lifesciences Limited through preferential allotment and warrant conversion, providing growth capital while adhering to statutory lock-in requirements.
