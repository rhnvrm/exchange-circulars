## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Kuber Udyog Limited. The bidding window runs from October 28, 2026, to November 11, 2026, with the settlement concluding on November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement Number: 2026129
- Bid Start Date: October 28, 2026
- Bid End Date: November 11, 2026
- Obligation Download: November 16, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 17, 2026
- Demat shares must be delivered via Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in both depositories.
- Market Type (Takeover) and Settlement No. 2026129 must be used by Members/Custodians.
- CDSL & NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts.
- Members and Custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
