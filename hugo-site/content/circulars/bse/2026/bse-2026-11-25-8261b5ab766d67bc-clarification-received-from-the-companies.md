## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips. Replies providing clarifications have been received from select companies, while responses from several other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received From:**
  1. ITI Ltd (Scrip Code: 523610)
  2. Windsor Machines Ltd (Scrip Code: 522029)
- **Pending Replies Awaited From 16 Companies:** Including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Camex Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, HBL Engineering Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and others.
- **Purpose:** To ensure trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information regarding unusual market activity.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance disclosure action.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies identified with significant price/volume movements are required to submit clarifications to the Exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 25 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding corporate responses to market surveillance inquiries.
