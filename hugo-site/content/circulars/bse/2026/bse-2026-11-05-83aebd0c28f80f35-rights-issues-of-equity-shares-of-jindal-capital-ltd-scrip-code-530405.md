## Summary

Jindal Capital Ltd. (Scrip Code: 530405) has announced a rights issue of equity shares. The company has fixed November 6, 2026, as the record date to determine shareholders eligible for the rights entitlement.

## Key Points

- Rights Issue Ratio: 3 (three) equity shares for every 2 (two) existing equity shares held as on the record date.
- Issue Price: Rs. 15/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- plus Premium of Rs. 5/-).
- Payment Terms: The entire amount of issue price of Rs. 15/- per share is payable on application.
- Ex-Rights Basis: Transactions in the equity shares will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from November 6, 2026 (DR-751/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the record date, ex-rights date, and settlement details for transactions in the equity shares of Jindal Capital Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Record Date: November 6, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date will receive rights entitlements in the specified ratio. Trading and settlement procedures will adjust for ex-rights transactions.
