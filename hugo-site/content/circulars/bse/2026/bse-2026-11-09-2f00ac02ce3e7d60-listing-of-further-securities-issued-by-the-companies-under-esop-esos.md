## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by seven companies under ESOP / ESOS are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Total Companies:** 7 listed companies issuing further shares under employee stock option schemes.
- **Companies and Quantities:**
  - CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited (17,000 shares)
  - ICICI Life Insurance Limited (2,520 shares)
  - Karnataka Bank Limited (58,065 shares)
  - KSH International Limited (35,182 shares)
  - Sudarshan Chemical Indus. Limited (19,350 shares)
  - Transport Corporation of India Limited (12,055 shares)
  - Travel Food Services Limited (6,040 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine issuance and listing of equity shares pursuant to ESOP/ESOS exercises.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the admission of further securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 9, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as this represents routine equity dilution from employee stock options.
