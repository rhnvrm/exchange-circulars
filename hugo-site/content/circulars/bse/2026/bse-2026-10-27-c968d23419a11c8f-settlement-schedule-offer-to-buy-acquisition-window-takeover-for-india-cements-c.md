## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for India Cements Capital Limited. The bidding window is scheduled from October 28, 2026, to November 11, 2026 under Settlement No. 2026130.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Period:** October 28, 2026 to November 11, 2026 (Settlement Type: Takeover, Settlement No: 2026130).
- **Obligation Download Date:** November 25, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 26, 2026.
- **Early Pay-in Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Securities mechanism available in both depositories, utilizing Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026130.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

Acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism is conducted in accordance with revised guidelines and BSE Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure that the effect of early pay-in of securities reflects in BSE/BSECL systems before prescribed timelines for accepting/confirming bids on the BSE OTB platform.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** November 25, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the orderly execution and settlement of the takeover offer for India Cements Capital Limited by providing clear operational workflows and timelines for members, custodians, and clearing participants.
