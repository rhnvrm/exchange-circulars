## Summary

Global Vision Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member no. 6853) has submitted an application to surrender its trading membership with BSE Ltd. in accordance with the exchange's rules, bye-laws, and regulations.

## Key Points

- Trading member name: Global Vision Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member number: 6853
- Department: Membership / Compliance
- Objection deadline: December 9, 2026 (within 15 days from the date of the notice)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative surrender of membership notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise objections regarding the surrender must submit them in writing to the Membership Department on or before December 9, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Objection Deadline: December 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market operations, affecting only the specific trading member's active status upon final acceptance of surrender.
