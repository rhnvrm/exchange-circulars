## Summary

BSE has announced the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for November 04, 2026, following the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) press release. Trading members and direct investors can place bids through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Auction Date:** November 04, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 05, 2026.
- **Securities Offered:**
  - 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T030227, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Min Lot: 100, Max Amt: ₹400 Cr)
  - 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T280527, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Min Lot: 100, Max Amt: ₹400 Cr)
  - 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T041127, Issue Size: ₹7,000 Cr, Min Lot: 100, Max Amt: ₹350 Cr)
- **Bidding Platform:** iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com >> NCB-GSec module).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of RBI's GoI Treasury Bills auction schedule under the non-competitive bidding facility as per Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 26, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must adhere to the specified bidding timelines and lot size/amount limits.
- Direct investors bid collection end date: November 03, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Trading members bid collection end date: November 04, 2026, till 8:00 AM.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** November 02, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** November 03, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** November 04, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless retail and institutional participation in Government of India Treasury Bills auctions through the electronic NCB-GSec module.
