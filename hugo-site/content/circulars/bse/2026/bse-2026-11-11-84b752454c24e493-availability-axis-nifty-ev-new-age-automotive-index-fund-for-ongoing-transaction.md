## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopening for ongoing transactions from November 12, 2026.
- Applicable ISINs include Regular Plan - Growth (INF846KA1622) and Direct Plan - Growth (INF846KA1630).
- Issued pursuant to previous Notice No. 20261015-25 dated October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This notice represents routine operational updates for mutual fund trading on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can execute transactions, SIPs, and redemptions for the specified scheme plans via the BSE StAR MF Platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members seamless platform access to ongoing investments and redemptions in the Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund.
