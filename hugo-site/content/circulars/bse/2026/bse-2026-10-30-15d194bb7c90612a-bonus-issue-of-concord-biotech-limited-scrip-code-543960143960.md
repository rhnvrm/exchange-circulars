## Summary

Concord Biotech Limited has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 1:1 (one bonus equity share of Re. 1/- each for every one existing equity share held). The company has fixed November 4, 2026, as the record date and ex-bonus date for the corporate action.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Concord Biotech Limited
- Scrip Code: 543960 / 143960
- Ratio: 1:1 (1 bonus share for every 1 existing share held)
- Face Value: Re. 1/- each
- Record Date: November 4, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Basis Date: November 4, 2026
- Allotment Details: 10,46,16,204 equity shares to be allotted as on November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice regarding a bonus issue.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the record date and ex-bonus date for proper settlement and execution of transactions in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date: November 4, 2026
- Allotment Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date will receive bonus shares, resulting in an expansion of the company's equity capital base (10,46,16,204 equity shares) and proportionate price adjustment on an ex-bonus basis.
