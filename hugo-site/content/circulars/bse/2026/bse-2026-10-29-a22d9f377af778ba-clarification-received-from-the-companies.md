## Summary

The BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 3 companies: M P K Steels (I) Ltd, Manoj Ceramic Ltd, and Seemax Resources Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 23 companies including Amtech Esters Ltd, Anna Infrastructures Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance monitoring action.

## Compliance Requirements

Awaited companies are required to submit their clarifications to the Exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice intended to maintain market transparency regarding unusual price/volume movements.
