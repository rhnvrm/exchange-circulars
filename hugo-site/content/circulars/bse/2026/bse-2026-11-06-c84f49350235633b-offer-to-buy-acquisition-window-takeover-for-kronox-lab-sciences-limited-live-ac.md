## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window/Takeover) of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited. The bidding period opens on Friday, 6th November 2026, and closes on Friday, 20th November 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Kronox Lab Sciences Limited (Scrip Code: 544187, Symbol: KRONOX, ISIN: INE0ATZ01017)
- **Offer Size:** Up to 95,70,000 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 157.27 per share.
- **Market Lot:** 1 Equity Share (Face Value: Rs. 10.00).
- **Session Timings:** Bid offering and modifications/cancellation are scheduled from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. between 6th November 2026 and 20th November 2026. Give-up / Take-up timings and final day closing hours are specified for clearing members and custodians.
- **Access Platforms:** iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet URLs are provided for trading members, custodians, and institutional participation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the UCC of investors is correctly registered/updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- If no securities are accepted, no End of Issue files will be generated on the Extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** 6th November 2026 (9.15 a.m.)
- **Closing Date:** 20th November 2026 (3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up Last Day:** 19th November 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Take-up Last Day:** 20th November 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation by various investor categories (FII, FI, MF, IC, QIBs, NIB, CO, IND) in the takeover offer for Kronox Lab Sciences Limited through designated OTB platforms.
