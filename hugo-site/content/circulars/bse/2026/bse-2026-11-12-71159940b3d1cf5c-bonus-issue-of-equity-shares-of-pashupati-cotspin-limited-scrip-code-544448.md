## Summary

Pashupati Cotspin Limited has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 3:2 (three bonus shares for every two existing equity shares held) with November 13, 2026, set as the record date. Transactions in the company's equity shares will be on an ex-bonus basis starting from the same date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pashupati Cotspin Limited
- Scrip Code: 544448
- Bonus Ratio: 3:2 (3 bonus equity shares of Re. 1/- each for every 2 existing equity shares of Re. 1/- each held)
- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: November 13, 2026
- Anticipated Allotment: 236,760,000 equity shares as of November 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice issued in accordance with listing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading and clearing members must account for the ex-bonus date and record date when processing trades and settlements for the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date: November 13, 2026
- Allotment Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders holding shares on the record date will receive bonus shares. The stock price will adjust accordingly on the ex-bonus date, enhancing stock liquidity.
