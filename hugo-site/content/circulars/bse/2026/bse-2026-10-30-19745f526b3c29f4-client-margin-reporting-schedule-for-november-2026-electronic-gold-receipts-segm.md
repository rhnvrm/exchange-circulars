## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the schedule for client margin reporting in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment for the month of November 2026. The circular outlines specific trade dates alongside their corresponding last dates for margin reporting and penalty dates.

## Key Points

- Applies to the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on BSE.
- Outlines the schedule of trade dates, margin reporting deadlines, and penalty dates for November 2026.
- Ensures timely compliance and reporting of client margins by clearing members.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine operational schedule notice issued for margin reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure that client margins are reported by the respective last dates specified in the schedule for each trade date in November 2026 to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Trade Dates Covered: November 2, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- Reporting and penalty deadlines range from November 8, 2026, through December 8, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact as this is a standard recurring schedule for clearing members operating in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment.
