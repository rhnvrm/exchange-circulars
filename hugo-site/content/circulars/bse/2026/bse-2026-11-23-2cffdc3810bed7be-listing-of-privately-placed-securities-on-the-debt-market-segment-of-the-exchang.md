## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies on the debt market segment effective November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers listed include issues from Tata Steel Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Birla Group Holdings Private Limited, and Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited.
- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form under their respective ISIN numbers.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adherence to standard trading and settlement norms for debt securities and commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment and redemption dates vary by issuer (ranging from November 2026 to February 2027).

## Impact Assessment

Standard routine listing activity on the debt segment with no direct material impact on general market operations.
