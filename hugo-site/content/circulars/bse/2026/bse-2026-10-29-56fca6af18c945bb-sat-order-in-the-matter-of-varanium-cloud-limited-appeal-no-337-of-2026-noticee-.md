## Summary

BSE has issued a notice communicating the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 09, 2026, passed in Appeal No. 337 of 2026 in the matter of Varanium Cloud Limited, involving noticees Jinesh Narottamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited. Pursuant to SEBI's email dated October 29, 2026, trading members are advised to take note of and comply with the SAT order.

## Key Points

- **SAT Order Date:** October 09, 2026 (Appeal No. 337 of 2026).
- **Matter:** Varanium Cloud Limited, Jinesh Narottamdas Mehta & Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited.
- **Tribunal Observation:** SAT noted that the appellant is not a signatory to the prospectus and siphoning of money took place 8 months after listing, directing that the impugned order shall remain stayed.
- **SEBI Direction:** SEBI via email dated October 29, 2026, directed compliance with the SAT order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the SAT stay order staying the previous regulatory/impugned order against the noticees in the Varanium Cloud Limited matter.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the SAT stay order and ensure necessary compliance as communicated by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **SAT Order Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Previous Exchange Notice:** October 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides legal relief/stay to the appellants regarding the impugned order while appellate proceedings continue before SAT.
