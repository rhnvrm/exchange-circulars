## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of MAGNUS STEEL AND INFRA LIMITED will be permitted on the Exchange effective from November 12, 2026. The rights issue is open from November 12, 2026 to December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** MAGNUS STEEL AND INFRA LIMITED
- **Scrip Code:** 751152
- **Scrip ID:** MAGNUS-RE1
- **ISIN No.:** INE898E20029
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** November 12, 2026 to November 25, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 12, 2026 to December 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Dealings in Rights Entitlements permitted on the Exchange.
- Settlement on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- Tradable in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs using the correct ISIN and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills containing purchase of REs must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before closure.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 12, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date (Renunciation):** November 25, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides existing shareholders and eligible investors the mechanism to trade, renounce, or subscribe to the Rights Entitlements of Magnus Steel And Infra Limited under specified settlement and compliance norms.
