## Summary

BSE has published the live activities schedule and operational details for the Offer to Buy (OTB) via the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Ltd. The issue is scheduled to run from November 2, 2026, to November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: ISHAAN INFRASTRUCTURES AND SHELTERS LTD (Scrip Code: 540134, ISIN: INE818R01011, Symbol: IISL).
- Offer Size: Up to 63,48,500 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 14.00 per share.
- Market Lot: 1 share; Face Value: Rs. 10.00.
- Session timings and access URLs (iBBS, RTRMS, Extranet) provided for trading and custodian members.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational guidelines and schedule adjustments for takeover bidding via the OTB mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure UCC of investors is updated with correct PAN and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 2nd November 2026
- Closing Date: 16th November 2026
- Bid Offering Period: 2nd November 2026 to 16th November 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 2nd November 2026 to 13th November 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Final Take-up (Last day of offer): 16th November 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Enables structured participation and settlement for trading members and custodians taking part in the takeover offer for Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Ltd.
