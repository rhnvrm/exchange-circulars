## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips. Replies containing clarifications have been received from several companies, while replies from other listed entities remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from: Amanta Healthcare Ltd, Gourmet Gateway India Ltd, Omega Interactive Technologies Ltd, Shayona Engineering Ltd, and Tasty Bite Eatables Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 21 other listed entities including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, and others.
- Notice issued by DOSS (Department of Surveillance).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price and volume movements upon inquiry by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice regarding market surveillance and company disclosures, ensuring transparency for investors and trading members.
