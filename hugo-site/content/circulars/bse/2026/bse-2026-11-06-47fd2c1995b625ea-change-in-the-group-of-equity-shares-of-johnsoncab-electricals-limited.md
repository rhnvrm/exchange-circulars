## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Johnsoncab Electricals Limited (Scrip Code: 545021) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Monday, November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Johnsoncab Electricals Limited
- Scrip Code: 545021
- Segment Shift: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20261105-23 dated November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of securities out of the Trade for Trade segment into the normal rolling segment allowing normal settlement cycles.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants to note the segment change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves trading liquidity and operational efficiency for investors dealing in the equity shares of Johnsoncab Electricals Limited by moving them from Trade for Trade settlement to normal rolling settlement.
