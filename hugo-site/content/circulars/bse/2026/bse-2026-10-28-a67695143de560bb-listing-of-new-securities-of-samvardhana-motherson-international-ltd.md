## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 10,32,09,960 new equity shares of Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd. with effect from October 29, 2026. These shares were allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIPs) pursuant to compulsorily convertible debentures issued on a QIP basis at an issue price of Rs. 140.50/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 517334
- **ISIN:** INE775A01035
- **Number of Shares:** 10,32,09,960 Equity Shares
- **Face Value:** Re. 1/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 140.50/- per share
- **Date of Allotment:** October 08, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 11710193255 to 11813403214
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice regarding equity shares allotted via QIP conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the admission of new equity shares ranking pari-passu with existing shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 08, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 10.32 crore new equity shares increases the total listed equity base of Samvardhana Motherson International Ltd., resulting in capital expansion and potential liquidity increase in the secondary market.
