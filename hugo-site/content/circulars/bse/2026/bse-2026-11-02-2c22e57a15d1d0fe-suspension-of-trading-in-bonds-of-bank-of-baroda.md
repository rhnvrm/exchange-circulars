## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific bonds of Bank of Baroda effective from November 11, 2026, on account of the record date fixed for the payment of interest and the exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bank of Baroda
- **Bond Details:** BOB-9.75%-PERPETUAL-PVT DR- 754/2026-2027 (ISIN: INE028A08265, Scrip Code: 973596)
- **Record Date:** November 11, 2026
- **No Dealings From:** November 11, 2026
- **Purpose:** Payment of Interest and Exercise of Call Option

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified bonds of Bank of Baroda from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / No Dealings From:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard corporate action procedure for debt instruments undergoing interest payment and call option exercise, with minimal impact on general market operations.
