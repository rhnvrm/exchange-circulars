## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the new ISIN number for Meenakshi India Ltd (Scrip Code: 544831) following the sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Meenakshi India Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544831
- New ISIN: INE208H01024
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares (Stock Split) from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- Ex-Date: 30-10-2026 (Reference: DR-746/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice for updating security identification details post stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trades in the sub-divided equity shares are executed using the new ISIN number effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28-10-2026
- Ex-Date: 30-10-2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating master files and trading terminals with the new ISIN for equity shares.
