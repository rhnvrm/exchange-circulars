## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Ltd, scheduled from November 2, 2026, to November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement Number: 2026132
- Bid Start Date: 02/11/2026
- Bid End Date: 16/11/2026
- Obligation Download: 20/11/2026
- Settlement Date: 23/11/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026132.
- No EPN file upload is required by members or custodians.
- Client-wise stamp duty charges can be referenced in the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that the effect of early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for bid acceptance and confirmation. Securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 02/11/2026
- Bid End Date: 16/11/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 20/11/2026
- Settlement Date: 23/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparent and orderly settlement operations for the takeover offer of Ishaan Infrastructures and Shelters Ltd through mandatory early pay-in mechanisms.
