## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in Demat Auction 760 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-760/2026-27 on Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No: DR-760/2026-27
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 20, 2026
- Auction Matching Timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement & Report Timing: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports Timing: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in Date: Monday, November 23, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine auction schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Members should take utmost care while punching securities codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should refer to the cutoff rate on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split) before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 20, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 23, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is restricted to trading members participating in settlement DR-760/2026-27 with short-delivered securities.
