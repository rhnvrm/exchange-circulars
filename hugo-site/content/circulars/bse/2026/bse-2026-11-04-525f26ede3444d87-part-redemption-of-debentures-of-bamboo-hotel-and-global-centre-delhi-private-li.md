## Summary

BSE has issued a notice clarifying the part redemption of non-convertible debentures of Bamboo Hotel and Global Centre (Delhi) Private Limited (Scrip Code: 976378, ISIN: INE755L07015). The company informed that the redemption was executed in terms of the number of debentures rather than face value, and consequently, the face value continues to remain at Rs. 100,000/- per debenture.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bamboo Hotel and Global Centre (Delhi) Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 976378
- ISIN: INE755L07015
- Security Description: BHGCPL-10.81%-31-1-28-PVT
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Face Value: Rs. 100,000/- per Debenture (unchanged)
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational clarification regarding debenture redemption parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that the debentures will continue to be traded with the specified face value of Rs. 100,000/- per debenture effective November 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members to ensure correct trading parameters and face value handling for the debentures.
