## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific Treasury Bills (T-bills) and Government Securities effective November 09, 2026, due to their maturity on the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects three specific T-bills/Government Securities reaching maturity.
- Effective date for suspension: November 09, 2026 (DR- 753/2026-2027).
- Affected scrip codes and ISINs:
  - Scrip Code: 805144 (364T121126, ISIN: IN002025Z336)
  - Scrip Code: 805215 (182T121126, ISIN: IN002026Y063)
  - Scrip Code: 805253 (91TB121126, ISIN: IN002026X198)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice regarding the maturity and subsequent suspension of trading for matured sovereign debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills and Government Securities with effect from November 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a routine maturity and trading suspension process for maturing government securities.
