## Summary

BSE has announced that effective from November 27, 2026, new securities of Fratelli Vineyards Ltd issued on a rights basis (partly paid-up equity shares) will be listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange under the 'XT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- **Security Details**: 33,18,503 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each partly paid-up of Rs. 6/- each issued on Rights Basis.
- **Scrip Code**: 890244
- **Group**: XT
- **Market Lot**: 1
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name**: BOLT FRATELLIPP
- **ISIN No.**: IN9401Z01017
- **Face Value**: Rs. 10/-
- **Paid-up Value**: Rs. 6/-
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 10/- (Rs. 6/- paid on application and Rs. 4/- to be paid on one or more subsequent calls as determined by the Board).
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 48472395 to 51790897
- **Date of Allotment**: 20/11/2026
- **Dividend Entitlement**: Proportionate to the amount paid-up.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary ISIN No. IN8401Z01026 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular Nos. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 regarding activation of ISIN in case of additional issue of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the listing of new partly paid-up shares under the 'XT' group and the suspension of the temporary ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: November 20, 2026
- **Effective Listing & Trading Date**: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of partly paid-up rights issue shares for Fratelli Vineyards Ltd on the exchange platform.
