## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of VEER GLOBAL INFRACONSTRUCTION LIMITED starting November 06, 2026. The rights issue is scheduled to open on November 06, 2026, and close on November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** VEER GLOBAL INFRACONSTRUCTION LIMITED
- **Type of Security:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** November 06, 2026 to November 20, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 751150
- **Scrip ID:** VEERG-RE
- **ISIN:** INE244W20036
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 06, 2026 to November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in Rights Entitlements is permitted on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis and settled on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of RE trading and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes for RE purchases must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchase grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce the REs before closure.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 06, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade or exercise rights entitlements within the specified timeline, facilitating capital raising for the company.
