## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 48 scrips, effective from November 02, 2026, as part of surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 48 scrips ranging from 2% to 20%.
- Effective date: November 02, 2026.
- Issued by Surveillance Department (DOSS).

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified scrips to manage market volatility and ensure orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the revised price bands while executing trades in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts trading limits and volatility controls for the listed scrips.
