## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 17 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans / Schemes (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026.
- **Total Companies:** 17 companies issuing further equity shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- **Notable Issuers:** IDFC First Bank Limited (1,417,510 shares), Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited (1,007,121 shares), Fractal Analytics Limited (484,065 shares), Aditya Birla Capital Limited (24,884 shares), and Yes Bank Limited (696,236 shares).
- **Lock-in Status:** No lock-in (NA) specified for the listed ESOP/ESOS shares across all mentioned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory or policy changes; this is a routine corporate listing notice under existing Exchange frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and BSE operational guidelines for ESOP allotment admissions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Minimal and routine. Incremental equity capital expansion resulting from employee stock option exercises.
- **Operational Impact:** Standard listing and trading admission of securities without lock-in restrictions.
