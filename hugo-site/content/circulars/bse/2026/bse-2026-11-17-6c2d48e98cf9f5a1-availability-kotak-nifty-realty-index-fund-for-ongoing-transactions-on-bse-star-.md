## Summary

BSE has announced that the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable ISINs cover various options including Regular Growth (INF174KA1H55), Regular IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1H71), Regular IDCW Payout (INF174KA1H63), Direct Growth (INF174KA1H22), Direct IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1H48), and Direct IDCW Payout (INF174KA1H30).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions through the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to execute transactions and investment plans for the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund seamlessly on the BSE StAR MF platform.
