## Summary

Effective Wednesday, November 04, 2026, the equity shares of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the T Group of Securities. The listing is pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement involving the demerger of the Biopharma Undertaking of India Glycols Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 545012
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** ENNATURE
- **ISIN No:** INE2WCT01016
- **Group:** T (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 5/- each fully paid
- **Total Securities:** 2,23,42,255 equity shares issued pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement (Share entitlement ratio: 1:3 from India Glycols Limited)
- **Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS):** Applicable for IPO and other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing admitted in accordance with SEBI Circular Nos. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012, CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012, and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.
- Market participants must adhere to Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) guidelines.
- Lock-in details as per Annexure I of the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 04, 2026
- **Appointed Date:** April 01, 2026
- **Demerger Effective Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of India Glycols Limited receiving shares under the demerger scheme can trade their holdings in Ennature Bio Pharma Limited starting November 04, 2026. The initial 10-day trade-for-trade mechanism ensures mandatory delivery-based trading.
