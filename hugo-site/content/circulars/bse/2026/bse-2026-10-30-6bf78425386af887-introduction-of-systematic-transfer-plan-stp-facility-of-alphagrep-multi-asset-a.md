## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of the Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facility under the Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform with immediate effect, based on communication received from Alphagrep Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facility is now enabled for Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF.
- Effective Date: With immediate effect (30 Oct 2026).
- Notice-cum-Addendum issued by Alphagrep Mutual Fund is attached for reference.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational update regarding mutual fund scheme facilities on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the STP facility availability while executing transactions in the aforesaid scheme on behalf of clients.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 Oct 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate (30 Oct 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with enhanced flexibility and automated transfer options for transactions in Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund on BSE StAR MF.
