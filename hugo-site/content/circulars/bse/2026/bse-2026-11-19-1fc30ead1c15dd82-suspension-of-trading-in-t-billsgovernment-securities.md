## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities on account of their maturity, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 800218
- Particulars: 815GOI2026
- ISIN: IN0020140060
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid securities from the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational suspension due to security maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further dealing or transactions take place in the matured T-bill/Government Security starting November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date (Suspension/Redemption): November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a standard expiration and redemption process for government debt instruments.
