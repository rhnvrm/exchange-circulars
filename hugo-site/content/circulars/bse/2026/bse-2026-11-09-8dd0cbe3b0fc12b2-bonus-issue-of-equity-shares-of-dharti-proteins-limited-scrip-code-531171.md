## Summary

Dharti Proteins Limited has fixed November 13, 2026, as the record date for the issuance of bonus equity shares. Eligible public shareholders will receive 1 bonus equity share of Rs. 10/- each for every 1 existing equity share held.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dharti Proteins Limited
- Scrip Code: 531171
- Bonus Ratio: 1:1 (One Bonus Equity Share for every One Equity Share held)
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Target Audience: Public shareholders only
- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: November 13, 2026
- Projected Allotment Date: November 16, 2026 (75,000 equity shares)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the ex-bonus date and record date for proper settlement and execution of transactions in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Basis From: November 13, 2026
- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Allotment Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The 1:1 bonus issue increases the liquidity and number of equity shares held by public shareholders while proportionately adjusting per-share market valuation.
