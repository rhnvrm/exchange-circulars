## Summary

BSE Limited has notified Trading Members, Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs), and intermediaries that the Call Money Module of the Internet Based Book Building Software (IBBS) platform has been activated for Ace Software Exports Limited. This is for the second and final call on partly paid-up equity shares of the company.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Ace Software Exports Limited
- **Type of Call:** Second and Final Call
- **Security Symbol:** ASELPPCM
- **No. of Partly Paid-up Equity Shares:** 54,71,101
- **Call Money Amount:** Rs. 30.25 per share
- **Issue Opening Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** December 11, 2026
- **Bidding Period:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted in accordance with SEBI circular dated December 8, 2020, and Exchange notice no. 20210104-37 dated January 4, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members and SCSBs are required to submit details of call money notices collected during the issue period through the IBBS platform in accordance with established guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders of Ace Software Exports Limited to pay the second and final call money of Rs. 30.25 per share for 54,71,101 partly paid-up shares through authorized SCSBs and trading members.
