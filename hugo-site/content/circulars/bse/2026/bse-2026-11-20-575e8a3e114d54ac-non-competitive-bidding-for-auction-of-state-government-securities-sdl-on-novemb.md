## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has published a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for November 23, 2026, following the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) auction announcement.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Platform:** BSE iBBS web-based system via the NCB-GSec module (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- **Eligible Participants:** Direct investors and trading members.
- **Covered States/Issuances:** Re-issued and new SDL securities from states including Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Manipur, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tamil Nadu.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates participation in RBI-conducted State Government Securities auctions via the Exchange's NCB-GSec module pursuant to existing operational guidelines (Exchange circular no. 20191122-28).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must ensure bid submissions adhere to prescribed minimum subscription amounts, unit multiples, and deadlines.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** November 20, 2026 (10:00 AM onwards)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** November 22, 2026 (till 5:00 PM)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** November 23, 2026 (till 8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides seamless non-competitive access for investors to participate in state government debt issuances with structured timelines and clear bidding parameters.
