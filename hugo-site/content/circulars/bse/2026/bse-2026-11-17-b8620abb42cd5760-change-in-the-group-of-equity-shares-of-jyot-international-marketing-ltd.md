## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Jyot International Marketing Ltd from the Trade for Trade segment (XT Group) to the Rolling segment (X Group), effective Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jyot International Marketing Ltd
- Scrip Code: 542544
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (XT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment ("X" Group)
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Basis: Completion of the first 10 trading days from commencement of trading on November 3, 2026, pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of trading segment from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment in accordance with SEBI guidelines for newly listed/shifted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and group designation for order placement and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal rolling settlement for equity shares of Jyot International Marketing Ltd, enhancing trading liquidity and operational efficiency.
