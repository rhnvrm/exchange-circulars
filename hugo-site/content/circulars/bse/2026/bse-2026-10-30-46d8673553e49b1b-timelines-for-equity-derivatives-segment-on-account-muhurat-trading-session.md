## Summary

In view of the Muhurat Trading session for Diwali Laxmi Pujan and pursuant to BSE Circular No. 20261019-10, BSE has outlined the revised settlement timelines, client margin reporting, and Custodian Participant (CP) code modification schedules for the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Pay-in and pay-out obligations for trades executed on November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled together on November 09, 2026, at 08:30 hrs.
- Client margin reporting schedule follows BSECL Circular No. 20261030-16.
- Custodian Participant (CP) code modification / Give up confirmation window for trades conducted on November 08, 2026, is set from 14:20 hrs. to 15:40 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary adjustment of the clearing and settlement calendar to accommodate trading activities on the Muhurat Trading session date (November 08, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and trading members must complete pay-in/pay-out obligations and CP code modifications within the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Trade Dates: November 06, 2026 and November 08, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 09, 2026 (08:30 hrs.)
- CP Code Modification Window: November 08, 2026 (14:20 hrs. – 15:40 hrs.)

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact; ensures smooth settlement operations and risk management for market participants during the festive trading session.
