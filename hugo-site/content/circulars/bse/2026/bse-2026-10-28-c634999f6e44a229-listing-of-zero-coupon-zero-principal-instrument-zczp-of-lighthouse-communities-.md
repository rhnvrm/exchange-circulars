## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) instruments of Lighthouse Communities Foundation on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment effective from October 29, 2026. A total of 31,96,103 securities of Rs. 1/- each have been listed under scrip code 798006 and ISIN INS0VU012023.

## Key Points

- **Entity Name:** Lighthouse Communities Foundation
- **Instrument:** Zero Coupon Zero Principal Instrument (ZCZP)
- **No. of Securities:** 31,96,103 securities of Rs. 1/- each
- **Scrip ID / Code:** LCFZCZP / 798006
- **ISIN No.:** INS0VU012023
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1)
- **Allotment Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** October 20, 2027
- **Registrar:** KFIN Technologies Limited
- **Trading Status:** The securities will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Impact Assessment

The listing pertains to non-tradable ZCZP instruments on the SSE segment and has no direct trading impact on mainstream equity markets.
