## Summary

OM Metallogic Limited has fixed November 20, 2026, as the Record Date for its upcoming Rights Issue of equity shares. Trading in the equity shares of the company will commence on an ex-rights basis starting from the same date under the rolling settlement segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Scrip Code: OM Metallogic Limited (544559)
- Notice Reference: Notice No. 20261117-43 (DR- 761/2026-2027)
- Record Date: November 20, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: November 20, 2026
- Rights Issue Ratio: 01 (ONE) equity share of Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 11/- per share (total issue price Rs. 21/- per share) for every 01 (ONE) equity share held.
- Payment Terms: Entire rights issue price of Rs. 21/- per share is payable at the time of application.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard corporate action notification and processing under BSE listing operations and rolling settlement guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure trades are executed on an ex-rights basis from the specified date. Eligible shareholders must pay the full issue price of Rs. 21/- per share at the time of application.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Record Date: November 20, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders to subscribe to additional equity shares in a 1:1 ratio, facilitating capital expansion for the company while adjusting share valuation post ex-date.
