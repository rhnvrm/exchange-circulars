## Summary

BSE has approved the surrender of trading membership of Jamnadas Virji Shares & Stock Brokers Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 339) in the Equity Derivative, Currency Derivative, and Interest Rate Derivative segments with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Jamnadas Virji Shares & Stock Brokers Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Registration Number: 339
- Segments: Equity Derivative, Currency Derivative, and Interest Rate Derivative segments
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative approval of membership surrender.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarifications can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 17, 2026
- Notice Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct market impact; administrative discontinuation of trading privileges for the specified member in derivative segments.
