## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of SFC Environmental Technologies Ltd effective from November 3, 2026, following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SFC Environmental Technologies Ltd
- Scrip Code: 545009
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SFCTECH
- ISIN: INE0PKN01028
- Number of Securities: 97,95,6724 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 97956724)
- Issue Price: Rs. 330/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 328/-)
- Group: 'B' Group of Securities
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in period marked in depositories records as per Annexure I.
- Trading members must note the demat-only trading requirement and SPOS guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of SFC Environmental Technologies Ltd equity shares on BSE, providing liquidity to IPO investors and expanding the exchange's listed offerings.
