## Summary

Effective from Friday, November 6, 2026, the equity shares of Vectras Enprocon Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vectras Enprocon Limited
- Scrip Code: 545020
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VECTRAS
- ISIN: INE1FD201014
- Total Securities: 1,90,56,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 19,056,000
- Issue Price: Rs. 105/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and Premium of Rs. 95/-)
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: November 4, 2026
- Market Maker: Spread X Securities Private Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited (Formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission to dealing granted in accordance with SEBI guidelines and exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded strictly in dematerialized form.
- Minimum market lot is set at 1,200 equity shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day (November 6, 2026).
- Necessary corporate actions executed for locking in shares in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 4, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading and liquidity for Vectras Enprocon Limited on the BSE SME platform, subject to initial trade-for-trade surveillance for 10 trading days.
