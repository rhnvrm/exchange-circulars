## Summary

Varvee Global Ltd has informed the Exchange that it has fixed November 27, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Ltd
- Scrip Code: 514274
- Record Date: 27/11/2026
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of existing equity shares from face value of Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 2/- each.
- Existing ISIN No. INE273D01027 (Rs. 5/- paid up) will cease to be valid for transactions on or after 27/11/2026.
- New ISIN for Rs. 2/- paid-up shares will be communicated separately.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record date and ensure transactions are aligned with the discontinuation of the old ISIN starting 27/11/2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19/11/2026
- Record Date: 27/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date will receive sub-divided shares under the new ISIN, and trading in the previous ISIN will be disabled.
