## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund has communicated revisions to minimum investment and redemption amounts under the Growth Option of certain schemes, effective immediately, as per the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by UTI AMC.

## Key Points

- Minimum Purchase Amount revised to ₹100.
- Minimum Additional Purchase Amount revised to ₹1.
- Minimum Redemption Amount revised to ₹1.
- Minimum SIP amount revised to ₹25.
- Minimum SWP and STP Amount revised to ₹100 and in multiples of ₹1.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of transaction threshold limits for specific mutual fund schemes managed by UTI AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the revised minimum amounts while submitting SIP, XSIP, AMCSTP, and SWP registrations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 November 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational flexibility for investors with lowered minimum thresholds for systematic investment and redemption plans in specified UTI Mutual Fund schemes.
