## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the equity shares of BLS Polymers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID: BLSPOLY
- Scrip Code: 545011
- ISIN No: INE0ZCQ01019
- Total Securities: 62,031,834 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 150/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 140/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure pursuant to public issue.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on November 3, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of BLS Polymers Limited shares on the BSE, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding the exchange's equity segment offerings.
