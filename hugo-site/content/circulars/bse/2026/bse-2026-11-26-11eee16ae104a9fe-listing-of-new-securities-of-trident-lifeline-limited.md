## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 24,000 new equity shares of Trident Lifeline Limited (Scrip Code - 543616) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Trident Lifeline Limited (Scrip Code: 543616, ISIN: INE0MKA01014).
- Securities: 24,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 256/- each (Total Issue Price: Rs. 266/- per share).
- Allottees: Promoters, issued on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Distribution Nos.: 11957001 to 11981000.
- Date of Allotment: October 17, 2026.
- Lock-in Details: 24,000 shares are under lock-in up to May 30, 2028.
- Ranking: New shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Listing approval granted in accordance with regulatory requirements for preferential issue and warrant conversion under SEBI ICDR regulations and BSE operating guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depositories must adhere to the lock-in restrictions applicable to the shares until May 30, 2028.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 17, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 27, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard corporate listing of shares arising from warrant conversion, increasing the company's paid-up equity capital while maintaining promoter lock-in compliance.
