## Summary

BSE has issued a notice extending the display period for investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook pursuant to a communication received from SEBI. The display is now required to continue until December 31, 2026, and certain effective dates under the SEBI circular dated October 1, 2026, are extended to November 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook must continue to be displayed by Trading Members on their trading applications and websites until December 31, 2026.
- Effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, are extended to November 15, 2026.
- Messages must be displayed in a bilingual format (Hindi and English).
- All other provisions from the earlier Exchange Notice dated October 5, 2026, and SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the Project Jagrook investor awareness message display timeline to December 31, 2026.
- Extension of specific effective dates under SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026 (paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b)) to November 15, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure continuous display of investor awareness messages in bilingual format (Hindi and English) on trading applications and websites in accordance with the revised timelines.
- Direct inquiries or clarifications to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- October 5, 2026: Original Exchange Notice date (Notice No. 20261005-37).
- November 15, 2026: Extended effective dates under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of SEBI Circular.
- December 31, 2026: Extended deadline for displaying investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook.

## Impact Assessment

Operational extension for trading members regarding compliance timelines for displaying investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook without major structural market disruption.
