## Summary

MEDPLUS HEALTH SERVICES LIMITED has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value of Rs. 2 each from the open market through Stock Exchanges. The maximum buyback price shall not exceed Rs. 750 per Equity Share for an aggregate amount not exceeding ₹ 130.00 Crores.

## Key Points

- Buyback Type: Open market through Stock Exchanges.
- Maximum Buyback Price: Rs. 750/- per Equity Share.
- Maximum Buyback Size: Up to ₹ 130.00 Crores/-.
- Date of Opening of the Buyback: Friday, November 13, 2026.
- Last Date for the Buyback: Earlier of Tuesday, February 16, 2027 (within 66 working days), completion of deployment of Maximum Buyback Size, or earlier closure date determined by the Board upon deploying the Minimum Buyback Size.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must adhere to the buyback guidelines, timelines, and payment obligations set forth under SEBI and Exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Date of Opening: November 13, 2026
- Last Date of Buyback: February 16, 2027 (or earlier upon completion/notice)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and capital return to shareholders of Medplus Health Services Limited through open market share repurchases up to Rs. 130 Crores.
