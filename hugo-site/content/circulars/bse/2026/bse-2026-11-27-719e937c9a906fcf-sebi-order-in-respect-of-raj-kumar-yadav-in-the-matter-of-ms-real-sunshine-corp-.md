## Summary

SEBI has disposed of the proceedings initiated against Shri Raj Kumar Yadav in the matter of M/s Real Sunshine Corp Limited vide order dated November 27, 2026, without any further directions.

## Key Points

- SEBI order no. QJA/SM/IMD /DoF-1/32867/2026-27 dated November 27, 2026.
- Disposes of proceedings initiated vide Order No. WTM/MPB/EFD-1-DRA-IV/139/2018 dated February 15, 2018.
- Individual involved: Shri Raj Kumar Yadav (PAN: AHWPY1598A).
- Company involved: M/s Real Sunshine Corp Limited.
- Proceedings disposed of without any further directions.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order concludes existing proceedings without imposing penalties or directions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance. No active compliance actions required as proceedings are disposed of.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect
- Previous Order Reference: February 15, 2018

## Impact Assessment

- Neutral impact as the proceedings against the individual have been disposed of without any adverse directions or penalties.
