## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 5,20,000 new equity shares of Caprihans India Limited (Scrip Code: 509486) with effect from November 30, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Caprihans India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 509486
- **ISIN:** INE479A01018
- **Number of Shares:** 5,20,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue Price: Rs. 200/-)
- **Allotment Date:** May 29, 2026
- **Distribution Numbers:** 16433972 to 16953971
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing approval for preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and lock-in requirements. The newly allotted shares are under lock-in up to May 29, 2028.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 29, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in capital expansion through preferential allotment to promoters, with standard lock-in restrictions applicable.
