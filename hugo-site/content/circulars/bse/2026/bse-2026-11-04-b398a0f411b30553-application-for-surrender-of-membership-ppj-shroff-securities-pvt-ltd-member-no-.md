## Summary

P.P.J. Shroff Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member no. 568) has applied to surrender its trading membership of BSE in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Member Name: P.P.J. Shroff Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Number: 568
- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Objection Deadline: November 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard membership surrender procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise any objections to the surrender of membership must submit their written objections to the Membership Department on or before November 18, 2026 (within 15 days from the date of the notice).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Objection Deadline: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice with minimal market impact. Any claims or objections must be filed within the stipulated timeframe.
