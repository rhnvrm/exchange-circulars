## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms of empanelled vendors or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can utilize this session to test trading applications under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026, for Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments.
- No new BOLT TWS Release will be deployed.
- Timings: Log-in from 11:15 am to 12:00 pm; Continuous Trading from 12:00 pm to 04:00 pm.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no legal rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the session by 05:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is an operational testing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Participation is voluntary but encouraged for testing robustness. Members are requested to submit feedback via email or contact numbers provided by 05:00 pm on the mock trading day.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 21, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The session allows market participants to verify system readiness and API stability without financial exposure.
