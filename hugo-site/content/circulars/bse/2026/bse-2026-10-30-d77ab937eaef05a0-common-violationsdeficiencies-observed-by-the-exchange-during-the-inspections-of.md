## Summary

BSE has issued a circular communicating the common violations and deficiencies observed during inspections of Trading Members and Authorised Persons for the half-year period of October 2025 to March 2026. Trading members are advised to review these areas and reinforce their internal systems and compliance procedures.

## Key Points

- Covers inspections of Trading Members and Authorised Persons for the period October 2025 to March 2026.
- Refers to earlier inspection guidance under Notice Nos. 20240822-19 and 20250127-22.
- Annexure A provides the detailed list of observed violations and deficiencies.
- Members are required to evaluate their compliance frameworks and rectify potential gaps.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations introduced; continuation and reinforcement of existing compliance monitoring standards via periodic inspections.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must examine the areas listed in Annexure A, implement necessary systems and controls, and ensure strict adherence to applicable regulatory standards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 October 2026
- Inspection Period Covered: October 2025 to March 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational integrity and regulatory awareness among Trading Members and Authorised Persons, reducing compliance risks across the exchange ecosystem.
