## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 2,335 new equity shares of Grasim Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 500300) are permitted to be traded with effect from November 5, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Grasim Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 500300
- ISIN: INE047A01021
- Number of Securities: 2,335 Equity shares
- Issue Type: Conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis (Face Value: Rs. 2/- each)
- Distinctive Numbers: 680477159 to 680479493
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities as per exchange norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market liquidity and capital structure due to the small number of shares (2,335 shares) being listed.
