## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in debt securities of JSW Paints Limited due to debt restructuring, effective from Wednesday, November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** JSW Paints Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 977287
- **ISIN:** INE754Y08027
- **Reason:** Debt restructuring
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified scrip code and ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the debt securities (ISIN: INE754Y08027) will experience trading suspension on the exchange platform due to restructuring activities.
