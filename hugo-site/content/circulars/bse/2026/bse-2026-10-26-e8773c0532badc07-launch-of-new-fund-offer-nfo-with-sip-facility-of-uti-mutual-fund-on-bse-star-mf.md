## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the UTI Financial Services Debt Fund NFO along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from October 27, 2026, to November 02, 2026. The NFO includes Regular and Direct plans for both Growth and IDCW Payout options.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered:
  - UTI Financial Services Debt Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AI17)
  - UTI Financial Services Debt Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF789F1AI33)
  - UTI Financial Services Debt Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AI25)
  - UTI Financial Services Debt Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF789F1AI41)
- NFO Period: October 27, 2026, to November 02, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 02, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 03, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on November 02, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 02, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 03, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 03, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to participate in the UTI Financial Services Debt Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF Platform with standard operational and funding guidelines.
