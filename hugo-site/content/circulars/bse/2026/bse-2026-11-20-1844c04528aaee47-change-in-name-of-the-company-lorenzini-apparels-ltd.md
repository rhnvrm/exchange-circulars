## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Lorenzini Apparels Ltd. has changed its name to MONTEIL LIMITED. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name for the company on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Lorenzini Apparels Ltd.
- New Company Name: MONTEIL LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 540952
- Existing Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): LORENZINI
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): MONTEIL
- Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): MONTEIL
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action reflecting a company name change.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to update their trading terminals, back-office systems, and records with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members and investors to reflect the new name and identifiers in trading systems. No impact on existing shareholdings or corporate rights.
