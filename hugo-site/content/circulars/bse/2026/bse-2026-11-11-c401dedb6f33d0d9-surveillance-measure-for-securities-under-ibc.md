## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective November 12, 2026, specific securities satisfying the criteria have been placed under or moved out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are listed in Annexure I with effect from November 12, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the IBC framework is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are provided in Annexure IV.
- Framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures for companies under the Insolvency Resolution Process pursuant to previous notices dating from June 2018 to August 2026.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework effective November 12, 2026.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors dealing in securities undergoing insolvency proceedings face specific surveillance measures and price band restrictions to safeguard market integrity.
