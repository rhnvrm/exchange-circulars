## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that SEBI, via email dated November 18, 2026, has directed compliance with the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 24, 2026, concerning appeals filed by M/s Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited, Vivid Mercantile Ltd. & Anr, Dharmesh Maldevbhai Godhania, and Rakshit Mahendra Shah.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260601-36 dated June 01, 2026.
- SAT order dated September 24, 2026 granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks from the date of the order.
- Penalty is to be kept in an interest-bearing account by SEBI.
- Appellants include Dharmesh Maldevbhai Godhania, Rakshit Mahendra Shah, Vivid Mercantile Limited, and Satishkumar Ramanlal Gajjar.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of SAT interim relief directions regarding penalty deposits for the specified appellants.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance as directed by SEBI and the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory compliance impact for the named appellants and general awareness for trading members regarding ongoing SAT proceedings and penalty deposit requirements.
