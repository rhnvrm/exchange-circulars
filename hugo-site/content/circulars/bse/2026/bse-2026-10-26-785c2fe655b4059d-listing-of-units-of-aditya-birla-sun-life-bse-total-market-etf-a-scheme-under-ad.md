## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 7,703,897 units of Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF under Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund, effective from Tuesday, October 27, 2026. The units will be traded in dematerialized form with a market lot of one unit under 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 545002
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** BIRLATOTAL
- **ISIN No:** INF209KC1746
- **Number of Units:** 7,703,897 Units
- **Face Value & Issue Price:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 22/10/2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** 27/10/2026
- **Market Makers:** Kanjalochana Finserv Private Limited, iRage Broking Services LLP, East India Securities Limited, Choice Equity Broking Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard security listing circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trades in this scrip are conducted in dematerialized form and in unit market lot of one unit as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional exchange-traded fund option tracking the BSE Total Market TRI, enhancing trading opportunities in the mutual fund segment.
