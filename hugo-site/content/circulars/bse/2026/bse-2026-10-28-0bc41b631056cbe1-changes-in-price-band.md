## Summary

BSE has issued a notice (Notice No. 20261028-31) regarding the revision of price bands for 9 scrips in the equity segment, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 9 scrips under the surveillance department.
- Effective date: October 29, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Muzali Arts Ltd, Shree Rama Multi-Tech Ltd, Manoj Ceramic Ltd, Pashupati Cotspin Ltd, Gautam Exim Ltd, Sampre Nutritions Ltd, Paramount Syntex Ltd, Pind Hospitality Ltd, and Raama Finance Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised price band limits:
  - Muzali Arts Ltd (539410): Revised to 20%
  - Shree Rama Multi-Tech Ltd (532310): Revised to 5%
  - Manoj Ceramic Ltd (544073): Revised to 10%
  - Pashupati Cotspin Ltd (544448): Revised to 10%
  - Gautam Exim Ltd (540613): Revised to 5%
  - Sampre Nutritions Ltd (530617): Revised to 5%
  - Paramount Syntex Ltd (544982): Revised to 5%
  - Pind Hospitality Ltd (544966): Revised to 5%
  - Raama Finance Ltd (538540): Revised to 5%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands while placing orders in the respective scrips to ensure compliance with exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors dealing in these scrips need to incorporate the updated price limits into their trading and risk management strategies.
