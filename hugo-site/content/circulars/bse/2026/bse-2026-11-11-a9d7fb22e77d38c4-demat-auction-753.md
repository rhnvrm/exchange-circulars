## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-753/2026-27. The notice specifies the timelines for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026, from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement & Report of Accepted Offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Thursday, Nov 12, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A] Routine procedural notice under existing settlement mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 11, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly clearance and settlement of short-delivered securities for the specified rolling settlement cycle.
