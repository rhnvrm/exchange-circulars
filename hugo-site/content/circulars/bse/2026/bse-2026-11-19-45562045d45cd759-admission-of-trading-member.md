## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the admission of Edgequity Ventures Private Limited as a Trading Member of the exchange, granting trading rights in the Cash and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Member Name: Edgequity Ventures Private Limited
- Member Number: 3321
- Approved Segments: Cash and Equity Derivatives
- Membership Type: New Membership
- Date of Admission: 17/11/2026

None.
