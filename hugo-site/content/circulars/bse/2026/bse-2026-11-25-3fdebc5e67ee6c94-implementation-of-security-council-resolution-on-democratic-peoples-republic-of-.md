## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) 1718 Sanctions Committee update concerning one entry on its Sanctions List of Designated Individuals and Entities, pursuant to Note SC/16512 dated November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notification of amendment to one entry in the UNSC 1718 Sanctions List via UN Note SC/16512 dated November 16, 2026.
- Reference to UNSC press release and Committee website resources for detailed lists of amended entries.
- Instructions to adhere to Ministry of Finance Order dated January 30, 2023, under Section 12A of the Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the UNSC 1718 Sanctions List reflecting amendments in designated individuals and entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must follow the procedure outlined in Ministry of Finance Order F.No.P-12011/14/2022-ES Cell-DOR, dated January 30, 2023.
- Scan all existing accounts and future accounts to ensure no account is held by or linked to any listed entities or individuals.
- Regularly visit the SEBI website under 'Media and Notifications' > 'UNSC Sanctions Committee List' for ongoing updates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- UNSC Note Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must maintain robust screening mechanisms against updated UNSC sanctions lists to prevent association with designated entities and ensure regulatory compliance.
