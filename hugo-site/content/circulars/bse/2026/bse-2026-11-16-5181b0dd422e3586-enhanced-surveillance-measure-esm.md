## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities that satisfy criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, and continued surveillance effective mid-November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 18, 2026, on all open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Specified securities are moved to a lower stage (Stage I) under the ESM framework w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of stricter surveillance controls including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must maintain 100% margins on open and new positions for affected securities and note the transition to trade-for-trade settlement.

## Important Dates

- November 17, 2026: Movement to lower stages / Stage II implementation / price band changes.
- November 18, 2026: 100% margin requirement effective; shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips face heightened surveillance, mandatory 100% margins, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, impacting short-term liquidity and trading participation.
