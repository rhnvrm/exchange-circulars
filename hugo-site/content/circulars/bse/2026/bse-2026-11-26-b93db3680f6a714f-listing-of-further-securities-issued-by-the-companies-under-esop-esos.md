## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 10 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Friday, November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 10 companies have issued further shares under ESOP / ESOS.
- Notable companies include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, CreditAccess Grameen Limited, Hero Motors Limited, and Poonawalla Fincorp Limited.
- All listed shares rank pari passu with existing equity shares of the respective companies.
- Effective Date for trading: November 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for securities issued under ESOP/ESOS.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing companies must ensure compliance with SEBI (ICDR) Regulations and BSE listing requirements for employee stock option allotments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action with minimal immediate market impact, representing incremental equity dilution due to employee stock option exercises.
