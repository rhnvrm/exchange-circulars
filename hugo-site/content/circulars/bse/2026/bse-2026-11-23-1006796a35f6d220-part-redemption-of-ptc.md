## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) managed by Catalyst Trusteeship Ltd, effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Partial redemption affects three specific PTC issuances by Samriddhi Trust and India Universal Trust.
- Samriddhi Trust (Scrip Code: 977513, ISIN: INE2QEZ15011): Reduced face value of Rs. 2,496,236.43 per PTC.
- India Universal Trust AL1 – Series A3 (Scrip Code: 976052, ISIN: INE16J715035): Reduced face value of Rs. 8,190,166.35 per PTC.
- India Universal Trust AL1 - Series A2 (Scrip Code: 976051, ISIN: INE16J715027): Reduced face value of Rs. 2,121,427.42 per PTC.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding debt instrument face value reduction.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note that the affected PTCs will be traded with the revised reduced face values starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members holding or trading the specified PTC debt securities, ensuring accurate valuation and settlement based on the reduced face values.
