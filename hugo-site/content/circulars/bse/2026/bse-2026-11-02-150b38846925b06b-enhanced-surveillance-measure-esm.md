## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of securities, movement to lower stages, and securities shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 04, 2026, on open positions as of November 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in ESM but are moved to lower Stage I w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 04, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, Trade-for-Trade settlement, 2% price bands, and Periodic Call Auction mechanisms for specified securities under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement rules, and price band restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **November 03, 2026**: Movement of securities to lower Stage I / Stage II (with Trade for Trade, 2% price band, and Periodic Call Auction); effective date for review.
- **November 04, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin on open positions (as of Nov 3) and new positions, and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will experience tightened controls, impacting short-term trading dynamics and settlement procedures.
