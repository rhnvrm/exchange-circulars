## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the empanelment of QICAP Management Private Limited as an approved Algo Provider for supplying Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members of the exchange, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Name:** QICAP Management Private Limited
- **Address:** 503 to 504A, Road 5E Zone 5, Gandhinagar, Gujarat - 382050
- **Contact Number:** +91 6354239383
- **Applicability:** With immediate effect

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational empanelment notice under trading operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions can engage with the empanelled vendor in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 28 Oct 2026
- **Effective Date:** With immediate effect (28 Oct 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with an additional empanelled vendor option for algorithmic trading infrastructure.
