## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 13,00,000 equity shares of RLF Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512618) issued on a preferential basis to promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company:** RLF Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 512618
- **ISIN:** INE629C01014
- **Securities:** 13,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 0.50/-.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10.50/-
- **Date of Allotment:** 02/09/2026
- **Listing Date:** 12/11/2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9988884 to 11288883
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked in up to 15/05/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate listing notice for preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements up to 15/05/2028 and trading permissions effective from November 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 12, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 2, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** May 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the paid-up equity capital of RLF Ltd. through preferential allotment to promoters, with standard lock-in restrictions applicable.
