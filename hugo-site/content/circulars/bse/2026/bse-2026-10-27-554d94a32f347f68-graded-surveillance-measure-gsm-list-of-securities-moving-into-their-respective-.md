## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the movement of specific securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. Effective October 28, 2026, these securities will be subjected to enhanced surveillance actions, margin requirements, and trading restrictions as per the established GSM framework.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher GSM stages (Stage I, II, III, or IV) starting October 28, 2026.
- Stage I: 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade settlement with 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade settlement with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD by buyers (Stage IV has no upward movement allowed).
- Settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter trading and surveillance controls under the GSM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities and ensure compliance with enhanced margin deposits, trade-for-trade settlement rules, and frequency restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for GSM Stage Movement: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in the affected securities will face significant liquidity constraints due to trade-for-trade settlement, reduced trading frequency in higher stages, and mandatory deposit requirements.
