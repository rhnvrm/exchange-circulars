## Summary

BSE has announced the activation of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) of HDFC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 12, 2026. Additionally, the H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund NFO scheme will be activated for order routing starting November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- HDFC Mutual Fund SIF is live on the BSE StAR MF Platform from November 12, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and Minor clients are permitted under HDFC Mutual Fund SIF schemes, excluding investors from Canada and the United States of America.
- H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund NFO scheme order routing enabled effective November 13, 2026.
- Detailed transaction acceptance processes are outlined in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/26 dated February 27, 2025, regarding the regulatory framework for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with minimum investment thresholds and country restrictions for NRI applications (Canada and USA applications are not allowed).

## Important Dates

- November 12, 2026: Activation of HDFC Mutual Fund SIF on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- November 13, 2026: Activation of H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund NFO scheme for order routing.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading and onboarding of investors, including NRIs and minors, into HDFC's Specialized Investment Funds through the BSE StAR MF platform.
