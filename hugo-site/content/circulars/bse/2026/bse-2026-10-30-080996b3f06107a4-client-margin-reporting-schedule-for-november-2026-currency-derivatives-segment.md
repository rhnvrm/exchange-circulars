## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the schedule for client margin reporting in the Currency Derivatives Segment for the month of November 2026, detailing trade dates, margin reporting deadlines, and penalty dates.

## Key Points

- Outlines day-by-day margin reporting schedules for trade dates across November 2026.
- Specifies corresponding penalty dates for delayed or non-reported margins.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited for members participating in the Currency Derivatives segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine operational schedule update for monthly margin compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and trading members must adhere to the stipulated margin reporting deadlines for each trade date to prevent levy of penalties.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- First Trade Date in Schedule: November 2, 2026
- Last Trade Date in Schedule: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational clarity and risk management compliance for market participants trading in currency derivatives throughout November 2026.
