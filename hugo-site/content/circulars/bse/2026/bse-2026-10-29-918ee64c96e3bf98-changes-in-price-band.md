## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 11 scrips, effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 11 scrips including Spencers Retail Ltd, Bajaj Holdings & Investment Ltd, and others.
- Revised price bands range from 2%, 5%, to 20% depending on the scrip.
- Effective date of implementation is October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of daily price limits for specified securities under surveillance and risk management frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their trading systems and RMS software reflect the updated price bands before market open on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for the listed scrips will be constrained within the newly defined percentage price bands, mitigating extreme intraday volatility.
