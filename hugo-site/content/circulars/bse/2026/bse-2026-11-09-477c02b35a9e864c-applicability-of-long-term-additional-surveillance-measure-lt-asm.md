## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities in accordance with the revised framework. The measures include the imposition of 100% margins, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and price band adjustments effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable w.e.f. November 13, 2026 on all open positions as on November 12, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Selected securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Lower Price Band becomes applicable w.e.f. November 11, 2026 for specified securities.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 11, 2026, with their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under the framework is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance action, not an adverse corporate action.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM), mandatory 100% margin requirements, lower price band restrictions, and transfers to specialized trading groups.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must adhere to the 100% margin requirements and account for price band and group transfer changes for the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- November 11, 2026: Movement to higher stages / exit from LT-ASM framework, application of lower price bands, and price band reinstatements.
- November 12, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions.
- November 13, 2026: 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity for the shortlisted securities may experience moderation due to 100% margin requirements and trading group restrictions.
