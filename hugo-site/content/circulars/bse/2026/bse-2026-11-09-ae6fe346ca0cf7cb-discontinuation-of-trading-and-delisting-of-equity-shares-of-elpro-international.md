## Summary

BSE has announced the voluntary delisting of equity shares of Elpro International Ltd (Scrip Code: 504000) pursuant to SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021. Trading in the equity shares will be discontinued effective November 17, 2026, and the scrip will be delisted from Exchange records effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Elpro International Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 504000
- **Trading Discontinuation Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Delisting Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Promoter / Acquirers:** I G E (India) Private Limited and Zenox Technology Services Private Limited
- **Exit Price:** Rs. 181.80 per Equity Share
- **Exit Option Period:** Up to one year from the date of delisting

## Regulatory Changes

Processed in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021 following compliance with voluntary delisting formalities by the company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the trading discontinuation and subsequent delisting dates. Remaining public shareholders may submit their option forms along with required documentation to the company for the exit option.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Discontinuation Date:** Tuesday, November 17, 2026
- **Delisting Date:** Wednesday, November 25, 2026
- **Exit Window Expiry:** Up to one year from the delisting date

## Impact Assessment

Equity shares of Elpro International Ltd will no longer be traded or listed on the BSE platform. Public shareholders holding shares beyond the delisting date must utilize the promoter-provided exit window at Rs. 181.80 per share within the one-year validity period.
