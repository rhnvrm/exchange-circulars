## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 28,21,411 new equity shares of South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (Scrip Code: 543986) issued at a price of Rs. 132/- per share (face value Rs. 10/- at a premium of Rs. 122/-) pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company:** South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited (Scrip Code: 543986, ISIN: INE980Y01015)
- **Security Type:** Equity shares issued on preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Number of Shares:** 28,21,411 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 132/- per share (including premium of Rs. 122/-).
- **Allotment Date:** July 10, 2026.
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 29, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing and trading permission granted for newly issued preferential equity shares following warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 10-07-2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** 29-10-2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (22,69,288 shares, Dist. Nos. 29830012 to 32099299):** 29-04-2028
- **Lock-in Expiry (5,52,123 shares, Dist. Nos. 32099300 to 32651422):** 29-04-2027

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital of South West Pinnacle Exploration Limited.
- Shares are subject to specified lock-in periods and distribution numbers.
