## Summary

SEBI vide its order dated October 30, 2026, issued final directions regarding EKI Energy Services Ltd. and several individuals, restraining them from accessing the securities market for specified periods.

## Key Points

- M/S. EKI Energy Services restrained for 2 months.
- Manish Kumar Oabkara restrained for 12 months.
- Naveen Sharma restrained for 5 months.
- Sonali Shekh restrained for 1 month.
- Mohit Kumar Agarwal restrained for 8 months.
- Permission granted to close out/square off open derivative positions within 3 months or upon contract expiry.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of market access restrictions and debarment by SEBI on the specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the SEBI order and ensure compliance across all trading segments.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 30, 2026
- Notice Date: November 2, 2026
- Square-off Window: Within 3 months from the order date or contract expiry.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct operational and market participation restriction on EKI Energy Services Ltd. and key personnel.
