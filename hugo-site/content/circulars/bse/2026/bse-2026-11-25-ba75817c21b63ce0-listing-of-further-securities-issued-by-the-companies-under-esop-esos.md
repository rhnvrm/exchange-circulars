## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 18 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers 18 listed companies including Gland Pharma, Aether Industries, RBL Bank, Tech Mahindra, Wipro, Shriram Finance, and others.
- Effective date for trading of these new securities: November 26, 2026.
- Shares are issued pursuant to exercise of options under company ESOP/ESOS schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action relating to employee stock option exercises and subsequent share listings.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must verify lock-in details (where applicable, such as for Metropolis Healthcare Limited, Annexure I) and ensure adherence to SEBI and stock exchange listing regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital and increase in listed share volume for the issuing companies without significant market or operational disruption.
