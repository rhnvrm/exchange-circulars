## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of KRONOX LAB SCIENCES LIMITED, scheduled to be conducted from November 6, 2026, to November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026135
- **Bid Start Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** December 4, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** December 7, 2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in depositories using Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement No. 2026135.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file; stamp duty charges are detailed in the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is completed and reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for bidding and confirmation windows.
- Shares will be earmarked or blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
