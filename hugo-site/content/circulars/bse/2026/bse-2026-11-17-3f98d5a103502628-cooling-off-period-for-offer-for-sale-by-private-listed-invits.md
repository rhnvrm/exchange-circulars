## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/17(7)2026-DDHS-POD2/I/24929/2026 dated November 02, 2026, concerning the cooling off period for offer for sale (OFS) by private listed Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs). The circular comes into force with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Implements SEBI guidelines on cooling off periods for offer for sale by private listed InvITs.
- Applicable to private listed Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs).
- Came into force with immediate effect from November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption and enforcement of SEBI's prescribed cooling off period regulations for OFS transactions conducted by private listed InvITs.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed private InvITs and associated market participants must take note of the prescribed cooling off period rules and ensure compliance during offer for sale transactions.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- BSE Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory clarity and structure for secondary market transactions and capital raising via offer for sale by private listed InvITs, ensuring orderly market operations.
