## Summary

BSE has announced a special Muhurat Trading session for Request for Quote (RFQ) on the NDS-RST platform on Sunday, November 8, 2026, on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, modifying previous trading holiday notices.

## Key Points

- **Platform:** NDS-RST (Request for Quote platform)
- **Trading Date:** Sunday, November 8, 2026
- **Market Timing:** 02:05 PM to 03:00 PM
- **Eligible Instruments:** Only Corporate Bonds (as defined under SEBI Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities Regulations)
- **Settlement Cycle:** T+1 settlement
- **Settlement Date:** November 9, 2026
- **Settlement Number:** 2627149

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification to notice no. 20251223-12 dated December 23, 2025, regarding Trading Holidays for Calendar Year 2026 for NDS-RST and Tri-Party Repo participants.

## Compliance Requirements

All trades executed during the Diwali Muhurat trading session shall result in settlement obligations. Participants may reach out to cm_settlements@icclindia.com or call 022-22725822 for further queries.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Session:** November 8, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; provides clarity on trading timings and settlement obligations for corporate bonds during the Diwali Muhurat session.
