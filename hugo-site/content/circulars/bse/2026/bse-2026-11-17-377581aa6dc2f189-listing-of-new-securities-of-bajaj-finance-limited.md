## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 114,146,341 new equity shares of Bajaj Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 500034, ISIN: INE296A01032) with effect from November 18, 2026, following allotment to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 114,146,341 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each.
- **Allotment Date:** November 17, 2026.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1025/- per share.
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 18, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 6225954305 to 6340100645.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the admission of new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 17, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base of Bajaj Finance Limited through QIP issuance of shares to QIBs at Rs. 1025/- per share.
