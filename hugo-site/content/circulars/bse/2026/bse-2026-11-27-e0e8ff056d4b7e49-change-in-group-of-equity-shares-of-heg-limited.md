## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of HEG Limited (formerly known as HEG Graphite Limited, BSE Scrip code: 544896) will be permitted under the 'B' group effective from Friday, December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Limited (formerly known as HEG Graphite Limited)
- BSE Scrip Code: 544896
- New Trading Group: 'B' Group
- Effective Date: December 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. This is a routine trading group reclassification notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the change in trading group for HEG Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Group Change: December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational or market impact as this represents a standard reclassification of the trading group on the exchange.
