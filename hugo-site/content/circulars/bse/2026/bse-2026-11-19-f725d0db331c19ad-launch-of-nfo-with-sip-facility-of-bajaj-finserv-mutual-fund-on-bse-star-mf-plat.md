## Summary

BAJAJ FINSERV MUTUAL FUND is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Bajaj Finserv Business Cycle Fund along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 20, 2026, to December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Bajaj Finserv Business Cycle Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- **NFO Window:** November 20, 2026, to December 04, 2026 (extended till midnight on Dec 04 for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 11, 2026.
- **SIP Registrations:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 10, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be completed and authenticated by 3:00 PM on December 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** November 20, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** December 04, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** December 10, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and trading members in participating in the new fund offer and setting up SIPs on the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines and fund clearance guidelines.
