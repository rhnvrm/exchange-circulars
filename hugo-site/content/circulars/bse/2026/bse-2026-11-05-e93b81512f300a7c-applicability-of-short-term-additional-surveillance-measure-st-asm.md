## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities specified in Annexure I, pursuant to the revised ASM framework. The applicable margin rate for Stage I is set at 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%), and for Stage II is set at 100% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%). These measures take effect from November 08, 2026, on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting specified surveillance criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I Margin: 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Stage II Margin: 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Effective Date: Effective from November 08, 2026, applicable to open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created from November 08, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for identified securities to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open positions as of November 06, 2026, and all new positions created from November 08, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Measures: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in securities listed under Annexure I will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin obligations (up to 100%), potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
