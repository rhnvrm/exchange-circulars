## Summary

BSE has announced the outcome of the Reverse Book Building Process (RBBP) under Regulation 17(3) of SEBI Delisting Regulations for Jindal Photo Limited, made by the acquirers Concatenate Power Advest Private Limited and Concatenate Advest Advisory Private Limited along with PAC Jindal India Power Limited.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Jindal Photo Limited
- Acquirers: Concatenate Power Advest Private Limited and Concatenate Advest Advisory Private Limited
- Person Acting in Concert (PAC): Jindal India Power Limited
- Offer Size: Acquisition of 26,46,183 Equity Shares held by Public Shareholders
- Floor Price: Rs. 1,119.50 per equity share
- Indicative Price: Rs. 1,120.00 per equity share
- Bidding Period: Monday, November 02, 2026, to Friday, November 06, 2026
- Registrar to the Offer: MUFG Intime India Private Limited (Formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)
- Manager to the Offer: Saffron Capital Advisors Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Disclosed in accordance with Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Acquirers, Registrar, and Manager to complete necessary procedural filings and notifications as per SEBI Delisting Regulations based on the RBBP outcome.

## Important Dates

- Bidding Period Start Date: November 02, 2026
- Bidding Period End Date / Outcome Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on the public shareholders and delisting status of Jindal Photo Limited on stock exchanges.
