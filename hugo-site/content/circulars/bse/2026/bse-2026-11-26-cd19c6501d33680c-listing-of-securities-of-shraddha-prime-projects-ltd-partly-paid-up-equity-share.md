## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that effective from Monday, November 30, 2026, new securities of Shraddha Prime Projects Ltd shall be listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following a rights issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shraddha Prime Projects Ltd
- Securities: 60,60,150 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each partly paid-up of Rs. 9/-
- Scrip Code: 890245
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SHRADDHAPP
- Group: B
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN No.: IN9311M01016
- Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 9/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 160/- (Rs. 144/- paid on application and Rs. 16/- to be paid on one or more subsequent calls as determined by the Board)
- Distinctive Numbers: 44186301 - 50246450
- Date of Allotment: 23/11/2026
- Dividend Entitlement: Proportionate to the amount paid-up

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary ISIN No. IN8311M01025 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 dated August 02, 2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 dated September 11, 2012 regarding activation of ISIN in case of additional issue of shares/securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the suspension of the temporary ISIN and the activation/listing of the new ISIN for trading effective November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 23, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables market participants and investors to trade the newly allotted partly paid-up rights shares of Shraddha Prime Projects Ltd on the BSE platform.
