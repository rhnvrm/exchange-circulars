## Summary

VRL Logistics Limited has announced an Offer to Buyback up to 87,50,000 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 5% of total paid-up equity share capital as of March 31, 2026) at a price of Rs. 320/- per equity share through the Tender Offer route. The total aggregate consideration is up to Rs. 28,000 Lakhs.

## Key Points

- **Company:** VRL Logistics Limited
- **Buyback Size:** Up to 87,50,000 Equity Shares (5% of total paid-up equity share capital)
- **Record Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Buyback Price:** Rs. 320/- per equity share (payable in cash)
- **Aggregate Consideration:** Up to Rs. 28,000 Lakhs
- **Offer Period:** Tuesday, October 27, 2026, to Monday, November 02, 2026
- **Route:** Tender Offer through Acquisition Window on Stock Exchange

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 (dated April 13, 2015) and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 (dated Dec 09, 2016), along with relevant Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the buyback window timings and guidelines for tender offers.
- Eligible shareholders as on the record date (October 22, 2026) can participate through the tender offer mechanism.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Buyback Opening Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Buyback Closing Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity and liquidity to eligible shareholders of VRL Logistics Limited through a share buyback at a specified tender price.
