## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding SEBI Circular no. HO/38/11/(9)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/24272/2026 dated October 23, 2026, concerning the review of regulatory requirements for the maintenance of call records of institutional clients by Research Analysts or Research Entities.

## Key Points

- Based on SEBI Circular no. HO/38/11/(9)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/24272/2026 dated October 23, 2026.
- Focuses on the maintenance of call records for institutional clients by Research Analysts and Research Entities.
- Provisions come into effect from November 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Review and update of the regulatory framework governing call record retention and maintenance for institutional interactions by research analysts and entities.

## Compliance Requirements

All Research Analysts and Research Entities must ensure compliance with the revised call record maintenance mandates as stipulated in multidimensional SEBI circular guidelines by the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 27, 2026
- **SEBI Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Research Analysts and Research Entities dealing with institutional clients must align their compliance, record-keeping, and communication archiving systems with the revised SEBI guidelines ahead of the effective date.
