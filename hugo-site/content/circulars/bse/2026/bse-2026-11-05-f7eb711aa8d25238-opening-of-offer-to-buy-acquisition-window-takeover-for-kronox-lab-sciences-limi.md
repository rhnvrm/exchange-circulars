## Summary

Indo Borax and Chemicals Limited ("Acquirer") along with Zenrock Chemicals Private Limited ("PAC") have announced an open offer to acquire up to 95,70,000 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 25.79% of the voting share capital) of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited ("Target Company") at an offer price of Rs. 157.27 per share payable in cash. The acquisition window opens on November 06, 2026, and closes on November 20, 2026 (excluding the SEBI Holiday on November 10, 2026). BSE is the designated stock exchange for this offer.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer**: Indo Borax and Chemicals Limited
- **PAC**: Zenrock Chemicals Private Limited
- **Target Company**: Kronox Lab Sciences Limited
- **Offer Size**: Up to 95,70,000 equity shares (25.79% of voting share capital)
- **Offer Price**: Rs. 157.27 per share payable in cash
- **Offer Period**: November 06, 2026 to November 20, 2026
- **Exclusions**: SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, November 10, 2026
- **Designated Stock Exchange**: BSE

## Regulatory Changes

Conducted pursuant to Regulation 3(1), Regulation 4, and other applicable provisions of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, along with SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 (April 13, 2015) and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 (December 09, 2016) and corresponding exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange via tender-offers under takeover regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date**: November 06, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday**: November 10, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date**: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited an exit and tender opportunity via the acquisition window on BSE at Rs. 157.27 per share.
