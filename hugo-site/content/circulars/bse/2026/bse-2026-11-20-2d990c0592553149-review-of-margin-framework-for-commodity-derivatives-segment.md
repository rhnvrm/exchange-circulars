## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the review of the margin framework for the commodity derivatives segment, referencing SEBI Circular no. HO/47/16/14(3)2026-MRD-POD1/I/25885/2026 dated November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- References SEBI circular dated November 17, 2026 regarding margin framework review.
- Encloses a copy of the SEBI circular as Annexure 1 for member reference.
- Applies to all members trading or clearing in the commodity derivatives segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the reviewed margin framework for commodity derivatives as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the revised margin framework guidelines and ensure compliance as per the enclosed SEBI circular.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts risk management and margin calculations for market participants operating in the commodity derivatives segment.
