## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips. Clarifications have been successfully received from three companies, while responses from twenty-one other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from:
  1. Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Ltd (Scrip Code: 500108)
  2. Aerpace Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 534733)
  3. KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration Ltd (Scrip Code: 544263)
- Replies awaited from 21 listed entities including Bharat Immunologicals, CMX Holdings, Spright Agro, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard surveillance communication to ensure market transparency and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities flagged for abnormal price or volume movements must submit requested clarifications to the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and transparency by keeping investors informed about corporate clarifications regarding stock price and volume movements.
