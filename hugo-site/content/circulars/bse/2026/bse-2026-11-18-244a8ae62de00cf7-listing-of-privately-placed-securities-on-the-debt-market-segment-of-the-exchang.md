## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Power Finance Corporation Ltd on the debt market segment effective November 18, 2026. The issuance includes two tranches of non-convertible debentures carrying interest rates of 8.21% and 8.05% per annum respectively.

## Key Points

- Power Finance Corporation Ltd has listed two tranches of privately placed debt securities (Scrip Codes: 978403 and 978404).
- Tranche 1: 296,800 units with 8.21% p.a. interest, maturing on 16/05/2028 (ISIN: INE134E08OH2).
- Tranche 2: 297,300 units with 8.05% p.a. interest, maturing on 17/11/2031 (ISIN: INE134E08OG4).
- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise and market lot of 1.
- Credit rating for both issuances is CARE AAA/Stable, CRISIL AAA/Stable, and ICRA AAA/Stable.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard corporate debt listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with dematerialised settlement requirements and reference the correct ISIN and scrip codes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Interest Payment Dates: Annually from 16/05/2027 (Tranche 1) and 17/11/2027 (Tranche 2)
- Redemption Dates: May 16, 2028 (Tranche 1) and November 17, 2031 (Tranche 2)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for investors holding Power Finance Corporation Ltd's privately placed debt instruments.
