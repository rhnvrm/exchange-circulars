## Summary

BSE has announced that Williamson Magor & Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 519224) will be moved out of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework with effect from November 2, 2026. This decision follows joint meetings with SEBI and Stock Exchanges regarding companies undergoing corporate restructuring and placed in Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) for price discovery.

## Key Points

- Williamson Magor & Company Ltd (519224) was previously retained under the GSM framework.
- Companies undergoing corporate restructuring placed in Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) for price discovery are moved out of surveillance frameworks.
- The scrip is moved out of GSM w.e.f. November 2, 2026, following the SPOS price discovery mechanism.
- Future inclusion under surveillance frameworks will depend on prescribed eligibility criteria and timelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of the security from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework post-SPOS price discovery pursuant to joint SEBI and stock exchange decisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants to take note of the removal of GSM restrictions for Williamson Magor & Company Ltd effective November 2, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of GSM Removal: November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading restrictions under the GSM framework are lifted for Williamson Magor & Company Ltd, facilitating regular market trading subject to future surveillance reviews.
