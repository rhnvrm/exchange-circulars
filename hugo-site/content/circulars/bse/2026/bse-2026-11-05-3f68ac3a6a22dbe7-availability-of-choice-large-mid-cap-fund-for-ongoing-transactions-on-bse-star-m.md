## Summary

BSE has announced that the CHOICE LARGE & MID CAP FUND shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from November 06, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261104-13 dated November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** CHOICE LARGE & MID CAP FUND
- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- **Eligible Plans & ISINs:**
  1. CHOICE LARGE & MID CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF2KCX01111)
  2. CHOICE LARGE & MID CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF2KCX01103)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine trading operations notification regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions, subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic plans for Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
