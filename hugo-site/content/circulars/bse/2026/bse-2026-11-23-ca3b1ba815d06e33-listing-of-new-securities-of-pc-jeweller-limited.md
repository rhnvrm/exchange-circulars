## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 3,63,62,222 equity shares of PC Jeweller Limited (Scrip Code: 534809) with effect from November 25, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PC Jeweller Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 534809
- **ISIN:** INE785M01021
- **Security Details:** 3,63,62,222 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 17/- (Issue Price: Rs. 18/-).
- **Basis of Allotment:** Preferential issue pursuant to conversion of warrants to promoters.
- **Effective Date:** Wednesday, November 25, 2026.
- **Date of Allotment:** 24/09/2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9771394856 to 9807757077.
- **Lock-in Details:** 3,63,62,222 shares are locked-in up to 10/06/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities admitted to dealings and the applicable lock-in period.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** June 10, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The issuance and listing of preferential shares increase the paid-up equity capital of the company. The shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares, subject to mandatory promoter lock-in regulations.
