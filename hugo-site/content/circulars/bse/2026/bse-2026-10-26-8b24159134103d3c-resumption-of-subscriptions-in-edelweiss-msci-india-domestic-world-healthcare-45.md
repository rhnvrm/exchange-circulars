## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions in the Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective October 26, 2026. Additionally, Edelweiss AMC has decided to permanently discontinue paused SIP/XSIP and STP-in registrations for several specific offshore funds.

## Key Points

- Subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations are resumed for Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund effective October 26, 2026.
- Existing paused SIP/XSIP and STP-in registrations for this healthcare index fund will be reactivated on or before November 20, 2026.
- Paused SIP/XSIP and STP-in registrations for six specified Edelweiss offshore funds are permanently cancelled with immediate effect.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, STP-outs, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted without changes for all mentioned schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Resumption of investor subscription workflows on the BSE StAR MF platform for the specified index fund.
- Permanent cancellation of legacy paused systematic instructions for select offshore equity and fund-of-fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors are advised to take note of the resumption of subscriptions and the cancellation of paused SIP/STP registrations when executing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **October 26, 2026**: Effective date for resumption of lumpsum, switch-in, and fresh SIP/STP registrations in Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund.
- **November 20, 2026 (or earlier)**: Reactivation date for existing paused SIP/STP registrations in the healthcare index fund.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund can resume fresh investments and regular contributions.
- Investors holding paused SIP/STP instructions in the listed offshore funds will need to set up fresh registrations if they wish to invest, as existing paused instructions stand cancelled.
