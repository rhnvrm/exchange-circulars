## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 16, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and settlement restrictions.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.
- Framework stages include Stage I, II, III, and IV with progressively stringent surveillance and margin requirements.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moving into GSM stages attract specific surveillance measures such as 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, 5% or lower price bands, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and restricted trading frequency (once a week for higher stages).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement without netting off, and deposit applicable ASD as mandated.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date of GSM Movement: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities is subject to heightened restrictions and margin obligations, impacting liquidity and settlement processes for market participants.
