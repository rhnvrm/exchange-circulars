## Summary

S.P. Apparels Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the fixation of the record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 10/- each are being sub-divided into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: S.P. Apparels Ltd
- Scrip Code: 540048
- Record Date: 10/12/2026
- Share Split Ratio: Existing face value of Rs. 10/- each into 5 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.
- Old ISIN (INE212I01016 of Rs. 10/- paid up) ceases to be valid for transactions done on or after 10/12/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action update regarding capital restructuring through stock split / sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the new ISIN (to be communicated separately) and the record date for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 10/12/2026
- Effective Date for Sub-Divided Paid-Up Value: 10/12/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will receive 5 shares of Rs. 2/- each for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. Liquidity and price per share will adjust accordingly in the rolling settlement segment.
