## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 13 companies under Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the exchange with effect from Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 13
- Effective listing date: November 08, 2026
- Participating companies include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Angel One Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, Cipla Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited, and Tata Communications Limited.
- Securities issued represent further equity issuances upon the exercise of employee stock options.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and listed entities must ensure adherence to standard depository and exchange guidelines for newly admitted ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Incremental equity capital expansion resulting from employee stock option exercises across participating companies. Negligible immediate impact on overall market liquidity or price discovery.
