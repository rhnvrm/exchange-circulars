## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 2,89,25,619 new equity shares of Aegis Logistics Limited issued to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) are listed and admitted for trading effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Details: 2,89,25,619 Equity shares of Re.1/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- Scrip Code: 500003
- ISIN No.: INE208C01025
- Issue Price: Rs. 1,210/-
- Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 351000001 - 379925619
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure for securities allotted under QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading parameters and take note of the new securities and distinctive numbers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 16, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base following QIP allotment for Aegis Logistics Limited.
