## Summary

BSE has announced that Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited has fixed November 20, 2026 as the Record Date for its tender offer (buyback) of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited (Scrip Code: 544088)
- Record Date: November 20, 2026
- Purpose: To determine shareholder entitlement and eligibility to participate in the buyback offer.
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible shareholders must note the record date and ex-entitlement date for participating in the buyback offer under the dematerialised securities rolling settlement segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 3, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Entitlement Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts shareholders of Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited wishing to tender their shares in the buyback offer.
