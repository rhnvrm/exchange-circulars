## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies for which significant price and volume movements were observed in the recent past. Replies providing clarifications have been received and attached for select companies, while replies from several other listed entities are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- Replies received from ENS Enterprises Ltd and NIS Management Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 21 other listed companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and others.
- Notice issued by the Surveillance department of BSE Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price and volume movements upon inquiry by the stock exchange to ensure investors have timely and relevant information.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding abnormal price and volume movements in specific scrips.
