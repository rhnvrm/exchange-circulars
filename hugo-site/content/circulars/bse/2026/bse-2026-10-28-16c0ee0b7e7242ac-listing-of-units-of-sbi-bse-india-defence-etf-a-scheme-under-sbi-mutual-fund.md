## Summary

Trading members are hereby informed that with effect from Thursday, October 29, 2026, the units of SBI BSE India Defence ETF, an open-ended scheme replicating or tracking the BSE India Defence Index, are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- **Mutual Fund Name:** SBI Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** SBI Funds Management Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545006
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** SBIDEFETF
- **ISIN No.:** INF200KB1CJ6
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 72.58 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 26/10/2026
- **No. of Units:** 1,984,770 Units
- **Market Lot:** 1 Unit

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notice under existing SEBI and exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000. Trades will be executed in unit market lot (one unit).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 26, 2026
- **Listing and Commencement of Trading:** October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with trading access to the SBI BSE India Defence ETF on the exchange platform.
