## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 14
- Effective date for trading: November 20, 2026
- Securities category: Equity shares issued under ESOP / ESOS
- Includes major financial institutions, pharmaceutical firms, and consumer goods companies.
