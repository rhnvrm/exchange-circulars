## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 14 securities under its surveillance and trading framework. The revised price bands will take effect from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261109-34
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)
- Total Securities affected: 14 scrips
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026
- Price band revisions range from 2% to 20% across various companies.

## Regulatory Changes

The exchange has shortlisted specific securities for revision of their existing price bands as part of surveillance and risk management measures.

- Aditya Forge Ltd (522150): Revised to 2%
- Alliance Integrated Metaliks Ltd (534064): Revised to 2%
- Amforge Industries Ltd (513117): Revised to 5%
- Cranes Software International Ltd (512093): Revised to 2%
- Fratelli Vineyards Ltd (751151): Revised to 20%
- Innovative Tech Pack Ltd (523840): Revised to 10%
- Kaya Ltd (539276): Revised to 2%
- Mipco Seamless Rings Gujarat Ltd (505797): Revised to 2%
- Pankaj Polymers Ltd (531280): Revised to 2%
- Silver Pearl Hospitality & Luxury Spaces Ltd (543536): Revised to 5%
- Transworld Shipping Lines Ltd (520151): Revised to 10%
- Vans Electroengineerings Ltd (544971): Revised to 2%
- Vertex Securities Ltd (531950 / 890234): Revised to 5%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems and terminals reflect the updated limits starting from the effective date. For clarifications, members may contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date for Revised Price Bands: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

These price band modifications directly impact intraday trading volatility, order placement limits, and risk parameters for the listed scrips. Market participants trading in these securities must adjust their strategies according to the new percentage thresholds.
