## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills/Government Securities due to maturity, effective November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 805141
- Particulars: 364TB61126
- ISIN: IN002025Z328
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026 (DR- 749/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational suspension upon security maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a routine maturity and trading suspension for a government T-bill.
