## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in four fixed maturity schemes of SBI Mutual Fund due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these schemes effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects four schemes of SBI Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) - Series 54 (1842 Days).
- Scrip codes involved: 543392, 543393, 543394, and 543395 with corresponding ISINs.
- Effective date of suspension: November 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational suspension following scheme maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes with effect from November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine conclusion of fixed maturity plans with no broader market impact.
