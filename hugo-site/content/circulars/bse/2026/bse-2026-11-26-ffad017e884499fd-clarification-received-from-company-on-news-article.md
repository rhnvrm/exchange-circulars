## Summary

The Exchange sought clarification from Spicejet Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to verify accuracy and inform investors. At the time of the notice, the company's reply was awaited.

## Key Points

- Company: Spicejet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285)
- Purpose: Verification of news articles appearing in media sources
- Status: Reply awaited from the company

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news reports when sought by the exchange under surveillance regulations.
