## Summary

BSE has announced that certain mutual fund schemes will observe non-business days on the BSE StAR MF Platform during November 2026 based on communication received from respective Mutual Funds.

## Key Points

- Markets will be closed for specific mutual fund schemes on account of "Non-Business Day" in November 2026.
- Transactions such as Subscription, Redemption, Switch, SIP, Normal STP, SWP, and AMC-STP will not be allowed under the affected schemes.
- Details of non-business day (NBD) dates and affected schemes are provided in the attached annexure (ANNEXURE_NOV_2026).

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the non-business day schedules for the specified mutual fund schemes to avoid failed transaction attempts on the BSE StAR MF platform during November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 October 2026
- Applicable Period: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to investors and distributors transacting in specific mutual fund schemes observing non-business days during November 2026.
