## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. has announced the exclusion of Triveni Power Transmission Limited (Scrip Code: 544824) from several BSE indices effective prior to the open of trading on Friday, October 30, 2026, as the company did not hit the lower circuit on October 27 and October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Triveni Power Transmission Limited (Scrip Code: 544824)
- Action: Exclusion from multiple BSE Indices
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Affected Indices: BSE SMALLCAP, BSE FAST MOVING CONSUMER GOODS, BSE ALLCAP, BSE 1000, BSE NEXT 500, BSE 250 MICROCAP, BSE 1000 MULTICAP EQUAL SIZE WEIGHTED (25%), BSE SMALLCAP 500, BSE TOTAL MARKET, BSE MIDSMALLCAP

## Regulatory Changes

- Follows standard BSE Index Services methodology for the removal of spun-off scrips from indices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index funds and market participants tracking the affected BSE indices must adjust their holdings to reflect the exclusions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Passive funds tracking affected indices will realign portfolios, impacting index constituents and trading weights.
