## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of RenServ Global Private Limited effective from November 20, 2026, due to the payment of interest and early redemption on the exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** RenServ Global Private Limited
- **Security Details:** RSGPL-RESET RATE-5-4-27-PVT (ISIN: INE0AY207046, Scrip Code: 975287, Ref: DR- 761/2026-2027)
- **Record Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Purpose:** Payment of Interest and Early Redemption on Exercise of Call Option
- **No Dealings From:** November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company starting from the specified record date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Record Date / No Dealings From:** November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Secondary market trading in the specified NCDs of RenServ Global Private Limited is restricted starting from the record date to facilitate interest payment and early redemption.
