## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers of multiple companies following the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as of the respective record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Trading in commercial papers for several companies is suspended due to upcoming redemption record dates.
- Record dates and ex-dates are primarily set for December 1, 2026, and December 2, 2026.
- Affected entities include Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Bajaj Housing Finance Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies corresponding to the specified scrip codes and ISINs during the suspension period.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: December 1, 2026, and December 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and prevents trading in commercial papers that have reached or crossed their redemption record dates.
