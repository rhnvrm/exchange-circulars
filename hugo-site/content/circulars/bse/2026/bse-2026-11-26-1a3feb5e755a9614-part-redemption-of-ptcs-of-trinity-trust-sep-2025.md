## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass-Through Certificates (PTCs) issued by Trinity Trust Sep 2025 (Scrip Code: 977166, ISIN: INE2JU715017, Scrip ID: TTS25-11.75%-21-9-27-PTC). Trading members are informed that the PTCs will trade at the reduced face value of Rs. 203.65 per PTC effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company/Trust Name: TRINITY TRUST SEP 2025
- Scrip Code: 977166
- ISIN: INE2JU715017
- Scrip ID: TTS25-11.75%-21-9-27-PTC
- Purpose: Partial Redemption of PTCs
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 203.65 per PTC
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational corporate action notice regarding debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the reduced face value for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value Trading: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact for holders and trading members dealing in the specified Pass-Through Certificates due to the revised face value following partial redemption.
