## Summary

BSE has notified that trading member South Asian Stocks Ltd. (Member No. 6329) has changed its name to Incred Money Broking Ltd. following a SEBI-approved change in control and management.

## Key Points

- Previous Name: South Asian Stocks Ltd.
- New Name: Incred Money Broking Ltd.
- Member Number: 6329
- Effective Date: November 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI-approved change in name pursuant to change in control.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members to update records. Contact membership.ops@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 3, 2026
- Effective Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative update regarding trading member identity with negligible impact on broader market operations.
