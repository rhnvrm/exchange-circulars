## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the trading and settlement programme for the cash segment covering both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles for the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules including trading dates, depository data entry, confirmation by custodians, and pay-in/pay-out dates for the cash segment under T+1 and T+0 cycles.
- Outlines auction schedules and submission timelines for delivery and money statements.
- Applicable for the entire month of December 2026 (Settlement Nos. DR-767/26-27 to DR-788/26-27 for T+1 and corresponding T+0 settlement numbers).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and custodians must adhere to the specified timelines for data entry, confirmation, and fund/security pay-in/pay-out.

## Important Dates

- Period Start Date: December 1, 2026
- Period End Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement execution for trading members and custodians participating in the cash segment during December 2026.
