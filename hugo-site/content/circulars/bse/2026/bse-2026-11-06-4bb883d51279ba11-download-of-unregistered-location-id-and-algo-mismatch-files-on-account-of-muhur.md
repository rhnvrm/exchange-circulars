## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the download schedule of Unregistered Location ID and Algo mismatch files on account of the special Muhurat Trading session scheduled for November 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schedule for Unregistered Location ID and Algo mismatch files announced for the Muhurat Trading session.
- UNLC1 and UNLC2 files download timings: 3:15 pm and 4:10 pm.
- ALGO_MM_1 file download timing: 3:30 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the specified download schedules and reconcile their location IDs and algorithmic trading files accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 6, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Session / File Download Date: November 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; ensures smooth technical operations and compliance during the special trading session.
