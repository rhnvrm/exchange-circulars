## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited (Exchange ticker: 545018) to the BSE IPO Index, effective from the open of trading on Monday, November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited is listed on BSE effective November 5, 2026.
- The stock will be included in the BSE IPO Index starting November 9, 2026.
- This corporate action is reflected in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants other than updating index tracking mechanisms.

## Important Dates

- **November 5, 2026**: Listing date and notice issuance date.
- **November 9, 2026**: Effective date for index addition.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact, relevant primarily to index funds, portfolio managers, and participants tracking the BSE IPO Index.
