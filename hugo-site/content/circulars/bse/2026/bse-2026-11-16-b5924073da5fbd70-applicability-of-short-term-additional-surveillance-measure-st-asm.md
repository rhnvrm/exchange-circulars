## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of 100%.
- Stage II applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of 100%.
- Surveillance measures apply to all open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created from November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework to monitor price volatility and safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for all open and new positions starting November 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 17, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted scrips will experience higher margin requirements, which may affect position sizing and short-term liquidity for those specific securities.
