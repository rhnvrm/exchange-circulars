## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 5,20,000 new equity shares of Caprihans India Ltd. (Scrip Code: 509486) issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants, effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Caprihans India Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 509486
- ISIN: INE479A01018
- Security Details: 5,20,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue Price: Rs. 200/- per share).
- Allottees: Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- Effective Trading Date: November 25, 2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 15913972 to 16433971.
- Allotment Date: May 28, 2026.
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: 5,20,000 shares under lock-in up to May 29, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the issuer company must adhere to standard exchange listing guidelines and lock-in requirements.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: May 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 25, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Reflects capital expansion of Caprihans India Ltd. via preferential warrant conversion for promoters, with standard lock-in compliance.
