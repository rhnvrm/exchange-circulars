## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 5,19,48,051 equity shares of Shalimar Paints Ltd with effect from November 26, 2026. These shares were allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) at an issue price of Rs. 77/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shalimar Paints Ltd
- Scrip Code: 509874
- ISIN: INE849C01026
- Number of Securities: 5,19,48,051 Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 77/- per share
- Allotment Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 26, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 83711179 to 135659229
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the admission of new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 20, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of QIP shares expands the paid-up equity capital and increases institutional participation in Shalimar Paints Ltd.
