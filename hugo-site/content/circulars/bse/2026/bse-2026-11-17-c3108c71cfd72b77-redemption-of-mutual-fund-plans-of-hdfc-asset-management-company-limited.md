## Summary

BSE has announced the maturity record date of November 17, 2026, for the redemption of specific mutual fund plans under HDFC Asset Management Company Limited. Trading members are advised not to deal in these plans w.e.f. November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Maturity Record Date:** November 17, 2026.
- **Trading Cessation Date:** November 18, 2026 (DR-759/2026-2027).
- **Schemes Involved:** HDFC Charity Fund for Cancer Cure (Direct and Regular plans with 50% and 75% IDCW Donation Options).
- **Scrip Codes & ISINs:** 
  - 543964 (INF179KC1HA0)
  - 543966 (INF179KC1HB8)
  - 543967 (INF179KC1HC6)
  - 543968 (INF179KC1HD4)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified mutual fund plans effective November 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Maturity Record Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Effective Date of Non-Dealing:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard closure and redemption procedure for maturing mutual fund plans with negligible impact on overall market operations.
