## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-762/2026-27. The auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and money statements are scheduled for November 25, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-762/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on November 25, 2026.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on November 25, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and Report: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on November 25, 2026.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on November 25, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Thursday, November 26, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is executed through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities has been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care when punching security codes and rates; punching errors are non-correctable.
- Trading members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and check corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 25, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 26, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Operational notice for trading members participating in BSE demat auctions under settlement DR-762/2026-27 with standard procedural compliance.
