## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movement in various scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, clarifications were sought from the concerned companies. Replies have been received from select companies, while others remain pending.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from: Sangam India Ltd (514234) and Subros Ltd (517168).
- Clarifications awaited from 16 companies including Bharat Immunologicals, CMX Holdings, Deep Polymers, Spright Agro, and others.
- Issued by the BSE Surveillance department.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; published under standard surveillance compliance monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing unusual market activity are required to clarify and disclose relevant information to the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and investor awareness regarding scrips exhibiting unusual price and volume volatility.
