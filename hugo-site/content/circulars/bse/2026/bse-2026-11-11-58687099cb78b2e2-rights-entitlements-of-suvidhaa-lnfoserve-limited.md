## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751155
- Scrip ID: SUVID-RE
- ISIN No.: INE018420013
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading period for Renunciation of Rights Entitlements: November 13, 2026 to November 16, 2026
- Rights Issue period: November 12, 2026 to November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action notice for rights entitlements trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced by the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.
- Trades shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Important Dates

- Rights Issue Opening Date: November 12, 2026
- RE Trading Commencement Date: November 12, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 13, 2026 to November 16, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for shareholders of Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited under standard regulatory frameworks.
