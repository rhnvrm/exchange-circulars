## Summary

BSE has announced a communication received from WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund regarding the change in the settlement type for the **WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF** scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF
- **Current Settlement Type:** T+3
- **Revised Settlement Type:** T+2
- **Effective Date:** Immediate effect (28 October 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational update regarding fund settlement cycles.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants on the BSE StAR MF platform must take note of the revised settlement type for transactions in the specified scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 28 October 2026
- **Effective Date:** Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Operational efficiency improvement as the settlement cycle is expedited from T+3 to T+2 for the scheme.
