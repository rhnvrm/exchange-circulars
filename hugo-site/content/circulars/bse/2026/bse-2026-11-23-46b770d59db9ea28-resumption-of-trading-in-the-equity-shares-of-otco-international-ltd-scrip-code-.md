## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the consolidated equity shares of OTCO International Ltd (Scrip Code: 523151) with a new face value of Rs. 10/- each (previously Rs. 2/-), effective from November 30, 2026, under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Key Points

- Trading in consolidated equity shares of OTCO International Ltd (Scrip Code: 523151) resumes w.e.f. November 30, 2026.
- New ISIN: INE910B01036.
- Market Lot: 1 (One).
- Share Consolidation: From Rs. 2/- fully paid up to Rs. 10/- fully paid up.
- Trading is restricted on account of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework and will take place on permitted days accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of ISIN and share face value following corporate action (share consolidation).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take abundant precautions while mentioning the rates for the equity shares of the company and note the GSM restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date (Resumption of Trading / New ISIN validity): November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members must update their systems with the new ISIN (INE910B01036) and note that trading is subject to GSM framework restrictions.
