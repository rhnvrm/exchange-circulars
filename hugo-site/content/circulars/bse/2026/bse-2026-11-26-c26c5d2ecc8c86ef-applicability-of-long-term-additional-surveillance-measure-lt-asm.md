## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to the revised framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, movement to higher surveillance stages, and transfers to T, XT, MT, or TS groups effective from late November and early December 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable w.e.f. December 01, 2026, on all open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands applied w.e.f. November 27, 2026.
- Specific securities continued in the LT-ASM framework are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. December 01, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated, subject to other active surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and ongoing market surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance controls under the revised LT-ASM framework, enforcing 100% margin obligations and price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements for affected securities and monitor position limits and group classifications.

## Important Dates

- **November 27, 2026:** Movement to higher stages / lower price band applicability / moving out of framework.
- **November 30, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **December 01, 2026:** 100% margins effective; transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and capital requirements for impacted securities will be constrained due to 100% margin enforcement and price band restrictions.
