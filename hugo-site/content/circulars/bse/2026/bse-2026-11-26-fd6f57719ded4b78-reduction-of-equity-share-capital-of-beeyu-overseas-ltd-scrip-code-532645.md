## Summary

BEEYU OVERSEAS LTD. (Scrip Code: 532645) has announced the reduction of its equity share capital as approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench. Trading members are informed about the record date and suspension of dealings in the equity shares of the company effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** Beeyu Overseas Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532645).
- **Record Date:** November 30, 2026.
- **Capital Reduction Details:** Accumulated losses are set off against the Capital Reserve and Securities Premium Reserve. The existing equity share capital comprising 1,40,58,633 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each is cancelled, extinguished, and rendered invalid.
- **Trading Suspension:** Suspension of dealings/no dealings w.e.f. November 30, 2026 (Notice Ref: DR-766/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

Capital reduction executed pursuant to the order passed by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested not to deal in the equity shares of Beeyu Overseas Ltd. w.e.f. November 30, 2026, and take note of the capital reduction and share cancellation.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on shareholders of Beeyu Overseas Ltd. as existing equity shares are cancelled and extinguished pursuant to the capital reduction scheme approved by NCLT.
