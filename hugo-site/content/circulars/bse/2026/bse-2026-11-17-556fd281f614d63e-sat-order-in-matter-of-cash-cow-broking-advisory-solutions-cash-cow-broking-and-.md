## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions & Ors. (Appeal No. 145/2026). In pursuance of SEBI's email dated November 17, 2026, trading members are instructed to instantly defreeze the PAN status of the appellants if they are still frozen or debarred.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20220118-57 dated January 18, 2022.
- SEBI directed the immediate defreezing of PANs for specific entities/individuals following SAT orders.
- Affected PANs include Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions (AAIFC2855R) and associated individuals: Shailendra Sen (BUXPS9559Q), Amit Jain (AGSPJ3021G), and Chirag Sharma (ASWPS7685C).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SAT order instructions regarding the removal of freezing/debarment status on specified PANs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must verify and instantly defreeze the PANs of the listed appellants if their status remains frozen or debarred.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational compliance update for trading members holding accounts or records of the specified entities.
