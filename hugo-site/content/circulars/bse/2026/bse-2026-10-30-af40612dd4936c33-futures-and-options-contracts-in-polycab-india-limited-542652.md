## Summary

NCLAT vide its order dated October 30, 2026, allowed the appeal filed by Polycab India Limited and set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026, concerning IBC proceedings. Consequently, the company moves out of the IBC framework, and corresponding surveillance actions are reverted.

## Key Points

- NCLAT set aside NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026 regarding IBC proceedings against Polycab India Limited (542652).
- Polycab India Limited moves out of the IBC framework and related surveillance actions are reverted.
- Futures and options contracts with January 2027 expiry will be available for trading w.e.f. November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reversion of surveillance actions initiated under the IBC framework.
- Resumption of trading in futures and options contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants to note the removal from IBC framework and availability of F&O contracts.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026**: NCLAT order allowing appeal and setting aside NCLT order / Notice date.
- **November 02, 2026**: F&O contracts with January 2027 expiry made available for trading.

## Impact Assessment

- Positive impact on market operations and investor sentiment for Polycab India Limited as trading restrictions and surveillance measures under IBC are lifted and F&O trading resumes.
