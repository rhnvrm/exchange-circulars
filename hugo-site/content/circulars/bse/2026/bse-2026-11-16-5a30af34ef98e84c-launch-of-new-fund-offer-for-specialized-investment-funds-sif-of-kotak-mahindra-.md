## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) of Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund (Kotak Infinity Equity Long-Short Fund) on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from November 17, 2026, to December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Offering INFINITY EQUITY LONG-SHORT FUND across regular and direct plans (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout).
- NFO window runs from November 17, 2026, to December 01, 2026 (extended till midnight on December 01, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative allotment date is December 07, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 07, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on December 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client fund initiation by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 07, 2026 for valid allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: December 01, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables members and investors to participate in the Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational guidelines.
