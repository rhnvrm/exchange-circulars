## Summary

BSE has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) relating to Kronox Lab Sciences Limited. The bidding window was previously conducted from November 6, 2026, to November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Kronox Lab Sciences Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover (Settlement No. 2026135)
- **Bidding Period:** 06/11/2026 to 20/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 26/11/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 27/11/2026
- **Share Delivery:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026135.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision and update of settlement timelines for the takeover acquisition window of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must utilize the specified Market Type and Settlement Number when processing share deliveries in the depository system.
- Members should consult the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file to verify client-wise Stamp Duty charges.
- Adherence to guidelines specified in historical Notice No. 20200528-32 regarding stock exchange share acquisition mechanisms is required.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 20/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 26/11/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 27/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth and compliant finalization, obligation downloading, and fund/share settlement for market participants involved in the Kronox Lab Sciences Limited takeover offer.
