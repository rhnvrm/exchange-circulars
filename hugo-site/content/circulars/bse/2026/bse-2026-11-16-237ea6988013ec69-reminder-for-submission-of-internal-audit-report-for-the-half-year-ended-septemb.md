## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026. Members must submit the report on or before November 30, 2026, through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).

## Key Points

- Reference to previous Exchange Notice Nos. 20261001-37, 20261016-18, and 20260417-45.
- Mandatory for all trading members to carry out and submit a complete internal audit for the half year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submission deadline is November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an operational reminder enforcing existing compliance requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit the Internal Audit Report in the prescribed format via BEFS.
- Non-submission or delayed submission will attract penal charges and/or disciplinary action in accordance with Exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Deadline for Submission:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely completion and filing of internal audit reports to prevent regulatory penalties or disciplinary actions.
