## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills)—91 Day, 182 Day, and 364 Day—scheduled for November 25, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered:** 
  - 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T240227, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Min Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amt: ₹400 Cr)
  - 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T260527, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Min Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amt: ₹400 Cr)
  - 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T251127, Issue Size: ₹7,000 Cr, Min Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amt: ₹350 Cr)
- **Bidding Platform:** NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- **Bid Collection Timelines:** 
  - Direct Investors: November 23, 2026, 10:00 AM onwards till November 24, 2026, 5:00 PM.
  - Members: November 23, 2026, 10:00 AM onwards till November 25, 2026, 8:00 AM.
- **Auction Date:** November 25, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine issuance notice pursuant to the RBI press release and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 26, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with bidding schedules, margin requirements, and fund pay-in obligations through ICCL Operations.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** November 23, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** November 24, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** November 25, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates retail and non-competitive participation in Government of India Treasury Bills auction with minimal market disruption.
