## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 28,12,120 new equity shares of Regency Fincorp Limited (Scrip Code: 540175) with effect from November 20, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Regency Fincorp Limited
- Scrip Code: 540175
- ISIN: INE964R01013
- Security Details: 28,12,120 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 12/- (Issue Price: Rs. 22/- per share).
- Allotment Date: June 26, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 86033951 to 88846070
- Lock-in Details: As per Annexure I

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and trading permission of the new securities ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 26, 2026
- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Regency Fincorp Limited following preferential warrant conversion.
