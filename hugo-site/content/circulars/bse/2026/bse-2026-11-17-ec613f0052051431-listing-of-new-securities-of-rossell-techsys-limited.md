## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Rossell Techsys Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rossell Techsys Limited
- Scrip Code: 544294
- ISIN: INE0OJW01016
- Number of Shares: 25,72,898 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 1166/- (at a premium of Rs. 1164/-)
- Date of Allotment: November 4, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 37746476 to 40319373
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 19, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the lock-in period applicable to the newly issued preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 4, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 19, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base through preferential allotment to promoters with standard lock-in compliance.
