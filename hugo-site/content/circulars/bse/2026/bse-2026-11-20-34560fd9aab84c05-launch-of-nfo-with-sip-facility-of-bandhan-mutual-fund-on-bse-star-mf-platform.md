## Summary

Bandhan Mutual Fund is offering the Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund NFO with SIP facility from November 23, 2026, to December 03, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details:** Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF194KB1LE6) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF194KB1LF3).
- **NFO Period:** November 23, 2026, to December 03, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 08, 2026.
- **Extended Period:** NFO remains extended till midnight on December 03, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 03, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** November 23, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** December 03, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 08, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** December 08, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors with operational timelines and instructions for participating in the Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund NFO on BSE StAR MF.
