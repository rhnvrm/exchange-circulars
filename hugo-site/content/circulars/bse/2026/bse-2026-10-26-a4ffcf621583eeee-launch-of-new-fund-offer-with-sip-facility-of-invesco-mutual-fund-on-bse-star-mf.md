## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund (FOF) along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 27, 2026, to November 9, 2026, with tentative allotment scheduled for November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund - Regular and Direct Plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO Period: October 27, 2026, to November 09, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 09, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date: November 16, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 16, 2026.

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be maintained in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 16, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 27, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: November 09, 2026 (midnight for lumpsum; SIP/XSIP auth by 3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 16, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 16, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund NFO with SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF platform.
