## Summary

SEBI, vide its Final Order No. QJA/BS/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-5/32816/2026-27 dated October 30, 2026, has passed a final order against Ms. Veena Sharma, proprietor of Research Guru Investment Adviser (SEBI Registration No. INA000005507), following an inspection.

## Key Points

- SEBI Final Order issued against Ms. Veena Sharma, proprietor of Research Guru Investment Adviser.
- SEBI Registration Number: INA000005507.
- Order Reference: QJA/BS/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-5/32816/2026-27 dated October 30, 2026.
- All Investment Advisers are advised to take note of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement action against a specific regulated entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Investment advisers must take note of regulatory orders and ensure strict compliance with SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 30, 2026
- Notice Date: November 12, 2026

Individual regulatory enforcement action with negligible direct impact on broader market operations.
