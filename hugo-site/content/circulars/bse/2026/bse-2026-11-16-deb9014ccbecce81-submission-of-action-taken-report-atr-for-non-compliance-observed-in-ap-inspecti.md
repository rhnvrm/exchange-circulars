## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding trading members to submit the Action Taken Report (ATR) in respect of non-compliances observed during Authorised Person (AP) inspections for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 (Quarter-2). The submission must be completed on or before December 31, 2026, via the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).

## Key Points

- ATR submission applies to non-compliances observed during AP inspections for Quarter-2 (ended September 30, 2026).
- Due date for submitting the ATR is December 31, 2026.
- Submissions must be made electronically through the BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System) platform.
- User manual explaining the reporting process is enclosed as Annexure A.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; reinforces existing compliance timelines for submitting AP inspection ATRs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review AP inspection observations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, take necessary remedial action, and file the ATR via BEFS on or before December 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Quarter Ended:** September 30, 2026 (Quarter-2)
- **ATR Due Date:** December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and monitoring of Authorised Persons by enforcing timely rectification and reporting of audit observations.
