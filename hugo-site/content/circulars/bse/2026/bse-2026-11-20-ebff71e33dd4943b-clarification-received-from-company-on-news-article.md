## Summary

BSE has sought clarification from SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) regarding news articles appearing across various media platforms to verify their accuracy and inform investors. As of the notice date, the reply from the company is awaited.

## Key Points

- BSE issued notice no. 20261120-25 on November 20, 2026.
- Clarification sought from SpiceJet Ltd concerning recent media reports.
- Reply is currently awaited from the company.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance query conducted by the exchange to verify news reports.

## Compliance Requirements

SpiceJet Ltd is required to submit its official clarification or response to the exchange regarding the cited news articles.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact, serving primarily to ensure market transparency and investor awareness pending the company's response.
