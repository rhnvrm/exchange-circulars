## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies that have provided clarifications regarding significant price and volume movements observed in their scrips in the recent past, as well as companies whose replies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 6 companies including Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd, Bharat Coking Coal Ltd, Fischer Medical Ventures Ltd, Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Ltd, Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd, and Triveni Turbine Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 18 other listed companies.
- The exercise is part of the Exchange's surveillance measures to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine surveillance disclosure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies flagged for significant price/volume movements must submit timely clarifications to the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market transparency regarding unusual price and volume movements in specific scrips, helping investors make informed decisions.
