## Summary

Zadley India Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document for an SME Initial Public Offering (IPO) with the BSE on November 16, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME exchange website to invite public comments for at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Zadley India Limited
- Filing Date: November 16, 2026
- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO (DCS-Listing)
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Public Comments Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- Disclosures made in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and public may submit comments on the draft offer document within 21 days of the notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from the notice date (approx. December 9, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public transparency and regulatory scrutiny prior to the finalization of the SME IPO process for Zadley India Limited.
