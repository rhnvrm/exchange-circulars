## Summary

Anand Kamalnayan Pandit (Promoter) has exercised the oversubscription option for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited, bringing the total offer size up to 2,65,00,000 equity shares (representing 5.42% of the total paid-up equity share capital).

## Key Points

- Base Offer Size: 2,37,95,818 Equity Shares (4.87% of paid-up equity share capital).
- Oversubscription Option Exercised: Additional 27,04,182 Equity Shares (0.55%).
- Total Offer Size: 2,65,00,000 Equity Shares (5.42%).
- Retail Allocation: 10% of the offer (26,50,000 Equity Shares) available for the Retail Category on T+1 day.
- Stock Exchanges: BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited.
