## Summary

Effective Thursday, October 29, 2026, the equity shares of Bondada Engineering Limited, previously listed on the BSE SME Platform, are migrated and admitted to dealings on the BSE Mainboard Platform under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bondada Engineering Limited
- Scrip Code: 543971
- Scrip ID / BOLT Symbol: BONDADA
- ISIN: INE0Q8P01029
- Securities: 111,671,485 Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each (Distinctive numbers: 1 to 111671485)
- Market Lot: 1 (unit market lot)
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; the migration is executed in accordance with established exchange and SEBI guidelines for SME-to-mainboard transitions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will continue to be traded exclusively in dematerialized form.
- Trades will be executed in unit market lots (one share).
- Audited financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, and the shareholding pattern as on September 30, 2026, have been provided as annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date of Migration: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transitioning to the mainboard platform increases market visibility, broadens the shareholder base, and enhances liquidity for retail and institutional investors trading in Bondada Engineering Limited.
