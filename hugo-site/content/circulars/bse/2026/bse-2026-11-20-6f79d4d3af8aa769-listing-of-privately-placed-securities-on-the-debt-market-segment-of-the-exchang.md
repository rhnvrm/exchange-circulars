## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis on the debt market segment effective November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Muthoot Fincorp Limited**: 4,400 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732986, ISIN: INE549K14DC0) maturing on 16/02/2027 at 8.94% interest rate; and 4,500 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732990, ISIN: INE549K14DB2) maturing on 18/02/2027.
- **Fedbank Financial Services Limited**: 5,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732987, ISIN: INE007N14FW6) maturing on 17/02/2027 at 8.45% interest rate.
- **Bharat Petroleum Corpn. Ltd.**: 84,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732988, ISIN: INE029A14CB8) maturing on 24/12/2026 at 6.68% interest rate.
- **Godrej Housing Finance Limited**: 4,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732989, ISIN: INE02JD14948) maturing on 21/12/2026 at 6.90% interest rate.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine debt listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with standard dematerialised settlement and denomination guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Redemptions: Dec 24, 2026 through Feb 18, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with negligible direct market-wide impact.
