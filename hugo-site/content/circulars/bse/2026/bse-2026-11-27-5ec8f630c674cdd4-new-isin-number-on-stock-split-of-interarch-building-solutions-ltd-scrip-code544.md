## Summary

BSE has announced the new ISIN number for Interarch Building Solutions Ltd following the stock split (sub-division) of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Interarch Building Solutions Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544232
- New ISIN: INE00M901026
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocation of new ISIN number for sub-divided equity shares in continuation of previous exchange notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to note the new ISIN and ensure systems are updated accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 30, 2026 (DR-759/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates seamless clearing, settlement, and trading of equity shares post stock split under the updated ISIN.
