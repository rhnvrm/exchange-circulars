## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) of multiple companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies from their respective ex-dates / record dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects Commercial Papers of various entities including Dolat Algotech, SBICAP Securities, Grasim Industries, Aarti Industries, Jindal Saw, Bajaj Finance, Nexus Select Trust, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates for redemption fall between November 13, 2026, and November 20, 2026.
- Trading members are cautioned against dealing in these specific CPs as of the specified dates.
- Redemption facilitates the closure and payout of maturing debt instruments.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a standard operational notice regarding corporate debt redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure zero trading activity in the listed Commercial Papers of the specified companies on and from their respective ex-dates / record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 6, 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: November 13, 2026 to November 20, 2026 (varying by company)

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to debt trading members and investors holding the specific maturing Commercial Papers, ensuring orderly market settlement upon redemption.
