## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Action Taken Report (ATR) for Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) for the financial year 2025-26. Members with open vulnerabilities identified by CERT-In empanelled auditors must submit the ATR through the BEFS Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to trading members with open vulnerabilities identified in their VAPT Report for FY 2025-26.
- Trading members registered with both BSE and NSE are required to submit their VAPT Action Taken Report to NSE only.
- Members not registered with NSE shall continue making submissions to BSE.
- Submission is valid only upon uploading the digitally signed report to the BEFS Portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforcement of cybersecurity compliance timelines and cross-exchange reporting harmonization.

## Compliance Requirements

- Electronic submission of the VAPT Action Taken Report via the BEFS Portal by November 30, 2026.
- Avoidance of penalties and disciplinary actions prescribed in Exchange circular no. 20260417-45.

## Important Dates

- **Submission Deadline:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures robust cybersecurity hygiene across trading members and prevents regulatory penalties or disciplinary measures.
