## Summary

BSE has announced additional surveillance measures and periodic reviews for securities relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective November 06, 2026. This framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under Additional Surveillance Measure for IRP as per IBC are subject to surveillance measures effective November 06, 2026 (Annexure I).
- List of securities moving out from the IBC framework effective November 06, 2026 is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post-recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are provided in Annexure IV.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation and periodic review of additional surveillance measures for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process under IBC.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and member firms must take note of the shortlisted securities under the IBC framework and ensure compliance with applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Notice Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading and price bands of specified securities undergoing insolvency proceedings or meeting specific non-promoter holding criteria.
