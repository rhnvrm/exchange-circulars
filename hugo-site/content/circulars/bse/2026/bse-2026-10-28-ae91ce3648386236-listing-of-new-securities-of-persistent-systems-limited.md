## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading admission of 76,83,981 new equity shares of Persistent Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 533179, ISIN: INE262H01021) issued through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) with effect from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- 76,83,981 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs).
- Issue Price: Rs. 5550/- per share.
- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 158150001 to 165833981.
- Effective Date of Trading: October 29, 2026.
- The newly allotted shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate listing procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to exchange listing and operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 26, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Persistent Systems Limited and increases institutional holding through the QIP route.
