## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the consolidated equity shares of Noble Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 539200) effective November 13, 2026, following a share consolidation from a face value of Rs. 5/- to Rs. 10/- per share.

## Key Points

- Trading in consolidated equity shares of Noble Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 539200) resumes w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- New ISIN: INE203Q01034.
- Market lot: 1 (One) fully paid-up share.
- Share consolidation involves changing face value from Rs. 5/- to Rs. 10/-.

## Regulatory Changes

- Consolidation of equity shares resulting in a new ISIN allocation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take abundant precautions while mentioning the rates for the equity shares of the company on the consolidated face value of Rs. 10/- each.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 6, 2026.
- Effective Date of Trading Resumption / New ISIN Validity: November 13, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders and trading members must note the new ISIN and consolidated face value to prevent execution errors in trading operations.
