## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) period for NIPPON INDIA CRISIL - IBX SDL - JUNE 2037 INDEX FUND on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- The NFO period has been extended up to midnight on November 30, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases.
- Revised tentative allotment date is December 03, 2026.
- Reopening date is revised to December 04, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Extended Closure Date: November 30, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 01, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 03, 2026
- Reopening Date: December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors additional time to participate in the NFO of Nippon India Mutual Fund's index fund scheme.
