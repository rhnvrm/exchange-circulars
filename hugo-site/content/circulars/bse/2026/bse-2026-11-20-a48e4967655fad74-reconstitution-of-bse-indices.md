## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the reconstitution results for key BSE indices, including BSE SENSEX, BSE 100, and BSE SENSEX NEXT 50. These changes will take effect at the open of Monday, December 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261120-18 dated November 20, 2026.
- Department: Index Cell.
- Covers additions and deletions across BSE SENSEX, BSE 100, and BSE SENSEX NEXT 50 indices.
- Effective Date: Open of Monday, December 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic index maintenance and reconstitution reflecting changes in market capitalization, liquidity, and trading activity of constituent companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Index funds, ETFs, portfolio managers, and institutional investors tracking BSE indices must align their portfolios with the reconstituted index composition prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Announcement Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: December 21, 2026 (Market Open)

## Impact Assessment

Significant rebalancing activity is expected in constituent stocks (additions and drops) as institutional funds and index trackers adjust their holdings to match the new index weights and constituents.
