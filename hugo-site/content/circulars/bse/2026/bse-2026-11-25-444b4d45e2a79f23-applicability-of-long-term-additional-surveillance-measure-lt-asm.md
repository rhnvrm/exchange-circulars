## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 30, 2026 on open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions onwards (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands applicable w.e.f. November 26, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM framework but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 30, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 26, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of LT-ASM framework measures implemented under extant exchange guidelines based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date for higher stages / price bands / moving out: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margins / group transfers: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities will experience enhanced margin requirements and price band constraints, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
