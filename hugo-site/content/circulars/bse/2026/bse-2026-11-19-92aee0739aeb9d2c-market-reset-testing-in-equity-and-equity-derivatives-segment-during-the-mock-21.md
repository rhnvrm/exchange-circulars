## Summary

BSE has scheduled mock trading sessions on 21 November 2026 for testing market reset scenarios in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments. ETI Manual Version 1.6.15 updates the scope of order cancellation during a market reset, requiring no structural or application changes from members or vendors.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for 21 November 2026 to test market reset behavior.
- ETI Manual Version 1.6.15 updates order cancellation scope; existing mechanisms support this without code changes.
- Market reset messages will contain Template ID 10307 and Enum value 102.
- Specific testing schedules provided for Equity and Equity Derivatives across multiple partitions.
- Members requested to verify all market data (EMDI, EOBI, and NFCAST) during and after each market reset event.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory rule changes; updates to ETI Manual Version 1.6.15 clarify order cancellation scope during market reset events.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and vendors may participate in the mock session to validate and fine-tune their applications for the Market Reset scenario.
- Members must verify market data feeds (EMDI, EOBI, NFCAST) and share observations or feedback with BSE Tech Support.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: 21 November 2026
- Equity Derivatives Partitions: 12:30 PM, 1:00 PM, 1:30 PM, and 2:00 PM
- Equity Partitions: 12:45 PM, 1:15 PM, 1:45 PM, and 2:15 PM

## Impact Assessment

- Operational testing opportunity for trading members and vendors to ensure proper handling of market reset scenarios and message verification.
