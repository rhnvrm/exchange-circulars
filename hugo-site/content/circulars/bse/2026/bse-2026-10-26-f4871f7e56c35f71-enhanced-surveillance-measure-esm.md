## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the securities entering, shifting stages within, or moving out of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, effective late October 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on open positions as of October 27, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 28, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework are effective from October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance measures including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must maintain 100% margins on open and new positions for affected scrips and manage orders in accordance with Trade-for-Trade segment rules.

## Important Dates

- October 26, 2026: Removal of securities from ESM framework (Annexure II).
- October 27, 2026: Shifting to lower Stage I / Stage II price band and Periodic Call Auction.
- October 28, 2026: 100% margin applicability and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity for securities under the ESM framework are restricted due to strict margin norms and trade-for-trade settlement.
