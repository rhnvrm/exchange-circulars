## Summary

BSE has announced the revised schedule for reporting holding statements on account of the Diwali Muhurat Trading Session held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Adjusted reporting timelines for holding statements covering dates from November 6 to November 10, 2026.
- Deadlines set for November 9 and November 11, 2026.
- Applicable to all trading members.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit holding statements as per the revised schedule:
- Reporting Date 06-Nov-2026: Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- Reporting Date 07-Nov-2026: Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- Reporting Date 08-Nov-2026: Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- Reporting Date 09-Nov-2026: Due Date 11-Nov-2026
- Reporting Date 10-Nov-2026: Due Date 11-Nov-2026

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05-Nov-2026
- Muhurat Trading: 08-Nov-2026
- Submission Deadlines: 09-Nov-2026 and 11-Nov-2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Members must align their compliance reporting processes with the revised submission timelines.
