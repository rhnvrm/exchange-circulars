## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of multiple companies following fixed record dates for interest payment or redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds as per the schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261026-40 dated October 26, 2026.
- Segment: Debt (Listing Operations).
- Affected entities include Power Finance Corporation Ltd, Mahindra Rural Housing Finance Limited, Indian Railway Finance Corporation, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd, IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Shriram Finance Limited, and others.
- Trading members advised not to deal in the debentures/bonds from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard debt market operational procedures for temporary suspension of trading in debentures/bonds around record dates for interest/redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no trading activity takes place in the listed non-convertible debentures and bonds during the suspension period corresponding to the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Record Dates and Ex-Dates: Early to mid-November 2026 (ranging from November 9, 2026 to November 16, 2026 for various scrips).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact limited to temporary trading suspension of specific debt instruments around interest payment and redemption record dates.
