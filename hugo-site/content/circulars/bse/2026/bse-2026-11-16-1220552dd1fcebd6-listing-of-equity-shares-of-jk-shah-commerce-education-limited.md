## Summary

Effective from November 18, 2026, the equity shares of J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the 'T' Group of Securities pursuant to a Composite Scheme of Arrangement.

## Key Points

- Company Name: J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited
- Scrip Code: 544956
- ISIN: INE2OD001016
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: JKSHAH
- Total Securities: 9,65,06,610 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days
- Group: T

## Regulatory Changes

- The listing is approved pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement sanctioned by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Chennai Bench, dated August 20, 2026.
- Complies with SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.

## Compliance Requirements

- Security will be part of the special pre-open session (SPOS) for IPOs and other category of scrips.
- Trading will be restricted to the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing: November 18, 2026
- First Appointed Date: August 31, 2026
- Second Appointed Date (Demerger): September 01, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited receiving shares pursuant to the demerger can now trade their shares on BSE under scrip code 544956.
- Enhanced liquidity and formal market presence for the newly carved-out commerce education business.
