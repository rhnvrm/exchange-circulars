## Summary

Catalyst Trusteeship Limited has informed the Exchange regarding the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued under India Universal Trust AL1 & AL2 – Series A2 & A3. Trading members are advised that the affected PTCs will trade at the reduced face value starting November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme/Trust: India Universal Trust AL2 – Series A2 & A3 (Catalyst Trusteeship Limited)
- Scrip Code 976207 (ISIN: INE1CBK15029): Reduced Face Value of Rs. 3,868,313.48 per PTC for `IUTAL2-8.09%-21-11-27-PTC`
- Scrip Code 976208 (ISIN: INE1CBK15037): Reduced Face Value of Rs. 8,940,399.10 per PTC for `IUTAL2-8.17%-21-11-30-PTC`
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory policy changes; standard corporate action processing for debt securities and PTC redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the reduced face value and ensure client transactions reflect the updated face value from November 27, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational adjustment for trading and settlement members dealing with debt segment PTCs of India Universal Trust AL2. Market impact is limited to holders of the specific debt securities undergoing partial redemption.
