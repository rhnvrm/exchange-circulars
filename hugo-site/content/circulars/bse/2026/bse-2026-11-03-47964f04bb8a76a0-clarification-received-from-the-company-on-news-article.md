## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in media, websites, or television channels. The company has provided its official reply, ensuring transparency and accuracy of information for investors.

## Key Points

- Clarification sought from Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 524715).
- Reply received and published by BSE Surveillance.
- Reference news source: The Hindu.

None.
