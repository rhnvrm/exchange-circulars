## Summary

BSE has approved the surrender of trading membership in the currency derivatives segment for Manu Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3294) with effect from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: MANU STOCK BROKING PVT. LTD.
- Member No.: 3294
- Segment: Currency Derivatives Segment
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarifications can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026
- Notice Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact as this is a routine surrender of membership by an individual broker.
