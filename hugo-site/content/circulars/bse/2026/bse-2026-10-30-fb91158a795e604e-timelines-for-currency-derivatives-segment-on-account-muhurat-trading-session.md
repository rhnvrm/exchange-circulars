## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has announced revised timelines and schedules for the currency derivatives segment in view of carnivorous trading activities during the Muhurat Trading session for Diwali Laxmi Pujan, pursuant to BSE Circular No. 20261019-11.

## Key Points

- Pay-in/pay-out obligations for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled together on November 09, 2026, at 08:30 hrs.
- Client Margin Reporting schedule is governed by BSECL Circular No. 20261030-15.
- Custodian Participant (CP) code modification and Give up confirmation for trades done on November 08, 2026, is permitted between 14:20 hrs. and 15:40 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; operational schedule adjustments for the festive trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must comply with the adjusted pay-in/pay-out schedules, margin reporting requirements, and specified CP code modification windows.

## Important Dates

- November 06, 2026: Trade Date
- November 08, 2026: Muhurat Trading Session & Trade Date
- November 09, 2026 (08:30 hrs.): Pay-in/Pay-out Settlement
- November 08, 2026 (14:20 hrs. – 15:40 hrs.): CP Code Modification / Give Up Confirmation Window

## Impact Assessment

Low impact; routine operational adjustments ensuring smooth settlement and margin handling over the Diwali festive period.
