## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that no dealings shall take place in the partly-paid equity shares of Ace Software Exports Ltd. (Scrip Code: 890230) on the record date (20/11/2026) fixed for the second and final call.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ace Software Exports Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 890230
- Record Date / No Dealings Date: 20/11/2026
- Purpose: Ascertaining shareholders of partly-paid equity shares eligible to pay the Second and Final Call of Rs. 30.25 per share (comprising Rs. 2.75 towards Face Value and Rs. 27.50 towards Premium).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the restriction and ensure no dealings in the partly-paid equity shares of the company on the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 November 2026
- Record Date / No Dealings From: 20 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on trading of partly-paid equity shares to facilitate the corporate action process for the second and final call.
