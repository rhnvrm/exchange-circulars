## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and part redemption on Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in these debentures/PTCs will take place with reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261111-8
- Notice Date: 11 Nov 2026
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Affected issuers include Indigo, KiPlatform Divine, Ironwell, IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited, Ascend Telecom Infrastructure Private Limited, Aadharshila Infratech Private Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Kanakadurga Finance Limited, Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited, and Aptus Finance India Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine corporate action notification regarding debt redemption and face value reduction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the reduced face values and respective record/ex-dates for trading in the listed NCDs and PTCs.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 12/11/2026, 13/11/2026, and 15/11/2026 depending on the specific scrip.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational update ensuring market transparency for debt securities undergoing part redemption and face value adjustments.
