## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The notice outlines the list of securities meeting inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or exiting the framework, along with applicable margin requirements and settlement modifications.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 26, 2026, on open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created from November 26, 2026.
- Specified securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. November 25, 2026 (Annexure I).
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 26, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 25, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM Framework w.e.f. November 25, 2026, are listed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework, affecting security settlement types, margin obligations, and price bands.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement rules, and applicable price band restrictions for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes and Exits: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Settlement: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips experience tighter trading restrictions, including 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement, which may impact liquidity and speculative activity.
