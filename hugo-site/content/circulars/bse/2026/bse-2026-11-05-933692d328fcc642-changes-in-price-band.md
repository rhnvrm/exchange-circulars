## Summary

BSE Ltd has announced the revision of existing price bands for 10 securities under its surveillance department. The revised price bands shall become effective starting November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total securities impacted: 10
- Effective date: November 06, 2026
- Department: Surveillance (DOSS)
- Segment: Equity
- Includes companies such as Affordable Robotic & Automation Ltd, AI Champdany Industries Ltd, Avance Technologies Ltd, Creative Newtech Ltd, Keltech Energies Ltd, Paragon Finance Ltd, Priority Jewels Ltd, Shellz India Ltd, Tijaria Polypipes Ltd, and Ucal Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for the listed securities have been revised to specific percentages (2%, 5%, 10%, and 20%) to manage volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and configure their trading systems accordingly effective from November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will operate under the newly assigned price limits, restricting extreme price movements and ensuring orderly market operations.
