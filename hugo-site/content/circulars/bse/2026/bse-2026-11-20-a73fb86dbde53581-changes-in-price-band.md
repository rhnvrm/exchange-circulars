## Summary

BSE has notified the revision of price bands for multiple listed securities in the equity segment, effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands for 37 securities have been revised to 5%, 10%, or 20%.
- Applies to trading members and market participants in the equity segment.
- Effective date of revised price bands is November 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of operating price ranges for specific listed scrips to manage market volatility and ensure orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their systems and take note of the revised price bands for the listed securities before market hours on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Permissible price movement for the affected scrips will be adjusted according to the newly assigned price band limits.
