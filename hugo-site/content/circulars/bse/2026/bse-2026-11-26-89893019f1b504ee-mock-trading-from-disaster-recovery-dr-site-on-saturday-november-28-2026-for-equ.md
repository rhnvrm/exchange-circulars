## Summary

The BSE has scheduled a mock trading session from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site for the Equity segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test trading applications, third-party platforms, and ETI APIs under exceptional market conditions and verify compliance with SEBI requirements.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity.
- **Location:** Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- **Participation:** Members can participate via mock trading or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory requirements under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- **Testing Scope:** Includes testing for closing auction session changes, market data API, block deal window, pre-open, continuous trading, special pre-open for IPO/re-listed scrips, and periodic call auction.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020, regarding testing of trading and risk management software.
- Incorporation of recent operational guidelines related to the Closing Auction Session and new Market data API versions in the Equity Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using third-party trading platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house developed systems through ETI APIs are requested to test their trading applications during the mock session.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Mock Trading Session Date:** November 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members from the Disaster Recovery site with no direct financial impact.
