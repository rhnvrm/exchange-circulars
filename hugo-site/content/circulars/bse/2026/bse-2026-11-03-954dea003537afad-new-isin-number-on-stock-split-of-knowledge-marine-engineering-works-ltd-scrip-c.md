## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number assigned to Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd consequent to the sub-division of its equity shares.

- **Scrip Code:** 543273
- **New ISIN:** INE0CJD01037
- **Corporate Action:** Sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 5/- to Re. 1/-
- **Ex-Date:** November 6, 2026

Operational transition for trading and clearing members to update security masters with the new ISIN ahead of the ex-date.
