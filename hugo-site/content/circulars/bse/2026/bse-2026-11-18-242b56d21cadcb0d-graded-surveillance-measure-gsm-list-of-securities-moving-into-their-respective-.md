## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure shall move to higher stages of GSM.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages, including 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-to-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and restricted trading frequency.
- Settlement for the affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the BSE GSM framework as per guidelines dated February 27, 2017, and subsequent notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Stage I:** 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade settlement with 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- **Stage III & IV:** Trade-for-trade settlement with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers (with no upward movement permitted in Stage IV).
- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 November 2026
- Effective Date: 19 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity in the affected securities will be constrained due to stringent margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement, and restricted trading frequencies.
