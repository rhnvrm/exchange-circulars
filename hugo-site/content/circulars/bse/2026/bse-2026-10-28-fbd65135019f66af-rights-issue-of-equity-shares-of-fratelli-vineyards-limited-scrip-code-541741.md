## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Fratelli Vineyards Limited has fixed October 30, 2026, as the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Eligible shareholders will receive 10 equity shares for every 131 equity shares held.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Fratelli Vineyards Limited (Scrip Code: 541741)
- Record Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: October 30, 2026
- Rights Issue Ratio: 10 Equity Shares for every 131 Equity Shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 90/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- + Premium Rs. 80/-)
- Payment Structure: Rs. 54/- per share payable on application, and the balance Rs. 36/- per share payable on one or more subsequent calls.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ensure transactions are executed on an ex-rights basis starting from October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are entitled to participate in the rights issue. The phased payment structure (application and subsequent calls) affects cash flow management for subscribing investors.
