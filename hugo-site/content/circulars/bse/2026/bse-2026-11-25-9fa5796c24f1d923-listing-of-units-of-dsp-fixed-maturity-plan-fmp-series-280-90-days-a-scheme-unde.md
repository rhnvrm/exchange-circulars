## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days under DSP Mutual Fund with effect from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days
- Mutual Fund: DSP Mutual Fund (Investment Manager: DSP Asset Managers Private Limited)
- Scrip Codes: 545029 (Regular Growth - ISIN: INF740KA1ZS6), 545030 (Direct Growth - ISIN: INF740KA1ZT4)
- Allotment Date: 19/11/2026
- Redemption Date: 17/02/2027
- Trading in dematerialized form only in unit market lot of 1 unit under "F" Group.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that units will be traded only in dematerialized form in accordance with SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 19, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: November 26, 2026
- Date of Redemption: February 17, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides exchange-traded liquidity for investors holding units of the closed-ended FMP scheme.
