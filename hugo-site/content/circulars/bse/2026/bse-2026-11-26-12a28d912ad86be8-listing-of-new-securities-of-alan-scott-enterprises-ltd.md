## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 9,34,157 new equity shares of Alan Scott Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 539115) following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Alan Scott Enterprises Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539115
- ISIN: INE273F01022
- Number of Shares: 9,34,157 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 8,17,571 (5717591 - 6535161) and 1,16,586 (6535162 - 6651747)
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities with effect from November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the fully paid-up equity base of the company without significant operational or market-wide impact.
