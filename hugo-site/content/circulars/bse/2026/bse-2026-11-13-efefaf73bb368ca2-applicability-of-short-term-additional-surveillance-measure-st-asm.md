## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Applicable margin rates are revised to 50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II, effective November 17, 2026, on all open positions as on November 16, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable rate of margin of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures take effect from November 17, 2026, on open positions as on November 16, 2026 and new positions.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions on specified securities as per Annexure I.
- Margin rate capping at 50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements for open and new positions in shortlisted securities effective November 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance / Margin Action: November 17, 2026 (on open positions as on November 16, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II may impact liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips.
