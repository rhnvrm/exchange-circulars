## Summary

Effective from Monday, November 2, 2026, the equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: EAAA India Alternatives Limited
- Scrip Code: 545008
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: EDELALTS
- ISIN No: INE528L01027
- Total Securities Listed: 64,655,246 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 5/- each, Issue Price Rs. 1665/- per share)
- Date of Allotment: October 29, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on November 2, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed to mark the lock-in period in depository records for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 29, 2026
- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading and liquidity for the equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited on the BSE exchange.
