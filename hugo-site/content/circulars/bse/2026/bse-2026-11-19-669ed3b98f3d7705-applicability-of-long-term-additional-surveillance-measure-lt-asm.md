## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the applicability and revision of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on specified securities, detailing 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups effective from November 20, 2026, and November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 25, 2026, on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price band applicable w.e.f. November 20, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM framework but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 25, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- List of securities moving out from LT-ASM framework w.e.f. November 20, 2026 given in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions and market surveillance, and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of LT-ASM measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and settlement group modifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements, revised price bands, and group transfers for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Lower Price Band / Moving Out: November 20, 2026
- Reference Date for Open Positions: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases margin requirements and trading restrictions on specified securities to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility.
