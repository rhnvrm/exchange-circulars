## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 22,25,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 76/- on a preferential basis by Antariksh Industries Limited, effective from November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Antariksh Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 501270
- **ISIN:** INE825M01017
- **Security Details:** 22,25,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 76/- (Issue Price: Rs. 86/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 204941 to 2429940
- **Date of Allotment:** 25/09/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 14,13,550 shares (Dist. Nos. 204941 to 1618490) locked in up to 15/05/2028.
  - 8,11,450 shares (Dist. Nos. 1618491 to 2429940) locked in up to 15/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading permissions as specified for the preferential issue shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 25/09/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 04/11/2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** 15/05/2027 and 15/05/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action allowing trading of newly allotted preferential equity shares on the exchange.
