## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261029-34 announcing the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure are moved to higher GSM stages effective October 30, 2026.
- Stage I: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade settlement with 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted once a week (Mondays), and 100% ASD.
- Settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 27, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities as settlements occur on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off.
- Buyers must deposit applicable Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and maintain required margins.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities may be restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement, mandatory ASD, and weekly trading frequency limits for higher stages.
