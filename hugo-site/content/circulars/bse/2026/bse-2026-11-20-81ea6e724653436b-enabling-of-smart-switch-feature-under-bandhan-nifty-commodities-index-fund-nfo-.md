## Summary

BSE has announced the enabling of the SMART Switch feature under the Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform. The facility will be available from November 23, 2026, until December 01, 2026, for eligible Bandhan Mutual Fund schemes.

## Key Points

- SMART Switch feature available via Manual Entry, Web-based Interface, and APIs.
- Applicable only for Bandhan Mutual Fund schemes, plans, and options specified in Annexure I.
- Restricted to lump sum subscriptions and non-Demat mode only.
- Existing unit holders in eligible schemes (e.g., Bandhan Overnight Fund) making additional purchases under NFO SMART Switch will have newly purchased units processed for switch out on a FIFO basis.
- Transactions accepted up to 2:30 PM on December 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is an operational platform enablement for a mutual fund NFO switch feature.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must use appropriate scheme credentials strictly in accordance with investor requirements for schemes listed in Annexure IV.
- Orders placed under SMART Switch can be identified via the Remarks column in Provisional Order and Order Status Reports on BSE StAR MF.
- Switch transactions to be reported to the RTA on December 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Feature Availability Start Date: November 23, 2026
- Feature Availability End Date / Transaction Cut-off: December 01, 2026 (up to 2:30 PM)
- RTA Reporting Date for Switch Transactions: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless switching for investors participating in the Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
