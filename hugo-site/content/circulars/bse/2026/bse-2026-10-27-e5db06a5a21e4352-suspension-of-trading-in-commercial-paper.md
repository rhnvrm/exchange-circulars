## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for various companies that have fixed record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as of the respective record and ex-dates.
- Affected entities include Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Dolat Algotech Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Titan Company Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Knowledge Realty Trust, Adani Enterprises Ltd., Navi Finserv Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Tata Housing Development Company Limited, Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited, and Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited.
- Record dates and settlement/ex-dates range from late October 2026 to early November 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational and trading suspension notice for commercial papers reaching redemption record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no trading activity occurs in the listed commercial papers on or after the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 27/10/2026
- **Record Dates:** 29/10/2026 to 10/11/2026
- **Ex-Dates:** 29/10/2026 to 09/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard procedure for debt instruments approaching redemption; no adverse market impact.
