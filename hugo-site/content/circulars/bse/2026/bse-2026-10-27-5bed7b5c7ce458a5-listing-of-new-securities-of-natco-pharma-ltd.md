## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and permission for trading of 1,70,58,082 new equity shares of Natco Pharma Ltd. (Scrip Code: 524816) issued on a rights basis, effective from Wednesday, October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Natco Pharma Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 524816
- ISIN: INE987B01026
- Number of Securities: 1,70,58,082 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 748/-
- Distribution Numbers (Dist. Nos.): 185557166 to 202615247
- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 28, 2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard corporate action notice regarding the listing of rights issue shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and trading permission of the new equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of Natco Pharma Ltd. through a rights issue without adverse market impact.
