## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. has announced changes in the methodology and names of several BSE sector indices, effective Friday, November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Impacted indices include BSE Auto, BSE Oil & Gas, BSE Metal, BSE Capital Goods, BSE Consumer Durables, and BSE Realty.
- Previous methodology selected stocks based on average float-adjusted market capitalization rank until 90% coverage per sector was achieved, with 90% trading frequency and 2% buffers.
- New methodology derives the eligible universe from constituents of the BSE 500 classified under the respective Sector Level as per the India Industry Classification.
- All stocks forming part of the eligible universe are selected in the respective index.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transitioned index eligibility and construction from float-adjusted market cap ranking to India Industry Classification based on BSE 500 constituents.
- Defined explicit inclusion and exclusion rules based on rebalancing and industry classification shifts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, fund managers, and benchmarking agencies must update their systems and reference data to align with the revised index methodology.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts index tracking funds, sector-specific portfolios, and derivatives referencing BSE sector indices due to changes in constituent eligibility.
