## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund has announced a revision in the minimum and additional application amounts for purchase, switch, and Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) for several of its schemes, effective from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Communication received from Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund regarding changes to minimum investment thresholds.
- Affected schemes include:
  1. Motilal Oswal Large and Midcap Fund
  2. Motilal Oswal Midcap Fund
  3. Motilal Oswal Small Cap Fund
  4. Motilal Oswal Quant Fund
  5. Motilal Oswal Active Momentum Fund
  6. Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund
  7. Motilal Oswal Quality Fund
- Changes cover purchases, switches, and SIP applications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised investment limits in accordance with the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by Motilal Oswal AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors must ensure that orders placed for the specified schemes comply with the revised minimum application and SIP amounts.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact; operational update affecting investors and distributors transacting in the mentioned Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund schemes.
