## Summary

BSE has notified trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion of the public issue of Carver Roboweld Limited, 9,96,800 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 80/- per equity share on October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Carver Roboweld Limited
- Segment: Equity / Listing
- Anchor Shares Subscribed: 9,96,800 equity shares
- Subscription Price: Rs. 80/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: October 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the anchor investor allocation details for their records and client disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency on institutional investor participation and pricing benchmarks ahead of the public issue rollout.
