## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party or in-house trading platforms are provided an opportunity to test their applications across various functionalities, including exceptional market conditions, call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and block deals.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Session timings cover log-in (01:15 pm - 01:50 pm), block deals, pre-open, continuous trading (02:20 pm - 03:30 pm), special pre-open for IPOs, periodic call auctions, closing sessions, and post-closing up to 04:00 pm / trade modification up to 04:15 pm.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock session by 04:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular relates solely to operational readiness and mock testing.

## Compliance Requirements

Participation is voluntary but recommended for trading members to verify system stability and application readiness. Members are encouraged to submit feedback to the Exchange helpdesk or tech support team by 04:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** October 31, 2026 (04:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact; ensures system readiness and robustness for trading operations in the equity segment.
