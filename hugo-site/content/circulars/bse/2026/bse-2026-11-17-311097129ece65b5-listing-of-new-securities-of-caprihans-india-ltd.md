## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 3,30,000 new equity shares of Caprihans India Ltd. (Scrip Code: 509486) effective from November 18, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Caprihans India Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 509486
- ISIN: INE479A01018
- Number of Securities: 3,30,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue Price: Rs. 200/-).
- Allotment Date: 25/03/2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 15583972 to 15913971.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: 3,30,000 shares under lock-in up to 29/05/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the listing of new securities and lock-in period compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 18, 2026
- Date of Allotment: March 25, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action regarding equity share listing from warrant conversion with minimal market impact.
