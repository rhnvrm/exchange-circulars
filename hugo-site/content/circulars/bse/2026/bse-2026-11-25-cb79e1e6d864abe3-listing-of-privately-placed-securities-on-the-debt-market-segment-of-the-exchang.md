## Summary

BSE has notified the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by companies including Navi Finserv Limited, Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited, and ONGC Petro Additions Limited, effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Navi Finserv Limited issued commercial papers with varying tenors, interest rates (9.70% to 9.75% p.a.), and ISINs (INE342T14IV0, INE342T14IU2, INE342T14IW8).
- Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited issued commercial papers with 9.20% p.a. interest rate under ISIN INE138I14156.
- ONGC Petro Additions Limited issued commercial papers with 7.80% p.a. interest rate under ISIN INE163N14634.
- All securities are traded in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing admission for privately placed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading requirements, standard denominations, and ISIN specifications.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 23, 2026 / November 25, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 25, 2026
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: Various dates between February 2027 and May 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market access for privately placed commercial papers on the BSE debt segment.
