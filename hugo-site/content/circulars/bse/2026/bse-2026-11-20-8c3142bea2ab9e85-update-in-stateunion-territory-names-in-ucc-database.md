## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updates and modifications to State and Union Territory names in its Unique Client Code (UCC) database, reflecting official nomenclature changes and mergers.

## Key Points

- Dadra & Nagar Haveli updated to Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (old nomenclature discontinued for new onboarding).
- Daman & Diu marked as merged with Dadra & Nagar Haveli.
- Pondicherry updated to Puducherry.
- Kerala updated to Keralam.
- Changes become effective on the UCC module on Saturday, November 21, 2026, at 2:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised State and UT names and merged entities within the Exchange's UCC system.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to update back-office systems, client onboarding workflows, and UCC management software. Fresh client registrations and modifications must use the revised nomenclature, and existing records should be updated where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 21, 2026 (2:00 PM onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate reporting and data validation of client demographics in compliance with updated administrative boundaries and naming conventions.
