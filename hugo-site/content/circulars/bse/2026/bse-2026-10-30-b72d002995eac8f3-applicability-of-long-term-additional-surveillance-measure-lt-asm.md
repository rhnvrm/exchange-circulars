## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfer of certain securities to specific groups (T / XT / MT / TS) based on periodic reviews and XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 04, 2026 on all open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective November 02, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 04, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective November 02, 2026, with price bands reinstated to previous levels.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is purely for market surveillance without being construed as adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on shortlisted securities as per Annexure I.
- Application of 100% margins on open and new positions.
- Price band restrictions and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and monitor price band adjustments for impacted scrips.
- Members may direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- November 02, 2026: Effective date for movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and movement out of LT-ASM.
- November 03, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to margin rules.
- November 04, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under the LT-ASM framework will experience increased margin obligations (100% margin) and trading restrictions such as lower price bands and group transfers, impacting liquidity and position management for affected scrips.
