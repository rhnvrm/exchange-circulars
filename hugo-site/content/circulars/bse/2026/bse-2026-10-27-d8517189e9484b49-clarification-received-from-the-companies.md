## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movements in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarifications from the concerned companies.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from: Goel Food Products Ltd and Sollfege Smart Electronics Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 20 listed companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, Black Opal Consultants Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Geetanjali Credit and Capital Ltd, Gujarat Raffia Industries Ltd, HBL Engineering Ltd, M P K Steels (I) Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Pind Hospitality Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Seemax Resources Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Symbiox Investment & Trading Company Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Vedant Asset Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price/volume movements as requested by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice regarding market surveillance and company disclosures with no direct financial impact.
