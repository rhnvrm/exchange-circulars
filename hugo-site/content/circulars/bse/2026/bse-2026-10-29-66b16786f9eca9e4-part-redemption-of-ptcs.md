## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the part redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) and fixed record dates for payment of interest/part redemption. Trading in these PTCs will take place with reduced face value effective from the specified record/ex-date.

## Key Points

- **Sansar Trust (Scrip Code: 975196, ISIN: INE0RVR15014):** Record Date 30/10/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 0.09 per debenture.
- **Indigo 041 (Scrip Code: 976473, ISIN: INE1PUF15021):** Record Date 30/10/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 105,957.92 per debenture.
- **Indigo 043 (Scrip Code: 976996, ISIN: INE2DNY15013):** Record Date 30/10/2026; Reduced face value of Rs. 238,321.38 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notification for debt segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the reduced face values and record dates for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 30/10/2026
- **Ex-Date:** 30/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and investors holding the respective PTCs reflecting the revised face value post part redemption.
