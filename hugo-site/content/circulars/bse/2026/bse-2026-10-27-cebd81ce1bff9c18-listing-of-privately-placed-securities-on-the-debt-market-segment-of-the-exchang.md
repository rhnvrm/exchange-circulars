## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by various companies on the debt market segment effective October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment**: Debt Market Segment
- **Issuances**: Commercial Papers (CPs) from Transport Corporation of India Ltd, A.K. Capital Services Ltd., Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, and Manappuram Finance Limited.
- **Trading Form**: Dematerialised form under designated ISINs.
- **Denomination**: Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paisa.
