## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 6,55,000 new equity shares of Kerala Ayurveda Limited issued to promoters on a preferential basis, effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kerala Ayurveda Limited (Scrip Code: 530163)
- Security Details: 6,55,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 180.37/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 190.37/- per share
- ISIN: INE817B01025
- Allotment Date: 21/10/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: November 23, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 12995086 to 13650085
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 01/06/2028 for all 6,55,000 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard listing approval for preferential allotment of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and depository participants must note the lock-in period and distinctive numbers of the newly allotted equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 21, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: June 1, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the equity capital base of Kerala Ayurveda Limited through promoter infusion via preferential allotment. Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
