## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,13,63,637 new equity shares of Seshachal Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 531794) with effect from November 27, 2026. These shares are issued at a price of Rs. 22/- each (face value Rs. 10/-, premium Rs. 12/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants and rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Seshachal Technologies Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 531794
- **ISIN:** INE331F01028
- **Security Details:** 1,13,63,637 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 12/- (Issue Price: Rs. 22/-)
- **Allotment Date:** October 3, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Distribution Range:** 694361 to 12057997
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 56,44,092 shares (Dist. Nos. 694361 - 6338452) locked-in up to June 29, 2027
  - 57,19,545 shares (Dist. Nos. 6338453 - 12057997) locked-in up to June 30, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; standard procedural listing of securities issued via preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions in these securities adhere to the specified distribution number ranges and observe the mandatory lock-in periods up to June 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 3, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** June 29, 2027 and June 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The capital expansion of over 1.13 crore equity shares increases the issued share base of Seshachal Technologies Ltd, while staggered lock-in periods ensure orderly liquidity management for non-promoter allottees.
