## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the tender offer (buyback) of equity shares by Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd (Scrip Code: 530343 / 130343, Settlement No. DR- 746/2026-2027). The company has fixed October 30, 2026, as the record date to determine shareholder entitlements and eligibility.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd
- Scrip Code: 530343 / 130343
- Record Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 30, 2026
- Purpose: To determine entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback offer.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notification under rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date while executing transactions and processing buyback applications for clients.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Record Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts shareholders of Genus Power Infrastructures Ltd intending to participate in the buyback program by setting the cutoff timeline for share eligibility.
