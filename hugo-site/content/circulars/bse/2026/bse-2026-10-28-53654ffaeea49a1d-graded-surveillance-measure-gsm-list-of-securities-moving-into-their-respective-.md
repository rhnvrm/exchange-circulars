## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from October 29, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and settlement procedures.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher stages of GSM effective October 29, 2026.
- Stage I attracts 100% margin and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II requires trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
- Stage III and IV require trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), 100% ASD, and no upward price band movement for Stage IV.
- Settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as established in previous notices (Feb 2017 to Nov 2023 frameworks).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, ensure correct margin and ASD deposits, and adhere to trade-to-trade settlement without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities will be restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement, mandatory surveillance deposits, and weekly trading frequency restrictions for higher stages.
