## Summary

BSE has announced the allocation of a new ISIN number (INE803L01024) for Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd following the sub-division of its equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd
- Scrip Code: 531911
- New ISIN: INE803L01024
- Stock Split Details: Sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.
- Ex-Date: October 30, 2026 (DR-746/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocation of a new ISIN number due to corporate action (stock split/sub-division).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISIN number for trades executed on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth settlement and tracking of equity shares following the share split and face value reduction.
