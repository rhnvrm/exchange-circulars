## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the upgraded Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026, operating from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site. This session is part of preparations and connectivity testing ahead of the go-live of the upgraded trading system (ETI version 13.1 and EOBI version 4).

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- Timings: Log-in from 09:15 am to 10:00 am; Continuous Trading for Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives from 10:00 am to 02:00 pm.
- Separate IP addresses and ports are designated for mock sessions so that production set-ups remain unaffected.
- User credentials remain unchanged; trading members can use their existing Currency Derivatives segment credentials.
- New version of BOLT Pro TWS 2.0 will be released for testing via DLOAD on leased line connectivity on November 28, 2026.
- Live trading on Monday, 30 November 2026, will continue using existing production IP addresses and ports, as the upgraded environment will not be live immediately.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition preparation to upgraded trading system architectures (ETI version 13.1 and EOBI version 4) for the Currency Derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to participate in the mock trading session, ensure necessary network connection parameters are enabled, and test connectivity to ensure a seamless transition.
- Members should complete testing using the new BOLT Pro TWS 2.0 version during the mock session window.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Mock Trading Session Date: November 28, 2026 (09:15 am to 02:00 pm)
- Live Trading Continuation: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness exercise for trading members; no direct financial or market impact on live trading as production environments remain unaffected during the mock session.
