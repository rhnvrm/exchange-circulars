## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin @15% in the equity derivatives segment for securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This framework is based on 3 months rolling data and is reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin of 15% applicable in the equity derivatives segment.
- Trigger criteria: Securities where top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL.
- Interaction with ASM: If additional surveillance margin is applicable, the higher of the two margins will be levied.
- Review frequency: Monthly review based on 3 months rolling data.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of risk management measures via additional exposure margins on concentrated derivative positions.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and trading members must account for the additional 15% exposure margin on the specified securities listed in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026 (following expiry of October 2026 contracts)
- Applicability: Until November 2026 expiry

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading participants and clients trading in specified equity derivative scrips by increasing margin requirements, thereby managing concentration risk and volatility.
