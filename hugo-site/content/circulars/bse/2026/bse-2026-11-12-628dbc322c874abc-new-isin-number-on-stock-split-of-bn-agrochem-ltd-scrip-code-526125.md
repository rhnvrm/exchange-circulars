## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the new ISIN number (INE00HZ01029) for the sub-division of equity shares of BN Agrochem Ltd from Rs. 10/- per share to Re. 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BN Agrochem Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526125
- New ISIN Number: INE00HZ01029
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- (Notice reference DR-757/2026-2027)
- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261106-4 dated November 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Corporate action implementation pursuant to exchange listing operations and procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure that trades in the sub-divided equity shares of BN Agrochem Ltd are executed under the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 16, 2026 (Effective for trades done on and from this date)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly trading, clearing, and settlement of equity shares post stock split under the updated ISIN.
