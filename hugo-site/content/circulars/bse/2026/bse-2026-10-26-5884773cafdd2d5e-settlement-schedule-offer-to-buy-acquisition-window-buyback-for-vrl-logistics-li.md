## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window) for the buyback of equity shares of VRL Logistics Limited. The bidding window is conducted from October 27, 2026, to November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement No.:** 2026128
- **Bid Start Date:** 27/10/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 02/11/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 06/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 09/11/2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Securities mechanism in CDSL and NSDL using Market Type (Buyback) and Settlement No. 2026128.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members must refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy introduced; operating under established guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must complete early pay-in of securities sufficiently in advance so that the effect is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before bidding and confirmation timelines close.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 27/10/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 02/11/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 06/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 09/11/2026

Ensures orderly execution and settlement of the VRL Logistics Limited buyback offer while clarifying depository earmarking and early pay-in procedures for market participants.
