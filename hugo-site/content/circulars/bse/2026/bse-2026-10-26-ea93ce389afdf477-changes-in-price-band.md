## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 12 scrips, effective from October 27, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands for 12 scrips have been revised from their existing levels.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 27, 2026.
- Most scrips have been revised to a 5% price band, with Triveni Power Transmission Ltd revised to 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scrip price band adjustments by BSE Surveillance Department.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure orders comply with the newly revised price bands across the specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the listed scrips will be subject to the new price limits, restricting excessive intraday price volatility.
