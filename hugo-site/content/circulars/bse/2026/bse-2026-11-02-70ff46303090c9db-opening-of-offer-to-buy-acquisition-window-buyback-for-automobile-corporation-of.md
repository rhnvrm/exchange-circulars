## Summary

Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited has announced an offer to buyback up to 1,44,639 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 2.38% of the total paid-up equity share capital, at a price of Rs. 2,005/- per equity share through the tender offer route on the stock exchange acquisition window. The aggregate buyback amount is up to Rs. 29,00,01,195/-.

## Key Points

- Buyback Size: Up to 1,44,639 equity shares (2.38% of paid-up equity share capital as of March 31, 2026).
- Buyback Price: Rs. 2,005/- per equity share payable in cash.
- Total Consideration: Aggregate amount of up to Rs. 29,00,01,195/-.
- Record Date: Wednesday, October 28, 2026.
- Offer Period: Tuesday, November 03, 2026, to Monday, November 09, 2026.
- Regulatory Framework: Conducted pursuant to SEBI Buyback Regulations and Stock Exchange mechanisms.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The notice outlines operational guidelines and schedules for a corporate buyback under existing SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must facilitate the tender-offer buyback mechanism pursuant to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131. Eligible shareholders holding shares as on the record date can participate through the stock exchange acquisition window during the offer period.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 28, 2026
- Buyback Offer Opening Date: November 03, 2026
- Buyback Offer Closing Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and capital return to eligible shareholders of Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited via a structured proportionate tender offer buyback.
