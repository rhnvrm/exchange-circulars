## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that new equity shares of Continental Controls Ltd. issued on a rights basis are listed and permitted for trading effective November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Continental Controls Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 531460
- Issue Details: 1,96,68,019 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued on a rights basis.
- ISIN: INE545B01022
- Distinctive Numbers: 6146257 to 25814275
- Date of Allotment: November 2, 2026
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the listing and commencement of trading of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 2, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of the newly allotted rights shares on the exchange platform.
