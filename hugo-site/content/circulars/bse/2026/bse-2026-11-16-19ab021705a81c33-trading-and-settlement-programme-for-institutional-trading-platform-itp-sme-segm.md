## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. (ICCL) has released the trading and settlement programme for the Institutional Trading Platform (ITP) under the SME segment for the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement numbers ITP-167/26-27 through ITP-188/26-27.
- Specifies corresponding depository settlement numbers, ITP trading dates, and pay-in/pay-out dates.
- Pay-in time is at 10:00 a.m. Members must submit pay-in instructions to depositories/banks latest by 09:50 a.m.
- Pay-out of funds and securities will be effected by 11:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must adhere to the prescribed timelines for pay-in and pay-out instructions during December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Programme Effective Period: December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and clarity on trading schedules for members operating in the ITP SME segment.
