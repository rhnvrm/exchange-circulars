## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into higher Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective from November 13, 2026. Trading members and investors are advised to take adequate precautions as these measures involve stricter margin requirements, price bands, and trade-for-trade settlement without netting off.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher stages of GSM starting November 13, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I to IV).
- Stage I: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD on buyers (with no upward movement in Stage IV).
- Settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

The securities listed in the attached Annexure are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures in accordance with the GSM Framework published by the Exchange on February 27, 2017, and subsequent notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities. Buyers are required to maintain applicable margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as specified for each GSM stage. No netting off of trades is permitted.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date of Movement: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The implementation of higher GSM stages restricts liquidity and trading frequency for the affected securities, increasing capital requirements for buyers through 100% margin or Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and enforcing trade-to-trade settlement.
