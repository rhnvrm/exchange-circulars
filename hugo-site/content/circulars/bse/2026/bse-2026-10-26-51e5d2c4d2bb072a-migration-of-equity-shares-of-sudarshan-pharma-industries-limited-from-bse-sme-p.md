## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, October 27, 2026, the equity shares of Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited, previously listed on the BSE SME Platform, are migrated and admitted to dealings on the BSE Mainboard Platform under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 543828
- ISIN: INE00TV01023
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT System: SUDARSHAN
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SUDARSHAN
- Securities: 254,470,600 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- Distinctive Numbers: 1 - 254470600
- Market Lot: 1 share (traded in dematerialized form only)
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are migrated from the BSE SME Platform to the Mainboard Platform under the 'B' Group.
- Trading will continue exclusively in dematerialized form in unit market lots as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems to reflect the migration to the Mainboard platform.
- Audited financial results for the half year and year ended March 31, 2026, and shareholding pattern as on September 30, 2026, are enclosed as Annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date of Migration: October 27, 2026
- Financial Year End: March 31

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced visibility and liquidity for shareholders as the company transitions from SME to Mainboard trading.
- Operational transition for trading members handling the scrip under 'B' Group on the Mainboard platform.
