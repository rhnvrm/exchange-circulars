## Summary

BSE has informed market participants that NAVI Mutual Fund has revised the minimum redemption amount for several of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- The minimum redemption amount has been revised from Rs. 10 (in multiples of Rs. 1/-) to Rs. 100 (in multiples of Rs. 1/- thereafter).
- Affected schemes include various index funds, liquid fund, flexi cap fund, large and midcap fund, aggressive hybrid fund, and US specific equity passive FoFs.
- Applicable on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational parameter revision (minimum redemption threshold) implemented by NAVI AMC across specified mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must ensure redemption requests comply with the new minimum amount of Rs. 100.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Immediate (Notice Date: November 16, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Minor operational impact on retail investors redeeming small ticket sizes below Rs. 100 in the listed NAVI Mutual Fund schemes.
