## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 527,760 units of Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF under Zerodha Mutual Fund, effective from Wednesday, November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF (Open-ended scheme replicating/tracking BSE Top 10 Banks Index)
- **Mutual Fund:** Zerodha Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** Zerodha CASE Asset Management Private Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545014
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** BANKCASE
- **ISIN No:** INF0R8F01265
- **Number of Units:** 527,760 Units
- **Group / Market Lot:** 'B' Group / 1 Unit
- **Face Value / Issue Price:** Rs. 100.00 per unit / Rs. 99.99 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 29/10/2026
- **Registrar & Transfer Agent:** Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing announcement pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Units will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Trades will be executed in unit market lot of 1 unit.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional exchange-traded fund option tracking the BSE Top 10 Banks Index, enhancing liquidity and investment choices in the mutual fund segment.
