## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members using third-party vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test trading functionalities such as risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- Date of Mock Trading: Saturday, November 21, 2026.
- Segment: Equity Derivatives.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Session Timings: Login (11:15 AM - 12:00 PM), Continuous Trading (12:15 PM - 04:10 PM), and Trade Modification (up to 04:20 PM).
- Trades resulting from the mock session do not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to participate actively and provide feedback on the mock trading session to BSE Tech Support or Help Desk by 05:00 PM on the mock trading day.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 21, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: November 21, 2026 (05:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Purely operational simulation to test trading member applications and infrastructure with zero financial or regulatory impact.
