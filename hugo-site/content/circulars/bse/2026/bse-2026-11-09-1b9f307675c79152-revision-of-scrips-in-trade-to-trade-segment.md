## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of scrips in the Trade-to-Trade segment pursuant to surveillance measures and the extant SEBI framework. Securities satisfying specific criteria will be transferred to T, XT, MT, or TS groups effective November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are transferred to T/XT/MT/TS Group w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Securities listed in Annexure II will continue to remain in T/XT/MT/TS/P/Z/ZP Group and attract a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off of positions allowed.
- A 100% VAR Margin is levied on these scrips pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20050805-12.
- Shortlisting of securities is based on XBRL submissions by respective listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic application of surveillance measures in consultation with SEBI to shift securities for settlement on a trade-to-trade basis to ensure market safety.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities, keeping in view the trade-to-trade settlement mechanism, prohibition of position netting, and 100% VAR margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and execution for the affected scrips will be impacted by the 100% margin requirement and mandatory delivery-based settlement without netting off.
