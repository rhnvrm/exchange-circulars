## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund is launching the H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (SIF) New Fund Offer (NFO) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from November 13, 2026, to November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Offering multiple plans under H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct, Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO period: November 13, 2026, to November 27, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases extended till midnight on November 27, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated on or before 3:00 PM on November 27, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date is December 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 01, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP client authentication required by 3:00 PM on NFO closure date.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 13, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 27, 2026
- SIP Authentication Deadline: November 27, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 01, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 01, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables seamless subscription and investment in HDFC Mutual Fund's Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) hybrid scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform.
