## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities that have satisfied inclusion criteria, moved to lower stages (Stage I), or been shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective November 04 and November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under the ESM framework shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 05, 2026, on all open positions as on November 04, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be subject to Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 04, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 05, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, with specific lists in Annexures I and II.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) requirements, including 100% margin requirements, price band caps, and mandatory shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment for applicable scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for listed scrips.
- Market participants must account for settlement segment changes and trading restrictions effective from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2026**: Effective date for stage movements, price bands, and Periodic Call Auction restrictions.
- **November 05, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened margin requirements and trading restrictions (such as Trade-for-Trade settlement and 2% price bands) will significantly impact liquidity and trading operations for securities included in the ESM framework.
