## Summary

Axis Mutual Fund has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-IN) under certain schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from the close of business hours on November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lumpsum investments, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) are disabled for specified schemes as per the Annexure.
- Existing SIP and STP-in registrations set up prior to the effective date will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without change.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, STP-outs, and SWPs remain fully operational and allowed.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on November 19, 2026, were not accepted or processed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational suspension implemented on the BSE StAR MF platform as per official communication received from Axis Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must note the suspension of fresh purchases and new SIP/STP registrations for the affected schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026 (Effective from closer of business November 19, 2026).
- Cut-off Time: Closer of business hours on November 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and distributors cannot initiate fresh subscriptions or new SIPs/STPs in the affected Axis Mutual Fund schemes via the BSE StAR MF platform, while existing systematic plans and exit transactions (redemptions, switch-outs) remain unaffected.
