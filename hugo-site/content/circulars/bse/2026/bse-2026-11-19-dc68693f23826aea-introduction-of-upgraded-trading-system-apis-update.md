## Summary

BSE has released an update regarding the upgrade of the Exchange trading platform aimed at strengthening market infrastructure and enhancing operational efficiency for trading members.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous Exchange Circular Nos. 20261029-31 and 20260925-31.
- Trading members are advised to refer to the updated EOBI Manual.
- Document link provided for the EOBI Manual: https://www.bseindia.com/downloads1/EOBI_Manual.pdf.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; operational update regarding trading APIs and system infrastructure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should review the updated EOBI document and ensure necessary technical alignments are made.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances operational efficiency and technical integration for trading members using BSE trading system APIs.
