## Summary

BLS Polymers Limited has announced the allocation of 51,00,000 equity shares to anchor investors at a price of Rs. 150/- per equity share under the anchor investors portion of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company: BLS Polymers Limited
- Issue Portion: Anchor Investors (AIs) portion
- Number of Shares Allocated: 51,00,000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 150/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: October 26, 2026

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are informed of the anchor investor allocation as per regulatory guidelines for public issues.
