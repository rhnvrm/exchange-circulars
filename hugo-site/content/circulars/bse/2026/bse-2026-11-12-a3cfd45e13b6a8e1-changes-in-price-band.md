## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members that 17 securities have been shortlisted for revision of their existing price bands. The revised price bands become effective from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revisions apply to 17 listed securities on the exchange.
- Price bands are revised to 2%, 5%, and 10% depending on the security.
- Effective date for the revised price limits is November 13, 2026.
- Members can direct clarifications to the surveillance department at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Affected Securities & Revised Price Bands

- **Aditya Ispat Ltd** (513513): 5%
- **Alfa Transformers Ltd** (517546): 5%
- **Flexituff Ventures International Ltd** (533638): 10%
- **Flomic Global Logistics Ltd** (504380): 10%
- **GG Automotive Gears Ltd** (531399): 5%
- **Gourmet Gateway India Ltd** (506134): 5%
- **Hasti Finance Ltd** (531387): 2%
- **Himalaya Nutravedics India Ltd** (544943): 10%
- **Jyoti Structures Ltd** (513250): 10%
- **Lorenzini Apparels Ltd** (540952): 5%
- **Magenta Lifecare Ltd** (544188): 5%
- **Makers Laboratories Ltd** (506919): 5%
- **Mardia Samyoung Capillary Tubes Company Ltd** (513544): 2%
- **Raghuvir Synthetics Ltd** (514316): 5%
- **Shivchem Agro Ltd** (544967): 5%
- **United Van Der Horst Ltd** (522091): 5%
- **Vantage Knowledge Academy Ltd** (539761): 5%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their order management and trading systems reflect the updated price bands prior to market opening on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Price band revisions help curb abnormal price volatility in specific scrips, protecting market participants and maintaining orderly trading conditions.
