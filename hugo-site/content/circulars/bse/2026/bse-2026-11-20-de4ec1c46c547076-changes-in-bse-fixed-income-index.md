## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced changes to the constituents of the BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index, effective Monday, November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Index Affected:** BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index.
- **Addition:** 6.53% GS 2031 (ISIN: IN0020260074, Maturity: 07-Sep-2031).
- **Deletion:** 7.18% GS 2033 (ISIN: IN0020230085, Maturity: 14-Aug-2033).
- **Unchanged Indices:** BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index, BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index, and BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None applicable to market participants or issuers.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index rebalancing update with minimal operational impact on market participants.
