## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by 10 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) with effect from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities issued under ESOP/ESOS by 10 companies are admitted for trading.
- Effective trading date: Monday, November 16, 2026.
- Participating companies include Equitas Small Finance Bank, Honasa Consumer, ICICI Life Insurance, IIFL Finance, Kansai Nerolac Paints, Lenskart Solutions, OnEMI Technology Solutions, Shadowfax Technologies, Shaily Engineering Plastics, and Zensar Technologies.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to BSE listing regulations and requirements for ESOP allotment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in minor equity expansion for the respective companies.
