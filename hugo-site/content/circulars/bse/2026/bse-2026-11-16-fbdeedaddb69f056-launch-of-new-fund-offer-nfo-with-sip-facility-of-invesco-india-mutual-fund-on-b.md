## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Invesco India Nifty Metal Index Fund by Invesco India Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Invesco India Nifty Metal Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Window:** November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 30, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- **SIP Facility:** Available; SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 30, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 04, 2026.
- **ISINs:** Regular Plan - Growth (INF205KA1CX3), IDCW Payout (INF205KA1CY1), IDCW Reinvestment (INF205KA1CZ8); Direct Plan - Growth (INF205KA1DA9), IDCW Payout (INF205KA1DB7), IDCW Reinvestment (INF205KA1DC5).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 04, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** November 16, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** November 30, 2026
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** November 30, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 04, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** December 04, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and SIP registration for investors participating in the Invesco India Nifty Metal Index Fund NFO through members on the BSE StAR MF platform.
