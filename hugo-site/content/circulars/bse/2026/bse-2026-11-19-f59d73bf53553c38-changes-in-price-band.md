## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 13 securities, effective from November 20, 2026, as part of surveillance and risk management measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 13 scrips including Bombay Coated And Special Steels Ltd, CHD Chemicals Ltd, Crescentis Capital Limited, and others.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the security.
- Effective date for the new price bands is November 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of price operating limits for specified securities to curb excessive volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and take note of the revised price bands effective from the trading sessions of November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading parameters and execution limits for the affected 13 scrips are updated, impacting intraday price movement boundaries.
