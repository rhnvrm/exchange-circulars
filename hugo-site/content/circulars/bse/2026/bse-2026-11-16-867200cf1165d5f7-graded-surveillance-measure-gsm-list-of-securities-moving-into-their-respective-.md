## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. These measures take effect from November 17, 2026, based on the provisions of the GSM framework published on February 27, 2017.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher GSM Stages (I, II, III, or IV) with specific surveillance actions.
- Stage I entails a 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II involves trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% price band and an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value.
- Stage III and IV involve trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/first trading day), and 100% ASD.
- Settlement for affected securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance actions under the GSM framework for specified securities to ensure market integrity and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities, ensuring compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules, margin requirements, and deposit obligations without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 November 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: 17 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin and deposit requirements along with restricted liquidity due to trade-for-trade settlement and weekly trading limits for higher stages, impacting trading volumes and participant positioning.
