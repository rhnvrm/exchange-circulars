## Summary

Anand Kamalnayan Pandit, the promoter of Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited, has announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 2,37,95,818 equity shares (representing 4.87% of paid-up equity share capital) with an oversubscription option of up to 50,00,000 equity shares (1.02%). The floor price for the offer is set at Rs. 240/- per equity share. The offering aims to achieve the minimum public shareholding requirement.

## Key Points

- **Seller/Promoter:** Anand Kamalnayan Pandit
- **Company:** Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited
- **Base Offer Size:** Up to 2,37,95,818 Equity Shares (4.87% of paid-up capital)
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 50,00,000 Equity Shares (1.02% of paid-up capital)
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 240/- per Equity Share
- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors):** November 03, 2026
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors & Non-Retail carrying forward):** November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer adheres to revised guidelines for bidding in Offer for Sale (OFS) segments issued by SEBI and BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for OFS bidding, including separate designated windows on BSE and NSE.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 02, 2026
- **T Day (Non-Retail Bidding):** November 03, 2026
- **T+1 Day (Retail Bidding):** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides an investment opportunity in Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited through the stock exchange mechanism while enabling the promoter to meet minimum public shareholding norms.
