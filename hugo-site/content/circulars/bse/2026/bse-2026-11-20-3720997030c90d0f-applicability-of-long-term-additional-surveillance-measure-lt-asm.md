## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability, review, and revision of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include the imposition of 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfers to T/XT/MT/TS groups for specified securities based on XBRL submissions and market surveillance.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 26, 2026, on all open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands applicable w.e.f. November 23, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 26, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 23, 2026, with price bands reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group reclassifications for securities shortlisted under the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions starting November 26, 2026, and adhere to revised price bands and group trading structures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for Price Bands & Moving Out / Higher Stages: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading liquidity and leverage for affected securities due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions. Market participants holding positions in these scrips must adjust risk management parameters accordingly.
