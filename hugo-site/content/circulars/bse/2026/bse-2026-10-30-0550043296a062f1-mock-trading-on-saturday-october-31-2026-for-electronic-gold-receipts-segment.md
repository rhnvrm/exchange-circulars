## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms, empaneled vendor systems, or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can test their trading applications under normal and exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- Allows testing of third-party trading platforms, vendor systems, and in-house applications via ETI APIs.
- Functionalities available for testing include risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and exceptional market conditions.
- No new BOLT TWS Release is involved in this session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, pay-out, or creation of rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to actively participate and submit their feedback regarding the mock trading session to the BSE Tech Support team or Help Desk by 04:00 PM on October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 31, 2026
- Log-in Timing: 01:15 PM to 02:05 PM
- Order Entry Period: 02:05 PM to 02:13 PM
- Continuous Trading: 02:20 PM to 03:30 PM
- Feedback Deadline: October 31, 2026, by 04:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Provides technical and operational validation for members operating in the EGR segment with zero financial risk or margin obligation.
