## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movement in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure that trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- Replies received from:
  1. Astec Lifesciences Ltd (Scrip Code: 533138)
  2. Kopran Ltd (Scrip Code: 524280)
- Replies awaited from 24 listed companies including Amanta Healthcare Ltd, Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Covance Softsol Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Geetanjali Credit and Capital Ltd, Gujarat Raffia Industries Ltd, HBL Engineering Ltd, Krupalu Metals Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Omega Interactive Technologies Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Sangam Finserv Ltd, Shayona Engineering Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Symbiox Investment & Trading Company Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, Tasty Bite Eatables Ltd, and Vedant Asset Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard surveillance procedure for monitoring price and volume movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies requested by the Exchange must submit required clarifications regarding significant price/volume movements in their scrips promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 13 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and timely dissemination of material information to investors regarding abnormal market movements.
