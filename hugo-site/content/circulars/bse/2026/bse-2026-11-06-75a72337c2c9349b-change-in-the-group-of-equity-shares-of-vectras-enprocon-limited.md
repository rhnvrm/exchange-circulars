## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of Vectras Enprocon Limited (Scrip Code: 545020) is being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Monday, November 23, 2026, in continuation of the Exchange's Notice No. 20261105-36.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vectras Enprocon Limited
- Scrip Code: 545020
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, November 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of equity shares trading category from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 6, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates regular rolling settlement for the equity shares of Vectras Enprocon Limited, enhancing liquidity and trading access.
