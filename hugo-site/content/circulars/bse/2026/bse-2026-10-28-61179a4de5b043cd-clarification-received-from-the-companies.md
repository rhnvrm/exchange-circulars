## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements in various scrips in the recent past. The exchange sought clarifications from multiple companies to ensure investors have the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 3 companies: Paramount Syntex Ltd, Pind Hospitality Ltd, and Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Ltd.
- Clarifications awaited from 22 companies including Amtech Esters Ltd, Anna Infrastructures Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, and others.
- Aimed at ensuring transparency and dissemination of relevant information to trading members and investors.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine surveillance disclosure under exchange monitoring mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies that have not yet responded are required to submit their clarifications regarding price/volume movements to the exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and alerts investors regarding heightened price and volume volatility in specific scrips.
