## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) and SIP facility for QUANT Mutual Fund (QUANT NIFTY NEXT 50 INDEX FUND) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from November 25, 2026, to December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: QUANT NIFTY NEXT 50 INDEX FUND (Regular and Direct plans available for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO Period: November 25, 2026, to December 07, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 09, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 09, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 25, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: December 07, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and SIP registration for the new index fund offering by Quant Mutual Fund for market participants and investors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
