## Summary

BSE has announced that certain securities will be moved to higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework with effect from November 05, 2026. Trading members must exercise adequate precautions as these securities will attract stricter surveillance actions.

## Key Points

- Effective date for movement to higher GSM stages: November 05, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I to IV).
- Settlement for affected securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed for certain stages.
- Notice references previous framework notices dated February 23, 2017, March 03, 2017, July 20, 2018, November 29, 2019, and November 17, 2023.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margin rates, lower price bands (5% or lower), trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) ranging from 50% to 100% of trade value depending on the stage.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities.
- No netting off is permitted for settlement of these securities.
- Members can refer to the FAQ link or contact bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions for securities moving into higher GSM stages, impacting liquidity and trading frequency (especially for stages III and IV where trading is permitted once a week).
