## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Avenique Ltd (Scrip Code: 530701), effective November 05, 2026. Trading will resume in the "XT" group following the company's compliance with annual listing fee payment requirements pursuant to SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading resumption date: November 05, 2026.
- Scrip Code: 530701 (Avenique Ltd).
- Securities will be traded in the "XT" group.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs & Relisted Scrips on November 05, 2026.
- Information Memorandum will be available on the Exchange's website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 (erstwhile SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/12 dated January 22, 2020).

## Compliance Requirements

- Company has fulfilled the requirement of payment of annual listing fees.
- Trading members and the company must note the relist session guidelines and SPOS procedures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026.
- Effective Date of Revocation / Resumption of Trading: November 05, 2026.
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): November 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume trading in Avenique Ltd shares under the XT group starting November 05, 2026, subject to special pre-open session guidelines.
