## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 93,500 new equity shares of Kanpur Plastipack Limited (Scrip Code: 507779) are listed and permitted to trade effective November 11, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Security: 93,500 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 120/- (Issue Price: Rs. 130/-).
- Allottees: Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants).
- Distinctive Numbers: 24493040 to 24586539.
- Date of Allotment: 13/08/2026.
- ISIN: INE694E01014.
- Lock-in Details: 93,500 shares (Dist. Nos. 24493040 to 24586539) locked-in up to 09/05/2028.
- Effective Date: Trading permitted from November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements up to May 9, 2028.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026.
- Listing/Trading Effective Date: November 11, 2026.
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 9, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity share capital of Kanpur Plastipack Limited due to preferential allotment and warrant conversion.
