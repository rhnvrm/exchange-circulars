## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in four fixed maturity plans (FMPs) of SBI Mutual Fund due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these schemes effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes maturing and suspended: SBI Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) - Series 53 (1839 Days) across Regular and Direct plans (Growth and IDCW Payout).
- Scrip Codes: 543379, 543380, 543381, 543382.
- ISINs: INF200KA16A4, INF200KA17A2, INF200KA18A0, INF200KA19A8.
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational notice regarding the maturity and suspension of trading of closed-ended debt mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no transactions are executed in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes effective from November 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading Suspension: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational and market impact as this represents the normal maturity lifecycle of fixed maturity mutual fund schemes.
