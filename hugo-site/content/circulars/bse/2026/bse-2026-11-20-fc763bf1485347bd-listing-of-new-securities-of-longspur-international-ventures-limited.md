## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,72,50,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued by Longspur International Ventures Limited on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters, effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Longspur International Ventures Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 504340
- **ISIN:** INE180M01033
- **Security Details:** 1,72,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** 24/06/2026, 26/06/2026, and 27/06/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine corporate listing of shares issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and distribution numbers specified for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 23, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** June 24, 2026; June 26, 2026; June 27, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** May 30, 2027 and May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital of Longspur International Ventures Limited by 1,72,50,000 shares, expanding liquidity and shareholding base subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
