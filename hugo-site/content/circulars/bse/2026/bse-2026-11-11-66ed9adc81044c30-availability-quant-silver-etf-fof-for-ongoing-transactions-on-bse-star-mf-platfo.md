## Summary

BSE has announced that the QUANT SILVER ETF FOF will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from November 12, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261103-29.

## Key Points

- Schemes covered: QUANT SILVER ETF FOF (Regular and Direct plans across IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth options).
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- ISINs: INF966L01FL6, INF966L01FM4, INF966L01FK8, INF966L01FI2, INF966L01FJ0, INF966L01FH4.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate ongoing transactions for the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates ongoing investment, redemption, and systematic transactions for investors in Quant Silver ETF FOF schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
