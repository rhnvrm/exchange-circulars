## Summary

BSE has announced that the Exchange will disseminate position limits for all indices in the Equity Derivatives segment, applicable for the next trading day with effect from November 23, 2026. The applicable limits in quantity terms have been made available on the Exchange website starting November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Position limits for index futures at the client category level depend on participant types (FPI-I/Mutual Funds/Trading Member Prop vs FPI-II).
- Trading Member level position limits apply to both Index Futures and Index Options (Higher of Rs. 7,500 Crores or 15% of total open interest).
- Client level limits in Index Options are monitored on Future Equivalent (FutEq/Delta) basis.
- Applicable limits in quantity terms are published on the BSE website under the report name 'F&O-Index Derivatives Position Limit'.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforces position limit monitoring for equity derivatives indices as specified in earlier BSE notices (20250630-86 and 20250403-58).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must review and adhere to the disseminated index position limits across client categories and trading member levels.

## Important Dates

- Website Availability Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and regulatory compliance regarding derivatives position limits across market participants and trading members.
