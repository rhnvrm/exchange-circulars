## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities on account of their maturity, effective from October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects three maturing securities: 182T291026 (Scrip Code: 805209, ISIN: IN002026Y048), 364T291026 (Scrip Code: 805138, ISIN: IN002025Z310), and 91TB291026 (Scrip Code: 805247, ISIN: IN002026X172).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from October 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no transactions are executed in the specified maturing T-bills and Government Securities from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure reflecting the maturity and de-activation of trading for matured sovereign debt instruments.
