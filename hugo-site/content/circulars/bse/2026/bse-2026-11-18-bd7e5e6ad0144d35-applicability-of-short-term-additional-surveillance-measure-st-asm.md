## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective November 20, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I attract a 50% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Effective date for surveillance actions is November 20, 2026, applicable on open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on shortlisted securities under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or 100% as applicable) on open positions as of November 19, 2026, and all new positions created from November 20, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact trading liquidity and open position carrying costs for market participants trading in these scrips.
