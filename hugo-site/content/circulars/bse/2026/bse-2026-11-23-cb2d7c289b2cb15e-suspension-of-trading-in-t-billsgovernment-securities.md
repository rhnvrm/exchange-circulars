## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their impending maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in the listed securities effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in three maturing securities: 182T271126 (ISIN: IN002026Y089), 91TB271126 (ISIN: IN002026X214), and 758GUJSDL26 (ISIN: IN1520230104).
- Effective date for cessation of trading: November 25, 2026.
- Issued by Listing Operations & Compliance department.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified T-bills and Government Securities from November 25, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine impact limited to market participants holding the maturing government securities and T-bills.
