## Summary

Geetanjali Credit and Capital Limited has fixed the Record Date for its upcoming Rights Issue of Equity Shares. Trading members and shareholders are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company will be on an ex-rights basis starting from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Geetanjali Credit and Capital Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539486
- **Record Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Rights Ratio:** 16 Equity Shares for every 1 Equity Share held
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 2.50 per share
- **Department:** Listing Compliance and Operations (DR- 759/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

Standard corporate action processing notice under the rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange must take note of the ex-rights date and ensure transactions are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders receive 16 rights shares for every 1 share held, impacting share capital structure and ex-rights market valuation.
