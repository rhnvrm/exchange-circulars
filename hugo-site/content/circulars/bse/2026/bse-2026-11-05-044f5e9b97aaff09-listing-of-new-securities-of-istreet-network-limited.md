## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing of 2,00,000 new equity shares of iStreet Network Limited (Scrip Code: 524622) with effect from November 6, 2026. These shares are issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** iStreet Network Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 524622
- **ISIN:** INE532B01020
- **Number of Shares:** 2,00,000 equity shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 6/- per share (Face value Rs. 4/- plus premium of Rs. 2/-)
- **Effective Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** July 2, 2026
- **Lock-in Period:** Locked-in up to May 30, 2027 (Distinctive numbers: 70500004 to 70700003)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 02/07/2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** 30/05/2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the equity capital base of iStreet Network Limited through preferential warrant conversion with specific lock-in constraints, having minimal direct market impact.
