## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Surveillance actions apply w.e.f. October 30, 2026 on all open positions as on October 29, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with increased margin rates (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open and new positions starting October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements may impact trading volumes and liquidity for the securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and II.
