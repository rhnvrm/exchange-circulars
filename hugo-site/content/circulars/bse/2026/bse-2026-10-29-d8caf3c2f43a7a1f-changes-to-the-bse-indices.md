## Summary

BSE has announced changes to its indices pursuant to Notice No. 20261029-7 issued by BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. Effective at the open of Friday, October 30, 2026, National Stock Exchange of India Ltd will be added to the BSE SELECT IPO index following a fast-tracked IPO review.

## Key Points

- Index Name: BSE SELECT IPO
- Stock Name: National Stock Exchange of India Ltd
- Exchange Ticker: 544937
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Reason: Fast-Tracked IPO review

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard index composition update based on fast-tracked IPO review guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clients maintaining portfolios or systems tracking the BSE SELECT IPO index must incorporate the updated index constituents effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Index funds and tracking portfolios benchmarking against the BSE SELECT IPO index will need to adjust their holdings to reflect the inclusion of National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.
