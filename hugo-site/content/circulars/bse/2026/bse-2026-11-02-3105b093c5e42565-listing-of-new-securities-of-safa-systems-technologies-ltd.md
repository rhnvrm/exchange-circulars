## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 23,90,000 new equity shares of Safa Systems & Technologies Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters, effective from November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 543461
- ISIN: INE0JNA01014
- Number of Shares: 23,90,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 10.47/- (Issue Price: Rs. 20.47/-)
- Allotment Date: 05/10/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 24977551 to 27367550
- Lock-in Period: Locked-in up to 30/05/2028
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and concerned entities must note the lock-in restrictions and listing details of the newly allotted shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 5, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital of Safa Systems & Technologies Ltd. through preferential allotment to promoters, with shares subject to a lock-in until May 2028.
