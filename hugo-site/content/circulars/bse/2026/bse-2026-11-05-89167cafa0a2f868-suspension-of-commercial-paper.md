## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers of specific companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies from their respective ex-dates.
- Affected issuers include Arka Fincap Limited, Titan Company Limited, Embassy Office Parks REIT, and Can Fin Homes Ltd.
- Record dates and ex-dates are scheduled between November 6, 2026, and November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice for debt segment corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no trading activity takes place in the listed commercial papers on or after the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Arka Fincap Limited:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 06/11/2026 (ISIN: INE03W114740)
- **Titan Company Limited:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 06/11/2026 (ISIN: INE280A14658) and 11/11/2026 (ISIN: INE280A14617)
- **Embassy Office Parks REIT:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 09/11/2026 (ISIN: INE041014114)
- **Can Fin Homes Ltd.:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 09/11/2026 (ISIN: INE477A14EM2)

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to market participants holding or trading the specified commercial papers ahead of their redemption.
