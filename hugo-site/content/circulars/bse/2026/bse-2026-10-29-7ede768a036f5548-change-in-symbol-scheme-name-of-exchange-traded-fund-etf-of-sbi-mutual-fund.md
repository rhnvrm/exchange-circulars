## Summary

BSE has announced a change in the trading symbols and scheme names for several Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) managed by SBI Mutual Fund, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affects 7 SBI Mutual Fund ETF schemes including SBI-ETF Nifty Bank, SBI Nifty 50 ETF, SBI Nifty50 Equal Weight ETF, SBI BSE PSU Bank ETF, SBI-ETF Gold, SBI Nifty Midcap 150 ETF, and SBI NIFTY 1D Rate ETF.
- Existing symbols such as SETFNIFBK, SETFNIF50, SBINEQWETF, SBIBPB, SETFGOLD, and SBINMID150 are being updated to new standardized symbols.
- Scheme name for the SBI Nifty 1D Rate ETF is updated to include 'SBI Mutual Fund - SBI Nifty 1D Rate Liquid ETF - IDCW' with symbol LIQUIDSBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative updates to ETF nomenclature and trading symbol identifiers on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals and back-office systems with the new symbols and scheme names.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on market operations; primarily ensures operational clarity and standardized naming conventions for SBI Mutual Fund ETFs.
