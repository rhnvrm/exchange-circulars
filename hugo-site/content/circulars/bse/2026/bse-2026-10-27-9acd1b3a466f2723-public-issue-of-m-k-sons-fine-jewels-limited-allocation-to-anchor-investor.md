## Summary

M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited has finalized the allocation of 51,00,000 Equity Shares to Anchor Investors at an issue price of Rs. 162 per equity share as part of its public issue.

## Key Points

- **Issue Details:** Public issue of M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited.
- **Anchor Investor Allocation:** 51,00,000 Equity Shares subscribed.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 162/- per equity share.
- **Announcement Date:** October 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance requirements for public issues and anchor investor lock-in norms as per SEBI regulations apply.

## Important Dates

- **Allocation Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor allocation demonstrates institutional investor participation and sets a benchmark for the public issue subscription.
