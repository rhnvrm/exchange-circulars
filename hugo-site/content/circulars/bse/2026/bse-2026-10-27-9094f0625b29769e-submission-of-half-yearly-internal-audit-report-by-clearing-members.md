## Summary

BSE has notified all clearing members to complete and submit their half-yearly internal audit report for the period ended September 30, 2026. The due date for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all clearing members who undertook clearing activities for at least one trade between April 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be made via BSECL’s Electronic Filing system (IEFS).
- Auditors empaneled with any recognized Stock Exchange are eligible to conduct the internal audit.
- Annexure II must be submitted in both Excel and digitally signed PDF formats, incorporating management comments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates ease of doing compliance by allowing auditors empaneled with any exchange to conduct internal audits for Clearing Members as advised by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Complete the internal audit for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.
- Adhere to sample selection guidelines in Annexure III and provide sample verification details in Annexure IV.
- Submit the digitally signed Internal Audit Certificate (Annexure I) and reports through IEFS by November 30, 2026.
- Late submissions attract penalties of Rs. 2,500 per day for the first week and Rs. 5,000 per day thereafter.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Period End Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Submission Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members must ensure timely coordination with empaneled auditors and document submission through IEFS to avoid monetary penalties and disciplinary actions.
