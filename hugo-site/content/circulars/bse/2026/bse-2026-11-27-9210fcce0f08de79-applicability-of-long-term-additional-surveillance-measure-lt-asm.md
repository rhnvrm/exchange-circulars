## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability, revisions, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective from November 30, 2026, and December 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. December 02, 2026, on all open positions as on December 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I part A).
- Lower Price Bands and stage revisions applicable w.e.f. November 30, 2026 (Annexure I part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. December 02, 2026 (Annexure I part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. November 30, 2026, with price bands reinstated (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins, stage revisions, and group transfers under the revised LT-ASM framework.
- Shortlisting based on XBRL submissions by listed companies for market surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for short-listed securities.

## Important Dates

- November 30, 2026: Effective date for stage revisions, lower price bands, and securities moving out of LT-ASM.
- December 01, 2026: Reference date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- December 02, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and price band restrictions may impact liquidity and trading volumes in securities shortlisted under the LT-ASM framework.
