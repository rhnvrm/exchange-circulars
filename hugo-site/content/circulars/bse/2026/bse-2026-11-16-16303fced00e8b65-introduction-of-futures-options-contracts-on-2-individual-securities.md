## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on two individual securities within the Equity Derivatives segment, effective November 27, 2026. The underlying securities are Acutaas Chemicals Ltd and Apar Industries Ltd.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives
- **Underlying Securities:**
  1. Acutaas Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 543349)
  2. Apar Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 532259)
- **Contract Master File:** Will be available on the end of day of November 26, 2026, via extranet and the BSE India website.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of new underlying securities in the F&O segment as part of ongoing market expansion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their contract master files and trading systems ahead of the trading commencement date.

## Important Dates

- **Contract Master File Date:** November 26, 2026 (End of Day)
- **Trading Commencement Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market liquidity and provides participants with derivative instruments for risk management on Acutaas Chemicals Ltd and Apar Industries Ltd.
