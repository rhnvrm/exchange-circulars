## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective November 20, 2026. The notice details securities entering and exiting the framework, consolidated lists, and scrips classified under IBC due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for IRP under IBC are placed under ASM effective November 20, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the IBC Framework effective November 20, 2026 are listed in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) below 25% are detailed in Annexure IV.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of additional surveillance measures for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings under the IBC framework, in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading parameters and price bands for specific securities undergoing corporate insolvency resolution processes or non-promoter holding adjustments.
