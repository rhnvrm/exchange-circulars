## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Shashank Traders Ltd has changed its name to Cosmic Energy & Motors Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name in the BOLT Plus trading system will be updated effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Shashank Traders Ltd
- New Company Name: Cosmic Energy & Motors Limited
- Scrip Code: 540221
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): COSENER
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): COSENER
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of company name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name across exchange trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems and records with the new name and scrip ID.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact limited to updating records and display names in trading terminals.
