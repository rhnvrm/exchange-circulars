## Summary

BSE has sought clarification from Waaree Energies Ltd regarding recent news articles appearing in various media outlets/websites to verify their accuracy and inform investors. As of the notice date, the reply from the company is awaited.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification from Waaree Energies Ltd (Scrip Code: 544277) regarding news articles.
- Article source reference: https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/
- Reply is currently awaited from the company.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

[No regulatory changes introduced in this circular.]

## Compliance Requirements

- Waaree Energies Ltd is required to submit its clarification/reply to the exchange regarding the referenced news article.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct market impact; informational notice regarding pending clarification from the listed entity.
