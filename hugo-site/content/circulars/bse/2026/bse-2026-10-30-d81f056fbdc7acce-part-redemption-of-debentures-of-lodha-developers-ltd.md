## Summary

BSE has informed trading members regarding the part redemption of debentures of Lodha Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 977293, ISIN: INE670K07349), specifying the actual reduced face value of Rs. 48,724 per debenture effective from November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lodha Developers Ltd
- Scrip Code: 977293
- ISIN: INE670K07349
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 48,724/- per Debenture
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to trade the debentures with the revised face value effective November 02, 2026.
