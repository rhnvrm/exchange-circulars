## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the record dates and ex-dates for part redemption and payment of interest/principal on non-convertible debentures, bonds, and PTCs issued by companies and trusts. Trading in these instruments will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Keertana Finserv Limited**: Scrip Code 975741 (INE0NES07113) - Reduced face value of Rs. 50,000/- per debenture. Ex-Date: 27/11/2026, Record Date: 28/11/2026.
- **VAJRA 018 TRUST**: Scrip Code 977524 (INE2QJS15015) - Reduced face value of Rs. 7,289,298.51 per PTC. Ex-Date: 27/11/2026, Record Date: 27/11/2026.
- **U.P. Power Corporation Limited**: Scrip Code 956150 (INE540P07178) - Reduced face value of Rs. 2,50,000/- per bond. Ex-Date: 30/11/2026, Record Date: 30/11/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notification for debt segment operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the reduced face values and respective record/ex-dates for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **27/11/2026**: Ex-Date for Keertana Finserv Limited and VAJRA 018 TRUST
- **28/11/2026**: Record Date for Keertana Finserv Limited
- **30/11/2026**: Ex-Date and Record Date for U.P. Power Corporation Limited

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and depositories to reflect reduced face values and process part redemption payouts.
