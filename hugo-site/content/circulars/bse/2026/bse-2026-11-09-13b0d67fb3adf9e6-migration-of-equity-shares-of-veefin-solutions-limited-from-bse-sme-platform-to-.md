## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the migration of equity shares of Veefin Solutions Limited from the BSE SME Platform to the BSE Mainboard Platform, effective Wednesday, November 11, 2026. The shares will be traded in the 'B' Group with scrip code 543931 and ISIN INE0Q0M01015.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 11, 2026.
- Company Name: Veefin Solutions Limited.
- Scrip Code: 543931; Scrip ID / BOLT Name: VEEFIN; ISIN: INE0Q0M01015.
- Trading Group: 'B' Group on Mainboard Platform.
- Total Securities: 25,539,417 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each (Distinctive numbers 1 to 25539417).
- Market Lot: 1 share (dematerialized form only).
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Bigshare Services Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are migrated from the SME platform to the Mainboard platform of BSE in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- Trading will continue exclusively in dematerialized form with a unit market lot.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems to reflect the migration of Veefin Solutions Limited from SME to Mainboard ('B' Group).
- Audited financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and shareholding pattern are enclosed as Annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026.
- Effective Date of Migration: November 11, 2026.
- Financial Year End: March 31.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members gain enhanced visibility and liquidity associated with the BSE Mainboard platform.
- Operations and settlement will proceed under standard Mainboard 'B' Group guidelines.
