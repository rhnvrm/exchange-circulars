## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The surveillance actions take effect from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created from November 13, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a market surveillance mechanism and not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on open positions and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Open Positions Reference Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact trading liquidity and leverage for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
