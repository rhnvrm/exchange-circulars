## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 759 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-759/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction Session Date: Thursday, November 19, 2026.
- Auction Offer Entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and Report of Accepted Auction Offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, November 20, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard settlement auction procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise utmost care while entering security codes and rates, as punching errors cannot be corrected.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: 19 Nov 2026
- Pay-in Date: 20 Nov 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process ensuring settlement completion for short-delivered securities.
