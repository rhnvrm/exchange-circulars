## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Ajwa Projects Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from Thursday, November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ajwa Projects Limited
- Scrip Code: 545016
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Notice No. 202611003-33 dated November 3, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading segment classification for Ajwa Projects Limited has been modified to normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and group classification for executing trades in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Moving from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment enables normal rolling settlement in the equity shares of Ajwa Projects Limited, potentially enhancing trading liquidity.
