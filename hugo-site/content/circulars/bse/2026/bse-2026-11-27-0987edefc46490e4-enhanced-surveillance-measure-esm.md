## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion and movement of securities under various stages of ESM effective from November 30, 2026, and December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Certain securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. December 01, 2026, on open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities continuing in ESM but moving to lower Stage I effective November 30, 2026, are listed in Annexure I. Consolidated list is in Annexure III.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. December 01, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 30, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM framework effective November 30, 2026, are given in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) involving 100% margin requirements, shifting to Trade-for-Trade settlement, price band restrictions, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions created for securities under ESM, and note changes in settlement segments and price bands.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band / Periodic Call Auction / movement out of ESM / moving to lower stage: November 30, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin / shift to Trade-for-Trade segment: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for impacted securities will be significantly affected due to 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions.
