## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and PTCs by various companies, with the record date and ex-date established for November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Progfin Private Limited**: ISIN INE0MYJ07120, reduced face value of Rs. 50,934.25 per debenture.
- **Duckett**: ISIN INE2ZJN15017, reduced face value of Rs. 858.03 per debenture.
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd**: Four ISINs (INE094I07049, INE094I07072, INE094I07080, INE094I07098) undergoing part redemption with respective reduced face values.
- **Record Date / Ex-Date**: November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that trading in the debentures and PTCs of the specified companies will proceed with reduced face values starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: November 11, 2026
- **Ex-Date**: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the debt instruments of the listed entities will receive part redemption payouts according to the schedule, and secondary market trading will reflect the updated reduced face values.
