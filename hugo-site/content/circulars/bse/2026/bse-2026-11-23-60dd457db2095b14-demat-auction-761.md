## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-761/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on November 23, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be conducted on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement where they short delivered such securities has been removed following the uniform membership structure implementation.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates standard operating procedures for demat auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- **23 Nov 2026**: Auction offer entry, matching, and statement generation.
- **25 Nov 2026**: Auction Pay-in at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Operational circular ensuring smooth settlement execution and clearing operations for short-delivery obligations.
