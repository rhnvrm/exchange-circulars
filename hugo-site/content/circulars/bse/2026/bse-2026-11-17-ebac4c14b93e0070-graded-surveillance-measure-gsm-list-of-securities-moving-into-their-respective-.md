## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261117-34 announcing the movement of certain securities into respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, IV) based on the surveillance framework.
- Surveillance actions include applicable margin rates of 100%, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Trading in Stage III and IV securities is permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 27, 2017, and subsequent notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensuring compliance with trade-to-trade settlement and deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading operations for securities moved into higher GSM stages due to mandatory trade-to-trade settlement and ASD requirements.
