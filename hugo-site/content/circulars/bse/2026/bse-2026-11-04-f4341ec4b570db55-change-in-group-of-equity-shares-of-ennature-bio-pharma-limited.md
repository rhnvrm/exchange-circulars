## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that trading in the equity shares of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited will be permitted under the 'B' group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ennature Bio Pharma Limited
- BSE Scrip Code: 545012
- Trading Group: 'B' group
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of the trading group for the company's equity shares on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants should take note of the group change and ensure internal systems and trading terminals are updated accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; standard administrative adjustment for trading participants.
