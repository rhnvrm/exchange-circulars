## Summary

An open offer has been made by Mr. Ghanshyam Hansrajani along with person acting in concert Mr. Umesh Hansrajani to acquire up to 12,57,048 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the voting share capital) of Mayur Leather Products Limited at an offer price of Rs. 27.92 per share payable in cash.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer & PAC:** Mr. Ghanshyam Hansrajani (Acquirer) and Mr. Umesh Hansrajani (PAC).
- **Target Company:** Mayur Leather Products Limited (MLPL).
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 27.92 per Equity Share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 12,57,048 Equity Shares (26.00% of fully paid-up equity and voting share capital).
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, December 02, 2026, to Tuesday, December 15, 2026.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Pursuant to Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) and in compliance with Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and revised guidelines of SEBI circulars on mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window mechanism for tender offers under takeover, buyback, and delisting as per SEBI guidelines.
- Tenders by public shareholders during the acquisition window from December 02, 2026, to December 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** December 02, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** December 15, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public shareholders of Mayur Leather Products Limited an exit opportunity through the stock exchange acquisition window at the specified offer price of Rs. 27.92 per share, accompanied by a change in control and management.
