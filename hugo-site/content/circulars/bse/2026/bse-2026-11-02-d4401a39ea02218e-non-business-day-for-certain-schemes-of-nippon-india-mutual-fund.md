## Summary

Nippon India Mutual Fund has declared Non-Business Days for specific schemes due to market closures. Transactions such as subscriptions, redemptions, switches, SIP, STP, and SWP will not be permitted on these dates through the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Non-Business Day for Nippon India Japan Equity Fund is November 23, 2026.
- Non-Business Day for Nippon India US Equity Opportunities Fund is November 26, 2026.
- Affected transactions include subscription, redemption, switch, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Restricted platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must ensure no orders or transactions are submitted for the affected schemes on their respective non-business days.

## Important Dates

- November 2, 2026: Notice issuance date
- November 23, 2026: Non-Business Day for Nippon India Japan Equity Fund
- November 26, 2026: Non-Business Day for Nippon India US Equity Opportunities Fund

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on processing transactions for the specified international/thematic mutual fund schemes via BSE StAR MF on the declared dates.
