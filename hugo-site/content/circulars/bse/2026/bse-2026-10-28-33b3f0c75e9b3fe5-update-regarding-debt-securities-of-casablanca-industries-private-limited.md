## Summary

BSE has issued a notice stating that the debt securities of Casablanca Industries Private Limited under specified scrip codes and ISINs will be available for trading with revised terms on account of debt restructuring, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20250917-20 dated September 17, 2025.
- Affected Scrip Code 976328 (ISIN: INE834U07027).
- Affected Scrip Code 976186 (ISIN: INE834U07019).
- Revised trading terms applicable from Thursday, October 29, 2026.
- Action arises from debt restructuring.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading terms for specific debt securities of Casablanca Industries Private Limited have been updated following debt restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the revised scrip codes, ISINs, and updated trading terms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited impact affecting trading members and investors holding the specific debt instruments of Casablanca Industries Private Limited.
