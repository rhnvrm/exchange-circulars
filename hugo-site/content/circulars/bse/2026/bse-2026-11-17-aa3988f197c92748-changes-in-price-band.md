## Summary

BSE has notified the revision of existing price bands for 16 securities under its surveillance mechanism, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 16 listed securities on the BSE equity segment.
- Revised price limits range across 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% levels.
- Effective date of implementation is November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Surveillance measures applied to update price restriction bands for specified scrips as per regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and ensure compliance with the revised price bands for orders placed in the affected scrips starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Price movement in the shortlisted securities will be restricted within the newly assigned percentage bands, mitigating extreme volatility.
