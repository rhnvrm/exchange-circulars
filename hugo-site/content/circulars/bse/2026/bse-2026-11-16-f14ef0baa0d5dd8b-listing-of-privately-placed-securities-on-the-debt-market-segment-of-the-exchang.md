## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers (CPs) issued by Tata Steel Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, and Muthoot Fincorp Limited on the debt market segment effective November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Tata Steel Limited**: Issued 30,000 CPs (Scrip Code: 732952, ISIN: INE081A14HV6, Rate: 6.70% p.a., Maturing: 11/12/2026) and 20,000 CPs (Scrip Code: 732953, ISIN: INE081A14HW4, Rate: 6.95% p.a., Maturing: 28/12/2026).
- **Bajaj Financial Securities Limited**: Issued 8,400 CPs (Scrip Code: 732954, ISIN: INE01C314IW2, Rate: 7.90% p.a., Maturing: 12/02/2027) and 4,000 CPs (Scrip Code: 732955, ISIN: INE01C314IY8, Rate: 7.90% p.a., Maturing: 05/01/2027).
- **Muthoot Fincorp Limited**: Issued 7,000 CPs (Scrip Code: 732956, ISIN: INE549K14DA4, Rate: 8.85% p.a.).
- **Trading Parameters**: Traded in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof; tick size is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine commercial paper listing under existing debt segment regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with standard dematerialised settlement and market lot denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date**: November 16, 2026
- **Allotment Date**: November 13, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from December 11, 2026, to February 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for the listed commercial papers on the BSE debt market segment.
