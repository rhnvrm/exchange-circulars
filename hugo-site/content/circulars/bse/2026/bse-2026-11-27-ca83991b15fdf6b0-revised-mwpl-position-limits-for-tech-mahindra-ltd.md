## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for stock derivatives contracts of Tech Mahindra Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532755) in pursuance of notice no. 20260922-54 dated September 22, 2026. The revised limits are applicable effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: Tech Mahindra Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532755
- Market Wide Limit (No. of Shares): 19,12,07,586
- Scheme of Mutual Fund: 1,91,19,600
- TM (Proprietary, FPI Cat II - Other than Individuals): 3,82,40,400
- TM (Proprietary + Client) / FPI (Cat I) / MF: 5,73,61,200

## Regulatory Changes

Stock derivatives position limits for Tech Mahindra Ltd. have been updated across market-wide limits, mutual funds, trading members, and FPI categories.

## Compliance Requirements

Stockbrokers, FPIs, Mutual Funds, and trading participants must monitor and ensure adherence to the updated position limits for Tech Mahindra Ltd. derivatives contracts.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update ensuring proper risk management and compliance with derivatives position thresholds for market participants.
