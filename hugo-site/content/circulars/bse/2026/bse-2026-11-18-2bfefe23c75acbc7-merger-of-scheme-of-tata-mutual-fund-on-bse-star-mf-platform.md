## Summary

TATA Mutual Fund has announced the merger of TATA NIFTY G-SEC DEC 2026 INDEX FUND (Transferor Scheme) into TATA SHORT TERM FUND (Transferee Scheme). The merger will be effective after the close of business hours on December 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Transferor Scheme:** TATA NIFTY G-SEC DEC 2026 INDEX FUND
- **Transferee Scheme:** TATA SHORT TERM FUND
- **Effective Date:** Close of business hours on December 18, 2026.
- **Exit Load Free Window:** Investors who do not wish to continue with the proposed merger may redeem or switch their investments to any other scheme of TATA Mutual Fund without exit load from November 19, 2026, to December 18, 2026.
- **Subscription Suspension:** Fresh subscriptions, lumpsum, additional investments, switch-ins, and SIP/XSIP registrations under the Transferor Scheme were suspended after the cut-off time on November 16, 2026.
- **Systematic Registrations Migration:** Existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP registrations under the Transferor Scheme will be automatically migrated to the corresponding plan/option of the Transferee Scheme after business hours on December 18, 2026, unless positive consent to continue or cancellation requests are submitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. This is a corporate action/fund scheme merger notice issued by TATA Mutual Fund through BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and trading/demat participants must take note of the suspension of fresh subscriptions and restrictions on transactions in the Transferor Scheme.
- Facilitate redemption or switch requests without exit load during the specified window (November 19, 2026, to December 18, 2026).
- Ensure proper migration and updating of systematic investment facilities (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) as per AMC guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **November 16, 2026:** Suspension of fresh subscriptions and SIP/XSIP registrations in the Transferor Scheme.
- **November 19, 2026 to December 18, 2026:** Exit-load free redemption and switch window for dissenting investors.
- **December 18, 2026 (Close of business hours):** Effective date of scheme merger and cessation of Transferor Scheme availability on BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the Transferor Scheme face scheme transition. Unitholders have the option to exit without exit load or have their investments seamlessly migrated to the Transferee Scheme. Trading members need to update operational workflows for systematic registrations and transaction processing.
