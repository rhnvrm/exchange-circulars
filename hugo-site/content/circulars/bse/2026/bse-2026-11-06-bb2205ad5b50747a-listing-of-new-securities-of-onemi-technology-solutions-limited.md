## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of OnEMI Technology Solutions Limited (Scrip Code: 544754) issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from November 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details**: 2,64,93,882 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 313.11/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 314.11/-).
- **Scrip Code**: 544754
- **ISIN**: INE12F801023
- **Distribution Nos.**: 178114103 to 204607984
- **Date of Allotment**: 23/10/2026
- **Ranking**: Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Lock-in Details**: Entire block of 2,64,93,882 shares (Distinctive Nos. 178114103 - 204607984) is locked in up to 16/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in period and ISIN details for settlement and trading.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: October 23, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date**: November 8, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: May 16, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital of OnEMI Technology Solutions Limited by over 2.64 crore shares, expanding liquidity upon lock-in expiry while ensuring near-term stability via statutory lock-in restrictions.
