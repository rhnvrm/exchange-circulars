## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria or moving across stages are subject to specific trading and margin restrictions effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 30, 2026, on all open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. November 27, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 27, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 30, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlist levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures, including mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction mechanisms for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) on open and new positions for securities listed under Annexure I, and manage settlement shifts accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Stage Movement / Effective Date for Stage Shifts: November 27, 2026
- Margin and Trade-for-Trade Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for impacted scrips will be significantly affected due to 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction rules.
