## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple entities on the debt market segment effective October 28, 2026. The listed securities will trade in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Key Points

- **DSP Finance Private Limited**: 4,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732843, ISIN: INE422H14750) maturing on 25/01/2027 at 8.45% interest.
- **Time Technoplast Limited**: 200 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732844, ISIN: INE508G14JO6) maturing on 25/02/2027 at 7.8% interest.
- **Northern Arc Capital Ltd.**: 2,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732845, ISIN: INE850M14DB0) maturing on 06/04/2027 at 8.90% interest.
- **Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited**: 1,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732846, ISIN: INE918K14DZ8) maturing on 23/07/2027 at 8.90% interest.
- **The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd.**: 800 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732847, ISIN: INE050A14AA0) maturing on 20/01/2027 at 8.50% interest.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard debt securities listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised form requirements, standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs, and a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**: October 27, 2026 and October 28, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date**: October 28, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from January 20, 2027 to July 23, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for privately placed commercial papers issued by the respective corporations.
