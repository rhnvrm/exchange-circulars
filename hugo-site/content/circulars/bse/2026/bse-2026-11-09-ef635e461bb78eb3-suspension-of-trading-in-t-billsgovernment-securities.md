## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific Government Securities and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) due to their upcoming redemption, effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading suspended for Sovereign Gold Bond 2019 Series (Scrip Code: 800289, ISIN: IN0020180314).
- Trading suspended for 5.74% GS 2026 (Scrip Code: 800583, ISIN: IN0020210186).
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in the aforesaid securities effective November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational suspension upon maturity of debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further transactions are executed in the specified T-bills and Government Securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process with negligible market impact, affecting only maturing instruments.
