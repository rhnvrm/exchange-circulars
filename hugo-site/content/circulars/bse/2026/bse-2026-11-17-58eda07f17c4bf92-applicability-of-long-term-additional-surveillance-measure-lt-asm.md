## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261117-31 regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on securities based on XBRL submissions. The notice outlines 100% margin requirements, higher stage movements, group transfers, and removals effective November 18 and November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 20, 2026 on all open positions as on November 19, 2026 and new positions created from November 20, 2026 onwards (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price band applicable w.e.f. November 18, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 20, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. November 18, 2026 with price bands reinstated (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions for securities listed in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price band applicability and movement to higher stages of LT-ASM for securities in Annexure I (Part B).
- Transfer of securities continuing in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS settlement groups effective November 20, 2026.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate 100% margin maintenance for affected securities starting November 20, 2026.
- Compliance with revised price bands and settlement group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS) for applicable scrips.
- For queries, members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **17 Nov 2026**: Notice issuance date.
- **18 Nov 2026**: Effective date for movement to higher stages, lower price bands, and securities moving out of LT-ASM framework.
- **19 Nov 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **20 Nov 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under Annexures I, II, and III will experience margin tightening, price band restrictions, or group reclassifications, impacting liquidity and trading operations for the specified scrips.
