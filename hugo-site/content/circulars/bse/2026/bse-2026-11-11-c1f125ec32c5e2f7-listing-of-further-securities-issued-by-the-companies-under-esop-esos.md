## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under ESOP / ESOS have been listed and admitted for trading with effect from Thursday, November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 14 companies issued further securities under ESOP / ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: November 12, 2026.
- Includes major companies such as Coforge Limited, Federal Bank Limited, Mphasis Limited, Wipro Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with applicable listing regulations and depository procedures for ESOP allotment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for the respective companies due to exercise of employee stock options.
