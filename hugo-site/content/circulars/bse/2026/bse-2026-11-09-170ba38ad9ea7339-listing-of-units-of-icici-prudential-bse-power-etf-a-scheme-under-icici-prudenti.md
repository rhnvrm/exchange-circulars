## Summary

Trading members are informed that units of ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF, an open-ended Index Exchange Traded Fund tracking the BSE Power Index, are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF
- **Mutual Fund:** ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545023
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** POWERIETF
- **ISIN:** INF109K1A724
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 71.77 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 04/11/2026
- **Market Lot:** 1 unit (traded in dematerialized form)
- **Group:** B

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Listing conducted in accordance with SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Units will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form in unit market lots (1 unit).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 4, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Date of Redemption:** December 30, 2099

## Impact Assessment

- Provides investors with an additional investment avenue tracking the BSE Power Index through exchange-traded funds.
