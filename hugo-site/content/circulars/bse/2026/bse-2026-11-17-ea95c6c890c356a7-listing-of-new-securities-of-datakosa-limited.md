## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 20,58,822 new equity shares of Datakosa Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 539122
- ISIN: INE104F01029
- Security Details: 20,58,822 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 7/- (Issue Price: Rs. 17/-).
- Allotment Date: October 8, 2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 21865571 to 23924392.
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 25, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the specified lock-in period and trading parameters for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 8, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 25, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading of preferential shares on the exchange while maintaining regulatory lock-in compliance.
