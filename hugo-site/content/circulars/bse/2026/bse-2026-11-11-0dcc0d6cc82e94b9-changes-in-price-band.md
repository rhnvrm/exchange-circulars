## Summary

BSE Limited has notified revisions to the existing price bands of 13 securities as part of its surveillance measures. The revised price bands take effect from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261111-29 dated November 11, 2026 issued by DOSS Surveillance Department.
- Total of 13 securities shortlisted for price band revision.
- Price bands revised to 2%, 5%, and 10% across various scrips.
- Effective date: November 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified equities to manage market volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance when placing orders for the affected securities starting November 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors dealing in the affected scrips (including Envair Electrodyne Ltd, Harshil Agrotech Ltd, Jagjanani Textiles Ltd, Jindal Capital Ltd, Makers Laboratories Ltd, Nivaka Fashions Ltd, Northlink Fiscal and Capital Services Ltd, OK Play India Ltd, Ovobel Foods Ltd, Piotex Industries Ltd, Rekvina Laboratories Ltd, Resonance Specialties Ltd, and Sungold Media and Entertainment Ltd) must operate within the newly established price limits.
