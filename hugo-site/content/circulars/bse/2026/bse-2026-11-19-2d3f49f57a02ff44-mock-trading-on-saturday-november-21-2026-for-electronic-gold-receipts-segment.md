## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test third-party or in-house trading applications under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, November 21, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- Purpose: Testing trading applications, ETI APIs, and exceptional market conditions (risk-reduction mode, trading halt, etc.).
- No margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or creation of rights and liabilities arising from mock trading.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 07:00 pm on the day of the session.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are encouraged to actively test their applications and submit feedback to BSE tech support or helpdesk by 07:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 21, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: November 21, 2026, by 07:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Purely optional/encouraged testing session with no financial or operational settlement implications.
