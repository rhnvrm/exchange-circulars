## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for multiple companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified entities starting from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to Commercial Papers of various corporate issuers including Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd., Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd., Capri Global Capital Limited, and others.
- Record and Ex-dates are scheduled between November 2, 2026, and November 13, 2026.
- Trading members must ensure adherence to trading restrictions to facilitate smooth redemption and settlement processes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice concerning debt instrument redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to execute transactions in the commercial papers of the listed companies mentioned in the circular as of their respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: November 2, 2026 to November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly market operations and prevents trading in commercial papers approaching maturity and redemption.
