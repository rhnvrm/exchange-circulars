## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund is launching its Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VC (114 Days) NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from November 05, 2026, to November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VC (114 Days) (Regular & Direct Plans across IDCW Payout, Growth, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Period: November 05, 2026, to November 17, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on November 17, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 18, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 18, 2026, for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: November 05, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 17, 2026 (up to 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for trading members participating in mutual fund NFO subscriptions on the BSE StAR MF platform.
