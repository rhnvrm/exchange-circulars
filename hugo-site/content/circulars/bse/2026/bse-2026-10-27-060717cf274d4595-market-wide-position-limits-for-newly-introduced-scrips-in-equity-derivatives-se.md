## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for newly introduced scrips in the equity derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261028-1
- Notice Date: 28 Oct 2026
- Category: Settlement/RMS
- Segment: Derivatives
- Department: BSECL
- Applies to newly introduced scrips in the equity derivatives segment.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment and monitoring of Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for newly added equity derivative contracts to maintain proper risk controls.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must monitor and adhere to the specified position limits for the newly introduced scrips in the derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly trading, risk management, and regulatory compliance for newly introduced derivative contracts.
