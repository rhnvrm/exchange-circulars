## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the equity shares of Wiscon Industries Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Wiscon Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 545010
- Scrip ID / Symbol: WISCON
- ISIN No.: INE0NZ501012
- Total Securities: 19,987,500 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 204/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 194/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited
- Market Maker: Shreni Shares Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members are informed that the securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines and will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on November 3, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.
- Lock-in details as per Annexure I and shareholding pattern as per Annexure II have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Wiscon Industries Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors while adhering to standard SME listing surveillance and trade-for-trade requirements.
