## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 469,179,280 new equity shares of Tuni Textile Mills Ltd (Scrip Code: 531411) issued on a rights basis. These shares will be available for trading effective from Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tuni Textile Mills Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 531411
- **ISIN:** INE560D01027
- **Security Details:** 469,179,280 equity shares of Rs. 1.00/- each issued at par on rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 132906001 to 602085280
- **Date of Allotment:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the listing and availability of the new equity shares for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing increases the paid-up capital base of Tuni Textile Mills Ltd through rights issue shares ranking pari-passu with existing shares, expanding liquidity and share count for market participants.
