## Summary

BSE has announced a revision in the price bands for 7 securities, effective from November 09, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Revised price bands apply to 7 scrips including Crestchem Ltd (10%), Laxmipati Engineering Works Ltd (5%), Naapbooks Ltd (5%), Optimus Finance Ltd (5%), Pashupati Cotspin Ltd (5%), Vantage Knowledge Academy Ltd (10%), and Yash Chemex Ltd (10%).
- Effective date of revised price bands: November 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- The Surveillance department of BSE has shortlisted securities for price band revisions to ensure orderly trading and curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the revised price bands and ensure trading systems are updated accordingly from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 08, 2026
- Effective Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in these scrips must operate within the new price band limits, which affects intraday volatility and order placement.
