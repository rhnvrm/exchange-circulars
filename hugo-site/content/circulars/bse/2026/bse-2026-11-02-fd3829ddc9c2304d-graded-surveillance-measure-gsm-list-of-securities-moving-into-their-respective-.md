## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 03, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher stages of the GSM framework.
- Effective date of implementation is November 03, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary across Stages I, II, III, and IV, ranging from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed for affected securities.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as per previous reference notices (dating from February 23, 2017 to November 17, 2023), enforcing enhanced margins, trade-for-trade execution, weekly trading restrictions, and ASD requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precautions while trading in these securities, ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement without netting off, and collect the required Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and margins from buyers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the affected securities will experience increased liquidity constraints due to trade-for-trade settlement, mandatory ASD requirements, and restricted trading frequencies for higher GSM stages.
