## Summary

 BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding process for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for November 09, 2026, conducted by the RBI. Trading members can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding platform: iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- Bid collection start date: November 07, 2026 from 10:00 AM onwards (24-hour availability).
- Bid collection end date (Direct Investor): November 08, 2026 till 5:00 PM.
- Bid collection end date (Members): November 09, 2026 till 8:00 AM.
- Auction Date: November 09, 2026.
- Settlement Date: November 11, 2026.
- Covers multiple SDL re-issues and new issues across states including Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, and Uttar Pradesh.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural notification under existing RBI and Exchange frameworks for SDL auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in non-competitive bidding for SDLs must ensure submissions are completed within the stipulated timeline via the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start: November 07, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid End: November 08, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Member Bid End: November 09, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: November 09, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth primary market participation in State Government Securities for eligible investors through the Exchange platform.
