## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 37,60,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each issued by Kabra Extrusion Technik Ltd. on a preferential basis, effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kabra Extrusion Technik Ltd. (Scrip Code: 524109)
- Number of Shares: 37,60,000 equity shares
- Issue Type: Preferential issue to Promoter and Non-Promoters
- Issue Price: Rs. 375/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 5/-, Premium: Rs. 370/-)
- ISIN: INE900B01029
- Effective Date of Trading: November 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in periods applicable to various tranches of the newly allotted shares as specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 02/10/2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: 30/11/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 29/05/2027, 30/05/2027, and 29/05/2028 depending on the tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving the listing of preferential shares with specified lock-in restrictions.
