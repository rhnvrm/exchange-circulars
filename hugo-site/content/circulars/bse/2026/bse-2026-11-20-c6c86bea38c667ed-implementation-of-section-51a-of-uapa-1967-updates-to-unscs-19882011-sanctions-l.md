## Summary

BSE has issued a notice communicating SEBI's communication regarding the implementation of Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, featuring amendments to 05 entries on the UNSC's 1988 (2011) Sanctions List.

## Key Points

- Amendments made to 05 entries in the UNSC's 1988(2011) Sanctions List (Taliban-linked).
- Intermediaries must adhere to the procedure outlined in the Central Government Order dated February 02, 2021.
- Mandatory scanning of all existing and future accounts against listed entities and individuals.
- De-listing requests must be submitted electronically to the Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA and Joint Secretary (UNP), MEA.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the UNSC Sanctions Lists under Section 51A of the UAPA, 1967, requiring securities market intermediaries to update their screening databases.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to listed individuals/entities.
- Follow clause 54 of SEBI AML/CFT Guidelines.
- Forward de-listing requests to jsctcr-mha@gov.in and jsunp@mea.gov.in.
- Regularly monitor the SEBI website for updates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

High compliance priority requiring immediate screening adjustments across market intermediaries to maintain regulatory conformity.
