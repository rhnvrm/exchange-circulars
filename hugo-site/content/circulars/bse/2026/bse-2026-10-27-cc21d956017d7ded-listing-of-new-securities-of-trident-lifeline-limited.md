## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Trident Lifeline Limited (Scrip Code - 543616) effective from October 28, 2026, following a preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Trident Lifeline Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543616
- **ISIN:** INE0MKA01014
- **Securities Issued:** 24,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 256/- (Issue Price: Rs. 266/- per share) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** 31/08/2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 11933001 to 11957000
- **Lock-in Details:** 24,000 shares locked-in up to 25/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and trading permissions of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 25, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard preferential allotment and warrant conversion listing with minor increase in paid-up equity share capital, having low overall market impact.
