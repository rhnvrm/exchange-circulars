## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Interactive Financial Services Limited has fixed the record date for its rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified record date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Interactive Financial Services Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539692
- **Rights Issue Ratio:** 17 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at par for every 2 Equity Shares held.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- per share payable on application.
- **Record Date:** 30/10/2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis From:** 30/10/2026 (DR- 746/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ensure transactions are executed on an ex-rights basis from 30/10/2026.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / Ex-Rights Date:** 30/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are entitled to the rights issue in the specified ratio. Trading in equity shares will reflect ex-rights pricing from the effective date.
