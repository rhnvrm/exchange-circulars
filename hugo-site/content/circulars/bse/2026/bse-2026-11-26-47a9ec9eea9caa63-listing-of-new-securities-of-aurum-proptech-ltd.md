## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 15,258 new equity shares of Aurum PropTech Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539289) resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis. Trading in these securities is permitted effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Aurum PropTech Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 539289
- **ISIN:** INE898S01029
- **Securities:** 15,258 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 5/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** Ranges include 72,855,614 to 72,863,134; 72,863,145 to 72,867,543; 72,867,544 to 72,868,593; and 72,868,594 to 72,870,881.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational listing notice for corporate action execution.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are informed of the availability of these shares for trading starting November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the capital increase and trading availability of the newly converted fully paid-up shares for Aurum PropTech Ltd.
