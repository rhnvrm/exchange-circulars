## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the schedule for client margin reporting and corresponding penalty dates for the Equity Segment for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for November 2026.
- Applies to trading members in the Equity Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure timely reporting of client margins as per the specified schedule to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Trade dates ranging from November 2, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- Corresponding margin reporting and penalty dates throughout November and early December 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification ensuring compliance with margin reporting timelines for clearing and settlement.
