## Summary

BSE has informed trading members of a change in the scrip ID for Max Earth Resources Ltd on the BOLT Plus system, updating from MAXERL to MAXEARTH effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 534563
- Company Name: Max Earth Resources Ltd
- Old Scrip ID: MAXERL
- New Scrip ID: MAXEARTH
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational identifier update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the new scrip ID in their trading systems and records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; affects only display identification on the BOLT Plus trading system.
