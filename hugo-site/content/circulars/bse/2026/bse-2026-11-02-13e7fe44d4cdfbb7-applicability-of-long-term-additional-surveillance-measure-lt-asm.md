## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and transfer to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS) effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 05, 2026 on all open positions as on November 04, 2026 and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands become applicable w.e.f. November 03, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 05, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 03, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is purely on account of market surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of stricter surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework, including mandatory 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, provided they are not subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and lower price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.
- Members can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band / Movement / Removal: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: November 05, 2026
- Reference Date for Open Positions: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will experience higher margin requirements, restricted price bands, or transfer to trade-for-trade segments, potentially impacting liquidity and short-term volatility.
