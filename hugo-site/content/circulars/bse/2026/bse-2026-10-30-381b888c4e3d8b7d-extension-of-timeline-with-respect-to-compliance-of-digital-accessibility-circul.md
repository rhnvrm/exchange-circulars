## Summary

BSE has issued a notice extending the timeline for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars based on SEBI's circular dated October 30, 2026. The deadline for appointing an IAAP Auditor, conducting digital platform accessibility audits, and remediating audit findings has been extended to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Extension granted for mandatory digital accessibility compliance by all Regulated Entities (REs).
- New deadline for audit appointment, execution, and remediation is January 31, 2027.
- Based on representations received and review of compliance status by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI circular ref. no. HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026, grants an extension modifying earlier deadlines under SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Trading Members and Regulated Entities must note the revised deadline and complete the appointment of IAAP Auditors, accessibility audits of digital platforms, and remediation of findings by January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026:** Extension circular issued.
- **January 31, 2027:** Extended deadline for IAAP Auditor appointment, accessibility audit, and remediation.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational relief and additional time for regulated entities to achieve full compliance with digital accessibility standards mandated under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
