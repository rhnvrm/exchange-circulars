## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) for various securities. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 09, 2026 on all open positions as on November 06, 2026 and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM framework and lower price band applicable w.e.f. November 05, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 09, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. November 05, 2026 (Annexure II); price bands reinstated.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition and revision of surveillance measures under the LT-ASM framework, affecting margin requirements, price bands, and trading group allocations for listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for applicable open and new positions, adhere to revised price bands, and note trading group transfers.

## Important Dates

- November 05, 2026: Effective date for higher stages, price band changes, and removal of securities from LT-ASM.
- November 06, 2026: Reference date for open positions subject to margin requirements.
- November 09, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin obligations and trading restrictions, potentially impacting liquidity and trading strategies for market participants.
