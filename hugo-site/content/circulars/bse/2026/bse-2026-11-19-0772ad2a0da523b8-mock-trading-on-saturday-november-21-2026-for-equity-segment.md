## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms can test their applications across various functionalities and exceptional market conditions, including call auction sessions, risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and block deals.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- No new BOLT TWS Release accompanying this session.
- Comprehensive schedule includes login, morning block deal window, continuous trading T+1 and T+0, special pre-open for IPO & re-listed scrips, periodic call auctions, and closing sessions.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 05:00 PM on the mock trading day.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational drill conducted by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively and submit feedback regarding the mock trading session via the designated contact numbers or email IDs by 05:00 PM on November 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Mock Trading Session Date: November 21, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: November 21, 2026 (05:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members an opportunity to test application robustness, order matching, and exceptional market condition handling without impacting live market operations.
