## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. The measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement to higher surveillance stages, revision of price bands, and transfer of specific securities to T, XT, MT, or TS groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective November 11, 2026, on all open positions as of November 09, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands become effective from November 08, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective November 11, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective November 08, 2026, provided in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated accordingly.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework as per previous notices to monitor market volatility and safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adhere to price band and group transfer restrictions for shortlisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- **November 08, 2026:** Lower price bands applicable / Securities moved to higher stages / Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework.
- **November 09, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **November 11, 2026:** 100% margins applicable / Transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective.

## Impact Assessment

Heightened margin requirements and trading restrictions will impact liquidity and trading volume for shortlisted securities, requiring careful risk and collateral management by market participants.
