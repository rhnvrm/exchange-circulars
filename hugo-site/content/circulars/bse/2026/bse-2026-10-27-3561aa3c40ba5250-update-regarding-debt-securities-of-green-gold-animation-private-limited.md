## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the update to the debt securities of Green Gold Animation Private Limited (Scrip Code: 974917), effective from October 28, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260909-23.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 974917
- **Existing Details:** ISIN INE05RZ07024, Maturity Date 15/09/2026, Scrip ID 7GAPL26, Scrip Long Name GAPL-7%-15-9-26-PVT
- **Revised Details:** ISIN INE05RZ07040, Maturity Date 15/09/2027, Scrip ID 7GAPL27, Scrip Long Name GAPL-7%-15-9-27-PVT
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of ISIN, maturity date, and scrip identifier nomenclature for the listed non-convertible debt securities of the issuer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the revised terms and update their trading and back-office systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Revised Maturity Date:** September 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for participants dealing in the debt securities of Green Gold Animation Private Limited.
