## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Infosys Ltd (INFY) on account of an interim dividend of Rs. 26.00/- per equity share for the financial year 2026-27, with the record date set for November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Infosys Ltd (Derivative Asset Code: INFY, Scrip Code: 500209).
- Corporate Action: Interim Dividend of Rs. 26.00/- per equity share.
- Record Date: November 03, 2026.
- Ex-Date: November 03, 2026.
- Effective Date for Adjustments: November 02, 2026 (end of day generation).

## Regulatory Changes

- Necessary adjustments for all available F&O contracts on INFY are executed in pursuance of Exchange notice number 20180710-26 and SEBI Master circular No SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the strike price adjustments for options and baseline reference rate adjustments for futures effective from November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Adjustment Date (F&O contracts): November 02, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Options contracts strike prices will be reduced by the full dividend amount (Rs. 26.00/-).
- Futures contracts base prices will be adjusted by deducting the dividend amount from the daily mark-to-market settlement price of November 02, 2026.
