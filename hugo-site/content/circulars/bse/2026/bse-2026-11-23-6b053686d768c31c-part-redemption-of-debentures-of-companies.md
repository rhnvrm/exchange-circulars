## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and fixation of record dates for Adani Airport Holdings Limited and SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited. Trading in these debentures will continue with reduced face value.

## Key Points

- **Adani Airport Holdings Limited**: Scrip Code 976065 (ISIN: INE0GCN07047), 9.35% NCDs with a reduced face value of Rs. 48,993/- per debenture.
- **SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited**: Scrip Code 978157 (ISIN: INE996U07545), 12.2% NCDs with a reduced face value of Rs. 41,666.69 per debenture.
- Settlement DR: 763/2026-2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action notice regarding debt securities redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the revised face values and ensure settlements are carried out in accordance with the specified record date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: 25/11/2026
- **Ex-Date**: 25/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the debentures will receive part redemption payments, and subsequent trading will occur at the reduced face value.
