## Summary

Bandhan Mutual Fund has announced the merger of **Bandhan CRISIL IBX 90:10 SDL Plus Gilt November 2026 Index Fund** (Transferor Scheme) into **Bandhan Short Term Fund** (Transferee Scheme). The merger is effective after business hours on November 30, 2026. Investors can redeem or switch without exit load until November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Transferor Scheme:** Bandhan CRISIL IBX 90:10 SDL Plus Gilt November 2026 Index Fund
- **Transferee Scheme:** Bandhan Short Term Fund
- **Effective Date:** Close of business hours on November 30, 2026.
- **Exit Load Waiver:** Redemption or switch to other schemes without exit load available from October 30, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- **Systematic Facilities:** Existing SIP/STP/SWP will continue into the Transferee scheme upon positive consent from investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme merger processed in accordance with regulatory guidelines and AMC notice-cum-addendum.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the transaction cut-off timelines and cessation of transferor scheme availability on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Investors wishing to discontinue systematic investment facilities must initiate cancellation within prescribed timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Demat Subscription Cut-off:** November 20, 2026
- **Non-Demat Subscription Cut-off:** November 30, 2026
- **Redemption / Switch-out Window (Exit Load Waiver):** October 30, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Merger:** November 30, 2026 (after business hours)

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding units in the transferor scheme are provided an exit load-free window to redeem or switch. Systematic investment plans will transition automatically upon positive investor consent, ensuring operational continuity.
