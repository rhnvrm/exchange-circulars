## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of select securities under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II, implementing stricter margin requirements starting November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract a 50% applicable margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract a 100% applicable margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions on specified securities pursuant to the revised ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain the mandated margin rates (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for affected scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the securities listed in Annexure I.
