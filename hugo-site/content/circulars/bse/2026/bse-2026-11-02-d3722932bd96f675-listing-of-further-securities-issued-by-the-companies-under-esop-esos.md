## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 12 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS). These securities become effective for trading starting Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 12 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading of these securities: November 3, 2026.
- Companies include major firms such as ICICI Bank, Infosys, Wipro, Birlasoft, Global Health, HDB Financial Services, Home First Finance, India Shelter Finance, Kellton Tech Solutions, Metropolis Healthcare, Ravindra Energy, and Triveni Turbine.
- Lock-in status is marked as 'NA' for most companies, with Metropolis Healthcare noting lock-in as per Annexure I.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate listing action under existing capital market operations and listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with BSE listing operational guidelines and verify ISIN and distinctive number ranges.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minor increase in paid-up equity share capital for the respective companies due to ESOP exercises.
- No significant market impact or volatility expected from routine ESOP listings.
