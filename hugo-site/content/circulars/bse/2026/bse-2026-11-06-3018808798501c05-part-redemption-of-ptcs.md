## Summary

Catalyst Trusteeship Ltd has announced the record date for part redemption and interest payment of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in the affected PTCs will occur with a reduced face value effective from the ex-date.

## Key Points

- **Stellar 1.5 2024 (Scrip Code: 976595, ISIN: INE1UUF15013)**: Reduced face value of Rs. 0.16 per debenture.
- **Ariel Trust 2026 (Scrip Code: 977938, ISIN: INE2XEV15016)**: Reduced face value of Rs. 0.95 per debenture.
- **Record Date & Ex-Date**: November 9, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face value and ensure transactions are executed accordingly from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: November 9, 2026
- **Ex-Date**: November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the respective PTCs will receive part redemption payouts according to the schedule, with subsequent trading adjusting to the lower face value.
