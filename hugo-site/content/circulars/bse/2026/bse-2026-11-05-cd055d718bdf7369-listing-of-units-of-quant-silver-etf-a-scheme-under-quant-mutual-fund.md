## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of quant Silver ETF under Quant Mutual Fund, effective from Friday, November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of Mutual Fund: Quant Mutual Fund
- Investment Manager: Quant Money Managers Limited
- Scrip Code: 545022
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SILVER001
- ISIN: INF966L01EI5
- Number of Units: 28,70,51,994 Units
- Group: E (Market Lot: 1 Unit)
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Market Makers: Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd, Sushil Financial Services Private Limited, Amrapali Capital & Finance Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Units will be traded only in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in unit market lot (one unit) in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: November 6, 2026
- Notice Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides investors with an additional trading venue and liquidity instrument for silver-backed ETFs on the exchange.
