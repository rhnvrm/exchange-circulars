## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by several financial institutions, effective November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bajaj Financial Securities Limited**: 2,000 units of Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732870, Scrip ID: BFSL021126, ISIN: INE01C314IQ4) issued at Rs. 4,90,105.00 with 8.01% interest maturing on 02/02/2027.
- **Navi Finserv Limited**: 200 units (Scrip Code: 732871, Scrip ID: NFL021126, ISIN: INE342T14II7) at 9.45% interest maturing on 02/02/2027; and 360 units (Scrip Code: 732872, Scrip ID: NFL21126, ISIN: INE342T14IJ5) at 9.40% interest maturing on 25/03/2027.
- **Indel Money Limited**: 500 units of Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732873, Scrip ID: IML021126, ISIN: INE0BUS14110) at 10% interest maturing on 30/04/2027.
- **Northern Arc Capital Ltd.**: 1,000 units of Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732874, Scrip ID: NACL31126, ISIN: INE850M14DE4) at 8.95% interest maturing on 30/04/2027.
- **Trading Parameters**: Securities will trade only in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the specified ISIN numbers, standard denominations, and dematerialised holding requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**: November 2, 2026 and November 3, 2026
- **Redemption / Maturity Dates**: February 2, 2027, March 25, 2027, and April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides formal exchange listing and secondary market trading availability for the newly issued commercial papers on the BSE Debt Segment.
