## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of JINDAL CAPITAL LIMITED effective from November 17, 2026. The trading period for renunciation runs from November 17, 2026 to November 26, 2026, and the rights issue will open from November 17, 2026 to December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JINDAL CAPITAL LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751157
- Scrip ID: JINDCAP-RE
- ISIN: INE356F20017
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Renunciation Trading Period: November 17, 2026 to November 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: November 17, 2026 to December 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Dealings in Rights Entitlements (REs) permitted on the Exchange.
- Settled on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes / bills must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before closure.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Rights Entitlements Trading / Renunciation Start Date: November 17, 2026
- Rights Entitlements Trading / Renunciation End Date: November 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: November 17, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares as of the record date receive REs which can be traded or renounced during the specified window.
- Investors must exercise or renounce REs before the deadline to avoid lapse and extinguishment.
