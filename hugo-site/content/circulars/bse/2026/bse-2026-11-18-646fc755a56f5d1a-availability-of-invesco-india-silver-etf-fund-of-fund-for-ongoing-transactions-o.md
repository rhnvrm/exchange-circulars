## Summary

BSE has announced that pursuant to Notice No. 20261117-42 dated November 17, 2026, the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Reopening of Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- Covers 6 plans across Regular and Direct options (Growth and IDCW variants).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for eligible schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and members with continued access to ongoing transactions and systematic investment facilities in the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund.
