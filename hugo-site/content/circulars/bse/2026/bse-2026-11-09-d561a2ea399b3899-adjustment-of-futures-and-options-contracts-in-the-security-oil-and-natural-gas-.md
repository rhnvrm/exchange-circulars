## Summary

BSE has announced necessary adjustments for all available Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd (ONGC) following the company's declaration of an interim dividend of Rs. 8.00 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- **Underlying Security:** Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd (Derivative Asset Code: ONGC, Equity Scrip Code: 500312).
- **Corporate Action:** Interim dividend of Rs. 8.00/- per equity share.
- **Record Date:** November 12, 2026.
- **Ex-Date:** November 12, 2026.
- **Adjustment Execution Date:** November 11, 2026 (end of day).

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments executed in line with Exchange notice number 20180710-26 and SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135 dated December 16, 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Options Contracts:** The full value of the interim dividend (Rs. 8.00/-) is deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of November 11, 2026. Adjusted strike prices become effective for trading on November 12, 2026.
- **Futures Contracts:** The adjusted futures price is calculated as the reference rate (daily mark-to-market settlement price on November 11, 2026) minus the dividend amount (Rs. 8.00/-). This adjusted rate acts as the base rate/previous close for trading on November 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **November 11, 2026:** Contract adjustments calculated at the end of trading hours.
- **November 12, 2026:** Ex-date, record date, and trading commencement date with adjusted strike prices and futures base rates.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants holding derivatives positions in ONGC must factor in the strike price reductions and adjusted futures base prices for positions carried forward to November 12, 2026.
