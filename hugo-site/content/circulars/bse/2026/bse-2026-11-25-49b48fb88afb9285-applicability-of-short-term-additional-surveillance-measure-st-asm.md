## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Surveillance action is purely for market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.
- Framework works in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- **ST-ASM Stage I:** Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective November 27, 2026, on all open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- **ST-ASM Stage II:** Applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective November 27, 2026, on all open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the affected securities will face increased margin requirements (up to 100%), which may impact liquidity and leverage for these scrips.
