## Summary

Vellora Impact Limited has announced a rights issue of equity shares, setting the record date as November 25, 2026. Eligible shareholders will receive 25 equity shares of Rs. 10 each at par for every 2 existing equity shares held.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vellora Impact Limited (Scrip Code: 531257)
- Record Date: November 25, 2026
- Ex-Right Basis Date: November 25, 2026
- Rights Ratio: 25 equity shares for every 2 equity shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/- per share (payable in full on application)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants must take note of the record date and ex-rights settlement mechanics.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Record Date: November 25, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders holding shares as of the record date are entitled to the rights issue, leading to price adjustment on the ex-rights date.
