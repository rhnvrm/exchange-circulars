## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the revised trading and settlement schedule for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment applicable for the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026. The notice outlines the settlement numbers, trading dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, auction settlement details, and specific settlement timings.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement dates for EGR segment transactions throughout November 2026.
- Details special pay-in and pay-out timings for specific settlement dates (e.g., DR-158, DR-159, DR-160, DR-161, DR-170, DR-171).
- Specifies depository and bank instruction deadlines for members.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable - Routine operational schedule update]

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and clearing members must submit pay-in instructions to depositories and banks latest by the specified deadlines (e.g., 01:50 p.m. or 04:50 p.m. depending on the batch).
- Funds and securities pay-out will be affected by the designated times.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: November 01, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- First Settlement Date (DR-154): Trading on 02-11-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 03-11-2026
- Last Settlement Date (DR-175): Trading on 30-11-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 01-12-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational execution and clear timelines for trading participants in the EGR segment during November 2026.
