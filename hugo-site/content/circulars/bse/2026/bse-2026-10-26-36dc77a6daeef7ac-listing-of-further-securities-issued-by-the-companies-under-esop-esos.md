## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 10 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Ownership Scheme (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from Tuesday, October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Total Companies:** 10 listed companies issuing shares under ESOP/ESOS
- **Notable Issuers:** ITC Limited (1,395,500 shares), ITC Hotels Limited (131,990 shares), ICICI Bank Limited (24,096 shares), HDFC Asset Management Company Limited (17,980 shares), and others.

### Issued Securities Details

1. **Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited:** 34,106 shares (ISIN: INE404A01024, Scrip Code: 543374)
2. **D.B.Corp Limited:** 16,503 shares (ISIN: INE950I01011, Scrip Code: 533151)
3. **EPL Limited:** 35,292 shares (ISIN: INE255A01020, Scrip Code: 500135)
4. **HDFC Asset Management Company Limited:** 17,980 shares (ISIN: INE127D01025, Scrip Code: 541729)
5. **ICICI Bank Limited:** 24,096 shares (ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip Code: 532174)
6. **ITC Hotels Limited:** 131,990 shares (ISIN: INE379A01028, Scrip Code: 544325)
7. **ITC Limited:** 1,395,500 shares (ISIN: INE154A01025, Scrip Code: 500875)
8. **Mufin Green Finance Limited:** 39,604 shares (ISIN: INE08KJ01020, Scrip Code: 542774)
9. **NAVA Limited:** 23,550 shares (ISIN: INE725A01030, Scrip Code: 513023)
10. **Oracle Financial Services Software Limited:** 34,605 shares (ISIN: INE881D01027, Scrip Code: 532466)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine capital change notification reflecting equity expansion due to employee stock option exercises.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the admission of these new shares for trading. Lock-in requirements apply as specified for respective company annexures (e.g., ITC Hotels).

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion resulting from ESOP/ESOS exercises. The impact on market liquidity and share capital is marginal for all participating companies.
