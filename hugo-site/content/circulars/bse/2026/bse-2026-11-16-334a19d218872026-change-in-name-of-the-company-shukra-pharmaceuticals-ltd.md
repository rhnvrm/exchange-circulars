## Summary

Shukra Pharmaceuticals Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the change of its company name. Effective November 20, 2026, the company will be known as Shukra Medtech Limited on the BOLT Plus system.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Shukra Pharmaceuticals Ltd
- New Company Name: Shukra Medtech Limited
- Scrip Code: 524632
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: SHUKRAMED
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: SHUKRAMED
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of company name and scrip identifiers across exchange records and trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and records with the new name and scrip ID for all transactions effective November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with minimal impact; affects only scrip identification and display names on the exchange.
