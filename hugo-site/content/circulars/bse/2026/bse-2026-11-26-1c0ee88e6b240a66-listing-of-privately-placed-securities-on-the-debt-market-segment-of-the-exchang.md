## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by various corporate entities on the debt market segment effective November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers issued by 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, and Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited have been admitted to trading.
- Securities trade in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine debt securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to ensure trading occurs in dematerialised form under the designated ISIN numbers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: November 25, 2026 and November 26, 2026.
- Redemption Dates: Between January 2027 and August 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for the newly issued commercial papers.
