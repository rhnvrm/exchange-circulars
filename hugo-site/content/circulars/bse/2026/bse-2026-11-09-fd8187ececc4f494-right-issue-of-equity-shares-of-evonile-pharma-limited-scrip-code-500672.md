## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Evonile Pharma Limited has fixed the record date for its Rights Issue of Equity Shares. Transactions in the equity shares of the company will be on an Ex-Rights basis effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Evonile Pharma Limited (Scrip Code: 500672)
- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From: November 11, 2026
- Rights Issue Ratio: 5 (Five) Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 855/- per share for every 12 (Twelve) Equity Shares held
- Issue Price Details: Total price of Rs. 860/- per share, payable as Rs. 645/- per share on application and Rs. 215/- per share on first and final call
- Department Reference: Listing Operations (DR- 754/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

Standard corporate action processing and ex-rights settlement execution under BSE listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update trading systems and take note of the record date, ex-rights date, and partly-paid payment structure for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts existing shareholders of Evonile Pharma Limited regarding rights entitlement, capital restructuring, and adjustment of share prices on an ex-rights basis.
