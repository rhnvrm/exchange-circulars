## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd (BSECL) has issued a circular regarding the reporting mechanism for currency derivative contracts involving INR, following SEBI's revision lowering the threshold for positions without underlying exposure from USD 100 million to a combined limit of USD 5 million across all exchanges.

## Key Points

- The threshold for positions without underlying exposure in INR currency derivatives has been lowered to a combined limit of USD 5 million.
- Clearing members of the currency derivative segment must report client details for positions exceeding USD 5 million to designate an Authorized Dealer/Custodian.
- A confirmation from an AD-1 Bank must be submitted to the Clearing Corporation.
- File formats for the AD-1 Bank letter (Annexure 1), email details (Annexure 2), and position details provided by BSECL (Annexure 3) must be strictly adhered to.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lowering of the position threshold without underlying exposure in INR currency derivatives from USD 100 million to USD 5 million (referencing SEBI circular dated November 05, 2026, and BSECL circular dated November 12, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must identify clients holding or wishing to take positions beyond USD 5 million (or equivalent) in INR contracts across all exchanges.
- Designate an Authorized Dealer/Custodian and submit the requisite AD-1 Bank confirmation.
- Send email details as per Annexure 2 format and utilize Annexure 1 and Annexure 3 formats for documentation and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and clients trading in currency derivatives involving INR face significantly tighter exposure limits and increased compliance reporting burdens to ensure underlying support or proper AD-1 bank authorization for positions exceeding USD 5 million.
