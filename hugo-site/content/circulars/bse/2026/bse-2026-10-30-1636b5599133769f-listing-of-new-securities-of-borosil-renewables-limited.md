## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 53,38,840 new equity shares of Borosil Renewables Limited (Scrip Code: 502219) with effect from November 2, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Borosil Renewables Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 502219
- **ISIN:** INE666D01022
- **Number of Shares:** 53,38,840 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 530/- per share (including premium of Rs. 529/-)
- **Allottees:** Non-Promoters on a preferential basis
- **Date of Allotment:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 2, 2026
- **Lock-in Period:** Shares are locked-in up to May 6, 2027 (Distinctive Nos. 146807150 to 152145989)
- **Ranking:** These new equity shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing of new securities and lock-in details.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 6, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing resulting in an increase in the paid-up equity capital of Borosil Renewables Limited due to warrant conversion.
