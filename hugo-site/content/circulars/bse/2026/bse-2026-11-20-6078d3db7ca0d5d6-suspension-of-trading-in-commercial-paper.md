## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers for companies that have fixed record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of specified companies with fixed record dates.
- Affected companies include Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited, L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited, and SG Finserve Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must restrict dealing in the commercial papers of the listed entities in accordance with the specified record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20/11/2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 23/11/2026 to 26/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market trading in specific commercial papers during their redemption processing.
