## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund of AlphaGrep Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective from November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** AlphaGrep Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund
- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP
- **Eligible Plans & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF2VOA30045
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF2VOA30060
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF2VOA30052
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF2VOA30011
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF2VOA30037
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF2VOA30029

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors using the BSE StAR MF platform can facilitate transactions for investors in the aforementioned schemes starting from the effective date in accordance with existing platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date for Transactions:** November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions, SIPs, and SWPs for the various plans of Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
