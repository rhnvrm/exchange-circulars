## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 12 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 12.
- Effective date for trading: November 18, 2026.
- Companies include Eicher Motors Limited, HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited, Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited, and others.
- All listed securities are issued under ESOP / ESOS with no lock-in requirement (NA).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with standard BSE listing and trading operational guidelines for ESOP issuances.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving the expansion of equity capital through employee stock options, with negligible immediate market impact.
