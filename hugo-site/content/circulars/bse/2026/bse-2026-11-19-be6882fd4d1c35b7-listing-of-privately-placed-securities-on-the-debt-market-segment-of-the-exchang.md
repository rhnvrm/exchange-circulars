## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by several companies on the debt market segment effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Sharekhan Limited**: Issued 10,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732978, ISIN: INE211H14BK0) at an issue price of Rs. 4,89,624.00, maturing on February 17, 2027, with an interest rate of 8.50% p.a.
- **Manappuram Finance Limited (Tranche 1)**: Issued 8,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732977, ISIN: INE522D14QN1) at an issue price of Rs. 4,89,676.50, maturing on February 16, 2027, with an interest rate of 8.55% p.a.
- **Manappuram Finance Limited (Tranche 2)**: Issued 1,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732979, ISIN: INE522D14QO9) at an issue price of Rs. 4,91,735.50, maturing on January 29, 2027, with an interest rate of 8.52% p.a.
- **Vivriti Capital Limited (Tranche 1)**: Issued 200 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732980, ISIN: INE0P1414311) at an issue price of Rs. 4,78,751.50, maturing on May 17, 2027, with an interest rate of 9.00% p.a.
- **Vivriti Capital Limited (Tranche 2)**: Issued 360 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732981, ISIN: INE0P1414329) at an issue price of Rs. 4,87,264.50, maturing on March 4, 2027, with an interest rate of 9.00% p.a.
- **Trading Parameters**: Securities are traded in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paisa.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate debt listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading rules and denomination sizes for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: November 19, 2026
- **Allotment Date**: November 18, 2026
- **Redemption / Interest Payment Dates**: January 29, 2027 (Manappuram), February 16, 2027 (Manappuram), February 17, 2027 (Sharekhan), March 4, 2027 (Vivriti), and May 17, 2027 (Vivriti).

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations for debt market participants and corporate issuers raising short-term funds through commercial papers.
