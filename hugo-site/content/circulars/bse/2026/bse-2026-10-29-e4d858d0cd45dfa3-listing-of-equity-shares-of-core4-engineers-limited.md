## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 30, 2026, the equity shares of Core4 Engineers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME platform under the 'MT' Group of Securities. In terms of SEBI circular dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Core4 Engineers Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545007
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** CORE4
- **ISIN:** INE1RY601016
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 93/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 83/-)
- **Total Securities:** 20,68,3245 Equity Shares fully paid up
- **Market Lot:** 1200 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** October 28, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will be placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 1200 equity shares.
- Corporate actions for locking in shares have been executed in depository records as per regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 28, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access to Core4 Engineers Limited shareholders via the BSE SME segment, subject to initial Trade-for-Trade surveillance mechanisms.
