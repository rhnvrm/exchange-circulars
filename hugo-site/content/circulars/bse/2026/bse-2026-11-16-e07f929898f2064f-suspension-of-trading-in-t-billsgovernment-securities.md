## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specified T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity, effective from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects three specific T-bills maturing on or around the redemption date.
- Scrip codes involved: 805218 (182T191126, ISIN: IN002026Y071), 805147 (364T191126, ISIN: IN002025Z344), and 805258 (91TB191126, ISIN: IN002026X206).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities starting from the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; routine operational suspension due to security maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the aforementioned T-bills and Government Securities with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the orderly closure and settlement of maturing sovereign debt instruments.
