## Summary

BSE has announced the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Evonile Pharma Limited (Scrip Code: 751158, ISIN: INE234A20017) effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: Evonile Pharma Limited
- Security Type: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751158
- Scrip ID: EVONILE-RE
- ISIN No.: INE234A20017
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 19, 2026 to November 27, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: November 19, 2026 to December 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action notice for rights entitlements trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must settle trades on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis. REs shall be tradable in dematerialized form only. Members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensure clients understand these are not ordinary shares. Disclaimers must be included in contract notes/bills regarding rights issue participation or renunciation requirements.

## Important Dates

- Rights Entitlements Trading Period: November 19, 2026 to November 27, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: November 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of Evonile Pharma Limited can trade their rights entitlements or apply for rights shares within the specified timeframe. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the issue closing date.
