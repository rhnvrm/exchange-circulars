## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies due to fixed record dates for interest payment and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds of the listed companies around the record and ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Niwas Housing Finance Limited, UNO Minda Limited, HDB Financial Services Limited, Earlysalary Services Private Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Auxilo Finserve Private Limited, Kosamattam Finance Limited, North Eastern Electric Power Corporation Ltd, Five-Star Business Finance Ltd., InCred Financial Services Limited, Muthoot Capital Services Ltd., and Keertana Finserv Limited.
- Record dates span from November 6, 2026, to November 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard operational suspension of trading in debt securities for corporate action record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by refraining from trading in the specified NCDs during the restricted period associated with the record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: November 6, 2026 to November 26, 2026 (varying by scrip).

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specific debt instruments to facilitate accurate interest payment and redemption processing.
