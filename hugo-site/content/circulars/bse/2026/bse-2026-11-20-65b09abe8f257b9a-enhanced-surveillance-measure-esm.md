## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining changes to security staging, margin requirements, price bands, and settlement mechanisms for specified securities.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying ESM criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 25, 2026, on open positions as on November 23, 2026 and new positions created from November 25, 2026.
- Certain securities are moved to lower Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. November 23, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. November 23, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 25, 2026.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated to their pre-shortlisting bands, subject to no other active surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on applicable open and new positions.
- Members must note the shift of applicable securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment and adjusted price bands.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- November 23, 2026: Effective date for movement to lower Stage I, Stage II Trade-for-Trade with 2% price band, and moving out of ESM framework.
- November 25, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under Annexures I, II, and III will experience enhanced margin obligations, trading restrictions, and settlement modifications.
