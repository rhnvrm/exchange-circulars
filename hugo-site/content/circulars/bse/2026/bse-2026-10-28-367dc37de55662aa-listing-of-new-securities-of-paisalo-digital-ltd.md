## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 2,59,12,548 equity shares of Paisalo Digital Ltd with effect from October 29, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paisalo Digital Ltd
- Scrip Code: 532900
- ISIN Number: INE420C01059
- Number of Shares: 2,59,12,548 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 44.74/- each
- Date of Allotment: 01/10/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 914149115 to 940061662
- Effective Date of Listing: October 29, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and admission for trading of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 1, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity base due to FCCB conversion with no adverse operational impact.
