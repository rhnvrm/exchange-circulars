## Summary

Concatenate Power Advest Private Limited and Concatenate Advest Advisory Private Limited, as part of the promoter group, along with Jindal India Power Limited as person acting in concert (PAC), have announced the opening of an offer for voluntary delisting of equity shares of Jindal Photo Limited. The acquisition window runs from Monday, November 02, 2026, to Friday, November 06, 2026, through the Reverse Book-Building Process (RBBP).

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Acquirers & PAC:** Concatenate Power Advest Private Limited (Acquirer 1), Concatenate Advest Advisory Private Limited (Acquirer 2), and Jindal India Power Limited (PAC).
- **Offer Size:** 26,46,183 Equity Shares representing 25.80% of the paid-up equity share capital held by public shareholders.
- **Offer/Floor Price:** Rs. 1,119.50 per equity share, aggregating up to Rs. 296.24 Crores.
- **Indicative Price:** Rs. 1,120 per equity share.
- **Bidding Period:** Monday, November 02, 2026, to Friday, November 06, 2026.
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the delisting schedule and tender shares through the acquisition window on BSE in accordance with established SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of Jindal Photo Limited can participate in the reverse book-building process to tender their shares within the specified acquisition window. BSE serves as the designated stock exchange.
