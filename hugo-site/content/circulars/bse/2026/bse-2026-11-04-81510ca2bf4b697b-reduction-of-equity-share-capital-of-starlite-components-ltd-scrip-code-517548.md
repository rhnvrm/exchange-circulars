## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the reduction of equity share capital of Starlite Components Ltd. (Scrip Code: 517548) pursuant to a Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench. Effective November 5, 2026, the entire shareholding of the existing promoters will be fully extinguished and reduced to zero, and existing public shareholders will be allotted shares to retain 6.40% of the post-modification share capital.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Starlite Components Ltd. (Scrip Code: 517548)
- Effective / Record Date: November 5, 2026
- Nature of Action: Reduction of Share Capital / Resolution Plan (DR-750/2026-2027)
- Promoter Shareholding: Fully extinguished and reduced to zero
- Public Shareholding: Retained / adjusted to 6.40% of post-modification share capital
- Trading Status: Equity shares of the company are currently under suspension

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of the NCLT-approved Resolution Plan for capital restructuring and equity reallocation of Starlite Components Ltd.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the capital reduction, promoter share extinguishment, and ongoing suspension of trading effective November 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Record Date / Effective Date: November 5, 2026
- Suspension / No Dealings Date: November 5, 2026 onwards

## Impact Assessment

Substantial corporate restructuring with total wipeout of promoter equity, adjustment of public shareholding, and continued trading suspension for Starlite Components Ltd.
