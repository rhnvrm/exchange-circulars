## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-7432026-27 (Demat Auction 743).

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 28, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 28, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 28, 2026.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 28, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Thursday, October 29, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard operating notice regarding uniform membership structure implementation and short delivery rules.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 28, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 29, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for clearing and settlement participants involved in rolling settlement DR-7432026-27.
