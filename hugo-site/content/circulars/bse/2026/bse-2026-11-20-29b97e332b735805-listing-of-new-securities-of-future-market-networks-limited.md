## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 30,00,000 equity shares of Future Market Networks Limited (Scrip Code - 533296) with effect from Monday, November 23, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Future Market Networks Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 533296
- **ISIN:** INE360L01017
- **Number of Shares:** 30,00,000 equity shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 11.21/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/- plus premium of Rs. 1.21/-)
- **Allotment Date:** 27/03/2026
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 30,00,000 shares locked-in up to 30/05/2028 (Dist. Nos. 60644382 to 63644381)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding the listing of securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** March 27, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

This listing increases the paid-up equity capital of Future Market Networks Limited due to warrant conversion by promoters. The impact is company-specific and standard for preferential allotments.
