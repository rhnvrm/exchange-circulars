## Summary

BSE has announced an extension of the timeline for Investment Advisers regarding compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars, extending the deadline to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Extension granted for the appointment of IAAP Auditor, conduct of accessibility audits for digital platforms, and remediation of audit findings.
- New deadline extended to January 31, 2027.
- Based on SEBI Circular No. HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026, referencing earlier SEBI and Exchange circulars under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timeline for digital accessibility compliance (audit and remediation) extended by SEBI/BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must complete the appointment of an IAAP Auditor, conduct digital platform accessibility audits, and remediate findings by January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Extended Compliance Deadline: January 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities (Investment Advisers) additional time to ensure full digital accessibility compliance for persons with disabilities.
