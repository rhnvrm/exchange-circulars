## Summary

BSE has announced the trading and settlement schedule for the Basket Repo and Special Repo segments for the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026, covering Ready Leg T+0 and T+1 settlement cycles.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for Basket Repo and Special Repo segments for December 2026.
- Includes transaction & give-up takeup dates, securities/funds pay-in dates, and pay-out dates for Ready Leg T+0 and T+1.
- Settlement numbers range from 2627163 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to the specified settlement timelines, pay-in, and pay-out dates for repo transactions during December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: 01.12.2026 to 31.12.2026
- Notice Date: 13.11.2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for repo participants with transparent scheduling of pay-in and pay-out obligations.
