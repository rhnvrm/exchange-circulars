## Summary

BSE has issued a notice (No. 20261113-15) regarding the revision of existing price bands for 34 securities. These changes are part of the exchange's surveillance measures to curb excessive volatility and will come into effect from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revisions apply to 34 listed securities with price bands adjusted to various percentages (e.g., 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%).
- Effective date: November 16, 2026.
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance).
- Contact email for clarifications: bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price bands for specified scrips have been revised pursuant to surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and risk management parameters to reflect the revised price bands for the listed securities effective from the start of trading on November 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced risk controls and volatility mitigation for the impacted securities, ensuring orderly trading and protecting investor interests.
