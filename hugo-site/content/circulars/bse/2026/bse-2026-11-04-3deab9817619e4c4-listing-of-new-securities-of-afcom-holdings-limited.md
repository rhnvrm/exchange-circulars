## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 15,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each of Afcom Holdings Limited (Scrip Code - 544224) issued on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion, effective from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Afcom Holdings Limited (Scrip Code: 544224)
- **Security Details:** 15,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 853.17 (Issue Price: Rs. 863.17/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Ranking:** Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Numbers:** 28913617 to 28928616
- **ISIN:** INE0OXY01013
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 14/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 15/09/2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** 05/11/2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** 14/05/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion via warrant conversion with negligible impact on overall market liquidity.
