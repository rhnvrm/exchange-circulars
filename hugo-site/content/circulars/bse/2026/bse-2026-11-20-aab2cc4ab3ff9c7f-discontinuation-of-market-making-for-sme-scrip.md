## Summary

BSE has announced that SS Corporate Securities Limited (Member Code: 3268) is no longer acting as the Market Maker for the SME scrip Karbonsteel Engineering Limited (Scrip Code: 544511) effective November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Member Name: SS Corporate Securities Limited (Member Code: 3268)
- Scrip Code: 544511
- Scrip Name: Karbonsteel Engineering Limited
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Segment: SME

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this notice pertains solely to the administrative discontinuation of market making services for a specific SME scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should update their records regarding the cessation of market making services by SS Corporate Securities Limited for this scrip.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Cessation of market making may affect liquidity, order placement, and bid-ask spreads for Karbonsteel Engineering Limited on the SME platform.
