## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that several companies and trusts have fixed record dates for interest payment and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs), PTCs, and bonds. Trading in these securities will be conducted with reduced face values effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Covers multiple issuers including Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited, Aptus Finance India Private Limited, Arman Financial Services Limited, Ratnaafin Capital Private Limited, Purple Finance Limited, Niyogin Fintech Limited, Swayam Trust, Sansar June 2024 Trust, Sansar Aug 2024 VII Trust, and NeoGrowth Credit Private Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates span across November 20, 2026, to November 24, 2026.
- Trading members are required to note the reduced face values per debenture/PTC for settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure settlements reflect the reduced face values and adhere to the specified record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 20/11/2026 to 24/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational notice for debt segment participants ensuring accurate valuation and settlement following part redemption.
