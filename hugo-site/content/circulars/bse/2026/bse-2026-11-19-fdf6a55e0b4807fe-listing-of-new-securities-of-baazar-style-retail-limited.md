## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 10,00,000 new equity shares of Baazar Style Retail Limited (Scrip Code: 544243) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Friday, November 20, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Baazar Style Retail Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544243
- **ISIN:** INE01FR01028
- **Number of Shares:** 10,00,000 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 5/- each, Issue Price: Rs. 328.25/- including premium)
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters (preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion)
- **Distribution Nos.:** 76117354 to 77117353
- **Date of Allotment:** 26/09/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 10,00,000 shares locked-in up to 31/05/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational listing notice for newly allotted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the listing and lock-in period for the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Trading:** November 20, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the paid-up equity capital of Baazar Style Retail Limited following warrant conversion, with relevant lock-in restrictions applicable until May 31, 2027.
