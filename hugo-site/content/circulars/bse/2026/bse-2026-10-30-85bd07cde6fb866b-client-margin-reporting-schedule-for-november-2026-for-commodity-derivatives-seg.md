## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the client margin reporting schedule and corresponding penalty dates for November 2026 for the Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Key Points

- Outlines margin reporting and penalty schedules for trade dates throughout November 2026.
- Applies to all members participating in the Commodity Derivatives Segment.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

Routine operational schedule update with no new regulatory mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure timely reporting of client margins by the specified deadlines for each trade date to avoid applicable penalties.

## Important Dates

- Schedule covers trade dates from November 2, 2026 (02-11-2026) to November 30, 2026 (30-11-2026).
- Individual reporting deadlines and penalty dates are mapped per trade date.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures operational clarity and compliance for clearing and settlement in the commodity derivatives market.
