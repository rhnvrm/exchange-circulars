## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The notice outlines securities entering the framework, moving to lower or higher stages, or exiting entirely, effective late October 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 30, 2026, on open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade settlement with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- Consolidated lists of securities under the framework are provided in Annexure III, while securities moving out of ESM are listed in Annexure II.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely due to market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures requiring 100% margins and movement from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Enforcement of 2% price bands and periodic call auctions for securities transitioning to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of minimum 100% margins on affected positions.
- Market participants must align trading operations with the revised settlement segments and price band constraints for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **October 29, 2026:** Effective date for moving to Stage I, moving out of ESM, and application of Stage II restrictions (Trade-for-Trade, 2% price band, periodic call auction).
- **October 30, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment for newly shortlisted securities.

## Impact Assessment

Shortlisted securities face increased margin obligations and trading restrictions (such as trade-for-trade settlement and tighter price bands), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
