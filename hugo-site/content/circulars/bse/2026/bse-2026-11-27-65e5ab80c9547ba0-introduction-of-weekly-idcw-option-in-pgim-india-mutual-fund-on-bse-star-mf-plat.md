## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of a Weekly Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option with payout facility for PGIM India Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Weekly IDCW option with payout facility under PGIM India Mutual Fund.
- Applicable schemes include PGIM India Ultra Short Term Fund and PGIM India Liquid Fund.
- Effective date: November 27, 2026.
- Available on the BSE StAR MF platform for members and investors.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational update regarding mutual fund scheme options on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions for the new weekly IDCW option through the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides enhanced payout flexibility for investors holding units in the specified PGIM India Mutual Fund schemes.
