## Summary

Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited has announced an Offer to Buyback up to 11,59,199 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 1.55% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of March 31, 2026) at a price of Rs. 425 per share through the 'Tender Offer' stock exchange mechanism. BSE serves as the designated stock exchange for this offer, scheduled from November 27, 2026, to December 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited
- **Buyback Size:** Up to 11,59,199 fully paid-up equity shares (1.55% of total paid-up capital as of March 31, 2026).
- **Buyback Price:** Rs. 425/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Aggregate Maximum Amount:** Not exceeding Rs. 49,26,59,575/- (excluding transaction costs).
- **Record Date:** Friday, November 20, 2026.
- **Offer Period:** Friday, November 27, 2026 to Thursday, December 3, 2026.
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars on mechanisms for acquisition of shares through stock exchange under takeover, buyback, and delisting regulations (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016) along with updated BSE guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate in the tender offer in accordance with the revised SEBI and Exchange guidelines during the acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Buyback Opening Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Buyback Closing Date:** December 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates capital return and liquidity for eligible shareholders of Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited via the stock exchange tender offer mechanism.
