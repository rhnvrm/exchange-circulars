## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30. In line with SEBI's advice, BSE has notified Research Analysts to take appropriate measures to implement its guidelines on simplifying digital interfaces, addressing language barriers using tools like Bhashini, and developing AI & ML-based public query systems.

## Key Points

- RBI released the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, dated December 01, 2025.
- Highlights that complex digital interfaces and language barriers impede informed customer choices and financial service uptake.
- Research Analysts must simplify digital interfaces, incorporate vernacular languages, and utilize AI-powered translation platforms such as Bhashini.
- Research Analysts are required to develop an AI & ML-based public query system on common securities market-related aspects via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporation of RBI's NSFI 2025–30 guidelines for Research Analysts regarding digital accessibility, vernacular localization, and AI/ML advisory systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must simplify digital interfaces to ensure ease of use.
- Address language barriers using vernacular languages and AI-powered translation tools like Bhashini.
- Implement an AI & ML-based public query system for common securities market queries on websites and social media channels.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- NSFI Date: December 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Research Analysts must upgrade their digital platforms and communication tools to include vernacular support, AI translation, and automated query systems, enhancing client accessibility and regulatory compliance.
