## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding a correction in the name of an anchor investor for the Initial Public Offer (IPO) of Jio Platforms Limited, continuing from Notice No. 20261019-46 dated October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Correction requested by the company regarding an anchor investor's name.
- The name 'Fidelity Securities Fund Fidelity Series Blue Chip Growth Fund' was inadvertently stated as 'Fidelity Advsior Series I Fidelity Advisor Series Equity Growth Fund' at Sr no 25 in the first table of the Anchor Intimation.
- All other contents, terms, and details of the original notice dated October 19, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a factual and clerical correction of investor details in the anchor allocation intimation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the revised anchor investor name.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Original Notice Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal to no operational impact as this is purely a clerical correction of an investor's name.
