## Summary

Stock exchanges periodically review price bands of securities to prevent excessive volatility and protect investor interests. In this notice, BSE has announced revised price bands for 36 securities under the equity trading segment, taking effect from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 36 securities shortlisted for price band revision.
- Revised price bands range from 5%, 10%, to 20% limits.
- Applicable to scrips across various sectors including AksharChem India Ltd, Ambar Protein Industries Ltd, Chatha Foods Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price bands for specified scrips have been modified to 5%, 10%, or 20% as detailed in the exchange annexure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals and risk management systems to reflect the revised price bands for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in the listed securities will be subject to the new daily price fluctuation limits starting from the effective date.
