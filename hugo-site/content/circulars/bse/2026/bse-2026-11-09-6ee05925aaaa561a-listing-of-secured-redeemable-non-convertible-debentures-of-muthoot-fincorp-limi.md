## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Muthoot Fincorp Limited, effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures to dealings on the Exchange.
- Effective date of trading: November 11, 2026, under 'F GROUP'.
- Trading will be conducted strictly in dematerialized form in unit market lots (One Bond).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members to adhere to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, requiring bonds to be traded in dematerialized form only.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice for debt securities with minimal impact on general equity trading.
