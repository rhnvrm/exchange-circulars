## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for its Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs are encouraged to test their applications and functionalities under normal and exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Applicable to trading members using third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs.
- No new BOLT TWS Release is associated with this session.
- Timings: Log-in (01:15 pm - 02:20 pm), Continuous Trading (02:20 pm - 03:30 pm), Trade Modification (02:20 pm - 04:00 pm).
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or create rights and liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 04:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new rule modifications have been introduced through this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Participation in the mock trading session is voluntary but strongly encouraged for testing trading applications. Members are requested to share their feedback with the BSE Helpdesk or Tech Support team by 04:00 pm on the day of the session.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** October 31, 2026, by 04:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to testing and familiarization. There are no financial implications, margin obligations, or settlement impacts resulting from the mock session.
