## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund has announced that the MOTILAL OSWAL MULTI ASSET - OMNI FUND OF FUNDS schemes will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reopening of ongoing transactions for Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds.
- Applicable schemes include Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW payout/reinvestment options (ISINs: INF247L01HV6, INF247L01HW4, INF247L01HX2, INF247L01HS2, INF247L01HT0, INF247L01HU8).
- Effective transaction date: November 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors can facilitate ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP transactions for investors on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures continuous trading accessibility and investment management for unitholders across regular and direct plans on the BSE StAR MF platform.
