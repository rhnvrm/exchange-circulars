## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to the revised framework. Effective November 02, 2026, 100% margins will be applicable on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions. Additionally, securities moving to higher stages, transferring to T/XT/MT/TS groups, or exiting the framework are effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 02, 2026, on all open positions as on October 30, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Lower Price Band applicable w.e.f. October 29, 2026, for securities moved to higher stages.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out from LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. October 29, 2026, with price bands reinstated.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework rules involving margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group transfer rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Oct 2026
- Higher Stage / Exit Effective Date: October 29, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 30, 2026
- Margin / Group Transfer Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational impact for traders and investors holding positions in securities shortlisted under LT-ASM due to stringent margin requirements (100%), price band restrictions, and group reclassifications.
