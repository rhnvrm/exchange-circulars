## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI KRA regulations instructing trading members that clients whose KYC records uploaded between October 1, 2026, and October 31, 2026, remain "On Hold" or non-validated by KRAs will be restricted from trading and squaring off open positions effective November 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded to KRAs and only clients with "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status are permitted to trade.
- Clients with non-validated or on-hold KYCs (both Aadhaar and Non-Aadhaar based OVD) uploaded between October 1, 2026, and October 31, 2026, are barred from trading and squaring off open positions effective November 21, 2026.
- Non-compliant PANs will be flagged by the exchange as "Not Permitted to Trade" starting November 21, 2026.
- Open positions for non-compliant clients will naturally expire on their respective contract expiry dates.
- The list of non-compliant clients is available for download at `EQ\TRANSACTION\Nov-2026\09-11-2026 Non_Validated_Clients_by_KRAs_Clgno_xxxx.TXT`.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of strict KYC validation rules through KRAs pursuant to SEBI circulars.
- Mandatory restriction on trading and square-off privileges for clients with non-validated KYC statuses.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download the non-compliant client records and take necessary actions.
- Members must ensure clients comply with KRA validation requirements to resume trading.
- Accounts that become KRA compliant will be permitted to trade on a T+1 basis based on updates received by the exchange from KRAs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Non-validation period covered: October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading and Square-off Restrictions: November 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clients with pending or non-validated KYC records face immediate trading and square-off freezes, impacting account operations and position management until validation requirements are fulfilled.
