## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the Launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) with SIP Facility of CHOICE Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. CHOICE AMC has revised the clear funds acceptance date for the CHOICE LARGE & MID CAP FUND NFO from November 5, 2026, to November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** CHOICE Large & Mid Cap Fund - NFO
- **Revised Clear Funds Date:** November 4, 2026 (by 6:00 PM in BSE Clearing Limited account)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Reopening Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Lumpsum Purchases:** NFO period extended until midnight on November 3, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **SIP/XSIP Registrations:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 3, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding NFO timelines and fund processing on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds for the CHOICE Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 4, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Closure / Lumpsum Extension:** November 3, 2026 (Midnight)
- **SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline:** November 3, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** November 4, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Reopening Date:** November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors participating in the CHOICE Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO must adhere to the expedited clear funds deadline of November 4, 2026, to ensure successful allotment.
