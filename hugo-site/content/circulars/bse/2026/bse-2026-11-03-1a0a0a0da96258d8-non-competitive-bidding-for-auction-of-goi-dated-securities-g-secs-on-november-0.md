## Summary

BSE has issued notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on November 06, 2026, pursuant to the RBI press release dated November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities offered for auction: 7.06% GS 2041 (Symbol: 706GS41N26) (Re-issue, Rs. 23,000 Cr) and 7.43% GS 2076 (Symbol: 743GS76N26) (Re-issue, Rs. 13,000 Cr).
- Bidding Platform: Exchange's iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- Bid Collection Start Date: November 03, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): November 06, 2026, till 8.00 am.
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): November 05, 2026, till 5.00 pm.
- Auction Date: November 06, 2026.
- Settlement Date: November 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Participation in Government Securities auction via non-competitive bidding facility as per Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 23, 2018 and RBI press release.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based system.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: November 03, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- Direct Investor Bid End: November 05, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Member Bid End: November 06, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: November 06, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation of trading members and retail/direct investors in the primary auction of GoI dated securities through electronic bidding platforms.
