## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by 8 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Ownership Schemes (ESOS), effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Members are informed that further new securities issued by 8 companies under ESOP/ESOS are listed and admitted for trading.
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.
- Companies involved include Fedbank Financial Services Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, L&T Technology Services Limited, Mahindra Lifespace Developers Limited, Renaissance Global Limited, Rose Merc. Limited, and Transrail Lighting Limited.
- Lock-in status for all listed tranches is noted as NA.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes. This is a routine listing operation conducted under applicable exchange bylaws and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing companies have completed the required disclosures and listing formalities for securities issued under ESOP/ESOS.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update reflecting the issuance and listing of employee stock options with negligible direct market impact.
