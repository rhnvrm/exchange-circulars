## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the admission of new trading members and additional segment memberships effective November 16 and November 17, 2026, pursuant to the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **1FC Securities Pvt. Ltd (Member No. 6694):** Admitted for Equity Derivatives Segment as Additional Membership on 16/11/2026.
- **Firstock Broking Private Limited (Member No. 6101):** Admitted for Commodity Derivatives Segment as Additional Membership on 16/11/2026.
- **Monetic Stock Broking Private Limited (Member No. 3318):** Admitted for Cash, Equity Derivatives, and Currency Derivatives as New Membership on 17/11/2026.
- **Ashta Siddhi Bullion and Jewellers LLP (Member No. 3319):** Admitted for Cash and Commodity Derivatives as New Membership on 17/11/2026.
- **SAS Aarthik Samadhan LLP (Member No. 3320):** Admitted for Debt Segment as New Membership on 17/11/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular serves as a formal notification of member admissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarifications may reach out via email to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Admission Dates:** November 16, 2026 and November 17, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update reflecting expansion of trading member participation across various segments on BSE.
