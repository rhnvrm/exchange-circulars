## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Johnsoncab Electricals Limited has finalized the allocation of 7,95,000 equity shares to anchor investors at a price of Rs. 200 per equity share as part of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Johnsoncab Electricals Limited
- Anchor Investor Portion: 7,95,000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs. 200/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: October 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate public issue notification regarding anchor investor allotment under existing exchange and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the anchor investor allocation details for the public issue.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Announcement Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The successful allocation to anchor investors indicates institutional backing and sets a positive tone for the public issue subscription and subsequent listing.
