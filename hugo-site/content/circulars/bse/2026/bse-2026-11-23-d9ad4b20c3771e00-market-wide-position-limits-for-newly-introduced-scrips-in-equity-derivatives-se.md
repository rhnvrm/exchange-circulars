## Summary

BSE has announced the Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for newly introduced scrips in the Equity Derivatives Segment, pursuant to notice No. 20260922-54 dated September 22, 2026. The limits are applicable to Trading Members, FIIs/FPIs (Category I & II), and Mutual Funds effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip: Apar Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 532259) - Market Wide Limit: 28,00,200 shares; Client/NRI/Scheme of Mutual Fund: 2,80,000 shares; TM (Proprietary, FPI Cat II): 5,60,000 shares; TM (Prop/Client)/FPI Cat I/MF: 8,40,050 shares.
- Scrip: Acutaas Chemicals Limited (Scrip Code: 543349) - Market Wide Limit: 82,69,449 shares; Client/NRI/Scheme of Mutual Fund: 8,26,800 shares; TM (Proprietary, FPI Cat II): 16,53,750 shares; TM (Prop/Client)/FPI Cat I/MF: 24,80,700 shares.
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Specifies revised market wide position limits and derivative participation thresholds for newly introduced equity derivative scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, FIIs/FPIs, and mutual funds must ensure compliance with the prescribed position limits for the specified scrips in the derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates risk management and compliance monitoring for trading members and institutional investors participating in newly introduced equity derivative contracts.
