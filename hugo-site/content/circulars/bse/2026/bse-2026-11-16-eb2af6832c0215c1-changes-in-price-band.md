## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the existing price bands for 16 securities, effective from November 17, 2026, as part of its ongoing surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Total of 16 securities shortlisted for price band revisions.
- Effective date: November 17, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Atharva Poly-Plast Ltd, Baazar Style Retail Ltd, Containe Technologies Ltd, Fresita Proteins Limited, Future Market Networks Ltd, Kama Holdings Ltd, Kesar Enterprises Ltd, Magnus Steel And Infra Limited, Maharashtra Scooters Ltd, Money Masters Leasing & Finance Ltd, Panth Infinity Ltd, Prozone Realty Ltd, Shraddha Prime Projects Ltd, Spectrum Foods Ltd, Transworld Shipping Lines Ltd, and TVS Holdings Ltd.
- Price bands revised range from 2% to 20% depending on the security.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified scrips to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed securities will be subject to the newly imposed price limits, restricting intraday price movements beyond the specified percentages.
