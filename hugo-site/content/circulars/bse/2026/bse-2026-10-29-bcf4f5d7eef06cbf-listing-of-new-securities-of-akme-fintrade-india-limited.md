## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited (Scrip Code: 544200) with effect from October 30, 2026. A total of 82,00,000 equity shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Akme Fintrade (India) Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544200
- **ISIN:** INE916Y01027
- **Securities Issued:** 82,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each at an issue price of Rs. 11.10/- (including premium of Rs. 10.10/-).
- **Allottees:** Promoter and Non-Promoter on a preferential basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 439749961 to 447949960.
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the lock-in details and distinctive number ranges for the newly listed preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 29, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Period Details:**
  - 72,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 439749961 - 446949960): Locked-in upto May 29, 2028
  - 10,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 446949961 - 447949960): Locked-in upto May 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited through preferential warrant conversion. The shares rank pari-passu with existing equity, subject to statutory lock-in restrictions.
