## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines regarding the special Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 8, 2026, on the occasion of Diwali Laxmi Pujan. Trading members must ensure UCC compliance by the specified deadline to participate.

## Key Points

- Special Muhurat Trading session will be held on Sunday, November 8, 2026.
- UCCs identified as compliant by the Exchange by 22:00 hrs on Saturday, November 7, 2026, are permitted to trade.
- Members must check the daily report (`SEG_TMCODE_UCC_TRADING_STATUS_DDMMYYYY_nn`) in the Extranet Transaction folder to verify permitted UCCs.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; administrative and scheduling notice for festive trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must verify UCC trading status via the Extranet report before the trading session.
- Ensure UCC compliance is updated by 22:00 hrs on November 7, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- UCC Compliance Cutoff: November 7, 2026, 22:00 hrs
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth execution of festival trading with verified client codes.
