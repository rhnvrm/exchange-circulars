## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the consolidation of equity shares of OTCO International Ltd (Scrip Code: 523151). The company has fixed November 6, 2026, as the record date for consolidating its existing equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company: OTCO International Ltd
- Scrip Code: 523151
- Record Date: 06/11/2026
- Consolidation Details: Five existing equity shares of Rs.2/- each are consolidated into ONE equity share of Rs.10/- each.
- No Dealings From: 06/11/2026 (Notice Ref: DR-751/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate action processed in accordance with exchange listing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the equity shares of OTCO International Ltd as per the schedule and restrictions effective from the record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 November 2026
- Record Date & No Dealings From: 06 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders will experience a change in face value and total number of held shares proportional to the 5:1 consolidation ratio, along with temporary trading restrictions on the record date.
