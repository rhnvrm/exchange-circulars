## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 3,30,000 equity shares of Caprihans India Ltd. with effect from November 5, 2026, issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Caprihans India Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 509486
- **ISIN:** INE479A01018
- **Security Details:** 3,30,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue Price: Rs. 200/-)
- **Allottees:** Promoters (preferential basis)
- **Effective Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 3,30,000 shares locked-in up to 29/05/2028 (Distribution Nos. 14923972 to 15253971)

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the listing and trading permission of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** March 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** May 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard corporate action reflecting the conversion of warrants into equity shares by promoters, increasing the paid-up capital of the company with standard lock-in provisions.
