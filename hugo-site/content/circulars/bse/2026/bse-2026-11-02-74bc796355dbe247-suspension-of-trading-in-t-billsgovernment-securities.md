## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities effective from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities maturing: 182TB51126 (Scrip Code: 805212, ISIN: IN002026Y055) and 91TB051126 (Scrip Code: 805250, ISIN: IN002026X180).
- Suspension effective date: November 03, 2026 (DR- 748/2026-2027).
- Department: Listing Operations.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice regarding security maturity and cessation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no further dealings take place in the specified T-bills and Government Securities from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal to none, as this is a standard operational procedure for matured government securities.
