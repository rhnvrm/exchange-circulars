## Summary

BSE has issued a notice drawing attention to SEBI circular ref. no. HO/38/14/(18)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25560/2026 dated November 11, 2026, regarding revised reporting requirements under the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) and Common Reporting Standard (CRS), updated through the Income-Tax Act, 2025 read with the Income-Tax Rules, 2026.

## Key Points

- Draws attention to SEBI circular dated November 11, 2026.
- Focuses on reporting requirements under FATCA and CRS.
- Incorporates updates from the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Income-Tax Rules, 2026.
- Members are requested to note and comply with the revised provisions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of FATCA and CRS reporting mandates aligned with the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Income-Tax Rules, 2026.
- Cascading compliance obligations communicated via SEBI circular HO/38/14/(18)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25560/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and regulated entities must take note of the contents of the aforesaid SEBI circular and ensure strict compliance with revised FATCA and CRS reporting frameworks.
- For queries, members can contact BSE Inspection department via phone or email (Bse.inspection@bseindia.com).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 16, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact requires compliance teams of trading members to review updated FATCA/CRS reporting obligations under the new Income-Tax Act framework.
