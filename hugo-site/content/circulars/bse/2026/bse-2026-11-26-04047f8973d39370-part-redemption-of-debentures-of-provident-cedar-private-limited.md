## Summary

Provident Cedar Private Limited has informed the Exchange regarding the partial redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value per debenture effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Provident Cedar Private Limited
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Scrip Code: 975169
- ISIN: INE0NF907023
- Security Code / Symbol: PCPL-ZC-16-4-28-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 72,575.49 per Debenture
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (DR- 765/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members and market participants are informed of the updated face value for trading purposes following the partial redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the debentures of the company will be traded with the aforementioned reduced face value starting November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date (DR): November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for debt segment trading members holding or trading debentures of Provident Cedar Private Limited, reflecting adjusted face value.
