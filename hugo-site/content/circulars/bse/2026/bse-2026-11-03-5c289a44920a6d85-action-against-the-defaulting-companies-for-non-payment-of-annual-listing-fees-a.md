## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding actions taken against defaulting companies for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Trading suspension for SPEL Semiconductor Ltd. is revoked effective November 6, 2026, moving from Group T to Group B, while trading for BKM Industries Limited remains suspended.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in equity shares of SPEL Semiconductor Ltd. (Scrip Code: 517166) is revoked w.e.f. November 6, 2026, and resumed to Normal Segment (Group B).
- Trading in equity shares of BKM Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 539043) continues to remain under suspension until further notice pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20241126-52.
- Actions are taken pursuant to Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revocation of trading suspension for compliant defaulting companies upon settlement of outstanding Annual Listing Fees.
- Continued enforcement of trading suspension for non-compliant entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must ensure timely payment of Annual Listing Fees as mandated under SEBI regulations to avoid regulatory actions and trading curbs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 3, 2026
- Suspension Revocation Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in SPEL Semiconductor Ltd. regain liquidity as trading resumes in Group B.
- Continued trading restrictions remain for BKM Industries Limited until further notice.
