## Summary

BSE has announced updates regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing inclusions, stage migrations, and exclusions effective from November 18 and November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 19, 2026, on open positions as of November 18, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II face trade-for-trade settlement with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- Certain securities move to lower Stage I or move out of the ESM framework entirely.
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisted securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Minimum 100% margin requirements applied to open and new positions.
- Stage-specific price bands and periodic call auctions implemented for applicable securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- November 18, 2026: Movement to lower Stage I, shifting to Stage II (with 2% price band & Periodic Call Auction), and moving out of ESM for applicable securities.
- November 19, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact for securities placed under stringent ESM stages due to 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
