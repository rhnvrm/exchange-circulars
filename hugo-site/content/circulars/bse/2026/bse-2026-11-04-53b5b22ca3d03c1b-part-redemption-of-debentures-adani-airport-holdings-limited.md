## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of debentures issued by Adani Airport Holdings Limited (Scrip Code: 976065, ISIN: INE0GCN07047). The actual reduced face value is set at Rs. 54,558/- per debenture effective November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Airport Holdings Limited
- Scrip Code: 976065
- ISIN: INE0GCN07047
- Debenture Description: AAHL-9.35%-8-9-28-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 54,558/- per debenture
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of previous notice dated October 16, 2026, updating the actual reduced face value following part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised that the debentures will be traded with the reduced face value starting November 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members regarding the revised face value of debentures in the debt segment.
