## Summary

BSE has issued the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Buyback) of equity shares of VRL Logistics Limited (Security Symbol: VRLLOG). The buyback window opens on October 27, 2026, and closes on November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: VRL Logistics Limited (Scrip Code: 539118, ISIN: INE366I01010, Symbol: VRLLOG).
- Offer Size: Up to 87,50,000 equity shares at an offer price of Rs. 320.00 per share.
- Opening Date: Tuesday, October 27, 2026.
- Closing Date: Monday, November 2, 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid offering and modifications from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on trading days via iBBS/RTRMS platforms.

## Regulatory Changes

None applicable (operational circular for corporate action).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Members and custodians can participate for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Follow operational instructions outlined in Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Closing Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Bid Offering & Modification Period:** October 27, 2026 to November 2, 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** October 27, 2026 to October 30, 2026, with final give-up on November 2, 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.) and take-up on November 2, 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Provides clear operational schedules and platform access guidelines for trading members and custodians participating in the VRL Logistics Limited buyback acquisition window.
