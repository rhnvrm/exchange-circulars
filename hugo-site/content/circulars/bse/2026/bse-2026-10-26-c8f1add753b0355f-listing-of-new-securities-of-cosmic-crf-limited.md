## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 1,42,000 new equity shares of Cosmic CRF Limited (Scrip Code: 543928) with effect from October 27, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 1,42,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1,604/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1,614/-).
- **Allotment Basis:** Issued to Promoters on a preferential basis upon warrant conversion.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9206244 to 9348243.
- **ISIN:** INE0ORA01015.
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,42,000 shares locked-in up to May 1, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised to take note of the listing and permitted trading of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 27, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 1, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's paid-up equity base following warrant conversion by promoters.
