## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Antala Industries Limited on the SME platform effective Wednesday, November 11, 2026. The scrip will be traded in the 'MT' Group and will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545024
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ANTALA
- ISIN: INE15Z101013
- Number of Securities: 12,156,736 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 66/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/-, premium Rs. 56/-)
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: November 6, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the Trade-for-Trade restriction for the first 10 trading days.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Date of Allotment: November 6, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Antala Industries Limited shares on the BSE SME segment with standard trade-for-trade surveillance controls for initial trading sessions.
