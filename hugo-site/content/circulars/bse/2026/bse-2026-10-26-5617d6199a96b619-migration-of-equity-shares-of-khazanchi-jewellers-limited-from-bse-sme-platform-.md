## Summary

Effective Tuesday, October 27, 2026, the equity shares of Khazanchi Jewellers Limited will be migrated and admitted to dealings on the BSE Mainboard Platform from the BSE SME Platform under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Khazanchi Jewellers Limited
- Scrip Code: 543953
- ISIN: INE0OWC01011
- Total Securities: 24,74,6900 Equity shares of Rs. 10/-
- Distinctive Numbers: 1 - 24746900
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Abbreviated Name / Scrip ID on BOLT System: KHAZANCHI
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Cameo Corporate Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Trades will be executed in unit market lot (one share).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and the company must comply with Mainboard listing norms and dematerialized trading requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date of Migration: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The migration enhances the company's market presence and liquidity by moving from the SME segment to the mainboard listing tier, allowing broader participation.
