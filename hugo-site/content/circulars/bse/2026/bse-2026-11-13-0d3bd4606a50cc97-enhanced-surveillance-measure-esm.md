## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities satisfying the criteria for the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin, shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move to lower stages with price band and periodic call auction restrictions.

## Key Points

- Certain securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under the ESM framework, attracting a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 17, 2026 on all open positions as on November 16, 2026 and new positions.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under ESM w.e.f. November 16, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 16, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) including margin enhancements, movement between stages, and shifting settlement segments.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the margin requirements, trading segment shifts, and price band restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins / Trade-for-Trade Settlement: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for shortlisted securities will be significantly impacted due to trade-for-trade settlement and 100% margin stipulations.
