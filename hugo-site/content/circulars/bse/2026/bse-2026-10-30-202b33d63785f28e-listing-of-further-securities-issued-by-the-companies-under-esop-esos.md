## Summary

BSE Limited has notified trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by 13 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Monday, November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 13 companies are issuing and listing new shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: November 02, 2026.
- Includes prominent entities such as Axis Bank Limited, Hero MotoCorp Limited, Larsen & Toubro Limited, and RBL Bank Limited.
- Securities are fully fungible and admitted for regular market trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine corporate listing procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities and trading members must verify ISIN and lock-in details prior to execution.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; routine expansion of paid-up capital due to employee stock option exercises.
