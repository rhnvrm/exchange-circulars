## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities included, moved to lower stages, or moving out of the framework effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 20, 2026 on all open positions as on November 19, 2026 and new positions created from November 20, 2026.
- Securities continuing in ESM but moving to lower Stage I under the framework w.e.f. November 19, 2026 are included in Annexure I.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 19, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM Framework w.e.f. November 19, 2026 is given in Annexure II, and a consolidated list is given in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, shifting to trade-for-trade settlement, price bands, and periodic call auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable margin requirements, settlement segment changes, and trading restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- November 19, 2026: Effective date for moving to lower Stage I, Stage II restrictions (Trade for Trade, 2% price band, Periodic Call Auction), and moving out of ESM Framework.
- November 20, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Directly affects liquidity and trading operations for shortlisted securities by enforcing stringent margin obligations, trade-for-trade settlement, and capped price bands.
