## Summary

Atul Limited has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each from the open market through the Stock Exchanges, in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The maximum buyback price is set at Rs. 7,500/- per equity share for an aggregate amount not exceeding Rs. 90,00,00,000.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Atul Limited
- **Maximum Buyback Price:** Rs. 7,500/- per equity share
- **Maximum Buyback Size:** Rs. 90,00,00,000 (Rupees Ninety Crores Only)
- **Company's Broker:** Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited (Clearing No. 3261)
- **Registrar to Buyback:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited
- **Manager to Buyback:** Vivro Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in compliance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must adhere to the buyback framework and schedule.
- Payment obligations relating to the buyback must be completed before the last date of the buyback.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Opening of Buyback:** Monday, November 02, 2026
- **Last Date for Buyback:** Earlier of (a) Sixty-six Working Days from opening (Tuesday, February 8, 2027), (b) completion of buyback by deploying maximum size, or (c) earlier closure date determined by the Board/Buyback Committee upon deploying minimum buyback size.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity or value realization for existing equity shareholders of Atul Limited through open market operations.
