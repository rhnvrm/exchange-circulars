## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher stages of GSM.
- Effective date of implementation is November 12, 2026.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I to IV).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, noting that settlement is on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off and mandatory applicable margin rates/Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in affected securities will face increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and potential trading frequency limits depending on the assigned GSM stage.
