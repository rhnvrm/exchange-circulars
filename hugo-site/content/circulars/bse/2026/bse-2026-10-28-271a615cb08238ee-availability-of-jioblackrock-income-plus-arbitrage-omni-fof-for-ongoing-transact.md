## Summary

BSE has announced that the JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from October 29, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261001-55 dated October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Eligible schemes and ISINs:
  - JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF22M001390)
  - JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF22M001382)
- Trading Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process ongoing transactions, subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic plans for the mentioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans in the JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF.
