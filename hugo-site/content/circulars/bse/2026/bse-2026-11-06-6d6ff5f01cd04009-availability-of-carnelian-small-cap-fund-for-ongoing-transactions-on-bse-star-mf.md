## Summary

Carnelian Small Cap Fund is set to reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting November 09, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261015-18.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Schemes included: Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can process transactions for the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to conduct ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans in Carnelian Small Cap Fund via BSE StAR MF.
