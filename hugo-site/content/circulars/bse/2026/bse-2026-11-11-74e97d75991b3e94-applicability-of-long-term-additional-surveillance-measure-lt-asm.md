## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework on various securities, which includes the imposition of 100% margins, movement to higher surveillance stages, transfer to specific trade-for-trade groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and price band revisions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 16, 2026 on all open positions as on November 13, 2026 and new positions created from November 16, 2026 onwards.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM Framework or subject to Lower Price Band w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM framework transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 16, 2026.
- Securities moving out from LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 12, 2026 with price bands reinstated.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submission by listed companies and market surveillance, not adverse company action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework measures including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
- Transfer of specific securities to trade-for-trade segments (T / XT / MT / TS Groups).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin compliance for affected securities effective November 16, 2026.
- Trading members must take note of price band revisions and segment transfers effective November 12 and November 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- November 12, 2026: Effective date for movement to higher stages, moving out of framework, and lower price band applicability.
- November 13, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margin.
- November 16, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirement and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and risk management requirements for securities placed under LT-ASM will experience tighter margin constraints and potential transfer to trade-for-trade segments, affecting market participants trading in these scrips.
