## Summary

BSE has issued the settlement schedule for shorter tenure contracts under the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) Scheme in the SLB Reverse Leg Segment for November 2026.

## Key Points

- Details the settlement programme for the SLB Reverse Leg Segment for November 2026.
- Provides depository settlement numbers, trade dates, and SLR pay-in/pay-out dates.
- Pay-in shall be conducted at 09:00 a.m., and members must submit instructions to Depositories or banks latest by 08:50 a.m.
- All other provisions relating to market timings, clearing, and risk management remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are required to submit pay-in instructions to Depositories or banks latest by 08:50 a.m. on the respective pay-in dates.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Period: November 01, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- First Trade Date: 02-11-2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out: 05-11-2026)
- Last Trade Date: 30-11-2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out: 03-12-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational calendar ensuring clarity on settlement timelines for market participants engaging in SLB reverse leg contracts.
