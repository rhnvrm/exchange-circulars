## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in Rights Entitlements (REs) for renunciation of Crescentis Capital Limited are permitted with effect from November 16, 2026. The trading period for REs runs from November 16, 2026 to November 20, 2026, and the rights issue is open from November 16, 2026 to November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: CRESCENTIS CAPITAL LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 751156
- Scrip ID: CRESCE-RE1
- ISIN: INE754C20028
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Rights Issue Period: November 16, 2026 to November 27, 2026
- Settlement: T+1 Rolling Settlement on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares of the company. Contract notes and bills for purchase of REs must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchase only grants the right to apply for shares or renounce REs before the issue closes, failing which they will lapse.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- RE Trading Period: November 16, 2026 to November 20, 2026
- Rights Issue Open/Close Date: November 16, 2026 to November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the demat trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for shareholders of Crescentis Capital Limited under standard T+1 trade-for-trade mechanisms.
