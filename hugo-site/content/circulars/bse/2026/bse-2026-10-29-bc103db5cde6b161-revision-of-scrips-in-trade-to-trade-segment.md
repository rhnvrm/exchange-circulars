## Summary

BSE has announced the periodic revision of scrips moving into and out of the Trade-to-Trade (T / XT / MT / TS) segment as a preventive surveillance measure to ensure market safety and safeguard investor interests.

## Key Points

- Scrips mentioned in Annexure I Part A will be transferred to the Trade-to-Trade segment effective November 03, 2026.
- Scrips in Annexure I Part B will continue to remain in the Trade-to-Trade segment.
- A 100% VaR margin and a price band of 5% or lower will be levied on these scrips, with no netting off positions allowed.
- Scrips in Annexure II are eligible for transfer but will remain in their respective P, Y, Z, ZP & ZY groups as they are already traded on a trade-to-trade basis.
- Scrips in Annexure III will be shifted back to their original groups from November 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of market surveillance measures pursuant to Exchange Notice Nos. 20161219-27, 20230925-51, and 20050805-12.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in affected scrips as settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off positions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Modifies trading settlement, margin requirements, and price bands for securities identified under surveillance review.
