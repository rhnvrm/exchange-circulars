## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities and sovereign green bonds scheduled for November 27, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four securities/sovereign green bonds are available for bidding: 6.20% GS 2029 (Re-issue, Rs. 9,000 Cr), 6.57% GS 2033 (Re-issue, Rs. 12,000 Cr), 7.63% GS 2056 (Re-issue, Rs. 9,000 Cr), and 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056 (Re-issue, Rs. 12,000 Cr).
- Bidding is conducted through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Minimum subscription units and multiples are 100, with minimum amount of Rs. 10,000 and maximum amount of Rs. 2 crores.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of RBI's auction schedule for Government dated securities through non-competitive bidding facility under Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 23, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: November 24, 2026 (10:00 AM onwards)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): November 26, 2026 (till 5:00 PM)
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): November 27, 2026 (till 8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: November 27, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates non-competitive participation for eligible investors in Government of India dated securities and sovereign green bonds auctions.
