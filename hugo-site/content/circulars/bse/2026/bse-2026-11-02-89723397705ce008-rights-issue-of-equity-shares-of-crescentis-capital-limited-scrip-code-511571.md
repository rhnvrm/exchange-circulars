## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Crescentis Capital Limited has fixed the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Trading in the company's equity shares will operate on an ex-rights basis starting from November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** Crescentis Capital Limited (Scrip Code: 511571)
- **Record Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Rights Ratio:** 3 (Three) Equity Shares for every 5 (Five) Equity Shares held
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 68/- per share for cash
- **Settlement Number:** DR-749/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action notice under the rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure that transactions in the company's equity shares comply with the ex-rights basis schedule.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Record Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders registered as of the record date will receive rights entitlements. Market participants must adjust trading and valuation strategies reflecting the ex-rights pricing.
