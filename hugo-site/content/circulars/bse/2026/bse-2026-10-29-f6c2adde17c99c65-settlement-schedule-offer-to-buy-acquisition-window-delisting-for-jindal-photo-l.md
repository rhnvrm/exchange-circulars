## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule and early pay-in guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window) pertaining to the delisting of equity shares of Jindal Photo Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to run from November 02, 2026, to November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Bidding Period:** November 02, 2026 to November 06, 2026
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Delisting, Settlement No. 2026131
- **Obligation Download:** November 17, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Early Pay-In:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Delisting)" and Settlement No. 2026131.
- **Depository Procedures:** CDSL clients must transfer directly to the member/custodian early pay-in account; NSDL clients must have securities earmarked/blocked and execute transfer instructions to the member's NSDL pool account.
- **EPN File:** Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy changes introduced; procedures follow standard stock exchange delisting acquisition window settlement guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities reflects in BSE/ICCL systems before the OTB platform bidding closure timelines.
- Correct market type and settlement number must be used in the depository system.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 02/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 17/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 18/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly execution, clearing, and settlement of the delisting offer for Jindal Photo Limited, providing clear timelines and compliance mechanics for market participants and custodians.
