## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the imposition, revision, and movement of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group transfers effective on specified dates in November 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. November 27, 2026, on all open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and lower price band applicable w.e.f. November 25, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continued in LT-ASM framework transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 27, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the revised LT-ASM framework including margin requirements, price band adjustments, and group transfers based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and account for lower price bands and group transfers from the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- November 25, 2026: Lower price band applicable; movement of securities to higher stages or moving out of LT-ASM.
- November 27, 2026: 100% margins applicable; transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Group.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in securities under LT-ASM will experience 100% margin blocking, price band restrictions, and potential liquidity changes due to group transfers.
