## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the ongoing rights issue of Fratelli Vineyards Limited has been extended up to Wednesday, November 18, 2026, in continuation of notice no. 20261105-2.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fratelli Vineyards Limited
- Rights Issue Extension: Extended up to Wednesday, November 18, 2026.
- Reference Notice: 20261105-2.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the revised schedule for submission and processing of applications related to the rights issue.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026
- Extended Rights Issue Closing Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Offers eligible shareholders and investors extended time to participate in the rights issue of Fratelli Vineyards Limited.
