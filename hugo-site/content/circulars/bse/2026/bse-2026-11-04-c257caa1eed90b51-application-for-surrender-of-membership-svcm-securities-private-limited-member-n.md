## Summary

SVCM Securities Private Limited (Member no. 6737) has applied to surrender its membership of the Exchange in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: SVCM Securities Private Limited
- Member Number: 6737
- Department: Membership / Compliance
- Objection Deadline: November 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative procedure regarding the surrender of exchange membership.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members wishing to raise any objection to the surrender of the aforesaid membership must submit their objections in writing to the Membership Department on or before November 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Objection Deadline: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact, restricted to the discontinuation of trading membership services for SVCM Securities Private Limited.
