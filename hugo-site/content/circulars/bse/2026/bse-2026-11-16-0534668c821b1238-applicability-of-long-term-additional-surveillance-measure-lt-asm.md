## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability and revision of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to the revised LT-ASM framework. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfer of securities to specific settlement groups (T / XT / MT / TS) effective November 17 and November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **100% Margins**: Applicable w.e.f. November 19, 2026, on all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created from November 19, 2026, onwards (Annexure I Part A).
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands**: Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM attract lower price bands w.e.f. November 17, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- **Group Transfers**: Securities continued in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. November 19, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- **Exits**: List of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. November 17, 2026 (Annexure II).
- **Consolidated List**: Attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Application of lower price bands for securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM.
- Transfer of specific securities under LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS settlement groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin maintenance for applicable securities from November 19, 2026.
- Trading participants must take note of price band revisions and group transfers effective November 17 and November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **17 Nov 2026**: Effective date for movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and removal of securities from LT-ASM framework.
- **18 Nov 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **19 Nov 2026**: Effective date for 100% margins applicability and transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities will experience increased margin requirements and liquidity adjustments due to lower price bands and transfer to trade-for-trade groups, ensuring market integrity and risk mitigation.
