## Summary

Tech Mahindra Limited has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 1:1 (one bonus equity share of Rs. 5/- each for every one existing equity share held). The company has fixed November 30, 2026, as the record date to determine eligible shareholders.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tech Mahindra Limited (Scrip Code: 532755)
- **Bonus Ratio:** 1:1 (1 bonus share for every 1 existing share held)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 5/- each
- **Ex-Bonus Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Record Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Estimated Allotment:** 980,655,356 equity shares as of December 1, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice under listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the ex-bonus date and record date for trade settlements in Tech Mahindra Limited shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Ex-Bonus Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Record Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the equity shares of Tech Mahindra Limited will be on an ex-bonus basis starting November 30, 2026. Shareholders appearing in the register as of the record date will receive additional bonus shares.
