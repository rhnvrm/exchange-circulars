## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills)—91-day, 182-day, and 364-day—scheduled for November 11, 2026, based on the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) press release.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Three T-Bills are offered: 91 Day T-Bill (Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (Issue Size: ₹7,000 Cr).
- Direct investors bid collection ends on November 10, 2026, at 5:00 PM, while members bid collection ends on November 11, 2026, at 8:00 AM.
- Auction date is November 11, 2026, and settlement date is November 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is a standard operational circular for Government Securities auctions conducted by RBI.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module within the specified timelines and ensure adequate margins/collateral and pay-in obligations are met through ICCL.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: November 09, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): November 10, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): November 11, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: November 11, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of retail and institutional investors in Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through electronic platforms with smooth settlement via ICCL.
