## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application, revision, and removal of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) for specified securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable w.e.f. October 30, 2026 on all open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands and higher stages of LT-ASM become effective from October 28, 2026 for securities in Annexure I (Part B).
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. October 30, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated, subject to no other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions including 100% margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations under the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the price band and group changes for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands & Moving Out / Higher Stages: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to strict margin and price band controls, affecting liquidity and position management for market participants.
