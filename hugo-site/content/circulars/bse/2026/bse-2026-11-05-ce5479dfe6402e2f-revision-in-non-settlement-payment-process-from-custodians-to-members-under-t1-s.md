## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced that the settlement cycle for non-settlement related payments from Custodians to Trading Members is being realigned from T+2 to T+1, in line with the Cash Market segment.

## Key Points

- Settlement cycle for non-settlement related payments realigned from T+2 to T+1.
- Custodians shall provide the non-settlement related payment file in the prescribed format (Annexure I) on T+1 (settlement) day by 4:00 PM.
- Clearing Corporation shall debit the Custodian's clearing bank account and credit the corresponding amount to the Clearing Member's clearing bank account on the same day.
- A report containing details of such payments shall be made available to Trading Members (Annexure II).

## Regulatory Changes

The settlement timeline for non-settlement related payments between custodians and trading members has been accelerated from T+2 to T+1.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members and Custodians must make necessary system and operational changes to ensure smooth implementation of the T+1 non-settlement payment process.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Example Trade Date 1: Thursday, 12th November, 2026 | File Upload: Friday, 13th November, 2026 | Credit Transfer: Monday, 16th November, 2026
- Example Trade Date 2: Friday, 13th November, 2026 | File Upload: Monday, 16th November, 2026 | Credit Transfer: Monday, 16th November, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational workflows between custodians and trading members will experience faster fund transfers and reporting aligned with the T+1 settlement cycle.
