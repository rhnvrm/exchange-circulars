## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Applicable surveillance actions include enhanced margin requirements effective November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 20, 2026 and new positions created from November 23, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) stages with stricter margin rates to control excessive volatility and speculation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) applicable to open positions as of November 20, 2026, and new positions from November 23, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date: November 20, 2026 (for open positions)
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities listed under Annexure I will experience higher margin blocking, impacting liquidity and capital allocation for open and new positions in the short term.
