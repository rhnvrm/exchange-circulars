## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) of Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from November 3, 2026, to November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement Number:** 2026133
- **Bid Start Date:** 03/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 09/11/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 16/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 17/11/2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Buyback)" and Settlement No. 2026133.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members can refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; standard execution schedule under the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities reflects in BSE/BSECL systems prior to bidding and custodian confirmation timelines.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Uploading of EPN files is not required.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** November 3, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** November 9, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** November 16, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution and settlement of the buyback offer for Automobile Corporation of Goa Limited, outlining clear operational deadlines and depository procedures for trading members and custodians.
