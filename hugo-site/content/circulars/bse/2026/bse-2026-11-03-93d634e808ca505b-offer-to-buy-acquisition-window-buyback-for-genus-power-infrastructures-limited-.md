## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window) for the buyback of equity shares of Genus Power Infrastructures Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from Wednesday, November 4, 2026, to Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: GENUS POWER INFRASTRUCTURES LIMITED
- Security Symbol: GENUSPOWER
- ISIN: INE955D01029
- Scrip Code: 530343
- Offer Size: Up to 47,50,000 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 312.00 per share
- Opening Date: Wednesday, 4th November 2026
- Closing Date: Wednesday, 11th November 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational schedule notice for an ongoing corporate action (buyback).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure UCC of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Refer to Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29 for operational instructions.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: November 4, 2026
- Closing Date / Bid Modifications / Give-up / Take-up: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of shareholders and trading members in the buyback acquisition window for Genus Power Infrastructures Limited.
