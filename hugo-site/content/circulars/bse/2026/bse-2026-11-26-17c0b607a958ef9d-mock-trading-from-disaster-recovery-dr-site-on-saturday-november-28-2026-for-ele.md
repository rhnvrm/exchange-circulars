## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026, enabling members to test trading applications and fulfill regulatory requirements.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment.
- Fulfills regulatory testing requirements under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- Applicable to TWS users, ETI users, third-party empaneled vendors, and in-house system developers.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 1:00 PM on the mock trading day.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can utilize this mock trading session or the UAT environment to fulfill software testing requirements related to trading and risk management in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: November 28, 2026
- Feedback Deadline: November 28, 2026 (1:00 PM)
- Session Timings: Log-in (09:15 AM - 10:00 AM), Pre-Open / Order Entry (10:00 AM - 10:08 AM), Matching (10:08 AM - 10:15 AM), Continuous Trading (10:15 AM - 12:00 PM), Block Deal (11:00 AM - 11:15 AM), Closing (12:00 PM - 12:10 PM), Post-closing (12:10 PM - 12:20 PM).

## Impact Assessment

Zero financial or operational impact as sessions are purely for testing, familiarization, and regulatory compliance.
