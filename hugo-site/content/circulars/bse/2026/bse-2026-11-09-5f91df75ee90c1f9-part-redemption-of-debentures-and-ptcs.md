## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that several companies have fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and PTCs. Trading in these securities will proceed with reduced face values effective from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Record Date & Ex-Date:** November 12, 2026.
- **Affected Issuers:** OXYZO Financial Services Limited, Achievers Finance India Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited, Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited, Pahal Financial Services Private Limited, and Home First Finance Company India Limited.
- **Segment:** Debt Segment (Listing Operations).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action notice regarding debt redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the reduced face values for the respective NCDs and ensure accurate settlement processing.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 9, 2026
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Bondholders and investors will receive part redemption payments as scheduled, with subsequent trading conducted on the revised reduced face values.
