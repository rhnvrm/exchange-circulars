## Summary

BSE has released the settlement schedule for shorter tenure contracts under the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) Reverse Leg Segment for the period from December 01, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates and corresponding SLR pay-in/pay-out dates for December 2026.
- Depository settlement numbers range from 2627870 to 2627890.
- Pay-in shall be conducted at 09:00 a.m., with members required to submit instructions to Depositories/banks by 08:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. All other provisions relating to market timings, clearing, settlement, risk management, and corporate actions remain unchanged.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/participants must submit pay-in instructions to Depositories or banks latest by 08:50 a.m. on the respective pay-in dates.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** December 01, 2026, to December 31, 2026
- **First Trade Date:** December 01, 2026 (Settlement No: 2627870, Pay-in/Pay-out: December 04, 2026)
- **Last Trade Date:** December 30, 2026 (Settlement No: 2627890, Pay-in/Pay-out: January 04, 2027)

## Impact Assessment

Routine scheduling update for market participants engaging in SLB reverse leg transactions during December 2026.
