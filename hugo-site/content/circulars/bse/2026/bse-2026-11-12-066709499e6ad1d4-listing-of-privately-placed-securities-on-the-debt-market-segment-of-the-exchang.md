## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers (CPs) on the debt market segment effective November 12, 2026. The issuances include companies such as Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Sammaan Capital Limited, and Network 18 Media & Investments Limited.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Securities:** Commercial Papers (CPs) issued on a private placement basis.
- **Trading Form:** Dematerialised form under standard ISINs with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples.
- **Tick Size:** 1 paise.
