## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in an auction regarding Rolling Settlement No. DR-748/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on November 04, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on November 04, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Thursday, November 05, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the 'Auction offer Entry Screen' before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date**: November 04, 2026
- **Pay-in Date**: November 05, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational settlement notice with minimal market impact, affecting only participants with short delivery obligations in Rolling Settlement DR-748/2026-27.
