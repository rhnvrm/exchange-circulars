## Summary

BSE has announced the opening of the acquisition window for the open offer to acquire up to 3,19,71,680 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the expanded voting share capital) of Kuber Udyog Limited at an offer price of Rs. 23.35 per share payable in cash.

## Key Points

- **Acquirers:** Mr. Manav Bahri (Acquirer 1), Mr. Dinesh Popli (Acquirer 2), Mr. Ajay Dutta (Acquirer 3), along with PACs (Trimudra Trade & Holdings Private Limited and Ms. Shraddha Gohil).
- **Target Company:** Kuber Udyog Limited.
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 23.35 per Equity Share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 3,19,71,680 fully paid-up equity shares (26.00% of Expanded Voting Share Capital).
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, October 28, 2026 to Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, November 10, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and SEBI circulars on acquisition mechanisms through stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delisting.
- Market participants must ensure compliance with BSE and SEBI operational guidelines during the offer period.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** October 28, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (No Trading):** November 10, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public shareholders of Kuber Udyog Limited an exit opportunity through the stock exchange acquisition window under SEBI takeovers regulations.
