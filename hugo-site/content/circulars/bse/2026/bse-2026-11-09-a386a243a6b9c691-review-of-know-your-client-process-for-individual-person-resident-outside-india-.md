## Summary

BSE has issued a notice drawing attention to SEBI circular ref. no. HO/38/30/12(3)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25175/2026 dated November 04, 2026, concerning the review of the Know Your Client (KYC) process for individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) clients.

## Key Points

- Draws attention to SEBI circular dated November 04, 2026.
- Focuses on the review of the KYC process for individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) clients.
- Encloses the SEBI circular as Annexure A.
- Members are required to take note of and comply with the guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Review and updates to the Know Your Client (KYC) requirements specifically for individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) clients as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review and comply with the revised KYC process for individual PROI clients as detailed in the enclosed SEBI circular.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 09, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures streamlined and updated KYC onboarding and compliance standards for foreign individual clients (PROI) trading through Indian stock exchanges.
