## Summary

BSE has published the settlement programme and schedule for the Futures and Options (F&O) segment for the period from December 01, 2026, to December 31, 2026, on behalf of Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd.

## Key Points

- Outlines daily trade dates and corresponding settlement dates for the F&O segment across December 2026.
- Issued by Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. under notice number 20261116-45.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; routine operational calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the settlement schedule to ensure timely funds and securities management for derivatives transactions throughout December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Programme Effective From: 01-12-2026
- Programme Effective To: 31-12-2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless clearing and settlement operations for market participants trading in the derivatives segment during the specified period.
