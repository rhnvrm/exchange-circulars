## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd) has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) pertaining to The South India Paper Mills Limited (Settlement No. 2026127).

## Key Points

- **Company:** The South India Paper Mills Limited
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026127
- **Original Bidding Period:** 22/10/2026 to 04/11/2026 (per Notice No. 20261019-2)
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 11/11/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 12/11/2026
- **Share Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism across depositories using Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026127.
- **Stamp Duty:** Client-wise stamp duty charges are available in the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of settlement timelines for the takeover acquisition window pursuant to established stock exchange mechanisms for share acquisitions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere to Notice No. 20200528-32 regarding settlement under revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchanges.
- Ensure demat shares are delivered via Early Pay-in of Shares using the correct Market Type and Settlement Number.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Period:** 22/10/2026 to 04/11/2026 (Concluded)
- **Revised Obligation Download:** November 11, 2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and custodians must update internal settlement calendars to reflect the revised obligation download (Nov 11, 2026) and settlement date (Nov 12, 2026), ensuring timely Early Pay-in of securities.
