## Summary

BSE has announced updates regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, including securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, moving to lower stages, or exiting the framework. Affected securities will be subject to revised margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on open positions as of October 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities will move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. October 28, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 28, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, moving securities between stages, shifting settlement segments to Trade-for-Trade, imposing 100% margins, and applying price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements (minimum 100%), settlement segment shifts (Rolling to Trade-for-Trade), and price band/periodic call auction rules for securities listed in the annexures. Clearances and queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- October 28, 2026: Effective date for securities moving to lower Stage I, securities shifting to Stage II (Trade-for-Trade with 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction), and securities moving out of ESM.
- October 29, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirement on open positions (as of Oct 28) and new positions, and shifting of qualifying ESM securities to Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in shortlisted securities will face higher margin obligations (100%), liquidity constraints due to Trade-for-Trade settlement, and price band restrictions, mitigating excessive volatility as part of exchange surveillance.
