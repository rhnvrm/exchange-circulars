## Summary

BSE has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of IRONWOOD EDUCATION LIMITED starting from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 751162
- Scrip ID: IRONWO-RE
- ISIN No: INE791H20011
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 25, 2026 to December 01, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: November 25, 2026 to December 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action notice for rights entitlements trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Proper disclaimers regarding renunciation and lapse of unsubscribed REs must be included in contract notes.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: November 25, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: December 01, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: November 25, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders of Ironwood Education Limited to trade or renounce their rights entitlements on a trade-for-trade basis under T+1 rolling settlement.
