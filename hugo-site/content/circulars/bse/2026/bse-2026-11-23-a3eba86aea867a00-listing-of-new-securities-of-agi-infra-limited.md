## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of new equity shares of AGI Infra Limited issued through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP). The shares will be available for trading starting November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** AGI Infra Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539042
- **ISIN:** INE976R01033
- **Security Details:** 1,03,77,358 Equity shares of Rs. 1/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 265/- per share
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 124997389 to 135374746
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Date of Allotment:** November 18, 2026

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the newly listed securities and their inclusion in trading segments.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The issuance and listing of QIP shares increases AGI Infra Limited's paid-up equity capital and enhances liquidity through institutional participation.
