## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 1,53,161 new equity shares of Mercantile Ventures Limited (Scrip Code: 538942) with a face value of Rs. 10/- each are listed and permitted for trading with effect from Monday, November 30, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of India Radiators Limited (Transferor Company) with Mercantile Ventures Limited (Transferee Company).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mercantile Ventures Limited
- Scrip Code: 538942
- ISIN Number: INE689O01013
- Number of Shares: 1,53,161 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Distribution Nos.: 111918196 - 112071356
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 30, 2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; execution of approved scheme of amalgamation under corporate restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing of new securities and ensure proper settlement procedures for transactions involving the new distribution numbers and ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Mercantile Ventures Limited following the amalgamation of India Radiators Limited, with standard operational and trading permissions on BSE.
