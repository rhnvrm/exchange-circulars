## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy acquisition window for the open offer made to the public shareholders of Tusaldah Limited (formerly known as High Street Filatex Limited) by Mr. Sandeep Agrawal and Mrs. Anupriya Sandeep Agrawal. The open offer is for up to 37,83,000 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the expanded equity and voting share capital) at an offer price of Rs. 20/- per share in cash.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Tusaldah Limited (formerly known as High Street Filatex Limited).
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Sandeep Agrawal (Acquirer 1) and Mrs. Anupriya Sandeep Agrawal (Acquirer 2).
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 20/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 37,83,000 fully paid-up equity shares representing 26.00% of the expanded equity and voting share capital.
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, December 2, 2026, to Tuesday, December 15, 2026.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Pursuant to Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 3(2) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 and subsequent amendments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016, and subsequent exchange notices regarding the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeovers, Buy Back, and Delisting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the operational schedule and guidelines for tendering shares through the acquisition window on the stock exchange platform during the offer period.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** December 2, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** December 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public shareholders of Tusaldah Limited an exit window via tender offer through the stock exchange mechanism under SEBI Takeover regulations.
