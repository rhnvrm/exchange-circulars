## Summary

The Exchange has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026, from the Primary Site (PR). Members can participate to test their trading applications, algorithms, and risk management software.

## Key Points

- Mock trading is scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- Applicable for the Commodity Derivatives segment from the Primary Site (PR).
- Helps members fulfill regulatory requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80 release details will be provided via a separate circular.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 regarding software testing and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using third-party platforms or in-house APIs can utilize this session to test functionalities such as risk-reduction mode and trading halts.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock trading session by 4:30 pm via contact numbers or email provided by BSE.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 14, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 10:15 am to 11:00 am
- **Continuous Trading (PR):** 11:00 am to 03:30 pm
- **Feedback Deadline:** November 14, 2026, by 4:30 pm

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational exercise with zero financial, margin, or settlement impact on market participants.
