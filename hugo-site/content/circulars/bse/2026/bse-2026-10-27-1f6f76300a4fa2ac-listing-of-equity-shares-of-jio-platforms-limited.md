## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Jio Platforms Limited effective from Wednesday, October 28, 2026, under the 'A' Group of Securities. A total of 9,20,90,30,830 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each fully paid up are listed under scrip code 545004 and ISIN INE0BJO01010.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID on BOLT System: JPL
- Scrip Code: 545004
- ISIN No.: INE0BJO01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 1119/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 1109/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Securities will be traded only in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are admitted under the 'A' Group of Securities and will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per prospectus/annexures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must adhere to demat trading guidelines and SPOS provisions for IPOs and relisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Jio Platforms Limited equity shares on the BSE exchange platform, providing liquidity to investors and expanding market participation.
