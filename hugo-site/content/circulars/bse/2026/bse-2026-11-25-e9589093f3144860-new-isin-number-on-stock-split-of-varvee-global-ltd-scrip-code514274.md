## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Varvee Global Ltd (Scrip Code: 514274) will be INE273D01035. This follows the sub-division of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Ltd
- Scrip Code: 514274
- New ISIN Number: INE273D01035
- Corporate Action: Stock Split / Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 5/- to Rs. 2/-
- Reference Exchange Notice: No. 20261119-12 dated November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records with the new ISIN number (INE273D01035) for all trades and transactions in Varvee Global Ltd equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 27, 2026 (DR-758/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

Trades executed on and from the ex-date (November 27, 2026) will be under the new ISIN. Shareholders and brokers must ensure systems reflect the revised ISIN and face value.
