## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 12,25,800 equity shares have been subscribed under the anchor investors portion in the public issue of Wiscon Industries Limited at Rs. 204/- per equity share on October 26, 2026.

- Company Name: Wiscon Industries Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO
- Anchor Shares Subscribed: 12,25,800 equity shares
- Issue / Allocation Price: Rs. 204/- per share
- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
