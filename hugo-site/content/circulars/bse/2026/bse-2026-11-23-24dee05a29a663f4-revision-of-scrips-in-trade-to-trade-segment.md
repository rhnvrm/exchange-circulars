## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of scrips in the trade-to-trade (T/XT/MT/TS) segment as part of surveillance measures based on the extant SEBI framework. Specified securities meeting criteria I, II, and III will be transferred to trade-to-trade settlement effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria I, II, and III will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective November 27, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II will continue to remain in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Group with a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed.
- A VAR Margin of 100% will be levied on these scrips pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20050805-12 dated August 05, 2005.
- The transfer is a preventive surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of eligible securities to trade-to-trade settlement categories as per surveillance review in consultation with SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, noting that settlement is on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off positions and subject to a 100% VAR margin.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Transfer: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading activity in affected scrips will face liquidity and settlement constraints due to the 100% VAR margin and absence of position netting, serving as a preventive market safety measure.
