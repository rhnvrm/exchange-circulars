## Summary

BSE has announced the deferral of the effective date for the introduction of interoperability among Clearing Corporations (CCs) for Tender Offers (OTB), previously scheduled for on or after November 27, 2026. A revised implementation date will be communicated through a subsequent notice.

## Key Points

- Implementation of interoperability among Clearing Corporations for Tender Offers (OTB) is deferred.
- Existing operational processes will continue unchanged until further notice.
- Clearing Members and Custodians clearing through BSECL in the Capital Market segment remain mapped to BSECL as their designated clearing corporation under the Tender Offer framework.
- Members and Custodians may change their designated Clearing Corporation for Tender Offers according to procedures prescribed by respective Clearing Corporations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Postponement of the interoperability framework implementation timeline for Tender Offers (OTB).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must maintain existing workflows and await further notices regarding the revised implementation schedule.

## Important Dates

- November 27, 2026: Original effective date (deferred).
- Future Date: Revised implementation date to be announced separately.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal immediate operational disruption as existing clearing and settlement workflows remain active.
