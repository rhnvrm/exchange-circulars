## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 20 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Monday, November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 20 companies issued further equity shares under ESOP / ESOS.
- Trading effective date: November 23, 2026.
- Companies include Ather Energy Limited, Federal Bank Limited, HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, ITC Limited, Mphasis Limited, Tata Consumer Products Limited, and others.
- Lock-in status is mostly 'NA' except for specific securities like ITC Limited (as per Annexure 1).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with exchange listing and trading guidelines for newly admitted ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marginal expansion of paid-up equity capital and equity float for the respective listed companies due to ESOP exercises.
