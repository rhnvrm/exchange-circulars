## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. Trading members can test their trading applications, third-party platforms, and APIs during this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026, for the EGR segment.
- Fulfills regulatory requirements under SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020, and BSE Notice No 20201201-22.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80 shall be released with separate circular details.
- Feedback on the mock trading session is requested by 4:30 pm via contact numbers or email provided.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; facilitates compliance testing under existing SEBI and BSE guidelines for software and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may choose to participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026
- Feedback Deadline: November 14, 2026 (by 4:30 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Mock trading is purely for testing and familiarization purposes; trades will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities.
