## Summary

Ashiana Housing Ltd has announced a part redemption of its debentures (Scrip Code: 973220, ISIN: INE365D08026), resulting in a reduced face value of Rs. 611,399.41 per debenture, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ashiana Housing Ltd
- Debenture Details: AHL-8%-31-5-41-PVT (ISIN: INE365D08026, Scrip Code: 973220)
- Purpose: Part redemption of debentures and reduction in face value.
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 611,399.41 per debenture.
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026 (DR-745/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to trade the debentures with the aforementioned reduced face value with effect from October 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Operational update for trading members regarding the revised face value and redemption of debentures for Ashiana Housing Ltd.
