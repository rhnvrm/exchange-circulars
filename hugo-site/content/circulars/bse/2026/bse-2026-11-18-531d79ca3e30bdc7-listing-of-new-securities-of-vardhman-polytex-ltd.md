## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 2,54,00,000 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued by Vardhman Polytex Ltd. to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vardhman Polytex Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 514175
- ISIN: INE835A01029
- Securities: 2,54,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 12.55/- per share (including premium of Rs. 11.55/-)
- Allottee: Promoter on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion
- Lock-in Details: All 2,54,00,000 shares are locked-in up to May 31, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and trading permissions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing action with minimal impact on overall market operations.
