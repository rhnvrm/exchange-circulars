## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 27, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure will move to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, or IV) starting November 27, 2026.
- Stage I attracts 100% margin and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II introduces trade-for-trade settlement and a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
- Stages III and IV restrict trading to once a week (Mondays) with 100% ASD and trade-for-trade settlement.
- Settlement will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures under the GSM framework to monitor and curb excessive price volatility in specific securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Ensure compliance with applicable margin rates, ASD deposits, and restriction on netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact on the securities moved to higher GSM stages due to stricter trading restrictions, mandatory margin deposits, and weekly trading limitations for higher stages.
