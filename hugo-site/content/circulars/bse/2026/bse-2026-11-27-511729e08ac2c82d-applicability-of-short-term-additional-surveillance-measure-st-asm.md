## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities that have satisfied the inclusion criteria, effective December 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective December 1, 2026, on all open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II will attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective December 1, 2026, on all open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- The shortlist is based purely on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions on specified securities pursuant to the revised ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on open positions as of November 30, 2026, and new positions created from December 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: December 01, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin obligations, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
