## Summary

BSE has issued a corrigendum to Notice No. 20260701-16 dated July 01, 2026, regarding the listing of 8,51,137 equity shares of JMJ Fintech Ltd. resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares of Rs. 10/- each. The exchange has revised the distinctive range of securities for trading members.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JMJ Fintech Ltd. (Scrip Code: 538834)
- Subject: Corrigendum to Notice of Listing of New Securities
- Revised Distinctive Ranges:
  - 6,11,860 shares: Distinctive numbers 36784412 to 37396271
  - 2,39,277 shares: Distinctive numbers 37406272 to 37645548
- All other terms and content of the original notice remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange should take note of the revised distinctive range of securities for JMJ Fintech Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Original Notice Date: July 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for trading members with no direct financial impact on market participants.
