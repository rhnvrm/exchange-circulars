## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the measure for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance, updating the list of securities included in or eligible to move out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective October 30, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework effective October 28, 2026.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities currently under the framework.
- Measures operate in conjunction with existing exchange regulations and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of specified encumbered securities under enhanced margin requirements (minimum 75% margin) and removal of eligible securities from the framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure minimum 75% margin requirements are maintained on open positions as of October 29, 2026, and new positions created from October 30, 2026, for securities listed in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for exit (Annexure II): October 28, 2026
- Open positions snapshot date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date for margin imposition (Annexure I): October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants holding positions in securities listed under Annexure I will experience increased margin obligations (minimum 75%), affecting capital allocation and liquidity in equity and derivatives segments.
