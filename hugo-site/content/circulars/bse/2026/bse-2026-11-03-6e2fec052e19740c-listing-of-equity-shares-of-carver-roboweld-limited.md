## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of 1,31,02,805 equity shares of Carver Roboweld Limited effective from Wednesday, November 4, 2026, under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Carver Roboweld Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 545015
- **Scrip ID / Symbol:** CRL
- **ISIN:** INE1FY301017
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 80/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 70/-)
- **Number of Securities:** 1,31,02,805 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 1,600 equity shares
- **Date of Allotment:** November 2, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Shreni Shares Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine admission of SME IPO securities to exchange trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure securities are traded in dematerialized form only.
- Trades must be executed in the minimum market lot of 1,600 equity shares.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on November 4, 2026.
- Lock-in period requirements have been marked in the depository's records as per applicable guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 2, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment Duration:** 10 trading days starting November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading of Carver Roboweld Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard surveillance measures.
