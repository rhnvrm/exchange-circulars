## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Kanel Industries Limited will commence on Monday, November 23, 2026. The trading period for renunciation is scheduled from November 23, 2026, to November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Kanel Industries Limited
- **Type of Security:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Scrip Code:** 751161
- **Scrip ID:** KANE-RE
- **ISIN No.:** INE252C20015
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** November 23, 2026 to November 25, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 23, 2026 to December 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Permits trading in Rights Entitlements on the Exchange under T+1 rolling settlement on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting the ISIN and clarifying that REs are not ordinary shares.
- Contract notes and bills for RE purchases must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce before closure.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 23, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Rights Issue Open Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Rights Issue Close Date:** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade, renounce, or utilize Rights Entitlements for Kanel Industries Limited within the defined timelines under strict trade-for-trade settlement.
