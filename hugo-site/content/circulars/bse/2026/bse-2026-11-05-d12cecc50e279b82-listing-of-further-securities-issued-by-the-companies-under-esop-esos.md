## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 19 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading starting Friday, November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 19
- Effective date for trading: November 6, 2026
- Includes prominent companies such as AXIS Bank Limited, InterGlobe Aviation Limited, Max Healthcare Institute Limited, and One 97 Communications Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and issuers must adhere to standard exchange listing requirements and applicable lock-in periods (e.g., Aeonx Digital Technology Limited shares locked-in up to 10/08/2027).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital expansion via ESOP exercise with negligible immediate market impact.
