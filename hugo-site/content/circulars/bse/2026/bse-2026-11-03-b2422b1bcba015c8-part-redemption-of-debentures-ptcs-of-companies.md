## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies and issuers have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Trading in these securities will take place with reduced face values effective from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Nashville 03 (Scrip Code: 975584):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 0.01 per debenture.
- **Rafale - 2025 (Scrip Code: 977338):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 0.13 per debenture.
- **Vajra 018 Trust (Scrip Code: 977524):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 7507097.15 per debenture.
- **Liquid Gold Series 18 (Scrip Code: 977555):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 574400.74 per debenture.
- **Platinum Well-2026 (Scrip Code: 977608):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 0.39 per debenture.
- **Aven Trust 2026 (Scrip Code: 978193):** Record date 05/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 9533.98 per debenture.
- **Indigo 045 (Scrip Code: 977201):** Record date 06/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 2138694.39 per debenture.
- **SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited (Scrip Code: 978285):** Record date 07/11/2026 (Ex-date 06/11/2026); reduced face value Rs. 75,000/- per debenture.
- **Vivriti Capital Limited (Scrip Code: 976532):** Record date 09/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 71,440/- per debenture.
- **Stellar 1.3 2024 (Scrip Code: 976572):** Record date 09/11/2026; reduced face value Rs. 1792586.15 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding debt redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure trading/settlement operations align with the updated redemption values.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** November 5, 2026, to November 9, 2026.
- **Ex-Dates:** November 5, 2026, to November 9, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures transparency in debt trading and accurate settlement pricing reflecting part redemption of principal amounts for investors.
