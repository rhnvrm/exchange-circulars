## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund has announced the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, and all existing investments through Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in three specific schemes, effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: ADITYA BIRLA SUN LIFE INTERNATIONAL EQUITY FUND, ADITYA BIRLA SUN LIFE GLOBAL EMERGING OPPORTUNITIES FUND, and ADITYA BIRLA SUN LIFE GLOBAL EXCELLENCE EQUITY FUND OF FUND.
- Fresh registrations for SIP and STP in these schemes have been disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STPAMC) will be paused and will not be triggered or processed until further notice.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions remain fully permitted without amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of fund-level restrictions on fresh inflows and systematic registrations in compliance with AMC notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must note that fresh SIP/STP registrations are disabled on BSE StAR MF.
- Existing systematic plans are paused automatically.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Cut-off: Close of business hours on November 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or planning investments in the affected Aditya Birla Sun Life global/international schemes will need to adjust their investment strategies.
- Trading members and distributors must update clients regarding the pause on systematic registrations and unavailability of fresh subscriptions.
