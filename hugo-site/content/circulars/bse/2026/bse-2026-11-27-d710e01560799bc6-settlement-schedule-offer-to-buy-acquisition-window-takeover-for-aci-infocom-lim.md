## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy (Takeover) of ACI Infocom Limited. The bidding window will remain open from November 30, 2026, to December 11, 2026, under settlement type Takeover (Settlement No. 2026143).

## Key Points

- **Bidding Period**: 30/11/2026 to 11/12/2026.
- **Settlement Type & No.**: Takeover, Settlement No. 2026143.
- **Obligation Download**: 17/12/2026.
- **Settlement Date**: 18/12/2026.
- **Early Pay-in**: Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL using Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026143.
- **Stamp Duty**: Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this notice pertains to standard settlement operations under revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in effects are reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before bidding/confirmation window closure.
- Shares will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts for the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date**: November 30, 2026
- **Bid End Date**: December 11, 2026
- **Obligation Download**: December 17, 2026
- **Settlement Date**: December 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth execution, settlement transparency, and timely delivery of shares for shareholders participating in the takeover offer of ACI Infocom Limited.
