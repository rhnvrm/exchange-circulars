## Summary

Interarch Building Solutions Ltd has announced the sub-division of its equity shares. Existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into five equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each, effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Interarch Building Solutions Ltd (Scrip Code: 544232)
- Action: Sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- each into 5 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.
- Record Date: 30/11/2026
- Old ISIN (Rs. 10/- paid up): INE00M901018 (invalid for transactions on/after 30/11/2026).
- New ISIN (Rs. 2/- paid up): To be notified separately by the exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replacement of existing ISIN with a new ISIN corresponding to the new face value of Rs. 2/- per share.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note that transactions on or after the record date (30/11/2026) will require the new ISIN and face value structure. Old ISIN INE00M901018 will cease to be valid for exchange transactions.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 30/11/2026
- Effective Date / W.E.F.: 30/11/2026
- Notice Date: 02/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares in dematerialised form will receive five shares of Rs. 2/- face value for every one share of Rs. 10/- face value held as of the record date. Trading liquidity and share price will adjust proportionately.
