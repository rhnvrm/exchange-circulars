## Summary

Trishakti Industries Limited has announced the listing and permission to trade 4,18,000 new equity shares issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Trishakti Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 531279)
- Number of Securities: 4,18,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 158.10/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 156.10/-)
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis
- ISIN: INE238C01022
- Distinctive Numbers: 16476551 to 16894550
- Lock-in Details: 4,18,000 shares locked in up to 29/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action processing under existing listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in period and distinctive numbers for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 02/09/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 29/10/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 29/04/2027

## Impact Assessment

Minor increase in equity capital base of the company with standard lock-in restrictions applied to preferential allottees.
