## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) of ARITRA GROWTH AQUA MARCH 2025 (Scrip Code: 976569, ISIN: INE1TBR15010) with effect from November 16, 2026, ahead of its maturity.

## Key Points

- PTC Name: AGAM25-11%-17-11-26-PTC
- Scrip Code: 976569
- ISIN: INE1TBR15010
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 16, 2026
- Maturity Date: November 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the aforesaid PTCs of ARITRA GROWTH AQUA MARCH 2025 with effect from November 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 16, 2026
- Maturity Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants holding these PTCs must take note of the suspension and maturity schedule.
