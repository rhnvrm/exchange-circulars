## Summary

BSE has announced a scheduled maintenance activity for the BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 platform. During this period, the application, web portal, and API interfaces will remain unavailable.

## Key Points

- Planned maintenance for BSE StAR MF Version 2.0.
- Scheduled time: Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 09:00 PM to Sunday, November 22, 2026, 01:00 AM.
- Web portal and API interfaces will be offline during the maintenance window.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to inform their respective technical teams and vendors to plan their activities accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Maintenance Window: November 21, 2026, 21:00 IST to November 22, 2026, 01:00 IST.

## Impact Assessment

Temporary downtime for BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 services. Trading and operational activities dependent on the platform should be scheduled outside the maintenance window.
