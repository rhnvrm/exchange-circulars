## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers listed for Nexus Select Trust (Scrip Code: 732906, ISIN: INE0NDH14197, Rs. 50 Lakhs face value, 7.85% p.a., maturing on 21/01/2027).
- Commercial papers listed for ASK Financial Holdings Private Limited (Scrip Code: 732907, ISIN: INE02QA14014, Rs. 50 Lakhs face value, 8% p.a., maturing on 05/02/2027).
- Commercial papers listed for DSP Finance Private Limited (Scrip Code: 732909, ISIN: INE422H14834, Rs. 50 Lakhs face value, 8.78% p.a., maturing on 29/01/2027).
- Commercial papers listed for Navi Finserv Limited (Scrip Code: 732908 & 732911, ISIN: INE342T14IO5 & INE342T14IN7, Rs. 50 Lakhs face value, 9.75% and 9.40% p.a., maturing in 2027).
- Securities are traded in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and 1 paise tick size.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with debt segment trading guidelines, dematerialised holding requirements, and standard denominations.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 06/11/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 09/11/2026
- Redemption / Interest Payment Dates: Various dates between January 2027 and May 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and trading of privately placed commercial papers on the BSE debt market segment for institutional and qualified investors.
