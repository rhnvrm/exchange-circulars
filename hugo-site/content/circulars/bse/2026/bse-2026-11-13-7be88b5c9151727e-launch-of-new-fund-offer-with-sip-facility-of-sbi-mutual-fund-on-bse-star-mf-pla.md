## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund is offering the SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **NFO Period:** November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 30, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 03, 2026.
- **Schemes & ISINs:**
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH: INF200KB1CK4
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT: INF200KB1CM0
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT: INF200KB1CL2
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH: INF200KB1CN8
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT: INF200KB1CP3
  - SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT: INF200KB1CO6

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 03, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 30, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** November 16, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 03, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** December 03, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new SBI Mutual Fund ETF Fund of Fund offering via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational workflows.
