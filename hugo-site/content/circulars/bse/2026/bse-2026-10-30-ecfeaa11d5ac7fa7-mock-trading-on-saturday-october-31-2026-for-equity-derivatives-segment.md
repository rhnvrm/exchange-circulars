## Summary

The BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and exceptional market conditions like risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives.
- **Purpose:** Testing trading applications, ETI APIs, and exceptional market conditions (e.g., risk-reduction mode, trading halt).
- **BOLT TWS Release:** No new BOLT TWS release is scheduled.
- **No Obligations:** Trades resulting from mock trading sessions do not attract margin, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback by 04:00 PM via contact numbers or email provided.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively to test their systems and provide feedback on the mock trading session to BSE Tech Support or Helpdesk.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** October 31, 2026, by 04:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational testing opportunity for trading members with zero financial impact or margin obligations.
