## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities meeting inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, shifting to trade-for-trade settlement, and attracting revised margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting ESM criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 13, 2026, on open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Specified securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. November 12, 2026, is provided in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) updates including margin adjustments, price band restrictions, and settlement segment migrations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must account for 100% margin requirements on applicable securities and comply with Trade-for-Trade settlement and price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- November 12, 2026: Effective date for movement to lower stages, stage II restrictions, and securities moving out of ESM.
- November 13, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Shortlisted securities under the ESM framework experience stricter surveillance, higher margin calls, and trade-for-trade settlement, impacting liquidity and market operations.
