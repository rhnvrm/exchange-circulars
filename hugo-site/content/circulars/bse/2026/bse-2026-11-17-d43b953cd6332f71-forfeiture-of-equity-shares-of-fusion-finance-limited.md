## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Fusion Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 543652) has forfeited 1,63,028 equity shares issued pursuant to its rights issue. These shares, bearing distinctive numbers from 7040919 to 7203946 with a face value of Rs. 10/- each, were partly paid-up and forfeited due to non-payment of allotment or call monies.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fusion Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 543652
- Number of Shares Forfeited: 1,63,028 equity shares
- Distinctive Numbers: 7040919 to 7203946
- Reason: Non-payment of allotment/call monies on partly-paid rights issue shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the forfeiture of these equity shares and ensure no further trading takes place in the forfeited distinctive number range.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The forfeiture of 1,63,028 partly-paid shares has a localized impact on Fusion Finance Limited's capital structure and does not pose systemic risks to the broader market.
