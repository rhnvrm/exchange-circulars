## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in Z group for companies suspended due to non-compliance with Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018, upon completion of the mandated six months period.

## Key Points

- Trading in securities of Vijay Textiles Ltd (Scrip Code: 530151) on a 'Trade for Trade' basis on the first trading day of every week will be discontinued w.e.f. December 15, 2026.
- The mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week completes on December 14, 2026, pursuant to SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023 (last updated January 30, 2026).
- Several companies (Aadhaar Ventures India Ltd, Kailash Auto Finance Ltd, Sanguine Media Ltd, Murae Organisor Ltd) remain already prohibited or suspended from trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of provisions under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 regarding discontinuation of weekly trading for non-compliant suspended companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must ensure continuous compliance with Regulation 76 regarding submission of reconciliation of share capital audit reports to avoid suspension and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **December 14, 2026**: Completion of the mandated six months of weekly trading.
- **December 15, 2026**: Effective date for discontinuation of weekly trading in affected securities.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects investors holding shares in Vijay Textiles Ltd and other listed suspended entities regarding liquidity and weekly trading availability on stock exchanges.
