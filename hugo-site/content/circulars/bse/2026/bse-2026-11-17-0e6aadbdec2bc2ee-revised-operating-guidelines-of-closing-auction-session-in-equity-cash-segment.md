## Summary

BSE has issued revised operating guidelines for conducting the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash Segment, referencing prior SEBI circulars and Exchange guidelines. The circular specifies the applicability of CAS, securities exceptions, precise session timings from 3:30 PM to 3:40 PM, and order handling rules.

## Key Points

- **Applicability**: CAS applies to securities having derivative contracts available at any of the Exchanges.
- **Exceptions**: CAS is not applicable to CDSL and BSE (unless invoked per Business Continuity guidelines), new securities before their first derivative trading date, securities past their last derivative trading date, ex-date scheme of arrangement securities without SPOS price determination, or when market-wide index circuit breakers trigger a day-long halt.
- **Session Duration**: Conducted for 10 minutes from 3:30 PM to 3:40 PM on all trading days.
- **Session Schedule & Phases**:
  - *3:30 PM - 3:31 PM*: Transition from CTS to CAS, dissemination of closing reference price/revised price band, cancellation of pending Stop loss, revealed quantity orders of CTS, and outstanding orders outside revised price band.
  - *3:31 PM - 3:36 PM*: Order Entry Period for both limit and market orders (addition/modification/cancellation, no trades executed).
  - *3:36 PM - 3:40 PM*: Order entry period for limit orders only (addition/modification/cancellation of limit orders only; no market order modification/cancellation; system-driven random stoppage during last 2 minutes).
  - *3:38 PM - 3:45 PM*: Order Matching & Confirmation Period (order matching starts immediately after order entry completion, trade confirmation, and closing price determination).
  - *3:45 PM - 3:50 PM*: Buffer Period facilitating transition to post-closing session (3:50 PM to 4:00 PM).
- **Market Order Restriction**: During 3:36 PM to 3:40 PM (limit orders only period), market order addition, modification, or cancellation is prohibited, and received requests are rejected with the message: "Market order cannot be placed during this period."

## Regulatory Changes

- Detailed operational guidelines and phase timings for the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity cash segment.
- Clear rule boundaries defining eligibility and strict rejection criteria for market orders during the limit-only order entry window.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their trading systems and operational workflows with the revised CAS session timings, phase restrictions, and order validation rules.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: 17 November 2026 (Notice Date)
- **Reference SEBI Circular Date**: October 15, 2026
- **Reference Exchange Circular Dates**: October 16, 2026, March 19, 2026, and June 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Bourse participants, trading members, and automated trading systems must update order management algorithms to adhere strictly to the phased entry windows, market order restrictions, and exception rules during the Closing Auction Session.
