## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF under ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund with effect from Wednesday, October 28, 2026. A total of 2,390,300 units are listed under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF
- Mutual Fund: ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 545003
- Scrip ID / BOLT System Name: PHARMAIETF
- ISIN: INF109K1A716
- Issue Price: Rs. 25.35 per unit
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Market Lot: 1 unit
- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

As per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, units of the Mutual Fund will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form with a unit market lot of one unit.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with trading access to the ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF on the exchange platform.
