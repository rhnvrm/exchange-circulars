## Summary

Jyothy Labs Limited has announced the opening of its share buyback offer through the stock exchange acquisition window. The company proposes to buyback up to 61,25,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹1 each, representing up to 1.67% of its total paid-up equity share capital, at a price of ₹240 per share.

## Key Points

- **Buyback Size:** Up to 61,25,000 equity shares representing 1.67% of the total paid-up equity share capital.
- **Buyback Price:** ₹240 per equity share payable in cash.
- **Total Consideration:** Not exceeding ₹147,00,00,150 (excluding transaction costs).
- **Record Date:** Wednesday, November 11, 2026.
- **Buyback Window Period:** Tuesday, November 17, 2026, to Monday, November 23, 2026.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Conducted pursuant to SEBI tender-offer buyback guidelines and relevant stock exchange notices.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The buyback is executed in accordance with existing SEBI circulars (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131) and exchange guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the operational guidelines issued by SEBI and BSE for tender-offer buybacks.
- Eligible shareholders holding shares as of the record date (November 11, 2026) may tender their shares through the acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Buyback Opening Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Buyback Closing Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and capital return to eligible shareholders of Jyothy Labs Limited through a proportionate tender-offer buyback mechanism on the stock exchange.
