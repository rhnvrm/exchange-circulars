## Summary

BSE has announced the trading and settlement programme for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment for the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026. The notice outlines settlement numbers, trading dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement schedules for EGR segment for December 2026 (Settlement Nos. DR-176/2026-2027 to DR-197/2026-2027).
- Pay-in of funds and securities must be completed by 03:00 p.m., with pay-out affected by 04:00 p.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:00 a.m. (members to submit instructions by 8:50 a.m.).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members trading in the EGR segment must adhere to the stipulated trading, pay-in, pay-out, and auction timelines for each settlement cycle.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: December 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.
- Individual settlement cycles run daily across the month as detailed in the schedule.

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for market participants trading in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR), ensuring smooth clearing and settlement operations throughout December 2026.
