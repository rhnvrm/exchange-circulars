## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) of Genus Power Infrastructures Limited. Bidding is scheduled from November 4, 2026, to November 11, 2026, with settlement finalized on November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Genus Power Infrastructures Limited
- **Settlement Type & No:** Buyback, Settlement No. 2026134
- **Bid Period:** 04/11/2026 to 11/11/2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL depositories.
- **Depository Procedures:** Securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts under the buyback market type and settlement number; members/custodians are not required to upload an EPN file.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should consult the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty details.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; follows established guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must complete early pay-in of securities in the depository system sufficiently in advance of prescribed timelines.
- Ensure early pay-in reflections in BSE/BSECL systems prior to bid acceptance and confirmation by custodians on the BSE OTB platform.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 04/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 11/11/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 16/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 17/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the corporate buyback, ensuring clear timelines and operational compliance for market participants and investors.
