## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for November 17, 2026, via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding platform: iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- Bid collection start date: November 16, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: November 16, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Bid collection end date for members: November 17, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Auction date: November 17, 2026.
- Settlement date: November 18, 2026.
- Includes multiple re-issue and new SDL securities from states such as Andhra Pradesh, Goa, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Telangana, Tripura, and West Bengal.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational auction schedule notification in continuation with previous RBI press releases and exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors wishing to participate in the non-competitive bidding for SDLs must submit their bids through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS platform within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: November 16, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid Collection End: November 16, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Members Bid Collection End: November 17, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: November 17, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides participating members and investors an operational window to bid for State Government Securities through the exchange mechanism.
