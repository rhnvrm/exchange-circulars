## Summary

BSE has announced the cancellation of the proposed suspension of trading in securities of Mercury Trade Links Ltd (Scrip Code: 512415), in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261021-15 dated October 21, 2026. The suspension was initially intended due to non-compliance with Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 and June 2026). Trading suspension will not be proceeded with w.e.f. November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mercury Trade Links Ltd
- Scrip Code: 512415
- Regulation Involved: Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 (non-compliance for March 2026 & June 2026 quarters).
- Previous Notice: Exchange Notice No. 20261021-15 dated October 21, 2026.
- Effective Date of Cancellation: November 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory change; withdrawal of enforcement action (suspension of trading) following resolution or reconsideration of compliance status.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and the company should note that trading will continue uninterrupted without suspension w.e.f. November 23, 2026. Ongoing compliance with SEBI LODR Regulations remains mandatory.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Cancellation: November 23, 2026
- Prior Notice Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Relief for shareholders and investors of Mercury Trade Links Ltd as trading in securities will not be suspended, ensuring continuous liquidity and normal market operations.
