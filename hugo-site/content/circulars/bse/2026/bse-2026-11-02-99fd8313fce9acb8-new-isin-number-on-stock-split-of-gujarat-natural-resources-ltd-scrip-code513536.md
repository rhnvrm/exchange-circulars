## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the assignment of a new ISIN number (INE207H01026) for Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd (Scrip Code: 513536) following the sub-division of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-. The new ISIN is effective for trades done on and from the ex-date of November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- New ISIN Number: INE207H01026
- Company Name & Scrip Code: Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd (513536)
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- Ex-Date: November 5, 2026 (DR-750/2026-2027)
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261016-7 dated October 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Assignment of a new International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) reflecting the capital restructuring and stock split of the company's equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and back-office records with the new ISIN (INE207H01026) for Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate identification, clearing, and settlement of equity shares post stock split, preventing operational discrepancies and ensuring smooth market execution.
