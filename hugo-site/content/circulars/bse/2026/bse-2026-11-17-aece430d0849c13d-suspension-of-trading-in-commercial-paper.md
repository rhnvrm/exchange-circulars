## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that multiple companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of Commercial Papers (CPs). Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies effective from their respective record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261117-46, dated November 17, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment / Listing Operations.
- Affected Issuers include REC Limited, Hinduja Housing Finance Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, Redington Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, Muthoot Mcred Limited, ECL Finance Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, and Bajaj Housing Finance Limited.
- Trading members are instructed not to execute transactions in the specified Commercial Papers beyond the applicable ex-dates/record dates.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed entities on or after the specified record and ex-dates to ensure smooth settlement and redemption.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17/11/2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: Between 20/11/2026 and 25/11/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational restriction ensuring orderly cessation of trading prior to commercial paper redemption.
