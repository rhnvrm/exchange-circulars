## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Praxis Home Retail Limited will be permitted on the Exchange starting November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Praxis Home Retail Limited
- **Type of Security:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Scrip Code:** 751163
- **Scrip ID:** PRAXIS-RE3
- **ISIN:** INE546Y20048
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **RE Trading Period:** November 27, 2026, to November 30, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 27, 2026, to December 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action procedure for rights entitlements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of RE trading, specifying the ISIN and clarifying that REs are distinct from ordinary shares.
- Include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills stating that purchase of REs grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before closure.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced by the issue closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 25, 2026
- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 27, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

REs will be traded in dematerialized form only and settled on a T+1 rolling settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis, facilitating the renunciation and subscription process for eligible shareholders.
