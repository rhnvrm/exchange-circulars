## Summary

BSE has conducted a review to identify securities for the Additional Measure for securities witnessing unusual price movement. Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion (Annexure I) and those moving out (Annexure II) take effect from November 16, 2026, with a consolidated list provided in Annexure III.

## Key Points

- Periodic surveillance review for securities experiencing unusual price movements.
- Securities listed in Annexure I added to the framework effective November 16, 2026.
- Securities listed in Annexure II moving out of the framework effective November 16, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.
- Member inquiries may be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation and periodic updating of surveillance measures for identified securities in accordance with exchange surveillance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align trading practices and risk parameters with the updated lists of securities entering or exiting the surveillance framework.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly affects trading margins and surveillance monitoring for specific equities entering or exiting the Additional Surveillance Measure framework.
