## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The session allows trading members to test their software, trading applications, and risk management systems under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- Scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026, from the Primary Site (PR).
- Helps members fulfill regulatory testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- Supports testing of third-party trading platforms, exceptional market conditions, call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and block deals.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80 release details to be published separately.
- Detailed timings provided for log-in, pre-open, continuous trading, periodic call auctions, and closing sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; facilitates compliance with existing SEBI testing mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may choose to participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational exercise with no margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or creation of actual trading rights and liabilities.
