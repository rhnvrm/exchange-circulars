## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026, from the Primary Site (PR). Trading members can test their trading applications, third-party platforms, and the new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026, for Equity Derivatives segment.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.80 release details to be published separately.
- Trading members using third-party empanelled vendors or in-house systems via IML/ETI APIs can test functionalities including risk-reduction mode and trading halt under exceptional market conditions.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or legal rights and liabilities.
- Feedback on the mock trading session is requested by 4:30 pm on the mock trading day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Fulfills regulatory requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020, and Exchange Notice No. 20201201-22 regarding testing of software used in or related to trading and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members may choose to participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock trading session to BSE support channels by 4:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 14, 2026 (Saturday)
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** November 14, 2026, by 4:30 pm

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal and non-disruptive, as mock trading is conducted on a Saturday for testing and familiarization purposes only without financial or margin implications.
