## Summary

BSE has issued operational guidelines for collateral management, clearing, and settlement under the interoperability framework for Tender Offer - Offer to Buy (OTB) covering Buyback, Takeover, and Delisting, effective on or after November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Tender Offer - Offer to Buy (OTB) for Buyback, Takeover & Delisting will be under the interoperability framework starting November 27, 2026.
- Clearing members and custodians can choose their Designated Clearing Corporation for OTB (such as BSECL) independently or maintain the same as the Equity Cash Segment.
- Early Pay-In for Tender Offer remains unchanged except that providing the TM Code is mandatory for Clearing Members.
- Give-up/take-up facility requires custodians to confirm bids through the BSECL RTRMS module by 4 PM on the last day of bidding; unacted bids are treated as rejected.
- End of Day (EOD) files remain unchanged except for Trade files and specific new file formats (Annexure A).

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of Tender Offer - Offer to Buy (OTB) into the interoperability framework amongst Clearing Corporations.
- Introduction of exchange identifiers in custodian take-up file formats.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must select their Designated Clearing Corporation and submit the required letters (Annexure 1 & 2).
- Clearing members must ensure the TM Code is provided while submitting Early Pay-In instructions.
- Custodians must confirm bids via the BSECL RTRMS module by 4:00 PM on the last bidding day.
- Back-office vendors and members must update their systems as per the new file formats and guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (Interoperability framework for OTB).
- Bidding Deadline for Custodian Take-up: 4 PM on the last day of bidding.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines clearing and settlement for tender offers across exchanges through interoperability.
- Enhances operational workflow clarity for clearing members, trading members, and custodians participating in OTB events.
