## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a surveillance notice highlighting scrips that experienced significant price or volume movements in the recent past. The exchange sought clarifications from multiple companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard surveillance monitoring and disclosure tracking notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies queried by the exchange are required to submit timely clarifications regarding any material price/volume movements to maintain market integrity and transparency.
