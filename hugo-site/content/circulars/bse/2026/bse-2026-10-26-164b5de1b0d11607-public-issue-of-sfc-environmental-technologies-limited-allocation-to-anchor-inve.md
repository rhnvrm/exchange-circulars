## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 60,68,460 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of SFC Environmental Technologies Limited at Rs. 330/- per equity share on October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SFC Environmental Technologies Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO Anchor Investor Allocation
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 60,68,460 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 330/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are informed of the anchor investor allocation details for reference and records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 26, 2026
- Announcement Date: October 26, 2026
