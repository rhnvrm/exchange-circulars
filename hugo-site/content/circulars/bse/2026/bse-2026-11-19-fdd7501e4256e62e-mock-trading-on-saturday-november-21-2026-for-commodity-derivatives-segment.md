## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their trading applications, including special functionalities like risk-reduction mode and trading halt.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Open for TWS users, third-party empaneled vendors, and in-house system developers using ETI APIs.
- Session Timings: Log-in from 02:15 PM to 03:00 PM; Continuous Trading from 03:00 PM to 06:00 PM.
- No new BOLT TWS Release associated with this mock session.
- Trades executed during mock trading do not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the session by 07:00 PM via designated email or contact numbers.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced through this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session and submit their feedback to BSE tech support or helpdesk by 07:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 21, 2026
- Log-in Time: 02:15 PM – 03:00 PM
- Continuous Trading Time: 03:00 PM – 06:00 PM
- Feedback Submission Deadline: 07:00 PM on November 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and positive, ensuring trading applications and vendor systems function reliably under exceptional market conditions without any financial exposure.
