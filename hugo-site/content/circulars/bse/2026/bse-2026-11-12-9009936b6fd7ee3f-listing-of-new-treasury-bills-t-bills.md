## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS segment, effective from Friday, November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code 805289 (ISIN: IN002026X321): 91TB110227 maturing on 11/02/2027 (Market Lot: 1).
- Scrip Code 805290 (ISIN: IN002026Y329): 182TB13527 maturing on 13/05/2027 (Market Lot: 1).
- Scrip Code 805291 (ISIN: IN002026Z326): 364T111127 maturing on 11/11/2027 (Market Lot: 1).
- Securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restriction period of two working days prior to the respective maturity/redemption dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Maturity Dates: February 11, 2027 (91TB110227), May 13, 2027 (182TB13527), November 11, 2027 (364T111127)

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading options in the debt segment with new sovereign short-term debt instruments.
