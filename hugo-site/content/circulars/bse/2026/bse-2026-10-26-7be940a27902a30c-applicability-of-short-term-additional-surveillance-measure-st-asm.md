## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The framework entails enhanced margin requirements to curb excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities identified under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities identified under ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable rate of margin of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created from October 28, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin rates (50% and 100%) under ST-ASM Stages I and II in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that appropriate margins (50% or 100% as applicable) are maintained on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities w.e.f. October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on October 27, 2026 and new positions created thereafter).

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes, mitigating excessive price volatility and protecting market participants.
