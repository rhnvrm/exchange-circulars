## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of existing price bands for 15 securities under its surveillance and trading segment, effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 15 companies, including Aerpace Industries Ltd, Antala Industries Ltd, Beeyu Overseas Ltd, Dc Infotech and Communication Ltd, GB Logistics Commerce Ltd, Infinity Infoway Ltd, Jaipan Industries Ltd, Jet Solar Ltd, Karnavati Finance Ltd, Patron Exim Ltd, Pharmaids Pharmaceuticals Ltd, Shubham Polyspin Ltd, Thomas Cook (India) Ltd, USG Tech Solutions Ltd, and Yaashvi Jewellers Ltd.
- Revised price bands range across 2%, 5%, and 10% limits.
- Effective date of implementation: November 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified scrips as part of routine market surveillance and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised price limits for the listed securities effective from the specified start date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the affected securities will be subject to the updated price volatility bands.
