## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 34,964 new equity shares of Affle 3I Limited (Scrip Code: 142752, ISIN: INE00WC01027) with effect from October 28, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Affle 3I Limited
- Scrip Code: 142752
- ISIN: INE00WC01027
- Securities Issued: 34,964 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1,485/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1,487/-).
- Allottees: Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Distribution Nos.: 140879385 to 140914348
- Date of Allotment: 14/08/2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 14/05/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing approval under SEBI (ICDR) Regulations and BSE listing procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure adherence to lock-in requirements and depository disclosures for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 28, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 14, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 14, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion for Affle 3I Limited resulting from warrant conversion by promoters, with negligible market impact.
