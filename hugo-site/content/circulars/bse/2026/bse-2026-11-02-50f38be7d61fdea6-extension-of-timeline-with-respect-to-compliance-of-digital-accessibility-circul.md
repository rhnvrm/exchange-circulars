## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the extension of the timeline for compliance with SEBI's Digital Accessibility Circulars, pursuant to SEBI Circular no. HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/202 dated October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261102-16, dated November 02, 2026.
- References SEBI Circular dated October 30, 2026 (enclosed as Annexure 1).
- Applies to all members of BSE Clearing Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of implementation timelines for digital accessibility compliance across market infrastructure institutions and intermediaries as directed by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the revised timeline and prepare their digital systems to comply with SEBI's accessibility mandates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 02, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities breathing room and additional preparation time to meet rigorous digital accessibility standards without immediate enforcement penalties.
