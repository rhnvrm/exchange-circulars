## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of several listed securities following surveillance reviews. These changes are designed to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Key Points

- Revised price bands apply to 38 listed scrips.
- Effective date for the new price bands is November 08, 2026.
- Price bands range from 1% to 20% depending on the security.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of existing price operating ranges for specified equity securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and RMS limits to reflect the revised price bands effective from November 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders holding or trading in the listed scrips will be subject to new upper/lower circuit limits, impacting intraday execution and liquidity.
