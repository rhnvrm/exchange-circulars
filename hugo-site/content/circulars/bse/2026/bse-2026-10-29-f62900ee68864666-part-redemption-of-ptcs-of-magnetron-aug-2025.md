## Summary

BSE has informed trading members of the part redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued under MAGNETRON AUG 2025, detailing reduced face values effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name / Scrip:** MAGNETRON AUG 2025 (MA2025-11.50%-28-07-28-PTC)
- **Scrip Code 977078 / ISIN INE2GPJ15012:** Reduced face value of Rs. 1,919.39 per PTC.
- **Scrip Code 977079 / ISIN INE2GPJ15020:** Reduced face value of Rs. 5,178.29 per PTC.
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notification for debt segment instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the reduced face values and ensure trading is conducted accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members holding the affected PTCs will experience adjusted face values in trading and settlement operations.
