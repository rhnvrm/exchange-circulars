## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of newly listed companies Jio Platforms Limited and Paras Healthcare Limited to the BSE IPO index, effective at the open of Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Jio Platforms Limited (Exchange ticker: 545004) and Paras Healthcare Limited (Exchange ticker: 545005) listed on BSE effective October 28, 2026.
- Both stocks will be included in the BSE IPO index starting from October 30, 2026.
- These actions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index trackers are advised to take note of the index additions and make necessary adjustments.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date: October 28, 2026
- Index Inclusion Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Inclusion in the BSE IPO index will increase the visibility and tracking of Jio Platforms Limited and Paras Healthcare Limited among institutional and retail index funds.
