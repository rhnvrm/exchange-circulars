## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-752/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026 from 10.30 a.m. to 11.15 a.m.
- Auction Matching: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026 from 11.15 a.m. to 11.30 a.m.
- Delivery Statement and Report of Accepted Offers: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026 from 11.30 a.m. to 12.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026 from 03.55 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Thursday, Nov 12, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- Auction Offer Entry & Matching Date: November 11, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: November 12, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice affecting market participants involved in settlement shortfalls and demat auctions for Rolling Settlement DR-752/2026-27.
