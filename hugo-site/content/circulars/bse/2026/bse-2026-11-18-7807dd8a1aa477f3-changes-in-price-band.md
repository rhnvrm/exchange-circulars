## Summary

BSE Ltd has announced changes in the price bands for 17 securities, effective from November 19, 2026, as part of its ongoing market surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261118-21, dated November 18, 2026, issued by the Surveillance Department (DOSS).
- Revised price bands apply to 17 listed entities with revised limits set at 5%, 10%, or 20%.
- Effective date of implementation: November 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of price bands for specified scrips to curb excessive volatility and ensure orderly trading in the equity segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance in their trading systems effective from November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be restricted within the newly assigned price limits, moderating sudden price swings and protecting investor interests.
