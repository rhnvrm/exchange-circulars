## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified the listing and permission for trading of new securities of Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532527) effective from November 18, 2026. A total of 34,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each have been issued at a premium of Rs. 586/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 532527
- **ISIN:** INE399G01023
- **Security Details:** 34,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 586/-.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 588/- per share (Face value Rs. 2/- + Premium Rs. 586/-).
- **Allotment Date:** 24/09/2026.
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 18, 2026.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 182170018 to 185570017.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Lock-in Details:** 34,00,000 shares are locked-in up to 30/05/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to note that the newly allotted shares are subject to lock-in requirements up to May 30, 2028, and rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** November 18, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The preferential allotment and subsequent listing of 3,400,000 equity shares resulting from warrant conversion increases the paid-up equity capital of Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd., leading to promoter stake consolidation while expanding the total outstanding share base.
