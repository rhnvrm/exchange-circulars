## Summary

BSE has announced the filing of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of Pradeepit Global Consulting Limited. The document is available on the SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pradeepit Global Consulting Limited
- Filing Date: November 04, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from the notice date
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine SME IPO draft document disclosure under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public are requested to submit any comments on the draft offer document to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 06, 2026
- Draft Offer Document Filing Date: November 04, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Pradeepit Global Consulting Limited.
