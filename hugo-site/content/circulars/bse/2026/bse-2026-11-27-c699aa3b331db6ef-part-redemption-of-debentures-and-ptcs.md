## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs), Pass Through Certificates (PTCs), and Bonds. Trading members are informed that trading in these securities will take place with reduced face value effective from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261127-40, issued on November 27, 2026 by Listing Operations.
- Specifies record dates and ex-dates of December 1, 2026 and December 2, 2026 for various entities including Universe Trust, Krazybee Services Ltd, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, DLF Cyber City Developers Limited, and Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited.
- Details reduced face values and ISINs for each participating security.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; standard operational notification for corporate debt and structured products.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and execute trades accordingly starting from the effective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: December 1, 2026 and December 2, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational update for market participants holding or trading the specified debt instruments and PTCs.
