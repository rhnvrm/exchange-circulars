## Summary

Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund has announced the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the MSIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (SIF) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Offering MSIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (SIF) along with SIP facility across Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Window: November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 17, 2026.
- Scheme ISINs include INF174V30076, INF174V30092, INF174V30084, INF174V30100, INF174V30126, and INF174V30118.

## Regulatory Changes

None specific to regulatory frameworks; this is a standard operational circular for Mutual Fund NFO execution on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Transactions with initiated payment remain pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 17, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on December 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 11, 2026
- SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline: December 11, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 17, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 17, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors clear operational guidelines, timelines, and compliance rules for participating in the Mahindra Manulife SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
