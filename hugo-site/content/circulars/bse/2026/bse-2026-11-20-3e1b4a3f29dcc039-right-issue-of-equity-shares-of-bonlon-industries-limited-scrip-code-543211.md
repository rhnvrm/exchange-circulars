## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Bonlon Industries Limited has fixed the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Eligible shareholders will receive 1 equity share for every 1 equity share held at an issue price of Rs. 30 per share.

## Key Points

- Company: Bonlon Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 543211
- Record Date: 25/11/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 25/11/2026
- Issue Ratio: 1:1 (One equity share for every one equity share held)
- Issue Price: Rs. 30 per share (Face Value Rs. 10 + Premium Rs. 20), payable entirely on application.
- Settlement Number: DR- 763/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to execute transactions in the equity shares on an ex-rights basis from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Rights Basis From: 25/11/2026
- Record Date: 25/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

The rights issue provides existing shareholders the opportunity to subscribe to additional shares at a predetermined price, impacting shareholding distribution and capital structure.
