## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of existing price bands for 16 securities, effective from November 25, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Revised price bands apply to 16 listed securities.
- Effective date of implementation: November 25, 2026.
- Price bands revised range from 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20% across various scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of daily operating price bands for specified scrips to curb excessive volatility and ensure orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips will be restricted within the newly assigned price limits starting November 25, 2026.
