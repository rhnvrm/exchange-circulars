## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has announced the implementation of an interoperability mechanism for Tender Offers (OTB) covering buyback, takeover, and delisting transactions, effective on or after November 27, 2026. This framework aims to facilitate seamless interaction and enhance operational efficiency for market participants across clearing corporations.

## Key Points

- Interoperability mechanism introduced for Tender Offers (OTB) including Buyback, Takeover, and Delisting.
- Implementation builds upon the existing interoperability framework communicated via circular dated April 30, 2019.
- Effective date for the interoperability framework is on or after November 27, 2026.
- Members and custodians in the capital market segment can change their designated clearing corporation for tender offers following established processes.
- Separate circulars will follow containing revised operational guidelines for risk management, clearing, and settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the clearing corporation interoperability framework to tender offers (buybacks, takeovers, and delistings) to improve settlement flexibility and operational efficiency.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, custodians, and market participants must ensure technical and operational readiness for the interoperability framework by November 27, 2026.
- Participants may opt to change designated clearing corporations for tender offers as per the prescribed process.
- Watch out for subsequent circulars detailing revised operational guidelines for risk management and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 31, 2026
- Effective Date: On or after November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The introduction of interoperability for tender offers streamlines processes for buybacks, takeovers, and delistings, allowing market participants greater flexibility in choosing clearing corporations, thereby reducing operational friction and improving market efficiency.
