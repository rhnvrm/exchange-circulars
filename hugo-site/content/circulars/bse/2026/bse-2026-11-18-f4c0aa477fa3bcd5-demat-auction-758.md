## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-758/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry: 18 Nov 2026, 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: 18 Nov 2026, 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement & Report: 18 Nov 2026, 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 18 Nov 2026, 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of Auction: 19 Nov 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise care while punching securities codes and rates as no corrections are permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: 18 Nov 2026
- Pay-in Date: 19 Nov 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operations for short-delivered securities in rolling settlement.
