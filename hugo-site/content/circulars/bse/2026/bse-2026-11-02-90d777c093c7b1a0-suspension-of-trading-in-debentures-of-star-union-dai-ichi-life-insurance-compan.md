## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Star Union Dai-Ichi Life Insurance Company Limited effective from November 9, 2026, due to the record date for interest payment and exercise of the first call option for full redemption and extinguishment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Star Union Dai-Ichi Life Insurance Company Limited
- Security Details: NCD (ISIN: INE503V08016, Scrip Code: 973587, Series: SUDLIC-7.75%-24-11-31-PVT)
- Record Date: November 9, 2026
- Purpose: Payment of Interest and Exercise of First Call Option for full Redemption and Extinguishment
- No Dealings From: November 9, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company starting from the specified no-dealings date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / No Dealings From: November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt instruments undergoing interest payment and full redemption.
