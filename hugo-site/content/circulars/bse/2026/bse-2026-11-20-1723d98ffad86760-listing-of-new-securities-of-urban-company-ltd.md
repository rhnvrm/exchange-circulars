## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of new equity shares of Urban Company Ltd with effect from November 23, 2026. A total of 16,02,56,410 equity shares were allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Urban Company Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 544515
- **ISIN:** INE0CAZ01013
- **Security Details:** 16,02,56,410 Equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Effective Date of Trading:** Monday, November 23, 2026.
- **Date of Allotment:** November 19, 2026.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 156/- per share.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1542189064 to 1702445473.
- **Ranking:** These new equity shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard corporate listing notice for securities issued via QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records with the new scrip code, ISIN, and equity share capital details for Urban Company Ltd.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 19, 2026
- **Notice Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 16,02,56,410 new equity shares expands Urban Company Ltd's equity capital base following its QIP, potentially increasing liquidity and trading volume for the scrip on the exchange.
