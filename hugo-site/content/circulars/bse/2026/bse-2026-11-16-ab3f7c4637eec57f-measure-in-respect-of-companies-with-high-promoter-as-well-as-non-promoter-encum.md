## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261116-37 regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance, in continuation of notice no. 20220131-43. Securities included in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. November 19, 2026 (on open positions as of November 18, 2026, and new positions).
- Securities in Annexure II move out from the framework effective November 17, 2026.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- Measures are in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of the framework for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrances, enforcing strict margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the margin requirements (minimum 75%) applicable to affected securities in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments starting November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Exit (Annexure II):** November 17, 2026
- **Snapshot Date for Open Positions:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margins (Annexure I):** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (75%) on securities with high encumbrance levels impact liquidity and trading positions for market participants in both Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
