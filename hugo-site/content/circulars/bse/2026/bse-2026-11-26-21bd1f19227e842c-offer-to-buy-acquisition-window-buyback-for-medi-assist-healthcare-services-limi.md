## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy (Buyback) of equity shares of Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited. The buyback window is scheduled from Friday, November 27, 2026, to Thursday, December 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited
- **Security Symbol:** MEDIASSIST
- **ISIN:** INE456Z01021
- **Scrip Code:** 544088
- **Offer Size:** Upto 11,59,199 Equity Shares
- **Face Value:** Rs. 5.00
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 425.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Friday, 27th November 2026
- **Closing Date:** Thursday, 3rd December 2026
- **Session Timings:** Bid Offering and Modifications/Cancellation from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational schedule notice for buyback acquisition window participation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** 27th November 2026
- **Closing Date:** 3rd December 2026
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** 27th November 2026 to 2nd December 2026 (Give-up last day: 3rd December 2026, Take-up last day: 3rd December 2026 up to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Establishes clear operational timelines and module access URLs (iBBS, RTRMS, Extranet) for trading members and custodians participating in the Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited buyback.
