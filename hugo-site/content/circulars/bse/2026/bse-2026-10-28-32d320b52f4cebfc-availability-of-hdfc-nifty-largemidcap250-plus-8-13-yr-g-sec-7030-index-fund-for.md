## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Notice No. 20261001-58 dated October 01, 2026, the HDFC Nifty LargeMidcap250 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP: HDFC NIFTY LARGEMIDCAP250 PLUS 8-13 YR G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund.
- Plans included: Regular Plan and Direct Plan across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification for mutual fund transaction availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to conduct ongoing transactions, redemptions, and SIP setups for the specified HDFC index fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform with no direct regulatory or market-wide systemic impact.
