## Summary

BSE and Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL) have issued revised collateral-related timings for the upcoming special Muhurat Trading session scheduled for November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised timings apply to cash deposits, cash releases, securities release cut-offs, and collateral allocations.
- Cash Deposit: 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Cash Release: 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Cut-off time for Securities Release: 5:00 PM.
- Collateral Allocation: 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational timings for collateral management have been specifically modified for the Muhurat Trading session on November 08, 2026, referencing Exchange Notice No. 20261019-9 dated October 19, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure that all cash deposits, cash releases, securities release requests, and collateral allocations are completed strictly within the specified revised timings.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date / Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact; operational schedule adjustment ensuring seamless collateral and clearing operations during the special festival trading session.
