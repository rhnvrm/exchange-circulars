## Summary

DCW Limited has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value of Rs.2/- each from the open market through the Stock Exchanges, in accordance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The maximum buyback price is Rs. 58 per equity share for an aggregate amount not exceeding Rs. 40,00,00,000.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DCW Limited
- Maximum Buyback Price: Rs. 58 per equity share
- Maximum Buyback Size: Rs. 40,00,00,000 (Rupees Forty Crores Only)
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- per equity share
- Opening Date: November 03, 2026
- Company's Broker: Ventura Securities Limited (Clearing No: M50471)
- Registrar to Buyback: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure all payment obligations relating to the buyback are completed before the last date for the buyback.

## Important Dates

- Date of Opening of Buyback: November 03, 2026
- Last Date for Buyback: February 8, 2027 (66 working days from opening), or earlier upon deployment of maximum buyback size or board-determined early closure after minimum buyback size.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and capital return to shareholders of DCW Limited through open market purchases on stock exchanges.
