## Summary

Pursuant to Notice No. 20261016-43, BSE has informed members that the NAVI MULTI ASSET OMNI FOF shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes covered: NAVI MULTI ASSET OMNI FOF - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF959L01HW4) and NAVI MULTI ASSET OMNI FOF - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF959L01HV6).
- Permitted transactions: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Trading Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification concerning mutual fund scheme trading availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates uninterrupted trading, investment, and systematic transfer/withdrawal operations for investors and members in the NAVI Multi Asset Omni FOF schemes.
