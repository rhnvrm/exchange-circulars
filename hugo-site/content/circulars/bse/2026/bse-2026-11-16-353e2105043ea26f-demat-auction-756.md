## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 756 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-756/2026-27 in the equity segment, detailing session timings for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No: DR-756/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on November 16, 2026.
- Auction matching timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on November 16, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report timing: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on November 16, 2026.
- Money Statement and final reports timing: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on November 16, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notice under existing settlement frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise utmost care while punching security codes and rates; no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should verify cutoff rates in the Auction offer Entry Screen and take note of corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 16, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: November 17, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
