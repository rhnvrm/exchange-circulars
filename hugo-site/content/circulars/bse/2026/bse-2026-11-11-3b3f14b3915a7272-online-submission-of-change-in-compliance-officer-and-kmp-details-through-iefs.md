## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced that the facility for online submission of "Change in Compliance Officer" and "KMP Details" is now available through ICCL's Electronic Filing System (IEFS).

## Key Points

- Online submission facility launched for Change in Compliance Officer and KMP Details.
- Accessible via the IEFS platform at https://iefs.bseindia.in/.
- User manual provided for navigation and guidance.
- Applicable to all clearing members.

## Regulatory Changes

Digitization of administrative and compliance submissions for clearing members.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must review the attached user manual before submitting any change requests for compliance officers or KMP details through IEFS.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines filing processes and enhances operational efficiency for clearing members.
