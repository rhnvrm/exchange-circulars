## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Notice No. 20261009-39 dated October 9, 2026, the Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund under Mirae Asset Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF platform effective November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Mirae Asset Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund
- **Effective Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Covered ISINs:**
  - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (INF205K30071)
  - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (INF205K30089)
  - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (INF205K30097)
  - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (INF205K30105)
  - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (INF205K30113)
  - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (INF205K30121)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Effective Date for Transactions:** November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational readiness for investors and distributors to transact in various growth and IDCW plans of the Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund via BSE StAR MF.
