## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of price bands for 11 securities under its surveillance measures, effective from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 11 securities to operating ranges of 2%, 5%, and 10%.
- Effective date: November 5, 2026.
- Issued by DOSS (Surveillance) Department.

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of price bands for specified scrips to prevent excessive volatility and ensure orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the revised price bands and ensure risk management systems reflect the updated limits.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed securities will be restricted within the newly assigned price bands starting November 5, 2026.
