## Summary

BSE has announced that trading members are permitted to trade in 5,51,289 new equity shares of Krishival Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 505196) with effect from Monday, November 02, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Krishival Foods Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 505196
- **NSE Symbol:** KRISHIVAL
- **ISIN:** INE0GGO01015
- **Securities Involved:** 5,51,289 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs.10/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 5,51,289 (25067262 to 25618550)
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Ranking:** These shares are pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notice for new securities arising from share conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should ensure compliance with relevant trading regulations regarding the newly listed fully paid-up shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minor capital expansion and conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid equity shares for Krishival Foods Limited with no direct negative impact on market operations.
