## Summary

Trading members are informed that Virat Industries Ltd has changed its name to Brahm Virat Industries Corporation Limited. Consequent to this change, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Virat Industries Ltd
- New Company Name: Brahm Virat Industries Corporation Limited
- Scrip Code: 530521
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: BRAHMVIRAT
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: BRAHMVIRAT

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update of company records and trading symbols following corporate name approval.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their back-office systems, records, and client databases with the new name and scrip ID.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminal symbols and back-office master files.
