## Summary

FDC Ltd. has fixed November 13, 2026, as the record date and ex-entitlement date for determining shareholder entitlement and eligibility to participate in the tender offer (buyback) of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: FDC Ltd. (531599)
- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 13, 2026
- Purpose: Determination of entitlement and names of eligible equity shareholders for the buyback offer.
- Reference: Notice No. 20261102-12 (DR-756/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement details for settlement and participation.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares in the dematerialised segment as of the record date are eligible to participate in the FDC Ltd. buyback offer.
