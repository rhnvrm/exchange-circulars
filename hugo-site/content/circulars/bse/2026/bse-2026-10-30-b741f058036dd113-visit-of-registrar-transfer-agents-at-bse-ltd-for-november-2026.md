## Summary

BSE Limited has released the schedule of visits by representatives of various Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) to the Department of Investor Services (DIS) for the month of November 2026. Members and investors with complaints against client companies of these RTAs can approach them directly during the specified timings.

## Key Points

- RTA representatives will be available at the Department of Investor Services, 1st Floor, Cama Building, Dalal Street, Mumbai–400001.
- Participating RTAs include KFin Technologies Ltd., Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd., MCS Share Transfer Agent Ltd., MUFG Intime India Private Ltd., Universal Capital Securities P. Ltd., Sharex Dynamics India P. Ltd., TSR Darashaw Ltd, Integrated Ent. (India) Ltd., Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd., Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd., Datamatics Financial Services Pvt. Ltd., and Cameo Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd.
- Specific dates and time slots are assigned for each RTA between November 2, 2026, and November 6, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors having unresolved grievances against client companies of the listed RTAs can utilize this schedule to meet representatives in person at the BSE DIS office.

## Important Dates

- RTA visit schedule active from November 2, 2026, to November 6, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates direct interaction between investors/members and RTAs for resolution of investor complaints and grievances.
