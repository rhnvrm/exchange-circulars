## Summary

Veer Global Infraconstruction Limited has announced the record date and ex-rights date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Eligible shareholders will receive 1 new equity share for every 5 existing equity shares held on the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veer Global Infraconstruction Limited (Scrip Code: 543241, Scrip ID: VGIL).
- Rights Issue Ratio: 1 (One) Equity Share for every 5 (Five) Equity Shares held.
- Issue Price: Rs. 30 per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- plus Premium of Rs. 20/- per share) for cash.
- Record Date: October 30, 2026.
- Ex-Rights Date: October 30, 2026.
- Settlement Number: DR- 746/2026-2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard corporate action processing under BSE listing operations and rolling settlement segment guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure transactions in the company's equity shares are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified ex-rights date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 28, 2026
- Record Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders face dilution unless they subscribe to the rights issue or renounce their rights entitlements. The issue provides capital raising opportunities for the company at a set premium.
