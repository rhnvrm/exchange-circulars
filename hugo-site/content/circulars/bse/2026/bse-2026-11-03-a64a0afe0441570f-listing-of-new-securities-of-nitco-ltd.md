## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 55,05,935 new equity shares of Nitco Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532722) with effect from November 4, 2026. These shares were issued to the promoter on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Nitco Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532722, ISIN: INE858F01012)
- **Securities Issued:** 55,05,935 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at an issue price of Rs. 92.25/- (including premium of Rs. 82.25/-).
- **Allottee:** Promoter on a preferential basis.
- **Ranking:** The newly allotted shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 247070171 to 252576105.
- **Date of Allotment:** July 24, 2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 55,05,935 shares are under lock-in up to May 7, 2028.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the listing and trading permissions for the new equity shares of Nitco Ltd. effective from November 4, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 24, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 7, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving equity issuance via warrant conversion, increasing the paid-up capital of Nitco Ltd. with specified lock-in periods for promoter shares.
