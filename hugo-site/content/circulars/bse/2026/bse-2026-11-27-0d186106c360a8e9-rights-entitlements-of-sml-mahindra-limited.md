## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of SML Mahindra Limited will be permitted on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** SML Mahindra Limited
- **Security Type:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** December 01, 2026 to December 04, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 751164
- **Scrip ID:** SMLMAH-RE
- **ISIN:** INE294B20019
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Rights Issue Period:** December 01, 2026 to December 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Rights Entitlements for Renunciation shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis and are tradable in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue by making an application with requisite application money or renouncing before the issue closes. REs neither subscribed nor renounced by the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** December 01, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** December 04, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** December 01, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** December 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides shareholders and investors with the opportunity to trade rights entitlements, subscribe to the rights issue, or renounce entitlements within the specified timeline.
