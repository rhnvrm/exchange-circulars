## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 745 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-745/2026-27, detailing offer entry, matching, reporting timings, and pay-in schedules.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No: DR-745/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 30, 2026.
- Auction Matching timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 30, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Monday, November 02, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members should exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections are permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 30, 2026
- Pay-in Date: November 02, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational circular affecting trading members participating in Demat Auction 745.
