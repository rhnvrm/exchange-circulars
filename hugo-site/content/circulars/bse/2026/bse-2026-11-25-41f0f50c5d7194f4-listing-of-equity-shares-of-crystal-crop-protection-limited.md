## Summary

Effective from Thursday, November 26, 2026, the equity shares of Crystal Crop Protection Limited will be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue price was Rs. 272/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 262/-).

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545031
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: CRYSTALCP
- ISIN No: INE101P01016
- Number of Securities: 163,177,583 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 163177583
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: November 23, 2026
- Group: 'B'

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notice for IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, November 26, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depositories' records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 23, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Crystal Crop Protection Limited shares on BSE, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding market participation in the agricultural inputs sector.
