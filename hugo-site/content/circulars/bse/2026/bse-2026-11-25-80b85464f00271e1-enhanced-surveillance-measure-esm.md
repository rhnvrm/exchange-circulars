## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The notice outlines securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or exiting the framework, along with associated trading restrictions, margin requirements, and settlement changes.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin effective November 27, 2026, on open positions as of November 26, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities continuing in ESM but moving to lower Stage I take effect on November 26, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 27, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Enhanced Surveillance Measures including mandatory 100% margins, Trade-for-Trade settlement, 2% price bands, and Periodic Call Auctions for designated scrips.
- Reinstatement of price bands to pre-shortlisting levels for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of minimum 100% margins on open and new positions for applicable securities.
- Market participants must adhere to settlement shifts (Rolling to Trade-for-Trade) and trading mechanisms (Periodic Call Auction and 2% price bands) as specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- **November 26, 2026:** Movement of securities to lower Stage I, shifting to Stage II (Trade for Trade with 2% price band & Periodic Call Auction), and removal of securities from the ESM framework.
- **November 27, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin on open positions (as of Nov 26, 2026) and new positions, and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity, settlement timelines, and margin obligations for securities listed in the annexures will be tightly regulated. Investors trading in these scrips will experience restricted price bands and specialized auction mechanisms.
