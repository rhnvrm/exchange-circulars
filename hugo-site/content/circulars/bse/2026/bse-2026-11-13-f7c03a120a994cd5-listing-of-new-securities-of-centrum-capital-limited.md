## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 1,16,87,704 new equity shares of Centrum Capital Limited issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion, effective from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Centrum Capital Limited (Scrip Code: 501150, ISIN: INE660C01027).
- Number of Securities: 1,16,87,704 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each.
- Issue Price: Rs. 28.52/- (including premium of Rs. 27.52/-).
- Allotment Basis: Issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing old equity shares.
- Distribution Numbers: 486829195 to 498516898.
- Lock-in Details: 1,16,87,704 shares locked-in up to May 15, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with statutory lock-in periods and exchange listing norms.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 16, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: May 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Increase in paid-up equity capital of Centrum Capital Limited due to promoter warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
