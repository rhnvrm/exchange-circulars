## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 25,00,00,000 equity shares of Jio Financial Services Limited (Scrip Code: 543940) with effect from November 19, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 543940
- **ISIN:** INE758E01017
- **Number of Shares:** 25,00,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 316.50/- per share (including premium of Rs. 306.50/-)
- **Allotment Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 19, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 25,00,00,000 shares locked in upto May 30, 2028 (Dist. Nos. 6603284189 to 6853284188).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of securities issued via preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the listing and locking-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The preferential allotment and conversion of warrants into equity shares increases the paid-up capital of Jio Financial Services Limited and expands promoter shareholding, subject to lock-in restrictions until May 2028.
