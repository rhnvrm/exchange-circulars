## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Interactive Financial Services Limited effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Interactive Financial Services Limited
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751149
- Scrip ID: INTFIN-RE1
- ISIN: INE064T20026
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 06, 2026 to November 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: November 06, 2026 to November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational circular for a corporate rights issue.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes and bills must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that the purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.
- REs must be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs on or before the closure date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: November 06, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: November 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: November 06, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides clear operational guidelines and timelines for market participants and investors holding or wishing to trade the Rights Entitlements of Interactive Financial Services Limited.
