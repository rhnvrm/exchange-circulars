## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of debentures and Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) for various series of India Universal Trust, resulting in reduced face values effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **India Universal Trust AL2 - Series A2** (Scrip Code: 976207, ISIN: INE1CBK15029): Reduced face value of Rs. 4,452,373.94 per PTC.
- **India Universal Trust AL2 - Series A3** (Scrip Code: 976208, ISIN: INE1CBK15037): Reduced face value of Rs. 9,007,474.67 per PTC.
- **India Universal Trust AL1 - Series A2** (Scrip Code: 976051, ISIN: INE16J715027): Reduced face value of Rs. 2,977,659.83 per PTC.
- **India Universal Trust AL1 - Series A3** (Scrip Code: 976052, ISIN: INE16J715035): Reduced face value of Rs. 8,292,586.49 per PTC.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the reduced face values and ensure trading proceeds accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update affecting debt segment trading parameters for specified PTC series.
