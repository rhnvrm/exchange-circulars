## Summary

FRESITA PROTEINS LIMITED has announced a rights issue of equity shares. The company fixed 30/10/2026 as the record date, and trading in the company's equity shares will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the same date under settlement number DR-746/2026-2027.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: FRESITA PROTEINS LIMITED (Scrip Code: 519242)
- Record Date: 30/10/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 30/10/2026
- Issue Details: 05 (FIVE) Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs.110/- per share for every 06 (SIX) Equity Shares held.
- Rights Issue Price: Rs.120/- per Rights Equity Share (including premium of Rs.110/- each), payable upon application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions in the company's equity shares comply with the ex-rights schedule and note the record date parameters.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28/10/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 30/10/2026
- Record Date: 30/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are entitled to subscribe to the rights shares according to the specified ratio, affecting capital structure and valuation.
