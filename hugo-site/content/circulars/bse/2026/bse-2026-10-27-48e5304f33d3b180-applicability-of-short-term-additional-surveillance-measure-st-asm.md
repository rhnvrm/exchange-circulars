## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective October 29, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I are subject to an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions created from October 29, 2026.
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II are subject to an applicable rate of margin of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. October 29, 2026 on all open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions created from October 29, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company or entity.
- The price band of a scrip moving out of the framework shall be reinstated to the original price band, subject to no other surveillance measures applying.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for shortlisted securities under the ST-ASM framework in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must ensure adherence to the enhanced margin requirements for open and new positions.
- For any clarifications, members may contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- October 28, 2026: Reference date for open positions.
- October 29, 2026: Effective date for the applicability of ST-ASM surveillance measures and margin requirements.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for participants trading in securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, potentially impacting liquidity and trading activity in those scrips.
