## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 1,09,974 equity shares of Prabha Energy Ltd. (Scrip Code: 544379) resulting from the conversion of partly-paid shares to fully paid-up securities issued on a rights basis are permitted for trading with effect from Wednesday, November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Prabha Energy Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 544379
- **ISIN:** INE0I0M01023
- **Security Details:** 1,09,974 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued on a rights basis, converted from partly-paid to fully paid-up.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 342286425 - 342396398
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the newly listed securities and note their availability for trading under the designated scrip code and ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action with minimal operational impact on overall market liquidity.
