## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 4,78,435 new equity shares of Tega Industries Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Tega Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 543413, ISIN: INE011K01018)
- Number of Shares: 4,78,435 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1984/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1994/-).
- Allotment Date: 15/10/2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 75127699 to 75606133.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 31/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to trading permissions and lock-in requirements as specified by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 15, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of Tega Industries Limited following preferential allotment to non-promoters.
