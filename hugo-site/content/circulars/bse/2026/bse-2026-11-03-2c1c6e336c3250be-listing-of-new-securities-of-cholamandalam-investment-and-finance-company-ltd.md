## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading admission of 30,54,849 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd., effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 511243
- **ISIN:** INE121A01024
- **Securities:** 30,54,849 Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted to QIPs pursuant to compulsorily convertible debentures.
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1,407.59/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 854455958 to 857510806
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the admission for trading of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard equity expansion via QIP conversion with minimal direct market disruption, increasing the total listed equity base of the company.
