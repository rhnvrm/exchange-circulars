## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the upcoming rights issue of equity shares by Mihika Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 538895). The company has fixed November 6, 2026, as the record date, and transactions will take place on an ex-rights basis starting on the same date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mihika Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 538895
- Rights Ratio: 09 (Nine) Equity Shares for every 01 (One) Equity Share held.
- Issue Price: Cash at par (Rs. 10/- each).
- Record Date: 06/11/2026.
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 06/11/2026.
- Segment: Dematerialised Securities – Rolling Settlement Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; standard corporate action processing under BSE listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure transactions in the equity shares are executed on an ex-rights basis effective from November 6, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 November 2026
- Record Date: 06 November 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From: 06 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders registered as of the record date are entitled to the rights issue ratio. Market participants and trading members need to account for the ex-rights price adjustment in trading systems.
