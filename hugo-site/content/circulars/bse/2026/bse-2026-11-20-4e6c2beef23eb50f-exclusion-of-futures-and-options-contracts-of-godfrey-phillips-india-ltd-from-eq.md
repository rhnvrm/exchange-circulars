## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Godfrey Phillips India Ltd from the Equity Derivatives segment, pursuant to SEBI circular guidelines on stock eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Godfrey Phillips India Ltd
- Derivatives Asset Code: GDPI
- Equity Scrip Code: 500163
- Discontinuation Date: January 29, 2027
- No new expiry month contracts will be generated after the announcement.

## Regulatory Changes

Reference is made to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024, regarding the eligibility criteria of stocks and BSE Notice no 20240902-3 dated September 02, 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that existing unexpired contracts for November 2026, December 2026, and January 2027 will continue to trade and expire on their respective expiry dates (November 26, 2026, December 31, 2026, and January 28, 2027).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- November 2026 Expiry: November 26, 2026
- December 2026 Expiry: December 31, 2026
- January 2027 Expiry (Final): January 28, 2027
- Discontinuation / Effective Date: January 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading in F&O contracts for Godfrey Phillips India Ltd will cease following the expiry of January 2027 contracts, impacting market participants holding positions in this security's derivatives segment.
