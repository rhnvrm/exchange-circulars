## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the debt securities of JSW Paints Limited, announcing revised terms, ISIN, Scrip ID, and names effective from November 25, 2026, in continuation of earlier Notice No. 20261120-4.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 977287
- Old ISIN: INE754Y08027 | New ISIN: INE754Y08084
- Old Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: 365JPL30 | New Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: RRJPL30
- Old Scrip Long Name: JSWPL-3.65%-10-11-30-PVT | New Scrip Long Name: JSWPL-RR-10-11-30-PVT
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Update in security identification details and terms for listed debt securities on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their systems with the revised ISIN and scrip particulars for smooth trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update affecting only the specific debt securities of JSW Paints Limited with no broader market impact.
