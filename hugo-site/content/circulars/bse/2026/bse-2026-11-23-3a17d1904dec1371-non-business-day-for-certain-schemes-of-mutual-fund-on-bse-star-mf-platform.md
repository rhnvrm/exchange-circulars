## Summary

BSE has announced a Non-Business Day for specific schemes of PGIM India Mutual Fund and Sundaram Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform on account of market closures on November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Fund Houses: PGIM India Mutual Fund and Sundaram Mutual Fund.
- Affected Schemes:
  1. PGIM India Emerging Markets Equity Fund of Fund
  2. PGIM India Global Equity Opportunities Fund of Fund
  3. PGIM India Global Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund
  4. Sundaram Global Brand Theme-Equity Active FOF
- Non-Business Day Date: November 26, 2026.
- Restricted Transactions: Subscription, redemption, switch, SIP/XSIP, STP, and SWP transactions are not permitted through the BSE StAR MF Platform on the specified date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational circular regarding scheme-specific non-business days.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the non-business day and ensure transaction execution is managed accordingly without initiating restricted transactions on November 26, 2026, for the specified schemes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Non-Business Day: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors cannot execute subscription, redemption, switch, SIP/XSIP, STP, or SWP transactions for the specified PGIM and Sundaram fund schemes on November 26, 2026, via the BSE StAR MF platform.
