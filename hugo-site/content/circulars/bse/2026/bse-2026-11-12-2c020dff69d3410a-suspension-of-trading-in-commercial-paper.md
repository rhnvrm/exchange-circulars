## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) of several companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified entities from their respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in Commercial Papers due to redemption record dates.
- Affected entities include JM Financial Properties and Holdings Limited, Kosamattam Finance Limited, Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Ltd., BOBCARD LIMITED, and Vertis Infrastructure Trust.
- Record and ex-dates range from November 13, 2026, to November 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed entities on and after the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 12/11/2026
- JM Financial Properties and Holdings Limited (Ex-Date/Record Date): 13/11/2026
- Kosamattam Finance Limited (Ex-Date/Record Date): 16/11/2026
- Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Ltd. (Ex-Date/Record Date): 16/11/2026
- BOBCARD LIMITED (Ex-Date/Record Date): 17/11/2026
- Vertis Infrastructure Trust (Ex-Date/Record Date): 19/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt segment commercial paper redemption; limited to routine market participants.
