## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliance with certain regulations of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Specifically, trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in the Z group on the first trading day of every week for MSR India Ltd (Scrip Code: 508922) will be discontinued with effect from December 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Relates to Exchange Notice No. 20260416-28 dated April 16, 2026, regarding trading suspension for non-compliance with Regulation 31 (Submission of Shareholding Pattern) for September 2025 and December 2025 quarters.
- As per SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023 (last updated January 30, 2026), the mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week completes on December 07, 2026.
- Weekly trading on 'Trade for Trade' basis in Z group for MSR India Ltd will cease effective December 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI Master Circular guidelines regarding the completion of the mandated six-month weekly trading period for non-compliant suspended companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must resolve pending non-compliances under SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **April 16, 2026:** Initial suspension notice issued.
- **December 07, 2026:** Completion of mandated six months of weekly trading.
- **December 08, 2026:** Discontinuation of weekly trading in securities on 'Trade for Trade' basis.

## Impact Assessment

- Limited impact affecting specific non-compliant entities such as MSR India Ltd (Scrip Code: 508922).
