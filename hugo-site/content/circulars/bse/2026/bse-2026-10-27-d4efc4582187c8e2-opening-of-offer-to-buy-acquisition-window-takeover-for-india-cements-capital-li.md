## Summary

Mr. Sandeep Jain, Mr. Vikas Garg, and Mr. Rahul Nagar, along with Mrs. Neha Agarwal (PAC), have announced an open offer to acquire up to 56,43,612 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the voting share capital) of India Cements Capital Limited at an offer price of Rs. 12/- per share in cash pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** India Cements Capital Limited.
- **Acquirers & PAC:** Mr. Sandeep Jain (Acquirer 1), Mr. Vikas Garg (Acquirer 2), Mr. Rahul Nagar (Acquirer 3), and Mrs. Neha Agarwal (PAC).
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 12.00 per fully paid-up equity share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 56,43,612 equity shares of face value ₹10 each, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, October 28, 2026, to Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, November 10, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeover, Buy Back, and Delisting as per SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131, along with exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians are required to participate in accordance with the revised guidelines of SEBI and BSE exchange notices for tender-offer acquisition windows.
- Letter of Offer is attached for market participants' perusal.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** October 28, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday:** November 10, 2026 (Excluding trading)
- **Offer Closing Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of India Cements Capital Limited have the opportunity to tender their equity shares during the acquisition window through stock exchange mechanisms within the specified dates.
