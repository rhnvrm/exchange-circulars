## Summary

BSE has notified the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities satisfying the relevant surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance measures include enhanced margin requirements effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Measures become effective on November 25, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created from November 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II enhanced margin constraints as per revised ASM framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in shortlisted securities will experience higher capital lock-in due to increased margin requirements (50% to 100%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
