## Summary

BSE has announced that the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective November 11, 2026, pursuant to previous notices dated October 19, 2026, and November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must take note of the scheme availability and ISIN details for processing client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless transaction processing for investors and distributors participating in the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
