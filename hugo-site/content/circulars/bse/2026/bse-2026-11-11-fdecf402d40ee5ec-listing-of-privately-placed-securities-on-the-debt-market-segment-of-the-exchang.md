## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers of Manappuram Finance Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, and L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited have been listed.
- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form under designated ISINs with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuing and paying agents include Axis Bank, IndusInd Bank, and ICICI Bank.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form using the specified ISINs and scrip codes.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 09/11/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 11/11/2026
- Redemption Dates: February/March 2027 (varying by issuance)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange admission for the newly issued commercial papers of the respective issuers.
