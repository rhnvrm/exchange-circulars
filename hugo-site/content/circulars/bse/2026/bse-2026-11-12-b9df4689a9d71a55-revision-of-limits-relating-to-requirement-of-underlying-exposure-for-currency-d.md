## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members and participants about SEBI Circular No. HO/(561)2026-MRD-POD2/I/25177/2026 dated November 05, 2026, regarding the revision of limits relating to the requirement of underlying exposure for currency derivatives contracts.

## Key Points

- References SEBI Circular No. HO/(561)2026-MRD-POD2/I/25177/2026 dated November 05, 2026.
- Focuses on currency derivatives contracts underlying exposure limits.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Ltd. Risk Management department.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of limits relating to the requirement of underlying exposure for currency derivatives contracts as stipulated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants are advised to take note of the revised limits and ensure adherence to SEBI guidelines regarding underlying exposure for currency derivatives.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in currency derivatives must verify compliance with the updated underlying exposure limits and manage risk management practices accordingly.
