## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window for the delisting of JINDAL PHOTO LIMITED (Notice No. 20261109-21). The bidding concluded between 02/11/2026 and 06/11/2026, with revised settlement and obligation download dates now scheduled for November 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Delisting, Settlement No. 2026131
- **Bidding Period:** 02/11/2026 to 06/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 12/11/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 13/11/2026
- **Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Delisting)" and settlement No. 2026131.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of settlement timeline for open offer / acquisition window under stock exchange delisting guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and Custodians must use the specified Market Type "(Delisting)" and settlement number 2026131 in the depository system. Client-wise stamp duty charges are specified in the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 02/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 06/11/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 12/11/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 13/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects clearing and settlement participants involved in the delisting offer of Jindal Photo Limited, requiring adherence to revised obligation and settlement timelines.
