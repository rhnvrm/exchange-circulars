## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in a specified 364-day Treasury Bill (Scrip Code: 805150, ISIN: IN002025Z351) effective November 23, 2026, due to its maturity.

## Key Points

- **Security Details**: 364T261126 (Scrip Code: 805150, ISIN: IN002025Z351).
- **Effective Date**: Trading is suspended with effect from November 23, 2026 (DR-762/2026-2027).
- **Reason**: Reaching redemption / maturity date.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding security maturity and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bill/Government Security from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a standard maturity event for government debt securities.
