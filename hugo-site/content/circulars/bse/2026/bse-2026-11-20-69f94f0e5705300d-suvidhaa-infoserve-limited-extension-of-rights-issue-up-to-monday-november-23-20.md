BSE has issued a notice regarding the extension of the ongoing rights issue of Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited. The closing date has been extended from Friday, November 20, 2026, to Monday, November 23, 2026.

- Company: Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited
- Action: Extension of Rights Issue
- Revised Closing Date: Monday, November 23, 2026
- Previous Closing Date: Friday, November 20, 2026

None.

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the revised timeline for the ongoing rights issue.
