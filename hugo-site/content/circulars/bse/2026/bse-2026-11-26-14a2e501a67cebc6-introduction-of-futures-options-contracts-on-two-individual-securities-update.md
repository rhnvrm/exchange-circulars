## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on two individual securities, Acutaas Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 543349, Derivative Asset Code: ACUL) and Apar Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 532259, Derivative Asset Code: APIL), with effect from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts introduced for Acutaas Chemicals Ltd (Market Lot: 150, Strike Interval: 100, Strikes: 7-1-7) and Apar Industries Ltd (Market Lot: 50, Strike Interval: 250, Strikes: 7-1-7).
- Effective date for trading in these F&O contracts is November 27, 2026.
- Contract details will be available in the derivatives contract master file generated at the end of day on November 26, 2026.
- Contract master file can be accessed via the extranet and BSE India website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the equity derivatives segment by adding F&O contracts for two additional individual securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to refer to exchange notice no. 20261116-30 dated November 16, 2026, and ensure their trading systems and contract masters are updated ahead of trading commencement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026
- Contract Master Generation: November 26, 2026 (End of Day)
- Effective Trading Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with expanded hedging and trading instruments in Acutaas Chemicals Ltd and Apar Industries Ltd.
