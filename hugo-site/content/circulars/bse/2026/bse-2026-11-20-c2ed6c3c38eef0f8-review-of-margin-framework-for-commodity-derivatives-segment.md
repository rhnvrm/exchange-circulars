## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has issued a notice regarding the review of the margin framework for the commodity derivatives segment, referencing SEBI circular HO/47/16/14(3)2026-MRD-POD1/I/25885/2026 dated November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI circular HO/47/16/14(3)2026-MRD-POD1/I/25885/2026 dated November 17, 2026.
- Focuses on the review of the margin framework for the commodity derivatives segment.
- Annexure 1 contains the detailed circular issued by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised margin framework guidelines for commodity derivatives as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants are requested to take note of the revised margin framework guidelines and ensure compliance with SEBI's directives.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances risk management practices and margin calculations across commodity derivative contracts.
