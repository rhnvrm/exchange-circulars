## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by nine companies under Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the exchange with effect from Wednesday, November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Total Companies:** 9 listed companies issuing further shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- **Companies & Quantities:**
  1. Anthem Biosciences Limited: 135,925 shares (ISIN: INE0CZ201020, Scrip Code: 544449)
  2. CarTrade Tech Limited: 1,000 shares (ISIN: INE290S01011, Scrip Code: 543333)
  3. Computer Age Management Services Limited: 7,130 shares (ISIN: INE596I01020, Scrip Code: 543232)
  4. DCB Bank Limited: 81,100 shares (ISIN: INE503A01015, Scrip Code: 532772)
  5. HDFC Bank Limited: 4,675,598 shares (ISIN: INE040A01034, Scrip Code: 500180)
  6. IIFL Capital Services Limited: 180,499 shares (ISIN: INE489L01022, Scrip Code: 542773)
  7. L&T Finance Limited: 52,876 shares (ISIN: INE498L01015, Scrip Code: 533519)
  8. Rishabh Instruments Limited: 1,000 shares (ISIN: INE0N2P01017, Scrip Code: 543977)
  9. Vaibhav Global Limited: 39,489 shares (ISIN: INE884A01027, Scrip Code: 532156)
- **Lock-in:** No lock-in (NA) for all listed shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing of shares pursuant to ESOP/ESOS exercise.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants to note the listing of additional equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 3, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Dilution impact is marginal given the routine nature of ESOP allotments across these companies. Shares are available for regular trading from the effective date.
