## Summary

BSE announced the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills) – 91 Day, 182 Day, and 364 Day – scheduled for October 28, 2026, following the RBI press release and Exchange guidelines.

## Key Points

- Bidding Platform: iBBS web-based system via the NCB-GSec module.
- Securities offered: 91 Day T-Bill (091T270127, Rs. 8000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (182T280427, Rs. 8000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T281027, Rs. 7000 Cr).
- Minimum lot & multiples: Rs. 10,000.
- Maximum amount: Rs. 400 Cr for 91-day and 182-day T-Bills, and Rs. 350 Cr for 364-day T-Bill.

## Regulatory Changes

Procedural notice regarding Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through RBI framework and BSE iBBS platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors are required to submit bids through the NCB-GSec module adhering to the prescribed timelines and bidding parameters.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: October 26, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): October 27, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 28, 2026, till 8:00 am
- Auction Date: October 28, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation of eligible investors in government debt issuances through structured exchange-based non-competitive bidding.
