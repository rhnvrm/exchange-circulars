## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that units of four plans under Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'F' Group with effect from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 (Duration: 125 days)
- Investment Manager: Kotak Mahindra Asset Management Company Limited
- Plans Listed:
  - Regular Plan Growth Option (Scrip Code: 545025, ISIN: INF174KA1I05, Allotment Date: 06/11/2026, Redemption Date: 11/03/2027)
  - Regular Plan IDCW Payout Option (Scrip Code: 545026, ISIN: INF174KA1I13, Allotment Date: 06/11/2026, Redemption Date: 11/03/2027)
  - Direct Plan Growth Option (Scrip Code: 545027, ISIN: INF174KA1H89, Allotment Date: 06/11/2026, Redemption Date: 11/03/2027)
  - Direct Plan IDCW Payout Option (Scrip Code: 545028, ISIN: INF174KA1H97, Allotment Date: 06/11/2026, Redemption Date: 11/03/2027)
- Market Lot: 1 unit
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Computer Age Management Services Ltd. (CAMS)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 6, 2026
- Listing Date: November 16, 2026
- Date of Redemption: March 11, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading and liquidity for investors of Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 on BSE.
