## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies where it observed significant price or volume movements in recent trading sessions and sought clarifications. Replies have been received from four companies, while responses from nineteen other companies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Notice No. 20261126-20 dated November 26, 2026, issued by the DOSS Surveillance department.
- **Companies with Replies Received:** 
  1. Bajaj Healthcare Ltd (Scrip Code: 539872)
  2. Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India Ltd (Scrip Code: 533088)
  3. Phaarmasia Ltd (Scrip Code: 523620)
  4. Yaashvi Jewellers Ltd (Scrip Code: 544770)
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** 19 companies including Ascensive Educare Ltd, Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Surveillance clarification disclosure)

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements as requested by the Exchange under surveillance guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to market participants regarding exchange inquiries into unusual price and volume activity across specified scrips.
