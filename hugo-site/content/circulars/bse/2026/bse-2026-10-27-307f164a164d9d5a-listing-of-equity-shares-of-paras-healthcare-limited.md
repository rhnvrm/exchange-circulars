## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, October 28, 2026, the equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- No. of Securities: 108,201,194 Equity Shares of Rs.1/- each fully paid up.
- Scrip ID / Code: PARASHLTH / 545005.
- ISIN No.: INE661T01029.
- Issue Price: Rs. 693/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- and premium Rs. 692/-).
- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026.
- Market Lot: 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the Company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Corporate action executed in respect of shares in demat form to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026.
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited on the exchange.
