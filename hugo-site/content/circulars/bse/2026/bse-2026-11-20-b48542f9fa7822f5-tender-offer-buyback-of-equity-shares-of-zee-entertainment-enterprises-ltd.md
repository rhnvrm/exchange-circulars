## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. has fixed November 25, 2026, as the Record Date for determining shareholder entitlement and eligibility to participate in the equity shares buyback offer.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd.
- BSE Security Code: 505537
- Record Date: November 25, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 25, 2026
- Purpose: Determination of entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback offer under the dematerialised securities rolling settlement segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for executing transactions and processing buyback entitlements accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 20, 2026
- Record Date: November 25, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders holding shares as of the record date can participate in the buyback tender offer. Trading members should ensure client positions and demat holdings align with the settlement schedule.
