## Summary

BSE has announced necessary adjustments for all available Futures & Options contracts on Coal India Ltd (Derivative Asset Code: COAL, Scrip Code: 533278) following the company's declaration of an interim dividend of Rs. 10.25 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- Coal India Ltd fixed November 04, 2026, as the Record Date for the payment of an interim dividend of Rs. 10.25/- per equity share.
- F&O contract adjustments will be executed by the Exchange on Tuesday, November 03, 2026.
- Options Contracts Adjustment: The full dividend value of Rs. 10.25/- will be deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of November 03, 2026.
- Futures Contracts Adjustment: The adjusted futures price will be the reference rate (daily MTM settlement price on Nov 03, 2026) minus the dividend amount of Rs. 10.25/-.

## Regulatory Changes

In line with SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135 dated December 16, 2016, and Exchange notice number 20180710-26 regarding corporate action adjustments for stock options.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Equity Derivatives Segment must take note of the strike price and base rate adjustments applicable from November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 30, 2026
- F&O Adjustment Date: November 03, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures fair valuation and continuous trading of existing Futures and Options contracts on Coal India Ltd post-dividend payout.
