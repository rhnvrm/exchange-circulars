## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding surveillance action concerning unsolicited videos and messages circulated on social media platforms, referencing earlier notice No. 20230224-38. Strict surveillance measures similar to GSM Stage IV are imposed on shortlisted scrips based on unusual price/volume movement and video circulations.

## Key Points

- Scrips shortlisted based on unsolicited video circulation, unusual price/volume movement, and other factors.
- Actions similar to GSM Stage IV imposed on shortlisted scrips.
- Certain securities will move out of the framework effective November 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trade for Trade with a price band of 5% or lower w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- Trading permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week) w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of trade value to be deposited by buyers with no upward movement w.e.f. November 18, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the trading restrictions, price bands, and ASD requirements for shortlisted scrips.
- Actions operate in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for exiting framework / Trade for Trade / price band: November 17, 2026.
- Effective date for ASD (100%): November 18, 2026.
- Effective date for weekly trading: November 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Significantly restricts liquidity and trading frequency for shortlisted securities to curb speculative activities driven by social media rumors and unsolicited messages.
