## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has issued the Tender Period / Pre-Expiry Margin Tracker applicable to futures contracts in the Commodity Derivatives segment expiring in December 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261118-15, issued by the BSECL Risk Department.
- Applies to the Commodity Derivatives segment for contracts expiring in December 2026.
- Outlines data pertaining to the Tender Period and applicable Tender Period Margin Percent for each Tender Period Day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard operational risk management notice regarding pre-expiry tender period margin requirements for commodity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must take note of the attached margin tracker and ensure adequate margin provisioning for December 2026 commodity contracts.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 Nov 2026
- Contract Expiry: December 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates proactive risk management and margin planning for market participants trading commodity derivative contracts approaching expiry.
