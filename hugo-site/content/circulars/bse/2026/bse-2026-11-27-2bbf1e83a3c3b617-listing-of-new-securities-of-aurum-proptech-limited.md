## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,97,93,309 new equity shares of Aurum Proptech Limited (Scrip Code: 539289, ISIN: INE898S01029) issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 231.42/- per share (face value Rs. 5/-, premium Rs. 226.42/-).

## Key Points

- Issuer: Aurum Proptech Limited.
- Scrip Code: 539289, ISIN: INE898S01029.
- Number of Securities: 1,97,93,309 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each.
- Issue Price: Rs. 231.42/- per share (including premium of Rs. 226.42/-).
- Distribution Numbers (Dist. Nos.): 78389283 to 98182591.
- Effective Date for Trading: Monday, November 30, 2026.
- Lock-in Details: 1,97,93,309 shares locked in up to June 14, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing of new securities issued under preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading parameters for the newly listed equity shares effective from November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: June 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity base of Aurum Proptech Limited through preferential allotment to non-promoters, subject to specified lock-in periods.
