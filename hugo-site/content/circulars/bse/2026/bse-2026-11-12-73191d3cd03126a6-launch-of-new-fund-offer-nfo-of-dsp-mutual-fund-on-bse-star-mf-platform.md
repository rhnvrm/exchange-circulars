## Summary

DSP Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from November 13, 2026, to November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days - Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth.
- NFO Period: November 13, 2026 to November 17, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 17, 2026 for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 19, 2026.
- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted subject to clear funds receipt.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure clear funds are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 18, 2026, for units allotment. Client funds must be initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 13, 2026
- NFO Close Date: November 17, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 18, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors access to DSP Mutual Fund's fixed maturity plan via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines.
