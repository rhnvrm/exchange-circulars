## Summary

BSE has announced the enabling of the SMART Switch feature for the BAJAJ FINSERV BUSINESS CYCLE FUND NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from November 20, 2026, until December 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- The SMART Switch feature is available through User Interface, Bulk Upload, and APIs for eligible Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund schemes.
- Applicable strictly for non-Demat lump sum subscriptions up to 1:00 PM on December 02, 2026.
- Switch out transactions are processed on a FIFO basis from existing units and newly purchased units.
- Orders placed under the SMART Switch feature can be identified via the Remarks column in the Provisional Order and Order Status Reports.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of operational guidelines and switch processing workflows for NFO SMART Switch transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must utilize appropriate scheme credentials in accordance with investor requirements and ensure all purchase orders in SMART Switch schemes are placed before 1:00 PM on December 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Closure Date & Time:** December 02, 2026 up to 1:00 PM
- **RTA Switch Reporting Date:** December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates streamlined investment switching into the Bajaj Finserv Business Cycle Fund NFO for market participants and investors utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform.
