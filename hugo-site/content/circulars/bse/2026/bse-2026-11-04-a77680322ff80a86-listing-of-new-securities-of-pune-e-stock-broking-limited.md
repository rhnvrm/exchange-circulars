## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 17,00,000 new equity shares of Pune E - Stock Broking Limited (Scrip Code: 544141) issued on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants are listed and permitted to trade effective November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Pune E - Stock Broking Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544141
- **ISIN:** INE510U01018
- **Securities:** 17,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 161/- (Issue Price: Rs. 171/-) to Promoters and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Effective Date:** November 5, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the lock-in periods for the respective shares as specified in the notice.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** 12/08/2026 (7,61,000 shares) and 14/08/2026 (9,39,000 shares)
- **Listing Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** May 9, 2027 and May 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the equity capital base of the company through warrant conversion.
