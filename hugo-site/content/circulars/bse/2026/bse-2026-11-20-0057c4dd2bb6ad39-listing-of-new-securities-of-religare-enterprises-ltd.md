## Summary

BSE has notified the listing and permission to trade 35,00,000 new equity shares of Religare Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 532915) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Religare Enterprises Ltd
- Scrip Code: 532915
- ISIN: INE621H01010
- Number of Shares: 35,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 235/- per share (including premium of Rs. 225/-)
- Date of Allotment: 06/08/2026
- Effective Trading Date: 23/11/2026
- Lock-in Details: 35,00,000 shares locked in up to 25/05/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the listing and trading permission granted to the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 06/08/2026
- Listing/Trading Effective Date: 23/11/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 25/05/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity share capital base of the company following warrant conversion.
