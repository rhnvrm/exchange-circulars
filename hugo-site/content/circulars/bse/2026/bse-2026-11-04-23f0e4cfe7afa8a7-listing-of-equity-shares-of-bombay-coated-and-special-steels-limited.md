## Summary

Effective from Thursday, November 5, 2026, the equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the trade-for-trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545018
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: BCSSL
- ISIN No.: INE0XFY01011
- Total Securities: 62,824,211 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 82/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 72/-)
- Date of Allotment: November 3, 2026
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on November 5, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the trade-for-trade segment restriction for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Demat lock-in period has been marked in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: November 5, 2026
- Date of Allotment: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited shares on BSE with initial trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
