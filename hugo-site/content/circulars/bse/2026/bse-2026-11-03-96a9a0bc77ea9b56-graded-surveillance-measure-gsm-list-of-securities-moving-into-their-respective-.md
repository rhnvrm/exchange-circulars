## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the movement of certain securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 04, 2026. These measures aim to enhance market integrity and safeguard investor interests.

## Key Points

- Effective date for movement into higher GSM stages: November 04, 2026.
- Settlement for affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM Stage (Stages I, II, III, and IV).

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to progressive surveillance actions based on their GSM stage under the framework published on February 27, 2017.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value.
- Stage III: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- Stage IV: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Settlements must be handled on a trade-to-trade basis without allowing netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 03, 2026.
- Effective Date of GSM Movement: November 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading activity in securities moving to higher GSM stages will face increased liquidity constraints due to trade-for-trade settlement and mandatory deposits (ASD), impacting short-term trading interest and price volatility.
