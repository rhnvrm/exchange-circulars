## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I or Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective November 02, 2026, on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective November 02, 2026, on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the revised ST-ASM framework for listed securities meeting surveillance thresholds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must maintain the prescribed margin rates (50% or 100%) for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Open Positions & New Positions: November 02, 2026 (based on open positions as on October 30, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
