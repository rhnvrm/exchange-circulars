## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their Commercial Papers (CPs). Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in Commercial Papers due to upcoming redemption record dates.
- Includes instruments from Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd, and others.
- Trading members must avoid transactions in these specific CPs past their record/ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers listed in the notice on or after their respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26/10/2026
- Record/Ex-Dates: 28/10/2026 to 05/11/2026 across various CPs.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt market instruments reaching their redemption milestone.
