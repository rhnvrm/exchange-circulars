## Summary

Catalyst Trusteeship Limited has informed the Exchange regarding the record date fixed for the payment of interest and part redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued under Samriddhi Trust Series 3. Trading members and investors are advised that trading will occur with a reduced face value.

- Scrip Code: 978119
- Scrip ID: SAMRIDDHI STS3-12.50%-15-8-31-PTC
- ISIN: INE303715010
- Record Date: 28/10/2026
- Ex-Date: 28/10/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 10,737,974.81 per PTC
