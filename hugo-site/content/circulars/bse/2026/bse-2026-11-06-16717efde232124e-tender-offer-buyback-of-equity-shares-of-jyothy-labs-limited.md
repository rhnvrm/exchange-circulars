## Summary

Jyothy Labs Limited has fixed a record date for determining the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the tender offer buyback of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Jyothy Labs Limited (BSE: 532926 / 132926)
- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Basis / From Date: November 11, 2026 (Distribution Reference: DR- 754/2026-2027)
- Segment: Dematerialised Securities – Rolling Settlement Segment

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement schedule for the buyback.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding equity shares of Jyothy Labs Limited as of the record date are eligible to participate in the buyback tender offer.
