## Summary

BSE has announced that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of FRESITA PROTEINS LIMITED will be permitted on the Exchange starting Thursday, November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** FRESITA PROTEINS LIMITED
- **Scrip Code:** 751154
- **Scrip ID:** FRESITA-RE
- **ISIN No:** INE995U20011
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** November 12, 2026 to November 20, 2026
- **Rights Issue Period:** November 12, 2026 to November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard rights entitlement trading guidelines apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must settle trades on a T+1 rolling settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- Rights Entitlements are tradable in dematerialized form only.
- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that the purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- **RE Trading Start Date:** November 12, 2026
- **RE Trading End Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for eligible shareholders of Fresita Proteins Limited under T+1 trade-for-trade settlement.
