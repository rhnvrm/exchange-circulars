## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 1,00,00,000 new equity shares of DHANSAFAL FINSERVE LIMITED (Scrip Code: 512048) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DHANSAFAL FINSERVE LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 512048
- ISIN: INE195E01020
- Securities: 1,00,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 3.31/- (Issue Price: Rs. 4.31/- per share)
- Allotment Date: 18/09/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: November 20, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 227140001 to 237140000
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 30/05/2028 for all 1,00,00,000 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 18, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine preferential allotment and listing of equity shares resulting in an increase in the paid-up capital of the company.
