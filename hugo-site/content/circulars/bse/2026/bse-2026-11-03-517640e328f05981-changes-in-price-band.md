## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 12 securities as part of its surveillance measures, effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revisions affect 12 listed securities on the equity segment.
- Price bands are revised to 2%, 5%, or 10% depending on the security.
- Effective date of the revised price bands is November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specific scrips as determined by the Surveillance department of BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to the new price bands, restricting excessive price volatility on the effective date.
