## Summary

Union Mutual Fund has announced the merger of UNION Dynamic Term Fund (Transferor Scheme) into UNION Corporate Bond Fund (Transferee Scheme), effective after the close of business hours on December 22, 2026. Transactions in the transferor scheme are subject to specific cut-offs and migration rules on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Merger takes effect after close of business hours on December 22, 2026.
- **Transferor Scheme:** UNION DYNAMIC TERM FUND
- **Transferee Scheme:** UNION CORPORATE BOND FUND
- **Fresh Subscriptions:** Suspended in the Transferor Scheme after the applicable cut-off time on November 20, 2026.
- **Redemptions & Switches:** Allowed up to timelines permitted by the AMC; investors not wishing to merge can redeem/switch without exit load between November 23, 2026, and December 22, 2026.
- **Systematic Registrations:** Existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP registrations in the Transferor Scheme will automatically migrate to the Transferee Scheme on December 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and platform participants must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsums, and additional investments in the Transferor Scheme from November 20, 2026.
- Investors wishing to opt out of the merger must submit redemption or switch requests via the BSE StAR MF Platform between November 23, 2026, and December 22, 2026, without exit load.
- Investors with active systematic facilities (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) who do not wish to migrate must initiate cancellation requests within AMC timelines.

## Important Dates

- **November 20, 2026:** Suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum, additional investments, and SIP/XSIP registrations in the Transferor Scheme after cut-off time.
- **November 23, 2026 to December 22, 2026:** Window for exit-load-free redemption or switch transactions for investors opting out.
- **December 22, 2026 (Close of business hours):** Effective date of merger and automatic migration of systematic registrations.

## Impact Assessment

Unitholders of UNION Dynamic Term Fund are transitioned to UNION Corporate Bond Fund unless they exercise the exit/switch option. Operations on the BSE StAR MF Platform will reflect automated scheme mapping post-merger.
