## Summary

BSE has announced the change in name of trading member Motisons Shares Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3267) to "MOTISONS SHARES LIMITED" with effect from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Trading Member Name: Motisons Shares Pvt. Ltd.
- New Trading Member Name: MOTISONS SHARES LIMITED
- Member Number: 3267
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; administrative record update only.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should note the updated corporate name for records and correspondence. Clarifications can be directed to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 12 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, strictly limited to updating records with the new name of the trading member.
