## Summary

BSE has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Petronet LNG Ltd (PLNG) following the announcement of an interim dividend of Rs. 7.00 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- Security: Petronet LNG Ltd (Derivative Asset Code: PLNG, Scrip Code: 532522).
- Corporate Action: Interim dividend of Rs. 7.00/- per equity share.
- Record Date: November 06, 2026.
- Adjustment Date: November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments performed in line with SEBI Master Circular No SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135 and Exchange notice number 20180710-26.

## Compliance Requirements

- Options Contracts: Rs. 7.00/- deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of November 05, 2026; adjusted strikes applicable from November 06, 2026.
- Futures Contracts: Adjusted base rate calculated as reference rate (daily MTM settlement price on Nov 05, 2026) less Rs. 7.00/- dividend amount.

## Important Dates

- Adjustment Date: November 05, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Record Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard F&O contract adjustment reflecting the cash dividend payout, ensuring contract parity for market participants.
