## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective November 5, 2026, on all open positions as on November 4, 2026, and new positions created.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective November 5, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM framework measures in conjunction with existing surveillance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 3, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 5, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 4, 2026 and new positions created from November 5, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience higher margin blocking (50% to 100%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the affected scrips.
