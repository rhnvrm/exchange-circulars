## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released an updated Joint Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for settlement price computation, incorporating recent regulatory changes relating to the Closing Auction Session (CAS).

## Key Points

- Joint SOP for settlement price computation updated.
- Changes reflect recent regulatory developments concerning the Closing Auction Session (CAS).
- Builds upon previous circular nos 20260320-1 dated 20 Mar 2026 & 20261016-23 dated 16 Oct 2026.
- Detailed guidelines provided in Annexure A.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revisions to settlement price computation methodology to align with the Closing Auction Session framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must review the updated Joint SOP in Annexure A for operational reference.
- Contact BSECL settlement operations or relationship managers for queries.

## Important Dates

- Circular issued and effective on 30 October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines settlement operations and ensures adherence to updated regulatory standards for closing auction pricing.
