## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 2,05,02,939 new equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each issued by 360 ONE WAM Limited (Scrip Code: 542772) on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants. The shares are permitted for trading effective from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: 360 ONE WAM LIMITED (Scrip Code: 542772, ISIN: INE466L01038)
- Securities: 2,05,02,939 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1029/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1030/-).
- Allotment Date: 22/10/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 407914201 to 428417139
- Lock-in Details: 2,05,02,939 shares locked-in up to 30/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate securities listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: November 17, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 22, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital of 360 ONE WAM Limited through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in restrictions.
