## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion, stage movement, and exclusion of securities effective November 2026. Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin and are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created w.e.f. November 08, 2026 for securities in Annexure I.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM framework have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlist levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ESM is strictly due to market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework modifications in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.
- Shifting of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Market participants must adhere to revised price bands, periodic call auctions, and settlement segment shifts.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II movements & moving out list: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin & Trade-for-Trade shifting: November 08, 2026
- Open positions reference date for margin: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and price volatility for shortlisted securities will be significantly impacted due to the shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement, 100% margin requirements, and periodic call auctions.
