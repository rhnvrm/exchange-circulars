## Summary

AXIS Mutual Fund is launching the AXIS Nifty Largemidcap 250 Index Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details:** AXIS Nifty Largemidcap 250 Index Fund (Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth).
- **ISINs:** INF846KA1648 (Regular Plan - Growth), INF846KA1655 (Direct Plan - Growth).
- **NFO Period:** November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for clients who do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on December 11, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 17, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** November 27, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** December 11, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** December 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform participants and investors access to the new AXIS Nifty Largemidcap 250 Index Fund scheme via BSE StAR MF with standard operational workflows.
