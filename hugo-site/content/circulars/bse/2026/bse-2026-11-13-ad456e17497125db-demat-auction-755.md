## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 755 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-755/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on Friday, Nov 13, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Monday, Nov 16, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notice regarding auction schedule and settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: November 13, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: November 16, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement of short deliveries through the auction mechanism with minimal operational impact on general market participants.
