## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under the framework criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective November 03, 2026. Additionally, certain securities are moving to lower stages or exiting the framework entirely as of November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy inclusion criteria, attracting a minimum 100% margin on all open and new positions w.e.f. November 03, 2026 based on positions as of November 02, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework or moving to Stage I are provided in Annexures II and I respectively, with a consolidated list in Annexure III.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including 100% margin requirements and compulsory Trade-for-Trade settlement for identified scrips.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) for open and new positions in securities under the ESM framework starting November 03, 2026.
- Market participants must account for trading restrictions, price bands, and periodic call auctions for securities classified under Stage II of ESM.

## Important Dates

- **November 02, 2026:** Effective date for securities moving to lower stages (Stage I), shifting to Stage II (Trade-for-Trade with 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction), and moving out of ESM.
- **November 03, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin application and shifting of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors holding securities listed under the ESM framework will experience increased margin requirements (100%) and liquidity constraints due to Trade-for-Trade settlement and periodic call auction mechanisms.
