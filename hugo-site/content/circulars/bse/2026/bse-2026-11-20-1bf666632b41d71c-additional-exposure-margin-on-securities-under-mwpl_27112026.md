## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment for securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market-Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This framework is based on 3-month rolling data and is reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- **Additional Exposure Margin:** 15% levied in the equity derivatives segment.
- **Applicability Criteria:** Securities where top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL based on 3 months rolling data.
- **Overlap with Surveillance:** If additional surveillance margin is applicable, the higher of the two margins will be levied.
- **Effective Dates:** Effective from November 27, 2026 (post-expiry of November 2026 contracts) until December 2026 expiry.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of risk-based additional exposure margins for concentrated derivative positions exceeding MWPL thresholds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must account for the additional 15% exposure margin on specified securities listed in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Applicability End:** December 2026 expiry

## Impact Assessment

- Increases capital requirements and margin obligations for trading members and clients holding concentrated positions in affected equity derivative scrips.
