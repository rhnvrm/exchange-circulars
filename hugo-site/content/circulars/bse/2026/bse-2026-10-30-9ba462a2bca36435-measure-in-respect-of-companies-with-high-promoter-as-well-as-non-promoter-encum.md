## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in the framework will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Securities specified in Annexure I satisfy inclusion criteria and will attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Margin applies to all open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions created from November 04, 2026.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective November 02, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of a minimum 75% margin requirement on securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirement across all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments starting from the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Moving out of framework effective date (Annexure II): November 02, 2026
- Open positions valuation date: November 03, 2026
- 75% margin applicability effective date (Annexure I): November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in affected securities will experience significantly higher margin obligations, impacting leverage, liquidity, and risk management parameters for those equity and derivative contracts.
