## Summary

JTL Defence Limited has fixed a record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares to existing public shareholders. Eligible shareholders will receive 3 equity shares for every 2 equity shares held on the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JTL Defence Limited
- Scrip Code: 537254
- Record Date: November 27, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 27, 2026
- Issue Size / Ratio: 3 (Three) Equity Shares for every 2 (Two) Equity Shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 311.40 per share (Face value Rs. 10/- plus premium of Rs. 301.40/-)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible public shareholders must take note of the record date and ex-rights date for executing transactions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 23, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 27, 2026
- Record Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders holding shares as of the record date are eligible to participate in the rights issue. Stock prices will adjust on an ex-rights basis from November 27, 2026.
