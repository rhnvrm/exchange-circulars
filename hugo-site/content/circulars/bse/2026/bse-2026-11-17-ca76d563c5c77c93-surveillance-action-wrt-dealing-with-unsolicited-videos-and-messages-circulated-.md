## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding surveillance actions concerning unsolicited videos and messages circulated on social media platforms. Securities shortlisted based on video circulation, unusual price and volume movements, and other factors will be subjected to strict surveillance measures similar to GSM Stage IV.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted based on circulation of unsolicited videos/messages and unusual market movements.
- Measures imposed are similar to GSM Stage IV.
- Certain securities will move out of the framework effective November 18, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trade for Trade with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Trading restricted to once a week (Every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of trade value to be deposited by buyers with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the trading restrictions, price bands, and deposit requirements for shortlisted scrips.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- November 18, 2026: Trade for Trade with 5% price band effective; certain securities move out of framework.
- November 19, 2026: 100% ASD requirement effective for buyers.
- November 23, 2026: Trading permitted once a week effective.

## Impact Assessment

Significant reduction in liquidity and trading frequency for shortlisted securities due to weekly trading limits and 100% cash deposit requirements.
