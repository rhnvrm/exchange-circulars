## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund has declared 26 November 2026 as a Non-Business Day for certain schemes due to market closures. Consequently, subscription, redemption, switch, SIP, STP, and SWP transactions will not be permitted through the BSE StAR MF Platform for these schemes on the specified date.

## Key Points

- **Non-Business Day Date**: 26 November 2026.
- **Affected Schemes**:
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL US BLUECHIP EQUITY FUND
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL GLOBAL STABLE EQUITY FUND
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL GLOBAL ADVANTAGE FUND
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL NASDAQ 100 INDEX FUND
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL PASSIVE MULTI-ASSET FUND OF FUNDS
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL STRATEGIC METAL AND ENERGY EQUITY FUND OF FUNDS
- **Restricted Transactions**: Subscription, redemption, switch, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Platform**: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Investors and trading members must note the non-business day status and avoid submitting subscription, redemption, or systematic transactions for the listed ICICI Prudential schemes on 26 November 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Non-Business Day**: 26 November 2026
- **Notice Date**: 20 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transactions in the affected global and thematic ICICI Prudential schemes will be temporarily unavailable through the BSE StAR MF platform on 26 November 2026, requiring investors to plan their cash flows and orders accordingly.
