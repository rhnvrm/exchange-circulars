## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-757/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offers accepted on trading terminals on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.
- Auction Offer Entry timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction scheduled for Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must check auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the 'Auction offer Entry Screen' and check corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split) issued by respective departments.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** November 18, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operation affecting members participating in demat auction DR-757/2026-27.
