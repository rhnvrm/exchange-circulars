## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in the Z group for securities of Nagarjuna Fertilizers and Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 539917) w.e.f. December 15, 2026. This follows the completion of the mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week pursuant to SEBI Master Circular requirements.

## Key Points

- Discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliances of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Applies to Nagarjuna Fertilizers and Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 539917).
- Mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week completes on December 14, 2026.
- Trading on 'Trade for Trade' basis in Z group will be discontinued effective December 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026.
- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260428-31 dated April 28, 2026, regarding suspension for non-compliance with Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (September 2025 and December 2025).

## Compliance Requirements

- Relevant entities and investors to note the discontinuation of weekly trading restrictions for the specified scrip effective from the effective date.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.soplodr@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026
- Completion of Mandated Six Months: December 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Discontinuation: December 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impact on Market Operations: Removes weekly trading restrictions for Nagarjuna Fertilizers and Chemicals Ltd upon fulfillment of regulatory timeline under SEBI Master Circular guidelines.
