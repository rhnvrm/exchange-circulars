## Summary

The BSE has noted significant price and volume movements across multiple scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from several companies, attaching replies received from some while noting that replies from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Replies received from: Globalspace Technologies Ltd, Imec Services Ltd, Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd (Volume Variation), Shivchem Agro Ltd, and Shree Manufacturing Company Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 20 companies including Bharat Immunologicals, Billwin Industries, CMX Holdings, Deep Polymers, El Forge, and others.
- Surveillance action taken to maintain market transparency and protect investor interests.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine administrative surveillance disclosure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies witnessing unusual price or volume movements are required to promptly clarify market queries and provide relevant information to the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency for investors trading in the mentioned scrips. Awaited replies may lead to further disclosures or surveillance measures depending on company responses.
