## Summary

BSE has announced changes to multiple BSE indices consequent to the scheme of arrangement involving the merger of Dr Agarwal's Eye Hospital Limited (Exchange ticker: 526783) with Dr. Agarwal's Health Care Limited (Exchange ticker: 544350), effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Dr Agarwal’s Eye Hospital Limited (526783) is merging into Dr. Agarwal’s Health Care Limited (544350).
- Effective Date: Wednesday, November 18, 2026.
- Affected Indices: BSE SMALLCAP, BSE HEALTHCARE, BSE ALLCAP, and BSE MIDSMALLCAP will drop ticker 526783.
- Corporate action updates have been processed in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Reconstitution of benchmark and thematic indices following corporate mergers and schemes of arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index funds, institutional investors, and market participants tracking BSE indices must update their constituent weights and portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited impact restricted to index tracking funds and automated trading systems referencing BSE Smallcap, Healthcare, Allcap, and Midsmallcap indices.
