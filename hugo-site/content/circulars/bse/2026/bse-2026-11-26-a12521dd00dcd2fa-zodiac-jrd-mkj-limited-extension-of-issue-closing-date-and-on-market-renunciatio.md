## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the ongoing Rights Issue and On-Market Renunciation dates for Zodiac JRD-MKJ Limited have been extended.

## Key Points

- The Rights Issue closing date is extended from December 03, 2026, to December 18, 2026.
- The last date for On-Market Renunciation for Rights Entitlements is extended from November 30, 2026, to December 15, 2026.
- The Issue Opening Date remains November 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Extension of timeline for the ongoing corporate action (rights issue) of Zodiac JRD-MKJ Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and systems with the revised schedule for trading and renunciation of rights entitlements.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 19, 2026
- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: December 15, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: December 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended window for investors to participate in the rights issue and execute on-market renunciations.
