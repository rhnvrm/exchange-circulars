## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion, stage shifting, and exclusion of certain securities effective November 8 and November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin effective November 09, 2026, on all open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Eligible securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. November 09, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, moving securities across different stages or out of the framework entirely.
- Price bands and settlement mechanisms modified for affected scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.
- Market participants should monitor Annexures I, II, and III for specific scrip classifications.

## Important Dates

- **November 08, 2026**: Effective date for shifting securities to lower Stage I / Stage II / moving out of ESM framework.
- **November 09, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement may impact liquidity and trading volume for securities listed under the ESM framework.
