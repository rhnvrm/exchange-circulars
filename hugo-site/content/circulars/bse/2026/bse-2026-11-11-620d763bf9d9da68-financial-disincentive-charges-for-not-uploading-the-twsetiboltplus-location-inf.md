## Summary

BSE has announced financial disincentives (charges) for trading members who failed to upload or incorrectly registered TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal location information for the month of October 2026. A penalty of ₹1,000/- per instance is levied on orders punched from unregistered or incorrectly registered locations.

## Key Points

- The financial disincentive file for October 2026 (filename: LCS1026.xxxx) has been downloaded to the Equity Transaction Folder in the Extranet.
- Charges of ₹1,000/- per instance will be debited to the General Charges Account based on the LCS file.
- Members seeking waivers or reviews must submit requests with documentary evidence to Waiver-Review@bseindia.com.
- Email subject line format: "Waiver / review of Financial Disincentive_<TM Name>_<Clg No.-->_LOCATION MISMATCH- OCTOBER-2026".

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of financial disincentives for terminal location mismatch/non-uploading as per Exchange Notice No. 20260417-45 dated April 17, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure strict compliance by uploading TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal location details before punching orders from those locations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 11 November 2026
- **Billing Period:** October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members with unregistered or incorrectly registered terminal locations will face financial penalties debited to their General Charges Account, requiring review requests if applicable.
