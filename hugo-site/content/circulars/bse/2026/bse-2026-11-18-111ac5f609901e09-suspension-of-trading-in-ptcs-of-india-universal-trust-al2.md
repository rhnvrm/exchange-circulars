## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) of INDIA UNIVERSAL TRUST AL2, as the debt securities are maturing on November 21, 2026. Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities starting from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** IUTAL2-8.03%-21-11-26-PTC
- **Scrip Code:** 976206
- **ISIN:** INE1CBK15011
- **Maturity Date:** November 21, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Notice No. 20261118-6 (DR-760/2025-2026) issued by Listing Operations department of BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debt securities with effect from November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** November 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational closure of trading for the specified PTC upon maturity; minimal impact on broader market.
