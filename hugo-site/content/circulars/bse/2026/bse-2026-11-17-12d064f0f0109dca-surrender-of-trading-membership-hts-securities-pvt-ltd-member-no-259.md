## Summary

BSE has approved the surrender of trading membership for HTS Securities Pvt Ltd (Member no. 259) as announced in the notice dated November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Member Name: HTS Securities Pvt Ltd
- Member Number: 259
- Department: Membership Department

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the surrender of membership and ensure no further trades are executed through this membership ID.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on the wider market, restricted to counterparties or clients previously interacting with HTS Securities Pvt Ltd.
