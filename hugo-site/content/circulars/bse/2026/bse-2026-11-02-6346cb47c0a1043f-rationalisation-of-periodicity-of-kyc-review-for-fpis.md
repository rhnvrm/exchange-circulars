## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about SEBI's circular regarding the rationalisation of the periodicity of Know Your Customer (KYC) reviews for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs).

## Key Points

- References SEBI Circular no. HO/19/34/14(10)2026-AFD-POD3/I/24814/2026 dated October 30, 2026.
- Focuses on streamlining and rationalising the KYC review frequency for FPIs.
- Annexure 1 provides the complete copy of the SEBI circular for reference.

## Regulatory Changes

- Rationalisation of KYC review periodicity for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) as prescribed in the referenced SEBI circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the guidelines and ensure appropriate implementation and adherence for FPI clients.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Notice Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines compliance obligations and periodic reviews for Foreign Portfolio Investors participating in the Indian securities market.
