## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. The Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarification has been received from Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd (Scrip Code: 532371).
- Replies are awaited from 22 other listed companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, and CMX Holdings Ltd.
- The notice is issued by the Surveillance department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine surveillance disclosure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies queried by the Exchange are required to provide timely clarifications regarding material price/volume movements to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding companies undergoing unusual market movements and tracks pending clarifications.
