## Summary

Canara Robeco Mutual Fund is launching the Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund NFO, along with SIP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from November 30, 2026, to December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered include Regular and Direct Plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options (ISINs: INF760K01LS8, INF760K01LU4, INF760K01LW0, INF760K01LT6, INF760K01LV2, INF760K01LX8).
- NFO Period: November 30, 2026, to December 07, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 09, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (December 07, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on December 08, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment at AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 30, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 07, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 08, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: December 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to participate in the Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform with established timelines and operational guidelines.
