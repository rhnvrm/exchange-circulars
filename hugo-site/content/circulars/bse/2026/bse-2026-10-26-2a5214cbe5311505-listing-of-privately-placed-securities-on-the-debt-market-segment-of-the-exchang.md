## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective October 26, 2026. The issuance comprises two tranches with coupon rates of 8.05% p.a. and 8.20% p.a., both maturing on October 27, 2031.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Codes & IDs: 978336 (805SPIL31) and 978337 (82SPIL31).
- ISIN Numbers: INE044A08023 and INE044A08015.
- Quantity & Face Value: 200,300 units (Scrip Code 978336) and 581,300 units (Scrip Code 978337) at Rs. 100,000 face value and issue price each.
- Coupon Rates: 8.05% p.a. and 8.20% p.a., payable annually from March 27, 2027 to October 27, 2031.
- Credit Ratings: CRISIL AAA/Stable and ICRA AAA/Stable.
- Trading Specifications: Traded strictly in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate debt listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions are executed in dematerialized form under the designated ISINs and adhere to the prescribed tick size rules.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026
- Listing & Trading Effective Date: October 26, 2026
- Interest Payment Period: Annually from March 27, 2027 to October 27, 2031
- Date of Redemption: October 27, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Routine debt listing with highest-grade credit ratings (CRISIL AAA / ICRA AAA), having minimal market impact and providing secure fixed-income investment options.
