## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule and acquisition window for the share buyback of Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited. The bidding is scheduled from November 27, 2026, to December 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement No.:** 2026142
- **Bid Start Date:** 27/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 03/12/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 09/12/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 10/12/2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type (Buyback) and Settlement No. 2026142.

## Regulatory Changes

- Governed under revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange (Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020).

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in reflection in BSE/BSECL systems prior to bidding closure timelines.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should check the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 27/11/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 03/12/2026

- **Settlement Date:** 10/12/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth execution and settlement of the buyback offer for market participants and investors holding shares in Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited.
