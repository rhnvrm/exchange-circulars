## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Wiscon Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 545010) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment under the M Group with effect from Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Equity shares of Wiscon Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 545010) are moving to the Rolling segment.
- Shifted from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to M Group.
- Issued in continuation to Exchange’s Notice No. 20261102-29 dated November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Segment transfer of equity shares from Trade for Trade (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the segment change for execution and settlement of trades in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal rolling settlement for the company's shares, enhancing liquidity and trading access for investors.
