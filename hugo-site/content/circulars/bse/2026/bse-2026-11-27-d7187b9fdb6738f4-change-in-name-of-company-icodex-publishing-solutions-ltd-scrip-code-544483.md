## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members about the change in name of the company from "Icodex Publishing Solutions Ltd" to "ICODEX LIMITED". The change in scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be effective from December 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name Change: From Icodex Publishing Solutions Ltd to Icodex Limited.
- Scrip Code: 544483.
- New Scrip ID on BOLT Plus System: ICODEX.
- New Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: ICODEX.
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational update reflecting corporate renaming.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to update their records and trading systems with the new name and scrip ID effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 27, 2026.
- Effective Date for System Changes: December 03, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Low operational and market impact; affects trading system display and nomenclature only.
