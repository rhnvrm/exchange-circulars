## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20261112-27 regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework across various securities. The measures include the imposition of 100% margins, movement to higher surveillance stages, lower price bands, and transfers to specific settlement groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective November 17, 2026, on open positions as of November 16, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective November 13, 2026.
- Certain securities to continue in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective November 17, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective November 13, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of LT-ASM framework stages on shortlisted securities based on XBRL submissions and market surveillance parameters.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and lower price band restrictions for applicable securities.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- November 13, 2026: Movement to higher stages / out of LT-ASM, lower price band applicability.
- November 16, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- November 17, 2026: 100% margins effective; transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective.

## Impact Assessment

Shortlisted securities will experience increased margin requirements, potential price band restrictions, and modified settlement groups, impacting liquidity and trading operations for investors and members holding positions in affected scrips.
