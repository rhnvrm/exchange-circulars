## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by multiple companies effective November 13, 2026, on the debt market segment of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited: Commercial Paper of Rs. 5,00,000/- each (Scrip Code: 732940, ISIN: INE472H14920, Maturity: 11/02/2027, Rate: 8.20% p.a.).
- Godrej Finance Limited: Commercial Paper of Rs. 5,00,000/- each (Scrip Code: 732941, ISIN: INE02KN14861, Maturity: 21/12/2026, Rate: 7.35% p.a.).
- Bajaj Financial Securities Limited: Two tranches of Commercial Papers (Scrip Codes: 732945, 732946, ISINs: INE01C314IV4, INE01C314IX0, Rates: 8.05% p.a. and 7.9% p.a.).
- Fedbank Financial Services Limited: Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732942, ISIN: INE007N14FU0).
- Trading shall take place in standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise in dematerialised form.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and issuers must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading requirements and standard debt market settlement guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 12/11/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 13/11/2026
- Redemption Dates: Between 21/12/2026 and 11/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for privately placed short-term debt instruments (commercial papers).
