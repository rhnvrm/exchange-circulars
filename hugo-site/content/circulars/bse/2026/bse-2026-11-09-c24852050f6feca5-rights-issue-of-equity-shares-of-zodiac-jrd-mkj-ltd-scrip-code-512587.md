## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the rights issue of equity shares by ZODIAC-JRD-MKJ LTD. (Scrip Code: 512587). The company has fixed November 11, 2026, as the record date for the rights issue, with trading on an ex-rights basis commencing on the same date.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: ZODIAC-JRD-MKJ LTD. (512587)
- Rights Issue Ratio: 145 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 15/- per share for every 100 Equity Shares held
- Issue Price: Entire rights issue price of Rs. 25/- per share is payable on application
- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From: November 11, 2026
- Distribution Reference (DR): 754/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the record date and ex-rights settlement terms for transactions in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 11, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders as of the record date will receive rights entitlements. Stock price adjustment will take effect on the ex-rights date.
