## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7145 of 2026 filed by Vikram Aggarwal against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought extensive information regarding the proposed Initial Public Offering (IPO) of the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE), including the Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) dated June 17, 2026, public comments, the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) dated September 10, 2026, representations concerning DBS Capital Markets Pvt Ltd, and details of 25 complaints received by NSE and BRLMs. The respondent invoked Sections 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, withholding commercial confidential information held in a fiduciary relationship.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought 10 specific categories of records pertaining to the NSE IPO DRHP, public comment filings, representations on disabled trading member status, and complaint categorization.
- The CPIO denied access to query nos. 1, 2, 3, 4, and 10, citing exemptions under Section 8(1)(d) (commercial confidence and competitive harm) and Section 8(1)(e) (fiduciary relationship) of the RTI Act, 2005.
- The appeal challenges the non-disclosure of records related to public comments, representations regarding DBS Capital Markets Pvt Ltd, and complaint handling during the NSE IPO process.
