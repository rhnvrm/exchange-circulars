## Summary

SEBI has cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 dated September 1, 2026, which was issued jointly and severally against 21 defaulters (including individuals and B B Commercial Ltd) for non-payment of penalty. This action is taken in view of the order passed by the Hon’َّة Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) dated November 4, 2026, in Appeal No. 388 of 2026.

## Key Points

- **Defaulters Involved:** 21 entities and individuals including Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, Khushbu Arjunbhai Padhiyar, B B Commercial Ltd, and others.
- **Original Penalty:** Rs. 45,37,000/- imposed vide Adjudicating Officer Order No. Order/AK/DS/2025-26/32082-32109 dated February 12, 2026.
- **Basis of Cancellation:** Hon’ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) Order dated November 4, 2026, in Appeal No. 388 of 2026.
- **Legal Authority:** Exercised under sub-section (1) of section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with section 413 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Recovery proceedings initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 have been formally withdrawn and cancelled.

## Compliance Requirements

- No active compliance obligations required as the recovery certificate stands cancelled pursuant to the SAT order.

## Important Dates

- **Adjudicating Officer Order Date:** February 12, 2026
- **Original Recovery Certificate Date:** September 1, 2026
- **SAT Order Date:** November 4, 2026
- **Cancellation Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides legal relief to the named appellants in accordance with the SAT directive regarding trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
