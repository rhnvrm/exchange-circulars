## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 04, 2026, lifting and releasing the attachments issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 002, 003, 004, and 005 of 2013 against Kamalnayan Harakchand (PAN: AAHPK8406D) in the matter of Pyramid Saimira Theater Ltd.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued on November 04, 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Kamalnayan Harakchand (PAN: AAHPK8406D).
- Recovery Certificates: RC Nos. 002, 003, 004, and 005 of 2013.
- Matter: Pyramid Saimira Theater Ltd.
- Action: Lifting/release of previous attachment orders following compliance or resolution of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal of enforcement attachment measures under the SEBI Act, 1992 upon satisfaction of recovery certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and authorities are notified to unfreeze the accounts and assets previously attached under the specified recovery certificates.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restores status of assets and accounts for the noticee following settlement or compliance with SEBI recovery proceedings.
