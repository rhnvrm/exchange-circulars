## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7696 of 2024 against S Nagarajan in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order directs the remittance of funds from attached bank and financial accounts towards the satisfaction of outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 7696 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: S Nagarajan.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees are required to remit funds from the attached accounts of the noticee as directed in the General Remittance Order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against defaulters in the Arvind Remedies Ltd. matter.
