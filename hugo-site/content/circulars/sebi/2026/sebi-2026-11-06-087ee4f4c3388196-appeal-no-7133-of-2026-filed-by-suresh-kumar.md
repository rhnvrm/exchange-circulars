## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7133 of 2026 filed by Suresh Kumar under the Right to Information Act, 2005, regarding land allotment details associated with PACL Ltd. refund claims.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought specific land allotment details (plot number, survey number, khasra number, village, tehsil, district, state, area, and allotment letter) for land allegedly allotted against PACL Certificate No. U08xxx.
- SEBI CPIO stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, which handles all PACL refund matters.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing central information commission precedents that information not held by SEBI cannot be furnished under the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new policy directives introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations for market participants; queries regarding PACL refund status and land allotment must be directed to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 29, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 06, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Zero market impact; routine individual statutory appeal disposal under the RTI Act.
