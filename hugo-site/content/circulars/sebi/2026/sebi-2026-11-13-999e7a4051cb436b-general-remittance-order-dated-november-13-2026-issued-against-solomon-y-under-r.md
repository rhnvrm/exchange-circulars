## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against Solomon Y under Recovery Certificate No. 7706 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order forms part of ongoing enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI for realization of dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Order Type: General Remittance Order (Recovery Proceedings)
- Date of Issue: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Number: RC No. 7706 of 2024
- Defaulter/Noticee: Solomon Y
- Associated Entity: Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for recovery of outstanding dues and penalties through attachment and remittance orders.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries are required to comply with remittance and attachment directives regarding funds and assets of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 7706 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals defaulting on dues related to market violations in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
