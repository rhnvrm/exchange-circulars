## Summary

SEBI has issued operational modalities following the amendment of SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012 on October 15, 2026, introducing a manager-led accreditation framework to ease and expedite investor accreditation across Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), and Portfolio Management Services.

## Key Points

- **Manager-Led Accreditation:** Managers (AIF managers, SIF AMCs, or portfolio managers) can determine and record accredited status at onboarding or during scheme tenure.
- **Group Recognition:** Determinations are recognized across managers within the same corporate group for AIFs, SIFs, and PMS.
- **Role of IAs and RAs:** Investment Advisers and Research Analysts cannot independently determine accreditation but may rely on valid determinations from Group managers.
- **Validity Period:** Accreditation is valid for three years from the date of determination. Investors accredited at onboarding remain accredited for the entire life of that scheme.
- **Securities Market Exposure Thresholds:** Minimum qualifying exposure is INR 5 crore for individuals, HUFs, family trusts, and sole proprietorships, and INR 20 crore for body corporates and other trusts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amended provisions of the SEBI (AIF) Regulations, 2012 implemented via operational guidelines allowing eligible managers to verify and issue accreditation.
- Accreditation Agency route continues to remain available as an alternative and optional route.

## Compliance Requirements

- Managers must record accreditation status, inform investors in writing with the date and validity period, and rely on existing valid Accreditation Agency certificates without fresh determination.
- Verification of securities market exposure must utilize dematerialized holdings, mutual fund folios, unlisted demat securities, overseas investments, and NPS investments based on eCAS statements.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately following the circular issuance date of November 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Eases onboarding friction and operational bottlenecks for accredited investors across AIFs, portfolio management services, and mutual fund specialized investment funds by decentralizing verification while maintaining robust eligibility criteria.
