## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9321 of 2026 dated November 06, 2026, against Natasha Sinha (PAN: AAUPS3146B) demanding a total sum of Rs. 2,01,01,000/- in the matter of Mideast Integrated Steel Limited (MISL).

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Recovery Certificate No. 9321 of 2026 issued under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Section 413 of the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Rule 225(2) of the Income-Tax Rules, 2026.
- **Entity Concerned:** Natasha Sinha (W/O Gautam Sinha), holding PAN AAUPS3146B.
- **Dues Breakdown:**
  - Penalty imposed by QJA vide Order No. QJA/KS/CFID/CFID-SEC4/30037/2023-24 dated 21.02.2024: Rs. 1,50,00,000/-
  - Interest (February 2024 to November 2026 @ 1% p.m.): Rs. 51,00,000/-
  - Recovery cost: Rs. 1,000/-
  - **Total Dues:** Rs. 2,01,01,000/-
- **Payment Mode:** Direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS to A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9321 of ICICI Bank (IFSC: ICIC0000106) or via the SEBI SI Portal / Recovery Payment module within 15 days.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, and relevant provisions of the Income-Tax Act and Rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Payment of Rs. 2,01,01,000/- within 15 days of receiving the notice.
- Prohibition on mortgaging, charging, leasing, or transferring any property belonging to the noticee without prior permission of the Recovery Officer.
- Submission of e-payment confirmation details to SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Original Order Date:** February 21, 2024
- **Payment Deadline:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice.

## Impact Assessment

- Failure to pay within the stipulated 15-day window will result in coercive recovery measures by SEBI, including attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, attachment of bank accounts, arrest and detention in prison, or appointment of a receiver.
