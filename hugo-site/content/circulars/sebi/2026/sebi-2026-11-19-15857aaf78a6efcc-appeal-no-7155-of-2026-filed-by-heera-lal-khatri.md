## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7155 of 2026 filed by Mr. Heera Lal Khatri under the Right to Information Act, 2005, against the CPIO, SEBI. The appeal concerns information and records related to Cinema Capital Venture Fund Scheme-I (CCVF), SEBI's order dated April 30, 2025, and subsequent valuation and NAV determinations.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated September 14, 2026, seeking certified copies of valuation reports, Action Taken Reports, NAV working papers, and related documents regarding CCVF.
- The CPIO responded on October 7, 2026, providing copies of valuation reports and the Action Taken Report for queries 1 and 7.
- The appellant filed an appeal on October 19, 2026, challenging the response regarding remaining queries concerning supplementary valuation reports, NAV calculations, and investor notices.
- The appellate authority reviewed the submissions and found that the respondent adequately addressed the queries based on available records.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an individual statutory appeal order under the RTI Act.

## Compliance Requirements

None. This order is specific to an RTI information request and creates no new regulatory obligations for market participants.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 14, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 7, 2026
- RTI Appeal Date: October 19, 2026

Negligible market impact as it pertains strictly to an individual RTI query resolution regarding alternative investment fund disclosures.
