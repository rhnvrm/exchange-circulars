## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 3834 of 2021 issued against Unicon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P) in the matter of Ashutosh Paper Mills Limited (now Tridev Infraestates Limited). The release order officially concludes the recovery proceedings and lifts previous attachments or restrictions associated with this certificate.

## Key Points

- Entity: Unicon Securities Pvt. Ltd. [Defaulter] (PAN: AAPFS5325P).
- Matter: Ashutosh Paper Mills Limited (BSE: ASHUTPM).
- Recovery Certificate: Certificate No. 3834 of 2021.
- Action: Formal Release Order issued by SEBI lifting previous recovery actions and attachments under the specified certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal and conclusion of enforcement and recovery actions initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 pursuant to the settlement or fulfillment of dues under Recovery Certificate No. 3834 of 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are notified of the release, allowing the removal of account freezes or restrictions previously placed pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 3834 of 2021.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory clearance and finality regarding the recovery proceedings under Certificate No. 3834 of 2021 for the named defaulter, with minimal direct market impact on general trading.
