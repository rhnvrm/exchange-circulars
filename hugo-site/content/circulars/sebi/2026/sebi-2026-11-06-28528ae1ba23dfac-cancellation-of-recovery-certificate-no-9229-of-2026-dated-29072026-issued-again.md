## Summary

SEBI has ordered the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9229 of 2026 dated July 29, 2026, issued against Rakeshbhai K Pandya in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9229 of 2026.
- Date of Original Recovery Certificate: July 29, 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Rakeshbhai K Pandya.
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal withdrawal of recovery and enforcement proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 against the specified individual.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries, banks, and financial institutions are advised of the cancellation and release of any respective attachment or freeze orders.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides formal closure and relief regarding the recovery certificate proceedings for the individual concerning ANI Integrated Services Limited.
