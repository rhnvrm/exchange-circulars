## Summary

This order resolves Appeal No. 7153 of 2026 filed by Jamil Mian against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought procedures and provisions for correcting the 'Land allotted' field from 'Yes' to 'No' in his PACL Limited refund claim application (Certificate No. U016372xxx).

## Key Points

- The appellant's PACL refund application could not be processed further because 'Yes' was declared for the 'Land allotted' field.
- The appellant sought 7 specific queries regarding correction provisions, procedures, reprocessing feasibility, applicable rules, affidavits, and competent authorities.
- The CPIO stated that the requested information was not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to public notices, press releases, and FAQs on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession as held in prior CIC precedents.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an appellate order under the RTI Act, 2005.
