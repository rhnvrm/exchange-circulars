## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15863 and 15864 of 2026) dated October 30, 2026, against Jalaj Agrawal (PAN: AUDPA0226H) under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9304 of 2026. This action is part of enforcement proceedings to recover dues and penalties related to market manipulation and fraudulent trading schemes using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- Notices of Attachment: AP Nos. 15863 and 15864 of 2026 issued on October 30, 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Jalaj Agrawal (PAN: AUDPA0226H).
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 9304 of 2026.
- Asset Categories Attached: Bank accounts, Post Office accounts, Demat accounts, and Mutual Fund folios.
- Underlying Matter: Market manipulation, pump-and-dump activities, and unauthorized trading tips disseminated via social media (Telegram) in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI under the SEBI Act, 1992 and related regulations for the realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement amounts resulting from fraudulent market practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, mutual fund houses, and intermediaries must immediately freeze the specified accounts, demat holdings, and mutual fund folios of the noticee, prohibiting any debits, transfers, or withdrawals until further orders from SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notices / Attachment: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance against individuals involved in social media-based stock manipulation schemes and reinforces asset freezing mechanisms to ensure compliance and penalty recovery.
