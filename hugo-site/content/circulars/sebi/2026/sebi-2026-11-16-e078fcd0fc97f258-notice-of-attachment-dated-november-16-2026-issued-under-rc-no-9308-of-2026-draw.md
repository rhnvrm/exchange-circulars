## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 16, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9308 of 2026 drawn against Niranjan D Agarwal. This action relates to recovery proceedings and enforcement measures in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9308 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Niranjan D Agarwal.
- Matter: Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in regulatory defaults relating to Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited.
