## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 29, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7544 of 2024 drawn against Rajesh Nath and Shubham Rai in the matter of Unregistered Investment Advisory. This action is part of recovery proceedings to realize dues and penalties under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 7544 of 2024.
- Defaulters/Noticees: Rajesh Nath and Shubham Rai.
- Matter: Unregistered Investment Advisory.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues / penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals operating unregistered investment advisory services.
