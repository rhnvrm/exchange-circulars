## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment dated October 28, 2026, bearing AP Nos. 15759 and 15760 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9300 of 2026 against Aahuti Rasik Mistry (PAN: AKSPM8453F) and Manish Mishra (PAN: AMPPM6823L) (‘Defaulters’) jointly and severally in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- Notices of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9300 of 2026.
- Attachment Reference AP Nos.: 15759 and 15760 of 2026.
- Defaulters / Noticees: Aahuti Rasik Mistry (PAN: AKSPM8453F) and Manish Mishra (PAN: AMPPM6823L) jointly and severally.
- Affected Assets: Bank accounts, Post Office accounts, Demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- Associated Scrip: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), stock brokers, mutual fund AMCs, and financial institutions are required to attach and freeze the specified bank, post office, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios of the defaulters and prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notices / Attachment: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities failing to pay dues related to irregularities in Sadhna Broadcast Limited, ensuring asset protection for recovery.
