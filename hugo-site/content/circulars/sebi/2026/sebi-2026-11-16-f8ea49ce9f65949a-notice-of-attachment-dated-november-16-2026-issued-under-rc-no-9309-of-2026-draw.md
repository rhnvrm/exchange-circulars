## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 16, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9309 of 2026 drawn against M/s. Arihant Securities (Proprietor Pravin Kumar Jain). This action is taken in the matter of Betala Global Securities Limited for the recovery of dues/penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9309 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: M/s. Arihant Securities (Proprietor: Pravin Kumar Jain).
- Underlying Matter: Betala Global Securities Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, demat accounts, and assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice to prevent unauthorized transfers or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and defaulters involved in securities law violations concerning Betala Global Securities Limited.
