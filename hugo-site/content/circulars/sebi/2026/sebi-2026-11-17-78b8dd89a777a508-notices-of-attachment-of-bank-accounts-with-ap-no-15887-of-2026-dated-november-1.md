## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice of Attachment of Bank Accounts (AP No. 15887 of 2026) dated November 17, 2026, against Sadhana Paresh Kariya (PAN: AJHPK6018N) under Recovery Certificate No. 9294 of 2026 in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to collect outstanding dues and penalties stemming from unauthorized portfolio management services and client fund shortfalls.

## Key Points

- Formal attachment notice AP No. 15887 of 2026 issued by SEBI on November 17, 2026.
- Initiated against Sadhana Paresh Kariya (PAN – AJHPK6018N), director of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
- Issued pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9294 of 2026.
- Part of comprehensive enforcement and recovery measures following regulatory orders regarding unauthorized derivative advisory services and assured returns.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A - Enforcement and recovery action under existing SEBI Act provisions rather than a new regulatory amendment.]

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and financial institutions holding accounts in the name of Sadhana Paresh Kariya are directed to attach the accounts and prevent any debits or withdrawals up to the specified recovery amount.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 17, 2026
- Prior Notice of Demand: September 3, 2026
- SAT Appeal Hearing: January 18, 2027 (Appeal No. 455 of 2024)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement stance on recovering dues from defaulting brokers and associated directors involved in severe market infractions and client fund misappropriation.
