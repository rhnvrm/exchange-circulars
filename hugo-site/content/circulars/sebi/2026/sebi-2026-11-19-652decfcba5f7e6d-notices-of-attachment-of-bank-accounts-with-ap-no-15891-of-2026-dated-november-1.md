## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 19, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9205 of 2026 (with Attachment Proceedings No. 15891 of 2026) against Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited (PAN: AAACW2920N) for the realization of dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9292 of 2026 and Attachment Proceedings No. 15891 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited (PAN – AAACW2920N).
- Action involves attachment of bank accounts and assets for recovery of pending dues/penalties.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of outstanding penalties or dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are directed to freeze bank accounts and prevent any debits or unauthorized transfers from the accounts of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited as specified in the attachment notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights rigorous regulatory enforcement and asset attachment measures by SEBI against defaulting intermediaries to safeguard market integrity and recover regulatory dues.
