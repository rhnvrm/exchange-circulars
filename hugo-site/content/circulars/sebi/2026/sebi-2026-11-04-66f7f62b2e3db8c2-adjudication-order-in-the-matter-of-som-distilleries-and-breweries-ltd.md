## Summary

SEBI has issued an adjudication order under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 5 of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995, in the matter of Som Distilleries and Breweries Limited and its officials. The investigation covered FY 2021 to FY 2024 and examined alleged manipulation of books of account, extension of undisclosed corporate guarantees to related parties, and lack of statutory approvals under LODR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Noticees: Som Distilleries and Breweries Ltd (Noticee 1), Jagdish Kumar Arora (Chairman & MD, Noticee 2), Nakul Kam Sethi (Whole-Time Director, Noticee 3), and Om Prakash Singh (Company Secretary, Noticee 4).
- Investigation Period: FY 2021 to FY 2024 regarding accounts representation and LODR compliance.
- Corporate Guarantee: Extension of a corporate guarantee worth ₹21.36 crore to State Bank of India (SBI) on behalf of related party Aryavrat Projects and Developers Private Limited (APDPL) without required statutory approvals.
- Subsidiary Restructuring: Rights issue undertaken by subsidiary Woodpecker Distilleries and Breweries Private Limited (WDBPL) reducing Som Distilleries' shareholding from 100% to 78.87%.
- Legal Proceedings: Adjudication proceedings initiated following Show Cause Notice (SCN) dated March 24, 2026, under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and monetary penalties imposed under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992, for violations of SEBI LODR Regulations and corporate governance standards.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must adhere strictly to related-party transaction disclosure requirements, obtain prior approvals for corporate guarantees, and ensure compliance with LODR Regulations regarding subsidiary capital restructuring.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: FY 2021 – FY 2024
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: February 20, 2026
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) Date: March 24, 2026
- Order Date: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Underscores rigorous SEBI oversight on corporate governance, related-party transactions, and the personal responsibility of directors and compliance officers in listed companies.
