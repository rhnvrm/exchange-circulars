## Summary

SEBI has completed the half-yearly review of 'Significant Indices' under the SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024, for the period from January 01, 2026, to June 30, 2026. The list of Significant Indices specified in Annexure-A of the earlier circular remains unchanged.

## Key Points

- A Benchmark or Index is classified as a 'Significant Index' if the daily average cumulative AUM tracking it across mutual fund schemes exceeds ₹20,000 Crore for each of the past six months (ending June 30 and December 31).
- During the review period (January 1, 2026, to June 30, 2026), BSE 200 and Nifty CPSE did not meet the threshold limit.
- Both BSE 200 and Nifty CPSE are retained in the list of Significant Indices in terms of Para 5 of the said circular.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; existing provisions under SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024 and previous circulars continue to apply with the list of Significant Indices remaining unchanged.

## Compliance Requirements

Index providers, stock exchanges, mutual funds, and asset management companies must continue compliance with the existing framework for Significant Indices.

## Important Dates

- Circular issuance date: November 25, 2026
- Effective date: Immediate effect
- Review period covered: January 01, 2026, to June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Neutral impact on market participants as index classifications and thresholds remain unchanged, with non-qualifying indices retained under existing exception rules.
