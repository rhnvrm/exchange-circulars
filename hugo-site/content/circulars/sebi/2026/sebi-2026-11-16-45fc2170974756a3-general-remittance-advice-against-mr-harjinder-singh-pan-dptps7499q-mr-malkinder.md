## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice against multiple defaulters—namely Mr. Harjinder Singh, Mr. Malkinder Singh, Mr. Mohammad Mukhtar Atif, Mr. Subhash Chander Sharma, and Mr. Vidya Bhushan Ambastha—in the matter of Unique Multi Foods Industries Limited under Recovery Certificate No. 5435 of 2022. This action is part of SEBI's ongoing recovery proceedings to collect outstanding dues arising from illegal mobilization of funds through debentures.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 5435 of 2022 against Unique Multi Foods Industries Limited.
- Defaulters targeted include Mr. Harjinder Singh, Mr. Malkinder Singh, Mr. Mohammad Mukhtar Atif, Mr. Subhash Chander Sharma, and Mr. Vidya Bhushan Ambastha.
- Issuance of General Remittance Advice to enforce recovery and attachment of assets/accounts.
- Relates to illegal fund mobilization via Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (SRNCDs) without regulatory compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - Enforcement and recovery action rather than regulatory amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Defaulters and associated entities must remit outstanding dues as specified under the recovery certificate.
- Financial institutions and banks must comply with SEBI's attachment and remittance directions regarding the accounts of the named defaulters.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate No. 5435 of 2022 issued: August 24, 2022.
- General Remittance Advice issued: November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory enforcement and investor protection by aggressively pursuing recovery from directors and entities involved in unauthorized collective investment / debenture schemes.
- Direct operational impact is restricted to the named defaulters and designated financial institutions holding their accounts.
