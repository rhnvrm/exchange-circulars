## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7151 of 2026 filed by Aakash Goel under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response. The appellant sought extensive details regarding the total market value and Assets Under Management (AUM) of mutual fund folios generating unclaimed dividends and redemptions, total number of such folios across AMCs, valuation tracking mandates, and procedures for tracing unitholders with missing or invalid PANs.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information on total market value/AUM of mutual fund folios source of Rupees 2689 crore unclaimed dividends and Rupees 1122 crore unclaimed redemptions reported in SEBI Annual Reports.
- Queries included statistics on folios with unclaimed dividends exceeding seven years and missing/invalid PANs.
- The CPIO stated that specific data requested was not available with SEBI, and certain queries sought clarifications/opinions falling outside Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations or circulars were issued; this document represents an appellate order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations are imposed on AMCs or market intermediaries by this order.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 25, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 15, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 15, 2026

Negligible market or operational impact as the adjudication relates solely to the scope of information disclosable under the RTI Act.
