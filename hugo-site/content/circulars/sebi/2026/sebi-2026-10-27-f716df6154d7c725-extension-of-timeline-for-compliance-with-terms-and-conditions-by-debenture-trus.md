## Summary

SEBI has extended the timeline for registered Debenture Trustees (DTs) to comply with the terms and conditions for carrying out activities outside the purview of SEBI—specifically the Separate Business Unit (SBU) framework—by an additional six months, moving the deadline from October 27, 2026, to April 27, 2027.

## Key Points

- Timeline extension granted up to April 27, 2027, for DTs to ensure compliance with SBU requirements.
- An Expert Working Group (EWG) was constituted in June 2026 to comprehensively review the extant regulatory framework for debenture trusteeship activities, including SBU-related guidelines.
- All other provisions of the SEBI circular dated November 25, 2025, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the compliance deadline for transferring non-SEBI regulated activities to separate business units within DTs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Debenture Trustees must complete all necessary systems, processes, and structural changes to comply with SBU guidelines by April 27, 2027.

## Important Dates

- October 27, 2026: Issuance date of the circular and prior deadline.
- April 27, 2027: New extended deadline for compliance.

## Impact Assessment

- Alleviates operational and implementation challenges faced by Debenture Trustees while broader regulatory reviews are conducted by the Expert Working Group.
