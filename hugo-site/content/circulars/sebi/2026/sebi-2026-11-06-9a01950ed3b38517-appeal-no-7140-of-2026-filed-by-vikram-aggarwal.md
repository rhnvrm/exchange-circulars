## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7140 of 2026 filed by Vikram Aggarwal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought procedural and system records regarding SCORES complaint No. SEBIE MH25 MUMB 018601 1 received against Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested 11 queries related to action history, transaction history, audit history, review notes, and ATR attachments concerning a SCORES complaint against Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited.
- The respondent (CPIO, SEBI) informed that the complaint handling process on SCORES is entirely electronic and the Action Taken Report (ATR) contains all information regarding the processing of the complaint.
- The respondent stated that no separate file notings or internal examination notes are maintained outside the system.
