## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) hosted the IOSCO Asia-Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) Regulatory Roundtable on Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) in Mumbai on November 16–17, 2026. The event brought together regional securities regulators, MIIs, and stakeholders to discuss Supervisory Technology (SupTech), artificial intelligence in market surveillance, accelerated T+1 settlement, and tokenization.

## Key Points

- SEBI hosted the IOSCO APRC Regulatory Roundtable on MIIs in Mumbai on November 16–17, 2026.
- Featured a SEBI Symposium on Technology Solutions demonstrating SupTech and AI applications for regulatory oversight, along with a Nasdaq demonstration on AI in market surveillance.
- Conducted a joint APRC-World Federation of Exchanges (WFE) session on the evolving role of exchanges and robust market infrastructure.
- Held roundtable discussions focusing on contemporary themes including accelerating settlement (T+1) and tokenization via distributed ledger technology.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes or circular-specific mandates were introduced; the event served as a collaborative high-level regulatory roundtable and symposium.

## Compliance Requirements

None. This press release is for informational purposes and introduces no new compliance obligations for market participants.

## Important Dates

- Event Dates: November 16–17, 2026
- Release Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes international regulatory cooperation and knowledge sharing regarding advanced technology solutions, surveillance capabilities, shorter settlement cycles, and tokenization frameworks across Asia-Pacific capital markets.
