## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order pursuant to a direction by the Hon’ble Bombay High Court in a writ petition filed by Ms. Vanita Krishnakant Gandhi regarding the alleged fraudulent dematerialization and transfer of 54,000 bonus shares of Balkrishna Industries Limited held in folio no. V00019. The matter examines the role and compliance of SEBI-registered intermediaries, specifically KFin Technologies Limited (Registrar and Share Transfer Agent) and Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd. (Depository Participant and Stock Broker).

## Key Points

- **Petitioner:** Ms. Vanita Krishnakant Gandhi alleged that 54,000 bonus shares of Balkrishna Industries Limited were fraudulently issued/dematerialized by impersonation.
- **Intermediaries Involved:** KFin Technologies Limited (Noticee No. 1, RTA) and Motilal Oswal Financial Services Ltd. (Noticee No. 2, DP).
- **High Court Direction:** Hon’ble Bombay High Court directed SEBI vide order dated July 29, 2026, to dispose of the representations dated January 28, 2025 and May 02, 2024 within six weeks.
- **Allegations:** Violations of specific SEBI circulars relating to RTA and DP operational standards, KYC, and Code of Conduct under respective regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; enforcement of existing SEBI circulars, RTA Regulations (1993 and 2025), and DP operating guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market intermediaries (RTAs and DPs) must adhere strictly to verification, KYC, dematerialization request processing, and documentation standards to prevent fraudulent share transfers and impersonation.

## Important Dates

- **Representation Dates:** May 02, 2024 and January 28, 2025
- **Bombay High Court Order Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Order Year:** 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights the critical importance of rigorous due diligence by RTAs and Depository Participants during share dematerialization and transfer processes to safeguard investor holdings against impersonation and fraud.
