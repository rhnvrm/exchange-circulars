## Summary

The appellant, P. Sachithanantham, filed an RTI appeal (Appeal No. 7128 of 2026) against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding grievances over the repeated return and delay of a share transmission request (from deceased father to son) by the Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA) over a period of 10 months.

## Key Points

- The appellant's share transmission request was returned four times by the RTA citing various reasons, including requirements for English notarized death certificates, attestation, name discrepancies in company records, and staggered requests for transmission affidavits, ISR forms, and indemnities.
- The appeal highlights operational inefficiencies and delays by RTAs in scrutinizing transmission documents.
- The matter was examined by the Appellate Authority based on the RTI application, CPIO response, and appeal records.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policies or circulars were introduced; this order deals solely with an individual RTI appeal regarding share transmission norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- RTAs and listed entities must ensure efficient processing of share transmission requests in compliance with established SEBI norms and guidelines (such as SEBI Circular dated May 18, 2022) without causing unwarranted procedural delays.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: August 28, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 17, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 30, 2026
- Receipt of Appeal: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low operational impact on broader markets; serves as an individual case reflection on investor grievance redressal and RTA compliance with transmission standards.
