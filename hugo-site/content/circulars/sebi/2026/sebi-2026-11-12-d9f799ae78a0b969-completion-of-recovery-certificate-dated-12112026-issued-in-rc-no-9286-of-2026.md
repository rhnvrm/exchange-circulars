## Summary

SEBI has officially completed the recovery proceedings initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 9286 of 2026 against Navnit Gadoya. The completion follows the receipt of the entire outstanding dues amounting to Rs. 5,36,000/-, which includes interest and costs related to penalties imposed for front-running.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9286 of 2026 was originally issued on August 19, 2026, against Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F) for a sum of Rs. 5,26,000/-.
- The penalty was originally imposed by the Adjudicating Officer (AO) vide Order No. Order/AK/DS/2026-27/32398-32400 dated April 29, 2026, in the matter of front-running involving Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal.
- Total dues recovered amount to Rs. 5,36,000/-, inclusive of interest and costs.
- Recovery stands completed under Section 28A(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an administrative execution order regarding recovery of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance requirements for the concerned individual as dues have been fully settled.

## Important Dates

- Original Order Date: April 29, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: August 19, 2026
- Completion Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement action completion with no broader market or systemic impact.
