## Summary

This order by the Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) pertains to Appeal No. 7169 of 2026 filed against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant had sought information regarding the receipt, processing status, file numbers, and actions taken on a confidential whistleblower disclosure and supplementary disclosure submitted concerning financial, regulatory, and corporate governance concerns regarding an unnamed entity.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated September 25, 2026, seeking details on whistleblower disclosures submitted on August 11, 2026, and August 23, 2026.
- CPIO, SEBI responded to the application on October 23, 2026.
- Dissatisfied with the response, the appellant filed an appeal dated October 29, 2026.
- The appeal deals with requests for diary numbers, file movements, action-taken records, and internal referrals regarding whistleblower disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an adjudicatory order under the RTI Act, 2005.

None applicable to market participants.

No direct impact on market operations, trading, or general compliance requirements as it is an individual RTI appeal proceeding.
