## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7121 of 2026 filed by Kshipra Sawant under the RTI Act, 2005. The appeal challenged the CPIO's denial of information regarding representations, suggestions, and feedback received from market participants on derivative contract settlement prices following the implementation of CAS on August 3, 2026. The Appellate Authority found that the CPIO failed to provide sufficient justification for invoking sections 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act for queries 1 and 2, directing reconsideration, while upholding confidentiality principles for query 3 regarding the specific nature of concerns.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought total counts and category-wise breakdown of submissions received by SEBI concerning derivative contract settlement price methodology post-CAS implementation (August 3, 2026).
- CPIO had claimed exemptions under sections 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, citing commercial confidentiality and fiduciary capacity.
- Appellate Authority held that the CPIO did not provide sufficient justification for withholding quantitative data regarding total submissions and category-wise numbers (Queries 1 and 2).
- Queries 1 and 2 were remanded back to the respondent for fresh consideration.
- For Query 3 (nature of concerns), the Appellate Authority noted that submissions are received in a fiduciary relationship and involve commercial/trade confidentiality.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct market policy change; this is an adjudicatory order under the RTI Act concerning information disclosure obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

- CPIO directed to reconsider queries 1 and 2 of the RTI application and issue a reasoned response in accordance with the observations.
