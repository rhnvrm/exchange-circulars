## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7137 of 2026 filed by Rahul Bagrecha under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding his SCORES complaints against Aurostar Investment.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of files, notings, and internal examination notes concerning his SCORES complaints and their disposal due to ODR/arbitration.
- SEBI CPIO stated that complaint handling on SCORES is electronic, and all actions, observations, and responses are recorded in the action history accessible to the appellant on the portal.
- No separate physical or offline file notings are maintained outside the SCORES system.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; this is an individual RTI appeal order.

## Compliance Requirements

- None applicable.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 09, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 08, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces that electronic action histories on SCORES serve as the complete repository of complaint processing records.
