## Summary

Saroj Sharma filed Appeal No. 7116 of 2026 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 before the SEBI Appellate Authority challenging the response of the CPIO regarding queries on the proposed IPO or DRHP application of Jio Platforms Limited.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details including the date of receipt of the IPO/DRHP application, final observation dates, certified copies of observation letters, processing status, responsible officers, and related queries.
- The appellant argued that the information involves substantial public interest given the multi-billion dollar scale of the proposed public offering.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- **August 30, 2026**: RTI application filed by the appellant.
- **September 28, 2026**: CPIO response issued.
- **September 30, 2026**: RTI appeal filed.

## Impact Assessment

Individual RTI statutory appeal with no direct impact on market operations or trading systems.
