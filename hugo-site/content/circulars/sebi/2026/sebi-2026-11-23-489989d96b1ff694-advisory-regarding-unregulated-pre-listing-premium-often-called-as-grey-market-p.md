## Summary

SEBI has issued an advisory cautioning investors against unregulated pre-listing premium, commonly known as Grey Market Premium (GMP), for upcoming Initial Public Offers (IPOs). SEBI noted that GMP figures are unofficial, unverified, and executed outside the formal exchange framework without regulatory oversight or settlement guarantees. All SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants are directed to discontinue publishing, displaying, or broadcasting GMP figures across any platform or communication channel.

## Key Points

- Unregulated pre-listing premium (GMP) figures are unofficial, unverified, and lack regulatory oversight or settlement guarantees.
- Publication or circulation of GMP figures can mislead investors and undermine transparency in the securities market.
- All SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants must refrain from publishing, displaying, or broadcasting GMP figures via websites, digital platforms, SMS, email, or social media.
- Investors are advised not to treat GMP as a listing price indicator and to avoid GMP-linked transactions entirely.
- No regulatory recourses, investor protection benefits, or grievance redressal mechanisms (such as smartodr.in) are available for GMP disputes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Prohibition on SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants from publishing, displaying, or broadcasting IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP) data.

## Compliance Requirements

- SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants must immediately halt all publishing, broadcasting, or display of GMP figures on websites, digital platforms, social media, SMS, emails, and other communication channels.

## Important Dates

- Issued on November 23, 2026 (PR No. 75/2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly curbs the public visibility of grey market pricing for upcoming IPOs, protecting retail investors from speculative misinformation and reinforcing reliance on official exchange mechanisms.
