## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9232 of 2026 issued against Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki (PAN: DCXPS3726R) in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9232 of 2026 has been officially cancelled.
- The matter pertains to investigations into trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Notice of Demand and attachment proceedings previously initiated are withdrawn or released with respect to this certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal withdrawal of enforcement and recovery measures under Recovery Certificate No. 9232.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries and financial institutions holding attached accounts or folios must release the restrictions upon receiving the release order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Relieves the named individual from recovery and attachment actions associated with Recovery Certificate No. 9232.
