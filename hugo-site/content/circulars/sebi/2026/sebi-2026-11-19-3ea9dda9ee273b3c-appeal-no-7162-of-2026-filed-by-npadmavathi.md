## Summary

N. Padmavathi filed an appeal under Section 19 of the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against the response of the CPIO, SEBI, concerning the status of complaints filed regarding PACL matters. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that SEBI does not possess the requested information and that refund proceedings and communications are managed entirely by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- **Appellant's Queries:** Sought steps taken on complaints regarding PACL refund submitted via India Post (Article/Booking Nos. ET178691200IN and EY513945793IN).
- **CPIO's Response:** Stated that information is not available with SEBI and advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, noting that PACL public notices and status reports are on the SEBI website.
- **Appellate Ruling:** Upheld the CPIO's reply, citing precedents that CPIO cannot invent information not in its possession and affirming that the Justice Lodha Committee is not a public authority under Section 2(h) of the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reiterates existing legal positions regarding RTI applicability to committee-managed refund processes.

## Compliance Requirements

- None for market participants or intermediaries.

- **Appeal Date:** October 23, 2026 (Received October 27, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- No direct impact on market operations, trading, or general compliance. Reinforces procedural routing for PACL refund queries.
