## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority issued an order on Appeal No. 7158 of 2026 filed by Sachin Yadav under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appeal relates to information sought regarding the appellant's candidature in the SEBI Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) 2025 Recruitment Exercise (Legal Stream).

## Key Points

- Appellant: Sachin Yadav
- Respondent: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- Subject Matter: RTI application transferred by RBI seeking candidate score records, component-wise marks, final scores, and objective response records for SEBI Officer Grade A 2025 Recruitment (Legal Stream).
- Appeal Scope: The appeal contested SEBI's response to query nos. 1-4, 6-12, and 18.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

No general compliance requirements for market participants; procedural disposal of an individual RTI appeal.

## Important Dates

- RBI Transfer Date: September 08, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 05, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 21, 2026
- Phase II Examination Date: February 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct impact on market operations, trading, or listed entities as it pertains solely to an individual RTI appeal regarding recruitment examination records.
