## Summary

SEBI has issued Release Order No. RRD/RD-3/1502/7825/2026 dated October 23, 2026, directing the release of bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios attached under Recovery Certificate No. 7825 of 2024 against Arun Panchariya, Vintage FZE (Alta Vista International FZE), and Emerging Markets Opportunities Fund, following an SAT order setting aside disgorgement.

## Key Points

- **SAT Order**: Hon’ble SAT vide order dated October 07, 2026, in Appeal no. 19 of 2024 set aside the direction of disgorgement against the Defaulters, cancelling the ongoing recovery proceedings.
- **Release of Accounts**: Banks, Mutual Funds, NSDL, and CDSL are directed to release attached Bank Accounts/lockers, Demat Accounts, and Mutual Fund Folios under attachment proceeding Nos. 12653 & 12654 of 2024 pursuant to RC No. 7825 of 2024.
- **Other Proceedings**: Notices of attachment issued against the Defaulters under other attachment proceedings shall remain in force until further instructions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Cancellation of specific recovery proceedings and lifting of attachment on financial accounts in compliance with the appellate tribunal's ruling.

## Compliance Requirements

- All banks, mutual funds, NSDL, and CDSL must release the attached accounts, lockers, and folios of the Defaulters under the specified notices.
- If a Defaulter holds no account or folio with the institution, no intimation to SEBI is required.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date**: October 23, 2026
- **SAT Order Date**: October 07, 2026
- **Recovery Certificate Date**: June 14, 2022

## Impact Assessment

- Restores access to frozen banking, demat, and mutual fund assets for Arun Panchariya, Vintage FZE, and Emerging Markets Opportunities Fund under the specified recovery certificate.
