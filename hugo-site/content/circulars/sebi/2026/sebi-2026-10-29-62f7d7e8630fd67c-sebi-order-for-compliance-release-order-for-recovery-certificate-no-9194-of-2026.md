## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the release of attachments and conclusion of recovery proceedings under Recovery Certificate No. 9194 of 2026 against Trdez Investment Private Limited (PAN: AAJCT5451G).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9194 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Trdez Investment Private Limited (PAN: AAJCT5451G).
- Action: Release order concerning recovery proceedings and attached assets.
- Regulatory Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, concluding attachment measures under Recovery Certificate No. 9194 of 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Release of frozen bank accounts, demat accounts, or other assets previously subjected to attachment under the recovery certificate, as directed by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Release: October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the finalization and closure of recovery certificate enforcement actions initiated against Trdez Investment Private Limited by SEBI.
