## Summary

SEBI has officially cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 7825 of 2024 dated June 14, 2024, issued against Arun Panchariya, Vintage FZE (now known as Alta Vista International FZE), and Emerging Markets Opportunities Fund (previously known as Highblue Sky Emerging Market Fund). The cancellation was executed pursuant to the order of the Hon'ble SAT dated October 07, 2026, in Appeal No. 19 of 2024.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 7825 of 2024 was originally issued for Rs. 52,01,00,459.54 along with interest, costs, and expenses for non-payment of disgorgement amounts directed by WTM Order dated October 22, 2021.
- The matter relates to the GDR issue of Southern Ispat and Energy Limited.
- Cancellation is carried out under sub-section (1) of section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with section 224 and rule 12 of the Second Schedule to the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal cancellation of the recovery certificate previously issued against the entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- No direct compliance requirements for general market participants; applies specifically to the parties involved in the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: June 14, 2024
- Hon'ble SAT Order Date: October 07, 2026
- Cancellation Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Resolves the immediate recovery proceedings under the cancelled certificate for the named entities in alignment with the SAT order.
