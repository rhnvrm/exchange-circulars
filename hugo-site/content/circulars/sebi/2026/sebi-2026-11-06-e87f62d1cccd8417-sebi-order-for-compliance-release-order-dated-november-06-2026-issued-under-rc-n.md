## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 06, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9229 of 2026 drawn against Rakeshbhai K Pandya in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under RC No. 9229 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Rakeshbhai K Pandya.
- Matter: Trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Action: Recovery proceedings concluded following compliance and payment of dues, resulting in the release order and cancellation of the recovery certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action concluded and release order issued pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Release of attached bank accounts, demat accounts, and other assets as recovery proceedings are satisfied and closed.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates successful conclusion of recovery proceedings and compliance by the noticee in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
