## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular informing all registered intermediaries, stock exchanges, and depositories about the revised reporting requirements and due diligence procedures under the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) and Common Reporting Standard (CRS). These changes are driven by the replacement of the Income-Tax Act, 1961 and IT Rules, 1962 with the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026, effective April 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Replaces previous framework (Income-Tax Act, 1961 and Rules 1962) with the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026 (effective April 1, 2026).
- Applies to all entities qualifying as Reporting Financial Institutions (RFIs) under Rule 238(8) of IT Rules, 2026.
- Mandates incorporation of AEOI reporting and due diligence requirements into customer onboarding and KYC frameworks.
- Requires updating Form 166 XML generation software, self-certification collection, controlling person identification, and account classification systems.
- FATCA and CRS certifications obtained from clients must be uploaded onto KYC Registration Agency (KRA) systems.

## Regulatory Changes

The statutory backing for FATCA and CRS reporting has shifted from Section 285BA of the Income-Tax Act, 1961 (and Rules 114F to 114H / Form 61B) to Section 508 of the Income-Tax Act, 2025, read with Rules 238, 239, and 240, and Form 166 of the IT Rules, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Registered intermediaries (RFIs) must:
- Incorporate AEOI reporting and due diligence requirements into customer onboarding and KYC frameworks.
- Update self-certification collection procedures to capture validity indicators.
- Update Controlling Person identification systems to capture roles (settlor, trustee, protector, beneficiary, others).
- Update account classification systems to capture account type and pre-existing/new status.
- Upgrade Form 166 XML generation software to include all new mandatory fields to avoid validation errors on the Income Tax portal.
- Upload client FATCA and CRS certifications onto KRA systems.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- IT Rules, 2026 Effective Date: April 1, 2026
- Guidance Note on FATCA and CRS issued by CBDT: July 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

All SEBI-registered intermediaries acting as RFIs face immediate operational and IT system update obligations. Failure to incorporate mandatory fields in Form 166 XML generation or update KYC/onboarding systems will lead to compliance failures and validation errors on the Income Tax portal.
