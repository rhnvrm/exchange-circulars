## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7129 of 2026 filed by Nikul Prajapati under the Right to Information Act, 2005, concerning PACL refund claims on the official portal (`sebipaclrefund.co.in`). The appellant sought details on land allotment records, rectification procedures for false land allotment objections, disbursement status for small investor claims (Rs. 10,000 and Rs. 15,000), higher-value claims, and grievance status.

## Key Points

- **RTI Application & Appeal:** Filed by Nikul Prajapati on behalf of investor Prajapati Rameshkumar Kurashibhai regarding multiple PACL registration numbers.
- **Information Sought:** Details of land allotment survey/plot numbers, physical possession receipts, rectification procedure for false land allotment objections, timelines for small investor claim disbursements, status of higher-value claims (Rs. 100,000+), and grievance file notings.
- **SEBI Response:** SEBI stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that all refund communications and processes are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; reiteration of the jurisdictional boundary regarding Justice R.M. Lodha Committee handling PACL refund matters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Appellants/investors seeking PACL refunds or rectifications are advised to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee directly through official channels/portal (`sebipaclrefund.co.in`).

## Important Dates

- **Application Date:** October 04, 2026
- **Respondent Reply Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Appeal Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Order Date:** November 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reaffirms the standard appellate stance that SEBI does not maintain or process specific PACL refund claims and land allotment dispute records directly, directing stakeholders to the dedicated Lodha Committee mechanism.
