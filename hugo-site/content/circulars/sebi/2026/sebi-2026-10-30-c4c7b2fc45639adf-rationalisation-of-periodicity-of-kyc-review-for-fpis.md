## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular rationalising the periodicity of KYC reviews for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) and eliminating requirements relating to the intimation of changes in previously furnished information at the time of registration continuance. These measures aim to reduce compliance burdens and promote ease of doing business.

## Key Points

- **Extended KYC Review Periodicity:** KYC review intervals for FPIs have been revised based on jurisdiction, category, and regulated status (e.g., up to every 8 years for specific Category I and non-high risk regulated entities).
- **Continuance Simplification:** Requirements for submitting change intimations or no-change declarations at the time of registration continuance have been dispensed with.
- **Exclusions:** Periodicity rules for GS-FPIs (Government Securities FPIs) and SWAGAT-FI FPIs remain unchanged.
- **Operational Timeline Adjustments:** Timelines in the Investor Charter for Designated Depository Participants (DDPs) have been updated to reflect the revised KYC review schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Master Circular Part A, Para 4:** Modified to remove sub-paragraph (ii) relating to intimation of changes in information during continuance, and adjusted fee payment timelines (fees to be paid at least 15 days prior to validity expiration).
- **Master Circular Part B, Para 5:** Table 6 substituted to outline the updated KYC review frequencies (such as every 8 years for high-risk Category I regulated under Regulation 5(a)(i), non-high risk regulated entities, and every 2 years for others and Category II).
- **Annexure-H:** Investor Charter for DDPs updated accordingly.

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories, Custodians, and DDPs must update their systems, processes, and client communication workflows to reflect the revised KYC review timelines and relaxed continuance requirements.
- FPIs must continue to pay registration continuance fees at least 15 days prior to the current validity of their registration.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effectiveness:** Immediate effect as specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduces compliance frequency and documentation overhead for FPIs and intermediaries during registration renewals.
- **Intermediary Adjustments:** DDPs, custodians, and depositories need to recalibrate automated KYC tracking systems and compliance checklists.
