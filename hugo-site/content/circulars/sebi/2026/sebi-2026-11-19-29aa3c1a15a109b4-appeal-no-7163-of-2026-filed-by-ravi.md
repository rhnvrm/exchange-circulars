## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7163 of 2026 filed by Ravi under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought extensive information regarding Delhi International Airport Limited ("DIAL") and its compliance with Chapter VA of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("LODR Regulations") following its classification under the High Value Debt Listed Entity (HVDLE) threshold.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application dated September 26, 2026, seeking compliance records of DIAL under LODR Regulations Chapter VA.
- CPIO responded on October 22, 2026, leading to the filing of Appeal No. 7163 of 2026 on October 27, 2026.
- Queries pertained to Related Party Transactions (Regulation 62K), Subsidiary Governance (Regulation 62L), and other governance disclosures for DIAL as an HVDLE.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the order deals with an RTI appeal under existing SEBI LODR Regulations and HVDLE compliance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations imposed on market participants; applies existing RTI Act transparency provisions to information requests.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 26, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 22, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low market impact as this is an individual RTI appeal adjudication regarding specific disclosures of an individual entity (DIAL).
