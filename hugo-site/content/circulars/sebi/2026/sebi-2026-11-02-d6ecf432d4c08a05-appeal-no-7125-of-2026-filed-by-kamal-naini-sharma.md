## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7125 of 2026 filed by Kamal Naini Sharma under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought specific information regarding the exact period for which an Account Freeze Action remained in force against Amrapali Capital and Finance Services Ltd. pursuant to NSDL orders and SEBI Circular dated June 20, 2019.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought clarification on the specific duration of the Account Freeze Action on Amrapali Capital and Finance Services Ltd. (PAN: AADCA4xxxx).
- CPIO, SEBI responded stating the query was vague, not specific, and sought clarification/opinion rather than existing information under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority (Ruchi Chojer) concurred with the CPIO, citing precedents (Naresh Kadyan vs. CPIO) that queries requiring deductions or inferences fall outside Section 2(f).
- The appeal was accordingly dismissed.
