## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order against Intelligent Money Managers Private Limited, a SEBI-registered merchant banker, following an inspection conducted for the period between April 1, 2022, and November 30, 2024. The inspection revealed violations concerning the improper billing and payment of delisting offer professional fees by target companies instead of acquirers, as well as a lack of arm's length independence where the compliance officer's remuneration was paid by a related group entity.

## Key Points

- Intelligent Money Managers Private Limited is a SEBI-registered Merchant Banker (Registration No. INM000012169).
- Inspection by SEBI covered the period from April 01, 2022, to November 30, 2024.
- Observations included raising professional fee invoices for delisting directly to target companies rather than acquirers.
- Compliance officer remuneration was funded by a related entity (J. Gupta & Co. LLP), compromising independence and arm's length separation.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement action under existing regulations]

## Compliance Requirements

Merchant bankers must ensure strict compliance with Delisting Regulations and Merchant Bankers Regulations, specifically ensuring that delisting expenses are borne by acquirers and maintaining complete operational independence and arm's length relationships from group entities, including independent remuneration of key compliance personnel.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2022 to November 30, 2024
- SCN Date: April 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory scrutiny on merchant banker governance, arm's length segregation of group entities, and accurate expense allocation in corporate delisting exercises.
