## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9308 of 2026 dated October 29, 2026, against Niranjan D. Agarwal in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited. This action marks the initiation of formal recovery proceedings following non-payment of penalties imposed in earlier adjudication orders.

## Key Points

- **Recovery Certificate:** Issued under RC No. 9308 of 2026 on October 29, 2026.
- **Defaulter:** Niranjan D. Agarwal (Promoter and Managing Director of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited).
- **Underlying Matter:** Follows previous SEBI enforcement orders penalizing the company and its promoters for fictitious export orders, misleading disclosures, and concealed loan defaults.
- **Recovery Action:** Empowers SEBI's recovery officer to attach bank accounts, properties, and execute other coercive recovery measures to recover unpaid dues.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of monetary penalties through formal recovery certificate mechanisms under SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Immediate settlement of outstanding dues by the noticee to avoid attachment of movable and immovable properties or other coercive recovery proceedings by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **October 29, 2026:** Date of Notice of Demand (RC No. 9308 of 2026).
- **July 30, 2026:** Date of underlying adjudication and enforcement order imposing penalties.

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on the promoter and company reputation and governance standing, underscoring SEBI's strict stance on non-compliance and execution of recovery proceedings for unpaid penalties.
