## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order concerning Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P) as a defaulter under Certificate No. 5163 of 2022. This order marks the formal release/withdrawal of recovery proceedings or attachment measures previously initiated against the entity.

## Key Points

- Entity: Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- PAN: AAPFS5325P
- Certificate No.: 5163 of 2022
- Action: Release Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure or lifting of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following settlement or satisfaction of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and market intermediaries are notified of the release of attachment/recovery proceedings against the specified entity, allowing normal operational status.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Removes prior recovery restrictions and attachment notices against Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd., restoring compliance standing under Certificate No. 5163 of 2022.
