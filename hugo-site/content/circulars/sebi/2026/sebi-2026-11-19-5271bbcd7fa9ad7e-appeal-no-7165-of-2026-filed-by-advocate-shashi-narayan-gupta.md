## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7165 of 2026 filed by Advocate Shashi Narayan Gupta under the RTI Act, 2005, concerning an investment made in Southern Horticulture & Rubber Plantation Ltd in October 1997. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that historical enforcement actions and court proceedings were provided and no refund process is currently pending or initiated by SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information on the status, refund amount, and refund procedure for an investment made on October 21, 1997, in Southern Horticulture & Rubber Plantation Ltd.
- SEBI CPIO stated that an order under Section 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992 was passed against the company on December 7, 2000, and prosecution was initiated against the company and its directors in November 2001.
- The prosecution was dismissed in May 2007, and SEBI's revision petition before the Bombay High Court was disposed of on April 2, 2008, with no pending court matters currently on record.
- The Appellate Authority concluded that the CPIO had adequately addressed the queries with available information and dismissed the appeal.
