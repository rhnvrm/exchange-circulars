## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7117 of 2026 filed by Goutam Mohanta under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought aggregate year-wise information from January 1, 2014, to July 31, 2026, regarding major economic offences, look out circulars (LOCs), travel restrictions, extradition requests, and asset recovery measures.

## Key Points

- The RTI application was originally transferred by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs to SEBI on August 17, 2026.
- CPIO, SEBI responded to the applicant on September 01, 2026.
- The appellant subsequently filed an appeal dated September 30, 2026.
- The queries pertain to macro-level statistics on bank frauds, economic offences, look out circulars, immigration alerts, Interpol notices, and international asset recovery.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes introduced; this is a quasi-judicial RTI appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance requirements for market participants or listed entities.

## Important Dates

- Application Transfer Date: August 17, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 01, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No operational or market impact as the matter is strictly confined to RTI appellate proceedings concerning historical enforcement and law enforcement statistics.
