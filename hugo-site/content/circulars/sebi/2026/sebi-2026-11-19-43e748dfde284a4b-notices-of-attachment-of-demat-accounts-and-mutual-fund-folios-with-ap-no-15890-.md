## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice of Attachment AP No. 15890 of 2026 on November 19, 2026, against Om Sri Sai Investments (PAN – AADFO8242K) under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9293 of 2026 in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited. This action mandates the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios to recover outstanding regulatory dues.

## Key Points

- Issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) on November 19, 2026.
- Attachment Notice Reference: AP No. 15890 of 2026 under RC No. 9293 of 2026.
- Target Entity: Om Sri Sai Investments (PAN – AADFO8242K), an associate entity linked to Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
- Action: Attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios to secure and recover pending dues.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not applicable - statutory enforcement and recovery action under existing regulations]

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants, mutual fund houses, and registrars/transfer agents (RTAs) must immediately freeze the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios, prohibiting any debits, transfers, or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate upon issuance and service

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict enforcement and asset preservation for recovery of dues associated with the Anugrah Stock and Broking default matter.
