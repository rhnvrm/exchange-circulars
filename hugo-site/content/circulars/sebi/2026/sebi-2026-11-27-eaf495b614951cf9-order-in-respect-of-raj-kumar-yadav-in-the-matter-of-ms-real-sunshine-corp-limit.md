## Summary

SEBI issued an order regarding Shri Rajkumar Yadav in the matter of M/s Real Sunshine Corp Limited (formerly Real Sunshine Agrotech Limited) concerning illegal fund mobilization through the issuance of Redeemable Preference Shares (RPS) without complying with the provisions of the Companies Act, 1956.

## Key Points

- SEBI previously issued an interim order on March 13, 2015, and a final order on February 15, 2018, against RSCL and its directors including Shri Rajkumar Yadav.
- The company raised at least Rs. 8,49,72,900 from at least 4,525 allottees during financial years 2009-2012 through RPS issuance.
- Violations included provisions of Sections 56(1), 56(3), 2(36) read with Sections 60, 73(1), 73(2), and 73(3) of the Companies Act, 1956.
- Directions include refunding collected money with 15% per annum interest, providing inventory of assets, restricting access to securities markets, and depositing proceeds in an Escrow Account.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and debarment from securities markets for directors involved in illegal fund raising.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities must refund investors with 15% interest per annum from the eighth day of collection.
- Prohibition from accessing securities markets or issuing prospectuses/offer documents.

## Important Dates

- Interim Order Date: March 13, 2015
- Final Order Date: February 15, 2018

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight against illegal collective fund raising schemes and ensures accountability of company directors.
