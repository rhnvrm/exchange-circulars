## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order regarding the trading activities of a client (Client M) suspected of front running the trades of Axis Mutual Fund during the period from April 01, 2020, to March 31, 2022. Notices were issued to Inventure Growth and Securities Ltd (Stock Broker) and Mr. Rushbha Vinay Nisar (Authorised Person through whom the client account was operated and orders executed).

## Key Points

- **Investigation Period:** April 1, 2020 to March 31, 2022.
- **Entities Involved:** Inventure Growth and Securities Ltd (Noticee 1) and Mr. Rushbha Vinay Nisar / Rushabh Vinay Nisar, Authorised Person (Noticee 2).
- **Allegations:** Client M executed trades suspected of front running the trades of Axis Mutual Fund using trading terminals allocated to the Authorised Person.
- **Legal Provisions:** Inquiry under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 5 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995, and Section 15HB for alleged violations of SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

None (Adjudication order enforcing existing regulations against front running and broker compliance).

## Compliance Requirements

Stock brokers and authorised persons must maintain stringent supervision over client trading accounts, prevent unauthorized access or misuse of trading terminals, and ensure robust controls against front running and fraudulent trading practices.

## Important Dates

- **Investigation Period:** April 01, 2020 – March 31, 2022
- **Show Cause Notice (SCN) Date:** November 11, 2025
- **Order Issuance:** November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory scrutiny on stock brokers and authorised persons regarding supervisory lapses and front running activities, emphasizing intermediary accountability when trading terminals are utilized in fraudulent schemes.
