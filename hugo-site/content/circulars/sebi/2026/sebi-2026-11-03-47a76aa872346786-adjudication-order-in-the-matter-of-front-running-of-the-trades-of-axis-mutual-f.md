## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order concerning the front-running of trades of Axis Mutual Fund during the period from April 1, 2021, to March 31, 2022. The investigation revealed that Woodstock Broking Pvt. Ltd., Marfatia Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd., and their respective directors facilitated front-running trades by enabling access to trading terminals and providing support from outside India.

## Key Points

- **Entities Involved:** Woodstock Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Noticee 1), its directors (Noticees 2 to 6), Marfatia Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Noticee 7), and its directors (Noticees 8 to 10).
- **Violation Period:** April 01, 2021 to March 31, 2022.
- **Allegations:** Facilitating front-running of trades of Axis Mutual Fund by enabling unauthorized terminal access and operational support from outside India.
- **Legal Provisions Violated:** Sections 12A(a), 12A(b), and 12A(c) of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Regulations 3(a), 3(b), 3(c), 3(d), and 4(1) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003, read with Section 27 of the SEBI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework amendments; enforcement of existing provisions under the SEBI Act and PFUTP Regulations against fraudulent and unfair trade practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Stock brokers and intermediaries must maintain stringent controls over trading terminal access, preventing unauthorized remote access and any facilitation of front-running activities.

## Important Dates

- **Investigation Period:** April 1, 2021 - March 31, 2022
- **Show Cause Notice Date:** October 31, 2023
- **Order Date:** November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict stance against front-running and unauthorized terminal usage by stock brokers, emphasizing accountability for intermediaries and their management.
