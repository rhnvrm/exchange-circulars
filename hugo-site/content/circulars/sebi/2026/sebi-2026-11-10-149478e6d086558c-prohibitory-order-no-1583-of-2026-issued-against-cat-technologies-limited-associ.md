## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1583 of 2026 on November 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 4398 of 2021 against CAT Technologies Limited. This enforcement action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to realize unpaid dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts ordered in prior regulatory investigations relating to Global Depository Receipt (GDR) issues.

## Key Points

- Order Issued: Prohibitory Order No. 1583 of 2026.
- Target Entity: CAT Technologies Limited.
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 4398 of 2021.
- Issuing Authority: Recovery and Refund Department, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- Context: Arises from historical investigations into GDR fundraising schemes and associated enforcement orders.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of statutory dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze designated accounts, funds, and assets of CAT Technologies Limited as specified in the prohibitory order to prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities failing to comply with disgorgement or monetary penalty orders stemming from past market violations.
