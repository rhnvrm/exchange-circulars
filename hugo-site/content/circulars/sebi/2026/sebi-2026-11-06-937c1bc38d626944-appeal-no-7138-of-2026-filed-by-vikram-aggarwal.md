## Summary

Vikram Aggarwal filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against the response of the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information and procedural records related to a SCORES complaint against Punjab National Bank processed during May and June 2025.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought detailed action history, transaction history, audit history, ATRs, review requests, and system metadata concerning a SCORES complaint against Punjab National Bank regarding an HUF account.
- The complaint involved action taken reports (ATRs) dated May 15, 2025 and May 16, 2025, first and second level reviews, and subsequent redirection/disposal to the Market Intelligence portal on June 2, 2025.
- The application specifically requested procedural and system logs rather than a reconsideration of the complaint's merits.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy directives were introduced through this order.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities and SEBI CPIO must handle RTI applications in accordance with statutory provisions, ensuring provision of severable non-exempt information under Section 10 where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 17, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 08, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct impact on market operations, trading, or corporate compliance frameworks, being an administrative disposal of an individual RTI appeal.
