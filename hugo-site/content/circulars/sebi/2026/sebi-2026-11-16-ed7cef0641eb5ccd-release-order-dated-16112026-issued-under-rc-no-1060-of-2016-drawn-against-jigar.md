## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 16, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 1060 of 2016 drawn against Jigar Praful Ghoghari in the matter of Goldstone Technologies Limited (now Aion-Tech Solutions Limited). This order directs the release and lifting of attachments on bank accounts, demat accounts, and other assets previously frozen for recovery of dues.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued on November 16, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No.: 1060 of 2016.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Jigar Praful Ghoghari (PAN: ASFPG8598L).
- Subject Company: Goldstone Technologies Limited (subject of 2014 SEBI adjudication order regarding PFUTP violations between 2009-2010).
- Action: Official lifting and release of attached bank accounts, demat accounts, and properties following the fulfillment/resolution of recovery dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure of recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues, resulting in the vacation of attachment notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depository participants are required to unfreeze the accounts and assets of Jigar Praful Ghoghari previously subject to attachment under RC No. 1060 of 2016.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 16, 2026
- Date of Original Recovery Certificate: 2016 (RC No. 1060 of 2016)

## Impact Assessment

Signals the conclusion of recovery proceedings and restoration of financial standing for the noticee with respect to the specific attachment order.
