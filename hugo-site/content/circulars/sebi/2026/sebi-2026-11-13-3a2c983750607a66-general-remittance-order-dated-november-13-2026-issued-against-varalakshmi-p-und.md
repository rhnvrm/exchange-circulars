## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7695 of 2024 against Varalakshmi P in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd., directing the remittance and recovery of dues pursuant to regulatory enforcement proceedings.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 7695 of 2024.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Varalakshmi P.
- Matter: Recovery of dues in the case of Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and recovery proceedings executed under the provisions of the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of penalties, disgorgement amounts, or dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries are required to comply with remittance and attachment orders, ensuring funds and assets are remitted or frozen as directed by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Reference: RC No. 7695 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement mechanism for recovering unpaid dues and penalties in corporate default matters.
