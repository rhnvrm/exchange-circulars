## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has officially cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 4948 of 2022 issued against L Muneashwari, L Naga Rama Krishna, and M/s Kepler Information Systems Private Limited. This action is taken in the matter of Bodhtree Consulting Limited, following the ruling by the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 4948 of 2022 (originally totaling approximately ₹93.61 lakh including interest and costs) was formally cancelled by SEBI's Recovery Officer.
- The cancellation stems from the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 6, 2026 (Appeal No. 447/2022), which set aside the fraud penalties previously imposed by SEBI.
- The entities involved include L Muneashwari and L Naga Rama Krishna (directors/promoters of Bodhtree Consulting Limited) along with M/s Kepler Information Systems Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal withdrawal and cancellation of recovery proceedings and associated certificate against the named noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- No active compliance obligations or monetary liabilities remain under the cancelled recovery certificate for the specified noticees regarding these specific penalties.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: October 6, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Cancellation Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Relief for the noticees as recovery proceedings for the set-aside penalties are officially terminated. Minimal broader market impact as this is an administrative implementation of an appellate tribunal ruling.
