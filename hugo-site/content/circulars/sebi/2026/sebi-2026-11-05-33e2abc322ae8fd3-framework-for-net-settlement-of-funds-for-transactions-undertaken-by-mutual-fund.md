## Summary

SEBI has issued a framework permitting net settlement of funds at the individual mutual fund scheme level for outright transactions undertaken in the cash segment of stock exchanges. This initiative aims to enhance operational efficiency, reduce temporary funding requirements, and improve settlement efficiency for mutual funds, while securities settlement continues on a gross basis.

## Key Points

- **Scheme-Level Netting**: Netting of funds is permitted exclusively within the same mutual fund scheme and within the same settlement cycle. Cross-scheme netting or adjustments between portfolios are strictly prohibited.
- **Outright Transactions**: Defined as either a purchase or a sale transaction (not both) in a security in the same settlement cycle. If a scheme has both purchase and sale transactions in the same security within the same cycle, they are treated as non-outright and are ineligible for netting.
- **Exclusions**: The framework does not apply to derivative transactions, securities lending and borrowing, short-sale related transactions, or other uncovered types.
- **Securities & Taxes**: Settlement of securities, Securities Transaction Tax (STT), and stamp duty will continue on a gross/delivery basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Permits net settlement of funds for outright cash market transactions by mutual fund schemes.
- Clarifies that securities settlement, STT, and stamp duty remain on a gross/delivery basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs and custodians must ensure that the netting mechanism does not interfere with scheme-wise accounting, NAV computation, asset allocation, investment limits, or unit-holder interests.
- Residual purchase obligations must be funded by the scheme, and excess sale proceeds cannot be offset against non-outright purchase obligations.

## Important Dates

- Issued on November 5, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly reduces temporary working capital and funding requirements for mutual fund schemes executing multiple outright cash transactions within a settlement cycle.
- Improves overall operational efficiency and liquidity management for Asset Management Companies (AMCs).
