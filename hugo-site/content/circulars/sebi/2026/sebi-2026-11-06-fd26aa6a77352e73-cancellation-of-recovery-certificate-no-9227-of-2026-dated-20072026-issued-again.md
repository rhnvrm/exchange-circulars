## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026 dated July 20, 2026, which was originally issued against Lataben Narotambhai Rangi (PAN: AYRPR7467F). The matter pertains to trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026.
- Original certificate date: July 20, 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Lataben Narotambhai Rangi (PAN: AYRPR7467F).
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).

## Regulatory Changes

Official withdrawal and cancellation of recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 against the individual.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are advised to withdraw any freeze or attachment orders placed pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate Date: July 20, 2026
- Cancellation Notice Date: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides relief to the individual by terminating recovery proceedings and lifting asset restrictions associated with the specified recovery certificate.
