## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 drawn against Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, Khushbu Arjunbhai Padhiyar, and 19 other noticees jointly and severally in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited. The release order follows an appeal and order from the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).

## Key Points

- Release Order issued on November 11, 2026, under RC No. 9290 of 2026.
- Noticees: Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, Khushbu Arjunbhai Padhiyar, Nitaben Babulal Khalas, Nimeshkumar Baldevbhai Parmar, Taraben Kirankumar Padhiyar, Ravi Dipakbhai Barupal, Rakeshbhai Kishanbhai Pandya, Jayesh Ganpatlal Pansal, Rajaniben Jayeshbhai Pansal, Bhavika Prakash Rathod, Rita Arvindbhai Rangi, Badamilal Banshilal Garg, Lataben Narotambhai Rangi, Navin Shankarlal Tank, Vishnu Shantibhai Parmar, Komal Vaghaji Chauhan, Dayaben Marvadi, B B Commercial Ltd, Bhargaviben Shamalbhai Raval, Prakash Chunilal Mudethiya, and Ramnath Sharma.
- Underlying Matter: Trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Action: Cancellation of recovery proceedings and release of attached bank accounts, mutual funds, and properties pursuant to SAT directives.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal and cancellation of enforcement actions and attachment notices previously initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992, in compliance with appellate tribunal rulings.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and mutual funds are instructed to lift freezes on the accounts and assets of the specified noticees.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 11, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9290 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restores financial and operational access for the released individuals and entities following the successful setting aside of the adjudication order at the SAT level.
