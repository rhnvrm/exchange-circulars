## Summary

SEBI has introduced a comprehensive framework for the issuance of Depository Receipts (DRs) on units of Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), aligning with the Depository Receipts Scheme 2014 and the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.

## Key Points

- **Definition**: Depository Receipts represent foreign currency-denominated instruments listed on international exchanges backed by InvIT units deposited with a domestic custodian.
- **Eligibility**: InvITs and existing unitholders can issue or transfer units for DRs subject to compliance with SEBI InvIT Regulations.
- **Foreign Depository**: Must be regulated in a permissible jurisdiction and permitted to acquire InvIT units.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operationalizes Regulation 14(5C) of the SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, permitting DR issuance on InvIT units.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment managers on behalf of InvITs must establish formal agreements with Indian depositories, domestic custodians, and foreign depositories.
- Ensure ongoing compliance with InvIT regulations and FEMA guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective from the date of circular issuance: November 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the investor base for Indian InvITs by enabling overseas retail and institutional investors to access InvIT units via international exchanges through depository receipts.
