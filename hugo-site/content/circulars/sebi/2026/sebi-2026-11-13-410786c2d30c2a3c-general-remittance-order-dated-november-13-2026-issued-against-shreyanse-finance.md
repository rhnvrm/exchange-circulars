## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against Shreyanse Finance Ltd under Recovery Certificate No. 7568 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice / Order Type: General Remittance Order (Recovery Proceedings)
- Date of Issue: November 13, 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Shreyanse Finance Ltd
- Recovery Certificate (RC) Number: RC No. 7568 of 2024
- Subject Matter: Recovery proceedings and remittance of dues in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action initiated pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are directed to remit funds or freeze assets as specified under the General Remittance Order to satisfy outstanding dues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in market violations and fraudulent activities in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
