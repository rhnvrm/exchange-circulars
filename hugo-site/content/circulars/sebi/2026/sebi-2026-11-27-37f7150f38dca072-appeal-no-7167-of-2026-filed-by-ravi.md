## Summary

This order resolves Appeal No. 7167 of 2026 filed by Ravi under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding FPI registration data, foreign entities trading in equity derivatives, and SEBI circulars on algorithmic and HFT trading.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought year-wise FPI registration details (category-wise and country-wise), lists of FPI names with DDPs, foreign entities trading in equity derivatives, and all circulars/SOPs on algorithmic trading since 2012.
- CPIO directed the appellant to SEBI and NSDL websites for existing FPI registration lists.
- Information regarding Designated Depository Participants (DDPs) was withheld under Section 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act as commercial confidential information held in a fiduciary capacity.
- SEBI stated that information on foreign entities and their subsidiaries permitted by stock exchanges to trade equity derivatives is not available with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory circulars or changes; this is an adjudication on an RTI appeal concerning existing data and disclosure transparency.

## Compliance Requirements

None. The order addresses information disclosure requests under the RTI Act.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 26, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 26, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides clarity on the availability and statutory exemptions regarding FPI data, DDP commercial confidentiality, and algorithmic trading regulatory information under the RTI Act.
