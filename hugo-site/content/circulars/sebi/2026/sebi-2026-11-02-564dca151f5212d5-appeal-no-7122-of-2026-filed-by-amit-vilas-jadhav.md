## Summary

This order addresses Appeal No. 7122 of 2026 filed by Shri Amit Vilas Jadhav under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, concerning records and file movements related to a whistleblower grievance regarding 3i Infotech Ltd.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of inward receipts, file movements, action-taken reports, file notings, and communications relating to Grievance Registration No. DCOYA/E/2026/0006755.
- The grievance pertained to corporate governance, whistleblower complaints, and alleged retaliation and victimization at 3i Infotech Ltd, which was closed on August 18, 2026.
- The appeal challenges the response/non-disclosure of records by the CPIO under the RTI Act framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory policy or market-wide rule changes; this is an adjudication under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicable to SEBI CPIO regarding statutory timelines and obligations under the RTI Act, 2005 for providing disclosable records and invoking appropriate exemptions under Section 8(1) and Section 10.

## Important Dates

- **Grievance Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Grievance Closure Date**: August 18, 2026
- **RTI Application Date**: September 05, 2026
- **CPIO Response Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Appeal Date**: October 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; pertains strictly to individual RTI applicant rights and disclosure protocols regarding corporate governance grievances.
