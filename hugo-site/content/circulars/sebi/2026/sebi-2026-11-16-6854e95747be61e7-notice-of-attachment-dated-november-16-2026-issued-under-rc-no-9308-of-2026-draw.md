## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 16, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9308 of 2026 against Niranjan D. Agarwal, Managing Director of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited. This action is taken due to the non-payment of monetary penalties imposed following investigations into disclosure lapses, misleading corporate announcements, and fund diversion.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9308 of 2026 on November 16, 2026.
- **Target Individual:** Niranjan D. Agarwal (Managing Director of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited).
- **Underlying Cause:** Non-payment of penalties totaling ₹40 lakh levied by SEBI in its final order dated July 30, 2026.
- **Company Background:** Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited is an Ahmedabad-based fabric manufacturer listed on the NSE SME platform.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to SEBI's powers for recovery of penalties under the SEBI Act, 1992, involving attachment of bank accounts, properties, or other assets of defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals subject to SEBI monetary penalties must settle dues promptly to avoid coercive recovery actions such as attachment notices, bank account freezes, and asset seizures.

## Important Dates

- **November 16, 2026:** Date of Notice of Attachment (RC No. 9308 of 2026).
- **July 30, 2026:** Date of SEBI Final Order imposing penalties.

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's strict enforcement and follow-through on recovery proceedings against promoters and directors defaulting on regulatory penalties, reinforcing corporate governance and accountability in the securities market.
