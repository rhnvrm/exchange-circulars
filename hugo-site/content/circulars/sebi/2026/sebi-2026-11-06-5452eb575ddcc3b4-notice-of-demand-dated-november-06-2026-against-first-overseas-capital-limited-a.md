## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Rule 225(2) of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 read with Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 against First Overseas Capital Limited (PAN: AAACL4737A) under Recovery Certificate No. 9322 of 2026. The total demand amounts to Rs. 5,16,000/-, which includes a penalty of Rs. 5,00,000/- imposed by the Adjudicating Officer in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited, along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- Entity in Default: First Overseas Capital Limited (PAN: AAACL4737A).
- Total Demand Amount: Rs. 5,16,000/- comprising Rs. 5,00,000/- penalty (pursuant to Order No. Order/JS/VC/2026-27/32690-32694 dated 31.08.2026), Rs. 15,000/- interest (September to November 2026 @ 1% p.m.), and Rs. 1,000/- recovery cost.
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of the notice via EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank account (A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9322, IFSC: ICIC0000106) or through the SEBI Recovery Payment portal.
- Consequences of Non-Payment: Attachment and sale of movable/immovable properties, attachment of bank accounts, arrest and detention, and appointment of a receiver.
- Asset Transfer Restrictions: Any direct or indirect transfer of property or monies held in bank accounts to spouse, minor child, or son's wife/minor child on or after August 31, 2026, is deemed to be the entity's property for recovery.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings pursuant to Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Income-tax Rules, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- The entity must remit the total sum of Rs. 5,16,000/- within 15 days of receiving the notice.
- Forward payment confirmation details (Case Name, Recovery Certificate Number, Name of Payee, Date of Payment, Amount Paid, Transaction No., Bank Details) to SEBI Recovery Officer and email contacts (paridhia@sebi.gov.in & recoveryho1@sebi.gov.in).
- Refrain from mortgaging, charging, leasing, or otherwise dealing with properties without permission from the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 06, 2026.
- Payment Due Date: Within 15 days from receipt of notice (approx. November 21, 2026).
- Relevant Order Date: August 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Targeted recovery action against First Overseas Capital Limited for outstanding regulatory dues. Minimal direct impact on broader market operations.
