## Summary

SEBI has announced the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 3834 of 2021 dated June 16, 2021, issued against Unicon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P), the defaulter in the matter of Ashutosh Paper Mills Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 3834 of 2021 issued on June 16, 2021.
- Defaulter: Unicon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P).
- Subject Matter: Ashutosh Paper Mills Limited.
- Official completion of recovery proceedings and release order issued by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and recovery proceedings conducted pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 have been successfully concluded for this certificate.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and relevant financial institutions are notified of the completion of recovery proceedings and the release under the specified recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Original Recovery Certificate: June 16, 2021
- Date of Completion Notice / Release: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marks the closure of recovery proceedings under this specific certificate against the defaulter, reflecting resolution or completion of the enforcement action in the Ashutosh Paper Mills Limited matter.
