## Summary

SEBI has released the regulatory framework for the issuance of depository receipts (DRs) on units of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) pursuant to Regulation 14(12A) of the REIT Regulations, 2014. This enables foreign investors to invest in REIT units via foreign currency-denominated depository receipts listed on international exchanges.

## Key Points

- **Reference Framework**: Aligned with the Depository Receipts Scheme, 2014, and Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.
- **Eligible Participants**: REITs and existing unitholders are eligible to issue or transfer units for DR creation, subject to compliance with SEBI REIT Regulations.
- **Definitions**: Formally defines terms including Depository Receipt, Foreign Depository, and Transfer of units of a REIT by existing unitholders.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operationalizes Regulation 14(12A) of SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, setting clear parameters for listing and trading REIT units on international exchanges through depository receipts.

## Compliance Requirements

- REIT Managers must enter into formal arrangements with Indian Depositories, Domestic Custodians, and Foreign Depositories as required.
- REITs must maintain ongoing compliance with SEBI REIT Regulations and FEMA guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately from the date of the circular, November 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Broadens the investor base for Indian REITs by facilitating international capital inflows through foreign currency-denominated instruments.
