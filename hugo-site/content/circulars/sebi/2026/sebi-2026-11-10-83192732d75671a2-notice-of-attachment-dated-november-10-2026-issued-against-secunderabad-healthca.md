## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a formal Notice of Attachment dated November 10, 2026, against Secunderabad Healthcare Limited under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7734 of 2024. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to recover outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- Date of Notice: November 10, 2026.
- Entity Involved: Secunderabad Healthcare Limited (BSE: 524540 / SEHE).
- Recovery Reference: RC No. 7734 of 2024 (following previous Notice of Demand dated March 15, 2024).
- Purpose: Attachment of assets, bank accounts, or properties to satisfy outstanding regulatory dues and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and execution of recovery proceedings under SEBI Act and relevant regulations for non-compliance and unpaid penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Secunderabad Healthcare Limited and associated entities/directors must settle outstanding dues immediately or face further coercive attachment and legal actions by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 10, 2026
- Previous Demand Notice: March 15, 2024

## Impact Assessment

High negative impact on the company's financial liquidity, operational continuity, and investor confidence due to asset attachment and regulatory enforcement actions.
