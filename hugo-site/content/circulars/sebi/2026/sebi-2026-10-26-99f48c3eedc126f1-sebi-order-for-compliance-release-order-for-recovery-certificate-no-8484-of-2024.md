## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024 against Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMGPA3864Q) in the matter of M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited. This order releases previously attached assets and concludes the specific recovery proceedings associated with this certificate.

## Key Points

- Issued by SEBI in the matter of M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited.
- Defaulter: Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMGPA3864Q).
- Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024.
- Formally releases prior asset attachments upon settlement or resolution of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal release of attachment orders previously issued against the defaulter's accounts and properties under Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance requirements for market participants; relevant financial institutions and authorities are notified of the release of attached assets.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to the release of specific attached assets for the named individual in relation to historical recovery proceedings of Austral Coke and Projects Limited.
