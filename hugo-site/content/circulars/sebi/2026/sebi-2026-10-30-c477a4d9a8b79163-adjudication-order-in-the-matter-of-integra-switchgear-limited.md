## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Integra Switchgear Limited and its promoters following a Draft Letter of Offer filed by Northvale Capital Partners Private Limited for the acquisition of equity shares. The examination revealed multiple violations of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (LODR Regulations) concerning shareholding pattern disclosures and promoter reclassification procedures.

## Key Points

- SEBI examined the Draft Letter of Offer submitted by Northvale Capital Partners Private Limited (Acquirer) for acquiring shares of Integra Switchgear Limited (Target Company).
- The company and its promoters (including Mayurbhai Jamnadas Vora, Deepak Jamnadas Vora, Pankaj Jamnadas Vora, and various HUFs and individuals) were found to be in violation of LODR Regulations.
- Violations include non-compliance with Regulation 31(1)(b), Regulation 31A(2), Regulation 31A(6)(a), Regulation 31A(7)(c), Regulation 31A(8)(c), and Regulation 4(1) of LODR Regulations.
- The proceedings focus on improper shareholding disclosures, unauthorized or non-compliant promoter reclassification, and failure to adhere to corporate governance standards.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; enforcement of existing LODR Regulations (Regulations 4, 31, and 31A).

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must strictly adhere to continuous disclosure norms under Regulation 31 and follow proper procedural requirements for promoter reclassification under Regulation 31A of the LODR Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 2026
- Review Period: Refers to Draft Letter of Offer filings and disclosures prior to June 2024 and ongoing proceedings.

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight on promoter shareholding disclosures and reclassification processes during corporate acquisitions and open offers, ensuring transparency and accountability for listed entities and their promoters.
