## Summary

Appeal No. 7134 of 2026 under the RTI Act, 2005 was filed by Mukesh Rajendra Parikh against CPIO, SEBI, seeking certified copies of records relating to SCORES Complaint No. SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031289/1 concerning CDSL Ventures Limited (CVL) and handled by HDFC Bank.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application dated September 05, 2026, seeking records for SCORES complaint SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031289/1 against CDSL Ventures Limited.
- Requested records include Action Taken Reports (ATRs) from HDFC Bank, first-level reviews, email communications, SCORES dashboard logs, and final disposal records.
- The CPIO responded on September 17, 2026, indicating that the complaint dated July 06, 2026 is registered in SCORES and processed electronically.
