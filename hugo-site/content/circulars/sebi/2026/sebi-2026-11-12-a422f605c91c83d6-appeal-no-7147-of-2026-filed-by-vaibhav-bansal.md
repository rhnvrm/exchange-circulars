## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7147 of 2026 filed by Vaibhav Bansal under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appeal challenged the CPIO's response regarding queries on SEBI Grade A Legal interview evaluation criteria and question-wise marks distribution for the Phase 2 Paper 2 descriptive examination.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information on how candidates without two years of qualifying experience are evaluated in SEBI Grade A Legal interviews and the question-wise marks breakdown for his Phase 2 Paper 2 descriptive exam.
- The CPIO stated that query no. 1 sought clarification or opinion, falling outside the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Regarding query no. 2, the CPIO noted that descriptive answer sheets are evaluated by IBPS under an MoU and evaluated copies are not shared with SEBI, though general Phase II examination and interview marks are published on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO's stance on query no. 1, citing precedents that queries seeking explanation, opinion, or advice are not maintainable under the RTI Act.
