## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 4948 of 2022 issued against L Muneashwari, L Naga Rama Krishna, and M/s Kepler Information Systems Private Limited. This follows the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) ruling setting aside the underlying penalties in the matter of Bodhtree Consulting Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 4948 of 2022 has been formally cancelled by SEBI.
- Noticees / Defaulters: L Muneashwari (ABWPL2445F), L Naga Rama Krishna (ADRPL5494M), and M/s Kepler Information Systems Private Limited (AAECK5818R).
- Matter pertains to Bodhtree Consulting Limited.
- Action taken consequent to SAT appellate rulings setting aside the penalty.

## Regulatory Changes

Cancellation of recovery proceedings and withdrawal of coercive attachment measures previously initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries holding accounts or assets of the noticees under the earlier recovery certificate are advised to release/unfreeze such assets as the recovery certificate stands cancelled.

## Important Dates

- Date of Cancellation / Order: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides legal relief to the concerned entities by officially withdrawing recovery proceedings and unfreezing assets following favorable SAT orders.
