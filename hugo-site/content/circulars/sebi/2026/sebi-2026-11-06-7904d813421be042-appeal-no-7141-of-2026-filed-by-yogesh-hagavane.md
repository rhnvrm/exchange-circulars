## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7141 of 2026 filed by Yogesh Hagavane under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought year-wise statistical data regarding settlement applications, High Powered Advisory Committee (HPAC) disposals, settlement amounts, and case categories.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought detailed aggregate data on settlement applications received, HPAC disposals, settlement amounts collected, and processing timelines.
- The CPIO responded that information regarding queries 1, 3, and 5 is available in SEBI Annual Reports on the SEBI website.
- For queries 2 and 6 to 8, the CPIO stated that the information is not maintained in the specified format and collating it would disproportionately divert public resources under Section 7(9) of the RTI Act.
- Query 4 was deemed vague and not constituting 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; this is an RTI statutory appeal order.

## Compliance Requirements

- None.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: August 22, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 18, 2026
- Appeal Filing Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct operational or market impact; reaffirms the availability of aggregate settlement statistics in SEBI Annual Reports and the application of Section 7(9) for custom data compilation requests.
