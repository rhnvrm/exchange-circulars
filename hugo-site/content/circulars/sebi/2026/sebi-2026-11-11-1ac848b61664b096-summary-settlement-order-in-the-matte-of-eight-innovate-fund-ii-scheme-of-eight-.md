## Summary

SEBI issued a Summary Settlement Order dated March 04, 2026, regarding Eight Innovate Fund II (scheme of Eight Innovate Investment Trust I), Eight I Advisory LLP (Manager), Vikram Chachra (KMP), and Venkatramani Vishwanath (KMP). The applicants settled potential enforcement proceedings by jointly and severally remitting a settlement amount of ₹12,75,000 for failing to contribute pro-rata to the fund.

## Key Points

- Entities involved: Eight Innovate Investment Trust I, Eight I Advisory LLP, Vikram Chachra, and Venkatramani Vishwanath.
- Violation observed: Non-compliance with regulation 10(d) of AIF Regulations, 2012 read with clause 11.1.2 of the Master Circular for Alternative Investment Funds (manager/sponsor not contributing pro-rata to investor contributions for June 2024 to December 2024).
- Additional code of conduct violations observed for Manager and KMPs under regulations 20(1), 20(2), and 20(5) read with clauses 2(a) and 2(c).
- Settlement amount of ₹12,75,000 paid jointly and severally by the applicants.
- SEBI confirmed credit of the settlement amount and disposed of the specified proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement proceedings settled under Section 15JB read with Section 19 of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 23 of the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Alternative Investment Funds, managers, and sponsors must strictly adhere to pro-rata contribution requirements under AIF Regulations and Master Circulars. Applicants must comply with settlement terms and conditions.

## Important Dates

- Notice of Summary Settlement Date: March 04, 2026
- Period of examination: June 2024 to March 2025 (violation period June 2024 to December 2024)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's regulatory oversight on alternative investment funds regarding pro-rata contributions by sponsors and managers, utilizing the summary settlement mechanism for prompt resolution of violations.
