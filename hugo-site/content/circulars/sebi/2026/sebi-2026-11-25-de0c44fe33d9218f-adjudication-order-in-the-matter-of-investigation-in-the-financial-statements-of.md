## Summary

SEBI has initiated adjudication proceedings under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 against Lloyds Enterprises Limited (Noticee 1), its Promoter and Managing Director Mr. Rajesh Rajnarayan Gupta (Noticee 2), and its Chief Financial Officer Mr. Viresh Shankar Sohoni (Noticee 3). The proceedings stem from a detailed investigation into alleged misrepresentation and misstatement in the financial statements of Lloyds Enterprises Limited, violating various provisions of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Key Points

- **Entity Involved:** Lloyds Enterprises Limited (LEL), along with key managerial personnel Mr. Rajesh Rajnarayan Gupta (MD) and Mr. Viresh Shankar Sohoni (CFO).
- **Investigation Scope:** Detailed investigation into financial statement misrepresentations/misstatements.
- **Alleged Violations by Noticee 1 (Company):** Regulations 4(1), 4(2)(e), 33(1)(a), 33(1)(c), 34(3), and 48 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015; Regulations 4(1), 4(2)(f), 4(2)(k), and 4(2)(r) of SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.
- **Alleged Violations by Noticee 2 (Managing Director):** PFUTP Regulations, LODR Regulations read with Section 27 of SEBI Act, 1992, specific corporate governance provisions under LODR Regulations, and Regulation 17(8) read with Part-B of Schedule II of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- **Alleged Violations by Noticee 3 (CFO):** Similar provisions under LODR, PFUTP, Section 27 of SEBI Act, 1992, and CFO certification requirements under Regulation 17(8).
- **Appointment:** Adjudicating Officer appointed vide communiqué dated May 05, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and adjudication action under existing SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees are required to respond to the adjudication notices, present their defense, and comply with the inquiry proceedings conducted by the Adjudicating Officer under Rule 5 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995.
- Listed entities must ensure complete accuracy and transparency in financial statements and disclosures under LODR Regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Appointment of AO:** May 05, 2025
- **Order / Notice Date:** November 2026

- Highlights regulatory strictness regarding financial statement integrity and corporate governance.
