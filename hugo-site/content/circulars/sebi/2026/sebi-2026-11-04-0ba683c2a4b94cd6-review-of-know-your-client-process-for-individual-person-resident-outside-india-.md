## Summary

SEBI has reviewed and modified the Know Your Client (KYC) framework for individual Person Resident Outside India (PROI) clients—including Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs), and foreign nationals—to ease their onboarding process.

## Key Points

- Relaxes the requirement for a client to be physically in India during digital on-boarding for individual PROI clients located in Financial Action Task Force (FATF) compliant countries.
- Mandates KRAs to tag individual attributes as “validated” when verified with official databases, while keeping all KYC records portable regardless of attribute validation status.
- Permits intermediaries to rely on KYC undertaken by other SEBI-registered intermediaries or regulated financial entities via KRA or CKYCRR records.
- Requires the authorised official of the intermediary to be present during Video In Person Verification (VIPV), subject to concurrent audit.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of extant provisions under the KYC Master Circular (SEBI/HO/MIRSD/SECFATF/P/CIR/2023/169 dated October 12, 2023) regarding physical presence and digital onboarding for PROI clients.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries can accept digital submission of KYC information and supporting documents from PROI clients in FATF-compliant countries.
- Intermediaries must seek PAN and CKYC ID when initiating account relationships or updating KYC.
- VIPV processes must include an authorized official and be subject to concurrent audit.

## Important Dates

- Date of circular: November 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Simplifies onboarding workflows for foreign investors, NRIs, and OCIs, encouraging broader participation in Indian securities markets while maintaining robust compliance standards.
