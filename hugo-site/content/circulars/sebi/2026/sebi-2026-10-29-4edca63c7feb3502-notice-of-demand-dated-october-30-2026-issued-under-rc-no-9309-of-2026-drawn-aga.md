## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 30, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9309 of 2026 drawn against M/s. Arihant Securities (Proprietor: Pravin Kumar Jain). This recovery action relates to proceedings in the matter of Betala Global Securities Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9309 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: M/s. Arihant Securities (Proprietor: Pravin Kumar Jain).
- Matter: Betala Global Securities Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and financial institutions are required to comply with the demand notice, restrict unauthorized transfers, and ensure realization of dues as directed by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Demand: October 30, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Number: RC No. 9309 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights stringent regulatory enforcement and recovery actions undertaken by SEBI against entities for non-compliance or unpaid dues in market-related matters.
