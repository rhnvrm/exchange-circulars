## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment of Demat Accounts and Mutual Fund Folios dated November 19, 2026, with AP No. 15892 of 2026 against Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited (PAN – AAACW2920N) under Recovery Certificate No. 9292 of 2026. This enforcement action is taken to recover outstanding dues and penalties in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9292 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited (PAN – AAACW2920N).
- AP No.: 15892 of 2026.
- Assets attached: Demat Accounts and Mutual Fund Folios.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to statutory recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 and associated regulations for the realization of dues and penalties from defaulting trading members and brokers.

## Compliance Requirements

Depositories (NSDL, CDSL), mutual fund houses, and financial intermediaries are required to forthwith freeze the demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by the noticee, prohibiting any debits, transfers, or disposal of securities and units.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and asset attachment measures by SEBI to secure recovery of unpaid dues, ensuring market integrity and accountability of defaulting brokers.
