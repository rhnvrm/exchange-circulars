## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 26, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 520 of 2014 against Suresh Hanswal. This order forms part of ongoing recovery proceedings directed at enforcing monetary penalties imposed for synchronized trading and fraudulent market practices.

## Key Points

- Issued on November 26, 2026, by SEBI.
- Relates to Recovery Certificate No. 520 of 2014.
- Issued against Suresh Hanswal regarding past synchronized trading violations.
- Focuses on the execution and remittance of recovered dues.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement action enforcing prior adjudicatory penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals subject to recovery certificates must comply with remittance directions and clear outstanding statutory dues.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 26, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Reference: 2014 (RC No. 520 of 2014)

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual enforcement action with no material impact on general market operations or listed company valuations.
