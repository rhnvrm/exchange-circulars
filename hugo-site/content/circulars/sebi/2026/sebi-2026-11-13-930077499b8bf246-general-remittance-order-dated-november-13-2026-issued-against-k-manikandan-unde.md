## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against K Manikandan under Recovery Certificate No. 7698 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order directs the remittance of attached funds and assets towards the recovery of dues and penalties ordered against the defaulter.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer on November 13, 2026.
- Defaulter: K Manikandan.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No.: 7698 of 2024.
- Underlying Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of outstanding penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees holding accounts or assets of the defaulter are required to remit the attached amounts and assets to SEBI as directed in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI to enforce compliance and collect dues from defaulters involved in past market violations.
