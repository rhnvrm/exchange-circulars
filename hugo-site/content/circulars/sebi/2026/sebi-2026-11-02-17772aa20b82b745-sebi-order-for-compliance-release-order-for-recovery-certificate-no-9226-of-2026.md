## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an 'Order for Compliance - Release Order' dated November 2, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9226 of 2026 against Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (PAN: ESZPK4415E). This release order concludes the recovery proceedings and releases the previously attached accounts and assets following the fulfillment of dues related to past trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer on November 2, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9226 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (PAN: ESZPK4415E).
- Scrip Involved: ANI Integrated Services Limited (Ticker: AISL).
- Action: Release of attached bank and demat accounts following satisfaction of penalty and recovery dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure of enforcement and attachment actions pursuant to the settlement or recovery of dues under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depository participants are notified to lift freezes on the accounts and assets of Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash previously attached under the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Indicates successful resolution and recovery closure for the specified individual, removing operating restrictions on the released accounts without broader market impact.
