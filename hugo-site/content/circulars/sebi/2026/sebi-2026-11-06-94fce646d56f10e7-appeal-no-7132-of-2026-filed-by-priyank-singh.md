## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7132 of 2026 filed by Shri Priyank Singh under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005, against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant had sought aggregate broker-wise statistics regarding IPO applications, rejections, allotments, and equity shares bid/allotted for specific IPOs (SS Retail Limited, Rentomojo Limited, Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited, and ESDS Software Solution Limited) across major brokers (Groww, Zerodha, Angel One, ICICI Securities, Upstox, Kotak Securities, and HDFC Securities). SEBI's Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply that the requested information is not maintained by SEBI, reiterating that public authorities are only required to provide information available and existing in their records under the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought aggregate statistical information on IPO bids, valid/rejected applications, allotments, and share quantities categorized by broker and IPO.
- IPOs specified: SS Retail Limited, Rentomojo Limited, Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited, and ESDS Software Solution Limited.
- Brokers specified: Groww, Zerodha, Angel One, ICICI Securities, Upstox, Kotak Securities, and HDFC Securities.
- SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority stated that the requested aggregate broker-wise data is not maintained by SEBI.
- Citing the Supreme Court judgment in *Central Board of Secondary Education & Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors.*, the Appellate Authority noted that the RTI Act only provides access to information that is available and existing in the records of a public authority.
