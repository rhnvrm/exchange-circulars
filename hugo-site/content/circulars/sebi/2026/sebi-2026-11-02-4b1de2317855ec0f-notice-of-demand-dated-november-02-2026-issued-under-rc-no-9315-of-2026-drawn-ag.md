## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated November 02, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9315 of 2026 drawn against Jalaj Agarwal (PAN: AUDPA0226H). This action pertains to recovery proceedings in the matter of stock recommendation tips in the scrip of Unison Metals Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under RC No. 9315 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Jalaj Agarwal (PAN: AUDPA0226H).
- Matter: Stock recommendation tips in the scrip of Unison Metals Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues arising from irregular stock recommendation practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulter and associated entities must comply with the demand notice by paying the specified dues immediately, failing which further coercive recovery measures including attachment of bank accounts, properties, and arrest may follow.

## Important Dates

- Date of Demand Notice: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement against individuals engaging in illegal stock recommendation tips and failing to pay levied penalties.
