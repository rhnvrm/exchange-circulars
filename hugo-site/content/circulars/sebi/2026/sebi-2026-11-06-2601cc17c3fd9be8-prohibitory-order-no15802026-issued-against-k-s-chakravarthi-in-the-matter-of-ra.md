## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1580/2026 on November 6, 2026, against K. S. Chakravarthi under Recovery Certificate Nos. 2826 of 2020 and 2827 of 2020 in the matter of Ranklin Solutions Ltd. This order enforces recovery of outstanding dues/penalties previously imposed for regulatory and insider trading violations.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order No.: 1580/2026
- Date of Order: November 6, 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: K. S. Chakravarthi
- Matter: Ranklin Solutions Ltd.
- Recovery Certificates: No. 2826 of 2020 and No. 2827 of 2020
- Action: Attachment and prohibition against transfer, alienation, or creation of charge on movable or immovable properties and bank/demat accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of penalties imposed due to non-compliance with insider trading regulations and disclosure norms.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and asset holders are instructed to freeze accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee and prohibit any unauthorized transfers or debits.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement by SEBI to recover dues from defaulters involved in past market violations associated with Ranklin Solutions Ltd.
