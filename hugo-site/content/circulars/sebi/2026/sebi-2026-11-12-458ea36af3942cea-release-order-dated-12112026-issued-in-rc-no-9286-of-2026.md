## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order dated November 12, 2026, revoking the attachment of bank accounts/lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios belonging to Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F) under Recovery Certificate No. 9286 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Full and final settlement amount of Rs. 5,36,000/- (exceeding the original certificate demand of Rs. 5,26,000/- inclusive of interest/costs) has been recovered by SEBI.
- All banks, mutual funds, NSDL, and CDSL are directed to release the attached bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios of the defaulter.
- The order is issued without prejudice to any other order of attachment or freeze by competent authorities under any other law.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks, mutual funds, NSDL, and CDSL must release the attached accounts and folios of the specified defaulter.
- No notification is required if the defaulter holds no accounts or folios with the institution.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational release of individual accounts upon full recovery settlement; no broader market impact.
