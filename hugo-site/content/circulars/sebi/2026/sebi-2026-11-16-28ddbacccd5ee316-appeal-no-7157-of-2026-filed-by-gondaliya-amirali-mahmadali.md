## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7157 of 2026 filed by Gondaliya Amirali Mahmadali against CPIO, SEBI, concerning grievances related to order placement failures via broker Angel One during Offer for Sale (OFS) issues for LIC and Hindustan Copper, alongside queries regarding broker business loans and partners. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance that grievances do not qualify as information under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act and advised utilizing the SCORES portal for grievance redressal.

## Key Points

- The appellant raised grievances regarding waiting times and order placement failures while attempting to apply for Offer for Sale (OFS) of LIC and Hindustan Copper through broker Angel One.
- The appellant questioned the basis of broker licensing and sought details regarding business loans and partners associated with the broker.
- The CPIO stated that matters concerning grievance resolution do not fall under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Investors were advised to lodge complaints against SEBI registered intermediaries on the SCORES website.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; reinforces the statutory distinction between information requests under the RTI Act and grievance redressal mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries must handle investor grievances through established redressal mechanisms such as SCORES.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: October 01, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct market or regulatory impact; affirms existing procedures for investor grievance handling.
