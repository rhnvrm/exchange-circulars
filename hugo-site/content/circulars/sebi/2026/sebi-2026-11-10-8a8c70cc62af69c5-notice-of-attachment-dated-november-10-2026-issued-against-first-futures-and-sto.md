## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7469 of 2023 against First Futures and Stocks Private Limited in the matter of First Futures and Stocks Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 7469 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: First Futures and Stocks Private Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- Date of attachment notice: November 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities are required to comply with the attachment orders and freeze accounts or assets as specified in the notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: November 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's active enforcement and recovery mechanism against defaulting entities and intermediaries to protect market integrity.
