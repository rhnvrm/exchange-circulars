## Summary

SEBI Chairman Shri Tuhin Kanta Pandey inaugurated the 'Bharat Ka Share Bazaar' pavilion at the 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) 2026 held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. Set up in association with major market institutions including NSE, BSE, MCX, MSEI, NCDEX, CDSL, NSDL, and AMFI, the pavilion showcases India's securities market legacy and promotes investor awareness under the theme 'Vikas aur Virasat'.

## Key Points

- The 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) is held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, from November 14–27, 2026, under the theme "Vikas aur Virasat".
- SEBI, alongside major market infrastructure institutions and associations (NSE, BSE, MCX, MSEI, NCDEX, CDSL, NSDL, KFin Technologies, CAMS, OBPP Association, CPAI, IRA, BIA, AMFI, and NISM), established the "Bharat Ka Share Bazaar" pavilion in Hall No. 4.
- The pavilion was inaugurated on November 14, 2026, by Shri Tuhin Kanta Pandey, Chairman, SEBI.
- The initiative highlights SEBI's campaign "Samajh Se Investing Simple" under Project Jagrook and the "Check, Validate and Verify" safety message.
- Features an AI Zone and an Investor Facilitation Camp (IFC), along with quizzes and interactive games to caution the public against illegal money mobilisation schemes and fraudulent social media investment offers.
