## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated November 12, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9324 of 2026 against Mr. Rushabh Pradeep Shroff for a total sum of Rs. 2,07,000/-. This includes a penalty of Rs. 2,00,000/- imposed by the Adjudicating Officer vide Order dated August 31, 2026, in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited, along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9324 of 2026 under Section 28A of SEBI Act, 1992 and Income-tax Act, 1961.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Mr. Rushabh Pradeep Shroff.
- Total Dues: Rs. 2,07,000/- (comprising Rs. 2,00,000 penalty, Rs. 6,000 interest from September to November 2026, and Rs. 1,000 recovery cost).
- Underlying Matter: Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited (Adjudication Order No. Order/JS/VC/2026-27/32690-32694 dated August 31, 2026).
- Payment terms: Within 15 days of receipt of notice via EFT/NEFT/RTGS or SEBI portal.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee is directed to pay the total amount of Rs. 2,07,000/- within 15 days of receipt of the notice. Failure to pay will result in coercive recovery measures including attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, bank account attachments, or arrest and detention. Noticee is restricted from transferring or dealing with any property without permission.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 12, 2026
- Original Adjudication Order Date: August 31, 2026
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of notice

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery actions by SEBI against individuals failing to pay penalties imposed in connection with listed company proceedings.
