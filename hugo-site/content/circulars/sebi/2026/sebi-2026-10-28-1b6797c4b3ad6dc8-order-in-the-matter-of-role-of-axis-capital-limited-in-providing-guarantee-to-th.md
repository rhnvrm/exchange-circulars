## Summary

SEBI issued an order examining the role of Axis Capital Limited (ACL) as a sale arranger and provider of guarantees/underwriting commitments for Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) amounting to INR 260 crore issued by Sojo Infotel Pvt. Ltd. in March 2021. The inspection identified non-compliances with SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations and debt listing norms.

## Key Points

- Axis Capital Limited acted as the Sale Arranger for Sojo Infotel Pvt. Ltd.'s NCD issue of INR 260 crore on March 25, 2021.
- Subscribed by Aditya Birla Finance, Sun TV Network, and Shriram Foundry.
- Sojo exercised a Call Option on September 13, 2023, which was not fully honored, leaving a balance of INR 174 crore outstanding and triggering an 'Event of Default'.
- Axis Capital deposited INR 166.84 crore in the NCD Escrow Account on March 19, 2024, to repay outstanding NCDs and interest under their arrangement, charging a fee of approximately INR 33 crore.
- SEBI inspection initiated on March 13, 2024, found non-compliances of MB Regulations regarding underwriting and guarantee commitments.

## Regulatory Changes

Strict enforcement of SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations, 1992, and debt securities issuance and listing regulations concerning informal guarantees, sale arrangements, and underwriting commitments.

## Compliance Requirements

Merchant bankers and intermediaries must strictly adhere to regulatory boundaries regarding guarantees, underwriting obligations, and transparent financial disclosures without engaging in prohibited risk-bearing financial arrangements.

## Important Dates

- Issue Date / Allotment Date: March 25, 2021
- Default / Asset Sale Event: September 25, 2023
- ACL Deposit in Escrow: March 19, 2024
- SEBI Inspection: March 13, 2024
- Show Cause Notice: December 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory scrutiny on merchant bankers participating in complex structured debt transactions, guarantees, and put/call option arrangements.
