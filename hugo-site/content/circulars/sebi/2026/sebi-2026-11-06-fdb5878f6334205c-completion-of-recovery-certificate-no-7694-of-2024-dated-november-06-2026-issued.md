## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 7694 of 2024 dated November 06, 2026, issued to Sathish Kumar N in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This signifies the successful satisfaction and formal conclusion of recovery proceedings against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Completion of Recovery Certificate No. 7694 of 2024.
- Issued on November 06, 2026.
- Noticee/Defaulter: Sathish Kumar N.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Formal conclusion of recovery and realization of dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 concluded upon full recovery of dues, resulting in the lifting of asset attachments and restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are notified of the completion of recovery and release of attached accounts or properties of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Completion/Order: November 06, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 7694 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Marks the closure of enforcement recovery action for this individual, with minimal direct impact on active market operations.
