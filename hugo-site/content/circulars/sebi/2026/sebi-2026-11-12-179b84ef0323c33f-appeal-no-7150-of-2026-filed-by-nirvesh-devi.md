## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7150 of 2026 filed by Nirvesh Devi under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought land details and refunds regarding investments made in PACL India Limited. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that asset disposal and investor refunds are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee pursuant to Supreme Court orders.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information, land details, and refund status regarding investments in PACL India Limited.
- CPIO stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and provided links to public notices, press releases, and status reports on the SEBI website.
- Appellate Authority cited Central Information Commission (CIC) precedent (Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI) affirming that CPIO cannot invent information not in its possession.
- Clarified that the responsibility for property disposal and investor repayment is entrusted to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee under Supreme Court orders dated February 2, 2016.
- Appeal dismissed as devoid of merit.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; order reiterates established jurisdiction of the Justice R.M. Lodha Committee for PACL matters.
