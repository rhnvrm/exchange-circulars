## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against Raja P under Recovery Certificate No. 7704 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order directs banks, financial institutions, and custodians to remit funds and assets held on behalf of the noticee to SEBI towards the realization of dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Order Type: General Remittance Order pursuant to recovery proceedings.
- Date of Order: November 13, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No.: 7704 of 2024.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Raja P.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of unpaid penalties, dues, and disgorgement amounts ordered against defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and intermediaries holding accounts or assets of Raja P are required to comply with the General Remittance Order by remitting specified funds and freezing further unauthorized withdrawals or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Issuance: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanism to execute recovery certificates and recover dues from defaulting entities and individuals in market manipulation cases such as Arvind Remedies Ltd.
