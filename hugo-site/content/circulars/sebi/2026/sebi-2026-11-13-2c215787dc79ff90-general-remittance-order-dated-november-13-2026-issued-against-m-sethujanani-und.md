## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against M Sethujanani under Recovery Certificate No. 7709 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to collect outstanding penalties and dues imposed due to financial irregularities and market violations.

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** M Sethujanani
- **Regulatory Body:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- **Matter:** Arvind Remedies Ltd. (RC No. 7709 of 2024)
- **Action Type:** General Remittance Order for recovery of unpaid dues and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures implemented pursuant to final orders against entities involved in the Arvind Remedies Ltd. manipulation case.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities, banks, or garnishees holding funds or assets on behalf of the noticee must comply with the general remittance order by remitting the specified dues to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Recovery Certificate Number:** RC No. 7709 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is restricted to the targeted noticee and associated accounts/assets under recovery proceedings, with no broad market implications.
