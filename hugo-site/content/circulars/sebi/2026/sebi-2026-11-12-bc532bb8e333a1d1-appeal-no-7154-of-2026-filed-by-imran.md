## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7154 of 2026 filed by Imran under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought information regarding the procedure and feasibility of correcting an inadvertent 'Yes' selection in the 'Land allotted' field of his PACL Limited refund claim application. SEBI's CPIO had informed the appellant that the requested information was not available with SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on correcting the 'Land allotted' field from 'Yes' to 'No' for his PACL refund claim.
- The CPIO responded that the information sought was not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to public notices, press releases, and FAQs on the SEBI website.
- The appellant filed an appeal alleging incomplete or misleading information was provided.
- The appellate authority upheld the response based on established RTI principles regarding non-availability of records.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an individual RTI appellate order.
