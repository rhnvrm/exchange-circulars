## Summary

SEBI has issued an exemption order under Section 11(1) and 11(2)(h) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 11(5) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 regarding the proposed direct and indirect acquisition of shares and voting rights in Emmbi Industries Limited by Rinku Appalwar Business Trust and Makrand Appalwar Business Trust.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Emmbi Industries Limited (listed on BSE and NSE).
- **Acquirers:** Rinku Appalwar Business Trust and Makrand Appalwar Business Trust, represented by trustees Mrs. Rinku Appalwar and Mr. Makrand Appalwar.
- **Exemption Sought:** Exemption from the applicability of Regulations 3(2) and 4 read with Regulation 5 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- **Promoter Shareholding:** Total promoter shareholding stands at 62.95% as of March 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is a case-specific exemption order under the SAST Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Acquirers must comply with the conditions and disclosures stipulated in the SEBI exemption order and applicable provisions of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: May 05, 2026 (with subsequent communications up to September 18, 2026).
- Order Date: November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates internal restructuring of promoter shareholding through business trusts without triggering mandatory open offer obligations under the SAST Regulations.
