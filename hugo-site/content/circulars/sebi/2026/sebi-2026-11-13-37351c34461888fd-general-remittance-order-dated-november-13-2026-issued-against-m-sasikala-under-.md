## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 7699 of 2024 against M. Sasikala in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd., directing remittance of attached funds towards dues.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 7699 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: M. Sasikala.
- Matter: Recovery proceedings and remittance of attached funds in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the remittance order by transferring attached funds to SEBI's recovery account.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in irregularities in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
