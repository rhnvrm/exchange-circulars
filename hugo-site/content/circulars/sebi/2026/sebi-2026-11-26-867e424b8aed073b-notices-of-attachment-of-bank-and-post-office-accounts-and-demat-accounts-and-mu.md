## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15901 and 15902 of 2026) dated November 26, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026, against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal (PAN: AACPD0426F) in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited. The notices order the immediate attachment and freezing of all bank accounts, post office savings accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios held by the noticee.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026 drawn up against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal (PAN: AACPD0426F).
- Attachment Proceedings AP Nos. 15901 and 15902 of 2026 issued on November 26, 2026.
- Covers all commercial/cooperative bank accounts, post office savings accounts, demat accounts (NSDL/CDSL), and mutual fund folios across all AMCs.
- Issued under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, empowering SEBI Recovery Officers to attach movable and immovable assets, bank accounts, securities, and mutual fund units for non-payment of regulatory dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, depository participants, and mutual fund RTAs/AMCs must immediately freeze all accounts, demat holdings, and mutual fund folios standing in the name of Satyen Bhupendra Dalal. No debit, transfer, or withdrawal is permitted without prior specific written approval from SEBI, and institutions must report balance and status details to the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 26, 2026.
- Effective Date: Immediate upon receipt by financial institutions and intermediaries.

## Impact Assessment

Immediate asset freeze for Satyen Bhupendra Dalal across all banking, depository, and mutual fund channels to enforce recovery of unpaid dues in connection with Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.
