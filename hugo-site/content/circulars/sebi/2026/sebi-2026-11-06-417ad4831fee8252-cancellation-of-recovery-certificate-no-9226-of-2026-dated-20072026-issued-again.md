## Summary

SEBI has officially cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9226 of 2026 (originally dated July 20, 2026) issued against Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash in the matter of trading in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9226 was issued on July 20, 2026, pursuant to enforcement or penalty proceedings.
- The cancellation order was issued on November 6, 2026, following settlement or compliance with dues by the noticee.
- Affected individual: Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (PAN: ESZPK4415E).
- Related scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an administrative cancellation of an enforcement recovery certificate.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance actions are required from market participants regarding this specific cancellation.

## Important Dates

- Original Certificate Date: July 20, 2026
- Cancellation Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This administrative action concludes recovery proceedings for the specified individual and scrip, resulting in no broader market or operational impact.
