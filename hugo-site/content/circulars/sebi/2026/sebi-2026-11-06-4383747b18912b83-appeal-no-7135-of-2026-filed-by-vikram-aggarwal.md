## Summary

Vikram Aggarwal filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information sought on SCORES complaint (No. SEBIE MH25 MUMB 024651 1) concerning HDFC Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought 10 procedural and system records regarding the handling, routing, action history, and closure of SCORES complaint SEBIE MH25 MUMB 024651 1.
- The CPIO responded to the application on October 07, 2026, leading to the current appeal filed on October 08, 2026.
- The appeal requests specific details including action history, ATR submission by HDFC Bank, assignment to BSE Limited, and reasons for closure.
