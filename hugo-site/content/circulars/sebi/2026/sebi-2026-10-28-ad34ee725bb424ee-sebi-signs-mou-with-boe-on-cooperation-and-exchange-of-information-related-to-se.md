## Summary

SEBI and the Bank of England (BoE) have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) concerning cooperation and the exchange of information regarding SEBI-recognised Clearing Corporations (CCs) and Central Counterparties (CCPs).

## Key Points

- Establishes a framework for BoE to rely on SEBI's regulatory and supervisory activities while safeguarding UK financial stability.
- Enables cooperation between SEBI and BoE regarding Central Counterparties in accordance with respective laws and regulations.
- Highlights the significance of cross-border regulatory cooperation in international clearing activities.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes or compliance burdens are imposed on market participants; the MoU formalizes cross-border supervisory cooperation between regulatory bodies.

## Compliance Requirements

None applicable to listed entities or intermediaries at this time.

## Important Dates

- **Date of MoU Signing:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances international trust, regulatory oversight coordination, and cross-border facilitation of clearing activities between India and the United Kingdom.
