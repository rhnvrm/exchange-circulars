## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order for Recovery Certificate No. 7413 of 2023 drawn against Yadunath Singh Thakur, proprietor of The Apex Global. This order concludes or releases attachment/recovery proceedings related to dues owed pursuant to SEBI's earlier enforcement orders against unregistered investment advisory activities.

## Key Points

- SEBI Recovery Proceedings: Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 7413 of 2023.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Yadunath Singh Thakur, proprietor of The Apex Global.
- Matter: Settlement or release of recovery/attachment proceedings regarding dues and penalties.
- Issuing Authority: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties imposed for unauthorized investment advisory services.

## Compliance Requirements

Release of frozen bank accounts, properties, or assets of the noticee upon satisfaction of recovery certificate requirements or completion of proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the execution and conclusion of SEBI recovery proceedings against entities involved in unauthorized market activities.
