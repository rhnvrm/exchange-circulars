## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Notice of Attachment (AP No. 15893 of 2026) dated November 19, 2026, against Paresh Mulji Kariya (PAN: AAEPK0469M) under Recovery Certificate No. 9295 of 2026 in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Issued by SEBI Recovery Officer under RC No. 9295 of 2026.
- Directs banks to attach and freeze bank accounts, including lockers, held by Paresh Mulji Kariya.
- Part of ongoing enforcement and recovery proceedings relating to Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal enforcement action directing banking institutions to prohibit debit operations in accounts held by the noticee while allowing credit operations as per recovery guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks and financial institutions must immediately freeze accounts and lockers associated with the specified PAN and details.
- Noticee is prohibited from transferring, charging, or otherwise dealing with funds in the attached accounts.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts financial liquidity of the noticee to facilitate recovery of dues owed pursuant to SEBI orders regarding Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
