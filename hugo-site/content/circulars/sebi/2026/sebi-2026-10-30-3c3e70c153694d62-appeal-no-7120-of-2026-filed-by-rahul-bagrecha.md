## Summary

This order under the Right to Information Act, 2005 disposes of Appeal No. 7120 of 2026 filed by Rahul Bagrecha against the CPIO, SEBI. The appeal relates to queries concerning M/s Aurostar Investment Advisory Private Limited, the 100% deposit requirement under ODR/arbitration, Section 34 challenges under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and jurisdiction/seat of arbitration.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought clarification on whether challenging an arbitral award under Section 34 suspends the mandatory 100% deposit requirement with MII/ODR mechanisms.
- Inquiries were made regarding time limits for deposit and reporting non-compliance, seat of arbitration rules based on investor KYC residence, and regulatory implications of forum circumvention.
- The CPIO directed the appellant to the Master Circular for Online Resolution of Disputes in the Indian Securities Market and noted that certain case-specific records regarding Aurostar Investment Advisory were not available.
