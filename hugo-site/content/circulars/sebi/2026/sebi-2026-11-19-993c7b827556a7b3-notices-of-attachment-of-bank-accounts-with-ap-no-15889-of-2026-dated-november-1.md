## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 19, 2026, with Attachment Proceedings (AP) No. 15889 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9293 of 2026 against Om Sri Sai Investments (PAN: AADFO8242K) in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9293 of 2026 (AP No. 15889 of 2026).
- Defaulter / Noticee: Om Sri Sai Investments (PAN – AADFO8242K).
- Matter: Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, and arrears.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze bank accounts and assets of Om Sri Sai Investments as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any unauthorized transfers or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in defaults concerning Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
