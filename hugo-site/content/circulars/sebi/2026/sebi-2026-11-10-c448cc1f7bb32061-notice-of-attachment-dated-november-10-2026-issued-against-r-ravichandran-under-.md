## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 6735 of 2023 drawn against R Ravichandran. This action relates to recovery proceedings and enforcement actions in the matter of Teledata Technology Solutions Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 6735 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: R Ravichandran.
- Matter: Recovery proceedings in Teledata Technology Solutions Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in regulatory violations related to Teledata Technology Solutions Limited.
