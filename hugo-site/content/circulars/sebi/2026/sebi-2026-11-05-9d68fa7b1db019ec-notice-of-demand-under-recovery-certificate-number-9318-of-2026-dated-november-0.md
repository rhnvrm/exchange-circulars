## Summary

On November 5, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9318 of 2026 to Priyanka Jain (PAN: BAZPJ9596E), the defaulter, in the matter of Vedic Ayurveda Ltd (formerly known as KD Leisures Ltd.). This enforcement action was initiated due to the failure to remit penalties imposed via adjudication proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: 9318 of 2026
- Date of Notice: November 05, 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Priyanka Jain (PAN: BAZPJ9596E)
- Company Involved: Vedic Ayurveda Ltd (formerly known as KD Leisures Ltd.)
- Underlying Cause: Failure to pay penalties imposed under SEBI Adjudication Orders for violations of securities laws.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings are executed pursuant to Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, empowering SEBI Recovery Officers to recover dues through asset attachment, bank account freezes, and other statutory mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and associated entities are required to immediately remit all outstanding dues, including principal penalties, applicable interest, and recovery costs.

## Important Dates

- November 05, 2026: Issuance date of the Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9318 of 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals failing to comply with monetary penalties and adjudication orders.
