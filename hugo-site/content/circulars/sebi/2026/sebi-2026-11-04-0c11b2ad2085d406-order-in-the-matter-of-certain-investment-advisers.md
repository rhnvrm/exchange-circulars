## Summary

SEBI has issued an order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against eight Investment Advisers for non-payment of renewal fees required under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- Noticees include Karvy Investment Advisory Services Limited, Sagacis Investment Advisor, Tathastu Investment Adviser Private Limited, Quant Capital Private Limited, Arkanis Capital, Ira Wealth Advisor, IDFC Alternatives Limited, and Indianivesh Investment Managers Pvt. Ltd.
- The entities failed to pay renewal fees every five years as mandated by Clause 3 of the Second Schedule of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.
- Summary proceedings were initiated to determine why certificates of registration should not be suspended or cancelled.
- Noticees were directed to submit replies with documentary evidence within 21 days of receiving the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

Strict enforcement of registration renewal fee obligations for registered Investment Advisers under the Intermediaries Regulations and Investment Advisers Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Registered Investment Advisers must pay renewal fees every five years within three months prior to the expiry of the fee period. Noticees must file their replies within the 21-day stipulated timeline.

## Important Dates

- Notice issuance: November 2026
- Submission deadline: 21 days from receipt of notice

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance for investment advisers, signaling potential cancellation or suspension of registrations for defaulting entities.
