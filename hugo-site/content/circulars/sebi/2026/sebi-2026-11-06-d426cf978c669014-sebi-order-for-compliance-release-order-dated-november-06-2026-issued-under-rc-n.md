## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 6, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9267 of 2026 drawn against S S TRADERS (Prop. Mr. Marvadi Parsotambhai) in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited. This order cancels the earlier recovery/attachment proceedings against the entity.

## Key Points

- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 9267 of 2026
- Entity Involved: S S TRADERS (Prop. Mr. Marvadi Parsotambhai)
- Target Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL)
- Action: Release Order / Cancellation of Recovery Certificate

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal release and withdrawal of recovery/attachment measures initiated under RC No. 9267 of 2026 against S S TRADERS.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance or recovery action required under this specific recovery certificate for S S TRADERS.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational Impact: Minimal and isolated to the named entity. Concludes recovery proceedings without broader market impact on ANI Integrated Services Limited.
