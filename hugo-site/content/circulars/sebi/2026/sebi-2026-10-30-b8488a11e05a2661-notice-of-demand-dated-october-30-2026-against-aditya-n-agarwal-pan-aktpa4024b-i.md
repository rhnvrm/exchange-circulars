## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9310 of 2026 dated October 30, 2026, against Aditya N Agarwal (PAN: AKTPA4024B) in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited. A total sum of Rs. 10,41,000/- is demanded, comprising a penalty of Rs. 10,00,000/-, interest of Rs. 40,000/-, and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000/-.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Issued under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Section 413 of the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Rule 225(2) of the Income-Tax Rules, 2026.
- **Entity Details:** Aditya N Agarwal, residing at 4, Shrinath Colony, Bhairavnath Road, Near Best High School Maninagar, Ahmedabad, Gujarat-380008 (PAN: AKTPA4024B).
- **Underlying Matter:** Penalty imposed by Whole Time Member (WTM) vide Order No. WTM/AS/CFD/CFD-SEC-4/32528/2026-27 dated 30.07.2026 in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited.
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 10,41,000/-.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific individual.

## Compliance Requirements

- The notice requires payment of the total amount within 15 days of receipt via direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS, online payment via the SEBI SI Portal, or the Recovery Payment module.
- Failure to pay will result in recovery measures including attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, attachment of bank accounts, and arrest/detention in prison.
- Any transfer of property or monies to spouse or minor children on or after July 30, 2026, is deemed void for recovery purposes.
- E-payment confirmations must be reported to the Recovery Officer with specific transaction details.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Original Order Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Payment Deadline:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual enforcement proceeding under SEBI's recovery mechanism and has no broader market or systemic impact.
