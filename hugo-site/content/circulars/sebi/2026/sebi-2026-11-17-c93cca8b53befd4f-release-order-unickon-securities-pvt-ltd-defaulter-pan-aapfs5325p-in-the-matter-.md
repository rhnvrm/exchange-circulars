## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 17, 2026, in the matter of Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025 concerning Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P). This order signifies the formal completion and conclusion of recovery proceedings and lifting of related attachments under this specific certificate.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued by SEBI under Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- PAN: AAPFS5325P.
- Date of Release Order: November 17, 2026.
- Signifies the successful conclusion of recovery actions and release of assets under this certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure and withdrawal of recovery/attachment measures under the provisions of the SEBI Act, 1992 for Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are notified that recovery proceedings under this specific certificate are completed and corresponding asset/bank account restrictions are released.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 17, 2026
- Original Recovery Certificate Date: May 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides legal clarity and finality regarding the recovery proceedings initiated against Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. under Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025.
