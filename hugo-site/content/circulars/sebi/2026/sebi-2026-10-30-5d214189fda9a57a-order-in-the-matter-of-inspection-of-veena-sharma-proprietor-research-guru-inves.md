## Summary

SEBI issued a final order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 27 of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against Ms. Veena Sharma, Proprietor of Research Guru (Investment Adviser, SEBI Reg No: INA000005507), following a surprise onsite inspection conducted in July 2022. The inspection uncovered multiple regulatory violations including non-compliance with employee qualification norms, failure to conduct proper risk profiling and suitability assessments, failure to maintain adequate records, collection of fees in unauthorized bank accounts, and misrepresentation to clients.

## Key Points

- Surprise onsite inspection conducted by SEBI from July 14 to July 18, 2022 covering the period from April 01, 2021 onwards.
- Employees and representatives of Research Guru failed to comply with the mandated qualification and certification requirements under the IA Regulations, 2013.
- Deficiencies identified in performing proper risk profiling, evaluating client suitability, maintaining comprehensive records, and submitting required information to SEBI.
- Violation of regulatory mandates prohibiting the collection of advisory fees in bank accounts other than the designated business bank account of the Investment Adviser.
- Failure to discharge fiduciary duties towards clients and instances of client misrepresentation.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and remedial orders issued pursuant to Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 27 of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Compliance Requirements

Registered Investment Advisers must strictly adhere to employee qualification norms, perform rigorous risk profiling and client suitability assessments, maintain meticulous records of all advisory interactions, route all fee collections strictly through designated business bank accounts, and uphold fiduciary responsibilities towards clients at all times.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2021 onwards
- Onsite Inspection Dates: July 14, 2022 - July 18, 2022
- Post-Enquiry SCN Date: February 18, 2026
- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous regulatory oversight on Investment Advisers, emphasizing strict compliance with operational standards, fee segregation, record-keeping, and investor protection mandates.
