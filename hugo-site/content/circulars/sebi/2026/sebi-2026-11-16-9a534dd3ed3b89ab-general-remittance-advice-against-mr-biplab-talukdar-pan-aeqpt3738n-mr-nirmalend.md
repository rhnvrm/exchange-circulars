## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 8700 of 2025 against multiple defaulters including Mr. Biplab Talukdar, Mr. Nirmalendu Bhowmik, Mr. Pintu Roy, Mr. Santanu Chakraborty, and Mr. Vivek Kumar in the matter of M/s Basil International Limited.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Advice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8700 of 2025.
- Named Defaulters: Mr. Biplab Talukdar (PAN: AEQPT3738N), Mr. Nirmalendu Bhowmik (PAN: ASFPB6456J), Mr. Pintu Roy (PAN: AKSPR6350A), Mr. Santanu Chakraborty (PAN: AEGPC4802C), and Mr. Vivek Kumar (PAN: ADNPK9149E).
- Company in matter: M/s Basil International Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions and relevant entities are required to remit recovered amounts and comply with recovery officer directions regarding the attached assets and accounts of the defaulters.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in unauthorized fund mobilization and non-compliance with SEBI refund orders in M/s Basil International Limited.
