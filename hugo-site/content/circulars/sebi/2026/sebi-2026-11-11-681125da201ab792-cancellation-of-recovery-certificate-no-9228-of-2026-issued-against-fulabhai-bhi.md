## Summary

SEBI has cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 dated July 22, 2026, issued against Fulabhai Bhithor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q) for a sum of Rs. 2,13,000/-. The cancellation is enacted in view of the order of the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) dated November 4, 2026, in Appeal No. 389 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 was originally issued for non-payment of penalty imposed vide Adjudicating Officer Order No. Order/AK/DS/2025-26/32082–32109 dated February 12, 2026.
- The proceedings relate to trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Cancellation is executed under sub-section (1) of section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with section 413 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an administrative cancellation of a specific recovery certificate following an appellate tribunal ruling.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance action required regarding this specific recovery certificate as it stands cancelled.

## Important Dates

- **February 12, 2026**: Adjudicating Officer Order date.
- **July 22, 2026**: Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 issuance date.
- **November 4, 2026**: Hon'ble SAT Order date in Appeal No. 389 of 2026.
- **November 11, 2026**: Date of cancellation order by the Recovery Officer.

## Impact Assessment

- Individual enforcement action nullified in accordance with SAT directions; no broader market or systemic impact.
