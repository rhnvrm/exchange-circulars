## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026 against Lataben Narotambhai Rangi (PAN: AYRPR7467F) in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued for Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Lataben Narotambhai Rangi (PAN: AYRPR7467F).
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Official release/lifting of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following compliance or resolution of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant authorities, banks, and financial institutions are notified of the release of attachment/recovery proceedings against the individual.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Concludes recovery proceedings and lifts attachment restrictions for the specified individual in relation to ANI Integrated Services Limited.
