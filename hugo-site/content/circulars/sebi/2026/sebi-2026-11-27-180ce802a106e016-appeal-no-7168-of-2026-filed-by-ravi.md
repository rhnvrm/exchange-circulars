## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7168 of 2026 filed by Ravi under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought extensive information regarding regulatory frameworks for algorithmic trading, high-frequency trading (HFT), API-based trading, direct market access (DMA), co-location, and proximity hosting. SEBI's CPIO had responded that the requested information is publicly available on the SEBI website.

## Key Points

- The appeal challenged the CPIO's response regarding queries on algorithmic trading, HFT, API trading, DMA, CTCL, co-location, and proximity hosting.
- The appellant contended that incomplete or misleading information was provided.
- SEBI directed the appellant to publicly available resources, including SEBI SECC Regulations, 2018 and the SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy updates were introduced through this order; it reaffirms the public availability of existing regulatory frameworks governing algorithmic and high-frequency trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Information seekers must refer to public disclosures, master circulars, and regulations available on the SEBI website for details on algorithmic trading and market infrastructure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Appeal: October 29, 2026
- Application Date: September 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal regulatory or market impact as this is an individual RTI appeal order affirming existing public disclosures.
