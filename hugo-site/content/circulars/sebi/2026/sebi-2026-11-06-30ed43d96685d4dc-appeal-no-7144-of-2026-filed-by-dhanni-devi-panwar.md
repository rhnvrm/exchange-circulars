## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7144 of 2026 filed by Smt. Dhanni Devi Panwar under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought updates regarding her PACL refund claim status and document submission. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that all communications and processes regarding PACL refund status and asset disposal are managed exclusively by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Dhanni Devi Panwar
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- **Subject**: RTI appeal seeking status of PACL refund claim and document submission.
- **SEBI Stance**: Information sought is not held by SEBI; PACL refund operations are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- **Decision**: Appeal dismissed as lacking merit against SEBI, with advice to approach the Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; reiteration of jurisdictional responsibility regarding PACL investor refunds.
