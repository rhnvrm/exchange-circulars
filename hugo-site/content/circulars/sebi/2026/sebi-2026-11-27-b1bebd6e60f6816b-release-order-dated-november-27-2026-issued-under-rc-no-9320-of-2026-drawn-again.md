## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 27, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026 drawn against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited. This release order follows previous recovery and attachment proceedings initiated by SEBI regarding the scrip and IPO irregularities of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued on November 27, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9320 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Satyen Bhupendra Dalal (PAN: AACPD0426F).
- Matter: Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.
- Concludes recovery proceedings and lifts previous attachment orders.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and conclusion of recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and issuance of release upon settlement or completion.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are notified of the release of attachment on accounts and assets of the noticee as per the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 27, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: RC No. 9320 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Signals successful conclusion of recovery proceedings and lifting of attachment restrictions for the individual involved in the Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited matter.
