## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7143 of 2026 filed by Shri Ashok Kumar Saini under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information pertaining to KBCL India Limited and its Collective Investment Schemes (CIS).

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of SEBI orders, repayment/refund reports, investor claim records, winding-up reports, and recovery/attachment details relating to KBCL India Limited.
- The CPIO directed the appellant to the public circular dated April 4, 2019, for certified copies of orders, noted that refund processes have not yet been initiated by SEBI, and stated that certain records were not available.
- SEBI has initiated recovery proceedings vide Certificate No. 1011 of 2016 dated November 10, 2016, for a sum of ₹118.69 Crores against M/s KBCL India Ltd and its directors for non-compliance with order dated May 26, 2014.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; this is an appellate order under the RTI Act.
