## Summary

SEBI has issued a clarification regarding media reports that misinterpret the Consultation Paper on "Strengthening the Governance of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)" issued on November 20, 2026. SEBI clarified that the consultation paper does not propose any change to the maximum age limit of 65 years applicable to Managing Directors (MDs) of MIIs.

## Key Points

- SEBI consultation papers only include proposals for provisions where changes are intended.
- The maximum age limit for MDs of MIIs remains unchanged at 65 years.
- Proposals relating to MDs address only their term and tenure within a single MII and across MIIs.
- Existing PIDs, MDs, or EDs holding office when amendments commence will continue until their ongoing term ends.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes implemented yet; the circular is purely a clarification regarding ongoing public consultation proposals.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit comments and suggestions on the consultation paper until December 11, 2026, via the online public comments form on the SEBI website.

## Important Dates

- Consultation Paper Issued: November 20, 2026
- Clarification Issued: November 27, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal immediate impact as this is a clarification of existing proposals currently open for public feedback. Assures stakeholders that MD age limits remain unaffected.
