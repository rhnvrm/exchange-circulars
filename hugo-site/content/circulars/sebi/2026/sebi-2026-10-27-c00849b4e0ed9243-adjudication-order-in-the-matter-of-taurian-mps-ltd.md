## Summary

SEBI conducted an examination concerning the offer document filed by Taurian MPS Ltd. for its Initial Public Offer (IPO) and initiated adjudication proceedings against Taurian MPS Limited, its directors, and the merchant banker Gretex Corporate Services Limited for alleged disclosure violations under the ICDR Regulations, 2018.

## Key Points

- SEBI received a complaint regarding the IPO offer document of Taurian MPS Ltd.
- Noticee 1 (Taurian MPS Ltd.), Noticees 2 & 3 (Directors), and Noticee 4 (Gretex Corporate Services Limited, Merchant Banker) were issued SCNs under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act read with Rule 5 of Rules, 1995.
- Allegations involve inadequate disclosures or non-disclosures regarding promoter group / non-promoter group entities, such as the liquidation status of Taurian Iron and Steel Private Limited and risk factors related to past defaults of Taurian Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.
- Proceedings are being adjudicated under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of ICDR Regulations, 2018 (specifically Regulation 245 and Part A of Schedule VI) regarding full and adequate disclosures in offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and merchant bankers must ensure transparent, complete, and accurate disclosures in IPO offer documents, including liquidation status of non-promoter entities and risk factors for promoter group defaults.

## Important Dates

- Appointment of AO: July 09, 2026
- SCN Date: July 31, 2026
- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces strict regulatory scrutiny on merchant bankers and issuers regarding IPO disclosures and risk factor reporting under ICDR Regulations.
