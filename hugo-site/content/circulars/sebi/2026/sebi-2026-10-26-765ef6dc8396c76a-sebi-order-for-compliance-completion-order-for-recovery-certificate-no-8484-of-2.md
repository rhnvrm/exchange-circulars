## Summary

SEBI has issued a Completion Order dated October 26, 2026, for Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024 against Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMPGPA3864Q) in the matter of M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited - II. This marks the formal conclusion of the recovery proceedings initiated against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Completion Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMPGPA3864Q).
- Associated Matter: M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited - II.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and formal closure of recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Formal completion of recovery proceedings signifies that the specified recovery process for this certificate has been concluded by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Completion Order: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the conclusion of enforcement and recovery measures regarding the specified recovery certificate in the matter of Austral Coke and Projects Limited.
