## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 9194 of 2026 against Trdez Investment Private Limited (PAN: AAJCT5451G). This order marks the conclusion of recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI for the realization of dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Completion order issued for Recovery Certificate No. 9194 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Trdez Investment Private Limited.
- PAN: AAJCT5451G.
- Conclusion of recovery proceedings and release of attachment/dues realization measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure of enforcement action and withdrawal of recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial institutions and authorities are notified of the completion and closure of recovery proceedings against the entity.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative and regulatory update indicating successful settlement or conclusion of recovery proceedings against the specified entity.
