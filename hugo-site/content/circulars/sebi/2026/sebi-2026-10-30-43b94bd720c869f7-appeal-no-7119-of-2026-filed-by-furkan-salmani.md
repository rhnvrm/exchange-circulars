## Summary

This order addresses Appeal No. 7119 of 2026 filed by Shri Furkan Salmani under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding PACL Limited refund claim status and associated land allotment details.

## Key Points

- The appellant stated he invested Rs. 60,000 across two PACL Limited FD certificates.
- Refund applications on the official SEBI portal could not be processed because the system indicated land had been allotted against the certificates.
- The appellant requested certified details including Khasra/Survey numbers, village, tehsil, district, state, exact address, location map, total area, allotment date, allotment orders, and ownership documents.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or policy shifts were introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance obligations are imposed on market participants.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: August 18, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 07, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order is specific to an individual RTI applicant and has no direct financial market or systemic impact.
