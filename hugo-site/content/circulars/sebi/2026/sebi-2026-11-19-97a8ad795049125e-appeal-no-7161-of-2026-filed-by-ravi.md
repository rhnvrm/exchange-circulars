## Summary

Appeal No. 7161 of 2026 was filed by Ravi against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought information regarding the High Value Debt Listed Entity (HVDLE) status, outstanding non-convertible debt securities, and board composition/independent directors of Delhi International Airport Limited (DIAL) under Regulation 62C and Chapter VA of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information under RTI Act, 2005 concerning Delhi International Airport Limited (DIAL).
- DIAL operates Indira Gandhi International Airport as a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) entity with listed non-convertible debt securities exceeding ₹6,200 crore.
- Queries pertained to DIAL's HVDLE status under Regulation 62C of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 (effective January 22, 2026) and board composition regarding independent directors under Regulation 62D.
- The appeal challenges the CPIO's response regarding access to the requested regulatory records and filings.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced by this order; it relates to an RTI appeal adjudication regarding the interpretation and disclosure of HVDLE compliance for DIAL under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities with listed non-convertible debt securities exceeding ₹5,000 crore qualify as High Value Debt Listed Entities (HVDLEs) under Chapter VA of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and must comply with enhanced corporate governance and board composition norms.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 26, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 23, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 27, 2026
- Amendment Effective Date (LODR Reg 62C threshold): January 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The order clarifies procedural handling of RTI applications concerning corporate governance and HVDLE compliance status of entities with listed debt securities.
