## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 6147 of 2022 issued against The Apex Global and its proprietor, Yadunath Singh Thakur, in the matter of Covidh Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued Recovery Certificate No. 6147 of 2022 following previous adjudication orders and proceedings against The Apex Global and Yadunath Singh Thakur.
- The current order represents a release/completion order regarding the recovery proceedings.
- Involves matters related to Covidh Technologies Limited and regulatory compliance enforcement.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy changes; this is an enforcement and recovery release order specific to the named entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals named have fulfilled or been released from the specific recovery certificate obligations under Recovery Certificate No. 6147 of 2022.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 2026
- Recovery Certificate No. 6147 of 2022 release.

## Impact Assessment

Administrative resolution of recovery proceedings for the specified entities with negligible broader market impact.
