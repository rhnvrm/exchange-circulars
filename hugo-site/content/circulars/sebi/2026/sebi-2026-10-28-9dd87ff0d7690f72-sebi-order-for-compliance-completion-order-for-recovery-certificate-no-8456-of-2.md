## Summary

SEBI has issued a Completion Order dated October 28, 2026, for Recovery Certificate No. 8456 of 2024 drawn against Dollar Advisory and Financial Service (Proprietor: Mr. Ravi Prakash Mohta). This concludes the recovery proceedings initiated following earlier enforcement actions against the entity for violations of SEBI regulations as an Investment Adviser.

## Key Points

- Completion Order issued for Recovery Certificate No. 8456 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Dollar Advisory and Financial Service (Proprietor: Mr. Ravi Prakash Mohta).
- Matter: Finalization and completion of recovery proceedings / dues realization.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Conclusion of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, upon fulfillment/satisfaction of recovery certificate requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Lifting of restrictions/attachments associated with Recovery Certificate No. 8456 of 2024, if applicable, and formal closure of the recovery file for the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Completion Order: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the conclusion of enforcement and recovery measures under Recovery Certificate No. 8456 of 2024 against Dollar Advisory and Financial Service and its proprietor Mr. Ravi Prakash Mohta.
