## Summary

Amit Harlalka filed an RTI application seeking information regarding SEBI's monitoring, surveillance, and performance tracking of stock buy and sell recommendations given on television news channels such as Zee Business and CNBC Awaaz, along with applicable rules and disclosure requirements for TV experts and research analysts. SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority held that queries seeking clarifications or opinions do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while directing the appellant to relevant public domain regulations including the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and the Master Circular for Research Analysts.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on SEBI's monitoring mechanisms, success ratios, and performance tracking of stock recommendations on television channels.
- SEBI stated that queries seeking explanations, clarifications, or opinions cannot be construed as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- SEBI directed the appellant to Regulations 18 and 21 of the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and paragraphs 19 and 20 of Annexure I of Chapter VII of the Master Circular for Research Analysts dated February 6, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; reaffirms existing provisions under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and Master Circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Experts and research analysts providing recommendations on television must comply with mandatory disclosures, conflict of interest disclosures, and operational guidelines specified under SEBI Research Analyst regulations.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 23, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 15, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct market or operational impact; reinforces existing disclosure frameworks and clarifies the scope of information disclosable under the RTI Act regarding regulatory surveillance.
