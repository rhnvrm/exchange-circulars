## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order against EKI Energy Services Ltd. and its key executives, finding them guilty of reckless financial misrepresentation and diversion of funds. The investigation uncovered premature revenue recognition of ₹197.04 crore and fictitious expenses of ₹26.34 crore.

## Key Points

- SEBI imposed a total penalty of ₹88 lakh across eight entities and individuals.
- Prematurely recognized revenue totaling ₹197.04 crore during the first three quarters of FY 2022-23.
- Booked fictitious expenses of ₹26.34 crore during FY 2021-22 and FY 2022-23, resulting in profit understatement.
- Market debarments imposed: EKI Energy (2 months), MD Manish Kumar Dabkara (12 months), CFO Mohit Kumar Agarwal (8 months), and other officials.
- MD Manish Kumar Dabkara directed to take steps to recoup the diverted ₹26.34 crore back to the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions taken under SEBI regulations regarding fraudulent and unfair trade practices and disclosure norms for material financial misstatements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees must pay their respective monetary penalties within 45 days of the order.
- Management must comply with debarment orders and recoup diverted funds.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 30, 2026.
- Penalty Payment Deadline: Within 45 days of the order.

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces stringent regulatory oversight on financial disclosures and accounting integrity, signaling strict penalties for top management involved in fund diversion and misrepresentation.
