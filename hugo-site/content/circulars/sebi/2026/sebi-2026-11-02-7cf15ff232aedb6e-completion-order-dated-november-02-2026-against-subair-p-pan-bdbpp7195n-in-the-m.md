## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion order dated November 02, 2026, regarding Recovery Certificate No. 9306 of 2026 against Subair P (PAN: BDBPP7195N) in the matter of M/s Inditrade Capital Limited (formerly known as M/s JRG Securities Ltd.), following the full payment of dues.

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** Subair P (PAN: BDBPP7195N)
- **Entity:** M/s Inditrade Capital Limited (formerly M/s JRG Securities Ltd.)
- **Recovery Amount:** Rs. 97,000/- along with further interest, costs, charges, and expenses.
- **Underlying Order:** Adjudicating Officer Order No. EAD-9/SM/227/2018-19 dated 18.01.2019.
- **Action:** Certificate completed under Section 28A(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Section 413 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Rule 225(12) of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

None - Recovery proceedings successfully completed upon payment.

## Important Dates

- **Recovery Certificate Date:** 21.10.2026
- **Completion Order Date:** 02.11.2026

## Impact Assessment

The recovery proceedings against the individual have been successfully concluded with the settlement of dues, having no broader market impact.
