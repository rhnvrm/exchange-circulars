## Summary

SEBI has extended the deadline for all Regulated Entities (REs) to comply with Digital Accessibility Circulars under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Deadline extended to January 31, 2027, for the appointment of an IAAP Auditor, conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms, and remediating audit findings.
- Applies to all Recognised Stock Exchanges, Recognised Clearing Corporations, Registered Depositories, Registered Intermediaries, AMFI, APMI, and BSE Limited in its capacity as IAASB/RAASB.
- Extension granted based on representations received from Regulated Entities and a review of the current compliance status.
- All other provisions of the referenced circulars remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifies the compliance timeline previously mandated across circulars dated July 31, 2025, August 29, 2025, September 25, 2025, December 8, 2025, and July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete the appointment of an IAAP auditor, conduct digital platform accessibility audits, and remediate findings no later than January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026:** Issuance date of the circular.
- **January 31, 2027:** Extended deadline for IAAP auditor appointment, accessibility audit, and remediation.

## Impact Assessment

- Relieves operational pressure on regulated entities by providing adequate time to ensure robust digital accessibility compliance across their platforms in alignment with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
