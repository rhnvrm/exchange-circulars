The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 7696 of 2024 issued against S Nagarajan (PAN: ARHPN2907P) in the matter of Arvind Remedies Limited.

- The release order signifies the conclusion or settlement of recovery proceedings for this certificate.

None.
