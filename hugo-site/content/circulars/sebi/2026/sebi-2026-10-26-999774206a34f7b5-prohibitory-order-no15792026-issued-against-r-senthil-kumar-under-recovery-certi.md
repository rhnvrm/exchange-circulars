## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1579/2026 against R Senthil Kumar under Recovery Certificate Nos. 7902 of 2024, 7904 of 2024, and 7905 of 2024 in the matter of Trustline Holdings Pvt. Ltd PMS and Trustline Deep Alpha AIF. The order prohibits the defaulter from transferring or alienating attached properties to recover unpaid dues.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: R Senthil Kumar
- Entities Involved: Trustline Holdings Pvt. Ltd (PMS) and Trustline Deep Alpha (AIF)
- Recovery Certificates: Nos. 7902 of 2024, 7904 of 2024, and 7905 of 2024
- Action: Prohibitory Order restricting transfer or alienation of attached assets

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings and asset freezing under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals are prohibited from transferring, alienating, or creating any charge on the attached properties.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and safeguards investor interests by enforcing asset recovery for non-compliance.
