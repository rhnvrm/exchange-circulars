## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with the Income-Tax Act, against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal (PAN: AACPD0426F) for a total sum of Rs. 3,10,000 in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice Details: Certificate No. 9320 of 2026, dated November 6, 2026.
- Amount Demanded: Rs. 3,10,000 (comprising Rs. 3,00,000 penalty imposed vide order dated 31.08.2026, Rs. 9,000 interest, and Rs. 1,000 recovery cost).
- Payment Mode: Direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9320 (IFSC: ICIC0000106) or via the SEBI online recovery payment portal within 15 days.
- Consequences of Default: Attachment of movable/immovable properties, bank accounts, arrest and detention, or appointment of a receiver.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Payment of dues within 15 days of receipt of notice and submission of e-payment confirmation details to SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 6, 2026
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of notice

## Impact Assessment

Individual recovery proceeding with no systemic impact on the broader market.
