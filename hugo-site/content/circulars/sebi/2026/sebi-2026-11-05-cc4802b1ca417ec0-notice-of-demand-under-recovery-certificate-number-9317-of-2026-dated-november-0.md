## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9317 of 2026 dated November 05, 2026, in the matter of Decillion Finance Limited. The notice is directed jointly and severally to Manish Mishra, Anshu Mishra, Dipak Dwiwedi, Vivek Chauhan, Ankur Sharma, Abhay Dwivedi, Neetu Dwivedi, Pallavi, and Amrit Pal Singh (Defaulters).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9317 of 2026 issued by SEBI on November 5, 2026.
- Issued jointly and severally to nine defaulters including Manish Mishra and associates.
- Stems from SEBI's investigation into fraudulent share price manipulation and pump-and-dump activities involving Decillion Finance Limited scrip.
- Relates to penalties imposed following misleading recommendations circulated via YouTube channel 'Stock Yatra'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action pursuant to SEBI's statutory powers for recovery of dues under recovery certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Defaulters are required to pay the demanded dues immediately pursuant to the recovery certificate to avoid further attachment of bank accounts, properties, and other coercive recovery measures by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight against market manipulators and entities engaging in unauthorized investment advice and pump-and-dump schemes.
