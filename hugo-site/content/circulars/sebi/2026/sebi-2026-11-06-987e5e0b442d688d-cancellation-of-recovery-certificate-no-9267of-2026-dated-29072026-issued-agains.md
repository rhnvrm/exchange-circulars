## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9267 of 2026 dated 29.07.2026 issued against S S Traders (Proprietor: Mr. Marvadi Parsotambhai). This action pertains to the investigation and enforcement proceedings in the trading activity of the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9267 of 2026 was originally issued on 29.07.2026 against S S Traders.
- The proceedings relate to fraudulent and unfair trade practices and non-compliance with summons in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Cancellation of the recovery certificate indicates successful recovery, settlement, or compliance with the dues demanded.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action concluding recovery proceedings against the specified entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure adherence to SEBI summons, disclosure norms, and avoid fraudulent or manipulative trading practices.

## Important Dates

- 29.07.2026: Recovery Certificate No. 9267 of 2026 issued.
- 06.11.2026: Cancellation of Recovery Certificate / Release Order issued.

## Impact Assessment

Resolves outstanding recovery proceedings against S S Traders, closing the specific enforcement case related to ANI Integrated Services Limited.
