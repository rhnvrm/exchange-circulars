SEBI has issued a notice regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC9307 of 2026 in respect of Sri Salasar Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AADCS8457R) in the matter of Decillon Finance Limited.

- Completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC9307 of 2026.
- Issued against Sri Salasar Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AADCS8457R).
- Matter relates to Decillon Finance Limited.
- Enforcement action executed under statutory recovery provisions.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

None as the recovery certificate proceedings have been completed.

- Notice Date: November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement and recovery actions concluded successfully with no broader market impact.
