## Summary

SEBI has announced measures to rationalise the periodicity of KYC reviews for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), aiming to reduce repetitive compliance and promote ease of doing business in the Indian securities market.

## Key Points

- KYC review periodicity increased from 1 year to 2 years, and from 3 years to 8 years depending on the FPI category.
- Periodicity for SWAGAT-FI FPIs remains unchanged at 10 years.
- Existing relaxations for GS-FPIs remain unchanged.
- Separate confirmation regarding changes in previously submitted information at the time of registration continuance is discontinued.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of periodic KYC review timelines for FPIs from 1 to 2 years and 3 to 8 years.
- Elimination of repetitive submissions and separate confirmations during registration continuance.

## Compliance Requirements

FPIs and Custodians/DDPs must update their compliance schedules and operational systems to align with the revised KYC review intervals effective from December 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Significantly eases compliance burdens for FPIs by curtailing redundant paperwork and review frequency, fostering an investor-friendly regulatory ecosystem.
