## Summary

## Key Points

- Completion of Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025.
- Date of Recovery Certificate: May 16, 2025.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P).
- Matter: Unickon Securities Pvt Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or certificate amounts.

Formal closure / completion of recovery proceedings upon satisfaction or resolution of the recovery certificate requirements as per SEBI regulations.
