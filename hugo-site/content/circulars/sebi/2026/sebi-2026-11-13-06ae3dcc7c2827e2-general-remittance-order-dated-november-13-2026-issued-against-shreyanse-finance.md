## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7708 of 2024 against Shreyanse Finance Ltd (PAN: AAGCS1052B) in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This order follows enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated for the realization of unpaid penalties and disgorgement amounts pursuant to prior SEBI adjudications regarding financial statement manipulations and offloading of shares while in possession of UPSI.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued by SEBI on November 13, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7708 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Shreyanse Finance Ltd (PAN: AAGCS1052B).
- Underlying Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd investigations and financial irregularities.
- Follows attachment notices and final order directives (WTM/SM/IVD/ID2/18493/2022-23).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, empowering recovery officers to issue general remittance orders for the attachment and recovery of funds, bank balances, and receivables due to defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, debtors, and garnishees holding funds or accounts on behalf of Shreyanse Finance Ltd are mandated to remit the specified amounts towards the recovery certificate dues and ensure compliance with SEBI's recovery directives.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 7708 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's robust regulatory enforcement framework and rigorous pursuit of recovery proceedings against entities involved in financial market violations and non-compliance with disgorgement or penalty orders.
