## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a completion order dated November 27, 2026, regarding Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026 issued against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal (PAN: AACPD0426F) in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026 was drawn on November 6, 2026, for a sum of Rs. 3,10,000/- along with interest, costs, and charges for non-payment of penalty.
- The underlying penalty was imposed by the Adjudicating Officer vide Order No. Order/JS/VC/2026-27/32690-32694 dated August 31, 2026, in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited.
- In view of the full payment of the amount due under the certificate, SEBI has formally completed the recovery certificate under Section 28A(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Section 413 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Rule 225(12) of the Income-tax Rules, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an administrative enforcement recovery completion order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Dues under Recovery Certificate No. 9320 of 2026 have been fully satisfied, concluding the recovery proceedings against the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: November 6, 2026
- Date of Completion Order: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Resolves the specific monetary recovery proceedings against Satyen Bhupendra Dalal with no broader market impact.
