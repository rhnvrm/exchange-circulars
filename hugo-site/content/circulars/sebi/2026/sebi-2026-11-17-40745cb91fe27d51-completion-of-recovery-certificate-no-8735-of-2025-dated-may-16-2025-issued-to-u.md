## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 8735 of 2025 dated May 16, 2025, issued to Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P) in the matter of Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd., signifying the resolution or closure of the recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 8735 of 2025 dated May 16, 2025.
- Defaulter: Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P).
- Matter: Completion/closure of recovery proceedings.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal conclusion and withdrawal/completion of recovery certificate proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

No further recovery actions under this specific certificate as proceedings are completed; release of any attached assets if applicable per the completion order.

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: May 16, 2025
- Completion / Order Date: November 2026
