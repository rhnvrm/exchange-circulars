## Summary

SEBI has issued notices of attachment against Paresh Mulji Kariya (PAN – AAEPK0469M) under Recovery Certificate No. 9295 of 2026 in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited (ASBPL). The attachment covers demat accounts and mutual fund folios to recover outstanding regulatory dues and penalties arising from severe market violations and fund misappropriation.

## Key Points

- Notice Details: Issued with AP No. 15894 of 2026 on November 19, 2026.
- Target Individual: Paresh Mulji Kariya, founder and director of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
- Recovery Proceeding: Initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 9295 of 2026.
- Action Taken: Attachment of demat accounts, bank accounts, and mutual fund folios to prevent asset dissipation and satisfy outstanding regulatory dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action executed under SEBI's statutory powers for recovery of penalties and dues resulting from unauthorized investment schemes and misappropriation of client funds.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants, mutual fund houses, registrars and transfer agents (RTAs), and banks are required to immediately freeze and attach the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by Paresh Mulji Kariya and prevent any debits, transfers, or redemptions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate upon receipt of attachment notice

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and stringent recovery mechanisms against defaulting brokers and promoters involved in massive client fund diversion and unauthorized Portfolio Management Services (PMS).
