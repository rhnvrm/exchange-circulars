## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a caution to investors regarding fraudsters impersonating SEBI-registered intermediaries on closed user groups of social media platforms (such as WhatsApp and Telegram). These fraudsters create a false sense of legitimacy, lure investors into opening trading accounts, and direct them to transfer funds to unauthorized third-party bank accounts for purported international and domestic trading opportunities.

## Key Points

- Fraudsters impersonate SEBI-registered intermediaries on closed social media groups (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) with international (+44, +65) or Indian (+91) mobile numbers.
- Lures include fraudulent claims such as recovering Indian portfolio losses in foreign markets, off-market negotiated block trades, and institutional trades.
- Investors are directed to download fraudulent trading apps and transfer funds to third-party bank accounts not belonging to SEBI-registered entities.
- Red flags include unverified invitation links, fake institutional trading accounts, misuse of SEBI website screenshots, and unauthorized use of SEBI letterheads.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Advisory/Cautionary Press Release).

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors are advised to verify SEBI registration status using official links before dealing with any entity.
- Avoid investment opportunities promising assured returns or requiring fund transfers to third-party accounts.

## Important Dates

- Issued in November 2026 (PR No. 76/2026).

## Impact Assessment

- High importance for investor awareness and protection against digital financial fraud and impersonation scams.
