## Summary

This order by the Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005, addresses Appeal No. 7156 of 2026 filed by B Venkatramani against the CPIO, SEBI. The appeal pertains to information sought regarding Bajaj Capital Investment Advisers Pvt. Ltd. (holder of SEBI Investment Adviser Registration Certificate No. INA000000985), arbitral awards under the SEBI Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) framework, SCORES complaints, CPGRAMS grievances, and associated internal SEBI file notes and referrals.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated August 25, 2026, seeking certified copies of records, file notings, emails, and data related to Bajaj Capital Investment Advisers Pvt. Ltd. and ODR proceedings.
- The CPIO responded on September 24, 2026, leading to the filing of Appeal No. 7156 of 2026 on October 20, 2026.
- Specific queries included the designation of the dealing department for referrals, dates of referral, present status, and communications received from the Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Ltd. (MSEI).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy directives were introduced through this appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations are established for market participants.

## Important Dates

- Date of RTI Application: August 25, 2026
- Date of CPIO Response: September 24, 2026
- Date of RTI Appeal: October 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct operational or market impact as it resolves an individual information request under the RTI Act.
