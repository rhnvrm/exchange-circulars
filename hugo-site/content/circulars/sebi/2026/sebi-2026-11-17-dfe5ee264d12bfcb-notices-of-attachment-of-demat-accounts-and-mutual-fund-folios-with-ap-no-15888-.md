## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 17, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9294 of 2026 and AP No. 15888 of 2026 against Sadhana Paresh Kariya (PAN – AJHPK6018N) in the matter of Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited. The notice orders the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios to recover outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9294 of 2026 and AP No. 15888 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sadhana Paresh Kariya (PAN – AJHPK6018N).
- Matter: Anugrah Stock and Broking Private Limited.
- Action: Attachment of Demat Accounts and Mutual Fund Folios.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, depositories, mutual fund houses, and financial intermediaries are required to freeze the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios and prevent any debits, transfers, or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against associated entities and defaulters in default cases involving stockbrokers.
