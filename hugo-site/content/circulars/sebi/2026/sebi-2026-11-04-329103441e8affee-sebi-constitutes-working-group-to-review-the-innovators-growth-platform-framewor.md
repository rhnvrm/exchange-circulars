## Summary

SEBI has constituted a Working Group to undertake a comprehensive review of the regulatory framework governing the Innovators Growth Platform (IGP) under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018. The review aims to adapt the framework for research-stage, pre-revenue, IP-led, deep-tech, and other technology-intensive enterprises while ensuring investor protection.

## Key Points

- Formation of a specialized Working Group comprising industry leaders from venture capital, private equity, biotechnology, aerospace, and industry associations.
- Terms of reference cover existing IGP framework evaluation, research-stage enterprise suitability, investor requirements, listing/capital raising norms, post-listing obligations, and Main Board migration.
- International benchmarking against global frameworks for innovative and growth-stage companies.
- Recommendations for regulatory amendments and implementation measures.

## Regulatory Changes

No immediate regulatory amendments have been enacted; the circular establishes a Working Group to review and recommend changes to the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 regarding the Innovators Growth Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

No immediate compliance requirements for market participants or issuers at this stage.

## Important Dates

- Press Release / Circular Date: November 2026 (PR No.69/2026)
- Working Group deliberations ongoing.

## Impact Assessment

The review is expected to significantly improve ease of capital access and listing for Indian start-ups and deep-tech/innovation-led enterprises, potentially boosting domestic venture investments and public market participation in high-growth sectors.
