## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated November 05, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9316 of 2026 drawn jointly and severally against Manish Mishra, Dipak Dwiwedi, Vivek Chauhan, Ankur Sharma, and Neetu Dwivedi. This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Decillion Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9316 of 2026.
- Defaulters/Noticees: Manish Mishra (PAN: AMPPM6823L), Dipak Dwiwedi (PAN: ATDPD4055C), Vivek Chauhan (PAN: AHPPC9620A), Ankur Sharma (PAN: BMIPS3640D), and Neetu Dwivedi (PAN: CTTPD8770N).
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of Decillion Finance Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with recovery certificate demands, freeze accounts/assets as specified, and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Demand: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in Decillion Finance Limited.
