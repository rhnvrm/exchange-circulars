## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Intelligent Money Managers Private Limited (IMMPL), a registered Merchant Banker (Registration No. INM000012169), for the period from April 01, 2022, to November 30, 2024. Following the inspection and subsequent notices, SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings resulting in an order addressing multiple regulatory infractions under the SEBI (Merchant Bankers) Regulations, 1992, and the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Intelligent Money Managers Private Limited (PAN: AACCI5042B).
- Inspection Period: April 01, 2022, to November 30, 2024.
- Delisting Offer Violations: Raising professional fees for delisting directly to the target company rather than the acquirer(s) as mandated by Delisting Regulations.
- Independence & Arm's Length Violations: Failure to maintain an arm's length relationship between merchant banking activities and group entities, notably with the Compliance Officer's remuneration being paid by a related entity (J. Gupta & Co. LLP) under the same control.
- Certification & Code of Conduct: Alleged breaches of NISM certification requirements and the Merchant Banker Code of Conduct.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and adjudication proceedings initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 5 of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995, and Section 15HB.

## Compliance Requirements

Merchant bankers must ensure strict adherence to delisting cost-bearing rules (ensuring acquirers pay professional fees), maintain absolute operational independence, ensure compliance officers are directly compensated by the merchant banking entity, and uphold arm's length separation from group/related entities.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Dates: April 07–09, 2025
- Adjudication Initiation Approval: February 06, 2026
- Adjudicating Officer Appointment: April 02, 2026
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) Date: April 22, 2026
- Order Release / Document Date: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights SEBI's continued enforcement focus on merchant bankers regarding conflict of interest management, independent reporting lines for compliance officers, and compliance with statutory delisting expense rules.
