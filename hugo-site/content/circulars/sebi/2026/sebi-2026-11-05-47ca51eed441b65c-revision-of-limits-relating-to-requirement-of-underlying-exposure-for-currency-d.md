## Summary

SEBI has revised the threshold limits for taking positions in currency derivatives contracts involving the Indian Rupee (INR) without establishing the existence of underlying exposure. This modification follows the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026, on Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings.

## Key Points

- Modifies Clause 5.3.3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024.
- Domestic clients and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) may take long or short positions in currency derivatives contracts involving INR, without establishing underlying exposure, up to a single limit of USD 5 million equivalent of notional value (outstanding at any point) across all currency pairs involving INR, combined across all recognised stock exchanges.
- FPIs must ensure that short positions at all stock exchanges across all contracts in FCY-INR pairs do not exceed USD 5 million.
- To take long positions in excess of USD 5 million in all contracts in FCY-INR pairs, FPIs are required to have underlying exposure in Indian debt or equity securities, including units of equity/debt mutual funds.
- Domestic clients taking positions in excess of USD 5 million in all contracts in FCY-INR pairs are subject to conditions specified in RBI Master Direction dated July 05, 2016, and RBI A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clause 5.3.3.1 through 5.3.3.7 of the SEBI Master Circular dated December 30, 2024, are updated to reflect the revised limits and compliance frameworks for unhedged currency derivatives positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock exchanges and clearing corporations must put in place implementation systems, make consequential amendments to bye-laws, rules, and regulations, notify stock brokers and clearing members, and communicate implementation status to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026.
- Effective immediately in accordance with SEBI and RBI directions.

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on market participants, FPIs, domestic clients, stock exchanges, and clearing corporations trading in INR currency derivatives by defining updated unhedged position thresholds and underlying exposure mandates.
