## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated November 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7470 of 2023 drawn against Chennai Commodities in the matter of First Futures and Stocks Private Limited. This action is part of recovery proceedings to realize outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 7470 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Chennai Commodities.
- Matter: First Futures and Stocks Private Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- Orders attachment of bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: November 10, 2026

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in regulatory violations.
