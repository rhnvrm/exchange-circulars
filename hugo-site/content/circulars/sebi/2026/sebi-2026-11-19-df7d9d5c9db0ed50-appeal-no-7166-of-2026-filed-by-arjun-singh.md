## Summary

Arjun Singh filed an RTI application dated September 27, 2026, seeking information and regulatory records regarding Image Career Limited (ICL), along with specific policy details. The CPIO responded on October 26, 2026, confirming that ICL is not registered with SEBI, that examinations and investigations are conducted confidentially, and that no complaints were received against ICL. The appellant filed an appeal on October 28, 2026, which the Appellate Authority disposed of by upholding the CPIO's response.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought records, regulatory proceedings, and complaint details regarding Image Career Limited (CIN U70102UP2008PLC036464).
- SEBI CPIO confirmed that ICL is not registered with SEBI as a Stock Broker, Investment Advisor, or Research Analyst.
- CPIO noted that SEBI maintains confidentiality regarding examinations and investigations, and enforcement actions are published on the SEBI website.
- No complaints were found on record against Image Career Limited.
- The Appellate Authority concluded that the CPIO adequately addressed the queries with the information available on record.
