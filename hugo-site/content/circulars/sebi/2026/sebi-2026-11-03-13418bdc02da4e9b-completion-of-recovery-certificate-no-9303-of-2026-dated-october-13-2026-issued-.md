## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion notice regarding Recovery Certificate No. 9303 of 2026 dated October 13, 2026, issued to Mr. Aviral Saxena (PAN: KVYPS1652D) in the matter of market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9303 of 2026
- Date: October 13, 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Mr. Aviral Saxena (PAN: KVYPS1652D)
- Matter: Market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited
- Action: Completion of Recovery Certificate / realization of dues

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues arising from market manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and intermediaries are notified regarding the completion of recovery proceedings and release/closure of related enforcement actions against the specified noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and successful conclusion of recovery proceedings in market manipulation cases involving listed scrips.
