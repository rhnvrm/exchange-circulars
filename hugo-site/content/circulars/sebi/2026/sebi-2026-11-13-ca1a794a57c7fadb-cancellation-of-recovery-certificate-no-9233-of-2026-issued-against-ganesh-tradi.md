## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has officially cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9233 of 2026 issued against Ganesh Trading (Proprietor: Mr. Vairagi Kalpeshpuri Ganeshpuri, PAN: DKSPG9993R). This action follows the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) ruling that set aside SEBI's earlier penalty order regarding trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9233 of 2026 on November 13, 2026.
- The certificate was originally issued against Ganesh Trading (Prop. Mr. Vairagi Kalpeshpuri Ganeshpuri, PAN: DKSPG9993R).
- Recovery proceedings stemmed from alleged trading irregularities in ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).
- The cancellation was executed pursuant to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order setting aside the underlying penalty.
- Any attachment of bank accounts or assets associated with this specific recovery certificate stands released.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal withdrawal and cancellation of recovery proceedings and associated attachment orders initiated by SEBI against the designated entity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries holding any embargoes or attachments on accounts/assets of Ganesh Trading pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9233 of 2026 must lift the restrictions immediately upon receipt of this order.

## Important Dates

- Cancellation Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as this is an administrative cancellation following a judicial/tribunal relief order for an individual trading entity involved in past scrip proceedings.
