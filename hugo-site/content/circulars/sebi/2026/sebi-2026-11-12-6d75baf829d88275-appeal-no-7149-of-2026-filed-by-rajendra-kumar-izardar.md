## Summary

Rajendra Kumar Izardar filed an RTI application seeking year-wise details of complaints received against brokers regarding unauthorized trading or excessive trading (churning) from January 1, 2023, to September 15, 2026, including broker names and complaint counts. SEBI CPIO responded that the information is not maintained in the requested format. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, citing Supreme Court and CIC precedents that public authorities are not obligated to create, collect, or collate information not available in their records.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought broker-wise unauthorized trading and churning complaints from Jan 1, 2023, to Sept 15, 2026.
- SEBI CPIO stated information is not maintained in the requested format/manner.
- Appellate Authority noted public authorities are not bound under the RTI Act to collate or create unmaintained information.
- Appeal dismissed as devoid of merit.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; order reinforces existing RTI compliance jurisprudence regarding data compilation.
