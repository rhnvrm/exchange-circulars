## Summary

SEBI has reviewed and revised the minimum Initial Margin (IM) and minimum Margin Period of Risk (MPOR) for the commodity derivatives segment, based on recommendations from the Working Group on 'Review of current regulatory norms applicable to Agri Commodity Derivatives Segment' and the Risk Management Review Committee (RMRC).

## Key Points

- Revised norms apply to Chapter 9 of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives segment dated August 04, 2023.
- Minimum Initial Margin (IM) and minimum MPOR are specified based on volatility category (Low, Medium, High) for both Non-Agri and Agri commodities:
  - Low volatility: IM 6% (Non-Agri/Agri), MPOR 2 (Non-Agri/Agri)
  - Medium volatility: IM 8% (Non-Agri/Agri), MPOR 2 (Non-Agri/Agri)
  - High volatility: IM 10% (Non-Agri/Agri), MPOR 3 (Non-Agri/Agri)
- The circular comes into force from December 01, 2026.
- Applicable for a period of one year from the date of issuance unless extended by the Board.

## Regulatory Changes

Paragraph 9.1.3.I.a(iv) regarding "Minimum value for Initial Margin and minimum MPOR" of Chapter 9 of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives has been updated to prescribe uniform minimum IM (6% for Low, 8% for Medium, 10% for High) and MPOR (2 for Low/Medium, 3 for High) across Agri and Non-Agri commodities based on volatility categories.

## Compliance Requirements

Recognized Clearing Corporations having a Commodity Derivatives Segment (MIIs) must:
- Take necessary steps and put systems in place for implementation.
- Make necessary amendments to relevant bye-laws, rules, and regulations.
- Disseminate the circular provisions to market participants and investors on their websites.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: December 01, 2026
- Validity Period: One year from issuance

## Impact Assessment

Clearing corporations and market participants dealing in commodity derivatives will need to update their risk management systems and margin computation engines to align with the revised minimum Initial Margin and MPOR requirements effective December 01, 2026.
