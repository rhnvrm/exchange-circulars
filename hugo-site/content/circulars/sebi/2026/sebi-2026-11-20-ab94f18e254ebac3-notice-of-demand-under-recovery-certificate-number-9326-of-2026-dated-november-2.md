## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated November 20, 2026, under Recovery Certificate Number 9326 of 2026 against Meenu Sharma, Proprietor of Commodity Pandit. This action relates to the realization of dues and penalties ordered in the matter of unregistered investment advisory services.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9326 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Meenu Sharma (PAN: KXYPS9957R), Proprietor of Commodity Pandit.
- Matter: Unregistered Investment Advisory Services.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulter and associated banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the demand notice, freeze assets/accounts if directed, and facilitate recovery of outstanding dues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Recovery Certificate: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities operating unregistered investment advisory services.
