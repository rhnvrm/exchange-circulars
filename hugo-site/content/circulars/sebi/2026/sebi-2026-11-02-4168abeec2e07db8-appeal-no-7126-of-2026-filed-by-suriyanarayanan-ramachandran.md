## Summary

Suriyanarayanan Ramachandran filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against the response of the CPIO, SEBI, regarding transaction details and order statuses from the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) for various companies (Nivaka Fashions Limited, Nandan Denims, Rajnish Retail Limited, and Nectar Life). The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI, noting that public authorities are not obligated to collect or collate information not forming part of their records.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Suriyanarayanan Ramachandran
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- **RTI Application Date**: September 06, 2026
- **Appeal Date**: October 05, 2026 (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/E/26/00378)
- **Information Sought**: Order status, quantities, and rejection remarks for transactions on BSE across Nivaka Fashions Limited, Nandan Denims, Rajnish Retail Limited, and Nectar Life.
- **Ruling**: The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply stating the information is not available with SEBI, citing Supreme Court and CIC precedents that the RTI Act does not require public authorities to collect or invent information not in their possession.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial RTI appeal order reinforcing existing legal positions regarding information availability under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- None for market participants. Reaffirms that SEBI is not required to procure or collate third-party exchange trading data not held in its official records.

## Important Dates

- **RTI Application Date**: September 06, 2026
- **CPIO Response Date**: October 05, 2026
- **Appeal Date**: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No regulatory or operational impact on market participants or stock exchanges. Reinforces standard jurisprudence on RTI disclosures.
