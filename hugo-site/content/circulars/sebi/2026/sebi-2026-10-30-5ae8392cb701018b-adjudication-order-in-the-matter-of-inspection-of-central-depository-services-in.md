## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Central Depository Services (India) Limited (CDSL) for the financial year 2023-24. Post-inspection communications and analysis revealed alleged violations of various SEBI regulations and circulars concerning depositories and participants.

## Key Points

- Inspection of CDSL conducted for FY 2023-24.
- Findings communicated to CDSL followed by responses and analysis by SEBI.
- Alleged violations include Regulation 79(5) of DP Regulations and multiple SEBI circulars related to operational guidelines, risk management, and compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Adjudication proceeding under Section 15-I of SEBI Act and Section 19H of Depositories Act).

## Compliance Requirements

- CDSL and regulated depositories must ensure strict adherence to DP Regulations and operational circulars regarding data protection, risk management, and systems compliance.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: FY 2023-24
- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight and accountability for Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) and depositories.
