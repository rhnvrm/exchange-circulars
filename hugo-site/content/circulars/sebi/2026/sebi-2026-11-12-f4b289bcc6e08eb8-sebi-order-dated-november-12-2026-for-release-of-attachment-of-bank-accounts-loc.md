## Summary

SEBI has issued an order dated November 12, 2026, regarding the release of attachment of bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios in respect of Recovery Certificate No. 7251 of 2023. This action formally lifts previous asset freezes imposed under enforcement and recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Order Date: November 12, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 7251 of 2023
- Action: Release/lifting of attachment on bank accounts, safety lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- Issuing Authority: Recovery Officer / SEBI

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement order under the SEBI Act, 1992, lifting attachment measures previously enacted for recovery of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and mutual fund houses/RTAs are required to unfreeze the specified accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios and permit normal operations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Release: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restores operational freedom for the concerned entity's financial accounts, securities holdings, and investments upon satisfaction or resolution of recovery certificate dues.
