## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1582 of 2026 dated November 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 4395 of 2021 against Dinesh Kumar Jaiswal in the matter of CAT Technologies Ltd. The order restrains the noticee from transferring, alienating, or encumbering any of his movable or immovable properties, bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund holdings.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order No. 1582 of 2026 issued by SEBI Recovery Officer on November 10, 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Dinesh Kumar Jaiswal.
- Associated Recovery Certificate: RC No. 4395 of 2021 in the matter of CAT Technologies Ltd.
- Restrains the noticee from disposing of or dealing with movable/immovable assets, bank accounts, and securities.
- Directs banks, financial institutions, and depositories to freeze accounts and assets held by the noticee.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Section 22/29 of the Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993 and the Income Tax Act, 1961 for recovery of outstanding dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), mutual fund houses, and registrars must immediately freeze accounts and prevent any debit, transfer, or encumbrance of assets held individually or jointly by Dinesh Kumar Jaiswal without prior written permission from SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 10, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against individuals associated with market irregularities and unpaid dues in CAT Technologies Ltd.
