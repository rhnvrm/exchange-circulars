## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Completion Order and Release Order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 7413 of 2023 issued against Yadunath Singh Thakur, proprietor of The Apex Global. This marks the conclusion of recovery proceedings initiated for dues arising from unauthorized investment advisory activities.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Yadunath Singh Thakur, Proprietor of The Apex Global.
- **Recovery Certificate:** Certificate No. 7413 of 2023.
- **Underlying Matter:** Recovery of dues amounting to approximately ₹1.41 crore (₹14,153,772 including interest and costs) ordered following unregistered investment advisory operations.
- **Action Taken:** Issuance of Completion and Release Orders by SEBI, signifying the resolution or execution of the recovery certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy amendments; this is an enforcement and recovery completion order specific to the entity.

## Compliance Requirements

With the issuance of the completion and release order, the specific recovery certificate obligations under RC No. 7413 of 2023 have been fulfilled or concluded, requiring no further compliance actions under this specific certificate.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct impact on general market operations or listed entities, as it is an isolated regulatory recovery closure action against an individual entity operating unregistered advisory services.
