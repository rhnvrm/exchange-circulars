## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7136 of 2026 filed by Mukesh Rajendra Parikh under the Right to Information Act, 2005, regarding information sought on SCORES Complaint No. SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031291/1 concerning CDSL Ventures Limited (CVL) and HDFC Bank.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated September 07, 2026, requesting certified copies of records relating to SCORES Complaint No. SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031291/1.
- Requested records included Action Taken Reports (ATRs) submitted by HDFC Bank with annexures, first level review submissions, email communications with SEBI officers, SCORES dashboard logs, and final disposal records.
- The respondent replied regarding the status and registration of the complaint in SCORES bearing registration no. SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031291/1.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a quasi-judicial order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations imposed on regulated entities.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 07, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 22, 2026
- RTI Appeal Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No market or operational impact; routine appellate order under the RTI framework.
