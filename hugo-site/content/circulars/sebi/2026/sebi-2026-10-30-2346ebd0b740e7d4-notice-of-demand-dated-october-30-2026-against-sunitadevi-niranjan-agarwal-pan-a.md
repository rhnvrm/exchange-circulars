## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 30, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9311 of 2026 read with Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Section 413 of the Income-Tax Act, 2025 against Sunitadevi Niranjan Agarwal (PAN: ADRPS3295H) in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited. The total amount due is Rs. 10,41,000/-.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9311 of 2026.
- Noticee: Sunitadevi Niranjan Agarwal (PAN: ADRPS3295H).
- Underlying Matter: Penalty imposed by WTM Order No. WTM/AS/CFD/CFD-SEC-4/32528/2026-27 dated 30.07.2026 in Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited.
- Dues Breakdown: Penalty of Rs. 10,00,000/-, Interest of Rs. 40,000/-, and Recovery Cost of Rs. 1,000/- (Total: Rs. 10,41,000/-).
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of notice via EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9311.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Income-tax rules, authorizing property attachment, bank account freezing, and arrest in case of default.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee must pay Rs. 10,41,000/- within 15 days and forward payment confirmation to SEBI Recovery Officers. Transfer or mortgage of properties by the noticee is prohibited without prior permission.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: October 30, 2026
- Penalty Order Date: July 30, 2026
- Payment Due: Within 15 days of notice receipt

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict enforcement and recovery proceedings by SEBI for realization of dues and penalties in listed entities.
