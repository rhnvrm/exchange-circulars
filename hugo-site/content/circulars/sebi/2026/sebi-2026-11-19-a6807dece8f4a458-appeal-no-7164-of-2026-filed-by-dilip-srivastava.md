## Summary

The Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7164 of 2026 filed by Dilip Srivastava under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought the official names and tenure details of the compliance officers for the SEBI-registered stockbroker entity, Sanjay Agarwal Broking Limited (Registration No. INZ000097519), from FY 2018-2019 to FY 2025-2026. The CPIO and the Appellate Authority upheld that SEBI does not maintain such historical records of compliance officers.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Dilip Srivastava
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai / Appellate Authority (Ruchi Chojer)
- **Entity Concerned**: Sanjay Agarwal Broking Limited (SEBI Reg. No. INZ000097519, CIN: U67200WB2018PLC228741)
- **Information Sought**: Official names and tenure of compliance officers from FY 2018-2019 to FY 2025-2026.
- **Ruling**: The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply that the requested information is not maintained by SEBI, citing the Supreme Court judgment in *Central Board of Secondary Education & Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors.* regarding the scope of the RTI Act.
