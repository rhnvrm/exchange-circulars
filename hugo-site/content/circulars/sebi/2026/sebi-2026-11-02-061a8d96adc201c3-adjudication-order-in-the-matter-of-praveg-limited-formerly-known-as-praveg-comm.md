## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order dated November 2, 2026, in the matter of Praveg Limited (formerly known as Praveg Communications India Limited), imposing a total monetary penalty of ₹2,00,000 under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Section 23H of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956 (SCRA). The penalty was levied due to disclosure lapses and non-compliance with SEBI circular requirements governing schemes of amalgamation and Regulation 11 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** Praveg Limited (formerly Praveg Communications India Limited).
- **Order Date:** November 2, 2026.
- **Violations:** Non-compliance with SEBI Circular No. CFD/DIL3/CIR/2017/21 dated March 10, 2017, and Regulation 11 of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015, regarding the scheme of amalgamation with Sword and Shield Pharma Limited.
- **Specific Lapses:** Failure to provide required information to shareholders during voting on the amalgamation and omission of mandatory shareholder-protection clauses in filings before the NCLT.
- **Penalty Imposed:** ₹2,00,000 in total (₹1,00,000 under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act and ₹1,00,000 under Section 23H of the SCRA).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes were introduced; the order enforces existing compliance frameworks governing schemes of arrangement and amalgamation under SEBI circulars and LODR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Praveg Limited is required to pay the monetary penalty of ₹2,00,000 within 45 days of receipt of the order.
- Listed entities must ensure strict adherence to SEBI circulars and LODR regulations when executing schemes of arrangement, including accurate shareholder disclosures and incorporation of mandatory protection clauses in NCLT filings.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Penalty Payment Deadline:** Within 45 days from the receipt of the adjudication order.

## Impact Assessment

This order reinforces SEBI's strict stance on disclosure standards and procedural compliance during corporate restructuring and amalgamation. While the monetary penalty has minimal financial impact on the company, it highlights the regulatory importance of comprehensive shareholder disclosures and adherence to scheme-of-arrangement guidelines.
