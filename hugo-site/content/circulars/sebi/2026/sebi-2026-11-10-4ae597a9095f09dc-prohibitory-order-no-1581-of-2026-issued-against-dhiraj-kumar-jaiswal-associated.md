## Summary

SEBI issued Prohibitory Order No. 1581 of 2026 against Dhiraj Kumar Jaiswal, associated with Recovery Certificate No. 4391 of 2021, in the matter of CAT Technologies Ltd. This order forms part of SEBI's ongoing recovery proceedings and enforcement actions relating to fraudulent Global Depository Receipt (GDR) issuance schemes.

## Key Points

- Target Individual: Dhiraj Kumar Jaiswal (Managing Director / Key Promoter of CAT Technologies Ltd.).
- Order Reference: Prohibitory Order No. 1581 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 4391 of 2021.
- Underlying Matter: Fraudulent GDR manipulation scheme dating back to 2007-2009 involving CAT Technologies Ltd. and Vintage FZE.
- Purpose: Asset attachment and recovery of outstanding monetary penalties and dues imposed by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy or market-wide rule changes introduced; this is a specific quasi-judicial recovery and prohibitory enforcement order against a defaulting entity and individual.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee (Dhiraj Kumar Jaiswal) and associated financial institutions/banks are prohibited from transferring, alienating, or charging any assets, bank accounts, or securities to the extent of the recovery certificate dues.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 10, 2026
- Recovery Certificate: RC 4391 of 2021
- Original Violation Period: July 2007 - November 2009

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the named individual and related entities regarding asset liquidity and enforcement compliance, reinforcing SEBI's rigorous recovery mechanisms for unfulfilled monetary penalties arising from market manipulation cases.
