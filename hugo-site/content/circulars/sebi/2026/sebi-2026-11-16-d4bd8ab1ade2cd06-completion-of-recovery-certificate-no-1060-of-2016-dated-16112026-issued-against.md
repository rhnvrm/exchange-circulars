## Summary

SEBI has issued an order dated November 16, 2026, regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 1060 of 2016 issued against Jigar Praful Ghoghari (PAN: ASFPG8598L) in the matter of Goldstone Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 1060 of 2016
- Date: November 16, 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Jigar Praful Ghoghari (PAN: ASFPG8598L)
- Matter: Goldstone Technologies Limited
- Action: Completion of Recovery Certificate

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action concluded pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Completion of recovery proceedings and release/withdrawal of attachment notices where applicable against the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Completion: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the conclusion of recovery proceedings against the individual in the matter of Goldstone Technologies Limited.
