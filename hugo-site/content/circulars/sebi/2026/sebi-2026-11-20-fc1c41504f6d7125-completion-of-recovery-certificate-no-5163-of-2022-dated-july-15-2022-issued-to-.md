## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 5163 of 2022 dated July 15, 2022, issued to Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Defaulter, PAN: AAPFS5325P) in the matter of Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. This signifies the conclusion of recovery proceedings under this specific certificate.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 5163 of 2022 dated July 15, 2022.
- Defaulter / Entity: Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAPFS5325P).
- Matter: Completion of Recovery Certificate / realization of dues in the matter of Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery action conclusion pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, indicating fulfillment or completion of recovery proceedings under Certificate No. 5163 of 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities, banks, and financial institutions are notified of the completion of recovery proceedings under this specific certificate, lifting associated restrictions/attachments where applicable under this recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: July 15, 2022
- Completion / Order Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the formal closure and completion of recovery proceedings under Recovery Certificate No. 5163 of 2022 against Unickon Securities Pvt. Ltd., marking the resolution of dues under this certificate.
