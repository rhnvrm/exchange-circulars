## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 26, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 8511 of 2025 against Add Life Pharma Ltd. This order is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to enforce unpaid monetary penalties and secure dues.

## Key Points

- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8511 of 2025 against Add Life Pharma Ltd.
- Directs general remittance of funds and enforcement of recovery measures.
- Originates from longstanding non-compliance regarding investor grievance and securities dematerialization.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory framework changes; enforcement action specific to the named entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial intermediaries and entities holding funds or assets of Add Life Pharma Ltd must comply with remittance and attachment directives.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 26, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Year: 2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory enforcement and recovery of dues from non-compliant entities, reinforcing market discipline.
