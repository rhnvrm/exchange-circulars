## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7148 of 2026 filed by Rajeev K R under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appeal concerned information regarding the status of a physical complaint and Market Intelligence Report submitted against Koiya International Limited (formerly Popees Cares, Archana Software Ltd) involving allegations of promoter fraud, asset stripping, and non-disclosure under SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought the official diary/inward registration number, status of forwarding to the Market Intelligence Investigation Enforcement Department, file noting action taken report, and the designated officer/division for the physical complaint.
- SEBI's CPIO provided the Corporate Finance Department (CFD) Head Office inward number (74916) and furnished the Action Taken Report (ATR) regarding the SCORES complaint.
- SEBI clarified that queries seeking clarification do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- SEBI maintained that preliminary examinations and internal file notings regarding sensitive investigations are confidential and exempt from disclosure until any regulatory action is taken.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None for market participants; standard RTI disclosure guidelines apply to SEBI.

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct impact on market operations or listed company compliance, reaffirming confidentiality norms regarding ongoing investigative examinations.
