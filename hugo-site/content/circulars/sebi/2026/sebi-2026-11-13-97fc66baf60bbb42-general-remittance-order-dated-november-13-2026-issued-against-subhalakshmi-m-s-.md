## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7712 of 2024 against Subhalakshmi M S (Noticee No. 33) in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to realize unpaid dues and penalties resulting from fraudulent trading and financial misrepresentation.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued on November 13, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) No.: 7712 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Subhalakshmi M S in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Originates from SEBI's investigation and final order regarding financial statement manipulation and misuse of UPSI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and garnishees are required to remit attached funds and comply with SEBI Recovery Officer directives.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement mechanisms and pursuit of monetary recovery in market manipulation cases.
