## Summary

Mohammad Akbar filed Appeal No. 7130 of 2026 before the SEBI Appellate Authority under the RTI Act, 2005, against the response of the CPIO regarding the correction of the 'Land allotted' field from 'Yes' to 'No' in his PACL Limited claim applications (Certificate Nos. U016350xxx and U016357xxx). The CPIO had stated that the information was not available with SEBI and advised approaching the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee which handles all refund status communications.

## Key Points

- The appellant inadvertently selected 'Yes' for the 'Land allotted' field while submitting claim applications for PACL Limited refunds.
- Queries sought information on procedures, rules, and methods for correcting the 'Land allotted' field and reprocessing the claim.
- SEBI CPIO responded that information is not available with SEBI and that refund communications are managed entirely by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The appeal was filed on the ground of receiving incomplete, misleading, or false information.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new rules were introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors seeking correction of PACL refund application details must direct their queries and representations to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee as per established procedures.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 02, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 22, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no systemic market impact, as it pertains strictly to an individual investor's RTI query regarding a PACL refund claim error.
