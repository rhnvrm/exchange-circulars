## Summary

Narpat Parmar filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against SEBI's CPIO response regarding query nos. 1 to 7 concerning G.C.A. Marketing Private Limited. The appellant sought complete asset inventories, immovable assets details, powers of attorney, and related correspondence.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought asset inventory and related documents for G.C.A. Marketing Private Limited.
- The CPIO denied the information citing exemptions under Section 8(1)(d) and Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, stating the information is held in a fiduciary capacity and contains commercially confidential information whose disclosure could harm the entity's competitive position.
- The Appellate Authority reviewed the application, response, and appeal to decide the matter based on available records.
