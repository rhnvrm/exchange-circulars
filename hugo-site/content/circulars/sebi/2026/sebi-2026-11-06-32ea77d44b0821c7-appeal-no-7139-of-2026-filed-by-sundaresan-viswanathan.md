## Summary

Sundaresan Viswanathan filed an appeal (No. 7139 of 2026) under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response dated September 30, 2026. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the queries regarding enforcement, recoveries, and auditing roles were general suggestions and opinions rather than requests for specific 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought action-taken reports and recoveries data relating to various acts (Companies Act, SICA, SEBI Act, RBI Act, IBC, RERA, etc.).
- CPIO responded that the queries were in the nature of suggestions and not specific information under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO's response and cited relevant precedents supporting the exclusion of suggestions from the scope of RTI.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new compliance mandates were introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

None. This is an adjudicatory order on an individual RTI appeal.

## Important Dates

- Application Transfer Date: September 7, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 30, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct market or operational impact. Reinforces the legal interpretation of Section 2(f) of the RTI Act regarding queries framed as suggestions.
