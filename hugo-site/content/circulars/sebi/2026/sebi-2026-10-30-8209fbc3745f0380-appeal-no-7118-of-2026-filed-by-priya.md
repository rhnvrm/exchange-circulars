## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7118 of 2026 filed by Priya against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought certified and attested copies of records, guidelines, and data related to human resources, recruitment planning for Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) across General and IT streams, and Document Verification (DV) rules for interviews.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application dated September 07, 2026, responded to by CPIO on September 30, 2026.
- Appeal filed on October 01, 2026 (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/E/26/00370).
- Queries cover sanctioned vs. actual strength at Officer Grade A level (General and IT streams), direct recruitment percentage mandates, internal office memorandums/file notings for 2026 recruitment planning, and direct recruitment at other grades.
- Additional queries pertain to Document Verification (DV) panel SOPs regarding NOC verification for candidates from Govt/PSU/Banks, parent employer NOC rejections, notarized affidavits in lieu of NOC, provisional selection, and prior intimation records.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes or policy directives affecting market participants were introduced in this order.

- None applicable to listed entities or market intermediaries.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: September 07, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 30, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- This order is specific to an individual RTI appeal concerning internal administrative and HR matters of SEBI and has no operational or financial impact on the securities market.
