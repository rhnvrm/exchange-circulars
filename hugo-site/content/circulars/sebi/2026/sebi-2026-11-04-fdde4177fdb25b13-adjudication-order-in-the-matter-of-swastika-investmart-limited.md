## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Swastika Investmart Limited, a registered merchant banker, covering the period from April 01, 2023, to March 31, 2025. The inspection revealed various non-compliances concerning ICDR Regulations, MB Regulations, Intermediaries Regulations, SAST Regulations, and PIT Regulations, particularly relating to SME IPO due diligence such as qualification and experience certificates of directors/KMPs and objects of the issue for client companies like Aditya Ultra Steel Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI inspected Swastika Investmart Limited from July 08 to July 11, 2025, covering FY 2023-25.
- Alleged violations include lapses in due diligence regarding SME IPO disclosures, director/KMP qualification certificates, and working capital estimations (object of the issue).
- Adjudication proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 3 of the Adjudication Rules to adjudge violations under Section 15HB.
- Noticee submitted replies explaining reliance on resumes/CVs and internal company assessments during the SME IPO of Aditya Ultra Steel Limited, noting enhanced subsequent documentation procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations introduced; enforcement action under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act for existing regulatory breaches.

## Compliance Requirements

- Merchant bankers must exercise rigorous due diligence, independently verify director/KMP credentials and qualifications, and ensure robust documentary evidence collection and retention.
- Accurate and substantiated disclosures regarding objects of the issue, working capital estimates, and project funding must be maintained.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2023 – March 31, 2025
- Inspection Dates: July 08, 2025 – July 11, 2025
- Appointment of Adjudicator: February 06, 2026
- Show Cause Notice Date: March 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight and accountability on merchant bankers, particularly concerning SME IPO due diligence and underwriting standards to protect investors.
