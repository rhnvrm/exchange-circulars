## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD) on November 3, 2026, to facilitate access to SEBI's services in regional Indian languages.

## Key Points

- The MoU was signed by Shri Amitabh Nag, CEO, DIBD, and Shri Sandip Pradhan, Whole Time Member, SEBI, in the presence of senior officers.
- DIBD is an independent division under Digital India Corporation, a Section 8 not-for-profit company established by MeitY, Government of India.
- Enables SEBI to leverage BHASHINI's language translation APIs, tools, and models including Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Text-to-Speech (TTS), Machine Translation, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
- Facilitates the enhancement of BHASHINI's language models with securities market-related terminology.
- The MoU comes into force immediately from November 3, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes for market participants; establishes an inter-organizational cooperation framework for linguistic accessibility.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for regulated entities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves accessibility of regulatory services for investors across diverse linguistic backgrounds through advanced AI-driven translation and speech technologies.
