## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated November 06, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9319 of 2026 against Investowryght Research Analytics Private Limited in the matter of Investowryght Research Analytics Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice issued by SEBI under Recovery Certificate No. 9319 of 2026.
- Target entity: Investowryght Research Analytics Private Limited.
- Date of demand: November 06, 2026.
- Action pertains to recovery of dues and enforcement of regulatory penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Investowryght Research Analytics Private Limited and associated entities are required to fulfill the demand dues specified under Recovery Certificate No. 9319 of 2026 in accordance with SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: November 06, 2026
- Payment / Compliance deadline as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Impact Assessment

Enforces regulatory compliance and recovery of outstanding dues from non-compliant entities, maintaining market integrity without broader systemic market impact.
