## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of merchant banker Swaraj Shares & Securities Private Limited from July 09, 2025, to July 11, 2025, covering the inspection period from April 01, 2022, to March 31, 2025. Consequently, adjudication proceedings were initiated under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992, for multiple alleged violations of SAST Regulations, Merchant Bankers Regulations, ICDR Regulations, and PIT Regulations.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Swaraj Shares & Securities Private Limited (PAN: AAGCS8286D, SEBI Reg. No.: INM000012980).
- Inspection Period: April 01, 2022 to March 31, 2025.
- Alleged violations span open offer management in G.D. Trading and Agencies Limited (now RRP Semiconductors Limited), merchant banker operational standards, ICDR disclosure norms, and insider trading compliance.
- Show Cause Notice was issued on September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and penalty proceedings under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act for non-compliance with statutory and regulatory obligations by intermediaries.

## Compliance Requirements

- Merchant bankers must ensure rigorous adherence to due diligence, disclosure standards, code of conduct, and internal controls mandated under SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2022 – March 31, 2025
- Inspection Dates: July 09, 2025 – July 11, 2025
- SCN Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory enforcement on merchant bankers regarding open offers, corporate disclosures, and compliance frameworks.
