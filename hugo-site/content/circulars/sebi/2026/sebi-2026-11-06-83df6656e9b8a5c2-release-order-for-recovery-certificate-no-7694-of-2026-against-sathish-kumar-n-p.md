## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order for Recovery Certificate No. 7694 of 2026 against Sathish Kumar N (PAN – DUWPS2496M) in the matter of Arvind Remedies Limited. This release order concludes or withdraws recovery proceedings and attachments previously initiated against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- Recovery Certificate: No. 7694 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sathish Kumar N (PAN – DUWPS2496M).
- Company Matter: Arvind Remedies Limited.
- Order Nature: Release Order indicating completion of recovery or lifting of attachment.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions under the SEBI Act, 1992, specifically concerning the release of recovery certificates upon settlement or compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant authorities are notified to lift any freezing of accounts or assets previously attached under the recovery certificate against the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the resolution and closure of regulatory recovery proceedings for the individual associated with Arvind Remedies Limited, with minimal broader market impact.
