## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated November 06, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026 drawn against Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas. This release order relates to the conclusion of recovery proceedings and cancellation of attachment notices regarding trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order and cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action concluded and recovery proceedings withdrawn pursuant to compliance/satisfaction of dues under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are notified of the release and withdrawal of attachment orders against the designated individual's accounts and assets.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: November 06, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9230 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects resolution of recovery proceedings and lifting of asset attachments concerning Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
