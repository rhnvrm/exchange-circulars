## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7142 of 2026 filed by Yatendra Sharma against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appeal pertains to queries regarding the winding-up of CRB Mutual Fund's Arihant Mangal Growth Scheme governed by Delhi High Court orders in CO.PET. 379/2009 (SEBI v. CRB Capital Markets Ltd.), specifically concerning Special Cell procedures and claim verifications.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details regarding the Special Cell constituted pursuant to Delhi High Court orders, claim procedures for dematerialised shares, public notices issued, and status of verification for specific Bank of Punjab Ltd. shares (Folio No BOP003998).
- SEBI's CPIO had responded that queries seeking clarifications or opinions do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Regarding the specific claim for Bank of Punjab Ltd. shares, SEBI noted that the claim was not included in the 34th interim report or the Delhi High Court order dated September 1, 2026, and thus fell outside the Special Cell's purview.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an administrative order under the RTI Act, 2005.
