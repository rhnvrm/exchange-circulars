## Summary

SEBI, based on feedback from market participants and to facilitate ease of doing business, issued a circular reducing the cooling off period for illiquid private listed Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) undertaking Offer for Sale (OFS). Paragraph 19.2 of Chapter 1 of SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, has been amended accordingly.

## Key Points

- The cooling off period for private listed InvITs whose units are illiquid is revised to ±8 weeks.
- Retail investor provisions specified in paragraph 19.1 do not apply to private listed InvITs OFS, and such OFS shall remain open for only one day (T day).
- Trading lot for InvITs OFS shall be the same as the secondary market trading lot prescribed under SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014.

## Regulatory Changes

- Paragraph 19.2 under Chapter 1 of SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, has been modified to incorporate the new cooling-off period of ±8 weeks for illiquid private listed InvITs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Recognized stock exchanges are advised to implement necessary systems, update relevant bye-laws, rules, and regulations, and disseminate the provisions to market participants and investors.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026 (Come into force with immediate effect).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational flexibility and eases fundraising and divestment mechanisms via OFS for private listed InvITs and their unit holders.
