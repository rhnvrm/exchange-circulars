## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order dated November 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 drawn against Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q). This order relates to recovery proceedings and trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued on November 11, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9228 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q).
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Action: Official release/cancellation of recovery proceedings and lifting of related attachments.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement order concluding recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following resolution or recovery of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are notified of the release and cancellation of attachment notices under Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects conclusion and settlement of recovery proceedings against the noticee in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
