## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Certificate No. 9325 of 2026 dated November 12, 2026, pursuant to Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Section 222 of the Income-tax Act, 1961. A total sum of Rs. 5,21,000/- along with further interest, costs, and charges is demanded from multiple noticees in the matter of Ultracab (India) Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9325 of 2026.
- Total Dues: Rs. 5,21,000/- plus interest, costs, and expenses.
- Underlying Order: Adjudicating Officer Order No. Order/MS/SM/2026-27/32592-32611 dated August 07, 2026.
- Company Involved: Ultracab (India) Limited.
- Noticees include Nitesh Parshottambhai Vaghasiya, Artiben Pankajkumar Shingala, Nitaben Pravinbhai Shingala, and other associated entities/individuals.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action under existing provisions of Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees are required to pay the demanded sum of Rs. 5,21,000/- along with applicable interest and recovery costs immediately to avoid further coercive recovery proceedings under the Income-tax Act and SEBI Act regulations.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 07, 2026
- Notice of Demand Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- This recovery proceeding has company-specific enforcement relevance for Ultracab (India) Limited and the named noticees, with negligible broader systemic impact on the wider stock market.
