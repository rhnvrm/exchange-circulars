## Summary

SEBI has notified that the SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026, originally notified on October 8, 2026, will come into force on November 7, 2026. Eligible persons may apply for settlement in accordance with these new regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026 come into force on November 7, 2026.
- FAQs, settlement amount calculation facility, online application forms, help desk framework, and explainer videos have been made available on the SEBI website.
- Eligible entities and persons can file settlement applications through the online portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026 starting November 7, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible persons seeking settlement of administrative and civil proceedings must file applications through the online portal in accordance with the Settlement Regulations, 2026.
- Applicants can utilize the provided calculation tool on the SEBI website to determine settlement amounts prior to filing.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Commencement Date: November 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides a streamlined and structured framework for settling administrative and civil proceedings with SEBI, featuring dedicated online calculation tools, FAQs, and a specialized help desk to assist applicants.
