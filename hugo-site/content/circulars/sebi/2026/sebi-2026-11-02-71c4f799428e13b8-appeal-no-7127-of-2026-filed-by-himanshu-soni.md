## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7127 of 2026 filed by Himanshu Soni under the Right to Information Act, 2005, concerning Action Taken Reports (ATRs) for complaints lodged on SEBI SCORES and SMART ODR platforms.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of Action Taken Reports (ATRs) for multiple complaints filed on SEBI SCORES and SMART ODR.
- SEBI CPIO responded that SCORES complaint handling is electronic, action history and ATR details are accessible directly on SCORES via user login credentials, and no separate external file notes are maintained.
- For SMART ODR complaints, the information sought was stated to be not available with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations or policy changes introduced; reinforces existing RTI disclosure practices regarding electronic grievance redressal portals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clarifies that grievance action history on SCORES is accessible to complainants online via credentials, satisfying RTI transparency norms without requiring separate manual file creation.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 15, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: October 01, 2026
- Appeal Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational clarity on accessing grievance redressal histories through digital platforms (SCORES and SMART ODR).
