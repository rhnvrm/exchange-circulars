## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7131 of 2026 filed by Ramapati Kuar under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought detailed land allotment records and status regarding a PACL certificate (No. U23008xxx). The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that all PACL refund matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought 10 points of information under the RTI Act regarding land allotment details and status of PACL Certificate No. U23008xxx.
- SEBI CPIO stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing precedence that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession.
- Clarified that refund status and disposal of PACL properties are exclusively managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; order reinforces existing RTI Act provisions and jurisdiction over PACL refunds.

## Compliance Requirements

None for market participants; applicants seeking PACL refund details should approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

No impact on market operations or listed companies. Reaffirms procedural stance on RTI queries concerning PACL refunds.
