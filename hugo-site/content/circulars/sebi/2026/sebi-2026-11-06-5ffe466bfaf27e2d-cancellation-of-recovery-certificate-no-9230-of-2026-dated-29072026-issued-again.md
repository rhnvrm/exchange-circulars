## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026 dated July 29, 2026, which was previously issued against Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas. This recovery proceeding and subsequent notice/attachment pertained to investigations into trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9230 of 2026
- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 29, 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited
- Action: Cancellation of Recovery Certificate and withdrawal of related enforcement measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Official revocation and cancellation of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following settlement or compliance of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries, banks, and financial institutions are advised of the cancellation of recovery proceedings and release of any corresponding freeze or attachment on accounts/assets of the noticee relating to this certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Recovery Certificate: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides relief to the individual by revoking enforcement and recovery actions previously initiated in the matter of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
