## Summary

Bhupinder Singh filed an RTI application dated September 07, 2026, seeking various records, circulars, and policy notes related to LODR and related-party transactions, fixed deposits, donations/grants, and CSR fund routing through related entities for listed banks. Following the CPIO's response and subsequent appeal dated October 04, 2026, the Appellate Authority reviewed the application for disposal under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested circulars, master circular provisions, and internal policy notes from April 1, 2014, to September 7, 2026, regarding related-party transactions between listed banks and foundations, trusts, societies, or Section 8 entities.
- Information was sought regarding the disclosure treatment under the LODR framework for fixed deposits accepted by banks from related parties.
- Queries covered whether donations/grants, expense reimbursements, deputed staff costs, premises/IT support, or interest on deposits with sponsored foundations are reportable related-party transactions.
- Requests also included data on listed banking entities disclosing related-party transactions with foundations, annual report review observations on CSR, deficiency/settlement letters, and exchange guidance.
- Details on investor complaints and references received concerning listed-company CSR foundations and related-party deposits from January 2021 to September 2026 were requested.
