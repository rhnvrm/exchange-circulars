## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the simplification of client on-boarding and rationalization of the risk management framework at KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs). Based on recommendations from a working group and public consultations, certain modifications are made to the KYC Master Circular to centralize common supplementary client information and standardize upload requirements for intermediaries.

## Key Points

- Centralization of supplementary client information common across intermediaries at the KRA level to ease subsequent on-boarding.
- Intermediaries are required to upload additional details such as CKYC ID, Digital Postal Index Number (DIGIPIN), and Officially Valid Document (OVD) expiry/validity dates into the KRA system.
- Updates affect Part II of the Account Opening Form (AOF) requirements and post-KYC upload timelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Paragraph 6 of Section 1 (Know Your Client Requirements for the Securities Market) of the KYC Master Circular is updated to mandate the collection of CKYC ID, DIGIPIN (if available), and OVD expiry date in Part II of the AOF.
- Paragraph 84 of the KYC Master Circular is updated to require intermediaries to upload specific Part II details within three working days of completing initial KYC, including income range, FATCA/CRS information, PEP status, occupation, CKYC ID, DIGIPIN, and OVD expiry date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries must update their account opening forms and internal systems to collect and upload CKYC ID, DIGIPIN, and OVD expiry dates.
- Within three working days of completing initial KYC, intermediaries must upload the specified Part I and Part II AOF information onto the KRA system.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026.
- Effective immediately as per regulatory applicability.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines multi-intermediary client on-boarding by centralizing supplementary profile and KYC data at the KRA level.
- Reduces redundant documentation and data entry for clients registering with multiple market intermediaries.
