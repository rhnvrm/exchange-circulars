## Summary

SEBI and the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA), in collaboration with Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) and RTAs, are jointly organizing the 8th 'Niveshak Shivir' in New Delhi on November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Jointly organized by SEBI, IEPFA, MIIs (BSE, CDSL, NSE, NSDL), and major RTAs.
- Aims to facilitate the transfer of unpaid dividends (up to 7 years).
- Assists investors with KYC and nomination updates.
- Helps in the transmission of securities and resolving pending IEPFA claims.
- Venue: India Habitat Centre, Jacaranda Hall, Lodhi Road, New Delhi on November 17, 2026 (10 AM to 4 PM).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shareholders or their representatives attending must bring official government identification documents such as Voter ID, Aadhaar Card, PAN card, or Driver's license.

## Important Dates

- Event Date: November 17, 2026 (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM)
- Circular Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides direct assistance to retail investors for claiming unpaid dividends and resolving long-pending securities transmission or IEPFA claims.
