## Summary

SEBI has issued an ex-parte interim order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Le Lavoir Limited and Others. The examination revealed prima facie findings regarding suspected price and volume manipulation in the scrip of Le Lavoir Limited, irregularities in preferential allotment of warrants, questionable funding of subscriptions by certain preferential allottees, utilization and movement of preferential issue proceeds, coordinated trading patterns, and non-disclosure of subsequent developments.

## Key Points

- **Ex-Parte Interim Order:** Issued by SEBI against Le Lavoir Limited and 14 other noticees including Ashok Dilipkumar Jain, Kajal Ashok Jain, Viha Ashok Jain, Sachin Vishnu Kapse, Ghantiram Foods Private Limited, Shree Vrajendra Foods Private Limited, and others.
- **Preferential Allotment & Funding:** Investigation uncovered systematic mechanisms involving preferential allotment of warrants and dubious funding arrangements to subscribe to the issue.
- **Price & Volume Manipulation:** Noticees contributed significantly to artificial price and volume movements in the scrip of Le Lavoir Limited.
- **Communications & Disclosures:** WhatsApp communications and corporate announcements highlighted concerted actions and non-disclosure of critical developments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of preventive and restrictive directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 against entities involved in market manipulation and fraudulent schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and concerned entities must comply with the interim directions, freezing of securities/accounts, and prohibitions on accessing the securities market as specified in the detailed order.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** November 2026
- **Effective Date:** Immediate upon issuance

## Impact Assessment

- Protects investors from fraudulent market practices and curbs artificial inflation of scrip prices through misuse of preferential warrants and coordinated trading.
