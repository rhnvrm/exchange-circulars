## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular permitting Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) to participate in non-cash (physically) settled non-agricultural commodity derivative contracts, expanding beyond the previously allowed cash-settled contracts and indices.

## Key Points

- FPIs are allowed to participate in non-agricultural commodity derivative contracts (except option in goods) and indices comprising non-agricultural commodity derivatives.
- Participation is permitted up to the commencement of the tender/staggered delivery period, requiring FPIs to unwind or roll over open positions prior to this period.
- Three trading days prior to the tender/staggered delivery period, fresh positions resulting in an increase in existing positions in expiring futures are restricted (excluding option devolvements).
- Residual open positions at the end of the day preceding the start of the tender period devolve upon the designated TM/TCM under special arrangements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Paragraph 4.2.2.(i) of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives Segment is revised to permit physically settled non-agricultural commodity derivative contracts for FPIs.
- Specific provisions added regarding tender/staggered delivery periods, position unwinding/rollover, and position devolvement to designated TM/TCM.

## Compliance Requirements

- Recognized stock exchanges and clearing corporations must update their operational frameworks and systems to accommodate FPI participation in physically settled non-agricultural commodity derivatives.
- Designated Trading Members (TM) / Trading and Clearing Members (TCM) must manage position devolvements according to the prescribed operational framework.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Deepens institutional participation and liquidity in the commodity derivatives segment by opening up physically settled non-agricultural commodity contracts to FPIs.
