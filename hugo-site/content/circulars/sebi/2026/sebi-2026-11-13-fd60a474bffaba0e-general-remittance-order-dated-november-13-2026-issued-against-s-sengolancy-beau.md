## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7711 of 2024 against S Sengolancy Beaula in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This action enforces recovery of dues pursuant to prior regulatory proceedings.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 7711 of 2024.
- Noticee / Defaulter: S Sengolancy Beaula.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions and banks are required to comply with remittance orders to transfer attached funds toward satisfying outstanding recovery certificates.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights SEBI's ongoing recovery and enforcement measures against entities and individuals associated with Arvind Remedies Ltd.
