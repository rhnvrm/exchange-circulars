## Summary

This document is an appellate order under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) passed by the Appellate Authority of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regarding Appeal No. 7124 of 2026 filed by Shri Bhupinder Singh against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought extensive information regarding scheduled commercial banks, SEBI circulars on listed entities' donations/grants, CSR foundations, charitable trusts, Section 8 companies, related-party classifications under LODR provisions, audit committee/shareholder approvals, and related adjudicatory records.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Bhupinder Singh.
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai.
- **Application Date**: September 08, 2026.
- **CPIO Response Date**: September 30, 2026.
- **Appeal Date**: October 04, 2026 (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/E/26/00376).
- **Subject Matter**: Information requests concerning listed commercial banks' universe (public and private sector during FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26), SEBI guidelines on donations/grants/CSR foundations/trusts/Section 8 companies, related-party transaction (RPT) classifications under LODR, and relevant query/adjudicatory records.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy frameworks were introduced by this order; it deals solely with an RTI appeal seeking information and records regarding existing regulatory frameworks and disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance actions are required by market participants as this is an administrative RTI order addressing information disclosure requests.

## Important Dates

- **Application Date**: September 08, 2026
- **CPIO Response Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Appeal Date**: October 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct regulatory or market impact as it addresses an individual RTI query regarding bank-linked NPO and CSR transactions.
