## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated November 13, 2026, against Murali Prakash Naidu under Recovery Certificate No. 7707 of 2024 in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This enforcement action is directed towards the recovery of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts ordered against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Order Type: General Remittance Order
- Date of Order: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 7707 of 2024
- Noticee / Defaulter: Murali Prakash Naidu
- Company Involved: Arvind Remedies Ltd

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of unpaid dues and penalties through attachment and remittance orders.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, debtors, and designated entities holding funds or assets on behalf of the noticee must remit or freeze the specified amounts pursuant to the Recovery Officer's directions.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: November 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Reference: RC No. 7707 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous recovery mechanisms and enforcement actions against defaulters and former promoters/directors associated with non-compliant entities.
