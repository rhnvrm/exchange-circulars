## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance dated October 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 2001 of 2019 against defaulter Chandrakant A. Parekh (PAN: AHXPP5708J). This action relates to the realization of dues and penalties in the matter of IPO irregularities during 2003-2005.

## Key Points

- General Remittance notice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 2001 of 2019.
- Defaulter: Chandrakant A. Parekh (PAN: AHXPP5708J).
- Matter: IPO Irregularities (2003-2005).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of disgorgement amounts and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and intermediaries are required to remit attached funds and maintain freezes on accounts standing in the name of the defaulter as specified in the recovery certificate proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates continuous SEBI enforcement and recovery efforts against defaulters from historical IPO investigations.
