## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Essen Speciality Films Limited on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) pursuant to its migration from the SME Emerge platform.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Essen Speciality Films Limited
- Scrip Code / Symbol: ESFL
- Segment: Capital Market Segment (Main Board)
- Action: Migration from SME Emerge platform

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard migration process from SME platform to Main Board as per SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must comply with ongoing listing agreements, disclosures, and regulations applicable to Main Board securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date of migration and trading on Main Board as per exchange notification.

## Impact Assessment

Provides enhanced visibility and liquidity for Essen Speciality Films Limited following its transition to the Main Board segment.
