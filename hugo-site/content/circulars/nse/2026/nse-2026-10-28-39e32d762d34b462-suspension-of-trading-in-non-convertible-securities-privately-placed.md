## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited due to redemption, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited
- **ISIN:** INE121A07MZ5
- **Suspension Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption
- **Regulation:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational suspension reflecting corporate action (redemption).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No broader market impact as this is a standard suspension resulting from the redemption of debt securities.
