## Summary

Trading in equity shares of Veefin Solutions Limited will be migrated from the SME Platform of BSE to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) with effect from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veefin Solutions Limited
- Symbol: VEEFIN
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0Q0M01015
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Number of Securities: 25,539,417 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 25539417
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes

## Regulatory Changes

The securities are admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to migration from the SME Platform of BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

The equity shares shall be traded in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors, subject to lock-in details as specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares of Veefin Solutions Limited can trade on the NSE Main Board normal market segment starting November 11, 2026, enhancing liquidity and visibility.
