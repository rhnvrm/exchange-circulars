## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular detailing common violations and deficiencies observed during inspections of Trading Members and Authorised Persons (APs) for the half-year period from October 2025 to March 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines common irregularities concerning Authorised Persons, including unauthorized fund transfers, unauthorized trading using client credentials, mis-selling, assured returns, and unapproved promotional content.
- Highlights deficiencies in Trading Member books, such as non-permissible fund transfers between USCNBA, DSCNBA, and other bank accounts.
- Directs Trading Members to review their systems, strengthen compliance mechanisms, and ensure rigorous oversight of Authorised Persons.
- Provides regional office contact details and helpdesks for member support and clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiterates existing compliance standards and inspection requirements without introducing new regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must institute robust internal controls and systems to address the observed deficiencies.
- Ensure proper inspection of Authorised Persons, maintenance of pre-trade confirmations, correct UCC mapping, and mandatory display of statutory notices at AP offices.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Inspection Period Covered: October 2025 to March 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Promotes higher compliance standards across broker networks and mitigates operational and regulatory risks for Trading Members and Authorised Persons.
