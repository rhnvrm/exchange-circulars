## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced measures regarding companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Dollex Agrotech Limited has been included under this framework, attracting a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- Dollex Agrotech Limited (Symbol: DOLLEX) has satisfied criteria for inclusion under the high encumbrance framework.
- A minimum 75% margin will be levied in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segment on all open positions as of October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework includes BPL, Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited, Dollex Agrotech Limited, Eureka Forbes Limited, Indo Tech Transformers Limited, and Race Eco Chain Limited.
- This measure is in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of a 75% margin requirement on securities identified under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 28(3) for high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of the minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments for the shortlisted securities starting October 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Inclusion / Margin Application: October 29, 2026 (on open positions as of October 28, 2026).
- Circular Date: October 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants dealing in DOLLEX and other listed securities under the framework face higher margin requirements (75%), potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in the affected scrips.
