## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) Action Taken Report (ATR) for the financial year 2025-26. Trading members with open vulnerabilities identified by CERT-In empanelled auditors must submit their ATR and compliance report through the Member Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to all trading members who have open vulnerabilities reported in their VAPT Report for FY 2025-26.
- Submission deadline is November 30, 2026.
- Submission is complete only upon successful submission and digital signing of the ATR by the auditor to the Exchange.
- Delayed submission, non-submission, or failure to close vulnerabilities within prescribed timelines will attract penalties and disciplinary action.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiterates compliance requirements established under previous circulars including NSE/INSP/74185 and penal provisions under NSE/INSP/73792.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must navigate the Member Portal via Inspection > VAPT > Submit VAPT ATR report, provide member remarks for open vulnerabilities, download the ATR PDF, sign the document (externally if the e-lock utility is unavailable), upload the signed PDF, and submit it before the deadline.

## Important Dates

- Due Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure prompt compliance and remediation of IT vulnerabilities to prevent regulatory penalties and operational restrictions.
