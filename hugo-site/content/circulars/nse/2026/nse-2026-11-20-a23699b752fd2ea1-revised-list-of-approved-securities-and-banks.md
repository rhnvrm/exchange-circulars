## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has released a revised list of approved securities and banks forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets. The circular outlines the permitted quantities, overall permitted quantities across all segments, and memberwise permitted quantities for various equity shares, subject to Value at Risk (VAR) haircuts and prudential norms.

## Key Points

- Revised list of equity shares approved for the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Applicable haircut for the listed securities is based on VAR (Value at Risk).
- Value-based prudential norm specifies that the exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.
- Includes major listed equities such as ABB, Adani group companies, Axis Bank, Bajaj group companies, Bharti Airtel, Coal India, ETERNAL, HCL Technologies, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the approved list of securities eligible for non-cash collateral and liquid asset components in clearing and settlement operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure that the exclusion or inclusion of pledged securities adheres to the specified prudential norms (maximum 25% margin shortfall at Clearing Member level).
- Monitor permitted quantities across all segments as per the revised schedule.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading members and clearing members utilizing non-cash collateral and equity shares for margin requirements, requiring alignment with the updated permitted quantities and VAR haircut frameworks.
