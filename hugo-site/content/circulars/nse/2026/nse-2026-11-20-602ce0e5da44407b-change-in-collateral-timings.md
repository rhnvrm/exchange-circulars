## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a partial modification regarding timings for collateral addition and release in the Currency Derivatives Segment based on member feedback.

## Key Points

- Revised cut-off times for collateral processes in the Currency Derivatives Segment.
- Allocation cut-off extended from 11:30 PM to 11:55 PM.
- G-Sec Addition via E-KUBER extended from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- G-Sec Allocation extended from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification to previous circular reference nos. 75317 (dated July 21, 2026) and 75458 (dated July 29, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

All members and custodians must update their internal operational timelines and systems to align with the new collateral and G-Sec cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Members and custodians get extended operational windows for collateral allocation and G-Sec additions through E-KUBER, reducing end-of-day operational stress and improving liquidity management.
