## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the movement of 1 security (Blue Chip India Limited) to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Blue Chip India Limited (Symbol: BLUECHIP, ISIN: INE657B01025).
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026.
- **Surveillance Action:** Moved to Stage II of GSM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading will be available in Trade for Trade segment.
- Price band applicable shall be 5% or lower.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take adequate precautions while trading in the security.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 11, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in BLUECHIP will be restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement and 50% ASD requirement, increasing capital requirements for buyers and eliminating netting off.
