## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision in collateral and G-Sec related cut-off timings for the Futures & Options segment, based on feedback received from members. These changes modify previous circular references and will take effect from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Allocation cut-off time revised from 11:30 PM to 11:55 PM.
- G-Sec Addition via E-KUBER revised from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- G-Sec Allocation cut-off time revised from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
- All other collateral-related timings from circular ref. nos. 75321 (July 21, 2026) and 75463 (July 29, 2026) remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification of operational cut-off timings for collateral management and G-Sec processing within the Futures & Options segment.

## Compliance Requirements

All members, custodians, and PCMs must update their internal operational workflows and systems to comply with the new cut-off timings effective November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The extended windows for G-Sec addition and collateral allocation provide operational flexibility to market participants, reducing end-of-day execution bottlenecks.
