## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers fresh issues of Commercial Papers (CPs) from multiple corporate and financial institutions.
- Issuing entities include Aditya Birla Money Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, IIFL Finance Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, and IDBI Capital Markets & Securities Limited.
- Allotment date for the CPs is November 19, 2026, with maturity/redemption dates ranging across January and February 2027 (e.g., 04-Jan-2027 to 18-Feb-2027).
- Total face value per unit is typically 500,000 with varying issue prices.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard listing notification under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must comply with ongoing reporting, disclosure, and redemption obligations associated with listed commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Date of Allotment: November 19, 2026
- Date of Effect / Listing: November 20, 2026
- Redemption Dates: January 4, 2027, through February 18, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Operational listing update for debt market participants facilitating secondary market trading of the listed commercial papers.
