## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Tata Capital Limited pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A due to redemption.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tata Capital Limited
- **ISIN:** INE306N07NO7
- **Suspension Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation reflecting the redemption and suspension of the debt security from trading on the exchange platform.
