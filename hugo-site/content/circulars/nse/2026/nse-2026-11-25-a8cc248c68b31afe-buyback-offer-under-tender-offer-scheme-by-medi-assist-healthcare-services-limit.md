## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the upcoming buyback offer of equity shares by Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited under the Tender Offer scheme. The issue period is scheduled from November 27, 2026, to December 3, 2026, with an offer price of Rs. 425 per equity share for up to 11,59,199 shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited
- **Security Symbol:** MEDIASSIST
- **ISIN:** INE456Z01021
- **Issue Type / Offer Type:** Fixed price / Buyback
- **Issue Size:** Up to 11,59,199 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 425/- per equity share
- **Issue Period:** 27-November-2026 to 03-December-2026
- **Bidding Platform:** Web-based IPO/Tender Offer bidding platform (https://eipo.nseindia.com)
- **Merchant Banker:** IIFL Capital Services Limited
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs or via admin terminals to create branches and users.
- Bidding time and custodial confirmation: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on issue closure up to 4.00 p.m.).
- Eligible investor categories include FII, MF, IC, FI, OTH, CO, IND, and NOH.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** 27-November-2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** 03-December-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates efficient tender offer buyback participation for shareholders and trading members through NSE's web-based bidding platform.
