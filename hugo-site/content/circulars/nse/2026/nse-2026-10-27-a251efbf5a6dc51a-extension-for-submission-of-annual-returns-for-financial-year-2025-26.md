## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular extending the due date for submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26 to December 31, 2026. This extension aligns with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Due date for submission of Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 is extended till December 31, 2026.
- Filings must be submitted through the ENIT portal (https://enit.nseindia.com/MemberPortal/).
- Based on MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 which relaxed additional fees for filing financial statements and annual returns under the Companies Act, 2013.
- Non-submission within the extended due date will attract disciplinary actions as stipulated in Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) issued General Circular No. 05/2026 granting relaxation of additional fees and extension of time for filing financial statements (AOC-4 series up to December 31, 2026) and annual returns (MGT-7, MGT-7A) under the Companies Act, 2013 for FY 2025-26.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to submit their Annual Returns for the Financial Year ended March 31, 2026, on or before December 31, 2026, via the ENIT portal. Members are also reminded to maintain Networth at all points of time.

## Important Dates

- MCA Circular Date: October 24, 2026
- NSE Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Extended Due Date for Annual Returns: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational relief to exchange members and corporate entities by extending statutory compliance deadlines without incurring additional penalty fees for late filings.
