## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 22,342,255 equity shares of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited with effect from November 04, 2026, pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement approved by the NCLT Allahabad Bench.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ennature Bio Pharma Limited
- Symbol: ENNATURE
- ISIN: INE2WCT01016
- Series: BE (Trade for Trade)
- No. of Securities: 22,342,255 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each
- Distinctive Number Range: 20001 to 22362255
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Trading lot size is 1.
- Subject to pre-open session as per SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will initially be available for trading in Series 'BE' (Trade for Trade) and subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI circular no. SEBI/CIR/ISD/1/2010 dated September 2, 2010.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective / Listing Date: November 04, 2026
- NCLT Order Date: July 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors can trade in the equity shares of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited under the designated symbol ENNATURE in the trade-for-trade segment starting November 04, 2026.
