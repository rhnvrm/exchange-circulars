## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with disclosures under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: EAAA India Alternatives Limited
- Share Holding Pattern Filed under: Reg. 31(1)(a) post-issue.
- Security Type: Equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Continuous compliance with SEBI LODR regulations and timely submission of specified securities holding patterns.

## Important Dates

- Date of Filing / Listing: 29.10.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public trading and market participation in the equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited.
