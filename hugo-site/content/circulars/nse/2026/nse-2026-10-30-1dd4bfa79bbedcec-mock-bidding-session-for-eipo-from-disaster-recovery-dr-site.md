## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock bidding session for eIPO from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled for October 31, 2026. Members do not need to make any configuration changes and can connect using the standard URL.

## Key Points

- Mock bidding session scheduled for October 31, 2026.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site (URL: https://eipo.nseindia.com/).
- No changes required at member end for DR site connection.
- Bids placed during the mock session will not attract any fund or security obligations.
- Security involved: Mock Govt Security (Symbol: MCKGS, Series: GS, Lot size: 10000, Price: Rs. 100).
- Market timings: Normal Market Open at 12:45 hrs, Normal Market Close at 17:00 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine operational mock drill.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to actively participate in the mock session to test connectivity. Announcements and updates will be posted on www.nseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Mock Session Date: October 31, 2026 (12:45 hrs to 17:00 hrs).

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a routine mock trading session without any live financial or security settlement obligations.
