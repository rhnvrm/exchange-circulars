## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 07, 2026, in the Capital Market segment.
- NEAT+ version remains at 7.9.0 (Download path: /cmftp/common/NEATPlus790).
- Reminder regarding discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10827) from December 07, 2026 for NNF members.
- Gateway details provided for new encryption mechanisms (Port 10831 and 10833).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock trading announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Members using NNF software are requested to review migration instructions for new encryption mechanisms ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline and participate in mock testing as required.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 07, 2026
- Discontinuation of old interactive port 10827: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; provides members an opportunity to test trading connectivity and order routing in a simulated environment.
