## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Dr. Agarwals Eye Hospital Limited pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Dr. Agarwals Eye Hospital Limited
- Symbol: DRAGARWQ
- Series: EQ
- Reason: Scheme of Amalgamation
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026 (closing hours of trading on November 17, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

Suspension executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the suspension and halt trading in the security from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Last Trading Date: November 17, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding equity shares under symbol DRAGARWQ will face trading restrictions due to the Scheme of Amalgamation.
