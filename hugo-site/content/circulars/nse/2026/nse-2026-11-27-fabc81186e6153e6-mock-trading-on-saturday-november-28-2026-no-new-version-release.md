## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The session will test trading operations across both Primary and DR sites with no new software version release.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: November 28, 2026.
- Trading Session-1 (Primary Site): Pre-open open at 10:30 Hrs, Normal Market open at 10:45 Hrs, Normal Market close at 12:15 Hrs.
- Trading Session-2 (DR Site): Pre-open open at 13:00 Hrs, Normal Market open at 13:15 Hrs, Normal Market close at 13:30 Hrs.
- Live Re-login Window: 18:30 to 19:00 Hrs.
- Default password for mock trading: Neat@EG1.
- No financial obligations or fund pay-in/pay-out will arise from mock trades.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular pertains solely to operational readiness and mock testing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members operating in the EGR segment are advised to note the schedule, verify login and broadcast parameters, and participate in the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 28, 2026
- Live Re-login: November 28, 2026 (18:30 - 19:00 Hrs)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures system readiness and connectivity for members trading in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment with zero financial risk.
