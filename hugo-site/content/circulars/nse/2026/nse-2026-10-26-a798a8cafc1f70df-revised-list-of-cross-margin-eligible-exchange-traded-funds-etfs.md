## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margin benefits, superseding circular no. 0347/2026 dated September 28, 2026. The revised list and minimum quantity requirements take effect from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised list of cross margin eligible ETFs published by NSE Clearing Limited.
- Effective date: October 28, 2026.
- Supersedes circular no. 0347/2026 (NCL/CMPT/76594) dated September 28, 2026.
- Specifies minimum quantity requirements for each eligible ETF symbol.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the approved securities and minimum quantity criteria for cross-margining in the capital market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and trading members must incorporate the updated ETF eligibility list and minimum quantity thresholds when managing cross-margin positions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts margin optimization and collateral management for market participants utilizing cross-margining facilities with eligible ETFs.
