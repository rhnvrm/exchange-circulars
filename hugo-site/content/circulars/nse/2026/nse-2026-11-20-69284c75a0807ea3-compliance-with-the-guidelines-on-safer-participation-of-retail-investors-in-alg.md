## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding compliance with guidelines on the 'Safer participation of Retail investors in Algorithmic trading'. Following complaints about the misuse of static IP facilities by certain exchange-empaneled algo providers under the guise of 'Tech-savvy' retail clients, the exchange has reiterated strict compliance mandates and outlined specific penal actions for non-adherence.

## Key Points

- Static IPs are strictly intended for 'Tech-savvy Retail Investors' who develop their own algorithms using programming knowledge, as defined by SEBI circular dated February 4, 2025.
- Trading Members and Algo Providers must not provide static IP access to retail clients who do not fall under this category.
- Trading Members registering tech-savvy retail clients must obtain a specific undertaking confirming compliance with the definition and waiving recourse to the Exchange Investor Grievance mechanism for losses incurred due to algorithm logic.
- Clear penal actions are specified for trading members (financial penalties with escalation) and algo providers (suspension or revocation of empanelment status) for non-compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of restrictions on hosting algos via static IP at client ends unless the client is a genuine tech-savvy retail investor developing their own algorithms.
- Mandatory undertakings required from tech-savvy retail clients regarding programming knowledge and waiver of grievance mechanism recourse.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure undertakings are obtained prior to registering any tech-savvy retail client for algorithmic trading.
- Algo Providers and Trading Members must immediately cease providing static IP access to non-qualifying retail clients.
- Exchanges will monitor compliance closely and enforce penalties for contraventions (e.g., Rs. 5,000 per client for missing undertakings, escalating on repeat instances; revocation of empanelment for algo providers).

## Important Dates

- Applicable with immediate effect from November 20, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases compliance overhead for trading members and algo providers offering retail algorithmic trading solutions.
- Eliminates unauthorized usage of static IP setups for retail clients who do not write their own code, enhancing risk management and market integrity.
