## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of a further issue of 5,51,289 equity shares of Krishival Foods Limited, effective from November 02, 2026. These shares represent the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares into fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Krishival Foods Limited
- **Symbol:** KRISHIVAL
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE0GGO01015 (Temporary ISIN: IN80GGO01014)
- **Number of Securities:** 5,51,289 equity shares
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** From 25067262 to 25618550

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for further issuance and conversion of partly paid-up securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving the conversion of partly paid-up shares to fully paid-up shares, increasing the total listed fully paid-up equity capital of Krishival Foods Limited with minimal market disruption.
