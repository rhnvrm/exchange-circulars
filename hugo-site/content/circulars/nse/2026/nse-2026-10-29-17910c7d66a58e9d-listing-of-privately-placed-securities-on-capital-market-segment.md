## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment for B N Agritech Limited and REC Limited, effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities will be identified solely by their designated codes for trading.
- Market lot for both securities is set at 1.
- Effective date of listing: October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities must adhere to the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 27-Nov-2028 (B N Agritech Limited), 31-Oct-2031 (REC Limited)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital market listing with minimal direct impact on broader market operations.
