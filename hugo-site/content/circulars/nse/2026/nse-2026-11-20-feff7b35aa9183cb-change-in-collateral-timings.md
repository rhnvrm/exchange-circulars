## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular announcing revised timings for collateral-related processes in the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment. Based on member feedback, changes have been made to the cut-off times for Cash and FDR addition/allocation as well as G-Sec addition and allocation through E-KUBER, effective November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised cut-off times for Cash, FDR, and G-Sec addition and allocation.
- Cash addition window available from 3:00 PM on days with only an evening commodity trading session.
- Partial modification to earlier circulars (Download Ref No. 75320 dated July 21, 2026, and 75461 dated July 29, 2026).
- All other collateral process timings remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Cash and FDR Allocation:** New cut-off time revised to 11:30 PM (previously 11:55 PM).
- **G-Sec Addition (Via E-KUBER):** New cut-off time revised to 5:00 PM (previously 9:00 PM).
- **G-Sec Allocation:** New cut-off time revised to 8:00 PM (previously 10:00 PM).
- **Cash and FDR Addition:** Remains at 10:00 PM.

## Compliance Requirements

All members, custodians, and participants (PCMs) trading in the Electronic Gold Receipt segment must update their internal operational schedules to comply with the revised collateral timing cut-offs.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational workflows for clearing and settlement in the Electronic Gold Receipt segment will need adjustment to align with the earlier G-Sec addition and allocation cut-off times.
