## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) is conducting a mock session on November 21, 2026, to test the Software as a Service (SaaS) model for two-way portability across clearing corporations, pursuant to SEBI circular dated December 20, 2023. NCL will invoke its SaaS setup between 14:30 hrs and 15:00 hrs and operate using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) between 15:15 hrs to 16:40 hrs.

## Key Points

- Mock session scheduled for November 21, 2026, to familiarize and train members on SaaS functionalities.
- NCL SaaS setup invocation time: 14:30 hrs – 15:00 hrs.
- NCL operational window under SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL): 15:15 hrs to 16:40 hrs.
- Members must reference previous guidelines in NCL circulars no 056/2023 and 059/2024.
- Existing login credentials can be used; new credentials can be requested via support@nsccl.co.in.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of quarterly mock sessions for SaaS-based business continuity across clearing corporations as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to actively participate, test all associated functionalities (RTRMS, Collateral, Extranet links), and ensure readiness on the mock day.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Mock Session Date: November 21, 2026
- SaaS Invocation Window: 14:30 hrs – 15:00 hrs (November 21, 2026)
- SaaS Operational Window: 15:15 hrs – 16:40 hrs (November 21, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures operational resilience and seamless business continuity for clearing and settlement through inter-clearing corporation portability.
