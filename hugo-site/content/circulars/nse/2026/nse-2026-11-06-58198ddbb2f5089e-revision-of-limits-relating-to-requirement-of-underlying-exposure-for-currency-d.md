## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular modifying the provisions of Clause 5.3.3 of the SEBI Master Circular dated December 30, 2024, regarding threshold limits for taking unhedged positions in foreign exchange and currency derivatives contracts involving INR. This change aligns with the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Domestic clients and FPIs may take long or short positions in currency derivatives contracts involving INR without establishing underlying exposure up to a single limit of USD 5 million equivalent notional value across all currency pairs and stock exchanges.
- FPIs must ensure short positions across all stock exchanges and contracts in FCY-INR pairs do not exceed USD 5 million.
- FPIs taking long positions in excess of USD 5 million must have underlying exposure in Indian debt or equity securities, including mutual fund units.
- Domestic clients taking positions in excess of USD 5 million are subject to conditions specified in RBI Master Direction dated July 05, 2016, and RBI A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clause 5.3.3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024, is modified to incorporate new USD 5 million threshold limits and underlying exposure requirements in accordance with RBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock exchanges and clearing corporations must put in place systems for implementation, make consequential bye-law/rule amendments, and communicate status to SEBI.
- Stock brokers and clearing members must be informed of the revised provisions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective: Immediate implementation as directed by SEBI

## Impact Assessment

- Affects market participants, FPIs, and domestic clients trading in currency derivatives by updating unhedged position thresholds and documentation requirements.
