## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets for clearing members. The circular outlines the permitted quantities, ISINs, security names, and applicable VaR haircuts along with value-based prudential norms.

## Key Points

- Updated list of equity shares and securities eligible for the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Applicable haircut for the listed securities is set to VaR (Value at Risk).
- Imposition of value-based prudential norms where the exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.
- Comprehensive annexures covering overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of approved securities list for collateral and liquid asset computation.
- Enforcement of prudential limits on pledged security exclusion to prevent excessive margin shortfalls.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must verify their pledged securities and collateral holdings against the revised approved list.
- Ensure adherence to the 25% margin shortfall threshold upon exclusion of pledged securities.
- Rebalance collateral portfolios if any held securities are modified or excluded in the revised list.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects clearing members maintaining non-cash liquid assets and collateral margins.
- Requires operational adjustments in risk management systems to reflect updated permitted quantities and VaR haircuts.
