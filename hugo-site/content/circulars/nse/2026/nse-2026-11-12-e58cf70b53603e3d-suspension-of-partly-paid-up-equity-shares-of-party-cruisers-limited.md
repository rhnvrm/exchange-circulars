## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in partly paid-up equity shares of Party Cruisers Limited (Symbol: PARTYPP, ISIN: IN906ZX01014) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. The suspension takes effect from the closing hours of trading on November 12, 2026, making it effective from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Party Cruisers Limited
- **Symbol:** PARTYPP
- **ISIN:** IN906ZX01014
- **Reason for Suspension:** Suspension on account of first call
- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026 (w.e.f. closing hours of November 12, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- Action executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the suspension and ensure that trading in the security is halted from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026 (closing hours of November 12, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the partly paid-up equity shares of Party Cruisers Limited is suspended due to the first call, temporarily halting market liquidity for this instrument.
