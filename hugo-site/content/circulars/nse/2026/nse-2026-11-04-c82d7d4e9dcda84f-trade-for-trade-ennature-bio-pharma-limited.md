## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective November 19, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ennature Bio Pharma Limited
- Symbol: ENNATURE
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Reference Circular: NSE/CML/77244 dated November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, securities meeting specified criteria are eligible for movement between trading segments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should note the change in trading series for Ennature Bio Pharma Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for Ennature Bio Pharma Limited shares, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
