## Summary

Genus Power Infrastructures Limited has approached the Exchange for usage of the web-based bidding platform for its upcoming buyback offer under the Tender Offer scheme. The issue period is scheduled from November 04, 2026, to November 11, 2026, at a fixed price of Rs. 312/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name / Acquirer:** Genus Power Infrastructures Limited
- **Security Symbol:** GENUSPOWER
- **Series:** BB
- **ISIN:** INE955D01029
- **Issue Size:** Up to 47,50,000 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 312/- per equity share
- **Offer Type:** Buyback (Fixed price, Tender Offer scheme)
- **Issue Period:** 04-November-2026 to 11-November-2026
- **Merchant Banker:** IIFL Capital Services Limited
- **Registrar:** Niche Technologies Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

The buyback offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members of the Capital Market segment are eligible to participate in the Tender Offer Facility.
- Members already using the web-based IPO bidding platform can use their existing user IDs.
- Members without system access must log into the admin terminal and create branches/users (User ID format: Adminxxxxx where xxxxx is the Member Code).
- Bidding and custodial confirmation time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on issue closure: 9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.).

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** 04-November-2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** 11-November-2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides eligible shareholders of Genus Power Infrastructures Limited an exit and liquidity opportunity through the tender offer buyback mechanism during the active bidding window.
