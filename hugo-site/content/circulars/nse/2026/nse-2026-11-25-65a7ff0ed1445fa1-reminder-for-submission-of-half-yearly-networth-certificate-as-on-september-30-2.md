## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited issued a reminder to all clearing/self-clearing members regarding the submission of the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026. The due date for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Members are advised to submit the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, on or before November 30, 2026.
- Net worth requirement shall be Base Net worth or Variable Net worth, whichever is higher, as per Chapter X of SEBI (Stockbrokers) Regulations, 2026.
- Clearing/Self-Clearing Members registered with NSE are required to submit their Net worth Certificate to NSE only via Samuhik Prativedan Manch, which will share it with other Clearing Corporations.
- Non-submission or shortfall will result in penal actions as prescribed in NCL Master Circular and administrative circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of compliance under Chapter X of SEBI (Stockbrokers) Regulations, 2026 and SEBI PR No. 58/2026 regarding Samuhik Prativedan Manch sharing mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and self-clearing members must submit the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, via the designated NSE/NCL portal on or before November 30, 2026.
- Members not registered with NSE must submit directly via the NCL portal (https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/).

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2026: Net worth certificate cutoff date.
- November 30, 2026: Due date for submission of the Networth Certificate.

## Impact Assessment

- Failure to submit by the due date or failure to rectify shortfalls will invite disciplinary and administrative actions as per NCL master circulars.
