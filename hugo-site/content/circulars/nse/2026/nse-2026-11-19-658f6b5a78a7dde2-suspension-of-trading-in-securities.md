## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the equity shares of Compuage Infocom Limited (COMPINFO) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations, Part A. The suspension is on account of the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench admitting the company into liquidation.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Compuage Infocom Limited (Symbol: COMPINFO)
- Regulatory Authority: Hon'ble NCLT, Mumbai Bench
- Order Details: Order dated November 18, 2026, passed in IA(LIQ.)/94/2025 IN C.P. (IB)/329(MB)2023 admitting the company into liquidation.
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 20, 2026 (closing hour of trading on November 19, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares of Compuage Infocom Limited is suspended in accordance with Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations, Part A, following the initiation of liquidation proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and investors must take note that no further trading in the equity shares of Compuage Infocom Limited will be permitted on the exchange platform effective from November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NCLT Order Date: November 18, 2026
- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares of Compuage Infocom Limited will face complete trading restrictions on the exchange platform as the company undergoes liquidation proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).
