## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited (IPO) under symbol PARASHLTH and ISIN INE661T01029.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paras Healthcare Limited
- Symbol: PARASHLTH
- ISIN: INE661T01029
- Specific listing date will be announced through a separate circular.

None.

Trading members should take note of the upcoming security listing and update their trading and back-office systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.
