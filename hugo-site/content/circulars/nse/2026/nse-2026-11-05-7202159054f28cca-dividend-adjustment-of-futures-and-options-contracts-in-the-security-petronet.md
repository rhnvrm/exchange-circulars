## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in PETRONET LNG LIMITED on account of a dividend of Rs 7/-. The effective ex-date for the adjustment is November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** PETRONET
- **Company Name:** PETRONET LNG LIMITED
- **Corporate Action:** Dividend
- **Face Value:** Rs 10/-
- **Dividend Amount:** Rs 7/-
- **Ex-Date & Effective Date:** 06-Nov-2026
- **Revised Option Strike Prices:** Adjusted in accordance with SEBI guidelines; details provided in Annexure 1 and contract files.

## Regulatory Changes

- F&O contracts are adjusted for corporate actions pursuant to SEBI guidelines.
- Strike prices and base prices are adjusted, and lot sizes are rounded off to the nearest integer as applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must load the updated contract.gz / MII contract (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread files on their trading applications before trading on the ex-date.
- Files are available via the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) and the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Contract Expiry:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate contract valuation and risk management for market participants trading PETRONET derivatives following the dividend payout.
