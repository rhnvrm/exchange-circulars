## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited
- Symbol: BCSSL
- Transfer from: Trade for trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Reference Circular: NSE/CML/77305 dated November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are moved from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling settlement segment following standard SEBI IPO listing guidelines and subsequent exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the change in series from BE to EQ for BCSSL effective from November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational shift enabling normal rolling settlement for Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited shares post-IPO.
