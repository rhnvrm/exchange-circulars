## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme of FDC Limited. Trading for the buyback is scheduled from November 19, 2026, to November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X
- Settlement No.: 2026139
- Trade Start Date: 19-NOV-2026
- Trade End Date: 26-NOV-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 02-DEC-2026
- Settlement Date: 03-DEC-2026
- Demat shares must be tendered exclusively by placing Early Pay-in of Shares through the early pay-in mechanism of depositories for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be completed well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable; routine settlement calendar issuance for corporate action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of shares is done in advance.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be executed post receipt of funds pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Important Dates

- Trade Start Date: November 19, 2026
- Trade End Date: November 26, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: December 2, 2026
- Settlement Date: December 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution, clearing, and settlement of shares tendered under the FDC Limited buyback offer.
