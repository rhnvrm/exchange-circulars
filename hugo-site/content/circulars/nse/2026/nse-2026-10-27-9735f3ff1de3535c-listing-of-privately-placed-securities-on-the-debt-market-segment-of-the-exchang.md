## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 27, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue and re-issue of commercial papers admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Issuing companies include Sundaram Home Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, LIC Housing Finance Limited, Jio Credit Limited, and Kotak Securities Limited.
- Allotment date for the securities is October 26, 2026 and October 27, 2026.
- Total of 8 company issuances listed with specific ISINs and redemption dates ranging from November 2026 to September 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Listing executed pursuant to standard regulatory provisions under NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must comply with ongoing reporting and redemption obligations associated with listed debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Effective Date: October 27, 2026
- Allotment Dates: October 26, 2026 and October 27, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Varying from November 27, 2026 to September 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital market operations facilitating debt trading and liquidity for commercial papers.
