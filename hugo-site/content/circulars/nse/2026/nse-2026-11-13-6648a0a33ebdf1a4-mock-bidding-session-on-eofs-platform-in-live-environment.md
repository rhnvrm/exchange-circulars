## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock bidding session on the eOFS platform in the Live environment scheduled for November 14, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 01:00 PM.

## Key Points

- Mock bidding session takes place on November 14, 2026, between 11:00 AM and 01:00 PM.
- Live Web URL for the platform is https://eofs.nseindia.com.
- Bids placed during the mock session will not attract any financial or securities obligations.
- Members are requested to place bids in the Employee Category under symbol "TESTOFS1".
- Security parameters include test securities TESTOFS1 and TESTOFS3.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock bidding session.
- Members should use the specified test symbol "TESTOFS1" under the Employee Category.
