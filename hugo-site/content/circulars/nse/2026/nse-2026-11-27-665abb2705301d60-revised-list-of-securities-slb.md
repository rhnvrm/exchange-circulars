## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of securities available for transactions under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of December 2026, pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/18/2013 dated May 30, 2013, and consolidated Circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Period:** December 2026.
- **Reference Circulars:** SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/18/2013 dated May 30, 2013, and NSE Clearing Ltd. consolidated Circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.
- **Approved Securities:** Annexure-I contains the comprehensive list of eligible securities for SLBS transactions.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine periodic update of approved securities for the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme in compliance with established SEBI and NSE Clearing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and Custodians must take note of the revised list of approved securities for SLBS transactions effective for December 2026 and ensure compliance with operational parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Effective Month:** December 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants with the updated scope of eligible scrips for lending and borrowing transactions during December 2026, ensuring operational continuity in SLBS.
