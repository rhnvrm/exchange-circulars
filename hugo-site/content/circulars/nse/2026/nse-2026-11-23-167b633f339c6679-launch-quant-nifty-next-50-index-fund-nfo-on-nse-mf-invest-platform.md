## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the Quant Nifty Next 50 Index Fund NFO by Quant Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 25, 2026, until December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Quant Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW-Payout, and IDCW-Reinvestment options).
- NFO Window: November 25, 2026 to December 07, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- ISINs include INF966L01FO0, INF966L01FP7, INF966L01FN2, INF966L01FR3, INF966L01FS1, and INF966L01FQ5.
- Allotment Date across schemes is scheduled for December 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard NFO operational circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 09, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: November 25, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 07, 2026
- Allotment Date: December 09, 2026
- Fund Clearance Deadline: December 09, 2026 (03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Standard platform onboarding and transaction availability for market participants investing in the new index fund offering.
