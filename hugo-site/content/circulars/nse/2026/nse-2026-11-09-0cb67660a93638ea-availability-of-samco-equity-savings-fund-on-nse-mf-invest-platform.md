## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that Samco Equity Savings Fund of SAMCO Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Samco Equity Savings Fund (SAMCO Mutual Fund)
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Schemes Included:**
  - Samco Equity Savings Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF0K1H01339, Category: Equity)
  - Samco Equity Savings Fund - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF0K1H01347, Category: Equity)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine scheme availability notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the specified schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform in compliance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for Samco Equity Savings Fund on the NSE platform.
