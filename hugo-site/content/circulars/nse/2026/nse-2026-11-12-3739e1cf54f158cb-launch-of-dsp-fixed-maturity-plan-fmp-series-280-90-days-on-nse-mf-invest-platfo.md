## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days of DSP Mutual Fund for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 13, 2026, to November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes Offered:
  - DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days-Regular-Growth (ISIN: INF740KA1ZS6)
  - DSP Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) – Series 280 - 90 Days-Direct-Growth (ISIN: INF740KA1ZT4)
- Category: Debt
- Transaction Window: November 13, 2026 to November 17, 2026 (till 11:45 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 18, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- Transaction Start Date: November 13, 2026
- Transaction End Date: November 17, 2026 (till 11:45 pm)
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 18, 2026 (till 03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and trading of debt-oriented fixed maturity plan units for investors through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
