## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the takeover offer under the tender offer scheme for G-Tec Jainx Education Limited by G-TEC Education Private Limited and Mr. Roychand Chenraj. The live bidding session is scheduled from November 18, 2026, to December 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: G-TEC Education Private Limited (Acquirer 1) and Mr. Roychand Chenraj (Acquirer 2).
- Issue Period: 18-Nov-2026 to 02-Dec-2026.
- Offer Price: Rs. 29/- per equity share.
- Issue Size: Up to 26,49,166 equity shares.
- Security Symbol: GTECJAINX, ISIN: INE586X01012.
- Live URL for Members: https://eipo.nseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.
- Order modification (quantity) or cancellation shall not be permitted for takeover offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members of the Capital Market segment are eligible to participate.
- Bidding time and custodial confirmation time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on issue closure day: 9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.).
- Members without existing access can log in to the admin terminal to create branches and users.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 18, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: December 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables eligible shareholders of G-Tec Jainx Education Limited to tender their shares through the web-based bidding platform during the specified issue period.
