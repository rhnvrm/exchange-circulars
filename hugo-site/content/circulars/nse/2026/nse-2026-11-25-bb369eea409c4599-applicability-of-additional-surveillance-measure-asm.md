## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Thinking Hats Entertainment Solutions Limited (Symbol: THESL, ISIN: INE0SHE01019) has been shortlisted under the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I effective November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted: Thinking Hats Entertainment Solutions Limited (THESL / INE0SHE01019) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 30, 2026 on all open positions as on November 27, 2026 and new positions created from November 30, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying specified criteria are placed under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin requirements are maintained on open positions and new positions for the affected securities as per the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion: November 26, 2026
- Open positions cut-off date for margin: November 27, 2026
- 100% margin applicable date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted security will attract a 100% margin requirement, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for market participants holding open positions.
