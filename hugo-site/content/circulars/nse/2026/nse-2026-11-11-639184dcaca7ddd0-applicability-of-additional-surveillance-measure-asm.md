## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted, including Rama Telecom Limited (RTL), which is placed under Stage I effective November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rama Telecom Limited (RTL, ISIN: INE14W901010) shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 16, 2026, on all open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026 onwards.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisting of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework based on market surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must apply 100% margin on all open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026 onwards for securities under Long-Term ASM.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Shortlisting: November 12, 2026
- Open positions cutoff date: November 13, 2026
- 100% Margin effective date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements (100%) for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for affected entities such as Rama Telecom Limited (RTL).
