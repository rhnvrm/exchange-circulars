## Summary

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission to dealings is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must comply with standard operational and reporting requirements applicable to debt market segment listings.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Admission Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from December 24, 2026 to May 25, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of commercial papers providing debt market liquidity and short-term funding instruments for the issuing entities.
