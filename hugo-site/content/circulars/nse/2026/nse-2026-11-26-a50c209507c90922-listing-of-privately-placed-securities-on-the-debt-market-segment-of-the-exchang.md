## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing of privately placed commercial papers and debt securities issued by several corporate entities on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue of Commercial Papers by Tata Capital Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, and The Ramco Cements Limited.
- Re-issue of Commercial Papers by Kotak Securities Limited.
- Re-issue of Other Debt Issue (NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited 8.50% 2032 Sr PP7).
- Allotment dates span November 25-26, 2026, with redemption dates ranging from January 2027 to February 2032.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the securities have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities allotted in dematerialized mode are credited under temporary ISINs where applicable in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/DDHS/CIR/P/2020/198 dated October 05, 2022. Issuers and participants must ensure compliance with depository and trading regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: November 26, 2026
- Allotment Dates: November 25-26, 2026
- Redemption Dates: January 20, 2027 to February 5, 2032

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and liquidity of privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers on the exchange debt market segment.
