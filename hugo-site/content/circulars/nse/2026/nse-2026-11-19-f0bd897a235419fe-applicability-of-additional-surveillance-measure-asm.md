## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the shortlisting of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Balaxi Pharmaceuticals Limited has been placed under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I effective November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted Security:** Balaxi Pharmaceuticals Limited (Symbol: BALAXI, ISIN: INE618N01022) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.
- **Margin Requirements:** 100% applicable rate of margin effective November 25, 2026, on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created from November 25, 2026 onwards.
- **Nature of Measure:** Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of Long-Term ASM Stage-I surveillance measures and associated margin restrictions on specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin collection on open and new positions for the affected scrip in accordance with the specified timeline.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 19, 2026
- **ASM Effective Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Open Position Date for Margin:** November 23, 2026
- **Margin Applicability Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations (100%) for trading in the affected security may impact liquidity and trading volumes for BALAXI.
