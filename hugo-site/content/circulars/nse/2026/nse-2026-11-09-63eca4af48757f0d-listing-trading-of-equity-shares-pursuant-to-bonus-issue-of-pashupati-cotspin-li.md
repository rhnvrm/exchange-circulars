## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and trading of 23,67,60,000 equity shares of Pashupati Cotspin Limited pursuant to a bonus issue, with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pashupati Cotspin Limited
- Symbol: PASHUPATI
- ISIN: INE124Y01028
- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Number of Bonus Equity Shares: 23,67,60,000
- Distinctive Number Range: 157840001 to 394600000
- Effective Date for Trading: November 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing of further securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the designated security codes and effective listing date for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 13, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing & Trading: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the total listed equity base and liquidity of Pashupati Cotspin Limited on the exchange platform.
