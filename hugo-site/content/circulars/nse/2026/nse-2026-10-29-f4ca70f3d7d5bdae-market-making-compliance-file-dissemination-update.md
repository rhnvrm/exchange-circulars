## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the dissemination of a separate market making compliance file for Small & Medium Enterprises (SME) scrips traded in the 'Periodic Call Auction Session (PCAS) Market', effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- NSE currently disseminates market making compliance files for SME scrips traded in 'Normal Market' on an hourly basis.
- Effective November 02, 2026, a second separate file for SME scrips in the PCAS Market will also be provided hourly on Extranet (/cmftp/Onlinebackup).
- Two separate files will now be available: MMComp_TM Code_CT_DDMMYYYY_hhmmss (Normal Market) and MMComp_PCAS_Session_TM Code_DDMMYYYY_hhmmss (PCAS Market).
- Defined bid-offer spread continues to be published daily in 'Security.txt' under 'common/ntneat'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a dedicated compliance file format for SME scrips traded under the Periodic Call Auction Session (PCAS) Market.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members acting as market makers for SME scrips must ingest and adhere to the dual-file compliance reporting structure as applicable.
- Members remain obligated to adhere to SEBI and Exchange market maker responsibilities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Circular Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update affecting market makers of SME scrips in PCAS market segments, requiring technical adjustments to parse the new compliance file format.
