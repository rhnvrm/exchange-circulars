## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for the equity shares of Varvee Global Limited (Symbol: VGL) effective from November 27, 2026, on account of a stock sub-division from Rs. 5/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Limited
- Symbol: VGL
- New ISIN: INE273D01035
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 5/- to Rs. 2/-
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- ISIN code update for equity shares of Varvee Global Limited in the depository system.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems with the new ISIN for all trades executed on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate identification and settlement of equity shares following the sub-division of face value. Minimal direct operational impact on routine trading other than system ISIN update.
