## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the criteria are subject to a minimum 100% margin, movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST), or movement across ESM stages effective November 02 and November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 03, 2026, on open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created from November 03, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules, including 100% margin requirements and shifting of identified securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and note the segment shifts from EQ/SM to BE/ST.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Stage II / Price Band):** November 02, 2026
- **Effective Date (Margin / Segment Shift):** November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on liquidity and trading operations for the specified securities due to 100% margin mandates and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions.
