## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a partial modification to previous circulars regarding timings for collateral addition and release in the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) segment, based on feedback received from members. The revised cut-off timings will be effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Allocation cut-off time extended from 11:30 PM to 11:55 PM.
- G-Sec Addition (via E-KUBER) cut-off time extended from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- G-Sec Allocation cut-off time extended from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
- All other timings related to the collateral process remain unchanged.
- Applicable to all Members, Custodians, and PCMs in the SLBS segment.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of operational cut-off timings for collateral-related processes under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must update their internal operational procedures and systems to accommodate the extended cut-off timings for collateral and G-Sec operations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The extended cut-off windows offer greater operational flexibility and convenience for members managing collateral and G-Sec allocations in the SLBS segment.
