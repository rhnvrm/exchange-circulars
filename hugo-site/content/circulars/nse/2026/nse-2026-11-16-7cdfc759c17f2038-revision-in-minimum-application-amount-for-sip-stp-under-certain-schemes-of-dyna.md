## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular notifying members about the revision in minimum application amounts for Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) under certain schemes of Dyna SIF, as well as the introduction of Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) in DynaSIF Equity Long Short Fund of 360 One Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monthly SIP amount revised from ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 for applicable schemes.
- Quarterly SIP amount revised from ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 for applicable schemes.
- Monthly STP amount revised from ₹20,000 to ₹1,000 for applicable schemes.
- Introduction of Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) in DynaSIF Equity Long Short Fund.
- Changes become effective on November 17, 2026 on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of changes on the NSE MF Invest platform as per the notice cum addendum issued by 360 One Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and trading participants must ensure adherence to the updated minimum application limits for SIP and STP while placing orders on behalf of investors.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational updates for distributors and investors utilizing the NSE MF Invest platform for 360 One Mutual Fund transactions.
