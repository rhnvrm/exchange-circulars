## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issuance of Commercial Papers admitted to dealings on the debt market segment.
- Issuing entities include Axis Securities Limited, UNO Minda Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, NABARD, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, EPL Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Motilal Oswal Wealth Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, and IIFL Capital Services Limited.
- Allotment date for all securities: October 27, 2026.
- Face value across all issuances is ₹5,00,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing notification under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure adherence to debt market trading and settlement regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranges from December 15, 2026 to February 16, 2027 across various tranches.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and trading venue for the newly listed commercial papers on the debt segment without broader market impact.
