## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of SML Mahindra Limited, effective from December 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SML Mahindra Limited
- Symbol: SMLMAH-RE
- ISIN: INE294B20019
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 4,134,756
- Trading Period for Renunciation: December 01, 2026 to December 04, 2026
- Date of Suspension: December 07, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of Rights Entitlements to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the Exchange pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills regarding purchase of REs, application requirements, and the risk of lapse/extinction if neither subscribed nor renounced by the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: December 01, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation: December 01, 2026 to December 04, 2026
- Date of Suspension: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables shareholders and investors to trade rights entitlements of SML Mahindra Limited securely in the trade-to-trade segment with specific risk disclaimers.
