## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies effective from October 29, 2026, following allotments made under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective October 29, 2026.
- Covers companies including Fedbank Financial Services Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, L&T Technology Services Limited, and others.
- Allotments primarily comprise equity shares issued under ESOP.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing compliance pursuant to NSE Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed entities.
