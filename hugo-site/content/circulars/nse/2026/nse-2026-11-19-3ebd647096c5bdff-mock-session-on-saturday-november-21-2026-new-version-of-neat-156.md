## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. The session will test the new version of NEAT 1.5.6, pre-trade risk controls, closing price broadcasts, and encryption mechanism migrations.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026, across primary and DR sites.
- Introduction and testing of NEAT version 1.5.6, available for download from November 20, 2026.
- Testing of pre-trade risk controls (pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations).
- Verification of closing price broadcast via transcode BCAST_ONLY_MBP (7208).
- Reminder regarding migration away from the old interactive port (10865) encryption mechanism by December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory migration to NEAT version 1.5.6.
- Transition to new encryption mechanisms with MD5 checksum removal and immediate acknowledgment for order-related messages on specified gateway ports.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must participate in the mock trading session on November 21, 2026.
- Members using NNF must test pre-trade risk controls and closing price broadcasts, providing feedback to msm@nse.co.in.
- Members must migrate to new encryption gateway ports (e.g., ports 10850, 10869, 10871) ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline.

## Important Dates

- NEAT 1.5.6 Availability: November 20, 2026 (18:00 hrs)
- Mock Trading Session: November 21, 2026
- Pre-Trade Risk Controls & Closing Price Broadcast LIVE Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Discontinuation of Old Interactive Port (10865) Encryption Mechanism: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth software upgrades, system stability, and robust risk management for commodity derivatives trading members while upgrading encryption and security protocols.
