## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 59 further equity shares of Adani Enterprises Limited following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Enterprises Limited
- Symbol: ADANIENT
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE423A01024 (Temporary ISIN: IN8423A01015)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Number of Securities: 59 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Distinctive Number Range: 1409035624 to 1409035682

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing action under National Stock Exchange Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the designated security codes, lot sizes, and ISIN details for trading operations in the capital market segment.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact, as the issuance represents a very small quantity (59 shares) resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up shares.
