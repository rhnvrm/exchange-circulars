## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Varvee Global Limited (VGL) on account of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs 5/- per share to Rs 2/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Security:** Varvee Global Limited (Symbol: VGL, Existing ISIN: INE273D01027).
- **Corporate Action:** Face Value Split (Sub-Division) from Rs 5/- per share to Rs 2/- per share.
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** November 27, 2026.
- **EPI Instructions:** Members executing early pay-in on November 27, 2026 (Settlement No. 2026224) and November 30, 2026 (Settlement Nos. 2026224 & 2026225) must use the old/existing ISIN without adjusting for conversion.
- **Block Mechanism:** In case of early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files should not be uploaded.
- **Pool Account:** Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantities.
- **Post-Record Date:** Market participants and custodians must execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions using the new ISIN after the record date.

## Regulatory Changes

Procedural guidelines for early pay-in handling during corporate actions (face value split) involving ISIN transitions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members, custodians, and PCMs must ensure early pay-in instructions are executed using the correct ISIN (old ISIN before/on record date, new ISIN after record date) and adhere to block mechanism and pool account allocation rules.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date / Record Date:** November 27, 2026
- **EPI Dates:** November 27, 2026 (Settlement 2026224) and November 30, 2026 (Settlement 2026224 & 2026225)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and prevents discrepancies in early pay-in of securities during the face value split of Varvee Global Limited (VGL).
