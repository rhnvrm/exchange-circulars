## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund NFO of Canara Robeco Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting from November 30, 2026, until December 07, 2026, at 11:59 PM.

## Key Points

- Scheme Availability: Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund NFO is available from November 30, 2026, to December 07, 2026.
- Available Plans & Options:
  - Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund - Direct Plan: Growth Option (ISIN: INF760K01LT6), IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF760K01LV2), IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF760K01LX8).
  - Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund - Regular Plan: Growth Option (ISIN: INF760K01LS8), IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF760K01LU4), IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF760K01LW0).
- Allotment Date: December 09, 2026 for all schemes.
- Category: Equity.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 08, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 30, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 07, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Fund Clearing / Pay-in Deadline: December 08, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members an opportunity to participate in the Canara Robeco Arbitrage Fund NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform with clear timelines for subscription and fund pay-in.
