## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the introduction of additional after-market short allocation reports (SA01/SA02/SA03) for collaterals. These reports are based on snapshots in the capital market segment aligned with the timing of intraday snapshots in Commodity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Additional SA01/SA02/SA03 reports will be provided after-market hours based on snapshots aligned with Commodity Derivatives segment intraday snapshots.
- Reports detail collateral value allocated by the clearing member to the client, securities repledged at the Clearing Corporation, and EOD minimum margins.
- Collaterals and margins in these additional reports are used solely to arrive at excess collateral for the client, which is considered towards short allocation in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- No change in the current computation of peak margins during market hours or short allocation based on intraday/EOD snapshots.
- File nomenclature, location, and format remain identical to existing intraday short allocation reports.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of supplementary after-market short allocation reports (SA01/SA02/SA03) specifically aligned with Commodity Derivatives segment snapshot timings to accurately calculate excess collateral adjustments.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must review and integrate the additional after-market short allocation reports into their operational risk and collateral monitoring workflows.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members dealing across capital market and commodity derivatives segments will benefit from more synchronized collateral and short allocation assessments, reducing mismatches in excess collateral reporting.
