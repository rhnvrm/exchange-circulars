## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund (Direct Growth & Regular Growth).
- **ISINs:** INF247L01HQ6 (Direct Growth), INF247L01HR4 (Regular Growth).
- **Category:** Equity / Index Fund.
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026.
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular regarding fund availability on the platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members participating in the NSE MF Invest Platform should take note of the availability of these schemes for their clients.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to transact in the specified Motilal Oswal index fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform with standard facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP.
