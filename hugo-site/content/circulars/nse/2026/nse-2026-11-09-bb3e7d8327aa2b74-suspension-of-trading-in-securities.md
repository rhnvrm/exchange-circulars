## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the potential suspension of trading in securities of Hampton Sky Realty Limited (HAMPTON) due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026) at BSE Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hampton Sky Realty Limited (Symbol: HAMPTON).
- Non-compliance: Regulation 33 of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026).
- Compliance Deadline: December 07, 2026.
- Suspension Date: December 10, 2026, if compliance is not achieved by the deadline.
- Post-Suspension Trading: After 15 days of suspension, trading will be allowed on a Trade for Trade basis (Z category) on the first trading day of every week for six months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of SEBI Master Circular provisions regarding non-compliant entities traded under the 'Permitted for Trade' category at NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Hampton Sky Realty Limited must comply with Regulation 33 and the Master Circular provisions on or before December 07, 2026, to avoid trading suspension.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: December 07, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: December 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in securities of Hampton Sky Realty Limited will be suspended if the company fails to regularize its non-compliance status, leading to restricted liquidity and trading permitted only under the Z category (Trade for Trade basis) once per week after 15 days of suspension.
