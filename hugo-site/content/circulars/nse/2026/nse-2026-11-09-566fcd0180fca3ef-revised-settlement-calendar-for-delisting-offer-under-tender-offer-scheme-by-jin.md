## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised settlement calendar for the delisting offer under the tender offer scheme by Jindal Photo Limited, superseding previous circulars dated April 30, 2026, and October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Settlement Type:** X
- **Settlement Number:** 2026131
- **Trade Start Date:** 02-NOV-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 06-NOV-2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 12-NOV-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 13-NOV-2026

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of the settlement schedule for the ongoing delisting tender offer of Jindal Photo Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

1. Demat shares must be tendered exclusively by placing Early Pay-in of Shares through the early pay-in mechanism of depositories for Market Type Buyback.
2. On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be executed well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
3. Securities and funds pay-out will be processed following the receipt of funds pay-in on the Settlement Day.
4. Members will receive a Tender Offer Acceptance Report detailing accepted, partially accepted, and rejected orders along with rejection reason codes.
5. Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Start Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Trade End Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and custodians dealing with the delisting offer of Jindal Photo Limited must take note of the revised timelines to ensure timely execution of early pay-ins, fund settlements, and compliance with rejection reason protocols.
