## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on predefined surveillance criteria. Effective October 28, 2026, applicable margin rates will be set at 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- ST-ASM Stage I applied to securities: Eventions Limited (EVENTIONS), Om Power Transmission Limited (OMPOWER), and Vishal Nirmiti Limited (VNL).
- No securities were listed under ST-ASM Stage II or movement between stages in this circular.
- Surveillance framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely preventive due to market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I provisions requiring enhanced margins for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 50% margin requirement (capped at 100%) on all open positions as of October 27, 2026, and all new positions created from October 28, 2026, onwards for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: October 28, 2026 (on open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in Eventions Limited, Om Power Transmission Limited, and Vishal Nirmiti Limited will experience increased margin requirements (50%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips during the surveillance period.
