## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of the Axis Nifty Large Midcap 250 Index Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform for member participation from November 27, 2026, to December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: Axis Nifty Large Midcap 250 Index Fund Direct Growth and Regular Growth.
- ISINs: INF846KA1655 (Direct Growth) and INF846KA1648 (Regular Growth).
- Category: Equity.
- NFO Window: November 27, 2026 to December 11, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Allotment Date: December 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 17, 2026, till 10:00 AM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 11, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 17, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- Allotment Date: December 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational circular enabling platform transactions for a new mutual fund scheme offering.
