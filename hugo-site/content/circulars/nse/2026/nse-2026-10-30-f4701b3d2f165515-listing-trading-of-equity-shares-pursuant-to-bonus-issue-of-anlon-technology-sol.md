## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Anlon Technology Solutions Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. A total of 30,03,920 equity shares will be admitted to dealings effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Anlon Technology Solutions Limited
- Symbol: ANLON
- ISIN: INE0LR101013
- Number of Bonus Securities: 30,03,920
- Distinctive Number Range: 7509801 to 10513720
- Record Date: November 02, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify the securities exclusively by their designated security codes for trading on the system.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 02, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 03, 2026
- Effective / Listing Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity shares available for trading on the exchange platform following a bonus issue.
