## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026. The circular also reminds members to migrate from the old interactive port encryption mechanism to the new encryption mechanisms by December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** Saturday, November 07, 2026.
- **Trading Timings:** Normal market open at 11:00 hrs, close at 12:30 hrs; Position Limit/Collateral cutoff and Trade Modification end at 12:40 hrs; Live re-login between 14:00 hrs and 14:30 hrs.
- **Encryption Migration Reminder:** Discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10865) effective December 07, 2026. Members must migrate to new ports (10869 or 10871).
- **NEAT Version:** 1.5.5.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- **NOTIS Availability:** NOTIS application will remain inaccessible during the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory migration to new encryption mechanisms supporting MD5 checksum removal and immediate acknowledgement of order messages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Commodity derivatives trading members must participate in the mock session using all trading software and re-login to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, November 09, 2026.
- Ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN upload and approval before cutoff time.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 07, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** 14:00 hrs to 14:30 hrs on November 07, 2026
- **Live Operational Readiness:** November 09, 2026
- **Encryption Mechanism Migration Deadline:** December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures readiness of member trading systems and infrastructure for the commodity derivatives segment with no financial obligations arising from mock trades.
