## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of December 2026, pursuant to circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- First Leg Settlement (Settlement Type ‘L’) schedules are outlined for trade dates in December 2026, spanning settlement numbers 2026226 to 2026247.
- Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type ‘P’) schedules cover series 12/XD (Settlement Number 2026512) for December 01, 2026, as well as subsequent series up to December 2027.
- Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type ‘C’) and associated timelines are established for participants and custodians.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine release of monthly settlement schedules for SLBS operations as per existing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must adhere to the specified trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates for First Leg, Reverse Leg, and Auction settlements.

## Important Dates

- December 01, 2026: First trade date and settlement date for initial December 2026 SLBS cycles.
- December 31, 2026: Final trade date of December 2026 for First Leg settlements.
- January 01, 2027: Final settlement date for December 31, 2026 trades.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational execution and settlement tracking for market participants engaged in Securities Lending and Borrowing during December 2026.
