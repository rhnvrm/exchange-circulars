## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. There is no new version release for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** November 21, 2026.
- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- **NEAT+ Version:** Version 1.0.2 (/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus102).
- **Password:** Default password is 'Neat@EG1'.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular pertains to operational mock trading arrangements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are advised to take note of the schedule, login parameters, and connection details for testing purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to testing systems, network connectivity, and broadcast streams ahead of live trading.
