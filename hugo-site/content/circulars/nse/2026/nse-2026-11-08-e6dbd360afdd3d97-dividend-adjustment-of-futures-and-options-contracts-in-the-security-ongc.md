## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security ONGC on account of a dividend of Rs 8.00/- per share (face value Rs 5/-) with an ex-date and effective date of November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Oil & Natural Gas Corporation Limited
- **Symbol:** ONGC
- **Type of Corporate Action:** Dividend
- **Face Value:** Rs 5/-
- **Dividend Amount:** Rs 8.00/-
- **Ex-Date & Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Revised Strike Prices & Lot Size:** Revised option strike prices, futures base prices, and lot sizes will be provided one day prior to the ex-date in the latest `contract.gz` file and MII contract/spread files.

## Regulatory Changes

- Position and contract adjustments are executed in pursuance of SEBI guidelines for corporate actions on F&O contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated `contract.gz` (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread (`NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) files from the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) or NSE website prior to trading on the ex-date.
- Methodology for position adjustments shall be intimated separately by the respective Clearing Corporation.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Contract Files Availability:** One day prior to the ex-date / effective date

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding F&O positions in ONGC will experience adjustments to strike prices, base prices, and lot sizes to account for the dividend payout.
