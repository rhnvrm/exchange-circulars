## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Aditya Birla Capital Limited, effective from November 05, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Birla Capital Limited
- ISIN: INE860H08DW7
- Suspension Date: 05-Nov-2026
- Reason: Redemption
- Department: Listing
- Circular Ref. No: 1995/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/77262)

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading suspension of specific non-convertible securities in accordance with NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to note that trading in the specified securities of Aditya Birla Capital Limited stands suspended from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: November 05, 2026
- Circular Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- This is a standard debt redemption and security suspension event with limited impact on broader equity markets or operations.
