## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has released the revised list of approved securities and banks, detailing equity shares that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets across segments.

## Key Points

- Lists approved equity shares for the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Applicable haircut for securities is based on VAR (Value at Risk).
- Value-based prudential norm specifies that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the eligible collateral securities list and associated limits for clearing members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure compliance with prudential norms regarding pledged security exclusions and margin shortfalls.
- Monitor approved quantities and haircut adjustments across designated securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts trading members and clearing members utilizing equity shares as non-cash collateral for liquid assets and margin requirements.
