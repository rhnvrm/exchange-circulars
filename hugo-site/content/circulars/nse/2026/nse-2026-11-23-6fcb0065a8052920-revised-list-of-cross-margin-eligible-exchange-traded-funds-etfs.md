## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margin benefits, superseding circular no. 0375/2026 dated October 26, 2026. The revised list and minimum quantity requirements are applicable with effect from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised list of cross margin eligible ETFs published by NSE Clearing Limited.
- Effective date for the revised list is November 25, 2026.
- Specifies minimum quantity requirements (and multiples thereon) for various ETFs such as NIFTYBEES, BANKBEES, ITBEES, SBINIFTY50, HDFCNIFTY, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the eligible securities list for cross-margin computations in the Capital Market Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading and clearing members must take note of the revised ETF list and minimum quantity requirements for cross-margining calculations effective November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects margin calculations and collateral efficiency for market participants trading derivative positions hedged with eligible ETFs.
