## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the request of Shree Kanha Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. for the segmental surrender of its membership in the Currency and IRF segment.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member:** Shree Kanha Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd.
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000222633
- **Surrendered Segment:** Currency and IRF Segment
- **Surrender Approval Date:** October 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine segmental membership surrender processed by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are advised to take note of the surrender of membership by the aforementioned entity.

## Important Dates

- **Approval Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an administrative update with no direct impact on general market operations or investors.
