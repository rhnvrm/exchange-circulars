## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced adjustments to Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Oil & Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) due to a dividend of Rs 8.00/- per share. The effective date for the corporate action and contract adjustments is November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Underlying Security: ONGC (OIL & NATURAL GAS CORPORATION LIMITED)
- Corporate Action Type: Dividend
- Dividend Amount: Rs 8.00/- (Face Value: Rs 5/-)
- Ex-date & Effective Date: November 12, 2026
- Revised option strike prices and futures base prices are adjusted accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of futures and options contracts in pursuance of SEBI guidelines on corporate actions.
- Revised strike/futures base prices and lot sizes rounded off according to exchange standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz / MII contract and spread files on their trading applications before trading on the ex-date.
- Files can be obtained from the Extranet server or the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Effective / Ex-date: November 12, 2026
- Expiry Date for reviewed contracts: 23-NOV-2026

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives traders and clearing members dealing in ONGC F&O contracts must update their trading systems with the revised strike prices and contract files to ensure accurate position management and execution from the effective date.
