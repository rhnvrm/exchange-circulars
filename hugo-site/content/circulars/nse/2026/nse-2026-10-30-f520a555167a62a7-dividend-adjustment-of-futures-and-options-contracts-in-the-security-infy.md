## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Infosys Limited (INFY) due to the declaration of a dividend of Rs 26/- per share with a face value of Rs 5/-. The effective date for the adjustment is November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Infosys Limited (INFY)
- Corporate Action: Dividend
- Face Value: Rs 5/-
- Dividend Amount: Rs 26/- per share
- Ex-date & Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Revised option strike prices, futures base prices, and lot size details will be available in the latest contract.gz file one day prior to the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments to futures and options contracts are performed in pursuance of SEBI guidelines on corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread files from the Extranet server or NSE website before trading on the ex-date.
- Revised strike/futures base prices and lot sizes shall be rounded off according to exchange norms (tick size / nearest integer).

## Important Dates

- Announcement Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Updated contract files available: One day prior to the ex-date.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading F&O contracts in INFY must account for price and lot size adjustments reflecting the Rs 26/- dividend payout to ensure accurate valuation and margin management.
