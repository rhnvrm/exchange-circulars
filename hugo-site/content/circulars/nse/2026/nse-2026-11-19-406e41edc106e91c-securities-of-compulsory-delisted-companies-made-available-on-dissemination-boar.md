## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the equity shares of Poddar Housing and Development Limited will be moved to the Dissemination Board (DB) of the Exchange with effect from November 20, 2026, following its compulsory delisting.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Poddar Housing and Development Limited
- Symbol: PODDARHOUS
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Action: Moved to the Dissemination Board (DB) for trading as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- The delisting and movement to the Dissemination Board are carried out in terms of Regulation 32(1) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulation 2021, Section 21A of the Securities Contract (Regulations) Act, 1956, Rule 21 of Securities Contract (Regulations) Rules, 1957, and Exchange Bye-Laws.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the transition of the security to the Dissemination Board pursuant to SEBI letter ref. no. CFD/DCR/OW/2016/16113 dated June 06, 2016.

## Important Dates

- Previous Circular Date: October 09, 2026 (NSE/CML/76832)
- Effective Date on Dissemination Board: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Equity shares of the compulsorily delisted company will be available for buying and selling exclusively on the Dissemination Board of the Exchange starting November 20, 2026.
