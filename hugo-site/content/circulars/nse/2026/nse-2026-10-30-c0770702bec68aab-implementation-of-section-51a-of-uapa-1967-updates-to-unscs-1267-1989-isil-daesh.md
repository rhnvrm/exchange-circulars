## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967. The updates include the removal of 03 entries and amendment of 03 entries on the sanctions list.

## Key Points

- Stock exchanges and registered intermediaries must ensure compliance with Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 and SEBI AML Master Circular guidelines.
- Verification must be performed to ensure no accounts are held by individuals or entities on the updated UNSC Sanctions List.
- UNSC Note SC/16488 dated October 24, 2026 announces the removal of 3 entries from the ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- UNSC Note SC/16490 dated October 26, 2026 enacts amendments to 3 entries on the sanctions list.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of UNSC updates concerning ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List under Chapter VII of the Charter of the United Nations.
- Removal of asset freezes, travel bans, and arms embargoes for the 3 delisted entries.
- Implementation of amended details for 3 entries specified with strikethrough/underline.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries and stock exchanges must screen client databases against the updated UNSC consolidated list and 1267/1989 specific lists.
- Ensure ongoing adherence to AML/CFT obligations and verification against terrorist-linked entity lists.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- UNSC Removal Notice Date: October 24, 2026 (SC/16488)
- UNSC Amendment Notice Date: October 26, 2026 (SC/16490)

## Impact Assessment

Operational compliance requirement for intermediaries to update their screening databases and ensure adherence to SEBI AML guidelines and UAPA mandates.
