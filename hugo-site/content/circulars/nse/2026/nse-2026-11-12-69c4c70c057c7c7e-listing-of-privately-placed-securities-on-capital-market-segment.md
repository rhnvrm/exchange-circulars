## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings effective November 13, 2026.
- Includes securities from Rupeek Capital Private Limited (Symbol: 11RCPL28B, ISIN: INE0DG907396) and Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited (Symbol: AAFS32E, ISIN: INE0L6807393).
- Trading shall be identified by designated security codes in specified market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed securities to the capital market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted using the designated security codes and applicable market lots.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational update for secondary market trading of newly listed privately placed debt securities with minimal direct impact on general market operations.
