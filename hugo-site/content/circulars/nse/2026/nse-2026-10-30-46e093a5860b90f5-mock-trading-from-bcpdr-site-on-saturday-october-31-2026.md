## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment from the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) / Business Continuity Plan (BCP) site on Saturday, October 31, 2026. The mock session includes two trading sessions followed by a live re-login from the Primary site.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** October 31, 2026.
- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- **Sessions:** Trading Session-1 from Primary Site (Pre-open 08:45 to 08:53, Normal Market 09:00 to 12:00) and Trading Session-2 from DR Site (Pre-open 12:45 to 12:53, Normal Market 13:00 to 17:00, Auction Market 14:30 to 15:15, Trade modification end 17:15).
- **Re-login Timings:** Live Re-login from 18:30 to 19:00 hrs.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **Default Password:** Default password for login is “Neat@EG1”.

## Regulatory Changes

- Non-graceful shutdown drill from the primary site during the switchover to the DR site, requiring members to follow circular NSE/MSD/48662 for system consistency.
- All outstanding orders will be purged before starting trading from the BCP site.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using NNF software must clear outstanding orders of session 1 before trading commences from the BCP site.
- Members must ensure correct NEAT Adapter settings and gateway router configurations as specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026.
- **Live Re-login Window:** 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs on October 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Helps members test disaster recovery switchover mechanisms and verify readiness for live operations without any financial exposure.
