## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (NCDs) issued by Power Finance Corporation Limited on the capital market segment, effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security 1: Power Finance Corporation Limited, Symbol: 805PFCL31, Series: N0, Market Lot: 1, Coupon: 8.05%, Maturity: 17-Nov-2031, ISIN: INE134E08OG4.
- Security 2: Power Finance Corporation Limited, Symbol: 821PFCL28, Series: N0, Market Lot: 1, Coupon: 8.21%, Maturity: 16-May-2028, ISIN: INE134E08OH2.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and specific lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 16-May-2028 and 17-Nov-2031

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing procedure with minimal operational impact on market participants.
