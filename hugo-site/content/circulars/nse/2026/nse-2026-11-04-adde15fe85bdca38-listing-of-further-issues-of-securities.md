## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP), effective from November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 05, 2026.
- Participating Companies: Alivus Life Sciences Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Indoco Remedies Limited, Intellect Design Arena Limited, Medplus Health Services Limited, Nazara Technologies Limited, Raymond Limited, SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited, Sterlite Technologies Limited, TATA CONSUMER PRODUCTS LIMITED, Tata Motors Limited, Univastu India Limited, and Fujiyama Power Systems Limited.
- Securities: Equity shares allotted under ESOP with specified face value, paid-up value, and distinctive number ranges detailed in the annexure.
- Market Lot: Pari Passu / 1 as applicable per security.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities solely by their designated security codes and execute trades in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine equity dilution and listing from ESOP exercises with minimal market impact.
