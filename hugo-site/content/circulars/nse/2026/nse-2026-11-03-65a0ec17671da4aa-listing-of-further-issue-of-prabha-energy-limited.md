## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of further issue of 1,09,974 equity shares of Prabha Energy Limited with effect from November 04, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Prabha Energy Limited
- **Symbol:** PRABHA
- **ISIN:** INE0I0M01023
- **Temporary ISIN:** IN80I0M01016
- **Number of Securities:** 1,09,974 equity shares
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 342286425 to 342396398
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI guidelines regarding activation of ISIN and dematerialized mode crediting for additional share issues.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action increasing the paid-up capital base. No material disruption expected for market operations.
