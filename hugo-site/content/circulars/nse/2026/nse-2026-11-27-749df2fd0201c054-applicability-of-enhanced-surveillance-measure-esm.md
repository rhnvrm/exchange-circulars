## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective November 30 and December 01, 2026, specific securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage I, while others are moving between stages.

## Key Points

- Securities in ESM Stage I shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. December 01, 2026, on all open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created from December 01, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. December 01, 2026.
- Securities moving from Stage II to Stage I include Cubex Tubings Limited, ICDS Limited, and Valiant Laboratories Limited.
- Oneclick Logistics India Limited is shortlisted under ESM Stage I.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisted securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions for the applicable securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage movements and Margin applicability: November 30, 2026 / December 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in shortlisted scrips will be subject to stricter surveillance, 100% margins, and Trade-for-Trade settlement, impacting liquidity and position management for market participants.
