## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has informed members that the Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund (offered by Axis Mutual Fund) is being reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Axis Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund (Direct Growth & Regular Growth)
- **ISINs:** INF846KA1630 (Direct Growth), INF846KA1622 (Regular Growth)
- **Category:** Equity / Index Fund
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants can facilitate investor transactions in the aforementioned scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform in accordance with existing platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform availability and operational readiness for investors and members to transact in the Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund.
