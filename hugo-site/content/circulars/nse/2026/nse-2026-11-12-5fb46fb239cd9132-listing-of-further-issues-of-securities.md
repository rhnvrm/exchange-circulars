## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies effective from November 13, 2026, following allotments under preferential issues and ESOPs.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective November 13, 2026.
- Companies include Commercial Syn Bags Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, IDFC First Bank Limited, Kfin Technologies Limited, Polycab India Limited, and Sai Life Sciences Limited.
- Allotments comprise shares issued under preferential issues and employee stock option plans (ESOP).
- Trading in these securities will be identified by designated codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; admission notified pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the listed securities shall adhere to designated codes, lot sizes, and applicable lock-in restrictions specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing event for the respective companies with minimal broader market impact.
