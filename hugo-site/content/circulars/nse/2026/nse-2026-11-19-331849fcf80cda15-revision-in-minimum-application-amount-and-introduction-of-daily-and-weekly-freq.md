## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about the revision in minimum application amounts and the introduction of Daily and Weekly frequency options for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) across certain schemes of Unifi Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026.
- **Schemes Involved:** Unifi Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund, Unifi Flexi Cap Fund, and Unifi Liquid Fund.
- **Frequency Options Introduced:** Daily and Weekly frequencies added alongside existing Monthly frequencies.
- **Revised Minimum Amount:** Minimum transaction amount for SIP, STP, and SWP revised to INR 250/- and in multiples of Re. 1/-.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of Daily (on business days / Monday to Friday) and Weekly frequency options for systematic transactions under Unifi Mutual Fund schemes.
- Downward revision of minimum SIP, STP, and SWP amounts from previous levels (INR 500/- for STP/SWP) to INR 250/-.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors trading on the NSE MF Invest platform must update their systems and take note of the revised frequency options and minimum transaction limits for Unifi Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with greater flexibility through daily and weekly systematic transaction options and lowers the entry barrier by reducing the minimum investment amount to INR 250/- for Unifi Mutual Fund schemes.
