## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has granted final empanelment to M/s Optibase Financial Technologies Private Limited as an Algo Provider for providing Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members, following the successful fulfillment of conditions from its provisional empanelment.

## Key Points

- **Vendor Name:** M/s Optibase Financial Technologies Private Limited
- **Type of Algo:** Whitebox
- **Correspondence Address:** 315 & 317, 3rd Floor, DLF Galleria, DLF City, Phase IV, Gurugram, Haryana 122002
- **Contact Details:** Ph. No.: +91 7455951966
- **Previous Provisional Circular:** NSE/INVG/74750 dated June 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular confirms the transition from provisional to final empanelment for the specified algorithmic trading vendor.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members engaging with the empanelled vendor must ensure adherence to exchange guidelines regarding algorithmic trading solutions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 18, 2026 (With immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members to utilize whitebox algorithmic trading solutions from the newly finalized vendor on the exchange platform.
