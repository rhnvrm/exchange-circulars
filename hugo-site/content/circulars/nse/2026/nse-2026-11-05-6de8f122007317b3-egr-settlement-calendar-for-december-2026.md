## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedules for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the month of December 2026 pursuant to Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for EGR Normal T+1 settlement type '6' (Annexure A).
- Covers settlement schedules for EGR Auction settlement type '7' (Annexure B).
- Provides trade start, trade end, and settlement dates for December 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; standard periodic settlement calendar release.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and clearing members must align their trading and settlement operations with the specified December 2026 calendar for the EGR segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective for settlement cycles spanning December 2026 through January 2027 (starting 01-Dec-2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational clarity and smooth clearing and settlement workflows for Electronic Gold Receipt transactions.
