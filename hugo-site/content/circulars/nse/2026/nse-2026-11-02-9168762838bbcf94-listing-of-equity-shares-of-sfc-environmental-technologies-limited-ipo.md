## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of SFC Environmental Technologies Limited (IPO) under symbol SFCTECH and ISIN INE0PKN01028.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SFC Environmental Technologies Limited
- Symbol: SFCTECH
- ISIN: INE0PKN01028
- The specific listing date will be announced via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the upcoming listing and ensure necessary operational readiness for trading in SFCTECH.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 02, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of equity shares of SFC Environmental Technologies Limited on the Exchange upon commencement of listing.
