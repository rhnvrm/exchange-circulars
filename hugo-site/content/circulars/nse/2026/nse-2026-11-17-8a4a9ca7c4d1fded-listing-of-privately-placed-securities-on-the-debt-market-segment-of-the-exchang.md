## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Ten tranches of commercial papers (CPs) from various companies have been listed.
- Issuing companies include Piramal Finance Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, SRF Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, TATA Projects Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Blue Star Limited, and Godrej Consumer Products Limited.
- All securities have an allotment date of November 16, 2026, with maturity dates ranging from January 2027 to March 2027.
- Face value per security is generally Rs. 5,00,000 with varying issue prices.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed debt securities/commercial papers pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure compliance with NSE debt market segment trading and settlement regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Admissions: November 17, 2026
- Redemption Dates: January 14, 2027 to March 16, 2027 (depending on the specific CP issuance)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and formal trading platform for privately placed commercial papers on the exchange debt segment.
