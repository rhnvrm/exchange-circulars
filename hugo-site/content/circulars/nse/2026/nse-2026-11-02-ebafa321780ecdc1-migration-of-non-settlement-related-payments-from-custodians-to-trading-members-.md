## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has announced the revision of the process for non-settlement related payments from Custodians to Trading Members in the Cash Market segment, aligning it with the T+1 settlement cycle migration.

## Key Points

- Custodians must upload the designated payment details file (Annexure I) by 4:00 PM on the settlement day.
- NCL debits the amount from the Custodian clearing bank account and credits the Clearing Member's (linked to the Trading Member) bank account on the same day.
- Non-settlement related payments are not commingled with regular settlement obligations, are not guaranteed by NCL, and are subject to realization from Custodians.
- Reports are generated and downloaded for Trading Members and Clearing Members as per specified naming conventions and file formats.

## Regulatory Changes

- Process for non-settlement related payments transitioned from T+2 timeline to T+1 settlement cycle alignment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Custodians and Trading Members must adhere to the updated file upload timelines (4:00 PM on settlement day) and file naming conventions (<Custodian Code>_NSP_DDMMYYYY.csv).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 16th November 2026 (T+1 settlement cycle for trade date 13th November 2026).
- Example timelines:
  - Trade Date: Thursday, 12 November, 2026 | File uploaded: Friday, 13 November, 2026 | Credit: Monday, 16 November, 2026
  - Trade Date: Friday, 13 November, 2026 | File uploaded: Monday, 16 November, 2026 | Credit: Monday, 16 November, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for market participants managing non-settlement cash flows between custodians and trading members under the T+1 regime.
