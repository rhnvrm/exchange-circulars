## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing of privately placed commercial papers (CPs) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective from November 04, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers for multiple issuers have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective date of listing: November 04, 2026.
- Issuers include Angel One Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, HDFC Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, L&T Finance Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, and Bharti Telecom Limited.
- Face value of Rs. 500,000 per security across various quantities and redemption dates ranging from November 2026 to May 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; this is a standard operational circular notifying debt security listings under existing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to trading and settlement guidelines on the debt market segment.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 03, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: November 04, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Varying from November 30, 2026, to May 03, 2027 (specific to each commercial paper issuance)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange listing status for the privately placed commercial papers of the respective financial institutions and corporate issuers.
