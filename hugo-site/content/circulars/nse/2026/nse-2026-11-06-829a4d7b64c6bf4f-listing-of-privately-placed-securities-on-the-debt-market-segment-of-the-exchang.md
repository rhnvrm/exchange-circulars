## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers (CPs) on its debt market segment with effect from November 06, 2026, in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Admission of fresh issue commercial papers issued by corporate entities including Piramal Finance Limited, CEAT Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Persistent Systems Limited, and Blue Star Limited.
- Effective date of listing and dealings: November 06, 2026.
- Allotment date across issues: November 05, 2026.
- Standard face value: ₹5,00,000 per security with discounted issue prices and varying maturity dates ranging from December 2026 to November 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The securities are admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must adhere to standard trading, clearing, and settlement guidelines applicable to debt market segment securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 06, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Varying maturity dates from December 18, 2026 to November 05, 2027 (e.g., Tata Steel on 18-Dec-2026, Kotak Securities on 24-Dec-2026 & 04-Feb-2027, Piramal Finance on 28/29-Dec-2026, Axis Securities on 04-Feb-2027, Blue Star on 03-Feb-2027, CEAT on 04-May-2027, Aditya Birla Capital on 04-Nov-2027, and Persistent Systems on 05-Nov-2027).

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity and official listing status for the newly issued commercial papers on the debt segment of the Exchange.
