## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of 15,258 equity shares of Aurum PropTech Limited effective from November 27, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aurum PropTech Limited
- Symbol: AURUM
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE898S01029 (Temporary ISIN: IN8898S01010)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Number of Securities: 15,258
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure for further issuance of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities using designated codes and trade in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital change with minimal market impact.
