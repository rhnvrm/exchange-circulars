## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the details for the upcoming buyback offer under the Tender Offer scheme by Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) Limited. The issue period is scheduled from 25-November-2026 to 01-December-2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) Limited
- **Security Symbol:** JMA
- **ISIN:** INE412C01023
- **Issue Size:** Up to 20,00,000 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 112/- per equity share
- **Issue Period:** 25-November-2026 to 01-December-2026
- **Bidding Platform:** Web-based IPO bidding platform (https://eipo.nseindia.com)

## Regulatory Changes

- Participation via the web-based bidding platform in accordance with Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating in the Capital Market segment can use existing user IDs or log in to the admin terminal to create branches and users.
- Bidding time and custodial confirmation: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on issue closure up to 4.00 p.m.).

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** 25-November-2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** 01-December-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides eligible shareholders of Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) Limited an opportunity to tender their equity shares in the buyback offer through trading members using the NSE web-based bidding platform.
