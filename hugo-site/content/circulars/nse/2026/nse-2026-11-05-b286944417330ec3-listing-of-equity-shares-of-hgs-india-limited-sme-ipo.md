## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of equity shares of HGS (India) Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: HGSINDIALT
- ISIN: INE813R01012
- Issue Price: Rs. 120/- per share
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Total Number of Securities: 10,858,713 Equity Shares
- Market Lot & Market Maker Quantity: Minimum 1,200 Equity Shares
- Initial Trading Series: Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - Settlement Type ‘W’)
- Registrar & Transfer Agent: KFin Technologies Limited
- Market Maker: Hem Finlease Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars.
- Trading will initially take place in Series 'ST' and subsequently be shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type ‘N’).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market makers and trading members must adhere to the specified lot sizes, spread requirements, and trading segments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Lock-in periods vary for promoter and pre-IPO shares ranging from November 10, 2027 to July 06, 2031.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public trading and liquidity for the equity shares of HGS (India) Limited on the NSE EMERGE SME platform.
