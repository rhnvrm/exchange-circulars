## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in INFOSYS LIMITED (INFY) on account of a dividend of Rs. 26/- per share. The adjustments include modifying futures contract prices and strike prices for options contracts based on the reference rate and dividend amount.

## Key Points

- Underlying Security: INFOSYS LIMITED (INFY)
- Dividend Amount: Rs. 26/- per share
- Last Cum-Dividend Date: November 02, 2026
- Ex-Dividend Date: November 03, 2026
- Futures contracts will be marked-to-market on November 02, 2026, and carried forward at daily settlement price less Rs. 26/-.
- Option contract strike prices will have the full dividend value of Rs. 26/- deducted on the ex-dividend date and adjusted to the nearest tick size.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; this is a routine corporate action adjustment circular in accordance with existing F&O contract adjustment methodologies.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and trading members must account for the adjusted carry-forward values, revised strike prices, and updated begin-of-day margins effective November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Last Cum-Dividend Date: November 02, 2026
- Ex-Dividend Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Positions in INFY F&O contracts will reflect the price reduction corresponding to the dividend payout. Begin-of-day margins on November 03, 2026, will be computed based on the adjusted values.
