## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the name and symbol for Virat Industries Limited under the Permitted to Trade category, effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Virat Industries Limited
- New Name: BRAHM VIRAT INDUSTRIES CORPORATION LIMITED
- Existing Symbol: VIRAT
- New Symbol: BRAHMVIRAT
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. The change is based on communication received from BSE Limited regarding the corporate name and symbol change.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the change in name and symbol for trading and record purposes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminals and records with the new name and symbol.
