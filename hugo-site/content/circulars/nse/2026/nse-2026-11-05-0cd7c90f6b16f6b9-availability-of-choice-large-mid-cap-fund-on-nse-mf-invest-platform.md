## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund (Invesco Mutual Fund) on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2KCX01111)
- **Scheme Name:** Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2KCX01103)
- **Category:** Equity
- **Facilities Available:** Subscription, Redemption, SIP, STP, SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can execute transactions in accordance with standard platform guidelines and operational norms.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment options on the NSE MF Invest platform, facilitating seamless execution of transactions and systematic investment plans for investors.
