## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 05, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuing companies include Axis Securities Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Alphagrep Securities Private Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, Motilal Oswal Finvest, Reliance Retail Ventures Limited, and IIFL Capital Services Limited.
- All securities admitted are Commercial Papers (CP) with a face value of Rs. 500,000 each.
- Date of allotment for all listed commercial papers is November 04, 2026, with redemption dates ranging from December 2026 to May 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of privately placed debt securities to dealings on the Debt Market Segment in compliance with NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must ensure adherence to ongoing listing, disclosure, and redemption obligations associated with commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing / Admission: November 05, 2026
- Redemption Dates: December 18, 2026, to May 05, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates short-term debt financing and liquidity management for corporate issuers through the exchange debt segment.
