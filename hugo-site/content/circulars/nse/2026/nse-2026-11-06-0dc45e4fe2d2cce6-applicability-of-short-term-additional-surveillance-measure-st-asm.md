## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for selected securities effective November 08, 2026. Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. November 09, 2026, on all open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created from November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include AIROLAM, AVANCE, LOANFRONT, RUSHIL, TONBO, and VEDPOWER.
- Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance action is November 09, 2026, on open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions.
- Stage II listings are Nil.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework requirements for specified scrips in conjunction with existing prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Inclusion: November 08, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions (Margins): November 09, 2026 (on open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to enhanced margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants trading in these scrips.
