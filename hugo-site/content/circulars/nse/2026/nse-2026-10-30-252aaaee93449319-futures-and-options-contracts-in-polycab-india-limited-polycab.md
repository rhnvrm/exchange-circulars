## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular announcing that pursuant to the NCLAT order dated October 30, 2026, which allowed the appeal filed by Polycab India Limited and set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026 regarding IBC proceedings, POLYCAB shall move out of the IBC framework. Consequently, surveillance actions have been reverted and futures and options (F&O) contracts with January 2027 expiry will be available for trading w.e.f November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- NCLAT order dated October 30, 2026 set aside NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026 regarding IBC proceedings against Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB).
- POLYCAB moves out of the IBC framework, and corresponding surveillance actions are reverted.
- Futures and options (F&O) contracts with January 2027 expiry made available for trading in the underlying POLYCAB w.e.f November 02, 2026.
- References previous circulars: NSE/SURV/75652 (August 07, 2026), NSE/SURV/76858 (October 12, 2026), and NSE/SURV/77205 (October 30, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of Polycab India Limited from the IBC framework following NCLAT relief.
- Reversal of surveillance measures previously imposed under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants can resume trading in F&O contracts of POLYCAB in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- NCLAT Order Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date for F&O Contract Availability: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Resumption of derivative trading in POLYCAB provides normal market operations and trading continuity for market participants.
- Removal from IBC framework alleviates regulatory and surveillance restrictions.
