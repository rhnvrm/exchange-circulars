## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026, to test the new NEAT+ version 7.9.1. Additionally, the exchange outlined discontinuation timelines for version 7.9.0, closing price broadcast checks, encryption mechanism migrations for NNF users, and an additional mock session scheduled for November 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date**: Saturday, November 21, 2026, in the F&O segment across Primary and DR sites.
- **New NEAT+ Version**: Version 7.9.1 available for download from November 20, 2026 (19:00 hrs) via `/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus791`.
- **Version Discontinuation**: Existing NEAT+ version 7.9.0 will be discontinued effective from the December 05, 2026 mock session.
- **NNF Encryption & Port Migration**: Discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10837) from December 07, 2026. Members must migrate to new ports (10841/10843) supporting MD5 checksum removal and immediate acknowledgements.
- **Additional Mock**: Another mock trading session is scheduled for November 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory upgrade to NEAT+ version 7.9.1.
- Migration of NNF interactive ports and encryption mechanisms as per circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- F&O trading members must participate in the mock trading session on November 21, 2026.
- Members using NNF must check closing price broadcasts (`BCAST_ENHNCD_ONLY_MBP (17208)`) on mock day and share feedback/confirmation at msm@nse.co.in.
- Migration to new gateway router IP and ports (10841 / 10843) prior to December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NEAT+ 7.9.1 Availability**: November 20, 2026 (19:00 hrs)
- **Mock Trading Session**: November 21, 2026
- **LIVE Implementation of NNF Broadcast changes**: November 23, 2026
- **Additional Mock Trading**: November 28, 2026
- **Discontinuation of NEAT+ 7.9.0**: December 05, 2026
- **Discontinuation of Old Interactive Port (10837)**: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their trading workstations to NEAT+ 7.9.1 and update network/router configurations to ensure uninterrupted F&O trading operations and compliance with NSE's security and protocol upgrades.
