## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- Security: Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited (Symbol: MAXENGS)
- Transfer from Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM).
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026.
- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (Circular ref. no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and previous exchange circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and series for trading in MAXENGS effective from November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling segment trading for Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited, improving trading convenience and liquidity.
