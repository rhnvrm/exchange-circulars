## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has introduced interoperability in the mechanism for acquiring shares through stock exchanges pursuant to tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delisting. Pursuant to this implementation, trading members can place their orders through the exchange of their preference, irrespective of their clearing member association with a specific clearing corporation. This feature is made available exclusively through E-IPO 2.0.

## Key Points

- Interoperability introduced for tender offer mechanisms (takeovers, buybacks, and delisting).
- Members can place orders on the exchange of their preference rather than being restricted by clearing member association.
- Feature available exclusively through E-IPO 2.0 (launched via circular NSE/IPO/77157 dated October 28, 2026).
- Compliant UCCs required for placing bids.
- EPI-based RMS validation and custodian confirmations updated across clearing corporations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of interoperability among clearing corporations for tender offers pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DRMNP/CIR/P/2018/145.
- Revision of operating guidelines/scheme for tender offers under takeover, buy back, and delisting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must onboard to E-IPO 2.0 by effecting necessary changes to the authentication mechanism for API.
- Front-end access to E-IPO 2.0 portal via https://bidcollection.nseindia.com/login.
- Adherence to revised operating guidelines and terms & conditions specified in Annexure A.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 30, 2026.
- Launch of E-IPO 2.0 reference: October 28, 2026 (NSE/IPO/77157).
- Prior effective date for compliant UCCs: July 04, 2022.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines tender offer participation for trading members by enabling cross-exchange order placement through interoperability.
- Requires technical updates and onboarding to E-IPO 2.0 for market participants.
