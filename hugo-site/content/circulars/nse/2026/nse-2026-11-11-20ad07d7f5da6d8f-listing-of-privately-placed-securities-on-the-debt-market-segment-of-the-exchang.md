## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and other debt securities) on the debt market segment with effect from November 11, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026.
- **Commercial Papers Issued by:**
  - Aditya Birla Money Limited (ABML CP 08/02/27, ISIN: INE865C14RQ7, Face Value: ₹5,00,000, Quantity: 2,000)
  - ICICI Securities Limited (ISEC CP 03/02/27, ISIN: INE763G14P90, Face Value: ₹5,00,000, Quantity: 4,000)
  - HDFC Securities Limited (HSL CP 08/02/2027, ISIN: INE700G14XE1, Face Value: ₹5,00,000, Quantity: 3,500)
  - Small Industries Development Bank of India - SIDBI (SIDBI CP 08/02/27 Sr 19, ISIN: INE556F14MX2, Face Value: ₹5,00,000, Quantity: 1,00,000)
  - Godrej Agrovet Limited (GAL CP 10/12/26 Sr 25, ISIN: INE850D14WF0, Face Value: ₹5,00,000, Quantity: 1,000)
- **Other Debt Securities Issued by:**
  - Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL 8.78% 2029 Sr H1, ISIN: INE674K07176, Coupon: 8.78%, Maturity: 06-Nov-2029, Quantity: 1,50,000; ABCL 8.78% 2031 Sr H2, ISIN: INE674K07184, Quantity: 75,000)

## Regulatory Changes

Admissions of privately placed debt securities and commercial papers in accordance with Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading participants and issuers must ensure adherence to designated security codes and operational guidelines for debt market segment dealings.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** November 9, 2026 (for CPs), November 6, 2026 (for ABCL debt securities)
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Dec 10, 2026 to Nov 6, 2029 depending on the specific security issue.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations providing liquidity and trading access for newly issued commercial papers and debt securities on the exchange's debt segment.
