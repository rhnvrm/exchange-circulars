## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP are enabled on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Covers both Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the eligible schemes via the NSE MF Invest Platform adhering to standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to access Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Fund schemes directly through the NSE MF platform with standard automated investment facilities.
