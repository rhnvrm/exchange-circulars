## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision in the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) and position limits for Participants, Institutional, and Non-Institutional clients for symbol SMLMAH under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS), effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: SMLMAH
- Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) increased from 593,792 to 648,950 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit increased from 59,379 to 64,895 shares.
- Participant Limit increased from 59,379 to 64,895 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit increased from 5,937 to 6,489 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of circular NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026, and partial modification of circular NCL/CMPT/77220 dated October 30, 2026, the position limits for SMLMAH in the SLB scheme have been revised.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised limits and ensure all trading and lending activities in SMLMAH comply with the updated position thresholds.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The upward revision of position limits allows increased participation and lending/borrowing capacity for market participants in SMLMAH.
