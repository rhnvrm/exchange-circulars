## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission to dealings of various State Development Loans (SDLs) and Treasury Bills (T-bills) on its capital market segment, effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026.
- **Securities Admitted:** Includes State Development Loans (SDLs) issued by Rajasthan, Delhi, Telangana, and Meghalaya, alongside 91-day and 364-day Treasury Bills.
- **Lot Size:** A uniform trading lot size of 100 is specified for all listed securities in this circular.
- **Identification:** Securities will be identified strictly by their designated security codes and ISINs on the trading system.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine admission of securities pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the correct designated security codes, series, and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective / Listing Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for the newly issued State Development Loans and Treasury Bills.
