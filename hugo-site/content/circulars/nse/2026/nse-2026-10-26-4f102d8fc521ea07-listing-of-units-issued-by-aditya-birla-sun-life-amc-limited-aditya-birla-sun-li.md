## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of units issued by Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited under the scheme Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF, effective from October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF
- Symbol: BIRLATOTAL
- ISIN: INF209KC1746
- Number of Units: 7,703,897
- Face Value / Issue Price / Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Date of Allotment: 22-Oct-2026
- Effective Date of Trading: 27-Oct-2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Market Makers: Kanjalochana Finserv Private Limited, iRage Broking Services LLP, East India Securities Limited, Choice Equity Broking Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and designated market makers must adhere to the specified security codes, trading lots, and settlement guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing and Trading: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF units on the NSE capital market segment for investors and authorised participants.
