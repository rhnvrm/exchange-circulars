## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for certain securities. Effective November 18, 2026, shortlisted securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying ESM criteria (Annexure I) will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 18, 2026, on all open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created from November 18, 2026.
- Affected securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I: Bombay Metrics Supply Chain Limited (BMETRICS) and Natural Capsules Limited (NATCAPSUQ).
- Shift of trading segment from EQ/SM to BE/ST.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure minimum 100% margin collection on open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created from November 18, 2026 for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM measures / Margin / Segment shift: November 18, 2026 (open positions as on November 17, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement may impact liquidity and trading volume in the shortlisted securities.
