## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Vedant Poly Fabs Limited from the trade for trade segment to the rolling segment pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vedant Poly Fabs Limited
- Symbol: VPFL
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (series: SM)
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 and exchange circular no. NSE/CML/77326 dated November 06, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members take note of the segment change for the security effective from November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- November 25, 2026: Effective date for transfer to rolling segment

## Impact Assessment

- Security moves to the regular rolling settlement segment, facilitating standard trading operations.
