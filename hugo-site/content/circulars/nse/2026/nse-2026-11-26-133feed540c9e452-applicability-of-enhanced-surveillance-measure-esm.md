## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective November 30, 2026, specific securities qualifying under ESM Stage I will attract a minimum 100% margin on open positions and new creations, and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Applicability:** Securities listed in Annexure I (BRACEPORT, NATIONSTD, TERASOFT) attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 30, 2026, on all open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- **Segment Shift:** Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 30, 2026.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework measures in conjunction with existing surveillance mechanisms.
- Mandatory 100% margin requirement and Trade-for-Trade settlement for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities.
- Members must account for the shift of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Margin & Segment Shift:** November 30, 2026 (on open positions as of November 27, 2026).
- **Circular Date:** November 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in Brace Port Logistics Limited, National Standard (India) Limited, and Tera Software Limited will experience increased margin obligations (100%) and liquidity constraints due to the Trade-for-Trade settlement mechanism.
