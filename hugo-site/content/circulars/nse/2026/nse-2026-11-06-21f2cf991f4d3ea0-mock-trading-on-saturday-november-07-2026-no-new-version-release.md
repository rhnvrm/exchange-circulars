## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026. The circular also details NEAT+ version 7.9.0 release and provides crucial reminders for migrating to new NNF encryption mechanisms ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 07, 2026, in the F&O segment.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 available on NSE Extranet path `/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus790`.
- Discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with the old interactive port (10837) effective December 07, 2026.
- Gateway Router IP 172.19.13.85 with new ports 10841 (MD5 checksum removal) and 10843 (MD5 removal + immediate Ack request).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; NNF users must manually clear orders.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with regulatory requirements via mock trading or simulated environments as per SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 and NSE circular NSE/MSD/46441. Mandatory migration to updated NNF encryption mechanisms as outlined in NSE/MSD/76883.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity. NNF members must complete migration to the new encryption ports before December 07, 2026. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN approved before cutoff will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 07, 2026
- Old Interactive Port Discontinuation: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational readiness for trading members, verifies system connectivity, and facilitates a smooth transition to enhanced encryption protocols without disrupting live market hours.
