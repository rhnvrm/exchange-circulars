## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a revised list of privately issued corporate bonds and debentures made eligible under the DVP-3 settlement mechanism on тво Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment. The circular includes comprehensive details such as instrument names, symbols, ISIN codes, issue dates, maturity dates, and credit ratings for securities issued by major institutions including Reliance Industries, REC Limited, HDB Financial Services, IRFC, NABARD, LIC Housing Finance, and State Bank of India.

## Key Points

- Revised list of privately issued corporate bonds and debentures eligible for DVP-3 settlement.
- Applicable on the Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment.
- Features various instruments with distinct ISIN codes, issue dates, and maturity dates up to 2041.
- Issuers include prominent entities such as Reliance Industries, REC Limited, IRFC, NABARD, and State Bank of India.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates and maintains the roster of approved corporate debt securities eligible for institutional DVP-3 settlement on NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must verify ISIN eligibility and settlement parameters when transacting privately issued corporate bonds on the institutional platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately upon circular issuance.
- Maturity and interest payment dates range across multiple years (2025–2041) as specified per ISIN in Annexure-I.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates smooth secondary market trading and secure settlement for institutional participants dealing in privately issued corporate bonds and debentures.
