## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the Mark to Market settlement schedule for the Currency Derivatives Segment for the month of December 2026, outlining daily trade dates, settlement dates, and final settlement dates.

## Key Points

- Covers daily Mark to Market settlement dates for December 2026 trading sessions.
- Final settlement for December 2026 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures Contracts & Currency Futures and Options Contracts is scheduled for December 31, 2026.
- Final settlement for December 2026 Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts & Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts is scheduled for January 01, 2027.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the trade and settlement dates for December 2026 to ensure timely funds and securities pay-in/pay-out.

## Important Dates

- First Trade Date: December 01, 2026
- Last Trade Date / Final Settlement (Futures & Options / T-Bill Futures): December 31, 2026
- Final Settlement (Interest Rate & MIBOR Contracts): January 01, 2027
