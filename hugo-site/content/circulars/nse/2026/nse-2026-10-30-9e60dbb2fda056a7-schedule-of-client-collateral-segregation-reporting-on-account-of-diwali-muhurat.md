## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the schedule for client collateral segregation reporting on account of the Diwali Muhurat Trading session held on Sunday, November 08, 2026. Members and custodians are required to adhere to the revised reporting timelines.

## Key Points

- Diwali Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Revised reporting timelines established for trade dates between November 06, 2026, and November 10, 2026.
- Failure to submit client collateral reports will attract non-compliance penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational schedule adjustment for holiday settlement and collateral reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must submit client collateral segregation reports according to the following schedule:
- Trade Date: 06-Nov-26 | Reporting Date: 09-Nov-26
- Trade Date: 07-Nov-26 | Reporting Date: 09-Nov-26
- Trade Date: 08-Nov-26 | Reporting Date: 09-Nov-26
- Trade Date: 09-Nov-26 | Reporting Date: 11-Nov-26
- Trade Date: 10-Nov-26 | Reporting Date: 11-Nov-26

## Important Dates

- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026
- Reporting Deadlines: November 09, 2026 and November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly collateral monitoring and reporting continuity across the festive holiday session.
