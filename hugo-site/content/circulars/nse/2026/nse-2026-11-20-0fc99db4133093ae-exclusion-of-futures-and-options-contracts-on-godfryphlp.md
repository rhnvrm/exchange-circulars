## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Godfrey Phillips India Limited (GODFRYPHLP) in accordance with SEBI eligibility criteria guidelines.

## Key Points

- New expiry month contracts for GODFRYPHLP will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contract months.
- Existing unexpired contracts for November 2026, December 2026, and January 2027 will remain available for trading until their respective expiries.
- New strikes will continue to be introduced in existing unexpired contract months.
- No contracts shall be available for trading in GODFRYPHLP with effect from January 27, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation in accordance with SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, and chapter 3.5 of Exchange circular No. NSE/FAOP/73928 dated April 28, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the exclusion timeline and ensure client positions are managed or squared off ahead of the final phase-out date.

## Important Dates

- November 20, 2026: Circular release date.
- January 27, 2027: Effective date when no contracts will be available for trading in GODFRYPHLP.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding derivative positions in GODFRYPHLP must square off or settle their positions before January 27, 2027, as no further expiry contracts or new positions will be supported.
