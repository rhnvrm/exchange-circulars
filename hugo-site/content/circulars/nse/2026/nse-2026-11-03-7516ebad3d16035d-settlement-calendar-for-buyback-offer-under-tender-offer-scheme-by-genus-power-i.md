## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar and operational instructions for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme by Genus Power Infrastructures Limited (Settlement Type: X, Settlement No: 2026134).

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type & Number:** Settlement Type X, Settlement No. 2026134.
- **Trade Dates:** Trade Start Date is 04-NOV-2026 and Trade End Date is 11-NOV-2026.
- **Demat Shares:** Demat shares must be tendered exclusively by placing Early Pay-in of Shares through the early pay-in mechanism of depositories for Market Type Buyback.
- **Order Acceptance:** On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be made well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- **Reports & Rejections:** Tender Offer Acceptance Report will detail accepted, partially accepted, and rejected orders along with reason codes (Annexure I).
- **Taxation:** Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; this circular provides specific settlement schedules and procedures referencing circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026, and NSE/CML/77227 dated November 03, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must execute early pay-in of demat shares through depository mechanisms.
- On the trade end date (11-NOV-2026), early pay-in must be completed in advance for orders accepted up to 03:30 p.m.
- Members should review the Tender Offer Acceptance Report and adhere to rejection reason codes outlined in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Start Date:** 04-NOV-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 11-NOV-2026
- **Obligation Download:** 16-NOV-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 17-NOV-2026
