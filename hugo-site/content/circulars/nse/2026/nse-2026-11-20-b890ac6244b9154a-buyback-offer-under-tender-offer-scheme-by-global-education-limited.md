## Summary

Global Education Limited has announced a buyback of up to 14,75,000 equity shares at an offer price of Rs. 162/- per share under the Tender Offer scheme. The live bidding session is scheduled from November 23, 2026, to November 30, 2026, via the NSE web-based bidding platform.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Global Education Limited
- Security Symbol / Series: GLOBAL / BB
- ISIN: INE291W01037
- Issue Size: Up to 14,75,000 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 162/- per equity share
- Issue Type: Fixed price / Buyback under Tender Offer scheme
- Issue Period: 23-November-2026 to 30-November-2026
- Bidding Platform: https://eipo.nseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- Conduct of buyback through the exchange web-based bidding platform in accordance with Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs or admin terminal access.
- Custodial confirmation is required during the issue period (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.) and up to 4.00 p.m. on issue closure.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 23, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: November 30, 2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.
- Custodial Confirmation on Closure: 9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity to shareholders of Global Education Limited through the buyback tender offer mechanism on NSE.
