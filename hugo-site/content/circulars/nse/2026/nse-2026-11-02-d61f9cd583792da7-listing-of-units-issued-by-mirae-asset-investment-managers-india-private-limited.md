## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited under the Mirae Asset Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VI - 3 to 6 Months, effective from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Mirae Asset Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VI - 3 to 6 Months
- **Effective Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** October 28, 2026
- **Date of Redemption:** March 10, 2027
- **Security Symbols & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth Option: `MIRAEF7RG` (ISIN: `INF769K01RX6`, 15,29,96,291 units)
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout Option: `MIRAEF7RD` (ISIN: `INF769K01RY4`, 27,009 units)
  - Direct Plan - Growth Option: `MIRAEF7D1` (ISIN: `INF769K01RZ1`, 41,50,46,492 units)
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout Option: `MIRAEF7D2` (ISIN: `INF769K01SA2`, 3,000 units)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with applicable regulations by Mirae Asset Trustee Company Private Limited, Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited, and KFin Technologies Limited as registrar.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 28, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Date of Redemption:** March 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard admission of mutual fund units to trading on the NSE capital market segment with no direct operational or financial impact on broader market equities.
