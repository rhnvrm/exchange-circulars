## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is scheduled from November 23, 2026, to December 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Names & ISINs**:
  - Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund Regular Plan-Growth (ISIN: INF194KB1LE6)
  - Bandhan Nifty Commodities Index Fund Direct Plan-Growth (ISIN: INF194KB1LF3)
- **Asset Category**: Equity
- **NFO Period**: November 23, 2026 to December 03, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date**: December 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular is a standard operational announcement for a mutual fund NFO.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds via any valid payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 07, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date**: November 23, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date**: December 03, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline**: December 07, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date**: December 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the NFO subscription through the NSE MF Invest Platform within the specified timelines.
