## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities effective November 13 and November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL) has been shortlisted under ESM Stage I.
- Milton Industries Limited (MILTON) and The Peria Karamalai Tea & Produce Company Limited (PKTEA) move from ESM Stage I to Stage II.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 16, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework measures including 100% margin requirement on open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026.
- Shift of eligible securities to Trade-for-Trade settlement and periodic call auction price bands.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trading restrictions for affected securities.
- Monitoring of security movement across ESM stages and settlement series changes (EQ/SM to BE/ST).

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Stage II shifts and Periodic Call Auction: November 13, 2026.
- Effective date for margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade shift: November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities experience increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement limitations, enhancing market surveillance and risk management.
