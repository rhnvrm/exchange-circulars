## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has introduced a facility in its N-Mass application allowing Clearing Members and Custodians to submit Early Pay In (EPI) reversal requests for Tender Offers under the Interoperability framework.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Early Pay In (EPI) reversal request facility in the N-Mass application under Tender Offer > TENDER-OFFER-EPI.
- Role management requires assigning the "Tender Offer" service (Tender Offer-EPI) under NMASS.
- Early Pay In (EPI) Enquiry module allows viewing and downloading EPI earmarked in specified formats.
- Early Pay In Reversal File Upload module allows clearing members to request EPI reversal during the bidding period (09:00 AM to 05:00 PM on bidding days) via file upload.
- Reversals are processed only if the Early Pay-in quantity is eligible (i.e. no bid received against the early pay-in).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of operational guidelines and file formats for Early Pay In (EPI) reversals under the Interoperability framework among Clearing Corporations for Tender Offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members and Custodians must assign the "Tender Offer" service via role management in NMASS.
- Members must adhere to file nomenclature and upload timelines (09:00 AM to 05:00 PM on the bidding day) for EPI reversal requests.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026.
- Effective date for Interoperability among CCs for Tender Offer (OTB) to be communicated shortly in a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the workflow for managing Early Pay In reversals for Tender Offers for Clearing Members and Custodians through automated NMASS file uploads.
