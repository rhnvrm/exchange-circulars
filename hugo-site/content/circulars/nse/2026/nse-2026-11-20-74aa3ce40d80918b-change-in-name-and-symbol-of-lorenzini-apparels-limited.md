## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the change in name and trading symbol for Lorenzini Apparels Limited. Effective November 27, 2026, the company will operate under the new name MONTEIL LIMITED and the new trading symbol MONTEIL.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Lorenzini Apparels Limited
- New Name: MONTEIL LIMITED
- Existing Symbol: LAL
- New Symbol: MONTEIL
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a corporate identification and naming update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, back-office systems, and records with the new name and symbol for Lorenzini Apparels Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact limited to ticker and name updates across exchange systems and member terminals.
