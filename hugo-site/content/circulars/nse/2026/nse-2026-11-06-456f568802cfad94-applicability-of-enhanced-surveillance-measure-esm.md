## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for securities satisfying specified criteria. The circular outlines the inclusion of securities in ESM Stage I, movement from Stage I to Stage II, and movement from Stage II to Stage I, along with associated trading restrictions and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Global Education Limited - GLOBAL, Optimus Finance Limited - OPTIFIN) attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 09, 2026, on open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to ESM Stage I are moved from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 09, 2026.
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II (Rollatainers Limited - ROLLT) are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Securities moving from Stage II to Stage I (Supreme Engineering Limited - SUPREMEENG, Tikona Communication Limited - TIKONA) are also detailed.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of ESM framework criteria, 100% margin requirements on open and new positions, and shifting of scrips between Rolling Settlement and Trade-for-Trade segments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin collection, trade-for-trade settlement mechanisms, and applicable price bands for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II shift / price band changes: November 08, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips face increased margin obligations, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction restrictions (for Stage II), which may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes.
