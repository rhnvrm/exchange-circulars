## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category of Sundaram Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The schemes will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Sundaram Mutual Fund
- Scheme: Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP
- Eligible ISINs include Direct Growth (INF903J30032), Direct IDCW Payout (INF903J30024), Direct IDCW Reinvestment (INF903J30016), Regular Growth (INF903J30065), Regular IDCW Payout (INF903J30057), and Regular IDCW Reinvestment (INF903J30040).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a routine platform enablement circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform in accordance with applicable platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Subscriptions/Redemptions: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the new Specialized Investment Fund schemes offered by Sundaram Mutual Fund through the NSE platform.
