## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision in the cut-off timings for collateral-related processes in the Commodity Derivatives Segment, based on feedback received from members. The updated timings will be effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Allocation cut-off timing revised from 11:55 PM to 11:30 PM.
- G-Sec Addition via E-KUBER cut-off timing revised from 9:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- G-Sec Allocation cut-off timing revised from 10:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- Cash and FDR addition cut-off timing remains unchanged at 10:00 PM (available from 3:00 PM on days with only evening commodity sessions).

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification to previous circular reference nos. NCL/COM/75319 (July 21, 2026) and 75460 (July 29, 2026) regarding collateral addition and release timings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, custodians, and PCMs must update their internal operational schedules and automated systems to adhere to the new collateral addition and allocation cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clearing members operating in the commodity derivatives segment must realign their back-office operations and treasury management to meet earlier cut-off times for G-Sec additions (5:00 PM) and allocation (8:00 PM).
