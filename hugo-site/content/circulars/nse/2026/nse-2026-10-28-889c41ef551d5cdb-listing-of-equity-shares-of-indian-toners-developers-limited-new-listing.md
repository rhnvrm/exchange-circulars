## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Indian Toners & Developers Limited (Symbol: INDTONER) effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indian Toners & Developers Limited
- Symbol: INDTONER
- ISIN: INE826B01026
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Total Securities: 51,958,660 equity shares (Distinctive number range: 1 to 51958660)
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading shall take place in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors under the designated security codes and market lot size.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date / Trading Commencement Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading of Indian Toners & Developers Limited equity shares on NSE, providing broader market access and liquidity for investors.
