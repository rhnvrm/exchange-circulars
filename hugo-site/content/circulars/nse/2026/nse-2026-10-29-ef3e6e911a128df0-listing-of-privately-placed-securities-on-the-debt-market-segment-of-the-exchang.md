## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and debt securities, on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers and debt securities admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective October 29, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- Issuing entities include Axis Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Wealth Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), Hero FinCorp Limited, PNB Housing Finance Limited, B N Agritech Limited, and REC Limited.
- Securities feature various maturity profiles and tenors ranging from November 2026 up to 2031.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing notification under existing debt market trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must comply with standard operational and reporting requirements for debt segment trades.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities and provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed commercial papers and debt instruments.
