## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of HEG Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective from December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: HEG Limited (Symbol: HEGRAPHITE).
- Transfer from Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) to Rolling segment (Series: EQ).
- Effective date: December 11, 2026.
- Pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved to the rolling segment following regulatory guidelines and periodic review.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should note the change in trading series for HEG Limited from Series BE to EQ effective December 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for HEG Limited shares starting December 11, 2026, improving liquidity and trading convenience.
