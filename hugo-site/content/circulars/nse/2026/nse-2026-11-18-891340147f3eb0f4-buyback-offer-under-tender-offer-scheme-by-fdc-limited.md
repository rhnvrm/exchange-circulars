## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that FDC Limited has approached the Exchange for the usage of its web-based bidding platform for an upcoming buyback offer under the Tender Offer scheme. The issue period is scheduled from November 19, 2026, to November 26, 2026, at an offer price of Rs. 500/- per equity share for up to 24,00,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: FDC Limited
- Security Symbol: FDC (ISIN: INE258B01022)
- Issue Size: Up to 24,00,000 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 500/- per equity share
- Issue Type: Fixed price buyback under Tender Offer scheme
- Issue Period: 19-November-2026 to 26-November-2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation up to 4.00 p.m. on issue closure)
- Live URL for Members: https://eipo.nseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs on the web-based IPO/tender offer bidding platform or via admin terminal creation of branches and users.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 19, 2026
- Issue Closure Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of FDC Limited have the opportunity to tender their equity shares during the buyback window at the fixed price of Rs. 500/- per share, impacting capital structure and shareholder returns.
