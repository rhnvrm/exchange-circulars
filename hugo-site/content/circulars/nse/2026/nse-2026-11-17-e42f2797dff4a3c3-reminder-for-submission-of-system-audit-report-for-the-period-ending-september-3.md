## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a reminder to all eligible trading members (Type - III) regarding the submission of mobility/system audit reports for the half year ending September 30, 2026. The submission deadline is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all Trading Members (Type - III) who traded using algorithm software or NNF trading software across segments during the period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Submission must be made electronically through the ENIT Member Portal.
- Failure to submit by the due date will attract penalties or disciplinary action as prescribed in Annexure 1.2 of Exchange circular no. NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a compliance reminder reinforcing existing mandates regarding system audit submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible Trading Members (Type - III) must submit their System Audit Reports electronically via the ENIT Member Portal on or before November 30, 2026, to prevent penalties and disciplinary actions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Audit Period Ending: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members falling under Type - III category must ensure timely electronic filing through ENIT to avoid regulatory penalties and compliance notices.
