## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the merger of Tata Nifty G-Sec Dec 2026 Index Fund (Merging Scheme) into Tata Short Term Fund (Surviving Scheme) under Tata Mutual Fund, effective after business hours on December 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: Tata Nifty G-Sec Dec 2026 Index Fund.
- Surviving Scheme: Tata Short Term Fund (formerly known as Tata Short Term Bond Fund).
- Effective Date of Merger: After business hours on December 18, 2026.
- Exit Load-Free Window: Unitholders not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch investments without exit load from November 19, 2026 to December 18, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM).
- Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue during the exit load period and will be shifted to the surviving scheme after business hours on December 18, 2026.
- Fresh inflows, lump sum, switches, and fresh registrations of SIP, STP, and SWP in the merging scheme are discontinued effective December 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme merger processed in compliance with regulatory requirements and Tata Mutual Fund addendum guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors wishing to exit without load must submit redemption or switch requests through the NSE MF Invest platform between November 19, 2026 and December 18, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM).

## Important Dates

- Exit Load-Free Period Start: November 19, 2026
- Exit Load-Free Period End / Record Date / Effective Date: December 18, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM for transactions)

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in the merging scheme are provided an exit opportunity without exit load penalties prior to the scheme consolidation.
