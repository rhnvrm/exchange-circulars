## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on the capital market segment effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills (91-day, 182-day, and 364-day) and three State Development Loans (Assam, Nagaland, and Bihar) are admitted to dealings.
- Trading will commence from October 30, 2026.
- Lot size for all listed securities is set at 100.
- Securities are identified by designated security codes and respective ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and correct lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading opportunities in government securities and state development loans on the capital market segment.
