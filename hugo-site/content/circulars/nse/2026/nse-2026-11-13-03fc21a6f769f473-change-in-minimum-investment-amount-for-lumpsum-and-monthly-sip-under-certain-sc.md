## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revision in minimum investment amounts for Lumpsum and Monthly Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) under certain schemes of Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Minimum Lumpsum and Monthly (SIP) investment amount revised from ₹500/- to ₹100/- (in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter).
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.
- Applicable across seven designated schemes of Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addendum to the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors take note of the revised minimum investment limits on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Notice Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances retail investor accessibility by lowering the entry barrier for SIP and Lumpsum investments in select Motilal Oswal mutual fund schemes.
