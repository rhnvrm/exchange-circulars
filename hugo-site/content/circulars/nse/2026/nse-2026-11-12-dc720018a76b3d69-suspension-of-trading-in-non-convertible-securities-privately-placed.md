## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for four companies due to redemption, effective from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension applies pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.
- Affected Companies and ISINs:
  - Tata Projects Limited (INE725H08162)
  - Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (INE206D08220)
  - Bharti Telecom Limited (INE403D08207)
  - LIC Housing Finance Limited (INE115A07PN6)
- Reason for suspension: Redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of trading suspension pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the suspension of trading in the aforementioned non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified non-convertible securities is halted on the NSE debt segment following their redemption.
