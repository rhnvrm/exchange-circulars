## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the scheme names and trading symbols for several Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) schemes managed by SBI Funds Management Limited will be changed with effect from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Change in scheme names and trading symbols affects multiple SBI Mutual Fund ETF schemes.
- Effective date for the changes is October 30, 2026.
- Affected schemes include SBI Nifty Bank ETF, SBI Nifty Next 50 ETF, SBI Nifty IT ETF, SBI Nifty Consumption ETF, SBI Nifty 50 ETF, SBI Gold ETF, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of scheme names and trading symbols across exchange trading systems for identification and operational alignment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and back-office databases with the new scheme names and symbols effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with minimal market impact; ensures clear identification and uniformity of SBI ETF schemes on the exchange platform.
