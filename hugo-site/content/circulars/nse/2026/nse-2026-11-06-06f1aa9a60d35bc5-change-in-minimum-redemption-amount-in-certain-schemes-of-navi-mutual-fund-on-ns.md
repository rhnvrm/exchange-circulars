## Summary

Navi AMC Limited has issued a notice cum addendum announcing the modification of minimum redemption and switch-out amounts for various schemes of Navi Mutual Fund, effective November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Minimum redemption amount revised to Rs. 100/- or 1 Unit or account balance (whichever is lower), in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter for applicable schemes.
- Switch-out amount revised to Rs. 100/- or 1 Unit or account balance (whichever is lower), in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter.
- Applies to prospective investments made from the Effective Date (November 6, 2026).
- Covered schemes include Navi Nifty 50 Index Fund, Navi Nifty Next 50 Index Fund, Navi Nifty Bank Index Fund, Navi Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund, Navi Nifty India Manufacturing Index Fund, Navi BSE Sensex Index Fund, Navi Nifty IT Index Fund, Navi Nifty 500 Multicap 50:25:25 Index Fund, Navi Total Stock Market US Specific Equity Passive FoF, Navi Nasdaq100 US Specific Equity Passive FOF, Navi Liquid Fund, Navi Flexi Cap Fund, Navi Large and Midcap Fund, and Navi Aggressive Hybrid Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of Scheme Information Document (SID) parameters concerning minimum redemption and switch-out limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and distributors must note the revised minimum redemption and switch-out thresholds when placing orders through the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact on investors and platforms, establishing a standardized Rs. 100/- minimum redemption/switch threshold across specified schemes.
