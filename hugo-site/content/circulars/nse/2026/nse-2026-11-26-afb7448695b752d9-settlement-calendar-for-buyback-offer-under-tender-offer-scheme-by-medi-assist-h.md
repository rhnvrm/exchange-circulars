## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and operational guidelines for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme by Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited. Trading is scheduled from November 27, 2026, to December 3, 2026, with final settlement on December 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Buyback offer under tender offer scheme for Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited.
- Trade start date: November 27, 2026; Trade end date: December 3, 2026.
- Obligation download date: December 9, 2026; Settlement date: December 10, 2026.
- Demat shares must be tendered through Early Pay-in mechanism for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be made well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will occur post receipt of funds pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the settlement day.
- Tender Offer Acceptance Report will detail accepted, partially accepted, and rejected orders along with specific rejection reason codes (Annexure I).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/Custodians must ensure early pay-in of demat shares is completed in advance for orders up to 03:30 p.m. on the trade end date.
- Ensure funds pay-in is completed on the settlement day prior to pay-out.
- Review Tender Offer Acceptance Report and rejection codes (such as demat account & name mismatch, excess shares tendered than eligibility, and joint holder signature differences) as outlined in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- **November 27, 2026**: Trade Start Date
- **December 03, 2026**: Trade End Date
- **December 09, 2026**: Obligation Download Date
- **December 10, 2026**: Settlement Date
