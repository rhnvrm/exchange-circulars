## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value split and change in ISIN for equity shares of BN Agrochem Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BN Agrochem Limited
- Symbol: BNAGROCHEM
- New ISIN: INE00HZ01029
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1 per share
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of ISIN and reduction in face value/paid-up value of equity shares in exchange records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the new ISIN and face value for trading and settlement purposes effective from the implementation date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational impact on market participants other than updating master files with the new ISIN (INE00HZ01029) and face value for BNAGROCHEM.
