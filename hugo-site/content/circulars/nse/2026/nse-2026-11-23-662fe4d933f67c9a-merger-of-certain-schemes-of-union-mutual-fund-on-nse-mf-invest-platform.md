## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about the merger of Union Dynamic Term Fund (Merging Scheme) into Union Corporate Bond Fund (Surviving Scheme) of Union Mutual Fund. The merger is effective after business hours on December 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: Union Dynamic Term Fund
- Surviving Scheme: Union Corporate Bond Fund
- Effective Date: After business hours on December 22, 2026.
- Exit Load-Free Window: Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem their investments or switch to other schemes of Union Mutual Fund without exit load from November 23, 2026, to December 22, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM) via the NSE MF Invest platform.
- Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP: Will continue to be processed in the merging scheme during the exit load period and will be migrated to the surviving scheme after business hours on December 22, 2026.
- Fresh Inflows: Fresh inflows, lump sum investments, switches, and fresh registrations for SIP, STP, and SWP in the merging scheme are discontinued effective December 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI granted its no-objection for the merger vide email dated November 05, 2026. The merger does not entail any change in the fundamental attributes of the Surviving Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must take note of the discontinuation of fresh inflows in Union Dynamic Term Fund and utilize the exit load-free window if opting to redeem or switch prior to the merger.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load-Free Period Start Date: November 23, 2026
- Exit Load-Free Period End Date & Time: December 22, 2026 up to 3:00 PM
- Effective Date of Merger: December 22, 2026 (after business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Unitholders in Union Dynamic Term Fund are provided a seamless exit option without load or automatic migration into Union Corporate Bond Fund. Platform routing and processing on NSE MF Invest are adjusted to reflect scheme discontinuation and migration.
