## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (AFFORDABLE, ASPIRE, VALUE360, VEEKAYEM) are shortlisted under ESM Stage - I w.e.f. November 27, 2026.
- Attracts a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 27, 2026, on all open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions created from November 27, 2026.
- Shifting from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules, imposing stricter trading and margin norms on identified securities to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the affected securities, and account for the shift to the trade-for-trade segment effective from November 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Margin / Shifting:** November 27, 2026 (on open positions as on November 26, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement reduce intraday leverage and liquidity for the affected securities, impacting trading participants holding positions in these stocks.
