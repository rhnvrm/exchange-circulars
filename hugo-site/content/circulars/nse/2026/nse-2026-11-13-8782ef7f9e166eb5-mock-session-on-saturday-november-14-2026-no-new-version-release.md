## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The session will be conducted from the DR Site, and members are advised to participate using all trading software to ensure smooth connectivity for live trading.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026, in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Normal Market open time: 12:15 hrs; Normal Market close time: 14:30 hrs.
- Position Limit/Collateral value setup cut-off time and Trade Modification end time: 14:40 hrs.
- Live Re-login start time: 16:00 hrs; close time: 16:30 hrs.
- Reminder for NNF members to migrate to new encryption mechanisms with MD5 checksum removal or immediate acknowledgement before December 07, 2026.
- NEAT Version: 1.5.5.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session (order purge).
- NOTIS application will not be accessible during the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; however, reminders regarding NNF encryption mechanism migration (per circular NSE/MSD/76883) are highlighted.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure seamless connectivity for Monday, November 16, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- NNF members must migrate from the old interactive port (10865) before December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: November 14, 2026
- Live Environment resumption: November 16, 2026
- NNF Encryption Migration Deadline: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. The mock session allows members to test system readiness and connectivity without any financial obligations or actual fund pay-in/pay-out.
