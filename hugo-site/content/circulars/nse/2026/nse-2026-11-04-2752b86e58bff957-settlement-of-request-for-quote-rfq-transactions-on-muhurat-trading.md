## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for Request for Quote (RFQ) transactions executed during the special live trading session held on account of Diwali Muhurat trading.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session for RFQ is scheduled on Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Settlement cycle follows a T+1 basis.
- Settlement date is set for Monday, November 09, 2026.
- Settlement number: 2611009.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular detailing holiday session settlement timelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants, custodians, and members must ensure readiness for settlement obligations as per the specified schedule.

## Important Dates

- Trading Date: November 08, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to ensuring smooth clearing and settlement for trades executed during the special holiday trading session.
