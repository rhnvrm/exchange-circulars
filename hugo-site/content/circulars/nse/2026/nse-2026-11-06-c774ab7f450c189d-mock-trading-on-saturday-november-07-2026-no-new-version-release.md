## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026. There is no new software version release associated with this mock session.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: November 07, 2026.
- Trading Session Timings: Normal Market open at 11:00 hrs and close at 12:30 hrs from the Primary Site.
- Live Re-login Timings: Start at 14:00 hrs and close at 14:30 hrs.
- Connectivity & Infrastructure: Refer to circular NSE/MSD/73993 for interactive connectivity details. Installation guide available at /slbftp/slbcommon/Installation_Procedure. NEAT Version 1.3.3.
- Order Purge: All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- No Financial Obligation: Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- UCC/PAN Validity: Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine operational mock trading session compliant with SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, November 09, 2026. Ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN and correct Box ID setup.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: November 07, 2026
- Live Resume Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for trading members in the SLBM segment with zero financial impact.
