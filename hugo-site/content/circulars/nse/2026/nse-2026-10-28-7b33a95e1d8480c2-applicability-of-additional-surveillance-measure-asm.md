## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities, effective October 29, 2026. Rhetan TMT Limited has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I: Rhetan TMT Limited (ISIN: INE0KKN01029).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 02, 2026, on all open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions created from November 02, 2026, onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework in accordance with established exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the affected securities starting November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework: October 29, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date for Margin: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in RHETAN must maintain a 100% margin on open positions and new positions, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected security.
