## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for selected securities effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Abhishek Integrations Limited, Delphi World Money Limited, Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited, Pushpa Jewellers Limited, and Baazar Style Retail Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. November 19, 2026, on all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created from November 19, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements of 50% (capped at 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Provisions: November 19, 2026 (on open positions as on November 18, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume for the affected stocks.
