## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities effective November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Acutaas Chemicals Limited, Ather Energy Limited, Credent Connect N Care Limited, Giriraj Civil Developers Limited, Hubtown Limited, Khfm Hospitality And Facility Management Services Limited, Mahickra Chemicals Limited, Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited, and Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited.
- No securities are listed under ST-ASM Stage II or moving from Stage I to Stage II.
- Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 04, 2026, on all open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions created from November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework on shortlisted securities as part of market surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ST-ASM: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Provisions: November 04, 2026 (on open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will attract higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes.
