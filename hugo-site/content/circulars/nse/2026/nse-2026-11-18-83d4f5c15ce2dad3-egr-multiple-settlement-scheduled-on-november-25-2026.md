## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced multiple settlement schedules for Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segments on November 25, 2026, due to a settlement holiday on November 24, 2026. The circular outlines revised pay-in and pay-out timings, revised auction cycles under T+1, and updated timings for uploading unpaid MTF files.

## Key Points

- Multiple settlements are scheduled on November 25, 2026, corresponding to trade dates 23-Nov-2026 and 24-Nov-2026.
- Revised pay-in and pay-out times have been established for various settlement types and numbers.
- Auction trading and settlement under the revised T+1 auction cycle are adjusted for trade dates spanning November 23 to November 25, 2026.
- Auction of shortages for settlement number 2026137 will be conducted in 2026138, with no auction conducted in 2026137.
- Specific cut-off and upload timings for unpaid MTF files for EGR Normal are provided for November 24 and November 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational adjustments in settlement schedules, auction cycles, and MTF file upload windows to accommodate the settlement holiday on November 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must take note of revised settlement timings, pay-in/pay-out cut-offs at depositories, and auction schedules to ensure timely fulfillment of obligations.
- Members must adhere to the revised timings for uploading unpaid MTF files for EGR Normal.

## Important Dates

- **Settlement Holiday:** November 24, 2026
- **Multiple Settlements Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Trade & Settlement Windows:** Spanning November 23, 2026 to November 27, 2026 as per the revised schedule.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants dealing in Electronic Gold Receipts must update their operational workflows, depository pay-in cut-offs, and auction participation to align with the compressed and revised settlement schedules on November 25, 2026.
