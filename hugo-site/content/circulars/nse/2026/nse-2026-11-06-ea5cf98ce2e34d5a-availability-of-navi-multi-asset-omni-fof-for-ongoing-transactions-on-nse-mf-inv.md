## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF of Navi Mutual Fund is reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name**: Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF (Direct Plan - Growth & Regular Plan - Growth)
- **Effective Date**: November 06, 2026
- **Available Facilities**: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **ISIN Details**:
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF959L01HV6
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF959L01HW4

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational notification regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can execute transactions in the specified schemes in accordance with standard platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to conduct ongoing transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF schemes on the NSE platform.
