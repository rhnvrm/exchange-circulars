## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from November 17, 2026. The circular outlines the designated security codes, market lot sizes, coupon rates, maturity dates, and ISINs for the admitted securities.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 17, 2026.
- Master Capital Services Limited: 10.5% NCD maturing on 12-May-2028 (Symbol: 105MCS28, ISIN: INE2UHF07023, Lot Size: 1).
- NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited: 8.50% NCD Sr PP7 maturing on 05-Feb-2032 (Symbol: 850NIIF32, ISIN: INE246R07988, Lot Size: 1).
- NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited: 0% NCD Sr OPII maturing on 12-Nov-2031 (Symbol: 0NIIF31, ISIN: INE246R07970, Lot Size: 1).
- Aditya Birla Capital Limited: 0% NCD Sr H3 maturing on 13-Nov-2030 (Symbol: 0ABCL30, ISIN: INE674K07192, Lot Size: 1).
- Trading in these securities shall be identified solely by their designated security codes in the specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings and designated with specific lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions are executed using the designated security codes and applicable market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Issue Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt instruments on the NSE capital market segment.
