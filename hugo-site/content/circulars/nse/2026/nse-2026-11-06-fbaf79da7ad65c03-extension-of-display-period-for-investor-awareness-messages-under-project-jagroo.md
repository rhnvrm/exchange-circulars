## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the extension of the display period for investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook until December 31, 2026, following communication from SEBI.

## Key Points

- Investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook shall continue to be displayed till December 31, 2026.
- Effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 1, 2026, stand extended to November 15, 2026.
- Messages must be displayed in a bilingual format (Hindi and English) on trading applications and websites.
- Schedule prescribed in paragraph 2(c) of the earlier Exchange circular remains unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of display timeline for Project Jagrook awareness messages to December 31, 2026.
- Revision of effective dates for specific provisions of the SEBI circular to November 15, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure continued bilingual display (Hindi and English) of investor awareness messages on trading applications and websites.
- Adhere to the extended deadlines for compliance parameters.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 06, 2026
- Extended Display Period Deadline: December 31, 2026
- Effective Date Extension: November 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must maintain uninterrupted display of investor awareness messages on their digital interfaces in bilingual format, ensuring compliance with updated SEBI and NSE timelines.
