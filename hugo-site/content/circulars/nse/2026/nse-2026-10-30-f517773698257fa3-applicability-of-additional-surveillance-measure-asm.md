## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular detailing the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities. Specifically, securities have been shortlisted under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. November 02, 2026: BN Agrochem Limited and Milton Industries Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 04, 2026 on all open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities meeting specific surveillance criteria are subjected to the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework, implementing strict margin controls.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure a 100% margin is maintained on all open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created from November 04, 2026 onwards for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Shortlisting Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Margin Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in BN Agrochem Limited and Milton Industries Limited will be restricted by 100% margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading participation.
