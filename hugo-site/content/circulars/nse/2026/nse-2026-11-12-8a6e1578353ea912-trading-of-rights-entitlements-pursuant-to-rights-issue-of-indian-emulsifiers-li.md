## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Indian Emulsifiers Limited under the SME EMERGE segment, effective from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indian Emulsifiers Limited
- Symbol: IEML-RE1
- ISIN: INE0RRU20024
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 18,333,222
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From November 16, 2026, to November 25, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 26, 2026
- Market Lot: 500

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment (SME EMERGE) under the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat form for all investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of RE trading, specifying its distinct ISIN so it is not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes and bills must feature a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce the REs before the issue closes.
- REs that are neither subscribed by making an application with requisite application money nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: November 16, 2026
- Trading Period / Renunciation Closure: November 25, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates organized trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for Indian Emulsifiers Limited within the SME EMERGE segment under trade-to-trade settlement.
