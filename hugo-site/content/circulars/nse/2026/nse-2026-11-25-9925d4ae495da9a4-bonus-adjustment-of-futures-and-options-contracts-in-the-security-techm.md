## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced adjustments to Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Tech Mahindra Limited (TECHM) on account of a bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1. The changes become effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tech Mahindra Limited (TECHM)
- Corporate Action Type: Bonus (1:1 ratio, Face Value Rs. 5/-)
- Ex-date & Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Adjustment Factor: 2
- Revised Market Lot: 1,200

## Regulatory Changes

- F&O contracts for TECHM are adjusted pursuant to SEBI guidelines for corporate actions.
- Revised strike prices, futures base prices, and lot sizes will be adjusted and rounded off to the nearest tick size/integer as per exchange standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to load the updated contract (NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) and spread (NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) files from the Extranet server (faoftp/faocommon) or NSE website before trading on the ex-date.
- Clearing Corporations will separately intimate the methodology for position adjustments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-Date: November 30, 2026
- Contract and Spread Files Availability: One day prior to the effective date

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading F&O contracts in TECHM must account for the revised lot size (1,200) and adjusted strike/futures base prices to ensure accurate risk management and order placement.
