## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the introduction of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on six individual securities, effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts will be introduced for six securities: Coromandel International Limited, City Union Bank Limited, Data Patterns (India) Limited, Karur Vysya Bank Limited, Navin Fluorine International Limited, and Syrma SGS Technology Limited.
- Trading in these F&O contracts commences on October 28, 2026.
- Market lot sizes are specified for each security (COROMANDEL: 300, CUB: 2325, DATAPATTNS: 125, KARURVYSYA: 1550, NAVINFLUOR: 75, SYRMA: 325).
- Strike schemes and reserve strikes are detailed in Annexure A.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of six new securities in the NSE F&O segment with specified lot sizes, tick sizes, and strike schemes as per existing framework circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz and NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files from the Extranet server (faoftp/faocommon) or website path into their trading applications before trading on October 28, 2026.
- Ensure applicable quantity freezes and strike price step values are correctly configured.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for F&O Trading: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the derivatives product offering on the exchange, providing additional hedging and trading opportunities for market participants in these six securities.
