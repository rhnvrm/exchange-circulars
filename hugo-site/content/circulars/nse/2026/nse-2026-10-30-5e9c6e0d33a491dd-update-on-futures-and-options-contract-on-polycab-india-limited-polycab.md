## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that the circular regarding the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) stands withdrawn following an NCLAT order allowing the company's appeal and setting aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order regarding IBC proceedings.

## Key Points

- NCLAT vide order dated October 30, 2026, allowed the appeal filed by Polycab India Limited and set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026, concerning IBC proceedings.
- Exchange circular no. NSE/FAOP/76865 dated October 12, 2026, regarding the exclusion of F&O contracts on POLYCAB stands withdrawn.
- F&O contracts with January 2027 expiry will be made available for trading effective November 02, 2026.
- Members must load updated contract and spread files before trading commences on November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal of F&O contract exclusion for Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) in light of the NCLAT order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz/MII contract and spread files from the Extranet server directory faoftp/faocommon before trading on November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026**: NCLAT order issued; NSE circular published.
- **November 02, 2026**: F&O contracts with January 2027 expiry made available for trading.

## Impact Assessment

Restores F&O trading in Polycab India Limited, providing certainty and liquidity to market participants following the resolution of IBC proceedings at the NCLAT level.
