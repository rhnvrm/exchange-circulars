## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and other debt securities) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective from November 02, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue of commercial papers and debt securities by various issuers has been admitted to dealings.
- Issuers include NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Tata Capital Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, Tata Projects Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, L&T Finance Limited, and Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited.
- Allotment dates are majorly dated 30-Oct-2026 with various maturity dates ranging from November 2026 to October 2036.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to depository and settlement procedures corresponding to the respective ISINs.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Varies by security (ranging from November 06, 2026 to October 30, 2036)

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading and settlement for the newly listed debt securities and commercial papers.
