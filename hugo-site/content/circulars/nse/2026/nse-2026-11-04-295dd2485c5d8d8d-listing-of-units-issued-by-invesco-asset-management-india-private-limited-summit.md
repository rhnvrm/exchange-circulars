## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of units issued by Invesco Asset Management (India) Private Limited for Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund, effective from November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Invesco Asset Management (India) Private Limited
- Scheme Name: Invesco Mutual Fund Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Face Value / Paid Up Value / Issue Price: Rs. 10/- each
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admissions of further securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and trading access to investors holding units of the Invesco Mutual Fund Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund across various plans (Direct and Regular, IDCW and Growth options).
