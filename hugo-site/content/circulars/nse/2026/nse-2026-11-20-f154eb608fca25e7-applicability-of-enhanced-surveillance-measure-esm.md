## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specific securities. The circular outlines securities shortlisted for Stage I, securities moving from Stage I to Stage II, and securities moving from Stage II to Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 25, 2026, on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance parameters and is not an adverse regulatory action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion and stage migration of securities under the ESM framework.
- Implementation of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Shifting of scrips between Rolling Settlement and Trade-for-Trade segments.
- Application of 2% price bands under Periodic Call Auction for Stage II scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure adherence to 100% margin maintenance and trade-for-trade settlement rules for all impacted scrips.
- Monitor open positions as of November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **November 23, 2026**: Effective date for stage movement and Periodic Call Auction price band applicability.
- **November 25, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting of securities to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

Shortlisted securities face enhanced surveillance, higher margin obligations, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, which may affect near-term liquidity and trading volumes.
