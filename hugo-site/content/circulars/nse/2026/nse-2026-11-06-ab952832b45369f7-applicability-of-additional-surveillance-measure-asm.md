## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) on certain securities based on objective criteria. The measures include 100% margin requirements and stage shifts effective from November 11, 2026, for trades executed from November 08, 2026 onwards.

## Key Points

- Securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion and movement under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 11, 2026, on all open positions as on November 09, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage IV are moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term ASM framework provisions including enhanced margin rates and segment shifts as per existing Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities and note the segment changes where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date for Actions:** November 11, 2026 (based on shortlisting effective November 08, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the affected scrips (such as Prizor Viztech Limited and Menon Bearings Limited) are subject to heightened margin obligations and potential segment movement, impacting liquidity and position management.
