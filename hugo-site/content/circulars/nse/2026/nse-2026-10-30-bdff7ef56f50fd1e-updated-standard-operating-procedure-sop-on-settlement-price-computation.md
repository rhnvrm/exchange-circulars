## Summary

NSE has released an updated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlining the methodology for computing settlement prices in the equity segment. The guidelines cover stocks with and without Continuous Auction Session (CAS) applicability, handling scenarios where trading occurs or does not occur in the last 30 minutes across single or multiple stock exchanges.

## Key Points

- Details settlement price computation for stocks where CAS is not applicable vs. where CAS is applicable.
- Specifies rules when stocks have traded in the last 30 minutes of the trading session or during the CAS session across one or multiple stock exchanges.
- Outlines fallback mechanisms when stocks have not traded in the last 30 minutes / CAS session but have traded during the day (using LTP or reference price).
- Mandates rounding of computed settlement prices having more than 2 decimals to 2 decimals using simple mathematical rounding.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized multi-exchange handling for Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP), Last Traded Price (LTP), and equilibrium prices.
- Clear multi-exchange averaging rules when prices are provided by more than one stock exchange versus a single stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and stock exchanges must align their settlement price computation systems and algorithms with the updated SOP guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures uniformity and transparency in settlement price determination across multiple stock exchanges for equity segment securities.
