## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to selected securities, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: APL Apollo Tubes Limited (APLAPOLLO), HMA Agro Industries Limited (HMAAGRO), Sunlite Recycling Industries Limited (SUNLITE), and Veefin Solutions Limited (VEEFIN).
- Applicable rate of margin is set at 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% effective November 27, 2026.
- Margin requirements apply to all open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions created from November 27, 2026.
- No securities were listed under Stage II or moving between stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance framework measures to monitor stock price volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements of 50% or higher (capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (on open positions as on November 26, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements may increase capital commitments for traders and investors holding positions in the shortlisted securities.
