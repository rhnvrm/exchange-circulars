## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares of Z-Tech (India) Limited effective from November 16, 2026. The shares were allotted pursuant to the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Z-Tech (India) Limited
- **Symbol:** ZTECH
- **ISIN:** INE0ISZ01012
- **Segment:** SME EMERGE
- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Tranche 1:** 318,300 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 10, Issue Price: Rs. 500) allotted on 31-Aug-2026 with distinctive numbers 14871449 to 15189748.
- **Tranche 2:** 585,300 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 10, Issue Price: Rs. 500) allotted on 13-Sep-2026 with distinctive numbers 15584949 to 16170248.
- **Market Lot:** 300 shares
- **Pari Passu:** Yes

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. This is a routine securities listing notification under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure compliance with lock-in details and restrictions as specified in Annexure A of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 16, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** August 31, 2026 and September 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Various dates spanning May 2027 to May 2028 as per Annexure A.

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of equity shares resulting from warrant conversion, enhancing liquidity and capital base for Z-Tech (India) Limited on the SME EMERGE platform.
