## Summary

Oriental Tollways Private Limited has approached the Exchange for a web-based tender offer facility to provide an exit opportunity to dissenting unitholders of Oriental InfraTrust (InvIT). The live bidding period is scheduled from November 11, 2026, to November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issue Period**: November 11, 2026 to November 17, 2026.
- **Offer Price**: Rs. 113/- per unit.
- **Security Symbol**: OSEINTRUST (Series: TO, ISIN: INE07Z523018).
- **Issue Size**: Up to 75,32,962 Units.
- **Lot Size**: 25,000 Units.
- **Merchant Banker**: Nuvama Wealth Management Limited.
- **Registrar**: KFin Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

The exit offer is conducted in accordance with Regulation 22(7) of SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014 and Chapter 11 of the Master Circular for Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) dated July 11, 2025, along with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs on the web-based IPO bidding platform (https://eipo.nseindia.com). Members without access can use the admin terminal to create branches and users with user ID format Adminxxxxx. Bidding time is 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m., with custodial confirmation on issue closure up to 4.00 p.m.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date**: November 11, 2026
- **Issue Closing Date**: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables dissenting unitholders of Oriental InfraTrust to tender their units through the exchange-provided web-based tender offer facility during the specified live bidding window.
