## Summary

NSE has announced that no transaction charges will be levied on trades in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) segment of the exchange until March 31, 2027, aimed at encouraging active participation.

## Key Points

- Zero transaction charges on trades in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) segment.
- Applicable until March 31, 2027.
- Supersedes previous circular NSE/FA/75523 dated July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exemption from transaction fees for all EGR segment trades.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate fee structures are reflected in client billings and trading terminals, adhering to the zero-charge policy for EGRs.

## Important Dates

- **March 31, 2027**: End date of the fee waiver.

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces trading overhead for market participants, boosting liquidity and engagement in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment.
