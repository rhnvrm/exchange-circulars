## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on its debt market segment, effective November 09, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Issuing Entities Include:** Godrej Industries Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Tata Teleservices Limited, CEAT Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Axis Securities Limited, and HDB Financial Services Ltd.
- **Instruments:** Commercial Papers (CP) with various issue sizes, face values, and redemption dates ranging from December 2026 to May 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing entities and trading members must ensure adherence to applicable debt market trading regulations and settlement guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Various dates between December 15, 2026 and May 06, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation facilitating debt fundraising and secondary market liquidity for commercial papers.
