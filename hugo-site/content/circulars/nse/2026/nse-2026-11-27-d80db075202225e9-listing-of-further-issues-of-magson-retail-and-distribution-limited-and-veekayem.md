## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for Magson Retail And Distribution Limited and Veekayem Fashion and Apparels Limited under the SME EMERGE segment, effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Magson Retail And Distribution Limited**: 526,800 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0O1S01012, Distinctive range: 14638235 to 15165034, Market lot: 1000, Lock-in up to 30-May-2027).
- **Veekayem Fashion and Apparels Limited**: 1,060,392 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0KTW01013, Distinctive range: 5875731 to 6936122, Market lot: 500, with lock-in details as per Annexure A).
- Trading in these securities will commence from November 30, 2026 under their designated codes and market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further issues to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with lock-in requirements and temporary ISIN guidelines in accordance with SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: November 27, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 30, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: 03-Sep-2026 (Magson), 07-Feb-2026 (Veekayem)

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the trading base and paid-up capital of Magson Retail And Distribution Limited and Veekayem Fashion and Apparels Limited on the SME platform.
