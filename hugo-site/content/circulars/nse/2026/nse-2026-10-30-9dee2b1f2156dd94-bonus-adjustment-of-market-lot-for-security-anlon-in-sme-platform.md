## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of the market lot for ANLON Technology Solutions Limited (Symbol: ANLON) on the SME platform on account of a bonus issue (Bonus Ratio 2:5). The new adjusted market lot is 280, effective from November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** ANLON Technology Solutions Limited (ANLON)
- **Corporate Action:** Bonus issue with a ratio of 2:5.
- **Adjustment Factor:** 1.4
- **Ex-date / Effective Date:** November 02, 2026.
- **Record Date:** November 02, 2026.
- **Adjusted Market Lot:** 280.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of market lot following corporate action (bonus issue) pursuant to exchange guidelines and previous circular references (NSE/CMTR/76688).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated `security.gz`/`nnf_security.gz`/`MII - Security File.gz` file in the trading application before trading on the effective date (November 02, 2026).
- Files can be obtained from the `common/NTNEAT` directory on the Extranet server or the NSE website reports section.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Ex-date:** November 02, 2026
- **Record Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members dealing in the SME security ANLON must update their trading terminals with the new market lot (280) and security files prior to the effective date to ensure smooth order execution and compliance.
