## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund for ongoing transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund is reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities including SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) are available.
- Covers multiple plans including Direct and Regular plans across IDCW (Payout/Reinvestment) and Growth options.
- Effective date for transactions is October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notification regarding fund availability on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must use the correct ISIN codes provided for respective scheme plans when placing orders on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and enables investors and members to transact seamlessly in various direct and regular plans of the Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund.
