## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the schedule for reporting of holding statements on account of the Diwali - Muhurat Trading Session held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised reporting schedule applies to trade dates from November 06, 2026, to November 10, 2026.
- Holding statements for trade dates 06-Nov-2026, 07-Nov-2026, and 08-Nov-2026 are due by 09-Nov-2026.
- Holding statements for trade dates 09-Nov-2026 and 10-Nov-2026 are due by 11-Nov-2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational timeline adjustment for holiday scheduling.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the revised schedule and ensure timely compliance with the holding statement reporting requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Date 06-Nov-2026:** Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- **Trade Date 07-Nov-2026:** Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- **Trade Date 08-Nov-2026:** Due Date 09-Nov-2026
- **Trade Date 09-Nov-2026:** Due Date 11-Nov-2026
- **Trade Date 10-Nov-2026:** Due Date 11-Nov-2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Ensures smooth regulatory compliance and holding statement submission around the Diwali festive trading schedule.
