## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The primary objective is to test the revised operational guidelines and trading modalities of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** November 28, 2026.
- **NEATPlus Version 7.9.2:** Introduced exclusively for Trading Session-1 of the mock to test CAS implementation.
- **Session Re-login/Reinstallation:** Members must reinstall and login with earlier NEATPlus versions (7.9.1 or 7.9.0) for Trading Session-2.
- **Multiple Sessions:** Includes Trading Session-1 from DR site, Block Deal Window sessions, Pre-Open sessions, Special Pre-open sessions, Normal Market sessions, T+0 Market sessions, and Call Auction Illiquid sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing of revised operational guidelines and trading modalities for the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to actively participate in all trading sessions using the specified NEATPlus versions for Trading Session-1 and Session-2.
- Ensure download and installation of NEATPlus version 7.9.2 from the designated Extranet path (`/cmftp/common/Mock/CM/28NOV2026/NEATPlus792`) by 07:00 hours on November 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026
- **NEATPlus 7.9.2 Availability:** November 28, 2026, by 07:00 hours
- **Live Re-login start time:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Helps trading members verify system readiness and compliance with revised Closing Auction Session (CAS) modalities ahead of live deployment.
