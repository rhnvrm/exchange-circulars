## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (SIF) offered by HDFC Mutual Fund will be available for subscription and transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 13, 2026, to November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: HDFC Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth and IDCW options)
- Category: Equity (Hybrid)
- Transaction Window: November 13, 2026, to November 27, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 1, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine New Fund Offer (NFO) listing announcement on the NSE MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 30, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 13, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 27, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 30, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless investment access for members and investors into HDFC Mutual Fund's new hybrid long-short fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest Platform with standard operational timelines.
