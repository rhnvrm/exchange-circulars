## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switches, and systematic registrations under IDCW (Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal) options for certain passive and fund-of-fund schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, pursuant to a Notice-cum-Addendum issued by the AMC.

## Key Points

- Fresh lumpsum subscription, switch-in, and fresh registrations under SIP and STP-in are disabled for 14 specified schemes of Axis Mutual Fund.
- Effective date for the suspension is November 20, 2026.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain unaffected.
- Existing SIPs and STPs will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without any changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of AMC addendum directives regarding suspension of fresh inflows into specific IDCW option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the restriction on fresh subscriptions and systematic registrations for the listed Axis Mutual Fund schemes effective November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh investments or new systematic registrations in the specified Axis Mutual Fund schemes will not be able to do so via the NSE MF Invest Platform. Existing investments and redemptions are completely unrestricted.
