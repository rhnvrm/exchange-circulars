## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) for quant Silver ETF FOF by Quant Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 04, 2026, to November 06, 2026, till 11:59 PM.

## Key Points

- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Fund House:** Quant Mutual Fund
- **NFO Window:** November 04, 2026 to November 06, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Eligible Schemes:** Includes various Direct and Regular plans (Growth, IDCW-Payout, IDCW-Reinvestment).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any approved payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 11, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** November 04, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** November 06, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Clearing Funds Cut-off:** November 11, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and trading members in participating in the quant Silver ETF FOF NFO through the NSE MF Invest Platform with clear deadlines for fund clearance and allotment.
