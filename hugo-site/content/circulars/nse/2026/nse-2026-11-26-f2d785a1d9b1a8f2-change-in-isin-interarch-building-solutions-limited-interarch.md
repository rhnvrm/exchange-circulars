## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has informed members about a change in the ISIN code for the equity shares of Interarch Building Solutions Limited (INTERARCH) effective November 30, 2026, following a corporate action involving the sub-division of shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Interarch Building Solutions Limited
- Symbol: INTERARCH
- New ISIN: INE00M901026
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

ISIN update reflecting the capital restructuring and share sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure their trading and back-office systems are updated with the new ISIN code prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with minimal market disruption, ensuring accurate identification and settlement of shares post sub-division.
