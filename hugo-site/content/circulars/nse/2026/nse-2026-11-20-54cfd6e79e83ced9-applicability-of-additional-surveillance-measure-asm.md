## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities. Skytech Infinite Platform Limited has been included in Long-Term ASM Stage-I, and Quicktouch Technologies Limited has moved from Stage-I to Stage-II.

## Key Points

- Skytech Infinite Platform Limited (Symbol: SKYTECH, ISIN: INE0VFE01017) shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Quicktouch Technologies Limited (Symbol: QUICKTOUCH, ISIN: INE0K4D01020) moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I to Stage-II w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 26, 2026 on all open positions as on November 25, 2026 and new positions created from November 26, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified under the Long-Term ASM framework are subjected to enhanced margin requirements and surveillance oversight to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the collection of 100% margins on open positions as of November 25, 2026, and all subsequent new positions effective November 26, 2026, for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Framework Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 25, 2026
- 100% Margin Applicability Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in SKYTECH and QUICKTOUCH will experience increased margin funding requirements and stricter risk management controls in line with the ASM framework.
