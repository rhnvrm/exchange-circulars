## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issue of 12,36,546 equity shares of Lloyds Engineering Works Limited (Symbol: LLOYDSENGG) consequent to the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares, effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lloyds Engineering Works Limited
- Symbol: LLOYDSENGG
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE093R01011 (Temporary ISIN: IN8093R01010)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- each
- Number of Securities: 12,36,546 equity shares
- Distinctive Numbers: From 1471003834 to 1472240379
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action reflecting capital expansion through conversion of partly paid-up shares, with no direct adverse market impact.
