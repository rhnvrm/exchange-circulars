## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for COAL INDIA LIMITED (COALINDIA) on account of a dividend payout of Rs 10.25 with a face value of Rs 10/-. The changes are effective from November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Underlying Security: COAL INDIA LIMITED (Symbol: COALINDIA)
- Corporate Action: Dividend
- Dividend Amount: Rs 10.25/-
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 4, 2026
- Revised option strike prices have been adjusted (e.g., strike 330.00 revised to 319.75 for 23-NOV-2026 expiry).

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of derivative contracts in pursuance of SEBI guidelines on corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz / MII contract and spread files on the trading application before trading on the ex-date / effective date.
- Files can be obtained from the directory faoftp/faocommon on the Extranet server or NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 03, 2026
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 04, 2026
- Option Expiry Date in Annexure: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding F&O positions in COALINDIA will see revised strike prices and base prices effective from the ex-date to account for the dividend distribution.
