## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to certain securities, effective from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100% w.e.f. November 13, 2026, on all open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance measures for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created from November 13, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted securities: Bombay Metrics Supply Chain Limited (BMETRICS), Hindustan Media Ventures Limited (HMVL), Shreeji Global FMCG Limited (SHETHJI), Transworld Shipping Lines Limited (TRANSWORLD), and Yasons Chemex Care Limited (YCCL).
