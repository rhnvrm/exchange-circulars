## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of privately placed securities on its capital market segment, effective from November 18, 2026. The circular covers debt securities issued by Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited and PNB Housing Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- **Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited**:
  - Symbol: 840TCHFL31
  - Series: N0
  - Issue Description: TCHFL 8.40% 2031 Sr G
  - Coupon Rate: 8.40%
  - Maturity Date: 14-Nov-2031
  - ISIN: INE033L07IY0
  - Market Lot: 1
- **PNB Housing Finance Limited**:
  - Symbol: 862PHFL30
  - Series: N0
  - Issue Description: PHFL 8.62% 2030 Sr LXXX
  - Coupon Rate: 8.62%
  - Maturity Date: 07-Jan-2030
  - ISIN: INE572E07332
  - Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 17, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 18, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: January 7, 2030 (PNB Housing Finance) and November 14, 2031 (Tata Capital Housing Finance)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and trading venue for the newly listed privately placed NCDs of the respective housing finance companies.
