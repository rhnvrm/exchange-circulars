## Summary

Indo Borax and Chemicals Limited, along with Zenrock Chemicals Private Limited (PAC 1), has announced a takeover offer under the Tender Offer scheme for Kronox Lab Sciences Limited. The live bidding session on the NSE web-based platform is scheduled from November 06, 2026, to November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Indo Borax and Chemicals Limited along with Zenrock Chemicals Private Limited (PAC 1).
- Target Company: Kronox Lab Sciences Limited (Security Symbol: KRONOX, ISIN: INE0ATZ01017).
- Issue Size: Up to 95,70,000 Equity Shares at an offer price of Rs. 157.27/- per share.
- Issue Period: 06-Nov-2026 to 20-Nov-2026.
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on closure day up to 4.00 p.m.).
- Live URL for Members: https://eipo.nseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs or admin terminals to create branches and users.
- Order modification (quantity) or cancellation shall not be permitted for Takeover offers.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 06, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and exit opportunity to shareholders of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited via the exchange's web-based bidding platform under the takeover tender offer mechanism.
