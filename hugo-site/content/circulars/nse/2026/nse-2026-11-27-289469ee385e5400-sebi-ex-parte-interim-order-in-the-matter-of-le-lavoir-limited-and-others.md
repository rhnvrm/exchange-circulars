## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI's Ex-Parte Interim Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-2/32866/2026-27 dated November 26, 2026. The order restrains Le Lavoir Limited, Ashok Dilip Kumar Jain, and several other noticees from accessing the securities market and prohibits them from buying, selling, or dealing in securities.

## Key Points

- SEBI Interim Order Date: November 26, 2026.
- Noticees include Le Lavoir Limited, Ashok Dilip Kumar Jain, Kajal Ashok Jain, Viha Ashok Jain, Sachin Vishnu Kapse, Ghantiram Foods Private Limited, Shree Vrajendra Foods Private Limited, Ghura Industries, Archana Jagdish Naik, Shreya Dheeraj Jain, Anuradha Patra, Shreeniwas Anand Sanjappa Kumara, Deepti Jairam Chandnani, Vinay Bhushan, and Sonali Abhaykumar Parmar.
- Noticees are restrained from accessing the securities market and buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly.
- Open derivative positions can be squared off/closed out within 3 months from the date of the order.
- Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions prior to the close of trading on the date of the order is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of SEBI's ex-parte interim restrictions against specified debarred entities and individuals.

## Compliance Requirements

NSE members are advised to take note of the debarred entities, ensure compliance, and refrain from facilitating prohibited transactions. A consolidated list of debarred entities is available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 26, 2026
- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Square-off Window for Open Derivative Positions: Within 3 months from the date of the order

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory restriction on named entities and individuals preventing their participation in the securities market, ensuring market integrity and compliance with SEBI directives.
