## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (FO) segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The session aims to test trading modalities and the new NEATPlus version (7.9.2) in alignment with updated changes in the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** November 28, 2026.
- **NEATPlus Version 7.9.2:** Applicable exclusively during Trading Session-1 of the mock. Available on Extranet from 07:00 hours on November 28, 2026.
- **Trading Session-2:** Members must reinstall and login with earlier NEATPlus versions (7.9.1 or 7.9.0) for Trading Session-2, conducted under existing modalities.
- **FINNIFTY Contracts:** Current month FINNIFTY index derivatives contracts with expiry date 29-Dec-2026 shall be pre-matured on 28-Nov-2026, with a revised expiry date of 28-Nov-2026 for mock session-1 testing.
- **Master Files:** Applicable master files for Mock Session 1 will be available on the Extranet by 08:00 hours on November 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing of revised changes in the Closing Auction Session (CAS) and stock/index future price band alignments within the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to download and load the specified NEATPlus version 7.9.2 and master files for Trading Session-1.
- Members must switch back to earlier NEATPlus versions for Trading Session-2 as trades from session 1 will not carry over to session 2.
- Active participation across all trading sessions is advised.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures broker readiness and system stability for upcoming closing auction session modifications and version updates in the F&O segment.
