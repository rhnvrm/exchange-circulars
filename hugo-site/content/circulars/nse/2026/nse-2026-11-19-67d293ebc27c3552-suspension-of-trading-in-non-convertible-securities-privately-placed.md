## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities of Muthoot Finance Limited due to redemption, effective from November 20, 2026, in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Muthoot Finance Limited
- **ISIN:** INE414G07IQ8
- **Suspension Date:** 20-Nov-2026
- **Reason:** Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension carried out in accordance with Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the discontinuation of trading in the specified security.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified non-convertible debentures of Muthoot Finance Limited is halted on the debt segment due to redemption.
