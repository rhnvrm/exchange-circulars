## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the name and trading symbol for A B Cotspin India Limited. Effective November 02, 2026, the company will be known as ABC Industries India Limited and will trade under the new symbol ABCIND.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: A B Cotspin India Limited
- New Name: ABC Industries India Limited
- Existing Symbol: ABCOTS
- New Symbol: ABCIND
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the change and update their systems, records, and client databases with the new name and symbol.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal, restricted to updating trading terminals, reference data, and records to reflect the new name and symbol.
