## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the SEBI order dated November 27, 2026, which disposes of the proceedings initiated vide Order No. WTM/MPB/EFD-1-DRA-IV/139/2018 dated February 15, 2018 against Shri Raj Kumar Yadav (PAN: AHWPY1598A) in the matter of M/s Real Sunshine Corp Limited (earlier known as Real Sunshine Agrotech Limited) without any further directions.

## Key Points

- SEBI disposed of proceedings against Shri Raj Kumar Yadav without any further directions.
- Reference is made to earlier NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/36991 dated February 15, 2018 and SEBI Order dated February 15, 2018.
- The order comes into force with immediate effect.
- Details are available on the SEBI website and the consolidated list of debarred entities on the NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Disposal of proceedings without further directions against the specified noticee by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note and ensure compliance with the updated list of debarred entities on the Exchange website.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Circular: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as proceedings against the individual have been disposed of without further directions.
