## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Shreeji Shipping Global Limited, scheduled to take place on November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026. Eligible trading members can participate using the eOFS platform.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Dates:** November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026.
- **Company Name:** Shreeji Shipping Global Limited.
- **Symbol:** SHREEJISPG.
- **ISIN:** INE1B6101010.
- **Issue Size:** Up to 30,00,000 Equity shares (Base Offer Size) and up to 13,50,000 Equity shares (Oversubscription Option) of face value Rs. 10/- each.
- **Platform:** NSE eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).
- **UCC Compliance:** Members must ensure compliant UCC of self (PRO) and clients are updated in the Unique Client Identification (UCI) system a day prior to saints/OFS bidding session.

## Regulatory Changes

- The Offer for Sale is conducted in accordance with the implementation of Interoperability among Clearing Corporations along with Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/76870 dated October 13, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure UCCs are compliant and updated in the UCI system by the specified cutoff.
- Admin user IDs for eOFS can be requested via ENIT portal (ENIT-NEW-TRADE -> Membership -> Enablement -> eOFS) processed at 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, and 3:00 PM.
- Non-retail investors have the option to resubmit carried forward bids on T+1 day in case of margin insufficiency, provided collateral requirements are met.

## Important Dates

- **OFS Bidding Dates:** November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026.
- **ENIT Request Cutoff:** Requests received post 3:00 PM are processed the next day by 8:45 AM.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates efficient capital divestment and public participation in Shreeji Shipping Global Limited through NSE's eOFS platform while maintaining strict UCC and margin compliance standards.
