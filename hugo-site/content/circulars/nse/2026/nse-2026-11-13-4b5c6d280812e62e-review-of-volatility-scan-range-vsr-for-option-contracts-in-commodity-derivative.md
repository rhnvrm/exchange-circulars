## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the applicable Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for option contracts across specified commodities in the commodity derivatives segment for the month of December 2026. This review is conducted in continuation of earlier circular no. 0365/2026 dated October 15, 2026, and in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- **Applicable Commodities & VSR Rates (December 2026):**
  - Copper: 5%
  - Gold: 5%
  - Silver: 6%
  - Zinc: 5%
  - Crude Oil: 30%
  - Natural Gas: 6%
- **Regulatory Reference:** In continuation to circular no. 0365/2026 (NCL/COM/76905) and SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DRMP/CIR/P/2021/08 dated January 11, 2021.
- **Issued By:** Chief Risk Officer, Huzefa Mahuvawala, on behalf of NSE Clearing Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review and establishment of standardized Volatility Scan Ranges (VSR) for option contracts in commodity derivatives to ensure robust risk management and margin adequacy.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and trading participants must take note of the specified VSR percentages for December 2026 and ensure proper risk management and margin provisioning for commodity option positions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Period:** December 2026

## Impact Assessment

The updated VSR parameters impact margin computations and risk parameters for market participants trading or clearing commodity option contracts on the exchange.
