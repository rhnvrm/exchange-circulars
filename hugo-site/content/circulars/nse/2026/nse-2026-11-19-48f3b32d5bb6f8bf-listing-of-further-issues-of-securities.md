## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for 16 companies, effective from November 20, 2026. These shares are allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026.
- Companies Involved: Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited, Dr. Agarwal's Health Care Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, Dabur India Limited, Five-Star Business Finance Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, Hexaware Technologies Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Indegene Limited, Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited, Marico Limited, Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Limited, Nuvama Wealth Management Limited, PC Jeweller Limited, and Baazar Style Retail Limited.
- Purpose: Admission of further equity shares to dealings on the Capital Market segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate listing action pursuant to NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A regarding designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Individual allotment dates vary by company.

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and ESOP allotment with minimal immediate market impact.
