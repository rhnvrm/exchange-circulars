## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that UTI Financial Services Debt Fund of UTI Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- UTI Financial Services Debt Fund reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities enabled.
- Covers four schemes: Regular Growth, Direct Growth, Regular Payout of IDCW, and Direct Payout of IDCW with respective ISINs.

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the specified debt fund schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform in compliance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless ongoing transaction capabilities and systematic investment options for investors in UTI Financial Services Debt Fund on the NSE MF platform.
