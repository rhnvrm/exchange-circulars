## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Vedant Poly Fabs Limited on the SME platform following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with shareholding pattern disclosures submitted under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 as of November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Vedant Poly Fabs Limited
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Shareholding pattern filed under Reg. 31(1)(a) post-issue of IPO as on November 6, 2026.
- Declarations regarding partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, ESOPs, depository receipts, locked-in shares, and encumbrances submitted as per statutory requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for newly listed SME entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Ongoing disclosures and maintenance of shareholding patterns as mandated under SEBI LODR regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Date of Listing: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Commencement of trading for Vedant Poly Fabs Limited equity shares on the NSE SME exchange, providing public market access and liquidity.
