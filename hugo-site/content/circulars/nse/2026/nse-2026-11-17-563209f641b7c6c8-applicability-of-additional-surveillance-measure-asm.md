## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on market surveillance criteria effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. November 18, 2026:
  1. E2E Networks Limited (Symbol: E2E, ISIN: INE255Z01027)
  2. Frog Innovations Limited (Symbol: FROG, ISIN: INE385O01018)
  3. SG Mart Limited (Symbol: SGMART, ISIN: INE385F01024)
  4. SMVD Poly Pack Limited (Symbol: SMVD, ISIN: INE702Y01013)
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 20, 2026 on all open positions as on November 19, 2026 and new positions created from November 20, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage I surveillance measures on identified securities to protect investors and curb excessive price volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that a 100% margin is levied on all open positions as on November 19, 2026, and on all new positions created from November 20, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting: November 18, 2026
- 100% Margin Applicability Date: November 20, 2026 (on open positions as of November 19, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will experience increased margin obligations (100%), which may impact liquidity and leverage for market participants holding open positions or trading in these scrips.
