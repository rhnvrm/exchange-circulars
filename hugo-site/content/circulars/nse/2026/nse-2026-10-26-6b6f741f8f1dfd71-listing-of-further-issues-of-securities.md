## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 27, 2026, pursuant to Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Listing of further equity shares for companies including Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC, Ajooni Biotech, D.B.Corp, EPL, HDFC Asset Management Company, ICICI Bank, ITC Limited, and others.
- Allotments include shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and preferential issues.
- Effective date for trading of these securities is October 27, 2026.
- Distinctive number ranges and market lots specified in the annexure for each security.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing of further issues authorized under Regulation 3.1.1 and lot sizes under Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the listed securities must be conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes (Market Lot 1).
- Adherence to lock-in details where applicable (e.g., Ajooni Biotech Limited preferential issue lock-in until 29-May-2028; others Not Applicable or as specified).

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., Oct 8, 2026 to Oct 22, 2026, and Aug 12, 2026 for Ajooni Biotech).

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed companies with minor dilution or capital expansion effects.
- Facilitates trading of ESOP and preferential allotment shares for investors and employees on the NSE capital market segment.
