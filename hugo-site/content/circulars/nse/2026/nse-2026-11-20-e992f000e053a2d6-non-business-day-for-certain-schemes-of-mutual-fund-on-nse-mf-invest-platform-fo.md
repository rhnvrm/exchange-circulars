## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for November 2026, as communicated by various Asset Management Companies (AMCs). Consequently, transactions such as Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be permitted for the listed schemes on their respective non-business dates.

## Key Points

- Applies to specific schemes managed by AMCs including Axis, DSP, Edelweiss, Franklin, ICICI Prudential, Invesco, Kotak, Nippon India, PGIM India, and Sundaram.
- Non-business dates span across November 2026 (e.g., November 3, 9, 11, and 26, 2026 depending on the scheme).
- Transactions impacted include Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), Systematic Transfer Plans (STPs), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWPs).
- Trading members and investors must take note of these non-business days while placing orders on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational notification regarding scheme-specific non-business days declared by AMCs on the NSE MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors should ensure no transactions are processed for the affected schemes on the specified non-business dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective/Non-Business Dates: Various dates in November 2026 (specifically 03.11.2026, 09.11.2026, 11.11.2026, and 26.11.2026 across different schemes).

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary suspension of transaction processing for specific mutual fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform, preventing execution errors or settlement delays for investors and members on non-business days.
