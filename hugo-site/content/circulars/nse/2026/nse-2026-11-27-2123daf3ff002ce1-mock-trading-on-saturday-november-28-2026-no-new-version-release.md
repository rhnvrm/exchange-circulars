## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. No new version release is scheduled, but members are informed about NEAT+ version details, NNF encryption mechanism migration, and connectivity parameters.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** November 28, 2026, covering Trading Session 1 from Primary Site and Trading Session 2 from DR Site.
- **NEAT+ Version Details:** Version 3.5.4 (discontinuation on December 05, 2026 during mock) and Version 3.5.5 (recommended).
- **Encryption Migration:** NNF members must migrate from the old interactive port (10885) encryption mechanism by December 07, 2026, to new encryption mechanisms supporting MD5 checksum removal (Port 10889) and immediate acknowledgment (Port 10891).
- **Order Management:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **NOTIS Availability:** The NOTIS application will not be accessible during the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; routine operational guidelines and compliance with existing exchange circulars and SEBI guidelines regarding mock trading and encryption standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, November 30, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.
- NNF members must migrate to the new encryption ports (10889 or 10891) ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026
- **NEAT 3.5.4 Discontinuation:** December 05, 2026 (mock)
- **Old Encryption Mechanism Discontinuation:** December 07, 2026
- **Live Re-login & Normal Trading:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades during mock sessions do not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out. The session ensures system readiness and connectivity for live trading, with operational impact limited to software version updates and encryption port migrations.
