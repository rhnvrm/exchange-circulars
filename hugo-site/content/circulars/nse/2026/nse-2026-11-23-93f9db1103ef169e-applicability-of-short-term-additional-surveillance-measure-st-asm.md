## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities. The surveillance actions come into effect from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: ASPIRE (Aspire & Innovative Advertising Limited) and HINDADH (Hindustan Adhesives Limited).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. November 26, 2026.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created from November 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of specified securities under Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I with enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective November 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Action / Margins: November 26, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 25, 2026
- Circular Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Traders and members holding positions in ASPIRE and HINDADH will be subject to higher margin requirements (50%), impacting leverage and capital allocation for these securities.
