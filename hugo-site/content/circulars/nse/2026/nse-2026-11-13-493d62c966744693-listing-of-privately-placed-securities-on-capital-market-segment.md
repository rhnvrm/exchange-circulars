## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment, effective from November 16, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.
- Issuing Entities: Tata Capital Limited and Sundaram Home Finance Limited.
- Securities admitted include NCDs with various coupon rates and maturity dates (Tata Capital 8.51% 2028, Sundaram Home Finance 8.58% 2031, and Tata Capital 8.565% 2030).
- Trading shall be identified by designated security codes with specified market lots (lot size of 1).

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must trade the listed securities using their designated security codes and adhere to the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Issuance Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 16, 2026
- Maturity Dates: November 10, 2028 (Tata Capital Sr G), February 12, 2030 (Tata Capital Sr F), November 12, 2031 (Sundaram Home Finance Sr 360).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and secondary market trading venue for the privately placed debt securities of Tata Capital Limited and Sundaram Home Finance Limited.
