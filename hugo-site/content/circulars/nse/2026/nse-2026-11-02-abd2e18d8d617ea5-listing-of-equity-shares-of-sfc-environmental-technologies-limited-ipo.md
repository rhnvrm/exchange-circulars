## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission of equity shares of SFC Environmental Technologies Limited to dealings on the Capital Market segment starting from November 03, 2026, following its initial public offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Symbol: SFCTECH
- ISIN: INE0PKN01028
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 330/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 97,956,724 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of SFC Environmental Technologies Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding investment options for market participants.
