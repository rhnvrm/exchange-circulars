## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the inclusion of Tender Offer / Offer to Buy (OTB) under the interoperability framework among clearing corporations, effective on or after November 27, 2026, referencing previous guidelines from April 2019.

## Key Points

- Tender Offer / Offer to Buy (OTB) is brought under the interoperability framework.
- Effective date for implementation is on or after November 27, 2026.
- Revised guidelines for risk management, clearing, settlement, and information dissemination will be notified separately.
- Members and custodians may continue maintaining their designated clearing corporation for Tender Offer and can change it as per established procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of Tender Offer/Offer to Buy (OTB) into the clearing corporation interoperability framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members and Custodians must note the upcoming interoperability framework for Tenders and await subsequent detailed notifications on risk management and settlement guidelines.
- Members may continue with their current designated clearing corporation or follow standard processes to change it.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (or thereafter).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational flexibility for clearing members and custodians participating in Tender Offers and Offer to Buy (OTB) mechanisms.
