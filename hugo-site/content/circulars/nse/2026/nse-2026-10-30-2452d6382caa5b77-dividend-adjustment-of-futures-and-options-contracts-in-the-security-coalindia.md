## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for COALINDIA on account of a dividend of Rs 10.25/- per share.

## Key Points

- Security: COALINDIA (COAL INDIA LIMITED)
- Corporate Action: Dividend
- Face Value: Rs 10/-
- Dividend Amount: Rs 10.25/-
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 4, 2026
- Revised option strike prices, futures base prices, and lot sizes will be provided one day prior to the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments to futures and options contracts are executed in pursuance of SEBI guidelines prescribed for corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must load the updated contract.gz (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread (`NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) files onto their trading applications before trading on the ex-date.
- Files can be obtained from the Extranet server directory `faoftp/faocommon` or the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 4, 2026
- Revised Contract Files Available: One day prior to the ex-date / effective date

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in COALINDIA derivatives must incorporate the revised strike prices, base prices, and lot sizes to ensure accurate contract valuation and risk management.
