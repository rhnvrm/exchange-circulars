## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the extension of trading of Rights Entitlement (RE) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Indian Emulsifiers Limited, updating previously notified timelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indian Emulsifiers Limited
- Symbol: IEML-RE1
- New Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: December 09, 2026
- New Date of Suspension: December 10, 2026
- Modifies previous schedule issued via circular NSE/CML/77402 dated November 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and extension of trading schedule for Rights Entitlements on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems and take note of the revised renunciation and suspension timelines.

## Important Dates

- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation (New): December 09, 2026
- Date of Suspension (New): December 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with extended flexibility to trade and renounce Rights Entitlements for Indian Emulsifiers Limited.
