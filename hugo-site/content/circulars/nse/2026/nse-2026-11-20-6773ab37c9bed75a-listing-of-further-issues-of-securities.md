## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies, effective from November 23, 2026. These issuances primarily comprise shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 23, 2026.
- Covers further equity share issuances for companies including Ather Energy Limited, Bluspring Enterprises Limited, CARE Ratings Limited, ITC Limited, HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, and others.
- Trading will be conducted using designated security codes and respective lot sizes specified in the circular annexure.
- Allotted shares rank pari passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that transactions in these securities are executed using the designated security codes and correct lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Early to mid-November 2026 across respective companies

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action reflecting equity expansion via ESOP allotments, having a negligible direct operational or market impact.
