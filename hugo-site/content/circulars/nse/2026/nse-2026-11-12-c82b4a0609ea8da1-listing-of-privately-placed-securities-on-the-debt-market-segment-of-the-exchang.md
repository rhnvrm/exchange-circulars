## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and debt securities, on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of commercial papers from issuers such as Piramal Finance Limited, Export Import Bank of India, Axis Securities Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, and Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited.
- Admission of debt securities including Rupeek Capital Private Limited's 11% 2028 Sr III.
- Effective date of admission: November 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuers must adhere to standard debt market trading and settlement norms for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: November 9, 2026, and November 11, 2026.
- Effective Listing Date: November 12, 2026.
- Redemption Dates: Various dates ranging from December 2026 to June 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed commercial papers and debt instruments.
