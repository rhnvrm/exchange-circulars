## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the introduction of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on two individual securities: Acutaas Chemicals Limited and Apar Industries Limited, effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities added: Acutaas Chemicals Limited (Symbol: ACUTAAS) and Apar Industries Limited (Symbol: APARINDS).
- Effective date for F&O trading: November 25, 2026, subject to fulfillment of eligibility criteria of the Quarter sigma computation cycle of November 2026.
- Market lot and scheme of strikes will be announced on November 23, 2026, through a separate circular.
- Quantity freeze details will be available in the contract file on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Based on stock selection criteria prescribed by SEBI master circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, and approval received from SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to load the updated `contract.gz` and `NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` files in their trading applications before trading on the effective date.
- Files can be obtained from the directory `faoftp/faocommon` on the Extranet server or via the NSE website derivatives reports section.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Market Lot & Scheme of Strikes Announcement: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for F&O Trading: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the derivatives product basket available to investors and market participants, enhancing trading liquidity and hedging capabilities for the newly included securities.
