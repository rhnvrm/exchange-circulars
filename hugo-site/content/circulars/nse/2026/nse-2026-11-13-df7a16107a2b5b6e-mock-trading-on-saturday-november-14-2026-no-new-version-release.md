## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. No new software version release is scheduled for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading conducted on Saturday, November 14, 2026, across primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 details and download path provided (/cmftp/common/NEATPlus790).
- Reminder for NNF members to migrate to new encryption mechanisms/ports before December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their trading systems, gateway routers, and encryption port configurations during the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026
- NNF Encryption Migration Deadline: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures member readiness, system stability, and smooth functioning during market operations and potential DR switchovers.
