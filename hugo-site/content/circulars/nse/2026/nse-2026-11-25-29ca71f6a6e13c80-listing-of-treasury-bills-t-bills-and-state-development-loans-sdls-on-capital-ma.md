## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of multiple State Development Loans (SDLs) and Treasury Bills (T-bills) on its capital market segment, effective from November 27, 2026. Trading will take place using designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular.

## Key Points

- Admission of 9 State Development Loans (SDLs) and 3 Treasury Bills (T-bills) to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
- Effective date for trading: November 27, 2026.
- Standard trading lot size for all listed securities is 100.
- Securities include SDLs from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Jammu & Kashmir, Haryana, Sikkim, Uttar Pradesh, and Kerala, along with 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day GOI Treasury Bills.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, these securities are admitted with specified lot sizes and designated codes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026
- Maturity Dates ranging from November 2029 to November 2046 for SDLs, and February 2027 to November 2027 for T-bills.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading opportunities in government securities and state development loans on the NSE capital market segment.
