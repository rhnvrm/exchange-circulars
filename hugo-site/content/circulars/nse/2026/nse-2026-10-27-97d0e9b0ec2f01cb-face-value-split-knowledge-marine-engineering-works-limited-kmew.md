## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value split for equity shares of Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW). The existing face value and paid-up value of Rs. 5 per share will be subdivided into Re. 1 per share, effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited
- Symbol: KMEW
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1 per share
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Subdivision of equity share face value from Rs. 5 to Re. 1 as per corporate action proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to take note of the change in face value and update trading terminals and systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Implementation Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The stock split increases the number of outstanding shares proportionally while reducing the share price correspondingly, enhancing retail liquidity and participation.
