## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of privately placed securities of REC Limited on the capital market segment with effect from November 25, 2026, pursuant to NSE trading regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer: REC Limited
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026
- Security 1: REC 7.89% 2036 PP 262B (Symbol: 789REC36, ISIN: INE020B08GP8, Maturity: 19-Nov-2036, Coupon: 7.89%, Lot Size: 1)
- Security 2: REC 7.99% 2031 Sr 262A (Symbol: 799RECL31, ISIN: INE020B08GO1, Maturity: 01-Dec-2031, Coupon: 7.99%, Lot Size: 1)

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified by their designated codes and traded in specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026
- Maturity Date (Sr 262A): December 1, 2031
- Maturity Date (PP 262B): November 19, 2036

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official market platform for trading of REC Limited's privately placed debt securities.
