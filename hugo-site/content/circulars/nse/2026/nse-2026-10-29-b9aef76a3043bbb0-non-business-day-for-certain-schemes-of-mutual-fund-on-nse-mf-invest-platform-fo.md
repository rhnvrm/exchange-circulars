## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes of various Asset Management Companies (AMCs) on the NSE MF Invest Platform for the month of November 2026. Subscriptions, redemptions, switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be processed for the specified schemes on their respective non-business dates.

## Key Points

- Affected AMCs include Axis, DSP, Edelweiss, Franklin, ICICI, Invesco, Kotak, Nippon, PGIM, and Sundaram.
- Non-business dates fall on various days in November 2026 (such as 03.11.2026, 09.11.2026, 11.11.2026, and 26.11.2026) depending on the specific scheme.
- Transactions impacted: Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs.

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must note the non-business days for the listed overseas and specific equity/debt funds to avoid transaction failures or delays on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Non-Business Dates: Various dates across November 2026 (e.g., Nov 3, Nov 9, Nov 11, Nov 26, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to investors and members trading in the specific mutual fund schemes listed in Annexure I on their respective non-business dates.
