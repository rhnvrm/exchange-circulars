## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a delisting offer under the Tender Offer scheme by Jindal Photo Limited. The acquirers, Concatenate Power Advest Private Limited and Concatenate Advest Advisory Private Limited along with Jindal India Power Limited (PAC), have approached the Exchange for using the web-based bidding platform. The issue period is scheduled from November 02, 2026, to November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Security Symbol:** JINDALPHOT
- **ISIN:** INE796G01012
- **Offer Type:** Delisting (Reverse Book Building)
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 1119.50 per Equity Share
- **Issue Period:** 02-Nov-2026 to 06-Nov-2026
- **Bidding Time:** 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation up to 4:00 p.m. on closure day)
- **Live URL:** https://eipo.nseindia.com
- **Merchant Banker:** Saffron Capital Advisors Private Limited
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

The delisting offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading members of the Capital Market segment are eligible to participate. Existing users of the web-based IPO bidding platform can use their existing user IDs. Members without access can log in via the admin terminal to create branches and users with the format Adminxxxxx (where xxxxx is the Member Code).

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** November 02, 2026 (9:15 a.m.)
- **Issue Closing Date:** November 06, 2026 (3:30 p.m.)
- **Custodial Confirmation on Closure Day:** Up to 4:00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

This delisting offer provides eligible shareholders of Jindal Photo Limited an exit opportunity through the reverse book-building mechanism at the floor/offer price of Rs. 1119.50 per share.
