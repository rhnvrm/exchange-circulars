## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the exclusion of Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (WILLAMAGOR) from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The security will be moved out of the GSM framework effective November 2, 2026, following completion of the price discovery process under the Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) mechanism.

## Key Points

- Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (WILLAMAGOR) was previously retained under the GSM framework as per Exchange Circular no. NSE/SURV/76204 dated September 4, 2026.
- Pursuant to joint meetings with SEBI and Stock Exchanges, companies undergoing corporate restructuring, corporate actions, or regulatory directions (such as suspension exceeding one year) placed in SPOS for price discovery are moved out from the Surveillance Framework.
- Upon completion of price discovery under SPOS, securities are considered for inclusion under applicable surveillance frameworks based on prescribed eligibility criteria and timelines.
- The scrip WILLAMAGOR shall be moved out of the GSM framework w.e.f. November 2, 2026, as per Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76935 dated October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of WILLAMAGOR from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- Transition out of surveillance framework following SPOS price discovery mechanism agreed upon with SEBI and Stock Exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- No direct compliance actions required by members other than noting the removal of trading restrictions under GSM for the scrip.
- Queries regarding surveillance can be directed to surveillance@nse.co.in.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 2, 2026 (scrip moved out of GSM framework).
- Previous Circular Dates: September 4, 2026 (NSE/SURV/76204) and October 15, 2026 (NSE/CML/76935).

## Impact Assessment

- Positive impact for shareholders and market participants trading in Williamson Magor & Company Ltd., as trading restrictions associated with the GSM framework are lifted following price discovery.
