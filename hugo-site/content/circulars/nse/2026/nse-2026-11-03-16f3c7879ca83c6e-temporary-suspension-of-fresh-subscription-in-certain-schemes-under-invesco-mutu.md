## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that fresh registrations and existing installment processing for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and IDCW Transfer Plans in select overseas Fund of Fund schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund are temporarily suspended on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes:
  1. Invesco India - Invesco Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  2. Invesco India - Invesco Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
  3. Invesco India - Invesco Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
- Suspension applies to fresh registrations under SIP and STP, as well as installments of existing SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plans already registered as of November 03, 2026.
- Existing SIPs, STPs, and IDCW Transfer Plans remain active in the system and will be reactivated once the temporary suspension is revoked.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain unaffected and will continue to be processed normally.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction on fresh subscriptions and ongoing installment processing for specific overseas fund-of-fund categories under Invesco Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors must take note of the suspension and inform clients regarding the temporary halt on SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan installments for the designated schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or planning investments through SIP/STP/IDCW transfer plans in the designated Invesco overseas fund-of-fund schemes will experience temporary suspension of installment debits and fresh registrations. Other transaction types such as redemptions and SWPs are unaffected.
