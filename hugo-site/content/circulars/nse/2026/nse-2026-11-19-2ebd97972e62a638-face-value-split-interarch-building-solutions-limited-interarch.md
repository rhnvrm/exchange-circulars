## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value and paid-up value split for equity shares of Interarch Building Solutions Limited (INTERARCH).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Interarch Building Solutions Limited
- Symbol: INTERARCH
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2 per share
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The face value of equity shares for Interarch Building Solutions Limited is subdivided from Rs. 10 to Rs. 2.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should note the change in face value and update their trading and risk management systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The stock split increases the number of outstanding shares proportionally while reducing the market price per share, enhancing liquidity and retail participation.
