## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I for specified securities. The applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I include Maitreya Medicare Limited (MAITREYA) and Medico Remedies Limited (MEDICO).
- Applicable rate of margin is 100% effective October 29, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term ASM framework measures including heightened margin requirements for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure 100% margin collection on open positions as on October 28, 2026, and all new positions created from October 29, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Open Positions / New Positions Margin: October 29, 2026 (on open positions as of October 28, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in MAITREYA and MEDICO will attract 100% margins under Long-Term ASM Stage I, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes.
