## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular detailing the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- ABM International Limited (ABMINTLLTD) is moving from ESM Stage I to Stage II effective October 27, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Affected securities attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created from October 28, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under ESM includes ABMINTLLTD, ACSTECH, and APSISAERO.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance measures requiring 100% margins and movement of listed securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and note the settlement changes.

## Important Dates

- October 27, 2026: Shifting to Stage II / Price band under Periodic Call Auction
- October 28, 2026: 100% margin applicability and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in shortlisted securities must manage liquidity and maintain required 100% margins for open and new positions.
