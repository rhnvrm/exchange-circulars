## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Quintegra Solutions Limited is moving from Stage I to Stage II, while other securities remain under the consolidated ESM list. These measures include trading restrictions, margin requirements, and settlement shifts.

## Key Points

- Quintegra Solutions Limited (QUINTEGRA) moves from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. October 30, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 30, 2026.
- Affected securities attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 02, 2026, on all open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework stages, moving specified securities between stages and applying strict trading controls in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Stage II transitions, price bands, and periodic call auction: October 30, 2026.
- Effective date for 100% margin requirements and settlement segment shift: November 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions in shortlisted securities will experience increased margin obligations and trading restrictions, enhancing risk management and curbing excessive volatility.
