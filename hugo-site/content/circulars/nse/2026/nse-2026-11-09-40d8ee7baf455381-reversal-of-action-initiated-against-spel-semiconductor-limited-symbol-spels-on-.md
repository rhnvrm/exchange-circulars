## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the reversal of action initiated against SPEL Semiconductor Limited (Symbol: SPELS) due to the payment of pending Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the Financial Year 2025-26.

## Key Points

- The Company has cleared pending ALF dues, prompting NSE to reverse prior trading restriction actions notified via Circular No. NSE/CML/76264 dated September 09, 2026.
- Trading in securities of SPEL Semiconductor Limited will move from Trade for Trade category (BE) to Rolling Settlement (EQ).
- The changes become effective from November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trading restrictions and reclassification of security series from BE to EQ.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company has successfully cleared its pending Annual Listing Fees compliance for FY 2025-26.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and market participants can resume regular rolling settlement trading in SPEL Semiconductor Limited (SPELS) effective November 11, 2026.
