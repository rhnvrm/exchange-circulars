## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) and Fund of Fund transactions for Invesco India Silver ETF and Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Subscription Window:** October 27, 2026 to November 09, 2026 till 11:59 pm.
- **Allotment Date:** November 16, 2026.
- **Payment Clearing Deadline:** Funds must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 16, 2026 till 12:00 pm.
- **Schemes Included:** Various regular and direct plans for Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund and Invesco India Silver ETF.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through authorized payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by the specified deadline.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** October 27, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** November 09, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** November 16, 2026 (12:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to silver ETF and fund of fund investment options via the NSE MF Invest Platform during the NFO period.
