## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The session comprises trading from both the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites, along with live re-login tests to ensure smooth operations for the upcoming week.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date**: November 28, 2026
- **Trading Session-1 (Primary Site)**: Normal Market open at 10:45 hrs, close at 12:15 hrs.
- **Trading Session-2 (DR Site)**: Normal Market open at 13:00 hrs, close at 13:30 hrs.
- **Live Re-login**: Start time at 18:30 hrs, close time at 19:00 hrs.
- **NEAT Versions**: NEAT 1.3.4 is recommended (Path: `/slbftp/slbcommon/NEAT1.3.4`). NEAT 1.3.3 discontinuation is slated for the mock of January 2027.
- **Order Management**: All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. NNF users must clear orders manually.
- **Financial Obligations**: No fund pay-in or pay-out will result from mock trades.
- **UCC & Compliance**: Only valid and approved UCC/PAN will be permitted. NOTIS application will remain unavailable during the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; conducted pursuant to routine market preparation and testing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible members should participate using existing User IDs, IPs, and Box ID mappings.
- Ensure telnet connectivity to the Exchange host and verify Box ID setups.
- Re-login to the live environment post-mock to ensure connectivity for Monday, November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date**: November 28, 2026
- **Live Operations Resume**: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Provides a controlled testing environment for members to validate trading systems, DR switchover readiness, and connectivity without financial risk.
