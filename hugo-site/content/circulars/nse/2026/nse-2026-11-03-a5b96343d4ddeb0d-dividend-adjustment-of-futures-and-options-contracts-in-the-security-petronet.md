## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for PETRONET LNG LIMITED on account of a dividend of Rs 7/- per share (face value Rs 10/-), effective from November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: PETRONET (PETRONET LNG LIMITED)
- Corporate Action Type: Dividend
- Face Value: Rs 10/-
- Dividend Amount: Rs 7/-
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 6, 2026
- Revised option strike prices and futures base prices will be published one day prior to the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments to futures and options contracts are executed in pursuance of SEBI guidelines on corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must load updated contract.gz and spread files (NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz and NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) from the Extranet server or NSE website before trading on the ex-date.
- Revised option strike prices, futures prices, lot size, and quantity freeze limits will appear in decimal places, rounded off to the nearest tick size and nearest integer respectively.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 6, 2026
- Contract files availability: One day prior to the ex-date/effective date.

## Impact Assessment

- Standard F&O contract adjustments for PETRONET ensuring continuity and correct valuation of derivative positions following the dividend payout.
