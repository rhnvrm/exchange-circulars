## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of securities for several companies including CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, Gabriel India Limited, ICICI Life Insurance Limited, Kanpur Plastipack Limited, KSH International Limited, The Karnataka Bank Limited, Sudarshan Chemical Industries Limited, Transport Corporation of India Limited, and Travel Food Services Limited, effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective November 11, 2026.
- Includes shares issued under ESOPs, preferential issues, and warrant conversions.
- Distinctive numbers and lock-in details specified as per the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the listed securities shall be identified by designated codes and standard lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing of further equity shares resulting from corporate actions (ESOP, preferential allotment, warrants), expanding the paid-up capital of the respective listed entities.
