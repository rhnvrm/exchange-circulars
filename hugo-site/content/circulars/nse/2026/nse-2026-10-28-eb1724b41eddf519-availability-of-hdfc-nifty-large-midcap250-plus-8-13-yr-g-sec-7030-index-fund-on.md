## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the HDFC Nifty Large Midcap250 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund of HDFC Mutual Fund is available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes covered include Direct and Regular plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- ISIN Details:
  - Direct Growth: INF179KC1LB0
  - Direct IDCW Payout: INF179KC1LC8
  - Direct IDCW Reinvestment: INF179KC1LD6
  - Regular Growth: INF179KC1LE4
  - Regular IDCW Payout: INF179KC1LF1
  - Regular IDCW Reinvestment: INF179KC1LG9

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can execute transactions in the listed schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform in compliance with existing platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment product availability on the NSE platform, enabling seamless access to hybrid index fund options for market participants.
