## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund schemes for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Quant Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund (Direct and Regular Plans across IDCW and Growth options)
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine platform availability notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the ISINs and scheme availability for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and members access to additional index fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform for systematic investments and lump sum transactions.
