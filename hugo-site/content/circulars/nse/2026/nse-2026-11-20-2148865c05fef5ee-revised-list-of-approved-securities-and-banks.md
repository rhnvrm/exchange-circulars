## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks under its Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) scheme and non-cash component of liquid assets.

## Key Points

- Annexure 1 outlines equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets and SLB.
- Applicable haircut for the listed securities is set to VAR.
- Value-based prudential norm specifies that the exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities for each security.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the list of eligible securities for non-cash collateral and SLB operations.
- Re-establishes permitted quantities and haircut applicability across clearing members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure that the exclusion of pledged securities does not exceed the 25% margin shortfall threshold.
- Members should verify permitted quantities and applicable VAR haircuts for all listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately upon circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for clearing members dealing with liquid assets, collateral management, and SLB trading.
