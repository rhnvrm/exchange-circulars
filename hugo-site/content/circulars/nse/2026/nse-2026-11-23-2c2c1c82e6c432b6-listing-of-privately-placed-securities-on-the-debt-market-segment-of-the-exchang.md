## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of fresh commercial papers issued by multiple companies.
- Effective date of admission: November 23, 2026.
- Issuers include Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Blue Star Limited, Trent Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Axis Securities Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, and Kotak Securities Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and related operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 20-Nov-2026
- Effective Listing Date: 23-Nov-2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from December 2026 to November 2027 across various issuances.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating secondary market trading for the listed commercial papers.
