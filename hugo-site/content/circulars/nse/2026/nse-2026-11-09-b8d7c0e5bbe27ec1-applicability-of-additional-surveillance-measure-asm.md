## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) Stage - I.

## Key Points

- Securities included under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I effective November 11, 2026:
  - Ather Energy Limited (ATHERENERG - INE0LEZ01016)
  - LCC Infotech Limited (LCCINFOTEC - INE938A01021)
  - Spectrum Electrical Industries Limited (SPECTRUM - INE01EO01010)
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities identified under Long-Term ASM are subjected to specific surveillance actions as per exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% on all open positions and new positions created as per the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for inclusion: November 11, 2026
- Margin applicability date: 100% margin w.e.f. November 13, 2026 on all open positions as on November 12, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will require 100% margins, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants holding or trading these scrips.
