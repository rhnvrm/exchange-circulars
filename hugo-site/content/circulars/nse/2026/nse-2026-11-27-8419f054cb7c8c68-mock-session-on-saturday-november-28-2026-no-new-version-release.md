## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 28, 2026. The session will operate from both Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites, with no new version release.

## Key Points

- Trading Session-1 (Primary Site): Normal Market open at 10:45 Hrs, close at 12:15 Hrs.
- Trading Session-2 (DR Site): Normal Market open at 13:00 Hrs, close at 13:30 Hrs.
- Position Limit/Collateral value Set up cut off time and Trade Modification end time: 13:40 Hrs.
- Live Re-login Timings: Start time 18:30 Hrs, close time 19:00 Hrs.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 1.5.6 (recommended) and NEAT 1.5.5 are supported. NEAT 1.5.5 discontinuation scheduled for January 2027.
- NNF encryption mechanism migration reminder for old interactive port (10865) discontinuation from December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational mock session and reminders for NNF encryption migration.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to participate in mock trading, clear outstanding orders before each session, and ensure migration to new NNF encryption mechanisms ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: November 28, 2026
- NNF Old Encryption Mechanism Discontinuation: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on live operations as the mock session is scheduled on a Saturday for system testing and readiness.
