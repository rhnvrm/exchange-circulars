## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares of Dudigital Global Limited allotted under ESOP, effective from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Dudigital Global Limited
- Symbol: DUGLOBAL
- Securities: 64,702 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/-
- ISIN: INE0HPK01020 (temporary ISIN IN8********* applicable for demat credit)
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

None.

Trading in these securities shall be in the specified market lot of 2,500 shares using designated codes.

- Date of Allotment: November 3, 2026
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Routine corporate action involving listing of further shares issued under ESOP, with minimal impact on market operations.
