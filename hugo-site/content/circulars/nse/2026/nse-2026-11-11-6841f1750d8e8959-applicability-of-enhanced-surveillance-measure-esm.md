## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Four securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I and will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ESM Stage I: Kabra Jewels Limited (KKJEWELS), Makers Laboratories Limited (MAKERSL), Positron Energy Limited (POSITRON), and Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited (SILKFLEX).
- A minimum 100% margin will be levied on all open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created from November 13, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage I.
- Mandatory 100% margin requirement on open positions as of November 12, 2026, and all subsequent new positions.
- Migration of securities from Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that the 100% margin requirement is collected and maintained on open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions from November 13, 2026.
- Market participants must facilitate trading in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- November 12, 2026: Open positions reference date for margin applicability.
- November 13, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

The inclusion under ESM Stage I increases trading friction and liquidity constraints for the affected securities due to the 100% margin requirement and Trade-for-Trade settlement, impacting market participants holding open positions or trading in these scrips.
