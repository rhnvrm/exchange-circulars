## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued circular reference NCL/CMPT/77578 (Circular Ref. No: 133/2026) regarding the applicable Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Trading Member (TM) limits, FII/FPI limits, and Mutual Fund limits for the introduction of two individual securities: ACUTAAS and APARINDS. These limits take effect from the trade date November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable for two securities: ACUTAAS and APARINDS.
- Effective trade date: November 25, 2026.
- Limits specified include Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), overall stock limits for clients, proprietary limits, trading member proprietary limits, FII/FPI (Category I & II), and Mutual Fund position limits.
- ACUTAAS Market Wide Position Limit: 8,269,449 shares.
- APARINDS Market Wide Position Limit: 2,800,200 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits and thresholds for trading members, clients, FII/FPIs, and mutual funds are formally established for the newly introduced F&O securities in pursuance of previous circulars nos. 050/2026 and 108/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the specified position limits for ACUTAAS and APARINDS starting from November 25, 2026, avoiding any breach of client level, proprietary, FII/FPI, or mutual fund ceilings.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Participants trading in the Futures & Options segment must align their risk management and position monitoring systems with the newly prescribed limits for ACUTAAS and APARINDS to ensure smooth execution and regulatory adherence.
