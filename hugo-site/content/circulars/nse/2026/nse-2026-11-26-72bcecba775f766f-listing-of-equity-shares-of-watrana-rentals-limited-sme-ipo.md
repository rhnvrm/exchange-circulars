## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Watrana Rentals Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform with effect from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Watrana Rentals Limited
- Symbol: WATRANA
- ISIN: INE11NS01011
- Issue Price: Rs. 245/- per share
- Number of Securities: 16,340,500 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 16340500
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares
- Series: 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment (TFTS) – Settlement Type ‘W’) and subsequently shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type ‘N’)

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings on the NSE EMERGE SME Platform pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Lot sizes specified pursuant to Regulation 2.5.5.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the security will be identified by its designated code WATRANA in lot sizes of 600 equity shares.
- Security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date / Trading Commencement Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading and liquidity for Watrana Rentals Limited on the NSE EMERGE platform, initially under the trade-for-trade surveillance segment before transitioning to the normal rolling segment.
