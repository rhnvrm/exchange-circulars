## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund (HDFC Mutual Fund) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The schemes will be open for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026.
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform (NMF TM Segment).
- **Fund House:** HDFC Mutual Fund.
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Eligible ISINs and Schemes:**
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Regular Plan - Growth (INF174KA1G98)
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1H14)
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Direct Plan - Growth (INF174KA1G64)
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1G80)
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (INF174KA1H06)
  - Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (INF174KA1G72)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants must take note of the listed ISINs and facilitate client orders accordingly on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for investors and members to transact in the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund across regular and direct growth/IDCW plans via the NSE MF platform.
