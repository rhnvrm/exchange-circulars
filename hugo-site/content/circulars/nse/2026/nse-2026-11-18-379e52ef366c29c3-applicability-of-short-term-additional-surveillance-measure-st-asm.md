## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for certain securities, effective November 20, 2026. Securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I will attract applicable margin rates of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%). Additionally, certain securities are shifted between ST-ASM stages.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Allcargo Global Limited (AGL), Amanta Healthcare Limited (AMANTA), Brace Port Logistics Limited (BRACEPORT), and Salasar Techno Engineering Limited (SALASAR).
- Dhariwalcorp Limited (DHARIWAL) has moved from ST-ASM Framework Stage II to Stage I.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage I is 50% (or existing, max 100%) w.e.f. November 20, 2026, on all open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or existing margin capped at 100%) for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: November 20, 2026 (on open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements will increase capital allocation requirements for trading participants holding positions in the affected securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
