## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Kotak Nifty Pharma Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO subscription window is open from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered include Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options.
- Subscription Window: November 16, 2026 to November 30, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Asset Category: Equity (Nifty Pharma Index Fund).
- Allotment Date: December 03, 2026 across all ISINs (INF174KA1I54, INF174KA1I70, INF174KA1I21, INF174KA1I47, INF174KA1I62, INF174KA1I39).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 02, 2026 till 10:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 16, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 30, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Funds Pay-in Cut-off Date: December 02, 2026 (10:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors access to invest in the Kotak Nifty Pharma Index Fund NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform with standard operational and settlement workflows.
