## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited, scheduled to take place on November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026. The circular outlines platform access, Admin User ID creation via the ENIT portal, UCC compliance guidelines, and margin/bidding rules.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Dates:** November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026.
- **Company Name & Symbol:** Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited (Symbol: LOTUSDEV, ISIN: INE0V9Q01010).
- **Issue Size:** Up to 2,37,95,818 Equity shares (Base Offer Size) and up to 50,00,000 Equity shares (Oversubscription Option) of face value Re 1/- each.
- **Categories:** IS (Non-retail category) and RS (Retail category – Bid value < or = Rs 2 lacs).
- **Platform:** NSE eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).

## Regulatory Changes

- The Offer for Sale is conducted in accordance with the implementation of Interoperability among Clearing Corporations along with Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/76870 dated October 13, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members must use the eOFS platform and ensure Admin User ID requests are raised through the ENIT portal (`ENIT-NEW-TRADE→Membership→Enablement→eOFS`).
- Compliant UCCs of self (PRO) and clients must be updated in the Unique Client Identification (UCI) system a day prior to the OFS bidding session.
- UCCs identified as compliant by the Exchange at 22:00 hrs on the previous day are permitted to trade for the next bidding day; UCCs updated on the day of bidding will not be accepted.
- Non-retail investors may resubmit carried-forward bids on T+1 day in case of margin insufficiency, subject to collateral requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 02, 2026
- **OFS Bidding Dates:** November 03, 2026, and November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members participating in the OFS must ensure timely UCC compliance and eOFS platform enablement to avoid participation restrictions during the live bidding session.
