## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the review of the margin framework for the commodity derivatives segment, referencing SEBI circular dated November 17, 2026. The framework revises the minimum Initial Margin (IM) and minimum Margin Period of Risk (MPOR) based on volatility categories for both agricultural and non-agricultural commodities.

## Key Points

- Revises Chapter 9 (Para 9.1.3.I.a(iv)) of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives dated August 4, 2023.
- Establishes updated minimum Initial Margin (IM) and Margin Period of Risk (MPOR) across Low, Medium, and High volatility categories.
- Non-Agri and Agri commodities maintain aligned baseline structures: Low volatility (6% IM, 2 MPOR), Medium volatility (8% IM, 2 MPOR), and High volatility (10% IM, 3 MPOR).
- Applicable for a period of one year from the date of issuance unless extended by the Board.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Minimum Initial Margin and MPOR Revision:**
  - **Low Volatility:** Non-Agri (6% IM, 2 MPOR) | Agri (6% IM, 2 MPOR)
  - **Medium Volatility:** Non-Agri (8% IM, 2 MPOR) | Agri (8% IM, 2 MPOR)
  - **High Volatility:** Non-Agri (10% IM, 3 MPOR) | Agri (10% IM, 3 MPOR)

## Compliance Requirements

- Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) must implement necessary system changes, update relevant bye-laws, rules, and regulations, and disseminate provisions to market participants and investors.

## Important Dates

- **Issuance Date:** November 17, 2026 (SEBI) / November 18, 2026 (NSE)
- **Effective Date:** December 01, 2026
- **Validity:** Applicable for one year from issuance

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in commodity derivatives will be subject to revised minimum margin requirements and risk periods starting December 01, 2026, impacting capital allocations and position monitoring.
