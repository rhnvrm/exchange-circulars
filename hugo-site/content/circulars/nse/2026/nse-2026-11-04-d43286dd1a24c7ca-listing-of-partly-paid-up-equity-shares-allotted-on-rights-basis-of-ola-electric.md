## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading of partly paid-up equity shares allotted on a rights basis by Ola Electric Mobility Limited, effective from November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ola Electric Mobility Limited
- **Symbol:** OLAPP
- **ISIN:** IN90LXG01014
- **Number of Securities:** 370,272,665 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each (Rs. 6/- partly paid up)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 27 per share (Rs. 16.20 paid on application)
- **Date of Allotment:** November 2, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of partly paid-up equity shares to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Trading Regulations Part A.
- Shares are currently credited under temporary ISIN (IN8*********) per SEBI circular guidelines for additional share issues prior to final listing/trading activation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure trades are conducted using the designated security code (OLAPP) and lot sizes.
- Adherence to temporary ISIN crediting and subsequent merging protocols as per applicable SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the capital base of Ola Electric Mobility Limited with 370,272,665 partly paid-up shares.
- Enables investors and traders to trade the partly paid-up rights shares under symbol OLAPP starting November 6, 2026.
