## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Concord Biotech Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. A total of 10,46,16,204 equity shares are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Concord Biotech Limited
- Symbol: CONCORDBIO
- ISIN: INE338H01029
- Record Date: November 04, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 05, 2026
- Number of Securities: 10,46,16,204
- Distinctive Number Range: 104616205 to 209232408
- Effective Date for Trading: November 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Processed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 04, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of equity capital base for Concord Biotech Limited with no adverse market or operational impact.
