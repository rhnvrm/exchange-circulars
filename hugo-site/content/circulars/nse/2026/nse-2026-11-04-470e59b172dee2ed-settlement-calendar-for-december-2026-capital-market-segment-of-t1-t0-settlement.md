## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Capital Market Segment covering T+1 and T+0 settlements for December 2026.

## Key Points

- Details settlement schedules for Normal Rolling (T+1) settlement type M.
- Provides schedules for Normal Auction settlement type A.
- Covers Trade for Trade (T+1) settlement type Z, ITP for SME (T+1) settlement type B, and Normal Rolling (T+0) settlement type 5.

## Regulatory Changes

- Notification issued in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, custodians, and PCMs must adhere to the notified settlement schedules for December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective for settlement cycles throughout December 2026 (starting from December 01, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market participants are aware of trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates to manage clearing and settlement obligations smoothly.
