## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for select securities pursuant to ongoing market surveillance. Effective November 26, 2026, specific securities are shortlisted under ESM Stage I, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Inclusion:** Securities including Dc Infotech And Communication Limited (DCI), M.V.K. Agro Food Product Limited (MVKAGRO), and Sotac Pharmaceuticals Limited (Sotac) have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I.
- **Margin Requirements:** A minimum 100% margin is applicable on all open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created from November 26, 2026.
- **Segment Shift:** Securities qualifying under ESM shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 26, 2026.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.
- Mandatory shifting to Trade-for-Trade settlement for included securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100% minimum) on open and new positions for the affected securities.
- Market participants must note the settlement segment changes (EQ/SM to BE/ST).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin / Settlement Shift:** November 26, 2026 (on open positions as on November 25, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the impacted securities (DCI, MVKAGRO, SOTAC) will be constrained due to the 100% margin requirement and trade-for-trade settlement mechanism.
