## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the migration of equity shares of Spectrum Talent Management Limited from the SME Emerge platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board). Trading on the Main Board will commence effective December 01, 2026 under the symbol SPECTSTM.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spectrum Talent Management Limited
- Symbol: SPECTSTM
- ISIN: INE0OL001018
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Number of Securities: 23,093,096
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 23093096
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: December 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of listing platform from SME Emerge to Capital Market Segment (Main Board) pursuant to exchange guidelines and company compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the security details, ISIN, and market lot for trading on the Main Board.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Migration / Listing: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members gain mainstream market access for Spectrum Talent Management Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and broader participation.
