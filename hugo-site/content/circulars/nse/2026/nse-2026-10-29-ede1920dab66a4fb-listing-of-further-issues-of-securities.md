## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for various companies including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Akme Fintrade (India) Limited, Dr. Agarwal's Health Care Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Ather Energy Limited, and others, effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective October 30, 2026.
- Includes share allotments under ESOPs and preferential conversion of warrants.
- Trading will be conducted in specified market lots and identified by designated security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine capital changes and listing of further issues under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and trading members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., October 23, 2026 for Aditya Birla Capital; July 29, 2026 for Akme Fintrade; October 20, 2026 for Dr. Agarwal's Health Care; October 21, 2026 for Asian Energy Services).

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action resulting in the expansion of paid-up equity capital for the respective listed companies with normal market operations.
