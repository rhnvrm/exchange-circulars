## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Bright Outdoor Media Limited on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) following its successful migration from the SME Platform of BSE.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bright Outdoor Media Limited
- Transition: Migrated from BSE SME Platform to NSE Main Board
- Associated shareholding pattern and regulatory filings submitted as per exchange norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of listing and trading venue to the NSE Main Board, subjecting the company to mainstream regulatory frameworks and continuous disclosure requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and NSE listing agreements for main board entities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date of migration and trading commencement on NSE Main Board as notified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced market visibility, liquidity, and broader investor participation opportunities for shareholders of Bright Outdoor Media Limited.
