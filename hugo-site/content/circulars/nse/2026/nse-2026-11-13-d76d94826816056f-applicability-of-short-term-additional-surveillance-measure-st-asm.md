## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I:
  - Halder Venture Limited (HALDER - INE115S01010)
  - Jyoti Structures Limited (JYOTISTRUC - INE197A01024)
  - Orchasp Limited (ORCHASP - INE215B01022)
  - Pulz Electronics Limited (PULZ - INE335X01014)
  - Spacenet Enterprises India Limited (SPCENET - INE970N01027)
  - Tera Software Limited (TERASOFT - INE482B01010)
- No securities are listed under ST-ASM Stage II in this circular.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. November 17, 2026, on all open positions as on November 16, 2026, and new positions created from November 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for identified scrips experiencing abnormal price movement or volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective November 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on November 16, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in the affected securities, aimed at curbing excessive volatility and ensuring market integrity.
