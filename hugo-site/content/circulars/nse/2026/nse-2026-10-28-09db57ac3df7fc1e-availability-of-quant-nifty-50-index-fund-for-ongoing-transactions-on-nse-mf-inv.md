## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that various plans of the Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund reopened for ongoing transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Facilities enabled: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.
- Covered schemes include Direct and Regular plans (IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, Growth).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine platform operational update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants can facilitate investor transactions for the specified ISINs through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with convenient access to trade and set up systematic investment plans for Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund through NSE's platform.
