## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the introduction of additional after-market short allocation reports (SA01/SA02/SA03) for collaterals in the Currency Derivatives segment, effective November 09, 2026. These reports are aligned with the timing of intraday snapshots in the Commodity Derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Additional SA01/SA02/SA03 reports will be provided after-market hours based on segment snapshots aligned with Commodity Derivatives segment intraday snapshot timings.
- Reports include details of collateral value allocated by clearing members to clients plus securities repledged at the Clearing Corporation and EOD minimum margins.
- Collateral and margin details in these additional reports are used to arrive at excess client collateral in the segment for short allocation assessment in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- No changes to the existing methodology for computing peak margins during market hours or short allocation calculations.
- File nomenclature, location, and format remain identical to existing intraday short allocation reports.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of supplementary after-market short allocation reports for collaterals to streamline cross-segment collateral utilization and short allocation tracking between currency derivatives and commodity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must take note of the additional reports and utilize them for tracking excess collateral and short allocations effectively.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational enhancement for clearing members managing collaterals across currency derivatives and commodity derivatives segments, improving transparency and accuracy in short allocation monitoring.
