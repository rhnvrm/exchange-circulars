## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the postponement of the migration of non-settlement related payments from custodians to trading members in the cash market segment from the T+2 to T+1 settlement cycle. The implementation, originally scheduled for November 16, 2026, has been deferred until further notice, and the current process will continue to apply as is.

## Key Points

- Postponement of process migration for non-settlement related payments between custodians and trading members.
- Applies to the cash market segment.
- Original implementation date of November 16, 2026 (via Circular Ref. No. 0379/2026) is deferred until further notice.
- Current existing process remains in effect.

## Regulatory Changes

- No immediate regulatory changes; the proposed transition to T+1 for non-settlement related payments is put on hold.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, custodians, and PCMs must continue adhering to the current operational process until further notice is issued by NSE Clearing Limited.

## Important Dates

- **November 13, 2026**: Circular issued announcing the postponement.
- **November 16, 2026**: Originally scheduled implementation date (now deferred).

## Impact Assessment

- Operational continuity is maintained as the existing process remains unchanged, avoiding immediate workflow modifications for custodians and trading members.
