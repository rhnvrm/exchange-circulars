## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced that the changes related to the streamlining of End-of-Day (EOD) information dissemination regarding Bhavcopy, previously notified under Circular No. NSE/MSD/77208 dated October 30, 2026, have been deferred until further notice.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous Exchange Circular No. NSE/MSD/77208 dated October 30, 2026.
- Implementation of changes regarding EOD information dissemination related to Bhavcopy is deferred.
- Status quo is maintained until further notices are issued by the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Deferment of previously announced operational changes for EOD information dissemination and Bhavcopy.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are not required to implement any immediate procedural or technical changes regarding Bhavcopy dissemination at this time and should continue with existing practices.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Immediate (Deferred till further notice).
- Previous Reference: Circular No. NSE/MSD/77208 dated October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational continuity is maintained as the proposed dissemination changes are put on hold, preventing any immediate disruption for members relying on existing Bhavcopy formats.
