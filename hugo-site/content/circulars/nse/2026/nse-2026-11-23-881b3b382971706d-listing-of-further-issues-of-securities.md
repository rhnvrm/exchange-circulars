## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for various companies under ESOP and QIP, effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective November 25, 2026.
- Includes further share allotments via ESOP and QIP for companies such as Archean Chemical Industries Limited, Agi Infra Limited, Arham Technologies Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Eureka Forbes Limited, and ICICI Bank Limited.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026
- Circular Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action resulting in increased paid-up capital and liquidity for the respective listed entities.
