## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures on companies with high 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Minimum 35% margin applicable in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created from November 06, 2026.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework effective November 04, 2026.
- Representations regarding inclusion in the framework must be submitted by 5:00 PM on November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance margins (minimum 35%) for companies with high promoter/share encumbrance under SEBI SAST regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements of at least 35% for open and new positions in the specified securities starting November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for exclusion (Annexure II): November 04, 2026
- Deadline for company representations: November 04, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Effective date for margin applicability (Annexure I): November 06, 2026 (on open positions as of November 05, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the affected securities face increased capital and margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading positions.
