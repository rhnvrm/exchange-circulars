## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular informing members about the change in scheme names of multiple schemes of Zerodha CASE Mutual Fund (formerly Zerodha Mutual Fund) on the NSE MF Invest platform with immediate effect, following the induction of CASE Platforms Private Limited as Co-Sponsor and SEBI registration updates.

## Key Points

- Fund name updated from Zerodha Mutual Fund to Zerodha CASE Mutual Fund across SAI, SIDs, and KIMs following SEBI registration on November 02, 2026.
- 15 existing mutual fund schemes have been renamed to include "CASE" in their scheme names.
- Applies to index funds, ELSS tax saver funds, ETFs, FOFs, overnight, arbitrage, and life cycle funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to the issuance of a fresh certificate of registration by SEBI on November 2, 2026, the fund name and scheme names are officially updated.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must update their trading and investment systems with the revised scheme names for Zerodha CASE Mutual Fund.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- SEBI Registration Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely administrative nomenclature update with no operational or financial impact on unitholders' existing investments.
