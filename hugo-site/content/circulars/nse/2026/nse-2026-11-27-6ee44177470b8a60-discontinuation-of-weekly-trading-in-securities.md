## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading on 'Trade for Trade' basis (Series “BZ”) for Nagarjuna Fertilizers and Chemicals Limited (NAGAFERT) w.e.f. December 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Nagarjuna Fertilizers and Chemicals Limited
- Symbol: NAGAFERT
- Regulation: 17(1) (Composition of Board)
- Effective Date: December 15, 2026 (closing hour of trading on December 14, 2026)
- Reference: In continuation to Exchange Circular Ref. 0698/2026 dated April 28, 2026, and SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023 (last updated January 30, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Completion of the mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week for non-compliance with Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in securities on 'Trade for Trade' basis on the first trading day of every week is discontinued after completing the required period.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: December 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading arrangements for NAGAFERT return to standard applicable operational norms after fulfilling the mandated weekly trading period under surveillance/compliance measures.
