## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued revised operational guidelines and trading modalities for the Equity Derivatives segment pursuant to SEBI's review of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity cash segment. These changes take effect from December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Trading Hours:** Normal market closes at 15:45 hrs for stock derivatives and index derivatives on regular days, while expiring index derivatives contracts cease trading at 15:30 hrs on expiry days. Trade modification end time is set at 16:15 hrs.
- **Reference Price Calculation:** Reference prices for futures price bands are determined using VWAP between 3:15 p.m. and 3:30 p.m., Last Traded Price (LTP), or theoretical pricing models depending on trading activity.
- **Price Bands:** Futures price band from 3:30 p.m. to 3:45 p.m. is set to +/- 3% from the reference price to align with the CAS cash segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised operational guidelines and trading modalities in the Equity Derivatives segment in accordance with Clause 5 of SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026, and associated exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems, risk management modules, and operational workflows to accommodate the revised trading hours, reference price methodologies, and 3:30 p.m. to 3:45 p.m. price bands starting December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and trading members need to adapt algorithmic execution, risk controls, and order management strategies during the close of market and post-market hours (3:15 p.m. to 3:45 p.m.) to comply with the new derivative timing and price band alignment.
