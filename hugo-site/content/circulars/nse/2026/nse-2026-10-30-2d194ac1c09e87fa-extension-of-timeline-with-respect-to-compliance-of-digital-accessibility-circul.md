## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) and SEBI have announced an extension of the timeline for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars based on representations received from regulated entities. The deadline for appointing an IAAP auditor, conducting digital platform accessibility audits, and remediating audit findings is now extended to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Extension granted by SEBI vide circular HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026.
- New deadline for IAAP auditor appointment, accessibility audits, and remediation is January 31, 2027.
- Applies to all recognized stock exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories, intermediaries, AMFI, APMI, and IAASB/RAASB.
- All other provisions of the original circulars remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the implementation timeline previously mandated under SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 and related circulars regarding the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete the appointment of an IAAP auditor, conduct accessibility audits of digital platforms, and remediate all identified findings on or before January 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- October 30, 2026: Issuance date of the circular.
- January 31, 2027: Extended deadline for compliance, audit completion, and remediation.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides necessary relief and additional time for trading members and regulated entities to fully comply with digital accessibility standards for persons with disabilities without facing immediate penal action.
