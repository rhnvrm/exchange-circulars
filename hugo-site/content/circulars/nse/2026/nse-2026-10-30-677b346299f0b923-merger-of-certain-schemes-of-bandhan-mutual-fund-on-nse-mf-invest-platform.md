## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the merger of Bandhan CRISIL IBX 90:10 SDL Plus Gilt Nov 2026 Index Fund (Merging Scheme) into Bandhan Short Term Fund (Surviving Scheme) under Bandhan Mutual Fund. The merger is effective after the close of business hours on November 30, 2026, following SEBI's 'No Objection' communication dated September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Merging Scheme:** Bandhan CRISIL IBX 90:10 SDL Plus Gilt November 2026 Index Fund.
- **Surviving Scheme:** Bandhan Short Term Fund (formerly Bandhan Short Duration Fund).
- **Effective Date:** After close of business hours on November 30, 2026.
- **Exit Option:** Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch to other schemes of Bandhan Mutual Fund without exit load from October 30, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM on November 30, 2026) through the NSE MF Invest platform.
- **SIP/SWP Handling:** Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue processing in the merging schemes during the exit load period and will shift to the surviving scheme after business hours on November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI accorded its 'No Objection' for the merger vide communication dated September 07, 2026. The merger does not entail any change in the fundamental attributes of the Surviving Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors should take note of the merger timeline, exit load waiver window, and handling of systematic transactions (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP).

## Important Dates

- **Exit Load Free Window Start Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Exit Load Free Window End Date / Time:** November 30, 2026 up to 3:00 PM
- **Merger Effective Date:** November 30, 2026 (after close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

The merger has minimal market impact as it consolidates debt index fund schemes within Bandhan Mutual Fund while providing complete liquidity and exit flexibility to unitholders without any exit load penalty.
