## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, November 21, 2026, to test the new version of NEAT SLBM (1.3.4).

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** November 21, 2026.
- **New Version Release:** NEAT SLBM version 1.3.4 will be available for download from November 20, 2026 (19:00 hrs) at `/slbftp/slbcommon/NEAT1.3.4`.
- **Version Migration Timeline:** Version 1.3.3 is active until the mock of January 2027, while version 1.3.4 is effective from November 21, 2026.
- **Trading Schedule (Primary Site - Session 1):** Normal market open at 11:00 hrs, contingency start at 11:45 hrs, contingency end at 13:15 hrs, and normal market close at 14:30 hrs.
- **Trading Schedule (DR Site - Session 2):** Normal market open at 15:15 hrs and normal market close at 16:00 hrs.
- **Live Re-login Timings:** 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Release and implementation of NEAT SLBM version 1.3.4 requiring member participation and system compatibility testing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using SLBM NOTIS API are requested to participate in the mock session, check trade downloads, and share confirmation or feedback via email to `msm@nse.co.in`.
- Members using NNF software must manually clear all outstanding orders in their systems before each session.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved prior to the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- **NEAT 1.3.4 Availability:** November 20, 2026 (19:00 hrs)
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 21, 2026
- **Version 1.3.3 Discontinuation:** Mock of January 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and participants must upgrade to NEAT 1.3.4 and participate in the mock trading session to ensure operational readiness and regulatory compliance.
