## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in name and trading symbol of Novartis India Limited under the Permitted to Trade category, following communication received from BSE Limited.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Novartis India Limited
- New Name: Evonile Pharma Limited
- Existing Symbol: NOVARTIND
- New Symbol: EVONILE
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, client databases, and back-office systems with the new name and symbol (EVONILE) for Novartis India Limited effective from November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating internal systems, records, and trading terminals with the new company name and symbol.
