## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities, effective November 13, 2026. Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. November 13, 2026: Flexituff Ventures International Limited (FLEXITUFF), Pioneer Investcorp Limited (PIONRINV), Supriya Lifescience Limited (SUPRIYA), and Unilex Colours and Chemicals Limited (UNILEX).
- Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. November 16, 2026, on all open positions as on November 13, 2026, and new positions created from November 16, 2026.
- Framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance measures including enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) are maintained for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ST-ASM: November 13, 2026
- Margin applicability date: w.e.f. November 16, 2026 on open positions as on November 13, 2026 and new positions created from November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
