## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for three companies—Innomet Advanced Materials Limited, Magson Retail And Distribution Limited, and Utssav CZ Gold Jewels Limited—issued under preferential allotment on the SME Emerge segment, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Innomet Advanced Materials Limited**: 450,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 131/- (ISIN: INE0S1D01010, Series: SM, Lot Size: 600). Includes specific lock-in details up to April/May 2027.
- **Magson Retail And Distribution Limited**: 389,140 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 93.25/- (ISIN: INE0O1S01012, Series: SM, Lot Size: 1000). Lock-in applicable until April 29, 2028.
- **Utssav CZ Gold Jewels Limited**: 850,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 222/- (ISIN: INE06IJ01010, Series: SM, Lot Size: 600). Lock-in applicable until July 31, 2028.
- Effective date for trading of these securities is October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) – SME Emerge in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be identified by designated codes and in specified lot sizes.
- Lock-in details as specified in the respective annexures must be strictly complied with.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 29, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: August 7, 2026 (Utssav), August 18, 2026 (Innomet), August 25, 2026 (Magson)
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates**: Various dates spanning April 2027 to July 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the admission and trading of further preferential shares on the SME Emerge platform, expanding the capital base of the respective companies without direct operational disruption.
