## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from November 18, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Issuing Entities:** Includes Tata Teleservices Limited, Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, UltraTech Cement Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, Tata Communications Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited.
- **Instrument Type:** Commercial Papers (CP)

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of commercial papers to dealings on the Debt Market Segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuing entities must ensure adherence to standard depository and settlement procedures for commercial paper transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 17, 2026
- **Listing/Admission Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from December 2026 to February 2027 (e.g., 08-Jan-2027, 28-Dec-2026, 15-Feb-2027, 16-Feb-2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital market operations providing liquidity and trading access for privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers.
