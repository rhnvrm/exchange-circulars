## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. There is no new software version release for this mock session, and NEAT+ version remains 7.9.0.

## Key Points

- Conducted across two trading sessions: Session-1 from Primary Site (09:15 to 11:30) and Session-2 from DR Site (12:30 to 14:15).
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 is to be used, available at `/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus790`.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Reminder to NNF members regarding upcoming encryption mechanism changes and migration requirements by December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; mock trading satisfies regulatory requirements as per SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in mock trading must ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN upload before cutoff.
- NNF members should review and plan migration for new encryption mechanism ports (10841/10843) by December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026
- Encryption Migration Deadline: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact; routine operational readiness exercise with no financial obligation.
