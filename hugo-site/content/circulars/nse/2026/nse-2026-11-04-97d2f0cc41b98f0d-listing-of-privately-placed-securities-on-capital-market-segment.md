## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities issued by Patna Municipal Corporation and National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) on the capital market segment, effective from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from November 05, 2026.
- Issuers include Patna Municipal Corporation (multiple STRPP tranches with 9.20% coupon maturing between 2030 and 2036) and National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NBRD 8.27% 2031 Sr 27C).
- All securities will trade under designated security codes with a market lot size of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 05, 2026.
- Maturity Dates: Range from November 03, 2030, to November 03, 2036 (for Patna Municipal Corporation) and December 01, 2031 (for NABARD).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for the newly listed municipal bonds and NABARD debt instruments on the capital market segment.
