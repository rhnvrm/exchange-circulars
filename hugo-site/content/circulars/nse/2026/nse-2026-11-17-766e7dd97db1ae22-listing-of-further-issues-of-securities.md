## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for De Neers Tools Limited and Kody Technolab Limited on the SME Emerge segment, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **De Neers Tools Limited**: 1,600,200 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0JWV01011, Symbol: DENEERS, Issue Price: Rs. 196, Market Lot: 600). Lock-in details apply as per Annexure A.
- **Kody Technolab Limited**: 140,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0Q7P01013, Symbol: KODYTECH, Issue Price: Rs. 975, Market Lot: 100). Lock-in up to May 31, 2027.
- Effective trading date for both securities is November 18, 2026.

[N/A - Standard further listing of securities under capital market trading regulations]

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to designated security codes, lot sizes, and lock-in expiry dates as specified in the circular and annexures.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: November 17, 2026
- **De Neers Tools Allotment Date**: September 12, 2026
- **Kody Technolab Allotment Date**: October 12, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 18, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates**: May 19, 2027, May 20, 2027, and May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of further issued shares on the NSE SME Emerge segment for the respective companies, ensuring compliance with capital market trading regulations.
