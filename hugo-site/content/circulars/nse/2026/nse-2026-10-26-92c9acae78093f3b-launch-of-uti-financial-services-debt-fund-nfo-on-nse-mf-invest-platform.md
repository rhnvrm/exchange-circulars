## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for UTI Financial Services Debt Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 27, 2026, to November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI Financial Services Debt Fund (Regular and Direct plans, Growth and Payout of IDCW options).
- NFO Window: October 27, 2026 to November 2, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- ISINs: INF789F1AI17 (Regular Growth), INF789F1AI25 (Direct Growth), INF789F1AI33 (Regular Payout of IDCW), INF789F1AI41 (Direct Payout of IDCW).
- Category: Debt.
- Allotment Date: November 3, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 03, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 02, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Fund Allotment Date: November 03, 2026
- Fund Clear Funds Deadline: November 03, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the new debt fund scheme seamlessly via the NSE MF platform within the specified timeline.
