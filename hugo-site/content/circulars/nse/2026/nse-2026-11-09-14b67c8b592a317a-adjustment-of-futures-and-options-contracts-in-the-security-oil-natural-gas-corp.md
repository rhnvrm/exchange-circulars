## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options contracts in Oil & Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) following a dividend announcement of Rs. 8.00 per share. The adjustments include mark-to-market processing and carry-forward price modifications on the last cum-dividend date.

## Key Points

- Futures contracts adjustment is based on the reference rate on November 11, 2026 (last cum-dividend date).
- The daily mark-to-market settlement price will be reduced by the dividend amount of Rs. 8.00.
- Open positions in futures will be carried forward at the daily settlement price less Rs. 8.00 from the ex-dividend date (November 12, 2026).
- Begin of day margins on November 12, 2026, will be computed based on the adjusted carry-forward value.

## Regulatory Changes

Clearing and settlement procedures for ONGC F&O contracts are adjusted to account for the corporate dividend action, ensuring fair valuation and contract continuity.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must take note of the adjusted carry-forward values, MTM processing, and margin calculations for ONGC F&O positions as of November 11 and 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **November 11, 2026**: Last cum-dividend date and reference date for futures contracts adjustment / MTM processing.
- **November 12, 2026**: Ex-dividend date; normal daily MTM settlement and adjusted carry-forward begin-of-day margins take effect.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding open futures and options positions in ONGC will experience a corresponding adjustment in contract values and margin requirements reflecting the dividend payout.
