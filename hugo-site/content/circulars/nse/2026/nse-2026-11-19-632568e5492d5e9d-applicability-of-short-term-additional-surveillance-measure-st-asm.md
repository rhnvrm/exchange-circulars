## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and stage movements for specified securities, effective November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Aristo Bio-Tech And Lifescience Limited (ARISTO), Bharat Parenterals Limited (BPLPHARMA), Dishman Carbogen Amcis Limited (DCAL), Oriana Power Limited (ORIANA), and Zeal Global Services Limited (ZEAL).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Framework Stage II to Stage I: Kore Digital Limited (KDL).
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100%.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) shall apply on all open positions as on November 20, 2026, and new positions created w.e.f. November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted stocks may lead to reduced leverage and impacted liquidity for the affected scrips.
