## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies effective from November 03, 2026, following allotments under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective November 03, 2026.
- Covers companies including Birlasoft, HDB Financial Services, Home First Finance, ICICI Bank, India Shelter Finance, Infosys, Kellton Tech, Global Health, Metropolis Healthcare, Ravindra Energy, Triveni Turbine, and Wipro.
- Allotments pertain to equity shares issued under ESOP with pari passu ranking and market lot of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing of further capital issues under existing trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A regarding security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Incremental increase in the paid-up equity capital and listed shares of the respective issuing companies.
