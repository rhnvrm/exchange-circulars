## Summary

Equity shares of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with effect from November 25, 2026. The total paid-up equity capital comprises 6,61,83,63,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Mahanadi Coalfields Limited
- Scrip Code / Symbol: EQUITY SHARES (Mahanadi Coalfields Limited Post Issue)
- Date of Listing: 25.11.2026
- Total No. of Fully Paid-up Equity Shares: 6,61,83,63,000
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 5,95,65,26,700 shares (90.00%)
- Public Holding: 66,18,36,300 shares (10.00%)
- Locked-in Shares: 6,12,52,94,955 shares (92.55% of total shares)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing circular issued upon the completion of an Initial Public Offering.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and ongoing stock exchange listing norms.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited equity shares on the NSE market platform.
