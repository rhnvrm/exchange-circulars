## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the empanelment of M/s Options Mart Consultancy Private Limited as an approved Algo Provider for supplying Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s Options Mart Consultancy Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox
- Address: Office no.3304/A, Thirty Three Floor, Prestige Truf Tower, Dr. Elijah Moses Road, Mahalaxmi (W) Mumbai 400011
- Contact Details: Phone +91 7030777761; Email: nnfreg_support@nse.co.in / invg@nse.co.in
- Applicability: With immediate effect

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative empanelment notice confirming vendor approval.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions from the empaneled vendor must adhere to exchange guidelines and risk management norms.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Expands the list of approved algorithmic trading solution providers available to trading members, offering additional choices for automated trading.
