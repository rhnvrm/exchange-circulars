## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I and will be subject to heightened margins and trade-for-trade settlement effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Annexure I (Refractory Shapes Limited and Shree Rama Multi-Tech Limited) will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026.
- Affected securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- The ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisting of securities under the ESM framework due to market surveillance parameters.
- Mandatory shifting of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment with applicable margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Systems must account for the shift of securities from EQ/SM to BE/ST series.

## Important Dates

- **October 28, 2026**: Effective date for reference open positions / surveillance action.
- **October 29, 2026**: Effective date for minimum 100% margin applicability and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the shortlisted securities will be restricted due to the Trade-for-Trade segment transfer and 100% margin requirement, mitigating excessive volatility and risk in the market.
