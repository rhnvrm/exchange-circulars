## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Oneclick Logistics India Limited on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) pursuant to its migration from the SME Emerge platform.

## Key Points

- **Listed Entity:** Oneclick Logistics India Limited
- **Scrip Symbol:** OLIL
- **Segment:** Capital Market Segment (Main Board)
- **Migration Source:** SME Emerge platform
- **Report Reference:** Shareholding Pattern filed for the quarter ending November 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of the company's securities from the SME platform to the Main Board trading segment of the exchange.
- Submission and dissemination of specified securities holding patterns in compliance with applicable regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Continued adherence to Main Board listing regulations, corporate governance norms, and periodic financial and shareholding disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Report/Quarter Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** As per NSE circular notification.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates broader investor participation, potentially improving liquidity and market visibility following the migration to the Main Board.
