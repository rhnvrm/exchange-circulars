## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security Name: J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited
- Symbol: JKSHAH
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, securities are moved between trading segments based on eligibility and compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should note the change in series for trading in JKSHAH.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for the security, improving liquidity and trading convenience.
