## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the name and trading symbol of Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited. Effective November 20, 2026, the company will operate under the new name Shukra Medtech Limited and the new symbol SHUKRAMED.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited
- New Name: Shukra Medtech Limited
- Existing Symbol: SHUKRAPHAR
- New Symbol: SHUKRAMED
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; this is an administrative update reflecting corporate restructuring and rebranding.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, back-office systems, and records with the new name and symbol for Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational or financial impact on the broader market. Market participants need to ensure their trading systems reflect the updated symbol to prevent order routing errors.
