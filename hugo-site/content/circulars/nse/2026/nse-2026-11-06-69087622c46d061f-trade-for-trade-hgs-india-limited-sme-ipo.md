## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of HGS (India) Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HGS (India) Limited
- Symbol: HGSINDIALT
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade for trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, eligible securities are moved from trade-for-trade to the rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change for HGS (India) Limited effective from November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables standard rolling settlement for HGS (India) Limited shares.
