## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the takeover offer under the tender offer scheme of G-Tec Jainx Education Limited. The trade period is scheduled from November 18, 2026, to December 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X
- Settlement No.: 2026137
- Trade Start Date: 18-NOV-2026
- Trade End Date: 02-DEC-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 07-DEC-2026
- Settlement Date: 08-DEC-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard settlement schedule issued pursuant to takeover/tender offer guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be tendered through the early pay-in mechanism of depositories for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be made well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post receipt of funds pay-in on the Settlement Day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.
