## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Motilal Oswal Asset Management Company Limited across five ETF schemes, effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes listed: Motilal Oswal Nifty500 Growth 50 ETF (MOGROWTH), Motilal Oswal Nifty Private Bank ETF (MOPVTBANK), Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty ETF (MOREIT), Motilal Oswal Nifty 10 yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF (MO10YGSEC), and Motilal Oswal Nifty PSU Bank ETF (MOPSUBANK).
- Date of Allotment for the units was November 20, 2026.
- Market lot for trading is 1 across all listed schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine admission to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must use the designated security codes and symbols for order entry and execution in the capital market segment.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for investors holding units of the newly listed Motilal Oswal ETF schemes.
