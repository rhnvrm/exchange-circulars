## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI's final order no. QJA/MN/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32818/2026-27 dated October 30, 2026. The order debars EKI Energy Services Ltd. and several key individuals from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities for specified periods.

## Key Points

- **EKI Energy Services Ltd.**: Debarred for 2 months (PAN: AACCE6986E).
- **Mr. Manish Kumar Dabkara**: Debarred for 12 months (PAN: AMPPD4048N).
- **Mr. Naveen Sharma**: Debarred for 5 months (PAN: BHPPS2657J).
- **Ms. Sonali Sheikh**: Debarred for 1 month (PAN: CXMPS1805G).
- **Mr. Mohit Kumar Agarwal**: Debarred for 8 months (PAN: AMAPK8905E).
- **Derivative Positions**: Open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts as of the order date may be squared off/closed out within 3 months or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- **Settlement**: Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed before the close of trading on the order date is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI has prohibited and restrained the mentioned noticees from accessing the securities market, buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly, or being associated with the securities market for their respective debarment periods.

## Compliance Requirements

NSE members must take note of the debarred entities list available on the NSE website (https://www.nseindia.com/regulations/member-sebi-debarred-entities) and ensure compliance. Queries can be directed to dl-invsg-all@nse.co.in.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 02, 2026
- **SEBI Order Date**: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory and operational restriction on EKI Energy Services Ltd. and associated individuals, prohibiting securities market participation and requiring members to enforce compliance with SEBI's debarment orders.
