## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of HDFC Bank Limited due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HDFC Bank Limited
- ISIN: INE040A08450
- Suspension Date: November 03, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the suspension and avoid executing transactions in the suspended ISIN from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation reflecting the redemption of privately placed non-convertible securities.
