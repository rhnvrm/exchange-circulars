## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the migration of equity shares of Bondada Engineering Limited from the SME Platform of BSE to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of NSE, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bondada Engineering Limited
- **Symbol:** BONDADA
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE0Q8P01029
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/-
- **Number of Securities:** 111,671,485 equity shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 111,671,485
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of equity shares to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment pursuant to migration from BSE SME platform in accordance with NSE Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading shall be in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors. Lock-in details include 265,000 shares locked in up to 30-Nov-2026 and 111,406,485 shares free.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** November 30, 2026 (for 265,000 shares)

## Impact Assessment

Enables broader investor participation and liquidity through Main Board listing on NSE for Bondada Engineering Limited.
