## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the launch of the Invesco India Financial Services Debt Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from November 25, 2026, to December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Invesco India Financial Services Debt Fund (Multiple Regular and Direct plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Period: November 25, 2026 to December 07, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Allotment Date: December 11, 2026.
- Designated Clearing Corporation Funds Clearance: On or before December 11, 2026, till 10:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 11, 2026, till 10:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 25, 2026
- NFO Close Date: December 07, 2026
- Allotment Date: December 11, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 11, 2026 (10:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors seamless access to subscribe to the new debt fund scheme via the NSE MF platform within the stipulated timelines.
