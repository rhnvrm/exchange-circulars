## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies, effective from November 30, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Listing of further issues for companies including Aurum PropTech Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bandhan Bank Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted to dealings effective November 30, 2026.
- Includes equity shares allotted on preferential basis and under ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan).
- Lot sizes and security codes are specified in the circular annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the listed securities must be conducted using designated security codes and prescribed lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., September 29, 2026; October 16, 2026; November 25, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital and liquidity of the respective issuing companies through preferential allotments and ESOP exercises.
