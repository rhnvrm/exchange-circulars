## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. The SAT has granted a stay of the impugned SEBI order dated June 30, 2026, subject to the deposit of the full penalty amount by the appellants.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026, which restrained certain entities from accessing the securities market.
- Noticees involved: Arvind Shantilal Shah, Rupal Bhavin Shah, Bharati Arvind Shah, Bhavin Arvind Shah, Yash Manish Mehta, and Ramnaresh Dashadeen Nirmal.
- Hon'ble SAT ordered a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated November 17, 2026, that the appellants have paid the penalty amount in favour of SEBI, and advised compliance with the attached SAT order.

## Regulatory Changes

Stay on the debarment and market restraint orders for the specified noticees following the deposit of penalties as directed by the SAT.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to note the compliance with mark-to-market/debarment status updates and refer to the consolidated list of debarred entities on the Exchange website.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 17, 2026
- SAT Order Date: November 02, 2026
- Original SEBI Order Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The specified individuals/entities have obtained a stay on their market debarment following penalty deposits, permitting them conditional access pending final appeal resolution.
