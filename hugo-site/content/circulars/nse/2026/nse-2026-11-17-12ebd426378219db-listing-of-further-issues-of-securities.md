## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies, effective from November 18, 2026. These securities include equity shares allotted under ESOP and QIP.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 18, 2026.
- Companies involved include Avalon Technologies Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Eicher Motors Limited, and several others.
- Allotment types include ESOP (Employee Stock Ownership Plan) and QIP (Qualified Institutional Placement).
- Trading will be conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Allotment Dates vary by company (e.g., October 30, 2026, November 17, 2026, November 6, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action admission to dealings on the capital market segment with negligible market impact.
