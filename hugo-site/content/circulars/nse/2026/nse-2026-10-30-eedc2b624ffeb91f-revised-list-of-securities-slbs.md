## Summary

NSE has released the revised list of securities eligible for participation in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS). Annexure I outlines the comprehensive set of approved stocks and ETFs available for lending and borrowing transactions.

## Key Points

- Lists over 200 eligible securities, including equities and ETFs, permitted under SLBS.
- Provides market participants with updated eligibility criteria for short selling and lending operations.
- Supersedes previous circulars regarding the list of approved securities for SLBS.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of eligible securities pool for the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must verify the updated security list before executing SLBS transactions.
- Ensure internal risk management and collateral systems are synchronized with the revised list of approved securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular release.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and lending/borrowing opportunities across the newly added and retained securities while ensuring compliance with exchange risk frameworks.
