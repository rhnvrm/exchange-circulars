## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of units issued by ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited under the ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF scheme, effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF
- Symbol: POWERIETF
- ISIN: INF109K1A724
- Number of Units: 845,562
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 71.77 per unit
- Date of Allotment: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market makers and trading members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and trading access for ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF units on the NSE capital market segment with a market lot of 1.
