## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the introduction of the Income Distribution cum capital withdrawal (IDCW) option for PGIM Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Introduction of weekly IDCW payout option under Regular and Direct Plans of PGIM India Liquid Fund and PGIM India Ultra Short Term Fund.
- Effective date: November 27, 2026.
- Weekly IDCW payout record date is every Friday (or the preceding business day if Friday is a holiday).
- Applicable to both existing and new investors on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of new scheme options on the exchange mutual fund platform in accordance with communication received from PGIM Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors to note the new scheme options available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026
- Record Date: Every Friday

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances investment options and payout flexibility for participants holding units in PGIM India Liquid Fund and PGIM India Ultra Short Term Fund.
