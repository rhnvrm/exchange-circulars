## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for selected securities. Effective November 05/06, 2026, specific securities are included under ESM Stage I, while others move between stages, attracting 100% margins and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Priority Jewels Limited, Virat Industries Limited) satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I with minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 06, 2026 (on open positions as of November 05, 2026, and new positions from November 06, 2026).
- Universus Photo Imagings Limited (UNIVPHOTO) moves from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. November 05, 2026.
- Securities shifting to ESM Stage I are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 05, 2026.
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, shifting affected securities to Trade-for-Trade settlement and imposing enhanced margin requirements (100%) and price bands under periodic call auction where applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions for shortlisted securities, and account for segment shifts (EQ/SM to BE/ST) and periodic call auction restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II movement & price band (UNIVPHOTO): November 05, 2026
- Open positions cutoff date for Stage I margin: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage I inclusion & Trade-for-Trade segment shift: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and operational flexibility for shortlisted securities (PRIORITY, VIRAT, UNIVPHOTO) are restricted due to 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction rules, aimed at curbing excessive volatility and enhancing market integrity.
