## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued revised operational guidelines and trading modalities for the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment, pursuant to SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026. The revised guidelines are effective from December 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Applicability**: CAS applies to securities on which derivative contracts are available in any exchange, with specific exceptions such as the Ex-date of a scheme of arrangement (if price not determined during SPOS) or index circuit break halts.
- **Session Timings**: CAS is a 10-minute session from 03:30 PM to 03:40 PM on all trading days.
- **Order Entry & Restrictions**: Special term orders (Stop loss, IOC, Disclosed Quantity) are not allowed. Market orders are restricted during specific phases and rejected with appropriate error messages if placed when restricted.
- **Order Matching & Cancellation**: Orders are matched at a single equilibrium price. Unexecuted orders are cancelled by the exchange at the end of CAS. Trade cancellation requests are not permitted for trades executed in CAS.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised operational guidelines and trading modalities for CAS in accordance with SEBI circular ref HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/23843/2026 dated October 15, 2026.
- Supersedes previous exchange circulars on CAS trading modalities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised schedule, order entry restrictions, and matching modalities for CAS effective December 07, 2026.
- Ensure trading systems and internal workflows are aligned with the new NEAT error messages and order handling rules during CAS.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date**: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances transparency and price discovery during market close for derivative-eligible securities in the cash segment.
- Requires operational readiness from market participants regarding order placement restrictions and timing adjustments.
