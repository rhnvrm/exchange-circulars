## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Aditya Birla Capital Limited on the capital market segment effective from November 12, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Aditya Birla Capital Limited
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Securities Listed:**
  - **878ABCL31:** ABCL 8.78% 2031 Sr H2 (ISIN: INE674K07184), Market Lot: 1, Coupon Rate: 8.78%, Maturity Date: 06-Nov-2031
  - **878ABCL29:** ABCL 8.78% 2029 Sr H1 (ISIN: INE674K07176), Market Lot: 1, Coupon Rate: 8.78%, Maturity Date: 06-Nov-2029

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the specified securities are admitted to dealings with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified solely by their designated security codes and conducted in the specified lot sizes as detailed in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** November 06, 2029 and November 06, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing providing trading access and liquidity for the privately placed debt securities of Aditya Birla Capital Limited on the NSE capital market segment.
