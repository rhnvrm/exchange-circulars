## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- One security, CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038), has been moved to Stage IV of the GSM.
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026.
- Trading is permitted only once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

- Security moved to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework with stringent trading controls.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading available in Trade for Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- Trading permitted once a week (Every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers with no upward movement.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on liquidity and trading frequency for CLC Industries Limited due to weekly trading restriction and 100% ASD requirement.
