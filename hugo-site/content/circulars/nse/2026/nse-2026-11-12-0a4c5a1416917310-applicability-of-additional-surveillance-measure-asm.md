## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Blue Pebble Limited has satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) Stage I.

## Key Points

- Blue Pebble Limited (ISIN: INE0SAK01011) has been shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 17, 2026, on all open positions as on November 16, 2026, and new positions created from November 17, 2026 onwards.
- The shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of securities in the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I triggers enhanced margin requirements as specified by exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure that 100% margins are maintained for open and new positions in the affected security starting November 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Inclusion: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin Requirement: November 17, 2026 (on open positions as of November 16, 2026, and new positions thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in Blue Pebble Limited will be subject to a 100% margin requirement, which may impact liquidity and trading volume for the security while it remains under the surveillance framework.
