## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. No new software version is being released for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- NEAT+ version details: Version 1.0.1 discontinuation date is November 14, 2026 (mock); Version 1.0.2 is recommended.
- Default login password for mock session: `Neat@EG1`.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes; scheduled operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in the mock trading session must adhere to the specified schedule, login parameters, and network configurations provided in the annexures.
- NEAT+ version 1.0.2 is recommended for future trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 14, 2026
- NEAT+ Version 1.0.1 Discontinuation Date (Mock): November 14, 2026
- Live Re-login start time on mock date: 16:00 hours to 16:30 hours.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational test only; no financial obligations or live settlement impact. Members can verify their trading setup and connectivity.
