## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the shortlist of securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework.

## Key Points

- HMA Agro Industries Limited (Symbol: HMAAGRO, ISIN: INE0ECP01024) has been moved from STASM to Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I effective November 27, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. December 01, 2026 on all open positions as on November 30, 2026 and new positions created from December 01, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) stage classification and enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities starting December 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Shortlisting):** November 27, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Effective Date (100% Margin):** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience 100% margin blocking, impacting liquidity and trading leverage for the affected scrip.
