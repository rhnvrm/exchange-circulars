## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the suspension of trading in various T-Bills, State Government Bonds (SG), Government Securities (GS), and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) on account of maturity and redemption, effective from various dates starting November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects 22 specific securities including Treasury Bills, State Government Bonds, Government Securities, and Sovereign Gold Bonds.
- Suspension is pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Suspension dates range from November 27, 2026, to December 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine procedural suspension due to security maturity and redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the suspension dates and ensure no trading takes place in the listed ISINs post their respective suspension dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- Suspension Effective Dates: Starting from November 27, 2026, through December 29, 2026, as per individual security maturity dates.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact as this is standard market procedure for maturing government securities and bonds.
