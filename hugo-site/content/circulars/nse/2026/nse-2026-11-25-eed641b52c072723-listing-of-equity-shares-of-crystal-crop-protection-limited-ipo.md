## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings and listing of equity shares of Crystal Crop Protection Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Crystal Crop Protection Limited
- **Symbol:** CRYSTALCP
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE101P01016
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 272/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities:** 163,177,583 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities will be conducted in accordance with the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, adhering to pre-open session norms for IPOs as per SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes, lot sizes, and lock-in details specified in the exchange annexures.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Various tranches ranging from December 2026 through May 2028 as per Annexure I.

## Impact Assessment

Brings Crystal Crop Protection Limited onto the active trading segments of the NSE, enabling public market participation and liquidity for its equity shares.
