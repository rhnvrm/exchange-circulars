## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for specified securities under Stage I, Stage II, and stage transitions effective October 30, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100% w.e.f. October 30, 2026, on open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100% w.e.f. October 30, 2026.
- Stage I securities: R.K. Fashion Accessories Limited (RKFAL), Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited (SMT), Suyog Telematics Limited (SUYOG), and Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited (TTML).
- Moving from Stage I to Stage II: Kore Digital Limited (KDL).
- Moving from Stage II to Stage I: Visaman Global Sales Limited (VISAMAN).
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) stages and enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted securities to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% or 100%) on open positions and new positions created from October 30, 2026, for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins: October 30, 2026 (on open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
