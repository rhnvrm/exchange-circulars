## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Watrana Rentals Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Watrana Rentals Limited
- Symbol: WATRANA
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Reference: Further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/77616 dated November 26, 2026 and SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer pursuant to SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are advised to note the segment change for trading in Watrana Rentals Limited securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: December 11, 2026
- Previous Circular Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for Watrana Rentals Limited shares starting December 11, 2026, improving liquidity and trading efficiency.
