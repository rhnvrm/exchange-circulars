## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines for the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in COAL INDIA LIMITED (COALINDIA) following a dividend payout of Rs. 10.25/- per share. The adjustments include modifications to futures contract carry-forward values and option strike prices.

## Key Points

- Futures contracts adjustment: Futures positions will be marked-to-market on the last cum-dividend date (Nov 03, 2026) and carried forward at daily settlement prices reduced by the dividend amount of Rs. 10.25/-.
- Options contracts adjustment: The full dividend value of Rs. 10.25/- will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date (Nov 04, 2026) and rounded to the nearest tick size.
- Open positions in existing strike prices will continue in the corresponding new adjusted strike prices.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price adjustment of derivative contracts to account for corporate dividend distributions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must account for adjusted carry-forward values, MTM settlements, and modified strike prices for COALINDIA F&O positions effective from the ex-dividend date.

## Important Dates

- Last Cum-Dividend Date: November 03, 2026
- Ex-Dividend Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures parity and fair valuation of derivative contracts following the stock's dividend payout.
