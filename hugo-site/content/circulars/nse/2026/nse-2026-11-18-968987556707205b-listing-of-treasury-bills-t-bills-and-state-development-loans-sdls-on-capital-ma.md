## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of various Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on the capital market segment, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: State Development Loans (SDLs) of Maharashtra, Karnataka, Punjab, and Rajasthan, alongside 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Government Treasury Bills.
- Effective Date: Trading in these securities commences on November 20, 2026.
- Lot Size: A uniform lot size of 100 is applicable for all listed securities.
- Identification: Securities will be identified by their designated symbols and ISIN codes on the trading system.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings with specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and compliant lot sizes (100 units).

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: November 20, 2026
- Maturity Dates: Ranging from February 18, 2027 (91D T-bill) to November 18, 2052 (SDL RJ 8.12%).

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading opportunities in government securities and state debt instruments on the NSE capital market segment.
