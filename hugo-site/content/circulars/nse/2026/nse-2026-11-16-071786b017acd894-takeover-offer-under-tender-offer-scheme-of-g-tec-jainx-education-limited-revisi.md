## Summary

G-TEC Education Private Limited and Mr. Roychand Chenraj (Acquirers) have announced a revision in the offer price to Rs. 33/- per equity share for the upcoming takeover offer of G-Tec Jainx Education Limited under the Tender Offer scheme.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: GTECJAINX (ISIN: INE586X01012)
- Issue Period: November 18, 2026 to December 02, 2026
- Revised Offer Price: Rs. 33/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-)
- Issue Size: Up to 26,49,166 Equity shares
- Live URL for Bidding: https://eipo.nseindia.com
- Merchant Banker: Navigant Corporate Advisors Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of the offer price for the takeover offer under the Tender Offer scheme in accordance with Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can participate using the web-based IPO bidding platform or admin terminal to create branches and users.
- Order modification (quantity) and cancellation are not permitted for takeover offers.
- Custodial confirmation is required between 9.15 a.m. and 3.30 p.m., with issue closure day confirmation ending at 4.00 p.m.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: November 18, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: December 02, 2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables eligible shareholders of G-Tec Jainx Education Limited to participate in the revised takeover tender offer through trading members during the specified issue period.
