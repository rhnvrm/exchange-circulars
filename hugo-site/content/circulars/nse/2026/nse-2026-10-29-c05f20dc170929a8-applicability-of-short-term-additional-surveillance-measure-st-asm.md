## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities effective October 30, 2026. Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 02, 2026, on open and new positions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Indo Us Biotech Limited (INDOUS), Milton Industries Limited (MILTON), and Mos Utility Limited (MOS).
- Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), subject to a maximum of 100% effective November 02, 2026, on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions created from November 02, 2026.
- Stage II, stage movements, and exclusions are nil in this circular.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse regulatory action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance measures on specified securities to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, whichever is higher, up to 100%) for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM: October 30, 2026
- Enhanced Margin Effective Date: November 02, 2026 (on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in INDOUS, MILTON, and MOS will experience increased margin requirements (50% to 100%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
