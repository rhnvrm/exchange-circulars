## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities that have satisfied criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 18, 2026, on all open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created from November 18, 2026 onwards.
- Flexituff Ventures International Limited (Symbol: FLEXITUFF, ISIN: INE060J01017) has been shortlisted and moved from STASM to LTASM framework (Stage I).
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of heightened margin requirements (100%) on securities qualifying under the Long-Term ASM framework to curb excessive speculation and volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin collection on open and new positions for the affected securities starting November 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 18, 2026 (based on open positions as on November 17, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements and restricted leverage for market participants trading in the impacted securities due to the 100% margin mandate.
