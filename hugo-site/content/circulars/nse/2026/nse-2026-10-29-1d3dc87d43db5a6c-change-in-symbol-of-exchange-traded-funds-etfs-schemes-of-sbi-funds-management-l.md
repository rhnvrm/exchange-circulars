## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a change in the trading symbols of several Exchange Traded Funds (ETF's) schemes of SBI Funds Management Limited under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme, effective from October 30, 2026, following circular no. NSE/CML/77115 dated October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading symbols for multiple SBI ETF schemes are being updated in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.
- Effective date of symbol change: October 30, 2026.
- Old symbols such as SBIBPB, SBIETFCON, SBIETFIT, etc., are replaced by new symbols like SBIPSUBNK, SBICONS, SBIIT, etc.
- All contracts in the affected securities will trade under the new symbols.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative symbol update reflecting changes initiated by NSE/CML/77115.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the new symbols and update their trading/back-office systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact; operational update ensuring alignment of trading symbols across segments.
