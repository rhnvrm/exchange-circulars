## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) orders in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. The circular references SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026, which restrained certain entities from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities.

## Key Points

- References SEBI orders restraining market access for specified entities.
- Highlights SAT orders (including Appeal No. 330 of 2026 for Anil Dhanuka and Appeal No. 329 of 2026 for Chiripal Suryansh Hari dated October 5, 2026) granting a stay of the impugned SEBI order.
- Interim relief and stay of the impugned order are subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks in an interest-bearing account.
- Lists noticees including Rajvi Naresh Shah, Anil Dhanuka, Sushila Ashokbhai Chiripal, and Jignes...

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of SAT directions staying previous SEBI market restriction orders upon fulfillment of penalty deposit conditions.

## Compliance Requirements

Concerned members and noticees must adhere to the specified terms of the SAT order, including deposit of penalties to maintain interim relief.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Order Date: June 30, 2026
- SAT Order Date: October 5, 2026
- Circular Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects the market access and trading status of named noticees pending final disposal of appeals before the Securities Appellate Tribunal.
