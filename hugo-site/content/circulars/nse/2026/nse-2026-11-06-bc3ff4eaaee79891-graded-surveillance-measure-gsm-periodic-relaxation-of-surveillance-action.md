## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a periodic review of securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Eligible securities will move to lower stages of the GSM framework effective November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review of securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- Relaxation of surveillance actions effective November 08, 2026.
- Includes movement of various securities to lower GSM stages or out of GSM.
- Note: Blue Chip India Limited and Digjam Limited have series changes from BE to EQ.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in the Annexure are moved to lower GSM stages or Stage 0.
- BLUECHIP: GSM Stage II to Stage I (Series change BE to EQ)
- CLCIND: GSM Stage IV to Stage III
- DIGJAMLMTD: GSM Stage II to Stage I (Series change BE to EQ)
- MORARJEE: GSM Stage II to Stage I
- ORTEL: GSM Stage III to Stage II
- UNIVAFOODS: GSM Stage IV to Stage III
- CBAZAAR: GSM Stage I to Stage 0

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members are advised to note the revised GSM stages and series changes for the respective securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for GSM relaxation: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Relaxation of surveillance measures reduces trading restrictions for the affected securities, improving liquidity and market access for investors.
