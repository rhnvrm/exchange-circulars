## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced multiple settlements scheduled for November 11, 2026, following a settlement holiday on November 10, 2026. The circular outlines revised pay-in and pay-out timings, auction cycles for T+1, and unpaid MTF file upload windows for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment.

## Key Points

- Multiple settlements are scheduled on November 11, 2026, due to the settlement holiday on November 10, 2026.
- Revised auction trading and settlement cycles under T+1 are defined for affected settlement numbers.
- Specific cut-off timings for pay-in at depositories and UNPAID MTF file uploads for EGR Normal have been established.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised settlement schedules and auction timings implemented for the Electronic Gold Receipt segment to accommodate the settlement holiday.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the revised settlement timings, pay-in/pay-out windows, and depository cut-off times.
- Members must adhere to the updated timings for uploading unpaid MTF files for EGR Normal.

## Important Dates

- **November 10, 2026**: Settlement holiday.
- **November 11, 2026**: Multiple settlements scheduled with revised pay-in and pay-out timings.
- **November 11 - 13, 2026**: Revised auction trade and settlement dates.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) must align their operational workflows, depository pay-in cut-offs, and MTF file upload schedules with the revised timings for November 11, 2026.
