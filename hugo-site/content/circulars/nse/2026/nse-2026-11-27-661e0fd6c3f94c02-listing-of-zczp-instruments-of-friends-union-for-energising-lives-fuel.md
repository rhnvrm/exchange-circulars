## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) instruments issued by Friends Union for Energising Lives (FUEL) with effect from November 30, 2026. These securities will be identified by their designated codes and will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Friends Union for Energising Lives (FUEL)
- **Security Description:** FUEL ZCZP 0%
- **Symbol:** FLZCZP-SE
- **Series:** N0
- **Number of Securities:** 5,000,000
- **ISIN:** INS21BF12027
- **Maturity Date:** July 31, 2029
- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Trading Status:** Will not be traded on the Exchange

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notification for ZCZP instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with listing norms as specified by NSE and applicable regulatory guidelines for ZCZP instruments.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** July 31, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on general market participants as these instruments are non-traded securities.
