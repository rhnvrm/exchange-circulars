## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to selected securities. Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under Long Term ASM Stage I effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long Term ASM Stage - I: Balkrishna Paper Mills Limited (BALKRISHNA) and Ortin Global Limited (ORTINGLOBE).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. December 02, 2026 on all open positions as on December 01, 2026 and new positions created from December 02, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure the collection of 100% margin w.e.f. December 02, 2026 on all open positions as on December 01, 2026 and new positions created onwards for the shortlisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026 / December 02, 2026.
- Margin applicability date: December 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin obligations may impact liquidity, trading interest, and price volatility for the affected securities.
