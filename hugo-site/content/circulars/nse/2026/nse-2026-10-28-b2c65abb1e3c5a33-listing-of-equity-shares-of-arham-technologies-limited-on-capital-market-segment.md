## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Arham Technologies Limited on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) pursuant to its migration from the SME Emerge platform.

- Listed Entity: Arham Technologies Limited
- Migration from SME Emerge platform to Capital Market Segment (Main Board).
- Details include shareholding patterns and specified securities information as per regulatory filings.
