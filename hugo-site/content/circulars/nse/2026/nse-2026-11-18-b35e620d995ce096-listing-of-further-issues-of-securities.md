## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of securities, including equity shares allotted under ESOPs, for multiple companies effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issuance of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of NSE.
- Covers multiple companies including 360 ONE WAM Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, and others.
- Effective trading date: November 19, 2026.
- Securities identified by designated codes and market lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

Executed pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (October/November 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital base for the respective companies through employee stock option plans and other corporate issuances.
