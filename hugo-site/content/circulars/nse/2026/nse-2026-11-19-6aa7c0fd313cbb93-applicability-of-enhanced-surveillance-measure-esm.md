## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) to specific securities. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I: Aprameya Engineering Limited (APRAMEYA) and Rushil Decor Limited (RUSHIL).
- Shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 20, 2026, and new positions created from November 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes ABM International Limited (ABMINTLLTD) under Stage II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) framework in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures to monitor volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must maintain a minimum 100% margin on open positions for shortlisted securities as of November 20, 2026, and new positions from November 23, 2026. Trading in these securities will be settled under the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Shifting / Margins / Restrictions: November 23, 2026 (Open positions as on November 20, 2026).
- Circular Date: November 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for affected securities (APRAMEYA, RUSHIL) will be restricted due to Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% margin requirements, impacting short-term trading interest.
