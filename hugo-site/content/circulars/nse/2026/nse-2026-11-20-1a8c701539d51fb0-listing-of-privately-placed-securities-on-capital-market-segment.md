## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Mangalam Global Enterprise Limited on the capital market segment effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mangalam Global Enterprise Limited
- Security Symbol: 11MGEL29
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Description: MGEL 11% 2029
- Coupon Rate: 11%
- Date of Maturity: 18-Nov-2029
- ISIN Code: INE0APB07013

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed securities to dealings on the capital market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Trading lot size established pursuant to Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities must be conducted using the designated security code and specified lot size.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 23, 2026
- Maturity Date: November 18, 2029

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and trading access for the newly listed privately placed securities of Mangalam Global Enterprise Limited on the NSE capital market segment.
