## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the suspension of the Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) security of Matoshri Jayaben Himmatlal Shah Charitable Trust effective November 20, 2026, on account of maturity.

## Key Points

- Organization: Matoshri Jayaben Himmatlal Shah Charitable Trust
- Symbol: MJHZCZP-SE
- ISIN: INS1GUM12019
- Suspension Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Reason: Maturity of the security

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified security stands suspended on the Exchange w.e.f. November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine maturity and suspension event with no adverse market impact.
