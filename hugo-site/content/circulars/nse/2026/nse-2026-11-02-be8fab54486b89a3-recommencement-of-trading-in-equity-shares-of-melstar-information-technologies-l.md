## Summary

NSE has announced the recommencement of trading in equity shares of Melstar Information Technologies Limited following a capital reduction pursuant to a Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Melstar Information Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 532307
- NSE Symbol: MELSTAR
- ISIN: INE817A01050
- Corporate Action: Capital Restructuring / Capital Reduction
- Reference Orders: NCLT orders dated November 1, 2023, June 25, 2024, and August 9, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in equity shares has been recommenced following successful capital restructuring and reduction as mandated by the NCLT-approved Resolution Plan.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and market participants must update their trading and back-office systems with the restructured capital and ISIN details for Melstar Information Technologies Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Allotment / Extinguishment Date: November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders and market participants to resume trading in the equity shares of Melstar Information Technologies Limited under the revised capital structure.
