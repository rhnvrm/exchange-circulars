## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for TECH MAHINDRA LIMITED (TECHM) on account of a 1:1 bonus issue. The changes become effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action:** Bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1.
- **Ex-Date & Effective Date:** November 30, 2026.
- **Adjustment Factor:** 2
- **Adjusted Revised Market Lot:** 1200
- **Revised Quantity Freeze Limit:** 36000
- **Revised Strike Prices:** Adjusted as per Annexure 1 (e.g., strike 1280.00 revised to 640.00).

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustments are carried out in pursuance of SEBI guidelines for corporate actions on futures and options contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must load the updated contract.gz (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread files before trading on the ex-date.
- Updated files can be obtained from the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) or the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** November 30, 2026.
- **Revised Strike Scheme Effective Date:** Trade date after the ex-date.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in TECHM F&O contracts must update their trading applications with the revised lot sizes, quantity freeze limits, and strike prices to ensure smooth execution and compliance.
