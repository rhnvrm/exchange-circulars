## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS) covering subscriptions, liquid subscriptions, and redemptions for December 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement schedule for MFSS Subscription (Settlement Type 'S').
- Settlement schedule for MFSS Liquid Subscription (Settlement Type 'K').
- Settlement schedule for MFSS Redemption (Settlement Type 'U').
- Category codes for scheme categories along with funds settlement days.

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must adhere to the specified settlement dates and verify scheme categories in the scheme master.

## Important Dates

- December 01, 2026 to December 31, 2026 (settlement cycle extending into January 2027).

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly operational settlement of mutual fund subscription and redemption transactions throughout December 2026.
