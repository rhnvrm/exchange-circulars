## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in non-convertible securities (privately placed) of Indusind Bank Limited, effective from November 23, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indusind Bank Limited
- ISIN: INE095A08058
- Suspension Date: 23-Nov-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Download Ref No: NSE/CML/77526

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, trading in the specified securities has been suspended.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure no further trades are executed in the suspended securities post the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Halt of secondary market trading for the specified non-convertible securities due to complete redemption.
