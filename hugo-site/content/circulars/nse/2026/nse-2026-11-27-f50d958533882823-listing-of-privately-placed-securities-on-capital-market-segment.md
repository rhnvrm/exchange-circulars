## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of further privately placed securities on the capital market segment, effective from November 30, 2026. The issuance includes debentures from Torrent Power Limited and Alphagrep Securities Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Securities Admitted: Privately placed NCDs and market-linked debentures.
- Issuing Companies: Torrent Power Limited and Alphagrep Securities Private Limited.
- Trading is permitted only in designated security codes and specified market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes and market lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Maturity Dates: Range from May 24, 2030 to November 26, 2036 across various tranches.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate debt listing activity with no direct operational impact on equity markets or general liquidity.
