## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share. Market participants and custodians must execute EPI instructions in the old ISIN (INE00HZ01011) for specific settlement numbers and trade dates without adjusting for conversion.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action:** Face Value Split from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share.
- **Existing ISIN:** INE00HZ01011 (Old/Existing ISIN to be used for EPI on specified dates).
- **Ex-Date & Record Date:** November 16, 2026.
- **EPI Dates & Settlement Numbers:** 
  - November 16, 2026: Settlement No. 2026216
  - November 17, 2026: Settlement No. 2026216 & 2026217
- **Block Mechanism:** Participants must adhere to SEBI's block mechanism guidelines (NCL circular NCL/CMPT/53386); client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded when using the block mechanism.
- **Pool Account EPI:** Custodians providing EPI through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on the actual traded quantity.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandates specific settlement procedures and ISIN usage for early pay-in during corporate actions (face value split) in compliance with NCL and SEBI guidelines on block mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in in the old ISIN for trades executed on the ex-date/record date and specified settlement numbers.
- Do not adjust quantities for conversion when executing EPI in the depository system for old ISIN.
- Post-record date, pay-in and early pay-in instructions must be executed in the New ISIN.
- For pool account EPI, upload client-wise allocation details matching actual traded quantities.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Ex-Date:** November 16, 2026
- **EPI Dates:** November 16, 2026 and November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants dealing in BN Agrochem Limited shares during the face value split, preventing mismatches in depository and exchange settlement systems.
