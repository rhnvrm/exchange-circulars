## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margining in the Futures & Options segment, superseding circular no. 119/2026 dated October 26, 2026. The updated list and minimum quantity requirements become effective on November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Supersedes previous circular no. 119/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/77124) dated October 26, 2026.
- Specifies revised minimum quantity requirements for cross-margining across various eligible ETFs.
- Covers ETFs from major fund houses including Nippon India, SBI Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential, HDFC Mutual Fund, Kotak Mahindra, UTI, and Aditya Birla Sun Life.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the roster and minimum quantity thresholds of approved ETFs eligible for cross margining benefits within the F&O segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must adhere to the updated minimum quantity requirements when computing cross-margining benefits effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts margin efficiencies and collateral offset calculations for market participants holding positions in eligible ETFs and offsetting F&O contracts.
