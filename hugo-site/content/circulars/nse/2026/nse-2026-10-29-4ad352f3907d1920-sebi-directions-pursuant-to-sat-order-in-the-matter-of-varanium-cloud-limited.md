## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 9, 2026, in the matter of Varanium Cloud Limited. The SAT order has stayed the impugned SEBI order (WTM/AS/CFID/CFID-TPD/32680/2026-27 dated August 25, 2026) which had restrained Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited from accessing the securities market for 2 years.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous NSE circular NSE/INVG/75978 dated August 25, 2026 and SEBI order dated August 25, 2026.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order 337 of 2026 dated October 09, 2026, stayed the impugned debarment order against Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta (PAN: AAJPM8210D) and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited (PAN: AAICA9336J).
- SEBI via email dated October 29, 2026, advised compliance with the SAT order staying the debarment.
- Consolidated list of debarred entities is available on the NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

- The 2-year debarment from accessing the securities market imposed on Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited stands stayed pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note of the SAT stay order and ensure compliance.
- Queries can be directed to dl-invsg-all@nse.co.in.

## Important Dates

- **August 25, 2026**: Original SEBI debarment order and NSE circular.
- **October 09, 2026**: Hon'ble SAT order (Appeal No. 337 of 2026) granting stay.
- **October 29, 2026**: SEBI email advice and NSE circular issuance date.
- **January 14, 2027**: Next hearing date before SAT.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides interim relief to the appellants (Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited) by staying their market debarment pending final adjudication by SAT.
