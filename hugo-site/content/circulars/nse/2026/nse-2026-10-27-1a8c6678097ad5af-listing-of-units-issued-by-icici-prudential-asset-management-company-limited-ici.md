## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited under the ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF scheme. Trading in these units will commence from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF
- Symbol: PHARMAIETF
- ISIN: INF109K1A716
- Number of Units: 2,390,300
- Face Value and Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 25.35/-
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notification pursuant to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market makers and trading members must adhere to designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 23, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF units on the NSE capital market segment, providing investors with exposure to the pharma sector ETF.
