## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issue of 2,335 equity shares of Grasim Industries Limited consequent to the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares, effective from November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Grasim Industries Limited
- **Symbol:** GRASIM
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE047A01021 (Temporary ISIN: IN8047A01020)
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/-
- **Number of Securities:** 2,335 equity shares
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 680477159 to 680479493
- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard listing of further issue of shares resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities using the designated security codes and trade in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on broader market operations; routine capital increase and listing for Grasim Industries Limited.
