## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective November 04, 2026, on all open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter. Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective November 02, 2026. A consolidated list is provided in Annexure III.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Future Market Networks Limited - FMNL) attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Effective date for margin implementation: November 04, 2026, applicable to open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities in Annexure II (Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited - BRAHMINFRA) move out of the framework effective November 02, 2026.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework (BPL, DOLLEX, EUREKAFORB, FMNL, INDOTECH, RACE).
- Measures operate in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.
- Shortlisting is not an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments on securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Reg. 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.
- Exclusion of specified securities from the surveillance encumbrance framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of minimum 75% margin on open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions for securities listed in Annexure I w.e.f. November 04, 2026.
- Market participants must take note of the consolidated list of securities under the framework.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Exclusion Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 03, 2026
- Margin Implementation Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (75%) on shortlisted securities affect trading liquidity and capital requirements for market participants holding open positions.
- Excluded securities experience normalization of margin rules.
