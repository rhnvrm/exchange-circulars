## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers on its debt market segment, effective November 16, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple entities have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective Listing Date: November 16, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Piramal Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, CEAT Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Tata Capital Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, L&T Finance Limited, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited, NTPC Limited, and Tata Steel Limited.
- Face value per security is 500,000 with varying issue prices, quantities, and redemption dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine notification of securities admission under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must adhere to standard debt market operational and settlement guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 16, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Spanning from December 2026 to November 2027 (e.g., NTPC on 29-Dec-2026, Tata Steel on 11-Dec-2026, L&T Finance on 12-Nov-2027).

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for privately placed commercial papers issued by prominent corporate and financial institutions.
