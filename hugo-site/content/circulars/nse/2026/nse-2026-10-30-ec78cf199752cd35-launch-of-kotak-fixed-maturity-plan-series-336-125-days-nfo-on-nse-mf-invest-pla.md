## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Kotak Fixed Maturity Plan Series 336 - 125 Days NFO of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for transactions from November 02, 2026 to November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include Regular and Direct plans for both Growth and IDCW Payout options.
- NFO Transaction Window: November 02, 2026 to November 04, 2026 till 11:59 pm.
- All ISINs are classified under Debt category with allotment scheduled for November 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 05, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: November 02, 2026
- NFO End Date: November 04, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 05, 2026 (12:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation in fixed maturity debt plans for investors through the NSE trading infrastructure.
