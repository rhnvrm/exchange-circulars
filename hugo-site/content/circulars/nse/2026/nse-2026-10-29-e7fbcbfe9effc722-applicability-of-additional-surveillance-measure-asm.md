## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities effective October 30, 2026. Securities shortlisted include Pashupati Cotspin Limited and Advit Jewels Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. October 30, 2026: Pashupati Cotspin Limited (PASHUPATI) and Advit Jewels Limited (RAMBHAJO).
- Pashupati Cotspin Limited moved from STASM to LTASM framework.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 03, 2026, on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain 100% margin on open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026
- Effective Date of ASM Shortlisting: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 03, 2026 (on open positions as on November 02, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will attract a 100% margin requirement, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for PASHUPATI and RAMBHAJO.
