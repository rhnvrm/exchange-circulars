## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the empanelment of M/s Equi Trend Financial Research Private Limited as an approved algo provider offering whitebox and blackbox algorithmic trading solutions to trading members.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s Equi Trend Financial Research Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox And Blackbox
- Correspondence Address: Unit No.407, 4th Floor, Capital Park, Capital Park Road, Ayyappa Society VIP Hills Silicon Valley, Madhapur, Hyderabad, Telangana – 500081
- Contact Details: Phone: +91 8331005105, Email: nnfreg_support@nse.co.in / invg@nse.co.in

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard empanelment circular updating the list of approved algorithmic trading solution providers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions from empaneled vendors must adhere to exchange guidelines and risk management frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 18, 2026 (With immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

Expands the availability of approved third-party algorithmic trading solutions for NSE trading members.
