## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) close date and allotment date for the Nippon India CRISIL - IBX SDL - June 2037 Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, based on communication from Nippon India Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- NFO Close Date revised from November 26, 2026 to November 30, 2026.
- Allotment Date revised from December 01, 2026 to December 03, 2026.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 01, 2026 01:00 am.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy change; this is an operational schedule update for a specific index fund scheme NFO.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the revised timelines and ensure clear funds are submitted by the stipulated deadline.

## Important Dates

- Revised NFO Close Date: November 30, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 01, 2026 (01:00 am)
- Revised Allotment Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended timeline for investors to participate in the Nippon India CRISIL - IBX SDL - June 2037 Index Fund NFO with minimal operational impact.
