## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of IGL Spirits Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective November 19, 2026, pursuant to previous exchange circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company: IGL Spirits Limited
- Symbol: IGLSPIRITS
- Action: Transferred from trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved from trade-for-trade segment to normal rolling settlement segment in accordance with SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to note the change in segment for trading in IGL Spirits Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for IGL Spirits Limited shares, improving liquidity and trading convenience for investors.
