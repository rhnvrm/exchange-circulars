## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in minimum investment and additional purchase amounts for Axis Silver Fund of Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme affected: Axis Silver Fund of Fund.
- Minimum Application Amount/Switch-in revised from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 100 (in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter).
- Minimum Additional Purchase Amount revised from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 100 (in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter).
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of scheme investment limits as per addendum issued by Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE Invest platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors trading on the NSE MF Invest platform must take note of the revised minimum investment thresholds.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.
- Notice Date: November 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact; lowers the entry barrier for investors participating in the Axis Silver Fund of Fund through lumpsum investments.
