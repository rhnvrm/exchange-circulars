## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBS) segment from the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) / Business Continuity Plan (BCP) sites on Saturday, October 31, 2026. The session aims to test system readiness, disaster recovery switchovers, and ensure smooth operations and re-login procedures ahead of Monday, November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Segment:** Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBS).
- **Session Schedule:**
  - *Primary Site (Session 1 - Normal Market):* 09:00 hrs to 12:00 hrs.
  - *BCP Site (Session 2 - Normal Market):* 12:45 hrs to 17:00 hrs.
  - *Re-Log in Session:* 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs.
- **Connectivity & Settings:** No changes in NEAT Adapter settings are required. Settings of the live session as of Friday, October 30, 2026, shall be retained.
- **Switchover & Shutdown:** The Exchange will shift operations from the primary site to the DR site via a non-graceful shutdown. Members must take necessary action as per circular NSE/MSD/48662.
- **Order Management:** All outstanding orders will be purged before the start of trading from the BCP site. Members using NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders of session 1.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; routine operational mock drill conducted pursuant to standard exchange guidelines and SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, November 02, 2026.
- Ensure only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff are used.
- Members using NNF software must clear outstanding orders between sessions.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 31, 2026.
- **Live Resume Date:** November 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness drill with zero financial impact. Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out, ensuring member systems remain resilient during site switchovers.
