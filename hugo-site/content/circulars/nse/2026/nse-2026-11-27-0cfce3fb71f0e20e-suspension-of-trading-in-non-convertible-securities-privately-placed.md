## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of NTPC Limited pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** NTPC Limited
- **ISIN:** INE733E07EK8
- **Suspension Date:** 30-Nov-2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None. The action is taken in pursuance of existing NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that trading in the specified non-convertible securities of NTPC Limited will be suspended from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date:** 30-November-2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational procedure following the redemption of debt securities and has minimal market impact.
