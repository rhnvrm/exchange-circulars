## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding securities moving to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Effective November 13, 2026, Digjam Limited (DIGJAMLMTD) will transition to Stage II surveillance actions.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Digjam Limited (Symbol: DIGJAMLMTD, ISIN: INE731U01028).
- **Series Change:** Series change from EQ to BE.
- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026.
- **Surveillance Action:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) to be deposited by buyers.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities in Stage II of GSM are subject to stricter trading controls to safeguard market integrity and protect investors from excessive volatility or speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the affected security shall be available on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Buyers must deposit an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of the trade value.
- Members are required to exercise adequate precautions while trading in this security.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Surveillance Measure:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders dealing in DIGJAMLMTD face restricted liquidity due to trade-for-trade settlement, mandatory 50% ASD, and a 5% price band.
