## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (non-convertible debentures) on the capital market segment, effective from November 09, 2026. The listing covers multiple tranches of securities issued by Dar Credit & Capital Limited, Persistent Systems Limited, and NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026.
- **Issuing Entities:** Dar Credit & Capital Limited, Persistent Systems Limited, and NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited.
- **Trading Parameters:** Securities will be identified by designated security codes and traded in specified lot sizes as per the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must execute transactions using the designated security codes and adhere to the prescribed market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** Ranging from May 2028 to November 2031 across the various listed security tranches.

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing of debt instruments with minimal direct impact on broader equity market operations.
