## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited with effect from November 20, 2026, pursuant to maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited
- Suspended Securities: Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)
- Symbols: 850CIFCL26, CIFCZC26
- ISINs: INE121A07RO8, INE121A07RK6
- Reason for Suspension: Maturity
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised that trading in the aforementioned NCDs will be suspended on the Exchange w.e.f. November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational procedure following the maturity of debentures and has no adverse impact on the company's equity shares or ongoing business operations.
