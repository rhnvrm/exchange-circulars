## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a reminder to all active trading members regarding the submission of Risk Based Supervision (RBS) data for the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The electronic submission must be completed on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory for all active members who executed at least a single trade during April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be made electronically through the Inspection module in the Member Portal.
- Non-compliance invites penal charges and disciplinary actions.

## Regulatory Changes

This is a compliance reminder reinforcing existing reporting mandates established under Exchange circular NSE/INSP/76664.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit the required Risk Based Supervision data via the Member Portal inspection module on or before November 30, 2026, to avoid penalties prescribed in Circular No. NSE/INSP/73792.

## Important Dates

- **Period Covered:** April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026
- **Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Failure to submit timely data will result in penal charges and regulatory disciplinary actions against defaulting trading members.
