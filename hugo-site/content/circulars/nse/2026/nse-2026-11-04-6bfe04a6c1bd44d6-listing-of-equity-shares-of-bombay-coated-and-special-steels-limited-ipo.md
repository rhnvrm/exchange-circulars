## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** BCSSL
- **Company Name:** Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited
- **Series:** BE (to be subsequently shifted to Series EQ as per SEBI circulars)
- **ISIN:** INE0XFY01011
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 82/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities:** 62,824,211
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities is subject to NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A and relevant SEBI guidelines for IPO pre-open sessions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the security codes, series BE, lot size of 1, and lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Various dates ranging from December 2, 2026 to May 3, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited on NSE.
