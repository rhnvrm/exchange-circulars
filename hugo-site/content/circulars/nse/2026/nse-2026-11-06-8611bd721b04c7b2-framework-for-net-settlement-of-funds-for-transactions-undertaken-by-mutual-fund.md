## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the framework for net settlement of funds for transactions undertaken by mutual fund schemes in the cash market. Permitting net settlement of funds at the individual scheme level for outright transactions aims to enhance operational efficiency and reduce temporary funding requirements, while settlement of securities remains on a gross basis.

## Key Points

- Scheme-level net settlement of funds is permitted for outright transactions in the cash segment of stock exchanges.
- Settlement of securities continues to be on a gross basis between the mutual fund scheme and the custodian.
- An 'outright transaction' is defined as either a purchase or a sale transaction (not both) in a security within the same settlement cycle.
- Transactions involving both purchase and sale in the same security within the same settlement cycle are treated as non-outright and are ineligible for net settlement.
- Netting is strictly restricted within the same mutual fund scheme and settlement cycle; cross-scheme netting or adjustments are prohibited.
- Excluded transactions include derivatives, securities lending and borrowing, and short sale-related transactions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Permits net settlement of funds for outright cash market transactions at the individual mutual fund scheme level.
- Retains gross settlement of securities, Securities Transaction Tax (STT), and stamp duty.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs and custodians must ensure that netting mechanisms do not impact scheme-wise accounting, NAV computation, asset allocation, investment limits, or unit-holder interests.
- Residual fund obligations must be funded by the scheme if outright sales are less than purchases; excess sale proceeds cannot offset non-outright purchase obligations.
- Non-outright purchase obligations must continue to be funded separately on a gross basis.

## Important Dates

- Circular issued on November 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency and reduces temporary liquidity and funding requirements for mutual fund schemes participating in the cash market.
- Streamlines cash flow management for Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and custodians while maintaining strict regulatory controls on securities settlement and risk parameters.
