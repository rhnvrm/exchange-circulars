## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the levy of additional exposure margins on securities under Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). An additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment will be applicable to securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the MWPL, based on 3 months rolling data reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin of 15% levied in the equity derivatives segment.
- Applies to securities where top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL.
- Higher of additional exposure margin or additional surveillance margin shall be levied if applicable.
- Framework based on 3 months rolling data with monthly reviews.
- Effective from November 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of additional exposure margin framework for securities exceeding client concentration thresholds under MWPL in the derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and trading members must take note of the shortlisted securities in Annexure 1 and maintain adequate margins in the equity derivatives segment as per the prescribed framework.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in the equity derivatives segment for the specified securities listed in Annexure 1, impacting capital allocation for positions in these counters.
