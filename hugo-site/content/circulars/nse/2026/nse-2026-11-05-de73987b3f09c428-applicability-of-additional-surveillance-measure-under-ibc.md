## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective November 06, 2026. The circular details the shortlisting of securities under ASM IBC Stage I and consolidated stages.

## Key Points

- Osia Hyper Retail Limited (OSIAHYPER) has been shortlisted in ASM for Companies as per IBC Stage I w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO) continues under Stage II in the consolidated ASM (IBC) list.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of the ASM IBC framework in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures enforced by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the securities shortlisted under ASM IBC and ensure compliance with applicable trading controls, surveillance margins, and monitoring requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, enhanced margin requirements, or trading controls as prescribed under the ASM IBC framework.
