## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in PETRONET LNG LIMITED (PETRONET) on account of a dividend of Rs. 7.00 per share.

## Key Points

- Futures contracts adjustment is based on the reference rate on Nov 05, 2026 (last cum-dividend date), reduced by the dividend amount of Rs. 7.00.
- Open positions in futures will be carried forward at the daily settlement price less Rs. 7.00 starting from the ex-dividend date (Nov 06, 2026).
- Begin of day margins on Nov 06, 2026, will be computed based on the adjusted carry forward value.
- For Option contracts, the full dividend value of Rs. 7.00 will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date and adjusted to the nearest tick size.

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action adjustments applied to derivative contracts for PETRONET to account for dividend payout.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must account for adjusted contract values, carry forward positions, and revised strike prices for PETRONET F&O contracts.

## Important Dates

- Last Cum-Dividend Date: November 05, 2026
- Ex-Dividend Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding open F&O positions in PETRONET will see mark-to-market settlements and carry-forward values adjusted by the dividend amount of Rs. 7.00.
