## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued clarifications regarding end-of-day (EOD) reports downloaded by members, following the updated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) on Settlement Price Computation previously communicated in circulars NCL/CMPT/76951 and NCL/CMPT/77212.

## Key Points

- **Contracts Report (CN01):** The 'Contract Underlying Price' column continues to be derived from the Closing Auction Session (CAS) for both expiring and non-expiring contracts.
- **F&O-Daily Volatility (FOVOLT):** The Underlying Close Price (A) and Underlying Previous Day Close Price (B) columns continue to be derived based on CAS.
- **Position Reports:** For options contracts, the 'SttlmPric' column contains the Underlying Price derived from Continuous Trading Session (CTS) prices for both expiring and non-expiring contracts. For futures contracts, it contains the contract settlement price for non-expiring contracts and CTS-derived underlying price for expiring contracts.
- **F&O Daily Settlement Prices (FOSett_prce):** The 'MTM Settlement Price' column contains prices derived based on CTS for both expiring and non-expiring contracts.
- **Delivery Margin Reports:** The 'MTM Price' column contains prices derived based on CTS.

## Regulatory Changes

Clarification of price computation methodologies (CAS vs. CTS) applied to specific EOD report columns for derivatives contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must take note of the specified report nomenclature and price derivation methodologies for operational integration and reporting reconciliation.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Reference Circulars:** October 16, 2026 (NCL/CMPT/76951) and October 30, 2026 (NCL/CMPT/77212)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational understanding and technical alignment for members processing F&O end-of-day reports and margin calculations under the revised settlement methodology.
