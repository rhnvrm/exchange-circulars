## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the empanelment of M/s Finharbour Fintech Private Limited as an Algo Provider to offer Whitebox algorithmic trading solutions to trading members of the exchange with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Empaneled Vendor: M/s Finharbour Fintech Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox
- Correspondence Address: 402, Zion Prime, Near Four Pole Structure, Thaltej, Daskroi, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, 380059
- Contact Details: Ph. No.: +91 7777963834
- The updated list of empaneled algo providers is available on the NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular is an administrative update regarding vendor empanelment for algorithmic trading solutions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members engaging with the empaneled vendor must adhere to exchange guidelines and standards concerning algorithmic trading and NNF regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Expands the availability of approved algorithmic trading solution providers for trading members seeking Whitebox algo systems.
