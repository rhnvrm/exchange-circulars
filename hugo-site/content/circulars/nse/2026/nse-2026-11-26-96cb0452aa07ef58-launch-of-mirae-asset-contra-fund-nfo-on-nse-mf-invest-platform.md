## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Mirae Asset Contra Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from November 27, 2026 to December 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription window: November 27, 2026 to December 11, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Covers multiple scheme options including Direct and Regular plans with Growth and IDCW Payout/Reinvestment options (ISINs: INF769K01SE4, INF769K01SG9, INF769K01SF1, INF769K01SD6, INF769K01SC8, INF769K01SB0).
- Allotment Date: December 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 17, 2026 till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: November 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: December 11, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 17, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: December 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors with seamless access to subscribe to the Mirae Asset Contra Fund NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
