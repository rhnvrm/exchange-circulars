## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the update made by the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) 1718 Sanctions Committee on its sanctions list concerning the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK). Stock exchanges, depositories, KRAs, and registered intermediaries are required to take note of Note SC/16512 dated November 16, 2026, and ensure compliance.

## Key Points

- The UNSC 1718 Sanctions Committee updated one entry under entities and other groups: Mansudae Overseas Project Group of Companies (KPe.050).
- Refers to Clause 54 of the SEBI Master Circular on Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards and CFT obligations under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA).
- Highlights statutory obligations under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (UAPA) and the Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendment of one entry in the UNSC 1718 Sanctions List (Mansudae Overseas Project Group of Companies, listed on 5 Aug 2017, amended on 26 Jul 2022 and 13 Nov 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges, Depositories, KRAs, and registered intermediaries must ensure they do not maintain any accounts or provide financial services to individuals or entities appearing on the updated UNSC sanctions lists.
- Take necessary measures in line with Central Government Order 'F. No. P-12011/14/2022-ES Cell-DOR' dated September 01, 2023.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- UNSC Note Date: November 16, 2026
- Amendment Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures robust compliance with international sanctions and domestic counter-terrorist financing frameworks across Indian securities market intermediaries.
