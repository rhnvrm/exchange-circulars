## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission of further issues of securities for several companies to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of further equity shares to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment effective October 28, 2026.
- Companies covered include Affle 3i Limited, Baid Finserv Limited, DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited, EFC (I) Limited, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, M & B Engineering Limited, Natco Pharma Limited, PDS Limited, Rubicon Research Limited, and Suzlon Energy Limited.
- Securities issued under preferential allotment with specific face values, paid-up values, issue prices, distinctive number ranges, and lock-in details.
- Market lot for trading is established as 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Carried out in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must identify securities solely by their designated security codes and execute trades in the specified lot sizes and lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the paid-up capital base of the respective corporate entities and facilitates trading of newly allotted shares on the exchange platform.
