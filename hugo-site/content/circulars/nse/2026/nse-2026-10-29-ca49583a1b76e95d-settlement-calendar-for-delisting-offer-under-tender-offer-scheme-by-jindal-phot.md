## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar and operational guidelines for the delisting offer under the tender offer scheme by Jindal Photo Limited. The trade start date is November 2, 2026, and the trade end date is November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jindal Photo Limited
- **Settlement Type:** X
- **Settlement No.:** 2026131
- **Trade Start Date:** 02-NOV-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 06-NOV-2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 17-NOV-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 18-NOV-2026
- Demat shares must be tendered via Early Pay-in of Shares for Market Type Buyback.
- Order acceptance on trade end date is up to 03:30 p.m. with early pay-in made in advance.
- Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure early pay-in of demat shares through depositories for Market Type Buyback well in advance up to 03:30 p.m. on the trade end date.
- Funds pay-in must be completed by buying trading members prior to settlement.
- Members should refer to the Tender Offer Acceptance Report for order status, partial allocations, and rejection reason codes.
