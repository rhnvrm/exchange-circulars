## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited
- Security/Symbol: MKJEWELS
- Event: Listing of equity shares on the exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and exchange bye-laws.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public trading access for the equity shares of M. K. Sons Fine Jewels Limited.
