## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the suspension of trading in several T-Bills, Sovereign Gold Bonds, Government Securities (GS), and State Government Bonds (SG) on account of maturity and redemption, effective from various dates in November 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading covers 21 securities including T-Bills, Sovereign Gold Bonds, Government Securities, and State Development Loans/Bonds.
- Suspension dates range from November 3, 2026, to November 25, 2026, based on individual maturity dates.
- Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A governs this action.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Routine market operations based on security maturity).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the suspension dates and ensure no trades are executed in the specified securities post their respective suspension dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Suspension Dates: November 3, 2026, through November 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as this is a standard procedural suspension for matured government securities and sovereign bonds.
