## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of fresh issues of commercial papers for various companies including Piramal Finance Limited, Bahadur Chand Investments Private Limited, IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, L&T Finance Limited, and Godrej Industries Limited.
- Effective date of admission: November 13, 2026.
- Details regarding ISINs, issue prices, face values, quantities, and redemption dates are specified for each security.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must comply with the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and respective listing conditions.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 13, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from January 2027 to October 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper series.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating secondary market trading for the newly listed debt securities.
