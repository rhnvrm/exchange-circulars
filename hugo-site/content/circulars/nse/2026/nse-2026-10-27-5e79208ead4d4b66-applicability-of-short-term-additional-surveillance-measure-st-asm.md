## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Effective October 28, 2026, specific securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, and certain securities have moved from Stage I to Stage II.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. October 28, 2026: BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM), Paushak Limited (PAUSHAKLTD), and Shivalic Power Control Limited (SPCL).
- Securities moving from Short-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. October 28, 2026: Dhariwalcorp Limited (DHARIWAL).
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework, applying enhanced margin requirements to curb excessive volatility in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) applicable to all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Framework: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: October 29, 2026 (on open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading leverage for the shortlisted securities (BNAGROCHEM, PAUSHAKLTD, SPCL, DHARIWAL) due to higher margin requirements.
