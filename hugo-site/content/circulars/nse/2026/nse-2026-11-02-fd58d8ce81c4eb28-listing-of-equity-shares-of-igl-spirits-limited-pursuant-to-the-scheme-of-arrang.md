## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of IGL Spirits Limited pursuant to a scheme of arrangement, effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: IGL Spirits Limited
- Symbol: IGLSPIRITS
- ISIN: INE2W6901017
- Number of Securities: 67,026,765 equity shares
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/- each
- Distinctive Number Range: 20001 to 67046765
- Trading Series: BE (Trade for Trade)
- Registrar and Share Transfer Agent: MCS Registrar and Transfer Agent Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Market lot for trading is set at 1.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities shall be available for trading in Series 'BE' (Trade for Trade) and subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI circular SEBI/CIR/ISD/1/2010 dated September 2, 2010.
- Securities will be part of the pre-open session as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and trading access to shareholders for the newly issued equity shares of IGL Spirits Limited under the trade-for-trade category.
