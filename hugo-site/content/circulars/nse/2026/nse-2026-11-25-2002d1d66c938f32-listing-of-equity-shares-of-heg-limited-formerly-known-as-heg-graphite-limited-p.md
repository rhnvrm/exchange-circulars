## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of HEG Limited (formerly known as HEG Graphite Limited) pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement. Trading in these securities will commence effectively from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Limited (formerly HEG Graphite Limited)
- Symbol: HEGRAPHITE
- Number of Securities: 19,29,77,530 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each
- ISIN: INE0Y9J01020
- Distinctive Number Range: 50001 to 193027530
- Market Lot: 1
- Trading Series: Series 'BE' (Trade for Trade), to be subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ'

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted under trading regulations part A, subject to pre-open session guidelines.
- Trading will initially be in Trade for Trade (BE) series in compliance with SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note designated security codes and trading lot sizes.
- Lock-in details apply as per Annexure I of the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity base of HEG Limited with proper ISIN and distinct numbering ranges active for trading on the Capital Market segment.
