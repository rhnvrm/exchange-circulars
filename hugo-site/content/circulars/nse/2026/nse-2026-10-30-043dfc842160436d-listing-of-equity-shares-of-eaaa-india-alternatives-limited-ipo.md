## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the forthcoming listing of equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited under an Initial Public Offering (IPO). The company's securities will be traded under the symbol EDELALTS.

## Key Points

- Company Name: EAAA India Alternatives Limited
- Trading Symbol: EDELALTS
- ISIN: INE528L01027
- Exchange Department: Listing

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing communication.

## Compliance Requirements

Exchange members should update their trading systems and records with the new symbol and ISIN details.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market readiness and trading preparation for the newly listed equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited.
