## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements (REs) of Shivam Autotech Limited pursuant to its ongoing rights issue. Trading in these REs will commence on October 28, 2026, and conclude on November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shivam Autotech Limited
- Symbol: SHIVAM-RE1
- ISIN: INE637H20024
- Total Number of Rights Entitlements: 99,936,366
- Trading Period for Renunciation: October 28, 2026 to November 13, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 16, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- REs are admitted under Regulation 3.1.1 and lot sizes under Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and clarify that REs are distinct from ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills for the purchase of REs must prominently display a disclaimer informing clients that REs grant the right to participate in the rights issue by applying with requisite application money or renouncing before the issue closes.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs as of the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: October 28, 2026
- Trading Renunciation End Date: November 13, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 16, 2026
