## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for certain securities, effective October 30, 2026. Securities qualifying under ESM will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment and will attract a minimum 100% margin.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I (including Arabian Petroleum Limited, Highway Infrastructure Limited, and Sp Refractories Limited) satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage - I.
- Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 30, 2026, on all open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 30, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Mandatory shifting of qualifying securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Imposition of a minimum 100% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) on open positions as of October 29, 2026, and new positions created from October 30, 2026.
- Market participants must account for settlement changes from rolling to trade-for-trade for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin and Trade-for-Trade Shifting: October 30, 2026 (on open positions as on October 29, 2026).
- Circular Date: October 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for impacted scrips may affect short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the listed securities.
