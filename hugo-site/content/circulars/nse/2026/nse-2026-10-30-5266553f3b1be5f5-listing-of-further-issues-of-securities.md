## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for three companies under the SME Emerge segment, effective from November 02, 2026. The companies involved are Vasa Denticity Limited, OBSC Perfection Limited, and Shree Karni Fabcom Limited.

## Key Points

- Vasa Denticity Limited: 150,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted pursuant to conversion of warrants issued on preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 578 (ISIN: INE0N5801013, Market Lot: 250).
- OBSC Perfection Limited: 1,439,600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 601 (ISIN: INE0YHV01011, Market Lot: 400).
- Shree Karni Fabcom Limited: 105,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 765 (ISIN: INE0S4Y01010, Market Lot: 150).
- Trading in these securities will be effective from November 02, 2026, in designated lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further issues of securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE in pursuance of Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with designated security codes, trading lot sizes, and lock-in requirements as specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Allotment Dates: May 15, 2026 (Vasa Denticity); September 11, 2026 (OBSC Perfection); August 25, 2026 (Shree Karni Fabcom).
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: April 30, 2027 and April 30, 2028 (as applicable).

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the paid-up equity base of the respective SME companies and enables trading of further issued shares on the exchange.
