## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage - I (ATLANTAA, FWSTC, LEMERITE, ZEEMEDIA) will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 08, 2026, on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created from November 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting from Stage I to Stage II (CKKRETAIL, TIJARIA, UCAL) will be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- Affected securities shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The shortlisting of securities under the ESM framework is implemented by the Exchange as part of market surveillance measures in conjunction with existing rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in shortlisted securities as specified.
- Market participants must take note of segment shifts and price band restrictions under Periodic Call Auction for securities entering Stage II.

## Important Dates

- Stage II shift and price band effective date: November 06, 2026
- Margin applicability and segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST) effective date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading mechanisms for the affected scrips are impacted due to mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement enforcement, and 2% price bands under Periodic Call Auction.
