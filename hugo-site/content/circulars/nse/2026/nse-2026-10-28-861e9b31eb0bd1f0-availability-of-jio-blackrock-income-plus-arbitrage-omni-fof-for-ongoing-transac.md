## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members that Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes involved: Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF22M001382) and Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF22M001390).
- Category: Debt.
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine platform availability notice for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants can facilitate transactions in the mentioned schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026
- Circular Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF debt schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform with standard operational and systematic investment facilities.
