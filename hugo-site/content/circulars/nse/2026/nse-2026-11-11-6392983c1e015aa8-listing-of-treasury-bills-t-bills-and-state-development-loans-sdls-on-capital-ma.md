## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission to dealings of various State Development Loans (SDLs) and Government Treasury Bills (T-bills) on the capital market segment with effect from November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Treasury Bills (91-day, 182-day, and 364-day) and State Development Loans (SDLs) from states including Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Nagaland, Jammu & Kashmir, Bihar, and Gujarat are admitted for trading.
- Designated lot sizes for all listed securities are set at 100.
- Trading commences on November 13, 2026, using designated security codes and ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must trade securities using the assigned security codes and mandated lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Circular Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine expansion of capital market debt instruments available for trading.
