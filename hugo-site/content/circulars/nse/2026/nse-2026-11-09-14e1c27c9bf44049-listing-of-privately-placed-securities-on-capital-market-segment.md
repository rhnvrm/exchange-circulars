## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of privately placed securities on the capital market segment effective from November 11, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on NSE Capital Market segment effective November 11, 2026.
- Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) 8.17% 2031 Sr VIII (ISIN: INE556F08LJ5, Symbol: 817SIDBI31, Lot Size: 1).
- Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited 9% 2029 (ISIN: INE281B07039, Symbol: 9LMAEL29, Lot Size: 1).
- Trading shall be conducted in designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities must be conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 11, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 06-Nov-2031 (SIDBI), 29-Oct-2029 (Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing enabling secondary market trading of privately placed debt securities for investors.
