## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from November 16, 2026. These issuances follow allotments under employee stock option plans (ESOPs) and conversions of warrants issued on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: November 16, 2026.
- Companies involved include Banka BioLoo Limited, Centrum Capital Limited, Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited, Honasa Consumer Limited, and others.
- Allotments include ESOP shares and equity shares resulting from warrant conversion.
- Trading will take place under designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities using their designated codes and trade in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Various (Oct-Nov 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing update for the respective listed companies with minimal overall market impact.
