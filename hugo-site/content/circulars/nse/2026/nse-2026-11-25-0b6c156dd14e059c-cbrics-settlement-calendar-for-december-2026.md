## Summary

NSE Clearing Ltd. has released the settlement calendar for the CBRICS (Corporate Bond) segment for the month of December 2026. Participants and custodians are required to transfer securities using the 'On-market' settlement mode based on the specified schedule.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Segment (Corporate Bond)
- **Clearing Member Name:** NSE Clearing Ltd.
- **CM-BP ID:** IN568177
- **Transfer Mode:** On-market
- **DP Details Note:** Previously used DP details (IN001002-10000617) are not considered effective September 3, 2019.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must adhere to the settlement dates and transfer securities via NSDL/CDSL using the specified CM-BP ID and 'On-market' mode.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** 25 November, 2026
- **Settlement Period:** December 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact for participants trading in corporate bonds via the CBRICS platform, ensuring smooth settlement cycles throughout December 2026.
