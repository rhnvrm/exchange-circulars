## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Members can facilitate ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) starting November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AI09, Category: Equity)
- **Scheme Name:** UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH91, Category: Equity)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational update regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in mutual fund transactions must take note of the ISINs and effective dates for client transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund through the NSE MF Invest platform smoothly.
