## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: RMC Switchgears Limited (Symbol: RMC, ISIN: INE655V01019) and SG Finserve Limited (Symbol: SGFIN, ISIN: INE618R01015).
- Applicable rate of margin under ST-ASM Stage I shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100% w.e.f. December 01, 2026, on all open positions as on November 30, 2026, and new positions created from December 01, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of tighter surveillance measures and enhanced margin requirements for securities meeting the short-term additional surveillance criteria to manage market volatility and risk.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing margin, capped at 100%) for open positions and new positions in the listed securities starting December 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date / Implementation: November 30, 2026 / December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors holding or trading shares in RMC and SGFIN will experience higher capital margin requirements, which may affect short-term trading liquidity and positions.
