## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of units issued by Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited under the Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VB (136 Days) scheme, effective from October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VB (136 Days)
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** October 27, 2026
- **Face Value & Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Trading Symbols & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: `ABSLFMVBRG` (ISIN: INF209KC1860, No. of Securities: 46,438,326)
  - Regular Plan - Payout of IDCW: `ABSLFMVBRN` (ISIN: INF209KC1886, No. of Securities: 16,512)
  - Regular Plan - Reinvestment of IDCW: `ABSLFMVBRQ` (ISIN: INF209KC1878, No. of Securities: 1,700)
  - Direct Plan - Growth: `ABSLFMVBDG` (ISIN: INF209KC1837, No. of Securities: 449,105,364)
  - Direct Plan - Payout of IDCW: `ABSLFMVBDN` (ISIN: INF209KC1852, No. of Securities: 1,500)
  - Direct Plan - Reinvestment of IDCW: `ABSLFMVBDQ` (ISIN: INF209KC1845, No. of Securities: 1,700)

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be identified only by designated codes and in specified market lots (Market Lot: 1).

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official trading platform access to investors holding units of the Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VB (136 Days).
