## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for 16 companies, including Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC, Aether Industries, Axis Bank, InterGlobe Aviation, and others, effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers further issuance and listing of equity shares allotted under ESOP and other corporate actions.
- Effective date for trading of these further issues: November 06, 2026.
- Includes 16 companies such as Axis Bank, InterGlobe Aviation, One 97 Communications, ICICI Lombard General Insurance, and Max Healthcare Institute.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure orders are placed using designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Allotment Dates: November 03-04, 2026 (varying by company)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase and addition of shares to existing listings with negligible immediate market impact.
