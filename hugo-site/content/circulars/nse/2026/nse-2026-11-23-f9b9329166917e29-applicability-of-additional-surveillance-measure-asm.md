## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Empower India Limited has satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure Stage-I.

## Key Points

- Empower India Limited (ISIN: INE507F01023) shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I w.e.f. November 25, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 27, 2026 on all open positions as on November 26, 2026 and new positions created from November 27, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I are subjected to enhanced margin requirements.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin maintenance on open positions as on November 26, 2026 and new positions created from November 27, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Inclusion: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) on Empower India Limited will impact leverage and liquidity for market participants trading in the security.
