## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II on the NSE MF Invest Platform for a specified transaction period from November 09, 2026, to November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II
- Transaction Period: November 09, 2026 to November 11, 2026
- Applicable NAV: Determined as per scheme cut-off timing based on receipt date/time and fund confirmation.
- Included Plans: IDCW Plan (Payout & Reinvestment), Direct IDCW Plan (Payout & Reinvestment), Growth Plan, and Direct Growth Plan across respective ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit subscription and redemption requests during normal business hours from November 09, 2026, to November 11, 2026, in accordance with scheme cut-off timings on the NSE MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Start Date: November 09, 2026
- End Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless processing of subscription and redemption transactions for investors in the Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II during the specified window.
