## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I: Aprameya Engineering Limited (APRAMEYA) and GNG Electronics Limited (EBGNG).
- Securities moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I to Stage - II: Creative Newtech Limited (CNL).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 09, 2026, on all open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Long-Term ASM surveillance framework and associated 100% margin requirements for listed securities meeting specific volatility and surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure 100% margin is maintained for open and new positions in the affected securities from November 09, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion/movement: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 09, 2026 (on open positions as of November 08, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities (APRAMEYA, EBGNG, and CNL) will experience heightened margin obligations, impacting liquidity and position sizing for market participants.
