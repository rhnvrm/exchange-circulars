## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of units issued by Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited under Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VC (114 Days) effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective November 23, 2026.
- Covers multiple options including Regular Plan-Growth, Direct Plan-Growth, Payout of IDCW, and Reinvestment of IDCW.
- Face value and paid-up value per unit are Rs. 10/-.
- Date of allotment: November 18, 2026; Date of redemption: March 11, 2027.
- Market lot is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notification in pursuance of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to trading regulations and designated security codes for market transactions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 20, 2026
- Date of Allotment: November 18, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 23, 2026
- Date of Redemption: March 11, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for investors in Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VC (114 Days).
