## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the introduction of Futures & Options (F&O) contracts on two individual securities: Acutaas Chemicals Limited (ACUTAAS) and Apar Industries Limited (APARINDS), effective from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Added**: Acutaas Chemicals Limited (Symbol: ACUTAAS, Lot Size: 150) and Apar Industries Limited (Symbol: APARINDS, Lot Size: 50).
- **Effective Date**: November 25, 2026.
- **Reference**: In continuation of Exchange circular Ref. No. NSE/FAOP/77353 dated November 06, 2026.
- **Strike Schemes & Ranges**: Detailed step values, strike schemes, and reserve strikes for narrow and wide ranges are provided in Annexure A of the circular.
- **Tick Size**: Futures contracts will have the same tick size as the underlying security in the CM Segment. Options tick size will adhere to circular NSE/FAOP/70382 dated September 23, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Expansion of the derivatives product suite to include new individual securities in accordance with regulatory frameworks for F&O contract introductions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must load the updated `contract.gz` and `NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` files in their trading applications prior to trading on November 25, 2026.
- Contract files can be obtained from `faoftp/faocommon` on the Extranet server or via the NSE website.
- Applicable quantity freeze details will be available within the contract file.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 23, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date**: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants can now trade futures and options on ACUTAAS and APARINDS, enabling enhanced portfolio hedging and speculative strategies on these securities.
