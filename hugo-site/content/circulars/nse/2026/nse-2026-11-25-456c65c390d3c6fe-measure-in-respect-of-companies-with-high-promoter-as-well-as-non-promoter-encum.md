## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Shalimar Paints Limited has been included under the measure, attracting a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- Shalimar Paints Limited (SHALPAINTS) has satisfied the criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- The margin is applicable w.e.f. November 30, 2026, on all open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- No securities are excluded from the framework in Annexure II.
- The consolidated list under the framework includes BPL, DOLLEX, EUREKAFORB, FMNL, INDOTECH, RACE, and SHALPAINTS.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of 75% margin requirements on securities meeting the high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance criteria under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the collection of a minimum 75% margin on open and new positions in the specified securities in accordance with the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 30, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Exclusions / List Update: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the shortlisted securities must adjust their collateral and margin allocations to comply with the heightened margin requirements.
