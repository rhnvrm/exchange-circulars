## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for various companies, effective from November 17, 2026, pursuant to preferential allotments, warrant conversions, and ESOP exercises.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective November 17, 2026.
- Covers companies including 360 ONE WAM Limited, Ambica Agarbathies & Aroma Industries Limited, Axis Bank Limited, AXISCADES Technologies Limited, and Centum Electronics Limited.
- Allotments comprise preferential issues, warrant conversions, and employee stock options (ESOP).
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and standard market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further issues of securities in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the specified distinctive number ranges, market lots, and lock-in details outlined in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Varies by company (e.g., 22-Oct-2026 for 360 ONE WAM, 25-Aug-2026 for Ambica Agarbathies, 11-Nov-2026 for Axis Bank, 02-Nov-2026 for AXISCADES).

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in the expansion of paid-up equity capital for the respective listed companies with no broader market-wide impact.
