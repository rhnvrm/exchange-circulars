## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers (CPs) issued by multiple corporate entities on the Debt Market Segment, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective date of listing: October 30, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Tata Capital Limited, NABARD, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, and Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited.
- Security details such as ISINs, face values, issue prices, allotment dates, and redemption dates are specified for each issuance.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational circular notifying the listing of debt securities under existing regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing entities must ensure compliance with all applicable regulations regarding debt market segment listings and disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 29, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 5, 2026, to March 11, 2027 (varying by specific instrument)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for the listed commercial papers on the exchange's debt segment.
