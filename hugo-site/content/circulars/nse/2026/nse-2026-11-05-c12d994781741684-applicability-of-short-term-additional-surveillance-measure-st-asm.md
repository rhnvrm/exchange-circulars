## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Moving Media Entertainment Limited (MMEL), Sadbhav Infrastructure Project Limited (SADBHIN), Supreme Infrastructure India Limited (SUPREMEINF), and Synoptics Technologies Limited (SYNOPTICS).
- No securities were shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II or movement between stages in this circular.
- Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100% w.e.f. November 08, 2026, on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created from November 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) are maintained for open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created from November 08, 2026, for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Shortlisting: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date of Margin Applicability: November 08, 2026 (on open positions as on November 06, 2026 and new positions created from November 08, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and volatility for the shortlisted stocks. Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.
