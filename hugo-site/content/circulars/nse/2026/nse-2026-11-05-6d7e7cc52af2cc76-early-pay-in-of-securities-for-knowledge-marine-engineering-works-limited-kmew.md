## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW) due to a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 5/- per share to Re 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Face value split of KMEW shares from Rs 5/- per share to Re 1/- per share.
- Record date and ex-date: November 06, 2026.
- Members must execute early pay-in in the old ISIN (INE0CJD01029) without adjusting for conversion on specific trade dates and settlement numbers (2026210, 2026211, 2026212).
- Custodians using the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantities.
- New ISIN must be used for pay-in/early pay-in instructions after the record date.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational instructions for handling corporate action (face value split) in depository and clearing systems in accordance with existing framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in in the old ISIN for the specified settlement numbers and ensure correct client-wise allocation or block mechanism compliance as outlined by NCL.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: November 06, 2026
- Record Date: November 06, 2026
- EPI Dates: November 06, 2026, November 08, 2026, and November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless settlement and clearing operations for market participants dealing in KMEW shares during the face value split transition.
