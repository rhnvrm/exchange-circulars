## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session for the Debt Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026. Members are advised to participate actively to test connectivity, noting that no changes are required at their end and no financial or securities settlement obligations will arise from the session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- Covers Order Matching Platform, Negotiated Trade Reporting Platform, and Tri-Party Repo Market Platform, along with EBP, CBRICS, and RFQ.
- No changes required in member configurations; existing URLs will be used.
- Trades resulting from the mock session will not attract any fund or securities pay-in/pay-out obligations.
- Any new UCC uploaded during the mock session will be removed upon completion and must be re-uploaded afterwards.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate actively in the mock session to verify connectivity.
- Do not transfer any data files for this session as trades do not attract settlement obligations.
- Newly uploaded Unique Client Codes (UCC) during the mock session will be purged and must be re-uploaded after the mock activity.

## Important Dates

- **October 31, 2026**: Mock trading session from Disaster Recovery (DR) site across Order Matching, Negotiated Trade Reporting, and Tri-Party Repo Market Platforms.

## Impact Assessment

Operational check for member connectivity with zero financial or settlement impact.
