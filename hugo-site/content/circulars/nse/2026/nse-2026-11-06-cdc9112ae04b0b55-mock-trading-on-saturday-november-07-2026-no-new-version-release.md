## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026. There is no new version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: November 07, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- Pre-open session open at 10:45 hours, close at 10:53 hours.
- Normal market open at 11:00 hours, close at 12:30 hours.
- Auction market open at 11:30 hours, close at 12:15 hours.
- Block deal window open at 11:45 hours, close at 12:00 hours.
- Live re-login start time at 14:00 hours, close at 14:30 hours.
- NEAT+ version details: Version 1.0.2 (recommended) available on extranet path /egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus102.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock trading and system testing session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are advised to participate in the mock session and take note of the login parameters, version details, and schedule.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 07, 2026
- NEAT+ Version 1.0.1 Discontinuation Date (Mock): November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Ensures system readiness and familiarity with trading parameters and NEAT+ version 1.0.2 in the EGR segment.
