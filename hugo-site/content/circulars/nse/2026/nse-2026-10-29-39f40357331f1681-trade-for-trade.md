## Summary

Pursuant to surveillance review to ensure market safety and safeguard investor interests, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the shifting of specified securities to and from the Trade for Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure 1 are shifted from Rolling segment (series: EQ/SM) to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a 5% price band.
- Certain securities continue to be available for trading in the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/BZ/ST/SZ) with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Securities listed in Annexure 3 are shifted back from Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) to Rolling segment (series: EQ/SM) at existing price bands.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of securities between Rolling and Trade for Trade segments as part of ongoing market surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, as settlement is executed on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 03, 2026
- Circular Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience trade-to-trade settlement restrictions and 5% price bands, limiting speculative trading and enhancing market integrity.
