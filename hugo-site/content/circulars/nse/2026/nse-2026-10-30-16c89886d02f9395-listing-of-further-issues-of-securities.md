## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, including Axis Bank Limited, Borosil Renewables Limited, CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited, Hero MotoCorp Limited, Larsen & Toubro Limited, and others, effective from November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective November 02, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and preferential allotments.
- Trading in these securities will take place under designated security codes and specified market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions are executed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company between August 2026 and October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action resulting in an increase in the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed companies with standard market participation.
