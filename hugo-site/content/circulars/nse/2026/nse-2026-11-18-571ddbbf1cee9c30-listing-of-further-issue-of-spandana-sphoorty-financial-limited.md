## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of 1,43,442 equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited with effect from November 19, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- **Symbol:** SPANDANA
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE572J01011 (Temporary ISIN: IN8572J01010 for dematerialized shares)
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Number of Securities:** 1,43,442 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities are admitted to dealings in accordance with National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase and listing for Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited with no adverse or unusual market impact.
