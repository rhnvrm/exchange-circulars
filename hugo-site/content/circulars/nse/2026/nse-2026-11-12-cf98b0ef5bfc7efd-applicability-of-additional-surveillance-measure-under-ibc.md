## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) with effect from November 13, 2026. Teerth Gopicon Limited has been shortlisted under ASM IBC Stage I.

## Key Points

- Teerth Gopicon Limited (Symbol: TGL, ISIN: INE0K6601012) shortlisted in ASM IBC Stage I w.e.f. November 13, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes Impex Ferro Tech Limited (Stage II) and other companies subject to ASM IBC framework.
- Framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying the criteria under ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) are placed under surveillance stages effective November 13, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures applicable to the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Circular Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance and trading restrictions apply to shortlisted securities under ASM IBC framework.
