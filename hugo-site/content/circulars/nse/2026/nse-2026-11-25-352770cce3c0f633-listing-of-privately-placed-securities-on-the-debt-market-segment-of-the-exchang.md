## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective November 25, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Issuing Entities:** Kotak Securities Limited, CEAT Limited, Tata Capital Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, and Tata Communications Limited.
- **Instruments:** Commercial Papers (CPs) with various maturities ranging from December 2026 to May 2027 and face value of INR 500,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine listing notification under Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard depository and exchange regulations for debt market issuances and commercial paper listings applies.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Various dates between December 22, 2026 and May 21, 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of commercial papers with no direct impact on secondary equity markets or broader market stability.
