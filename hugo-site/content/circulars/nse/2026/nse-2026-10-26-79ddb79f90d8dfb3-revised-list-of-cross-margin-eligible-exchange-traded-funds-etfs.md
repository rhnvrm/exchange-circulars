## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margining benefits, superseding the previous circular issued on September 28, 2026. The revised list and minimum quantity requirements become effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the revised cross-margin eligible ETFs list is October 28, 2026.
- Supersedes circular no. 110/2026 (Download Reference No: NCL/CMPT/76595) dated September 28, 2026.
- Specifies minimum quantity requirements (in multiples thereon) for each eligible ETF symbol.
- Includes ETFs from various fund houses such as Nippon India, SBI Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential, HDFC Mutual Fund, Kotak Mahindra, UTI, Aditya Birla Sun Life, and Mirae Asset.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the list of approved securities eligible for cross-margining facility in the Futures & Options segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must take note of the revised minimum quantity requirements for cross-margining calculations effective October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects margin requirements and efficiency for market participants trading in cross-margin eligible ETFs.
