## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the extension of the allotment date for the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, following communication from Sundaram Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- The allotment date for Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund NFO is revised.
- Previous Allotment Date: November 6, 2026.
- Revised Allotment Date: November 9, 2026.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must have been received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 4, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; operational schedule modification for a specific mutual fund NFO allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors to take note of the revised allotment schedule and fund clearing timelines.

## Important Dates

- Revised Allotment Date: November 9, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 4, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on overall market operations; affects only investors and members participating in the specific Sundaram Mutual Fund NFO.
