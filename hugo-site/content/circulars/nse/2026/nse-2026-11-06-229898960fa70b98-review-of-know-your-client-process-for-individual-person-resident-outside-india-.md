## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI's review of the Know Your Client (KYC) process for individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) clients. SEBI has decided to relax the requirement that a client must be physically present in India during digital on-boarding, permitting digital submission of KYC information and documents for individual PROI clients located in Financial Action Task Force (FATF) compliant countries.

## Key Points

- Relaxes the extant requirement for digital KYC where the physical location of the client was mandated to be in India.
- Applies to individual Person Resident Outside India (PROI) clients, which include Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs), and foreign nationals.
- On-boarding intermediaries can accept digital submission of KYC information and supporting documents provided the client is located in a Financial Action Task Force (FATF) compliant country.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of SEBI's Master Circular on KYC requirements (SEBI/HO/MIRSD/SECFATF/P/CIR/2023/169 dated October 12, 2023).
- Removal of the restrictive physical location in India rule during digital KYC for eligible PROI clients.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and intermediaries must take note of the revised framework and ensure compliance while on-boarding individual PROI clients from FATF-compliant jurisdictions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Eases the digital on-boarding process for non-resident clients and foreign nationals, potentially boosting foreign participation by removing geographical presence barriers during KYC registration.
