## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the provision of additional short allocation reports (SA01/SA02/SA03) after market hours. These reports are based on snapshots in the segment aligned with the time of intraday snapshots in Commodity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Additional SA01/SA02/SA03 reports will be provided after-market hours.
- Reports are based on segment snapshots aligned with Commodity Derivatives segment intraday snapshot timings.
- Details include collateral value allocated by the clearing member to the client, securities repledged at Clearing Corporation, and EOD minimum margins.
- Used to arrive at excess collateral for the client in the segment towards short allocation in the Commodity derivatives segment.
- No change in the current manner of computation of peak margins during market hours and short allocation.
- File nomenclature, location, and format remain identical to existing intraday short allocation reports.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of additional after-market short allocation reports (SA01/SA02/SA03) for collaterals mapped to commodity derivatives snapshot timings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members should note the availability of additional short allocation reports and incorporate them into their risk and collateral monitoring workflows.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational enhancement for clearing members managing cross-segment margins and collaterals between F&O and Commodity Derivatives segments.
