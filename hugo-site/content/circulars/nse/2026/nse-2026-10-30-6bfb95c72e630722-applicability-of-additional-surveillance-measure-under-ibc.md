## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced updates regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for Companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective November 02, 2026. Securities meeting the criteria or exclusion requirements are detailed in the annexures.

## Key Points

- Securities moving to exclusion criteria under ASM for Companies as per IBC effective November 02, 2026.
- Affected securities include AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA), Setubandhan Infrastructure Limited (SETUINFRA), and Teerth Gopicon Limited (TGL).
- ASM IBC framework works in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) surveillance framework as referenced in earlier exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants must note the surveillance status changes for affected securities and ensure compliance with applicable price bands and trading norms.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading parameters and surveillance monitoring for the listed securities are adjusted in line with the IBC surveillance framework.
