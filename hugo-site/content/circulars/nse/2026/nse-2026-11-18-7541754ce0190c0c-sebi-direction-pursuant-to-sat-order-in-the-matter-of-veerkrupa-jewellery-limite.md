## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) issued orders on September 24, 2026, in appeals filed against SEBI's directions concerning Veerkrupa Jewellery Limited. In Appeal No. 255 of 2026 (Rakshit Mahendra Shah vs SEBI), SAT granted a stay on the impugned SEBI order subject to the deposit of the full penalty amount within four weeks. In Appeal No. 246 of 2026 (M/s. Vivid Mercantile Ltd. & Anr. vs SEBI), the tribunal admitted the appeal, allowed time for reply and rejoinder, and noted appellants' submissions regarding pre-IPO share purchases and subsequent holding periods.

## Key Points

- SAT admitted Appeal No. 255 of 2026 and Appeal No. 246 of 2026 relating to SEBI's orders in the matter of Veerkrupa Jewellery Limited.
- For Rakshit Mahendra Shah (Appeal No. 255), SAT granted a stay of the impugned SEBI order imposing a Rs. 10 Lakh penalty and debarment, subject to the deposit of the full penalty amount within four weeks in an interest-bearing account.
- For M/s. Vivid Mercantile Ltd. (Appeal No. 246), SAT admitted the appeal and set a schedule for reply (six weeks) and rejoinder (three weeks thereafter).
- Both appeals are posted for further hearing along with Appeal No. 181 of 2026 on November 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Interim stay granted by SAT on SEBI's penalty and debarment directives pending final hearing, subject to compliance with penalty deposit conditions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Appellant Rakshit Mahendra Shah must deposit the full penalty amount within four weeks from September 24, 2026, to maintain the stay.
- Respondent SEBI to file replies within six weeks, with rejoinders to be filed within three weeks thereafter.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 24, 2026
- Next Hearing Date: November 26, 2026 (along with Appeal No. 181 of 2026)
- Penalty Deposit Deadline: Within four weeks from September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides temporary relief (stay of penalty and debarment) to the appellants pending final adjudication by the Securities Appellate Tribunal.
