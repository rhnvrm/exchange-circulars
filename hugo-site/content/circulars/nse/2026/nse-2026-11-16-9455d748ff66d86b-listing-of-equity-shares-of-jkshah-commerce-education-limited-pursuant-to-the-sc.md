## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited effective from November 18, 2026, pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement approved by the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Chennai Bench.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited
- **Symbol:** JKSHAH
- **Security Description:** Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted pursuant to Scheme of Arrangement
- **ISIN:** INE2OD001016
- **Number of Securities:** 9,65,06,610
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 96506610
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Trading Series:** Series 'BE' (Trade for Trade), to be subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI circular no. SEBI/CIR/ISD/1/2010.
- **Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

## Regulatory & Scheme Details

The listing follows the NCLT Division Bench (Court I), Chennai order dated August 20, 2026, approving the Scheme of Arrangement involving Veranda Learning Solutions Limited, Veranda XL Learning Solutions Private Limited, and J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 16, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders and market participants can trade in the equity shares of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited under the 'BE' series starting November 18, 2026. The scrip will be part of the pre-open session.
