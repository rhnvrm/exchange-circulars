## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular informing members that the SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF managed by SBI Mutual Fund will be available for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription Window: Available from November 16, 2026, to November 30, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Covered Schemes: Includes 6 variants (Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Allotment Date: Scheduled for December 03, 2026 across all listed schemes.
- Fund Details:
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1CK4)
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Regular IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1CL2)
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Regular IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF200KB1CM0)
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1CN8)
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Direct IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1CO6)
  - SBI BSE India Defence ETF FOF - Direct IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF200KB1CP3)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational product availability notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through permitted payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 02, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: November 16, 2026
- Closing Date: November 30, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 02, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless investment access for market participants into the new defence sector-themed mutual fund schemes via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
