## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from November 19, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh issuance of Commercial Papers (CPs) admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective November 19, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Axis Securities Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, and Kotak Securities Limited.
- Face value per security is Rs. 500,000 with maturity dates ranging from December 2026 to May 2027.
- Date of allotment for all listed commercial papers is November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with operational guidelines and note the respective ISINs and security codes for debt market transactions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: November 18, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 19, 2026
- Redemption Dates: December 23, 2026 to May 19, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market dealings and liquidity for commercial paper issuances of participating financial and corporate entities.
