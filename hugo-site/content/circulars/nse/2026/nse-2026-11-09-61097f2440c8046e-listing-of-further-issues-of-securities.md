## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for Aimtron Electronics Limited and Kataria Industries Limited under the SME Emerge segment, effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Aimtron Electronics Limited**: 433,122 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under preferential issue at an issue price of Rs. 680.64 (Distinctive numbers: 20841358 to 21274479, Market Lot: 100). Specific lock-in expiry dates apply as per Annexure A.
- **Kataria Industries Limited**: 4,744,200 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted on a preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 106 (Distinctive numbers: 21531235 to 26275434, Market Lot: 600, Lock-in expiry: May 15, 2027).
- **Effective Date**: Securities will be admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment (SME Emerge) effective November 11, 2026.
- **Temporary ISIN**: Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode are initially credited under temporary ISINs as per applicable SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action circular regarding the listing of further securities under NSE trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trading in these securities is conducted using the designated security codes and respective lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 09, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 11, 2026
- **Aimtron Allotment Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Kataria Allotment Date**: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and trading of newly allotted preferential shares for Aimtron Electronics Limited and Kataria Industries Limited on the exchange.
