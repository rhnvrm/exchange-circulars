## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for four entities, including NHPC Limited, NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited, IIFL Samasta Finance Limited, and Bank of Baroda, effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension applies to Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.
- Affected entities and ISINs:
  - NHPC Limited (INE848E07757)
  - NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited (INE246R07541)
  - IIFL Samasta Finance Limited (INE413U07210)
  - Bank Of Baroda (INE028A08265)
- Reason for suspension: Redemption and redemption for exercising Call Option.
- Effective suspension date: November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an administrative suspension execution under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants are advised to ensure no further trades are executed in the suspended ISINs on and after the suspension date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Secondary market trading in the specified non-convertible securities will be halted following their redemption or call option exercise.
