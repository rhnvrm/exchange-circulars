## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that quant Silver ETF FOF of Quant Mutual Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Fund House:** Quant Mutual Fund
- **Schemes Included:** Direct and Regular plans (Growth and IDCW options) for quant Silver ETF FOF.
- **ISINs Covered:** INF966L01FI2, INF966L01FJ0, INF966L01FH4, INF966L01FL6, INF966L01FM4, INF966L01FK8

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine platform availability notification for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the specified schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading, investment, and systematic transactions (SIP/STP/SWP) in quant Silver ETF FOF through NSE infrastructure.
