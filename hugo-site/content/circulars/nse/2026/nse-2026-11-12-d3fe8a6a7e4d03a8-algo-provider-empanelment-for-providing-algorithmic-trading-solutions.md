## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the empanelment of M/s Ai4Trades LLP as an approved vendor for providing algorithmic trading solutions to trading members.

## Key Points

- Empaneled Vendor: M/s Ai4Trades LLP
- Type of Algo: Whitebox
- Correspondence Address: A3 Orchid Bungalows, Thaltej Shilaj Road, Nr Baghban Party Plot, Thaltej, Ahmedabad – 380059, Gujarat
- Contact Details: Phone: +91 7600001212

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is an administrative empanelment notice under the Investigation Department.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members engaging with the empaneled vendor must ensure adherence to exchange guidelines concerning algorithmic trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 12, 2026 (Applicable with immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members looking to deploy whitebox algorithmic solutions can utilize the services of the newly empaneled vendor, subject to exchange compliance norms.
