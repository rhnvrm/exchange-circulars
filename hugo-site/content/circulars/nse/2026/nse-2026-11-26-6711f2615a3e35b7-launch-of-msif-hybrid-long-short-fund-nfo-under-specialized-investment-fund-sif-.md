## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the launch of MSIF Hybrid Long Short Fund NFO under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Subscription Period:** November 27, 2026 to December 11, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Allotment Date:** December 17, 2026.
- **Fund House:** Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund.
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 17, 2026 till 10:00 am.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** November 27, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** December 11, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** December 17, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** December 17, 2026 (10:00 am)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and subscription of the new fund offer schemes across regular and direct plans on the NSE MF platform.
