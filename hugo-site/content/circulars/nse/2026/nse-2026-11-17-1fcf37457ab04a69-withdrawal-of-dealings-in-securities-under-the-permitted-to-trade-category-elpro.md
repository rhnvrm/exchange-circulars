## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the withdrawal of dealings in securities of Elpro International Limited under the Permitted to Trade (PTT) category with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Elpro International Limited
- Symbol: ELPROINTL
- ISIN: INE579B01039
- Category: Permitted to Trade (PTT)
- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Dealings in securities of Elpro International Limited under the Permitted to Trade category have been withdrawn with immediate effect.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the withdrawal of dealings and ensure no further trades are executed under this category.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can no longer trade in Elpro International Limited under the Permitted to Trade category on the NSE platform.
