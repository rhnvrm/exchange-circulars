## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) is conducting a mock session on November 21, 2026, to test the Software as a Service (SaaS) model for two-way portability across Clearing Corporations, in compliance with SEBI guidelines for business continuity.

## Key Points

- Mock session scheduled for November 21, 2026, to test SaaS setup and interfaces.
- NCL will invoke its SaaS setup between 14:30 hrs and 15:00 hrs.
- NCL will be operational using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) between 15:15 hrs and 16:00 hrs.
- Members should use existing login credentials or request credentials via support@nsccl.co.in.
- Links provided for RTRMS, Collateral, and Extranet access.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; conducted pursuant to SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to actively participate, test all functionalities associated with the SaaS setup, and be guided by NCL circular no 025/2023 dated April 13, 2023.

## Important Dates

- Mock Session Date: November 21, 2026
- SaaS Setup Invocation: 14:30 hrs – 15:00 hrs (November 21, 2026)
- SaaS Operational Window (NCL@ICCL): 15:15 hrs – 16:00 hrs (November 21, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness test for members to familiarize themselves with business continuity and interoperability features across clearing corporations.
