## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. November 19, 2026: Aatmaj Healthcare Limited (Symbol: AATMAJ, ISIN: INE0OB201016).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 23, 2026, on all open positions as on November 20, 2026, and new positions created from November 23, 2026, onwards.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisting of scrips under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Framework Stage I with strict margin provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure a 100% margin requirement is maintained on all open positions as of November 20, 2026, and on new positions created from November 23, 2026, onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Stage I: November 19, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date for Margin: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in AATMAJ will be subject to heightened surveillance and 100% margin requirements, impacting leverage and liquidity for market participants.
