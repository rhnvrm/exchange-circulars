## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued guidelines pursuant to amendments in SEBI KYC (Know Your Client) Registration Agency (KRA) Regulations, 2011, and relevant SEBI circulars. Trading members are directed to ensure all client KYCs are uploaded and validated, and to restrict trading for non-compliant PANs and deceased investors effective November 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- PANs uploaded till October 31, 2026 with Primary KYC status "On-Hold" are restricted from trading or squaring off open positions effective November 21, 2026.
- Exchange provides a file `<TMcode>_KRA_NOT_AVAILABLE` for PANs not uploaded by members.
- Exchange provides a list of demised clients (`<TMcode>_Demise_Clients_by_KRA`), requiring members to mark corresponding UCCs as "Closed" after due diligence before November 21, 2026.
- Compliant PANs subsequently updated will be permitted to trade on T+1 based on KRA information.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of strict KYC compliance via KRAs, including handling of "On-Hold", "Not Available", and "Demise" statuses in coordination with SEBI's centralized mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review KRA status reports available on ENIT (ENIT > Log > Downloads).
- Members must block debit transactions and mark UCCs as "Closed" for demised investors.
- Members must ensure clients hold "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status to permit trading.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading Restrictions & UCC Closures: November 21, 2026
- Cut-off for PAN uploads/status check: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must immediately act on KRA reports to prevent regulatory penalties and ensure affected clients regularize their KYC status prior to the November 21, 2026 deadline.
