## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Zerodha CASE Asset Management Private Limited under the scheme Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF, effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF
- Symbol: BANKCASE
- ISIN: INF0R8F01265
- Number of Units: 527,760
- Series: EQ
- Date of Allotment: October 29, 2026
- Face Value and Paid Up Value: Rs. 100/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 99.99
- Market Lot: 1
- Market Makers: East India Securities Limited, Kanjolachana Finserve Private Limited, Parwati Capital Market Private Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, iRage Broking Services LLP, Fyres Securities Private Limited, and Dolat Algotech Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard listing circular in pursuance of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adherence to designated security codes and lot sizes for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 29, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading access for market participants in the newly listed Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF.
