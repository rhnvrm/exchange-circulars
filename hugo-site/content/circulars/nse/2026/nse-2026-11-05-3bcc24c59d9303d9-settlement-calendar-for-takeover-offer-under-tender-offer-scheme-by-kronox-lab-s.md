## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and instructions for the Takeover Offer under the Tender Offer scheme of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited. The settlement period spans from trade start date November 6, 2026, to settlement date December 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X
- Settlement Number: 2026135
- Trade Start Date: 06-NOV-2026
- Trade End Date: 20-NOV-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 04-DEC-2026
- Settlement Date: 07-DEC-2026
- Demat shares must be tendered via Early Pay-in of Shares for Market Type Buyback mechanism.
- Annexure I outlines rejection codes for partial or non-acceptance of orders (e.g., joint holder not signed, signature differences, invalid transfer deeds).

## Regulatory Changes

Operational settlement schedule issued in accordance with circular NCL/CMPT/73996 and NSE/CML/77308 regarding the takeover offer scheme of Kronox Lab Sciences Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Early pay-in of shares must be executed well in advance on the trade end date for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post receipt of funds pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 5, 2026
- Trade Start Date: November 6, 2026
- Trade End Date: November 20, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: December 4, 2026
- Settlement Date: December 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides clear operational timelines and rejection guidelines for clearing members, custodians, and trading members participating in the Kronox Lab Sciences Limited takeover tender offer.
