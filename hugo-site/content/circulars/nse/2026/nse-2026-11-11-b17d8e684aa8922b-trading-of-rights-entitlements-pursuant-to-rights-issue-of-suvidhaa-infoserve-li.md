## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited (Symbol: SUVID-RE, ISIN: INE018420013). Trading will commence on November 13, 2026, in the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited
- Symbol: SUVID-RE (Display Name: Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited-RE)
- ISIN: INE018420013
- Series: BE
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 78,638,771
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 13, 2026 to November 16, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 17, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine operational circular notifying admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements pursuant to a rights issue under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must include a prominently displayed disclaimer in contract notes/bills for purchase of REs, informing clients that purchase gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce before closing.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: November 13, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 13, 2026 to November 16, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade Rights Entitlements of Suvidhaa Infoserve Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment during the specified renunciation window, facilitating participation in the rights issue or monetization of entitlements.
