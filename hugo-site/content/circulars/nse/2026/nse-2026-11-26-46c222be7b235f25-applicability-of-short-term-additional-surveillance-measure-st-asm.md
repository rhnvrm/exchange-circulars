## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I based on market surveillance criteria.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 30, 2026, on all open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.
- Shortlisted stocks include Greaves Cotton Limited (GREAVESCOT), North Eastern Carrying Corporation Limited (NECCLTD), Paramatrix Technologies Limited (PARAMATRIX), Thomas Cook (India) Limited (THOMASCOOK), Urban Enviro Waste Management Limited (URBAN), Vertoz Limited (VERTOZ), and Vedant Poly Fabs Limited (VPFL).
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance framework and associated margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with the 50% (or existing, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) margin requirement on open positions as on November 27, 2026, and new positions created from November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026 (open positions as on November 27, 2026 and new positions).

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
