## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies under the SME Emerge segment, effective from November 25, 2026. The companies include Pritika Engineering Components Limited, Sheetal Universal Limited, Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited, and Urban Enviro Waste Management Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026.
- **Segment:** National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE.
- **Companies Involved:**
  - Pritika Engineering Components Limited (Symbol: PRITIKA)
  - Sheetal Universal Limited (Symbol: SHEETAL)
  - Silkflex Polymers (India) Limited (Symbol: SILKFLEX)
  - Urban Enviro Waste Management Limited

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings and designated lot sizes are specified.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in such securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure, subject to lock-in details and pari passu terms.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Pritika Engineering Allotment Date:** 17-Oct-2026
- **Sheetal Universal Allotment Date:** 10-Oct-2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of newly allotted preferential shares on the SME Emerge platform with specific lock-in periods and market lots.
