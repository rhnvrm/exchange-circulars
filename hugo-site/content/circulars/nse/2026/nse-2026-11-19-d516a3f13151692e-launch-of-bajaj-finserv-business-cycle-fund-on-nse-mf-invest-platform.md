## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the Bajaj Finserv Business Cycle Fund of Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund will be available for subscription and transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from November 20, 2026, to December 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Availability: Bajaj Finserv Business Cycle Fund (Direct and Regular plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- Subscription Window: November 20, 2026, to December 04, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- ISINs Issued: INF0QA701CP5, INF0QA701CQ3, INF0QA701CO8, INF0QA701CM2, INF0QA701CN0, INF0QA701CL4.
- Allotment Date: December 11, 2026.
- Asset Category: Equity.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 10, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- Subscription Start Date: November 20, 2026
- Subscription End Date: December 04, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: December 10, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: December 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and investors seamless access to subscribe to the newly launched equity mutual fund scheme via the NSE MF platform.
