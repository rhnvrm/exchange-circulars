## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Agi Infra Limited (AGIIL), Borosil Limited (BOROLTD), Durlax Top Surface Limited (DURLAX), Innovision Limited (INNOVISION), Marsons Limited (MARSONS), Sotac Pharmaceuticals Limited (SOTAC), Teamtech Formwork Solutions Limited (TEAMTECH), and Yaap Digital Limited (YAAP).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Surveillance action applies to all open positions as on November 04, 2026, and new positions created from November 05, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions on listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements on applicable securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 05, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 04, 2026
- Circular Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Traders holding positions in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%), impacting leverage and capital allocation.
