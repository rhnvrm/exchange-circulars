## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited (TFCILTD) has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I effective November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited (Symbol: TFCILTD, ISIN: INE305A01023).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities satisfying the specified criteria are subjected to the Long-Term ASM framework, restricting leverage through 100% margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure that 100% margin is maintained on open positions and new positions in the affected security as per the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 03, 2026
- Inclusion Date under Long-Term ASM Stage I: November 04, 2026
- 100% Margin Effective Date: November 06, 2026 (on open positions as on November 05, 2026 and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in TFCILTD will require full 100% margins, impacting liquidity and capital requirements for market participants holding positions in the security.
