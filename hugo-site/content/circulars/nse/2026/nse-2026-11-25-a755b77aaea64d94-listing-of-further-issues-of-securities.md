## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of securities for various companies, effective from November 26, 2026, pursuant to ESOP allotments.

## Key Points

- Admission of further equity shares to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
- Effective date of listing is November 26, 2026.
- Covers 20 companies including Aether Industries, Gland Pharma, Tech Mahindra, Wipro, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading must be conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via ESOP issuance with minimal market impact.
