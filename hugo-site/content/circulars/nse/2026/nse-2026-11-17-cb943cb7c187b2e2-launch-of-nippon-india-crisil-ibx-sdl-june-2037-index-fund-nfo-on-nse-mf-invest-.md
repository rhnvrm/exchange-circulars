## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Nippon India CRISIL - IBX SDL - June 2037 Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from November 18, 2026, to November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: Nippon India CRISIL - IBX SDL - June 2037 Index Fund Direct Growth Plan (ISIN: INF204KC1IA7) and Regular Growth Plan (ISIN: INF204KC1HZ6).
- Asset Category: Debt (SDL Index Fund).
- NFO Transaction Window: November 18, 2026 to November 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Allotment Date: December 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any permitted payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 27, 2026, till 10:00 AM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: November 18, 2026
- NFO End Date: November 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 27, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- Allotment Date: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the Nippon India CRISIL - IBX SDL - June 2037 Index Fund NFO through the NSE MF Invest platform.
