## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the revision of Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), Trading Member-wise position limits, FII/FPI (Category I & II), and Mutual Fund position limits for Tech Mahindra Limited (TECHM), effective November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** TECHM (Tech Mahindra Limited)
- **Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL):** 191,207,586 shares
- **Overall Stock Limit for Client:** 19,119,600 shares
- **Overall Stock Limit for Proprietary:** 38,240,400 shares
- **Overall Stock Limit for TM (Client + Proprietary), FII (Category I) & MF:** 57,361,200 shares
- **FPI Category II (other than individuals, family offices and corporates):** 38,240,400 shares
- **FPI Category II (individuals, family offices and corporates):** 19,119,600 shares

## Regulatory Changes

These revisions are made in pursuance of circular no. 050/2026 (NCL/CMPT/73997) dated April 30, 2026, and in partial modification of circular no. 108/2026 (NCL/CMPT/76459) dated September 21, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, FIIs/FPIs, and mutual funds must ensure that their aggregate and individual derivative positions in Tech Mahindra Limited comply with the newly specified share limits to prevent position violations.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and institutional investors dealing in Tech Mahindra Limited derivatives must update their compliance and risk management systems to reflect the revised position caps.
