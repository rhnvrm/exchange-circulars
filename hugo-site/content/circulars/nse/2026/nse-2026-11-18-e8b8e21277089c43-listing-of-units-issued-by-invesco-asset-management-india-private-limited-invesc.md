## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Invesco Asset Management (India) Private Limited under the Invesco India Silver ETF starting November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: IVZSILVER
- ISIN: INF205KA1CP9
- Number of securities: 7,917,815
- Face Value & Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 16.99/-
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine admission to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security code (IVZSILVER) and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 18, 2026
- Date of Allotment: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Invesco India Silver ETF units on the NSE capital market segment, providing investors with liquidity and investment opportunities in silver.
