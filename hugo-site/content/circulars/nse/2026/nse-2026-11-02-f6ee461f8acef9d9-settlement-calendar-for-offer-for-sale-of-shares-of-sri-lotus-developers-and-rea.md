## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/77247, Circular Ref. No: 0380/2026). The circular outlines key dates, margin categories, and settlement procedures for non-retail and retail investors.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar covers Offer for Sale for Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited.
- Separate schedules provided for non-retail (IS) and retail (RS) categories across November 3, 2026, and November 4, 2026.
- Funds Pay-in is scheduled for T+1 day by 11:00 am.
- Securities & Funds Pay-out is scheduled for T+1 day by 01:30 pm.
- Applicable securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; settlements follow existing secondary market rules and circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Members, custodians, and participating clients must adhere to the specified order dates, custodial confirmation times, margin categories, and pay-in/pay-out timelines.

## Important Dates

- **03-NOV-2026:** Order date and custodial confirmation for initial non-retail tranche (Settlement No. 2026207).
- **04-NOV-2026:** Settlement date for 2026207, and order date for retail/non-retail tranche (Settlement No. 2026208).
- **05-NOV-2026:** Settlement date for Settlement No. 2026208.
- **T+1 day by 11:00 am:** Funds Pay-in.
- **T+1 day by 01:30 pm:** Securities & Funds Pay-out.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants engaging in the Offer for Sale of Sri Lotus Developers and Realty Limited.
