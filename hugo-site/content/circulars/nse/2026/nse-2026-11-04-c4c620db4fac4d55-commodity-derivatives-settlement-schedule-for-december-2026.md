## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement schedule for the Commodity Derivatives segment for the month of December 2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/COM/77275, Circular Ref. No: 0384/2026).

## Key Points

- Outlines daily trade dates and corresponding settlement dates for December 2026.
- Covers trade dates starting from December 1, 2026, through December 31, 2026.
- Final settlement date for the month is January 1, 2027.
