## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited on the capital market segment, effective from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited
- **Security Symbol:** 870TCHFL36
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** TCHFL 8.70% 2036 Sr F
- **Coupon Rate:** 8.7%
- **Date of Maturity:** 04-Nov-2036
- **ISIN Code:** INE033L07IX2

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings with designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified solely by their designated codes and executed in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** November 04, 2036

## Impact Assessment

This is a routine listing of privately placed debt securities, enabling secondary market trading for eligible investors with minimal impact on overall market operations.
