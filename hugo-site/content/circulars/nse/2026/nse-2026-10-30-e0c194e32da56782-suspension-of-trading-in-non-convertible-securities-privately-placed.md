## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, the NSE has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Limited and Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited.

## Key Points

- **Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Limited**: ISIN INE995S08010 suspended from trading effective November 02, 2026, due to redemption for exercising call option.
- **Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited**: ISIN INE053F09EL2 suspended from trading effective November 02, 2026, due to redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; routine enforcement of debt market trading regulations regarding security suspension upon redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant issuers and market participants must take note of the trading suspension in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date**: November 02, 2026
- **Circular Date**: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities will be halted on the NSE debt segment from the effective suspension date following redemption.
