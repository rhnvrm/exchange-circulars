## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies, effective from November 04, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Anthem Biosciences Limited**: 135,925 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (Allotment Date: 22-Oct-2026, ISIN: INE0CZ201020).
- **Computer Age Management Services Limited**: 7,130 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (Allotment Date: 27-Oct-2026, ISIN: INE596I01020).
- **Cartrade Tech Limited**: 1,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (Allotment Date: 28-Oct-2026, ISIN: INE290S01011).
- **Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited**: Equity shares allotted pursuant to conversion of optional/compulsory securities (ISIN: INE121A01024, Issue Price: Rs. 1407.59).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate listing notice under existing trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the listed securities shall be identified by their designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and inclusion of shares issued via ESOP and conversions into trading segments with minimal direct market impact.
