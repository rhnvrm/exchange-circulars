## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Jio Platforms Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment, effective from October 28, 2026. The security will be identified by symbol JPL and will form part of Group 1 securities.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jio Platforms Limited
- **Symbol:** JPL
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE0BJO01010
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1119/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities Admitted:** 9,20,90,30,830 equity shares
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Group:** Group 1 securities
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5.
- Security will be part of pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the designated security codes and trading lot sizes specified in the circular.
- Adherence to pre-open session guidelines for IPO category scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** October 28, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Various tranches with lock-in expiration dates ranging from April 26, 2027 to October 26, 2029 (and certain shares free).

## Impact Assessment

- Major capital market listing providing liquidity and public trading access to Jio Platforms Limited shares, impacting index weights and sectoral trading volumes.
