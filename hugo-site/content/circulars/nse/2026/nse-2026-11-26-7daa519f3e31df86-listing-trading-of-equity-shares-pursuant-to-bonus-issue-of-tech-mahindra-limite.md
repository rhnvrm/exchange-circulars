## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Tech Mahindra Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. The shares will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from December 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tech Mahindra Limited
- Symbol: TECHM
- ISIN: INE669C01036
- Record Date: November 30, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: December 01, 2026
- Number of Securities: 98,06,55,356
- Distinctive Number Range: 1001240357 to 1981895712

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action listing pursuant to regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the designated security codes are used for trading and identification purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 30, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: December 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing & Trading: December 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Bonus issue results in an increase in the number of equity shares of Tech Mahindra Limited, enhancing liquidity in the market.
