## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for securities that have satisfied the prescribed criteria. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I: Apex Ecotech Limited (APEXECO), Cadsys (India) Limited (CADSYS), and Q-Line Biotech Limited (QLINE).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. November 25, 2026, on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created from November 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I framework for identified securities under market surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected scrips effective November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Framework Date: November 23, 2026
- Margin applicability date: November 25, 2026 (on open positions as on November 23, 2026 and new positions created from November 25, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in APEXECO, CADSYS, and QLINE may affect liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
