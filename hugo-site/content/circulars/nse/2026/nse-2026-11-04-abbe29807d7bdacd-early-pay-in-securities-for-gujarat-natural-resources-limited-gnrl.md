## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Gujarat Natural Resources Limited (GNRL) on account of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs. 10/- per share to Re. 1/- per share. Members are advised to execute early pay-in in the existing/old ISIN (INE207H01018) for specific settlement dates surrounding the record date and ex-date of November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Gujarat Natural Resources Limited (GNRL)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per share
- Old/Existing ISIN: INE207H01018
- Record Date / Ex Date: November 05, 2026
- Applicable EPI Dates and Settlements:
  - November 05, 2026: Settlement No. 2026209 (Old ISIN)
  - November 06, 2026: Settlement Nos. 2026209 & 2026210 (Old ISIN)

## Regulatory Changes

Operational adjustments and clearing procedures for processing early pay-in during corporate actions involving face value splits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for trade dates corresponding to the ex-date/record date without adjusting for conversion.
- For early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files must not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through pool accounts must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantities to pass benefits to clients.
- Pay-in and early pay-in instructions in the New ISIN must be executed after the record date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: November 05, 2026
- Early Pay-in Dates: November 05, 2026 and November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and correct ISIN handling during corporate action (face value split) for market participants and custodians dealing in GNRL shares.
