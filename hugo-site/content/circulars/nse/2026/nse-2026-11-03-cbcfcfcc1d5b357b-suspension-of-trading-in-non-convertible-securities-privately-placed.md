## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, NSE has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities of NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited
- ISIN: INE246R07525
- Suspension Date: November 04, 2026
- Reason: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; executed under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is limited to holders of the specific NCDs due to redemption, with no broader market impact.
