## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the reversal of action initiated against BKM Industries Limited (Symbol: BKMINDST) after the company cleared its pending Annual Listing Fees (ALF) dues for the Financial Year ended 2023-24.

## Key Points

- BKM Industries Limited cleared its pending Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for FY 2023-24.
- Previous restricted trading action notified via Circular No. NSE/CML/62205 dated May 27, 2024, has been reversed.
- Trading in the securities of the company shall nevertheless remain suspended as per Exchange Circular No. NSE/CML/65272 dated November 27, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reversal of restricted trading action previously imposed for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees for FY 2023-24.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company has fulfilled its pending ALF compliance for the specified financial year; however, separate suspension orders remain in effect.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 03, 2026
- Continuation Circular (Restricted Trading): May 27, 2024 (NSE/CML/62205)
- Suspension Circular: November 27, 2024 (NSE/CML/65272)

## Impact Assessment

- Limited direct market impact as trading in the company's securities continues to remain suspended under a separate circular.
