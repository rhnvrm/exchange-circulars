## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks, specifically detailing equity shares that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets and collateral across segments.

## Key Points

- Lists approved equity shares along with their overall permitted quantities and memberwise permitted quantities.
- Applicable haircut for listed securities is based on Value at Risk (VAR).
- Value-based prudential norms specify that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic update to the list of eligible securities accepted as collateral and non-cash liquid assets by clearing corporations.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure that collateral maintenance and margin shortfalls comply with the revised permitted quantities and prudential exclusion norms.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Impacts clearing members and trading members utilizing non-cash collateral and liquid asset components for margin requirements.
