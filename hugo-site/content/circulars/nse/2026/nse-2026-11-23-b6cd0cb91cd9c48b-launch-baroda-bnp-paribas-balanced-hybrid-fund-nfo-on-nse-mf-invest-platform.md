## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of the Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be open for transactions from November 25, 2026, to December 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Hybrid Fund
- Available Plans: Regular and Direct options covering Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment.
- Allotment Date: December 14, 2026 across all ISINs.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 11, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 25, 2026 (up to 11:59 PM)
- NFO Close Date: December 08, 2026 (up to 11:59 PM)
- Fund Pay-in Deadline: December 11, 2026 (up to 03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to transact seamlessly in the new hybrid fund scheme through the NSE mutual fund infrastructure.
