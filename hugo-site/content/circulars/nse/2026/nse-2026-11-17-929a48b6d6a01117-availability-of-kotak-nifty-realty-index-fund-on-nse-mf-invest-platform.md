## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund under Kotak Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name**: Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund
- **Fund House**: Kotak Mutual Fund
- **Effective Date**: November 18, 2026
- **Platform**: NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Available Facilities**: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- **Schemes & ISINs**:
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Regular Plan - Growth (INF174KA1H55, Equity)
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1H71, Equity)
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Direct Plan - Growth (INF174KA1H22, Equity)
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (INF174KA1H48, Equity)
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (INF174KA1H63, Equity)
  - Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (INF174KA1H30, Equity)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard platform availability circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the availability of these schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 17, 2026
- **Effective Date**: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment options for investors on the NSE MF Invest Platform in the realty sector index fund category.
