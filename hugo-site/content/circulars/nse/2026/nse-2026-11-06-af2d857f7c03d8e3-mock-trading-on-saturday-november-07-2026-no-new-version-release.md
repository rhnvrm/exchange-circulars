## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, November 07, 2026, from the primary site. No new version release is scheduled for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: November 07, 2026.
- Normal Market open time: 11:00 Hrs; close time: 12:30 Hrs.
- Trade Modification end time: 12:40 Hrs.
- Live Re-login start time: 14:00 Hrs; close time: 14:30 Hrs.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 3.5.4.
- Reminder for NNF members to migrate to the new encryption mechanism with MD5 checksum removal or immediate acknowledgement before December 07, 2026.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members must manually clear orders in NNF software.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates prior directives regarding the discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10885) effective December 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, November 09, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- NNF members must migrate to new gateway ports (e.g., 10889, 10870, 10871, 10891) in compliance with circular NSE/MSD/76883.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: November 07, 2026
- Live Re-login Timings: 14:00 Hrs to 14:30 Hrs on November 07, 2026
- Encryption Migration Deadline: December 07, 2026
- Normal Live Operations: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational testing and validation for trading members in the Currency Derivatives segment, ensuring system readiness and connectivity prior to regular trading days.
