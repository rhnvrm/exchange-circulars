## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the ISIN code for the equity shares of Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW) effective from November 06, 2026, following a stock sub-division.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited
- Symbol: KMEW
- New ISIN: INE0CJD01037
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 5/- to Re. 1/-
- Effective Date (Ex-date): November 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update reflecting a corporate action (stock sub-division).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for all trades executed on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date / Ex-date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard ISIN update corresponding to equity share sub-division.
