## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective November 03, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh issues of commercial papers include issuances by Aditya Birla Money Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, and Aditya Birla Capital Limited.
- Re-issues of commercial papers include issuances by L&T Finance Limited, Tata Steel Limited, and Aditya Birla Capital Limited.
- All securities specified in the Annexure have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine administrative listing notification under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must ensure ongoing compliance with depository, listing, and exchange regulations for debt market segment operations.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: November 03, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Varies by issuance, ranging from November 30, 2026, to March 15, 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations facilitating trading and liquidity for commercial papers on the debt market segment.
