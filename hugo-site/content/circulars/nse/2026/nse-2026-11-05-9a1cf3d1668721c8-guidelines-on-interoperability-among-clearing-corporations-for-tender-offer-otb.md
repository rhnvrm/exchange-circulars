## Summary

NSE has issued guidelines and report specifications regarding interoperability among clearing corporations for Tender Offer (OTB). Annexure I outlines the comprehensive list of operational reports, file naming conventions, and record structures required for trading and clearing members.

## Key Points

- Details 13 specific reports for Tender Offer (OTB), including successful/failed direct client funds payouts, client allocation details, securities payout and acceptance reports, STT reports, demat delivery/receipt statements, provisional and final funds payouts, trade reports, and stamp duty reports.
- Defines precise naming conventions for trading and clearing member files (e.g., Tender Offer_T_<TMCODE>_DIRECTPO_S_DDMMYYYY, C_CADT_memcode__X_<settno>DDMMYYYY.csv).
- Specifies mandatory header types, segment indicators (e.g., C), order numbers, TM codes, transaction descriptions, UCC, bank details, payout amounts, and field widths.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardization of report formats and statements across clearing corporations to support the interoperability framework for tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading and clearing members must configure their systems to process and generate the standardized OTB report formats and adhere to the prescribed naming conventions.

## Important Dates

- Refer to the primary circular for effective dates and operational rollout schedules.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency, reconciliation accuracy, and seamless processing of tender offers across multiple clearing corporations.
