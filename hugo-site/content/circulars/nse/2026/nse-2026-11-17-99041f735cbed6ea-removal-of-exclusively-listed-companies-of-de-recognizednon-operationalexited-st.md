## Summary

Pursuant to SEBI circulars and Master Circular guidelines, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the removal of Darshan Housing and Infrastructure Limited from its Dissemination Board (DB).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Darshan Housing and Infrastructure Limited
- Effective Date of Removal: November 19, 2026
- Reason for Removal: Exit Option
- Applicable Frameworks: SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2016/110, SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2017/92, and Chapter 6 of Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD2/PoD-2/CIR/P/2023/171.

## Regulatory Changes

Facilitates the removal of exclusively listed companies of de-recognized, non-operational, or exited stock exchanges from the Dissemination Board in accordance with established SEBI exit norms.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants are advised to note that the company will not be available on the DB of NSE effective November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is restricted to the specific company being removed from the Dissemination Board and its registered stakeholders.
