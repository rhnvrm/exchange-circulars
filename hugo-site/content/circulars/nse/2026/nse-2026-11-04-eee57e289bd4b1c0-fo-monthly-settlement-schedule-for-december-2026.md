## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the Futures & Options (F&O) segment monthly settlement schedule for December 2026 via Circular Ref. No: 125/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/77279), dated November 04, 2026. The schedule outlines the trade dates and their corresponding settlement dates for the entire month.

## Key Points

- Covers all trading days in the F&O segment for December 2026.
- Maps each trade date to its respective settlement date, starting from trade date December 01, 2026, settling on December 02, 2026, up to the final trade date of December 31, 2026, settling on January 01, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine monthly operational schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the settlement schedule and manage their fund and securities pay-in/pay-out obligations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **First Trade Date of Month:** 01-Dec-2026 (Settlement: 02-Dec-2026)
- **Last Trade Date of Month:** 31-Dec-2026 (Settlement: 01-Jan-2027)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations by providing advance notice of settlement cycles for the F&O segment during December 2026.
