## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for several listed companies, including Coforge Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Manaksia Coated Metals & Industries Limited, Max Estates Limited, Meesho Limited, MphasiS Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, Strides Pharma Science Limited, Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited, TTK Prestige Limited, Viyash Scientific Limited, VA Tech Wabag Limited, and Wipro Limited, effective from November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective November 12, 2026.
- Shares issued and allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).
- Trading symbols, security codes, distinctive number ranges, and lot sizes are governed as per the exchange annexure.
- All newly allotted shares rank pari passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action listing under NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions in these securities are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital for the respective companies with negligible direct systemic impact.
