## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has provided functionality on the ENIT portal for trading members to submit Action Taken Reports (ATR) along with the status of closure of observations reported during inspections of Authorised Persons (APs), establishing clear quarterly submission timelines.

## Key Points

- Functionality is live on the ENIT portal for reporting ATR for AP inspections.
- Standardized quarterly submission timelines for AP inspection reports and corresponding ATRs have been established.
- ATR for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, is due by December 31, 2026.
- Members undertaking no inspections in a given quarter must submit a "NIL" declaration via the ENIT portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formalizes the requirement and workflow for submitting Action Taken Reports (ATR) on AP inspection observations through the ENIT portal.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit ATR and observation closure status within the stipulated quarterly timelines.
- Submit "NIL" declarations if no AP inspections were conducted during a quarter.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2026**: Due date for ATR for Q1 inspections (April-June).
- **December 31, 2026**: Due date for ATR for Q2 inspections (July-September).
- **March 31**: Due date for ATR for Q3 inspections (October-December).
- **June 30**: Due date for ATR for Q4 inspections (January-March).

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines compliance reporting and monitoring of Authorised Persons by trading members through automated ENIT portal workflows and clearly defined deadlines.
