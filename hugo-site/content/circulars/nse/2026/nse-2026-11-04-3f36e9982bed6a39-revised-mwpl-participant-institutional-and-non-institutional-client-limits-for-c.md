## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision to the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) and position limits applicable for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional Clients for CONCORDBIO under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme, effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: CONCORDBIO
- Old MWPL: 5,849,409 shares
- New MWPL: 11,698,819 shares
- Old Institutional/Participant Limit: 584,940 shares
- New Institutional/Participant Limit: 1,169,881 shares
- Old Non-Institutional Client Limit: 58,494 shares
- New Non-Institutional Client Limit: 116,988 shares

## Regulatory Changes

The position limits in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for CONCORDBIO have been revised in pursuance of earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 and NCL/CMPT/77220).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised limits and ensure compliance while executing trades and managing positions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The upward revision of position limits allows higher participation and liquidity for CONCORDBIO in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.
