## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of equity shares of several Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Securities transferred from trade-for-trade segment (BE) to rolling segment (EQ):
  - Maharashtra Scooters Limited (MAHSCOOTER) - Effective Date: 17-Nov-2026
  - TVS Holdings Limited (TVSHLTD) - Effective Date: 17-Nov-2026
  - GFL Limited (GFLLIMITED) - Effective Date: 18-Nov-2026
  - Nalwa Sons Investments Limited (NSIL) - Effective Date: 18-Nov-2026
  - Pilani Investment and Industries Corporation Limited (PILANIINVS) - Effective Date: 18-Nov-2026
  - SIL Investments Limited (SILINV) - Effective Date: 20-Nov-2026
  - Kalyani Investment Company Limited (KICL) - Effective Date: 25-Nov-2026
- Securities continuing in trade for trade segment (BE) and under call auction:
  - Williamson Magor & Company Limited (WILLAMAGOR) will continue in the trade-for-trade segment until further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, and Exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76935 dated October 15, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems to reflect the change in series for the specified securities from their respective effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective dates for transition to rolling segment: November 17, 2026, to November 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Transitioning to the rolling segment (EQ) enables normal rolling settlement for these equity shares, improving liquidity and market access for investors.
