## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and debt securities) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include commercial papers and other debt securities from various issuing companies.
- Issuing companies include IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, CEAT Limited, and Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited.
- Allotment dates are majorly October 23, 2026 and October 26, 2026, with varying maturity dates ranging from January 2027 to October 2031.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the securities are admitted to dealings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the designated security codes and ISINs for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: October 26, 2026
- Allotment Dates: October 23, 2026 and October 26, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Between January 8, 2027 and October 27, 2031

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers.
