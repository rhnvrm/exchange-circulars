## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Edelweiss Asset Management Limited under Edelweiss Mutual Fund - Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF, effective from November 25, 2026. A total of 1,853,902 units are admitted with ISIN INF754K01XZ1 and trading symbol EENERGY.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Edelweiss Mutual Fund - Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF
- **Symbol:** EENERGY
- **ISIN:** INF754K01XZ1
- **Number of Securities:** 1,853,902 units
- **Face Value & Paid Up Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 36.98/- per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** November 25, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Market Makers:** Choice Equity Broking Private Limited, East India Securities Limited, Parwati Capital Market Private Limited, Irage Broking Services LLP, Neo Wealth Management Private Limited, Kanjolachana Finserve Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Standard listing of ETF units in pursuance of Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify the security by its designated code and adhere to the specified market lot size.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 18, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading access for investors in the Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF on the NSE Capital Market segment.
