## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision in collateral timings for the capital market segment based on feedback received from members. This circular is issued in partial modification of earlier circulars dated July 21, 2026, and July 29, 2026, and will be effective from November 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Collateral Allocation:** New cut-off time is 11:55 PM (previously 11:30 PM).
- **G-Sec Addition via E-KUBER:** New cut-off time is 9:00 PM (previously 5:00 PM).
- **G-Sec Allocation:** New cut-off time is 10:00 PM (previously 8:00 PM).
- Effective Date: November 23, 2026.
- All other timings mentioned in the previous circulars remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification to circular reference nos. 75318 (dated July 21, 2026) and 75459 (dated July 29, 2026) regarding timings for collateral addition and release.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Members, Custodians, and PCMs must update their operational systems and internal workflows to accommodate the revised cut-off timings starting from November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The extended windows for G-Sec addition and collateral allocation provide members with greater operational flexibility at the end of trading days, reducing scheduling pressures.
