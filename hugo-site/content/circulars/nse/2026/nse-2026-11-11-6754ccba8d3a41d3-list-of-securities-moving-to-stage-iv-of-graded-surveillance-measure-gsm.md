## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Univa Foods Limited (Symbol: UNIVAFOODS, ISIN: INE275F01019) is moving to Stage IV effective November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Univa Foods Limited (UNIVAFOODS, ISIN: INE275F01019).
- Effective Date: November 12, 2026.
- Trading Frequency: Permitted once a week (Every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- Surveillance Action: Trade for Trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower and 100% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value with no upward movement.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities placed under Stage IV GSM are subject to stringent surveillance measures, including trade-for-trade execution with no netting off allowed, weekly trading availability, and 100% ASD deposited by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precaution while trading in the affected security and ensure compliance with trade-for-trade settlement and 100% ASD deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for Univa Foods Limited will be severely constrained due to weekly trading limits, trade-for-trade settlement, and 100% cash/ASD requirements.
