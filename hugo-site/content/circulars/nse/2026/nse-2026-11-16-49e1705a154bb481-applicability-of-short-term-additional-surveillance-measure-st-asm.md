## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II. Effective November 18, 2026, specific securities satisfying the inclusion criteria have been placed under ST-ASM, attracting stricter margin requirements on open and new positions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- Margin requirements apply to all open positions as on November 17, 2026, and new positions created from November 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II on specified securities.
- Margin requirement enhancements up to 100% for affected securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% to 100% as applicable) on open positions as of November 17, 2026, and all new positions created from November 18, 2026.
- Monitor surveillance circulars and applicable security lists regularly.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 16, 2026
- Open positions evaluated as of: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action / Margin applicability: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin obligations for traders and members holding positions in the shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
