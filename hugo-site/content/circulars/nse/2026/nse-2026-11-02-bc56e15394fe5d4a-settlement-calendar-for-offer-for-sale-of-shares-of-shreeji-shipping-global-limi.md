## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Shreeji Shipping Global Limited, referencing circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026. Settlements for securities in the OFS follow secondary market transaction rules.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar covers retail (RS) and non-retail (IS) categories across multiple settlement numbers (2026207 and 2026208).
- Order dates span November 3, 2026, and November 4, 2026.
- Funds pay-in is scheduled on T+1 day by 11:00 AM.
- Securities and funds pay-out is scheduled on T+1 day by 01:30 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicable securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day as per standard regulatory norms for OFS transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members, custodians, and primary corporate members (PCMs) must ensure adherence to margin requirements (100% for retail and non-retail categories as specified), order dates, custodial confirmation timelines, and timely funds pay-in by T+1 day 11:00 AM.

## Important Dates

- Order Date (Settlement 2026207): November 03, 2026
- Settlement Date (Settlement 2026207): November 04, 2026
- Order Date (Settlement 2026208): November 04, 2026
- Settlement Date (Settlement 2026208): November 05, 2026
- Funds Pay-in: T+1 day by 11:00 AM
- Securities & Funds Pay-out: T+1 day by 01:30 PM

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and regulatory compliance for market participants participating in the Shreeji Shipping Global Limited Offer for Sale.
