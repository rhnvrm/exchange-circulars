## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced updated quantity freeze limits for futures and options (F&O) contracts on indices, effective from November 02, 2026, pursuant to the F&O consolidated circular.

## Key Points

- New quantity freeze limits established for index symbols: NIFTY (1800), BANKNIFTY (900), FINNIFTY (1800), MIDCPNIFTY (2800), NIFTYNXT50 (600), and NIFTYFPI (8500).
- Members are required to upload updated contract files into their trading applications before the effective date.
- Files can be retrieved from the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) or NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

Computation and revision of index quantity freeze limits as per methodology specified in consolidated circular NSE/FAOP/73928.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading applications with the latest `contract.gz` and `NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` files prior to trading on November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth order execution and compliance with risk management parameters for index derivatives trading.
