## Summary

NSE has issued circular regarding revisions in formats for registration of Non-Neat Frontend (NNF) software documents and other updates, specifically introducing updated net worth certificate formats and computation guidelines for algo providers seeking empanelment.

## Key Points

- Introduces Annexure 13 outlining the revised Networth Certificate format for Corporates, Firms, and Individuals applying for Algo Provider empanelment.
- Requires certification on the letterhead of a certifying Chartered Accountant or Company Secretary.
- Specifies computation requirements based on Base Networth and Variable Networth as per Schedule VI of SEBI (Stock Brokers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2022.
- Details non-allowable assets to be deducted from Capital and Free Reserves, including fixed assets, pledged securities, unlisted securities, bad deliveries, and certain loans and advances.
- Mandates disclosure of PAN, UDIN, and membership details of the certifying CA/CS firm and its partners/directors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated net worth computation methodology aligning with SEBI Gazette Notification No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2022/73 dated February 23, 2022.
- Standardized documentation and verification formats for algo provider empanelment and NNF software registration.

## Compliance Requirements

- Algo providers must submit net worth certificates in the newly prescribed format certified by a qualified Chartered Accountant or Company Secretary.
- Ensure accurate calculation of Base Networth and Variable Networth, deducting all specified non-allowable assets.
- Include mandatory details such as UDIN, CA/CS firm PAN, and partner/director particulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per circular guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Standardizes compliance documentation across all algo providers and software vendors.
- Enhances transparency and financial integrity in algorithmic trading empanelment.
