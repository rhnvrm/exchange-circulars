## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for three companies—Purple United Sales Limited, Sheetal Universal Limited, and Unihealth Hospitals Limited—under preferential allotment on the SME EMERGE segment, effective from November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Purple United Sales Limited**: 781,750 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 10, Issue Price: Rs. 360) allotted on 08-Oct-2026 under preferential issue, ISIN: INE0P5R01014, Market Lot: 250.
- **Sheetal Universal Limited**: 259,000 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 10, Issue Price: Rs. 210) allotted on 18-Sep-2026 under preferential issue, ISIN: INE04VX01019, Market Lot: 500, with specified lock-in details.
- **Unihealth Hospitals Limited**: 1,250,000 equity shares (Face Value: Rs. 10, Issue Price: Rs. 520.31) allotted on 07-Sep-2026 under preferential issue, ISIN: INE0PRF01011, Market Lot: 250, with specified lock-in details.
- All securities are pari passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action admission pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions in these securities are conducted using the designated security codes and respective market lot sizes, adhering to applicable lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: November 5, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: 07-Sep-2026 (Unihealth), 18-Sep-2026 (Sheetal), 08-Oct-2026 (Purple United)

## Impact Assessment

Standard admission of further capital for SME companies expanding their equity base through preferential allotments.
