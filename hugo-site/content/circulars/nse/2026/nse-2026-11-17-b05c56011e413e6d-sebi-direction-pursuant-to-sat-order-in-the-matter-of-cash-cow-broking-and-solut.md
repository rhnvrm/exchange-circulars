## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order staying the debarment of Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions and its partners, and defreezing them instantly.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/51070 and SEBI orders dated January 18, 2022 and March 13, 2026.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated June 10, 2026, ordered that the debarment of the entities shall remain stayed.
- SEBI vide email dated November 17, 2026, directed that the entities be defreezed instantly in compliance with the SAT orders.
- Entities affected: M/s. Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions, Mr. Shailendra Sen, Mr. Amit Jain, and Mr. Chirag Sharma.

## Regulatory Changes

- Debarment restrictions placed on Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions and its partners have been stayed pursuant to the SAT order, and their accounts/access have been defreezed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 17, 2026
- SAT Order Date: June 10, 2026
- SEBI Email Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Specific entities previously debarred are now defreezed and permitted access in accordance with the SAT stay order. No direct operational impact on general market participants.
