## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch and availability of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Infinity Equity Long-Short Fund under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category by Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Kotak Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Infinity Equity Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct Plans including Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options).
- ISINs: INF174K30103, INF174K30129, INF174K30111, INF174K30079, INF174K30095, INF174K30087.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform (NMF TM Segment).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any accepted payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 04, 2026, till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: November 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: December 01, 2026
- Fund Pay-in Deadline: November 04, 2026 (12:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access and transaction processing for market participants and investors looking to subscribe to Kotak Mutual Fund's Specialized Investment Fund NFO offerings via the NSE platform.
