## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of privately placed securities of Sundaram Finance Limited on the capital market segment, effective from October 29, 2026.

- Company Name: Sundaram Finance Limited
- Symbol: 0SFL30
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Description: SFL 0% 2030 Sr AA3
- Coupon Rate: 0%
- Date of Maturity: 25-Oct-2030

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings with specified lot sizes and designated codes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified only by their designated codes and executed in the specified lot sizes.

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 25, 2030

Routine admission of privately placed debt securities to trading on the capital market segment with minimal direct operational impact on existing secondary market trading.
