## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for 30,000,000 partly paid-up equity shares of Century Extrusions Limited allotted on a rights basis, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Extrusions Limited
- Symbol: CENTEXTPP
- ISIN: IN9281A01016 (Temporary ISIN: IN8*********)
- Face Value: Re. 1 per share (Paid-up Value: Rs. 0.50 per share)
- Issue Price: Rs. 15 per share (Rs. 7.5 paid on application)
- Number of Securities: 30,000,000
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 30000000
- Market Lot: 1 (Pari Passu: Yes)

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be identified by the designated code (CENTEXTPP) and conducted in specified lot sizes. Shares allotted in dematerialized mode are credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date / Trading Commencement Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of partly paid-up rights shares for Century Extrusions Limited on the NSE capital market segment, providing liquidity to allottees.
