## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the face value and ISIN of equity shares of Gujarat Natural Resources Limited (GNRL) will be changed effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Gujarat Natural Resources Limited
- Symbol: GNRL
- New ISIN: INE207H01026
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1 per share
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Stock split leading to a change in the face value and ISIN of the company's equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to update their systems with the new ISIN (INE207H01026) and face value (Re. 1) for Gujarat Natural Resources Limited (GNRL) effective November 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares of Gujarat Natural Resources Limited will see their holdings split accordingly, and trading systems must reflect the new ISIN and face value.
