## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of Carnelian Small Cap Fund for ongoing transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Carnelian Small Cap Fund schemes (Regular and Direct plans across IDCW and Growth options) are reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP will be available.
- Effective date of availability: November 09, 2026.
- Includes ISINs: INF30CN01145, INF30CN01111, INF30CN01137, INF30CN01103, INF30CN01129, and INF30CN01095.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure proper mapping of scheme codes and ISINs for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the Carnelian Small Cap Fund via the NSE MF platform.
