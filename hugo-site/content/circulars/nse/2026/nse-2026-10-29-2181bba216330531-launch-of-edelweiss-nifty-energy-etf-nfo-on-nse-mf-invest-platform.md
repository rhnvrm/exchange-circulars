## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch and availability of the Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 30, 2026, to November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF - Regular Plan Growth and Direct Plan Growth.
- ISIN: INF754K01XZ1.
- NFO Period: October 30, 2026, to November 13, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Allotment Date: November 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 18, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 30, 2026
- NFO End Date: November 13, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 18, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors access to transact in the Edelweiss Nifty Energy ETF NFO via the NSE platform.
