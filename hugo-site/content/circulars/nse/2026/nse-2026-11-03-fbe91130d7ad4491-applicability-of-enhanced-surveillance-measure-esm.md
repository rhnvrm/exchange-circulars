## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability and movement of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin and are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST), while others move between stages with specific price bands and periodic call auctions.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Inclusions:** Securities including 21st Century Management Services Limited (21STCENMGM) and Milton Industries Limited (MILTON) have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I.
- **Stage Migration:** JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Limited (RETAIL) is moving from ESM Stage I to Stage II.
- **Margin Requirements:** Shortlisted securities attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 05, 2026, on all open positions as on November 04, 2026, and new positions created from November 05, 2026.
- **Segment Shift:** Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 05, 2026.
- **Stage II Restrictions:** Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The NSE has updated the list of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure framework in accordance with exchange surveillance guidelines and previous circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement terms for the affected securities, and note the price band restrictions for Stage II securities.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2026:** Effective date for stage movement and price band restrictions (Stage II for RETAIL).
- **November 05, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin and shifting from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) for ESM Stage I securities.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in 21STCENMGM, MILTON, and RETAIL will experience heightened margin obligations, settlement restrictions (Trade-for-Trade), and price band constraints, impacting liquidity and trading execution for these securities.
