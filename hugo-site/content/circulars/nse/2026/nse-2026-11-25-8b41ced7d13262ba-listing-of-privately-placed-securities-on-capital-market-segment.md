## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of privately placed securities of LIC Housing Finance Limited on the capital market segment with effect from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LIC Housing Finance Limited
- Symbol: 833LICH29
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Description: LICH 8.33% 2029 Sr 460
- Coupon Rate: 8.33%
- Date of Maturity: 20-Feb-2029
- ISIN Code: INE115A07RL6

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine admission of securities to dealings pursuant to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by designated security codes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 26, 2026
- Maturity Date: 20-Feb-2029

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access for the newly listed privately placed securities on the capital market segment.
