## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of their Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026. Members must submit the certificate through the ENIT portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Requirement:** Submission of Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026.
- **Platform:** ENIT portal (https://enit.nseindia.com/MemberPortal/).
- **Deadline:** November 30, 2026.
- **Reference Circulars:** NSE/COMP/76716 (October 05, 2026) and NSE/INSP/73792 (April 17, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a compliance reminder reinforcing existing periodic financial reporting obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must upload the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, via the ENIT portal by November 30, 2026. Failure to comply will invite penal or disciplinary action as stipulated under Annexure 1.3 of NSE circular ref no. NSE/INSP/73792.

## Important Dates

- **Deadline:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate financial health monitoring of trading members by the exchange. Timely submission avoids regulatory penalties and disciplinary actions.
