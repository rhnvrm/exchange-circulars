## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs) for Axis Bank Limited and Union Bank of India, effective November 06, 2026, due to redemption and call option exercise.

## Key Points

- **Axis Bank Limited (ISIN: INE238A08419):** Trading suspended effective November 06, 2026, due to redemption.
- **Union Bank of India (ISIN: INE692A08169):** Trading suspended effective November 06, 2026, due to redemption by exercise of call option.
- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; routine enforcement of debt market trading regulations regarding security suspension upon redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the trading suspension in the specified non-convertible securities and adjust trading records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected debt market instruments will no longer be available for trading on the NSE debt segment following redemption and call option execution.
