## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that Invesco India Nifty Metal Index Fund and Invesco India BSE Top 10 Banks ETF will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transaction Window: November 16, 2026 to November 30, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Covers multiple plans for Invesco India Nifty Metal Index Fund (Regular and Direct, Growth and IDCW options) and Invesco India BSE Top 10 Banks ETF (Direct and Regular Plans).
- Allotment Date for all schemes is scheduled for December 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before December 04, 2026, till 10:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Subscription Start Date: November 16, 2026
- Subscription End Date: November 30, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: December 04, 2026 (10:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: December 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market participation and subscription for investors in the newly launched Invesco Mutual Fund schemes through the NSE platform.
