## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the continuation of SEBI debarment and directions against specified entities in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd and other scrips, following proceedings and orders from the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).

## Key Points

- References SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026.
- Notes that pursuant to the SAT order dated October 5, 2026, and subsequent SEBI communication, the debarment of specified PANs/entities continues.
- Prohibited entities are restrained from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities directly or indirectly.
- Consolidated lists of debarred entities are available on the NSE and Hon'ble SAT websites.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of regulatory debarment and market access restrictions on indicted entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the debarred entities, prevent them from accessing the securities market, and ensure ongoing compliance.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- SAT Order Date: October 5, 2026
- Original SEBI Order Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures rigorous enforcement of market prohibitions and regulatory compliance across member broker networks.
