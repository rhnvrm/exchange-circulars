## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST), while others move between stages or are excluded.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Stage I) attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 17, 2026, on open positions as on November 16, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities in Annexure I (Stage I) shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- Securities moving from Stage II to Stage I w.e.f. November 16, 2026 include Capital Trust Limited (CAPTRUST) and Veljan Denison Limited (VELJAN).
- Shortlisted securities: Prozone Realty Limited (PROZONER) and Transworld Shipping Lines Limited (TRANSWORLD) added to ESM Stage - I.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) criteria for shortlisted securities, including mandatory 100% margins and migration to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for affected securities and note the segment shifts to Trade-for-Trade.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II to Stage I movement and price band changes: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin requirements and Segment shifts (EQ/SM to BE/ST): November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading of specified scrips will be affected due to the shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement and higher margin requirements under the ESM framework.
