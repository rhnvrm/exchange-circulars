## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Mafatlal Industries Limited (Symbol: MAFATIND) on the Capital Market segment effective from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mafatlal Industries Limited
- Symbol: MAFATIND
- ISIN: INE270B01035
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Number of Securities: 7,21,78,930 equity shares (Distinctive number range: 1 to 72178930)
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in compulsory demat mode and identified by its designated code (MAFATIND) with a market lot of 1.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Mafatlal Industries Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity and broader market access for investors.
