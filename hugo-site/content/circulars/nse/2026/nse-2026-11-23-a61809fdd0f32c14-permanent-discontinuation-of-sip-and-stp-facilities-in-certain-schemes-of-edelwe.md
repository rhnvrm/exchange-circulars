## Summary

Edelweiss Asset Management Limited has announced a notice-cum-addendum regarding the reopening of subscriptions in the Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund and the permanent discontinuation of paused SIP and STP facilities in six specific overseas equity schemes due to overseas investment limits.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions (lumpsum, SIP, STP) under Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund are resumed effective October 26, 2026, as overseas investment headroom became available.
- Paused SIPs and STPs for Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund are scheduled for reactivation by November 20, 2026.
- Paused SIPs and STPs in six specific overseas schemes are permanently closed and will not be reactivated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to Scheme Information Document (SID), Key Information Memorandum (KIM), and Statement of Additional Information (SAI) of the designated schemes to reflect permanent closure of SIP/STP facilities for select overseas funds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and distributors must note the permanent discontinuation of SIP/STP facilities in the affected overseas schemes and the resumption of subscription/systematic plans in the designated healthcare index fund.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Resumption of Subscriptions: October 26, 2026
- Scheduled Reactivation Date for Paused SIPs/STPs in Healthcare Index Fund: November 20, 2026
- NSE Circular Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding paused SIPs or STPs in the six specified overseas schemes will experience permanent discontinuation of those automated instructions, requiring alternative investment strategies.
- Investors in the Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund benefit from the restoration of fresh investments and systematic transaction plans.
