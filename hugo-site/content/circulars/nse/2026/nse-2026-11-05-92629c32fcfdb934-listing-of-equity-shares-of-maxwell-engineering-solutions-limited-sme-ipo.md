## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of equity shares of Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited under its SME IPO segment. Trading in these securities commences effective from the listing date.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited
- Scrip Code / Symbol: MAXENGS
- Total Equity Shares: 13,698,275 equity shares
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 9,775,075 shares (71.36%)
- Public Holding: 3,923,200 shares (28.64%)
- Number of Shareholders: 1,095 total (7 promoter, 1,088 public)

## Regulatory Changes

- Official listing and admission to dealings on the NSE SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR) Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Report / Quarter Ending Date: 04/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Maxwell Engineering Solutions Limited shares on the NSE SME exchange platform, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding the company's investor base.
