## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the Due Date Rates (DDR) for Monthly Electricity Futures in the commodity derivatives segment that expired on October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Commodity:** ELECMBL (Monthly Electricity Futures)
- **Expiry Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Due Date Rate (DDR) Price:** 4565.00
- **Base Quantity:** 1
- **Base Unit:** MWh

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine final settlement price notification issued pursuant to established procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members are requested to take note of the finalized Due Date Rates for final settlement calculations and obligations.

## Important Dates

- **Contract Expiry Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Circular Issue Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparent final settlement pricing for market participants holding open positions in the expired electricity futures contract.
