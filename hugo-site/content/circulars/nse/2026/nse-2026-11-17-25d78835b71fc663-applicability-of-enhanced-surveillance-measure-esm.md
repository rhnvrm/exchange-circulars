## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability and movement of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I attract a minimum 100% margin and are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, while certain securities move between Stage I and Stage II.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I: Balaji Phosphates Limited (BALAJIPHOS), Flexituff Ventures International Limited (FLEXITUFF), and Pulz Electronics Limited (PULZ).
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: Bhagyanagar India Limited (BHAGYANGR) and Supreme Engineering Limited (SUPREMEENG).
- ESM framework applies in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisted securities under ESM Stage I shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 19, 2026, on all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created from November 19, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM will shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 18, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for affected securities.
- Trading participants must note the segment shift from EQ/SM to BE/ST for applicable securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Stage II price band and movement: November 18, 2026.
- Effective date for margin requirements and settlement segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): November 19, 2026 (based on open positions as on November 18, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanics for affected securities due to 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement, enhancing market integrity and cautioning investors.
