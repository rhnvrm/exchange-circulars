## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the extension of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures contracts for an additional period of 6 months.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures has been extended.
- The scheme will remain in effect until June 14, 2027.
- This extension references previous circulars NSE/COM/74471 (May 29, 2026) and NSE/COM/76043 (August 28, 2026).
- The Exchange reserves the right to amend or discontinue the scheme at any time with an advance notice of 15 days.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the circular solely extends the validity period of the existing Liquidity Enhancement Scheme for Brent Crude Oil futures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the scheme must continue to comply with the terms and provisions specified in the prior circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Extension:** November 26, 2026
- **Scheme Expiry Date:** June 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Ensures continued market liquidity support and ongoing incentives for trading Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures on the exchange platform.
