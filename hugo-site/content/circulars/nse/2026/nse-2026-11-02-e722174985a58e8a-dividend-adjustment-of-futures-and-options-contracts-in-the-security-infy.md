## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security Infosys Limited (INFY) following a corporate action for a dividend of Rs 26/- with a face value of Rs 5/-. The effective ex-date is November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: INFOSYS LIMITED (Symbol: INFY)
- Corporate Action Type: Dividend
- Face Value: Rs 5/-
- Dividend Amount: Rs 26/-
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Revised option strike prices and futures base prices are adjusted accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of SEBI guidelines for adjustments to futures and options contracts on announcement of corporate actions, strike prices and base prices have been revised.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz/ MII contract (NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) and spread files on the trading application before trading on the ex-date.
- These files can be obtained from the directory faoftp/faocommon on the Extranet server or the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date & Effective Date: November 3, 2026
- Expiry Date for affected contracts: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Traders holding F&O positions in INFY will see revised strike prices effective November 3, 2026, ensuring parity post-dividend payout.
