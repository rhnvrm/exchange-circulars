## Summary

NSE has released a revised list of approved Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), Government Securities (G-Secs), and Treasury Bills (T-Bills) accepted as collateral that form part of the cash component of liquid assets.

## Key Points

- Lists approved Sovereign Gold Bonds eligible for collateral acceptance.
- Specifies ISIN codes, security descriptions, trading symbols, and applicable haircuts (0.1) for each security.
- All SGB/G-Sec/T-Bill issued by RBI during the month shall be accepted from the date of issue.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated schedule and eligibility parameters for debt securities accepted as liquid asset collateral.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must take note of the revised list and applicable haircuts when pledging SGBs and government securities as collateral.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate valuation and risk management for cash component liquid asset collateral in trading operations.
