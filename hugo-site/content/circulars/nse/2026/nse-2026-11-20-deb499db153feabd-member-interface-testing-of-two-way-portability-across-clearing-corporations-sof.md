## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) is conducting a mock session on November 21, 2026, to test the Member Interface and two-way portability across Clearing Corporations using the Software as a Service (SaaS) Model, in compliance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023.

## Key Points

- Mock session scheduled for November 21, 2026, to test SaaS setup and member interfaces.
- NCL will invoke its SaaS setup between 14:30 hrs – 15:00 hrs.
- NCL will operate using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) between 15:15 hrs to 16:30 hrs.
- Members should follow guidelines and SOPs outlined in previous NCL circulars (NCL/CMPT/56360 and NCL/CMPT/61960).
- Existing credentials can be used or requested via support@nsccl.co.in.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of quarterly mock sessions for SaaS-based business continuity as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to actively participate and test all functionalities associated with the SaaS setup on the mock day.

## Important Dates

- Mock Session Date: November 21, 2026
- NCL SaaS Invocation Time: 14:30 hrs – 15:00 hrs
- NCL SaaS Operational Time: 15:15 hrs – 16:30 hrs

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational readiness and familiarity with clearing corporation business continuity setups; no direct financial impact.
