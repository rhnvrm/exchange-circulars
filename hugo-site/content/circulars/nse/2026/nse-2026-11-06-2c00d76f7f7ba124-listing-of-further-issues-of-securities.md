## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies admitted to dealings with effect from November 08, 2026, pursuant to ESOP allotments.

## Key Points

- Listing of further equity shares for companies including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Angel One Limited, Aye Finance Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, Cipla Limited, CreditAccess Grameen Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective November 08, 2026.
- Allotments primarily comprise equity shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).
- Trading will take place in designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine admission of further issue of securities under Regulation 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Member brokers and listed entities must ensure proper trading identifiers and lot sizes are used as per the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base for the respective companies with minimal immediate market disruption.
