## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Alphagrep Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund by Alphagrep Mutual Fund is available for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Available facilities include SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: November 09, 2026.
- Includes 6 scheme variants covering Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure correct ISIN mapping and operational readiness on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment offerings on the NSE MF Invest Platform by enabling transactions in specialized hybrid fund categories with full SIP/STP/SWP support.
