## Summary

NSE has issued guidelines regarding the special Muhurat Trading session on account of Diwali, scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026. Trading members must ensure Unique Client Codes (UCCs) are identified as compliant by the specified deadline.

## Key Points

- Special Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- UCCs identified as compliant by the Exchange by 22:00 hrs on Saturday, November 07, 2026, are permitted to trade.
- Members are required to refer to the daily exchange report named "SEG_TMCODE_UCC_TRADING_STATUS_DDMMYYYY_nn" for trading status.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Ensure all relevant UCCs are compliant and approved by 22:00 hrs on November 07, 2026.
- Monitor daily download reports to verify UCC trading status.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- UCC Compliance Deadline: November 07, 2026 (22:00 hrs)
- Muhurat Trading Session Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal regulatory impact; ensures operational readiness and compliance for trading members participating in the special festival trading session.
