## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced that various plans of the Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Includes Regular Plan and Direct Plan variants (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- ISINs covered: INF205KA1CQ7, INF205KA1CR5, INF205KA1CS3, INF205KA1CT1, INF205KA1CU9, and INF205KA1CV7.
- Facilities enabled: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular regarding platform scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are informed of the scheme availability for client transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Circular Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading, investment, and systematic planning in Invesco India Silver ETF Fund of Fund schemes for market participants and investors.
