## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by AlphaGrep Investment Management Private Limited for Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund on the Capital Market segment, effective from November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** AlphaGrep Mutual Fund - Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund
- **Direct - Growth:** Symbol **EHLDG**, ISIN `INF2VOA30011`, No. of Securities: 25,628,723
- **Regular - Growth:** Symbol **EHLRG**, ISIN `INF2VOA30045`, No. of Securities: 13,297,340
- **Date of Allotment:** November 04, 2026
- **Face Value & Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard admission to dealings pursuant to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trading in these securities is conducted using the designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing/Trading:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading and liquidity for investors in the Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund on the NSE platform.
