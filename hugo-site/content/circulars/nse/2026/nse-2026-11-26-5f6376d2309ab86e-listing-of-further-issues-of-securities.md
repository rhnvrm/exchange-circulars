## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from November 27, 2026. These shares were allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Companies involved include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Creditaccess Grameen Limited, Greenply Industries Limited, Hero Motors Limited, ICICI Life Insurance Limited, INOX India Limited, and others.
- All securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing companies must ensure compliance with applicable listing regulations and depository procedures for the newly allotted shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Various dates between October 29, 2026, and November 24, 2026 depending on the company.

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase through ESOP allotments with negligible market impact.
