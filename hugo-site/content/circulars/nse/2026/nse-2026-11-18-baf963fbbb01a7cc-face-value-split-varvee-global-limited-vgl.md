## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value split for Varvee Global Limited (VGL). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will be reduced from Rs. 5 to Rs. 2 per share, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Limited
- Symbol: VGL
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action notification processed under NSE listing department guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and depositories must update their records and trading systems to reflect the new face value for VGL shares ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The stock split changes the share denomination, potentially enhancing retail liquidity and trading volumes for VGL shares.
