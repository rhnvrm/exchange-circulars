## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the extension of the due date for the submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26. This follows the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026, which extended the due date for filing Financial Statements and Annual Returns under the Companies Act, 2013.

## Key Points

- Due date for filing Financial Statements in e-forms (AOC-4, AOC-4 (CFS), AOC-4 (XBRL), etc.) for FY 2025-26 is extended up to December 31, 2026.
- Due date for submission of Annual Returns for Financial Year 2025-26 is extended till December 31, 2026.
- No extension is granted for statutory timelines for holding AGMs under the Companies Act, 2013.
- Filings made after the currency of the General Circular will attract additional fees as per the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014.

## Regulatory Changes

Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular No. 05/2026 provides relaxation of additional fees and extension of time for filing statutory forms under the Companies Act, 2013 for the financial year 2025-26.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and applicable companies must ensure submission of their Annual Returns and Financial Statements on or before the revised due date of December 31, 2026, to avoid additional fees.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- MCA Circular Date: October 24, 2026
- Extended Due Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides necessary timeline relaxation and compliance relief to clearing members and entities for completing statutory filings for the financial year 2025-26 without additional fee penalties.
