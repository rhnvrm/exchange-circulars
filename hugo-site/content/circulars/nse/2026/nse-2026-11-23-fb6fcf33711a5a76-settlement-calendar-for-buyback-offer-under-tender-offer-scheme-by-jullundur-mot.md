## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement calendar and guidelines for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme by Jullundur Motor Agency (Delhi) Limited. Trading is scheduled from November 25, 2026, to December 1, 2026, with settlement concluding on December 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X, Settlement Number: 2026141.
- Trade Start Date: November 25, 2026; Trade End Date: December 1, 2026.
- Obligation Download Date: December 7, 2026; Settlement Date: December 8, 2026.
- Demat shares must be tendered through Early Pay-in of shares via depositories for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be completed well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post receipt of funds pay-in on Settlement Day along with applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT).
- Annexure I outlines rejection codes and reasons for partial or non-acceptance of orders (e.g., joint holder signature mismatches, demat account name mismatches, excess shares tendered than eligibility).

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of a specific clearing and settlement schedule along with mandatory early pay-in mechanisms and rejection reason code classifications for corporate buyback tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of shares is executed prior to 03:30 p.m. on the trade end date, adhere to eligibility criteria as of the record date, and account for applicable STT on the settlement day.

## Important Dates

- Trade Start Date: 25-NOV-2026
- Trade End Date: 01-DEC-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 07-DEC-2026
- Settlement Date: 08-DEC-2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and timeline certainty for members and investors participating in the Jullundur Motor Agency buyback offer, minimizing settlement risks through transparent rejection criteria and structured pay-in deadlines.
