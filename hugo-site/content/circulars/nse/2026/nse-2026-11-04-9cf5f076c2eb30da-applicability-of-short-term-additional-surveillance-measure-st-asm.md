## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) stages for certain securities effective November 05, 2026, with applicable surveillance actions taking effect from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Nelcast Limited** (NELCAST) included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **OCCL Limited** (OCCLLTD) moved from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II.
- **Fascinate Textiles Limited** (FASCINATE) moved from ST-ASM Stage II to Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities shortlisted under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework are subject to heightened margin requirements to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the applicable margin requirements (50% or 100% depending on the stage) are duly collected and maintained for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Surveillance Actions Effective Date:** November 06, 2026 (on open positions as on November 05, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Traders and investors dealing in the affected securities (NELCAST, OCCLLTD, FASCINATE) will experience higher margin blocking, impacting liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
