## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Interarch Building Solutions Limited (INTERARCH) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share. Members are advised on executing early pay-in using the existing/old ISIN (INE00M901018) for specific settlement dates.

## Key Points

- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 10/- to Rs 2/- per share.
- Existing ISIN: INE00M901018.
- Record Date & Ex-Date: November 30, 2026.
- EPI Dates and Settlements: Settlement 2026225 on 30-NOV-2026; Settlements 2026225 and 2026226 on 01-DEC-2026.
- Instructions: Early pay-in should be executed in the old ISIN without adjusting for conversion during the ex-date/record date period.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandates proper handling of old vs. new ISINs during corporate actions (face value split) for early pay-in and settlement processes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for the ex-date/record date.
- For early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files must not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per actual traded quantities to pass benefits to clients.
- Post record date, pay-in/early pay-in instructions must be executed in the New ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 30, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 30, 2026
- EPI Date 30-NOV-2026 (Settlement Number: 2026225)
- EPI Date 01-DEC-2026 (Settlement Numbers: 2026225 & 2026226)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and accurate early pay-in allocation for market participants handling trades affected by the stock split of Interarch Building Solutions Limited.
