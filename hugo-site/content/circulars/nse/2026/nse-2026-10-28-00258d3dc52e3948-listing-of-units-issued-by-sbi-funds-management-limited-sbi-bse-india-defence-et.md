## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by SBI Funds Management Limited under SBI BSE India Defence ETF, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: SBI BSE India Defence ETF
- Symbol: SBIDEFETF
- ISIN: INF200KB1CJ6
- Number of Units: 1,984,770
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 72.58
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard listing circular under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date / Admission to Dealings: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to trade in the newly listed SBI BSE India Defence ETF units on the NSE capital market segment.
