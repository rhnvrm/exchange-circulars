## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of privately placed securities on its capital market segment with effect from November 03, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Listing of privately placed securities effective from November 03, 2026.
- Involves issuances from Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited and Care Health Insurance Limited.
- Trading will take place using designated security codes and specified lot sizes pursuant to Regulation 2.5.5.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of new privately placed debt securities to dealings on the capital market segment under NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading participants must ensure trades are conducted using the designated security codes and market lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026
- Maturity Dates: November 16, 2029 (Aditya Birla Sr G1), October 30, 2036 (Care Health Sr 3), and October 30, 2036 (Aditya Birla Sr G2).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market trading access for the newly listed privately placed debt securities.
