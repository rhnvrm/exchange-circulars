## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited under its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shares will be traded under the symbol MAHACOAL with ISIN INE03K401026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mahanadi Coalfields Limited
- Trading Symbol: MAHACOAL
- ISIN: INE03K401026
- Specific listing date will be announced via a separate circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should update their trading systems with the new symbol and ISIN for Mahanadi Coalfields Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced in a separate circular.
