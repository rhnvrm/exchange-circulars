## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the removal of exclusively listed companies of de-recognized, non-operational, or exited stock exchanges placed on the Dissemination Board (DB). Pursuant to relevant SEBI circulars, five companies will be removed from the DB of NSE effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Five companies are being removed from the Dissemination Board (DB) of NSE.
- Effective date of removal: November 17, 2026.
- Affected companies include Jyot International Marketing Ltd, Sharp Commercial Enterprises Ltd, Electra (Jaipur) Ltd., Medexport (India) Ltd, and Global Stone India Limited (Formerly known as STI Granite India Limited).
- Reasons for removal include having nation-wide trading terminals on other stock exchanges or being delisted from them.

## Regulatory Changes

This action is executed pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2016/110 dated October 10, 2016, SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2017/92 dated August 01, 2017, and Chapter 6 of Master Circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD2/PoD-2/CIR/P/2023/171 dated October 16, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the mentioned companies shall not be available on the DB of NSE with effect from November 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors holding shares in the specified companies should take note of their removal from the Dissemination Board of NSE.
