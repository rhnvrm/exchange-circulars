## Summary

Trading in equity shares of Baheti Recycling Industries Limited will be migrated from the SME Platform (EMERGE) and admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market Segment – Main Board) with effect from November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Baheti Recycling Industries Limited
- Symbol: BAHETI
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE029Q01017
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share fully paid up
- Number of Securities: 1,04,46,905
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 10446905
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Migration of securities from SME Emerge platform to Capital Market Segment (Main Board) pursuant to regulatory guidelines for migration.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and entities must take note of the security details, ISIN, and effective date for trading on the Main Board.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date / Migration Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to trade equity shares of Baheti Recycling Industries Limited on the Main Board segment of NSE.
