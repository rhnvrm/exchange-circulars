## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular detailing the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities. The circular outlines the shortlisting of securities under Long-Term ASM Stage I and the movement of securities from Stage I to Stage II, along with corresponding margin and trading restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Aeroflex Neu Limited (AERONEU), Nupur Recyclers Limited (NRL), Praxis Home Retail Limited (PRAXIS), and Tree House Education & Accessories Limited (TREEHOUSE).
- Securities shortlisted to move from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II: BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 19, 2026, on all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not reflect an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I and Stage II measures for designated scrips.
- Mandating 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin is levied and maintained for the shortlisted securities as per the specified timelines.
- Market participants must account for surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM shortlisting / movement: November 17, 2026.
- 100% Margin Effective Date: November 19, 2026 (on open positions as on November 18, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened margin requirements will impact liquidity, trading volumes, and capital allocation for market participants dealing in the affected securities.
