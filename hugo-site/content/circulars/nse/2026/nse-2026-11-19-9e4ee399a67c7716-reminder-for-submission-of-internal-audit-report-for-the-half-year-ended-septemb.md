## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has issued a reminder to all Clearing Members to submit their Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026, on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reminder for submission of Internal Audit Report for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submission deadline: November 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be made through the NCL Member Portal.
- Non-compliance or delayed submission will attract penalties and disciplinary actions as per circular NCL/CMPL/58437.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; this is a operational reminder enforcing existing submission timelines.

## Compliance Requirements

All Clearing Members must complete the internal audit and submit the report in the prescribed format via the NCL portal (https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/) by November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Half Year Ended:** September 30, 2026
- **Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures adherence to regulatory oversight and risk management standards. Clearing Members failing to meet the deadline face financial penalties and disciplinary actions.
