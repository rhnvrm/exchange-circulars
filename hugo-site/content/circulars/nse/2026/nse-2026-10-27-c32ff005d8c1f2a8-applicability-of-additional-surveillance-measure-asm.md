## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) for specific securities, effective October 28, 2026. This includes inclusions in Stage-I and shifts between surveillance stages.

## Key Points

- Steel Strips Wheels Limited (SSWL) and Yudiz Solutions Limited (YUDIZ) shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- Vivid Electromech Limited (VIVIDEL) moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I to Stage-II.
- Applicable rate of margin set at 100% effective October 30, 2026, on all open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisting of securities under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework with enhanced margin requirements and surveillance monitoring in conjunction with prevailing measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for the affected securities starting October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting / Movement: October 28, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 30, 2026 (on open positions as of October 29, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in SSWL, YUDIZ, and VIVIDEL will face 100% margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading strategies in these scrips.
