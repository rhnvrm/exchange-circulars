## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for several securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- 11 securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.
- No securities were listed under ST-ASM Stage II or moving from Stage I to Stage II in this circular.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 50% margin requirement on open positions as on November 11, 2026 and new positions created from November 12, 2026 for securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities starting November 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: November 12, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities may impact liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips. Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not reflect an adverse action against the companies.
