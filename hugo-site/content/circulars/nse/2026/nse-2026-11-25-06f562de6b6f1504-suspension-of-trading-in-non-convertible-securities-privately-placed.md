## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs) of LIC Housing Finance Limited and Sundaram Finance Limited due to redemption, effective November 26, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Affected Issuers: LIC Housing Finance Limited and Sundaram Finance Limited.
- Securities Involved: Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) with ISINs INE115A07RA9 and INE660A07RU2.
- Suspension Date: November 26, 2026.
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading suspension executed under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A following the redemption of the securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs and ensure no further trades are executed on the exchange platform from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact limited to the cessation of secondary market trading for the redeemed privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of LIC Housing Finance Limited and Sundaram Finance Limited.
