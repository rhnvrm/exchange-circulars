## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in name and trading symbol of UMIYA BUILDCON LIMITED, effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: UMIYA BUILDCON LIMITED
- New Name: MRO-TEK Networks Limited
- Existing Symbol: UMIYA-MRO
- New Symbol: MROTEK
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or compliance policy changes; this is an administrative update of corporate identity and symbol parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, clearing members, and market participants are required to update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and back-office records with the new name and symbol.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to system updates for trading under the new symbol MROTEK.
