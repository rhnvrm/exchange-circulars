## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (SIF) under Invesco Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are open for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Covers both Direct and Regular plans across Growth, Payout, and Reinvestment options.
- ISINs include INF205K30105, INF205K30113, INF205K30121, INF205K30071, INF205K30089, and INF205K30097.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must note the availability of the schemes for investor transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with expanded investment options through the NSE MF Invest Platform with full support for systematic transaction facilities.
