## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued updates regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective November 20, 2026, specific securities have met exclusion criteria, while others continue under designated surveillance stages.

## Key Points

- Compuage Infocom Limited (COMPINFO) has satisfied the exclusion criteria and is removed from the ASM IBC framework w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO) is listed under Stage II of the consolidated ASM IBC framework.
- No new securities were shortlisted under Annexure I for fresh shortlisting or stage transitions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review and updates of securities shortlisted under the ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised surveillance applicability and ensure compliance alongside other prevailing exchange measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market transparency regarding companies undergoing IBC proceedings and applies appropriate surveillance controls on trading.
