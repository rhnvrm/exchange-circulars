## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities satisfying the criteria. Effective November 05, 2026, securities such as A G Universal Limited (AGUL) have been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 08, 2026 on all open positions as on November 06, 2026 and new positions created from November 08, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities under the Long-Term ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced 100% margin requirements for the shortlisted securities as per the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting: November 05, 2026
- Position Date for Margins: November 06, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in shortlisted securities (such as AGUL) will experience higher margin requirements (100%), impacting liquidity and trading strategies for these specific scrips.
