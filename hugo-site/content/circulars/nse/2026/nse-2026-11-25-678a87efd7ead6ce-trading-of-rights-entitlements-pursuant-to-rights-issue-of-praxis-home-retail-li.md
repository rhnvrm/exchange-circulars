## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the commencement of trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Praxis Home Retail Limited effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Praxis Home Retail Limited
- Symbol: PRAXIS-RE3
- ISIN: INE546Y20048
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 103,216,356
- Trading Period for Renunciation: November 27, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- Date of Suspension: December 01, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of Rights Entitlements to dealings on the Capital Market segment under Regulation 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of RE trading and ensure ISINs are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Prominent disclaimers must be included in contract notes regarding application or renunciation before the issue closing date, after which unapplied REs lapse and extinguish.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: November 27, 2026
- Trading Renunciation End Date: November 30, 2026
- Date of Suspension: December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding eligible securities can trade or renounce their rights entitlements during the specified window in the Trade to Trade segment.
