## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock bidding session on the eOFS platform from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled for October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Session Date**: October 31, 2026.
- **Platform**: eOFS (URL: https://eofs.nseindia.com).
- **Connectivity**: No changes are required at members' end for connecting to the Primary/DR site.
- **Financial Obligations**: Bids from the mock session do not attract any obligation in terms of funds and securities.
- **Switchover Duration**: 12:00 hrs to 12:45 hrs.
- **Market Timings**: Normal Market Open at 09:15 hrs, Normal Market Close at 15:30 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine technical mock session for disaster recovery preparedness.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock bidding session to check connectivity and system readiness.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: October 30, 2026
- **Mock Session Date**: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The mock session ensures trading member connectivity and system robustness during a disaster recovery switchover without any real financial or settlement impact.
