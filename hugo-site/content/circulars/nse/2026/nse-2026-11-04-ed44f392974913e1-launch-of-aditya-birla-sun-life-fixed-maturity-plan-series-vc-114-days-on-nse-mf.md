## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch and availability of Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan-Series VC (114 days) schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for subscription from November 05, 2026 to November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available for transaction: Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan-Series VC (114 days) across Regular and Direct plans (IDCW, Growth, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- Subscription window: November 05, 2026 to November 17, 2026 till 11:59 pm.
- Allotment Date: November 18, 2026 across all listed ISINs.
- Fund House: Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 18, 2026 till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- Subscription Start Date: November 05, 2026
- Subscription End Date: November 17, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Funds Clearing Deadline: November 18, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the new fixed maturity plan series through the NSE MF Invest Platform smoothly.
