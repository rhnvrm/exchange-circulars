## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in TECH MAHINDRA LIMITED (TECHM) due to a bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1. The ex-date for the corporate action is November 30, 2026, with an adjustment factor of 2.

## Key Points

- Corporate Action: Bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1.
- Adjustment Factor: 2.
- Ex-Date: November 30, 2026.
- Futures Contracts Adjustment: Existing open positions after end-of-day on November 27, 2026, will be adjusted based on the settlement price and market lot changes.
- Mark-to-Market: Positions will be marked-to-market on November 27, 2026, and carried forward at the adjusted value.

## Regulatory Changes

Procedures updated in pursuance of NSE Clearing Byelaws, SEBI circular SMDRP/DC/CIR-8/01, and associated NSE Clearing circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure open positions and margins for TECHM F&O contracts are adjusted according to the specified formula and carry forward values ahead of November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-Date: November 30, 2026
- Position Adjustment Reference Date (EOD): November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth continuity and fair valuation of open derivative positions in TECHM following the 1:1 bonus issue without abrupt value disruptions.
