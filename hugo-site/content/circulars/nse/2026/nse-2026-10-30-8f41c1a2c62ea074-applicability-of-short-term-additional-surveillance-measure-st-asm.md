## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities effective November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Sakuma Exports Limited (SAKUMA), Tera Software Limited (TERASOFT), and Welspun Investments and Commercials Limited (WELINV).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Spencer's Retail Limited (SPENCERS).
- Applicable margin rate for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. November 03, 2026, on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. November 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The Exchange has placed specific securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework based on market surveillance criteria. This framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the collection of applicable margins (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created from November 03, 2026, onwards for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026 (based on open positions as on November 02, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements up to 50% or 100% for the shortlisted securities may affect trading volumes and liquidity. The shortlisting is a standard market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.
