## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the shifting of specified securities to the Trade for Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower, effective November 13, 2026, as part of periodic market surveillance review.

## Key Points

- Securities shifted from Rolling segment (EQ) to Trade for Trade (BE): Affordable Robotic & Automation Limited, Banka Bioloo Limited, Lorenzini Apparels Limited.
- Securities shifted from Rolling segment (SM) to Trade for Trade (ST): A G Universal Limited.
- Securities continuing in Trade for Trade: Quintegra Solutions Limited, Arshiya Limited, Shrenik Limited.
- Settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Capital Market Segment Trading Regulations Part - A, 2.6, trading in listed securities is moved to the Trade for Trade segment to ensure market safety and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to exercise caution and note that settlement for the affected securities will occur on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 9, 2026
- Effective Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading operations for the specified securities will be subject to Trade for Trade restrictions and a 5% price band.
