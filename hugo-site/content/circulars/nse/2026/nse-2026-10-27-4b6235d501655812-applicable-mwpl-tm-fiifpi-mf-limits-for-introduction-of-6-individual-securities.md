## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the applicable market-wide position limits, trading member-wise position limits, FII/FPI (Category I & II) limits, mutual fund position limits, trading member proprietary limits, and client level limits for the introduction of 6 individual securities.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026.
- **Securities Included:** Coromandel International, City Union Bank (CUB), Data Patterns, Karur Vysya Bank, Navin Fluorine International, and Syrma SGS Technology.
- **Applicable Limits:** Detailed share counts specified for Market Wide Position Limits, Client limits, Proprietary limits, TM + FPI(Cat I) + MF overall limits, and FPI Category II limits.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits are established in pursuance of circular nos. 050/2026 (NCL/CMPT/73997) and 108/2026 (NCL/CMPT/76459) for the newly introduced securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the specified position limits across client, proprietary, FII/FPI, and mutual fund categories effective from the trade date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures proper risk management and regulatory adherence for trading participants dealing in the derivatives segment for the newly introduced individual securities.
