## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities. Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST). Additionally, securities moving from Stage I to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage I (Arvee Laboratories, Fascinates Textiles, Giriraj Civil Developers, Smarten Power Systems, Viaz Tyres) shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 12, 2026, on all open positions as on November 11, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting from Stage I to Stage II (Agastya Energy and Infrastructure, Innomet Advanced Materials, Kaya Limited) shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 11, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. November 12, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages, 100% margin requirements, price band restrictions under periodic call auction, and settlement segment modifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions, and account for trade-for-trade settlement and price band restrictions for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Stage II price band and movement: November 11, 2026
- Cut-off date for open positions for Stage I margin: November 11, 2026
- Effective date for Stage I inclusion, margins, and segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and operational requirements for shortlisted securities will be significantly impacted due to mandatory 100% margins and trade-for-trade settlement mechanisms.
