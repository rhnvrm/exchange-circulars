## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited and PNB Housing Finance Limited on the capital market segment with effect from October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited**: Two series admitted (Symbol: 805SPIL31, Coupon: 8.05%, Maturity: 27-Oct-2031, ISIN: INE044A08023; Symbol: 82SPIL31, Coupon: 8.2%, Maturity: 27-Oct-2031, ISIN: INE044A08015) with market lot 1.
- **PNB Housing Finance Limited**: One series admitted (Symbol: 8455PHFL31, Coupon: 8.46%, Maturity: 23-Oct-2031, ISIN: INE572E07324) with market lot 1.
- Trading will be conducted in designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and approved market lots.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 27, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: October 23, 2031 and October 27, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading availability of privately placed debt securities on the capital market segment for investors.
