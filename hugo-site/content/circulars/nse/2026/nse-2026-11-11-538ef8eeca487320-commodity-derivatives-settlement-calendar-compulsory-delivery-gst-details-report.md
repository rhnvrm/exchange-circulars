## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the delivery settlement schedule and GST details confirmation schedule for commodity derivatives futures contracts expiring in December 2026. The circular details timelines for compulsory delivery (primary delivery centre), delivery marking, pay-in and pay-out of commodities and funds, supplementary settlements, and GST details uploading windows for buyers and sellers.

## Key Points

- Covers delivery settlement schedules for commodity futures contracts including GOLDM, GOLD1G, GOLD, SILVER, SILVERM, SILVER100, GOLDGUINEA, GOLD10G, ALUMINIUM, ALUMINI, COPPER, LEAD, LEADMINI, NICKEL, ZINC, and ZINCMINI.
- Specifies timelines for T+1 delivery settlement type under compulsory delivery logic.
- Details supplementary settlements for weight, location, quality differentials, making charges, and GST.
- Outlines seller and buyer member GST details uploading windows and timings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard operational schedule update for December 2026 commodity derivative contract settlements and GST reporting requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must adhere to the specified delivery marking, pay-in, pay-out, and supplementary settlement deadlines.
- Seller and buyer members must upload GST details within the designated date and time windows for December 2026 expiry contracts.

## Important Dates

- Contract expiry dates across November and December 2026 (including 23-Nov-26, 25-Nov-26, 30-Nov-26, and 01-Dec-26).
- Delivery pay-in and pay-out dates spanning late November through December 2026.
- GST uploading windows active between 23-Nov-26 and 10-Dec-26 depending on the contract and expiry date.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational workflows, timely settlement of physical delivery contracts, and accurate GST reporting for market participants trading in NSE commodity derivatives.
