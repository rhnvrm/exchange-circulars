## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has approved the request of members SPAN Caplease Pvt. Ltd. and C.D. Commodities Broking Limited for the total surrender of their trading membership.

## Key Points

- SPAN Caplease Pvt. Ltd. (SEBI Regn. No. INZ000183933, Last Trade Date: 16-May-2022) has surrendered its membership.
- C.D. Commodities Broking Limited (SEBI Regn. No. INZ000083930, Last Trade Date: Not Applicable) has surrendered its membership.
- Surrender approval date for both members is November 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the surrender of membership by the aforementioned entities.

## Important Dates

- Surrender Approval Date: November 20, 2026
- Circular Issue Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct impact on market operations as these are routine surrenders of inactive or exited memberships.
