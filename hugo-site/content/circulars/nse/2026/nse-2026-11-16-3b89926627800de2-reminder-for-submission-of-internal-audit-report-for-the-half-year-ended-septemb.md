## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026. Members must submit the report on or before November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all trading members.
- Covers the half year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submission deadline: November 30, 2026.
- Submission mode: Inspection module in the Member Portal.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a reminder reiterating existing compliance requirements established under previous circulars (NSE/INSP/76701, NSE/INSP/76784, and NSE/INSP/73792).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must complete their internal audit in the prescribed format and submit the report through the Member Portal Inspection module by November 30, 2026, to avoid penal charges or disciplinary action.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 16, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Failure to submit the internal audit report by the due date will attract penal charges and potential disciplinary action as per exchange guidelines. Trading members must ensure timely completion and submission to maintain compliance.
