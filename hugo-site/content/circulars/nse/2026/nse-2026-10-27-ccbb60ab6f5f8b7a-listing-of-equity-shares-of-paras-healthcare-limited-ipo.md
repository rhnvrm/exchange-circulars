## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), along with the shareholding pattern submitted under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as of 26.10.2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Paras Healthcare Limited
- Security Class: Equity Shares
- Filing Type: Shareholding Pattern under Reg. 31(1)(a) of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015
- Status: Post Issue
- Date: 26.10.2026

## Regulatory Changes

Submission of post-issue shareholding pattern in compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI LODR Regulation 31 regarding disclosure of specified securities for newly listed entities.

## Important Dates

- Post Issue Date: 26.10.2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables commencement of trading in equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited on the NSE platform and ensures transparent disclosure of shareholding distribution.
