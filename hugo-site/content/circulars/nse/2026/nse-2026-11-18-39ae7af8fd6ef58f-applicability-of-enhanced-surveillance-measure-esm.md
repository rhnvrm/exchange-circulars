## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) via circular ref. no. 885/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/77481) dated November 18, 2026. Securities qualifying under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- **MMEL** (Moving Media Entertainment Limited) and **SHREEKARNI** (Shree Karni Fabcom Limited) are shortlisted under ESM Stage I w.e.f. November 19, 2026, attracting 100% margin and shifting to Trade-for-Trade w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- **UHTL** (United Heat Transfer Limited) moves from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. November 19, 2026, and will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 19, 2026.
- ESM shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions created from November 20, 2026. Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 19, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities, and note the transfer to trade-for-trade settlement and periodic call auction price bands where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **November 19, 2026:** Stage II / Periodic call auction price band effective date; applicability for movement between stages.
- **November 20, 2026:** 100% margin requirement effective date; shift from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanics for the specified securities (MMEL, SHREEKARNI, UHTL) by enforcing stringent margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.
