## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options segment on Saturday, October 31, 2026, operating from both Primary and Disaster Recovery (BCP) sites, followed by a re-login session from the primary site.

## Key Points

- Mock trading covers two trading sessions: Session-1 from the Primary Site (09:00 hrs to 12:00 hrs) and Session-2 from the BCP Site (12:45 hrs to 16:15 hrs).
- A primary site live re-login session is scheduled from 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs on October 31, 2026.
- Non-graceful shutdown from the primary site will occur during the switchover to the DR site; members must take necessary action as per circular NSE/MSD/48662.
- All outstanding orders will be purged before the start of trading from the BCP site.
- Multicast TBT sequence numbers will start from '1' once trading resumes from the BCP site.
- Trades executed during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; conducted as a standard market operations and disaster recovery preparedness drill.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must participate or test their systems to meet exchange and SEBI mock trading requirements, clear outstanding orders between sessions, and ensure proper NEAT adapter and NNF software configurations.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 31, 2026
- Re-login Session: October 31, 2026 (18:30 hrs - 19:00 hrs)
- Normal Live Trading Resume: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on live markets as sessions are conducted on a Saturday for system verification and disaster recovery readiness.
