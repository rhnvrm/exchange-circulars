## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the reporting of client-level pay-in shortages for Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) first leg and reverse leg securities pay-in. A file upload facility is provided on the NMASS portal for SLBS participants to report delivering client-wise security shortages effective from trade date November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- File upload facility provided on NMASS portal under 'SLB CLIENT LEVEL PAYIN SHORTAGES' module.
- Super admins must grant module access to users.
- Cut-off times: 07:50 a.m. on settlement day for SLB First Leg and 8:50 a.m. for SLB Reverse Leg.
- Upload window opens from trade date for SLB First Leg and settlement -1 working day for SLB Reverse Leg.
- Full file submissions are required for modifications; previous batches are ignored.
- Files must be submitted in a running sequence of batch numbers.
- Securities pay-in for reverse leg auction transactions are accepted through both NSDL and CDSL.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduces standardized reporting and guidelines for SLBS first leg and reverse leg shortages superseding/supplementing previous circulars NCL/CMPT/69197 and NCL/CMPT/73999.

## Compliance Requirements

- SLBS Participants and Custodians must upload shortage files via the NMASS portal within specified timelines (07:50 a.m. for First Leg, 8:50 a.m. for Reverse Leg).
- Ensure proper sequencing of batch files and adherence to quantity validation rules where reported shortage cannot exceed pay-in obligation towards NCL.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Trade date November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines shortage reporting for SLBS transactions, enhancing transparency and operational efficiency in settlement processing for participants and custodians.
