## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in four schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund (HDFC Charity Fund for Cancer Cure) on account of redemption, effective from November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects four schemes/series of HDFC Charity Fund for Cancer Cure.
- Schemes affected: HDFC Charity Fund for Cancer Cure - DP - 50% IDCWPO (Symbol: HFMPCC50DD, ISIN: INF179KC1HA0), DP - 75% IDCWPO (Symbol: HFMPCC75DD, ISIN: INF179KC1HB8), RP - 50% IDCWPO (Symbol: HFMPCC50RD, ISIN: INF179KC1HC6), and RP - 75% IDCWPO (Symbol: HFMPCC75RD, ISIN: INF179KC1HD4).
- Trading in these units was closed after trading hours on November 18, 2026.
- Suspension date is November 19, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

[No action required by market participants other than noting the suspension of trading in the specified mutual fund units.]

## Important Dates

- Closing Hours of Trading: November 18, 2026
- Suspension Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The suspension is a routine corporate/fund action following the redemption of mutual fund units and has no broader market impact.
