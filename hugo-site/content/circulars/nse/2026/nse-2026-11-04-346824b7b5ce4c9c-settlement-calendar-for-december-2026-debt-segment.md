## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Debt Segment and Debt Auction Settlement (Rolling T+1) for the month of December 2026 in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for Debt Settlement (Settlement Type 'I') as outlined in Annexure A.
- Covers settlement schedules for Debt Auction Settlement (Settlement Type '2') as outlined in Annexure B.
- Applicable to all members, custodians, and PCMs participating in the debt segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine monthly settlement calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to the trade dates, daily settlement dates, and settlement numbers specified for December 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trades executed throughout December 2026, starting from December 01, 2026, through December 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and clearing workflows for participants trading in the debt segment and debt auction segment.
