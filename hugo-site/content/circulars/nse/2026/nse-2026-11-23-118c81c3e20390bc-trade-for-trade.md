## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the shifting of specified securities to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a price band of 5% or lower, effective November 27, 2026, pursuant to periodic surveillance reviews.

## Key Points

- BALAXI PHARMACEUTICALS LIMITED (BALAXI) is shifted from Rolling segment (series: EQ) to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE).
- QUICKTOUCH TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED (QUICKTOUCH) is shifted from Rolling segment (series: SM) to Trade for Trade segment (series: ST).
- Securities continuing in Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/BZ) under current fortnightly review: BHAGYANAGAR INDIA LIMITED (BHAGYANGR), SUPREME ENGINEERING LIMITED (SUPREMEENG), FLEXITUFF VENTURES INTERNATIONAL LIMITED (FLEXITUFF), and MORARJEE TEXTILES LIMITED (MORARJEE).
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- The transfer is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Capital Market Segment Trading Regulations Part - A, 2.6, trading in the specified securities shall be available in the Trade for Trade segment at a price band of 5% or lower.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the above securities, as settlements are executed on a trade-to-trade basis with zero netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026 (Friday)

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities may experience moderation due to mandatory trade-to-trade settlement and 5% price bands.
