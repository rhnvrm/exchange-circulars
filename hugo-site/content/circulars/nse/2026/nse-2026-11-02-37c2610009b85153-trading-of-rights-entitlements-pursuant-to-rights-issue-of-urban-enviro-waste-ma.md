## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Urban Enviro Waste Management Limited (Symbol: URBAN-RE, ISIN: INE0O0220019) on the SME EMERGE segment effective from November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Description: Trading of Rights Entitlement pursuant to Rights Issue
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 8,660,000
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From November 04, 2026, to November 13, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 16, 2026
- Market Lot: 800 shares
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting the specific ISIN and ensuring clients do not mistake REs for ordinary shares of the company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes and bills for the purchase of REs, stating that purchase only confers the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue by applying with requisite money or renouncing before closure. Unexercised or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the issue closing date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 04, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Begins: November 04, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Ends: November 13, 2026
- Date of Suspension: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible investors to trade rights entitlements or participate in the rights issue of Urban Enviro Waste Management Limited within the defined timeline under trade-to-trade settlement.
