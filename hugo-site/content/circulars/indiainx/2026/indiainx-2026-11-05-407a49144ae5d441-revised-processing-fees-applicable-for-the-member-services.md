## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (IFSC) Limited (India ICC) has announced a revision to the processing fee structure applicable for various member services, superseding the earlier circular dated March 06, 2026. The revised processing fees come into effect from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- The revised processing fee structure applies to various Member Services offered by India ICC.
- Fees prescribed under the earlier circular (No. 20260306-1 dated March 06, 2026) cease to be applicable from September 28, 2026.
- All processing fees levied for Member Services are strictly non-refundable.
- Upon receipt of a Member Service application, India ICC will debit the relevant processing fee directly from the member's dues account.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replacement of the previous fee schedule with a comprehensive revised schedule of processing fees for member services.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the revised fee structure and ensure adequate balance in their dues accounts for processing fee debits upon submitting member service applications.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Circular Date:** November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to financial accounting and fee debits for member service applications submitted to India ICC.
