## Summary

India International Exchange (India INX) has announced that the IFSCA turnover fees for the month of October 2026 will be debited from members' designated bank accounts on November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- IFSCA turnover fees for October 2026 will be debited on the 3rd working day of the month, November 4, 2026.
- Members must ensure sufficient funds are available in their respective bank accounts for the fee deduction.
- Turnover details and debit amounts will be made available for download in the INX EXTRANET under the file naming convention `INX_TURNSF_<MEMBER CODE>_MMYYYY.PDF`.

## Regulatory Changes

- Turnover-based fee rates are governed in accordance with IFSCA circular ref. no. IFSCA-DTFA/1/2026 dated March 2, 2026, and Exchange circular ref. no. 20260303-1 dated March 3, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must maintain adequate balances in their bank accounts to avoid payment failures.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2026**: Debit date for October 2026 IFSCA turnover fees.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational circular with minimal impact, affecting only member cash management for regulatory fee dues.
