## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has announced the adjustment of futures and options contracts of PETRONET LNG LTD (PETRONET) on account of a dividend payment of 7.00/- per equity share for financial year 2026-27. The record date is set for Friday, November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PETRONET LNG LTD
- **Symbol:** PETRONET
- **Corporate Action:** Dividend of 7.00/- per equity share
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** Friday, November 06, 2026
- **Trading Schedule:** PETRONET available for trading on Thursday, November 05, 2026, only till 15:30 hours.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the price and strike adjustments for PETRONET futures and options contracts effective from November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Last Trading Date (Pre-adjustment):** November 05, 2026 (till 15:30 hours)
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Futures contracts will be adjusted by deducting the dividend amount of 7.00/- from the Daily Settlement Price (DSP) as on November 05, 2026. Options contracts will have the dividend amount of 7.00/- deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of November 05, 2026.
