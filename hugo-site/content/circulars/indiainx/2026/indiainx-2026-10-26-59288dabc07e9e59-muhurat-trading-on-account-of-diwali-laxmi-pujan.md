## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has announced that a special Muhurat Trading session will be conducted on Sunday, November 08, 2026, on the occasion of Diwali Laxmi Pujan.

## Key Points

- Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Trading Session Timings: 14:20:00 hrs to 15:40:00 hrs.
- Trade Confirmation Timings: 14:20:00 hrs to 15:55:00 hrs.
- Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) shall not be applicable during the Muhurat Trading Session.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to note the special trading schedules and contact their designated Relationship Manager for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- November 08, 2026: Muhurat Trading Session (14:20 hrs to 15:40 hrs)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to special trading hours and non-applicability of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme during the session.
