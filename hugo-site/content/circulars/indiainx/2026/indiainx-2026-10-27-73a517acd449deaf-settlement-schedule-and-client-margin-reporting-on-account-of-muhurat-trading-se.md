## Summary

India International Exchange (India INX) has announced the settlement schedule and client margin reporting requirements for the Muhurat Trading Session held on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan on November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trades executed on November 08, 2026, are scheduled for settlement on November 09, 2026.
- Funds pay-in must be completed by 09:00 AM and funds pay-out will be processed by 09:30 AM on November 09, 2026.
- Members must report margins collected from trading members, constituents, and clients for trades executed on November 08, 2026, by November 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard procedural schedule for special trading sessions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must adhere to the specified settlement timelines and submit client margin reports by the stipulated deadline.

## Important Dates

- Trade Date: November 08, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 09, 2026
- Funds Pay-In: November 09, 2026 (09:00 AM)
- Funds Pay-Out: November 09, 2026 (09:30 AM)
- Margin Reporting Deadline: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations and transparent margin reporting for market participants during the Diwali holiday trading session.
