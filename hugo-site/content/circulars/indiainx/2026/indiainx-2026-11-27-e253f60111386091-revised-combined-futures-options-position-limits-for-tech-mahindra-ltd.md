## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (IFSC) Ltd. (IndiaINX) has issued revised combined Futures & Options (F&O) position limits for Tech Mahindra Ltd., following Notice No. 20261126-3 dated November 27, 2026. Trading members must strictly adhere to the updated Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), Trading Member Position Limit (TMPL), and Client Position Limit (CPL).

## Key Points

- Underlying Security: Tech Mahindra Ltd.
- Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL): 85,745 contracts
- Trading Member Position Limit (TMPL): 17,149 contracts
- Client Position Limit (CPL): 8,574 contracts
- Segment: ALL / Risk Management_ICC

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members must update their surveillance and risk management parameters to reflect the revised position limits for Tech Mahindra Ltd. in the derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that client and proprietary positions comply with the revised CPL and TMPL limits, preventing any breach of market-wide limits.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective: Immediate / ongoing adherence as per clearing corporation guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly trading and risk mitigation in derivatives contracts for Tech Mahindra Ltd.
