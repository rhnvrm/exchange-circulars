## Summary

India Infrastructure Finance Company Limited has established a U.S.$2,000,000,000 Medium Term Note Programme on the Global Securities Market (GSM) of India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited, effective from November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: India Infrastructure Finance Company Limited
- Type of Issue: Medium Term Note Programme
- Programme Value: U.S. $2,000,000,000 (USD Two Billion Only)
- Exchange Segment: Debt, Global Securities Market (GSM)
- Effective Date: 27 November 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes announced; standard listing notification for a Medium Term Note Programme on the Global Securities Market.

## Compliance Requirements

Refer to the offer document at the India INX issuer details page for specific compliance and offering terms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 27 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates international debt fundraising for India Infrastructure Finance Company Limited on the IFSC Global Securities Market.
