## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (IFSC) Limited has announced an extension of the due date for the submission of Audited Annual Returns for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, by clearing members to December 31, 2026. Additionally, the requirement for submitting the Auditor's Report (Annexure-1) has been waived off to promote ease of doing business.

## Key Points

- Due date for submitting annual returns for FY 2025-26 is extended from October 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
- The requirement of submitting the Auditor's Report (Annexure-1) is waived off.
- All other document submissions as prescribed in the earlier Circular No. 20261013-2 remain unchanged.
- Decision follows representations from market participants and aligns with MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Waiver of Annexure-1 (Auditor's Report) requirement for clearing members' annual returns submission.
- Alignment with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) relaxation of filing timelines under the Companies Act, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must submit their annual returns to the Exchange on or before December 31, 2026, via email to membership@indiaicc.com.
- Submission of Annexure-1 is no longer mandatory; all other prescribed annexures and documents must still be submitted as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026.
- Extended Due Date: December 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Relieves clearing members from immediate documentation burdens and provides additional time for annual compliance filings, supporting ease of doing business.
