## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (India INX) has announced a mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 21, 2026. Trading members are encouraged to participate actively to test their trading applications, algorithms, and third-party or in-house systems under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Date & Timing**: Mock trading takes place on Saturday, November 21, 2026, in two sessions (1st Session: 15:00:00 to 16:00:00 Hrs; 2nd Session: 16:00:01 to 17:00:00 Hrs).
- **Access Methods**: Members can access the environment via BoltPlus on Web and ETI APIs using in-house or vendor-developed software.
- **Algorithmic Trading Mandate**: All user IDs approved for algorithmic trading must participate in the mock trading session.
- **Feedback**: Members are requested to share feedback with the Business Operations team by 17:15 Hrs on the day of the session.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular is purely operational and intended for system testing and familiarization.

## Compliance Requirements

Mandatory participation for all user IDs approved for algorithmic trading.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date**: November 21, 2026
- **Feedback Submission Deadline**: November 21, 2026 by 17:15 Hrs

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from the mock trading session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create actual rights and liabilities.
