## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (IndiaINX) has announced the discontinuation of weekly and quarterly futures and options contracts across specified currency and index underlying asset groups. No new weekly or quarterly contracts will be introduced for these securities, and existing unexpired contracts for December 2026, January 2027, and February 2027 will cease to be available for trading starting November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Weekly Options and Futures discontinued for INRUSD, USDINR, and SENSEX50.
- Quarterly Futures and Options discontinued for GBPUSD, EURUSD, JPYUSD, and INRUSD/USDINR.
- Complete prohibition on the introduction of new weekly or quarterly contracts in the affected securities.
- Existing unexpired contracts for December 2026, January 2027, and February 2027 affected.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal of specific derivative maturities (weekly and quarterly formats) from active trading on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that no new weekly or quarterly contracts are initiated in the affected underlyings and manage existing open positions prior to the effective cessation date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026 (Existing unexpired contracts for Dec 2026, Jan 2027, and Feb 2027 no longer available for trading)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading currency and index derivatives on IndiaINX must review their portfolios and adjust hedging strategies due to the withdrawal of weekly and quarterly contract offerings.
