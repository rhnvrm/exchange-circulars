## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (IndiaINX) has announced the exclusion and discontinuation of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for SNX30. No SNX30 F&O contracts will be available for trading with effect from November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts of SNX30 are discontinued and will no longer be available for trading.
- Existing unexpired December 2026 and January 2027 contracts of SNX30 are also discontinued.
- Effective date of discontinuation: November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Discontinuation and delisting of SNX30 underlying derivatives contracts from the exchange trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the discontinuation and square off or settle existing unexpired positions in December 2026 and January 2027 SNX30 F&O contracts ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date of Discontinuation: November 30, 2026
- Affected Contracts: December 2026 and January 2027 unexpired contracts

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in SNX30 derivatives must adjust their portfolios and square off positions prior to November 30, 2026, to manage risk associated with the contract exclusion.
