## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (India ICC) has announced combined futures and options position limits for single stock derivatives, referencing earlier circular No. 20260930-2 dated September 30, 2026. Members must adhere to the specified Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), Trading Member Position Limit (TMPL), and Client Position Limit (CPL).

## Key Points

- Adherence mandated for Combined Futures & Options Position Limits (MWPL, TMPL, CPL).
- Applies to single stock futures and options contracts.
- Detailed limits provided in Annexure-1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of combined position limit frameworks for single stock derivatives under risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure their trading and client positions comply with the limits specified in Annexure-1.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026 (MWPL in Single Stock Futures and Options Contracts applicable)

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances risk surveillance and management controls across single stock derivatives segments.
