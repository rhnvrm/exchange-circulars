## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has announced adjustments to the futures and options (F&O) contracts of Tech Mahindra Ltd. (TECHM) following a bonus issue in the proportion of 1:1.

## Key Points

- Company / Symbol: TECH MAHINDRA LTD (TECHM)
- Corporate Action: Bonus issue (1:1 proportion, i.e., 1 new equity share for every 1 fully paid equity share held)
- Adjustment Factor: 2 ((1+1)/1)
- Record Date / Ex-date: November 30th, 2026
- Strike Price Adjustment: Old strike price divided by the adjustment factor (2)
- Contract Size Adjustment: Old contract size multiplied by the adjustment factor (2), resulting in a revised contract size of 12 (from 6)
- Position Adjustment: Old position multiplied by the adjustment factor (2)
- Futures Price Adjustment: Old futures price divided by the adjustment factor (2), rounded off to the nearest tick size

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustments are carried out in pursuance of Exchange circular no. 20180709-2 and SEBI master circular guidelines regarding the review and adjustment of corporate actions for stock futures and options contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the revised contract specifications, strike prices, and positions. The symbol TECHM will be available for trading on Friday, November 27th, 2026, only until 15:30 hours.

## Important Dates

- Last trading under old terms / restricted hours: November 27, 2026 (till 15:30 hours)
- Record Date / Ex-date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives traders and clearing members holding positions in TECHM F&O contracts will experience proportional adjustments to maintain economic equivalence following the bonus issue.
