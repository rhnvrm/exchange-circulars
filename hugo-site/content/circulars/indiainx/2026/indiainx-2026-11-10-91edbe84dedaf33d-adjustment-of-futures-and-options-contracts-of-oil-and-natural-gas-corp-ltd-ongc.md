## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (IndiaINX) has announced the adjustment of futures and options contracts of OIL AND NATURAL GAS CORP LTD (ONGC) on account of a dividend of 8.00 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27, with the record date set for November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: OIL AND NATURAL GAS CORP LTD (Symbol: ONGC)
- Corporate Action: Dividend of 8.00/- per equity share
- Record Date: Thursday, November 12, 2026
- Ex-Date: Thursday, November 12, 2026
- Trading on ONGC contracts available only till 15:30 hours on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments performed in accordance with SEBI Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135, circular No. CIR/MRD/DoP-1/P/00108/2018, and SEBI/HO/MRD2_DCAP/P/CIR/2022/09.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the price and strike adjustments for ONGC futures and options contracts effective November 12, 2026.
- Futures contracts base price adjusted by subtracting the dividend amount (8.00/-) from the Daily Settlement Price (DSP) of November 11, 2026.
- Options contracts strike prices adjusted by deducting the dividend amount (8.00/-) from all generated strike prices.

## Important Dates

- Last trading time before adjustment: November 11, 2026, 15:30 hours
- Ex-Date / Record Date / Effective Date for Adjusted Prices: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures fair valuation and continuity of derivatives trading for ONGC contracts following the dividend payout.
