## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (IndiaINX) has announced the expulsion of broker dealer Karvy Broking (IFSC) Limited (Member Code: 5055, IFSCA Registration Number: INZ000213735) effective from November 12, 2026. This action follows the cancellation of its Certificate of Registration by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) vide its Order dated May 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Expelled Entity:** Karvy Broking (IFSC) Limited
- **Member Code:** 5055
- **IFSCA Registration Number:** INZ000213735
- **Effective Date of Expulsion:** November 12, 2026
- **Underlying Reason:** Cancellation of Certificate of Registration by IFSCA

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal removal and termination of exchange membership and trading privileges for the broker dealer following regulatory deregistration by IFSCA.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and market participants are requested to take note of the expulsion and exercise caution.
- For any clarifications or inquiries, members may contact the Membership Department at 079-61993134/35 or via email at inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com.

## Important Dates

- **IFSCA Order Date:** May 21, 2026
- **Expulsion Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Upholds regulatory standards and market integrity within the IFSC framework by ensuring deregistered entities no longer maintain active trading memberships.
