## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited has notified members and participants regarding the notification issued by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) dated November 04, 2026, titled The Companies (Listing of equity instruments in permissible jurisdictions) Amendment Rules, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notification issued by IFSCA on November 04, 2026.
- Relates to The Companies (Listing of equity instruments in permissible jurisdictions) Amendment Rules, 2026.
- Detailed copy enclosed as Annexure-1 for member reference.

## Regulatory Changes

Adopts and communicates the amendments introduced via IFSCA notification regarding equity instrument listings in permissible jurisdictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are requested to take note of the amended rules for compliance.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- IFSCA Notification Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory clarity for market participants regarding cross-border listings and equity instruments in permissible jurisdictions.
