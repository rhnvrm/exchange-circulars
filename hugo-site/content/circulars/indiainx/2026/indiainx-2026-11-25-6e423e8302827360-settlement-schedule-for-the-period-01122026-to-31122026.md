## Summary

India INX has issued the settlement schedule for all segments covering the period from December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026 (Circular No: 20261125-2, Attachment: INXSETTMAS_1226).

## Key Points

- Covers trade and settlement schedule for all segments on India INX for December 2026.
- Details business start dates, settlement numbers, session numbers, trade start/end times, and settlement start/end times.
- Ensures transparent clearing and settlement timelines for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational circular establishing the trading and settlement calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clearing members must adhere to the specified trade session times and settlement timelines for December 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Schedule Period:** December 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
- **Circular Date:** November 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity for trading and clearing members executing transactions on India INX throughout December 2026.
