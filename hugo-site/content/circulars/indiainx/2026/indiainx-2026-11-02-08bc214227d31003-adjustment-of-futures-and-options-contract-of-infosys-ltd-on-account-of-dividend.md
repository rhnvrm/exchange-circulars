## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has announced the adjustment of futures and options contracts of Infosys Ltd (Symbol: INFY) on account of a dividend payment of Rs. 26.00 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Infosys Ltd
- Symbol: INFY
- Corporate Action: Dividend of Rs. 26.00 per equity share
- Record Date / Ex-date: Tuesday, November 03, 2026
- INFY available for trading on Monday, November 02, 2026, only till 15:30 hours before adjustment.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedure follows standard corporate action adjustment guidelines as per SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the adjusted futures prices and strike prices applicable from November 03, 2026. Futures prices will be adjusted by deducting the dividend amount from the Daily Settlement Price (DSP) of November 02, 2026. Strike prices will have the dividend amount deducted.

## Important Dates

- Pre-adjustment Trading Date: November 02, 2026 (till 15:30 hours)
- Record Date / Ex-date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Adjusted Base/Strike Prices: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives contracts of INFY will reflect price and strike adjustments to account for the Rs. 26.00 dividend payout, ensuring market neutrality and fair valuation for open positions.
