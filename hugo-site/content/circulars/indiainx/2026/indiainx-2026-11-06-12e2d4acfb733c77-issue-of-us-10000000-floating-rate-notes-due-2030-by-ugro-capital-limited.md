## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (India INX) has announced that debt securities issued by UGRO Capital Limited will be listed on the Global Securities Market and available for trading effective Monday, November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** UGRO Capital Limited
- **Issue Type:** Standalone
- **Issue Date:** 09 October 2026
- **Issue Size:** USD 10,000,000 (USD Ten Million)
- **Coupon Rate:** 4.50 per cent + Term SOFR
- **Maturity Date:** 27 September 2030
- **Credit Rating:** CareEdge: B+/Stable
- **ISIN:** INIFD2405030
- **Scrip Code:** 1100200
- **Security Symbol:** 4500UGRO0930
- **Minimum Denominations:** 10 (Ten) Bonds and in multiples of 1 (One) Bond thereafter
- **Contract Master Token Number:** 1100177
- **Tick Size:** 0.0001

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard debt listing circular on the Global Securities Market.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and trading members should note the listing details, ISIN, and scrip code for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Date:** 09 October 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 09 November 2026
- **Maturity Date:** 27 September 2030

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and investment in UGRO Capital Limited's USD 10 million floating rate notes on India INX Global Securities Market.
