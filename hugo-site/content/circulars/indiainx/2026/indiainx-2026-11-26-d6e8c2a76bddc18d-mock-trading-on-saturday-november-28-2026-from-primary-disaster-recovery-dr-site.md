## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has scheduled a mock trading session on Saturday, November 28, 2026, spanning both the Primary Site (GIFT City) and the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site. Market participants and trading members can test their systems, including ETI APIs and BoltPlus web interfaces.

## Key Points

- **Session Date:** Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- **Primary Site 1st Session:** 09:00:00 Hrs. to 10:15:00 Hrs.
- **Trading Halt & DR Resume:** Trading halts abruptly and resumes from DR Site by 11:00:00 Hrs.
- **DR Site 1st Session:** 11:00:00 Hrs. to 11:30:00 Hrs.
- **DR Site 2nd Session:** 11:30:01 Hrs. to 12:30:00 Hrs.
- **Mandatory Participation:** All user-ids approved for Algorithmic trading must participate.
- **Non-Financial:** Mock trading trades do not create margin, pay-in/pay-out obligations, rights, or liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- All algorithmic trading user-ids must participate in the mock trading session.
- Members should test third-party trading platforms or in-house developed ETI API systems.
- Feedback regarding the mock trading session must be submitted to the Business Operations team by 12:45 Hrs.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** November 28, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** November 28, 2026, by 12:45 Hrs

## Impact Assessment

Provides members an opportunity to validate trading applications, connectivity parameters, and Disaster Recovery switchover readiness without financial risk.
