## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited has approved the surrender of trading membership for HDFC Bank Limited IBU (Member No- 5067), effective November 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- HDFC Bank Limited IBU (Member No- 5067) has surrendered its trading membership.
- The surrender has been formally approved by the Exchange.
- For any clarifications, members may reach out to the Membership department via email at inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com or phone at 079-61993134/5.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory policy changes; routine membership status update.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant market participants must take note of the surrender of trading membership by HDFC Bank Limited IBU.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is limited to the cessation of trading operations and membership status for HDFC Bank Limited IBU on India International Exchange.
