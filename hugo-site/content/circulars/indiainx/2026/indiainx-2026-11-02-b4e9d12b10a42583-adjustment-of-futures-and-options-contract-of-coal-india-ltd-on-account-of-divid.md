## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (India INX) has announced adjustments to the futures and options contracts of Coal India Ltd. (COALINDIA) on account of a dividend of Rs. 10.25 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27. The record date is Wednesday, November 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Coal India Ltd. (Symbol: COALINDIA)
- **Corporate Action:** Dividend of Rs. 10.25 per equity share.
- **Record Date & Ex-date:** Wednesday, November 4, 2026.
- **Trading Schedule:** COALINDIA will be available for trading on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, only until 15:30 hours.
- **Futures Adjustment:** The adjusted futures price will be the Daily Settlement Price (DSP) on November 3, 2026, less the dividend amount of Rs. 10.25, applicable as the base price from November 4, 2026.
- **Options Adjustment:** The dividend amount of Rs. 10.25 will be deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of November 3, 2026, and rounded adjusted strike prices will apply from November 4, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment executed in pursuance of India INX circular number 20180709-2 and SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DoP-1/P/00108/2018 and SEBI/HO/MRD2_DCAP/P/CIR/2022/09).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the contract adjustments, revised strike prices, and trading schedule for COALINDIA derivatives contracts.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Cut-off:** November 3, 2026 (till 15:30 hours)
- **Record Date / Ex-date:** November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures fair pricing and continuity for derivatives contracts following the dividend payout without disrupting market operations.
