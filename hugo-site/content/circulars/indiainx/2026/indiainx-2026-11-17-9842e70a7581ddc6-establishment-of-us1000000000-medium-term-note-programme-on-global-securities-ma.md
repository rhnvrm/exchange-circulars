## Summary

UCO Bank has established its U.S.$1,000,000,000 Medium Term Note Programme on the Global Securities Market (GSM) of India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited, effective November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: UCO Bank
- Type of Issue: Medium Term Note Programme
- Programme Value: U.S. $1,000,000,000 (USD One Billion Only)
- Effective Date: Tuesday, 17 November 2026
- Exchange: India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (Global Securities Market)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and members should refer to the official offer document provided by the issuer for further details regarding the note programme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 17 November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing announcement for debt issuance program establishment on the Global Securities Market, with minimal direct market impact.
