## Summary

IndiaINX has issued a circular requiring all Clearing Banks to submit their half-yearly Net Worth Certificate, Auditor’s Report, Annual Audit Certificate, and Audited Annual Accounts for IBUs and bank levels as of September 30, 2026, for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- Clearing banks must submit financial documents on or before November 30, 2026.
- Required documents include net worth certificates, CAR (Capital Adequacy Ratio) at bank and IBU branch levels, net profit for the last 3 financial years, and IBU branch SWIFT codes.
- Standard formats for Annexure 1 (Net Worth Certificate for other than IBUs and for IBUs) and Annexure 2 (Computation of Net Worth) are provided in the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; this is a periodic mandatory filing requirement for clearing banks and IBU branches.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of Bank level and IBU Branch level Net Worth Certificates.
- Submission of Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) computations.
- Provision of Auditor’s Report and Audited Annual Accounts in accordance with Annexure 1 and Annexure 2.
- Submissions must be completed on or before November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2026:** Financial position / reference date for net worth certificate.
- **November 30, 2026:** Deadline for submission of documents.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures continuous financial monitoring, regulatory oversight, and capital adequacy compliance of clearing banks and IBU branches participating in IndiaINX clearing and settlement operations.
