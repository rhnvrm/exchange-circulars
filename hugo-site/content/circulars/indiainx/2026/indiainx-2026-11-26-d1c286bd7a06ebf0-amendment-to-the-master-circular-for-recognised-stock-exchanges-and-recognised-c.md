## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited has issued a circular notifying members and participants of IFSCA circular no. IFSCA-MIIT0IINX/1/2021-Capital Markets dated November 25, 2026, regarding amendments to the "Master Circular for recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing Corporations".

## Key Points

- References IFSCA circular dated November 25, 2026.
- Encloses Annexure-1 containing the detailed amendments.
- Applicable to all members and participants of India INX.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption and dissemination of amendments to the Master Circular governing recognised Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations as issued by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA).

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are required to take note of the updated regulatory guidelines and ensure adherence accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- IFSCA Circular Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and alignment with updated IFSCA master circular standards for stock exchanges and clearing corporations within the IFSC framework.
