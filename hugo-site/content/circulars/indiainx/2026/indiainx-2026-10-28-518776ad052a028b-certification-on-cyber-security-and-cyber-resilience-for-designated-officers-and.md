## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited has issued a circular drawing attention to IFSCA circular no. IFSCA-CSD/MSC/4/2026-DCS dated October 27, 2026, regarding certification on cyber security and cyber resilience for designated officers and other specified employees of Regulated Entities in the IFSCs.

## Key Points

- Refers to IFSCA circular dated October 27, 2026 (IFSCA-CSD/MSC/4/2026-DCS).
- Focuses on cyber security and cyber resilience certification.
- Applies to designated officers and other specified employees of Regulated Entities in IFSCs.
- Enclosed as Annexure-1 for members and participants.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption and enforcement of IFSCA guidelines requiring formal certification in cyber security and cyber resilience for key personnel and designated officers in IFSC-regulated entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities must ensure that designated officers and specified employees complete the required cyber security and cyber resilience certifications in accordance with the enclosed IFSCA guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- IFSCA Circular Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Strengthens operational resilience and information security posture by ensuring key personnel across IFSC regulated entities maintain verified competency in cyber security.
