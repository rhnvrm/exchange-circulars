## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (IndiaINX) has extended the due date for submitting the Annual Audited Net Worth Certificate, Auditor’s Report, and Audited Annual Accounts (Annual Returns) for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Due date extended from October 31, 2026, to December 31, 2026.
- Alignment with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) General Circular No. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026.
- Applies to all members regarding financial year 2025-26 filings.

## Regulatory Changes

- MCA General Circular No. 05/2026 grants relaxation of additional fees and extension of time for filing financial statements and annual returns under the Companies Act, 2013 with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) up to December 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit Annual Returns to the Exchange on or before December 31, 2026.
- Submissions must be made through the IIEFS portal and via email to inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Extended Deadline:** December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory relief and additional time for members to complete their annual filings and audits, aligning exchange compliance timelines with MCA extensions.
