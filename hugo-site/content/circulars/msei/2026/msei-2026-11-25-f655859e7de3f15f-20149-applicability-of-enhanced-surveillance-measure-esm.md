## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) issued Circular No. 20149 regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) to certain securities to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Implementation of enhanced surveillance actions based on objective parameters including price variation and market capitalization.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities identified under ESM are subjected to stringent monitoring and trading controls.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must adhere to enhanced margin requirements and trading restrictions for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026 / November 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on liquidity, margin requirements (up to 100% margin), and trading mechanism (Trade-for-Trade / Periodic Call Auction) for the shortlisted securities.
