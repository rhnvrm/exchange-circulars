## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified members regarding the change in name and trading symbol of Virat Industries Ltd under the permitted to trade category, effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Company Name: Virat Industries Ltd
- New Company Name: BRAHM VIRAT INDUSTRIES CORPORATION LIMITED
- Old Symbol: VIRAT
- New Symbol: BRAHMVIRAT
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate name and symbol change circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and back-office records with the new name and symbol for the company.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records and trading references for the company.
