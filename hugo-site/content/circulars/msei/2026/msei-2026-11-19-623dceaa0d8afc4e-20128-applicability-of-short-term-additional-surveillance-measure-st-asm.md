## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20128/2026 dated November 19, 2026, regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Effective November 20, 2026, specific securities have been shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, while others have been excluded or consolidated.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. November 23, 2026, on all open positions as on November 20, 2026, and new positions created from November 23, 2026 onwards.
- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I: Dishman Carbogen Amcis Limited (DCAL) and Bharat Parenterals Ltd (BPLPHARMA).
- Securities excluded from Short-Term ASM Framework: Hindustan Media Ventures Limited (HMVL) and Ovobel Foods Ltd (OVOBELE).
- Consolidated list includes Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Ltd (GRANDOAK - Stage I), Bacil Pharma Ltd (BACPHAR - Stage II), Restaurant Brands Asia Limited (RBA - Stage I), Deep Industries Limited (DEEPINDS - Stage I), and JOJO Ltd (JOJO - Stage I).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) provisions in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or higher, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities effective November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Exclusions / Movements: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 23, 2026 (on open positions as on November 20, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience higher margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and volatility in the short term.
