## Summary

MSEI has announced the market lot sizes for the introduction of futures and options contracts on individual securities, in continuation of Exchange circular Ref. No. MSE/TRD/20074/2026 dated November 09, 2026. The contracts will be available for trading starting November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Acutaas Chemicals Limited (Symbol: ACUTAAS):** Market lot size is set at 150.
- **Apar Industries Limited (Symbol: APARINDS):** Market lot size is set at 50.
- **Effective Date:** November 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of derivative contracts with specified market lot sizes for individual securities under the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the prescribed market lot sizes while placing orders in the respective futures and options contracts.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 23, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading participants and investors to trade in futures and options contracts for Acutaas Chemicals Limited and Apar Industries Limited with standardized lot sizes.
