## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Crystal Crop Protection Limited for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Crystal Crop Protection Limited
- **Symbol:** CRYSTALCP
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE101P01016
- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard admission procedure under the Permitted to Trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access for members in the equity shares of Crystal Crop Protection Limited under the Permitted to Trade category.
