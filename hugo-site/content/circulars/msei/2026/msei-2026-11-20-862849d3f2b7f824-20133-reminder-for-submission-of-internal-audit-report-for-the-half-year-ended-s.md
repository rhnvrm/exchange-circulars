## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Internal Audit Report for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- References earlier Exchange Circulars Ref. No. MSE/INSP/19916/2026, MSE/INSP/19976/2026, and MSE/INSP/18897/2026.
- Reminds trading members to carry out and submit a complete internal audit for the half year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submission must be completed via the Inspection OS Portal.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this circular reinforces existing compliance and reporting mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading members must submit the Internal Audit Report in the prescribed format on or before November 30, 2026, through the Inspection OS Portal.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Period:** Half year ended September 30, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Non-compliance or late submission of the internal audit report will attract penal charges and disciplinary action in accordance with Exchange Circular Ref. No. MSE/INSP/18897/2026.
