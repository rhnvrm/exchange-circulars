## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has announced a change in the trading symbol for Swati Projects Limited, effective from November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swati Projects Limited
- ISIN: INE210F01016
- Existing Symbol: SWATI
- New Symbol: SWATIPRO
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the symbol change and update their trading terminals and systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact, restricted to updating the trading symbol from SWATI to SWATIPRO across trading systems.
