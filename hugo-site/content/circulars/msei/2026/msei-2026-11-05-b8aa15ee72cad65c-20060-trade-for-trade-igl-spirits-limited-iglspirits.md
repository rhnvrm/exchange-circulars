## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the transfer of trading in the security of IGL Spirits Limited (IGLSPIRITS) from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** IGL Spirits Limited
- **Symbol:** IGLSPIRITS
- **Action:** Transferred from trade for trade segment ('BE' series) to rolling segment ('EQ' series) on the Primary Stock Exchange.
- **Reference:** Continuation of Exchange circular no. MSE/LIST/20049/2026 dated November 03, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

The security is no longer subject to mandatory trade-for-trade restrictions on the exchange and moves to normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with respective series settlement parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Circular Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal liquidity and rolling settlement for investors trading in IGL Spirits Limited on the exchange.
