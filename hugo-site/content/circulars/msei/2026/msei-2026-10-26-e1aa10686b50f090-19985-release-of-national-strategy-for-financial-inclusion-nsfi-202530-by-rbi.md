## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, emphasizing the simplification of digital interfaces, use of vernacular languages, and AI-powered translation tools to promote financial inclusion. As advised by SEBI, MSEI trading members are required to adopt these measures and develop AI/ML-based public query systems for securities market-related aspects.

## Key Points

- RBI released the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30.
- Chapter IV, paragraph I.6(f) emphasizes simpler digital interfaces, vernacular languages, and AI-powered translation tools like Bhashini.
- Chapter IV, paragraph IV.6(a) highlights the development of AI & ML-based public query systems on common banking and finance-related aspects.
- Trading Members must facilitate the development of AI & ML-based public query systems on common securities market-related aspects via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of RBI's National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30 guidelines for trading members regarding digital accessibility and AI tools.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must simplify digital interfaces for ease of use.
- Address language barriers by incorporating vernacular languages.
- Integrate AI-powered language translation platforms such as Bhashini.
- Develop and maintain an AI & ML-based public query system for common securities market-related queries on websites and social media extensions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 26, 2026
- NSFI Reference Date: December 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances customer accessibility, transparency, and digital inclusion across trading member platforms through modern AI/ML and vernacular solutions.
