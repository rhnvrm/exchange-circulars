## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in ISIN for Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW) consequent to the sub-division of existing equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW)
- Old ISIN: INE0CJD01029
- New ISIN: INE0CJD01037
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of existing equity shares from One Equity share of Rs. 5/- into Five Equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- Effective Date (Ex-Date): November 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update reflecting the corporate action executed by the company.

## Compliance Requirements

Members of the Exchange are advised to take note of the change in ISIN and ensure that trades executed on and from the ex-date are settled under the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 03, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date for New ISIN: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members and clearing participants regarding the security identification number (ISIN) change for KMEW shares.
