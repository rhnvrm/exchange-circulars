## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to an Hon'ble SAT order dated June 10, 2026, staying the debarment of Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions and associated entities. SEBI instructed via email dated November 17, 2026, to defreeze the entities instantly.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous circular MSE/ID/11392/2022 and SEBI orders debarring specific entities from accessing the securities market.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated June 10, 2026, stayed the debarment.
- SEBI vide email dated November 17, 2026, advised that the entities be defreezed instantly in compliance with the SAT order.
- Affected entities: M/s. Cash Cow Broking & Advisory Solutions, Mr. Shailendra Sen, Mr. Amit Jain, and Mr. Chirag Sharma.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of SEBI debarment directions against the specified entities in accordance with the SAT order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the stay and defreezing of the mentioned entities.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: June 10, 2026
- SEBI Email Date: November 17, 2026
- Circular Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Permits the previously debarred entities to access the securities market and resume operations pursuant to the stay order.
