## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Oneclick Logistics India Limited (OLIL) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective November 26, 2026. Companies admitted under this category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Oneclick Logistics India Limited
- **Symbol:** OLIL
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE0OPS01011
- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Category:** Permitted to Trade (Capital Market Segment)

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities under the "Permitted to Trade" category without a formal listing agreement with MSEI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should note that companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not provide full disclosures as they have not executed a listing agreement with the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading options for members on the MSEI capital market segment by enabling trading in Oneclick Logistics India Limited shares.
