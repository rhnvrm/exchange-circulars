## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the shift of securities between the Trade-to-Trade settlement segment (series: BE) and the Rolling settlement segment (series: EQ) as part of ongoing surveillance measures to ensure market safety and investor protection.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure 1 are shifted from rolling settlement (series: EQ) to trade-to-trade settlement (series: BE) with a 5% price band.
- Securities listed in Annexure 2 are shifted from trade-to-trade settlement (series: BE) to rolling settlement (series: EQ).
- No netting off will be allowed for securities in the trade-to-trade segment.
- The transfer is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading and settlement mechanism for specified securities is modified to trade-to-trade or rolling segments based on surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must note the change in settlement segments and ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules (no netting off) for applicable securities starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience changes in liquidity and settlement terms, requiring market participants to adjust trading and clearing workflows accordingly.
