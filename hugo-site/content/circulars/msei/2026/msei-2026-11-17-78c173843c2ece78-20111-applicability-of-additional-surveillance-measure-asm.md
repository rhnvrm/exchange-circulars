## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Effective November 18, 2026, specific securities have been shortlisted under Stage I of the Long-Term ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Shortlisted securities under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I (w.e.f. November 18, 2026):
  - **E2E Networks Limited** (ISIN: INE255Z01027, Symbol: E2E)
  - **SG Mart Limited** (ISIN: INE385F01024, Symbol: SGMART)
- Applicable rate of margin is set to 100% effective November 20, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (shifted to Stage IV) will be moved from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective November 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of identified securities into the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework.
- Implementation of stringent margin requirements (100% margin) to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in the affected securities starting November 20, 2026.
- Trading members should note the shift of applicable securities between segments as per surveillance criteria.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Shortlisting:** November 18, 2026
- **Applicability of 100% Margin:** November 20, 2026 (on open positions as of November 19, 2026, and new positions onward)
- **Segment Shift (if applicable):** November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The inclusion of securities under the Long-Term ASM framework increases margin requirements to 100%, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for E2E Networks Limited and SG Mart Limited. This measure is purely preventative and intended to safeguard market integrity and protect investors from excessive price volatility.
