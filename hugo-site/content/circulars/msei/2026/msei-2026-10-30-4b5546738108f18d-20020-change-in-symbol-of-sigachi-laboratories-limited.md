## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has notified members regarding a change in the trading symbol for Sigachi Laboratories Limited. Effective November 2, 2026, the symbol will be updated from SIGACHI to SIGALABS.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sigachi Laboratories Limited
- ISIN: INE368H01018
- Old Symbol: SIGACHI
- New Symbol: SIGALABS
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Segment: Capital Market

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an administrative update to the company's trading symbol on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Exchange members must update their trading terminals and back-office systems with the new symbol (SIGALABS) for Sigachi Laboratories Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading and order routing systems with the new symbol.
