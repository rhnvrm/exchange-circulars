## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) will conduct a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Member participation is voluntary but encouraged to test systems and connectivity.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract margin obligations, pay-in, pay-out, or create rights and liabilities.
- Connectivity via Leased Line and Internet (SSL VPN URL: https://trading.msei.in) supported.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational mock drill.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and CTCL members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session from all trading software and verify setup configurations.

## Important Dates

- **Leased Line File Download / SFTP Setup:** Friday, October 30, 2026 (after trading hours) / Anytime through internet.
- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Log-in Time:** 08:15 hrs onwards.
- **Normal Market Open Time:** 09:00 hrs.
- **Normal Market Close Time:** 15:30 hrs.
- **Trade Modification End Time:** 15:40 hrs.

## Impact Assessment

- No financial or market impact, as mock trades do not carry margin or settlement obligations. Helps members test Disaster Recovery site connectivity and trading software readiness (TWS/MAT Version 11.6.27.0).
