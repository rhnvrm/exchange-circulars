## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for certain securities. Shortlisted securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Shree Rama Multi-Tech Limited (SHREERAMA) is shortlisted under ESM Stage - I effective October 28, 2026.
- Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 29, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM framework measures including 100% margin requirements and transfer of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and account for the shifting of specified securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 28 / October 29, 2026
- Margin Applicability: w.e.f. October 29, 2026 on open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted securities must manage increased margin obligations and trade settlement under the Trade-for-Trade segment.
