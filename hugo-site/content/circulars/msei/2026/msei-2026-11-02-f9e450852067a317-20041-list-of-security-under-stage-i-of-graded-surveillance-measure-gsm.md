## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20041/2026 dated November 02, 2026, announcing the shifting of specific securities to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 03, 2026 (Tuesday).
- **Securities Involved:** Munoth Financial Services Ltd. (MUNOTHFI) and Mahesh Developers Limited (MAHESH).
- **Trading Restrictions:** Trading in these securities will be available with a margin rate of 100% and a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in the annexure are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures under GSM Stage I.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to exercise adequate precaution while trading in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margins (100%) and restricted price bands (5% or lower) for MUNOTHFI and MAHESH aim to curb excessive volatility and protect investors, impacting liquidity and trading behavior for these counters.
