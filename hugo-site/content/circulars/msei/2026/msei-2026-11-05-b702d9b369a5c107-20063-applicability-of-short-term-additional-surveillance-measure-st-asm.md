## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for select securities effective November 05, 2026, with applicable surveillance actions taking effect from November 08, 2026. Additionally, several securities have been excluded from the ST-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion:** Securities including Sadbhav Infrastructure Project Limited (SADBHIN), Supreme Infrastructure India Limited (SUPREMEINF), and Bridge Securities Ltd (BRIDGESE) have satisfied criteria for ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Surveillance Actions:** Applicable margin rate is set to 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 08, 2026, on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- **Exclusions:** Securities such as Augmont Enterprises Limited, One Point One Solutions Limited, Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited, Suyog Telematics Limited, Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited (TTML), and Dhvija Finance Limited are excluded from the ST-ASM framework.
- **Nature of Action:** ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures and is purely for market surveillance without being construed as an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I provisions and margin requirements for listed securities under surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 50% margin requirement (capped at 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective November 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin/Actions:** November 08, 2026 (on open positions as on November 06, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume for these specific equities.
