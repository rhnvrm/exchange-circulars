## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20103/2026 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM are subject to specific trading restrictions, margin requirements, and segment shifts effective November 16 and November 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage-I Inclusions:** Securities such as Prozone Realty Limited (PROZONER) and Transworld Shipping Lines Limited (TRANSWORLD) satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract minimum 100% margin on open and new positions w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- **Stage Shifts:** Securities moving from ESM Stage-II to Stage-I include Capital Trust Limited (CAPTRUST), Veljan Denison Limited (VELJAN), and Jaipan Industries Ltd (JAIPAN) w.e.f. November 16, 2026.
- **Segment Shift:** Securities qualifying under ESM shift from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 17, 2026.
- **Exclusions:** 10 securities including 3i Infotech Limited (3IINFOLTD), Aarti Surfactants Limited (AARTISURF), and Rama Steel Tubes Limited (RAMASTEEL) are moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under ESM Stage-I with 100% margin requirements.
- Shifting of qualifying securities from Rolling segment (EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE).
- Reclassification of securities between Stage-I and Stage-II, and removal of eligible securities from the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the 100% margin requirements on open positions as of November 16, 2026, and new positions created from November 17, 2026.
- Ensure compliance with Trade-for-Trade settlement requirements for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Stage Shifts & Exclusions / Stage II Periodic Call Auction):** November 16, 2026
- **Effective Date (ESM Stage-I Inclusions & Segment Shifts):** November 17, 2026 (with margins applicable on open positions as on November 16, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in affected securities must manage liquidity and margin requirements accordingly. The surveillance measures are preventive market oversight actions and do not reflect adverse actions against the companies.
