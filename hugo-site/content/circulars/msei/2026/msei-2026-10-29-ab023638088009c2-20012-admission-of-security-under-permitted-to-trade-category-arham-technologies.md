## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Arham Technologies Limited for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Arham Technologies Limited
- Symbol: ARHAM
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE0L2Y01011
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026
- Note: Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard admission under permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the trading availability and ISIN details for member operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members of the exchange to trade in equity shares of Arham Technologies Limited under the permitted category.
