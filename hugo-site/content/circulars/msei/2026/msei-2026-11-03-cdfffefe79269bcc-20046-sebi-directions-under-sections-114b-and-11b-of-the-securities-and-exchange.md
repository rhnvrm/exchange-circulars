## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the revocation of debarment and market restraint against Akar Laminators Limited and its three directors (Mr. Hitesh Ravindra Shah, Mr. Hasmukhbhai Tarachand Sheth, and Mr. Vinodbhai Tarachand Sheth). This follows confirmation from SEBI dated September 04, 2026, that all investor complaints lodged against the entity on SCORES have been successfully disposed of.

## Key Points

- Revocation of debarment and market restraint for Akar Laminators Limited and its directors.
- Original restraint was imposed vide SEBI Order No. WTM/KMA/OIAE/328/12/2010 dated December 08, 2010, for pending investor grievances.
- Noticees relieved: Mr. Hitesh Ravindra Shah (PAN: ABIPS5836K), Mr. Hasmukhbhai Tarachand Sheth (PAN: AAIPS6247B), and Mr. Vinodbhai Tarachand Sheth (PAN: AAIPS6243F).
- SEBI confirmed via mail on September 04, 2026, that all SCORES complaints are resolved.

## Regulatory Changes

Revocation of restrictions on buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in securities market imposed under Sections 11(4)(b) and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants are advised to take note that the debarment has been lifted with immediate effect.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 03, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: September 04, 2026
- Original Order Date: December 08, 2010

## Impact Assessment

Restores normal market access for Akar Laminators Limited and its directors following full resolution of investor grievances.
