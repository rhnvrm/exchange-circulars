## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the change in series of equity shares for various Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs). Pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars, specified securities under the permitted to trade category are being transferred from the Trade to Trade segment (BE) to the Rolling segment (EQ), while others will continue in the BE segment under call auction.

## Key Points

- Equity shares of Kama Holdings, Maharashtra Scooters, and TVS Holdings move from BE to Rolling segment (EQ) effective November 17, 2026.
- Equity shares of GFL Limited, Nalwa Sons Investments, and Pilani Investment move to EQ effective November 18, 2026.
- Sil Investments moves to EQ on November 20, 2026, and Kalyani Investment Company moves on November 25, 2026.
- Williamson Magor & Company and Haryana Capfin will continue in the Trade to Trade segment (BE) under call auction until further notice.
- Changes are in line with SEBI circular reference no. SEBI/CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of trading segments for designated investment and holding companies from Trade to Trade (BE) to Rolling (EQ) based on exchange and regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the segment changes, effective dates, and ISIN details to ensure appropriate order routing and settlement processing.

## Important Dates

- November 17, 2026: Effective date for KAMAHOLD, MAHSCOOTER, and TVSHLTD.
- November 18, 2026: Effective date for GFLLIMITED, NSIL, and PILANIINVS.
- November 20, 2026: Effective date for SILINV.
- November 25, 2026: Effective date for KICL.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding or trading shares moving to the Rolling segment (EQ) benefit from normal rolling settlement and enhanced liquidity. Securities remaining in the BE segment (WILLAMAGOR, HARYNACAP) continue to face trade-to-trade restrictions and call auction mechanisms.
