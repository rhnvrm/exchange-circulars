## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20083/2026 dated November 09, 2026, detailing the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, moved between stages, or excluded from the framework effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. November 12, 2026, on all open positions as on November 11, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I include MM Forgings Limited (MMFL), Lorenzini Apparels Limited (LAL), Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Limited (KALAMANDIR), Ramky Infrastructure Limited (RAMKY), Entero Healthcare Solutions Limited (ENTERO), Kothari Industrial Corporation Ltd (KOTIC), Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited (SYMBIOTEC), Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited (SMT), Banka Bioloo Limited (BANKA), and SC Agrotech Ltd (SCAGRO).
- Spencer's Retail Limited (SPENCERS) moved from Short-Term ASM Framework Stage II to Stage I.
- Securities excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework include AVRO India Limited (AVROIND), Phoenix International Ltd (PHOENXINTL), and Hero Motors Limited (HEROMOTORS).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) provisions in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure a minimum margin of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on open positions as of November 11, 2026, and all new positions created from November 12, 2026 onwards for securities under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action: November 11, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will attract heightened margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and short-term price volatility for the affected scrips.
