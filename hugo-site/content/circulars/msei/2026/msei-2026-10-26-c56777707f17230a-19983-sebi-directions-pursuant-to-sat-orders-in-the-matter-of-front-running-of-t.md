## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to Hon'ble SAT orders (Appeal No. 07 of 2026 and Appeal No. 08 of 2026) dated August 10, 2026, concerning front running of orders of a big client (certain family trusts) by certain entities.

## Key Points

- Hon'ble SAT modified the impugned order qua appellants, reducing the penalty to ₹5 Lakhs on each appellant and debarment period to one year.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated October 23, 2026, that the appellants have paid the required penalty amounts.
- Members are advised to comply with the attached SAT orders and take note of the modifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Debarment periods and financial penalties for the specified entities/appellants involved in front running have been revised/reduced as per SAT orders.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must take note of the modified debarment and penalty directions and ensure compliance with SEBI and SAT directives.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: August 10, 2026
- SEBI Email Confirmation Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures enforcement of modified SAT rulings regarding penalties and debarment periods for entities involved in front running.
