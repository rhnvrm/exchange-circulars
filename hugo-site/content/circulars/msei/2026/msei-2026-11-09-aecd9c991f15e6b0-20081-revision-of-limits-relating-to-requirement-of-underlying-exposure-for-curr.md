## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular notifying the revision of limits relating to the requirement of underlying exposure for currency derivatives contracts. This update follows SEBI circular ref. no. HO/(561)2026-MRD-POD2/I/25177/2026 dated November 05, 2026, and RBI A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated October 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Threshold limits for taking positions in foreign exchange derivatives involving the Indian Rupee (INR) without establishing underlying exposure have been revised.
- Domestic clients and Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) may take long or short positions in currency derivatives contracts involving INR, without having to establish underlying exposure, up to a single limit of USD 5 million equivalent of notional value across all currency pairs combined.
- FPIs must ensure that their short positions across all contracts in FCY-INR pairs do not exceed USD 5 million.
- To take long positions in excess of USD 5 million in FCY-INR pairs, FPIs must have underlying exposure in Indian debt or equity securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifies Clause 5.3.3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024, incorporating RBI's updated risk management and threshold limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and FPIs must monitor and maintain positions within the stipulated USD 5 million unhedged threshold across INR currency pairs.
- FPIs exceeding USD 5 million in long positions must maintain valid underlying exposure in Indian securities as prescribed.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standardizes position limits and hedging requirements for market participants trading in currency derivatives, ensuring alignment between exchange regulations, SEBI guidelines, and RBI directives.
