## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Capital Market segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026, to test trading system readiness and connectivity.

## Key Points

- **Date**: Saturday, November 21, 2026.
- **Scope**: Equity Capital Market segment covering morning block deals, pre-open, normal market, special pre-open session for IPO/relisted securities, call auction illiquid session, and closing auction.
- **Mock Trades**: Trades executed during the session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and will not create rights or liabilities.
- **Participation**: Active participation is encouraged for all trading members and CTCL members across all trading software.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational mock trading exercise.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to log into the Member Administrator Terminal (MAT) and Trader Workstation (TWS) to download setup files (`MSE-EQTWSCLIENT.msi` and `MSE-EQMEMADMIN.msi`) from the SFTP server or internet.
- Setup download is available starting Friday, November 20, 2026, after trading hours.
- Members must configure TWS/MAT settings according to their connectivity mode (Leased Line or Internet).

## Important Dates

- **Setup Download Date**: November 20, 2026 (post trading hours)
- **Mock Trading Date**: November 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Zero financial or settlement impact. The session helps members test infrastructure, software upgrades, and connectivity ahead of live trading.
