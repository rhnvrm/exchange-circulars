## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in ONGC due to the declaration of a dividend of Rs. 08/- per equity share. The effective ex-date for the adjustment is November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: ONGC (Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited)
- Dividend Amount: Rs. 08/- per equity share (Face Value: Rs. 05/- each)
- Ex-Date: November 12, 2026
- Futures Contracts Adjustment: The base price of futures contracts on November 12, 2026, will be the reference rate (daily mark-to-market settlement price) less the aggregate dividend amount of Rs. 08/-.
- Options Contracts Adjustment: The full dividend value of Rs. 08/- will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date.
- Rounding: Revised strike/base prices will be rounded off to the nearest tick size, and lot sizes to the nearest integer.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Processed in compliance with Exchange Rules, Bye-Laws, Regulations, and relevant SEBI Master Circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised F&O strike and base prices for ONGC effective November 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Ex-Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational adjustment ensuring fair value and contract continuity for market participants holding F&O positions in ONGC.
