## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20160 regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) on specified securities traded on the exchange. This surveillance framework is designed to monitor and curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in specific securities.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) on designated securities.
- Application of increased margin requirements (up to 100% applicable margin) for scrips placed under ST-ASM stages.
- Price band restrictions (such as 5% or applicable lower bands) applied to affected securities to mitigate sudden price fluctuations.
- Periodic review of securities for inclusion, staging, or exclusion from surveillance measures based on ongoing market behavior.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities meeting objective surveillance parameters for price variation, volatility, and volume are subjected to ST-ASM framework requirements as mandated by SEBI and Stock Exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin collections and restricted price bands for scrips listed under ST-ASM.
- Clients trading in affected scrips must maintain requisite margins to avoid shortfall penalties.

## Important Dates

- Effective from the date specified in the circular during November 2026 trading sessions.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance measures help curb speculative excess and excessive volatility, protecting retail investors while temporarily increasing margin burdens and trading restrictions on affected securities.
