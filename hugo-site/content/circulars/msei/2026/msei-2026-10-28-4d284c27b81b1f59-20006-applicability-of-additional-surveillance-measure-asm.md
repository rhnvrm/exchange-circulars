## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities meeting specific criteria.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Rhetan TMT Limited (ISIN: INE0KKN01029, Symbol: RHETAN).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 02, 2026 on all open positions as on October 30, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage I framework provisions for shortlisted securities.
- Imposition of 100% margin on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain 100% margin on open positions as on October 30, 2026, and all new positions created from November 02, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ASM Action: November 02, 2026.
- Open Position Cut-off Date: October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) for trading in Rhetan TMT Limited may impact liquidity and trading volume for the security.
