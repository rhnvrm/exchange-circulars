## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has notified members regarding the change in ISIN for Galaxy Agrico Exports Limited (GALAGEX) consequent to the sub-division of existing equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Galaxy Agrico Exports Limited
- Symbol: GALAGEX
- Old ISIN: INE803L01016
- New ISIN: INE803L01024
- Purpose: Sub-division of existing equity shares from One Equity share of Rs.10/- into Ten Equity shares of Re.1/- each
- Effective Date (Ex-Date): October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- In continuation of Exchange Circular No.: MSE/LIST/19902/2026 dated October 13, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants are required to update their trading and back-office systems with the new ISIN for trades executed on and from October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures seamless clearing, settlement, and trading operations for GALAGEX under its new ISIN following the stock sub-division.
