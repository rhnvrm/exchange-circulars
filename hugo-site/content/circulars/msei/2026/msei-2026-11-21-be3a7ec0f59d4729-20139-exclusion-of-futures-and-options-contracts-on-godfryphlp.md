## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Godfrey Phillips India Limited (GODFRYPHLP), referencing SEBI Circular Ref. No. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 regarding stock eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- New expiry contracts for GODFRYPHLP will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contracts.
- Existing contracts for November 2026, December 2026, and January 2027 will remain available for trading until their respective expiry dates, and new strikes may be introduced in existing contracts.
- No F&O contracts will be available for trading in GODFRYPHLP starting January 27, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI guidelines on stock eligibility for derivative segments, resulting in the exclusion of F&O contracts for GODFRYPHLP.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the cessation timeline and manage existing client positions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- November 21, 2026: Circular issuance date.
- January 27, 2027: Effective date when all trading in F&O contracts for GODFRYPHLP ceases.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding derivative positions in GODFRYPHLP must square off or settle positions prior to the final expiry and phase-out date.
