## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20057 regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) across eligible securities.

## Key Points

- Implementation of ST-ASM (Stage I and Stage II) frameworks.
- Designed to safeguard investors and curb excessive volatility and price variation in shortlisted securities.
- Margin requirements revised as per the applicable surveillance stage.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisted securities are subjected to applicable Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure frameworks in accordance with regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements and surveillance parameters for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: November 4, 2026
- Effective Date: As specified in the circular and exchange guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements (up to 100% or applicable rates) on shortlisted securities help mitigate excessive speculation and market risk.
