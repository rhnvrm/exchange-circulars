## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20171 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The measure is designed to monitor and curb excessive price volatility and speculative trading in micro and small-cap securities.

## Key Points

- Implementation of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for shortlisted securities.
- Shortlisting based on objective parameters including price variation, volatility, and high-low range.
- Imposition of stricter trading controls such as 100% margins and reduced price bands.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of vulnerable scrips into the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages with heightened surveillance controls.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must comply with 100% upfront margin requirements on open positions for affected securities.
- Transactions are subject to Trade-for-Trade settlement or Periodic Call Auction depending on the assigned ESM stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: December 01, 2026
- Issuance Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market safety and investor protection by curbing speculative volatility, while tightening liquidity and trading flexibility for the shortlisted securities.
