## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has issued a formal press release on February 10, 2026, refuting inaccurate assertions in a Reuters article titled "Exclusive: India regulator stalls new exchanges' entry into options market, sources say". MSE clarified that it faces no regulatory restrictions from SEBI regarding equity derivatives or other approved products.

## Key Points

- MSE explicitly refutes claims that SEBI has imposed gaps or halted equity derivatives launches.
- Confirms MSE is a fully operational, SEBI-recognized stock exchange meeting all infrastructure standards.
- Reaffirms commitment to advancing cutting-edge systems, including the appointment of market makers to bolster liquidity in the Equity Segment.
- Urges Reuters to retract misleading references and restore market confidence.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The circular is a clarification and rebuttal regarding existing regulatory standing.

## Compliance Requirements

- None specified for market participants.

## Important Dates

- February 10, 2026: Date of press release and clarification.

## Impact Assessment

- Reassures market participants regarding MSE's operational and regulatory compliance status, mitigating uncertainty caused by speculative media reports.
