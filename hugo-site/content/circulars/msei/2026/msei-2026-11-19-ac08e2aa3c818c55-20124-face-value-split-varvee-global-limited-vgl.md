## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the face value split of equity shares for Varvee Global Limited (VGL). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will be sub-divided from Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 2/- each, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Limited
- Symbol: VGL
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- Existing ISIN (INE273D01027) of Rs. 5/- paid-up will not be valid for transactions on or after November 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of corporate action involving the sub-division of equity shares under the permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the face value change and ensure that transactions on or after the effective date comply with the updated terms.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date / ISIN Invalidation Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the old ISIN ceases on November 27, 2026, requiring market participants to update their trading systems and records for VGL.
