## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20169 regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities traded on the exchange. The framework aims to safeguard investor interests and curb excessive price volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities are shortlisted for ASM based on objective parameters including price variation, client concentration, and volume variation.
- The measure serves as a pre-emptive alert to market participants regarding heightened volatility in specific scrips.
- Shortlisting under ASM is not an adverse regulatory action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance mechanisms such as higher margin requirements and/or price band restrictions on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must take note of the applicable surveillance measures and margin obligations when trading in securities listed under ASM.

## Important Dates

- Effective date as specified in the circular and accompanying annexures for November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market safety and transparency while moderating excessive speculation in volatile scrips.
