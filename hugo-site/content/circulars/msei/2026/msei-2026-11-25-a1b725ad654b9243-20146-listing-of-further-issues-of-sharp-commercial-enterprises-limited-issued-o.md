## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and commencement of trading of further issues of equity shares of Sharp Commercial Enterprises Limited issued on a preferential basis, effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Sharp Commercial Enterprises Limited
- **Symbol:** SCEL
- **ISIN:** INE023F01013
- **Security Description:** Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under preferential basis.
- **Number of Securities:** 32,06,000 shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 15,90,201 to 47,96,200
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10 / Rs. 10
- **Effective Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Lock-in Upto:** 15-June-2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action listing circular under MSEI trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes, lot sizes, and lock-in details specified for the scrip.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date / Listing Date:** November 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** June 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of newly allotted preferential shares on the MSEI capital market segment for investors and stakeholders.
