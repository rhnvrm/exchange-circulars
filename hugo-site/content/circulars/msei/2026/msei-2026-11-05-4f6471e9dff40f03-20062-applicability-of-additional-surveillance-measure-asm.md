## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework for specific securities effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- EBGNG (Gng Electronics Limited) has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.
- CNL (Creative Newtech Limited) has been moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I to Stage - II.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 09, 2026, on all open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion and stage migration under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the application of 100% margins on open positions as of November 08, 2026, and all new positions created from November 09, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM actions: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin requirement: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (100%) on affected scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted securities.
