## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure Stage-I.

## Key Points

- Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited (Symbol: TFCILTD, ISIN: INE305A01023) has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created from November 06, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying criteria for Long-Term ASM Stage-I are subject to a 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain 100% margin on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities as specified.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Inclusion:** November 04, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% Margin Requirement:** November 06, 2026 (on open positions as of November 05, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in Tourism Finance Corporation of India Limited (TFCILTD) will be subject to stringent margin requirements, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants holding open positions.
