## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Bondada Engineering Limited (BONDADA) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective from October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bondada Engineering Limited
- Symbol: BONDADA
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE0Q8P01029
- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed about the admission and operational details of the security for trading on the Capital Market Segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading members of MSEI to trade in equity shares of Bondada Engineering Limited under the permitted category.
