## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I, while some have been excluded or listed under consolidated stages effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. October 30, 2026, on all open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created from October 30, 2026 onwards.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Dhvija Finance Limited (DHVIJAFIN), Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited (SMT), Suyog Telematics Limited (SUYOG), and Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited (TTML).
- Securities excluded from Short-Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 29, 2026: Rays Of Belief Limited (MOMSBELIEF) and Rita Finance And Leasing Limited (RFLL).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company or entity.

## Regulatory Changes

The exchange has updated the list of securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in continuation of earlier circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure the applicable margin of 50% or higher (capped at 100%) is maintained for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action / Framework: October 29, 2026 / October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for positions in these scrips.
