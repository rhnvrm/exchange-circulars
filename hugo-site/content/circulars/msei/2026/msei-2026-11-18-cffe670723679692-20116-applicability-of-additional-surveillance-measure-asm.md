## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20116/2026 regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Skyline Millars Ltd has been shortlisted under the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.

## Key Points

- Skyline Millars Ltd (ISIN: INE178E01026, Symbol: SKYLMILAR) shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 23, 2026 on all open positions as on November 20, 2026 and new positions created from November 23, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long-Term ASM) Stage-I provisions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin maintenance on open and new positions for affected securities starting November 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Inclusion Date: November 19, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 20, 2026
- Margin Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements (100%) impact trading liquidity and capital allocation for securities placed under Long-Term ASM Stage-I.
