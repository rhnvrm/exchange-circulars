## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in its Capital Market segment from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, October 31, 2026. All members and CTCL participants are encouraged to actively participate. Trades generated during this mock session do not carry any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out.

## Key Points

- **Session Date:** Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Segment:** Capital Market.
- **Environment:** Disaster Recovery (DR) Site.
- **Financial Implications:** None (no margin obligation, pay-in, or pay-out).
- **Participant Scope:** All members and CTCL participants using MAT and TWS.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and CTCL participants are advised to actively participate in the mock trading session across all trading software to test system readiness and DR connectivity.

## Important Dates

- **File Download (Leased Line):** Friday, October 30, 2026, after trading hours.
- **Mock Trading Session:** October 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures operational resilience and validates trading connectivity from the Disaster Recovery site without any real financial or settlement impact.
