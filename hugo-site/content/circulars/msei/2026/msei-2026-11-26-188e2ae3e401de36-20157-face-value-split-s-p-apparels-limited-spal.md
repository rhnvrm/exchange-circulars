## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a face value split (sub-division) of equity shares for S. P. Apparels Limited (SPAL). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will change from Rs. 10/- each to Rs. 2/- each, effective from December 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: S. P. Apparels Limited (SPAL)
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- Ratio / Sub-Division: One equity share of Rs. 10/- each into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Old ISIN (INE212I01016 of Rs. 10/- paid up) will become invalid for transactions from the effective date onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members are notified of the change in face value and the invalidation of the existing ISIN under permitted-to-trade category provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take note of the new face value and ensure that transactions executed on or after December 10, 2026, adhere to the updated ISIN and share structure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- Effective Date: December 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members holding or trading shares of S. P. Apparels Limited must account for the five-for-one stock split and the corresponding adjustment in share price and quantity in their portfolios and systems.
