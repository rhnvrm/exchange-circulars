## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a reminder for all eligible trading members (Type – III) regarding the submission of the System Audit Report for the half-year period ending September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all eligible trading members (Type – III).
- Submission deadline is November 30, 2026.
- Electronic submission must be made through the Inspection.os Portal.

## Regulatory Changes

None (reminder of existing reporting requirements under previous circulars).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit the System Audit Report electronically via the Inspection.os Portal on or before the due date to avoid any penalties or disciplinary actions as prescribed in Annexure 1.2 of Exchange circular no. MSE/INSP/18897/2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Previous Reference Date: October 21, 2026 (MSE/INSP/19956/2026)
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Failure to submit the report by the deadline may invite penalties or disciplinary action as per Exchange guidelines. Trading members must ensure timely compliance through the designated portal.
