## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSE) has successfully implemented an upgrade to its system and market infrastructure effective January 27, 2026, aimed at enhancing performance, scalability, and operational resilience. Additionally, the exchange is in the process of appointing market makers to strengthen liquidity and market depth in its Equity Segment.

## Key Points

- Successfully upgraded system and market infrastructure effective January 27, 2026.
- Upgrade aims to improve performance, scalability, and operational resilience.
- Exchange is currently appointing market makers to boost liquidity and market depth in the Equity Segment.
- Announcement regarding the effective date for market makers will be made shortly.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes announced; this is an operational infrastructure and market development update.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance or reporting actions required by market participants based on this announcement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Infrastructure Upgrade: January 27, 2026
- Market Maker Appointment Date: To be announced shortly

## Impact Assessment

The infrastructure upgrade enhances overall platform reliability, while upcoming market maker appointments are expected to improve liquidity and market depth in MSE's Equity Segment.
