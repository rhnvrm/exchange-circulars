## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced adjustments to Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Tech Mahindra Limited (TECHM) on account of a 1:1 bonus issue declared by the company.

## Key Points

- Corporate Action: Bonus issue in the ratio of 1:1.
- Adjustment Factor: Defined as (A+B)/B, resulting in an adjustment factor of 2 for a 1:1 bonus.
- Market Lot Size: Revised from 600 to 1200 (Old market lot size x Adjustment factor).
- Futures and Strike Prices: Adjusted in accordance with SEBI guidelines and the calculated adjustment factor, rounded off to the nearest tick size.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI guidelines for corporate action adjustments in derivative contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the contract adjustments, revised market lot size, and price adjustments for TECHM derivative contracts.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 30, 2026
- Circular Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Derivative positions in TECHM will be adjusted automatically as per exchange guidelines effective from November 30, 2026, impacting margin calculations, strike prices, and lot sizes.
