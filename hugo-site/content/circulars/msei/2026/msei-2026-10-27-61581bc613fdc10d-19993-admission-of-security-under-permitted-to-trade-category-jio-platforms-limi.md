## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Jio Platforms Limited (JPL) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jio Platforms Limited
- **Symbol:** JPL
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE0BJO01010
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Segment:** Capital Market Segment

## Regulatory Changes

Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the admission and ensure compliance with trading and settlement procedures on the Capital Market Segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides MSEI trading members and investors access to trade equity shares of Jio Platforms Limited under the permitted category.
