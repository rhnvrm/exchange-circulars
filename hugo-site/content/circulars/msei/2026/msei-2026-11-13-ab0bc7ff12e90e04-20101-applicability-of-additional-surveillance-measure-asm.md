## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) on securities satisfying inclusion criteria, effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margin applicable w.e.f. November 18, 2026 on all open positions as on November 17, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Stage IV) shifted from Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities include Flexituff Ventures International Limited, Aerpace Industries Ltd, Taylormade Renewables Ltd, and Tahmar Enterprises Ltd.
- Action is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of Long-Term ASM surveillance framework actions including enhanced margin requirements and segment migration.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure 100% margin compliance for open and new positions in shortlisted securities starting November 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Open Positions Cut-off Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance/Margin/Segment Shift: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience tightened liquidity and risk management controls due to 100% margin requirements and potential trade-for-trade settlement.
