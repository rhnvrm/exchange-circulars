## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20008/2026 dated October 28, 2026, detailing the applicability and updates of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specified securities.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage-I attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on October 29, 2026, and new positions created from October 30, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective October 30, 2026.
- Highway Infrastructure Limited (HILINFRA) has been shortlisted under ESM Stage-I effective October 29, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, including SANGINITA, BROOKS, VLEGOV, PENINLAND, ALFATRAN, GAUDIUMIVF, HPBL, and LASTMILE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities under ESM are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Mandatory minimum 100% margin requirement applicable on open and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement and account for the shift of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- October 29, 2026: Effective date for shortlisting under ESM Stage-I.
- October 30, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the affected securities face stricter surveillance norms, including 100% margins and mandatory Trade-for-Trade settlement, which may influence liquidity and short-term trading dynamics.
