## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the change in name and trading symbol for Sugal & Damani Share Brokers Ltd, which is under the permitted to trade category.

## Key Points

- Old Name: Sugal & Damani Share Brokers Ltd
- New Name: Sugal Earthen Spaces Ventures Limited
- Old Symbol: SUGALDAM
- New Symbol: SUGALESL
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular reflects corporate branding and identification changes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading terminals, client records, and back-office systems with the new name and symbol (SUGALESL) effective from November 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Ensure accurate referencing of the new trading symbol and company name across all systems.
