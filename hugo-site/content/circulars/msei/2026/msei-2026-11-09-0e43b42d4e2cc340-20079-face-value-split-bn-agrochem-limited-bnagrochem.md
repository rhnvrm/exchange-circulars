## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular informing members about the face value split (sub-division) of equity shares of BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM). The face value is being changed from Rs. 10/- per share to Re. 1/- per share, effective from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BN Agrochem Limited
- Symbol: BNAGROCHEM
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per equity share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- per equity share (One equity share subdivided into ten equity shares)
- Existing ISIN (INE00HZ01011) will not be valid for transactions on or after November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Sub-division of existing equity shares under the permitted to trade category on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the share split and ensure transactions comply with the updated face value and valid ISIN requirements effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Implementation Date: November 16, 2026
- Circular Date: November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors dealing in BNAGROCHEM must update their records for the new face value of Re. 1/- and ensure old ISIN INE00HZ01011 is not used for transactions executed on or after November 16, 2026.
