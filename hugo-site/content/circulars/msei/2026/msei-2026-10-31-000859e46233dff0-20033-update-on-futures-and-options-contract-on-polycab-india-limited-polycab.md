## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has withdrawn its earlier circular regarding the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB). This follows the NCLAT order dated October 30, 2026, which allowed the company's appeal and set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order concerning IBC proceedings.

## Key Points

- Earlier circular MSE/TRD/19900/2026 dated October 13, 2026, excluding F&O contracts on POLYCAB, stands withdrawn.
- NCLAT set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026, regarding IBC proceedings against Polycab India Limited.
- Futures and options contracts with January 2027 expiry will be made available for trading effective November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal of the exclusion of F&O contracts on POLYCAB in light of the NCLAT order setting aside the IBC proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the withdrawal of the exclusion and take note of the availability of F&O contracts for trading.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026:** NCLAT order allowing the appeal and MSEI circular issuance.
- **November 02, 2026:** F&O contracts with January 2027 expiry available for trading.

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading in derivatives for POLYCAB as the legal hurdle concerning IBC proceedings has been addressed by NCLAT's order.
