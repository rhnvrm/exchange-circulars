## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of four companies under the “Permitted to Trade” category, effective October 27, 2026. These companies do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- Equity shares of four companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category.
- Effective date: October 27, 2026.
- Companies admitted: Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited, Khazanchi Jewellers Limited, Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited, and Triveni Power Transmission Limited.
- Companies under this category do not execute listing agreements with the Exchange and do not provide full disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes; routine admission of securities under the permitted to trade framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note that these securities are traded under the permitted category without formal listing agreements or full disclosures from the issuing companies.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities on the Capital Market Segment of MSEI for investors interested in the admitted securities.
