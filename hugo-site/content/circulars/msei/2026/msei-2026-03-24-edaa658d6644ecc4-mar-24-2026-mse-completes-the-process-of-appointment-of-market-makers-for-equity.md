## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has successfully completed the appointment of Market Makers for its Equity Segment. Effective April 1, 2026, MSE will initiate liquidity enhancement measures to deepen market participation and foster a competitive trading ecosystem.

## Key Points

- Successful completion of market maker appointments for the Equity Segment.
- Liquidity enhancement and deepening measures set to begin on April 1, 2026.
- Aims to strengthen market depth, support efficient price discovery, and enhance the overall trading experience.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes announced; this is an operational development by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

None specified for market participants beyond normal trading compliance.

## Important Dates

- **March 24, 2026**: Announcement date of market maker appointments.
- **April 1, 2026**: Effective date for initiating liquidity enhancement measures.

## Impact Assessment

Expected to improve market liquidity, enhance price discovery, and provide a more vibrant trading environment across MSE's equity segment.
