## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has extended the due date for the submission of Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26 to December 31, 2026. This extension follows the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) circular extending the ROC filing timeline for financial statements.

## Key Points

- Due date for submission of Annual Returns for Financial Year 2025-26 is extended to December 31, 2026.
- Trading members registered with multiple exchanges (NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX, NCDEX) must submit their Annual Returns and Annual Confirmations to NSE pursuant to Exchange Circular MSE/INSP/17523/2025.
- Exclusive MSE members must submit their returns via email to annualreturns@mse.co.in.
- Non-submission within the extended due date will attract disciplinary actions as stipulated in Circular MSE/INSP/18897/2026.
- Members are required to maintain required Networth at all points of time and submit revised Networth certificates within stipulated timelines to avoid penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- MCA Circular Ref. 05/2026 dated October 24, 2026, extended the due date for filing Financial Statements with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) to December 31, 2026, leading to this corresponding extension by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submit Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 on or before December 31, 2026.
- Ensure appropriate channel submission (NSE for multi-exchange members, email to annualreturns@mse.co.in for exclusive MSE members).
- Maintain Networth requirements and report shortfalls promptly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Extended Due Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Grants trading members necessary relief and additional time to complete and file their Annual Returns and financial statements for FY 2025-26 without facing immediate penal actions.
