## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular extending the display period for investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook until December 31, 2026, pursuant to SEBI communication. Additionally, effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 01, 2026, are extended to November 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook must continue to be displayed by Trading Members on their trading applications and websites until December 31, 2026.
- The schedule prescribed in paragraph 2(c) of the earlier Exchange circular MSE/MEM/19849/2026 remains unchanged.
- Effective dates specified under paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of the SEBI Circular dated October 01, 2026, stand extended to November 15, 2026.
- Messages must be displayed in a bilingual format (Hindi and English).

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of display deadlines for investor awareness messages under Project Jagrook to December 31, 2026.
- Extension of specific effective dates under SEBI circular to November 15, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Trading Members must ensure continuous display of investor awareness messages in bilingual format (Hindi and English) on trading apps and websites in accordance with revised timelines.

## Important Dates

- October 06, 2026: Reference circular MSE/MEM/19849/2026.
- November 09, 2026: Circular issuance date.
- November 15, 2026: Extended effective date for paragraphs 4.1(b) and 4.2(b) of SEBI circular.
- December 31, 2026: Extended display period deadline for Project Jagrook messages.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact on Trading Members, requiring continuation of existing investor awareness message displays with extended timelines.
