## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20150 regarding the applicability of surveillance measures on companies with high promoter encumbrance in accordance with Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Applies to companies with high levels of promoter share encumbrance / pledging.
- Imposes enhanced margin requirements and surveillance controls to manage market volatility and risk.
- Securities shortlisted are reviewed periodically by the exchange.
- Measures are purely for market surveillance and risk management, not adverse actions against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- Application of additional margins on trading in equity and equity derivatives segments for affected securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must monitor applicable margin rates for shortlisted securities.
- Ensure adherence to enhanced margin and risk management requirements specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified in the circular annexures (typically within 2-3 trading days).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced risk management for trading in securities with high promoter encumbrance.
- Safeguards market participants against potential volatility arising from encumbered promoter shares.
