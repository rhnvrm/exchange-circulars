## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. Trades resulting from this session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- Mandatory participation for members with approved algorithmic software using all approved user IDs for Algo trading.
- Setup files can be downloaded from the SFTP server starting Friday, November 13, 2026, after trading hours.
- Connectivity options include Leased Line (Dedicated/Normal) and Internet modes.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; conducted in terms of Rules, Bye-Laws & Regulations of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members having approved algorithmic software must mandatorily participate in the mock trading session using all approved user IDs for Algo trading.
- All members are advised to actively participate to test connectivity and CTCL application software.
- Setup instructions include installing VC++ Redistributable 2008 and updating TWS/MAT client versions (Version 25.0.9.0).

## Important Dates

- **Setup Download Date:** November 13, 2026 (after trading hours)
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 14, 2026
- **Pre-open Session:** 09:00 hrs - 09:07/08 hrs*
- **Order Entry Period:** 09:15 hrs - 15:40 hrs
- **Trade Modification End Time:** 15:50 hrs

## Impact Assessment

Operational exercise with no financial, settlement, or risk impact on members since mock trades do not create real rights, liabilities, or margin obligations.
