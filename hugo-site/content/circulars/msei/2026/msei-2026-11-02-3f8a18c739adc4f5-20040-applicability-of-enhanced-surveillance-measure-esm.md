## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the specified criteria are included under the ESM framework, attracting a minimum 100% margin on open and new positions, and shifting from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I (such as Le Lavoir Limited) satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage-I.
- Minimum 100% margin applies on all open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions created from November 04, 2026.
- Qualifying securities shift from Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 04, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages.
- Migration of securities from Rolling segment (EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE).
- Mandatory 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions with Periodic Call Auctions for applicable stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM measures: November 03, 2026 / November 04, 2026.
- Open positions reference date: November 03, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and settlement for affected securities (including Le Lavoir Limited, Sanginita Chemicals, Brooks Laboratories, Vlee Governance, Peninsula Land, Alfa Transformers, Gaudium IVF, HCP Plastene Bulkpack, Last Mile Enterprises, and Mid East Portfolio Management) are impacted due to Trade-for-Trade restrictions and enhanced margin requirements.
