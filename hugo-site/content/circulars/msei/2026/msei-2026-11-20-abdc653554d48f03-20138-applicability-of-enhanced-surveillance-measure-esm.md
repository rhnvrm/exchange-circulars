## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20138/2026 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities have been shortlisted for inclusion in Stage-I, movement between stages, and removal from the ESM framework effective November 23, 2026, and November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 25, 2026, on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 25, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 23, 2026.
- Multiple securities are moving between stages or moving out of the ESM framework entirely.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) restrictions and margin requirements.
- Mandatory shifting of securities between Rolling (EQ) and Trade-for-Trade (BE) segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for applicable securities.
- Market participants must adhere to segment shifts, price band restrictions, and periodic call auctions where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Stage shifts: November 23, 2026.
- Effective Date for ESM Stage-I margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: November 25, 2026 (based on open positions as of November 23, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities is subject to stringent margin requirements and settlement restrictions, helping mitigate excessive volatility and safeguarding market integrity.
