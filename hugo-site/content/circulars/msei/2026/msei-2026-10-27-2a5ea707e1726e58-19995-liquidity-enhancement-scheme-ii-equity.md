## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Ltd (MSE) has notified the inclusion of Jio Platforms Limited under the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme II - Equity (LES-II) with effect from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Jio Platforms Limited (JPL)
- ISIN: INE0BJO01010
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026
- Circular No: MSE/BD/19995/2026

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of an additional security under the existing Liquidity Enhancement Scheme II - Equity framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are advised to take note of the inclusion of JPL under LES-II.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates enhanced liquidity support and trading participation for Jio Platforms Limited on the exchange.
