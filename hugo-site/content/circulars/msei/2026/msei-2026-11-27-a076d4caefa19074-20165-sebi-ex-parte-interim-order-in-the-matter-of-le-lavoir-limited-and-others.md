## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified members regarding SEBI Ex-Parte Interim Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-2/32866/2026-27 dated November 26, 2026, issued in the matter of Le Lavoir Limited and Others.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 2 (Ashok Dilipkumar Jain) is restrained from accessing the securities market and prohibited from buying, selling, or dealing in securities.
- Open derivative positions of Noticee No. 2 may be squared off within 3 months from the date of the order.
- Noticees No. 3 to 15 are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in the scrip of Le Lavoir Limited (LLL) until further orders.
- List of noticees includes Le Lavoir Limited, Ashok Dilipkumar Jain, Kajal Ashok Jain, Viha Ashok Jain, Sachin Vishnu Kapse, Ghantiram Foods Private Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

Directions issued under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 restricting market access and trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure strict compliance with the interim order and prevent prohibited trading activities by the named noticees.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: November 26, 2026
- Circular Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on named noticees and trading accounts dealing in Le Lavoir Limited scrip or derivative positions.
