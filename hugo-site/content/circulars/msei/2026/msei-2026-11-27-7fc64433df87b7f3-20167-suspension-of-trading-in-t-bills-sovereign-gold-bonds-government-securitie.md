## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBDEC26) under the permitted to trade category, effective December 29, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: SGBDEC26
- Series: GB
- ISIN: IN0020180389
- Reason: Suspension of trading on Primary Stock Exchange on account of redemption.
- Effective Date: December 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational suspension following the redemption schedule of the Sovereign Gold Bond.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must take note of the suspension and ensure no trades are executed in SGBDEC26 on or after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: December 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is a scheduled redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds.
