## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Steel Strips Wheels Limited (SSWL) has been shortlisted and included under the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Steel Strips Wheels Limited (ISIN: INE802C01033, Symbol: SSWL) included in Long-Term ASM Stage - I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 30, 2026 on all open positions as on October 29, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Long-Term ASM framework to deter excessive price volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in securities shortlisted under the Long-Term ASM framework from the specified effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: October 30, 2026 (on open positions as on October 29, 2026 and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (100%) for securities under Long-Term ASM Stage - I may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
