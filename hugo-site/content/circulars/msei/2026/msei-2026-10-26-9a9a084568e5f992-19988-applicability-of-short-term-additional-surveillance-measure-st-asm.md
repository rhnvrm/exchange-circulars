## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the shortlisting of securities under Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I as well as exclusions from the framework, effective from October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Om Power Transmission Limited, Kabsons Industries Ltd, and Orient Tradelink Ltd.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework include West Coast Paper Mills Limited, Sejal Glass Limited, Advance Agrolife Ltd, Kesar Petroproducts Ltd, and Star Delta Transformers Ltd.
- Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Ltd is included in the consolidated list under Stage I.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures and enhanced margin requirements under the ST-ASM framework to monitor market volatility and safeguard market participants.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must implement the required 50% to 100% margin rates on open and new positions for securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 27, 2026 / October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements on shortlisted securities may impact short-term liquidity and trading activity for affected stocks.
