## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective November 06, 2026. Osia Hyper Retail Limited has been shortlisted under Stage I of the framework.

## Key Points

- Osia Hyper Retail Limited (Symbol: OSIAHYPER, ISIN: INE06IR01021) has been shortlisted under ASM for Companies as per IBC Stage I effective November 06, 2026.
- No other securities are reported moving out of the IBC Framework or between other surveillance stages in this circular.
- The framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchange.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance parameters and does not constitute an adverse action against the entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification of previous exchange circulars (MSE/SURV/6417/2018, MSE/SURV/11848/2022, and MSE/SURV/13038/2023) regarding Additional Surveillance Measure for companies undergoing or subject to IBC proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the surveillance measures and monitor trading activity in the shortlisted securities accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities like OSIAHYPER will be subject to heightened surveillance and applicable ASM margin requirements under the IBC framework.
