## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that 7 securities will be shifted from the rolling segment (series: EQ) to the Trade-to-Trade segment (series: BE) with a price band of 5% or lower, effective November 13, 2026, as part of surveillance reviews.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026 (Friday).
- **Action:** Shifting of 7 securities to Trade-to-Trade settlement (series: BE) with a 5% price band.
- **Settlement:** Securities will not be available in the rolling segment; settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- **Securities Involved:** AFFORDABLE ROBOTIC & AUTOMATION LIMITED, BANKA BIOLOO LIMITED, LORENZINI APPARELS LIMITED, ALFA TRANSFORMERS LTD, JINDAL CAPITAL LTD, Raghuvir Synthetics Ltd, and Vantage Knowledge Academy Ltd.
- **Nature of Action:** Implemented purely on account of market surveillance to ensure market safety and investor protection, and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in specified securities moved to Trade-to-Trade segment (series: BE).
- Price band restricted to 5%.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that transactions in the affected securities are executed and settled on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased settlement friction and restricted price movement (5% band) for the listed securities, impacting short-term liquidity and trading operations.
