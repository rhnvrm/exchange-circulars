## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20130/2026 dated November 19, 2026, regarding updates to the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective November 20, 2026, Compuage Infocom Limited is moving out of the IBC framework.

## Key Points

- No new securities have been shortlisted under Stage 0, Stage I, or stage transitions of the IBC ASM framework.
- Compuage Infocom Limited (Symbol: COMPINFO, ISIN: INE070C01037) is excluded from the IBC framework effective November 20, 2026.
- The surveillance measures operate in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification of previous exchange circulars (MSE/SURV/6417/2018, MSE/SURV/11848/2022, and MSE/SURV/13038/2023) concerning Additional Surveillance Measures for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must take note of the exclusion of Compuage Infocom Limited from the IBC ASM framework and update their compliance and trading systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The removal of Compuage Infocom Limited from the IBC framework lifts the specific surveillance restrictions associated with it, returning the security to normal trading parameters.
