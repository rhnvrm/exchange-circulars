## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular extending the timeline for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars pursuant to SEBI's circular dated October 30, 2026. The deadline for conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms and remediating findings has been extended to January 31, 2027.

## Key Points

- Extension granted based on representations received from Regulated Entities (REs).
- New extended deadline for accessibility audit and remediation: January 31, 2027.
- Applies to all trading members and regulated entities under SEBI's purview regarding the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI circular HO/53/12/13(2)2026-ITD5-M2IPC/I/24856/2026 dated October 30, 2026, extends the timeline for appointment of IAAP auditor, conduct of accessibility audit for digital platforms, and remediation of audit findings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and regulated entities must ensure that accessibility audits of all digital platforms are completed and findings remediated on or before January 31, 2027.
- All other provisions of the original SEBI and Exchange circulars remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 31, 2026
- **Extended Deadline:** January 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational relief to trading members and regulated entities by granting additional time to comply with digital accessibility standards and audit requirements without immediate penalty risks.
