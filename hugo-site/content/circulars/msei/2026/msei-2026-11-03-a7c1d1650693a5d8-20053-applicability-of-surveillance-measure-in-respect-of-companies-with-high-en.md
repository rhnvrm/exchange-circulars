## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures for companies with high ‘Encumbrance’ pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 35% margin, while securities in Annexure II are excluded from the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Annexure I will attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Margin applies w.e.f. November 06, 2026, on all open positions as on November 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities listed in Annexure II move out of the surveillance framework effective November 04, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011 relating to high promoter/share encumbrance.
- Additional margin requirements imposed on specific flagged securities to manage market volatility and risk.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of the minimum 35% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities effective November 06, 2026.
- Company representations regarding placement in the framework must be submitted by 5:00 PM on November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Exclusions (Annexure II): November 04, 2026
- Cut-off for Company Representations: November 04, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Open Positions Reference Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Margins (Annexure I): November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the affected securities (including Polyplex, Sagar Cements, Vedanta group entities, etc.) will experience increased margin obligations, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage in the equity and derivatives segments.
