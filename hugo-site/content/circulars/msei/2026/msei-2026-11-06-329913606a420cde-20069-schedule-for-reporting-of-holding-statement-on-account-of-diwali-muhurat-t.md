## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/INSP/20069/2026 outlining the reporting schedule for holding statements on account of the Diwali Muhurat Trading Session held on Sunday, November 08, 2026. All trading members are required to adhere to the revised due dates specified for trade dates around the festive period.

## Key Points

- Covers reporting schedule for trade dates from November 06, 2026, to November 10, 2026.
- Due dates for submission are assigned to November 09, 2026, and November 11, 2026.
- Applicable to all trading members across all segments.
- Queries can be directed to the Inspection Department via phone or email.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule circular for holiday trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure timely submission of holding statements in accordance with the specified reporting schedule to maintain compliance.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Date 06-November-2026:** Due Date 09-November-2026
- **Trade Date 07-November-2026:** Due Date 09-November-2026
- **Trade Date 08-November-2026 (Diwali Muhurat Trading):** Due Date 09-November-2026
- **Trade Date 09-November-2026:** Due Date 11-November-2026
- **Trade Date 10-November-2026:** Due Date 11-November-2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on trading members, ensuring orderly reporting and compliance workflows during the Diwali holiday schedule.
