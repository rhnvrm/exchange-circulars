## Summary

In continuation of Exchange circular no. MSE/SURV/20018/2026 dated October 29, 2026, the Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that four securities are eligible to move out of the trade-for-trade category.

## Key Points

- **Effective Action:** Four scrips are moved out of the trade-for-trade settlement category.
- **Applicable Scrips:** ASAHI SONGWON COLORS LTD (ASAHISONG), CEENIK EXPORTS INDIA LTD (CEENIK), SC AGROTECH LTD (SCAGRO), and SCAN STEELS LTD (SCANSTL).
- **Reference:** Follows up on previous circular MSE/SURV/20018/2026 dated October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities previously subjected to trade-for-trade settlement restrictions are restored to normal trading categories as per exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revision in settlement category for the specified scrips and ensure adherence to trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves liquidity and normalizes trading mechanics for the affected scrips by removing trade-for-trade restrictions.
