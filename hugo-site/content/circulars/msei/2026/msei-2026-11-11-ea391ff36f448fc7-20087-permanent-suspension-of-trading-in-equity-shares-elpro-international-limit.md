## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the permanent suspension of trading and withdrawal of admission to dealings in equity shares of Elpro International Limited (ELPROINTL) pursuant to voluntary delisting on the primary stock exchange under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.

## Key Points

- Trading in equity shares of Elpro International Limited will not be available under the permitted to trade category on the Exchange effective November 17, 2026.
- Admission to dealings (Permanent Suspension) in the security will be withdrawn effective November 25, 2026.
- Reason: Voluntary Delisting on Primary Stock Exchange pursuant to SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of voluntary delisting under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021 leading to cessation of trading on secondary/permitted exchanges like MSEI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the suspension timelines and cease trading activities in ELPROINTL accordingly.

## Important Dates

- November 11, 2026: Circular issuance date.
- November 17, 2026: Trading ceases under permitted to trade category.
- November 25, 2026: Admission to dealings withdrawn (Permanent Suspension).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding shares of Elpro International Limited on MSEI will no longer be able to trade the security on the exchange after the specified effective dates.
