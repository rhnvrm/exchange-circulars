## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that Kabirdas Investments Ltd (KABIRDAS) will be transferred from the Trade to Trade segment (BE) to the Rolling segment (EQ) with effect from November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Kabirdas Investments Ltd.
- Symbol: KABIRDAS
- ISIN: INE268D01019
- Segment Change: Trade to Trade (BE) to Rolling (EQ)
- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The transfer is executed in terms of provisions of the Rules, Bye-Laws and Regulations of the Exchange, continuation of circular MSE/LIST/2907/2015 dated April 16, 2015, and pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. SEBI/CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the change in series and update their trading terminals and systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Equity shares of Kabirdas Investments Ltd will trade in the Rolling segment (EQ) starting November 25, 2026, facilitating normal rolling settlement.
