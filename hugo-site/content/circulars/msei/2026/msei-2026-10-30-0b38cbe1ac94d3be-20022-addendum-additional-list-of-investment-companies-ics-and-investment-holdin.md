## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued an addendum to circular MSE/LIST/19954/2026 dated October 21, 2026, announcing an additional list of Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) under the permitted to trade category that are eligible for special call-auctions.

## Key Points

- Additional securities included: Kama Holdings Limited (KAMAHOLD) and Haryana Capfin Limited (HARYNACAP).
- If the call auction is not successful on day 1, it will continue on the next day until price discovery is achieved.
- Effective date of the circular is November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Addition of securities to the special call-auction mechanism under the permitted to trade category in alignment with primary stock exchanges and SEBI circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the additional list of securities eligible for special call-auctions and ensure necessary operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates price discovery for additional investment companies and holding companies under the permitted to trade segment on the exchange.
