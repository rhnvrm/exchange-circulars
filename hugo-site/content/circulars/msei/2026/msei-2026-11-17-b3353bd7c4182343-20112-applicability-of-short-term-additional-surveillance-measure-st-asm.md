## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20112/2026 detailing the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities. Applicable margin rate is set to 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Delphi World Money Ltd, Dhanlaxmi Bank Ltd, Baazar Style Retail Ltd, Cochin Minerals & Rutiles Ltd, Kumbhat Financial Services Ltd, Paragon Finance Ltd, and Rodium Realty Ltd.
- Kabsons Industries Ltd is excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework.
- Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Ltd is included in the consolidated list under Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on open positions and new positions.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of ST-ASM Stage I framework measures on specified securities to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must maintain the mandated 50% or higher margin requirements on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective from November 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM measures / Exclusions: November 18, 2026 / November 19, 2026
- Circular Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to stricter margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
