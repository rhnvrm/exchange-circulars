## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20051/2026 dated November 03, 2026, announcing the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities. Applicable margin rate is set at 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective November 05, 2026, on open positions as of November 04, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Shortlisted Securities**: Includes Agi Infra Limited (AGIIL), Borosil Limited (BOROLTD), Innovision Limited (INNOVISION), Marsons Limited (MARSONS), and Fundviser Capital (India) Ltd (FUNDVISER) effective November 04, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements**: 50% margin or existing margin, whichever is higher (max 100%), effective November 05, 2026 on all open positions as on November 04, 2026 and new positions.
- **Exclusions**: Om Power Transmission Limited (OMPOWER) and Orient Tradelink Ltd (ORIENTTR) are excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework effective November 04, 2026.
- **Consolidated List**: Includes securities like Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Ltd (GRANDOAK, Stage I) and Bacil Pharma Ltd (BACPHAR, Stage II).
- **Nature of Action**: Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of Exchange Circular nos. MSE/SURV/6932/2018, MSE/SURV/9816/2020, and MSE/SURV/11774/2022 regarding Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM).

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to the 50% margin requirement on open positions as of November 04, 2026, and new positions created from November 05, 2026 onwards for securities under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: November 03, 2026
- **Effective Date (Shortlisting & Exclusions)**: November 04, 2026
- **Margin Applicability Date**: November 05, 2026 (on open positions as of November 04, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to stricter margin surveillance norms, impacting liquidity and positioning requirements for market participants holding or trading these shares.
