## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20107 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular outlines securities subjected to enhanced surveillance to curb abnormal price volatility and protect investor interests.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework on identified securities.
- Securities are placed under surveillance stages such as Trade-for-Trade (T2T) settlement and restricted price bands.
- Designed to enhance market integrity and safeguard retail investors from excessive speculation in micro and small-cap companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities meeting specific surveillance criteria are moved into the ESM framework.
- Price bands and settlement mechanisms are adjusted according to the applicable ESM stage.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with trading restrictions and settlement mechanisms applicable to securities under ESM.
- Clients and investors should be informed of the trade-for-trade settlement and price band limitations.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: As specified in the circular (November 16, 2026 or immediate implementation).

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volume in affected securities may be moderated due to trade-for-trade settlement and price band restrictions, curbing speculative price movements.
