## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI order no. QJA/MN/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32818/2026-27 dated October 30, 2026. SEBI has prohibited and restrained M/s. EKI Energy Services Ltd and several key individuals from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities for specified debarment periods.

## Key Points

- M/s. EKI Energy Services Ltd is debarred for 2 months (PAN: AACCE6986E).
- Mr. Manish Kumar Dabkara is debarred for 12 months (PAN: AMPPD4048N).
- Mr. Naveen Sharma is debarred for 5 months (PAN: BHPPS2657J).
- Ms. Sonali Sheikh is debarred for 1 month (PAN: CXMPS1805G).
- Mr. Mohit Kumar Agarwal is debarred for 8 months (PAN: AMAPK8905E).
- Noticees with open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts as on the date of the order may square off/close out positions within 3 months or upon contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions before the close of trading on the order date is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI enforcement action prohibiting the noticees from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities directly or indirectly.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take note of the SEBI debarment order and ensure compliance with trading restrictions for the listed noticees.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 30, 2026
- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Derivative Close-out Window: Within 3 months from the order date or contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

Members and market participants must restrict the named entities from securities market access in compliance with the SEBI and MSEI circular.
