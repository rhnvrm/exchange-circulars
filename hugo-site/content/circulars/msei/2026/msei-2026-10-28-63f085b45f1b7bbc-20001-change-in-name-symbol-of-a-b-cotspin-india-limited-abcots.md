## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a change in the name and trading symbol for A B Cotspin India Limited under the permitted to trade category.

## Key Points

- Old Name: A B Cotspin India Limited
- New Name: ABC Industries India Limited
- Old Symbol: ABCOTS
- New Symbol: ABCIND
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the change and update their records and trading systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading symbols and company names in member back-office and trading terminals.
