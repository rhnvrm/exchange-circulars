## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a face value split for the equity shares of Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW). The existing face value and paid-up value of Rs. 5/- per share is being sub-divided into five equity shares of Re. 1/- each, effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (KMEW)
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- per share (5 shares for every 1 existing share)
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Old ISIN INE0CJD01029 will not be valid for transactions on or after November 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Sub-division of existing equity shares under the permitted to trade category on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note that transactions on or after November 06, 2026, cannot be conducted using the old ISIN (INE0CJD01029).
- Ensure systems and trading terminals are updated to reflect the new face value and ISIN changes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date / Implementation Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of KMEW will receive 5 shares of Re. 1/- each for every 1 share of Rs. 5/- held as of the record/effective date.
- Trading members need to update order entry and settlement parameters to account for the new ISIN and face value.
