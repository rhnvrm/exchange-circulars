## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20084/2026 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Several securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage-I or shifting between stages effective November 11 and November 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions and new positions created effective November 12, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Securities shifting to Stage II will trade under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction mechanism effective November 11, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework restrictions in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.
- Mandatory shifting of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trading restrictions on the specified securities.
- Trading members should review open positions and updated consolidated lists in Annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage-II price band and periodic call auction changes: November 11, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage-I margin requirements and segment shifting: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and trading restrictions apply to listed securities under ESM Stage-I and Stage-II, potentially affecting liquidity and execution for the impacted scrips.
