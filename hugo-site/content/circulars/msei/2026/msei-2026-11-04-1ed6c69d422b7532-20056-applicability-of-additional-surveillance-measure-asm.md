## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has released Circular No. 20056 regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. This circular outlines the updated list of securities subjected to surveillance measures to protect investor interests and curb excessive price volatility.

## Key Points

- Details the list of securities shortlisted under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM).
- Imposes heightened surveillance and margin requirements on specified securities.
- Aimed at alerting market participants and investors regarding abnormal price or volume movements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities identified under ASM are subject to periodic review and specific surveillance actions in alignment with regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable margin requirements and trading restrictions for securities listed under ASM.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 4, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and potential trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and short-term price volatility.
