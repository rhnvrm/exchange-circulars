## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for Interarch Building Solutions Limited (INTERARCH) due to the sub-division of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Interarch Building Solutions Limited
- **Symbol:** INTERARCH
- **Old ISIN:** INE00M901018
- **New ISIN:** INE00M901026
- **Purpose:** Sub-division of existing equity shares from one equity share of Rs. 10/- into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the new ISIN for trades in the equity shares of Interarch Building Solutions Limited effective from November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Ex-Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades executed on and from November 30, 2026, will reflect the new ISIN corresponding to the sub-divided equity shares (face value Rs. 2/- each).
