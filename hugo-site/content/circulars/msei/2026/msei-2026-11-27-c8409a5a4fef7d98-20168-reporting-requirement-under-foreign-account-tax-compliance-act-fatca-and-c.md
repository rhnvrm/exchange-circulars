## Summary

SEBI and MSEI have issued guidelines regarding revised reporting requirements and due diligence under the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) and Common Reporting Standard (CRS), updated through the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and Income-Tax Rules, 2026 (effective April 1, 2026). All Reporting Financial Institutions (RFIs) and registered intermediaries must incorporate these revised rules into their onboarding, KYC, and Form 166 XML generation processes.

## Key Points

- Replaces the previous regime (Income-Tax Act, 1961 read with Rules, 1962) with the Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026.
- Governed by Section 508 of the Income Tax Act, 2025 read with Rules 238, 239, and 240, and Form 166.
- Registered intermediaries (RFIs) must update self-certification collection, controlling person identification, account classification, and Form 166 XML generation.
- FATCA and CRS certifications obtained from clients must be uploaded onto KRA systems.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition from Income-Tax Act, 1961 to Income-Tax Act, 2025 and IT Rules, 2026.
- Specific adherence required to Rules 238 to 240 and Form 166 for Automatic Exchange of Information (AEOI) reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Update self-certification collection procedures to capture validity indicators.
- Update controlling person identification systems (capturing roles such as settlor, trustee, protector, beneficiary, or other).
- Revise account classification systems to capture account type and pre-existing/new status.
- Update Form 166 XML generation software to include all new mandatory fields to prevent portal validation errors.
- Upload client FATCA/CRS certifications onto KRA systems.

## Important Dates

- IT Rules, 2026 Effective Date: April 1, 2026
- Circular Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must immediately review and upgrade their technology systems, KYC frameworks, and compliance processes to align with Form 166 requirements and avoid validation errors on the Income Tax portal.
