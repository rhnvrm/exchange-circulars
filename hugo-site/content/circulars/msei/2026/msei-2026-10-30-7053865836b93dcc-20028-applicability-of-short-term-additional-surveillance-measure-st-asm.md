## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I, Stage II, stage migrations, and exclusions for specified securities effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 03, 2026, on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I: Sakuma Exports Limited, Tera Software Limited, Welspun Investments & Commercials Limited, Mahesh Developers Limited, Sangam Finserv Ltd.
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Spencer's Retail Limited.
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage II to Stage I: Avro India Limited (AVROIND).
- Excluded securities from ST-ASM Framework: Dcm Shriram Industries Ltd., BN Agrochem Ltd, Generic Engineering Construction And Projects Ltd, Transworld Shipping Lines Limited, Lagnam Spintex Limited, Jaykay Enterprises Ltd, Achyut Healthcare Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) frameworks on selected scrips to monitor market volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Margin applicability date: November 03, 2026 (on open positions as on Nov 02, 2026 and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for listed securities under ST-ASM may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
