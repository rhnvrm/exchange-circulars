## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I for certain securities effective October 30, 2026. Securities shortlisted include Pashupati Cotspin Limited and Advit Jewels Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. October 30, 2026: Pashupati Cotspin Limited (ISIN: INE124Y01028) and Advit Jewels Limited (ISIN: INE1SJO01012).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 03, 2026 on all open positions as on November 02, 2026 and new positions created from November 03, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I framework for identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must apply 100% margin on open positions and new positions for the affected securities effective November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ASM Shortlisting: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 03, 2026 (on open positions as on November 02, 2026 and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements (100%) on affected securities will impact trading liquidity and leverage for market participants holding positions in Pashupati Cotspin and Advit Jewels.
