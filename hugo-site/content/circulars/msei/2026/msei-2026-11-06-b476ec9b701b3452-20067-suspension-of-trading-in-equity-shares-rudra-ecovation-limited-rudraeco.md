## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Rudra Ecovation Limited (RUDRAECO) under the permitted to trade category with effect from November 06, 2026. This action follows the primary stock exchange's action on account of a Scheme of Amalgamation pursuant to the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Chandigarh Bench.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rudra Ecovation Limited
- Symbol: RUDRAECO
- Segment: Capital Market
- Circular No: MSE/LIST/20067/2026
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Reason: Action taken as per Primary Stock Exchange on account of Scheme of Amalgamation pursuant to Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in equity shares of Rudra Ecovation Limited (RUDRAECO) is no longer available under the permitted to trade category on MSEI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange are advised to take note that trading in equity shares of Rudra Ecovation Limited is suspended w.e.f. November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and investors holding RUDRAECO shares cannot execute trades in the scrip on MSEI following the suspension linked to the amalgamation scheme.
