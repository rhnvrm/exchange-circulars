## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced adjustments to Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Infosys Limited (INFY) following the declaration of a dividend of Rs. 26/- per equity share (face value Rs. 5/- each). The effective ex-date for the adjustment is November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Infosys Limited (INFY)
- Dividend Amount: Rs. 26/- per equity share (Face Value: Rs. 5/-)
- Ex-Date: November 3, 2026
- Futures Adjustment: Base price on November 3, 2026, will be the daily mark-to-market settlement price less the aggregate dividend amount of Rs. 26/-.
- Options Adjustment: Rs. 26/- will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date.
- Rounding: Prices/strike prices rounded to the nearest tick size; lot sizes rounded to the nearest integer.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments executed in accordance with SEBI Master Circular and Exchange Rules/Bye-Laws regarding corporate actions in derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised base prices for futures and strike prices for options effective November 3, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date for Dividend & F&O Adjustment: November 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Derivative contracts on INFY will reflect the dividend adjustment to maintain fair pricing and prevent arbitrage opportunities on the ex-dividend date.
