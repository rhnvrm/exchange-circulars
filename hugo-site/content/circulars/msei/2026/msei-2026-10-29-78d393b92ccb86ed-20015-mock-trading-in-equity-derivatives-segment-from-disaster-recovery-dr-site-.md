## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Members are encouraged to actively participate to test connectivity and trading operations.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 31, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives Segment from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site.
- Pre-open session starts at 09:00 hrs with random closure in the last two minutes; normal market runs from 09:15 hrs to 15:40 hrs.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract any margin obligation, pay-in, or pay-out, and create no rights and liabilities.
- Setup files for Version 25.0.9.0 available on the SFTP/Extranet server.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and CTCL members are advised to actively participate in the mock trading session across all trading software and terminals.
- Leased line and Internet users must follow the specified configuration steps and use SSL VPN login via https://trading.msei.in where applicable.

## Important Dates

- File Download (Leased Line): October 30, 2026 (after trading hours)
- Mock Trading Session Date: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill for system resilience with zero financial, margin, or settlement impact on members.
