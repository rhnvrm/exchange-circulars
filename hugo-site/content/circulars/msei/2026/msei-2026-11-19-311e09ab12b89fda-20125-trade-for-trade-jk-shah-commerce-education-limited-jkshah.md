## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that trading in the security of J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited (JKSHAH) will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) effective December 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited
- Symbol: JKSHAH
- Action: Transferred from 'BE' series (trade for trade) to 'EQ' series (rolling segment)
- Effective Date: December 03, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange circular no. MSE/LIST/20110/2026 dated November 17, 2026 and SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The transfer follows standard exchange procedures and SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) in alignment with the Primary Stock Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with trading norms for the security in the rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: December 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normalizes trading for J.K. Shah Commerce Education Limited on the Exchange by moving it from trade-for-trade to the regular rolling settlement segment.
