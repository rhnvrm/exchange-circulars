## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20058 regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) on specific securities. This framework is designed to enhance market integrity, safeguard investor interests, and curb excessive volatility in scrips meeting specified surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities identified under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) are subjected to special surveillance actions.
- Imposes restrictions to prevent abnormal price movements and speculative trading.
- Regular periodic review of securities included in the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance frameworks in alignment with regulatory guidelines set by SEBI and stock exchanges.
- Application of trading restrictions such as trade-for-trade settlement or other risk containment measures as applicable under ESM stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, trading members, and investors must take note of the securities placed under ESM and adhere to the applicable trading and margining norms.
- Brokers are required to inform clients about the trading restrictions and margin requirements associated with ESM securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 4, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring increases transparency and reduces unwarranted volatility in listed securities.
- Trading liquidity for scrips under ESM may be temporarily constrained due to stricter margin requirements and trading safeguards.
