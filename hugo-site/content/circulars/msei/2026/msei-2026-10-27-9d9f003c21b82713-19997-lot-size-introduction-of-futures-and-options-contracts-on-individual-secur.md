## Summary

In continuation of Exchange circular Ref. No. MSE/TRD/19918/2026 dated October 15, 2026, the Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the market lot sizes for Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on six individual securities, available for trading from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Coromandel International Limited (COROMANDEL)**: Lot size of 300
- **City Union Bank Limited (CUB)**: Lot size of 2325
- **Data Patterns (India) Limited (DATAPATTNS)**: Lot size of 125
- **Karur Vysya Bank Limited (KARURVYSYA)**: Lot size of 1550
- **Navin Fluorine International Limited (NAVINFLUOR)**: Lot size of 75
- **Syrma SGS Technology Limited (SYRMA)**: Lot size of 325

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of derivative contracts (Futures and Options) on specified individual securities along with their corresponding market lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the specified lot sizes and ensure compliance with trading parameters for the respective derivative contracts.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables market participants to trade Futures and Options on the newly included securities with standardized lot sizes, expanding derivatives trading opportunities in the equity segment.
