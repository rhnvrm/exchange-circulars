## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Ltd (MSE) has issued a public caution against fraudulent documents and notices circulating with forged SEBI letterheads, logos, and names, demanding direct payment of Securities Transaction Tax (STT) for releasing funds under the Finance Act, 2004.

## Key Points

- Fraudulent notices misuse SEBI's name, logo, and letterhead to demand direct STT payments.
- SEBI never issues letters demanding direct payment of taxes like STT from individuals for fund releases.
- All genuine SEBI circulars are hosted exclusively on SEBI's official website (www.sebi.gov.in).
- Investors are advised not to respond, share personal details, or make payments based on such suspicious communications.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an investor awareness and fraud advisory notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Remain vigilant and verify the authenticity of any communication claiming to be from SEBI or MSE.
- Report any suspicious communications immediately to SEBI or MSE through official grievance redressal mechanisms.

## Important Dates

- Press Release Date: February 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Protects retail investors from financial fraud and unauthorized market practices by raising awareness about forged tax notices.
