## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited (BCSSL) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the “Permitted to Trade” category effective November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited
- Symbol: BCSSL
- Series: BE
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE0XFY01011
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026
- Companies admitted under the “Permitted to Trade” category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard admission under permitted category.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the admission of the security for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members to trade in the equity shares of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited under the permitted category.
