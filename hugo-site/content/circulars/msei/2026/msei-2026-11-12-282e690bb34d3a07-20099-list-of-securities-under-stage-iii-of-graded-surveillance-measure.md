## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that Mahesh Developers Limited (ISIN: INE01MO01013, Symbol: MAHESH) is being moved to Stage III of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Affected: Mahesh Developers Limited (Symbol: MAHESH, ISIN: INE01MO01013).
- Surveillance Stage: Moved to Stage III of GSM.
- Effective Date: November 13, 2026.
- Trading Restrictions: Moved to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE) with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Trading Frequency: Permitted only once a week (every Monday / first trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD): 100% of trade value collected from the buying trading member and retained until further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

In continuation of earlier circulars (MSEI/SURV/4942/2017 and MSE/SURV/8388/2019), surveillance measures are intensified for specified securities exhibiting abnormal price/volume behavior to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while executing trades in the affected security and ensure compliance with the 100% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date of GSM Stage III: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for MAHESH is severely restricted due to weekly trading frequency, 100% cash-equivalent ASD deposit requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement, leading to high operational and financial impact for investors and trading members.
