## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has conducted a periodic review of securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Ten securities have been found eligible to move to lower stages of the GSM framework or exit surveillance effective November 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review carried out in coordination with other stock exchanges under the GSM framework.
- Affects 10 securities moving to lower stages or Stage 0 (relaxation).
- Effective date for the revised GSM stages: November 8, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in the annexure are moved to lower stages or relaxed from GSM surveillance restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to take note of the revised GSM stages for the specified securities and ensure compliance with applicable trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory relief and relaxation for listed securities meeting review criteria under surveillance measures, affecting market operations and liquidity for the affected scrips.
