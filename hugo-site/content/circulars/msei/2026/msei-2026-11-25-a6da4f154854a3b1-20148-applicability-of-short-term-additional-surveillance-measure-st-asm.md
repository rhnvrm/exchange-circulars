## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20148 regarding the Applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). This circular lists the securities shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework pursuant to SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for specified securities.
- Securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I or Stage II based on objective surveillance criteria.
- Applicable margin rates and surveillance actions take effect as specified in the circular.
- Aimed at safeguarding investors and curbing excessive price volatility in specific scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities meeting the objective criteria for price variation, volatility, and concentration are subjected to additional surveillance measures including enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable margin requirements (e.g., 50% or 100% margin as applicable for Stage I or Stage II) for transactions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 25, 2026
- Effective Date: Trading day following the circular issuance

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities may experience moderation due to higher margin requirements, enhancing overall market safety and investor protection.
