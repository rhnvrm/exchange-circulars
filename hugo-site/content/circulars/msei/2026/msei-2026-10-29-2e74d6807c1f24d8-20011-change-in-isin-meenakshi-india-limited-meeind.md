## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in ISIN for Meenakshi India Limited (MEEIND) following the sub-division of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Meenakshi India Limited
- Symbol: MEEIND
- Old ISIN: INE208H01016
- New ISIN: INE208H01024
- Purpose: Sub-division of existing equity shares from one equity share of Rs. 10/- into two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each.
- Effective Date / Ex-Date: October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine update reflecting corporate restructuring and share sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for all trades executed on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate identification, clearing, and settlement of shares following the stock split.
