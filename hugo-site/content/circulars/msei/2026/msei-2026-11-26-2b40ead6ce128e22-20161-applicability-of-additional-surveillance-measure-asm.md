## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20161 regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities in order to enhance market integrity and protect investors from excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities identified under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework based on objective parameters such as price variation, volatility, and concentration of client ownership.
- Imposition of applicable surveillance actions, including higher margin requirements and trading restrictions.
- Framework designed to alert market participants about heightened risk in specific securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI and stock exchange surveillance guidelines regarding short-term and long-term Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM).
- Mandatory application of enhanced surveillance controls for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable margin requirements (such as 100% margins or applicable surveillance margins) for scrips placed under ASM.
- Investors and trading members should verify the list of securities under ASM prior to executing trades.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Applicable as specified in the circular (November 2026).
- Ongoing monitoring and periodic review as per exchange surveillance schedule.

## Impact Assessment

- Short-term liquidity and trading volume in affected securities may experience moderation due to higher margin requirements.
- Enhanced protection for retail and institutional investors against abnormal price movements and speculative volatility.
