## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that equity shares of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited shall be admitted for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited
- **Symbol:** JKSHAH
- **Series:** BE
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE2OD001016
- **Effective Date:** November 18, 2026
- **Category:** Permitted to Trade

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard admission procedure under permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 17, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the equity shares of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited on the Capital Market segment starting from November 18, 2026.
