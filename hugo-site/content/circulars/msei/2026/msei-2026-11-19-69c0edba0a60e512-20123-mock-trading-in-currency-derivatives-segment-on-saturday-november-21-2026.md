## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) announced that it will conduct a mock trading session in its Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Members are advised to participate actively.

## Key Points

- **Schedule:** Login starts at 10:15 hrs onwards; Normal Market opens at 11:00 hrs and closes at 15:30 hrs for Currency Derivatives, Interest Rate Futures, and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- **Trade Modification:** Trade modification end time is 15:40 hrs.
- **Financial Implications:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and do not create any rights or liabilities for members.
- **Participation:** All CTCL members are advised to actively participate using all trading software.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must follow the procedures outlined in the annexure to download and install the SFTP server setup, VC++ Redistributable 2008, and the updated TWS/MAT client software versions (Version 11.6.27.0).

## Important Dates

- **Setup Download:** Friday, November 20, 2026, after trading hours.
- **Mock Trading Session:** Saturday, November 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness exercise for trading members and CTCL software systems with zero financial, settlement, or margin impact.
