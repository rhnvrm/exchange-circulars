## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Inox Wind Limited (INOXWIND) in reference to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024.

## Key Points

- New expiry contracts for INOXWIND will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contracts.
- Existing contracts for October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026 will continue to be available for trading till their respective expiry.
- New strikes will continue to be introduced in the existing active contracts.
- No contracts will be available for trading in INOXWIND with effect from December 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of stock eligibility criteria for derivatives as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the discontinuation schedule and manage open positions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **October 26, 2026:** Circular issuance date.
- **December 30, 2026:** Effective date from which no contracts will be available for trading.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding derivative positions in INOXWIND must square off or settle their positions before December 30, 2026, as no further new expiry contracts or new positions will be permitted.
