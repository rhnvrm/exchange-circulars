## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20102 regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to certain securities, along with exclusions, effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. November 16, 2026: Halder Venture Limited, Jyoti Structures Limited, Spacenet Enterprises India Limited, Tera Software Limited, Consecutive Commodities Ltd, Flomic Global Logistics Ltd, and Gourmet Gateway India Ltd.
- Securities excluded from Short-Term ASM Framework w.e.f. November 16, 2026: Banka BioLoo Limited, Flexituff Ventures International Limited, Lorenzini Apparels Limited, Sadbhav Infrastructure Project Limited, Supreme Infrastructure India Limited, and Transworld Shipping Lines.
- Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- Enhanced margins apply to all open positions as on November 16, 2026, and new positions created from November 17, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework to monitor and curb excessive price volatility in specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must apply the specified margin rate (50% or higher, capped at 100%) on open and new positions for securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16 / November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases margin requirements for trading in shortlisted ST-ASM securities while removing surveillance constraints for excluded securities.
