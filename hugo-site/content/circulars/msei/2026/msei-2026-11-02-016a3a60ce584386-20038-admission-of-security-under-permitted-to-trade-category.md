## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of two companies under the “Permitted to Trade” category with effect from November 03, 2026. These companies do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- Companies admitted: BLS Polymers Limited (Symbol: BLSPOLY, ISIN: INE0ZCQ01019) and SFC Environmental Technologies Limited (Symbol: SFCTECH, ISIN: INE0PKN01028).
- Segment: Capital Market Segment.
- Effective Date: November 03, 2026.
- Disclosure status: Companies admitted under this category do not execute listing agreements with the Exchange and do not provide full disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard admission procedure under the permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the admission of securities for trading. No specific new compliance obligations beyond standard trading norms.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access for BLSPOLY and SFCTECH on the MSEI capital market segment for market participants.
