## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities, as well as the exclusion of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- Short-Term ASM Stage I applied to Salasar Techno Engineering Limited, Amanta Healthcare Limited, and Allcargo Global Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin set at 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective November 20, 2026, on all open positions as on November 19, 2026, and new positions.
- Six securities (MM Forgings Limited, Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Limited, Entero Healthcare Solutions Limited, Kothari Industrial Corporation Ltd, Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited, and Spencer's Retail Limited) have been excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance measures and enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted securities to curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure collection of applicable margins (50% or higher, capped at 100%) on open and new positions for shortlisted securities effective November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 18, 2026
- Framework Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Margin Requirement Effective Date: November 20, 2026 (on open positions as on November 19, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted securities, whereas exclusions remove trading restrictions for the specified entities.
