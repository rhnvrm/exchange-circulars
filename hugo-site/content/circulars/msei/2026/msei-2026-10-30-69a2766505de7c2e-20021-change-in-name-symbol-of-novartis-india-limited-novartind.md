## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the change in name and trading symbol of Novartis India Limited. Effective November 04, 2026, the company will be renamed from Novartis India Limited to Evonile Pharma Limited, and its trading symbol will be updated from NOVARTIND to EVONILE.

## Key Points

- Old Company Name: Novartis India Limited
- New Company Name: Evonile Pharma Limited
- Existing Symbol: NOVARTIND
- New Symbol: EVONILE
- Segment: Capital Market (Permitted to trade category)
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; this is a corporate identification and branding update processed through exchange listing rules.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the change and update their internal systems, trading software, and client records with the new name and symbol prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. The update is restricted to administrative and system record-keeping adjustments for trading members and participants.
