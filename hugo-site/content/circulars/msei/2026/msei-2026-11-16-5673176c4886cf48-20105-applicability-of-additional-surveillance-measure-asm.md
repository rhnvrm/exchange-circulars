## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20105 regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities. This framework is designed to protect investors and curb excessive price volatility and speculative trading.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for shortlisted securities.
- Imposition of higher margin requirements and tighter price bands.
- Aimed at safeguarding investors against market volatility and speculative anomalies.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisted securities are subjected to special surveillance controls including enhanced margin requirements and price restrictions as per SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and investors must ensure compliance with applicable higher margin calls and trade-related restrictions for securities appearing under the ASM framework.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately upon issuance of Circular No. 20105 on November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities may experience reduced speculative volume and increased liquidity requirements due to higher margin obligations.
