## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20170 announcing the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) on specified securities in accordance with regulatory guidelines.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) on select securities based on objective parameters like price variation and volatility.
- Securities placed under ST-ASM are subject to enhanced surveillance controls and margin requirements.
- Designed to alert investors and curb excessive speculation in volatile scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of surveillance and monitoring mechanisms for designated securities under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with applicable surveillance margins and risk management parameters for client positions in affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the schedule specified in the circular for the applicable trading sessions.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market integrity and risk containment by applying stricter surveillance controls on volatile securities.
