## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Half Yearly Networth Certificate along with the computation of networth as on September 30, 2026. The due date for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reminder for submitting the Half Yearly Networth Certificate and computation as on September 30, 2026.
- Due date for submission is November 30, 2026.
- NSE-registered trading members who are also registered with other exchanges (such as BSE, MSE, MCX, NCDEX) must submit networth details to NSE, which will share them with respective exchanges as per circular MSE/INSP/17523/2025.
- Exclusive MSE members must submit directly to MSE.
- Non-submission or shortfall without a revised certificate will attract disciplinary actions as stipulated in circular MSE/INSP/18897/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; reinforces existing compliance mandates for networth certification and inter-exchange sharing mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit their Half Yearly Networth Certificate and computation as on September 30, 2026, on or before November 30, 2026. Dual-registered members (NSE and MSE/others) must submit through NSE, while exclusive MSE members submit directly to MSE.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2026:** Networth reference date.
- **November 30, 2026:** Due date for submission of Networth Certificate.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures financial soundness and regulatory compliance among trading members. Non-compliance risks disciplinary measures by the Exchange.
