## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of EAAA India Alternatives Limited (EDELALTS) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: EAAA India Alternatives Limited
- Symbol: EDELALTS
- ISIN: INE528L01027
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Category: Permitted to Trade (companies admitted under this category do not execute the listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures).

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares admitted for trading under the "Permitted to Trade" category without executing a formal listing agreement with the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members note that the company does not provide full disclosures as no listing agreement is executed with the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading access for investors on the Capital Market Segment of MSEI for EAAA India Alternatives Limited shares.
