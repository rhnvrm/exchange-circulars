## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for certain securities, effective November 20, 2026, based on open positions as on November 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM Stage-I shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 20, 2026 on all open positions as on November 19, 2026 and new positions created from November 20, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is strictly on account of market surveillance and does not represent an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under ESM are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment with applicable margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement terms for the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 20, 2026 (open positions as on November 19, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations and trade-for-trade settlement for impacted securities to curb excessive speculation and ensure market integrity.
