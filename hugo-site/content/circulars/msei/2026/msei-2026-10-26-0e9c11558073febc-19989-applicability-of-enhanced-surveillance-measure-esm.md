## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specific securities. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin on open and new positions and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Key Points

- **ABM International Limited (ABMINTLLTD)** is moving from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II effective October 27, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created from October 28, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework includes SANGINITA, BROOKS, VLEGOV, PENINLAND, ALFATRAN, GAUDIUMIVF, HPBL, LASTMILE, and MIDEASTP.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework measures in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shifting of shortlisted securities from Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **October 27, 2026:** Effective date for stage shifts (Stage II for ABMINTLLTD) and price band / Periodic Call Auction applicability.
- **October 28, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanisms for the affected scrips will be restricted due to the Trade-for-Trade segment transfer and heightened margin requirements.
