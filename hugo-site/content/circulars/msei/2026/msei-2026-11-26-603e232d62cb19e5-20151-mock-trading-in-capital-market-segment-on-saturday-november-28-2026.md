## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Trades resulting from this mock session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and do not create any rights or liabilities for members.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- Covers various sessions including Morning Block Deal Window, Pre-Open, Normal Market, CTS VWAP Window, Closing Auction Session (CAS), and Post Close Session.
- Setup and file downloading instructions provided for Member Administrator Terminal (MAT) and Trader Workstation (TWS).
- No financial obligations, margins, pay-ins, or pay-outs arise from mock trades.

## Regulatory Changes

Conducted in terms of Exchange Rules, Bye-Laws, and Regulations, and with reference to SEBI circular no. HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/23843/2026 regarding review of closing auction session, market timings, and settlement methodology for derivatives contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to download setup files from the SFTP Server (EquityCommon\SETUP\Mock) after trading hours on Friday, November 27, 2026, install VC++ Redistributable 2008, MSE-EQTWSCLIENT.msi (Version 25.0.11.0), and MSE-EQMEMADMIN.msi (Version 25.0.11.0), and configure connectivity settings as specified for Leased Line mode.

## Important Dates

- Setup Download: Friday, November 27, 2026 (after trading hours)
- Mock Trading Session: Saturday, November 28, 2026 (09:15 hrs onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness exercise for trading members to verify terminal logins, software versions, and connectivity from the DR site without any live market risk or financial impact.
