## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Petronet on account of a dividend of Rs. 7/- per equity share (face value of Rs. 10/- each). The effective ex-date for the adjustment is November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Petronet
- Dividend Amount: Rs. 7/- per equity share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-)
- Effective Ex-Date: November 06, 2026
- Futures Contracts Adjustment: Base price on November 06, 2026, will be the reference rate (daily mark-to-market settlement price) less the aggregate dividend of Rs. 7/-.
- Options Contracts Adjustment: The full dividend value of Rs. 7/- will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date.
- Rounding: Revised strike/base prices and lot sizes will be rounded off to the nearest tick size and nearest integer respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustments executed in accordance with Exchange Rules, Bye-Laws, Regulations, and relevant SEBI Master Circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to note the revised strike prices and base prices for F&O contracts in Petronet effective from November 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 04, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational adjustments for derivatives market participants holding open positions in Petronet F&O contracts.
