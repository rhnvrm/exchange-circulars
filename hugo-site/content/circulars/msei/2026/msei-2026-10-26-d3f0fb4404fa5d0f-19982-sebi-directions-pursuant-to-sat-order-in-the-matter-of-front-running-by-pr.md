## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to a SAT order in the matter of front running by Prijesh Kurani and Others. SEBI, vide email dated October 23, 2026, advised lifting the debarment with respect to Ms. Vilpa Vora following the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated July 03, 2026, which stayed the impugned SEBI order.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous Exchange circular MSE/ID/18983/2026 dated May 04, 2026, and SEBI Order dated April 30, 2026.
- Noticee Vilpa Vora (PAN: AGJPV0381Q) was initially restrained from accessing the securities market for 1 year.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated July 03, 2026, stayed the impugned order pending disposal of the appeal.
- SEBI advised lifting the debarment with respect to Ms. Vilpa Vora pursuant to the SAT order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Market access restrictions and debarment on Ms. Vilpa Vora are lifted in accordance with the SAT stay order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 26, 2026
- SAT Order Date: July 03, 2026
- SEBI Advice Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Individual compliance update with zero direct impact on listed entities or overall market operations.
