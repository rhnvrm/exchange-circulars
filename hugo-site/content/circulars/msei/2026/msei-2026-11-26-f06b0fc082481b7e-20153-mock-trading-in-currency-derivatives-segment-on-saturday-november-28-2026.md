## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives and Interest Rate Futures segments from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, November 28, 2026. Trades executed during this session do not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and create no rights or liabilities for members.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, November 28, 2026.
- **Segments:** Currency Derivatives, Interest Rate Futures, and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 hrs onwards.
- **Normal Market Open Time:** 10:00 hrs.
- **Normal Market Close Time:** 12:30 hrs.
- **Trade Modification End Time:** 12:40 hrs.
- **Connectivity:** Supports both Leased Line and Internet modes with specific TWS/MAT configuration requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational mock trading exercise.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to participate actively. Setup files (Version 11.6.19) can be downloaded via SFTP after trading hours on November 27, 2026. Members must install VC++ Redistributable 2008 and update to Trader Workstation (TWS) and Member Administrator Terminal (MAT) Test Version 11.6.27.0.

## Important Dates

- **Setup Download Date:** Friday, November 27, 2026 (post-trading hours)
- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, November 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members to verify their trading system setup, connectivity, and disaster recovery readiness with zero financial or risk impact.
