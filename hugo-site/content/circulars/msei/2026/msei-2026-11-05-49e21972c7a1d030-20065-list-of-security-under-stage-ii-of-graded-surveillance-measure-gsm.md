## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the movement of Mahesh Developers Limited to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Mahesh Developers Limited (ISIN: INE01MO01013, Symbol: MAHESH).
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026 (Friday).
- Trading Segment: Moved to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE).
- Price Band: 5% or lower.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD): 50% of trade value will be collected from the buying trading member and retained till further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities under Stage II of GSM are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures, including mandatory trade-for-trade settlement and a restricted price band of 5% or lower, along with an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the specified security and ensure collection of the 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit from buying members.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in Mahesh Developers Limited will experience liquidity restrictions due to the trade-for-trade mechanism and 5% price band, alongside additional capital requirements via ASD for buyers.
