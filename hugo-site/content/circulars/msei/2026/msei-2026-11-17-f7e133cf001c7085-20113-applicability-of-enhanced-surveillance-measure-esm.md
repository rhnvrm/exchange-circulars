## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities pursuant to prior exchange circulars. Securities qualifying under ESM are subject to heightened margin requirements, price bands, periodic call auctions, and/or shifting from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Key Points

- Flexituff Ventures International Limited (FLEXITUFF) satisfies criteria for inclusion under ESM and attracts a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. November 19, 2026, and is shifted from Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Bhagyanagar India Limited (BHAGYANGR) moves from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II, attracting Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 18, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework includes Sanginita Chemicals Limited, Brooks Laboratories Limited, Vlee Governance & IT Solutions Limited, Peninsula Land Limited, Alfa Transformers Ltd, Gaudium IVF And Women Health Limited, HCP Plastene Bulkpack Ltd, and Last Mile Enterprises Ltd.
- Actions taken under ESM are purely on account of market surveillance and do not constitute adverse actions against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 18, 2026, and new positions created from November 19, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Securities moving to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and segment transfers for open and new positions in the listed securities.
- Members should account for the price band and periodic call auction mechanisms applicable to Stage-II securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage-II moves and price bands: November 18, 2026
- Open positions cut-off date for margin applicability: November 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage-I inclusion, 100% margin, and segment shifts: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanisms for shortlisted securities are directly impacted due to 100% margin requirements, Trade-for-Trade settlement, and price restrictions.
