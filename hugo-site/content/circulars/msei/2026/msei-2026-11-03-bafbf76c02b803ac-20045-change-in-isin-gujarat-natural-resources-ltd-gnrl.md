## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in ISIN for Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd (GNRL) effective November 05, 2026, following the sub-division of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd
- Symbol: GNRL
- Old ISIN: INE207H01018
- New ISIN: INE207H01026
- Purpose: Sub-Division of existing Equity Shares from One Equity Share of Rs. 10/- into Ten Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each
- Effective Date / Ex-Date: November 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

In continuation of Exchange Circular No.: MSE/LIST/19941/2026 dated October 19, 2026, the ISIN has been updated for securities under the permitted to trade category to reflect the share sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

Exchange members must ensure that all trades and settlements for GNRL on and from November 05, 2026, utilize the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date / Ex-Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading and clearing operations will transition to the new ISIN INE207H01026, ensuring accurate reflection of holdings post-sub-division.
