## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026. Members are advised to participate actively to test connectivity, trading applications, and algorithmic software.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- Covers Currency Derivatives, Interest Rate Futures, and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- Login starts at 08:15 hrs; Normal market open at 09:00 hrs; Normal market close at 15:30 hrs; Trade modification end time at 15:40 hrs.
- Setup files available for download from SFTP server starting Friday, November 13, 2026, after trading hours.
- Mandatory participation for members using approved algorithmic software across all approved Algo user IDs.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Mandatory participation for members with approved algorithmic software using all approved user IDs.
- Members are requested to actively participate to test connectivity and CTCL application software.

## Important Dates

- Setup download available: Friday, November 13, 2026 (after trading hours)
- Mock Trading Session date: Saturday, November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No financial impact as mock trades do not attract margin obligations, pay-ins, pay-outs, or create real rights and liabilities. Serves to verify system readiness and connectivity.
