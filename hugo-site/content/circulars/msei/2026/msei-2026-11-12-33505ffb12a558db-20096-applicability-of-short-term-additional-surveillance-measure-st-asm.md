## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20096 dated November 12, 2026, detailing the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for shortlisted securities in the capital market segment.

## Key Points

- Circular Number: 20096
- Issue Date: November 12, 2026
- Subject: Applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM)
- Department: Surveillance and Risk Management

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including applicable margin rates (e.g., 50% to 100% or existing margins) for securities identified under ST-ASM stages.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and clearing corporations must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements and risk management parameters for positions in the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 12, 2026
- Effective Date: Trading day following the circular issuance (November 13, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Aims to monitor volatility and curb excessive speculation in specific scrips, prompting increased due diligence by market participants.
