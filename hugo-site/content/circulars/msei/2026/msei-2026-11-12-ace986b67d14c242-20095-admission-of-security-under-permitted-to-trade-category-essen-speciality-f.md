## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Essen Speciality Films Limited (ESFL) under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective from November 13, 2026. Since the security is admitted under this category, the company has not executed a listing agreement with the Exchange and does not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Essen Speciality Films Limited
- Symbol: ESFL
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE0ITO01014
- Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Category: Permitted to Trade

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares admitted for trading under the "Permitted to Trade" segment without a formal listing agreement with MSEI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should note that companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not provide full disclosures or execute a listing agreement with the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026
- Circular Date: November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading access for ESFL equity shares on the MSEI Capital Market segment for market participants.
