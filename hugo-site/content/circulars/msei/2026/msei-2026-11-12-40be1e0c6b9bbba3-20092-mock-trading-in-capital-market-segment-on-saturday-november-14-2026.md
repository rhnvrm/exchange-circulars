## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) is conducting a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, November 14, 2026. Members are requested to participate actively to test connectivity and CTCL application software.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, November 14, 2026.
- **Mandatory Participation:** Members having approved algorithmic software are required to mandatorily participate using all approved User IDs for Algo trading.
- **Financial Obligations:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and do not create any rights or liabilities.
- **Setup & Download:** Setup files can be downloaded from the EquityCommon\SETUP\MOCK folder on the SFTP server or via https://www.sftp.mseindia.com on Friday, November 13, 2026, after trading hours.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock trading session announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Mandatory participation for members with approved algorithmic trading software across all approved user IDs.
- Active participation recommended for all members to test connectivity and CTCL applications.
- Installation of VC++ Redistributable 2008 and updated Trader Workstation (TWS) / Member Administrator Terminal (MAT) versions (Version 25.0.9.0).

## Important Dates

- **Setup Download Date:** November 13, 2026 (after trading hours)
- **Mock Trading Date:** November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No impact on live market operations, funds, or securities, as mock trades are simulated and carry zero financial or margin obligations.
