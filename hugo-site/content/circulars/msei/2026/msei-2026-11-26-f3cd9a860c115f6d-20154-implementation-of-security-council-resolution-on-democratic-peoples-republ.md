## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/ID/20154/2026 informing members about SEBI's communication regarding updates made by the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) 1718 Sanctions Committee to one entry on its sanctions list concerning the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).

## Key Points

- SEBI informed the Exchange via e-mail dated November 20, 2026, regarding the UNSC 1718 Sanctions Committee update.
- Note SC/16512 dated November 16, 2026 details the amendment of one entry in the UNSC's 1718 Sanctions List.
- Members must ensure compliance with Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (UAPA).

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the UNSC 1718 Sanctions List involving one entry related to the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).
- Reinforcement of Clause 54 of the SEBI Master Circular on Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT).

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock exchanges and registered intermediaries must ensure they do not maintain any accounts or financial relationships with individuals or entities appearing on the updated UNSC sanctions list.
- Members are required to review the updated lists accessible on the UN Security Council website and take note for immediate compliance.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 26, 2026
- SEBI E-mail Date: November 20, 2026
- UNSC Note Date: November 16, 2026 / Annexure Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to screening client databases against the updated UNSC sanctions list to ensure no prohibited accounts or transactions exist.
