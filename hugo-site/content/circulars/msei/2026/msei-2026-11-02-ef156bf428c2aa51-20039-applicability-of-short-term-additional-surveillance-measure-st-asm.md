## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20039/2026 dated November 02, 2026, regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities have been shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, moved between stages, or excluded from the framework effective November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 04, 2026, on all open positions as on November 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I include Acutaas Chemicals Limited, Ather Energy Limited, Hubtown Limited, Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited, Ekansh Concepts Ltd, Nexome Capital Markets Ltd, and Resonance Specialties Ltd.
- Phoenix International Ltd moves from ST-ASM Stage II to Stage I w.e.f. November 03, 2026.
- Securities excluded from the Short-Term ASM framework include Kanani Industries Limited, Priority Jewels Limited, Sunflag Iron And Steel Company Limited, Globalspace Technologies Ltd, and Mahesh Developers Ltd.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and stage adjustments for specified securities in accordance with exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities w.e.f. November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework / Stage Changes / Exclusions: November 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 04, 2026 (on open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants holding positions in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
