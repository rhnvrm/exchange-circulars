## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in the name and trading symbol of Lorenzini Apparels Limited under the permitted-to-trade category.

## Key Points

- Old Name: Lorenzini Apparels Limited
- New Name: Monteil Limited
- Existing Symbol: LAL
- New Symbol: MONTEIL
- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine exchange administration update for corporate identity change.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the change in name and symbol and update their trading terminals and back-office systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating security identifiers and nomenclature across trading systems.
