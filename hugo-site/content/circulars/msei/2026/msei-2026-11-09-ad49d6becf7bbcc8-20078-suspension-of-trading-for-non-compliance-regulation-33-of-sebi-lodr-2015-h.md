## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Hardoli Paper Mills Limited and Sunayana Investment Company Limited w.e.f. December 10, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 & June 2026).

## Key Points

- Affected Companies: Hardoli Paper Mills Limited (Symbol: HARDOLI, ISIN: INE699F01010) and Sunayana Investment Company Limited (Symbol: SUNAYANA, ISIN: INE724R01011).
- Reason for Suspension: Non-compliance with Regulation 33 (Financial Results) for March 2026 and June 2026 quarters.
- Effective Date of Suspension: December 10, 2026 (if companies fail to comply on or before December 07, 2026).
- Promoter shareholding and all other securities in promoter demat accounts will remain frozen during the suspension period.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular no. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated July 11, 2023 (last updated January 30, 2026) regarding SOP for suspension and revocation of equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies must comply with all provisions of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 on or before December 07, 2026 to avoid suspension.
- If suspended, 15 days post-suspension, trading will be permitted on a Trade for Trade basis in Series 'BZ' on the first trading day of every week for six months.
- Revocation is subject to full compliance with procedures and norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: November 09, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: December 07, 2026
- Suspension Date (if non-compliant): December 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Direct trading restriction for investors in HARDOLI and SUNAYANA shares starting December 10, 2026 unless compliance is achieved prior to the deadline.
- Freezing of promoter shareholdings and demat accounts.
