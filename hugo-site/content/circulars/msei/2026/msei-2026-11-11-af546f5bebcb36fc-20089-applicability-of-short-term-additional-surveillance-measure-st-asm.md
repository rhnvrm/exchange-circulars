## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for certain securities effective November 12, 2026, aimed at ensuring market integrity and investor protection.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure are placed under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM).
- Applicable margin rates are set at 50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II (or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a cap of 100%).
- Actions are based on objective surveillance parameters like price variation, volatility, and client concentration.
- Measures are administrative risk-management steps and do not reflect adverse punitive action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the ST-ASM framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain the enhanced margin rates (50% or 100%) for all open and new positions in the affected securities starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 12, 2026.
- Circular Date: November 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the shortlisted securities in the short term, mitigating excessive volatility and safeguarding market participants.
