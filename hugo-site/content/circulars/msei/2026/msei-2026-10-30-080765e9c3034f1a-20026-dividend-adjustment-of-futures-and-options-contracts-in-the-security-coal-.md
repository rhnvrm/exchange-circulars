## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in Coal India Limited (COALINDIA) following the declaration of a dividend of Rs. 10.25/- per equity share of face value Rs. 10/-. The adjustments become effective on November 4, 2026, which is the ex-date.

## Key Points

- Underlying Security: Coal India Limited (COALINDIA)
- Dividend Details: Rs. 10.25/- per equity share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-)
- Effective Ex-Date: November 4, 2026
- Futures Contract Adjustment: Base price on November 4, 2026, will be the reference rate (daily mark-to-market settlement price) less the aggregate dividend amount of Rs. 10.25/-.
- Options Contract Adjustment: The full dividend value of Rs. 10.25/- will be deducted from all cum-dividend strike prices on the ex-dividend date.
- Rounding Rules: Revised options strike prices and futures base prices will be rounded off to the nearest tick size, and lot sizes will be rounded off to the nearest integer.

## Regulatory Changes

- Executed in compliance with SEBI Master Circulars, Rules, and Bye-Laws of the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to note the revised base prices and strike prices for F&O contracts in COALINDIA effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market integrity and contract continuity by adjusting derivative strike and base prices to account for the corporate dividend payout.
