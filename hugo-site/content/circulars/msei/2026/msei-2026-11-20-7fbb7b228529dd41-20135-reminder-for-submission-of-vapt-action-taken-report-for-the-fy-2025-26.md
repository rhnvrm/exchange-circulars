## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a reminder to trading members regarding the submission of the Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) Action Taken Report (ATR) for the financial year 2025-26. Members with open vulnerabilities identified by CERT-In empaneled auditors must submit the ATR by November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous circulars MSE/INSP/19076/2026 and MSE/INSP/17882/2025.
- Trading members registered with NSE must submit their VAPT ATR to NSE only.
- Members not registered with NSE must submit their ATR via email to compliancemsx@mse.co.in or through the Common Member Portal.
- Submission is complete only upon receipt of a digitally signed ATR by the auditor.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members with identified open vulnerabilities must submit the VAPT Action Taken Report on or before November 30, 2026.
- Failure to submit or non-closure of vulnerabilities within timelines will attract penalties and disciplinary actions as specified in Exchange circular no. MSE/INSP/18897/2026.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures cybersecurity compliance across trading members and adherence to regulatory standards set by the Exchange.
