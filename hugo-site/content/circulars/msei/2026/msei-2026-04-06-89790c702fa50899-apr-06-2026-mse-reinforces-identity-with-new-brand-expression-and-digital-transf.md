## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSE) has announced a strategic evolution of its corporate identity, featuring a refreshed brand expression, a dynamic new logo, and migration to a new digital domain (www.mseindia.com). The initiative is supported by technology infrastructure upgrades, including a state-of-the-art data center designed to enhance execution speeds, operational resilience, and scalability.

## Key Points

- Domain Migration: Official transition from msei.in to www.mseindia.com to strengthen digital presence and reflect the simplified 'MSE' identity.
- Brand Refresh: Introduction of a dynamic new logo video highlighting attributes of being young, agile, and futuristic.
- Technology Upgrades: Integration of a state-of-the-art data center to support enhanced trading execution speeds and operational resilience.
- Market Segments: Continued operation across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, and Debt Segments under SEBI recognition.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes were announced in this press release.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and stakeholders should update their records and communication channels to reflect the new domain www.mseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- April 6, 2026: Effective date of the brand identity launch and domain migration announcement.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on market participants, primarily involving awareness of the updated digital domain and brand communication channels.
