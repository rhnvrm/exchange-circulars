## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified members of a change in ISIN for BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM) effective November 16, 2026, pursuant to a sub-division of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BN Agrochem Limited
- Symbol: BNAGROCHEM
- Old ISIN: INE00HZ01011
- New ISIN: INE00HZ01029
- Purpose: Sub-division of existing Equity Shares from One Equity Share of Rs.10/- each into Ten Equity Shares of Re.1/- each.
- Previous Circular Reference: MSE/LIST/20079/2026 dated November 9, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Update of security identification number (ISIN) following corporate action for shares under the permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading terminals and back-office systems with the new ISIN (INE00HZ01029) for BNAGROCHEM effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 13, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update ensuring accurate settlement and trading records post share split.
