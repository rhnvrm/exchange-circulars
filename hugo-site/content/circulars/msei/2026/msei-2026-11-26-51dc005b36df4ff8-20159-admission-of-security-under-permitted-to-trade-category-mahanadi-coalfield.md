## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Mahanadi Coalfields Limited (MAHACOAL) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Mahanadi Coalfields Limited
- **Symbol:** MAHACOAL
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE03K401026
- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026
- **Note:** Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine trading admission under existing exchange frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the admission of the security for dealing on the Capital Market Segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading availability for members on the MSEI Capital Market Segment for MAHACOAL equity shares.
