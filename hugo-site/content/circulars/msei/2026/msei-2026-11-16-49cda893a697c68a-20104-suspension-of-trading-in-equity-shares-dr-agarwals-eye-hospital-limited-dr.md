## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Dr. Agarwals Eye Hospital Limited under the Permitted to trade category, effective November 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Symbol: DRAGARWQ
- Company Name: Dr. Agarwals Eye Hospital Limited
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026
- Reason: Action taken as per Primary Stock Exchange pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Division Bench, Chennai.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the equity shares of the company is discontinued under the Permitted to trade category on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note and ensure compliance regarding the suspension of trading in the aforementioned security.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and investors will no longer be able to trade equity shares of Dr. Agarwals Eye Hospital Limited on MSEI as of the effective date.
