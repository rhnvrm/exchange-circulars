## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has announced the introduction of its Tender Offer Platform for market participants. The platform incorporates an interoperability mechanism for Tender Offers (OTB) covering Buyback, Takeover, and Delisting transactions, in line with relevant SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Introduction of the Tender Offer Platform at MSE for market participants.
- Implementation of the Interoperability Framework among Clearing Corporations for Tender Offers (OTB).
- Scope covers Buybacks, Takeovers, and Delistings.
- References prior SEBI circulars on share acquisition mechanisms through stock exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of the Interoperability Framework among Clearing Corporations for tender offer mechanisms (OTB) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the new platform introduction.
- Detailed operating guidelines and the specific go-live date will be communicated via subsequent circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 11, 2026
- Go-Live Date: To be announced in due course

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency and streamlines interactions among market participants for corporate control transactions and exits.
