## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the observance of Vigilance Awareness Week (VAW) 2026, scheduled from October 26, 2026, to November 01, 2026, based on the Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) circular.

## Key Points

- **Theme:** "सत्यनिष्ठा से समृद्धि" / “Probity for Prosperity”.
- **Duration:** October 26, 2026, to November 01, 2026.
- **Action for Members:** Publish information on websites and social media accounts.
- **Stakeholder Outreach:** Send bulk SMS/WhatsApp messages to listed companies, intermediaries, investors, and demat account holders.
- **E-Pledge:** Encourage all stakeholders to take the e-pledge via the CVC website (https://pledge.cvc.nic.in/).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an informational and advisory circular based on CVC guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to:
- Publish VAW 2026 observance details on their official websites and social media channels.
- Circulate e-pledge information via bulk SMS/WhatsApp to stakeholders including listed companies, intermediaries, and investors.

## Important Dates

- **VAW 2026 Period:** October 26, 2026, to November 01, 2026.
- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The circular serves an awareness-raising and educational purpose for market participants.
