## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for securities trading in the Equity Capital Market segment effective October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I effective October 28, 2026 include Paushak Ltd, BN Agrochem Ltd, and NMS Global Limited.
- Securities excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework effective October 28, 2026 include Modi Naturals Limited, Bombay Super Hybrid Seeds Limited, and Shree Rama Multi-Tech Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. October 29, 2026 on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

The exchange has updated the list of securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in continuation of earlier circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or existing margin, capped at 100%) are maintained for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ST-ASM Applicability / Exclusions: October 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: October 29, 2026 (on open positions as on October 28, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced margin requirements to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility.
