## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability and movement of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for Companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework, effective November 02, 2026. This circular is in partial modification of earlier circulars dated June 11, 2018, May 20, 2022, and February 10, 2023.

## Key Points

- **Security Movements:**
  - AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA) and Setubandhan Infrastructure Limited (SETUINFRA) are moving from Stage I to 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
  - Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) is moving out from 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
- **Framework Integration:** The ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.
- **Clarification:** Shortlisting under this framework is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of securities list under the IBC Additional Surveillance Measure framework based on exchange surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the surveillance status changes and ensure appropriate risk management and compliance when dealing in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading surveillance parameters and disclosures for the listed securities, ensuring market integrity and transparency regarding companies undergoing IBC proceedings.
