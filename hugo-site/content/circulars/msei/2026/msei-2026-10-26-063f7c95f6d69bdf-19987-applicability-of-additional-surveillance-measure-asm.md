## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities effective October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage-I have been identified.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 29, 2026, on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026 onwards.
- Securities listed: MEDICO REMEDIES LIMITED (MEDICO, ISIN: INE630Y01024) and CROPSTER AGRO LTD (CROPSTER, ISIN: INE293E01031).
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures, including 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure 100% margin is maintained on open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026 onwards for the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Applicability Date: October 29, 2026
- Reference Date for Open Positions: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (100%) for shortlisted securities will impact liquidity and trading volumes for MEDICO and CROPSTER.
