## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) is moving out of the IBC framework following an NCLAT order dated October 30, 2026, which set aside the earlier NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order. Consequently, surveillance actions on the scrip have been reverted, and futures and options contracts with January 2027 expiry will be available for trading effective November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- NCLAT allowed the appeal filed by Polycab India Limited and set aside the NCLT Ahmedabad Bench order dated October 07, 2026.
- Scrip POLYCAB is removed from the IBC framework, and related surveillance actions are reverted.
- Futures and options contracts with January 2027 expiry are made available for trading in POLYCAB effective November 02, 2026.
- References previous Exchange circulars regarding securities under the IBC framework (MSE/SURV/19525/2026, MSE/SURV/19895/2026, and MSE/SURV/20030/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal of IBC framework surveillance actions on Polycab India Limited.
- Re-introduction of F&O trading for the underlying scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members can resume derivatives trading in POLYCAB contracts as per the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- **October 30, 2026:** NCLAT order allowing the appeal and setting aside NCLT proceedings.
- **November 02, 2026:** Futures and options contracts with January 2027 expiry become available for trading.

## Impact Assessment

Restoration of normal trading and derivatives availability in POLYCAB provides clarity to market participants following the resolution of IBC proceedings at the appellate level.
