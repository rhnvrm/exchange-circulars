## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20106 regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for securities meeting objective parameters of price variation, volatility, and volume variation.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted based on objective parameters for price variation, volatility, and volume variation.
- Implemented in accordance with the joint framework of SEBI and Stock Exchanges to enhance market integrity.
- Stage I: Margin rate increased to 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, up to 100%).
- Stage II: Margin rate increased to 100% or existing margin.
- Surveillance action is preventive and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Placement of shortlisted securities under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) with enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements and trading restrictions applicable to securities under ST-ASM.

## Important Dates

- Effective from November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margins and surveillance measures aim to curb excessive volatility and protect investors in shortlisted securities.
