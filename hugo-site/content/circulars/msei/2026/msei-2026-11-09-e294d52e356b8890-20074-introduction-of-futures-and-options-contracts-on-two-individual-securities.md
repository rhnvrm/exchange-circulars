## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on two additional securities, Acutaas Chemicals Limited and Apar Industries Limited, effective November 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Futures and options contracts will be available for trading starting November 25, 2026.
- Securities included: Acutaas Chemicals Limited (Symbol: ACUTAAS) and Apar Industries Limited (Symbol: APARINDS).
- Eligibility is based on SEBI circular guidelines dated August 30, 2024, and the quarter sigma computation cycle of November 2026.
- Market lots for the securities will be announced on November 23, 2026, via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the upcoming F&O contracts and ensure readiness for trading and risk management as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Market Lot Announcement Date: November 23, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the derivative product offering on the exchange for market participants, enabling hedging and trading opportunities in ACUTAAS and APARINDS.
