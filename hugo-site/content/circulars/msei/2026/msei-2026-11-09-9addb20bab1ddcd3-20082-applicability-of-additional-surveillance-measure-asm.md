## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I to specified securities. A 100% margin requirement is applicable on open positions and new positions created from November 13, 2026 onwards.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for Long-Term ASM Stage I have been identified.
- Applicable rate of margin is set to 100% w.e.f. November 13, 2026, on all open positions as on November 12, 2026, and new positions created from November 13, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisted securities include Spectrum Electrical Industries Limited, LCC Infotech Limited, Ather Energy Limited, and Raghuvir Synthetics Ltd.
- The surveillance measure is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I provisions for shortlisted scrips in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in the affected securities effective from November 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance / Inclusion: November 11, 2026 / November 13, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: November 13, 2026 (on open positions as of November 12, 2026, and onwards)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will experience increased margin constraints (100%), potentially affecting liquidity and open position management for market participants.
