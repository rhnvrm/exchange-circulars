## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20137/2026 dated November 20, 2026, regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, while others have been excluded or consolidated.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. November 25, 2026 on all open positions as on November 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted in Short-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. November 23, 2026 include AICHAMP, CORALAB, LWSKNIT, PHARMAID, and REXNORD.
- Securities excluded from Short-Term ASM Framework w.e.f. November 23, 2026 include PIONRINV, SUPRIYA, and GOURMET.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework and margin adjustments for specified securities in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must maintain the applicable margin of 50% or existing margin (capped at 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM/Exclusions: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 25, 2026 (on open positions as on November 23, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I may affect liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
