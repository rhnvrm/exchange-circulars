## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter ‘Encumbrance’ as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities shortlisted in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Dollex Agrotech Limited) satisfy the criteria for inclusion under the framework and will attract a minimum 75% margin.
- No securities are excluded from the framework effective October 27, 2026 (Annexure II is NIL).
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- This measure operates in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, targeting companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance levels.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that a minimum 75% margin is maintained on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions created from October 29, 2026, for securities listed in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Exclusions (Annexure II): October 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements (Annexure I): October 29, 2026 (on all open positions as on October 28, 2026, and new positions thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the affected securities will experience increased margin obligations, which may impact liquidity and position sizing in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
