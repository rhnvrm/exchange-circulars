## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities, along with exclusions, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. October 30, 2026: INDOUS, MIDEASTP, RAGHUSYN, and RFLL.
- Securities excluded from ST-ASM w.e.f. October 30, 2026: DELTACORP, POLICYBZR, PASHUPATI, AMAL, and BALTE.
- Surveillance Action: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of Exchange Circulars MSE/SURV/6932/2018, MSE/SURV/9816/2020, and MSE/SURV/11774/2022 regarding Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM).

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that the applicable margin rate of 50% or existing margin (capped at 100%) is applied on all open positions as on October 30, 2026, and new positions created from November 02, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Shortlisting/Exclusion: October 30, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the affected securities must account for higher margin requirements (50% to 100%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these specific scrips.
