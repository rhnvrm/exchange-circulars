## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of two companies under the "Permitted to Trade" category on the Capital Market Segment, effective November 06, 2026. Companies admitted under this category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- Baheti Recycling Industries Limited (Symbol: BAHETI, Series: EQ, Market Lot: 1, ISIN: INE029Q01017)
- Bright Outdoor Media Limited (Symbol: BRIGHTOUTD, Series: EQ, Market Lot: 1, ISIN: INE0OMI01019)
- Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Segment: Capital Market Segment

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares admitted for dealing under the permitted to trade category without executing a formal listing agreement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are notified that these securities do not provide full disclosures due to the absence of a listing agreement with the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities on the Capital Market Segment for trading members by enabling dealings in BAHETI and BRIGHTOUTD.
