## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has notified members regarding the change in ISIN for Varvee Global Limited (VGL). Following the sub-division of existing equity shares from a face value of Rs. 5/- each to Rs. 2/- each, a new ISin has been assigned effective November 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varvee Global Limited
- Symbol: VGL
- Old ISIN: INE273D01027
- New ISIN: INE273D01035
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares (Face Value from Rs. 5/- to Rs. 2/-)
- Effective Date / Ex-Date: November 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- ISIN update for trading under the permitted-to-trade category in accordance with exchange rules and regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange members must update their systems with the new ISIN for trades executed on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: November 27, 2026

- Seamless operational transition for market participants reflecting the revised ISIN for VGL.
