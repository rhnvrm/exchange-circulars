## Summary

MSEI has issued circular no. MSE/ID/19981/2026 dated October 26, 2026, regarding SEBI Order No. QJA/MN/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-1/32790/2026-27 dated October 23, 2026. The order restrains Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited and associated individuals from buying, selling, or dealing in securities due to violations concerning unregistered investment advisory, research analyst activities, and fraudulent/manipulative trade practices.

## Key Points

- Noticee 1: Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited (PAN: AATCA1675K) debarred for 2 years.
- Noticee 2: Avadhut Dinkar Sathe (PAN: BCSPS7915K) debarred for 2 years.
- Noticee 3: Gouri Avadhut Sathe (PAN: BIUPS6942Q) debarred for 6 months.
- Entities holding open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts as of the order date are permitted to close out or square off such positions within 3 months or upon contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed before the close of trading on the date of the order is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of SEBI debarment orders against entities engaging in unregistered investment advisory and PFUTP violations under sections of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the debarment orders by restricting the specified entities from accessing securities markets, while facilitating permitted square-offs of open derivative positions within the specified 3-month timeline.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Order Date: October 23, 2026
- MSEI Circular Date: October 26, 2026
- Derivative Square-off Deadline: Within 3 months from the date of order or contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

Members must update their compliance and surveillance systems to block the debarred entities and monitor orderly close-out of any existing derivative positions.
