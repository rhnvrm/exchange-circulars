## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/20136/2026 dated November 20, 2026, regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities.

## Key Points

- **Gourmet Gateway India Ltd** (ISIN: INE512D01028, Symbol: GOURMET) has satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long-term ASM) Stage - I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective November 26, 2026, on all open positions as on November 25, 2026, and new positions created from November 26, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage I framework provisions for shortlisted securities.
- 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in GOURMET starting November 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 20, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margins:** November 26, 2026 (on open positions as of November 25, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in GOURMET will face stricter margin requirements (100%), impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants holding or creating positions in the security.
