## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the transfer of security of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited (BCSSL) from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) effective November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited (BCSSL)
- Action: Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (series: BE) to Rolling segment (series: EQ)
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026
- Reference: Continuation to Exchange circular no. MSE/LIST/20055/2026 dated November 04, 2026 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012

## Regulatory Changes

The trading in the security of Bombay Coated and Special Steels Limited under the permitted to trade category moves from 'BE' series to 'EQ' series pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the segment change and ensure systems reflect the updated series for BCSSL.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates normal rolling settlement in the security on the exchange, supporting improved operational liquidity and standard trading procedures for investors.
