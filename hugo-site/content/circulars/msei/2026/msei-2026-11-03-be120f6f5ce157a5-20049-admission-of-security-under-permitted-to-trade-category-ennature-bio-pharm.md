## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Ennature Bio Pharma Limited and IGL Spirits Limited for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the 'Permitted to Trade' category.

## Key Points

- **Companies Admitted:** Ennature Bio Pharma Limited (Symbol: ENNATURE, ISIN: INE2WCT01016) and IGL Spirits Limited (Symbol: IGLSPIRITS, ISIN: INE2W6901017).
- **Series / Market Lot:** Series BE, Market Lot 1 for both securities.
- **Permitted to Trade Nature:** Companies admitted under this category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.
- **Pre-Open Session:** The securities will be part of the pre-open session as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; executed pursuant to standard exchange admission guidelines for permitted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the admission status and trading parameters for the respective securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading opportunities for members in the specified securities under the permitted category.
