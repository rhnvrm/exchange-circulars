## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in name and trading symbols for four exchange-traded fund (ETF) schemes managed by SBI Funds Management Limited, effective October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme name and trading symbol changes apply to four SBI ETF schemes under the permitted to trade category.
- Effective date for the changes is October 30, 2026.
- Affected schemes include SBI-ETF Nifty Bank, SBI-ETF Nifty Next 50, SBI-ETF Nifty 50, and SBI-ETF Gold.
- Table of changes:
  - SBI-ETF Nifty Bank (ISIN: INF200KA1580): SETFNIFBK -> SBIBANKNIF (New Name: SBI Nifty Bank ETF)
  - SBI-ETF Nifty Next 50 (ISIN: INF200KA1598): SETFNN50 -> SBINEXT50 (New Name: SBI Nifty Next 50 ETF)
  - SBI-ETF Nifty 50 (ISIN: INF200KA1FS1): SETFNIF50 -> SBINIFTY50 (New Name: SBI Nifty 50 ETF)
  - SBI-ETF Gold (ISIN: INF200KA16D8): SETFGOLD -> SBIGOLD (New Name: SBI Gold ETF)

## Regulatory Changes

Update of scheme names and trading symbols in exchange records and trading systems for SBI Funds Management Limited ETF schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the revised names and trading symbols for order placement and system updates effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; participants must update their trading terminals and systems with the new symbols.
