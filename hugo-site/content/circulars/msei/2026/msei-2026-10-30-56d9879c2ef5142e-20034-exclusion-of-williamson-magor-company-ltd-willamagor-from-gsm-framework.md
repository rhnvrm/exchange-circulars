## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the exclusion of Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (WILLAMAGOR) from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework w.e.f. November 02, 2026, following price discovery under the Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) mechanism.

## Key Points

- Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (WILLAMAGOR) was previously retained under the GSM framework per Circular no. MSE/SURV/19697/2026 dated September 07, 2026.
- Pursuant to joint meetings with SEBI and Stock Exchanges regarding companies undergoing corporate restructuring/actions or regulatory directions, companies placed in SPOS for price discovery are moved out from the Surveillance Framework.
- Upon completion of price discovery under SPOS, securities are considered for inclusion under applicable surveillance frameworks based on prescribed eligibility criteria and timelines.
- The security is moved out of the GSM framework effective November 02, 2026, based on Exchange Circular no. MSE/LIST/19954/2026 dated October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of Williamson Magor & Company Ltd. (WILLAMAGOR) from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take adequate precaution while trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Exclusion: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalizes trading surveillance status for WILLAMAGOR following corporate restructuring and SPOS price discovery.
