## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the mandatory submission of Risk Based Supervision (RBS) data for the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, referencing earlier circular MSE/INSP/19920/2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory for all active trading members who executed at least one trade during April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026.
- Submission must be made electronically through the Enhanced Supervision Portal of the Exchange.
- Failure to submit by the due date will attract penal charges and disciplinary action.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a compliance deadline reminder reinforcing existing guidelines under Exchange Circular No. MSE/INSP/18897/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must electronically submit the Risk Based Supervision data through the Enhanced Supervision Portal on or before November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Due Date for Submission: November 30, 2026
- Period Covered: April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Active trading members failing to meet the submission deadline face potential penal charges and disciplinary actions as prescribed by the Exchange.
