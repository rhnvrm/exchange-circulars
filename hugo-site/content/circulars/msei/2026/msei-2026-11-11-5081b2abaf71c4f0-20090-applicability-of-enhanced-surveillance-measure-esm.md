## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20090 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework to monitor abnormal price volatility in listed securities.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Application of strict surveillance actions to vulnerable micro and small companies.
- Safeguarding investors against excessive volatility and speculative trading practices.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under ESM are subjected to enhanced monitoring and risk containment measures.
- Inclusion of applicable stocks into surveillance frameworks in alignment with SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adherence to trading restrictions and margin norms for securities placed under ESM.
- Clients trading in shortlisted securities must comply with applicable margin and settlement terms.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases trading friction and margin requirements for affected securities, reducing speculative activity and protecting market integrity.
