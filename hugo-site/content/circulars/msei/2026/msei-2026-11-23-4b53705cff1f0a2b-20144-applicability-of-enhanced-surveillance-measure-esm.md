## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) issued Circular No. 20144 on November 23, 2026, regarding the Applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). The circular specifies the list of securities shortlisted under the ESM framework based on objective parameters such as price variation and standard deviation.

## Key Points

- ESM framework applies to micro-small companies (Mainboard and SME segments) meeting specific objective parameters.
- Securities listed under ESM are subject to enhanced monitoring and surveillance measures.
- The framework aims to safeguard investor interests and maintain market integrity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance actions including 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and/or periodic call auction as per the applicable ESM stage.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with margin and settlement requirements for scrips shortlisted under ESM.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected scrips will experience restricted liquidity and tighter settlement norms, impacting investors holding or trading in shortlisted securities.
