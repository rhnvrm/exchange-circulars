## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the addition of Mahanandi Coalfields Limited under the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme – II (LES-II) for the Equity segment.

## Key Points

- Security Added: Mahanandi Coalfields Limited (Symbol: MAHACOAL, ISIN: INE03K401026).
- Effective Date: November 30, 2026.
- Segment: Equity Capital Market.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of MAHACOAL in addition to the securities specified in the Exchange’s circular no. MSE/BD/19161/2026 dated June 08, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange participating in LES-II should take note of the inclusion for trading and incentive calculations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market liquidity and trading interest for MAHACOAL on the MSEI Equity segment.
