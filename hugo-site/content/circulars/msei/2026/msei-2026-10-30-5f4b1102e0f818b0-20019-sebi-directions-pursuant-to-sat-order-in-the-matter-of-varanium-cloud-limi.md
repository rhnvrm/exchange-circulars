## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to a Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 9, 2026, in the matter of Varanium Cloud Limited. The SAT order has stayed the impugned SEBI debarment order against Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Reference to Exchange Circular No. MSE/ID/19627/2026 dated August 26, 2026, and SEBI Order dated August 25, 2026, which restrained specific noticees from the securities market.
- Appellants affected: Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta (PAN: AAJPM8210D) and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited (PAN: AAICA9336J).
- Hon’ble SAT vide order 337 of 2026 dated October 09, 2026, stayed the impugned SEBI order noting that the appellant was not a signatory to the prospectus and siphoning of money occurred 8 months after listing.
- SEBI advised compliance with the Hon'ble SAT order via email dated October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Impugned debarment and restraint directions against Jinesh Narrotamdas Mehta and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited have been stayed pursuant to the SAT order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the stay on the debarment and ensure compliance with the SAT order and SEBI directives.

## Important Dates

- August 25, 2026: Original SEBI WTM Order
- August 26, 2026: MSEI Circular No. MSE/ID/19627/2026
- October 9, 2026: Hon'ble SAT Order No. 337 of 2026 staying the impugned order
- October 29, 2026: SEBI email advising compliance with SAT order
- October 30, 2026: MSEI Circular issuance date and immediate enforcement

## Impact Assessment

- Provides relief to the named appellants from market debarment pending final adjudication by SAT (next hearing on January 14, 2027), ensuring member records and compliance controls reflect the stay.
