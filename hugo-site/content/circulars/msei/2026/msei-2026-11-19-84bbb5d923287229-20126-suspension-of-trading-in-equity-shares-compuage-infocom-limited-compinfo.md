## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified members regarding the suspension of trading in equity shares of Compuage Infocom Limited (COMPINFO) under the 'Permitted to trade' category, effective from November 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading in equity shares of Compuage Infocom Limited (COMPINFO) is suspended under the permitted to trade category.
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026.
- Reason: Action initiated as per the Primary Stock Exchange following the liquidation order passed by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, on November 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading privileges for COMPINFO have been revoked in alignment with actions taken by the primary stock exchange and NCLT liquidation directives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange members and trading participants must note the suspension and ensure no trades are executed in COMPINFO shares starting November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NCLT Order Date: November 18, 2026
- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and market participants holding shares of Compuage Infocom Limited face complete trading restrictions on MSEI due to ongoing liquidation proceedings.
