## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities under the Long-Term ASM Framework, effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- BN Agrochem Ltd (Symbol: BNAGROCHEM, ISIN: INE00HZ01011) has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. November 02, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 04, 2026 on all open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created from November 04, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities in Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long term - ASM) Stage I.
- Imposition of 100% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin collection on open positions as on November 03, 2026 and new positions created from November 04, 2026 onwards for securities under ASM.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 30, 2026
- ASM Framework Effective Date: November 02, 2026
- Margin Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

100% margin requirements for impacted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for shortlisted stocks such as BN Agrochem Ltd.
