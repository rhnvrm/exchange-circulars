## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued Circular No. 20143 dated November 23, 2026, announcing the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to certain securities in the Capital Market segment.

## Key Points

- Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for identified securities.
- Designed to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in specific scrips.
- Margin requirements and trading restrictions applied in accordance with regulatory surveillance frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of ST-ASM stages, including enhanced margin requirements for securities meeting surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable margin rates and trading restrictions for securities placed under ST-ASM.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: November 23, 2026
- Effective Date: As specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor protection by monitoring and placing trading controls on securities exhibiting abnormal price or volume movements.
