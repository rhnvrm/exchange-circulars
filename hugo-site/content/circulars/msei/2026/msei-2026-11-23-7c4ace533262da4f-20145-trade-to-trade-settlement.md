## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. 20145 regarding the periodic review and shifting of securities to the Trade to Trade (T2T) settlement segment. This measure is designed to contain market volatility, curb excessive speculation, and safeguard investor interests.

## Key Points

- Securities identified under joint surveillance criteria by SEBI and stock exchanges are moved to the Trade to Trade settlement segment.
- Trading in the T2T segment requires 100% upfront margins and mandatory delivery of shares.
- Intraday netting, square-off, and Buy Today Sell Tomorrow (BTST) transactions are prohibited for scrips in this segment.
- The objective is to ensure heightened surveillance and risk management for volatile or speculative scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Affected securities are transferred to the 'BE' or 'BZ' series for settlement purposes.
- Brokers and trading members must update their trading systems and RMS limits to reflect the T2T status of applicable scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients holding positions in affected securities about the mandatory delivery and margin requirements.
- Compliance with gross settlement rules and prohibition of intraday square-offs for T2T scrips must be strictly enforced.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 23, 2026 (as per circular issuance).

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity in the affected scrips may experience a temporary reduction due to the absence of speculative and intraday trading.
- Enhanced protection for retail investors against abnormal price volatility and speculative manipulation.
