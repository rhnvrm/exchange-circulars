## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the list of securities shortlisted under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, including new additions to Stage-I, upgrades to Stage-II, and the consolidated list effective November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage-I include Le Lavoir Limited (LELAVOIR) and Twentyfirst Century Management Services Ltd (21STCENMGM).
- Securities moving from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II include JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Ltd (RETAIL).
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework across Stage-I and Stage-II is detailed in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures to monitor price volatility and ensure market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable surveillance margins and trading restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities listed under the ESM framework is subject to stricter margin requirements and monitoring mechanisms.
