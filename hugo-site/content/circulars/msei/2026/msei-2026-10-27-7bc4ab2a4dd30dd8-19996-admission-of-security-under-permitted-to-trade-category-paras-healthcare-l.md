## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Paras Healthcare Limited under the "Permitted to Trade" category, effective from October 28, 2026. Companies admitted under this category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Paras Healthcare Limited
- **Symbol:** PARASHLTH
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE661T01029
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Segment:** Capital Market Segment

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard admission under the permitted to trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the admission. Companies admitted under the permitted to trade category do not execute listing agreements with the Exchange and do not provide full disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine trading availability for members in the Capital Market segment for the specified security.
