## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBNOV26) on account of redemption, effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Sovereign Gold Bond (Symbol: SGBNOV26, Series: GB, ISIN: IN0020180314).
- Reason for suspension: Redemption on the Primary Stock Exchange.
- Effective date of suspension: November 11, 2026.
- Trading in the specified security will no longer be available under the permitted to trade category on the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; execution of standard redemption procedures under Exchange Rules, Bye-Laws, and Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note of the trading suspension in SGBNOV26 effective from November 11, 2026, and avoid placing trades in this security thereafter.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Suspension Date: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding SGBNOV26 will have their bonds redeemed as per redemption guidelines. Trading discontinuation on MSEI capital market segment for this ISIN.
