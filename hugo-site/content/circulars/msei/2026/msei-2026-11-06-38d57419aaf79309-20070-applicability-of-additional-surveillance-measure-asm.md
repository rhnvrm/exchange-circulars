## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities, including Vantage Knowledge Academy Ltd (VKAL) and Menon Bearings Limited (MENONBE), effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Vantage Knowledge Academy Ltd (Symbol: VKAL, ISIN: INE427T01025) shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Menon Bearings Limited (Symbol: MENONBE, ISIN: INE071D01033) moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I to Stage - II w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 11, 2026 on all open positions as on November 09, 2026 and new positions created from November 11, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified under the Long-Term ASM framework are subjected to enhanced margin requirements and surveillance measures to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities as per the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM: November 08 / November 09, 2026
- 100% Margin Effective Date: November 11, 2026 (on open positions as on November 09, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will experience tightened liquidity and risk management controls due to 100% margin requirements and surveillance monitoring.
