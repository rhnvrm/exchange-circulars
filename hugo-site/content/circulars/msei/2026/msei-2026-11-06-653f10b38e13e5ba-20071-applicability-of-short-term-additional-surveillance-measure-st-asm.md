## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities effective November 08, 2026, alongside exclusions of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I effective November 08, 2026 include Rushil Decor Ltd, Airo Lam Limited, Vedanta Power Limited, Tonbo Imaging India Limited, AI Champdany Industries Ltd, Jindal Capital Ltd, Kaizen Agro Infrabuild Ltd, Paragon Finance Ltd, Sharda Ispat Ltd, and Yash Chemex Ltd.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. November 09, 2026 on all open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework w.e.f. November 08, 2026 include SMC Global Securities Ltd, Media Matrix Worldwide Limited, Uttam Sugar Mills Limited, and Indo Us Biotech Limited.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must maintain applicable margin rates (50% or higher up to 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted ST-ASM securities starting November 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 06, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM Stage I and Exclusions: November 08, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: November 09, 2026 (on open positions as on November 08, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the affected securities will experience higher margin requirements (up to 100%), impacting liquidity and trading leverage for the shortlisted scrips.
