## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced that specific securities will be moved to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective November 13, 2026, in continuation of previous circulars.

## Key Points

- Affected securities: ABHIINFRA (ISIN: INE01MO01013) and DIGJAMLMTD (ISIN: INE731U01028).
- Trading for these securities will be available in the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE).
- Price band restricted to 5% or lower.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value will be collected from buying trading members and retained till further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved to Stage II of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions and ensure compliance with Trade for Trade settlement and ASD deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 13, 2026 (Friday).
- Circular Date: November 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased liquidity restrictions and margin requirements for trading in the specified securities under surveillance.
