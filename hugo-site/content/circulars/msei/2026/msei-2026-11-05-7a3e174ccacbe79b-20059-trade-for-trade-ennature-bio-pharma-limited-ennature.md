## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the transfer of trading in Ennature Bio Pharma Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ennature Bio Pharma Limited
- Symbol: ENNATURE
- Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) to Rolling Segment (Series: EQ)
- Reference: In continuation of Exchange circular no. MSE/LIST/20049/2026 dated November 03, 2026, and SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- The security is moved from the trade-for-trade category to the normal rolling settlement segment on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange are advised to take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with the applicable trading specifications for the rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Effective Date: November 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the security will now occur under the rolling segment (series: EQ), affecting settlement mechanics in accordance with standard rolling settlement frameworks.
