## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd and other scrips, stating that the debarment of Jignesh Sudhirbhai Shah (PAN: BBNPS1083E) shall continue pursuant to the SAT order dated October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Continuation of debarment for Jignesh Sudhirbhai Shah (PAN: BBNPS1083E) from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities.
- Based on the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 5, 2026, in Appeal No. 318 of 2026 and subsequent SEBI communication.
- Members are advised to take note and ensure strict compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of continued market debarment and prohibition on buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly for the specified entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the restrained entity is not allowed to trade, access the securities market, or be associated with securities in any manner.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 20, 2026
- SAT Order Date: October 5, 2026
- Next SAT Hearing Date: December 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and enforces market integrity by upholding securities market debarments for entities under investigation.
