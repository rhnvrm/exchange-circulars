## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20129/2026 dated November 19, 2026, announcing the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specified securities.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 20, 2026, and new positions created from November 23, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective November 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% with Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 20, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework restrictions in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.
- Mandatory margin requirements and segment shifting for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **November 19, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **November 20, 2026**: Effective date for Stage II price band / open position calculations / shifting.
- **November 23, 2026**: Effective date for margin applicability and segment shifting to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE).

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, including 100% margins and Trade-for-Trade settlement, impacting liquidity and positioning for the affected scrips.
