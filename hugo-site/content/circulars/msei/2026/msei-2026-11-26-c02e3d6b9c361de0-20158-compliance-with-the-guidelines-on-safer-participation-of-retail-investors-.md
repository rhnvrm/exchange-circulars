## Summary

MSEI has issued guidelines regarding compliance with the safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading, specifically addressing the misuse of static IP facilities by algorithmic providers and trading members for retail clients.

## Key Points

- Static IPs are strictly intended for 'Tech-savvy Retail Investors' who develop their own algorithms using programming knowledge, as per SEBI circular dated February 04, 2025.
- Trading members and algo providers must not provide static IP access to retail clients who do not fall under this category.
- Trading members registering tech-savvy clients must obtain an undertaking confirming that the client meets SEBI's definition and has no recourse to the Exchange Investor Grievance mechanism for losses incurred from algorithm logic.
- Violations will lead to penal action against both the trading member and the algo provider, alongside potential suspension or revocation of provisional empanelment status.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict enforcement of SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 and subsequent MSEI circulars regarding algorithmic trading infrastructure.
- Prohibition of unauthorized static IP provisioning under the guise of 'tech-savvy' retail clients.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must verify that tech-savvy retail clients genuinely develop their own algorithms using programming knowledge.
- Mandatory execution of client undertakings regarding qualification and waiver of investor grievance recourse for algorithmic losses.
- Strict monitoring by exchanges to penalize unauthorized static IP usage.

## Important Dates

- Effective framework date: April 01, 2026 (based on SEBI glide path).
- Circular date: November 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on trading members and algorithmic providers offering automated trading solutions to retail clients, requiring immediate audit and cessation of unauthorized static IP allocations.
