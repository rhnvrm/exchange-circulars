## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) issued Circular No. 20097 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specified securities. The measure is designed to monitor volatility and enhance market integrity in micro-cap and shortlisted companies.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted for the first time attract a 100% margin requirement.
- Securities are categorized into different surveillance stages with specific trading controls.
- Stage I securities are subject to Trade-for-Trade settlement and price bands.
- Stage II securities trade through periodic call auctions.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance action under the ESM framework, aligning with SEBI guidelines to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in designated securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open positions and adhere to applicable trading restrictions and price bands for securities listed in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for New Inclusions (Annexure I margins): November 16, 2026 (on open positions as of November 13, 2026).
- Effective Date for Stage Movements and Exclusions (Annexure II): November 13, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and price movements of securities placed under ESM will be restricted due to enhanced margin requirements (100% margin) and strict trading mechanisms, safeguarding investors against undue volatility.
