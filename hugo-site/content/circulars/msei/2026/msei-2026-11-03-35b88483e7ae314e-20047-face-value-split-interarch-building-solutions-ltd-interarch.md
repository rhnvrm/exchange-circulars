## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the face value split (sub-division) of equity shares for Interarch Building Solutions Ltd (INTERARCH). Effective November 30, 2026, the existing equity shares with a face value and paid-up value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Interarch Building Solutions Ltd (INTERARCH)
- **Existing Face & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **New Face & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/- per share
- **Sub-Division Ratio:** One equity share of Rs. 10/- into five equity shares of Rs. 2/-
- **ISIN Impact:** Existing ISIN INE00M901018 will no longer be valid for transactions on the Exchange on or after November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of face value and update of capital structure for the company under the permitted-to-trade category.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange members must take note that transactions executed on or after November 30, 2026, cannot be processed using the old ISIN (INE00M901018).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The stock split enhances retail participation and liquidity by lowering the nominal price per share while increasing the total circulating volume proportionately.
