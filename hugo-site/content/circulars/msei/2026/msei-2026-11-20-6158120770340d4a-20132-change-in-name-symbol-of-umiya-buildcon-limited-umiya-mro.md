## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that the name and symbol of Umiya Buildcon Limited (permitted to trade category) will be changed effective from November 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Company Name: UMIYA BUILDCON LIMITED
- New Company Name: MRO-TEK Networks Limited
- Existing Symbol: UMIYA-MRO
- New Symbol: MROTEK
- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate symbol and name update under exchange rules and bye-laws.

## Compliance Requirements

Members of the exchange are advised to take note of the change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Market participants must update trading terminals and records with the new name and symbol.
