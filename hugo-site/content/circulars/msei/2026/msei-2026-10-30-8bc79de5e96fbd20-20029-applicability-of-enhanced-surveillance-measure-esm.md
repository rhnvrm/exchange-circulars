## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin on open and new positions and will be shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted in ESM Stage-I:** Dcm Shriram Industries Ltd. (DCMSRIND) effective November 03, 2026.
- **Moving from Stage-II to Stage-I:** 9 securities including RamaSteel Tubes Limited, Nupur Recyclers Limited, and Tirupati Forge Limited effective November 02, 2026.
- **Exiting ESM Framework:** 7 securities including Orient Press Limited and Sical Logistics Limited are moving out of the ESM framework.
- **Trading Segment Shift:** Affected securities are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion under ESM attracts a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 02, 2026, and new positions created from November 03, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the margin applicability and trading segment changes for the specified securities.
- Trading members must ensure appropriate risk management and margin collections in line with the ESM framework.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage-I / Stage Shifts / Exits:** November 02, 2026 / November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to heightened surveillance measures, margin requirements, and Trade-for-Trade settlement, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
