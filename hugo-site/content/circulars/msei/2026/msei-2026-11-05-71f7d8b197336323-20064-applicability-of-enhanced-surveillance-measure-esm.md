## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specified securities, including new shortlistings and stage migrations effective November 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage-I: Atlantaa Limited, Zee Media Corporation Limited, and Le Merite Exports Limited w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II: UCAL Limited w.e.f. November 06, 2026.
- ESM Stage-I securities attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on November 06, 2026, and new positions created from November 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage-II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework, including shifting securities between Rolling (EQ) and Trade-for-Trade (BE) segments and imposing heightened margins.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements and account for trading restrictions, price bands, and segment shifts for all impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 05, 2026
- Stage-II Shifting / Price Band Effective Date: November 06, 2026
- Open Positions Evaluation Date: November 06, 2026
- Stage-I / 100% Margin Effective Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shortlisting under ESM introduces heightened surveillance, strict margin rules, and liquidity limitations for affected entities, potentially impacting trading volumes and price volatility.
