## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of MK Sons Fine Jewels Ltd (MKJEWELS) for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the “Permitted to Trade” category w.e.f. November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MK Sons Fine Jewels Ltd
- Symbol: MKJEWELS
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE2IXO01012
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Companies admitted for dealing under the “Permitted to trade” category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the admission of the security for trading on the Capital Market Segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members the ability to trade equity shares of MK Sons Fine Jewels Ltd on the MSEI platform under the permitted category.
