## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of Veefin Solutions Limited for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the \u201cPermitted to Trade\u201d category effective November 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Veefin Solutions Limited
- **Symbol:** VEEFIN
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE0Q0M01015
- **Effective Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Segment:** Capital Market Segment

## Regulatory Changes

None. Companies admitted under the \u201cPermitted to Trade\u201d category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures directly to the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the admission and ensure compliance with trading parameters for securities under the Permitted to Trade category.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access for members in Veefin Solutions Limited shares on the MSEI platform under permitted trading norms.
