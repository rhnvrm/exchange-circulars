## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in equity and equity derivatives segments, while securities in Annexure II move out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Minimum 75% margin imposed in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments on securities listed in Annexure I (Future Market Networks Ltd - FMNL) w.e.f. November 04, 2026, on open positions as of November 03, 2026, and new positions.
- Brahmaputra Infrastructure Ltd (BRAHMINFRA) excluded from the framework effective November 02, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework includes Forbes & Company Ltd, Forbes Precision Tools and Machine Parts Ltd, Race Eco Chain Ltd, BPL Ltd, Indo Tech Transformers Ltd, Eureka Forbes Ltd, Dollex Agrotech Ltd, and Future Market Networks Ltd.
- Measure applies in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 75% margin requirements on securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 75% margin is maintained for open and new positions in shortlisted securities (FMNL) effective November 04, 2026.
- Market participants to take note of exclusions and consolidated list under the framework.

## Important Dates

- November 02, 2026: Effective date for exclusion of securities (Annexure II) and consolidated list applicability.
- November 03, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 75% margin.
- November 04, 2026: Effective date for 75% margin imposition on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (75%) for FMNL positions significantly impact liquidity and trading cost for market participants holding open positions in the security.
