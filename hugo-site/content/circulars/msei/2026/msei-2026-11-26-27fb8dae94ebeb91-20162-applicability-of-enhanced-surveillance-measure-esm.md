## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) issued Circular No. 20162 on November 26, 2026, regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) to securities traded on the exchange. This circular details the periodic review and identification of securities falling under the ESM framework to enhance market integrity and protect investor interests.

## Key Points

- **Framework:** Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) applicable to designated securities.
- **Scope:** Covers micro-small companies and specific securities meeting surveillance criteria as prescribed by SEBI and stock exchanges.
- **Actionable Measures:** Securities identified under ESM are subject to stringent surveillance controls including trade-for-trade settlement and additional margin requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework stages (Stage I / Stage II) as per regulatory guidelines.
- Application of trading restrictions, such as 100% margin requirements and periodic call auctions where applicable, to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in vulnerable securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, trading members, and clearing members must ensure adherence to the surveillance parameters and margin requirements specified for the listed securities.
- Systems must be updated to handle the trade-for-trade settlement and applicable price bands for securities moved into ESM stages.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Issuance:** November 26, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 27, 2026 (or subsequent trading day as per exchange specifications)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Integrity:** Enhances transparency and reduces speculative risk in identified securities.
- **Liquidity & Trading:** May impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the ESM framework due to stricter margin and settlement norms.
