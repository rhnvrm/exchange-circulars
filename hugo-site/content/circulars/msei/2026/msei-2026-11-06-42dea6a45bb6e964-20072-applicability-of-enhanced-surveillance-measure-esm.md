## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability, stage migrations, and removal of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The changes become effective on November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions and new positions w.e.f. November 09, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. November 08, 2026.
- Multiple securities are moving out of the ESM framework entirely.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework restrictions in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margins and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: November 09, 2026 (and November 08, 2026 where specified for Stage-II / moving out).

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading mechanisms for specified securities are restricted due to shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment and imposition of surveillance margins.
