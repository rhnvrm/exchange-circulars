## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/TRD/20158/2026 regarding compliance with guidelines on the safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading, specifically concerning tech-savvy retail investors.

## Key Points

- Static IPs are strictly intended only for 'Tech-savvy Retail Investors' who develop their own algos using programming knowledge.
- Trading Members and Algo Providers must not provide Static IP access to retail clients who do not fall under this category.
- Trading Members must obtain a specific undertaking from registering Tech Savvy Clients confirming they meet SEBI's definition and waive recourse to the Exchange Investor Grievance mechanism for losses incurred from algo logic.
- Violations will result in penal action against both the Trading Member and Algo Provider, alongside review, suspension, or revocation of the Algo Provider's provisional empanelment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration and enforcement of SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD/P/CIR/2025/0000013 and previous MSEI circulars requiring strategies to run on broker servers, except where static IPs are legally permitted for tech-savvy retail developers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no misuse of static IP facilities by algo providers or retail clients.
- Mandatory collection of undertakings from tech-savvy clients regarding programming knowledge and waiver of grievance mechanism.
- Strict adherence and immediate corrective action where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Circular issued on November 26, 2026.
- Applicable with immediate effect.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances risk controls and regulatory oversight on retail algorithmic trading, holding brokers and algo providers directly accountable for unauthorized static IP allocations and compliance breaches.
