## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/20127/2026 dated November 19, 2026, announcing the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I for certain securities.

## Key Points

- Three securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure Stage I effective November 20, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities: Balaxi Pharma Ltd (BALAXI), Centenial Surgical Suture Ltd (CSURGSU), and Galaxy Bearings Ltd (GALXBRG).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. November 25, 2026 on all open positions as on November 23, 2026 and new positions created from November 25, 2026 onwards.
- Other stages (Stage II, III, IV and movement between stages) have nil securities listed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I are subjected to enhanced margin requirements (100% margin).
- The framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the 100% margin requirement on open positions and new positions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 19, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion: November 20, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: November 25, 2026 (on open positions as on November 23, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities (BALAXI, CSURGSU, GALXBRG) will face higher capital requirements due to the 100% margin rule, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
