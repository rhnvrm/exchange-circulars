## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives segment from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, November 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, November 28, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or create rights and liabilities.
- Setup files available for download from the SFTP server.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in terms of provisions of Rules, Bye-Laws & Regulations of the Exchange and reference to SEBI circular regarding review of market timings and settlement methodology for derivatives contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to participate actively in the mock trading session.
- Download setup files from SFTP server on Friday, November 27, 2026, after trading hours.
- Follow configuration steps for Leased Line or Internet mode as specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Setup Download: November 27, 2026
- Mock Trading Session: November 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No financial obligations or margin requirements arise from mock trades; ensures operational readiness and DR site resilience.
