## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified members regarding the change in name and trading symbol for Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited under the permitted to trade category.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited
- New Name: Shukra Medtech Limited
- Existing Symbol: SHUKRAPHAR
- New Symbol: SHUKRAMED
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate identification update reflecting company name and symbol modification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to update their trading systems and records with the new name and symbol (SHUKRAMED) effective from November 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: November 17, 2026
- Effective Date: November 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market operations. Members and investors must use the new trading symbol SHUKRAMED for all future transactions and record-keeping.
