## Summary

SEBI and recognized stock exchanges have jointly released a rationalized and standardized penalty framework for trading members. The initiative addresses differential penalty structures across multiple exchanges, aiming to eliminate regulatory arbitrage and enhance the ease of doing business for stockbrokers.

## Key Points

- Stockbrokers holding memberships across multiple exchanges previously faced inconsistent penalty frameworks.
- A dedicated Working Group comprising representatives from stock exchanges and stockbroker associations (ANMI, BBF, CPAI) was constituted by SEBI.
- Procedural lapses are now distinguished from material violations and classified as 'financial disincentive'.
- The unified framework applies across recognized Indian stock exchanges including NSE, BSE, MCX, and MSEI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Rationalization and standardization of penalty frameworks levied by stock exchanges on trading members.
- Differentiation between procedural lapses and material violations/non-compliances.
- Reclassification of certain erstwhile procedural violations into 'financial disincentive'.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and stockbrokers must align with the standardized penalty framework and compliance guidelines adopted uniformly across all stock exchanges.

## Important Dates

- **Press Release Date:** October 10, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reduces compliance friction, enhances transparency, and removes regulatory arbitrage for brokers operating across multiple exchanges.
