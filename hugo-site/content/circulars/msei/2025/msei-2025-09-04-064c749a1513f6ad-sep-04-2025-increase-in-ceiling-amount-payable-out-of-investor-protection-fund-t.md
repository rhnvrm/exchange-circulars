## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) announced an increase in the maximum permissible limit against a single claim from the Investor Protection Fund Trust (IPF) from Rs. 2 Lakh to Rs. 2.2 Lakh per investor per claim, pursuant to Chapter XVI, Clause 13 of the Exchange Byelaws.

## Key Points

- Single claim limit increased from Rs. 2 Lakh to Rs. 2.2 Lakh per investor per claim.
- Applicable to claims received against trading members declared as defaulters or expelled.
- Effective immediately from the date of the press release.

## Regulatory Changes

- Upward revision of the Investor Protection Fund Trust claim ceiling under Chapter XVI, Clause 13 of the Exchange Byelaws.

## Compliance Requirements

- Exchange mechanisms and administrative processes updated to reflect the enhanced compensation limit for eligible investor claims against defaulting or expelled members.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 4, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced financial safety and higher compensation ceilings for retail investors affected by trading member defaults or expulsions.
