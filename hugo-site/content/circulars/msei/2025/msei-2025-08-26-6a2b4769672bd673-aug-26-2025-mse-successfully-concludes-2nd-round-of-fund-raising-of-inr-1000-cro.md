## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSE) has successfully concluded its second round of capital raising, securing INR 1000 crores to strengthen its market position, operational capabilities, and technological infrastructure. Additionally, shareholders have approved the reappointment of Ms. Latika S Kundu as Managing Director and CEO for a further term of three years.

## Key Points

- Successfully completed the second round of capital raising, securing INR 1000 crores.
- Shareholders approved the reappointment of Ms. Latika S Kundu as MD & CEO for a 3-year term.
- Funds will be utilized to deepen market liquidity in Equity Cash and Derivatives segments, introduce innovative products, and establish a state-of-the-art data center.
