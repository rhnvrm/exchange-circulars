## Summary

Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs), comprising exchanges, clearing corporations, and depositories, successfully implemented the Direct Payout Settlement mechanism for securities on February 25, 2025, under the guidance of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Key Points

- Implementation Date: February 25, 2025.
- Guided by SEBI across MIIs (Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, and Depositories).
- Ensures seamless direct credit of securities to clients' demat accounts.
- Enhances market integrity, transparency, and operational efficiency.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption and rollout of the Direct Payout Settlement mechanism for securities market transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market intermediaries and participants must align operations with the direct payout settlement workflow.

## Important Dates

- Implementation Date: February 25, 2025
- Announcement Date: March 02, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly improves investor protection and settlement efficiency by streamlining the direct transfer of securities to investor demat accounts.
