## Summary

On October 10, 2025, during World Investor Week 2025 (WIW-2025), the MSE IPF Trust launched 'Nivesh ka Hasyamanch', a unique initiative merging finance and humour through stand-up comedy. The event featured SEBI Whole Time Member Shri Kamlesh Varshney and actor Rajpal Yadav, emphasizing financial scam prevention, safe investment habits, and retail investor education.

## Key Points

- MSE IPF Trust launched 'Nivesh ka Hasyamanch' on October 10, 2025, to promote financial literacy through stand-up comedy and storytelling.
- The event was graced by Chief Guest Shri Kamlesh Varshney (Whole Time Member, SEBI) and Special Guest Rajpal Yadav.
- Comedians performed routines blending practical investing concepts with awareness against financial frauds and pump-and-dump schemes.
- SEBI WTM Shri Kamlesh Varshney highlighted that over 90% of retail traders experience losses in Futures and Options (F&O) and cautioned senior citizens against fake investment apps.
- Ms. Latika S. Kundu (MD & CEO, MSE) emphasized the Exchange's commitment to reaching all sections of society to foster an investment-driven mindset.
