## Summary

Stock exchanges issued a joint press release on March 28, 2025, announcing the revised list of designated Qualified Stock Brokers (QSBs). QSBs are stock brokers occupying significant market positions due to client assets, trading volumes, and client funds handled, and are subject to enhanced compliance and monitoring obligations under SEBI circulars.

## Key Points

- Lists 11 stock brokers designated as QSBs based on parameters including active clients, total assets of clients, trading volumes, end-of-day margin obligations, proprietary trading volumes, compliance scores, and grievance redressal scores.
- Features dedicated lists for brokers meeting specific compliance and grievance redressal score parameters.
- Mandates that designated QSBs meet enhanced obligations and discharge additional responsibilities as per comprehensive operating guidelines issued by NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX, and NCDEX.
- Existing QSBs not in the current list must continue complying with enhanced obligations for an additional period of 3 financial years or as specified by MIIs in consultation with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated designation of Qualified Stock Brokers (QSBs) pursuant to SEBI Circulars No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/24 and SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2024/14.

## Compliance Requirements

- Designated QSBs must comply with enhanced operational, risk management, and compliance guidelines.
- Transition rules apply to previously designated QSBs not appearing in the current list.

## Important Dates

- Press Release Date: March 28, 2025
- Framework effective per prior SEBI circulars (February 06, 2023, and March 11, 2024) and MII operating guidelines (June 2023).

## Impact Assessment

- Strengthens monitoring and compliance standards for major stock brokers, enhancing investor protection and market stability across Indian securities markets.
