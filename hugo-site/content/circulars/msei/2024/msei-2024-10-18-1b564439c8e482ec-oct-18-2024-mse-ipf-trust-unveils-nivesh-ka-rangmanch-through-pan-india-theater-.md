## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange Investor Protection Fund Trust (MSE IPF Trust) hosted the grand finale of its pan-India theater competition 'Nivesh ka Rangmanch' on October 17, 2024, as part of World Investor Week 2024 (WIW-2024). The event combined art and storytelling with finance to educate the next generation of investors on key concepts such as compounding, risk management, fraud prevention, and asset diversification.

## Key Points

- Over 250 college and professional theater groups participated, including prestigious institutions like IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, and IIT Kharagpur.
- The event was graced by Chief Guest Mr. G.P. Garg (Executive Director, SEBI) along with industry veterans and Bollywood personalities as judges.
- Naatakwaale Theater Company won the competition, followed by Roobaru Roshni Theater Company as first runner-up and Aawara Theater Group as second runner-up.
- The initiative aims to leverage theater and storytelling to foster financial literacy and inclusive economic growth across smaller cities and towns in India.
