## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued a public caution against unauthorized dabba trading and illegal trading platforms operated by individuals Mohit Sharma and Santosh Yadav associated with 'V Money Traders' (mobile numbers 8718080802 and 9244121923).

## Key Points

- Unregistered entities Mohit Sharma and Santosh Yadav (V Money Traders) are illegally providing dabba/unauthorized trading platforms.
- A police complaint has been lodged against the individuals involved.
- Contravention of SCRA sections 13, 16, 17, or 19 attracts imprisonment up to 10 years, fines up to ₹25 crores, or both under Section 23(1).
- Dabba trading also violates IPC sections 406 (criminal breach of trust), 420 (cheating), and 120-B (criminal conspiracy).
- Investors participating in such platforms do not have access to exchange dispute resolution mechanisms, investor protection, or grievance redressal.

## Regulatory Changes

None (Public advisory and caution notice).

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors are strongly advised NOT to trade on illegal dabba trading platforms or subscribe to unauthorized schemes.
- Verify registration status of entities and authorized persons before engaging in securities trading.

## Important Dates

- **Press Release Date:** April 19, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Protects retail investors from financial fraud and illegal dabba trading schemes while reinforcing regulatory compliance and enforcement actions by the Exchange.
