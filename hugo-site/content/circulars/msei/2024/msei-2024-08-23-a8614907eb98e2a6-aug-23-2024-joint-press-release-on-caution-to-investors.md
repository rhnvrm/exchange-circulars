## Summary

MSEI and other stock exchanges have issued a joint press release cautioning investors against fraudulent activities perpetrated by unscrupulous entities operating through social media platforms (WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram) and mobile numbers. Fraudsters impersonate reputed financial institutions and SEBI, falsely offering trading opportunities through Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) or Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) sub-accounts and fake institutional accounts with special privileges.

## Key Points

- Fraudsters utilize unregulated trading platforms and unauthorized applications resembling legitimate trading members.
- Common deceptive practices include offering guaranteed returns in capital, forex, and commodities, managing trading accounts via stolen credentials, and false promises of pre-IPO subscriptions.
- Investors are lured into WhatsApp and Telegram groups promoting upper circuit stocks, block deals, and preferential allotment.
- Victims are instructed to transfer funds to third-party bank accounts where money is immediately siphoned off, leaving only fake paper profits visible on fraudulent apps.
- Suspected fraud communications can be reported via Chakshu (www.sancharsaathi.gov.in) and cybercrime helpline 1930 (www.cybercrime.gov.in).

## Regulatory Changes

- Reaffirms existing prohibitions against unauthorized portfolio management, guaranteed return schemes, and illegal dabba trading services.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors must exercise due caution, avoid joining unverified stock advisory groups on social media, refrain from downloading unauthorized trading apps, and never share login credentials or sensitive information.

## Important Dates

- Issued on August 23, 2024.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases market awareness and vigilance against digital financial scams, protecting retail investors from fraudulent trading apps and unauthorized advisory services.
