## Summary

On July 20, 2024, a joint press release was issued by all exchanges addressing the global Microsoft systems outage of July 19, 2024. All Indian stock exchanges and clearing corporations functioned without any impact, with only 11 trading members out of a 1400+ ecosystem reporting disruptions that were resolved or under resolution.

## Key Points

- Global Microsoft systems outage occurred on July 19, 2024, causing disruptions worldwide.
- All Indian Exchanges and Clearing Corporations functioned without any impact.
- Only 11 out of 1400+ trading members reported disruptions, which were swiftly handled.
- Detailed institutional profiles of NSE and NCDEX highlighting robust infrastructure.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced through this press release.

No compliance requirements or actions are mandated for market participants.

- Press Release Date: July 20, 2024

## Impact Assessment

The global IT outage had a negligible impact on Indian securities trading and clearing activities, demonstrating high market resilience and robust technological infrastructure.
